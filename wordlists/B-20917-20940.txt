shoe
/ʃuː/
核心：名词，可数：鞋：a pair of shoes；马蹄铁。动词（shod, shod）：给（马）钉马蹄铁。习语：in somebody's shoes 是处在某人的位置；the shoe is on the other foot 是情况颠倒过来了。
① I need a new pair of shoes for my cousin's wedding next month.
= 下个月我表姐的婚礼，我需要一双新鞋。
shoe = 鞋
a pair of shoes = 一双鞋
② We went to the shoe shop on the high street to buy school shoes for the children.
= 我们去大街上的鞋店给孩子们买上学穿的鞋。
shoe shop = 鞋店
③ If I were in your shoes, I would tell the teacher the truth.
= 如果我处在你的位置，我会把实话告诉老师。
in someone's shoes = 设身处地
④ He used to boss me around at school, but now he works for me, so the shoe is on the other foot.
= 他上学时老对我指手画脚，可现在他给我打工，情况整个颠倒过来了。
the shoe is on the other foot = 情况颠倒过来了
⑤ The blacksmith shod the horse in the village square while the children watched.
= 铁匠在村子广场上给那匹马钉马掌，孩子们在一旁看着。
shod = 钉了马蹄铁（shoe 的过去式）
⑥ Put a shoe tree in your leather boots after you take them off, so that they keep their shape.
= 皮靴脱下来后撑上鞋楦，这样能保持靴形。
a shoe tree = 鞋楦

shoebox
/ˈʃuːbɒks/
核心：名词，可数：鞋盒；（口语）极小的房子或房间。
① My grandmother kept all her old letters and photos in a shoebox under the bed.
= 我奶奶把所有旧信和照片都放在床底下的一个鞋盒里。
a shoebox = 鞋盒
shoebox = 鞋盒
② They live in a shoebox of a flat in the city centre, but they love being close to everything.
= 他们住在市中心一套鸽子笼似的公寓里，但很喜欢那里去哪儿都近。
a shoebox of a flat = 鸽子笼似的公寓
