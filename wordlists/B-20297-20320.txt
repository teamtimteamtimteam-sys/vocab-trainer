secretive
/ˈsiːkrətɪv/
核心：形容词：（人）惯于保密的、遮遮掩掩的：be secretive about something。副词 secretively 是遮遮掩掩地。名词 secretiveness 另立条。
① He has always been very secretive about his work, so we have no idea how much he earns.
= 他对自己的工作一向遮遮掩掩，所以我们根本不知道他挣多少钱。
be secretive about something = 对某事遮遮掩掩
secretive = 惯于保密的
② The two girls whispered secretively in the corner and laughed whenever anyone came near.
= 那两个女孩在角落里偷偷地说悄悄话，谁一走近她们就笑。
secretively = 遮遮掩掩地

secretiveness
/ˈsiːkrətɪvnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：遮遮掩掩、守口如瓶的习性。
① His secretiveness about money made his wife suspicious, so she checked the bank statements.
= 他在钱的事上遮遮掩掩，引起了妻子的怀疑，于是她查了银行对账单。
somebody's secretiveness = 某人的遮遮掩掩
secretiveness = 遮遮掩掩
② The secretiveness of the company made journalists even more curious about its plans.
= 那家公司的守口如瓶让记者们对它的计划更加好奇。
the secretiveness of something = 某机构的守口如瓶

sect
/sekt/
核心：名词，可数：（宗教的）派别、教派；（常含贬义）邪教团体；（政治）派系、宗派。
① The village was home to a small religious sect whose members wore only black clothes.
= 那个村子住着一个小教派，教徒们只穿黑衣服。
a religious sect = 教派
a sect = 派别
② The police were worried that the sect was keeping its members away from their families.
= 警方担心那个邪教团体在阻止成员与家人接触。
the sect = 邪教团体

sectarian
/sekˈteəriən/
核心：形容词：宗派的、教派之间的：sectarian violence；（贬义）宗派主义的、狭隘排外的。
① Years of sectarian violence divided the city into two parts that rarely met.
= 多年的教派暴力把那座城市分成了两半，彼此很少往来。
sectarian violence = 教派暴力
sectarian = 宗派的
② The new leader promised to end the sectarian divisions in the party.
= 新领导人承诺消除党内的宗派分歧。
sectarian divisions = 宗派分歧
