flourish
核心：三支。一是兴旺、蓬勃：business flourished；二是（植物）长得茂盛；
三是名词，夸张的一挥或花体笔画：with a flourish。
① The business flourished for a decade and closed in one very bad winter.
= 那门生意兴旺了十年，在一个极糟的冬天关了张。
flourish = 兴旺
② Basil flourishes on that windowsill and dies everywhere else in the flat.
= 罗勒在那个窗台上长得好，这套房子别处都活不成。
flourish = 长得茂盛
③ He signed with a flourish and spelled his own name wrong.
= 他大笔一挥签了字，把自己的名字拼错了。
with a flourish = 夸张地一挥

floury
核心：两支。一是沾满面粉的：floury hands；二是（土豆）粉质易散的。
① Floury hands on a door handle mark the whole morning.
= 沾着面粉的手一摸门把手，一上午的痕迹就留下了。
floury hands = 沾满面粉的手
② A floury potato mashes well and falls apart in a salad.
= 粉质土豆很好压泥，做沙拉就散了。
a floury potato = 粉质土豆

flout
核心：（正式）公然违抗、无视规则：flout the rules。
注意别跟 flaunt（炫耀）弄混，这是高频错。
① They flout the parking rules and treat the fines as a cost of business.
= 他们公然无视停车规定，把罚款当经营成本。
flout the rules = 公然违反规定
② A law that everybody flouts gets replaced or gets forgotten.
= 一条人人都不理的法律，不是被改掉就是被忘掉。
flout something = 公然违抗某事

flow
核心：四支。一是流动：water flows；二是（车流、信息的）流：a flow of traffic；
三是名词，流量；四是习语 go with the flow，随大流。
① Water flows through that channel for about four months a year.
= 水在那条水道里流的时间，一年大约四个月。
flow = 流动
② A steady flow of traffic past the window is what she misses about the city.
= 窗外川流不息的车，正是她怀念城市的地方。
a flow of traffic = 车流
③ Go with the flow for one meeting and you will find yourself on the committee.
= 随一次大流，你就发现自己进了委员会。
go with the flow = 随大流
flow from = 由…产生
Everything else flows from one decision taken in a corridor.
= 其余的一切，都由走廊上作出的一个决定而来。
flow-on = 连带影响
The flow-on from that strike reached three industries within a fortnight.
= 那场罢工的连带影响，两周之内波及三个行业。

flower
核心：三支。一是花：a bunch of flowers；二是动词，开花；
三是习语 in flower，正在花期。
① A bunch of flowers on that desk lasts nine days and is never watered.
= 那张桌上一束花能撑九天，从来没人浇水。
a bunch of flowers = 一束花
② The tree flowers in May and drops the lot in one windy afternoon.
= 那棵树五月开花，一个刮风的下午全落光。
flower = 开花
③ The orchard is in flower for a fortnight and photographed for a year.
= 那片果园花期两个星期，照片能发一年。
in flower = 正在花期
flower arranger = 插花的人
The flower arranger uses what the churchyard grows and buys nothing.
= 那位插花的人只用教堂墓地里长的花，一样也不买。
flower arranging = 插花
Flower arranging fills the hall on Tuesdays and empties it by four.
= 周二的插花班把礼堂坐满，四点就空了。
flower children = 花童一代
The flower children of that summer are grandparents with strong opinions.
= 那年夏天的花童一代，如今是主见很强的祖父母。
flower girl = 婚礼上的花童；卖花女
The flower girl dropped the basket and threw the petals by hand.
= 那个花童把篮子掉了，改用手一把把地撒花瓣。
flower power = 花的力量（六十年代和平主张）
Flower power lasted one summer and the posters lasted rather longer.
= 花的力量只热了一个夏天，海报倒留得久些。

flowered
核心：印花的、有花纹的：多作定语 —— a flowered dress。
① A flowered dress and a plain coat is the compromise she settled on.
= 印花裙配素外套，是她定下的折中。
a flowered dress = 印花连衣裙
② The flowered wallpaper came off in one piece and left an outline behind.
= 那面带花墙纸整张揭了下来，留下一个轮廓。
flowered wallpaper = 带花墙纸

flowering
核心：两支。一是开花这件事：the flowering of the cherry；
二是引申，（文化、才能的）繁盛。不可数为主，也作定语。
① The flowering lasts nine days and the coach parties book eleven.
= 花期九天，旅游大巴订了十一天。
the flowering of something = 某物的开花
② A flowering of poetry in that decade came out of two cheap printing presses.
= 那十年诗歌的繁盛，出自两台便宜的印刷机。
a flowering of something = 某事的繁盛

flowerpot
核心：花盆。可数。
① The flowerpot cracked in the frost and the plant carried on regardless.
= 那个花盆冻裂了，里头的植物照样长。
a flowerpot = 花盆
a clay flowerpot = 陶花盆
A clay flowerpot dries out twice as fast and the plants prefer it.
= 陶花盆干得快一倍，植物倒更喜欢。
② Nine flowerpots by the step and one of them hides the spare key.
= 台阶边九个花盆，其中一个底下藏着备用钥匙。

flowery
核心：两支。一是花朵图案的、花香的；二是（文风）辞藻华丽的。
① A flowery scent from the hedge reaches the road on warm evenings.
= 暖和的傍晚，树篱那股花香能飘到路上。
a flowery scent = 花香
② The letter is flowery and says nothing that a postcard could not.
= 那封信辞藻华丽，说的话一张明信片就写得下。
flowery language = 辞藻华丽的措辞

flu
核心：流感：influenza 的常用简说。不可数 —— have the flu、a flu jab。
① She had the flu for a week and worked through five days of it.
= 她得了一星期流感，其中五天照样上班。
have the flu = 得流感
② The flu jab is free after sixty-five and queued for in October.
= 六十五岁以上打流感疫苗免费，十月里要排队。
a flu jab = 流感疫苗针

flub
核心：（美式，非正式）搞砸、说错：flub a line 是台词说错。
① He flubbed the line and the audience laughed at the right moment anyway.
= 他把台词说错了，观众照样在该笑的地方笑了。
flub a line = 说错台词
② She flubbed the first serve twice and won the game regardless.
= 她第一发球连错两次，那一局照样赢了。
flub something = 把某事搞砸

fluctuate
核心：波动、起伏不定：说价格、体重、人数。名词 fluctuation。
① Numbers fluctuate between forty and ninety and that room seats sixty.
= 人数在四十到九十之间浮动，那间屋子坐得下六十。
fluctuate between = 在……之间波动
② Prices fluctuate with the weather and settle down in September.
= 价格随天气波动，到九月才稳下来。
fluctuate = 波动

fluctuation
核心：波动、起伏：可数不可数都行 —— fluctuations in something。
① Fluctuations in temperature ruin the wine and the cellar has no thermometer.
= 温度的起伏毁掉那些酒，地窖里却没有温度计。
fluctuations in something = 某物的波动
② A daily fluctuation of two kilos is water and not weight.
= 一天两公斤的浮动是水分，不是体重。
a fluctuation = 一次波动

flue
核心：烟道：炉子或壁炉通到屋外的那条管道。可数。
① The flue was blocked by a nest and the whole room filled with smoke.
= 烟道被一个鸟窝堵住，整间屋子灌满了烟。
a flue = 烟道
② A flue should be swept once a year and is remembered once a decade.
= 烟道一年该扫一次，实际上十年才想起一回。
sweep a flue = 清扫烟道

fluency
核心：流利、流畅：说语言，也说演奏与动作。不可数。
① Fluency in three languages got her the job and the hardest desk in it.
= 三门语言的流利让她拿到那份工作，也拿到里头最难的位子。
fluency in a language = 某语言的流利
② Fluency comes back a good deal faster than grammar does.
= 流利度回来得比语法快得多。
fluency = 流利

fluent
核心：两支。一是（语言）说得流利的：be fluent in Spanish；
二是（文笔、动作）流畅自如的。副词 fluently。
① She is fluent in Spanish and reads it a good deal better than she speaks it.
= 她西班牙语说得流利，读得比说得还好得多。
be fluent in something = 某语言说得流利
② His prose is fluent and the argument inside it goes round in circles.
= 他文笔流畅，里头的论证却在原地打转。
fluent prose = 流畅的文字
副词 fluently：流利地，多修饰 speaks、reads、writes。
③ He speaks fluently and stops the moment somebody asks a real question.
= 他讲得流利，一有人问真问题就卡住。
speak fluently = 说得流利
④ She writes fluently in a language she only learned at forty.
= 她用一门四十岁才学的语言写得很流畅。
write fluently = 写得流畅

fluff
核心：三支。一是绒毛、线头：pocket fluff；二是（非正式）无关紧要的软性内容；
三是动词，把台词说砸：fluff your lines。形容词 fluffy。
① Pocket fluff and a bus ticket is everything that was in that coat.
= 那件外套里全部的家当，就是些口袋绒毛加一张公交票。
fluff = 绒毛、线头
② The programme is fluff and gets four times the audience of the news.
= 那档节目全是软性内容，收视是新闻的四倍。
fluff = 空洞无营养的内容
③ He fluffed his lines twice and got the biggest laugh of the night.
= 他两次说砸台词，反倒赢得全场最响的笑声。
fluff your lines = 把台词说砸

fluffy
核心：两支。一是毛茸茸的、松软的：a fluffy towel；二是（贬）内容空洞的。
① A fluffy towel lasts two washes and then behaves like every other towel.
= 松软的毛巾洗两回，就跟别的毛巾没两样了。
a fluffy towel = 松软的毛巾
② The report is fluffy in the middle and precise about the money.
= 那份报告中间部分空泛，说到钱倒很精确。
fluffy = 空洞的

flugelhorn
核心：（乐器）富鲁格号：形状近似小号、音色更圆润的铜管乐器。可数。
① The flugelhorn takes the melody and the trumpet takes everything above it.
= 富鲁格号担着旋律，小号包了上面所有的音。
a flugelhorn = 富鲁格号
② A flugelhorn is heavier than it looks and warmer than it sounds on record.
= 富鲁格号比看上去沉，真声也比录音里更暖。

fluid
核心：三支。一是液体：brake fluid；二是（局势、形状）尚在变动的：a fluid situation；
三是（动作）流畅的。名词 fluidity。
① Brake fluid absorbs water and gets changed less often than it should be.
= 刹车油会吸水，换的次数总比该换的少。
brake fluid = 刹车油
② The situation is fluid and the deadline is not.
= 局势尚在变动，截止日期可不变。
a fluid situation = 尚在变动的局势
fluid ounce = 液量盎司
A fluid ounce is not the same on both sides of the Atlantic.
= 大西洋两岸的液量盎司，不是同一个量。

fluidity
核心：两支。一是流动性；二是（动作、界线的）灵活多变。不可数。
① The fluidity of the paint decides how far a single brushstroke carries.
= 颜料的流动性，决定一笔能拖多远。
the fluidity of something = 某物的流动性
② Fluidity in that timetable means nobody knows when anything starts.
= 那份时间表太多变，谁也不知道什么时候开始。
fluidity = 灵活多变

fluke
核心：三支。一是侥幸、凑巧：by a fluke；二是（鲸尾、锚上的）叶片；
三是（寄生虫）吸虫。可数。形容词 fluky。
① The goal was a fluke and the replay makes it look deliberate.
= 那个进球纯属侥幸，回放看着倒像故意的。
a fluke = 侥幸
② By a fluke the two of them caught the same delayed train.
= 巧得很，他们俩赶上了同一班晚点的火车。
by a fluke = 碰巧
③ Liver fluke gets into the sheep from the wet corner of that field.
= 肝吸虫是从那块地潮湿的角落钻进羊身上的。
liver fluke = 肝吸虫

fluky
核心：（非正式）侥幸的、飘忽不定的：说结果靠运气，也说风向没准。也拼 flukey。
① A fluky win in the first round paid for the whole season.
= 第一轮一场侥幸的胜利，养活了整个赛季。
a fluky win = 侥幸的胜利
② The wind was fluky all afternoon and every crew blamed it.
= 那一下午风向飘忽不定，每条船的船员都怪它。
fluky = 飘忽不定的

flume
核心：两支。一是（水上乐园的）滑水道；二是引水槽：山里送木材与水的木槽。可数。
① The flume takes ninety seconds and the queue for it takes fifty minutes.
= 滑一趟九十秒，排队要五十分钟。
a flume = 滑水道
② The old flume carried timber down the valley and rotted in one wet decade.
= 那条老引水槽把木材顺山谷送下去，在一个多雨的十年里烂掉了。
a flume = 引水槽

flummery
核心：两支。一是（旧）空话套话、恭维的废话；二是旧式甜点奶冻。不可数。
① The speech was flummery and the decision had been taken in the car park.
= 那番讲话是空话，决定早在停车场里就做完了。
flummery = 空话套话
② Flummery in that cookbook takes four hours and sets in nine minutes.
= 那本食谱里的奶冻做四个钟头，九分钟就凝住。
flummery = 奶冻
