ice
/aɪs/
核心：冰 —— 水冻成的固体，不可数：ice on the roads、two cubes of ice。从「冰」长出：
一、冰块、冰饮与冰上的一切：一大串复合词 —— ice cream、ice cube、ice rink、ice skating、
ice dancing、ice lolly、ice pack、ice cap、ice sheet、ice shelf、ice age……；
二、英式里可数的 an ice 指一份冰淇淋、冰棍；
三、动词：给蛋糕抹糖霜（ice a cake）；ice over / ice up（结冰、被冰封住）；
四、比喻「冷」：语气、眼神冷冰冰（ice in her voice）；
五、习语：break the ice（打破沉默，A 表教过）、on thin ice（如履薄冰）、
put something on ice（把事搁置）、cut no ice with somebody（打动不了某人）；
俚语里 ice 还指钻石。
① There was ice on the roads, so the school buses were cancelled.
= 路面结了冰，校车都停开了。
ice = 冰
本义，不可数：说「一块冰」要说 a piece of ice、an ice cube。
② Would you like ice in your drink?
= 你的饮料要加冰吗？

③ He dropped two ice cubes into the glass and poured the lemonade.
= 他往杯子里放了两块冰，倒上柠檬汽水。
an ice cube = 冰块
④ The champagne was chilling in an ice bucket beside the table.
= 香槟放在桌边的冰桶里冰着。
an ice bucket = 冰桶
⑤ They kept the fish fresh in an ice chest on the back of the boat.
= 他们把鱼放在船尾的冰柜里保鲜。
an ice chest = 冰柜、冷藏箱
⑥ In summer the waiters bring a jug of ice water to every table.
= 夏天服务员给每张桌子送上一壶冰水。

⑦ The children had chocolate ice cream after the swim.
= 游完泳，孩子们吃了巧克力冰淇淋。

⑧ She bought three ice lollies from the van outside the school.

an ice lolly = 冰棍（英式）
⑨ Two strawberry ices, please, and a coffee.
= 两份草莓冰淇淋，再来一杯咖啡，谢谢。
an ice = 一份冰淇淋、冰棍（英式）
可数用法：英式口语里指一份冰点。
⑩ Put an ice pack on your ankle to keep the swelling down.
= 在脚踝上敷个冰袋，消消肿。
an ice pack = 冰袋
⑪ The climbers cut steps in the glacier with an ice axe.
= 登山者用冰镐在冰川上凿出台阶。
an ice axe = 冰镐
⑫ He chipped the block apart with an ice pick.
= 他用冰锥把冰块凿开了。
an ice pick = 冰锥
⑬ The town has an ice rink that stays open until midnight at weekends.

⑯ She was a promising ice skater before she became an ice dancer.

ideal
/aɪˈdɪəl/
核心：形容词：理想的、完美的 —— 最合适的；也指只存在于想象中的。
名词：理想、典范 —— 一种追求的原则或完美的标准。
同族并入本条：ideally（理想的情况下；最合适地）、idealism / idealist / idealistic
（理想主义；A 表教过 idealist）、idealize（把…理想化）与名词 idealization。
① The weather was ideal for a picnic, warm but not too hot.
= 天气非常适合野餐，暖和而不太热。
be ideal for something = 非常适合…
② In an ideal world, everyone would have a garden.
= 在理想的世界里，每个人都会有个花园。
in an ideal world = 理想情况下
③ She never gave up the ideals she had held as a student.
= 她从没放弃学生时代就坚持的理想。
ideals = 理想、信念
④ Ideally, the meeting should take place before the end of the month.
= 最理想的是，会议在月底前开。
ideally = 理想的情况下
⑤ The house is ideally situated, five minutes from the sea.
= 那栋房子的位置非常理想，离海只有五分钟。
ideally situated = 位置非常理想
⑥ She's an idealist who believes every problem can be solved by talking.
= 她是个理想主义者，相信每个问题都能靠谈话解决。
an idealist = 理想主义者
⑦ The idealism of the young volunteers impressed everyone.
= 年轻志愿者们的理想主义打动了所有人。
idealism = 理想主义
⑧ His plans were idealistic and took no account of the budget.
= 他的计划很理想化，完全没考虑预算。
idealistic = 理想主义的、不切实际的
⑨ Old people tend to idealize their childhood.
= 老人往往会把自己的童年理想化。
idealize something = 把某事理想化
⑩ The film is an idealization of country life that ignores the hard work.
= 那部电影把乡村生活理想化了，全然不顾其中的辛苦。
an idealization of something = 对…的理想化
⑪ They talked idealistically about starting a farm but never bought any land.
= 他们满怀理想地谈起要办农场，却从没买过一块地。
idealistically = 理想主义地

ideate
/ˈaɪdieɪt/
核心：（正式，商业与设计用语）构思、形成想法。名词 ideation（构思、创意）并入本条。
① The team spent a morning ideating new names for the product.
= 团队花了一个上午为产品构思新名字。
ideate = 构思
② The workshop moves from research to ideation to testing in a single week.
= 那个工作坊一周之内从调研到构思再到测试。
ideation = 构思、创意阶段

idée fixe
/ˌiːdeɪ ˈfiːks/
核心：（借自法语）执念、固定观念 —— 某人念念不忘、摆脱不了的想法。复数 idées fixes。
① Proving that his father was innocent became an idée fixe that took over his life.
= 证明父亲清白成了他的执念，占据了他的一生。
an idée fixe = 执念
② Her idée fixe was that the neighbours were stealing her post.
= 她的执念是邻居在偷她的信。
somebody's idée fixe = 某人的执念
