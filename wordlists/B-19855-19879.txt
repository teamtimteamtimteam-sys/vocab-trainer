sardonic
/sɑːˈdɒnɪk/
核心：形容词：嘲讽的、讥讽的、冷笑的：a sardonic smile、sardonic humour；带一种看不起人的冷淡幽默。副词 sardonically 是嘲讽地。
① He gave a sardonic smile when the manager promised that things would get better next year.
= 经理承诺明年情况会好转时，他冷冷地笑了笑。
a sardonic smile = 冷笑
sardonic = 嘲讽的
② The writer is famous for her sardonic humour and her sharp comments about politicians.
= 那位作家以冷嘲式幽默和对政客的犀利评论出名。
sardonic humour = 冷嘲式幽默
③ "Well, that went well," he said sardonically, looking at the broken window.
= 「哎，这下可好了。」他看着破窗户讥讽地说。
sardonically = 嘲讽地
