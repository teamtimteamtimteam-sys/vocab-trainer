inculcate
核心：（正式）反复灌输、谆谆教诲 —— 把观念、习惯一点一点地、反复地教进人心里。
句型两种：inculcate something in/into somebody、inculcate somebody with something。名词 inculcation 并入本条。
① Good teachers try to inculcate a love of reading in their pupils.
= 好老师设法让学生养成热爱阅读的习惯。
inculcate something in somebody = 向某人灌输…
② The school inculcated its students with a strong sense of duty.
= 那所学校向学生灌输了强烈的责任感。
inculcate somebody with something = 向某人灌输…
③ The inculcation of good habits should start at a very young age.
= 良好习惯的培养应从很小的时候开始。
the inculcation of something = …的灌输、培养

incumbent
核心：现任的；在职者 —— 名词指现任官员、在位者（the incumbent），形容词指现任的（the incumbent president）。
另有正式的「有责任的」：it is incumbent on/upon somebody to do something。名词 incumbency（任期、在职）并入本条。
① The incumbent president is expected to win a second term easily.
= 现任总统预计会轻松赢得第二个任期。
the incumbent president = 现任总统
② The incumbent lost her seat to a young challenger by just forty votes.
= 那位在任议员仅以四十票之差把席位输给了一位年轻的挑战者。
the incumbent = 现任者、在任者
③ It is incumbent on all of us to report anything suspicious to the police.
= 我们大家都有责任向警方报告任何可疑情况。
be incumbent on somebody to do something = 某人有责任做…
④ During his incumbency the school doubled in size.
= 在他任职期间，学校的规模扩大了一倍。
somebody's incumbency = 某人的任期

incur
核心：招致、遭受 —— 因自己的行为而引来（不好的东西）：费用、债务、损失、罚款、怒气。
过去式 incurred，双写 r。
① The company incurred heavy losses when the factory had to close for six months.
= 工厂被迫关闭半年，公司蒙受了重大损失。
incur losses = 蒙受损失
② You may incur extra charges if you return the car late.
= 还车晚了可能会产生额外费用。
incur charges = 产生费用
③ He incurred the anger of his colleagues by taking credit for their work.
= 他把同事的功劳揽到自己身上，惹恼了大家。
incur somebody's anger = 惹某人发怒

incurable
核心：无法治愈的；（谑）改不了的。前一支说病；后一支说人的习性：an incurable romantic。
副词 incurably 并入本条。
① He was diagnosed with an incurable disease but lived for another fifteen years.
= 他被诊断出一种不治之症，却又活了十五年。
an incurable disease = 不治之症
② She is an incurable romantic who still believes in love at first sight.
= 她是个不可救药的浪漫主义者，至今还相信一见钟情。
an incurable romantic = 不可救药的浪漫主义者
③ Some of the patients on the ward are incurably ill and need care rather than treatment.
= 病房里有些病人已无法治愈，需要的是照护而不是治疗。
incurably ill = 病得无法治愈

incurious
核心：（正式）不好奇的、漠不关心的 —— 对新事物、周围的事没兴趣去了解。副词 incuriously 并入本条。
① The guards were incurious about the prisoners and never asked why they were there.
= 看守们对囚犯毫无好奇心，从不问他们为什么被关在那里。
be incurious about something = 对…不感兴趣
② The cat looked incuriously at the new dog and went back to sleep.
= 那只猫漠然地看了看新来的狗，又接着睡了。
incuriously = 漠然地

incursion
核心：（正式）侵犯、突袭；侵入 —— 本义是短时间攻入别国领土；比喻「闯入」一个新领域（an incursion into politics）。
① Border villages suffered repeated incursions by armed groups from across the river.
= 边境村庄屡次遭到河对岸武装团伙的袭扰。
an incursion into a territory = 侵入某领土
② The actor's brief incursion into politics ended after one election.
= 那位演员涉足政坛的短暂尝试一次选举后就结束了。
an incursion into something = 涉足…

indebted
核心：感激的；负债的。两支：受了别人的帮助而心怀感激（be indebted to somebody for something）；
欠钱的（heavily indebted countries）。名词 indebtedness 并入本条，也两支。
① I am deeply indebted to my parents for their support during my studies.
= 我非常感激父母在我求学期间的支持。
be indebted to somebody for something = 为…感激某人
② Many heavily indebted farmers were forced to sell their land.
= 许多负债累累的农民被迫卖地。
heavily indebted = 负债累累的
③ The author acknowledges her indebtedness to the work of earlier historians.
= 作者承认受益于前辈历史学家的研究。
indebtedness to somebody = 对某人的感激、受益
④ The level of household indebtedness has risen sharply since the crisis.
= 自那次危机以来，家庭负债水平急剧上升。
household indebtedness = 家庭负债

indecent
核心：下流的、猥亵的；不得体的。两支：跟性有关的不雅（indecent photographs）；
（非正式）不合适、过分的（with indecent haste，迫不及待得不像话）。
法律术语 indecent assault（猥亵罪）、indecent exposure（露阴罪）并入本条；
名词 indecency 与副词 indecently 并入本条。
① He was arrested for sending indecent photographs to a colleague.
= 他因给一位同事发送下流照片而被捕。
indecent photographs = 下流照片
② They sold their father's house with indecent haste, only a week after the funeral.
= 葬礼才过一周，他们就急不可耐地把父亲的房子卖了。
with indecent haste = 迫不及待得不像话
③ The man was charged with indecent assault after the attack on the bus.
= 那名男子在公交车上作案后被控猥亵罪。
indecent assault = 猥亵罪
④ Police arrested a man for indecent exposure in the park.
= 警方以露阴罪逮捕了公园里的一名男子。
indecent exposure = 露阴罪
⑤ The comedian's jokes crossed the line into indecency, and several viewers complained.
= 那位喜剧演员的笑话越界成了下流话，好几名观众投诉了。
indecency = 下流、猥亵
⑥ She was indecently rich, even by the standards of the neighbourhood.
= 就算按那一带的标准，她也富得离谱。
indecently = 过分地、离谱地

indecipherable
核心：无法辨认的、难以破解的 —— 字迹、密码、声音看不懂或听不清。
① The doctor's signature at the bottom of the prescription was completely indecipherable.
= 处方底部医生的签名完全认不出来。
an indecipherable signature = 认不出的签名
② The message was written in an indecipherable code that took experts months to break.
= 那条信息是用一种难以破译的密码写的，专家花了几个月才破解。
an indecipherable code = 难以破译的密码

indecision
核心：犹豫不决、优柔寡断 —— 拿不定主意的状态。不可数。形容词是 indecisive。
① After weeks of indecision, she finally accepted the job in Madrid.
= 犹豫了几个星期之后，她终于接受了马德里的那份工作。
weeks of indecision = 几周的犹豫不决
② The government's indecision over the new airport has cost millions.
= 政府在新机场问题上的举棋不定已经耗费了数百万。
indecision over something = 在…上的举棋不定

indecisive
核心：优柔寡断的；非决定性的。两支：人拿不定主意；结果（战斗、选举）没分出胜负。
副词 indecisively 与名词 indecisiveness 并入本条。
① He is so indecisive that it takes him twenty minutes to order a coffee.
= 他优柔寡断到点杯咖啡都要二十分钟。
be indecisive = 优柔寡断
② The election was indecisive, and no party won a majority.
= 选举没分出胜负，没有哪个政党赢得多数席位。
an indecisive result = 不分胜负的结果
③ She hovered indecisively at the door, unsure whether to go in.
= 她在门口犹犹豫豫地徘徊，拿不准要不要进去。
indecisively = 犹豫不决地
④ His indecisiveness frustrated everyone on the team.
= 他的优柔寡断让团队里每个人都很恼火。
indecisiveness = 优柔寡断

indecorous
核心：（正式）不得体的、有失体面的 —— 违反礼节、不合身份。
① It would be indecorous to laugh during the ceremony, however long the speeches are.
= 无论讲话多长，仪式上笑出声都是有失体面的。
it would be indecorous to do something = 做…是有失体面的
② The bishop was shocked by the indecorous behaviour of the wedding guests.
= 主教被婚礼宾客们不得体的举止惊到了。
indecorous behaviour = 不得体的行为

indeed
核心：确实、真的 —— 副词，用来肯定、加强语气。几个位置：
一是加强肯定的回答（Yes, indeed）；二是放在 very + 形容词之后加强（very good indeed，英式）；
三是承接上文、补充更强的一点（= 事实上，其实）；四是表示惊讶或讽刺（Indeed?）；
五是跟 if 连用：if indeed（如果真的…的话）。
① "Did you see the match?" "I did indeed, and what a finish it was!"
= 「你看比赛了吗？」「当然看了，最后那段太精彩了！」
I did indeed = 确实如此
加强肯定的回答。
② Thank you very much indeed for all your help with the move.
= 非常感谢你帮忙搬家。
very much indeed = 非常（英）
放在 very + 形容词或副词之后，英式特有的加强法。
③ The plan was risky; indeed, some thought it was impossible.
= 那个计划很冒险，事实上，有些人认为根本不可能。
indeed = 事实上、其实
承接上文，引出一个更进一步的说法。
④ "He says he's too busy to help." "Busy indeed! He's been watching TV all day."
= 「他说他太忙没空帮忙。」「忙？他看了一整天电视！」
busy indeed = 还真是忙（讽刺）
重复对方的词再加 indeed，表示不信或讥讽。
⑤ The talks will resume next month, if indeed they resume at all.
= 谈判将于下月恢复 —— 如果真能恢复的话。
if indeed = 如果真的…的话

indefatigable
核心：（正式）不知疲倦的、孜孜不倦的 —— 多用来称赞人工作、奔走的劲头。副词 indefatigably 并入本条。
① She was an indefatigable campaigner for women's rights for over forty years.
= 四十多年来，她一直是孜孜不倦的女权运动者。
an indefatigable campaigner = 不知疲倦的活动家
② The volunteers worked indefatigably through the night to fill sandbags.
= 志愿者们通宵不知疲倦地装沙袋。
indefatigably = 不知疲倦地

indefensible
核心：站不住脚的、无法辩护的；（军事）守不住的。前一支说行为、观点；后一支说阵地。
① The company's decision to cut the nurses' pay was morally indefensible.
= 公司削减护士工资的决定在道义上站不住脚。
morally indefensible = 在道义上站不住脚
② The general knew the town was indefensible and ordered his troops to withdraw.
= 将军知道那个镇守不住，就下令部队撤退。
an indefensible position = 守不住的阵地

indefinable
核心：难以确切描述的、说不出的 —— 多说感觉、气质。副词 indefinably 并入本条。
① There was an indefinable sadness about the old house.
= 那座老房子透着一种说不出的哀伤。
an indefinable sadness = 说不出的哀伤
② The room felt indefinably different, though nothing seemed to have moved.
= 那间屋子感觉哪里不一样了，可又说不出来，东西好像都没动过。
indefinably = 说不出地

indefinite
核心：无限期的；不确定的、模糊的。两支：时间没有定下终点（an indefinite period）；
（数量、意思）不明确。语法上：the indefinite article（不定冠词 a/an）、indefinite pronoun（不定代词）并入本条。
副词 indefinitely（无限期地）并入本条。
① The workers are on indefinite strike until the company agrees to talks.
= 工人们在无限期罢工，直到公司同意谈判为止。
an indefinite strike = 无限期罢工
② She is staying with us for an indefinite period while her flat is repaired.
= 她的公寓在修，她会在我们家住一段时间，不定多久。
for an indefinite period = 无限期地、不定期地
③ In English you use the indefinite article before a noun the listener has not heard about yet.
= 英语里，听话人头一回听说的名词前面要用不定冠词。
the indefinite article = 不定冠词
④ Learners often mix up indefinite pronouns such as someone and anyone in negative sentences.
= 学英语的人在否定句里常把「someone」「anyone」这类不定代词弄混。
an indefinite pronoun = 不定代词
⑤ The concert has been postponed indefinitely because the singer is ill.
= 歌手病了，音乐会无限期推迟。
postpone something indefinitely = 无限期推迟…

indelible
核心：擦不掉的；难以忘怀的。本义说墨水、笔迹、污渍洗不掉；比喻说印象、记忆、影响抹不去。
副词 indelibly 并入本条。
① Voters had their fingers marked with indelible ink so they could not vote twice.
= 选民的手指被涂上擦不掉的墨水，防止重复投票。
indelible ink = 擦不掉的墨水
② The war left an indelible mark on everyone who lived through it.
= 那场战争在每个经历过它的人身上都留下了无法磨灭的印记。
an indelible mark = 无法磨灭的印记
③ The sight of the burning city was indelibly printed on her memory.
= 城市燃烧的景象深深地印在了她的记忆里。
indelibly = 不可磨灭地

indelicate
核心：（正式）不得体的、粗俗的 —— 话题、问题、举止让人尴尬，多涉及钱、身体、私事。
名词 indelicacy 并入本条。
① It seemed indelicate to ask the widow how much the funeral had cost.
= 问那位遗孀葬礼花了多少钱，似乎不大得体。
an indelicate question = 不得体的问题
② She apologized for the indelicacy of her remark about his weight.
= 她为自己拿他的体重说事的冒失话道了歉。
the indelicacy of something = …的不得体

indemnify
核心：（法律）保障、赔偿 —— 保证对可能的损失给予补偿（indemnify somebody against something），
或为已发生的损失赔偿。名词 indemnification 并入本条。
① The contract indemnifies the builder against any claims for damage to neighbouring houses.
= 合同保障建筑商不承担任何邻居房屋受损的索赔。
indemnify somebody against something = 保障某人免于…
② The airline agreed to indemnify passengers for their lost luggage.
= 航空公司同意赔偿乘客丢失的行李。
indemnify somebody for something = 就…赔偿某人
③ The company is seeking indemnification from its insurers for the fire.
= 公司正向保险公司就那场火灾寻求赔偿。
indemnification = 赔偿、补偿

indemnity
核心：（法律）保障、赔偿（金）—— 不可数时指免于损失的保障；可数时指一笔赔偿金。
① The policy provides indemnity against loss or damage to the building.
= 这份保单保障建筑物的损失或损坏。
indemnity against something = 对…的保障
② The defeated country was forced to pay a huge indemnity to the victors.
= 战败国被迫向战胜国支付巨额赔款。
pay an indemnity = 支付赔款

indent
核心：缩进；订购。三支：排版上把一行开头往里缩（indent the first line）；
（英）正式订购、申请物资（indent for supplies）；名词指缩进或（英）订单。
形容词 indented（缩进的；边缘凹凸不平的）与名词 indentation（缩进；凹痕）并入本条。
① Indent the first line of each paragraph by one centimetre.
= 每段第一行缩进一厘米。
indent a line = 缩进一行
② The hospital indented for new beds, but the order took six months to arrive.
= 医院申领了新床，可订货六个月才到。
indent for something = （英）申领、订购…
③ The coast is deeply indented, with hundreds of small bays and inlets.
= 海岸线曲折，有几百个小海湾和小港湾。
a deeply indented coastline = 曲折的海岸线
④ His ring had left a small indentation on the table.
= 他的戒指在桌面上留下了一个小凹痕。
an indentation = 凹痕
⑤ Use the tab key to create an indentation at the start of the paragraph.
= 用跳格键在段首缩进。
create an indentation = 缩进

indenture
核心：（旧）契约；学徒合同 —— 旧时雇主与学徒、契约劳工签订的合同，约定服务若干年。
形容词 indentured（契约束缚的）并入本条，多说殖民时期的契约劳工。
① At fourteen he signed an indenture to serve a printer for seven years.
= 十四岁时，他签了一份契约，给一个印刷商当七年学徒。
sign an indenture = 签订学徒契约
② Thousands of indentured labourers were shipped to the sugar plantations.
= 成千上万的契约劳工被运到了甘蔗种植园。
indentured labourers = 契约劳工

independence
核心：独立 —— 不受别人控制、不依赖别人。两支：国家、地区脱离统治（gain independence from）；
个人经济上、生活上自立（financial independence）。形容词 independent 另立。
① The country gained independence from Britain in 1962.
= 那个国家于一九六二年脱离英国独立。
gain independence from somebody = 脱离…独立
② Leaving home at eighteen gave her a sense of independence she had never felt before.
= 十八岁离家让她有了一种从未有过的独立感。
a sense of independence = 独立感
③ Getting a job was the first step towards financial independence.
= 找到工作是迈向经济独立的第一步。
financial independence = 经济独立
④ The paper has always prided itself on its editorial independence.
= 那家报纸一向以编辑独立而自豪。
editorial independence = 编辑独立

independent
核心：独立的；不偏不倚的；（英）私立的。几支：国家独立（an independent state）；个人自立（fiercely independent）；
不受控制、中立（an independent inquiry）；互不相关的（two independent studies）；
英式说学校、电视台「非公立的」（an independent school）。
语法 independent clause（独立分句）、统计学 independent variable（自变量）并入本条；副词 independently 并入本条。
① The island became an independent state after a peaceful vote.
= 那个岛经过一次和平投票成为独立国家。
an independent state = 独立国家
② At ninety my grandmother is still fiercely independent of her children and refuses any help with shopping.
= 我祖母九十岁了，还是非常要强，不靠子女，买东西不肯让人帮忙。
fiercely independent = 非常独立、要强
be independent of somebody = 不依赖某人
③ The government has ordered an independent inquiry into the accident.
= 政府已下令对这起事故进行独立调查。
an independent inquiry = 独立调查
④ Two independent studies reached exactly the same conclusion.
= 两项彼此独立的研究得出了完全相同的结论。
independent studies = 彼此独立的研究
⑤ She was educated at an independent school and then went to Oxford.
= 她在一所私立学校上学，后来进了牛津。
an independent school = （英）私立学校
⑥ Each independent clause in a long sentence could stand alone as a complete sentence.
= 长句里的每个独立分句都可以单独成为一个完整的句子。
an independent clause = 独立分句
⑦ In this experiment, temperature is the independent variable.
= 在这个实验里，温度是自变量。
an independent variable = 自变量
⑧ The two scientists made the same discovery independently of each other.
= 两位科学家各自独立地作出了同一项发现。
independently of somebody = 独立于某人
