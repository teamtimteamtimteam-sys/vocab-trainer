scared
/skeəd/
核心：形容词：害怕的、恐惧的：be scared of something、be scared to do something、be scared that…；scared stiff / scared to death 是吓得要死。
① I was too scared to look down when we stood on the glass floor at the top of the tower.
= 站在塔顶的玻璃地板上时，我吓得不敢往下看。
be scared to do something = 害怕做某事
scared = 害怕的
② She was scared that she would fail the exam and have to repeat the whole year.
= 她害怕考试不及格，要重读一整年。
be scared that = 害怕……
③ My brother was scared stiff when the plane began to shake in the storm.
= 飞机在暴风雨中开始颠簸时，我哥哥吓得要死。
scared stiff = 吓得要死

② Don't listen to the scaremongers who say that the bridge is going to fall down.
= 别听那些危言耸听的人说桥要塌了。
a scaremonger = 散布恐慌的人
