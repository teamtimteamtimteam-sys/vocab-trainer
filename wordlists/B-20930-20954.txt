shop
/ʃɒp/
核心：名词，可数：商店、店铺：a shop、go to the shops；（工厂的）车间、作坊：a repair shop。动词（shopped, shopped）：购物、买东西：shop for something、go shopping；（英式口语）告发。习语：talk shop 是谈论工作上的事；all over the shop 是到处、乱七八糟。
① There is a small shop at the end of our street that sells bread and newspapers.
= 我们街尽头有一家小店，卖面包和报纸。

② We go shopping every Saturday morning at the market in the town centre.
= 我们每个星期六早上都去镇中心的市场购物。

③ She is shopping for a new coat, but she hasn't found one she likes yet.
= 她在买新外套，可还没找到喜欢的。
shop for something = 选购某物

④ My father works in the repair shop at the bus station, fixing engines.
= 我父亲在汽车站的修理车间修发动机。

= 那两位医生只要在聚会上碰到，整晚都在聊工作。
talk shop = 谈工作上的事
多带不满的口气，嫌别人在社交场合还谈工作。
⑥ After the storm, the branches were all over the shop and the garden looked terrible.
= 暴风雨过后，树枝散得到处都是，花园一片狼藉。

⑦ It is worth shopping around before you buy a new phone, because prices vary a lot.
