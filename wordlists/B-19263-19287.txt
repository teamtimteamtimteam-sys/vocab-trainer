righteous
/ˈraɪtʃəs/
核心：（正式）一是正直的、正当的、公正的；二是（愤怒等）有正当理由的、义正词严的：righteous anger。副词 righteously；名词 righteousness，不可数。并入本条。
① He was a righteous man who always told the truth.
= 他是个正直的人，总说真话。
a righteous man = 正直的人
righteous = 正直的
② She was filled with righteous anger when she saw the dog being beaten.
= 看见那条狗挨打，她义愤填膺。
righteous anger = 义愤
③ He spoke righteously about the need for honest government.
= 他义正词严地讲到需要一个廉洁的政府。
righteously = 义正词严地
④ The priest preached about the path of righteousness.
= 神父讲道时谈到正义之路。
righteousness = 正直、正义

rightful
/ˈraɪtfl/
核心：（正式）合法的、正当的、应有的：the rightful owner。只放在名词前。副词 rightfully，并入本条。
① The police returned the stolen car to its rightful owner.
= 警方把那辆被盗的车归还给了它的合法车主。
the rightful owner = 合法主人
rightful = 合法的
② She took her rightful place as head of the family business.
= 她理所应当地成了家族企业的掌门人。
your rightful place = 应有的位置
③ The land rightfully belongs to the people of the village.
= 那块地理应属于村里的人。
rightfully = 合法地、理应

rightist
/ˈraɪtɪst/
核心：（政治）名词，可数：右派分子、保守派。形容词：右派的、右翼的。
① The rightists in the party opposed any rise in taxes.
= 党内的右派反对任何加税。
the rightists = 右派分子
a rightist = 右派分子
② The country was run by a rightist government for twenty years.
= 那个国家由一个右翼政府统治了二十年。
a rightist government = 右翼政府
