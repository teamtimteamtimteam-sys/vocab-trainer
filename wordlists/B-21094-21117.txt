sidestep
/ˈsaɪdstep/
核心：动词（sidestepped, sidestepped）：（向旁边）跨一步避开；回避（问题、困难）：sidestep a question。名词，可数：横跨一步；回避。
① The young player sidestepped the tall defender and ran straight towards the goal.
= 那名年轻球员一个侧步晃过高个子后卫，直冲球门。
sidestep somebody = 侧身避开某人
sidestep = 跨步避开
② The minister sidestepped the question about the hospital and talked about schools instead.
= 那位部长回避了关于医院的问题，转而谈起学校来。
sidestep a question = 回避问题

sidestroke
/ˈsaɪdstrəʊk/

① My grandmother still swims the sidestroke every morning, because it is easy on her back.
= 我奶奶每天早上还游侧泳，因为对她的背不费劲。
the sidestroke = 侧泳
sidestroke = 侧泳
② Lifeguards learn sidestroke because it lets them pull someone through the water.
= 救生员要学侧泳，因为侧泳便于把人拖着游。
learn sidestroke = 学侧泳

sideswipe
/ˈsaɪdswaɪp/
核心：名词，可数：顺带的批评、旁敲侧击：take a sideswipe at somebody；（车的）擦撞。动词，及物（美式）：擦撞（车的侧面）。
① In his speech, the mayor took a sideswipe at the newspaper that had criticized him.
= 市长在讲话中顺带讥讽了一下批评过他的那家报纸。
take a sideswipe at somebody = 顺带讥讽某人
sideswipe = 旁敲侧击
② A truck sideswiped our car on the highway, but luckily nobody was hurt.
= 一辆卡车在公路上擦撞了我们的车，幸好没人受伤。
sideswipe a car = 擦撞汽车

sidetrack
/ˈsaɪdtræk/
核心：动词，及物（常用被动）：使离开正题、使分心：get sidetracked。名词，可数：（美式）（铁路的）侧线。
① I meant to finish my homework, but I got sidetracked by a programme on television.
= 我本打算做完作业，可被电视上一个节目分了心。
get sidetracked = 分心、跑题
sidetrack = 使分心
② The teacher tried to sidetrack the class into a discussion about football.
= 老师想把全班引到关于足球的讨论上去。
sidetrack somebody into something = 把某人引到某事上去

sidewalk
/ˈsaɪdwɔːk/

sidewalk = 人行道

sideways

③ She was not promoted; it was a sideways move into another department.
= 她没升职，只是平调到另一个部门。
a sideways move = 平调

siege

sight
/saɪt/
核心：名词：视力（不可数）：lose your sight；看见、看到（不可数或单数）：at the sight of something；视野、视线（不可数）：in sight、out of sight；景象、情景（可数）：a beautiful sight；（复数 the sights）名胜、风景：see the sights；（枪的）瞄准器（常用复数）。动词，及物：看到、发现（尤指找了很久的东西）。习语：at first sight 是一见、乍看；catch sight of 是瞥见；lose sight of 是看不见了、忘记；know somebody by sight 是面熟。
① From the top of the cliff, the sunset over the sea was a beautiful sight.
= 从悬崖顶上看，海上的落日真是一幅美景。
sight = 景象
② My grandfather lost his sight when he was seventy, but he still loves listening to the radio.
= 我祖父七十岁时失明了，但他仍然喜欢听收音机。
sight = 视力
③ They fell in love at first sight and were married within a year.
= 他们一见钟情，不到一年就结了婚。
at first sight = 一见
④ I caught sight of my old teacher in the crowd, but she had gone before I could say hello.
= 我在人群中瞥见了我以前的老师，可没等我打招呼她就走了。
catch sight of = 瞥见
⑤ There was not a house in sight, only fields and trees for miles.
= 一眼望去一座房子都没有，方圆几英里只有田野和树木。
in sight = 在视野内
⑥ We waved from the harbour as the boat sailed away, and it was soon out of sight.
= 我们在港口挥手，那条船开走了，很快就看不见了。
out of sight = 看不见
⑦ Don't lose sight of why you started this job in the first place.
= 别忘了你当初为什么干这份工作。
lose sight of = 忘记、看不见了
⑧ I don't know her name, but I know her by sight; she works in the bank.
= 我不知道她叫什么，但她面熟，她在银行上班。
know somebody by sight = 面熟
⑨ We spent the first day in Rome seeing the sights and eating ice cream.
= 我们在罗马的第一天游览名胜古迹、吃冰激凌。
see the sights = 观光、游览名胜
⑩ The little children in the park screamed at the sight of the big black dog.
= 公园里的小孩子们一看到那条大黑狗就尖叫起来。
at the sight of something = 一看到某物
⑪ A tall pillar blocked the sight-line, so we could not see half of the stage.
= 一根高柱子挡住了视线，我们看不到半个舞台。
a sight-line = 视线
⑫ She can sight-read music, so she played the new piece without practising.
= 她能视奏乐谱，所以那首新曲子她没练就弹了出来。
sight-read = 视奏、视唱
⑬ He is a good sight-reader and is often asked to play at short notice.
= 他视奏能力很强，常被临时请去演奏。
a sight-reader = 能视奏的人
⑭ Sight-reading is part of the piano exam, so you have to play a piece you have never seen.
= 视奏是钢琴考试的一部分，你得弹一首从没见过的曲子。
sight-reading = 视奏
