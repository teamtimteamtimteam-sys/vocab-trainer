protective
/prəˈtektɪv/
核心：两支。一是防护的、保护用的，只放在名词前：protective clothing；二是（对人）呵护的、爱护的、护着的：protective of、towards。
副词 protectively；名词 protectiveness，不可数。并入本条。
① The workers must wear protective clothing and a mask at all times.
= 工人必须一直穿防护服、戴口罩。
protective clothing = 防护服
a protective layer = 保护层
② She is fiercely protective of her younger brother at school.
= 在学校里她拼命护着弟弟。
be protective of somebody = 护着某人
be protective towards somebody = 呵护某人
③ He put his arm protectively round the child's shoulders.
= 他护着似的搂住孩子的肩膀。
protectively = 护着似的
④ Her protectiveness towards the puppy made the other dogs keep away.
= 她对那只小狗护得很紧，别的狗都不敢靠近。
protectiveness = 呵护、护着

protector
/prəˈtektə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是保护者、保护人；二是保护用的东西、护具，常构成复合词：a chest protector。
① The old king saw himself as the protector of the poor.
= 老国王自认是穷人的保护者。
the protector of somebody = 某人的保护者
a protector = 保护者
② The cricketer wore a chest protector under his shirt.
= 那名板球手在衬衫里戴了护胸。
a chest protector = 护胸
a screen protector = 屏幕保护膜

protectorate
/prəˈtektərət/
核心：保护国、保护地：由一个更强的国家保护并部分控制的国家或地区。可数。
① The islands were a British protectorate until the nineteen sixties.
= 那些岛直到一九六〇年代还是英国的保护地。
a British protectorate = 英国的保护地
a protectorate = 保护国
② The treaty turned the kingdom into a protectorate of its powerful neighbour.
= 那项条约把那个王国变成了强邻的保护国。
a protectorate of something = 某国的保护国
跟殖民地不同：名义上还留着自己的统治者。

protégé
/ˈprɒtəʒeɪ/
核心：门生、被提携的人：受一位有地位、有经验的人关照、栽培的年轻人。可数。女性也拼作 protégée，读法一样。
① The young conductor was a protégé of the director of the opera house.
= 那位年轻指挥是歌剧院院长栽培的人。
a protégé of somebody = 某人栽培的人
a protégé = 门生
② She chose one of her own protégés to take over the firm.
= 她挑了一个自己一手带出来的人接管公司。
her own protégés = 她自己带出来的人
提携人的那一方叫 mentor、patron。

protein
/ˈprəʊtiːn/
核心：蛋白质：肉、蛋、鱼、豆子里含的、身体生长和修复要用的一类营养物质。可数也可不可数。
① Beans and lentils are a cheap source of protein for people who eat no meat.
= 对不吃肉的人来说，豆子和扁豆是便宜的蛋白质来源。
a source of protein = 蛋白质来源
protein = 蛋白质
② The athletes follow a diet that is high in protein and low in fat.
= 运动员们吃的是高蛋白、低脂肪的饮食。
high in protein = 蛋白质含量高
a high-protein diet = 高蛋白饮食
③ The scientists have identified a protein that helps the virus to enter the cell.
= 科学家找到了一种帮病毒进入细胞的蛋白质。
a protein = 一种蛋白质
这样用可数。

protest
/ˈprəʊtest/（名）/prəˈtest/（动）
核心：名词，重音在前：抗议、反对，可数也可不可数；抗议活动、示威，可数：a protest against。
动词，重音在后：抗议、反对：protest against、about、at，美式直接跟宾语；坚决地说、申辩：protest that、protest your innocence。
习语 under protest 是心里不服地、勉强地。
① The workers staged a protest against the closing of the factory.
= 工人们举行了抗议，反对关闭工厂。
a protest against something = 反对某事的抗议
stage a protest = 举行抗议
② She resigned from the committee in protest at the decision.
= 她辞去了委员会的职务，以示对那项决定的抗议。
in protest at something = 以示对某事的抗议
in protest = 以示抗议
③ The plan was dropped after a storm of protest from local people.
= 当地人群起反对，那个计划作罢了。
a storm of protest = 群起反对
without protest = 一声不吭地、没有异议地
④ Thousands of students marched through the capital to protest against the war.
= 成千上万的学生在首都游行，抗议那场战争。
protest against something = 抗议某事
protest about something = 就某事抗议
⑤ The crowd had gathered to protest the new tax on fuel.
= 人群聚在一起，抗议新征的燃油税。
protest something = 抗议某事
直接跟宾语是美式用法。
⑥ He has always protested his innocence, even after ten years in prison.
= 即使坐了十年牢，他也一直申辩自己无罪。
protest your innocence = 申辩自己无罪
⑦ "I never touched the money," she protested, close to tears.
= 「那钱我碰都没碰。」她争辩道，眼泪都快出来了。
protest that… = 坚决地说……
这一支是「争辩、申辩」，不是上街抗议。
⑧ I paid the fine under protest and wrote to the council the same day.
= 我心里不服地交了罚款，当天就给镇议会写了信。
under protest = 心里不服地、勉强地

Protestant

= 那本书梳理了新教在北欧的传播。
Protestantism = 新教

protestation
/ˌprɒtəˈsteɪʃn/
核心：（正式）（郑重的）声明、申辩、表白：坚决说某事是真的，尤指别人不信的时候。可数，多用复数。常说 protestations of。

= 那些信找到之后，他再怎么申辩无罪也没人信了。
protestations of innocence = 无罪的申辩

protestations of friendship = 口口声声的友情
多带「说得好听」的意味。

protester
/prəˈtestə(r)/
核心：抗议者、示威者：公开表示反对的人。可数。也拼作 protestor。
① The protesters sat down in the road and refused to move.
= 抗议者在路上坐下来，不肯挪动。
a protester = 抗议者

arrest a protester = 逮捕抗议者

protocol
/ˈprəʊtəkɒl/
核心：四支。一是（外交、正式场合的）礼仪、礼节，不可数；二是（术语）（条约的）草案、议定书、附加条款，可数；
三是（计算机）协议：机器之间传数据的一套规则，可数；四是（术语）（实验、治疗的）方案、规程，可数。
① According to protocol, the president must enter the room last.
= 按礼仪，总统得最后一个进屋。
according to protocol = 按礼仪
a breach of protocol = 失礼、违反礼仪
② The two countries signed a protocol on the sharing of river water.
= 两国签了一份分享河水的议定书。
sign a protocol = 签议定书
a protocol on something = 关于某事的议定书
③ The two machines cannot talk to each other because they use different protocols.
= 那两台机器用的协议不一样，彼此连不上。
a protocol = （计算机的）协议
a network protocol = 网络协议
④ The nurses followed the hospital's protocol for treating burns.
= 护士们照医院治烧伤的规程办。
a protocol for something = 某事的规程
follow the protocol = 照规程办

proton

= 质子带正电，电子带负电。

proud
/praʊd/
核心：形容词。一是自豪的、得意的、引以为荣的：proud of、proud to do、proud that；二是值得自豪的，只放在名词前：a proud moment；
三是（含贬义）骄傲的、傲慢的、自以为了不起的；四是自尊心强的、要面子的；五是（文学）壮观的、雄伟的。
副词 proudly。并入本条。
习语：do somebody proud 是（旧式）给某人争光，也指盛情款待某人；(as) proud as a peacock 是得意洋洋；
（英式）proud of 也指高出：stand proud of the surface。
① Her parents are very proud of her for winning the scholarship.
= 她拿到了奖学金，父母很为她骄傲。
be proud of somebody = 为某人骄傲
be proud of something = 以某事为荣
② I am proud to say that every one of my pupils passed.
= 我可以自豪地说，我的学生全都及格了。
be proud to do something = 以做某事为荣
be proud that… = 为……自豪
③ He is the proud owner of a new bicycle with twenty gears.
= 他得意地拥有了一辆二十挡的新自行车。
the proud owner of something = 得意的某物主人
the proud parents = 满心骄傲的父母
④ It was the proudest moment of his life when his daughter became a doctor.
= 女儿当上医生的那一刻，是他一生中最自豪的时刻。
a proud moment = 自豪的时刻
a proud history = 值得自豪的历史
⑤ She was too proud to admit that she had been wrong.
= 她太傲，不肯承认自己错了。
be too proud to do something = 太傲不肯做某事
这一支带贬义。
⑥ They were poor but proud, and would not accept money from anyone.
= 他们穷归穷，却要强，谁的钱也不肯收。
poor but proud = 穷而要强
这一支说的是自尊心强。
⑦ The team did the whole town proud by reaching the final.
= 那支球队打进了决赛，给全城争了光。
do somebody proud = 给某人争光
也指盛情款待：Our hosts did us proud。
⑧ He walked up to collect the prize as proud as a peacock.
= 他得意洋洋地走上去领奖。
proud as a peacock = 得意洋洋
也说 as proud as a peacock。
⑨ The head of the nail stands slightly proud of the surface of the wood.
= 钉子头稍微高出木头的表面。
stand proud of something = 高出某物
这是英式用法。
⑩ She proudly showed us the photographs of her first grandchild.
= 她骄傲地给我们看她头一个孙辈的照片。
proudly = 骄傲地、自豪地
名词是 pride。

prove
/pruːv/
核心：动词。一是证明、证实：prove something、prove that、prove somebody wrong；二是系动词：后来发现是、结果是：prove (to be) useful；
三是 prove yourself：证明自己有本事；四是（面团）发起来。过去式 proved，过去分词 proved，美式多用 proven。
proven 作形容词：经过证明的、靠得住的；苏格兰法律里 not proven 是罪证不足。形容词 provable 是可证明的。并入本条。
习语：prove a point、prove your point 是证明自己说得对；the exception that proves the rule 是反而证明了规律的例外；
prove somebody right、wrong 是证明某人对、错。
① The letters prove that he knew about the plan from the start.
= 那些信证明他从一开始就知道那个计划。
prove that… = 证明……
prove something = 证明某事
② Can you prove to the court that you were at home that night?
= 你能向法庭证明那天夜里你在家吗？
prove something to somebody = 向某人证明某事
③ Everyone said she would fail, and she was determined to prove them wrong.
= 人人都说她会失败，她下决心要证明他们错了。
prove somebody wrong = 证明某人错了
prove somebody right = 证明某人对了
④ He was proved innocent when the real thief confessed.
= 真正的贼招了，他被证明是清白的。
prove somebody innocent = 证明某人清白
prove somebody guilty = 证明某人有罪
⑤ The new assistant has proved to be extremely useful in the office.
= 那位新助手后来证明在办公室里极有用。
prove to be something = 结果是……
⑥ The task proved more difficult than any of us had expected.
= 那件差事结果比我们谁料想的都难。
prove difficult = 结果很难
这样用是系动词，后面直接跟形容词。
⑦ She was given one season in the first team to prove herself.
= 她得到一个赛季在一队打球的机会，好证明自己。
prove yourself = 证明自己有本事
prove yourself to somebody = 向某人证明自己
⑧ He ate the whole chilli just to prove a point.
= 他把整根辣椒吃了下去，就为了证明自己说得没错。
prove a point = 证明自己说得对
prove your point = 证明自己的论点
⑨ Most boys of his age hate reading, and he is the exception that proves the rule.
= 他那个年纪的男孩大多讨厌看书，他是个例外，倒更见得这条规律不假。
the exception that proves the rule = 反而证明了规律的例外
⑩ Leave the dough in a warm place to prove for about an hour.
= 把面团放在暖和的地方发一个小时左右。
leave the dough to prove = 让面团发起来
这一支是英式说法；美式说 rise。
⑪ She is a teacher of proven ability with twenty years' experience.
= 她是位能力久经证明、有二十年经验的教师。
proven ability = 久经证明的能力
a proven record = 靠得住的履历
⑫ The jury in Edinburgh returned a verdict of not proven.
= 爱丁堡的陪审团作出了罪证不足的裁决。
not proven = 罪证不足
这是苏格兰法律里的说法。
⑬ The claim is not provable, because none of the records has survived.
= 那个说法没法证明，因为记录一份也没留下来。
provable = 可证明的
名词是 proof。

Provençal
/ˌprɒvɒnˈsɑːl/

proverb
/ˈprɒvɜːb/
核心：谚语、俗语：人人知道的、说出一条道理或劝告的短句子，如 Waste not, want not。可数。
① My grandmother had a proverb for every occasion.
= 我奶奶什么场合都有一句俗语对得上。
a proverb = 谚语、俗语
an old proverb = 老话
② As the old proverb says, a stitch in time saves nine.
= 老话说得好，及时一针省九针。
as the proverb says = 俗话说
跟 saying 意思相近，proverb 多带劝诫。

proverbial
/prəˈvɜːbiəl/
核心：两支。一是谚语里说的、俗话所说的，只放在名词前：借用一句谚语、成语时用；二是尽人皆知的、出了名的。
副词 proverbially，并入本条。
① Looking for the ring in the long grass was like hunting the proverbial needle in a haystack.
= 在深草里找那枚戒指，真像俗话说的大海捞针。
the proverbial needle in a haystack = 俗话说的大海捞针
the proverbial = 俗话说的那个……
② His meanness with money is proverbial in the village.
= 他抠门，在村里是出了名的。
be proverbial = 是出了名的
③ The trains in that country are proverbially punctual.
= 那个国家的火车准点是出了名的。
proverbially = 出了名地、如俗话所说

provide
/prəˈvaɪd/
核心：及物。一是提供、供给：provide something、provide somebody with something、provide something for somebody；
二是（正式）（法律、规定）规定：provide that。
短语动词：provide for somebody 是供养、养活；provide for something 是为（可能发生的事）预作安排，也指（法律）对……作出规定；
provide against something 是（正式）预防、防备。
连词 provided (that)、providing (that) 是只要、在……的条件下。提供者、供应商叫 provider，也指养家的人。并入本条。
① The hotel provides a free bus to the airport every half hour.
= 那家旅馆每半小时有一班去机场的免费大巴。
provide something = 提供某物
provide a service = 提供服务
② The school provides every child with a hot meal at midday.
= 那所学校中午给每个孩子供一顿热饭。
provide somebody with something = 给某人提供某物
③ The charity provides shelter for people who have lost their homes.
= 那个慈善机构给无家可归的人提供住处。
provide something for somebody = 为某人提供某物
不说 provide somebody something。
④ The rules provide that a player may be replaced at any time.
= 规则规定球员随时可以换下。
provide that… = 规定……
这一支是正式、法律上的说法。
⑤ He worked at two jobs to provide for his wife and five children.
= 他打两份工，养活妻子和五个孩子。
provide for = 供养、为……作安排
provide for somebody = 供养某人
⑥ The plan provides for a rise in the number of pupils over ten years.
= 那个计划为十年间学生人数的增加预留了余地。
provide for something = 为某事预作安排
也指法律「对某事作出规定」。
⑦ The insurance provides against loss of income if you are too ill to work.
= 你病得不能上班的话，那份保险保你不受收入损失。
provide against = 预防、防备
provide against something = 防备某事
⑧ You may borrow the car, provided that you bring it back by six.
= 车你可以借，只要六点以前还回来。
provided that… = 只要……
provided = 只要
⑨ We shall eat outside, providing it does not rain before the evening.
= 只要傍晚前不下雨，我们就在外面吃。
providing = 只要
providing that… = 只要……
⑩ The firm is the largest provider of school meals in the country.
= 那家公司是国内最大的学校供餐商。
a provider = 提供者、供应商
a service provider = 服务供应商
⑪ After her husband's accident she became the family's only provider.
= 丈夫出事以后，她成了家里唯一养家的人。
the only provider = 唯一养家的人
这一支指挣钱养家的人。
