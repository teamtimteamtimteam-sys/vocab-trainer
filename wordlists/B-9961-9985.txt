② A crowd gawped from the far pavement and the police moved them twice.
= 一群人在对面人行道上围观，警察赶了两回。
gawp from somewhere = 在某处围观
③ Stop gawping and pass me the spanner.
= 别愣着看了，把扳手递给我。
stop gawping = 别愣着看
