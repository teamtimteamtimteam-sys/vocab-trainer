riskiness
/ˈrɪskinəs/
核心：危险性、风险程度。不可数。
① The bank refused the loan because of the riskiness of the business plan.
= 由于那份商业计划风险太大，银行拒绝了贷款。
the riskiness of something = 某事的风险程度
riskiness = 危险性
② Parents worried about the riskiness of letting young children walk to school alone.
= 家长们担心让小孩独自走路上学太危险。
worry about the riskiness = 担心危险性

risky
/ˈrɪski/
核心：危险的、有风险的、冒险的。比较级 riskier，最高级 riskiest。副词 riskily，并入本条。
① Putting all your savings into one company is a risky thing to do.
= 把全部积蓄投进一家公司是有风险的做法。
a risky thing to do = 冒险的做法
risky = 有风险的
② Lending money to friends can be risky, because it sometimes ends the friendship.
= 借钱给朋友可能有风险，因为有时会断送友谊。
can be risky = 可能有风险
③ He drove riskily along the narrow mountain road, passing lorries on the bends.
= 他在狭窄的山路上冒险开车，在弯道上超卡车。
drive riskily = 冒险开车
riskily = 冒险地
④ The company invested riskily in new technology and lost most of its money.
= 那家公司冒险投资新技术，赔掉了大部分钱。
invest riskily = 冒险投资

risotto
/rɪˈzɒtəʊ/
核心：（意大利）调味饭、烩饭：用米加肉汤、蔬菜等慢煮而成。可数或不可数，复数 risottos。
① She made a creamy mushroom risotto with rice, onions and a little cheese.
= 她用米、洋葱和一点奶酪做了一份奶香蘑菇烩饭。
a mushroom risotto = 蘑菇烩饭
risotto = 意式烩饭
② You have to keep stirring the risotto and adding hot stock for about twenty minutes.
= 做烩饭得不停搅拌，一点点加热汤，大约二十分钟。
stir the risotto = 搅拌烩饭

risque
/ˈriːskeɪ/
核心：（笑话、故事）有点下流的、带色情意味的、低俗的。也拼作 risqué。
① The comedian told a few risque jokes that made some of the older guests uncomfortable.
= 那个喜剧演员讲了几个荤笑话，让一些年长的客人不自在。
a risque joke = 荤笑话
risque = 有点下流的
② Her dress was thought rather risque when she wore it to the film festival.
= 她穿那条裙子去电影节时，人们觉得有点暴露。
rather risque = 有点暴露、有点出格

rissole
/ˈrɪsəʊl/
核心：（英式）炸肉饼、炸丸子：碎肉或蔬菜做成小饼，裹面包屑油炸。可数。
① My mother made rissoles from the leftover beef and served them with mashed potatoes.
= 我妈妈用剩下的牛肉做了炸肉饼，配土豆泥吃。
a rissole = 炸肉饼
② The school canteen served vegetable rissoles every Friday for the children who did not eat meat.
= 学校食堂每星期五给不吃肉的孩子做蔬菜炸饼。
vegetable rissoles = 蔬菜炸饼

rite
/raɪt/
核心：（宗教等的）仪式、典礼：funeral rites。可数。
习语：rite of passage 是成年礼、人生重要关口；last rites 是（天主教为临终者举行的）临终圣事。
① The funeral rites lasted for three days, and the whole village took part.
= 葬礼仪式持续了三天，全村人都参加了。
funeral rites = 葬礼仪式
a rite = 仪式
② For many teenagers, passing the driving test is an important rite of passage.
= 对很多青少年来说，考过驾照是一道重要的人生关口。
a rite of passage = 成年礼、人生关口
③ A priest was called to the hospital to give the dying man the last rites.
= 医院请来一位神父，为垂死的病人行临终圣事。
the last rites = 临终圣事

ritual
/ˈrɪtʃuəl/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：（宗教的）仪式、典礼；（习惯性的）老规矩、例行公事。
形容词：仪式的、典礼的；例行的、习惯性的。副词 ritually，并入本条。
① The priests performed an ancient ritual to ask the gods for rain.
= 祭司们举行古老的仪式，祈求神灵降雨。
perform a ritual = 举行仪式
ritual = 仪式
② Making a pot of tea when he gets home from work has become a daily ritual.
= 下班回家泡一壶茶，成了他每天的老规矩。
a daily ritual = 每天的老规矩
③ The ritual dances are performed only once a year at the spring festival.
= 那些祭祀舞蹈只在每年春节时跳一次。
ritual dances = 祭祀舞蹈
ritual = 仪式的
④ Each morning the old soldier ritually raised the flag in his front garden.
= 每天早上，那位老兵照例在前院升起旗子。
ritually = 照例地、按仪式地

ritualistic
/ˌrɪtʃuəˈlɪstɪk/
核心：仪式的；（贬义）刻板的、例行公事的。
① The killings had a ritualistic quality that frightened the police.
= 那几起杀人案带有仪式的色彩，让警方害怕。
a ritualistic quality = 仪式色彩
ritualistic = 仪式的
② The meeting began with the ritualistic reading of last month's report, which nobody listened to.
= 会议照例先念上个月的报告，谁也没在听。
ritualistic = 例行公事的

ritualize
/ˈrɪtʃuəlaɪz/
核心：使仪式化、使程式化。英式也拼作 ritualise。
① In some animals, fighting has been ritualized so that nobody gets seriously hurt.
= 在有些动物中，打斗已经程式化了，谁也不会受重伤。
be ritualized = 被仪式化
ritualize = 使仪式化
② The family has ritualized the opening of presents on Christmas morning, oldest first.
= 那家人把圣诞早上拆礼物弄成了一套仪式，最年长的先拆。
ritualize something = 把某事弄成仪式

ritzy
/ˈrɪtsi/
核心：（口语）豪华的、奢华的、时髦的。
① They stayed in a ritzy hotel in Monte Carlo with a swimming pool on the roof.
= 他们住在蒙特卡洛一家屋顶有泳池的豪华酒店。
a ritzy hotel = 豪华酒店
ritzy = 豪华的
② She moved to a ritzy part of town after her first film made her rich.
= 第一部电影让她发了财，她就搬到了城里的高档地段。
a ritzy part of town = 高档地段

rival
/ˈraɪvl/
核心：名词，可数：竞争对手、对手：a rival for。也作形容词：竞争的、对立的：rival companies。
动词，过去式 rivalled（美式 rivaled）：比得上、与……匹敌：rival something in。
① The two boys were rivals for the same girl at school.
= 那两个男孩在学校里追求同一个女孩，是情敌。
a rival for something = 争夺某物的对手
rival = 对手
② Two rival companies are fighting to win the contract to build the new bridge.
= 两家竞争公司都在争夺建新桥的合同。
rival companies = 竞争公司
③ Few cities in the world can rival Paris for good food and beautiful buildings.
= 论美食和漂亮建筑，世界上没几座城市比得上巴黎。
rival something for something = 在某方面比得上某物
rival = 比得上

rivalry
/ˈraɪvlri/
核心：竞争、较量、敌对：rivalry between。可数或不可数。
① There has always been a friendly rivalry between the two football clubs in the city.
= 城里那两家足球俱乐部一直有友好的较量。
rivalry between = 之间的竞争
rivalry = 竞争
② The two sisters grew up in a spirit of sibling rivalry, always trying to beat each other.
= 两姐妹在手足相争中长大，总想压对方一头。
sibling rivalry = 手足相争

riven
/ˈrɪvn/
核心：（正式）四分五裂的、被分裂的：riven by / with。
① The party was riven by arguments about the war and lost the election.
= 那个政党因战争问题争吵不休，四分五裂，输掉了选举。
be riven by something = 被某事搞得四分五裂
riven = 四分五裂的
② It is a small town riven with old hatreds between the families who live there.
= 那是个小镇，镇上几家人之间积怨已久，早已分裂。
be riven with something = 充满某种分裂

river
/ˈrɪvə(r)/
核心：河、江：the River Thames；（液体的）巨流、大量流淌：a river of。可数。
习语：sell somebody down the river 是出卖某人；up the river 是（美式俚语）坐牢。
① There is a narrow river at the bottom of the garden where the children catch fish.
= 花园尽头有条小河，孩子们在那儿捉鱼。
river = 河
② We had to cross the river by boat, because the bridge had been washed away.
= 桥被冲走了，我们只好坐船过河。
cross the river = 过河
③ Rivers of hot lava poured down the side of the volcano.
= 滚烫的熔岩如河流般从火山侧面倾泻而下。
a river of something = 某物的巨流
④ He trusted his business partner, but she sold him down the river to save herself.
= 他信任自己的生意伙伴，可她为了自保出卖了他。
sell somebody down the river = 出卖某人
⑤ If you keep stealing cars, you will end up up the river for ten years.
= 你再偷车，就得去坐十年牢。
up the river = 坐牢
⑥ River blindness is spread by small flies that breed in fast-flowing rivers in Africa.
= 河盲症由在非洲湍急河流里繁殖的小飞虫传播。
river blindness = 河盲症

riverbank

riverfront

riverine

riverine forests = 沿河森林

riverside

rivet
/ˈrɪvɪt/
核心：名词，可数：铆钉。动词：用铆钉固定、铆接；吸引（注意）、使（目光）集中：be riveted to / on；使呆住不动：be riveted to the spot。
① The metal sheets of the old ship were held together with thousands of rivets.
= 那艘旧船的钢板是用成千上万颗铆钉连在一起的。
a rivet = 铆钉
② The workers riveted the steel plates together to build the frame of the bridge.
= 工人们把钢板铆接起来，搭成桥的框架。
rivet something together = 把某物铆接在一起
③ Everyone's eyes were riveted on the screen as the final penalty was taken.
= 罚最后一个点球时，大家的眼睛都紧紧盯着屏幕。
be riveted on something = 目光紧盯着某物
④ She was riveted to the spot with fear when she saw the snake.
= 看到那条蛇，她吓得呆在原地动不了。
be riveted to the spot = 呆住不动

a riveting speech = 引人入胜的演讲
