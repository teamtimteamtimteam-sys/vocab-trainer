minimum
核心：最低的、最小的、最少的（只用在名词前）；最低限度、最小量。与 maximum 相对。
搭配 minimum wage（最低工资）与（美）minimum security prison（低度戒备监狱）并入本条。
① The minimum age for voting is eighteen.
= 最低投票年龄是十八岁。
the minimum age = 最低年龄
复数 minimums 或 minima。
② You need a minimum of three people to play the game.
= 玩那个游戏至少要三个人。
a minimum of = 至少
③ Keep the noise to a minimum after ten at night.
= 晚上十点以后把噪音降到最低。
keep something to a minimum = 把…降到最低
④ She earns only the minimum wage at the supermarket.
= 她在超市只挣最低工资。
the minimum wage = 最低工资
法律规定的最低时薪。
⑤ He served two years in a minimum security prison for fraud.
= 他因诈骗在一所低度戒备监狱服刑两年。
a minimum security prison = （美）低度戒备监狱

③ Just a minute; I have not finished speaking, and I still have two points to make.

just a minute = 等一下
④ The bus from the station should be here any minute now, so get your bags ready.

⑤ Call me the minute you arrive at the hotel, however late it is.

⑥ Come here this minute and tidy up your room!

⑦ They changed the plan at the last minute and moved the wedding indoors because of the rain.

⑨ I did not believe his story about the broken window for a minute.
