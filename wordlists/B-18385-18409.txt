rapid
/ˈræpɪd/
核心：迅速的、快速的、急促的：多说变化、增长、动作。副词 rapidly；名词 rapidity，不可数。并入本条。
复合词：rapid transit 是（城市的）快速交通；rapid-response 是快速反应的。
① Over the last twenty years the country has seen a rapid growth in its population.
= 近二十年来那个国家人口增长迅速。
rapid growth = 迅速的增长
rapid = 迅速的
② There has been a rapid rise in house prices this year.
= 今年房价飞涨。
a rapid rise = 迅速的上涨
rapid = 快速的（正式，多说变化）
③ He drove a fast car and made rapid progress through the traffic.
= 他开着一辆快车，在车流里迅速前进。
fast = 快的（通用，多说速度）
④ The doctors were surprised by her rapid recovery, but she gave only a quick nod when they praised her.
= 医生们对她的迅速康复感到吃惊，他们夸她时她却只快快点了下头。
quick = 快的（短促的动作）
⑤ The situation at the border is changing rapidly, and the army has been put on alert.
= 边境局势变化很快，军队已进入戒备状态。
rapidly = 迅速地
change rapidly = 迅速变化
⑥ The fire spread through the dry forest with frightening rapidity.
= 火势在干燥的森林里蔓延得快得吓人。
with rapidity = 迅速地
rapidity = 迅速
⑦ The city plans to build a rapid transit system to link the airport with the centre.
= 那座城市计划修建快速交通系统，连接机场和市中心。
rapid transit = 快速交通
⑧ After the earthquake the army sent in a rapid-response unit within hours.
= 地震之后几小时内，军队就派出了一支快速反应部队。
a rapid-response unit = 快速反应部队
