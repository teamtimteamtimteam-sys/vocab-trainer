payload
/ˈpeɪləʊd/
核心：三支，可数也可不可数。一是（飞机、车辆的）有效载荷：载的乘客和货物，能挣钱的那部分重量；
二是（导弹、炸弹里的）炸药量、弹头；三是（火箭、航天器送上天的）仪器设备、卫星。
① The new aircraft can carry a payload of forty tonnes over long distances.
= 新飞机能载着四十吨的货远程飞行。
a payload of forty tonnes = 四十吨的载荷
② The missile carries a payload of five hundred kilograms of explosive.
= 那种导弹装着五百公斤炸药。
a payload of explosive = 炸药装量
③ The rocket's payload was a weather satellite built in Japan.
= 那枚火箭运载的是一颗日本造的气象卫星。
the rocket's payload = 火箭运载的设备

paymaster
/ˈpeɪmɑːstə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是（多含贬义）出钱的主子：出钱养着一个人、一个组织，因而能支配它的人；二是（军队、政府、工厂里）发薪的官员、出纳。
① The newspaper never criticizes its paymasters in the oil industry.
= 那份报纸从不批评它在石油业的金主。
somebody's paymasters = 某方的金主
② The paymaster handed each soldier a brown envelope on Friday morning.
= 星期五上午，军需官发给每个士兵一个牛皮纸信封。
the paymaster = 发薪的军需官

payment
/ˈpeɪmənt/
核心：三支。一是付款、支付这件事，不可数：payment for、in payment；二是付的一笔钱、款项，可数：monthly payments；
三是报答、报偿，不可数或单数。
down payment 是首付、定金；payment in kind 是以实物、劳务抵付；on payment of 是付了……之后。
① Payment must be made within thirty days of the date on the invoice.
= 须在发票日期后三十天内付款。
make payment = 付款
② We offer a discount for payment in advance.
= 预先付款我们给折扣。
payment in advance = 预付
③ He makes monthly payments of two hundred pounds on the car.
= 那辆车他每月还两百英镑。
monthly payments = 每月还的款
④ They put down a payment of ten thousand on the flat.
= 他们为那套公寓付了一万的首付。
a down payment = 首付
⑤ The farm workers received part of their wages as payment in kind.
= 农场工人有一部分工钱是拿实物抵的。
payment in kind = 以实物抵付
⑥ Entry to the garden is free on payment of the annual fee.
= 交了年费就可以免费进园。
on payment of something = 付了某款之后
⑦ Is this all the payment I get for my efforts?
= 我费了那么大劲，就得这么点报答？
payment for something = 对某事的报答

pea

pea green = 豆绿色
④ He turned up the collar of his navy pea coat against the wind.

a pea coat = 水手短大衣
⑤ The boys fired dried peas at the windows with their pea-shooters.
= 男孩子们用射豆枪朝窗户上射干豌豆。
a pea-shooter = 射豆枪
⑥ In the old film the detective loses the thief in a London pea-souper.

a pea-souper = 黄色的浓雾

peace
/piːs/
核心：本义是「没有争斗、没有搅扰」。不可数，三支：和平：没有战争，也指和约、停战，这时可作单数：a lasting peace；
安宁、清静：没有吵闹和打搅：peace and quiet；内心的平静：peace of mind 是心安；还指人与人和睦相处：live in peace。
习语：make peace with 是跟……讲和、和好，make your peace with 是跟……和解；at peace 是处于和平中、心里安宁，也委婉地指已经安息；
keep the peace 是维持治安、不闹事；hold your peace 是（旧）保持沉默；rest in peace 是安息，刻在墓碑上；
disturb the peace 是扰乱治安；peace in our time 是「我们这一代的和平」，多用来讽刺靠让步换来的和平。
复合词：peace process 是和平进程；peace offering 是求和的礼物；peace pipe 是（北美印第安人的）和平烟斗；peace dividend 是和平红利：裁军省下来的钱；
peace officer 是（美式）治安官、执法人员；peacenik 是（口语，有时含贬义）反战分子。并入本条。
① The two countries have been at peace with each other for seventy years.
= 那两个国家彼此和平相处已经七十年了。
at peace = 处于和平中
② After six years of fighting the two sides finally signed a peace.
= 打了六年，双方终于签了和约。
sign a peace = 签和约
a lasting peace = 持久的和平
③ I just want an hour of peace and quiet to finish my book.
= 我只想清清静静待一个钟头，把书看完。
peace and quiet = 清静
④ For your own peace of mind, have the brakes checked before the journey.
= 为了自己心安，出门前把刹车检查一下吧。
peace of mind = 心安
⑤ The two communities have lived together in peace for generations.
= 那两个族群世世代代和睦相处。
live in peace = 和睦相处
⑥ He made peace with his brother before the old man died.
= 老人去世之前，他跟兄弟讲和了。
make peace with = 跟……讲和
make your peace with somebody = 跟某人和解
⑦ She seemed at peace with herself for the first time in years.
= 多年来她头一回显得心里安宁。
at peace with yourself = 心里安宁
⑧ Extra police were sent to keep the peace during the march.
= 游行期间加派了警察维持治安。
keep the peace = 维持治安
⑨ He wanted to object but decided to hold his peace until the vote.
= 他想反对，可还是决定表决之前不作声。
hold your peace = 保持沉默
⑩ She suffered for many years at the end, and may she now rest in peace.
= 她临终前受了多年的苦，愿她如今安息。
rest in peace = 安息
⑪ Two men were arrested outside the pub for disturbing the peace.
= 两个男人因扰乱治安在酒吧外面被捕。
disturb the peace = 扰乱治安
⑫ The treaty was sold to the voters as peace in our time.
= 那项条约是当作「我们这一代的和平」推销给选民的。
peace in our time = 我们这一代的和平
⑬ The killing of the envoy brought the peace process to a halt.
= 特使被杀，和平进程就此停顿。
the peace process = 和平进程
⑭ He bought her flowers as a peace offering after the quarrel.
= 吵架之后，他买了花给她，算是赔不是。
a peace offering = 求和的礼物
⑮ The chiefs smoked a peace pipe to seal the agreement.
= 酋长们抽了和平烟斗，把协议定了下来。
a peace pipe = 和平烟斗
⑯ The government promised to spend the peace dividend on schools.
= 政府许诺把裁军省下来的钱花在学校上。
the peace dividend = 和平红利
⑰ It is a crime to lie to a peace officer in that state.
= 在那个州，对执法人员撒谎是犯罪。
a peace officer = 治安官、执法人员
⑱ The general dismissed the protesters as a bunch of peaceniks.
= 将军把抗议的人说成一帮反战分子，不屑一顾。
a peacenik = 反战分子

peak
/piːk/
核心：本义是「尖顶」。名词，可数：山峰、尖尖的山顶；任何尖形的东西：打发的蛋白挑起来的尖；（英式）帽舌；
引申为顶峰、最高点：at the peak of、reach a peak。
动词：达到最高点。不及物。
形容词，只作定语：最高的、高峰时期的：peak hours、in peak condition。peak time 是高峰时段，也指（英式）电视的黄金时段；peak rate 是高峰时段的收费；
peak oil 是石油产量见顶（的那个时候）。
派生的 peaked 两义：有帽舌的：a peaked cap；（美式）脸色苍白憔悴的，英式说 peaky。并入本条。
① The peaks of the mountains were hidden in cloud all day.
= 群山的峰顶一整天都藏在云里。
a peak = 山峰
② Whisk the egg whites until they form stiff peaks.
= 把蛋白打到能挑起硬挺的尖。
stiff peaks = 硬挺的尖
③ He pulled the peak of his cap down over his eyes.
= 他把帽舌拉下来遮住眼睛。
the peak of a cap = 帽舌
④ She retired from tennis at the peak of her career.
= 她在事业的巅峰期退出了网坛。
at the peak of something = 在某事的巅峰
⑤ Traffic reaches a peak between eight and nine in the morning.
= 交通在早上八点到九点之间达到高峰。
reach a peak = 达到高峰
⑥ Unemployment peaked at twelve per cent and then began to fall.
= 失业率升到百分之十二的最高点，然后开始回落。
peak at twelve per cent = 在百分之十二见顶
⑦ Trains are crowded during peak hours and almost empty at noon.

⑧ The athletes are in peak condition for the start of the season.

⑨ Calls made at peak time cost three times as much.

peak time = 高峰时段

⑩ The book argues that the world passed peak oil some years ago.
= 那本书认为世界的石油产量几年前就见顶了。
peak oil = 石油产量见顶
⑪ The driver touched his peaked cap and opened the door of the car.

a peaked cap = 有帽舌的帽子
⑫ You look a little peaked, so sit down and have some water.
= 你脸色有点差，坐下喝点水吧。
look peaked = 脸色苍白憔悴

pearl
/pɜːl/
核心：本义是珍珠：一些牡蛎壳里长出的又小又硬的白色圆珠，很贵重，做首饰，可数；

习语：pearls of wisdom 是金玉良言，多带玩笑、挖苦；cast pearls before swine 是对牛弹琴、把好东西给不识货的人。
派生的 pearly 是珍珠似的、有珍珠光泽的；the pearly gates 是（玩笑）天堂的大门；
pearl barley 是珍珠麦：磨去了皮的大麦粒，放在汤里；伦敦的 pearly king、pearly queen 是穿着缀满珍珠纽扣的黑衣服、为慈善募捐的小贩。并入本条。
① She wore a string of pearls that had belonged to her grandmother.

② The knife has a handle of pearl and a silver blade.
= 那把刀是珍珠母的柄，银的刀身。
a handle of pearl = 珍珠母的柄
③ Pearls of dew hung on the spider's web in the early light.
= 晨光里，露珠挂在蜘蛛网上。
pearls of dew = 露珠
④ The little island is a pearl among the holiday places of the south.
= 那座小岛是南方度假胜地里的一颗明珠。
a pearl among something = 某类里的明珠
⑤ Thank you for those pearls of wisdom, but I think I can manage alone.
= 谢谢您的金玉良言，不过我想我自己对付得了。
pearls of wisdom = 金玉良言
⑥ Lecturing that class on poetry was like casting pearls before swine.

⑦ The baby smiled and showed two pearly teeth.
= 那个婴儿一笑，露出两颗珍珠似的小牙。
pearly = 珍珠似的
⑧ He joked that he would complain about the food when he reached the pearly gates.
= 他开玩笑说，等到了天堂门口，他要投诉那儿的伙食。

⑨ She thickened the lamb soup with a handful of pearl barley.
= 她抓了一把珍珠麦放进羊肉汤里，让汤稠一些。
pearl barley = 珍珠麦
⑩ A pearly king and queen led the parade, their suits covered with buttons.
= 一对缀满纽扣的「珍珠王」和「珍珠后」走在游行队伍的前头。
a pearly king = 珍珠王
a pearly queen = 珍珠后
