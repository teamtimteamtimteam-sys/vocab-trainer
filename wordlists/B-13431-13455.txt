/ˈliːmə(r)/

① The ring-tailed lemur has a long tail with black and white bands.
= 环尾狐猴有一条黑白环纹相间的长尾巴。
a ring-tailed lemur = 环尾狐猴
② Lemurs live only on one large island off the coast of Africa.
= 狐猴只生活在非洲海岸外的一座大岛上。
lemurs = 狐猴

lend
/lend/
核心：借给、借出；（银行）贷款给；（正式）给…增添（某种气氛、分量）。过去式、过去分词 lent。
注意方向：lend 是「借出去」，borrow 是「借进来」。
习语：lend a hand（帮个忙）、lend an ear（听人诉说）、
lend support to（支持）、lend your name to（挂名支持）。
名词 lender（出借人、放款机构）、lending（放贷）并入本条；搭配 lending rate（贷款利率）、lending library（可外借的图书馆）。
① Could you lend me ten pounds until Friday?
= 你能借我十英镑吗？星期五还你。
lend somebody something = 借给某人…
② The museum has lent three of its best paintings to a gallery in Paris for the summer.
= 博物馆把最好的三幅画借给了巴黎的一家画廊，展一个夏天。
lend a painting to a gallery = 把画借给画廊展出
lent 是过去式。两种句型都行：lend + 人 + 物，或 lend + 物 + to + 人。
③ The bank refused to lend them the money for the new shop.
= 银行拒绝贷款给他们开新店。
the bank lends money = 银行放贷
④ Can you lend a hand with these boxes?
= 你能搭把手搬这些箱子吗？
lend a hand = 帮个忙
⑤ She is always ready to lend an ear when I have a problem.
= 我有了难处，她总愿意听我说说。
lend an ear = 听人诉说
⑥ The story lends itself to being made into a film.
= 那个故事很适合拍成电影。
lend itself to = 适合于、便于
⑦ The candles lent a romantic air to the room.
= 蜡烛给房间添了一份浪漫的气氛。
lend an air to something = 给…增添气氛
⑧ The new figures lend support to the idea that prices are falling.
= 新数据印证了物价在下跌的看法。
lend support to = 支持、印证
⑨ Several famous actors have lent their names to the campaign.
= 好几位著名演员挂名支持了那场运动。
lend your name to something = 挂名支持…
⑩ The bank is the largest mortgage lender in the country.
= 那家银行是全国最大的房贷放款机构。
a lender = 放款机构、出借人
⑪ Bank lending to small businesses fell last year.
= 去年银行对小企业的放贷减少了。
lending = 放贷
⑫ The bank raised its lending rate by half a per cent.
= 那家银行把贷款利率上调了半个百分点。
the lending rate = 贷款利率
⑬ The town has had a free lending library since 1890.
= 那座小城从一八九〇年起就有一座免费外借的图书馆。
a lending library = 可外借的图书馆

length
/leŋθ/
核心：长度；（时间的）长短；（书、文章的）篇幅；（绳、布、管子的）一段；（游泳池的）一个池长。
习语：at length（详尽地、长时间地；文：终于）、go to great lengths（不遗余力、想尽办法）、
the length and breadth of（…的四面八方、全境）、at arm's length（保持距离地；伸直手臂拿着）。
副词 lengthways（纵向地，也说 lengthwise）并入本条。
① The room is five metres in length and four in width.
= 那个房间长五米，宽四米。
five metres in length = 长五米
② The length of the film put many people off going to see it.
= 那部电影太长，很多人因此不想去看。
the length of a film = 电影的时长
③ He was surprised by the length of the queue outside the shop.
= 商店外面的队排得那么长，他很吃惊。
the length of the queue = 队伍的长度
④ She cut a length of string and tied the parcel with it.
= 她剪了一段绳子，用它把包裹捆好。
a length of string = 一段绳子
⑤ I swim thirty lengths of the pool three times a week.
= 我每周三次在泳池里游三十个池长。
thirty lengths = 三十个池长
⑥ The committee discussed the plan at length but reached no decision.
= 委员会把那个方案讨论了很久，却没作出决定。
at length = 详尽地、长时间地
⑦ She went to great lengths to keep the party a secret from him.
= 为了把聚会瞒着他，她费尽了心思。
go to great lengths = 不遗余力、想尽办法
⑧ He has travelled the length and breadth of the country selling books.
= 他走遍全国各地卖书。
the length and breadth of = …的全境
⑨ She has always kept her neighbours at arm's length.
= 她一向和邻居保持距离。
at arm's length = 保持距离地
⑩ Cut the carrots in half lengthways and roast them with honey.
= 把胡萝卜纵向对半切开，抹上蜂蜜烤。
lengthways = 纵向地

lengthen
/ˈleŋθən/
核心：加长、延长；变长。与 shorten 相对。

② The days begin to lengthen in January, though the weather stays cold.
= 一月里白天开始变长，不过天气仍然寒冷。

③ The waiting time for an operation has lengthened to six months.
= 手术的等候时间已延长到六个月。
lengthen to six months = 延长到六个月
-en 把名词、形容词变成动词：strengthen、widen。

lengthy
/ˈleŋθi/

less
/les/
核心：较少的、更少的（little 的比较级）；副词：较不、不那么；代词：较少的量。介词：减去。
接不可数名词；可数名词严格说用 fewer，口语里常混用。
习语：more or less（或多或少、差不多）、no less than（多达、不少于）、less and less（越来越少）、

① You should eat less salt and drink more water.
= 你应该少吃盐，多喝水。

② The journey takes less time by train than by car.

③ The second film was less interesting than the first.
= 第二部电影不如第一部有意思。

④ I see him less often now that he has moved away.
= 他搬走以后，我见他的次数少了。
less often = 不那么经常
⑤ The job took less than an hour.
= 那活儿不到一个小时就干完了。
less than an hour = 不到一小时
⑥ I have more or less finished the report.
= 那份报告我差不多写完了。
more or less = 差不多、或多或少
⑦ No less than half the class failed the exam.
= 班上不及格的竟有一半之多。
no less than = 多达
强调数量大得惊人。
⑧ She goes out less and less since her husband died.
= 丈夫去世后，她越来越少出门。
less and less = 越来越少
⑨ He can hardly walk, much less run a race.
= 他连路都走不稳，更不用说赛跑了。

⑩ He was less than pleased to find us in his office.
= 发现我们在他办公室里，他很不高兴。
less than pleased = 很不高兴
委婉的反话。
⑪ You will get a month's pay, less tax.
= 你会拿到一个月的工资，要扣掉税。
less tax = 扣掉税
这一支是介词：减去。

④ The gallery also shows some of the painter's lesser-known works.
= 那家画廊还展出那位画家一些不太出名的作品。

lesson
/ˈlesən/
核心：一节课；（复数）课程；教训；（教堂里读的）一段经文。
习语：learn your lesson（吸取教训）、teach somebody a lesson（给某人一个教训）。
① The first lesson on Monday morning is maths, followed by an hour of French.
= 星期一上午的第一节课是数学，接着是一小时法语。
the first lesson = 第一节课
② She is taking driving lessons with her uncle in the car park behind the factory.
= 她在工厂后面的停车场跟叔叔学开车。
take driving lessons = 上驾驶课
take lessons = 上课、跟人学
③ The accident taught him a lesson about driving too fast.
= 那次事故让他明白了开快车的后果。
teach somebody a lesson = 给某人一个教训
④ I forgot my umbrella and got soaked, so I have learned my lesson.
= 我忘了带伞，淋得透湿，这下长记性了。
learn your lesson = 吸取教训
⑤ There are lessons to be learned from the failure of the project.
= 那个项目的失败有教训可以吸取。
lessons to be learned = 可吸取的教训
⑥ Let that be a lesson to you not to leave your bike unlocked.
= 这回你该记住了，自行车不能不锁。
let that be a lesson to you = 这回你该记住了
⑦ The headmaster read the lesson at the Christmas service in the village church.
= 在村教堂的圣诞礼拜上，校长读了经文。
read the lesson = 读经文
⑧ The flood was an object lesson in the dangers of building on a marsh.
= 那场洪水是在沼泽上盖房子有多危险的活教材。
an object lesson = 活生生的实例
