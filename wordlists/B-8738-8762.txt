fightback
核心：（英式）反击、逆转：从落后处一路追回来。可数。
① The fightback started in the second half and ended two points short.
= 那场反击从下半场开始，最后差两分。
a fightback = 反击、逆转
② A fightback needs a plan and about forty minutes of clock.
= 要逆转得有方案，还得有大约四十分钟的时间。
stage a fightback = 展开反击

fighter
核心：三支。一是斗士、不服输的人；二是拳击手、格斗选手；
三是战斗机：a fighter jet。可数。
① She is a fighter and the ward staff say so in her notes.
= 她是个不服输的人，病房的护士在记录里也这么写。
a fighter = 斗士、不服输的人
② The fighter came in low over the estuary and set off nine car alarms.
= 那架战斗机贴着河口低飞过来，惊响了九个汽车警报。
a fighter jet = 喷气式战斗机

fighting
核心：两支。一是战斗、打斗，不可数：heavy fighting；
二是作定语，好斗的、战斗用的：a fighting spirit、fighting talk。
① Heavy fighting closed the road and the market moved to the next village.
= 激烈的战斗封了路，集市挪到了邻村。
heavy fighting = 激烈的战斗
② A fighting spirit and one working ambulance kept that clinic open.
= 一股不服输的劲头，加一辆还能开的救护车，撑住了那家诊所。
a fighting spirit = 不服输的劲头
fighting talk = 放狠话、示强的话

figment
核心：凭空想出来的东西：几乎只用在 a figment of somebody's imagination 里。可数。
① The deadline turned out to be a figment of one manager's imagination.
= 那个截止日期，原来是某位经理凭空想出来的。
a figment of somebody's imagination = 某人凭空想出来的东西
② The noise is not a figment and three neighbours have written it down.
= 那声音不是幻觉，三个邻居都记下来了。
a figment = 凭空想出来的东西

figurative
核心：两支。一是比喻的、引申的：a figurative sense，跟字面义 literal 相对；
二是（艺术）具象的：figurative painting。副词 figuratively。
① The phrase has a figurative sense that the sign painter did not consider.
= 那个说法有一层比喻义，做招牌的人没考虑到。
a figurative sense = 比喻义
② Figurative painting came back into the galleries as it was leaving the schools.
= 具象绘画回到画廊的时候，正从学校里退场。
figurative painting = 具象绘画
副词 figuratively：比喻地说，多用 figuratively speaking 打头。
③ Figuratively speaking, that department has been on fire since March.
= 打个比方说，那个部门从三月起就一直在着火。
figuratively speaking = 打个比方说
④ She meant it figuratively and the minutes recorded it as a threat.
= 她说的是比喻，会议记录却当成了威胁记下来。
mean something figuratively = 意在比喻

figure
核心：五支。一是数字、数目：sales figures；二是身材；三是人物：a public figure；
四是（远处的）人影；五是动词，想明白：figure something out。
习语 a figure of speech 是修辞说法，cut a figure 是显出某种模样。
① Sales figures for June arrived in August and changed twice after that.
= 六月的销售数字八月才到，之后又改了两回。
sales figures = 销售数字
② He became a public figure by accident and never got used to it.
= 他是稀里糊涂成了公众人物的，一直没习惯。
a public figure = 公众人物
③ A figure at the end of the platform turned out to be a coat on a trolley.
= 站台尽头那个人影，原来是搭在推车上的一件大衣。
a figure = 人影
④ It took her a whole weekend to figure out what the second switch does.
= 她花了整整一个周末才弄明白第二个开关是干什么的。
figure something out = 弄明白某事
⑤ Saying that the door is watching you is a figure of speech, or was.
= 说那扇门在看着你，是个修辞说法 —— 至少从前是。
a figure of speech = 修辞说法
cut a figure = 显出某种模样

figured
核心：（织物、木材）有花纹的：figured silk、figured walnut。多作定语。
① Figured silk catches the light and hides the stain from two angles.
= 提花绸缎能吃光，从两个角度还遮得住那块污渍。
figured silk = 提花绸缎
② The panel is figured walnut and cost more than the rest of the room.
= 那块面板是花纹胡桃木，比屋里其余的东西加起来还贵。
figured walnut = 花纹胡桃木

figurehead
核心：两支。一是（船首的）雕像；二是有名无实的首脑，只挂个名。可数。
① The figurehead from that ship is in a museum and the ship itself is not.
= 那条船的船首像在博物馆里，船本身不在。
a figurehead = 船首像
② The chairman is a figurehead and the deputy signs absolutely everything.
= 董事长是挂名的，什么都由副手签。
a mere figurehead = 纯粹挂名的首脑

figurine
核心：小雕像、小塑像：摆在架子上的那种瓷或树脂小人。可数。
① A figurine of a shepherdess survived four house moves and one flood.
= 一尊牧羊女小瓷像，扛过四次搬家和一次水灾。
a figurine = 小雕像
② The shelf holds nine figurines and a dust cloth that never gets used.
= 那个架子上摆着九尊小塑像，还有一块从没用过的抹布。
a china figurine = 瓷制小塑像

Fijian
核心：斐济的、斐济人，也指斐济语：形容词与名词同形。
① The Fijian team sings before the match and again after it.
= 斐济队赛前唱歌，赛后再唱一次。
the Fijian team = 斐济队
② Two Fijians in that squad grew up within a mile of each other.
= 那支队里的两个斐济人，是在相距不到一英里的地方长大的。
a Fijian = 斐济人

filament
核心：两支。一是（灯泡的）灯丝；二是（生物、材料）细丝、丝状体。可数。
① The filament goes the moment you switch the lamp on, never while it burns.
= 灯丝总在开灯那一下断，从不在亮着的时候断。
a filament = 灯丝
② Each filament is thinner than a hair and carries the whole current.
= 每一根细丝比头发还细，却承着全部电流。
a fine filament = 极细的丝

filbert
核心：榛子：一种榛果，跟 hazelnut 基本是一回事，果壳更长些。可数。
① Filberts fall in September and the squirrels get a fortnight's start.
= 榛子九月落下，松鼠比人早开工两个星期。
a filbert = 榛子
② A pound of filberts makes two jars of something nobody can stop eating.
= 一磅榛子能做出两罐让人停不下嘴的东西。
filberts = 榛子（复数）

filch
核心：（非正式）顺手拿走：偷不值钱的小东西，语气比 steal 轻得多。
① Somebody filched the good scissors and left the blunt pair behind.
= 有人顺走了那把好剪刀，把钝的那把留下了。
filch something = 顺走某物
② Biscuits get filched from that tin faster than anybody replaces them.
= 那个铁罐里的饼干，被顺走的速度比补进去的还快。
be filched = 被顺走

file
核心：五支。一是文件夹、卷宗：a personnel file；二是（电脑）文件：save a file；
三是动词，归档、正式提交：file a report、file for divorce；
四是锉刀，也作动词锉；五是一列纵队：in single file。习语 on file 是存档备查。
① The personnel file has three sheets and a photograph nobody recognises.
= 那份人事档案有三页纸，还有一张谁也认不出的照片。
a personnel file = 人事档案
on file = 存档备查的
② Save the file before you answer the phone, not after it.
= 接电话之前存文件，别接完了再存。
save a file = 保存文件
③ She filed for divorce on a Tuesday and told nobody until the Friday.
= 她周二递了离婚申请，到周五才跟人说。
file for divorce = 申请离婚
file a report = 提交报告
④ He filed the key down until it turned and then had two copies cut.
= 他把钥匙锉到能转动，然后配了两把。
file something down = 把某物锉小
⑤ The children came in in single file and left in every direction at once.
= 孩子们排成一列进来，散开时各奔东西。
in single file = 排成一列纵队

filename
核心：（电脑）文件名：后面多带扩展名。可数。
① The filename has a date in it and the date is wrong.
= 那个文件名里带着日期，日期是错的。
a filename = 文件名
② A filename with a space in it breaks that script every single time.
= 文件名里带空格，那个脚本每回都崩。
change the filename = 改文件名

filet
核心：（美式拼法）去骨的肉片、鱼片：英式写 fillet；
最常见的说法是 filet mignon。
① The filet mignon is the smallest cut and the biggest number on the menu.
= 菲力牛排是最小的一块肉，菜单上却是最大的那个数字。
filet mignon = 菲力牛排
② A filet of sole cooks in the time it takes to warm the plate.
= 一片去骨鳎鱼，盘子热好的工夫就熟了。
a filet = 去骨的肉片或鱼片

filial
核心：（正式）子女的、孝顺的：filial duty、filial piety。
① Filial duty in that family means a phone call every Sunday at six.
= 那家人的孝道，就是每周日六点打个电话。
filial duty = 子女的本分
② Filial piety and a train ticket do not always arrive together.
= 孝心跟一张火车票，未必总是同时到位。
filial piety = 孝道

filibuster
核心：（政治）冗长发言阻挠议事：靠一直讲话把议案拖死。名词与动词同形。
① The filibuster lasted eleven hours and the bill passed the following week.
= 那场冗长阻挠讲了十一个钟头，法案下一周照样通过。
a filibuster = 冗长发言阻挠
② He filibustered until the clock ran out and drank nine glasses of water.
= 他一直讲到散会时间，喝了九杯水。
filibuster a bill = 用冗长发言拖死一项议案

filigree
核心：金银丝细工：把细丝盘成花纹的装饰；引申指精细繁复的纹样。不可数。
① The filigree on the lid is silver and the box underneath it is tin.

② A filigree of frost covered the window and lasted until nine.

a filigree of something = 某物结成的细密花纹

filing

fill
核心：四支。一是装满、填满：fill a glass；二是填补空缺：fill a vacancy；
三是名词，足量：eat your fill；四是短语动词 fill in 与 fill up。
习语 fill the bill 是「正合适」。
① Fill the glass to the line and not to the top.
= 倒到那条线为止，别倒满。
fill a glass = 把杯子倒满
② The vacancy was filled by somebody who had left the year before.
= 那个空缺，由一位前一年离职的人补上了。
fill a vacancy = 填补空缺
③ They ate their fill and took the rest home in a box.
= 他们吃了个饱，剩下的装盒带回家。
eat your fill = 吃个饱
④ Fill in the form in black ink and fill up the car before you set off.
= 表格用黑墨水填，出发前把车加满油。
fill in something = 填写某物
fill up = 加满
⑤ A folding table fills the bill and costs eleven pounds.
= 一张折叠桌正合适，才十一镑钱。
fill the bill = 正合适

filler

= 木用腻子干了之后比木头浅，修过的地方谁都看得出。
wood filler = 木用腻子

= 那档新闻插了一条关于鸭子的补白，把议会那条撤了。

fillet
