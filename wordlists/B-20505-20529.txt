self-regulation
/ˌself ˌreɡjuˈleɪʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：（行业的）自律、自我监管；（个人的）自我调节。
① Critics say that self-regulation of the banks failed to stop the financial crisis.
= 批评者说，银行业的自律没能阻止那场金融危机。
self-regulation = 自律
② Young children are still learning self-regulation and often cannot control their anger.
= 幼儿还在学习自我调节，常常控制不住怒气。
learn self-regulation = 学习自我调节

self-reliance
/ˌself rɪˈlaɪəns/
核心：名词，不可数：自立、自力更生。
① Living alone in the countryside for a year taught her self-reliance and practical skills.
= 独自在乡下住了一年，教会了她自立和实用的本领。
self-reliance = 自立
多用于个人，也可说国家经济的自力更生。
② The school encourages self-reliance by letting older children organize their own trips.
= 那所学校让高年级学生自己组织出游，鼓励他们自立。
encourage self-reliance = 鼓励自立

self-report
/ˌself rɪˈpɔːt/
核心：名词，可数：（研究中的）自我报告、自述。动词：自我报告。多作定语：self-report data。
① The study is based on self-report, so people may not always tell the truth about how much they drink.
= 那项研究基于自我报告，所以人们未必总会如实说自己喝多少酒。
self-report = 自我报告
② Patients self-report their pain on a scale from one to ten every morning.
= 病人每天早上按一到十的等级自己报告疼痛程度。
self-report = 自我报告（动词）

self-respect
/ˌself rɪˈspekt/
核心：名词，不可数：自尊、自重。
① Losing his job took away his self-respect, and he stopped looking after himself.
= 失业夺走了他的自尊，他也不再照顾自己了。
self-respect = 自尊
偏「自重、有骨气」，指不肯做降低身份、有失尊严的事。
② No one with any self-respect would accept such a rude offer from the company.
= 有点自尊心的人都不会接受那家公司那么无礼的条件。
anyone with self-respect = 有自尊心的人

self-respecting
/ˌself rɪˈspektɪŋ/
核心：形容词，只作定语：自重的、有自尊的；（幽默）够格的、像样的：no self-respecting cat would…。
① No self-respecting chef would serve frozen vegetables in an expensive restaurant like this.
= 稍有自尊的厨师都不会在这样的高档餐馆里上冷冻蔬菜。
no self-respecting chef = 稍有自尊的厨师
self-respecting = 自重的
② Every self-respecting teenager wants the latest phone, according to the advertisements on television.
= 按电视广告的说法，每个像样的青少年都想要最新款的手机。
every self-respecting teenager = 每个像样的青少年

self-restraint
/ˌself rɪˈstreɪnt/
核心：名词，不可数：自我克制、自制。
① It took a lot of self-restraint not to eat the whole box of chocolates at once.
= 忍住不一口气吃光一整盒巧克力，需要很强的自制力。
self-restraint = 自我克制
② The police showed great self-restraint when the protesters threw bottles at them.
= 抗议者朝警察扔瓶子时，警察表现得非常克制。
show self-restraint = 表现得克制

self-righteous
/ˌself ˈraɪtʃəs/
核心：形容词（贬义）：自以为是的、自命清高的：觉得只有自己对、道德比别人高。副词 self-righteously 是自以为是地。名词 self-righteousness 另立条。
① I hate his self-righteous tone when he tells everyone else how they should live.
= 他告诉别人该怎么生活时那种自以为是的口气，我很讨厌。
a self-righteous tone = 自以为是的口气
self-righteous = 自以为是的
② "I would never do such a thing," she said self-righteously, although we all knew she had.
= 「我绝不会做那种事。」她自命清高地说，可我们都知道她做过。
self-righteously = 自以为是地

self-righteousness
/ˌself ˈraɪtʃəsnəs/
核心：名词，不可数（贬义）：自以为是、自命清高。
① The self-righteousness of the new manager made him unpopular with the older staff.
= 新经理的自以为是让老员工很不待见他。
self-righteousness = 自以为是
② The film makes fun of the self-righteousness of people who judge others but ignore their own faults.
= 那部电影嘲讽了那些评判别人、却无视自己毛病的人的自命清高。
the self-righteousness of somebody = 某人的自命清高

self-rule

win self-rule = 赢得自治
self-rule = 自治
② The islanders voted for self-rule, but they wanted to keep close ties with the mainland.
= 岛民们投票赞成自治，但希望和大陆保持密切联系。
vote for self-rule = 投票赞成自治

the self-sacrifice of somebody = 某人的自我牺牲

self-sacrificing

① She was a kind, self-sacrificing woman who always put her family before herself.

a self-sacrificing doctor = 舍己为人的医生

a self-selected group = 自选的群体
self-selected = 自选的

= 自选样本得出的结果未必代表全体人口的想法。
a self-selected sample = 自选样本

self-selecting
/ˌself sɪˈlektɪŋ/

① The class for advanced students is self-selecting, because only the keenest choose to join it.

self-selecting polls = 自愿参与的投票

self-selection
/ˌself sɪˈlekʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：自我选择：（研究中）参与者自行决定是否参加，可能造成偏差。
① Self-selection is a problem in the study, because only healthy people chose to take part.
= 自我选择是那项研究的一个问题，因为只有健康的人选择参加。
self-selection = 自我选择
② The researchers tried to avoid self-selection by choosing people at random from the phone book.
= 研究人员从电话簿里随机挑选参与者，设法避免自我选择偏差。
avoid self-selection = 避免自我选择偏差

self-sufficiency
/ˌself səˈfɪʃnsi/

① The farm aims at complete self-sufficiency, growing all its own food and making its own electricity.
= 那座农场以完全自给自足为目标，粮食全靠自己种，电也自己发。
complete self-sufficiency = 完全自给自足
self-sufficiency = 自给自足
② The government wants the country to reach self-sufficiency in energy within twenty years.
= 政府希望国家在二十年内实现能源自给。
self-sufficiency in something = 某方面的自给自足
用于国家时多指粮食、能源不依赖进口。

self-sufficient
/ˌself səˈfɪʃnt/
核心：形容词：自给自足的：be self-sufficient in something；（人）自立的、不依赖别人的。
① The island is self-sufficient in vegetables, but it has to bring in meat and fuel by boat.
= 那座岛蔬菜能自给自足，但肉和燃料得靠船运进来。
be self-sufficient in something = 某方面自给自足
self-sufficient = 自给自足的
② Living alone in the mountains made her very self-sufficient and good at fixing things.
= 独自住在山里让她变得非常自立，也很会修东西。
very self-sufficient = 非常自立
说人时有时略带「不需要别人、难以亲近」的意味。
