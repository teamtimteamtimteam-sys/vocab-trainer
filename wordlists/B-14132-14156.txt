materialism
/məˈtɪəriəlɪzəm/
核心：物质主义、实利主义 —— 把钱财、物质享受看得最重；（哲学）唯物主义。
名词 materialist（物质主义者；唯物主义者）与形容词 materialistic（物质主义的）并入本条。
① The priest spoke against the materialism of modern life.
= 神父抨击现代生活中的物质主义。
materialism = 物质主义
日常多含贬义。
② He is a materialist who judges people by the cars they drive.
= 他是个物质主义者，看人只看开什么车。
a materialist = 物质主义者
③ Children are growing up in a very materialistic society.
= 孩子们在一个非常物质化的社会里长大。
a materialistic society = 物质化的社会

① The pay rise that the manager promised us last spring never materialized.
= 经理去年春天答应我们的加薪始终没有兑现。
never materialize = 始终没有实现
② A waiter materialized at our table with the bill before we had finished the coffee.
= 我们咖啡还没喝完，一个服务员就拿着账单出现在桌旁。
materialize at the table = 突然出现在桌旁
③ The materialization of the plan depends on money from the government.
= 那个计划能否落实，取决于政府拨不拨款。
materialization = 实现

= 警方正在调查俱乐部钱款失踪一事。

⑤ It does not matter if you are a few minutes late; we never start on time.
= 你晚几分钟没关系，我们从来不准点开始。
it does not matter = 没关系
⑥ Money matters less to her than time with her children.

⑧ As a matter of fact, I have never been to Paris, though I teach French.

⑩ The ambulance arrived in a matter of minutes.

= 我不喜欢那部电影，说到这个，书我也不喜欢。

= 护士照例给每位病人量体温。
as a matter of course = 照例、理所当然地
a matter of course = 理所当然的事

to make matters worse = 更糟的是
⑭ Whether the film is good is a matter of opinion.
= 那部电影好不好，见仁见智。
a matter of opinion = 见仁见智的事
⑮ Getting the child to hospital was a matter of life and death.
= 把孩子送到医院是生死攸关的事。
a matter of life and death = 生死攸关的事
⑯ Scientists study how matter behaves at very low temperatures.
= 科学家研究物质在极低温度下的表现。
