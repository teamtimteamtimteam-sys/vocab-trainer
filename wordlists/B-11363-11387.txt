hostler
核心：（美式，旧）马夫 —— 旅店里照料客人马匹的人；也指铁路上照看机车的工人。
① The hostler took our horses round to the stable behind the inn.
= 马夫把我们的马牵到旅店后面的马厩里。
a hostler = 马夫
② He worked as a hostler at the rail yard, moving engines in and out of the shed.
= 他在铁路调车场当机车看管工，把机车开进开出库房。
a hostler = （铁路）机车看管工

hot
核心：底层意思是「温度高」—— 热的、烫的。比较级 hotter、最高级 hottest。
跟 warm 的分界：warm 是舒服的暖和，这个词是热到让人出汗、烫手。
从「热」长出七支：
一、温度：hot weather、a hot bath、I'm hot；复合词 hot-water bottle、hot tub、hot flush；
二、味道辣：a hot curry —— 辣是一种「烧」的感觉；
三、情绪激烈：hot-tempered、hot-headed、hot-blooded、hot under the collar、
hot and bothered、blow hot and cold；
四、（口语）性感的、有性吸引力的 —— have the hots for somebody 是「迷上某人」；
五、热门的、新鲜的：a hot topic、the hot favourite、hot off the press、sell like hot cakes；
六、危险、棘手：a hot potato、too hot to handle、in hot water、in the hot seat；
七、紧追不放：hot on the heels of、in hot pursuit。
此外还有口语的「擅长」（be hot on something、not so hot）、赃物（hot goods）、
电气与计算机里的「通电的、即时的」（hot-wire、a hot key、hot-swap、a hot shoe）。
英式短语动词 hot up：变得更激烈、更热闹。
① It was too hot to sleep, so we sat on the balcony until two in the morning.
= 热得睡不着，我们在阳台上一直坐到凌晨两点。
too hot to do something = 热得没法做某事
本义：温度高到让人难受。A 表教过的「热的、烫的」都在这一支。
② Be careful, the plate is very hot, it's just come out of the oven.
= 小心，盘子很烫，刚从烤箱里拿出来。
be very hot = 很烫
说东西摸着烫；比 warm 高得多。注意别跟连写的「电炉灶」那个词混。
③ She filled a hot-water bottle and took it up to bed.
= 她灌了一个热水袋带上床。
a hot-water bottle = 热水袋
④ The hotel has a hot tub on the roof with a view of the mountains.
= 那家酒店的屋顶上有一个热水浴池，看得见群山。
a hot tub = 热水浴池
⑤ Her hot flushes woke her several times a night during the menopause.
= 更年期期间，潮热让她一夜醒好几次。
a hot flush = 潮热（英式）
美式说 a hot flash。
⑥ The curry was so hot that he drank three glasses of water.
= 那份咖喱辣得他喝了三杯水。
a hot curry = 辣咖喱
第二支：辣。A 表教过「hot = 辣的」；要说「热而不辣」得换 warm。
⑦ He has a hot temper and has been thrown out of two pubs this year.
= 他脾气火暴，今年已经被两家酒吧赶了出来。
a hot temper = 火暴脾气
第三支：情绪「烧」起来。
⑧ A hot-tempered driver got out of his car and shouted at the cyclist.
= 一个脾气火暴的司机下车冲着骑车的人大喊。
hot-tempered = 脾气火暴的
⑨ Her hot-headed brother was always getting into fights at school.
= 她那个鲁莽的哥哥在学校里老是跟人打架。
hot-headed = 鲁莽冲动的
⑩ The novel is about two hot-blooded young men in love with the same woman.
= 那部小说讲两个热血方刚的年轻人爱上了同一个女人。
hot-blooded = 热血的、易激动的
⑪ Dad got very hot under the collar when the waiter brought the wrong bill.
= 服务员拿错账单时，老爸气得够呛。
hot under the collar = 气恼、激动
⑫ She was hot and bothered after running for the train in the midday sun.
= 大中午在太阳底下跑着赶火车，她又热又烦躁。
hot and bothered = 又热又烦躁、心烦意乱
⑬ The company has blown hot and cold about the new factory for two years.
= 两年来，那家公司对新建工厂的事一会儿热心一会儿冷淡。
blow hot and cold = 忽冷忽热、摇摆不定
⑭ Everyone at the party thought her new boyfriend was hot.
= 派对上的人都觉得她的新男友很性感。
hot = 性感的（口语）
第四支：身体上的「热」引申为性吸引力。
⑮ He's had the hots for his sister's friend since they were at school.
= 他从上学时就迷上了他姐姐的一个朋友。
have the hots for somebody = 迷上某人（俚语）
⑯ Climate change is the hot topic at every dinner party this year.
= 今年每场晚宴上的热门话题都是气候变化。
a hot topic = 热门话题
第五支：「热」就是受关注、正当红。
⑰ The home team are the hot favourites to win the cup.
= 主队是夺杯的大热门。
the hot favourite = 大热门
⑱ Tickets for the final sold like hot cakes and were gone in an hour.
= 决赛的票卖得飞快，一个钟头就没了。
sell like hot cakes = 卖得飞快、抢手
⑲ The first copies are hot off the press and still smell of ink.
= 第一批样书刚刚印出来，还带着油墨味。
hot off the press = 刚印出来的；最新的
⑳ Her new designs are hot stuff among young buyers this season.
= 她的新设计这一季在年轻买家中很抢手。
hot stuff = 抢手货；性感的人（口语）
㉑ School funding is a political hot potato that no minister wants to touch.
= 学校经费是一个没有哪个部长愿意碰的烫手山芋。
a hot potato = 烫手山芋
第六支：太烫拿不住，比喻危险、棘手。
㉒ The story was too hot to handle, so the paper dropped it.
= 那则报道太棘手，报社就撤了。
too hot to handle = 太棘手、碰不得
㉓ He's in hot water with the tax office over his old accounts.
= 他因旧账跟税务局闹上了麻烦。
be in hot water = 陷入麻烦
㉔ The new minister is in the hot seat now that the scandal has broken.
= 丑闻一曝光，新部长就坐上了火山口。
in the hot seat = 处于要负责的位置、坐在火山口上
㉕ Immigration has become a hot button in the election campaign.
= 移民问题已成为这场竞选中一触即发的敏感话题。

㉖ The police were in hot pursuit and caught the thieves at the end of the street.
= 警察紧追不舍，在街尾抓住了小偷。
in hot pursuit = 紧追不舍
第七支：追得「热乎乎地」贴在身后。
㉗ The second album came hot on the heels of the first.
= 第二张专辑紧跟着第一张推出。
hot on the heels of something = 紧跟在…之后
㉘ The new head is very hot on punctuality and locks the door at nine.
= 新校长特别讲究守时，九点一到就锁门。
be hot on something = 特别讲究、精通某事（英式口语）
㉙ I'm not so hot at maths, so my sister checks the bills.
= 我数学不怎么样，所以账单都是我姐姐核对的。
not so hot = 不怎么样、不擅长（口语）
㉚ The watches turned out to be hot, and the market trader was arrested.
= 那些手表原来是赃物，市场摊贩被捕了。
hot = 偷来的、赃物（俚语）
㉛ The race is hotting up as the leaders reach the final bend.
= 领先的几位选手到了最后一个弯道，比赛越来越激烈。
hot up = 变得更激烈、更热闹（英式）
㉜ He hot-wired a car outside the station and drove it to the coast.
= 他在车站外面用线路短接发动了一辆车，一路开到了海边。
hot-wire a car = 不用钥匙短接点火发动汽车
㉝ Press the hot key to save the file without leaving the game.
= 按那个快捷键，不用退出游戏就能存档。
a hot key = 快捷键
㉞ The drives can be hot-swapped without switching off the server.
= 那些硬盘不用关服务器就能热插拔。
hot-swap = 热插拔
㉟ He slid the flash into the hot shoe on top of the camera.
= 他把闪光灯插进相机顶部的热靴里。
the hot shoe = 热靴（相机上的闪光灯插座）
㊱ The office has switched to hot-desking, so nobody has their own desk any more.
= 办公室改成了轮用工位，谁都没有固定的桌子了。
hot-desking = 轮用工位
名词形式说这种制度；具体那张轮用的桌子叫 a hot desk。
㊲ She grabbed a hot desk by the window before the others arrived.
= 别人还没到，她先抢了一张靠窗的轮用工位。
a hot desk = 轮用工位（一张桌子）
㊳ They sold hot dogs and cold drinks from a cart outside the stadium.
= 他们在体育场外推着小车卖热狗和冷饮。
a hot dog = 热狗
㊴ Most of the minister's speech was hot air, with no figures at all.
= 部长的讲话大多是空话，一个数字都没有。
hot air = 空话、大话
㊵ He spent his weekends building a hot rod in his father's garage.
= 他周末都在父亲的车库里改装一辆高速赛车。
a hot rod = 改装的高速汽车
㊶ She wore hot pants and a hot pink jacket to the seventies party.
= 她穿着超短热裤和一件艳粉色夹克去参加七十年代主题派对。
hot pants = 超短热裤
hot pink = 艳粉色
㊷ He was on a hot streak and couldn't miss, and the fans said he had the hot hand.
= 他手感火热，投什么进什么，球迷说他手热。

核心：本义是（园艺）温床 —— 铺着发酵肥料、给早春秧苗保温的苗床。

= 六十年代末，那所大学是激进政治的温床。

由「猎犬追着不放」长出动词：hound somebody（纠缠、不停地骚扰）；
hound somebody out of / from somewhere（逼得某人离开）。
口语里 -hound 放在别的词后面指「热衷于某事的人」：a news hound、a publicity hound。
① The hounds picked up the scent and ran off across the field.
= 猎犬嗅到了气味，飞奔着穿过田野。
hounds = 猎犬（复数）
② An old hound dog lay asleep on the porch in the afternoon heat.
= 一条老猎狗躺在门廊上，在午后的炎热里睡着了。
a hound dog = 猎犬、狗（美式口语）
③ Photographers hounded her everywhere she went after the divorce.
= 离婚之后，她走到哪里，摄影记者就追到哪里。
hound somebody = 纠缠、不停地骚扰某人
④ The minister was hounded out of office by a campaign in the tabloids.
= 小报发起的一场攻势把那位部长逼下了台。
hound somebody out of something = 逼得某人离开
⑤ The family was hounded from the village after the trial.
= 审判之后，那一家人被逼得离开了村子。
hound somebody from somewhere = 把某人撵出某地
⑥ He was a publicity hound who would open a supermarket if there was a camera there.
= 他是个出名狂，只要有摄像机，连超市开张都肯去剪彩。
a publicity hound = 爱出风头的人

houndstooth
核心：千鸟格 —— 由小小的锯齿形方格交错组成的布料图案，也叫犬牙纹。不可数，常作定语。
① He wore a black and white houndstooth jacket to the races.
= 他穿着一件黑白千鸟格夹克去看赛马。
houndstooth = 千鸟格
② The chairs were covered in a grey houndstooth fabric that hid every stain.
= 椅子套着灰色千鸟格布，什么污渍都看不出来。
houndstooth fabric = 千鸟格布料

hour
核心：小时 —— 一天的二十四分之一。首字母 h 不发音，所以前面用 an：an hour。
从「计时单位」长出：
一、钟点、整点：on the hour（每个整点）、the small hours（凌晨）；
二、一段固定的时间：office hours、opening hours、after hours（下班后）、rush hour；
三、计算报酬的单位：paid by the hour；
四、某个特定的时刻或时期：at the eleventh hour（最后一刻）、the man of the hour（风云人物）、
in somebody's hour of need（在某人最需要的时候）。
① The train takes about an hour, and there's one every thirty minutes.
= 火车大约要一个钟头，每半小时一班。
an hour = 一小时
h 不发音，所以用 an。
② Trains leave on the hour, every hour, until midnight.
= 每逢整点发一班车，一直到午夜。
on the hour = 在整点
③ The party went on until the small hours and the neighbours complained.
= 派对一直开到凌晨，邻居们都来投诉了。
the small hours = 凌晨时分
④ The shop's opening hours are nine to six, but the owner often stays after hours.
= 那家店的营业时间是九点到六点，但店主常常下班后还留着。
opening hours = 营业时间
after hours = 下班后、营业时间以外
⑤ The roads are jammed during rush hour, so we left at six.
= 高峰时间路上堵得厉害，所以我们六点就出发了。
rush hour = 交通高峰时间
⑥ Cleaners are paid by the hour and get nothing when the office is closed.
= 清洁工按小时计酬，办公室关门时就一分钱都拿不到。
by the hour = 按小时（计酬）
⑦ The deal was saved at the eleventh hour, ten minutes before the deadline.
= 那笔交易在最后一刻被挽救了下来，离截止时间只差十分钟。
at the eleventh hour = 在最后一刻
⑧ After the rescue, the young firefighter was the man of the hour.
= 救援之后，那个年轻的消防员成了风云人物。
the man of the hour = 风云人物
⑨ They argued for hours on end and never reached a decision.
= 他们一连争论了好几个小时，也没做出决定。
for hours on end = 一连几个小时
⑩ Hour after hour the rain kept falling, and the river kept rising.
= 雨一个钟头接一个钟头地下，河水不停地上涨。
hour after hour = 一个钟头接一个钟头

hourglass
核心：沙漏 —— 两个玻璃球上下相连、靠细沙流下来计时的器具。
比喻：an hourglass figure，腰细、胸与臀丰满的女性身材。
① An old hourglass stood on the desk, and the sand ran out in exactly three minutes.
= 桌上立着一个旧沙漏，沙子恰好三分钟流完。
an hourglass = 沙漏
② The dress was designed for an hourglass figure.
= 那条裙子是为沙漏形身材设计的。
an hourglass figure = 沙漏形身材（腰细、胸臀丰满）

hourly
核心：每小时的、每小时一次的；按小时计的。形容词与副词同形。
① There is an hourly bus service from the station to the airport.
= 从车站到机场有每小时一班的巴士。
an hourly service = 每小时一班
② The nurses checked his temperature hourly throughout the night.
= 护士们整夜每小时量一次他的体温。
hourly = 每小时一次地
③ The hourly rate for weekend work is half as much again.
= 周末工作的时薪要多出一半。
the hourly rate = 时薪

house
核心：底层意思是「供人居住的一栋建筑」。跟 home 的分工是 A 表教过的一组：
house 是建筑本身，home 是在里面过日子的归属感 —— 一栋空房子仍是 house，却不是谁的 home。
从「一栋房子」长出几支：
一、住宅与住在里面的人：move house、set up house、keep house，the whole house（全家人）；
一整串复合词说跟住房有关的人与事：house-hunting、a house-warming、a house guest、a house call、
house-sit、a house husband、house-proud、house-trained、house arrest……
二、有专门用途的建筑：an opera house、the house of God（教堂）；
三、议会的一院：the House of Commons、the House；
四、公司、机构：a publishing house、a fashion house —— house style 是一家出版社自己的体例，
a house organ 是机构的内部刊物；
五、剧院里的观众：a full house、bring the house down；
六、王室家族、王朝：the House of Windsor；
七、英国寄宿学校里的分院。
俚语 on the house 是「店家请客、免费」；house music 是一种电子舞曲。
动词 house 读音不同（尾音浊化），意思是给…提供住处、容纳：The museum houses…。
① They bought a three-bedroom house with a garden on the edge of town.
= 他们在城边买了一栋带花园的三居室房子。
a house = 房子、住宅
本义：一栋独立的住宅建筑；公寓说 flat 或 apartment。
② We moved house twice in the first year of our marriage.
= 结婚第一年，我们搬了两次家。
move house = 搬家（英式）
③ They set up house together in a tiny flat above a bakery.
= 他们在一家面包店楼上的小公寓里一起安了家。
set up house = 安家、成家
④ She kept house for her brother, a widower with four children.
= 她给丧偶、带着四个孩子的哥哥料理家务。
keep house = 持家、料理家务
⑤ The whole house was woken by the smoke alarm at three in the morning.
= 凌晨三点，烟雾报警器把全家人都吵醒了。
the whole house = 全家人
从「房子」转到「住在房子里的人」。
⑥ They spent six months house-hunting before they found anything they could afford.
= 他们看了六个月的房，才找到一套买得起的。
house-hunting = 找房子、看房
⑦ Every house-hunter in the area was queuing to see the flat on Saturday.
= 那一带找房子的人星期六都排着队去看那套公寓。
a house-hunter = 找房子的人
⑧ They had a house-warming party the week after they moved in.
= 他们搬进去后的第二个星期办了一场乔迁派对。
a house-warming = 乔迁派对
⑨ Their house guest stayed for three weeks and never once offered to cook.
= 他们家那位客人一住就是三个星期，一次都没提出过要做饭。
a house guest = 住在家里的客人
⑩ The old family doctor still made house calls to patients who couldn't travel.
= 那位老家庭医生仍然上门给行动不便的病人看病。
a house call = （医生的）出诊
⑪ She house-sits for her neighbours every summer and waters their tomatoes.
= 她每年夏天替邻居看房子，给他们的番茄浇水。
house-sit = 替人看房子
⑫ They found a house sitter to look after the cats while they were abroad.
= 他们出国期间找了一个人看房子、照顾猫。
a house sitter = 替人看房子的人
⑬ He gave up his job to become a house husband when the twins were born.
= 双胞胎出生后，他辞了工作当起了家庭主夫。
a house husband = 家庭主夫
⑭ His mother was so house-proud that nobody was allowed to sit on the good sofa.
= 他母亲特别讲究家里的整洁，谁都不许坐那张好沙发。
house-proud = 讲究家居整洁的（英式）
⑮ Their new puppy isn't house-trained yet, so the carpets have gone to the garage.
= 他们的新小狗还没学会在屋外便溺，所以地毯都搬到车库去了。
house-trained = （宠物）会在屋外便溺的（英式）
⑯ The cat is fully house-broken and uses the flap in the back door.
= 那只猫已经养成了规矩，会从后门的猫洞出去解手。
house-broken = （宠物）会在屋外便溺的（美式）
⑰ The opposition leader has been under house arrest for two years.
= 那位反对派领袖已经被软禁了两年。
under house arrest = 被软禁
⑱ They were invited to a house party at a country house in Scotland.
= 他们受邀去苏格兰一座乡间宅邸参加一场留宿的聚会。
a house party = （在乡间宅邸留宿几天的）家庭聚会
⑲ Police made house-to-house enquiries along the whole street.
= 警察沿着整条街挨家挨户地调查。
house-to-house = 挨家挨户的
⑳ The house dust mite is a common cause of asthma in children.
= 屋尘螨是儿童哮喘的常见诱因。
the house dust mite = 屋尘螨
㉑ A pair of house martins nest under the eaves every summer.
= 每年夏天都有一对毛脚燕在屋檐下筑巢。
a house martin = 毛脚燕
㉒ A house sparrow hopped across the table looking for crumbs.
= 一只家麻雀在桌上蹦跳着找面包屑。
a house sparrow = 家麻雀
㉓ They were married in the house of God where her parents had married.
= 他们在她父母当年结婚的那座教堂里结了婚。
the house of God = 教堂
第二支：有专门用途的建筑。前面加名词说用途：an opera house、a coffee house。
㉔ The opera house was built in 1850 and restored after the fire.
= 那座歌剧院建于一八五〇年，火灾后修复。
an opera house = 歌剧院
㉕ The whole plan was a house of cards and collapsed as soon as the bank pulled out.
= 整个计划不堪一击，银行一撤资就垮了。
a house of cards = 用纸牌搭的房子；（喻）不堪一击的计划
㉖ The bill passed the House of Commons by eleven votes.
= 那项议案以十一票之差在下议院获得通过。
the House of Commons = （英国）下议院
第三支：议会的一院；单说 the House 就指议会。
㉗ The minister apologised to the House for the misleading figures.
= 部长就那些误导性的数字向议会道歉。
the House = 议会
㉘ She works as an editor at a small publishing house in Edinburgh.
= 她在爱丁堡一家小出版社当编辑。
a publishing house = 出版社
第四支：公司、机构 —— 多用于出版、时装、金融这类行业。
㉙ The magazine follows a strict house style for dates and numbers.
= 那本杂志对日期和数字有一套严格的内部体例。
house style = （出版社的）内部体例
㉚ He edits the house organ, a monthly newsletter that nobody admits to reading.
= 他负责编那份内部刊物，一份没人承认会看的月刊。
a house organ = 机构内部刊物
㉛ He was a house officer at a London hospital for his first year after medical school.
= 医学院毕业后的第一年，他在伦敦一家医院当住院实习医生。
a house officer = 住院实习医生（英式）
㉜ The play opened to a full house, and there were queues for returns.
= 那出戏首演满座，还有人排队等退票。
a full house = 满座
第五支：剧院里的观众。
㉝ Her last song brought the house down, and the audience stood for ten minutes.
= 她的最后一首歌博得满堂喝彩，观众起立鼓掌了十分钟。
bring the house down = 博得满堂喝彩
㉞ The House of Windsor has ruled Britain since 1917.
= 温莎王朝自一九一七年起统治英国。
the House of Windsor = 温莎王朝
第六支：王室家族、王朝，首字母大写。
㉟ She was captain of her house at boarding school and won the cup for it twice.
= 她在寄宿学校时是自己分院的院长，两次为分院赢得奖杯。
somebody's house = （英国寄宿学校的）分院
㊱ The first round of drinks is on the house, to celebrate the reopening.
= 为了庆祝重新开张，第一轮酒由店家请客。
on the house = 店家请客、免费
㊲ The two of them got on like a house on fire from the first day.
= 他们俩从第一天起就一见如故。
get on like a house on fire = 一见如故、相处融洽
㊳ Before the election the party needs to put its own house in order.
= 选举之前，那个党得先把自己内部整顿好。
put your house in order = 整顿好自己的事
㊴ A job in the civil service used to be as safe as houses.
= 公务员的工作过去是铁饭碗。
safe as houses = 非常稳妥（英式）
㊵ People who live in glass houses shouldn't throw stones, and he has debts of his own.
= 自己也有短处就别去挑别人，他自己也欠着债呢。
people who live in glass houses shouldn't throw stones = 自己有短处就别揭别人的短
㊶ The club plays house music until four in the morning.
= 那家俱乐部放浩室音乐一直放到凌晨四点。
house music = 浩室音乐（电子舞曲）
㊷ The new building will house the library and two lecture theatres.
= 新楼将容纳图书馆和两个阶梯教室。
house something = 容纳、收藏某物
动词读音尾音浊化，跟名词不同。
㊸ The council has a duty to house families who have nowhere to live.
= 市议会有责任为无处栖身的家庭提供住房。
house somebody = 给某人提供住处
近义对照：
house = 房子（一栋建筑）
home = 家（在里面生活的归属感）
