parent
/ˈpeərənt/
核心：名词，可数。本义是父亲或母亲，常用复数；引申为（动植物的）亲本、亲代；parent company 是母公司：掌有别的公司的那一家。
动词：养育、当……的父母，较少用。
派生：parentage 是出身、家世，不可数；parenthood 是为人父母的身份，不可数；parenting 是养育子女（的方式），不可数；
parents-in-law 是公婆、岳父母；parent-teacher association 是家长教师联谊会，简称 PTA。并入本条。
① Both her parents were teachers at the village school.
= 她父母都是村里小学的老师。
somebody's parents = 某人的父母
② It is hard to be a single parent with a full-time job.
= 又当单亲家长又做全职工作，很不容易。
a single parent = 单亲家长
③ The seedlings were taller than either parent plant.
= 这些幼苗比两株亲本都高。
a parent plant = 亲本植株
④ The decision was taken by the parent company in Tokyo.
= 那个决定是东京的母公司作的。
a parent company = 母公司
⑤ The book asks whether adults today parent their children too anxiously.
= 那本书问的是：如今的大人带孩子是不是太焦虑了。
parent a child = 养育孩子
⑥ He was a young man of unknown parentage, brought up by an aunt.
= 他是个身世不明的年轻人，由一位姑妈带大。
of unknown parentage = 身世不明
⑦ Nothing had prepared them for the tiredness of early parenthood.
= 初为父母的那种累，他们事先毫无准备。
parenthood = 为人父母
⑧ The course teaches parenting skills to young fathers.
= 那门课教年轻的父亲怎么带孩子。
parenting skills = 育儿本领
⑨ They spent every Christmas with his parents-in-law in the north.
= 他们每个圣诞节都跟他岳父母在北方过。
parents-in-law = 公婆、岳父母
⑩ The parent-teacher association raised the money for the new library.
= 新图书馆的钱是家长教师联谊会筹的。
a parent-teacher association = 家长教师联谊会

parental leave 是育儿假：父母为照顾孩子请的假；parental control、parental controls 是家长控制：电视、电脑、手机上让家长限制孩子能看什么的功能。并入本条。

parental consent = 父母的同意

parental guidance = 父母的管教

parental leave = 育儿假

parental controls = 家长控制

parenthesis
/pəˈrenθəsɪs/
核心：两支，可数，复数 parentheses。一是插入语：插在句子里的补充说明，用括号、破折号或逗号隔开；
二是（常用复数）圆括号，美式和正式的说法，英式日常说 brackets。in parenthesis、in parentheses 是在括号里、作为附带的话。
形容词 parenthetical 是插入的、附带说的，副词 parenthetically。并入本条。
① The date of each battle is given in parentheses after its name.
= 每场战役的名称后面都用括号注了日期。
in parentheses = 在括号里
② The long parenthesis in the middle of the sentence makes it hard to follow.
= 句子中间那段长长的插入语让人读不顺。
a parenthesis = 插入语
③ I should add, in parenthesis, that the author was only nineteen at the time.
= 我得附带说一句，作者当时才十九岁。
in parenthesis = 附带说一句
④ The book is full of parenthetical remarks about the author's own childhood.
= 那本书里满是作者顺带讲自己童年的插话。
parenthetical remarks = 插话
⑤ He mentioned parenthetically that he had once met the queen.
= 他顺带提了一句，说自己见过女王一面。
parenthetically = 顺带地

a breakfast parfait = 早餐芭菲

pariah

parish
/ˈpærɪʃ/
核心：三支，可数。一是（基督教的）堂区、教区：有自己的教堂和神职人员的一片地方；二是（英格兰乡间最小的）行政区：有自己选出的 parish council（堂区议会）；
三是堂区的全体居民、全体教友，单数，动词单复数都行。常作定语：the parish church。
派生与复合词：parishioner 是堂区的教友；parish clerk 是堂区执事；parish register 是堂区登记簿：记受洗、婚礼、葬礼的册子；
parish-pump 是（英式，贬）只管本地鸡毛蒜皮的、眼界狭小的，只作定语。并入本条。
① He has been the priest of this parish for nearly thirty years.
= 他在这个堂区当了将近三十年神父。
a parish = 堂区
② They were married in the parish church where her parents had been married.
= 他们在她父母当年结婚的那座堂区教堂里结了婚。
the parish church = 堂区教堂
③ The parish council meets once a month to discuss footpaths and street lights.
= 堂区议会每月开一次会，讨论人行小路和路灯的事。
the parish council = 堂区议会
④ The whole parish turned out for the old doctor's funeral.
= 老医生出殡那天，整个堂区的人都来了。
the whole parish = 整个堂区的人
⑤ The vicar visits elderly parishioners who cannot get to church.
= 牧师上门探望那些去不了教堂的年老教友。
a parishioner = 堂区的教友
⑥ Her birth in 1794 is recorded in the parish register.
= 她生于一七九四年，这一笔记在堂区登记簿上。
the parish register = 堂区登记簿
⑦ The parish clerk kept the keys of the church and rang the bell on Sundays.
= 堂区执事管着教堂的钥匙，星期天还负责敲钟。
the parish clerk = 堂区执事
⑧ The debate never rose above parish-pump politics.
= 那场辩论始终没跳出地方上鸡毛蒜皮的政治。
parish-pump politics = 地方上鸡毛蒜皮的政治

parity
/ˈpærəti/
核心：两支，不可数。一是（正式）同等、平等：尤指工资、地位相同，介词用 with、between；
二是（金融）平价：两种货币的价值相等，也指官定的比价。
① The nurses are demanding pay parity with other hospital staff.
= 护士们要求跟医院其他员工同工同酬。
parity with somebody = 与某人同等
pay parity = 同工同酬
② The two currencies reached parity for the first time in twenty years.
= 两种货币二十年来头一次达到平价。
reach parity = 达到平价

park
/pɑːk/
核心：名词，可数。本义是围起来的一片绿地：城里的公园；（英式）乡间大宅周围带树木草地的庄园；
「名词 + park」指专作某种用途的一片场地：a business park、a science park、a wildlife park；national park 是国家公园；
美式指球场：a ball park；英式口语里 the park 是足球场。
动词：停（车）、泊车；口语里指把东西、把自己暂时搁在某处：park yourself 是一屁股坐下；也指把（想法、问题）先搁一搁。
park up 是（英式）找地方把车停好。park and ride 是停车换乘：把车停在城外的停车场，再坐公交进城。并入本条。
① We went for a walk in the park after lunch.
= 午饭后我们去公园散了步。
in the park = 在公园里
② The house stands in a park of two hundred acres with a herd of deer.
= 那座大宅坐落在一片两百英亩、养着一群鹿的庄园里。
a park = 庄园
③ The firm has moved to a business park on the edge of the city.
= 那家公司搬到了城边的一个商务园区。
a business park = 商务园区
④ Hunting is forbidden inside the national park.
= 国家公园内禁止打猎。
a national park = 国家公园
⑤ He hit the ball right out of the park in the last inning.
= 最后一局，他一棒把球打出了球场。
out of the park = 打出球场
⑥ You cannot park here, because it is the entrance to the hospital.
= 这儿不能停车，这是医院的入口。
park = 停车
⑦ She parked the car under a tree and walked the rest of the way.
= 她把车停在一棵树下，剩下的路走着去。
park a car = 停车
⑧ He parked himself in the best armchair and picked up the newspaper.
= 他一屁股坐进最好的那把扶手椅，拿起了报纸。
park yourself = 一屁股坐下
⑨ Just park your bags in the hall until your room is ready.
= 房间收拾好之前，先把包搁在门厅吧。
park something somewhere = 把某物暂时搁在某处
⑩ Let us park that question for now and come back to it next week.
= 那个问题咱们先搁一搁，下星期再谈。
park a question = 把问题先搁一搁
⑪ We parked up behind the church and went to look for a café.
= 我们把车停在教堂后面，去找咖啡馆。
park up = 找地方把车停好
⑫ The city runs a park and ride from a car park beside the motorway.
= 那座城市在高速公路旁的一个停车场开了停车换乘线。
park and ride = 停车换乘

parking

a parking lot = 停车场

Parkinson's disease
/ˈpɑːkɪnsnz dɪziːz/

① Her father was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease in his early sixties.
= 她父亲六十出头时被诊断出帕金森病。

② He has had Parkinson's for ten years and can no longer hold a pen steady.
= 他得帕金森病十年了，笔已经拿不稳。
have Parkinson's = 得帕金森病

Parkinson's law
/ˈpɑːkɪnsnz lɔː/

① By Parkinson's law, a report that could be written in a day took the committee a month.
= 照帕金森定律，一天就能写完的报告，委员会花了一个月。
Parkinson's law = 帕金森定律
② The manager quoted Parkinson's law whenever anyone asked for a later deadline.
= 只要有人要求把期限推后，经理就搬出帕金森定律。
quote Parkinson's law = 搬出帕金森定律

parliament
/ˈpɑːləmənt/
核心：三支。一是议会、国会：一国里选出来制定法律的机构，可数；

三是一届议会：两次大选之间的那段会期，可数。单数作主语，英式里动词单复数都行。
① The German parliament voted by a large majority to approve the treaty.

a parliament = 议会
② She entered Parliament in 1997 as the member for a northern seat.
= 她一九九七年作为北方一个选区的议员进了议会。
enter Parliament = 进议会
③ He stood for Parliament three times before he was elected.
= 他竞选了三次议员才当选。

④ The bill is unlikely to become law before the end of this parliament.

parliamentarian
/ˌpɑːləmənˈteəriən/
核心：两支，可数。一是议员：尤指资深、精通议会事务的议员；二是 Parliamentarian：（英国十七世纪内战中）议会派的成员，跟保王派相对。
① A group of European parliamentarians visited the refugee camp last week.
= 一批欧洲的议员上星期访问了那座难民营。
a parliamentarian = 议员
② She is respected on both sides of the House as a skilled parliamentarian.
= 她精通议会事务，两边的议员都敬重她。
a skilled parliamentarian = 精通议会事务的议员
③ The town was held by the Parliamentarians for most of the Civil War.
= 内战的大部分时间里，那座城在议会派手里。
the Parliamentarians = 议会派

parliamentary
/ˌpɑːləˈmentri/
核心：议会的、国会的：parliament 的形容词，多作定语。
parliamentary privilege 是议会特权：议员在议会里发言不受诽谤法追究的权利，不可数。
英国政府里的几个职位：parliamentary secretary、parliamentary undersecretary 是政务次官：协助大臣的低一级的官员；
parliamentary private secretary 是议会私人秘书：给大臣当助手的议员，简称 PPS。并入本条。
① The country held its first parliamentary elections for fifteen years.
= 那个国家举行了十五年来的头一次议会选举。
parliamentary elections = 议会选举
② The government has a parliamentary majority of only four.
= 政府在议会里只有四席的多数。
a parliamentary majority = 议会多数
③ He made the accusation under parliamentary privilege and so could not be sued.
= 他是在议会特权的保护下提出那项指控的，所以没法告他。
parliamentary privilege = 议会特权
④ She began her career in government as a parliamentary secretary at the transport ministry.
= 她的从政生涯是从交通部的政务次官做起的。
a parliamentary secretary = 政务次官
a parliamentary undersecretary = 政务次官
⑤ He resigned as parliamentary private secretary to the health minister in protest at the cuts.
= 他辞去卫生大臣议会私人秘书一职，以抗议削减开支。
a parliamentary private secretary = 议会私人秘书

parlour
/ˈpɑːlə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是（旧）客厅、起居室：旧时住宅里招待客人的那间屋；二是「名词 + parlour」：某一类店铺：an ice-cream parlour、a beauty parlour、a funeral parlour。
美式拼作 parlor。
parlour game 是（旧）室内游戏：在家里玩的猜词、问答一类；parlourmaid 是旧时在饭桌旁伺候的女仆。并入本条。
① The visitors were shown into the front parlour and given tea.
= 客人被领进前面的客厅，有人端上了茶。
the front parlour = 前客厅
② There is a new ice-cream parlour on the sea front.
= 海滨那条路上新开了一家冰淇淋店。
an ice-cream parlour = 冰淇淋店
a beauty parlour = 美容院
③ After dinner the family played parlour games until midnight.
= 晚饭后，一家人玩室内游戏一直玩到半夜。
a parlour game = 室内游戏
④ Her great-grandmother went into service as a parlourmaid at the age of fourteen.
= 她的曾祖母十四岁就出去给人家当了伺候饭桌的女仆。
a parlourmaid = 伺候饭桌的女仆

parlous
/ˈpɑːləs/
核心：（正式）岌岌可危的、极糟的：多说状况、财务。常用在 in a parlous state、a parlous condition 里。
① The club's finances are in a parlous state after three years of losses.
= 连亏三年之后，俱乐部的财务状况岌岌可危。
in a parlous state = 岌岌可危
② The report describes the parlous condition of the bridges in the county.
= 那份报告描述了郡里那些桥梁的危险状况。
a parlous condition = 危险的状况
