/ˈreɪmənt/
核心：（古语，文学）衣服、服装。不可数。
① The king appeared in raiment of gold and purple.
= 国王身着金紫两色的衣装出现了。
raiment = 衣服
② The angel in the painting is dressed in white raiment.
= 那幅画里的天使穿着白衣。
white raiment = 白衣
只在古旧、诗意的文字里用。

rain
/reɪn/
核心：名词：雨、雨水，不可数：heavy rain；the rains 是（热带地区的）雨季，复数；（雨一样的）一阵、一大堆：a rain of。
动词：下雨：it rains、it is raining；（使）大量落下、像雨点般落下：rain down on、rain blows on somebody。
短语动词 be rained off、rained out 是（比赛等）因雨取消。
习语：come rain or shine 是不论晴雨、无论如何；rain cats and dogs 是下倾盆大雨；take a rain check 是（口语）改天再说、另约时间；
right as rain 是（口语）身体完全好了；it never rains but it pours 是（英式）祸不单行。
① The rain stopped just as we reached the beach.
= 我们刚到海滩雨就停了。
the rain = 雨
heavy rain = 大雨
② We got caught in the rain on the way home.
= 我们在回家的路上淋了雨。
get caught in the rain = 淋雨
in the rain = 在雨里
③ The farmers are waiting for the rains to come.
= 农民们在等雨季来临。
the rains = 雨季
④ A rain of stones fell on the soldiers from the roofs.
= 石头像雨点般从屋顶上砸向士兵。
a rain of something = 一阵某物
⑤ It rained all day, so we stayed indoors.
= 下了一整天雨，我们就待在屋里。
it rains = 下雨
rain = 下雨
⑥ Ash from the volcano rained down on the town.
= 火山灰像下雨一样落在镇上。
rain down on something = 像雨点般落在某处
⑦ The match was rained off after twenty minutes.
= 比赛进行了二十分钟就因雨取消了。
be rained off = 因雨取消
be rained out = （美式）因雨取消
⑧ He walks the dog every morning, come rain or shine.
= 不论晴雨，他每天早上都遛狗。
come rain or shine = 不论晴雨
⑨ It was raining cats and dogs when we left the theatre.
= 我们离开剧院时正下着倾盆大雨。
rain cats and dogs = 下倾盆大雨
⑩ I am too busy for lunch today, so can I take a rain check?
= 我今天太忙，没空吃午饭，改天行吗？
take a rain check = 改天再说
⑪ After a few days in bed he was as right as rain.
= 卧床几天之后，他就完全好了。
right as rain = 身体完全好了
⑫ First the car broke down, then I lost my keys; it never rains but it pours.
= 先是车坏了，后来钥匙又丢了，真是祸不单行。
it never rains but it pours = 祸不单行

raise
/reɪz/
核心：动词，及物。一是举起、抬起、使升高：raise your hand；二是提高、增加（价格、数量、水平）；三是筹集（钱）、征集（军队）；
四是（尤其美式）养育（孩子）、饲养（动物）、种植（作物）；五是提出（问题、反对意见）；六是引起、激起（感情、怀疑）；七是（数学）使自乘：raise to the power of。
名词，可数：（美式）加薪，英式说 rise。不及物的「自己上升」用 rise。
习语：raise your voice 是提高嗓门、发火；raise the alarm 是发出警报；raise eyebrows 是让人吃惊、引人侧目；raise a glass 是举杯祝酒。
① Raise your hand if you know the answer.
= 知道答案的举手。
raise your hand = 举手
raise = 举起
② Two men raised the heavy box onto the back of the lorry.
= 两个人把那只沉重的箱子抬上了卡车后面。
raise something onto something = 把某物抬到某处
raise = 举起某物（及物）
③ The price of the cake rose, but they did not raise the price of bread.
= 蛋糕涨价了，他们却没提面包的价。
raise the price = 提价
rise = 自己上升（不及物）
④ The government plans to raise taxes next year.
= 政府打算明年加税。
raise taxes = 加税
raise standards = 提高水准
⑤ The school is trying to raise money for a new library.
= 那所学校正设法为新图书馆筹款。
raise money = 筹款
raise funds = 筹集资金
⑥ She raised four children on her own after her husband died.
= 丈夫死后，她独自养大了四个孩子。
raise children = 养孩子
raise = 养育
⑦ They raise sheep and grow wheat on the farm.
= 他们在农场里养羊种小麦。
raise sheep = 养羊
raise crops = 种庄稼
⑧ Several parents raised the question of school meals at the meeting.
= 好几位家长在会上提出了学校午餐的问题。
raise a question = 提出问题
raise an objection = 提出反对意见
⑨ The sudden noise raised fears of another earthquake.
= 那突如其来的响声让人担心又要地震了。
raise fears = 引起担心
raise doubts = 引起怀疑
⑩ Two raised to the power of three is eight.
= 二的三次方是八。
raised to the power of something = 某数的几次方
raise to = 使自乘到（某次方）
⑪ She asked her boss for a raise after two years in the job.
= 干了两年之后，她向老板要求加薪。
a raise = （美式）加薪
⑫ Do not raise your voice at me in front of the children.
= 别当着孩子的面冲我嚷嚷。
raise your voice = 提高嗓门
⑬ A neighbour saw the smoke and raised the alarm.
= 一位邻居看见冒烟，发出了警报。
raise the alarm = 发出警报
⑭ His decision to leave the band raised a few eyebrows.
= 他决定离开乐队，让好些人吃了一惊。
raise eyebrows = 让人吃惊
⑮ Let us raise a glass to the bride and groom.
= 让我们为新娘新郎举杯。
raise a glass = 举杯祝酒
