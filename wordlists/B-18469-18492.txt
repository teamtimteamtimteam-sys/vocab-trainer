ready
/ˈredi/
核心：形容词，不放在名词前：准备好的、预备好的：be ready for、be ready to do；（东西）准备好的、可以用了的：dinner is ready；乐意的、愿意的：ready to help；
就要、快要：ready to cry、ready to drop。放在名词前：迅速的、现成的、随时可用的：a ready answer、ready cash。
动词：（正式）使作好准备：ready yourself for。名词 the ready 是（英式口语）现金。
习语：ready and waiting 是准备就绪、只等着；get ready 是作准备。
复合词：ready-made 是现成的；ready reckoner 是（英式）计算便查表；ready-to-wear 是（衣服）成衣的、现成的。
① Are you ready to go, or do you need more time?
= 你准备好走了吗，还是要再等一会儿？
be ready to do = 准备好做某事
ready = 准备就绪的
② The team is ready for the final on Saturday.
= 球队已为星期六的决赛做好了准备。
be ready for something = 为某事做好准备
③ Hurry up and get ready, for the taxi will be here in five minutes.
= 快点做准备，出租车五分钟后就到。
get ready = 做准备
get ready for something = 为某事作准备
④ Dinner is ready, so come and sit down.
= 饭好了，过来坐吧。
dinner is ready = 饭好了
⑤ She is always ready to help her neighbours.
= 她总是乐意帮邻居的忙。
be ready to help = 乐意帮忙
⑥ I was so tired that I was ready to drop.
= 我累得快要倒下了。
be ready to drop = 累得快倒下
⑦ He always has a ready answer for every question.
= 每个问题他都有现成的答案。
a ready answer = 现成的答案
ready cash = 现金
⑧ The soldiers readied themselves for the attack.
= 士兵们作好了进攻准备。
ready yourself for something = 为某事作好准备
⑨ The car was ready and waiting outside the door.
= 车已经在门口等着了。
ready and waiting = 准备就绪、只等着
⑩ They bought ready-made curtains instead of having them made.
= 他们买了现成的窗帘，没去订做。
ready-made = 现成的
⑪ The shop sells ready-to-wear clothes by famous designers.
= 那家店卖名设计师的成衣。
ready-to-wear = 成衣的
⑫ The old shopkeeper used a ready reckoner to work out the prices.
= 那个老店主用计算便查表算价钱。
a ready reckoner = 计算便查表
