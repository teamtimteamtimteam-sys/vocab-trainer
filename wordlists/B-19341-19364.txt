rivalry
/ˈraɪvlri/
核心：竞争、较量、敌对：rivalry between。可数或不可数。
① There has always been a friendly rivalry between the two football clubs in the city.
= 城里那两家足球俱乐部一直有友好的较量。
rivalry between = 之间的竞争
rivalry = 竞争
② The two sisters grew up in a spirit of sibling rivalry, always trying to beat each other.
= 两姐妹在手足相争中长大，总想压对方一头。
sibling rivalry = 手足相争

riven
/ˈrɪvn/
核心：（正式）四分五裂的、被分裂的：riven by / with。
① The party was riven by arguments about the war and lost the election.
= 那个政党因战争问题争吵不休，四分五裂，输掉了选举。
be riven by something = 被某事搞得四分五裂
riven = 四分五裂的
② It is a small town riven with old hatreds between the families who live there.
= 那是个小镇，镇上几家人之间积怨已久，早已分裂。
be riven with something = 充满某种分裂

river
/ˈrɪvə(r)/
核心：河、江：the River Thames；（液体的）巨流、大量流淌：a river of。可数。
习语：sell somebody down the river 是出卖某人；up the river 是（美式俚语）坐牢。
① There is a narrow river at the bottom of the garden where the children catch fish.
= 花园尽头有条小河，孩子们在那儿捉鱼。
river = 河
② We had to cross the river by boat, because the bridge had been washed away.
= 桥被冲走了，我们只好坐船过河。
cross the river = 过河
③ Rivers of hot lava poured down the side of the volcano.
= 滚烫的熔岩如河流般从火山侧面倾泻而下。
a river of something = 某物的巨流
④ He trusted his business partner, but she sold him down the river to save herself.
= 他信任自己的生意伙伴，可她为了自保出卖了他。
sell somebody down the river = 出卖某人
⑤ If you keep stealing cars, you will end up up the river for ten years.
= 你再偷车，就得去坐十年牢。
up the river = 坐牢
⑥ River blindness is spread by small flies that breed in fast-flowing rivers in Africa.
= 河盲症由在非洲湍急河流里繁殖的小飞虫传播。
river blindness = 河盲症

riverbank

① We spread a blanket on the riverbank and had our picnic in the shade of the trees.
= 我们在河岸上铺了条毯子，在树荫下野餐。
the riverbank = 河岸
② The old man sat on the riverbank every morning, fishing and watching the boats go by.
= 那位老人每天早上坐在河岸上钓鱼，看船来船往。
sit on the riverbank = 坐在河岸上

riverfront
/ˈrɪvəfrʌnt/

① The city has built new restaurants and a walkway along the riverfront.

the riverfront = 河滨
② They bought a riverfront flat with a balcony that looks over the water.
= 他们买了一套临河公寓，阳台正对着河面。
a riverfront flat = 临河公寓

riverine
/ˈrɪvəraɪn/
核心：（术语）河的、河边的、生活在河里的。
① Many riverine fish cannot survive when the water becomes polluted.
= 许多河鱼在河水被污染后就活不下去了。
riverine fish = 河鱼
riverine = 河里的
② The scientists studied the riverine forests that grow along the banks of the Amazon.
= 科学家们研究了亚马孙河两岸的沿河森林。
riverine forests = 沿河森林

riverside
/ˈrɪvəsaɪd/
核心：河边、河畔，单数：the riverside。也作形容词：河边的：a riverside pub。
① We walked along the riverside as the sun went down over the water.
= 太阳在水面上落下时，我们沿着河边散步。
the riverside = 河边
② They had lunch at a riverside pub and watched the rowing boats go past.
= 他们在一家河边酒馆吃午饭，看划艇驶过。
a riverside pub = 河边酒馆

rivet
/ˈrɪvɪt/
核心：名词，可数：铆钉。动词：用铆钉固定、铆接；吸引（注意）、使（目光）集中：be riveted to / on；使呆住不动：be riveted to the spot。
① The metal sheets of the old ship were held together with thousands of rivets.
= 那艘旧船的钢板是用成千上万颗铆钉连在一起的。
a rivet = 铆钉
② The workers riveted the steel plates together to build the frame of the bridge.
= 工人们把钢板铆接起来，搭成桥的框架。
rivet something together = 把某物铆接在一起
③ Everyone's eyes were riveted on the screen as the final penalty was taken.
= 罚最后一个点球时，大家的眼睛都紧紧盯着屏幕。
be riveted on something = 目光紧盯着某物
④ She was riveted to the spot with fear when she saw the snake.
= 看到那条蛇，她吓得呆在原地动不了。
be riveted to the spot = 呆住不动

riveting
/ˈrɪvɪtɪŋ/
核心：吸引人的、引人入胜的、扣人心弦的。
① The documentary about life in the deep ocean was absolutely riveting from start to finish.
= 那部讲深海生物的纪录片从头到尾都扣人心弦。
absolutely riveting = 扣人心弦
riveting = 引人入胜的
② She gave a riveting speech about her journey across the desert on foot.
= 她讲述自己徒步穿越沙漠的经历，演讲引人入胜。
a riveting speech = 引人入胜的演讲

riviera
/ˌrɪviˈeərə/
核心：（海滨的）度假胜地、海滨避暑区；the Riviera 是（法国、意大利的）里维埃拉海岸。可数。
① Every summer the rich and famous sail their yachts along the French Riviera.
= 每年夏天，富人和名流都驾着游艇沿法国里维埃拉海岸航行。

② The local tourist board likes to call this stretch of coast the English riviera.
= 当地旅游局喜欢把这段海岸叫作“英格兰的海滨胜地”。
a riviera = 海滨度假胜地

rivulet

① Rivulets of rain ran down the window as we waited for the storm to pass.

rivulets of rain = 一道道雨水

a small rivulet = 小溪

riyal

ro-ro
/ˈrəʊ rəʊ/

= 我们在多佛直接把车开上滚装渡轮，九十分钟后又在加来开了下来。
a ro-ro ferry = 滚装渡轮
ro-ro = 滚装的
② The port has built a new ro-ro terminal so that lorries can load and unload much faster.
= 港口新建了一个滚装码头，卡车装卸快多了。
a ro-ro terminal = 滚装码头

roach
/rəʊtʃ/
核心：一是（美式口语）蟑螂，是 cockroach 的简称；二是（俚语）大麻烟屁股；三是拟鲤：欧洲一种淡水鱼。复数一般作 roaches，鱼也可作 roach。
① The old flat was full of roaches, so we called a man to come and get rid of them.
= 那套旧公寓里到处是蟑螂，我们只好请人来灭。
a roach = 蟑螂
② The police found a roach in the ashtray and asked who had been smoking cannabis.
= 警察在烟灰缸里发现一个大麻烟屁股，问是谁抽了大麻。
a roach = 大麻烟屁股
③ The boys spent the whole Saturday catching roach and perch in the canal behind the school.
= 男孩们整个星期六都在学校后面的运河里钓拟鲤和鲈鱼。
roach = 拟鲤

road
/rəʊd/
核心：路、公路、马路：by road、along the road、cross the road；（城市里的）街道，用于街名，缩写为 Rd：Station Road；（达到某目标的）道路、途径：the road to success。可数或用于固定搭配。
习语：on the road 是在路上、在旅途中，也指（乐队等）在巡回演出；hit the road 是（口语）上路、出发；down the road 是将来、以后；the end of the road 是尽头、末路；one for the road 是（口语）临走前再喝一杯；off the road 是（车）不能上路的。
① The road to the village is very narrow, so drive slowly on the bends.
= 去村子的路很窄，转弯时开慢点。
road = 路
② Look both ways before you cross the road, even when the lights are green.
= 过马路前要左右看，就算是绿灯也一样。
cross the road = 过马路
③ It is cheaper to send heavy goods by road than by air.
= 重货走公路运输比空运便宜。
by road = 走公路
④ My grandmother lives at 42 Station Road, just past the post office.
= 我奶奶住在车站路 42 号，就在邮局过去一点。
Station Road = 车站路
⑤ Hard work is the only road to success in this business.
= 在这一行，勤奋是成功的唯一途径。
the road to something = 通往某事的道路
⑥ We have been on the road since six o'clock this morning and the children are tired.
= 我们从早上六点就上路了，孩子们都累了。
on the road = 在路上、旅途中
⑦ The band will be on the road for most of the summer, playing in twenty cities.
= 乐队这个夏天大部分时间都在巡回演出，要去二十个城市。
on the road = 巡回演出
⑧ It is getting late, so I think we should hit the road before the traffic gets bad.
= 天不早了，我看我们该在堵车前上路了。
hit the road = 上路、出发
⑨ We do not need a bigger house now, but we might need one further down the road.
= 我们现在不需要大房子，但以后可能需要。
down the road = 将来、以后
⑩ After three years of losses, the company has reached the end of the road.
= 连亏三年，那家公司已经走到了尽头。
the end of the road = 尽头、末路
⑪ "Just one for the road," he said, and ordered another glass of beer.
= 他说“走之前再来一杯”，又点了一杯啤酒。
one for the road = 临走前再喝一杯
⑫ My old car has been off the road since it failed its safety test.
= 我那辆旧车自从安检没过就一直不能上路。
off the road = 不能上路
