recapitulate
/ˌriːkəˈpɪtʃuleɪt/
核心：（正式）概括、扼要重述（要点）。及物也可不及物。名词 recapitulation，可数也可不可数。口语简称 recap。并入本条。
① At the end of the lecture the professor recapitulated the main points.
= 讲座结束时，教授概括了一下要点。
recapitulate the main points = 概括要点
recapitulate = 扼要重述
② The final chapter is a recapitulation of the arguments in the book.
= 最后一章是对全书论点的总结。
a recapitulation of something = 对某事的总结
