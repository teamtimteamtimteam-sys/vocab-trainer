reduce
/rɪˈdjuːs/
核心：动词，及物。一是减少、降低、缩小（数量、价格、大小）：reduce something by、from、to；二是 reduce somebody、something to 是使沦为、使陷入（更坏的状态）：reduce somebody to tears；
三是（烹饪）（通过煮沸）浓缩（汁、汤）；四是（化学）还原。习语 reduce to doing 是被迫做（某种丢脸的事）。
① The company has reduced the price of its cars by ten per cent.
= 那家公司把汽车降价百分之十。
reduce the price = 降价
reduce = 减少
② Smoking less will reduce the risk of heart disease.
= 少抽烟会降低患心脏病的风险。
reduce the risk = 降低风险
③ The number of staff was reduced by a third.
= 员工人数减少了三分之一。
reduce by = 减少了多少
④ The shop is trying to increase sales and reduce costs at the same time.
= 那家店想同时增加销量、降低成本。
increase = 增加
⑤ The old town was reduced to rubble by the bombs.
= 那座老城被炸成了一片废墟。
be reduced to rubble = 被夷为废墟
reduce something to something = 使某物沦为某物
⑥ The cruel remark reduced her to tears.
= 那句刻薄话把她弄哭了。
reduce somebody to tears = 把某人弄哭
⑦ After losing his job, he was reduced to begging in the street.
= 丢了工作之后，他沦落到在街上乞讨。
be reduced to doing something = 被迫做某事
reduce to doing = 沦落到做某事
⑧ Boil the sauce for ten minutes to reduce it.
= 把酱汁煮十分钟，让它变浓。
reduce a sauce = 把酱汁收浓

/rɪˈdʌkʃənɪzəm/

① Critics accused the scientist of reductionism for explaining love as a matter of chemistry.
= 那位科学家把爱情解释成化学问题，批评者指责他搞还原论。

② The book takes a reductionist view of human behaviour.
= 那本书对人类行为持还原论观点。
a reductionist view = 还原论观点
reductionist = 还原论的
