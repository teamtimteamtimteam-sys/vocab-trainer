sermon
/ˈsɜːmən/
核心：名词，可数：（教堂里的）布道、讲道：preach a sermon；（贬义）说教、训诫：give somebody a sermon on something。
① The priest preached a long sermon about forgiveness, and some of the old men fell asleep.
= 神父做了一场关于宽恕的长篇布道，有几个老人睡着了。
sermon = 布道
preach a sermon = 布道
词族：
preach = 布道
② When I came home late, my father gave me a sermon on the dangers of walking alone at night.
= 我回家晚了，父亲给我上了一课，讲夜里独自走路有多危险。
give somebody a sermon on something = 就某事对某人说教

sermonize
/ˈsɜːmənaɪz/
核心：动词，不及物（贬义）：说教、训诫、讲大道理。英式也拼作 sermonise。
① My uncle likes to sermonize about hard work, but he has never had a job for more than a year.
= 我叔叔爱讲勤劳的大道理，可他从没在一份工作上干满一年。
sermonize = 说教
sermonize about something = 就某事说教
② The film tells a strong story without sermonizing, so the audience can make up their own minds.
= 那部电影讲了一个有力的故事，却不说教，让观众自己判断。
without sermonizing = 不说教

sesame
/ˈsesəmi/
核心：名词，不可数：芝麻；芝麻籽：sesame seeds、sesame oil。习语：open sesame 是「芝麻开门」，比喻轻易得到某物的秘诀。
① The baker puts sesame seeds on top of the bread rolls before they go into the oven.
= 面包师在面包卷进烤箱前往上面撒芝麻。
sesame seeds = 芝麻
sesame = 芝麻
② A few drops of sesame oil make the noodles taste much better.
= 滴几滴芝麻油，面条就好吃多了。
sesame oil = 芝麻油
③ For many young people, a degree from that university is an open sesame to a good job in the city.
= 对许多年轻人来说，那所大学的学位是在城里找到好工作的敲门砖。
an open sesame = 敲门砖、成功秘诀
