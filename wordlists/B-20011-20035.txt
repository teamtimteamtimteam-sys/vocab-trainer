scenery
/ˈsiːnəri/
核心：名词，不可数：风景、景色（自然风光的总称）；（舞台的）布景。不说 a scenery、sceneries。
① The scenery in the Scottish Highlands is wild and beautiful, with mountains, lakes and empty valleys.
= 苏格兰高地的风景荒凉而美丽，有群山、湖泊和空旷的山谷。
scenery = 风景
the scenery in a place = 某地的风景
② The students painted all the scenery for the school play themselves during the Easter holidays.
= 学生们在复活节假期里自己画了校戏的全部布景。
the scenery for a play = 戏剧布景
③ After ten years in the same job, she felt she needed a change of scenery.
= 同一份工作干了十年，她觉得需要换个环境。
a change of scenery = 换个环境

scenester
/ˈsiːnstə(r)/
核心：名词，可数（口语，常含贬义）：（音乐、时尚等）潮流圈里的人、赶时髦的人。
① The new bar is full of scenesters in expensive vintage clothes and big glasses.
= 那家新酒吧里挤满了穿着昂贵复古服装、戴大框眼镜的潮人。
scenesters = 潮流圈里的人
② He used to be a scenester who went to every new band's first concert.
= 他以前是个赶时髦的人，每支新乐队的首场演出都去。
a scenester = 赶时髦的人

scenic
/ˈsiːnɪk/
核心：形容词：风景优美的：a scenic route、scenic views；（舞台）布景的。副词 scenically 是在风景方面。
① We took the scenic route along the coast, although it was much slower than the motorway.
= 我们走了沿海岸的风景线，虽然比高速公路慢得多。
the scenic route = 风景优美的路线
scenic = 风景优美的
② The small family hotel is scenically placed on a hill above the lake and the forest.
= 那家家庭小旅馆坐落在湖泊和森林上方的小山上，风景优美。
scenically = 在风景方面

scent
/sent/
核心：名词：（愉悦的）香味、气味（可数或不可数）：the scent of roses；（动物留下的）气味、臭迹（可数）：follow the scent、lose the scent；（英式）香水（可数或不可数）。动词：（动物）嗅出；使充满香气；（比喻）察觉：scent danger。
① The sweet scent of roses filled my grandmother's garden on warm summer evenings.
= 温暖的夏夜，玫瑰的甜香弥漫了我祖母的整个花园。
the scent of something = 某物的香味
scent = 香味
② The dogs followed the scent of the fox for miles across the fields.
= 猎狗循着狐狸的气味穿过田野追了好几英里。
follow the scent = 循着气味追踪
③ She wore a light flowery scent that her husband had bought her in Paris.
= 她搽着丈夫在巴黎给她买的一款淡淡的花香香水。
a scent = 香水
④ The team scented victory when they scored their third goal in the second half.
= 下半场打进第三球时，球队嗅到了胜利的气息。
scent victory = 嗅到胜利的气息
⑤ Wolves leave scent marks on trees and rocks to show other wolves where their land begins.
= 狼在树和岩石上留下气味标记，向别的狼表明自己的地盘从哪里开始。
a scent mark = 气味标记

scented
/ˈsentɪd/
核心：形容词：有香味的、加了香料的：scented candles、scented soap。
① She lit a few scented candles in the bathroom and lay in a hot bath.
= 她在浴室里点了几根香薰蜡烛，躺进热水澡里。
scented candles = 香薰蜡烛
scented = 有香味的
② On that warm night, the air in the little garden was heavy and scented with jasmine.
= 那个温暖的夜晚，小花园里的空气浓浓的，满是茉莉花的香味。
scented with something = 散发着某物的香味

scentless

① These modern roses are beautiful to look at, but sadly they are almost scentless.
= 这些现代玫瑰看上去很美，可惜几乎没有香味。

colourless and scentless = 无色无味

sceptic
/ˈskeptɪk/
核心：名词，可数：怀疑者、怀疑论者：不轻信的人。美式拼作 skeptic。形容词 sceptical、名词 scepticism 另立条。
① Sceptics say the new train line will cost far more than the government has promised.
= 怀疑者说，那条新铁路的花费会远远超过政府承诺的数字。
sceptics = 怀疑者
② I was a sceptic about online dating until I met my wife that way.
= 我本来对网上交友持怀疑态度，直到我在网上认识了我妻子。
a sceptic = 怀疑者

sceptical
/ˈskeptɪkl/
核心：形容词：怀疑的、不相信的：be sceptical about / of something。美式拼作 skeptical。副词 sceptically 是怀疑地（美式拼作 skeptically）。名词 scepticism 是怀疑态度（另立条）。
① Many doctors are sceptical about the new diet and say there is no proof that it works.
= 许多医生对那种新饮食法持怀疑态度，说没有证据证明它有效。
be sceptical about = 对…存疑
sceptical = 怀疑的
词族：
scepticism = 怀疑态度
② She looked at me sceptically when I said that I had cleaned my room.
= 我说我打扫了房间，她怀疑地看着我。
sceptically = 怀疑地

scepticism
/ˈskeptɪsɪzəm/
核心：名词，不可数：怀疑态度、不轻信；（哲学）怀疑论。美式拼作 skepticism。
① The plan was met with scepticism by the local people, who had heard similar promises before.
= 当地人对那项计划持怀疑态度，因为他们以前听过类似的承诺。
be met with scepticism = 遭到质疑
scepticism = 怀疑态度
② A healthy scepticism is useful when you read stories on the internet.
= 在网上看新闻时，保持适度的怀疑很有用。
a healthy scepticism = 适度的怀疑

sceptre
/ˈseptə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（帝王的）权杖：象征王权的装饰杖。美式拼作 scepter。
① The queen held a golden sceptre in one hand and a jewelled orb in the other.
= 女王一手握着金权杖，一手托着镶宝石的宝球。
a sceptre = 权杖
② The museum shows the old American scepter that was carried at the governor's ceremonies.
= 博物馆展出了那根在总督仪式上使用的老式美国权杖。
scepter = 权杖（sceptre 的美式拼法）

schadenfreude
/ˈʃɑːdnfrɔɪdə/
核心：名词，不可数：幸灾乐祸：看到别人倒霉时暗自高兴的心情。来自德语，英语里也常大写。
① I felt a little schadenfreude when the man who had pushed in front of us missed his train.
= 那个插队到我们前面的人没赶上火车时，我有点幸灾乐祸。
schadenfreude = 幸灾乐祸
② When the rich banker lost his fortune, the newspapers reported his problems with obvious schadenfreude.
= 那位富有的银行家破产时，报纸带着明显的幸灾乐祸报道了他的麻烦。
with obvious schadenfreude = 带着明显的幸灾乐祸

schedule
/ˈʃedjuːl/
核心：名词：日程安排、计划表（可数或不可数）：a busy schedule、on / behind / ahead of schedule；（北美）时刻表：a train schedule；（正式文件的）附表。动词：安排、预定（时间）：be scheduled for / to do something。形容词 scheduled 是预定的：scheduled flight 是定期航班（与包机相对）。北美读 /ˈskedʒuːl/。
① The new manager has a very busy schedule, so you will have to wait until next week to see her.
= 新经理日程排得很满，你得等到下周才能见她。
schedule = 日程
a busy schedule = 繁忙的日程
② The building work is on schedule and should be finished by the end of May.
= 施工按计划进行，五月底前应该能完工。
on schedule = 按计划
③ The new bridge is three months behind schedule because of the bad weather.
= 由于天气恶劣，新桥工期比计划晚了三个月。
behind schedule = 落后于计划
④ Check the train schedule before you leave, because there are fewer trains on Sundays.
= 出发前查一下火车时刻表，星期天车次少一些。
a train schedule = （北美）火车时刻表
⑤ The meeting with the new owners has been scheduled for Thursday afternoon at three o'clock.
= 和新业主的会议定在星期四下午三点。
be scheduled for = 定于……
⑥ We took a scheduled flight to Rome because the charter flights were all full.
= 包机都满了，我们只好坐定期航班去罗马。
a scheduled flight = 定期航班
