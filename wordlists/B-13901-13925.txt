mail
/meɪl/
核心：邮政、邮递系统；邮件、信件（美式常用，英式多说 post）；电子邮件。动词：邮寄；发电子邮件；mail out（成批寄发）。
搭配：by mail（用邮寄方式）、mail order（邮购）、mail merge（邮件合并）、mailing list（邮寄名单；邮件列表）、
（美）mail carrier / mailman（邮递员）、mail slot（投信口）、mail drop（收信处）、mail bomb（邮件炸弹）。
名词 mailbox（美：信箱、邮筒；电子邮箱）、mailbag（邮袋；来信）、mailroom（收发室）、mailshot（英：广告邮件）、
mailer（美：邮寄用的信封盒子；广告邮件）、mailing（寄发的一批邮件）并入本条。
① The parcel arrived in the mail this morning.
= 那个包裹今天早上寄到了。
in the mail = 在邮件里、寄到
英式说 in the post。
② Is there any mail for me today?
= 今天有我的信吗？
any mail = 有信
③ We will send you the tickets by mail.
= 我们会把票邮寄给你。
by mail = 用邮寄方式
④ I check my mail as soon as I get to the office.
= 我一到办公室就查邮件。
check your mail = 查邮件
今天多指电子邮件。
⑤ She mailed the letter on her way to work.
= 她上班路上把信寄了。
mail a letter = （美）寄信
⑥ The charity mailed out ten thousand letters asking for money.
= 那家慈善机构寄出了一万封募捐信。
mail out = 成批寄发
⑦ She buys most of her clothes by mail order.
= 她的衣服大多是邮购的。
mail order = 邮购
网购之前的远程购物方式。
⑧ Use mail merge to put each customer's name at the top of the letter.
= 用邮件合并把每位顾客的名字放在信的开头。
mail merge = 邮件合并
⑨ Please take my name off your mailing list.
= 请把我的名字从你们的邮寄名单上去掉。
a mailing list = 邮寄名单；邮件列表
⑩ The mail carrier leaves parcels with the neighbours when we are out.
= 我们不在家时，邮递员把包裹留给邻居。
a mail carrier = （美）邮递员
⑪ The dog barks at the mailman every single morning.
= 那条狗每天早上都冲邮递员叫。
a mailman = （美）邮递员
⑫ There was nothing in the mailbox except bills.
= 信箱里除了账单什么也没有。
a mailbox = （美）信箱
英式说 letter box 或 postbox。
⑬ My mailbox is full, so new messages are being sent back.
= 我的邮箱满了，新邮件都被退回了。
a mailbox = 电子邮箱
⑭ He pushed the newspaper through the mail slot in the door.
= 他把报纸从门上的投信口塞了进去。
a mail slot = （美）投信口
⑮ The programme received a huge mailbag after the interview.
= 那次访谈之后，节目收到了大量来信。
a mailbag = 来信；邮袋
⑯ He started in the mailroom and ended up running the company.
= 他从收发室干起，最后掌管了公司。
the mailroom = 收发室
美国人说「从收发室干起」：从最底层做起。
⑰ The shop sent a mailshot to every house in the town.
= 那家店给城里每户人家都寄了广告邮件。
a mailshot = （英）广告邮件
⑱ The books came in a padded mailer.
= 那几本书是用带衬垫的邮寄袋寄来的。
a mailer = （美）邮寄袋；广告邮件
⑲ The first mailing went out to five thousand members.
= 第一批邮件寄给了五千名会员。

⑳ He uses a mail drop in the city because he has no fixed address.

registered mail = 挂号信
英式多说 registered post。
㉓ She sent the letter back by return mail.

/meɪn/

= 一根自来水总管爆了，把街道淹了。
a water main = 自来水总管
这一支是名词。
⑤ Turn the electricity off at the mains before you change the switch.
= 换开关之前先把总电源关掉。
at the mains = （英）在总开关处

⑨ The village is on the main line from London to Edinburgh.

the main verb = 主要动词

= 好奇心是她一切研究的原动力。
the mainspring = 主要动力

常见结构：be made of（由…做成，看得出原料）、be made from（由…制成，原料已变样）、be made into（被做成）、
be made to do（被迫做，被动式要加 to）。
短语动词：make for（朝…走去；有助于）、make towards（朝…走去）、make of（对…怎么看）、make into（把…变成）、
make off（匆匆逃走；make off with = 偷了…逃走）、make out（看清、听清、弄懂；开（支票）；声称；口语：进展）、
make over（把…转让给；彻底改造）、make up（编造；组成；化妆；和好；补足）、make up for（弥补）、
make up to（英，口语：巴结；make it up to = 补偿某人）。
习语：make sure（确保）、make sense（讲得通）、make a difference（有影响）、make do（凑合）、make it（办成、赶到）、
make up your mind（拿定主意）、make fun of（取笑）、make the most of（充分利用）、make way（让路）、
make ends meet（勉强维持生计）、make believe（假装）。
并入本条：made-up（编造的；化了妆的）、made to measure（量身定做的）、made to order（定做的）、
make-believe（假装、幻想）、make-work（美：没事找事的活儿）。
① She made a cake for her daughter's birthday.
= 她为女儿的生日做了一个蛋糕。
make a cake = 做蛋糕
made 是过去式。
② The factory makes parts for cars and lorries.
= 那家工厂生产汽车和卡车的零件。
make parts = 生产零件
名词 maker：制造商。
③ The table is made of solid oak.
= 那张桌子是实心橡木做的。
be made of = 由…做成
看得出原料：木头还是木头。
④ Most of the paper in these books is made from wood grown in northern forests.
= 这些书的纸大多是用北方森林里的木材造的。
be made from = 由…制成
原料已变了样：木头成了纸。
⑤ The old barn has been made into a house.
= 那座旧谷仓被改成了住宅。
make into = 把…变成
⑥ The sad film about the old dog made me cry in the cinema.
= 那部讲老狗的悲伤电影把我在影院里看哭了。
make somebody do something = 使某人做…
主动式不带 to。
⑦ We were made to wait outside for an hour.
= 我们被迫在外面等了一个小时。
be made to do something = 被迫做…
被动式要加 to。
⑧ The news of her son's safe return made her very happy.
= 儿子平安归来的消息让她非常高兴。
make somebody happy = 使某人高兴
make + 宾语 + 形容词。
⑨ I have to make a decision by Friday.
= 我得在星期五之前作出决定。
make a decision = 作决定
make a mistake / an effort / a call 同此。
⑩ She makes forty thousand pounds a year.
= 她一年挣四万英镑。
make money = 挣钱
make a living：谋生。
⑪ Two and two make four, as every child in the first class knows.
= 二加二等于四，一年级的孩子个个都知道。
make four = 等于四
⑫ She will make a good doctor one day.
= 她有朝一日会成为一名好医生。
make a good doctor = 会成为好医生
这一支是「具备当…的素质」。
⑬ We ran all the way but did not make the last train.
= 我们一路跑去，还是没赶上末班火车。
make the train = 赶上火车
口语，同 catch。
⑭ After years of small parts, she finally made it as an actress.
= 演了多年小角色之后，她终于在演艺界闯出了名堂。
make it = 办成、成功
⑮ I am sorry, I cannot make it to the party on Saturday.
= 对不起，星期六的聚会我来不了。
make it to the party = 赶到聚会
⑯ Make sure that you lock the door when you leave.
= 走的时候务必把门锁上。
make sure = 确保
后面接 that 从句或 of。
⑰ What he said about the money does not make sense.
= 他关于那笔钱的说法讲不通。
make sense = 讲得通
make sense of：弄懂。
⑱ A few kind words can make a big difference.
= 几句暖心的话就能起很大的作用。
make a difference = 有影响、起作用
⑲ We have no butter, so we will have to make do with oil.
= 我们没有黄油，只好用油凑合。
make do = 凑合
常说 make do with something。
⑳ I cannot make up my mind which dress to buy.
= 我拿不定主意买哪条裙子。
make up your mind = 拿定主意
㉑ The other children made fun of his accent.
= 别的孩子取笑他的口音。
make fun of = 取笑
多含恶意。
㉒ We made the most of the sunshine and had lunch in the garden.
= 我们趁着阳光好，在花园里吃了午饭。
make the most of = 充分利用
㉓ The crowd made way for the ambulance.
= 人群给救护车让了路。
make way = 让路
常说 make way for。
㉔ With three children and one wage, they struggle to make ends meet.
= 三个孩子、一份工资，他们勉强维持生计。
make ends meet = 勉强维持生计
让收入和支出两「头」接上。
㉕ The children made believe that the garden shed was a castle.
= 孩子们假装花园的棚屋是一座城堡。
make believe = 假装
㉖ When the rain started, we made for the nearest shelter.
= 雨一下，我们就朝最近的避雨处走去。
make for = 朝…走去
㉗ Good lighting makes for a pleasant place to work.
= 光线好，工作的地方就舒服。
make for = 有助于
㉘ He got up from the table and made towards the door.
= 他从桌边站起来，朝门口走去。
make towards = 朝…走去
㉙ What do you make of the new manager and her plans for the office?
= 你觉得新来的经理和她对办公室的那些打算怎么样？
make of = 对…怎么看
㉚ The thieves made off before the police arrived.
= 警察赶到之前，那伙贼逃走了。
make off = 匆匆逃走
多说做了坏事之后。
㉛ Someone made off with my bicycle while I was in the shop.
= 我在店里的时候，有人把我的自行车偷走了。
make off with = 偷了…逃走
㉜ I could just make out a figure in the fog.
= 我在雾里勉强看出一个人影。
make out = 看清、听清
常与 can / could 连用。
㉝ He keeps changing his story, and I cannot make out what he wants.
= 他的说法变来变去，我弄不懂他想要什么。
make out what… = 弄懂…
㉞ Please make the cheque out to the school.
= 支票抬头请写学校。
make out a cheque = 开支票
㉟ He is not as rich as he makes out.
= 他没有他自称的那么有钱。
make out = 声称、装作
㊱ He made the house over to his son before he died.
= 他去世前把房子过户给了儿子。
make over = 把…转让给
另一义「彻底改造」见 makeover。
㊲ She made up an excuse about missing the bus.
= 她编了个没赶上公交车的借口。
make up = 编造
形容词 made-up。
㊳ Women make up sixty per cent of the students.
= 女生占学生的六成。
make up = 组成、占
㊴ She makes herself up carefully before she goes on stage.
= 她上台前仔细化妆。
make yourself up = 化妆
㊵ The two sisters had a quarrel over the car but soon made up.
= 姐妹俩为了车吵了一架，不过很快就和好了。
make up = 和好
kiss and make up：亲一下，和好。
㊶ Nothing can make up for the years he spent in prison.
= 他在牢里度过的那些年，什么也弥补不了。
make up for = 弥补
make up for lost time：把耽误的时间补回来。
㊷ I am sorry I forgot your birthday; I will make it up to you.
= 对不起我忘了你的生日，我会补偿你的。
make it up to somebody = 补偿某人
㊸ He is always making up to the boss in the hope of a pay rise.
= 他老是巴结老板，指望加薪。
make up to = （英，口语）巴结
㊹ What make of car does she drive?
= 她开的是什么牌子的车？
a make of car = 汽车的牌子
这一支是名词。
㊺ The whole story about the lost dog was made-up.
= 丢狗的那一整套说法都是编的。
made-up = 编造的
也指「化了妆的」。
㊻ He wears made to measure suits from a tailor in London.
= 他穿伦敦一位裁缝量身定做的西装。
made to measure = 量身定做的
㊼ The kitchen cupboards were made to order by a local carpenter.
= 厨房的柜子是当地一位木匠定做的。
made to order = 定做的
㊽ The children live in a world of make-believe.
= 孩子们生活在幻想的世界里。
make-believe = 假装、幻想
㊾ The new forms are just make-work for the office staff.
= 那些新表格纯粹是给办公室人员没事找事。
make-work = （美）没事找事的活儿

makeover
/ˈmeɪkəʊvə(r)/
核心：改头换面 —— 给人换发型、妆容、穿着的全面改造；（房屋、形象的）彻底翻新。
① The magazine gave three readers a free makeover.
= 那本杂志给三位读者做了免费的形象改造。
a makeover = 形象改造
② The old hotel has had a complete makeover and reopened last month.
= 那家老酒店彻底翻新了，上个月重新开业。
a complete makeover = 彻底翻新
来自 make over（彻底改造）。

maker
/ˈmeɪkə(r)/
核心：制造者、制造商；（作后一成分）做…的人或机器；the Maker / your Maker（造物主、上帝）。
① The company is the largest maker of washing machines in Europe.
= 那家公司是欧洲最大的洗衣机制造商。
a maker of something = …的制造商
② She bought a coffee maker for the office.
= 她给办公室买了一台咖啡机。
a coffee maker = 咖啡机
decision-maker、film-maker 同此构词。
③ The old sailor said that he was ready to meet his Maker.
= 那位老水手说，他已准备好去见上帝了。
meet your Maker = 去见上帝（去世）
带点幽默的说法。

makeshift
/ˈmeɪkʃɪft/
核心：临时凑合的、权宜的 —— 没有合适的东西，暂时拿别的顶替。
① The refugees slept in makeshift tents made of plastic sheets.
= 难民们睡在用塑料布搭的临时帐篷里。
makeshift tents = 临时帐篷
② We used a door as a makeshift table for the party.
= 我们拿一扇门当聚会的临时桌子。
a makeshift table = 临时凑合的桌子
来自 make shift（设法凑合）。

makeweight
/ˈmeɪkweɪt/
核心：凑数的人或物 —— 本身不重要，只为补足数量；本义是添秤的小东西。
① He was included in the team as a makeweight when two players fell ill.
= 两名球员病倒了，他被拉进队里凑数。
a makeweight = 凑数的人
② The last chapter is a mere makeweight and adds nothing to the book.
= 最后一章纯属凑数，对那本书毫无增益。
a mere makeweight = 纯属凑数的东西

making
/ˈmeɪkɪŋ/
核心：制作、形成（的过程）。
习语：in the making（正在形成中的）、of your own making（自己造成的）、be the making of（使…成才）、
have the makings of（有成为…的素质）。
① The film shows the making of a violin from start to finish.
= 那部影片从头到尾展示了一把小提琴的制作过程。
the making of something = …的制作
② The crowd in the square knew that they were watching history in the making.
= 广场上的人群知道，他们正在见证历史的创造。
in the making = 正在形成中的
③ Most of the problems of the company are of its own making, not the result of bad luck.
= 那家公司的问题大多是它自己造成的，不是运气不好。
of your own making = 自己造成的
④ He was a lazy boy, but two years in the army were the making of him.
= 他原是个懒孩子，可在军队的两年使他成了才。
be the making of somebody = 使某人成才
⑤ At fourteen, she already has the makings of a great tennis player.
= 才十四岁，她已经有了成为伟大网球选手的素质。
have the makings of = 有成为…的素质
这一支用复数 makings。

maladjusted
/ˌmæləˈdʒʌstɪd/
核心：（尤指儿童）适应不良的 —— 有情绪、行为问题，难以和人相处。名词 maladjustment 并入本条。
① The school takes maladjusted children who have been excluded from other classes.
= 那所学校接收被别的班开除的适应不良儿童。
maladjusted children = 适应不良的儿童
② The report links the boy's maladjustment to years of trouble at home.
= 那份报告把男孩的适应不良归因于家里多年的不安宁。
maladjustment = 适应不良
前缀 mal- 表示「坏、不良」。

maladministration
/ˌmælədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃən/
核心：（正式）管理不善、行政失当。
① The inquiry found the council guilty of maladministration in handling the housing fund.
= 调查认定市政会在管理住房基金上行政失当。
guilty of maladministration = 行政失当
② Years of maladministration left the hospital with huge debts.
= 多年管理不善让那家医院负债累累。
years of maladministration = 多年的管理不善
