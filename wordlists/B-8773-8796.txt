filter
核心：三支。一是滤器、滤芯：a coffee filter；二是动词，过滤：filter water；
三是（信息、消息）筛除与渗透：filter out、filter through。
① The coffee filter is paper and that machine takes a different size.
= 那种咖啡滤纸是纸的，那台机器要的却是另一个尺寸。
a coffee filter = 咖啡滤纸
② They filter the water twice and still boil it before drinking.
= 他们把水过滤两遍，喝之前还是要煮开。
filter water = 过滤水
③ The system filters out anything with two exclamation marks in it.
= 那套系统会把带两个感叹号的东西全筛掉。
filter out = 筛除
④ News of the closure filtered through the building by lunchtime.
= 关门的消息到午饭时已经在整栋楼里传开。
filter through = 渐渐传开

filth
核心：两支。一是污秽、脏东西：be covered in filth；二是（贬）下流的言语与图像。
不可数。形容词 filthy。
① The cellar was covered in filth that turned out to be nine years of soot.
= 地窖里满是污垢，原来是积了九年的烟灰。
be covered in filth = 满是污垢
② The letter is filth and the newspaper printed only the polite half.
= 那封信满纸污言秽语，报纸只登了客气的那一半。
filth = 下流的东西

filthiness
核心：肮脏、污秽：不可数；也说言语的下流。
① The filthiness of the kitchen was the only thing the inspector wrote down.
= 厨房之脏，是检查员唯一记下来的事。
the filthiness of something = 某处的肮脏
② Filthiness in a hospital corridor is a management failure, not a cleaning one.
= 医院走廊脏，是管理的失败，不是保洁的失败。
filthiness = 肮脏

filthy
核心：三支。一是极脏的：filthy hands；二是下流的：a filthy joke；
三是（英式口语）糟透的：filthy weather；另有 filthy rich，富得流油。副词 filthily。
① He came in with filthy hands and washed them in the wrong sink.
= 他手脏兮兮地进来，在不该用的那个水槽里洗了。
filthy hands = 脏手
② A filthy joke at that table cost him the contract.
= 在那张桌上讲了个下流笑话，他把合同弄丢了。
a filthy joke = 下流笑话
③ Filthy weather kept the boats in the harbour and the pub full.
= 恶劣的天气把船留在港里，把酒馆挤满。
filthy weather = 糟透的天气
filthy rich = 富得流油
副词 filthily：脏兮兮地、下流地，用得不多。
④ The yard was filthily neglected and the garden behind it was immaculate.
= 那个院子脏得没人打理，后头的花园却一尘不染。
filthily neglected = 脏得无人搭理

filtrate
核心：（技术）滤液：过滤之后流出来的那部分液体。可数不可数都行。
① The filtrate is clear and the residue is where the interesting part sits.
= 滤液是清的，有意思的东西都在滤渣里。
a filtrate = 滤液
② They keep the filtrate for testing and throw the rest of it away.
= 他们留下滤液做检测，其余的都倒掉。
collect the filtrate = 收集滤液

filtration
核心：过滤这个过程：不可数 —— a filtration system、water filtration。
① The filtration system runs all night and the pool is clear by six.
= 那套过滤系统整夜运转，六点池水就清了。
a filtration system = 过滤系统
② Water filtration removes the taste and the argument about it continues.
= 水过滤把那股怪味去掉了，关于它的争论还在继续。
water filtration = 水过滤

fin
核心：三支。一是鱼鳍；二是（飞机、火箭的）尾翼；三是潜水用的脚蹼。可数。
① A fin broke the surface twice and the beach emptied within a minute.
= 一只鳍两次划开水面，海滩一分钟就空了。
a fin = 鱼鳍
② The tail fin is the only part of that aircraft they repainted.
= 尾翼是那架飞机上唯一重新喷过漆的部分。
a tail fin = 尾翼

finagle
核心：（非正式）耍手腕弄到手、连蒙带混地办成：
finagle something out of somebody。
① He finagled two tickets out of a man who had none to spare.
= 他从一个根本没有余票的人手里，硬是弄到了两张。
finagle something = 耍手腕弄到某物
② She finagled her way onto the list and stayed on it for nine years.
= 她连蒙带混挤进了名单，一待就是九年。
finagle your way into something = 想方设法挤进某处

final
核心：三支。一是最后的：the final chapter；二是（决定）不可更改的；
三是名词，决赛：the cup final；复数 finals 指期末大考。
① The final chapter answers a question nobody asked in the first eleven.
= 最后一章回答的那个问题，前十一章里没人问过。
the final chapter = 最后一章
② The decision is final and the letter says so in bold type.
= 那个决定不可更改，信上用黑体写着。
the decision is final = 决定不可更改
③ The cup final was decided by a goal that took four minutes to allow.
= 那场杯赛决赛，是被一个判了四分钟才算数的进球定下来的。
the cup final = 杯赛决赛
finals = 期末大考

finale
核心：（演出、乐曲的）终场、终曲：最后那一段最热闹的部分。可数。
① The finale needs nine people on stage and eleven were available.
= 终场要九个人上台，能到场的有十一个。
the finale = 终场、终曲
② A grand finale with fireworks emptied the car park before the encore.
= 一场带烟火的盛大终场，返场之前就把停车场清空了。
a grand finale = 盛大的终场

finalist
核心：进入决赛的选手或队伍。可数。
① Every finalist gets a certificate and exactly one of them gets the cheque.
= 每位决赛选手都有证书，支票只有一个人拿。
a finalist = 决赛选手
② Two finalists trained in the same pool and never met until that day.
= 两位决赛选手在同一个泳池训练，到那天才头一回见面。

finality
核心：（正式）终局性、不容更改：说语气或裁定摆明了没得商量。不可数。
① There was a finality in her tone that ended the discussion on the spot.
= 她那语气里有种不容置疑的意味，讨论当场就结束了。
a finality = 不容更改的意味
② The finality of the ruling surprised even the side that had won.
= 那项裁定的终局性，连赢的一方都吃了一惊。
the finality of something = 某事的终局性

finalization
核心：（正式）定案、最后敲定：英式也拼 finalisation。不可数。
① Finalization of the accounts waits on one signature and one holiday.
= 账目定案，就等一个签名和一个人休完假。
finalization = 定案
② The finalization of the contract took three weeks longer than the negotiation.
= 合同的最后敲定，比谈判本身多花了三个星期。
the finalization of something = 某事的最后敲定

finalize
核心：把……定下来、敲定：英式也拼 finalise，多说 finalize the details。
① Finalize the seating plan before the invitations go out, not after.
= 请柬发出去之前把座位表定下来，别等发完再定。
finalize the details = 敲定细节
② The route was finalized in March and then changed twice in April.
= 那条路线三月定了案，四月又改了两回。
be finalized = 被最终定下

finally
核心：三支。一是终于、总算：等了很久之后，带一点松口气的意味；
二是列举时的「最后一点」，跟 firstly、secondly 成套；三是一锤定音地、最终地。
① The parcel finally arrived on a day when nobody was in.
= 那个包裹总算到了，偏偏那天家里没人。
finally = 终于、总算
② Finally, and briefly, the roof still leaks.
= 最后一点，简单说：屋顶还在漏。
finally = 最后（列举用语）
③ The matter was finally settled by a letter that nobody kept.
= 那件事最终是靠一封没人留存的信了结的。
be finally settled = 最终了结

finance
核心：三支。一是财政、金融：public finance，不可数；二是资金：raise finance；
三是动词，出资：finance a project。复数 finances 指某人的财务状况。
① Public finance is dull until the year the money runs out.
= 公共财政很枯燥，直到钱花光的那一年。
public finance = 公共财政
personal finance = 个人理财
② They raised finance from three banks and spent it on one building.
= 他们从三家银行筹到资金，全花在了一栋楼上。
raise finance = 筹措资金
③ Her finances recovered a good deal faster than her patience did.
= 她的财务状况恢复得比她的耐心快得多。
somebody's finances = 某人的财务状况

financial
核心：财政的、金融的、财务的：the financial year、financial difficulties。
副词 financially。
① The financial year ends in March and the panic starts in February.
= 财政年度三月结束，恐慌二月就开始了。
the financial year = 财政年度
② Financial difficulties closed the shop and the landlord kept the sign.
= 财务困难让那家店关了门，招牌被房东留下了。
financial difficulties = 财务困难
副词 financially：在财务上、经济上，多说 financially viable、financially independent。
③ The scheme is financially viable and politically impossible.
= 那个方案财务上可行，政治上没门。
financially viable = 财务上可行的
④ She has been financially independent since she was nineteen.
= 她从十九岁起就在经济上独立了。
financially independent = 经济独立的

financials
核心：（商业）财务报表：一家公司那套账目数字，作复数。
① The financials look tidy and the footnotes are where the year really is.
= 那套财务报表看着规整，这一年的实情都在脚注里。
the financials = 财务报表
② Nobody reads the financials until somebody wants to buy the company.
= 财务报表没人看，直到有人想买下这家公司。
publish the financials = 公布财务报表

financier
核心：（正式）金融家、出资人：靠资本运作为业的人。可数。
① A financier bought the pier and left it exactly as it was.
= 一位金融家买下那座栈桥，原样放着没动。
a financier = 金融家
② Two financiers backed the scheme and only one of them read it first.
= 两位出资人支持那个方案，事先读过的只有一位。

financing
核心：融资、资金安排：不可数 —— arrange financing。
① Financing for the bridge took longer to arrange than the bridge took to build.
= 那座桥的融资谈下来，比造桥还费时间。
arrange financing = 安排融资
② The financing falls apart if any one bank changes its mind.
= 只要有一家银行改主意，那笔融资就散了。
financing = 融资

finch
核心：雀类小鸟：喙短而厚、专吃种子的一类小鸟，金翅雀就是其中一种。可数。
① A finch takes the sunflower seeds and leaves the rest of the mix.
= 那只雀专挑葵花籽吃，混合料里别的一概不碰。
a finch = 雀类小鸟
② Finches turn up in twos and quarrel over a single perch.
= 雀鸟总是成对地来，为一根站杆吵个不停。

find
核心：五支。一是找到、发现：find a seat；二是发觉、觉得：find it hard to do something；
三是（法律）裁定：find somebody guilty；四是名词，发现物：a lucky find；
五是短语动词 find out。习语 find your feet 是适应下来。
过去式与过去分词都是 found。
① Find a seat near the door and you can leave when the speeches start.
= 找个靠门的座位，一开始致辞你就能溜。
find a seat = 找个座位
② She finds it hard to say no and very easy to look busy.
= 她很难说出一个「不」字，装忙倒容易得很。
find it hard to do something = 觉得做某事很难
③ The jury found him guilty on one count and disagreed on the rest.
= 陪审团裁定他一项罪名成立，其余的没能一致。
find somebody guilty = 裁定某人有罪
④ The bowl was a lucky find in a box of broken things.
= 那只碗是在一箱破烂里捡到的宝。
a lucky find = 捡到的宝
⑤ It took her a term to find her feet and a year to find out why.
= 她用一个学期适应下来，用一年弄明白为什么。
find your feet = 适应下来
find out = 弄清楚

finder
核心：拾得者、找到东西的人；也指望远镜上的寻星镜。可数。
习语 finders keepers 是「谁捡到归谁」，多是孩子话。
① The finder handed the wallet in and left no name at the desk.
= 捡到钱包的人交了上去，在前台没留名字。
a finder = 拾得者
② Finders keepers works in a playground and nowhere at all in law.
= 「谁捡到归谁」在操场上说得通，在法律里哪儿都说不通。
finders keepers = 谁捡到归谁

finding
核心：两支。一是（调查、研究的）结论，多用复数 findings；
二是（法律）裁定：a finding of negligence。可数。
① The findings fill nine pages and the summary contradicts two of them.
= 那些结论写了九页，摘要跟其中两条自相矛盾。
the findings = 调查结论
publish the findings = 公布结论
② A finding of negligence changed what the insurer was willing to pay.
= 一项过失认定，改变了保险公司愿意赔的数目。
a finding of negligence = 过失认定
