scriptorium
/skrɪpˈtɔːriəm/
核心：名词，可数，复数 scriptoria 或 scriptoriums：（中世纪修道院的）缮写室：修士抄写书籍的房间。
① In the scriptorium, monks copied books by hand by the light of the windows.
= 在缮写室里，修士们借着窗户的光手抄书籍。
the scriptorium = 缮写室
② The museum has rebuilt a medieval scriptorium with desks, inks and old pens.
= 那座博物馆复建了一间中世纪缮写室，有书桌、墨水和旧笔。
a medieval scriptorium = 中世纪缮写室

scriptural
/ˈskrɪptʃərəl/
核心：形容词：圣经的、经文的、宗教经典的。
① On Sunday the priest used several scriptural passages to explain his ideas about forgiveness.
= 星期天，神父引用了几段经文来阐述他对宽恕的看法。
scriptural passages = 经文段落
scriptural = 经文的
② There is no scriptural basis for some of the rules that the old church followed.
= 那个老教会遵守的一些规矩在经典里找不到依据。
a scriptural basis = 经典依据
