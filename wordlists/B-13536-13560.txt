lightning
核心：闪电。形容词：闪电般的、极快的。
搭配：lightning conductor（英：避雷针；美式说 lightning rod，也比喻招来批评的人）、lightning strike（雷击；英：突击罢工）、
lightning bug（美：萤火虫）。习语 like lightning（闪电般地）。
① A flash of lightning lit up the whole valley.
= 一道闪电照亮了整个山谷。
a flash of lightning = 一道闪电
② The old oak was struck by lightning during the storm.
= 暴风雨中，那棵老橡树被雷劈了。
be struck by lightning = 被雷击
③ The cat moved like lightning and caught the bird.
= 那只猫闪电般一动，逮住了鸟。
like lightning = 闪电般地
④ The goalkeeper made a save with lightning speed.
= 守门员以闪电般的速度把球扑了出去。
lightning speed = 闪电般的速度
⑤ A lightning conductor runs down the side of the church tower.
= 一根避雷针顺着教堂塔楼的侧面通下来。
a lightning conductor = （英）避雷针
⑥ The minister has become a lightning rod for anger about the new tax.
= 那位部长成了人们对新税的怒火集中的对象。
a lightning rod = （美）避雷针；招来批评的人
⑦ The fire was started by a lightning strike.
= 那场火是雷击引起的。
a lightning strike = 雷击
英式也指不预先通知的突击罢工。
⑧ The children caught lightning bugs in a jar.
= 孩子们用罐子捉萤火虫。
a lightning bug = （美）萤火虫
注意拼写：没有 e，和 lightening（变亮）不同。

lightweight
核心：轻便的、轻量的；（贬）没分量的、浅薄的；名词：（拳击）轻量级选手；没分量的人。
① He wore a lightweight jacket that folds into a small bag.
= 他穿了一件能叠进小袋子的轻便外套。
a lightweight jacket = 轻便外套
与 heavyweight 相对。
② The critics called it a lightweight novel with nothing new to say.
= 评论家说那是一部没分量的小说，毫无新意。
a lightweight novel = 没分量的小说
③ He was the lightweight champion of the world for three years.
= 他当了三年世界轻量级冠军。
the lightweight champion = 轻量级冠军
④ His rivals see him as a political lightweight.
= 对手把他看成政坛上没分量的人物。
a political lightweight = 政坛上没分量的人

like
核心：两个词。其一（动词）：喜欢；想要（would like）。名词 likes（喜好）。
其二（介词、连词）：像、如同；比如；（口语）好像。形容词（正式）：相似的。名词：the like（诸如此类的东西）。
口语里还当填充语和引语标记用（I was like, …）。
句型与习语：like doing / like to do（喜欢做）、would like（想要，比 want 客气）、if you like（如果你愿意）、
look like（看起来像）、feel like（想要；摸起来像）、what is it like（…怎么样）、and the like（诸如此类）、
the likes of（像…这样的人）、like-minded（志趣相投的）、like father, like son（有其父必有其子）。
① She likes walking in the hills with her two dogs at the weekend.
= 她周末喜欢带着两条狗去山里走走。
like doing something = 喜欢做…
② I like to get up early and read before breakfast.
= 我喜欢早起，早饭前看会儿书。
like to do something = 喜欢（习惯于）做…
③ I would like a cup of tea and a piece of cake, please.
= 请给我一杯茶和一块蛋糕。
would like = 想要
④ Would you like to come to dinner on Friday and meet my parents?
= 你星期五愿意来吃晚饭、见见我父母吗？
would you like to… = 你愿意…吗
⑤ We can walk to the station along the river path, if you like.
= 你要是愿意，我们可以沿着河边小路走去车站。
if you like = 如果你愿意
⑥ He looks like his father in the old wedding photograph when he smiles.
= 他笑起来像结婚旧照上的他父亲。
look like = 看起来像
⑦ It looks like rain over the hills, so take a coat and an umbrella.
= 山那边看样子要下雨，带件外套和一把伞吧。
it looks like rain = 看样子要下雨
⑧ I feel like a walk after sitting at the desk all day.
= 在桌前坐了一整天，我想去走走。
feel like = 想要
⑨ What is the new maths teacher like? Is she strict about homework?
= 新来的数学老师怎么样？对作业要求严吗？
what is it like = …怎么样
问的是特点，不是「喜欢什么」。
⑩ She sings like an angel, but she is too shy to join the church choir.
= 她唱得像天使一样，却害羞得不肯加入教堂唱诗班。
like an angel = 像天使一样
这是介词：像。
⑪ I enjoy team sports like football and hockey.
= 我喜欢足球、曲棍球之类的团体运动。
like football = 比如足球
⑫ It is just like him to forget his own birthday.
= 连自己的生日都忘，这正是他的作风。
it is just like somebody to… = …正是某人的作风
⑬ We grew up in the same street, and nobody understands her like I do.
= 我们在同一条街上长大，没有人像我这样了解她。
like I do = 像我这样
这是连词，口语；正式写作用 as。
⑭ He has worked here for a week, and he acts like he owns the place.
= 他才来一个星期，那副做派就好像这地方是他的。
like = （口语）好像
正式说 as if。
⑮ He told me the ticket cost two hundred pounds, and I was like, "Are you serious?"
= 他跟我说那张票要两百英镑，我就说：“你当真？”
I was like = （口语）我就说
年轻人口语里引出话语、反应。
⑯ We are all different, with our own likes and dislikes.
= 我们各不相同，各有各的好恶。
likes and dislikes = 好恶
⑰ The little shop beside the school sells pens, notebooks and the like.
= 学校旁边那家小店卖钢笔、笔记本之类的东西。
and the like = 诸如此类
⑱ A hotel like that is not for the likes of us.
= 那样的酒店不是给我们这种人住的。
the likes of = 像…这样的人
⑲ She joined a club to meet like-minded people.
= 她加入了一个俱乐部，想结识志趣相投的人。
like-minded = 志趣相投的
⑳ He became a doctor, just as his father had: like father, like son.
= 他跟他父亲一样当了医生，真是有其父必有其子。
like father, like son = 有其父必有其子

likeable
核心：讨人喜欢的、可爱的 —— 性格好，容易让人有好感。也拼 likable。
① He is a likeable young man who gets on with everyone in the office.
= 他是个讨人喜欢的年轻人，和办公室里的每个人都处得来。
a likeable young man = 讨人喜欢的年轻人
② The hero of the film is a likable thief who steals only from the rich.
= 影片的主人公是个招人喜欢的贼，只偷富人。
likable = likeable 的另一种拼法
美式多用不带 e 的拼法。

likelihood
核心：可能性。常说 in all likelihood（十有八九）。
① The farmers need water, but there is little likelihood of rain before the weekend.
= 农民需要水，可周末之前下雨的可能性很小。
little likelihood of something = …的可能性很小
② In all likelihood, the meeting will be moved to next week because of the strike.
= 因为罢工，会议十有八九要改到下周。
in all likelihood = 十有八九
③ Smoking increases the likelihood that you will have a heart attack.
= 吸烟会增加心脏病发作的可能性。
the likelihood that… = …的可能性

likely
核心：很可能的；合适的、有希望的。副词：很可能（多与 most、very 连用）。
句型 be likely to do（很可能做）、it is likely that…。习语 more than likely（十有八九）、not likely（口语：才不呢）。
① The last train from London is likely to be late because of the snow.
= 因为下雪，从伦敦开来的末班火车很可能晚点。
be likely to = 很可能
② It is likely that prices will rise again next year.
= 明年物价很可能再涨。
it is likely that… = 很可能…
③ The most likely cause of the fire was an old heater.
= 火灾最可能的起因是一台旧取暖器。
the most likely cause = 最可能的原因
④ This flat rock beside the stream looks like a likely place for a picnic.
= 小溪边这块平石头看上去挺适合野餐。
a likely place = 合适的地方
⑤ His flight was delayed, so he will most likely arrive after dinner.
= 他的航班延误了，所以他很可能晚饭后才到。
most likely = 很可能
这里是副词。
⑥ More than likely, she has simply forgotten the time.
= 十有八九，她只是忘了时间。
more than likely = 十有八九
⑦ "Are you going to lend him the money?" "Not likely!"
= “你打算借钱给他吗？”“才不呢！”
not likely = （口语）才不呢

liken

Likewise. = （口语）我也一样

limb
核心：肢体 —— 胳膊或腿；（树的）大枝。

① He lost a limb in a farm accident when he was young.
= 他年轻时在一次农场事故中失去了一条肢体。
lose a limb = 失去一条肢体

= 她伸了伸疲乏的四肢，仰面躺在草地上。
tired limbs = 疲乏的四肢

④ I am going out on a limb here, but I think the plan will fail.
= 我这么说是担着风险的，不过我认为那个方案会失败。
out on a limb = 处境孤立、担着风险

⑤ Firefighters risk life and limb every time they enter a burning building.
= 消防员每次进入着火的建筑都冒着生命危险。
risk life and limb = 冒生命危险
life and limb = 性命和肢体

limit
核心：限度、极限；（规定的）限额、限制；（复数）界限、范围。动词：限制、限定。
常说 limit something to（把…限制在）、limit yourself to（只…）。
习语：off limits（禁止入内的；不准碰的）、within limits（在一定限度内）、over the limit（酒后超标）、
the sky is the limit（没有上限）、there is a limit to（…是有限度的）。
形容词 limiting（起限制作用的）与 limitless（无限的）并入本条。
① There is a limit to how much work one person can do in a day.
= 一个人一天能干多少活是有限度的。
a limit to something = …的限度
② The speed limit on this road is thirty miles an hour.
= 这条路限速每小时三十英里。
the speed limit = 限速
age limit（年龄限制）、time limit（时限）同此。
③ The climb pushed the team to the limits of their strength.
= 那次攀登把队员们逼到了体力的极限。
the limits of your strength = 体力的极限
④ The kitchen is off limits to the children while I am cooking.
= 我做饭的时候，孩子们不准进厨房。
off limits = 禁止入内的
⑤ You can spend what you like, within limits.
= 你想花多少花多少，只要别太过分。
within limits = 在一定限度内
⑥ He was stopped by the police and found to be over the limit.
= 他被警察拦下，查出酒精超标。
over the limit = 酒后超标
⑦ With a voice like hers, the sky is the limit.
= 凭她那样的嗓子，前途不可限量。
the sky is the limit = 没有上限
⑧ The hall is small, so we must limit the audience to two hundred people.
= 礼堂小，我们得把观众限制在两百人以内。
limit to = 把…限制在
⑨ I try to limit myself to two cups of coffee a day.
= 我尽量每天只喝两杯咖啡。
limit yourself to something = 只…、把自己限制在
⑩ Lack of water is the main limiting factor for farming in the region.
= 缺水是限制那个地区农业的主要因素。
a limiting factor = 限制因素
科学、经济文章里的常用搭配。
⑪ The desert seemed limitless under the burning sun.
= 烈日下，沙漠显得无边无际。
limitless = 无限的、无边的

limitation
核心：限制（这一行为或规定）；（复数）局限、不足之处。
① The treaty puts a limitation on the number of warships each country may build.
= 那项条约对各国可造军舰的数量作了限制。
a limitation on something = 对…的限制
② The film is good despite the limitations of its small budget.
= 尽管预算小、受了局限，那部电影还是不错。
the limitations of something = …的局限
③ A good manager knows her own limitations and asks for help.
= 好经理知道自己的不足，会开口求助。
know your limitations = 知道自己的不足
