/ˈrætɪfaɪ/

= 那项条约必须得到十二个国家议会的批准。
ratify a treaty = 批准条约
be ratified by something = 得到某机构批准
② The union members voted to ratify the new agreement on pay.
= 工会会员投票批准了新的工资协议。

③ The ratification of the treaty took three years because of opposition in two countries.
= 因为两个国家有人反对，那项条约花了三年才获批准。
the ratification of something = 某事的批准
ratification = 批准
