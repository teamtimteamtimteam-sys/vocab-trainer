shaman
/ˈʃeɪmən/
核心：名词，可数：萨满、巫师（某些部落中被认为能和神灵沟通、治病的人）。
① The village shaman danced around the fire and called on the spirits to bring rain.
= 村里的萨满绕着火堆跳舞，祈求神灵降雨。
the village shaman = 村里的萨满
shaman = 萨满
② People travelled for days to see the shaman, who was said to cure illnesses with plants and songs.
= 人们走好几天去见那位萨满，据说他能用草药和歌声治病。
the shaman = 巫师

/ʃəˈmænɪk/

① The museum has a collection of drums and masks used in shamanic rituals in Siberia.
= 那座博物馆收藏了西伯利亚萨满仪式上用过的鼓和面具。
shamanic rituals = 萨满仪式
shamanic = 萨满的
② Some modern healers say they use shamanic methods to help people with stress.
= 一些现代治疗师说他们用萨满的方法帮人缓解压力。
shamanic methods = 萨满的方法

shamateur
/ˈʃæmətə(r)/

① In the old days, many Olympic athletes were shamateurs who were secretly paid by their governments.
= 早年许多奥运选手是伪业余运动员，偷偷拿着政府的钱。
shamateurs = 伪业余运动员
shamateur = 伪业余运动员
② The newspaper called the rugby player a shamateur after it found out about his free car and flat.
= 报纸查出那名橄榄球员白得了车和公寓后，称他是个伪业余选手。
a shamateur = 伪业余选手

/ˈʃæmətərɪzəm/
