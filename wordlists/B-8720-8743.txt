fielding
核心：（板球、棒球）守场：接球、拦球、传球那一整套本事。不可数。
① Their fielding won a match that their batting had almost lost.
= 他们的守场赢下了一场差点被击球断送的比赛。
fielding = 守场
② Fielding practice happens in the rain because the nets are booked out.
= 守场训练在雨里进行，因为练习棚被人订满了。
fielding practice = 守场训练

fieldsman
核心：（板球，英式）守场员：跟 fielder 同义，略旧。复数 fieldsmen。
① The fieldsman at the boundary has the longest walk and the quietest game.
= 站在边界线上的守场员，路走得最远，比赛过得最清静。
a fieldsman = 守场员
② Two fieldsmen swapped ends and neither of them told the umpire.
= 两名守场员换了边，谁都没告诉裁判。

fieldwork
核心：田野工作、实地考察：在实验室与书桌之外收集材料。不可数。
① Fieldwork means three months of mud and about one page of conclusions.
= 田野工作意味着三个月的泥泞，外加大约一页结论。
fieldwork = 田野工作
② Her fieldwork was done in a language she had learned on the boat over.
= 她的田野调查，是用一门在渡船上学会的语言做的。
do fieldwork = 做田野调查

fieldworker
核心：田野工作者、实地调查员：在现场收集资料的人。可数。
① A fieldworker with a notebook gets further than a form on a clipboard.
= 一个带笔记本的调查员，比夹板上的一张表问得深。
a fieldworker = 田野调查员
② Two fieldworkers now cover a district that used to have six.
= 如今两名实地调查员，负责一个从前配六个人的区。

fiend
核心：三支。一是恶魔、狠毒的人；二是（非正式）……迷、上瘾的人：a fitness fiend；
三是（旧）魔鬼。可数。形容词 fiendish。
① The papers called him a fiend and the file called him a bookkeeper.
= 报纸管他叫恶魔，档案里写的是记账员。
a fiend = 恶魔、狠毒的人
② A crossword fiend in that office finishes it before the kettle boils.
= 办公室里有个填字迷，水还没烧开就填完了。
a fitness fiend = 健身狂

fiendish
核心：两支。一是凶残的、恶魔般的；二是（口语）刁钻的、难得吓人的。
副词 fiendishly。
① A fiendish plan needs a fiendish amount of paperwork these days.
= 如今一个恶毒的计划，得配上一大堆吓人的文书。
a fiendish plan = 恶毒的计划
② The last clue is fiendish and the rest of them took nine minutes.
= 最后一条线索刁钻得很，其余的九分钟就解完了。
a fiendish puzzle = 刁钻的谜题
副词 fiendishly：刁钻地、极其地，多修饰 difficult、clever、complicated。
③ The instructions are fiendishly clever and completely unreadable.
= 那份说明构思刁钻，却根本读不下去。
fiendishly clever = 刁钻聪明的
④ The last two questions were fiendishly difficult and worth one mark each.
= 最后两道题难得吓人，每道却只值一分。
fiendishly difficult = 难得吓人

fierce
核心：三支。一是（动物、人）凶猛的；二是（竞争、批评）激烈的：fierce competition；
三是（天气、火势）猛烈的。副词 fiercely，名词 fierceness。
① A fierce dog behind that gate has never once been seen by anybody.
= 那道门后有条凶猛的狗，谁都没见过。
a fierce dog = 凶猛的狗
② Fierce competition on that route brought the fare down and the seats closer.
= 那条航线竞争激烈，票价降了，座位也挤了。
fierce competition = 激烈的竞争
副词 fiercely：猛烈地、极其地，多修饰 defended、burned、independent。
③ She is fiercely independent and rings her mother every single day.
= 她极其独立，却天天给母亲打电话。
fiercely independent = 极其独立的
④ The fire burned fiercely for an hour and left the chimney standing.
= 那场火猛烧了一个钟头，烟囱倒还立着。
burn fiercely = 猛烈燃烧

fierceness
核心：凶猛、激烈：不可数。
① The fierceness of the reaction surprised the people who had drafted it.
= 反应之激烈，让起草那份东西的人吃了一惊。
the fierceness of something = 某事的激烈
② She defends the allotment with a fierceness the vegetables cannot repay.
= 她守着那块菜地的那股凶劲，蔬菜可回报不了。
with fierceness = 凶猛地

fiery
核心：三支。一是火一样的、燃烧的：a fiery glow；二是（性情）暴烈的：a fiery temper；
三是（食物）辛辣的。
① A fiery glow behind the hill turned out to be a security light.
= 山后那团火似的光，原来是一盏安保灯。
a fiery glow = 火红的光
② His fiery temper clears a room and cools down in about four minutes.
= 他那暴烈脾气能把屋里人吓走，四分钟左右就消。
a fiery temper = 暴烈的脾气
③ The sauce is fiery and comes with a spoonful of yoghurt as an apology.
= 那种酱辣得厉害，配一勺酸奶算是赔罪。
fiery = 辛辣的

fiesta
核心：（西语国家）节庆：宗教节日或地方庆典，带游行、乐队与摊子。可数。
① The fiesta closes the square for three days and the bakery makes a year's profit.
= 那场节庆把广场封三天，面包房赚下一年的利润。
a fiesta = 节庆
② A village fiesta needs a saint, a band and somebody who owns a generator.
= 一场乡村节庆要有一位主保圣人、一支乐队，还有一个有发电机的人。
a village fiesta = 乡村节庆

fife
核心：（乐器）横笛：军乐队里那种高音小笛，多与鼓合奏。可数。
① The fife carries over a drum better than any other instrument that size.
= 同样大小的乐器里，横笛的声音最盖得过鼓。
a fife = 横笛
② Two fifes and three drums make more noise than the whole brass section.
= 两支横笛加三面鼓，比整个铜管组还响。
fife and drum = 笛鼓合奏

fifteen
核心：十五：基数词；在橄榄球里也指一支球队，因为一队十五人。
① Fifteen people signed up for the trip and nine of them came.
= 报名那趟出行的有十五人，来了九个。
fifteen = 十五
② The school fifteen trains on Tuesdays and loses on Saturdays.
= 校橄榄球队周二训练，周六输球。
a rugby fifteen = 一支橄榄球队

fifteenth
核心：第十五；也指十五分之一。序数词，写作 15th。
① The fifteenth of the month is when the rent leaves and the panic starts.
= 每月十五号房租一扣，恐慌就开始了。
the fifteenth = 第十五（日）
② A fifteenth of the crop went to the landlord and the rest went to market.
= 收成的十五分之一交给地主，其余的拿去卖。
a fifteenth = 十五分之一

fifth
核心：三支。一是第五：the fifth of June；二是五分之一；三是（音乐）五度音程。
序数词写作 5th。副词 fifthly 用在列举里。
① The fifth of June is the only date anybody in that family remembers.
= 六月五号，是那家人唯一都记得的日子。
the fifth = 第五
② A fifth of the class had never been on a train before that trip.
= 那趟出行之前，班上五分之一的人没坐过火车。
a fifth = 五分之一
副词 fifthly：列举时的「第五」，跟 firstly、secondly 一路排下来，正式行文用。
③ Fifthly, and everybody had stopped listening by then, the roof needs money.
= 第五点 —— 讲到这儿已经没人在听了 —— 屋顶需要钱。
fifthly = 第五（列举用语）

fiftieth
核心：第五十；也指五十分之一。序数词，写作 50th。
① Her fiftieth birthday was marked with a cake and a very long silence.
= 她五十岁生日，就一个蛋糕，外加一段很长的沉默。
the fiftieth = 第五十
② A fiftieth of a second is long enough to blur that photograph.
= 五十分之一秒，就足够把那张照片拍糊。
a fiftieth = 五十分之一

fifty
核心：五十：基数词；复数 fifties 指五十几岁、五十年代，也指五十元的钞票。
① Fifty people booked and the room holds thirty-eight at a squeeze.
= 订位的有五十人，那屋子挤挤能装三十八个。
fifty = 五十
② She is in her fifties and cycles further than anybody else in the office.
= 她五十几岁，骑车比办公室里谁都远。
in your fifties = 五十几岁
③ The fifties in that town meant one bus a day and no telephone.
= 那个镇子的五十年代，意味着一天一班车、没有电话。
the fifties = 五十年代

fig
核心：无花果：软甜多籽的果实，也晒成干果卖。可数。
习语 not give a fig 是「一点也不在乎」，略旧。
① Fresh figs bruise on the way home and taste better than the dried ones anyway.
= 新鲜无花果拿回家路上会碰伤，味道还是比干的好。
a fig = 无花果
② He does not give a fig what the committee thinks and says so at every meeting.
= 委员会怎么想他一点也不在乎，每次开会都要说一遍。
not give a fig = 一点也不在乎

fight
核心：五支。一是打架、搏斗：get into a fight；二是（拳击等）比赛；
三是斗争：fight for something、fight against something；四是名词，斗志：
put up a fight；五是短语动词 fight back 与 fight off。
过去式与过去分词都是 fought。
① Two boys got into a fight over a bicycle that belonged to neither of them.
= 两个男孩为一辆谁的都不是的自行车打了一架。
get into a fight = 打起来
② She fought for the funding and against the timetable at the same time.
= 她一边为经费争取，一边跟时间表较劲。
fight for something = 为某事奋争
fight against something = 对抗某事
③ The smaller team put up a fight and lost by a single point.
= 实力较弱的那队顽强抵抗，只输了一分。
put up a fight = 顽强抵抗
④ He fought back tears and answered the question anyway.
= 他强忍住眼泪，还是把那个问题答了。
fight back = 反击、强忍住
⑤ She fought off a cold for a week and gave in on the Saturday.
= 她硬扛了一星期感冒，周六还是倒下了。
fight off something = 抵御住某物
⑥ Arguing with that department is fighting a losing battle with better stationery.
= 跟那个部门争，是打一场文具更好的败仗。
fight a losing battle = 打一场必败的仗

fightback
核心：（英式）反击、逆转：从落后处一路追回来。可数。
① The fightback started in the second half and ended two points short.
= 那场反击从下半场开始，最后差两分。
a fightback = 反击、逆转
② A fightback needs a plan and about forty minutes of clock.
= 要逆转得有方案，还得有大约四十分钟的时间。
stage a fightback = 展开反击

fighter
核心：三支。一是斗士、不服输的人；二是拳击手、格斗选手；
三是战斗机：a fighter jet。可数。
① She is a fighter and the ward staff say so in her notes.
= 她是个不服输的人，病房的护士在记录里也这么写。
a fighter = 斗士、不服输的人
② The fighter came in low over the estuary and set off nine car alarms.
= 那架战斗机贴着河口低飞过来，惊响了九个汽车警报。
a fighter jet = 喷气式战斗机

fighting
核心：两支。一是战斗、打斗，不可数：heavy fighting；
二是作定语，好斗的、战斗用的：a fighting spirit、fighting talk。
① Heavy fighting closed the road and the market moved to the next village.
= 激烈的战斗封了路，集市挪到了邻村。
heavy fighting = 激烈的战斗
② A fighting spirit and one working ambulance kept that clinic open.
= 一股不服输的劲头，加一辆还能开的救护车，撑住了那家诊所。
a fighting spirit = 不服输的劲头
fighting talk = 放狠话、示强的话

figment
核心：凭空想出来的东西：几乎只用在 a figment of somebody's imagination 里。可数。
① The deadline turned out to be a figment of one manager's imagination.
= 那个截止日期，原来是某位经理凭空想出来的。
a figment of somebody's imagination = 某人凭空想出来的东西
② The noise is not a figment and three neighbours have written it down.
= 那声音不是幻觉，三个邻居都记下来了。
a figment = 凭空想出来的东西

figurative
核心：两支。一是比喻的、引申的：a figurative sense，跟字面义 literal 相对；
二是（艺术）具象的：figurative painting。副词 figuratively。
① The phrase has a figurative sense that the sign painter did not consider.
= 那个说法有一层比喻义，做招牌的人没考虑到。
a figurative sense = 比喻义
② Figurative painting came back into the galleries as it was leaving the schools.
= 具象绘画回到画廊的时候，正从学校里退场。
figurative painting = 具象绘画
副词 figuratively：比喻地说，多用 figuratively speaking 打头。
③ Figuratively speaking, that department has been on fire since March.
= 打个比方说，那个部门从三月起就一直在着火。
figuratively speaking = 打个比方说
④ She meant it figuratively and the minutes recorded it as a threat.
= 她说的是比喻，会议记录却当成了威胁记下来。
mean something figuratively = 意在比喻

figure
核心：五支。一是数字、数目：sales figures；二是身材；三是人物：a public figure；
四是（远处的）人影；五是动词，想明白：figure something out。
习语 a figure of speech 是修辞说法，cut a figure 是显出某种模样。
① Sales figures for June arrived in August and changed twice after that.
= 六月的销售数字八月才到，之后又改了两回。
sales figures = 销售数字
② He became a public figure by accident and never got used to it.
= 他是稀里糊涂成了公众人物的，一直没习惯。
a public figure = 公众人物
③ A figure at the end of the platform turned out to be a coat on a trolley.
= 站台尽头那个人影，原来是搭在推车上的一件大衣。
a figure = 人影
④ It took her a whole weekend to figure out what the second switch does.
= 她花了整整一个周末才弄明白第二个开关是干什么的。
figure something out = 弄明白某事
⑤ Saying that the door is watching you is a figure of speech, or was.
= 说那扇门在看着你，是个修辞说法 —— 至少从前是。
a figure of speech = 修辞说法
cut a figure = 显出某种模样
