papoose
/pəˈpuːs/
核心：两支，可数。一是婴儿背袋：把婴儿背在背上的一种袋子；二是（旧，冒犯）对北美印第安人婴儿的称呼，如今不要用。
① She carried the baby up the hill in a papoose on her back.
= 她用背袋把婴儿背在背上，上了山。
a papoose = 婴儿背袋
② He walked round the market with his daughter asleep in a papoose.
= 他在集市上转悠，女儿在背袋里睡着了。
in a papoose = 在背袋里
第二支是冒犯的旧称，读旧书时会碰到。

paprika
/ˈpæprɪkə/
核心：红辣椒粉：用一种甜椒晒干磨成的红色粉末，不太辣，用来给菜调味、上色。不可数。
① She sprinkled a little paprika over the eggs just before serving them.
= 上桌前，她往鸡蛋上撒了一点红辣椒粉。
paprika = 红辣椒粉
② The stew gets its deep red colour from two spoonfuls of smoked paprika.
= 那锅炖菜的深红色来自两勺烟熏红椒粉。
smoked paprika = 烟熏红椒粉

papyrus
/pəˈpaɪrəs/
核心：三支。一是纸莎草：一种长在水边的高草，不可数；二是莎草纸：古埃及人用它的茎做的书写材料，不可数；
三是写在莎草纸上的文献，可数，复数 papyri。
① Papyrus still grows thickly along the banks of the river.
= 纸莎草至今仍沿着河岸长得密密的。
papyrus = 纸莎草
② The accounts were written on papyrus with a reed pen.
= 那些账目是用芦苇笔写在莎草纸上的。
on papyrus = 在莎草纸上
③ The museum owns several papyri from a tomb near the Nile.
= 那家博物馆藏有几件尼罗河附近一座墓里出土的莎草纸文献。
papyri = 莎草纸文献

par
/pɑː(r)/
核心：本义是「相等、标准的水平」。不可数。一是（高尔夫）标准杆：打完一个洞、一场球应该用的杆数；二是（金融）票面价值，也说 par value。
习语都从「标准水平」来：below par、under par 是不如平常、不够好，也指身体欠佳；up to par 是达到应有的水平；
on a par with 是与某人、某事物同等、不相上下；par for the course 是意料之中的、（坏事）照例如此。
① She finished the round three under par and took the lead.
= 她以低于标准杆三杆打完这一轮，取得领先。
under par = 低于标准杆
高尔夫里杆数越少越好，under par 是好成绩。
② The fourth hole is a par five with a lake in front of the green.
= 第四洞是五杆洞，果岭前面有个湖。
a par five = 五杆洞
③ The shares were first sold at par and now trade at twice that price.
= 那些股票当初按面值发售，现在的成交价是面值的两倍。
at par = 按面值
par value = 票面价值
④ I have been feeling a little below par since I had the flu.
= 得了流感以后，我一直觉得身体有点不对劲。
below par = 不如平常、身体欠佳
⑤ His work this term has not been up to par.
= 他这学期的功课没有达到应有的水平。
up to par = 达到应有的水平
⑥ The new law puts part-time staff on a par with their full-time colleagues.
= 新法律让兼职员工跟全职同事享有同等的地位。
on a par with = 与……同等
⑦ The train was forty minutes late, which is par for the course on this line.
= 火车晚了四十分钟，在这条线上是常有的事。
par for the course = 意料之中的、照例如此

parade
/pəˈreɪd/

动词：游行、列队行进；让（士兵）列队受检阅；把（某人）拉出来示众；招摇地走来走去；炫耀（财富、学问）；parade as 是冒充、打着……的幌子。
习语 rain on somebody's parade 是（美式口语）扫某人的兴、泼冷水。
parade ground 是阅兵场、操场。并入本条。

a carnival parade = 狂欢节游行
② The regiment was on parade in front of the palace when the rain began.
= 下起雨来的时候，那个团正在王宫前受检阅。
on parade = 在受检阅
a military parade = 阅兵式
③ The inquiry heard from a parade of witnesses over six weeks.
= 六个星期里，调查组听取了一长串证人的证词。
a parade of witnesses = 一长串证人
④ There is a chemist's in the parade of shops opposite the station.
= 车站对面那排商店里有一家药房。
a parade of shops = 一排商店
⑤ The winning team paraded through the city in an open-top bus.
= 获胜的球队乘着敞篷大巴在城里游行。
parade through the city = 在城里游行
⑥ The colonel paraded his men in the square at six every morning.
= 上校每天早上六点让手下在广场上列队。
parade troops = 让部队列队
⑦ The prisoners were paraded before the television cameras.
= 俘虏被拉到电视镜头前示众。
be paraded before the cameras = 被拉到镜头前示众
⑧ She paraded up and down the room in her new dress.
= 她穿着新连衣裙在屋里走来走去地显摆。
parade up and down = 招摇地走来走去
⑨ He never misses a chance to parade his knowledge of wine.
= 他从不放过炫耀自己懂葡萄酒的机会。
parade your knowledge = 炫耀学问
⑩ The report is merely opinion parading as fact.
= 那份报告只不过是冒充事实的个人看法。
parade as something = 冒充某事物
⑪ The recruits drilled on the parade ground for two hours before breakfast.
= 新兵早饭前在操场上操练了两个钟头。
a parade ground = 阅兵场、操场
⑫ I hate to rain on your parade, but the tickets were sold out weeks ago.
= 我不想扫你的兴，可是票几个星期前就卖光了。
rain on someone's parade = 扫某人的兴
美式口语；字面是「往人家的游行上下雨」。

paradigm
/ˈpærədaɪm/

= 学拉丁语的人得把每个动词的变位表背下来。
the paradigm of a verb = 动词的变位表
④ The Internet brought about a paradigm shift in the way people buy music.
= 互联网让人们买音乐的方式发生了根本转变。
a paradigm shift = 范式转移、根本转变
⑤ The case is paradigmatic of the problems facing small farms.
= 这个案例是小农场所面临问题的典型。
be paradigmatic of something = 是某事的典型

paradox
/ˈpærədɒks/

⑤ Paradoxically, the more money he earned, the less he enjoyed spending it.

paradoxically = 说来矛盾的是

parallel
/ˈpærəlel/
核心：本义是「平行」。形容词：平行的：两条线始终隔着同样的距离、永不相交，介词用 to 或 with；
引申为同时发生的、相似而并行的；（计算机）并行的：几项运算同时进行。副词用法跟形容词同形：run parallel to。
名词：相似的人、事、情形，可数；相似之处，可数，常用复数：draw a parallel between 是拿两者作比；也指纬线：the 49th parallel。
in parallel 是同时、并行地；have no parallel、without parallel 是无与伦比。
动词：与……相似、与……同时发生；比得上。过去式 paralleled，不双写 l。
复合词：parallel bars 是双杠；parallel parking 是侧方停车，动词 parallel park；parallel processing 是并行处理；parallel port 是并行端口；
parallel imports 是平行进口货：不经厂家授权的渠道从国外进来的正品；parallel ruler 是平行尺；parallel turn 是（滑雪）双板平行转弯。并入本条。
① The road runs parallel to the railway for about ten miles.
= 那条路跟铁路平行着走了十英里左右。
run parallel to something = 与某物平行
② Draw two parallel lines about three centimetres apart.
= 画两条相隔三厘米左右的平行线。
parallel lines = 平行线
③ The two scientists made parallel discoveries without knowing of each other's work.
= 两位科学家互不知道对方的工作，却作出了相似的发现。
parallel discoveries = 相似而并行的发现
④ The program splits the job into parts for parallel processing.
= 那个程序把任务拆成几部分做并行处理。
parallel processing = 并行处理
⑤ The older printer is connected to the computer through a parallel port.
= 那台旧打印机是通过并行端口接到电脑上的。
a parallel port = 并行端口
⑥ There are clear parallels between her childhood and that of her heroine.
= 她的童年跟她笔下女主人公的童年有明显的相似之处。
parallels between A and B = 甲与乙的相似之处
⑦ The writer draws a parallel between the fall of Rome and our own times.
= 作者拿罗马的衰亡跟我们这个时代作比。
draw a parallel between = 拿……作比
⑧ The flood was a disaster without parallel in the history of the town.
= 那场洪水是镇上有史以来无可比拟的灾难。
without parallel = 无与伦比
have no parallel = 无与伦比
⑨ The two investigations will be carried out in parallel.
= 两项调查将同时进行。
in parallel = 同时、并行地
⑩ The border follows the 49th parallel for hundreds of miles.
= 那条边界沿着北纬四十九度线延伸了几百英里。
the 49th parallel = 北纬四十九度线
⑪ The rise in crime parallels the rise in unemployment.
= 犯罪率的上升跟失业率的上升是同步的。
parallel something = 与某事相似、同时发生
⑫ His career as a goalkeeper has never been paralleled in this country.
= 他当守门员的成就在这个国家至今无人能比。
never been paralleled = 无人能比
⑬ The gymnast fell from the parallel bars and broke her wrist.
= 那名体操运动员从双杠上摔下来，摔断了手腕。
the parallel bars = 双杠
⑭ He failed his driving test because he could not parallel park.
= 他驾驶考试没过，因为不会侧方停车。
parallel park = 侧方停车
parallel parking = 侧方停车
⑮ The shop sells parallel imports of cameras at well below the official price.
= 那家店卖平行进口的相机，价钱比官方价低得多。
parallel imports = 平行进口货
⑯ The navigator drew the course on the chart with a parallel ruler.
= 领航员用平行尺在海图上画出了航线。
a parallel ruler = 平行尺
⑰ By the end of the week she could make a neat parallel turn on the steep slope.
= 到周末，她已经能在陡坡上做出漂亮的双板平行转弯了。
a parallel turn = 双板平行转弯

parallelism
/ˈpærəlelɪzəm/
核心：两支。一是（正式）相似、对应：两件事在某些方面平行相当，不可数或可数；
二是（修辞、语法）排比、对偶：接连几句用同样的结构，不可数。
① There is a striking parallelism between the two careers.
= 两人的经历有惊人的相似。
a parallelism between A and B = 甲乙之间的相似
② The speech gets its rhythm from parallelism, with every sentence built in the same way.
= 那篇演讲的节奏来自排比，每句话的结构都一样。
parallelism = 排比

parallelogram
/ˌpærəˈleləɡræm/
核心：（几何）平行四边形：两组对边分别平行而且相等的四边形。可数。
① The pupils were asked to work out the area of a parallelogram.
= 老师让学生算一个平行四边形的面积。
a parallelogram = 平行四边形
② The sun through the window made a bright parallelogram on the kitchen floor.
= 阳光从窗户照进来，在厨房地板上投下一块明亮的平行四边形。
a bright parallelogram = 明亮的平行四边形
