derrick
核心：两支。一是钻井架：油井上那座高塔形的钢架；
二是船上或码头上的吊杆式起重装置。可数。
名字来自十七世纪伦敦一位刽子手的姓，最早指绞架。
① The derrick was visible from the coast road for the whole of that summer.
= 那个夏天，从海边公路一路都看得见那座钻井架。
a derrick = 钻井架
② Two men worked the derrick and swung the crates onto the quay.
= 两个人操作吊杆，把板条箱吊到码头上。
a derrick = 吊杆式起重装置
构词：源自十七世纪伦敦刽子手 Derrick 的姓
注意用法：石油业里几乎专指井架。

derringer
核心：袖珍手枪：枪管短、能藏在口袋或袖子里的小型手枪，
十九世纪美国最有名。可数，来自制造者的姓。
① The museum label says the derringer was found under a floorboard in 1911.
= 博物馆的说明牌上写着，那把袖珍手枪是一九一一年在地板下发现的。
a derringer = 袖珍手枪
② A derringer holds one or two rounds and is useless past a few paces.
= 袖珍手枪只装一两发子弹，隔几步远就没什么用。
carry a derringer = 带一把袖珍手枪
构词：制造者 Henry Deringer 的姓，进英语后多写两个 r
注意语域：历史与西部题材里常见。

dervish
核心：苏非派的托钵修士：以旋转舞蹈作为修行方式的那一支最有名。
可数。英语里最常用的其实是比喻说法 like a whirling dervish，
形容人转个不停、忙得团团转。
① The ceremony lasted an hour and the dervishes never once broke step.
= 那场仪式持续了一个钟头，修士们一次也没乱过步子。
a dervish = 苏非派托钵修士
② She went round the kitchen like a whirling dervish and finished in twenty minutes.
= 她在厨房里团团转，二十分钟就干完了。
like a whirling dervish = 像陀螺一样转个不停
构词：波斯语 darvīsh 贫者
注意用法：比喻那一支在日常英语里比本义常见。

des res
核心：（英式口语，戏谑）好房子：房产广告里 desirable residence 的截短，
拿来调侃中介的用词。可数，两个词分写。
① The advertisement called a two-room flat above a chip shop a des res.
= 广告把炸鱼薯条店楼上的两居室称作「理想居所」。
a des res = 理想居所（戏谑）
② Anything with a garden gets called a des res round here.
= 在这一带，只要带个院子就会被说成好房子。
be called a des res = 被称作理想居所
构词：desirable residence 的截短
注意语域：英式口语与调侃，正式文字不用。

desalinate
核心：海水淡化：把海水里的盐除掉，变成能用的淡水。及物动词，
名词 desalination，淡化厂叫 a desalination plant。
① The island desalinates most of its drinking water and pays for it in electricity.
= 那座岛的饮用水大半靠海水淡化，代价是电费。
desalinate something = 给某物脱盐
② A desalination plant takes four years to build and one drought to justify.
= 一座海水淡化厂要建四年，而一场旱灾就足以让它显得值。
a desalination plant = 海水淡化厂
构词：de- 去掉 加 saline 含盐的
注意用法：也拼作 desalinize，但 desalinate 更常见。

descale
核心：除水垢：把水壶、锅炉、管道里的钙垢清掉。及物动词，英式常用。
① Descale the kettle every couple of months or the element gives up.
= 每两个月给水壶除一次垢，不然加热管就废了。
descale something = 给某物除垢
② The boiler was descaled twice a year and still lasted only nine.
= 那台锅炉一年除两次垢，也才用了九年。
be descaled = 被除垢
构词：de- 去掉 加 scale 水垢
注意别混：scale 在这里是水垢，不是鳞片，也不是刻度。

descant
核心：（音乐）高音助唱声部：在主旋律之上另加一条更高的旋律，
唱诗班在最后一段常这么处理。可数。
也作动词，引申着说「就某事喋喋不休地发议论」（descant on something），偏旧。
① The choir sang a descant on the last verse and the congregation stopped singing to listen.
= 唱诗班在最后一段加了高音助唱，会众都停下来听。
a descant = 高音助唱声部
② He descanted on the subject of parking for the whole of the meeting.
= 整场会上他都在就停车问题喋喋不休。
descant on something = 就某事长篇大论
③ A descant recorder is the one every school buys and nobody practises.
= 高音竖笛是每所学校都买、却没人练的那一种。
a descant recorder = 高音竖笛
竖笛家族里音最高的那把，也是学校音乐课的标配。
构词：中世纪拉丁语 discantus 另一条唱腔
注意用法：音乐义常见；发议论那一支偏旧、带讥诮。

descend
核心：三支。一是（正式）下降、走下去（descend the stairs）；
二是 be descended from somebody 是「是某人的后代」，
后代叫 a descendant，名词 descent 兼指「下降」与「血统」；
三是 descend on somewhere 指一群人突然涌到某处。
① They descended the last stretch in the dark and reached the hut at midnight.
= 最后一段路他们是摸黑下来的，午夜才到小屋。
descend something = 走下、下降
② She is descended from a family of clockmakers on her mother's side.
= 她母亲那边的祖上是做钟表的。
be descended from somebody = 是某人的后代
③ Two hundred relatives descended on the village for the weekend.
= 两百个亲戚周末涌进了那个村子。
descend on somewhere = 一拥而至某处
④ The plane began its descent forty minutes early and nobody explained why.
= 飞机提前四十分钟开始下降，没人解释原因。
a descent = 下降
⑤ Her descendants still farm the same two fields.
= 她的后代至今还种着那两块地。
a descendant = 后代
⑥ He is of Irish descent and has never been further west than Bristol.
= 他有爱尔兰血统，却从没往西走过布里斯托。
be of Irish descent = 有爱尔兰血统
⑦ The debate descended into shouting, descended to name-calling, and finally descended upon the caretaker.
= 辩论沦为叫嚷，降格成互相辱骂，最后把火烧到了看门人身上。
descend into something = 沦为某种糟糕局面
descend to something = 降格去做某事
descend upon somebody = 突然涌向某人
三个介词三个意思：into 说局面变坏，to 说自甘堕落，upon 说一拥而上。
构词：拉丁语 descendere 走下去
注意别混：descent 是下降与血统，dissent 是异议 —— 读音接近，别写错。

describe
核心：描述：把某人某事是什么样子说出来。及物动词，
最常用的两个结构是 describe something 与 describe somebody as something。
名词 description，形容词 descriptive 说「描述性的」（跟 prescriptive 规定性的相对），
a descriptor 是描述用的标签，describable 是「说得出来的」。
① She described the room so exactly that he recognized it from the doorway.
= 她把那个房间描述得极准，他一进门口就认了出来。
describe something = 描述某物
② The report describes him as a reliable witness and then quotes nobody else.
= 报告把他描述成可靠的证人，之后再没引用别人。
describe somebody as something = 把某人说成某种样子
③ The description on the website matched neither the size nor the colour.
= 网站上的描述跟尺寸和颜色都对不上。
a description = 描述
④ Descriptive grammar records what people say; prescriptive grammar tells them what to say.
= 描写语法记录人们怎么说，规定语法告诉人们该怎么说。
descriptive grammar = 描写语法
⑤ Each job has three descriptors and the middle one is always the vaguest.
= 每个岗位配三条描述标签，中间那条永远最含糊。
a descriptor = 描述标签
⑥ The smell was barely describable and everybody tried anyway.
= 那股味道几乎没法形容，可人人都试着形容了一番。
be describable = 说得出来的
构词：拉丁语 describere 写下来
注意用法：说成什么样子用 as，不用 like。

descry
核心：（文学，旧）远远望见：在远处或昏暗中辨认出。及物动词，
今天几乎只在旧小说与诗里读到。
① From the tower they descried a sail that took another day to arrive.
= 从塔上他们远远望见一片帆，那船又走了一天才到。
descry something = 远远望见某物
② He descried a light on the far shore and steered towards it all night.
= 他望见对岸有一点灯火，整夜朝着它掌舵。
descry a light = 望见一点灯火
构词：古法语 descrier 喊出所见
注意别混：descry 是望见，decry 是谴责 —— 只差一个字母，意思毫不相干。

desecrate
核心：亵渎：破坏圣地、坟墓、圣物，或以不敬的方式对待它们。
及物动词，多用被动。名词 desecration。
① The chapel was desecrated twice in one winter and the door was bricked up.
= 那座小教堂一个冬天被亵渎了两回，门后来被砌死了。
be desecrated = 被亵渎
② The desecration of the graves united a village that agreed on nothing else.
= 墓地被毁一事，让一个在别的事上从不同心的村子团结了起来。
the desecration of something = 对某物的亵渎
构词：de- 反向 加 consecrate 祝圣
注意别混：desecrate 是亵渎圣物，deconsecrate 是正式撤除宗教用途。

desegregate
核心：取消种族隔离：把按种族分开的学校、场所重新合到一起。
及物动词，多用被动。名词 desegregation，多说美国五六十年代那段历史。
① The schools were desegregated by court order and by very little else.
= 那些学校是靠法院命令取消隔离的，别的力量几乎没起作用。
be desegregated = 被取消种族隔离
② Desegregation on paper and desegregation in practice were twenty years apart.
= 纸面上的取消隔离和实际上的取消隔离，相隔了二十年。
desegregation = 取消种族隔离
构词：de- 反向 加 segregate 隔离
注意用法：多用于美国民权运动的叙述。

deselect
核心：两支。一是英式政治用语：政党把现任候选人从下届提名中拿掉；
二是一般的「取消选中」——在界面上把勾去掉。及物动词，名词 deselection。
① He was deselected by his own local party three months before the election.
= 选举前三个月，他被本地党组织拿掉了候选资格。
be deselected = 被取消候选资格
② Deselect the second option before you save, or it prints in colour.
= 保存前把第二项的勾去掉，不然会彩印。
deselect something = 取消选中某项
③ The deselection of two sitting members made the national news.
= 两名现任议员被取消候选资格，上了全国新闻。
deselection = 取消候选资格
构词：de- 反向 加 select 选中
注意语域：政治那一支是英式用语，美式说 primary somebody out。

desensitize
核心：使不再敏感。两支：一是心理上「看多了就麻木」
（desensitize somebody to violence）；二是医学上的脱敏治疗，
让身体对过敏原不再过度反应。及物动词，英式也拼 desensitise，
名词 desensitization。
① Years of the same footage desensitize an audience to almost anything.
= 同样的画面看上几年，观众对几乎什么都麻木了。
desensitize somebody to something = 使某人对某事麻木
② The course of injections desensitized him to bee stings over two summers.
= 那个疗程用两个夏天让他对蜂蜇脱了敏。
desensitize somebody = 给某人脱敏
③ Desensitization takes months and has to be finished before the pollen season.
= 脱敏治疗要好几个月，必须在花粉季之前做完。
desensitization = 脱敏
构词：de- 去掉 加 sensitize 使敏感
注意用法：心理义带贬义，医学义中性。

desert
核心：三支，重音不同。名词 a desert 是沙漠，重音在前；
动词 desert 是抛弃、擅离职守（desert your post），重音在后，
逃兵是 a deserter，名词 desertion。
形容词 deserted 说地方「空无一人」。另有复数 deserts 只活在
get your just deserts（罪有应得）里。土地沙漠化叫 desertification。
① The desert gets colder at night than most people expect.
= 沙漠夜里比多数人想的还冷。
a desert = 沙漠
② He deserted his post twice and was court-martialled the second time.
= 他两次擅离职守，第二次被送上军事法庭。
desert your post = 擅离职守
③ The village was deserted and every door had been left standing open.
= 那个村子空无一人，每一扇门都敞着。
be deserted = 空无一人的
④ Two deserters were caught at the border and sent back the same week.
= 两名逃兵在边境被抓，当周就被送了回去。
a deserter = 逃兵、背弃者
⑤ Desertion rates rose in the third winter and the records stop after that.
= 第三个冬天逃亡率上升，记录到那里就断了。
desertion = 逃亡、遗弃
⑥ He got his just deserts and nobody in the village pretended otherwise.
= 他罪有应得，村里没人装作不是这么回事。
get your just deserts = 罪有应得
⑦ Desertification has taken two hundred metres of grazing land a year.
= 沙漠化每年吞掉两百米的牧地。
desertification = 沙漠化
⑧ They spent a fortnight on what the brochure called a desert island and wore desert boots the whole time.
= 他们在宣传册所谓的荒岛上待了两周，全程穿着沙漠靴。
a desert island = 荒岛
desert boots = 沙漠靴（麂皮系带短靴）
desert island 常出现在「只能带三样东西」那类游戏里。
构词：拉丁语 desertus 被弃的 —— 沙漠就是「被弃之地」
注意别混：desert（沙漠）与 dessert（甜点）只差一个 s，重音也不同。

deserve
核心：应得：好事坏事都能说。及物动词，不用进行时。
常说 deserve something、deserve to do something。
形容词 deserved 说「应得的」（a deserved win），副词 deservedly；
deserving 说人「值得帮的」（a deserving cause）。
① They deserve a proper explanation and have been given three different ones.
= 他们该得到一个像样的解释，可拿到的是三个不同的说法。
deserve something = 应得某物
② She deserves to be told before the announcement goes out.
= 消息发出去之前，理应先告诉她。
deserve to do something = 理应……
③ It was a deserved win and even the other manager said so afterwards.
= 那场胜利实至名归，连对方主教练赛后都这么说。
a deserved win = 实至名归的胜利
④ He was deservedly first on a list that had taken a year to compile.
= 那份名单编了一年，他排在第一位是当之无愧的。
deservedly = 理所应当地
⑤ The fund exists for deserving cases and turns down two in three.
= 那笔基金是为真正需要帮助的人设的，三份申请里驳回两份。
a deserving case = 值得帮助的个案
构词：拉丁语 deservire 尽心服务
注意用法：不用进行时；后面接不定式，不接动名词。

déshabillé
核心：（法语借词，旧）衣衫不整的样子：多指室内穿着随便、没有正式着装，
在旧小说里带暧昧意味。不可数，常说 in déshabillé，也拼作 deshabille。
① She received the visitor in déshabillé and the neighbours talked for a month.
= 她穿着家常便衣就见了客，邻居议论了一个月。
in déshabillé = 衣衫随便地
② The portrait shows her in a studied deshabille that took hours to arrange.
= 那幅肖像画上她是一副精心安排的慵懒装束，摆弄了好几个钟头。
a studied deshabille = 刻意做出的慵懒装束
构词：法语 déshabiller 脱衣
注意语域：旧词、文学词，今天多带戏谑。

desiccated
核心：干燥脱水的：一是食品脱去水分（desiccated coconut 椰蓉）；
二是比喻的「干巴巴、没有生气的」，说文章、学问、人。
名词 desiccation 指脱水这件事，动词 desiccate 少用。
① The recipe wants desiccated coconut, not the fresh kind.
= 那个方子要的是椰蓉，不是新鲜椰肉。
desiccated coconut = 椰蓉
② His prose is accurate, desiccated and impossible to read for long.
= 他的文字准确、干巴，读久了受不了。
desiccated prose = 干巴巴的文字
③ Desiccation of the topsoil began within two seasons of the trees coming down.
= 树一砍倒，表土两季之内就开始干化。
desiccation = 干化、脱水
构词：拉丁语 desiccare 弄干
注意用法：食品那一支最常见，比喻义偏文学批评。

desideratum
核心：（正式）所欲之物、必备条件：一件事情理想上应当具备的东西。
拉丁语借词，复数 desiderata。学术与政策文字里用。
① Transparency is the one desideratum every draft mentions and none defines.
= 透明是每份草案都提、却没有一份界定的必备条件。
a desideratum = 必备条件、所欲之物
② The desiderata were listed on one page and contradicted each other twice.
= 那些理想条件列在一页纸上，自相矛盾了两处。
desiderata = 理想条件（复数）
构词：拉丁语「被期望的东西」
注意用法：复数不规则，写作 desiderata。

design
核心：三支。一是设计（名词与动词都常用）；二是图案花样（a floral design）；
三是「意图」——by design 是「有意为之」，跟 by accident 相对；
have designs on something 是「打某样东西的主意」，带贬义。
做设计的人是 a designer，也作定语说 designer clothes（名牌服装）。
① The design was changed three times and the foundations twice.
= 设计改了三次，地基改了两次。
a design = 设计（方案）
② She designed the whole system on paper before anybody wrote a line of code.
= 她在纸上把整套系统设计完，之后才有人写第一行代码。
design something = 设计某物
③ The plates carry a floral design that nobody has been able to date.
= 那些盘子上有一种花卉纹样，谁也断不出年代。
a floral design = 花卉纹样
④ The gap was left by design and the architect explained it twice.
= 那道缝是有意留的，建筑师解释过两遍。
by design = 有意为之
⑤ He has designs on the corner office and makes no secret of it.
= 他打着那间角落办公室的主意，也不避讳。
have designs on something = 打某物的主意
⑥ The designer left after the first season and the label never recovered.
= 那位设计师第一季之后就走了，那个牌子再没缓过来。
a designer = 设计师
构词：拉丁语 designare 标出
注意用法：by design 与 by accident 成对；designer 作定语指名牌货。

designate
核心：两支，读音不同。动词是「指定、划定」（designate an area、
designate somebody as something）；形容词后置，指「已获任命、尚未就职的」，
写作 the director designate。名词 designation 是名称或职称。
① The whole valley was designated a conservation area in the seventies.
= 整条山谷七十年代被划为保护区。
designate something as something = 把某物指定为……
② The chairman designate attended every meeting and voted at none of them.
= 候任主席出席了每一场会议，一次也没投票。
the chairman designate = 候任主席
③ Her designation changed three times and her actual job never did.
= 她的职称改了三次，实际干的活儿一次也没变。
a designation = 名称、职称
构词：拉丁语 designare 标出
注意用法：形容词那一支放在名词后面，不加连字符。

desirable
核心：两支。一是值得拥有、令人向往的（a desirable job）；
二是（正式）「可取的、宜于……的」（it is desirable that…）。
名词 desirability。房产广告里的 a desirable residence 被英国人缩成 des res 打趣。
① Working from home was desirable until everybody had to do it.
= 在家办公本来是件让人羡慕的事，直到人人都被迫如此。
be desirable = 令人向往的
② It is desirable that both parties sign before the end of the month.
= 双方最好在月底之前签字。
it is desirable that… = ……是可取的
③ The desirability of the site had more to do with the train than the view.
= 那块地之所以抢手，靠的是火车而不是风景。
the desirability of something = 某物的可取之处
构词：desire 加 -able
注意用法：正式建议句式用 it is desirable that，后面跟原形动词。

desire
核心：名词与动词都偏正式。名词是强烈的愿望（a desire to do something），
也指情欲；动词是「渴望」，比 want 正式得多，日常少用。
形容词 desirous（正式，渴望的）几乎只在公文里出现。
习语 leave a lot to be desired 是「差得远」，是这一族最常听到的说法。
① She had no desire to explain herself twice in the same evening.
= 同一个晚上把自己解释两遍，她一点也不想。
a desire to do something = 做某事的愿望
② The service leaves a lot to be desired and the prices do not.
= 那儿的服务差得远，价钱倒一点不含糊。
leave a lot to be desired = 差得远、很不理想
③ The board is desirous of a settlement before the annual meeting.
= 董事会希望在年会之前达成和解。
be desirous of something = 渴求某事（公文用语）
构词：拉丁语 desiderare 期望
注意语域：动词偏正式，日常说 want；desirous 只见于公文。

desist
核心：（正式）停止、罢手：法律文书里的用词，
最常见的搭配是 cease and desist（停止并不得再犯）。
不及物，后面接 from something。
① The letter told him to desist from contacting any member of staff.
= 那封信要求他停止联系任何一名员工。
desist from something = 停止做某事
② A cease and desist order arrived nine months after the first complaint.
= 第一次投诉之后九个月，一纸停止侵害令送到了。
a cease and desist order = 停止侵害令
构词：拉丁语 desistere 站住不动
注意语域：法律与正式文书用语；日常说 stop。

desk
核心：书桌、办公桌。可数。几个搭配要记：a desk job 是坐办公室的工作，
the front desk 是前台，the news desk 是新闻编辑部的某个部门。
同桌叫 a deskmate；桌面与电脑桌面都叫 the desktop。
① He has had a desk job for eleven years and misses none of the driving.
= 他坐了十一年办公室，一点也不想念开车那份活。
a desk job = 坐办公室的工作
② Leave the key at the front desk if you go out before seven.
= 七点前出门的话，把钥匙留在前台。
the front desk = 前台
③ His deskmate copied everything and got two marks more.
= 他同桌什么都抄，分数还比他高两分。
a deskmate = 同桌
④ The desktop is covered in files he has been meaning to sort since March.
= 桌面上堆满了文件，他从三月起就打算整理。
the desktop = 桌面（也指电脑桌面）
⑤ He sits at a desk for nine hours a day and walks the dog twice to make up for it.
= 他一天在办公桌前坐九个钟头，靠遛两回狗来补救。
sit at a desk = 坐在办公桌前
介词固定用 at；说把东西放桌上才用 on the desk。
构词：中世纪拉丁语 desca 桌子
注意用法：说岗位用 a desk job；说电脑桌面也是 desktop。
