mush
/mʌʃ/
核心：三支。一是糊状物、烂糊，不可数或单数，多含嫌弃；二是（贬）滥情、腻人的话或作品；
三是（美）玉米粥。
① The vegetables had been boiled for so long that they turned to mush.
= 那些蔬菜煮得太久，都成了一摊烂糊。
turn to mush = 变成烂糊
② After nine hours of meetings his brain felt like mush.
= 开了九个小时的会，他觉得脑子成了一团浆糊。
feel like mush = 像一团浆糊
③ The film starts well and then dissolves into sentimental mush.
= 那部片子开头不错，后来就化成了一摊滥情的东西。
sentimental mush = 滥情的东西
④ The children ate cornmeal mush with syrup before the long walk to school.
= 孩子们吃了浇糖浆的玉米粥，再走远路去上学。
cornmeal mush = 玉米粥
这一支是美式说法。

musher
/ˈmʌʃə(r)/
核心：两支。一是赶狗拉雪橇的人；二是特指参加长途狗拉雪橇比赛的选手。可数。
① The musher checked every dog's paws before the team set off across the lake.
= 赶橇人把每条狗的爪子都查了一遍，狗队才出发过湖。
a musher = 赶狗拉雪橇的人
② Sixty mushers started the race and fewer than forty reached the coast.
= 六十名雪橇手出发比赛，到达海岸的不足四十人。
mushers in a race = 参赛的雪橇手

mushroom
/ˈmʌʃrʊm/
核心：名词是蘑菇，可数。动词有两支：一是像雨后蘑菇那样迅速增多、扩大，常带「快得出乎意料」的意思；
二是 go mushrooming，去采蘑菇。
① She fried a handful of sliced mushrooms in butter and piled them on toast.
= 她用黄油煎了一把切片的蘑菇，堆在烤面包上。
sliced mushrooms = 切片的蘑菇
② Never eat a wild mushroom unless an expert has identified it for you.
= 野生蘑菇除非有行家替你认过，否则千万别吃。
a wild mushroom = 野生蘑菇
③ The number of coffee shops in the town has mushroomed in five years.
= 五年里，镇上咖啡馆的数量猛增。
mushroom = 迅速增多
④ We used to go mushrooming in the woods early on autumn mornings.
= 我们从前常在秋天的清早去树林里采蘑菇。
go mushrooming = 去采蘑菇
⑤ The photograph shows a mushroom cloud rising over the test site in the desert.
= 照片上，沙漠里的试验场上空升起一朵蘑菇云。
a mushroom cloud = 蘑菇云

mushy

music
/ˈmjuːzɪk/
核心：音乐。不可数：一首曲子说 a piece of music，不说 a music。
另一支是乐谱：read music 是识谱，sheet music 是散页乐谱。
习语 face the music 是硬着头皮承担后果；music to your ears 是正想听到的好消息；set something to music 是给诗文谱曲。
① She puts on some music as soon as she gets home from work.
= 她下班一到家就放点音乐。
put on some music = 放点音乐
② The orchestra opened with a piece of music that nobody in the hall recognised.
= 乐团开场奏了一首曲子，大厅里没人听出是什么。
a piece of music = 一首曲子
量词用 piece。
③ He has loved classical music since an uncle took him to a concert at nine.
= 自从九岁那年舅舅带他去听了场音乐会，他就爱上了古典音乐。
classical music = 古典音乐
pop music = 流行音乐
④ The café plays background music so quietly that customers have to strain to hear it.
= 那家咖啡馆的背景音乐放得很轻，客人得竖起耳朵才听得见。
background music = 背景音乐
⑤ She studied music at college and now teaches the violin to beginners.
= 她大学念的是音乐，如今教初学者拉小提琴。
study music = 学音乐
⑥ He plays the guitar well by ear but has never learned to read music.
= 他凭耳朵把吉他弹得很好，却从没学过识谱。
read music = 识谱
这里指乐谱。
⑦ The pianist turned over two pages of her music at once and had to stop.
= 那位钢琴手一下翻过去两页乐谱，只好停下来。
somebody's music = 某人的乐谱
sheet music = 散页乐谱
⑧ The poem was set to music by a composer who had never met its author.
= 那首诗由一位从未见过作者的作曲家谱了曲。
set to music = 谱上曲
set something to music = 给某物谱曲
⑨ He broke the window, hid for an hour, and then went home to face the music.
= 他打碎了窗户，躲了一个钟头，然后回家硬着头皮挨训。
face the music = 硬着头皮承担后果
⑩ After a month of delays, the news that the parts had arrived was music to her ears.
= 耽搁了一个月，零件到货的消息在她听来真是悦耳。
music to your ears = 正想听到的好消息
music to somebody's ears = 某人听着悦耳的话
⑪ The child wound up the music box and watched the tiny dancer turn.
= 那孩子给八音盒上了发条，看着小小的舞者转圈。
a music box = 八音盒
⑫ Her grandfather sang comic songs in the music halls before the war.
= 战前，她祖父在歌舞杂耍剧场里唱滑稽歌。
a music hall = 歌舞杂耍剧场
⑬ The wind blew the pages off the music stand in the middle of the solo.
= 独奏到一半，风把谱架上的乐谱吹掉了。
a music stand = 谱架
⑭ The band spent more on the music video than on recording the song.
= 那支乐队拍音乐短片花的钱，比录那首歌还多。
a music video = 音乐短片
⑮ My parents met at a rock music festival in the summer of 1985.
= 我父母是在 1985 年夏天的一个摇滚音乐节上认识的。
rock music = 摇滚乐

musical
/ˈmjuːzɪkəl/
核心：形容词三支。一是音乐的、与音乐有关的，只作定语；二是有音乐天赋的、爱好音乐的；三是（声音）悦耳的。
名词是音乐剧，可数。
副词 musically 与名词 musicality（乐感）并入本条。
① The school lends musical instruments to pupils who cannot afford to buy one.
= 学校把乐器借给买不起的学生。
a musical instrument = 乐器
② Her musical career began in a church choir at the age of seven.
= 她的音乐生涯，是七岁那年从教堂唱诗班开始的。
a musical career = 音乐生涯
③ Neither parent is musical, yet all three children play in the county orchestra.
= 父母都没有音乐细胞，三个孩子却都在郡乐团里演奏。
be musical = 有音乐天赋
这一支可以作表语。
④ She has a low musical voice that makes even a shopping list sound pleasant.
= 她嗓音低沉悦耳，连念购物清单都好听。
a musical voice = 悦耳的嗓音
⑤ The novel was turned into a musical that ran in London for eleven years.
= 那部小说改编成了音乐剧，在伦敦连演十一年。
a musical = 音乐剧
⑥ The theatre reopened with a musical comedy from the nineteen-thirties.
= 那家剧院重新开张，上演的是一出三十年代的音乐喜剧。
a musical comedy = 音乐喜剧
⑦ The children are musically gifted but have no patience for daily practice.
= 那几个孩子有音乐天分，却没耐心天天练习。
musically gifted = 有音乐天分的
⑧ The judges praised the musicality of her playing more than its accuracy.

musicality = 乐感
⑨ A musical box on the shelf played the same eight notes every time the lid was lifted.

⑩ The party ended with musical bumps, and the smallest guest won the prize.

musical chairs = 走马灯式的人事对调

⑬ The musical director rehearsed the chorus for six weeks before opening night.
= 首演之前，音乐总监带着合唱队排了六个星期。
a musical director = 音乐总监
⑭ An old man outside the station plays hymns on a musical saw with a violin bow.
= 车站外有位老人，用小提琴弓在锯琴上拉赞美诗。

must
/mʌst/
核心：情态动词，后面接不带 to 的动词原形，没有人称变化，也没有过去式。弱读时元音含糊，强调时才读足。
两条主线。一是「必须」：说话人自己认定非做不可；否定 must not（缩写 mustn't）是「不许」，不是「不必」——「不必」要说 do not have to。
过去的「必须」借 had to 来说。
二是推断「一定是」：must be 推断现在，must have done 推断过去；这一支的否定用 cannot，不用 must not。
口语里还用来热情地劝人、邀人：You must come and see us。
I must say、I must admit 用来加重语气；if you must 是勉强答应。
名词 a must 指非有不可、非看不可的东西；must-have、must-see、must-read 是同一路的复合词。
① You must wear a helmet whenever you ride on the main road.
= 只要在大路上骑车，你就必须戴头盔。
must do something = 必须做某事
② I must remember to post this letter before the collection at five.
= 我得记着赶在五点收信之前把这封信寄掉。
I must remember to do something = 我得记着做某事
说话人给自己下的命令。
③ All visitors must report to reception and sign the book.
= 所有来访者必须到前台登记签名。
visitors must do something = 来访者必须…
告示、规章里的用法。
④ You must not touch the wires until the power has been switched off.
= 电源关掉之前，你绝对不许碰那些电线。
must not do something = 不许做某事
mustn't = must not 的缩写
是禁止，不是「不必」。
⑤ You have been driving since dawn, so you must be exhausted.
= 你从天亮一直开到现在，一定累坏了。
must be = 一定是
这里是推断，不是命令。
⑥ There must be some mistake, because we paid the bill in March.
= 一定是哪里弄错了，因为那笔账我们三月就付了。
there must be = 一定有
⑦ The ground is wet, so it must have rained during the night.
= 地是湿的，夜里一定下过雨。
must have done = 当时一定…
推断过去的事，后面接完成式。
⑧ She must have been waiting for an hour by the time the bus arrived.
= 等公共汽车到的时候，她一定已经等了一个钟头。
must have been doing = 当时一定一直在…
⑨ You must come and stay with us when the house is finished.
= 等房子弄好了，你一定要来我们这儿住几天。
you must come = 你一定要来
热情的邀请，不是命令。
⑩ You must try the soup here, because nobody in town makes it better.
= 你一定得尝尝这儿的汤，全镇没有谁做得更好。
you must try something = 你一定得试试
⑪ I must say, the hotel was far better than the price suggested.
= 说实在的，那家旅馆比它的价钱看上去要好得多。
I must say = 说实在的
I must admit = 我得承认
⑫ Take the car if you must, but be back before dark.
= 你非要开车就开吧，不过天黑前回来。
if you must = 你非要这样的话
勉强答应，带点不情愿。
⑬ Must you whistle while I am trying to add up these figures?
= 我正算这些数呢，你非得吹口哨不可吗？
must you do something? = 你非得…不可吗？
用疑问句表示不耐烦。
⑭ Good walking boots are a must if you plan to cross the moor in winter.
= 你要是打算冬天穿过那片荒原，一双好的徒步靴少不了。
a must = 必不可少的东西
这里是名词。
⑮ The shop sells this year's must-have toy and ran out by ten o'clock.
= 那家店卖今年人人必买的那款玩具，十点钟就卖光了。
a must-have = 人人必备的东西
must-have = 必备的
⑯ The castle is a must-see for anyone spending more than a day in the city.
= 在这座城市待一天以上的人，那座城堡非看不可。
a must-see = 非看不可的东西
a must-read = 非读不可的书

mustachioed

mustachioed generals = 蓄着大胡子的将军

mustang
/ˈmʌstæŋ/

① A herd of mustangs galloped along the ridge and vanished into the dust.
= 一群野马沿着山脊飞奔，消失在尘土里。

② It took the rancher a whole summer to break the mustang he had caught.

break a mustang = 驯服一匹野马

mustard
/ˈmʌstəd/
核心：四支。一是芥末酱：黄色或褐色的辛辣调料，不可数；二是芥菜这种植物，种子用来做芥末；
三是芥末黄这种颜色；四是习语。cut the mustard 是够格、达到要求，多用于否定；keen as mustard 是（英式）劲头十足。
① He spread a thin layer of mustard on the ham before closing the sandwich.
= 他在火腿上抹了薄薄一层芥末，才把三明治合上。
a layer of mustard = 一层芥末
English mustard = 英式芥末（很辣的那种）
② Fields of mustard turn the whole valley yellow at the end of April.
= 四月底，一片片芥菜地把整条山谷染成黄色。
a field of mustard = 一片芥菜地
mustard seed = 芥菜籽
③ She wore a mustard scarf that nobody else could have carried off.
= 她围了一条芥末黄的围巾，换了别人谁也驾驭不了。
a mustard scarf = 芥末黄的围巾
④ He was a fine player once, but at forty he can no longer cut the mustard.
= 他从前是个好球员，可到了四十岁已经不够格了。
cut the mustard = 够格、达到要求
多用在否定句里。
⑤ The new assistant is keen as mustard and arrives before the doors are unlocked.
= 新来的助手劲头十足，门还没开锁人就到了。
keen as mustard = 劲头十足

⑥ Thousands of soldiers were blinded by mustard gas in the trenches.

mustard gas = 芥子气

mustard greens = 芥菜叶

muster
/ˈmʌstə(r)/
核心：动词两支。一是鼓起、攒足（勇气、力气、支持），常说 muster up，多与 can、could 连用；二是召集、集合（士兵、船员）。
名词是集合、点名。习语 pass muster 是合格、过得了关。
① She mustered all her courage and knocked on the head teacher's door.
= 她鼓足全部勇气，敲了校长的门。

② He answered with as much dignity as he could muster in a borrowed dressing gown.

= 那个星期的第四场会，我实在提不起多大劲头。
muster up enthusiasm = 提起劲头
muster up = 鼓起、攒足
④ The opposition failed to muster enough votes to block the bill.
= 反对党没能凑够票数拦下那项法案。
muster votes = 凑票数
muster support = 争取支持
⑤ The captain mustered the crew on deck at first light.
= 天一亮，船长就把船员召集到甲板上。

⑥ The morning muster showed that two men had slipped away during the night.
= 早上一点名，发现有两个人夜里溜走了。
a muster = 集合、点名
⑦ The repairs were rough, but they would pass muster until spring.

pass muster = 合格、过得了关

mutable
