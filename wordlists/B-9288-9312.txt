foresee
核心：三支。一是预见、料到：foresee a problem；二是预计将会发生；
三是多用于否定：could not have foreseen something。
注意：三态是 foresee / foresaw / foreseen。
① Nobody foresaw the problem and three people had written it down.
= 没人预见到这个问题，倒有三个人把它写下来过。
foresee a problem = 预见到问题
② She foresees a shortage by March and has bought nothing extra.
= 她预计三月前会短缺，却一点没多买。
foresee a shortage = 预计会短缺
③ They could not have foreseen a week of rain in that month.
= 那个月份下一整周的雨，他们无从预料。
could not have foreseen something = 无从预料某事

foreseeable
核心：三支。一是可预见的：the foreseeable future；
二是（结果）本该料到的；三是法律上「可合理预见」的。
① Nothing changes in the foreseeable future and everybody plans as if it will.
= 可预见的将来什么都不会变，人人却照着会变来打算。
the foreseeable future = 可预见的将来
② The damage was foreseeable and the insurer said so in one line.
= 那损失本可预见，保险公司一句话就写明了。
be foreseeable = 可以预见
③ Only foreseeable losses are recoverable under that clause.
= 按那一条，只有可预见的损失才能索回。
foreseeable losses = 可预见的损失

foreshadow
核心：三支。一是预示、成为…的前兆：foreshadow a change；
二是（文学）为后文埋下伏笔；三是（事件）提前显出苗头。
① The letter foreshadowed a change that took two more years to arrive.
= 那封信预示了一场变化，那变化又过了两年才来。
foreshadow a change = 预示变化
② The broken clock in chapter one foreshadows everything that follows.
= 第一章里那只坏钟，为后面所有事埋下了伏笔。
foreshadow something = 为某事埋伏笔
③ A quiet January foreshadowed the worst summer the trade had seen.
= 一个冷清的一月，预示了这行见过的最糟的夏天。
foreshadow a bad season = 预示糟糕的一季

foreshore
核心：三支。一是海滩上高低潮线之间那一段：walk the foreshore；
二是河岸退潮后露出的滩地；三是法律上属公共的那条滩带。
① They walk the foreshore at low tide and come back with pockets of glass.
= 退潮时他们走那段滩涂，回来时口袋里全是玻璃碎。
walk the foreshore = 走滩涂
② The foreshore is public and the field just above it is not.
= 那段滩涂是公用的，紧挨着的那块地不是。
the foreshore is public = 滩涂属公用
③ Two boats sit on the foreshore from October until Easter.
= 从十月到复活节，两条船一直搁在滩涂上。
sit on the foreshore = 搁在滩涂上

foreshorten
核心：三支（绘画、摄影）。一是把远处物体画短、作透视压缩：a foreshortened arm；
二是（时间）显得缩短：a foreshortened season；三是使景物显得比实际近。
① The arm is foreshortened and the hand ends up bigger than the head.
= 那条手臂做了透视压缩，手最后比头还大。
a foreshortened arm = 透视压缩的手臂
② A foreshortened season gave every team four games fewer.
= 缩短的赛季让每支队少打四场。
a foreshortened season = 缩短的赛季
③ The long lens foreshortens the hill and flatters the climb.
= 长焦把山压扁了，把那段爬坡拍得轻松。
foreshorten a view = 把景深压扁

foresight
核心：三支。一是先见之明、远见：have the foresight to do something；
二是事先的谋划；三是（枪械）准星。
① She had the foresight to book two rooms and nobody thanked her.
= 她有先见之明订了两间房，没人谢她。
have the foresight to do something = 有先见之明做某事
② A little foresight in March would have saved the whole of August.
= 三月里稍有点谋划，整个八月就都省下了。
a little foresight = 一点谋划
③ He lined the foresight up on the target and then put the rifle down.
= 他把准星对准靶子，又把枪放下了。
line up the foresight = 对准准星

foreskin
核心：三支（解剖学）。一是包皮；二是护理、清洗时说的那个部位；
三是包皮环切手术里切除的那部分。
① The foreskin retracts on its own and usually needs no help at all.
= 包皮会自行退缩，通常根本不用管。
the foreskin retracts = 包皮退缩
② The leaflet explains how to clean under the foreskin in four lines.
= 那张单页用四行讲清了包皮下面怎么清洗。
clean under the foreskin = 清洗包皮下方
③ The operation removes the foreskin and takes about twenty minutes.
= 那个手术切除包皮，大约二十分钟。
remove the foreskin = 切除包皮

forest
核心：四支。一是森林：a pine forest；二是（英，旧）供狩猎的王家林地；
三是比喻，密密麻麻的一片：a forest of masts；四是动词，植树造林。
① A pine forest starts at the gate and runs for eleven miles.
= 一片松林从门口开始，绵延十一英里。
a pine forest = 松林
② The whole hill was royal forest and one village was cleared for it.
= 整座山曾是王家猎林，为此清掉了一个村子。
royal forest = 王家猎林
③ A forest of masts filled the harbour before the fleet went out.
= 船队出海前，港里满是密密麻麻的桅杆。
a forest of masts = 密密麻麻的桅杆
④ They forested the slope twice and the sheep ate it both times.
= 他们在那面坡上造过两回林，两回都被羊啃光。
forest a slope = 在坡上造林

forestall
核心：三支。一是抢先一步阻止：forestall a strike；
二是抢在别人之前把话说了：forestall a question；三是（旧）囤积居奇。
① Two concessions forestalled a strike that was already printed on posters.
= 两项让步，抢先化解了一场海报都印好的罢工。
forestall a strike = 抢先化解罢工
② She forestalled the question by answering it in the first minute.
= 她在头一分钟就把那个问题答了，堵住了对方的口。
forestall a question = 抢先答掉问题
③ Merchants who forestalled the grain market were fined by name.
= 囤积粮食抬价的商人被点名罚款。
forestall a market = 囤积居奇

forested
核心：三支。一是长满树林的：a forested slope；
二是人工造过林的；三是说森林覆盖比例的：a third forested。
① The forested slope holds the soil and the bare one loses it every spring.
= 有林子的那面坡保得住土，光秃的那面每年春天都流走。
a forested slope = 长满树的坡
② Half the estate was forested in the 1950s with a single species.
= 五十年代，庄园一半的地只用一个树种造了林。
be forested = 造上林
③ The country is a third forested and cuts more than it plants.
= 那个国家三分之一的地方有森林，砍的却比种的多。
a third forested = 三分之一有林

forester
核心：三支。一是林务员、护林人：work as a forester；
二是（旧）林地的看管人；三是学林业出身的人。
① He worked as a forester for thirty years and planted one wood twice.
= 他当了三十年林务员，同一片林子种了两遍。
work as a forester = 当林务员
② The forester marks the trees and the contractor takes them down.
= 林务员在树上做记号，承包方来伐。
the forester marks the trees = 林务员给树做记号
③ She trained as a forester and ended up managing car parks.
= 她学的是林业，最后管起了停车场。
train as a forester = 学林业出身

forestry
核心：三支。一是林业：work in forestry；二是林学这门学问；
三是（英）负责造林的那个部门。
① Forestry pays badly and nobody in it says they would change jobs.
= 林业挣得少，干这行的却没人说想换工作。
work in forestry = 干林业
② She read forestry and spent two of the four years outdoors.
= 她读林学，四年里有两年在野外。
read forestry = 读林学
③ Forestry planted this whole ridge in a single winter.
= 林业部门一个冬天就把整道山脊种满了。
forestry plants something = 林业部门造林

foretaste
核心：三支。一是（好事或坏事的）先兆、预先体验：a foretaste of things to come；
二是提前尝到的那一点；三是先发出来的一份样品。
① Tuesday was a foretaste of things to come and nobody read it that way.
= 周二那天是后来一切的先兆，当时没人这么看。
a foretaste of things to come = 后事的先兆
② The cold snap gave us a foretaste of a winter that never arrived.
= 那阵寒潮让我们提前尝到一个后来并未到来的冬天。
a foretaste of something = 对某事的预先体验
③ One chapter was published as a foretaste of the whole book.
= 先发了一章，作为整本书的尝鲜。
a foretaste = 尝鲜的一份

foretell
核心：三支（正式或书面）。一是预言、预示：foretell the future；
二是（迹象）预告某事；三是多用于否定：impossible to foretell。
注意：三态是 foretell / foretold / foretold。
① Nobody can foretell the future and everybody in that room tried.
= 没人能预言未来，那屋里的人个个都试了。
foretell the future = 预言未来
② The cracks foretold the collapse by a good six months.
= 那些裂缝提前整整六个月预示了坍塌。
foretold the collapse = 预示了坍塌
③ The outcome was impossible to foretell from the first three rounds.
= 从头三个回合无从预料结果。
impossible to foretell = 无从预料

forethought
核心：三支。一是事先的考虑、周全的谋划：with a little forethought；
二是（法律）预谋的成分；三是替旁人着想的那份准备。
① With a little forethought the whole trip would have cost half as much.
= 稍作谋划，整趟行程本可以只花一半的钱。
with a little forethought = 稍作谋划
② The judge found no forethought and reduced the sentence accordingly.
= 法官认定并无预谋，据此减了刑。
find no forethought = 认定并无预谋
③ Somebody had the forethought to leave a torch by the door.
= 有人想得周到，在门边放了一支手电。
have the forethought to do something = 想得周到而做某事

forever
核心：四支。一是永远：last forever；二是（口语）好久好久：take forever；
三是老是、不停地：be forever doing something；四是分写的 for ever，用法相同。
① Nothing here lasts forever and the sign at the gate says otherwise.
= 这里没有什么能永远存在，门口的牌子却说不然。
last forever = 永远持续
② The kettle takes forever and the queue behind it never shortens.
= 那把水壶烧得没完，后面排的队一直不见短。
take forever = 花好久好久
③ He is forever losing gloves and never loses anything else.
= 他老是丢手套，别的东西一件不丢。
be forever doing something = 老是做某事
④ They said goodbye at the gate and meant it for ever this time.
= 他们在门口道别，这一回是真的永别。
for ever = 永远

forewarn
核心：三支。一是预先警告：forewarn somebody of something；
二是提前知会；三是分词 forewarned 常单独作形容词用。
① The office forewarned everyone of the noise and then started on a Sunday.
= 办公室提前通知了所有人会有噪音，最后还是挑了周日动工。
forewarn somebody of something = 预先警告某人某事
② She was forewarned by a friend and still arrived without an umbrella.
= 有朋友提前提醒过她，她还是没带伞就来了。
be forewarned = 事先得到提醒
③ Nobody forewarned the neighbours and the letters started on Monday.
= 没人事先知会邻居，投诉信周一就开始了。
forewarn the neighbours = 事先知会邻居

forewarning
核心：三支。一是预先的警告、预兆：without forewarning；
二是提前通知这件事；三是（复数）一连串征兆。
① The gate closed without forewarning and two people were still inside.
= 门毫无预警地关上，里头还有两个人。
without forewarning = 毫无预警地
② A week's forewarning would have been enough to move everything out.
= 提前一周通知，就足够把东西全搬走。
a week's forewarning = 提前一周的通知
③ There were forewarnings all spring and the file stayed shut.
= 整个春天都有征兆，那份卷宗一直没打开。
forewarnings = 一连串征兆

forewoman

forge

= 两所学校缔结的伙伴关系，比两位校长都长命。

⑤ Sales forged ahead in a year when everybody else lost money.
= 在别人都亏钱的一年里，销售却大步前进。
