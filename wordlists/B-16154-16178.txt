pain
/peɪn/
核心：名词三支。一是（身体的）疼痛：可数也可不可数：have a pain in 加部位、be in pain；
跟 ache 的分别：ache 是持续的钝痛，这个词多指较尖锐、较强的疼；二是（精神上的）痛苦，不可数；
三是（口语）讨厌的人或事，单数：a pain、a pain in the neck。
复数 pains 另有一义：辛苦、努力：take pains to do something 是费尽心思，be at pains to do something 是极力要做；

动词是（正式）使痛心：it pains me to say this。形容词 pained 是痛苦的、受了委屈似的。

= 我一爬楼梯胸口就疼。

= 手术之后她疼得厉害，睡不着。
in pain = 处于痛苦中
③ The tablets should ease the pain within half an hour.
= 那些药片半个钟头内应该能缓解疼痛。
ease the pain = 缓解疼痛
④ Nothing can take away the pain of losing a child.

the pain of something = 某事带来的痛苦
⑤ Filling in the same form three times is a real pain.
= 同一张表填三遍，真烦人。
be a pain = 真烦人
a pain in the neck = 讨厌的人或事
⑥ She took great pains to make every guest feel welcome.
= 她费尽心思，让每位客人都觉得宾至如归。
take pains = 费尽心思
take pains to do something = 费尽心思做某事
⑦ The minister was at pains to point out that nobody would lose a job.

be at pains to = 极力要
⑧ He mended the neighbour's fence and got a bill for the nails for his pains.

⑨ It pains me to say so, but the first version was better.

painter
/ˈpeɪntə(r)/
