secondary
/ˈsekəndri/
核心：形容词：次要的、第二位的：of secondary importance；（教育）中等的、中学的；（医学）继发的；次生的、间接的。副词 secondarily 是次要地、间接地。
① For him, money is of secondary importance; what matters is enjoying his work.
= 对他来说，钱是次要的，重要的是喜欢自己的工作。
of secondary importance = 次要的
secondary = 次要的
② Children in this country start secondary school at the age of eleven.
= 这个国家的孩子十一岁上中学。
secondary school = 中学
③ The new government wants to give every child in the country a good secondary education.
= 新政府希望让全国每个孩子都受到良好的中等教育。
secondary education = 中等教育
④ He went to a secondary modern in the 1960s and left school at fifteen.
= 他二十世纪六十年代上的是现代中学，十五岁就离开了学校。
a secondary modern = （英式旧时）现代中学
⑤ In our geography lesson we learned that factories that make cars belong to secondary industry.
= 地理课上我们学到，造汽车的工厂属于第二产业。

= 那项法律禁止间接纠察，所以工人只能在本厂罢工。
secondary picketing = 间接纠察
⑦ A newspaper report about a battle is a secondary source, while a soldier's diary is a primary one.
= 关于一场战役的报纸报道是二手资料，而士兵的日记是一手资料。

⑧ In the word "dictionary", the main stress is on "dic" and the secondary stress is on "na".

⑨ The doctor explained that the disease affects the lungs primarily and the heart only secondarily.
= 医生解释说，那种病主要影响肺部，对心脏的影响是次要的。
secondarily = 次要地
