self-conscious
/ˌself ˈkɒnʃəs/
核心：形容词：（在人前）不自在的、难为情的：be self-conscious about something；（文风、手法）刻意的、做作的。副词 self-consciously 是不自在地、刻意地。名词 self-consciousness 另立条。
① She felt self-conscious about her new glasses and kept taking them off in class.
= 她对新戴的眼镜感到难为情，上课时一直摘下来。
be self-conscious about something = 对某事难为情
self-conscious = 难为情的
② The new building has a self-conscious modern style that many people find cold.
= 那栋新楼有一种刻意的现代风格，许多人觉得冷冰冰的。
self-conscious = 刻意的、做作的
③ He smiled self-consciously when everyone at the party started singing "Happy Birthday" to him.
= 聚会上大家开始为他唱《生日快乐》时，他不自在地笑了。
self-consciously = 不自在地

self-consciousness
/ˌself ˈkɒnʃəsnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：难为情、局促不安；（文风的）刻意、做作。
① Her self-consciousness disappeared once she started dancing and forgot about the audience.
= 她一开始跳舞、忘了观众，局促不安就消失了。
self-consciousness = 局促不安
② The film suffers from a kind of self-consciousness, as if the director wanted everyone to notice how clever he was.
= 那部电影有一种做作感，仿佛导演想让人人都注意到他有多聪明。
a kind of self-consciousness = 一种做作感

= 那座房子分成了三套独立公寓，每套都有自己的厨房和浴室。
a self-contained flat = 独立公寓
self-contained = 设施齐全独立的
② He is a quiet, self-contained man who does not seem to need other people.
= 他是个沉默独立的人，似乎不需要别人。
a self-contained man = 不依赖别人的人
③ Each chapter of the book is self-contained, so you can read them in any order.
= 那本书的每一章都自成一体，所以可以按任何顺序读。
be self-contained = 自成一体

self-contradiction
/ˌself ˌkɒntrəˈdɪkʃn/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：自相矛盾（的话）。
① His argument is full of self-contradiction; he says taxes are too high and then asks for more spending.
= 他的论点满是自相矛盾：一边说税太高，一边又要求多花钱。
self-contradiction = 自相矛盾
② Telling people that you never lie, while lying to them every day, is a complete self-contradiction.
= 一边每天对人撒谎，一边告诉别人你从不撒谎，这完全是自相矛盾。
a self-contradiction = 自相矛盾的说法
