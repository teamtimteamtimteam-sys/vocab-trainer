examiner
核心：两支。一是主考人、阅卷人：出题或评分的人；二是（正式）检查员、审查员。可数。
① The examiner marks four hundred scripts and remembers three of them.
= 那位阅卷人批四百份卷子，记得住三份。
an examiner = 主考人、阅卷人
② An external examiner reads a sample and signs the whole cohort off.
= 校外考官抽读一部分，签字放行整届学生。
an external examiner = 校外考官

example
核心：三支。一是例子、实例；二是榜样、典范；三是儆戒的例子。可数。
习语：for example 是例如；set an example 是树立榜样；
make an example of somebody 是拿某人开刀儆戒。
① Give one example and the room will argue about it for an hour.
= 举一个例子，屋里的人能就它争一个钟头。
an example = 例子
for example = 例如
② She sets an example by doing the job nobody signed up for.
= 她去做那件没人报名的活儿，以身作则。
set an example = 树立榜样
follow somebody's example = 效法某人
③ They made an example of one supplier and the rest paid on time.
= 他们拿一家供应商开刀儆戒，其余的都按时付了款。
make an example of somebody = 拿某人做儆戒

exasperate
核心：使恼火、把人惹得没脾气：说反复的小麻烦磨得人光火。
名词 exasperation，形容词 exasperated 与 exasperating。
注意别混：exasperate 是把人惹恼，exacerbate 是让事情变糟。
① The forms exasperate everybody and the office blames the software.
= 那些表格把人人惹得冒火，办公室怪软件。
exasperate somebody = 把某人惹恼
② What exasperates her is being asked the same thing in three emails.
= 让她光火的，是同一件事被三封邮件问三遍。
exasperate = 使恼火

exasperated
核心：恼火的、被磨得没脾气的：be exasperated by something、
be exasperated with somebody。
① She was exasperated by the third form and polite on all three.
= 第三张表把她惹恼了，三张表她都填得客客气气。
be exasperated by something = 被某事惹恼
② An exasperated parent counts to ten and gets to four.
= 一位快没脾气的家长数到十，其实数到四就爆了。
an exasperated sigh = 一声无奈的叹息
副词 exasperatedly：恼火地、没好气地：修饰说话、叹气、摆手的方式。
③ He shrugged exasperatedly and answered the question anyway.
= 他没好气地耸了耸肩，还是把问题答了。
shrug exasperatedly = 没好气地耸肩
④ She repeated the instruction exasperatedly and wrote it on the wall.
= 她恼火地又说了一遍那条说明，索性写到了墙上。
say something exasperatedly = 没好气地说
构词：exasperated 加 -ly

exasperating
核心：（事情）让人光火的、磨人的：说反复出小毛病的东西或做法。
① The lock is exasperating in winter and perfectly normal in June.
= 那把锁冬天磨人得很，到六月就好端端的。
exasperating = 让人光火的
② It is exasperating to explain the same rule to the same person.
= 同一条规矩对同一个人讲了又讲，实在磨人。
be exasperating = 令人恼火

exasperation
核心：恼火、无奈：被反复磨得没脾气的那股情绪。不可数。
① Her exasperation showed in the punctuation and not in the words.
= 她的恼火显在标点上，没显在措辞上。
exasperation = 恼火、无奈
in exasperation = 恼火之下
② In exasperation he took the door off its hinges.
= 一气之下，他把门从铰链上卸了下来。
a note of exasperation = 一丝无奈的口气

excavate
核心：两支。一是挖掘、开挖（土方）：excavate a trench；
二是（考古）发掘：把埋着的遗迹清理出来。名词 excavation。
① They excavated the site in one week and stopped for nine months.
= 他们一周就把场地挖开，然后停了九个月。
excavate a site = 开挖场地
② The tomb was excavated in 1923 and catalogued in 1978.
= 那座墓一九二三年发掘，一九七八年才编目。
excavate a tomb = 发掘一座墓

excavation
核心：两支。一是挖掘、开挖（这件事，也指挖出的坑）；
二是（考古）发掘工作。可数不可数都行。
① The excavation filled with water twice before the pump arrived.
= 泵还没到，那个基坑就灌了两回水。
an excavation = 挖开的坑
② Excavation of the site is licensed for three summers.
= 那个遗址的发掘获准做三个夏天。
the excavation of a site = 遗址的发掘

excavator
核心：两支。一是挖掘机：带铲斗的工程机械；二是（考古）发掘者。可数。
① The excavator took out in a day what nine people had planned for a week.
= 九个人计划干一周的活，挖掘机一天就挖完了。
an excavator = 挖掘机
② The excavator kept a notebook that is now worth more than the finds.
= 那位发掘者留下的笔记本，如今比出土物还值钱。
the original excavator = 最初的发掘者
构词：excavate 加 -or

exceed
核心：两支。一是超过（数量、限度）：exceed the limit；
二是超出（预期、职权）：exceed expectations、exceed your authority。
副词 exceedingly。
① The final bill exceeds the builder's estimate by about a third.
= 最后那张账单，比施工方的估价高出大约三分之一。
exceed something = 超过某数
exceed the speed limit = 超速
② The result exceeded every expectation and nobody had written those expectations down.
= 那个结果超出了所有预期，可那些预期谁也没白纸黑字写下来过。
exceed expectations = 超出预期

exceedingly
核心：（略正式）极其、非常：多修饰形容词，语气比 very 重。
① The instructions are exceedingly clear and the machine ignores them.
= 那份说明极其清楚，机器不理它。
exceedingly clear = 极其清楚
② It is exceedingly rare and the museum has four of them.
= 那东西极为罕见，那家博物馆有四件。
exceedingly rare = 极为罕见
构词：exceeding 加 -ly

excel
核心：擅长、出类拔萃：在某方面比别人强，搭 excel at 或 excel in something。
过去式 excelled，双写 l。名词 excellence。
习语 excel yourself 是（英式）超常发挥、比平时还出色。
① She excels at the part of the job everybody else avoids.
= 那份工作里别人都躲的部分，她做得最好。
excel at something = 在某方面出色
excel in something = 擅长某事
② He excelled himself and burnt only one of the four pans.
= 他超常发挥，四口锅里只烧糊了一口。
excel yourself = 超常发挥

excellence
核心：卓越、优秀：做得极好的那种水准。不可数。
习语 par excellence 是「典型的、首屈一指的」，放在名词后面。
① Excellence in that trade takes a decade and one good teacher.
= 那一行要做到出色，得十年，外加一位好师傅。
excellence = 卓越
academic excellence = 学业上的优异
② He is the committee man par excellence and has never chaired one.
= 他是典型的委员会型人物，却从没当过主席。
par excellence = 首屈一指的

Excellency
核心：阁下：对大使、总督一类高官的尊称，
当面说 Your Excellency，提到时说 His Excellency 或 Her Excellency。可数。
① The note begins Your Excellency and goes on to ask for a parking permit.
= 那张便条以「阁下」开头，接下去要的是一张停车许可。
Your Excellency = 阁下（当面称呼）
② His Excellency arrived late and left before the toast.
= 那位阁下来得晚，祝酒之前就走了。
His Excellency = 某某阁下

excellent
核心：极好的、优秀的：比 good 强得多；也作感叹表示赞许。副词 excellently。
① The food is excellent and the service is a matter of luck.
= 那儿的菜极好，服务全看运气。
excellent = 极好的
an excellent idea = 极好的主意
② 'They said yes.' 'Excellent.'
= 「他们答应了。」「太好了。」
excellent = 好极了（表示赞许）
副词 excellently：出色地、极好地：修饰做事的水准。
③ The piece was played excellently and programmed badly.
= 那首曲子演得极好，编排得很糟。
be played excellently = 演奏得极出色
④ She writes excellently about things she has not done.
= 她写自己没做过的事，写得极好。
write excellently = 写得出色
构词：excellent 加 -ly

except
核心：介词、连词与动词。介词是除…之外：everyone except him；
连词是「只是、要不是」：I would go, except it rains；
动词（正式）是把…除外：present company excepted。
注意别混：except 是「除…之外（不包括）」，besides 多是「除…之外（还有）」。
① Everyone except the caretaker had a key and he had all of them.
= 除了看门的，人人都有一把钥匙，而他手里全部都有。
except = 除…之外
except for something = 除某物之外
② I would walk, except that the last stretch has no pavement.
= 我本想走过去，只是最后那段没有人行道。
except that... = 只是…
③ Present company excepted, nobody reads the appendix.
= 在座各位除外，没人读附录。
be excepted = 被除外

excepting
核心：（正式）除…以外：介词，多用在 not excepting 与 always excepting 里，
日常用 except。
① Every window was painted shut, not excepting the one over the sink.
= 每扇窗都被漆死了，水槽上头那扇也不例外。
not excepting something = 连某物也不例外
② All the shops open on Sunday, always excepting the butcher.
= 所有店周日都开，肉铺永远除外。
excepting = 除…以外

exception
核心：两支。一是例外：不适用一般规则的人或事；
二是异议：take exception to something 是对某事表示不满。可数。
习语：with the exception of 是除…之外；without exception 是无一例外。
① There is one exception and it is written in a footnote.
= 有一个例外，写在脚注里。
an exception = 例外
make an exception = 破一次例
② With the exception of one afternoon, it rained all week.
= 除了一个下午，那一周都在下雨。
with the exception of something = 除某物之外
without exception = 无一例外
③ She took exception to the word and not to the decision.
= 她不满的是那个词，不是那个决定。
take exception to something = 对某事表示不满

exceptional
核心：两支。一是（水准）出众的、卓越的；二是例外的、不寻常的：
in exceptional circumstances 是在特殊情况下。副词 exceptionally。
① An exceptional year for apples means a bad year for the branches.
= 苹果特别好的年份，对树枝来说是难熬的年份。
exceptional = 出众的、异乎寻常的
② Leave is granted in exceptional circumstances and defined nowhere.
= 特殊情况下才准假，可什么算特殊哪儿也没写。
exceptional circumstances = 特殊情况
副词 exceptionally：两支。一是格外地、异常地：exceptionally cold； 二是例外地、破例地：exceptionally, we can wait。
③ It was an exceptionally cold March and the pipes agreed.
= 那年三月格外冷，水管也这么认为。
exceptionally cold = 格外冷
④ Exceptionally, the deadline was moved and moved back the same day.
= 那次破例把截止日期挪了，当天又挪了回来。
exceptionally = 破例地
构词：exceptional 加 -ly

excerpt
核心：节选、摘录：从书、演讲或乐曲里取出的一段。可数；
也作动词（多用被动）：be excerpted from something。
① The excerpt runs for two minutes and gives away the ending.
= 那段节选放两分钟，把结局给漏了。
an excerpt = 节选
an excerpt from something = 从某处摘出的一段
② The passage was excerpted in a magazine and read by more people than the book.
= 那一段被杂志摘登，读它的人比读那本书的还多。
be excerpted from something = 摘自某处

excess
核心：三支。一是过量、过度：不可数，to excess 是过度地；
二是超出的部分：an excess of something；
三是（英式保险）免赔额：the excess on a policy。也作定语：excess baggage。
① Excess salt is the reason that soup tastes of one thing.
= 盐放多了，那锅汤就只剩一个味道。
excess = 过量
an excess of something = 过多的某物
② He does everything to excess and sleeps four hours a night.
= 他干什么都过头，一晚睡四个钟头。
to excess = 过度地
③ The excess on that policy is more than the repair.
= 那份保单的免赔额，比修车还贵。
the excess = 免赔额
excess baggage = 超重行李

excessive
核心：过多的、过分的：说数量、程度或做法超出该有的限度。副词 excessively。
① The charge is excessive and the letter explaining it is longer than the bill.
= 那笔收费过高，解释它的信比账单还长。
excessive = 过多的、过分的
excessive force = 过度的武力
② Excessive noise is defined in decibels and argued about in person.
= 噪音过量是用分贝定的，争起来却是当面吵。
excessive noise = 过量的噪音
副词 excessively：过分地、过度地：修饰形容词与动词。
③ The room is excessively warm and the window opens two centimetres.
= 那屋子热得过分，窗户只开得了两厘米。
excessively warm = 热得过分
④ He apologised excessively and repeated the mistake on Thursday.
= 他道歉道得过了头，周四又犯了同样的错。
apologise excessively = 过分地道歉
构词：excessive 加 -ly

exchange
核心：名词四支、动词两支。名词一是交换、互换：an exchange of letters；
二是（简短的）交谈、争执；三是交易所：the stock exchange；
四是（学生的）交流项目。动词一是交换：exchange something for something；
二是互换（意见、眼神）。
习语 in exchange for something 是作为交换。
① The exchange of contracts took nine weeks and one afternoon of panic.
= 交换合同花了九个星期，外加一个下午的手忙脚乱。
an exchange = 交换
an exchange of letters = 往来书信
② She exchanged the shirt for one two sizes larger.
= 她把那件衬衫换成了大两号的。
exchange something for something = 把某物换成某物
③ They took the studio in exchange for two paintings a year.
= 他们拿到那间画室，作为交换，每年给两幅画。
in exchange for something = 作为对某物的交换
④ The stock exchange closed early and the news arrived later.
= 交易所提前收市，消息来得更晚。
the stock exchange = 证券交易所

exchangeable
核心：可交换的、可调换的：说票、券、商品能换成别的或退换。
① The ticket is exchangeable once and refundable never.
= 那张票能换一次，退是不能退的。
be exchangeable = 可以调换
② Vouchers are exchangeable for goods and not for money.
= 那些代金券能换东西，不能换钱。
exchangeable for something = 可换成某物
构词：exchange 加 -able

exchequer
核心：（英国）国库、财政部：the Exchequer 指国家财政，
the Chancellor of the Exchequer 是财政大臣。单数，常带 the。
① The Exchequer collects it in April and spends it in the same week.
= 国库四月把钱收上来，同一周就花出去。
the Exchequer = 国库、财政部
② A windfall for the Exchequer is a bill for somebody else.
= 国库的一笔意外之财，是另一头的一张账单。
the Chancellor of the Exchequer = 英国财政大臣
