horrific
核心：骇人听闻的、令人震惊的 —— 比 horrible 重，多说事故、伤势、罪行，新闻里常见。
副词 horrifically 并入本条。
① Survivors described the scene of the crash as horrific.
= 幸存者形容事故现场惨不忍睹。
horrific = 骇人听闻的、惨不忍睹的
② He was horrifically injured in the fire and spent a year in hospital.
= 他在火灾中伤得极重，在医院里住了一年。
horrifically injured = 伤得极重

horrify
核心：使惊骇、使震惊 —— 多用被动 be horrified by / at something。
形容词 horrified（惊骇的）、horrifying（令人惊骇的）与副词 horrifyingly 并入本条。
① The pictures of the flood damage horrified everyone who saw them.
= 洪水破坏的照片让每个看到的人都大为震惊。
horrify somebody = 使某人惊骇
② She was horrified to learn that her son had been sleeping in his car.
= 得知儿子一直睡在车里，她大为惊骇。
be horrified to do something = 得知…大为惊骇
③ It was a horrifying experience that she still can't talk about.
= 那是一段令人惊骇的经历，她至今都说不出口。
a horrifying experience = 令人惊骇的经历
④ The number of children living in poverty is horrifyingly high.
= 生活在贫困中的儿童人数高得吓人。
horrifyingly high = 高得吓人

horror
核心：恐惧、惊骇；也指可怕的事物本身。
不可数时说那种感觉：in horror、to somebody's horror；
可数复数 the horrors of something 说一连串可怕的事：the horrors of war。
作定语是恐怖体裁：a horror film、a horror story（也比喻「糟糕的经历」）。
口语里 a little horror 是淘气包；horror of horrors 是半开玩笑的「最可怕的是」。
形容词 horror-struck（吓呆了的）并入本条。
① She watched in horror as the car slid towards the edge of the cliff.
= 她惊恐地看着那辆车往悬崖边滑去。
in horror = 惊恐地
② To my horror, I realised I had sent the email to the whole company.
= 让我惊恐的是，我发现那封邮件发给了全公司。
to somebody's horror = 令某人惊恐的是
③ The book describes the horrors of war through the letters of one soldier.
= 那本书通过一个士兵的书信描写了战争的恐怖。
the horrors of something = …的恐怖
④ They stayed up watching horror films until three in the morning.
= 他们熬夜看恐怖片，一直看到凌晨三点。
a horror film = 恐怖片
⑤ Everyone has a horror story about booking flights with that company.
= 每个人都有一段在那家公司订机票的糟糕经历。
a horror story = 恐怖故事；（喻）糟糕的经历
⑥ Her youngest is a little horror and has broken three windows this year.
= 她最小的孩子是个淘气包，今年已经打破了三扇窗户。
a little horror = 淘气包（口语）
⑦ And then, horror of horrors, my mother walked in.
= 然后，最要命的是，我妈走了进来。
horror of horrors = 最要命的是（口语，半开玩笑）
⑧ The crowd stood horror-struck as the bridge began to collapse.
= 桥开始坍塌时，人群吓呆了。
horror-struck = 吓呆了的

hors d'oeuvre
核心：（开胃）小吃、开胃菜 —— 正餐前或酒会上的小份食物。借自法语，复数 hors d'oeuvres 或不变。
也写作 hors d’oeuvre（弯撇号）。
① Waiters carried trays of hors d'oeuvres around the garden before dinner.
= 晚饭前，侍者端着一盘盘开胃小吃在花园里走动。
hors d'oeuvres = 开胃小吃
② Smoked salmon on rye was the only hors d'oeuvre that ran out.
= 黑麦面包配熏三文鱼是唯一被吃光的一道开胃菜。
an hors d'oeuvre = 一道开胃菜

hors de combat
核心：（正式）失去战斗力的；借自法语「在战斗之外」。
既说伤兵，也谐指因伤病暂时无法上场、干活。
① Two of their best players are hors de combat with knee injuries.
= 他们有两名主力因膝伤无法上场。
be hors de combat = 失去战斗力、无法上场
② Under the treaty, soldiers who are hors de combat must not be attacked.
= 根据条约，失去战斗力的士兵不得受到攻击。
soldiers hors de combat = 失去战斗力的士兵

horse
核心：马。从这个本义长出三支：
一、骑马与赛马的一整套说法：ride a horse、fall off a horse、on horseback、
horse riding（英式；美式说法收在「马背」那一条）、horse racing、a horse race、a horse show、
horse-drawn（马拉的）、a horse trailer（美式运马拖车）；
二、体操器械：跳马、鞍马，因形状像马而得名；
三、一大批习语，几乎都从养马、买马、赛马的经验里来：straight from the horse's mouth、
flog a dead horse、eat like a horse、a dark horse、put the cart before the horse、
horses for courses、look a gift horse in the mouth、wild horses wouldn't…、
change horses in midstream、a one-horse town。
动词只有短语：horse around（英式也说 horse about），胡闹、嬉闹。
horse-trading 从马市上的讨价还价来，比喻政治交易。
俚语里 horse 还指海洛因；horse mackerel 是一种鱼，horse's neck 是一种鸡尾酒。
① She learned to ride a horse before she could read, on her uncle's farm.
= 她在叔叔的农场上学会了骑马，那时她还不识字。
ride a horse = 骑马
② He fell off a horse at twelve and broke his collarbone.
= 他十二岁时从马上摔下来，摔断了锁骨。
fall off a horse = 从马上摔下来
③ Mounted police patrolled the square on horseback during the match.
= 比赛期间，骑警骑着马在广场上巡逻。
on horseback = 骑着马
④ Tourists can take a horse-drawn carriage round the old town.
= 游客可以乘马车游览老城。
horse-drawn = 马拉的
⑤ The Derby is the most famous horse race in the English calendar.
= 德比赛马是英国一年里最有名的一场赛马。
a horse race = 一场赛马
比喻两强之争时说 a two-horse race。
⑥ He lost most of his savings on horse racing and never told his wife.
= 他把大部分积蓄输在了赛马上，一直没告诉妻子。
horse racing = 赛马（运动）
⑦ She goes horse riding every Saturday morning in the forest.
= 她每个星期六上午都去森林里骑马。
go horse riding = 去骑马（英式）
⑧ The horse show attracted riders and breeders from all over the country.
= 那场马术表演赛吸引了全国各地的骑手和育马人。
a horse show = 马术表演赛
⑨ They towed the horse trailer behind a pickup truck all the way to Kentucky.
= 他们用一辆皮卡拖着运马车，一路开到了肯塔基。
a horse trailer = 运马拖车（美式）
⑩ The children took turns vaulting over the horse in the school gym.
= 孩子们在学校体育馆里轮流跳马。
the horse = 跳马、鞍马（体操器械）
第二支：器械因形状像马得名。
⑪ Stop horsing around in the pool before somebody gets hurt.
= 别在泳池里胡闹了，当心有人受伤。
horse around = 胡闹、嬉闹
英式也说 horse about，意思相同。
⑫ The children horsed about in the back of the car for the whole journey.
= 孩子们一路都在车后座上打打闹闹。
horse about = 打闹（英式）
⑬ After weeks of horse-trading, the two parties agreed to form a government.
= 经过几个星期的讨价还价，两党同意联合组阁。
horse-trading = 讨价还价、政治交易
从马市上买卖双方的精明较量来，带点贬义。
⑭ I got it straight from the horse's mouth: the manager is leaving in June.
= 我是听当事人亲口说的：经理六月走。
straight from the horse's mouth = 消息来自当事人本人
第三支：习语。据说看马的牙齿最能看出马的年纪，所以最可靠的消息「出自马嘴」。
⑮ Arguing with him about it again would be flogging a dead horse.
= 再跟他争这件事就是白费力气了。
flog a dead horse = 白费力气（英式）
美式说 beat a dead horse。
⑯ Their son eats like a horse and is still as thin as a rake.
= 他们的儿子饭量大得惊人，却还是瘦得像根竹竿。
eat like a horse = 饭量很大
⑰ The new candidate is a dark horse, and nobody knows much about her views.
= 那位新候选人是匹黑马，谁都不太了解她的观点。
a dark horse = 黑马；深藏不露的人
⑱ Buying the furniture before you've found a flat is putting the cart before the horse.
= 还没找到公寓就先买家具，这是本末倒置。
put the cart before the horse = 本末倒置
⑲ Some people learn best from books and others by doing; it's horses for courses.
= 有人看书学得最好，有人动手学得最好，各有所长。
horses for courses = 各有所长、因人而异（英式）
⑳ The car is old, but it was free, so don't look a gift horse in the mouth.
= 车是旧了点，可那是白送的，别挑三拣四了。
look a gift horse in the mouth = 对送上门的好处挑三拣四
几乎总用否定：don't look a gift horse in the mouth。
㉑ Wild horses wouldn't drag me to another of his dinner parties.
= 说什么我也不会再去参加他的晚宴了。
wild horses wouldn't drag somebody somewhere = 说什么也不去
㉒ It's too late to change horses in midstream, so we'll finish with the old system.
= 现在中途换人换办法已经太晚了，我们就用老系统做完。
change horses in midstream = 中途换马、半路改变计划
㉓ He grew up in a one-horse town with a single shop and a petrol station.
= 他在一个只有一家商店和一个加油站的小镇上长大。
a one-horse town = 偏僻冷清的小镇
㉔ The detective found out that the singer had been using horse for years.
= 侦探查出那个歌手吸海洛因已经好几年了。
horse = 海洛因（俚语）
㉕ Horse mackerel is cheap at the fish market and very good grilled.
= 鱼市上的竹荚鱼很便宜，烤着吃很香。
horse mackerel = 竹荚鱼
㉖ He ordered a horse's neck, brandy with ginger ale and a long curl of lemon peel.
= 他点了一杯「马颈」鸡尾酒：白兰地兑姜汁汽水，配一长条卷曲的柠檬皮。
a horse's neck = 马颈鸡尾酒

horseback
核心：马背 —— 多用在 on horseback（骑着马）里；也作形容词：骑马的。
美式 horseback riding（骑马，英式说 horse riding）并入本条。
① The only way to reach the village in winter is on horseback, along the river path.
= 冬天去那个村子唯一的办法，是沿着河边小路骑马进去。
on horseback = 骑着马
② The farm offers horseback tours of the vineyards at sunset.
= 那座农场提供日落时骑马游览葡萄园的项目。
horseback = 骑马的
③ They went horseback riding in the mountains on the last day of the holiday.
= 假期最后一天，他们去山里骑马了。
horseback riding = 骑马（美式）

horsebox
核心：（英式）运马车 —— 装马的封闭式厢车或拖车；美式说 horse trailer。
① The horsebox broke down on the motorway on the way to the show.

② They converted an old horsebox into a mobile coffee bar.
= 他们把一辆旧运马车改成了流动咖啡车。
an old horsebox = 旧运马车

horseflesh
核心：两支：一是（总称的）马 —— 多用在 a judge of horseflesh（会相马的人）；二是马肉。不可数。
① He's a good judge of horseflesh and has never bought a bad animal.
= 他很会相马，从来没买到过一匹差马。
a judge of horseflesh = 会相马的人
② In some countries horseflesh is sold in ordinary butcher's shops.
= 在有些国家，普通肉铺里就卖马肉。

horsefly
核心：马蝇 —— 叮咬马和人、个头较大的蝇。可数，复数 horseflies。
① A horsefly bit her on the ankle and the swelling lasted a week.
= 一只马蝇叮了她的脚踝，肿了一个星期。
a horsefly = 马蝇
② The cattle stood in the river to escape the horseflies on hot afternoons.
= 炎热的下午，牛群站在河里躲马蝇。
horseflies = 马蝇（复数）

horsehair
核心：马毛 —— 马的鬃毛与尾毛，作为一种材料说时不可数。
① The old sofa was stuffed with horsehair and scratched the backs of your legs.
= 那张旧沙发里填着马毛，扎得腿后面发痒。
be stuffed with horsehair = 填着马毛
② The violin bow is strung with horsehair that has to be replaced every year.
= 小提琴的琴弓用马尾毛做弓毛，每年都得换。
be strung with horsehair = 用马毛做弦

horseman

名词 horsemanship（骑术）并入本条。
① He was a fine horseman who had ridden since he could walk.
= 他是个出色的骑手，从会走路起就开始骑马。
a fine horseman = 出色的骑手
② The display showed off the riders' horsemanship over fences and water.
= 那场表演展示了骑手们跨越栅栏与水障的骑术。
horsemanship = 骑术

horseplay
核心：打闹、胡闹 —— 粗手粗脚、闹哄哄的嬉戏，多说孩子或年轻人。不可数。
① The teacher put a stop to the horseplay at the back of the bus.
= 老师制止了公交车后座的打闹。
horseplay = 打闹、胡闹
② Two boys were hurt in rough horseplay beside the swimming pool.
= 两个男孩在游泳池边粗野地打闹，受了伤。
rough horseplay = 粗野的打闹

horsepower
核心：马力 —— 功率单位，缩写 hp。复数不变。比喻义：实力、能力。
① The boat has a two-hundred-horsepower engine and burns fuel like a bonfire.
= 那条船装着一台两百马力的发动机，烧油像烧篝火一样。
two hundred horsepower = 两百马力
② The small firm simply lacked the horsepower to compete with the big banks.
= 那家小公司根本没有跟大银行竞争的实力。
the horsepower to do something = 做某事的实力（比喻）

horseradish

horseradish sauce = 辣根酱

horseshoe

a horseshoe for luck = 带来好运的马蹄铁

horsey

horticulture

hosanna

hose
核心：两个词：
一是软管、水管（a garden hose），动词 hose something down 是用水管冲洗；
二是（旧，或美式商店用语）袜类：长袜、连裤袜，不可数。
① He watered the tomatoes with a hose every evening in the dry weather.
= 天旱时，他每天傍晚都用水管给番茄浇水。
a hose = 软管、水管
② After the market closed, the traders hosed down the square.
= 集市散了以后，摊贩们用水管把广场冲洗干净。
hose something down = 用水管冲洗某处
③ She wore black hose and flat shoes to the funeral.
= 她穿着黑色长袜和平底鞋去参加葬礼。
hose = 长袜、连裤袜（旧或美式）

hosepipe
核心：（英式）浇水用的软管 —— 同 hose 的第一支。
旱季的 hosepipe ban 是禁止用软管浇花洗车的限水令。
① He left the hosepipe running and flooded the neighbour's garden.
= 他没关水管，把邻居家的花园淹了。
the hosepipe = 浇水软管
② A hosepipe ban was announced after the driest spring in fifty years.
= 在五十年来最干旱的春天之后，当局宣布了禁用水管令。
a hosepipe ban = 禁用水管令（英式）

hosiery
核心：（商店用语）袜类 —— 长袜、连裤袜、短袜的统称。不可数。
① The hosiery department is on the ground floor, next to the gloves.
