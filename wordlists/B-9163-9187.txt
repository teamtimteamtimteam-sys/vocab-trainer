dense foliage = 茂密的枝叶
② She buys a bunch of foliage with the flowers and throws the flowers out first.
= 她买花时连绿叶一起买，先扔的反倒是花。
a bunch of foliage = 一束配叶
③ Carved foliage runs along the top of every door in the hall.
= 门厅里每道门的上沿都刻着一圈叶形纹样。
carved foliage = 雕刻的叶形纹样

a foliar spray = 叶面喷施
③ Foliar uptake beats anything the roots manage in a dry summer.
= 干旱的夏天，叶面吸收胜过根部所能做到的一切。
foliar uptake = 叶面吸收

follow
核心：五支。一是跟随、跟着走；二是沿着（路、河）走：follow the river；
三是听懂、跟得上：follow an argument；四是照着做：follow the instructions；

= 照印好的顺序装，架子就立得住。

⑤ One shop closed and three others followed within the same year.
= 一家店关了，同一年之内又跟着关了三家。
follow = 随后发生
⑥ The others followed suit and put their phones face down on the table.
= 其他人照着做，把手机都扣在桌上。

⑦ The rules are as follows and the first one is never enforced.
