self-assertive
/ˌself əˈsɜːtɪv/
核心：形容词：（敢于）坚持己见的、自信果断的。
① She has become much more self-assertive since she started her own business.
= 自从开始自己做生意，她变得自信果断多了。

② The new manager is self-assertive but always listens to what his team has to say.
= 新经理坚持己见，但总会听取团队的意见。
