其二（名词）：矿、矿井；地雷、水雷；a mine of information（知识宝库）。动词：开采（矿）；在…布雷。
并入本条：miner（矿工）、mining（采矿业）、mineworker（矿工，正式说法）、mineshaft（矿井的竖井）、minefield（雷区；
（比喻）危机四伏的局面）、minesweeper（扫雷艇）、mine detector（探雷器）。
① That bag is mine, and the blue one is yours.
= 那个包是我的，蓝色那个是你的。
be mine = 是我的
后面不能再接名词；my 才接名词。
② He is an old friend of mine from university.
= 他是我大学时的一位老朋友。
a friend of mine = 我的一个朋友
③ His father worked down a coal mine for thirty years.
= 他父亲在煤矿井下干了三十年。
a coal mine = 煤矿
这是另一个 mine：名词。
④ The lorry was destroyed when it drove over a mine.
= 那辆卡车压上一颗地雷，被炸毁了。
a mine = 地雷
⑤ My grandmother is a mine of information about the history of the village.
= 说起村子的历史，我奶奶是个活宝库。
a mine of information = 知识宝库
⑥ Gold has been mined in these hills for two hundred years.
= 这些山里采金已有两百年。
mine gold = 采金
名词是 mining、miner。
⑦ The army mined the road to the bridge before it left.
= 军队撤离前在通往桥的路上布了雷。

⑧ Three miners were trapped underground by the flood.

⑭ A minesweeper cleared the entrance to the harbour.
= 一艘扫雷艇清除了港口入口处的水雷。
a minesweeper = 扫雷艇
⑮ The engineer walked ahead with a mine detector.
= 那名工兵拿着探雷器走在前面。
a mine detector = 探雷器
