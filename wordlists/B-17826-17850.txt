proofread
/ˈpruːfriːd/
核心：校对：把稿子、校样读一遍，找出并改正错字。及物也可不及物；过去式、过去分词 proofread，读 /ˈpruːfred/。
做这件事的人叫 proofreader。并入本条。
① Always proofread your essay before you hand it in to the teacher.
= 作文交给老师之前，一定要校对一遍。
proofread an essay = 校对作文
proofread something = 校对某物
② The letter had clearly not been proofread, for it was full of mistakes.
= 那封信显然没校对过，满篇都是错。
be proofread = 经过校对
过去分词拼法不变，读音变。
③ She works at home as a proofreader for a firm that publishes law books.
= 她在家给一家出法律书的公司做校对。
a proofreader = 校对员
work as a proofreader = 做校对

prop
/prɒp/
核心：名词，可数：支柱、支撑物；比喻靠山、精神支柱；（戏剧、电影的）道具，多用复数；（橄榄球）支柱前锋；（口语）螺旋桨：propeller 的截短。
动词：把……靠在、支在：prop something against、on；用东西把……撑住：prop the door open。
短语动词 prop up 是撑住、支起，也指扶持、支撑（快垮的政权、行业）。过去式 propped。
（口语）props 是应得的称赞、敬意：give somebody props。
① The roof of the old mine was held up by wooden props.
= 那座旧矿井的顶是用木头支柱撑着的。
a wooden prop = 木头支柱
a pit prop = 坑木
② After his wife died, his work became his only prop.
= 妻子死后，工作成了他唯一的精神支柱。
a prop = 靠山、精神支柱
这是比喻的用法。
③ She is in charge of the props for the school play this term.
= 这学期学校那出戏的道具归她管。
the props = 道具
a stage prop = 舞台道具
④ He propped his bicycle against the wall and went into the shop.
= 他把自行车靠在墙上，进了商店。
prop something against something = 把某物靠在某物上
prop something on something = 把某物支在某物上
⑤ She propped the door open with a chair to let the air in.
= 她用一把椅子把门撑开，好让空气进来。
prop the door open = 把门撑开
prop something open = 把某物撑开
⑥ He propped himself up on one elbow and looked at the clock.
= 他用一只胳膊肘撑起身子，看了看钟。
prop yourself up = 撑起身子
prop up = 撑住、支起
⑦ The government spent millions to prop up the failing steel industry.
= 政府花了几百万扶持快垮的钢铁业。
prop up an industry = 扶持一个行业
prop up a regime = 支撑一个政权
⑧ You have to give her props for finishing the race with a broken toe.
= 她断了一根脚趾还跑完了全程，这一点你不能不佩服。
give somebody props = 佩服某人、称赞某人
这是美式口语。

propaganda
/ˌprɒpəˈɡændə/
核心：（多含贬义）宣传、鼓吹：为了让人接受一种政治主张而散布的、常常片面或不实的说法。不可数。
搞宣传的人叫 propagandist，可数；动词 propagandize 是（正式，含贬义）搞宣传，英式也拼作 propagandise。并入本条。
① The film was a piece of enemy propaganda made to frighten the troops.
= 那部片子是敌方的宣传品，拍来吓唬士兵的。
enemy propaganda = 敌方的宣传
a piece of propaganda = 一件宣传品
② The party ran a propaganda campaign in the newspapers before the vote.
= 投票之前，那个党在报上搞了一场宣传攻势。
a propaganda campaign = 宣传攻势
这个词带贬义：片面、不尽不实。
③ He worked as a propagandist for the regime throughout the war.
= 整个战争期间他都在替那个政权搞宣传。
a propagandist = 搞宣传的人
④ The teachers were accused of propagandizing in the classroom.
= 那些教师被指责在课堂上搞宣传。
propagandize = 搞宣传
这个动词少见，带贬义。

propagate
/ˈprɒpəɡeɪt/
核心：三支。一是（正式）传播、宣传（思想、信仰、消息），及物；二是繁殖（植物）、（植物）繁殖，及物也可不及物；
三是（物理）（波、声、光）传播。名词 propagation，不可数；繁殖幼苗用的保温育苗箱叫 propagator。并入本条。
① The group uses the internet to propagate its ideas among the young.
= 那个团体利用互联网在年轻人当中传播自己的主张。
propagate ideas = 传播主张
propagate a belief = 传播一种信仰
② The plant is easy to propagate from cuttings taken in the spring.
= 那种植物用春天剪的插条很容易繁殖。
propagate a plant = 繁殖一种植物
propagate from cuttings = 用插条繁殖
③ Sound waves propagate more quickly through water than through air.
= 声波在水里比在空气里传得快。
waves propagate = 波传播
这是物理学里的说法。
④ The book explains the propagation of roses by grafting.
= 那本书讲怎样用嫁接繁殖玫瑰。
the propagation of something = 某物的繁殖、传播
⑤ She raises her tomato seedlings in a heated propagator on the windowsill.
= 她在窗台上一个带加热的育苗箱里育番茄苗。
a propagator = 育苗箱

propane

propane = 丙烷

a propane heater = 丙烷取暖器

propel
/prəˈpel/
核心：两支，及物，多用被动。一是推进、推动：让……往前走；二是驱使、促使、把（某人）推到（某种境地）：propel somebody into、towards。
过去式 propelled。-propelled 构成形容词：jet-propelled。
① The boat is propelled by a small engine at the back.
= 那条船由船尾的一台小发动机推进。
be propelled by something = 由某物推进
propel a boat = 推动船
② He took her by the arm and propelled her gently towards the door.
= 他抓住她的胳膊，轻轻把她往门口推。
propel somebody towards something = 把某人往某处推
③ The success of the film propelled her to fame almost overnight.
= 那部片子的成功让她几乎一夜成名。
propel somebody to fame = 把某人推上成名的路
propel somebody into something = 把某人推进某种境地
④ The army relied on rocket-propelled grenades to stop the tanks.
= 那支军队靠火箭推进的榴弹挡坦克。
rocket-propelled = 火箭推进的
jet-propelled = 喷气推进的

propellant
/prəˈpelənt/
核心：两支，可数也可不可数。一是（喷雾罐里把东西压出来的）喷射剂、推进气体；二是（火箭、枪炮的）推进剂、发射药。
① The spray uses compressed air as a propellant in place of harmful gases.
= 那种喷雾用压缩空气当喷射剂，代替有害的气体。
a propellant = 喷射剂
use something as a propellant = 用某物当喷射剂
② The rocket carries four hundred tonnes of liquid propellant in its tanks.
= 那枚火箭的燃料箱里装着四百吨液体推进剂。
liquid propellant = 液体推进剂
solid propellant = 固体推进剂

propeller
/prəˈpelə(r)/
核心：螺旋桨：装在船尾或飞机上、靠转动的叶片把船或飞机往前推的装置。可数。口语简称 prop。
① The fishing net had wrapped itself round the propeller of the boat.
= 渔网缠在了船的螺旋桨上。
the propeller of a boat = 船的螺旋桨
a propeller = 螺旋桨
② It was a small plane with a single propeller on its nose.

a single propeller = 单螺旋桨
a propeller blade = 螺旋桨的叶片

propensity
/prəˈpensəti/
核心：（正式）（天生的）倾向、习性：propensity for、propensity to do。可数，复数 propensities。
① He has a propensity for getting into arguments with strangers on trains.
= 他有个毛病，爱在火车上跟陌生人争起来。
a propensity for something = 好某事的倾向
a propensity for doing something = 好做某事的倾向
② The dog showed a propensity to chase anything that moved.
= 那条狗见什么动就爱追什么。
a propensity to do something = 爱做某事的倾向
比 tendency 正式，多指天性。

proper
/ˈprɒpə(r)/
核心：形容词：（英式口语）像样的、真正的、名副其实的，只放在名词前：a proper meal；正确的、恰当的、合适的；
（社会上、道德上）得体的、规矩的、正派的；放在名词后：严格意义上的、本身：the city proper；
（英式口语）十足的、彻底的：a proper fool；proper to 是（正式）为……所特有的。
副词 properly：正确地、恰当地、好好地；得体地；（英式口语）十足地、真正地。习语 properly speaking 是严格说来。并入本条。
（语法）proper noun、proper name 是专有名词。
① We have not had a proper holiday since the children were born.
= 自打孩子出生，我们就没正经度过一次假。
a proper holiday = 像样的假期
a proper meal = 一顿像样的饭
② Please put the books back in their proper place on the shelf.
= 请把书放回书架上原来的位置。
the proper place = 该放的位置
the proper way to do something = 做某事的正确办法
③ It is not considered proper to arrive at a funeral in bright colours.
= 穿着鲜艳的衣服去参加葬礼，是不合规矩的。
it is not proper to do something = 做某事不合规矩
right and proper = 理所应当
④ Her grandmother was a very proper lady who never raised her voice.
= 她奶奶是位规规矩矩的老太太，从不高声说话。
a very proper lady = 规规矩矩的女士
prim and proper = 一本正经
⑤ They live in a village outside the city proper, twenty minutes by bus.
= 他们住在市区以外的一个村子里，坐公共汽车二十分钟。
the city proper = 市区本身
这一支放在名词后面。
⑥ I felt a proper fool standing there in the rain with no umbrella.
= 我没带伞站在雨里，觉得自己真是个大傻瓜。
a proper fool = 十足的傻瓜
这样用是英式口语。
⑦ The teacher showed the dignity proper to her position.
= 那位老师显出了与她的身份相称的庄重。
proper to something = 为某物所特有的、与某物相称的
这一支是正式用法。
⑧ The machine will not work unless it is properly connected.
= 那台机器接得不对就开不动。
properly connected = 接得对
do something properly = 把某事做对、做好
⑨ You cannot eat properly on the money they pay you.
= 靠他们给的那点钱，你吃不上像样的饭。
eat properly = 好好吃饭
sleep properly = 好好睡觉
⑩ He does not know how to behave properly at the table.
= 他不知道在饭桌上该怎么守规矩。
behave properly = 举止得体
dress properly = 穿得得体
⑪ Properly speaking, a tomato is a fruit and not a vegetable.
= 严格说来，番茄是水果，不是蔬菜。
properly speaking = 严格说来
⑫ "London" and "Mary" are proper nouns and begin with a capital letter.
= 「London」和「Mary」是专有名词，首字母大写。
a proper noun = 专有名词
a proper name = 专有名词

property

healing properties = 疗效
the properties of something = 某物的性质
⑥ He made his fortune as a property developer in the nineteen eighties.

= 我把那把伞交到了车站的失物招领处。

⑧ The song is the intellectual property of the woman who wrote it.

⑨ In those days only the propertied classes had the right to vote.

the propertied classes = 有产阶级

prophecy

prophesy
/ˈprɒfəsaɪ/

prophet

日常说 favourable。

proponent
/prəˈpəʊnənt/
核心：（正式）倡导者、支持者、拥护者：公开主张一种想法、做法的人。可数。常说 a proponent of。反义 opponent。
① She is a leading proponent of teaching music in every primary school.
= 她是主张每所小学都教音乐的带头人。
a proponent of something = 某事的倡导者
a leading proponent = 带头倡导的人
② Proponents of the scheme say that it will create two thousand jobs.
= 支持那个方案的人说它会带来两千个工作岗位。
proponents of the scheme = 支持那个方案的人
跟 advocate 意思相近。

proportion
/prəˈpɔːʃn/
核心：名词：（整体里的）部分、份额，可数：a large proportion of；比例、比：甲与乙在数量、大小上的关系，可数也可不可数：the proportion of A to B；
匀称、相称，不可数，也用复数；proportions 是（很大的）规模、尺寸、程度；（数学）比例，不可数。
习语：in proportion to 是与……成比例、相对于……来说；in proportion 是比例合适，也指看得恰如其分；
out of proportion 是不成比例、过分；out of all proportion to 是跟……完全不相称；
keep something in proportion 是恰如其分地看待；a sense of proportion 是分寸感、分得清轻重。
派生：proportional、proportionate 是成比例的、相称的，副词 proportionally、proportionately；proportionality 是（正式）相称、比例原则，不可数；
-proportioned 构成形容词：well-proportioned 是匀称的。并入本条。
① A large proportion of the students come from villages in the north.
= 很大一部分学生来自北方的村子。
a large proportion of something = 某物的很大一部分
a small proportion = 一小部分
② The proportion of women to men in the profession has risen steadily.
= 那一行里女性对男性的比例稳步上升。
the proportion of A to B = 甲对乙的比例
the proportion of women = 女性所占的比例
③ Mix the sand and the cement in the proportion of three to one.
= 把沙子和水泥按三比一的比例拌在一起。
in the proportion of three to one = 按三比一的比例
in equal proportions = 等量地
④ The architect admired the fine proportions of the old town hall.
= 建筑师很欣赏老市政厅优美匀称的比例。
fine proportions = 优美匀称的比例
这一支多用复数。
⑤ The crisis has now reached alarming proportions in the cities of the south.
= 那场危机在南方的城市里已经到了惊人的程度。
reach alarming proportions = 到了惊人的程度
of epic proportions = 规模极大的
⑥ The fee you pay is in proportion to the size of your house.
= 你交的费用跟你房子的大小成比例。
in proportion to = 与……成比例
in proportion to something = 相对于某物来说
⑦ The head of the figure in the drawing is not in proportion to the body.
= 那幅画里人物的头跟身子不成比例。
be in proportion = 比例合适
not in proportion = 不成比例
⑧ The newspapers have blown the whole affair out of proportion.
= 报纸把整件事夸大得不像话。
out of proportion = 不成比例、过分
blow something out of proportion = 把某事夸大
⑨ The punishment was out of all proportion to the seriousness of the offence.
= 那个处罚跟过错的轻重完全不相称。
out of all proportion = 完全不相称
out of all proportion to something = 跟某事完全不相称
⑩ Try to keep the problem in proportion, for nobody has been hurt.
= 这个问题别看得太重，毕竟没人受伤。
keep something in proportion = 恰如其分地看待某事
反过来是 get something out of proportion。
⑪ He has lost all sense of proportion since the argument with his neighbour.
= 自打跟邻居吵了那一架，他就完全分不清轻重了。
a sense of proportion = 分寸感
lose all sense of proportion = 完全分不清轻重
⑫ The pay is proportional to the number of hours you have worked.
= 报酬跟你干的钟点数成正比。
be proportional to something = 与某物成比例
directly proportional = 成正比
⑬ Small firms spend proportionally more on rent than large ones do.
= 按比例算，小公司花在房租上的钱比大公司多。
proportionally = 按比例地
proportionally more = 按比例算更多
⑭ The court held that the fine was not proportionate to the damage done.
= 法院认定那笔罚款跟造成的损害不相称。
be proportionate to something = 与某物相称
反义是 disproportionate。
⑮ Prices have risen, but wages have not increased proportionately.
= 物价涨了，工资却没有相应地涨。
proportionately = 相应地、成比例地
⑯ The judges considered the proportionality of the sentence to the crime.
= 法官们斟酌了量刑与罪行是否相称。
proportionality = 相称、比例原则
这是法律、正式文体里的词。
⑰ It is a well-proportioned room with tall windows on two sides.
= 那是一间比例匀称的屋子，两面都有高窗。
well-proportioned = 匀称的
badly proportioned = 比例不好的

proposal
/prəˈpəʊzl/
核心：两支，可数。一是提议、建议、方案：a proposal for、a proposal to do、a proposal that；二是求婚。也作不可数，指「提出」这件事。
① The council has rejected a proposal to build flats on the playing field.
= 镇议会否决了在操场上盖公寓的提议。
a proposal to do something = 做某事的提议
reject a proposal = 否决提议
② The minister put forward proposals for reforming the tax system.
= 部长提出了改革税制的方案。
proposals for something = 关于某事的方案
put forward a proposal = 提出方案
③ She accepted his proposal of marriage on the spot.
= 她当场答应了他的求婚。
a proposal of marriage = 求婚
accept a proposal = 答应求婚

propose
/prəˈpəʊz/
核心：动词。一是提议、建议：propose something、propose doing、propose that；二是（正式）打算、计划：propose to do、propose doing；
三是求婚：propose (to somebody)；四是（正式）提名、推举：propose somebody for、as；在会上提出（动议）；
五是（正式）提出（一种理论、解释）供人考虑。习语 propose a toast (to somebody) 是提议（为某人）干杯。提议人、动议人叫 proposer。并入本条。
① The committee proposed a number of changes to the rules of the club.
= 委员会对俱乐部的章程提出了几处修改。
propose a change = 提出修改
propose something = 提议某事
② She proposed that the meeting should be put off until Monday.
= 她提议把会推到星期一。
propose that… = 提议……
propose doing something = 提议做某事
③ How do you propose to pay for a car on your wages?
= 凭你那点工资，你打算怎么买车？
propose to do something = 打算做某事
这一支是正式说法，问话时常带怀疑。
④ He proposed to her on the beach where they had first met.
= 他在两人初次相遇的那片海滩上向她求了婚。
propose to somebody = 向某人求婚
propose marriage = 求婚
⑤ I should like to propose Mrs Harris for the post of treasurer.
= 我想提名哈里斯太太当司库。
propose somebody for something = 提名某人任某职
propose somebody as something = 推举某人当某职
⑥ The scientist proposed a new explanation for the sudden rise in temperature.
= 那位科学家对气温骤升提出了一种新解释。
propose an explanation = 提出一种解释
propose a theory = 提出一种理论
⑦ The best man stood up and proposed a toast to the bride and groom.
= 伴郎站起来，提议为新娘新郎干杯。
propose a toast = 提议干杯
propose a toast to somebody = 提议为某人干杯
⑧ The proposer of the motion spoke for ten minutes in its favour.
= 动议的提出人替它讲了十分钟。
the proposer of a motion = 动议的提出人
a proposer = 提议人

proposition
/ˌprɒpəˈzɪʃn/
核心：名词，可数：（生意上的）提议、建议；（要对付的）事情、问题：a different proposition；（正式）主张、论点、命题；
（美国）（交给公民投票的）法律提案；（数学）命题。动词：向（人）提出性要求，尤指唐突、冒犯地。
形容词 propositional 是命题的。并入本条。
① I have a business proposition that I should like to put to you.
= 我有个生意上的提议想跟你谈谈。
a business proposition = 生意上的提议
put a proposition to somebody = 向某人提出建议
② Climbing the mountain in winter is a very different proposition.
= 冬天爬那座山可完全是另一回事。
a different proposition = 另一回事
a tough proposition = 难对付的事
③ The book defends the proposition that all people are born equal.
= 那本书为「人人生而平等」这个主张辩护。
the proposition that… = ……这个主张
这一支是正式用法。
④ Voters in the state rejected the proposition by a narrow margin.
= 那个州的选民以微弱多数否决了那项提案。
a proposition = （付公民投票的）提案
这是美国的说法。
⑤ She was propositioned by a stranger in the bar of the hotel.
= 她在旅馆的酒吧里遭到一个陌生人的非礼搭讪。
proposition somebody = 向某人提出性要求
这样用带冒犯、唐突的意思。
⑥ The course begins with the rules of propositional logic.
= 那门课从命题逻辑的规则讲起。
propositional logic = 命题逻辑
propositional = 命题的

propound
/prəˈpaʊnd/
核心：（正式）提出（一种理论、主张）供人考虑、讨论。及物。
① The theory was first propounded by a Greek thinker two thousand years ago.
= 那个理论最早是两千年前一位希腊思想家提出的。
propound a theory = 提出一种理论
be propounded by somebody = 由某人提出
② He used the lecture to propound his own views on education.
= 他借那场讲座宣讲自己对教育的看法。
propound your views = 提出自己的看法
日常说 put forward。
