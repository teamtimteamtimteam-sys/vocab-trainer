purge
/pɜːdʒ/
核心：动词：清洗、清除（组织里不要的人）：purge A of B、purge B from A；purge yourself、somebody of 是（正式）涤除、洗去（坏念头、罪过）。
名词，可数：（政治上的）清洗、清除。
① The new leader purged the party of everyone who had opposed him.
= 新领袖把党内反对过他的人统统清洗了。
purge A of B = 把乙从甲里清除
purge the party = 清洗党
② Hundreds of officers were purged from the army after the failed coup.
= 政变失败后，好几百名军官被清出了军队。
be purged from something = 被从某处清除
purge somebody from something = 把某人从某处清除
③ He went on a long walk to purge himself of his anger.
= 他去走了很长的路，好把怒气消掉。
purge yourself of something = 涤除某种情绪
这一支是正式用法。
④ Thousands died in the purges of the nineteen thirties.
= 成千上万的人死于一九三〇年代的大清洗。
a purge = 清洗
a purge of somebody = 对某些人的清洗

purify
/ˈpjʊərɪfaɪ/
核心：及物。一是净化、提纯：把脏东西、杂质去掉；二是（宗教上）使洁净、涤罪：purify somebody of；三是（术语）提炼、精制。
名词 purification，不可数；purifier 是净化器，可数。并入本条。
① The tablets are used to purify water from rivers before it is drunk.
= 那种药片用来净化河水，然后才能喝。
purify water = 净化水
purify something = 净化某物
② The pilgrims bathe in the river to purify themselves of their sins.
= 朝圣者在那条河里沐浴，洗去罪孽。
purify yourself of something = 洗去某种罪过
这一支是宗教上的说法。
③ The gold is purified by heating it until the other metals burn away.
= 金子是靠加热到别的金属都烧掉来提纯的。
be purified = 被提纯
④ The town has built a new plant for the purification of its drinking water.
= 那座城新建了一座净化饮用水的厂子。
the purification of water = 水的净化
water purification = 水的净化
⑤ They bought an air purifier for the baby's room during the smoky season.
= 烟雾重的那个季节，他们给婴儿的房间买了一台空气净化器。
an air purifier = 空气净化器
a water purifier = 净水器

purist
/ˈpjʊərɪst/
核心：纯粹主义者、讲究正统的人：认为语言、艺术、做事都该照老规矩来、不容走样的人。可数。这种态度叫 purism，不可数。并入本条。
① Purists object to the use of "hopefully" at the beginning of a sentence.
= 讲究正统的人反对把「hopefully」用在句子开头。
a purist = 讲究正统的人
purists object to something = 讲究正统的人反对某事
② A jazz purist would not call this music jazz at all.
= 爵士乐的正统派根本不会管这种音乐叫爵士。
a jazz purist = 爵士乐的正统派
③ His purism about grammar makes him a difficult man to write for.
= 他在语法上的洁癖让人很难给他写稿子。
purism = 纯粹主义、讲究正统

puritan
/ˈpjʊərɪtən/

形容词：清教徒的、清教徒式的。puritanical 是（含贬义）清教徒式的、道德上极严苛的；puritanism 是清教徒式的严苛、Puritanism 是清教主义，不可数。并入本条。
① He is a puritan who thinks that dancing on a Sunday is wicked.
= 他是个清教徒式的人，认为星期天跳舞是罪过。
a puritan = 清教徒式的人
② The Puritans sailed to America in search of freedom to worship as they chose.
= 清教徒乘船去美洲，寻求照自己的方式礼拜的自由。
the Puritans = 清教徒
首字母大写指历史上的那一派。
③ She was brought up in a puritan household where cards and novels were forbidden.
= 她生长在一个清教徒式的家庭，纸牌和小说都不许碰。
a puritan household = 清教徒式的家庭
④ Her father had a puritanical attitude to drink and never allowed it in the house.
= 她父亲对酒的态度严得像清教徒，从不许家里有酒。
a puritanical attitude = 清教徒式的态度
puritanical = 道德上极严苛的
⑤ The book describes the puritanism of the small town where he grew up.
= 那本书写的是他长大的那座小城里的清教徒式风气。
puritanism = 清教徒式的严苛

purl

purpose
/ˈpɜːpəs/
核心：名词：目的、意图、用途，可数：the purpose of、for the purpose of；purposes 是（某种）需要、用途，复数：for tax purposes；
（人生的）意义、目标，不可数：a sense of purpose；决心、意志，不可数。
习语：on purpose 是故意地；to no purpose 是（正式）白白地、毫无结果；serve a purpose 是有用、派得上用场；
for all practical purposes 是实际上、事实上，也说 to all intents and purposes；at cross purposes 是各说各的、互相误会；
for the purposes of 是为了……、就……而言；to little purpose 是没什么用。
派生：purposeful 是有目标的、坚定的，副词 purposefully，名词 purposefulness；purposeless 是没有目的的、无意义的；
purposely 是故意地；purposive 是（正式）有目的的。并入本条。
① The purpose of the meeting is to choose a new chairman.
= 开这个会的目的是选一位新主席。
the purpose of something = 某事的目的
a purpose = 目的、用途
② The building is no longer used for its original purpose.
= 那栋楼已经不作原先的用途了。
its original purpose = 原先的用途
for this purpose = 为此
③ He went to Paris for the purpose of buying paintings for the gallery.
= 他去巴黎是为了给画廊买画。
for the purpose of doing something = 为了做某事
④ The house is treated as an office for tax purposes.
= 为报税起见，那栋房子算作办公室。
for tax purposes = 为报税起见
for the purposes of something = 为了某事、就某事而言
⑤ Since she retired, she has lost her sense of purpose.
= 退休以后，她觉得日子没了奔头。
a sense of purpose = 目标感、奔头
give somebody a purpose = 给某人一个奔头
⑥ She walked into the room with an air of great purpose.
= 她一副打定主意的样子走进屋来。
with purpose = 坚决地
这一支说的是决心，不可数。
⑦ He broke the window on purpose to get into the house.
= 他是故意砸窗户好进屋的。
on purpose = 故意地
反过来是 by accident。
⑧ We argued with the manager for an hour, but to no purpose.
= 我们跟经理争了一个小时，却毫无结果。
to no purpose = 白白地、毫无结果
to little purpose = 没什么用
⑨ The old shed is ugly, but it serves a purpose in the winter.
= 那间旧棚子难看，可是冬天派得上用场。
serve a purpose = 有用、派得上用场
serve no purpose = 毫无用处
⑩ The two towns have grown together and are, for all practical purposes, one city.
= 那两座城已经连成一片，实际上就是一座城。
for all practical purposes = 实际上、事实上
to all intents and purposes = 实际上
⑪ We were talking at cross purposes, for I meant the son and she meant the father.
= 我们各说各的：我说的是儿子，她说的是父亲。
at cross purposes = 各说各的、互相误会
⑫ She walked with a purposeful stride towards the manager's office.
= 她迈着坚定的步子朝经理办公室走去。
purposeful = 有目标的、坚定的
a purposeful stride = 坚定的步子
⑬ He moved purposefully through the crowd towards the stage.
= 他目标明确地穿过人群朝舞台走去。
purposefully = 目标明确地、坚定地
⑭ There was a new purposefulness about the team after the change of manager.
= 换了主教练之后，那支球队有了一股新的劲头。
purposefulness = 目标明确、坚定
⑮ The film is full of purposeless violence that adds nothing to the story.
= 那部片子里满是无谓的暴力，对故事毫无帮助。
purposeless = 没有目的的、无谓的
⑯ He purposely left the door unlocked so that she could get in.
= 他故意没锁门，好让她进得来。
purposely = 故意地
⑰ The study treats play as a purposive activity and not as a waste of time.
= 那项研究把玩耍看成有目的的活动，而不是浪费时间。
purposive = 有目的的
这是正式、学术用词。
