shakedown
/ˈʃeɪkdaʊn/
核心：名词，可数（美式口语）：敲诈、勒索；彻底搜查；（新船、新飞机的）试航、试运行。
① The shop owner told the police about the shakedown, and the gang members were arrested.
= 店主把被敲诈的事告诉了警方，那伙人被捕了。
a shakedown = 敲诈
shakedown = 勒索
② The prison guards carried out a shakedown of every cell, looking for phones and knives.
= 狱警对每间牢房进行了彻底搜查，查找手机和刀具。
a shakedown of something = 对某处的彻底搜查
③ The new ship went on a shakedown cruise to make sure everything worked before its first long voyage.
= 那艘新船首次远航前先做了一次试航，确保一切运转正常。
a shakedown cruise = 试航

shaker
/ˈʃeɪkə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（撒盐、胡椒等的）瓶、罐：a salt shaker；（调鸡尾酒的）摇杯；（大写 Shaker）震颤派教徒；习语：a mover and shaker 是有影响力的人物。
① Could you pass me the salt shaker? The soup needs a little more salt.
= 能把盐瓶递给我吗？汤得再加点盐。
a salt shaker = 盐瓶
shaker = 撒料瓶
② The barman put ice and orange juice into the cocktail shaker and shook it hard.
= 酒保往鸡尾酒摇杯里放了冰块和橙汁，用力摇了起来。
a cocktail shaker = 鸡尾酒摇杯
③ She is one of the movers and shakers in the music business, and every new band wants to meet her.
= 她是音乐界的风云人物之一，每支新乐队都想见她。
a mover and shaker = 有影响力的人物
