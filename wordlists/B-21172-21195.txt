similar
/ˈsɪmələ(r)/

= 我和我妹妹长得很像，人们常以为我们是双胞胎。
similar = 相似的
② Your bag is very similar to mine, so let's put our names on them.
= 你的包和我的很像，咱们在上面写上名字吧。
be similar to = 与…相似
similar = 相似
近义对照：
different = 不同
③ The two schools in our town teach maths to young children in a similar way.
= 我们镇上的两所学校教小孩子数学的方法相似。
in a similar way = 用相似的方法
④ At the wedding, the boys were similarly dressed in blue shirts and black trousers.
= 在婚礼上，男孩们都穿着同样的蓝衬衫和黑裤子。
similarly = 类似地

simmer
/ˈsɪmə(r)/
核心：动词：（用文火）炖、煨、慢煮：simmer the soup for an hour；（怒气、矛盾）酝酿、即将爆发：simmer with anger。名词，单数：文火、慢煮：bring something to a simmer。
① Let the soup simmer for twenty minutes until the vegetables are soft.
= 让汤用小火炖二十分钟，直到蔬菜变软。

② Bring the tomato sauce to a simmer over a low heat and then add the cream.
= 用小火把番茄酱汁煮到微微冒泡，再加奶油。
bring something to a simmer = 把某物煮到微沸
③ She was simmering with anger, but she said nothing in front of the children.
= 她心里憋着一肚子火，但当着孩子们的面什么也没说。
simmer with anger = 憋着一肚子火
④ When the class got too noisy, the teacher told everyone to simmer down and open their books.
= 班上太吵时，老师叫大家安静下来，打开课本。
simmer down = 平静下来
比 boil 火小，水面只是微微冒泡。
