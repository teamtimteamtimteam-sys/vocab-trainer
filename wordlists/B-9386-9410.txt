fowl
核心：三支。一是家禽，鸡鸭鹅的总称：keep fowl；二是（旧）鸟类；
三是禽肉：boiled fowl。
① They keep fowl at the bottom of the garden and sell eggs at the gate.
= 他们在园子尽头养家禽，在门口卖蛋。
keep fowl = 养家禽
② Wild fowl come to that lake in November and leave before the ice.
= 野禽十一月飞到那个湖，结冰前就走。
wild fowl = 野禽
③ Boiled fowl and rice was Sunday dinner for most of that decade.
= 那十年里，多数星期天的正餐就是白煮禽肉配米饭。
boiled fowl = 白煮禽肉
