mileage
核心：行驶里程；（汽车的）每加仑行驶英里数、油耗；（口语）好处、可利用之处。也拼 milage。
① The car is ten years old but has a low mileage.
= 那辆车有十年了，但里程数不高。
a low mileage = 里程数不高
milage = 里程（mileage 的另一种拼法）
② You get better mileage if you drive more slowly.
= 开慢一点更省油。
get better mileage = 更省油
③ The newspapers got a lot of mileage out of the minister's mistake.
= 报纸拿部长的那个失误大做文章。
get mileage out of something = 从…中捞到好处

mileometer
核心：（英）里程表 —— 汽车上显示总行驶里程的仪表。也拼 milometer。美式说 odometer。
① The mileometer shows that the car has done ninety thousand miles.
= 里程表显示那辆车跑了九万英里。
the mileometer = （英）里程表
② The dealer was fined for turning back the milometer on a used car.
= 那个车商因把一辆二手车的里程表往回拨而被罚款。
the milometer = mileometer 的另一种拼法

milestone
核心：里程碑 —— 路边标明里程的石碑；（比喻）重大事件、转折点。
① An old milestone by the road says that London is forty miles away.
= 路边一块旧里程碑上写着离伦敦四十英里。
a milestone = 里程碑
本义：路边每隔一英里立的石头。
② The birth of their first child was a milestone in their lives.
= 头一个孩子的出生是他们人生中的一件大事。
a milestone in your life = 人生的里程碑
③ The company passed an important milestone when it sold its millionth car.
= 那家公司卖出第一百万辆车，过了一个重要的关口。
pass a milestone = 过了一个重要关口

milieu
核心：（正式）社会环境、周围的圈子。复数 milieux 或 milieus。
① She grew up in an artistic milieu of painters and musicians.
= 她在一个画家和音乐家的艺术圈子里长大。
an artistic milieu = 艺术圈子
② The novel describes the social milieu of London in the 1920s.
= 那部小说描写了二十世纪二十年代伦敦的社会环境。
the social milieu = 社会环境
法语借词，读如 meel-yuh。

militant
核心：好斗的、激进的 —— 为政治、社会目标不惜采取强硬甚至暴力手段的；名词：激进分子、武装分子。
名词 militancy（好斗、激进）与副词 militantly 并入本条。
① The union has become more militant since the pay cut.
= 降薪以后，工会变得更激进了。
become militant = 变得激进
不一定动武，但态度强硬、准备斗争。
② Militants attacked a police station near the border last night.
= 昨晚武装分子袭击了边境附近的一个警察局。
militants = 武装分子、激进分子
③ The growing militancy of the students worried the government.
= 学生们日益激进，让政府担忧。
militancy = 激进、好斗
④ She is militantly opposed to any new road through the forest.
= 她强硬反对任何穿过森林的新公路。
militantly opposed = 强硬反对

militarism
核心：（贬）军国主义、黩武主义 —— 主张扩军、靠武力解决问题。
名词兼形容词 militarist（军国主义者）与形容词 militaristic（军国主义的）并入本条。
① The book traces the rise of militarism in the country before the war.
= 那本书追溯了战前该国军国主义的兴起。
the rise of militarism = 军国主义的兴起
是批评的说法。
② The old generals were militarists who distrusted every treaty.
= 那些老将军是军国主义者，对每一项条约都不信任。
a militarist = 军国主义者
③ The children were brought up in a militaristic society, marching to school in uniform.
= 那些孩子在军国主义的社会里长大，穿着制服列队上学。
a militaristic society = 军国主义社会

militarize
核心：把军队派驻到（某地区）；使军事化 —— 多用被动。名词 militarization 并入本条。英式也拼 militarise。
① The border between the two countries is heavily militarized.
= 两国之间的边境重兵把守。
be heavily militarized = 重兵把守
反义 demilitarized（非军事化的）。
② Critics fear that the police are being militarized with army weapons.
= 批评者担心警察正用军队的武器装备起来，走向军事化。
be militarized = 被军事化
③ The treaty forbids the militarization of space.
= 那项条约禁止太空军事化。
the militarization of something = …的军事化

military
核心：军事的、军队的；the military（军队、军方）。副词 militarily 并入本条。
搭配：military service（兵役）、military police（宪兵）、military band（军乐队）、military base（军事基地）。
① The country spends a tenth of its budget on military equipment.
= 那个国家把预算的十分之一花在军事装备上。
military equipment = 军事装备
与 civilian（民用的、平民的）相对。
② The military took control of the government after the riots.
= 骚乱之后，军方接管了政府。
the military = 军方、军队
这一支是名词，带 the。
③ Every young man had to do two years of military service.
= 每个年轻男子都得服两年兵役。
military service = 兵役
有些国家是义务的。
④ The military police arrested two soldiers outside the bar.
= 宪兵在酒吧外面逮捕了两名士兵。
the military police = 宪兵
⑤ A military band played as the president inspected the troops.
= 总统检阅部队时，军乐队奏着乐。
a military band = 军乐队
⑥ The planes took off from a military base in the desert.
= 那些飞机从沙漠里的一个军事基地起飞。
a military base = 军事基地
⑦ The small country is militarily weak but rich in oil.
= 那个小国军事上弱，石油却很丰富。
militarily = 在军事上

militate
核心：militate against（正式）—— 妨碍、不利于、使难以实现。
① The high cost of housing militates against young people who want to start a family.
= 高昂的住房成本对想成家的年轻人很不利。
militate against = 妨碍、不利于
② His bad temper militated against his chances of becoming captain.
= 他的坏脾气妨碍了他当队长的机会。
militate against his chances = 妨碍他的机会
别和 mitigate（减轻）弄混。

militia
核心：民兵组织、民兵（总称）。名词 militiaman（民兵）并入本条。
① The village formed a militia to guard the bridge at night.
= 那个村子组织了一支民兵夜里守桥。
form a militia = 组织民兵
不是正规军，由平民组成。
② The city is controlled by an armed militia loyal to the general.
= 那座城市被一支效忠那位将军的武装民兵控制着。
an armed militia = 武装民兵
③ A young militiaman stopped the car and asked for our papers.
= 一个年轻的民兵拦下车，要看我们的证件。
a militiaman = 民兵

milk
核心：奶、牛奶；（椰子、植物的）乳白色汁液。动词：挤奶；（贬）榨取、从…身上捞好处。

milk float（英：送奶电瓶车）、milk round（英：送奶路线；大公司到各大学招聘的巡回）、milk run（常走的例行路线）、
milch cow（奶牛；摇钱树）。
派生词 milky（乳白色的；含奶多的）、milkshake（奶昔）、milkman（送奶工）、milkmaid（旧：挤奶女工）、milking（挤奶）并入本条。

= 她每晚睡前喝一杯牛奶。
a glass of milk = 一杯牛奶
不可数。

= 你喝茶加奶吗？
take milk in your tea = 喝茶加奶
③ The soup is made with coconut milk and ginger.
= 那道汤是用椰奶和姜做的。
coconut milk = 椰奶
④ The farmer milks forty cows before breakfast.
= 那位农夫早饭前要给四十头牛挤奶。
milk a cow = 给牛挤奶
这一支是动词的本义。
⑤ The agent milked the old lady of her savings.
= 那个中介把老太太的积蓄榨了个干净。
milk somebody of something = 榨取某人的…
⑥ The newspapers milked the story for all it was worth.
= 各家报纸把那件事炒作到了极点。
milk something for all it is worth = 把…利用到极点
⑦ The vase is broken, and there is no use crying over spilt milk.
= 花瓶已经碎了，懊恼也没用。
cry over spilt milk = 为无可挽回的事懊恼
⑧ She is full of the milk of human kindness and cannot pass a beggar.
= 她满怀恻隐之心，见了乞丐走不过去。
the milk of human kindness = 恻隐之心

⑨ I prefer milk chocolate to the dark kind.
= 比起黑巧克力，我更喜欢牛奶巧克力。
milk chocolate = 牛奶巧克力
与 dark chocolate、white chocolate 并列。
⑩ The climbers carried milk powder because it is light.
= 登山者带的是奶粉，因为轻。
milk powder = 奶粉
⑪ Rice cooked slowly in the oven makes a simple milk pudding.
= 米放进烤箱慢慢烤，就是一道简单的牛奶布丁。
a milk pudding = （英）牛奶布丁
⑫ The little girl lost her first milk tooth yesterday.
= 小女孩昨天掉了第一颗乳牙。
a milk tooth = （英）乳牙
⑬ The milkman left two bottles on the step every morning.
= 送奶工每天早上在台阶上留两瓶奶。
a milkman = 送奶工
⑭ The milk float moved slowly down the dark street.
= 送奶电瓶车沿着漆黑的街道慢慢开。

⑮ The big banks visit the university on the milk round every autumn.
= 各大银行每年秋天到那所大学做招聘巡回。
the milk round = （英）大公司的校园招聘巡回

⑰ The government treats drivers as a milch cow for new taxes.

⑱ The children ordered chocolate milkshakes.

牛奶加冰淇淋、果味打成的饮料。
⑲ She likes her coffee very milky.
= 她喝咖啡喜欢多加奶。

mill
核心：磨坊；（纺织、造纸、炼钢的）工厂；（咖啡、胡椒的）研磨器。动词：磨（粉）；mill around / about（（人群）乱转）。
习语：put somebody through the mill（让某人吃尽苦头）、run-of-the-mill（平平常常的）、grist to the mill（可资利用的东西）。
并入本条：miller（磨坊主）、milling（碾磨；（人群）乱转的）、millpond（磨坊水池；like a millpond = 平静如镜）、
millstream（推动水车的水流）、mill wheel（磨坊水车轮）。

= 河边的老磨坊还在给村里磨面粉。
the old mill = 老磨坊
靠风力的叫 windmill，靠水力的叫 watermill。
② Her grandmother worked in a cotton mill from the age of twelve.
= 她祖母十二岁起就在棉纺厂做工。
a cotton mill = 棉纺厂
steel mill、paper mill 同此。
③ He ground the beans in a small coffee mill.
= 他用一个小咖啡磨把豆子磨了。
a coffee mill = 咖啡磨
④ The wheat is milled into fine white flour.
= 小麦被磨成细白的面粉。
be milled into flour = 被磨成面粉
⑤ Crowds of tourists were milling around in the square.
= 广场上成群的游客在乱转。
mill around = （人群）乱转
英式也说 mill about。
⑥ The lawyers put the witness through the mill for three hours.
= 律师们把那名证人折腾了三个小时。
put through the mill = 让…吃尽苦头
⑦ It was a run-of-the-mill detective story, with nothing new.
= 那是一部平平常常的侦探小说，毫无新意。
run-of-the-mill = 平平常常的
原指工厂里没挑拣过的普通货。
⑧ Every mistake by the minister is grist to the mill of the newspapers.
= 部长的每一个失误都成了报纸的好材料。
grist to the mill = 可资利用的东西
⑨ The miller sold his flour at the market every Friday.
= 磨坊主每个星期五在集市上卖面粉。
a miller = 磨坊主
⑩ We pushed our way through the milling crowd at the station.
= 我们在车站从乱转的人群里挤了过去。
a milling crowd = 乱转的人群
⑪ The sea was as calm as a millpond when we set sail.
= 我们启航时，海面平静如镜。
a millpond = 磨坊水池
⑫ The children caught small fish in the millstream.
= 孩子们在磨坊的水流里捉小鱼。
the millstream = 推动水车的水流
⑬ The old mill wheel still turns slowly in the stream.
= 那只旧水车轮还在溪水里慢慢转动。
a mill wheel = 磨坊水车轮

millennial
