sauce
/sɔːs/
核心：名词：酱、酱汁、调味汁（可数或不可数）：tomato sauce、soy sauce、cheese sauce；（英式旧式口语）无礼的话、放肆（不可数）；（北美俚语）the sauce 是酒。与 seasoning 的分别：sauce 是湿的、浇在菜上的；seasoning 是盐、胡椒这类干调料。复合词 sauce boat 是船形调味汁壶。
① My grandmother made a rich tomato sauce for the pasta with fresh basil from the garden.
= 祖母用园子里的新鲜罗勒给意面做了浓浓的番茄酱汁。
sauce = 酱汁
sauce = 酱汁（湿的）
tomato sauce = 番茄酱
近义对照：
seasoning = 调料（干的）
② At our family dinners we always dip the dumplings in a little soy sauce mixed with vinegar.
= 家里聚餐时，我们总是把饺子蘸一点兑了醋的酱油吃。
soy sauce = 酱油
③ That's enough of your sauce, young man; go and help your mother in the kitchen.
= 小子，别耍嘴皮子了，去厨房帮你妈干活。
sauce = （英式旧式）放肆的话
④ My uncle has been off the sauce for a year and says he feels much healthier.
= 我叔叔戒酒一年了，说他觉得健康多了。
be off the sauce = （俚语）戒酒
⑤ The gravy was served in a silver sauce boat at the end of the long dinner table.
= 肉汁盛在一只银色船形调味汁壶里，放在长餐桌的一头。
a sauce boat = 船形调味汁壶

save
/seɪv/
核心：动词，本义是「使免于损失、危险」：拯救、救：save somebody's life、save somebody from；存（钱）、攒：save money、save up for；节省、省下（时间、钱、力气）：save time；保留、留着：save a seat for somebody；（计算机）保存：save a file；（球类）扑救：save a penalty；使免去（麻烦）：save somebody the trouble。名词，可数：（足球等）扑救。介词（正式）：除……之外。
习语：save face 是保全面子；save the day 是力挽狂澜；save your breath 是别白费口舌；save your skin 是保全自己。
① The firefighters saved the family from the burning house just in time.
= 消防员及时把那一家人从着火的房子里救了出来。
save = 拯救
save somebody from something = 把某人从某处救出
② The doctor's quick action saved the boy's life after he was hit by a car.
= 男孩被车撞后，医生的果断处置救了他的命。
save somebody's life = 救某人的命
③ She saves money every month so that she can travel around Asia next year.
= 她每个月存钱，好在明年去亚洲旅行。
save money = 存钱
也说 save up；不及物时宾语可省：I am saving for a car。
④ He is saving up for a new bike and puts all his birthday money in a box.
= 他在攒钱买新自行车，把生日收到的钱全放进一个盒子里。
save up for something = 为某物攒钱
⑤ Taking the motorway instead of the old coast road will save us at least an hour.
= 走高速公路而不走那条老海滨路，至少能给我们省一个小时。
save time = 省时间
⑥ Could you save me a seat near the front? I will be ten minutes late.
= 能在前排给我留个座位吗？我会晚到十分钟。
save somebody a seat = 给某人留座位
⑦ Don't forget to save your work before you turn off the computer.
= 关电脑之前别忘了保存你的工作。
save = 保存
计算机用语，也说 save something as a new file。
⑧ The goalkeeper saved the penalty and became the hero of the match.
= 守门员扑出了那个点球，成了全场的英雄。
save a penalty = 扑出点球
⑨ He apologized in public to save face after his comments were reported in the newspapers.
= 他的言论被报纸报道后，他公开道歉以保全面子。
save face = 保全面子
⑩ Just when we thought the party would be a disaster, my uncle arrived with music and saved the day.
= 就在我们以为聚会要砸了的时候，我叔叔带着音乐来了，力挽狂澜。
save the day = 力挽狂澜
⑪ Save your breath; she has already made up her mind and will not listen.
= 别白费口舌了，她已经拿定主意，不会听的。
save your breath = 别白费口舌
⑫ He lied to the police to save his own skin, and his friend went to prison instead.
= 他为了保全自己对警察撒了谎，结果他朋友进了监狱。
save your skin = 保全自己
⑬ If you post the letter for me, it will save me the trouble of going to the post office.
= 你要是帮我寄这封信，我就省得跑邮局了。
save somebody the trouble = 省得某人麻烦
⑭ Everyone in the family came to the wedding, save my youngest brother, who was ill.
= 全家人都来参加婚礼了，只有我最小的弟弟病了没来。
save = （正式）除……之外

savoury
/ˈseɪvəri/
核心：形容词：（英式）咸味的、咸鲜的（与 sweet 相对）：savoury snacks；（多用于否定）体面的、名声好的：not a very savoury character。名词，可数（英式）：咸味小吃。美式拼作 savory；savory 另指香薄荷，一种香草（不可数）。
① I prefer savoury snacks like crisps and cheese to cakes and sweets.
= 比起蛋糕和糖果，我更喜欢薯片、奶酪这类咸味零食。
savoury snacks = 咸味零食
savoury = 咸味的
② Her new boyfriend is not a very savoury character, and her parents are worried.
= 她的新男朋友名声不太好，她父母很担心。
not a very savoury character = 名声不太好的人
③ The caterers served tea, cakes and a selection of savouries at the meeting.
= 承办方在会上提供茶、蛋糕和各式咸味小点。
savouries = 咸味小点
④ The American cookbook on her shelf has recipes for savory pies, savory breakfasts and sweet desserts.
= 她书架上那本美国菜谱里有咸味派、咸味早餐和甜点的做法。
savory = 咸味的（savoury 的美式拼法）
⑤ Add a little dried savory and thyme to the beans while they cook.
= 豆子煮的时候加一点干香薄荷和百里香。
savory = 香薄荷（一种香草）

savoy
/səˈvɔɪ/

① We had roast chicken with potatoes and some steamed savoy from the garden.
= 我们吃了烤鸡配土豆，还有一些园子里摘的清蒸皱叶甘蓝。
savoy = 皱叶甘蓝
② The crinkly leaves of the savoy cabbage hold the creamy sauce well when you serve it with chicken.
= 配鸡肉吃的时候，皱叶甘蓝起皱的叶子很能挂住奶油酱汁。
savoy cabbage = 皱叶甘蓝

savvy
/ˈsævi/
核心：名词，不可数（口语）：实际知识、精明能干：business savvy、political savvy。形容词：精明的、懂行的：savvy shoppers、tech-savvy。
① She has plenty of business savvy and turned her small shop into a big company.
= 她很有生意头脑，把小店做成了大公司。
business savvy = 生意头脑
savvy = 精明能干
② Savvy shoppers always compare prices online before they buy anything expensive in the shops.
= 精明的顾客在店里买贵重东西前，总会先上网比价。
savvy shoppers = 精明的顾客
③ At eighty, my grandmother is surprisingly tech-savvy and video calls us every Sunday afternoon.
= 我祖母八十岁了，却出人意料地懂科技，每个星期天下午都跟我们视频通话。
tech-savvy = 懂科技的
