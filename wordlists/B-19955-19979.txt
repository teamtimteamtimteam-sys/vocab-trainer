scale
/skeɪl/
核心：名词：规模、程度（单数或不可数）：the scale of the problem、on a large scale；（地图、模型的）比例、比例尺：a scale of 1:50,000、to scale；等级、级别、刻度（可数）：on a scale of 1 to 10、a pay scale；（复数 scales）秤、天平：bathroom scales；（音乐）音阶（可数）；（鱼、蛇的）鳞（可数）；水垢（不可数）。动词：攀登：scale a wall；扩大（规模）。
习语：tip the scales 是起决定性作用；economies of scale 是规模经济。
① Nobody understood the scale of the problem until the floods reached the city centre.
= 直到洪水淹到市中心，人们才明白问题有多严重。
the scale of the problem = 问题的严重程度
② Farmers in the area grow rice on a large scale and sell it all over the country.
= 那一带的农民大规模种植水稻，卖到全国各地。
on a large scale = 大规模地
介词用 on，不用 in。
③ The walking map we bought in the village has a scale of one centimetre to one kilometre.
= 我们在村里买的那张徒步地图，比例是一厘米代表一公里。
scale = 比例
④ The model of the ship is built to scale, so every part is exactly one hundredth of its real size.
= 那艘船的模型是按比例做的，每个部件都正好是实物的百分之一。
to scale = 按比例
⑤ On a scale of one to ten, how would you rate the hotel where you stayed?
= 如果打一到十分，你给你住的那家酒店打几分？
on a scale of one to ten = 按一到十分
⑥ I stand on the bathroom scales every morning to check my weight.
= 我每天早上站到浴室的秤上称体重。
scales = 秤
英式多用复数 scales，美式常用单数 a scale。
⑦ The children practise their scales on the piano for ten minutes before every lesson.
= 孩子们每节课前都在钢琴上练十分钟音阶。
practise scales = 练音阶
⑧ The fish's silver scales shone in the sun as the fisherman pulled it out of the water.
= 渔民把鱼拉出水时，银色的鱼鳞在阳光下闪闪发亮。
scales = 鳞
⑨ The prisoners scaled the high wall with a rope and escaped into the forest.
= 囚犯们用绳子翻过高墙，逃进了森林。
scale a wall = 攀越围墙
⑩ The company scaled down its plans for a new factory because there was not enough money.
= 因为资金不足，那家公司缩小了新工厂的计划规模。
scale down = 缩小规模
⑪ When the new shop was a success, they decided to scale up and open five more.
= 新店生意很好，他们决定扩大规模，再开五家。
scale up = 扩大规模
⑫ Her experience abroad tipped the scales, and she got the job instead of the other candidates.
= 她的海外经历起了决定作用，她击败了其他候选人得到了那份工作。
tip the scales = 起决定性作用
⑬ Big supermarkets can sell food more cheaply because of economies of scale.
= 大超市因为规模经济，能把食品卖得更便宜。
economies of scale = 规模经济
