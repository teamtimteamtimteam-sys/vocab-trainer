scrape
/skreɪp/
核心：动词，本义是「用硬边刮」：刮、刮掉：scrape the mud off your boots；擦伤、刮破：scrape your knee；（与硬物摩擦）发出刺耳声；（比喻）勉强做到、艰难地得到。名词，可数：擦伤、刮痕；刮擦声；（口语）困境：get into a scrape。
和 scrub、wipe 的分别：scrape 是用硬边刮；scrub 是用刷子或粗布使劲擦洗；wipe 是用布轻轻抹。
短语动词：scrape by 是勉强维持生活；scrape in / into 是勉强进入、勉强考上；scrape out 是刮出、挖出；scrape through 是勉强通过；scrape together / up 是好不容易凑齐（钱、人）。习语 scrape the bottom of the barrel 是（没办法时）退而求其次、用最差的。
① Scrape the mud off your boots before you come into the kitchen.
= 进厨房之前，先把靴子上的泥刮掉。
scrape something off something = 把某物从某处刮掉
② The little boy fell off his bike and scraped his knee on the road.
= 小男孩从自行车上摔下来，膝盖在路上擦破了。
scrape = 擦伤
scrape your knee = 擦破膝盖
近义对照：
scrub = 用力刷洗（要使劲）
scrub = 用力擦洗
wipe = 擦（轻轻抹）
③ We could hear the chairs scraping on the stone floor as the guests sat down.
= 客人们坐下时，我们听得见椅子在石地板上刮出的刺耳声。
chairs scrape = 椅子发出刮擦声
④ He had a few cuts and scrapes after the accident, but nothing serious.
= 事故后他身上有几处割伤和擦伤，不过不严重。
cuts and scrapes = 割伤和擦伤
⑤ The boys got into a scrape with the police for climbing onto the school roof.
= 男孩们因为爬上学校屋顶，跟警察闹了点麻烦。
get into a scrape = 惹麻烦
⑥ After he lost his job, the family scraped by on her small salary for two years.
= 他失业后，一家人靠她那点微薄的薪水勉强维持了两年。
scrape by = 勉强维持生活
⑦ Our team scraped in to the final with a goal in the last minute.
= 我们队凭最后一分钟的进球勉强挤进了决赛。
scrape in = 勉强进入
⑧ She scraped into university with the lowest grades they would accept, and then worked very hard.
= 她以学校能接受的最低分数勉强考上了大学，后来学习非常刻苦。
scrape into = 勉强考上
⑨ Cut the pumpkin in half and scrape out the seeds with a large spoon.
= 把南瓜切成两半，用大勺子把籽刮出来。
scrape out = 刮出、挖出
⑩ My brother didn't study much last year, but he scraped through his final exams in June.
= 我哥哥去年没怎么用功，但六月的期末考试勉强通过了。
scrape through = 勉强通过
⑪ We managed to scrape together enough money for a week's holiday by the sea.
= 我们好不容易凑够了去海边度一周假的钱。
scrape together = 好不容易凑齐
⑫ The coach could only scrape up ten players for the match because so many were ill.
= 生病的人太多，教练只凑出了十名球员去比赛。
scrape up = 勉强凑出
⑬ If they are asking him to play in goal, they really are scraping the bottom of the barrel.
= 要是他们让他去守门，那真是没人可用了。
scrape the bottom of the barrel = 没办法时退而求其次

a scrawny boy = 瘦骨伶仃的男孩
scrawny = 骨瘦如柴的
② A few scrawny chickens were pecking at the dry ground in front of the hut.
= 几只瘦巴巴的鸡在小屋前的干地上啄食。
scrawny chickens = 瘦巴巴的鸡

scream
/skriːm/
核心：动词：尖叫、惊叫：scream with fear / pain / laughter；大声喊叫：scream at somebody；（风、机器）发出尖啸声。名词：尖叫声（可数）；（口语，单数）非常好笑的人或事：be a scream。scream out 是尖声喊出。副词 screamingly 是极其（好笑等）（另立条）。
① My sister screamed when she saw a huge black spider in the bath this morning.
= 我姐姐今天早上在浴缸里看见一只大黑蜘蛛，尖叫起来。
scream = 尖叫
② The children screamed with laughter as the clown fell into the bucket of water.
= 小丑掉进水桶时，孩子们笑得尖叫起来。
scream with laughter = 笑得尖叫
with 后面接原因：scream with fear、scream with pain、scream with delight。
③ Please don't scream at me like that; I am only trying to help you with the move.
= 请别那样冲我嚷嚷，我只是想帮你搬家。
scream at somebody = 冲某人嚷嚷
④ We heard a scream from the house next door and called the police.
= 我们听见隔壁房子里传来一声尖叫，就报了警。
a scream = 尖叫声
⑤ You must meet my aunt; she is an absolute scream at parties.
= 你一定要见见我姑姑，她在聚会上可逗了。
be a scream = 非常逗
⑥ "Help me, please!" the little girl screamed out from the bottom of the old well.
= 「请救救我！」小女孩在那口老井的井底尖声喊道。
scream out = 尖声喊出

screamingly
/ˈskriːmɪŋli/
核心：副词（口语）：极其、非常：多用于 screamingly funny、screamingly obvious。
① The play was screamingly funny, and I laughed until my stomach hurt.
= 那出戏好笑极了，我笑得肚子都疼了。
screamingly funny = 好笑极了
② After a while, it became screamingly obvious that he had never been to Paris.
= 过了一会儿，大家都看得一清二楚，他根本没去过巴黎。
screamingly obvious = 再明显不过
