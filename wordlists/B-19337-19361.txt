ritualistic
/ˌrɪtʃuəˈlɪstɪk/
核心：仪式的；（贬义）刻板的、例行公事的。
① The killings had a ritualistic quality that frightened the police.
= 那几起杀人案带有仪式的色彩，让警方害怕。
a ritualistic quality = 仪式色彩
ritualistic = 仪式的
② The meeting began with the ritualistic reading of last month's report, which nobody listened to.
= 会议照例先念上个月的报告，谁也没在听。
ritualistic = 例行公事的

ritualize
/ˈrɪtʃuəlaɪz/
核心：使仪式化、使程式化。英式也拼作 ritualise。
① In some animals, fighting has been ritualized so that nobody gets seriously hurt.
= 在有些动物中，打斗已经程式化了，谁也不会受重伤。

ritualize = 使仪式化
② The family has ritualized the opening of presents on Christmas morning, oldest first.
= 那家人把圣诞早上拆礼物弄成了一套仪式，最年长的先拆。
ritualize something = 把某事弄成仪式
