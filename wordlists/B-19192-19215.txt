= 三十年后，她重访了自己长大的那个村子。
revisit somewhere = 重访某地
revisit = 重访
② The committee agreed to revisit the question of school meals next year.
= 委员会同意明年重新讨论学校午餐的问题。
revisit a question = 重新讨论问题

revitalize
/ˌriːˈvaɪtəlaɪz/
核心：使恢复生机、使重新充满活力、振兴。及物。英式也拼 revitalise。名词 revitalization，不可数。并入本条。
① The new shops and cafés have revitalized the old town centre.
= 新开的商店和咖啡馆让老城中心焕发了生机。
revitalize something = 使某处恢复生机
revitalize = 振兴
② The government has a plan for the revitalization of the old mining towns.
= 政府有一项振兴老矿业城镇的计划。
the revitalization of something = 对某处的振兴

revival
/rɪˈvaɪvl/
核心：可数也可不可数。一是复兴、复苏、重新流行：a revival of、in；二是（旧戏的）重演、再上演；三是苏醒、恢复。
宗教上的复兴运动叫 revivalism，不可数；搞这种运动的人叫 revivalist。并入本条。
① There has been a revival of interest in old crafts such as basket making.
= 人们对编篮子这类老手艺的兴趣又复苏了。
a revival of something = 某事的复兴
revival = 复兴
② The theatre is putting on a revival of a play from the nineteen fifties.
= 那家剧院在重演一出一九五〇年代的戏。
a revival of a play = 一出戏的重演
③ The book tells the story of religious revivalism in America.
= 那本书讲美国宗教复兴运动的故事。
revivalism = 宗教复兴运动
④ A revivalist preacher held meetings in a big tent outside the town.
= 一位复兴派传教士在城外的大帐篷里布道。
a revivalist = 复兴派人士

/rɪˈvaɪv/

① The doctors managed to revive the boy after he was pulled from the river.

revive somebody = 使某人苏醒
revive = 使苏醒

revive a custom = 恢复习俗
③ The new factory has helped to revive the town's economy.
= 新工厂帮助振兴了那个镇的经济。

④ The revival of the old market brought people back to the town centre.
= 老集市的复兴把人们又带回了镇中心。

revoke

revolt
/rɪˈvəʊlt/
核心：名词，可数也可不可数：反抗、叛乱、起义：in revolt、a revolt against。动词：反抗、叛乱、造反：revolt against；（使）厌恶、（使）反感，及物。
分词 revolting 作形容词：令人作呕的，见另条。
① The people rose in revolt against the cruel king.
= 人民起来反抗残暴的国王。

④ The smell from the bins was absolutely revolting.
= 垃圾桶里的气味简直令人作呕。
revolting = 令人作呕的

revolting

revolution

= 一七八九年的革命永远改变了法国。

revolution = 革命
② The computer has caused a revolution in the way we work.
= 电脑给我们的工作方式带来了一场革命。
a revolution in something = 某方面的巨大变革
③ The wheel makes three thousand revolutions per minute.
= 那个轮子每分钟转三千转。
revolutions per minute = 每分钟转数
