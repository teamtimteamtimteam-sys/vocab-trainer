hurdle
核心：本义是跨栏赛跑里的栏架；复数 the hurdles 指跨栏比赛。

动词 hurdle：跨越（障碍）。跨栏运动员 hurdler、跨栏运动 hurdling 并入本条。
① She cleared every hurdle and won the race by a metre.
= 她跨过了每一个栏，以一米之差赢得比赛。
clear a hurdle = 跨过栏架
② He won gold in the 110 metres hurdles.
= 他获得了一百一十米栏的金牌。

③ Getting planning permission was the biggest hurdle we faced.
= 拿到规划许可是我们面临的最大难关。
a hurdle = 障碍、难关
④ The plan fell at the first hurdle when the bank refused the loan.
= 银行拒绝了贷款，那个计划一开头就失败了。
fall at the first hurdle = 一开头就失败
⑤ She was a promising hurdler before a knee injury ended her career.
= 她曾是个前途看好的跨栏运动员，后来一次膝伤结束了她的运动生涯。
a hurdler = 跨栏运动员
⑥ Hurdling needs speed and perfect timing more than strength.
= 跨栏靠的是速度和完美的节奏，而不是力量。
hurdling = 跨栏运动

hurry

⑤ Sorry, I can't stop to chat, I'm in a hurry to get to the bank before it closes.
= 对不起，我不能停下来聊，我得赶在银行关门前过去。

⑥ Take your time over the menu, we're in no hurry and the kitchen is open late.
= 菜单慢慢看，我们不着急，厨房开到很晚。
be in no hurry = 不着急
⑦ What's the hurry? The shop doesn't close until nine.
= 急什么？商店九点才关门。
What's the hurry? = 急什么？
⑧ She hurried on before anyone could interrupt her.
= 她赶紧接着往下说，不让任何人打断。
hurry on = 赶紧接着说（做）下去

hurt
核心：过去式、过去分词都是 hurt。四支（A 表教过前两支）：
一、弄伤、使受伤（hurt yourself、get hurt）；
二、疼 —— 主语是身体部位：My back hurts；
三、伤害感情、使难过：hurt somebody's feelings、be hurt、feel hurt；
四、损害、对…不利：The scandal hurt his chances。
口语 it won't hurt (somebody) to do something：做某事没坏处。
形容词 hurtful（伤人的）与副词 hurtfully 并入本条。

= 他从梯子上摔下来，伤了背。

= 事故中没人受重伤，但车报废了。
be hurt = 受伤
③ My feet hurt after walking round the museum all day.
= 在博物馆里走了一整天，我脚疼。
something hurts = …疼
④ I didn't mean to hurt your feelings, I was only joking.
= 我不是有意伤你的感情，我只是开个玩笑。
hurt somebody's feelings = 伤某人的感情
⑤ She felt hurt that nobody had invited her to the wedding.
= 没人请她参加婚礼，她觉得很受伤。
feel hurt = 感到受伤、难过
⑥ The scandal hurt his chances of becoming party leader.
= 那桩丑闻损害了他当党魁的机会。
hurt somebody's chances = 损害某人的机会
⑦ It wouldn't hurt you to say thank you once in a while.
= 你偶尔说声谢谢也没什么坏处。
it would not hurt to do something = 做某事没坏处
⑧ His comments about her weight were hurtful and completely unnecessary.
= 他对她体重的评论很伤人，而且完全没必要。
hurtful = 伤人的
⑨ "You never listen," she said hurtfully, and left the room.
= 「你从来都不听。」她伤人地说了一句，离开了房间。
hurtfully = 伤人地
