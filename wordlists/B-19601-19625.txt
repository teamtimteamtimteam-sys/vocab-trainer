ruminant
/ˈruːmɪnənt/
核心：名词，可数：反刍动物：牛、羊、鹿等把吞下的食物吐回嘴里再嚼的动物。形容词：反刍动物的。
① Cows and sheep are ruminants, which means they bring food back up from the stomach to chew it again.
= 牛和羊是反刍动物，也就是说它们会把胃里的食物倒回嘴里再嚼一遍。
a ruminant = 反刍动物
② The vet explained that ruminant animals need a lot of grass and hay to stay healthy.
= 兽医解释说，反刍动物需要吃大量的草和干草才能保持健康。
ruminant animals = 反刍动物

ruminate
/ˈruːmɪneɪt/
核心：动词，不及物：（正式）沉思、反复思考：ruminate on / about / over something；（动物）反刍。本义是反刍，引申为把一件事在脑子里翻来覆去地想。
① She sat by the window for hours, ruminating on what her father had said to her before he died.
= 她在窗边坐了几个小时，反复回想父亲临终前对她说的话。
ruminate on something = 反复思考某事
比 think about 正式，带「翻来覆去、慢慢琢磨」的意味，有时近于钻牛角尖。
② The cows lay in the shade of the trees, ruminating quietly through the hottest part of the afternoon.
= 下午最热的时候，牛群躺在树荫下静静地反刍。
ruminate = 反刍

rumination
/ˌruːmɪˈneɪʃn/
核心：名词：沉思、冥想（可数或不可数，多用复数）；（动物的）反刍（不可数）。
① After weeks of rumination, he finally decided to leave his job and go back to university.
= 经过几个星期的反复思考，他终于决定辞职回大学读书。
after much rumination = 经过反复思考
② The book is full of the old poet's ruminations on love, age and death.
= 那本书满是老诗人对爱情、衰老和死亡的沉思。
ruminations on something = 对某事的沉思

ruminative
/ˈruːmɪnətɪv/
核心：形容词：（正式）沉思的、若有所思的：a ruminative mood。副词 ruminatively 是若有所思地。
① He was in a ruminative mood after the funeral and did not want to talk to anyone.
= 葬礼过后他一直沉浸在思绪里，不想跟任何人说话。
a ruminative mood = 沉思的心境
② The old farmer chewed his pipe ruminatively and looked out over the fields before he answered.
= 老农若有所思地咬着烟斗，望着田野，过了一会儿才回答。
ruminatively = 若有所思地

rummage
/ˈrʌmɪdʒ/
核心：动词，不及物：翻找、乱翻：rummage through / in / around something。名词，单数：翻找：have a rummage；（北美）旧杂物。
① She rummaged through her bag for a pen, pulling out keys, tissues and old receipts.
= 她在包里翻找钢笔，掏出了钥匙、纸巾和旧收据。
rummage through something = 在某处翻找
强调翻得乱七八糟、东西被扒拉出来，比 search 随意。
② I had a quick rummage in the attic and found a box of my old school books.
= 我在阁楼上快速翻了一下，找到一箱我以前的课本。
have a rummage = 翻找一番
③ I bought this old lamp for one dollar at a rummage sale in the church hall.
= 这盏旧灯是我在教堂礼堂的旧货义卖会上花一美元买的。
a rummage sale = （北美）旧货义卖
英式说 jumble sale，所得常捐给教会或慈善机构。

rummy
/ˈrʌmi/
核心：拉米纸牌：一种把手中的牌凑成同花顺或同点组的纸牌游戏。不可数。
① On wet afternoons my grandmother taught us to play rummy at the kitchen table.
= 下雨的下午，祖母在厨房桌边教我们玩拉米牌。
play rummy = 玩拉米牌
② We played rummy for small coins all evening, and my uncle won almost every game.
= 我们一晚上都在用小硬币赌拉米牌，几乎每局都是叔叔赢。
a game of rummy = 一局拉米牌

rumour
/ˈruːmə(r)/
核心：名词：谣言、传闻（可数或不可数）：spread a rumour、there are rumours that、rumours of something；rumour has it that 是据传、听说。美式拼作 rumor。形容词 rumoured 是传说中的（另立条）。
① A rumour spread through the school that the head teacher was leaving at the end of the year.
= 学校里传开一个谣言，说校长年底要走。
rumour = 谣言
spread a rumour = 散布谣言
② Rumour has it that the old factory is going to be turned into flats.
= 据传那座老工厂要改建成公寓。
rumour has it = 据传
固定说法，不加冠词，后面可接 that 从句。
③ The company denied rumours that it was going to close its office in the city centre.
= 那家公司否认了要关闭市中心办事处的传闻。
deny rumours = 否认传闻
传闻多用复数，常和 deny、spread、hear、fuel 搭配。
④ There were rumours of a secret wedding, but the singer refused to answer any questions about it.
= 有人传说那位歌手秘密结婚了，可她对此拒不回答任何问题。
rumours of something = 关于某事的传闻
⑤ Most of the stories about him are just rumour and gossip, so do not believe everything you hear.
= 关于他的大多数说法只是道听途说，所以别听到什么都信。
rumour and gossip = 流言蜚语
⑥ The local rumour-monger told everyone in the village that the vicar was going to marry the baker.
= 村里那个爱传闲话的人跟所有人说，牧师要娶面包师了。
a rumour-monger = 造谣的人、爱传闲话的人
-monger 表示「贩卖……的人」，多带贬义。

rumoured
/ˈruːməd/
核心：形容词：传说中的、据传的：be rumoured to be / that…；作定语也说 his rumoured affair。美式拼作 rumored。多用被动结构。
① The singer is rumoured to be planning a world tour next year, but nothing has been announced.
= 据传那位歌手在计划明年的世界巡演，但还没有公布任何消息。
be rumoured to be doing something = 据传正在做某事
几乎只用被动，没有主动的 rumour somebody to be。
② It is rumoured that the minister will resign before the end of the month.
= 据传那位部长会在月底前辞职。
it is rumoured that = 据传
③ The newspapers were full of stories about the actor's rumoured romance with his co-star.
= 报纸上满是那位演员和搭档传说中恋情的报道。
a rumoured romance = 传说中的恋情

rump
/rʌmp/
核心：名词，可数：（动物的）臀部、后腿以上的部分；（牛的）臀肉：rump steak；（幽默）（人的）屁股；（组织分裂后的）残余部分：the rump of a party。
① The farmer slapped the cow gently on the rump to make it walk into the barn.
= 农夫在牛屁股上轻轻拍了一下，让它走进牛棚。
the rump of an animal = 动物的臀部
② At the old pub by the river we ordered two rump steaks with chips and a green salad.
= 在河边那家老酒馆，我们点了两份牛臀排，配薯条和蔬菜沙拉。
rump steak = 牛臀排
英式菜单上常见，比 sirloin 便宜、肉更结实。
③ After most members left to form a new party, only a small rump of the old one remained.
= 大多数党员离开另组新党后，旧党只剩下一小撮残余。
a rump of something = 某组织的残余部分
政治新闻用语，带「元气大伤、只剩空壳」的意思。

rumple
/ˈrʌmpl/
核心：动词：弄皱、弄乱（衣服、头发、床单）：rumple somebody's hair。名词 rumple 不常用；形容词多用过去分词 rumpled：皱巴巴的、乱糟糟的。
① He rumpled his son's hair and told him that he had played well, even though the team had lost.
= 他揉乱儿子的头发，跟他说虽然球队输了，他踢得很好。
rumple somebody's hair = 揉乱某人的头发
② She arrived at the meeting in a rumpled suit after sleeping on the night train.
= 她坐了一夜的卧铺火车，穿着皱巴巴的西装来开会。
a rumpled suit = 皱巴巴的西装

rumpus
/ˈrʌmpəs/
核心：（口语）喧闹、吵闹；激烈的争吵：cause a rumpus、kick up a rumpus。可数，多用单数。
① The children were making such a rumpus upstairs that I could not hear the telephone.
= 孩子们在楼上闹得那么凶，我连电话都听不见。
make a rumpus = 吵闹
② The decision to cut the school's music lessons caused a rumpus among the parents.
= 削减学校音乐课的决定在家长中引起了一场轩然大波。
cause a rumpus = 引起轩然大波
口语色彩重，比 row、argument 轻松，常带点戏谑。

rumpy pumpy
/ˌrʌmpi ˈpʌmpi/
核心：（英式口语，幽默）性爱、云雨之事。不可数。多见于小报和喜剧的调侃口吻。
① The tabloids were full of stories about rumpy pumpy between the footballer and a television presenter.
= 小报上满是那名足球运动员和一位电视主持人风流韵事的报道。
rumpy pumpy = 风流韵事
② The comedy is a silly tale of rumpy pumpy in a country hotel, with lovers hiding in every cupboard.
= 那部喜剧讲的是乡间旅馆里一段荒唐的风流事，每个衣柜里都藏着情人。
a tale of rumpy pumpy = 风流故事

run
/rʌn/
核心：本义是「快速地向前移动」，过去式 ran、过去分词 run。由此长出四大支：
一、人和动物的移动：跑、奔跑：run fast、run for the bus；跑（比赛）：run a marathon；（口语）开车送：run somebody home。
二、东西的运转与延续：（机器）运转：an engine runs；（车辆）按班次运行：buses run every ten minutes；（道路、河流）延伸：the road runs beside the river；（戏剧、合同）持续、上演：run for three years；运行（程序）、进行（检查）：run tests；（报纸）刊登：run a story；竞选：run for president。
三、液体的流动：（液体）流：water runs；放（水）：run a bath；流鼻涕：your nose is running；（颜色）掉色、洇开：colours run。
四、经营与掌控：经营、管理：run a business、run the show。
名词：跑步：go for a run；一连串：a run of bad luck；（银行）挤兑：a bank run；（板球、棒球）一分；连续上演的场次：a successful run；（丝袜）脱丝；（家禽的）圈栏：a chicken run；滑雪道：a ski run。
习语：in the long run 是从长远看；on the run 是在逃；make a run for it 是拔腿就跑；something runs in the family 是家族遗传；run wild / run riot 是撒野、闹事；run short 是快用完；run dry 是干涸；run its course 是自然发展到结束；run late 是晚点；run a temperature 是发烧；in / out of the running 是有 / 没有获胜希望。
① As soon as the bell rang for the end of the school day, the children ran as fast as they could towards the gate.
= 放学铃一响，孩子们就拼命朝校门跑去。
run = 跑
② My sister ran her first marathon last spring and finished in just under four hours.
= 我姐姐去年春天跑了她的第一个马拉松，用时不到四个小时。
run a marathon = 跑马拉松
及物用法，宾语是赛程或距离：run a race、run ten kilometres。
③ I had to run for the bus this morning because my alarm clock did not go off.
= 我今天早上闹钟没响，只好跑着去赶公交车。
run for the bus = 跑着赶公交车
④ Her parents run a small hotel by the sea and work seven days a week in the summer.
= 她父母在海边经营一家小旅馆，夏天一周干七天。
run = 经营、管理
run a hotel = 经营旅馆
「经营」这一支是把「跑」引申为「让事情转起来」，宾语是店、公司、学校、项目。
⑤ The manager thinks he is in charge, but everyone knows that his assistant really runs the show.
= 经理以为是他说了算，可谁都知道真正主事的是他的助理。
run the show = 主事、说了算
⑥ The engine was still running when the police arrived and found the car empty by the side of the road.
= 警察赶到时，那辆车停在路边、里面没人，发动机还在转。
an engine runs = 发动机运转
机器义是「转动不停」，也说 leave the engine running。
⑦ The buses run every ten minutes during the day but only once an hour after midnight.
= 白天公交车每十分钟一班，半夜以后一小时才一班。
buses run = 公交车运行
说班次、线路时用一般现在时，强调按时刻表固定运行。
⑧ The old railway line runs beside the river for about forty miles before it turns towards the hills.
= 那条老铁路沿着河延伸大约四十英里，然后转向山里。
a road runs beside something = 道路沿某处延伸
⑨ The musical ran for three years in London before it moved to New York.
= 那部音乐剧在伦敦连演三年，之后才移师纽约。
a play runs = 连续上演
「延续」这一支也用于合同、展览、签证：run until、run for。
⑩ The doctors ran some tests on his heart but could not find anything wrong with it.
= 医生给他的心脏做了些检查，却没查出什么毛病。
run tests = 做检查
⑪ Several newspapers ran the story on their front pages the next morning, with photos of the fire.
= 第二天早上好几家报纸都在头版刊登了这条新闻，还配了火灾的照片。
run a story = 刊登报道
⑫ She has decided to run for president at the next election, even though she has little money.
= 她决定在下次选举中竞选总统，尽管她没什么钱。
run for president = 竞选总统
英式也常说 stand for，美式多用 run for。
⑬ Water ran from the broken pipe all night and flooded the kitchen floor before anyone woke up.
= 破水管里的水流了一整夜，大家还没醒，厨房地板就淹了。
run = （液体）流
water runs = 水流
「流」这一支的主语是液体，也可以是流着液体的东西：the tap is running。
⑭ Shall I run you a hot bath while you finish your dinner and read your letters?
= 你吃完饭、看信的时候，我给你放一缸热洗澡水好吗？
run a bath = 放洗澡水
⑮ My nose has been running all day, and I think I am getting a cold.
= 我一整天都在流鼻涕，我想是要感冒了。
your nose runs = 流鼻涕
⑯ I washed my new red shirt with the white sheets, and the colour ran and turned everything pink.
= 我把新红衬衫和白床单一起洗了，结果掉色，全都染成了粉红色。
colours run = 掉色
洗衣标签上常见 colours may run，提醒深浅分开洗。
⑰ Can I run you home after the party, or is your brother coming to collect you?
= 聚会结束后我开车送你回家吧，还是你哥哥会来接你？
run somebody home = 开车送某人回家
⑱ Red hair runs in the family; my mother, my aunt and my grandfather all had it.
= 红头发是家族遗传：我母亲、姑姑和祖父都是红头发。
something runs in the family = 家族遗传
⑲ The children were allowed to run wild in the woods behind the house all summer long.
= 整个夏天，孩子们都可以在屋后的树林里撒野。
run wild = 撒野
也说植物疯长：the garden had run wild。
⑳ Angry fans ran riot in the city centre after the match, breaking shop windows and setting fire to cars.
= 赛后愤怒的球迷在市中心闹事，砸商店橱窗、烧汽车。
run riot = 闹事、撒野
㉑ We ran short of milk on the camping trip and had to drink our tea black for two days.
= 露营途中我们的牛奶快用完了，只好喝了两天不加奶的茶。
run short of something = 某物快用完
指「还有一点但不够了」，主语也可以是东西：time is running short。
㉒ The village well ran dry during the long hot summer, so people had to carry water from the river.
= 漫长炎热的夏天里村里的井干了，人们只好从河里挑水。
run dry = 干涸
㉓ The doctor said there was no medicine for the virus and that it would simply have to run its course.
= 医生说这种病毒没药可治，只能等它自己好。
run its course = 自然发展到结束
㉔ The trains are running late because of the snow, so we will probably miss the start of the film.
= 火车因为下雪晚点了，我们多半赶不上电影开头了。
run late = 晚点
㉕ The baby is running a temperature, so I am taking her to see the doctor this afternoon.
= 宝宝在发烧，所以我今天下午带她去看医生。
run a temperature = 发烧
㉖ Whatever the weather is like, I go for a run before work every morning, usually twice around the park.
= 不管天气怎样，我每天早上上班前都去跑步，通常绕公园跑两圈。
go for a run = 去跑步
㉗ When the guard looked away for a moment, the prisoner made a run for it across the field.
= 看守一转眼的工夫，那个犯人就拔腿穿过田野逃跑了。
make a run for it = 拔腿就跑
㉘ The two men who robbed the bank were on the run for three weeks before the police caught them.
= 抢银行的那两个人在逃三个星期后才被警察抓住。
on the run = 在逃
口语里也指忙得团团转：I have been on the run all day。
㉙ Buying good shoes is cheaper in the long run, because cheap ones fall apart after a few months.
= 从长远看买好鞋更省钱，因为便宜的鞋几个月就坏了。
in the long run = 从长远看
反过来说眼前、短期是 in the short run。
㉚ The team has had a run of bad luck this season, with three players injured in the same week.
= 这个赛季球队霉运连连，同一个星期就有三名球员受伤。
a run of bad luck = 一连串霉运
㉛ News that the bank was in trouble started a bank run, as thousands of people tried to take out their savings.
= 那家银行有麻烦的消息传出后引发了挤兑，成千上万的人想把存款取出来。
a bank run = 银行挤兑
名词 run 的「蜂拥」义，也指人们争相抛售某种货币。
㉜ He scored a hundred runs in the cricket match and was named player of the day.
= 他在那场板球赛里得了一百分，被评为当天的最佳球员。
score runs = （板球、棒球）得分
㉝ Three candidates are still in the running for the job of head teacher at the village school.
= 村小学校长的职位还有三名候选人有希望。
in the running = 有希望获胜
㉞ After losing to Spain last night, our team is now out of the running for the cup.
= 昨晚输给西班牙之后，我们队已经没有夺杯的希望了。
out of the running = 没有希望获胜
㉟ The show enjoyed a successful run of two years in London and then went on a tour of the whole country.
= 那部剧在伦敦成功连演两年，之后又去全国巡演。
a successful run = 连演成功
㊱ Just before she was due to go on stage, she noticed that her new tights had a long run.
= 她就要上台时，发现新丝袜脱了一长道丝。
tights have a run = 丝袜脱丝
㊲ We keep six hens in a chicken run behind the shed and collect the eggs every morning.
= 我们在棚屋后面的鸡圈里养了六只母鸡，每天早上去捡蛋。
a chicken run = 鸡圈
㊳ Because of the high winds, all the ski runs were closed on the north side of the mountain.
= 因为风大，山北坡的滑雪道全都关了。
a ski run = 滑雪道
㊴ I ran across an old friend from school while I was waiting for a train in Manchester.
= 我在曼彻斯特等火车时偶然碰到了一个老同学。
run across = 偶然碰到、偶然发现
宾语可以是人，也可以是东西：run across an old letter。
㊵ Run along now, children, and let the grown-ups talk in peace for a while.
= 孩子们，现在去玩吧，让大人们安安静静地说一会儿话。
run along = （旧式口语）走开、去玩吧
多是大人打发孩子的话，对成年人说显得居高临下。
㊶ Her parents were worried because she had started to run around with a group of older boys.
= 她父母很担心，因为她开始跟一伙年纪大些的男孩混在一起。
run around with = 和……鬼混
口语，带不赞成的口气，常指和坏朋友混在一起。
㊷ The dog barked and ran at the postman, but it stopped as soon as he held out his hand.
= 那条狗冲着邮递员叫着扑过去，可他一伸手它就停住了。
run at = 向……冲过去
也指（比率）达到：inflation is running at five per cent。
㊸ Can I run a few ideas by you before I write the final report for the manager?
= 给经理写最终报告之前，我能先跟你说说我的几个想法吗？
run by = 把……说给某人听（征求意见）
结构是 run something by somebody，和 run something past somebody 同义。
㊹ Can I run these ideas past you before the meeting, just to hear what you honestly think?
= 开会前我能不能把这些想法先说给你听听，听听你的真实意见？
run past = 先给某人过目、征求意见
结构是 run something past somebody，口语常用。
㊺ The old man was run down by a car as he crossed the road outside the post office.
= 那位老人在邮局外面过马路时被一辆车撞倒了。
run down = 撞倒；（电池）耗尽；贬低
也说 the battery has run down、stop running down your own work。
㊻ The police ran him in for being drunk and shouting at people in the street late at night.
= 他深夜喝醉了在街上冲人嚷嚷，警察把他抓了进去。
run in = （口语）拘捕；磨合（新车）
英式也说 run in a new car，指新车头几百英里慢开磨合。
㊼ Guess who I ran into at the supermarket this morning? My old maths teacher from primary school!
= 你猜我今天早上在超市碰见谁了？我小学的数学老师！
run into = 偶然遇见；撞上；遇到（困难）
也说 run into trouble / problems，以及 the car ran into a tree。
㊽ The secretary ran off fifty copies of the timetable and put one in every teacher's pigeonhole.
= 秘书印了五十份时间表，在每位老师的信件格里各放了一份。
run off = 复印、印出；跑掉
也指逃跑：the thief ran off down the street。
㊾ The club treasurer ran off with all the money that the members had raised for the new roof.
= 俱乐部的财务卷走了会员们为修新屋顶筹的全部钱款。
run off with = 卷走（钱财）；和……私奔
也说 he ran off with his best friend's wife。
㊿ The meeting ran on for three hours, and by the end half the people in the room were asleep.
= 会一直开了三个小时，到最后屋里一半的人都睡着了。
run on = 持续得比预期久；（机器）以……为动力
也说 the bus runs on electricity，指车辆用什么作动力。
❶ The teacher marked several run-on sentences in my essay and told me to use more full stops.
= 老师在我的作文里标出了好几处串句，叫我多用句号。
a run-on sentence = 串句、流水句（该断不断的句子）
语法用语，指两个独立分句之间没有恰当的标点或连词。
❷ We ran out of petrol on the motorway and had to walk two miles to the nearest garage.
= 我们在高速公路上汽油用光了，只好走两英里去最近的加油站。
run out = 用完、耗尽；（期限）到期
也说 time is running out、my passport runs out next month。
❸ He ran out on his wife and three children and was never seen in the town again.
= 他抛下妻子和三个孩子跑了，从此再没在镇上露过面。
run out on = 抛弃（某人）
口语，指在别人需要时扔下他们不管。
❹ Be careful when you reverse; last week my neighbour ran over our cat in the drive.
= 倒车的时候小心点；上星期我邻居在车道上轧了我们家的猫。
run over = （车）轧过；超时；溢出
也说 the meeting ran over by twenty minutes、the bath ran over。
❺ Let's run through the plan once more before the guests arrive, so that nobody forgets anything.
= 客人到之前我们再把计划过一遍，免得谁忘了什么。
run through = 把……过一遍、排练；（想法）掠过
也说 a thought ran through my mind、run through a song。
❻ The cost of repairing the old church roof could run to more than a million pounds.
= 修那座老教堂屋顶的费用可能高达一百多万英镑。
run to = （数量）达到；（钱）够支付
也说 our budget won't run to a new car，指钱不够买。
❼ She ran up a huge bill on her credit card during her three weeks in New York.
= 她在纽约那三个星期，信用卡上欠下了一大笔账。
run up = 欠下（账、债）；赶制（衣服）
也说 run up a flag，指把旗子升起来。
❽ The newspaper decided to run with the story, even though some of the facts had not been checked.
= 尽管有些事实还没核实，那家报纸还是决定刊发这条新闻。
run with = 采纳、着手推进（想法、计划）
也说 let's run with your idea，指就照你的主意办。

/ˈrʌnɪŋ/
