secret
/ˈsiːkrət/
核心：名词，可数：秘密：keep a secret、tell somebody a secret；（单数）秘诀、诀窍：the secret of success；奥秘：the secrets of nature。形容词：秘密的、保密的：a secret meeting；（人）不公开的、暗中的：a secret admirer。副词 secretly 是秘密地、偷偷地。名词 secrecy 另立条。
习语：in secret 是秘密地；an open secret 是公开的秘密。
复合词：secret agent 是特工、密探；secret police 是秘密警察；secret service 是特工机构、情报机关。
① Can you keep a secret? I am going to ask her to marry me tonight.
= 你能保密吗？我今晚要向她求婚。
keep a secret = 保密
② The two countries held secret talks for months before they announced the agreement.
= 两国在宣布那项协议之前，秘密谈判了好几个月。
secret talks = 秘密谈判
secret = 秘密的
③ The secret of her success is hard work and a lot of patience.
= 她成功的秘诀是勤奋和极大的耐心。
the secret of = ……的秘诀
④ The group met in secret in a cellar under the old church.
= 那个团体在老教堂下面的一个地窖里秘密集会。
in secret = 秘密地
⑤ It is an open secret that the minister is going to resign after the summer.
= 部长夏天过后要辞职，这已是公开的秘密。
an open secret = 公开的秘密
⑥ He secretly saved money for years so that he could buy his wife a ring.
= 他偷偷攒了好几年钱，好给妻子买一枚戒指。
secretly = 偷偷地
⑦ In the film, the quiet bank clerk is really a secret agent working for the government.
= 那部电影里，那个沉默寡言的银行职员其实是为政府工作的特工。
a secret agent = 特工
⑧ People were afraid to speak freely because the secret police were everywhere.
= 人们不敢自由说话，因为到处都有秘密警察。
the secret police = 秘密警察
⑨ He worked for the secret service for thirty years, but his family never knew what he did.
= 他在情报机关工作了三十年，可家里人从不知道他是干什么的。
the secret service = 特工机构、情报机关

secretarial
/ˌsekrəˈteəriəl/
核心：形容词：秘书的、文秘的：secretarial work、a secretarial course。
① She did a secretarial course after school and got a job in a lawyer's office.
= 她中学毕业后上了一个文秘课程，在一家律师事务所找到了工作。
a secretarial course = 文秘课程
secretarial = 秘书的
② The office needs someone to help with typing, filing and other secretarial work.
= 办公室需要一个人帮忙打字、归档和做其他文秘工作。
secretarial work = 文秘工作
