harpoon
核心：名词与动词，鱼叉 —— 带倒钩的长矛，后面连着绳，用来捕鲸与大鱼；
现代的多由炮发射，那门炮叫 a harpoon gun。可数。
倒钩是关键：扎进去拔不出来，绳才拉得住 —— 这也是它跟普通鱼叉的分别。
① A harpoon line runs out faster than anybody can stop it.
= 鱼叉上的绳放出去的速度，谁也拦不住。
a harpoon = 鱼叉
② They harpooned the fish twice and lost it both times.
= 那条鱼他们叉中两回，两回都跑了。
harpoon something = 用鱼叉叉中某物
③ A harpoon gun on the bow was removed when the law changed.
= 法律一变，船头那门捕鲸炮就被拆了。
a harpoon gun = 捕鲸炮

harpsichord
核心：名词，羽管键琴 —— 巴洛克时期的键盘乐器：按键带动拨子拨弦，
不是像钢琴那样击弦。可数。
**关键的一条**：拨弦的力道固定，所以弹轻弹重出来一样响 ——
钢琴（本名 fortepiano，「强弱琴」）正是靠解决这一点把它取代了。
① A harpsichord sounds the same however hard you hit the keys.
= 羽管键琴不管你按多重，响度都一样。
a harpsichord = 羽管键琴
② The harpsichord needs tuning twice in one concert.
= 一场音乐会里，那台羽管键琴要调两次音。
the harpsichord = 那台羽管键琴
③ Two harpsichords in that hall are older than the hall itself.
= 那座厅里的两台羽管键琴，比厅本身还老。
harpsichords = 羽管键琴（复数）

harridan
核心：名词（旧，贬），凶婆子、泼妇 —— 骂上了年纪、脾气凶的女性。可数。
**这个词带着明显的年龄与性别歧视**，跟 hag 一路；今天只见于旧小说与引用，
**认得即可，别拿来说人**。
① A harridan in that novel runs the boarding house and everybody in it.
= 那部小说里的一个凶婆子，管着那家寄宿屋和屋里所有人。
a harridan = 凶婆子
② Calling a woman a harridan is a way of not answering her.
= 管一个女人叫泼妇，是一种回避她的办法。
call somebody a harridan = 管某人叫泼妇
③ Two harridans in that play are the only characters who tell the truth.
= 那出戏里被叫作泼妇的两个人，恰是唯一说实话的角色。
harridans = 泼妇（复数）

harrow
核心：两支。一是耙 —— 拖在犁后面把土块打碎、把地整平的农具，也作动词。
二是形容词 harrowing（令人揪心的），今天远比农具义常用，并入本条。
两支之间的桥很清楚：耙把地翻得稀烂，「揪心」用的正是那个画面。
① A harrow breaks up the clods that the plough turns over.
= 耙把犁翻起来的土块打碎。
a harrow = 耙
② They harrow that field twice before anything is sown.
= 播种之前，那块地要耙两遍。
harrow a field = 耙地
③ A harrowing account of the crossing takes up nine pages.
= 关于那次渡海的一段揪心记述，占了九页。
a harrowing account = 揪心的记述

harry
核心：动词（正式），袭扰、追逼 —— 军事上指反复骚扰而不决战，
日常指没完没了地催逼：harry somebody into doing something。
跟 harass 的分别：harass 说的多是对个人的持续骚扰，而且是法律用语；
harry 偏军事与政治，带着「追着打」的意味。
注意大写的 Harry 是人名，跟这个词没有关系。
① Light cavalry harried the column for three days without attacking it.
= 轻骑兵袭扰那支纵队三天，却没有正面进攻。
harry somebody = 袭扰某人
② The press harried the department into publishing the figures.
= 媒体一路追逼，迫使那个部门公布了数字。
harry somebody into doing something = 逼得某人做某事
③ They were harried from one office to another for four months.
= 四个月里，他们被从一个部门推到另一个部门。
be harried = 被追着折腾

harsh
核心：四支。一是（条件）严酷的：a harsh winter；二是（批评、惩罚）严厉的：
a harsh sentence；三是（声音、光线、颜色）刺耳刺眼的；
四是（口语）说人太狠了：that seems harsh。
副词 harshly 与名词 harshness 并入本条。
① A harsh winter in that valley cuts the road for weeks at a time.
= 那道山谷里的严冬，能把路一次断上好几周。
a harsh winter = 严冬
② A harsh sentence for a first offence was reduced on appeal.
= 初犯却判得很重，上诉之后减了刑。
a harsh sentence = 严厉的判决
③ The light in that room is harsh and nobody looks well in it.
= 那屋里的光很刺眼，谁在里头气色都不好。
be harsh = 刺眼的
④ He was judged harshly by people who had not read the file.
= 他被一群没读过卷宗的人严厉评判。
harshly = 严厉地
⑤ The harshness of that reply cost more than the mistake did.
= 那个回复的严苛，代价比那个错误本身还大。
harshness = 严苛

hart
核心：名词（古旧或诗体），牡鹿 —— 尤指成年的雄赤鹿。可数。
今天只见于旧文、民谣、纹章与店名地名；现代英语一律说 a stag 或 a male deer。
它跟 hind（母鹿）成对，是英语里最古老的一组动物称谓。
① A hart in that ballad leads the hunters into the water.
= 那首民谣里的牡鹿，把猎人引进了水里。
a hart = 牡鹿
② Two harts fought on the ridge and the herd waited below.
= 两头牡鹿在山脊上角斗，鹿群在下面等着。
harts = 牡鹿（复数）
③ The inn has been called the White Hart since the fifteenth century.
= 那家客栈从十五世纪起就叫「白牡鹿」。
the White Hart = 白牡鹿（常见店名）

harvest
核心：三支。一是收割、收获的时节：the harvest；二是收成（收上来的量）：
a good harvest；三是动词，收割、采集 —— 今天也说采集数据与摘取器官：
harvest data。名词 harvester（收割机；收割的人）并入本条。
搭配：a harvest festival（丰收节）、a bumper harvest（大丰收）。
① The harvest starts when the weather says so and not when the calendar does.
= 收割什么时候开始，看天说了算，不看日历。
the harvest = 收割时节
② A good harvest two years running paid off the machinery.
= 连着两年好收成，把机器的钱还清了。
a good harvest = 好收成
③ They harvest the data every night and look at it once a quarter.
= 他们每晚采集数据，一个季度才看一次。
harvest data = 采集数据
④ The harvester broke down in the one dry week of that month.
= 收割机偏偏在那个月唯一一个干燥的星期坏了。
a harvester = 收割机
⑤ The harvest festival fills a church that is empty the rest of the year.
= 丰收节把一座一年到头空着的教堂填满了。
a harvest festival = 丰收节
⑥ The harvest moon came up orange and big enough to work by.
= 收获月升起来时是橙色的，大得能就着它干活。
the harvest moon = 秋分前后的那轮满月
离秋分最近的一次满月，升得早、看着特别大。
⑦ A harvest mouse weighs less than a two-pound coin and climbs wheat stalks.
= 巢鼠比一枚两镑硬币还轻，能顺着麦秆往上爬。
a harvest mouse = 巢鼠（欧洲最小的鼠类）

hash
核心：四支。一是（烹饪）碎肉土豆煎饼：corned beef hash；
二是习语 make a hash of something，把某事搞砸；
三是井号「#」：the hash key —— 美式叫 the pound key 或 hashtag 里的那个符号；
四是（口语）大麻树脂 hashish 的缩略。
名词 hashtag 并入本条。
① Corned beef hash uses up what is left and tastes better than it sounds.
= 咸牛肉土豆饼是用剩料做的，比听着好吃。
corned beef hash = 咸牛肉土豆饼
② He made a hash of the first attempt and got it right on the second.
= 头一回他搞砸了，第二回做对了。
make a hash of something = 把某事搞砸
③ Press the hash key twice and the machine starts again.
= 按两下井号键，机器就重启。
the hash key = 井号键
④ One hashtag carried that campaign further than any advertisement.
= 一个话题标签，把那场活动带得比任何广告都远。
a hashtag = 话题标签
⑤ Hashish was sold openly in that quarter for about two years.
= 有那么两年，那一带公开卖大麻树脂。
hashish = 大麻树脂
口语缩略成 hash，跟前面几支只是同形。
⑥ Hash browns come with everything in that café, whether you ask or not.
= 那家馆子什么都配薯饼，你问不问都一样。
hash browns = 薯饼（切碎煎成的马铃薯）
恒作复数；美式早餐的常客。
⑦ They hashed out the last two clauses in a corridor and signed at midnight.
= 最后两个条款是他们在走廊里吵定的，半夜签的字。
hash something out = 争论着把某事谈定（美）
hash out 跟 hammer out 差不多，只是更口语、更带着吵的意味。

Hasid
核心：哈西德派犹太人 —— 十八世纪东欧兴起的犹太教虔敬运动的信徒。
复数 Hasidim。这个拼法比 Chasid 常见；两条各举各的例子，内容互补。
可数。
① A Hasid on the next seat prayed quietly through the whole of the flight.
= 邻座的一位哈西德派教徒，整趟航程都在低声祷告。
a Hasid = 哈西德派犹太人
② Hasidim from four countries filled the hall on the anniversary of the rebbe's death.
= 拉比忌日那天，来自四个国家的哈西德派信徒挤满了大厅。
Hasidim = 哈西德派教徒（复数）
③ A Hasid follows one particular rebbe, and that choice decides which group he belongs to.
= 哈西德派教徒各追随一位拉比，追随谁就决定了他属于哪一支。
follow one particular rebbe = 追随某一位拉比
注意拼写：Chasid 与 Chassid 是同一个词的别种转写，各有词条。

Hasidic
核心：哈西德派的 —— 形容那一支犹太教的做法、音乐、服饰与社区。
这个拼法比 Chasidic 常见。常搭 a Hasidic court（某位拉比及其追随者）、
Hasidic dress、Hasidic tales。
① The boys in Hasidic dress were counted twice and photographed once.
= 那些穿哈西德派服饰的男孩被点了两遍名、拍了一次照。
Hasidic dress = 哈西德派服饰
② Hasidic tales are short, funny and end somewhere you did not expect.
= 哈西德派的故事短小诙谐，结尾总落在你想不到的地方。
Hasidic tales = 哈西德派故事
③ Each Hasidic court keeps its own tunes and will not borrow another's.
= 每个哈西德派门庭守着自己的曲调，不肯借用别家的。
a Hasidic court = 哈西德派门庭（一位拉比及其追随者）
注意拼写：Chasidic 是同一个词的别种转写，另有词条。

Hasidism
核心：哈西德主义：那一支犹太教运动本身。它主张亲近上帝不必靠深奥的
经院学问，普通人凭虔诚与喜乐也做得到 —— 正因为这一点，
它在东欧穷苦犹太社群里传得极快，也正因为这一点，遭到学者一派的抵制。
不可数。这个拼法比 Chasidism 常见。
① Hasidism put a living teacher between the believer and the book.
= 哈西德主义在信徒与经书之间安进了一位活着的老师。
Hasidism = 哈西德主义
② Two centuries on, Hasidism is the fastest-growing branch of Orthodox Judaism.
= 两百年后的今天，哈西德主义是正统犹太教中增长最快的一支。
Hasidism = 哈西德主义（作主语）
③ The opponents of Hasidism were called the mitnagdim, which simply means opponents.
= 哈西德主义的反对者被称作「反对派」，这个词的意思就是反对者。
the opponents of Hasidism = 哈西德主义的反对者
注意拼写：Chasidism 是同一个词的别种转写，另有词条。

hasp
核心：名词，搭扣 —— 装挂锁用的那副铁片：一片带环，一片带槽，扣上再穿锁。可数。
跟 latch 的分别：latch 自己就能把门关住，hasp 只是为挂锁准备的，
单靠它锁不住任何东西。
① A hasp and a padlock hold that shed better than the door does.
= 一副搭扣加一把挂锁，比那扇门本身还顶用。
a hasp = 搭扣
② The hasp was unscrewed from the outside and left on the step.
= 那副搭扣是从外面拧下来的，就撂在台阶上。
the hasp = 那副搭扣
③ Two hasps on one gate means two people have keys.
= 一道门上装两副搭扣，说明有两个人各拿着钥匙。
hasps = 搭扣（复数）

hassle
核心：两支（口语）。一是名词，麻烦事、费劲的事：it is too much hassle；
二是动词，缠着人催逼：hassle somebody about something。可数与不可数都有。
跟 trouble 的分别：trouble 可以很严重，hassle 只是嫌麻烦 ——
费时费事，但谈不上危险。
① Parking there is more hassle than the meeting is worth.
= 在那儿停车的麻烦，比那场会本身还不值。
be a hassle = 麻烦、费事
② Stop hassling me about a form that is not due until June.
= 别再为一张六月才交的表催我了。
hassle somebody about something = 为某事催逼某人
③ They went by train to avoid the hassle of two airports.
= 他们坐火车，省得在两个机场之间折腾。
the hassle of something = 某事的麻烦

hassock
核心：两支。一是（英）跪垫 —— 教堂长椅前跪着祷告用的厚垫；
二是（美）软墩、脚凳。可数。
英国乡村教堂有一项传统：跪垫由教友一块一块手工绣制，每块一个图案 ——
所以那一排垫子常常就是一部村史。
① A hassock in every pew is embroidered by a different person.
= 每排长椅前的跪垫，都是不同的人绣的。
a hassock = 跪垫
② She put her feet on the hassock and fell asleep in the chair.
= 她把脚搁在软墩上，在椅子里睡着了。
the hassock = 软墩
③ Two hundred hassocks took that parish eleven years to finish.
= 两百块跪垫，那个教区绣了十一年。
hassocks = 跪垫（复数）

haste
核心：名词，匆忙 —— 不可数，多用在两个格式里：in haste（匆忙地）
与 make haste（赶快，旧）。
动词 hasten（加快；赶紧）、形容词 hasty 与副词 hastily 并入本条。
谚语 more haste, less speed：越急越出错，反而更慢。
现代英语里最常用的一支其实是 hasten to add / hasten to say：赶紧补一句。
① A note written in haste explains less than no note at all.
= 匆匆写下的字条，解释得还不如不写。
in haste = 匆忙地
② More haste, less speed — the second attempt took half the time.
= 欲速则不达：第二次反而只花了一半时间。
more haste, less speed = 欲速则不达
③ She hastened to add that the figures were three years old.
= 她赶紧补了一句：那些数字是三年前的。
hasten to add = 赶紧补一句
④ A hasty decision in the first week cost them the whole year.
= 头一周一个仓促的决定，毁了他们一整年。
a hasty decision = 仓促的决定
⑤ He hastily corrected himself and made the whole thing worse.
= 他匆忙纠正自己，反而把事情弄得更糟。
hastily = 匆忙地

hat
核心：两支，外加七条习语与一族复合词。一是帽子 —— 有檐或成形的那种
（软针织帽多说 a cap 或 a beanie）；二是比喻，身份与角色：wear two hats。
习语：at the drop of a hat（一声招呼就动手）、old hat（老一套）、
take your hat off to somebody（佩服某人）、keep something under your hat（保密）、
pass the hat round（凑份子）、throw your hat into the ring（宣布参选）、
talk through your hat（信口开河）。
复合词：a hat-trick、a hatstand、a hatbox。
① A hat that size needs one hand on it in any wind at all.
= 那么大的一顶帽子，只要有点风就得用手按着。
a hat = 帽子
② She wears two hats in that organization and gets paid for one.
= 她在那个机构里身兼两职，却只拿一份钱。
wear two hats = 身兼两职
③ He would drive four hours at the drop of a hat.
= 一声招呼，他就能开四个钟头的车。
at the drop of a hat = 一声招呼就动手
④ That idea is old hat in every trade except this one.
= 那个想法在别的行当早就不新鲜了，只有这一行还当新东西。
old hat = 老一套、不新鲜
⑤ I take my hat off to anybody who finished that form alone.
= 谁能一个人填完那张表，我都佩服。
take your hat off to somebody = 佩服某人
⑥ Keep it under your hat until the letters have gone out.
= 信寄出去之前，这事先别声张。
keep something under your hat = 保密
⑦ They passed the hat round and had enough by the second table.
= 他们凑了一圈份子，到第二桌就够了。
pass the hat round = 凑份子
⑧ She threw her hat into the ring a week before nominations closed.
= 提名截止前一周，她宣布参选。
throw your hat into the ring = 宣布参选
⑨ He is talking through his hat and everybody in the trade knows it.
= 他在信口开河，行里人人都知道。
talk through your hat = 信口开河
⑩ A hat-trick in that game had not been scored for eleven years.
= 那种比赛里的帽子戏法，十一年没出现过了。
a hat-trick = 帽子戏法
⑪ The hatstand by the door holds four coats and not one hat.
= 门边那个衣帽架挂着四件外套，一顶帽子也没有。
a hatstand = 衣帽架
a hatbox = 帽盒
A hatbox from that shop is worth more now than the hat inside it.
= 那家店的帽盒，如今比里头的帽子还值钱。
⑫ A silk hatband in a different colour marks the regiment he once served with.
= 一条颜色不同的丝质帽带，标出他从前服役的团。
a hatband = 帽带
帽冠底部那一圈带子；换颜色、换徽记是学校与军团区分身份的老办法。
⑬ She pushed a long hatpin through the crown and the wind never took it again.
= 她把一根长帽针穿过帽顶，风再没把帽子刮走过。
a hatpin = 帽针
旧时女帽全靠它固定在发髻上，长到十英寸，一度被当作防身武器。
⑭ The hatter measured his head twice and charged him for the second attempt.
= 帽匠量了两遍他的头围，第二遍还照收了钱。
a hatter = 帽匠
⑮ He has been as mad as a hatter since the second winter he spent up there alone.
= 自从独自在山上过了第二个冬天，他就疯疯癫癫的了。
as mad as a hatter = 疯疯癫癫
旧时制毡帽要用汞处理毛料，帽匠中毒后手抖神乱，这个说法就是这么来的。
⑯ A hat tip to the reader who spotted the error in the second paragraph.
= 向发现第二段那个错的读者致意。
a hat tip = 致意、鸣谢（网络上常缩成 h/t）
本义是抬一下帽檐打招呼；如今主要用在网上标明消息来源。

hatch
核心：四支。一是（蛋）孵化、破壳：the eggs hatched；二是动词，策划：hatch a plan；
三是名词，舱口、递物窗口：a hatch in the wall；四是（绘画）排线。
名词 hatchback（掀背车）、hatchery（孵化场）与 hatchet（短柄斧）并入本条。
习语：don't count your chickens before they hatch（别高兴太早）、
down the hatch（干杯时说的「干了」）。
① The eggs hatched three days late and all of them survived.
= 那些蛋晚了三天才孵，全都活了下来。
hatch = 孵化
② They hatched a plan that needed four people and got two.
= 他们谋划了一个要四个人的计划，结果只来了两个。
hatch a plan = 谋划一个计划
③ Food comes through a hatch in the wall and nobody sees the kitchen.
= 饭菜从墙上的窗口递出来，没人看得见厨房。
a hatch = 递物窗口、舱口
④ A hatchback holds more than an estate car of the same length.
= 同样长度下，掀背车比旅行车装得多。
a hatchback = 掀背车
⑤ The hatchery releases nine thousand fish and counts the ones that return.
= 那家孵化场放流九千条鱼，再数有多少回来。
a hatchery = 孵化场
⑥ A hatchet and a bow saw do everything that needs doing up there.
= 一把短柄斧加一把弓锯，那上头要干的活全够了。
a hatchet = 短柄斧
⑦ Do not count your chickens before they hatch — the offer is not signed.
= 别高兴太早，那个报价还没签字。
count your chickens before they hatch = 高兴得太早
⑧ Every hatchling makes for the water on its own within a minute of breaking out.
= 每只刚出壳的幼龟，破壳后不到一分钟就自己往水里爬。
a hatchling = 刚孵出的幼雏（幼龟、幼鸟）
复数 hatchlings 更常见，因为它们总是整窝一起出壳。
⑨ The hatchway was bolted from the inside and nobody on deck heard the knocking.
= 舱口从里面闩上了，甲板上没人听见敲击声。
a hatchway = 舱口、升降口
指开口本身，盖在上面的那块板才是 hatch cover。

hate
核心：三支。一是恨、厌恶：hate somebody；二是（口语里弱化了的）不愿意、不喜欢：
I hate to ask；三是名词，仇恨：hate speech、a hate crime。
名词 hatred 与形容词 hateful 并入本条。
**语气跨度很大**：口语里 I hate mushrooms 只是「不爱吃」，
可 hate speech 与 hate crime 里的 hate 是最重的那一支，是法律术语 ——
同一个词，两头差得远。
① They hated each other for a decade and worked in the same room.
= 他们彼此厌恶了十年，还在同一间屋里上班。
hate somebody = 厌恶某人
② I hate to ask, but the second invoice has not arrived either.
= 不好意思开口，可第二张发票也没到。
I hate to ask = 不好意思开口
③ A hate crime is prosecuted differently and reported far less often.
= 仇恨犯罪的起诉方式不同，报案率却低得多。
a hate crime = 仇恨犯罪
hate speech = 仇恨言论
Hate speech is defined differently in every country that bans it.
= 每个禁止仇恨言论的国家，对它的定义都不一样。
④ She hates waiting far more than she hates the appointment itself.
= 比起看病本身，她更讨厌等。
hate doing something = 讨厌做某事
⑤ Hatred of that kind takes a generation to cool.
= 那种仇恨，要一代人才凉得下来。
hatred = 仇恨
⑥ A hateful remark on page one ruined a book that got better later.
= 头一页一句可憎的话，毁了一本后面越写越好的书。
hateful = 可憎的
⑦ Every hater in the comments had watched the whole film twice before complaining.
= 评论区里每一个开骂的人，都是把整部片子看了两遍才来抱怨的。
a hater = 一味挑刺、恶意攻击的人（口语）
网络用语，复数 haters 比单数常见得多，最出名的说法是 haters gonna hate。
⑧ A campaign built on hatred of one small group ended by frightening everybody.
= 一场建立在仇视某个小群体之上的运动，最后把所有人都吓着了。
hatred = 仇恨、憎恶
不可数；常见搭配是 racial hatred、stir up hatred、a deep hatred of something。
比 hate 作名词更书面，程度也更重。
⑨ Hate mail arrived for a fortnight and then stopped as suddenly as it started.
= 恐吓谩骂的信来了两个星期，然后来得多突然、停得多突然。
hate mail = 谩骂恐吓的信件
不可数；寄的人多半匿名。

haughty
核心：形容词，傲慢的 —— 摆出一副看不起人的样子：a haughty manner。
副词 haughtily 与名词 haughtiness 并入本条。
词跟 high 同源，经法语 haut 进来，字面就是「把自己摆得高」——
同源的还有名词 hauteur，另立一条。
跟 proud 的分别：proud 可以是褒义（为某事自豪），haughty 一定是贬的。
① A haughty manner at that counter loses the shop two customers a day.
= 柜台上那副傲慢的态度，一天丢掉两个顾客。
a haughty manner = 傲慢的态度
② She looked haughtily at the form and then filled it in perfectly.
= 她轻蔑地看了那张表一眼，却填得一丝不苟。
haughtily = 轻蔑地
③ The haughtiness of that reply was noticed by everybody who read it.
= 那个回复的傲气，凡读过的人都看出来了。
haughtiness = 傲气

haul
核心：四支。一是（用力）拖、拉：haul something out of somewhere；
二是名词，一段难走的路程：a long haul；三是名词，一次的捕获量或缴获物：
a haul of fish、a drugs haul；四是货运：road haulage。
名词 haulage 与 haulier（货运商，英式；美式说 hauler）并入本条。
习语 the long haul 指长期的辛苦：be in it for the long haul。
① They hauled the boat out of the water before the storm arrived.
= 风暴来之前，他们把船拖上了岸。
haul something out of somewhere = 把某物从某处拖出来
② It is a long haul to that coast and the last hour is the worst.
= 到那片海岸路途遥远，最后一个钟头最难熬。
a long haul = 长途跋涉
③ A haul of fish that size fills the hold and ruins the price.
= 那么大的一网鱼，装满了鱼舱，也把价钱压垮了。
a haul of fish = 一网鱼
④ Road haulage in that country moves more than the railways do.
= 那个国家的公路货运，运量比铁路还大。
road haulage = 公路货运
⑤ A haulier with two lorries competes with companies that have four hundred.
= 一个只有两辆卡车的货运商，要跟四百辆的公司竞争。
a haulier = 货运商
⑥ They are in it for the long haul and say so every single year.
= 他们打算长期干下去，而且年年都这么说。
be in it for the long haul = 打算长期干下去
a long haul flight = 长途航班
A long haul flight in that seat is eleven hours of regret.
= 坐那种座位飞长途，是十一个钟头的后悔。
in the long haul = 从长远看（美式，同 in the long run）
Those repairs pay for themselves in the long haul.
= 那些修缮从长远看能把钱赚回来。

haunch
核心：两支。一是臀部与大腿上部 —— 多用复数：sit on your haunches 是蹲着，
屁股不挨地；二是（动物的）后腿肉：a haunch of venison。可数。
跟 squat 的分别：squat 说的是动作，on your haunches 说的是保持着的那个姿势。
① He sat on his haunches beside the wheel for twenty minutes.
= 他在车轮旁蹲了二十分钟。
sit on your haunches = 蹲着
② The dog dropped onto its haunches and refused to move at all.
= 那条狗一屁股蹲下，赖着不动。
drop onto its haunches = 蹲坐下来
③ A haunch of venison feeds a table of eight with something left over.
= 一条鹿后腿够八个人吃，还有剩。
a haunch of venison = 一条鹿后腿

haunt
核心：四支。一是（鬼魂）出没于：the house is haunted；
二是（记忆、决定）缠着人不放：that decision haunts him；
三是（问题）长期困扰：be haunted by debt；四是名词，常去的地方：an old haunt。
形容词 haunted 与 haunting 并入本条。
**haunting 是褒义的**：说一段旋律 haunting，是说它美得让人忘不掉 ——
跟前几支的阴郁完全不同，这一点最容易误用。
① The house is haunted according to four people and no records at all.
= 有四个人说那房子闹鬼，档案上却一笔记载也没有。
be haunted = 闹鬼
② That decision has haunted him for eleven years.
= 那个决定缠了他十一年。
haunt somebody = 缠着某人不放
③ The company was haunted by a debt it had already paid twice.
= 那家公司被一笔已经还过两遍的债缠得不得安生。
be haunted by something = 被某事长期困扰
④ They went back to an old haunt and found it had become a bank.
= 他们回到从前常去的地方，发现那里成了一家银行。
an old haunt = 从前常去的地方
⑤ A haunting melody from that film turns up in supermarkets.
= 那部电影里一段萦绕难忘的旋律，如今连超市里都放。
a haunting melody = 萦绕难忘的旋律
⑥ The tune is hauntingly close to a hymn that nobody has sung for a century.
= 那支曲子像极了一首一百年没人唱过的圣歌，听得人心里发紧。
hauntingly = 令人难忘地、萦绕心头地
形容美得或哀得挥之不去，常见的是 hauntingly beautiful。

Hausa
核心：两支。一是豪萨人：西非最大的民族之一，主要在尼日利亚北部与尼日尔；
二是豪萨语 —— 西非最通行的商贸共同语，说的人以千万计，
用拉丁字母也用阿拉伯字母书写。也作形容词。
① Hausa is the language most people reach for in a market across that whole region.
= 在那一带的市场上，多数人开口就用豪萨语。
Hausa = 豪萨语
② The Hausa have traded across the desert edge for a very long time.
= 豪萨人在沙漠边缘做买卖已有很久的历史。
the Hausa = 豪萨人
③ The broadcast goes out in Hausa at six and in English an hour later.
= 那档节目六点用豪萨语播出，一小时后再播英语版。
in Hausa = 用豪萨语

hauteur
核心：名词（正式、书面），高傲、居高临下的姿态。不可数，从法语借来，
读作 oh-TUR —— **开头的 h 不发音**，这是它最常被读错的地方。
跟 haughty 同源（都来自法语的 haut「高」），但 hauteur 是名词、更书面，
还带一点讽刺的距离感：用它多半是在写一个人，而不是当面骂人。
① The hauteur of that reply turned a small complaint into a legal letter.
= 那个回复的高傲，把一桩小投诉变成了一封律师函。
the hauteur of something = 某事透出的高傲
② She received the inspectors with a hauteur that emptied the room by four.
= 她以一种高傲的姿态接待检查组，四点前屋里就走空了。
with hauteur = 带着高傲
③ Hauteur in that trade passes for standards until somebody checks the accounts.
= 在那一行，高傲被当成有标准 —— 直到有人去查账。
hauteur = 高傲
