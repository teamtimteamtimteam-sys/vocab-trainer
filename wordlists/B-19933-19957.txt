/seɪ/
核心：动词（过去式和过去分词 said）：说、讲：say something、say that…、say something to somebody；（文字、钟表、标志）写着、显示：the sign says…；（表示看法）认为、以为：I'd say…；（假设）假如、比方说：say you won…、let's say；（举例）比如：say, ten pounds。名词，单数或不可数：发言权、决定权：have a say in something、have your say。和 tell 的分别：say 后面直接接说的话，tell 后面接人：tell somebody something。
习语：that is to say 是也就是说；needless to say 是不用说；you can say that again 是你说得太对了；to say the least 是至少可以这么说（往轻里说）；it goes without saying 是不言而喻；when all is said and done 是归根到底；I must say 是说真的、不得不说。on somebody's say-so 是凭某人一句话的许可、授权。形容词 said（法律）是上述的、该：the said property。
① "I'm really tired after the long journey," she said, and went straight to bed without eating.
= 「长途旅行后我真的累了。」她说完，饭也没吃就直接上床了。
say = 说
② He said that he would be home by six, but he did not arrive until nine.
= 他说他六点前到家，可直到九点才回来。
say something = 说某事
said = 说（say 的过去式、过去分词）
③ Don't say anything to your sister about the party; it is a surprise.
= 聚会的事别跟你妹妹说，这是个惊喜。
say something to somebody = 对某人说某事
近义对照：
tell somebody = 告诉某人
say 不能直接接人作宾语，不说 say me，要说 tell me 或 say to me。
④ The sign on the door says that the shop is closed on Mondays.
= 门上的牌子写着那家店星期一休息。
the sign says = 牌子上写着
⑤ I'd say she is about forty, but she looks much younger in photos.
= 我看她大约四十岁，不过照片上显得年轻得多。
I'd say = 我看、我认为
⑥ Say you won a million pounds tomorrow, what would you do with it?
= 假如你明天中了一百万英镑，你会拿它来干什么？
say = 假如
⑦ We could meet at, say, eight o'clock outside the cinema, if that suits you.
= 要是你方便的话，我们可以在，比如说，八点钟在电影院外面见。
say = 比如说
⑧ The workers want to have a say in how the company is run.
= 工人们希望在公司的经营方式上有发言权。
have a say in something = 在某事上有发言权
⑨ Everyone will get the chance to have their say at the meeting on Friday.
= 星期五的会上每个人都有机会发表意见。
have your say = 发表意见
⑩ The film was disappointing, to say the least; half the audience left before the end.
= 那部电影至少可以说令人失望，一半观众没看完就走了。
to say the least = 至少可以这么说
⑪ Needless to say, we missed the last train and had to walk home in the rain.
= 不用说，我们错过了末班车，只好冒雨走回家。
needless to say = 不用说
⑫ It goes without saying that you can stay with us whenever you come to London.
= 不用说，你什么时候来伦敦都可以住我们家。
it goes without saying = 不言而喻
⑬ "This coffee is terrible." "You can say that again — I am going to complain."
= 「这咖啡真难喝。」「你说得太对了，我要去投诉。」
you can say that again = 你说得太对了
⑭ The two cities are very different; that is to say, one is old and quiet and the other is new and busy.
= 这两座城市很不一样，也就是说，一座古老安静，一座新兴繁忙。
that is to say = 也就是说
⑮ When all is said and done, he is still your brother, and you should help him.
= 归根到底，他还是你哥哥，你应该帮他。

⑯ I must say, I did not expect the food to be this good in such a small village.

⑰ In this bank, nobody can open the safe in the back office without the manager's say-so.
= 在这家银行，没有经理的许可，谁都不能打开后台办公室的保险柜。

= 我们在港口边一家小餐馆吃了大蒜黄油煎新鲜扇贝。
scallops = 扇贝
② The edge of my grandmother's best tablecloth was decorated with tiny white scallops of lace.
= 祖母最好的那块桌布，边上饰有白色的小扇形蕾丝花边。
scallops = 扇形花边
