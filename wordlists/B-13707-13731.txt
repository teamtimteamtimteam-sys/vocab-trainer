lonely
/ˈləʊnli/
核心：孤独的、寂寞的；（地方）偏僻的、人迹罕至的。名词 loneliness 并入本条；
lonely heart（寻找伴侣的单身者）、lonely hearts column（征友栏）。
① She felt lonely in the big city during her first winter there.
= 在那座大城市的头一个冬天，她感到孤独。
feel lonely = 感到孤独
② The farm stands on a lonely road five miles from the village.
= 那座农场坐落在离村子五英里的一条偏僻路上。
a lonely road = 偏僻的路
③ He spent many lonely evenings in his hotel room.
= 他在酒店房间里度过了许多寂寞的夜晚。
lonely evenings = 寂寞的夜晚
④ Loneliness is a serious problem among old people who live alone.
= 孤独是独居老人中的一个严重问题。
loneliness = 孤独
⑤ They met through the lonely hearts column of a local newspaper.
= 他们是通过当地一家报纸的征友栏认识的。
a lonely hearts column = 征友栏
a lonely heart = 寻找伴侣的单身者
alone 只是「一个人」，lonely 才是「心里孤单」。

loner
/ˈləʊnə(r)/
核心：独来独往的人 —— 喜欢独处、不爱结伴的人。
① He was a loner at school and spent his lunch breaks in the library.
= 他上学时独来独往，午休都泡在图书馆里。
a loner = 独来独往的人
② She is something of a loner and has few close friends.
= 她有点不合群，没几个亲近的朋友。
something of a loner = 有点不合群
不一定是贬义。

lonesome
/ˈləʊnsəm/
核心：（美）孤独的、寂寞的；偏僻荒凉的。
① I get lonesome when the children are away at camp.
= 孩子们去夏令营的时候，我会觉得寂寞。
get lonesome = （美）觉得寂寞
② The train whistle sounded across the lonesome prairie.
= 火车的汽笛声在荒凉的草原上回荡。
the lonesome prairie = 荒凉的草原
乡村歌曲里的常客；英式说 lonely。

long
/lɒŋ/
核心：长的；（时间）久的；有…长。副词：长久地。动词（另一个词）：渴望 —— long for、long to do。
比较级 longer，最高级 longest。形容词 longish（偏长的）、副词 longways（纵向地）与 longed-for（盼望已久的）并入本条。
习语：as long as / so long as（只要）、no longer（不再）、before long（不久）、for long（很久，多用于否定、疑问）、
long ago（很久以前）、all day long（一整天）、in the long run（从长远看）、at long last（终于）、
the long and the short of it（总而言之）。
复合词：long-term（长期的）、long-distance（长途的；long distance 长途）、long-range（远程的；长远的）、
long-standing（由来已久的）、long-running（持续多年的）、long-lasting（持久的）、long-lived（长寿的）、
long-life（英：可长期保存的）、long-serving（任职多年的）、long-time（多年的）、long-stay（长期停留的）、
long-awaited（期待已久的）、long-lost（失散多年的）、long-drawn-out（拖得太久的）、long-winded（啰嗦的）、
long-suffering（长期忍受的）、long-sighted（英：远视的；名词 long-sightedness）。
搭配：long jump（跳远）、long weekend（连休的长周末）、long wave（长波）、long division（长除法）、
long johns（秋裤）、long drink（大杯饮料）、long ball（足球：长传）、long-distance footpath（英：长距离步道）。
① She has long dark hair and green eyes.
= 她有一头长长的黑发和一双绿眼睛。
long hair = 长发
反义 short。
② The bridge is two hundred metres long.
= 那座桥长两百米。
two hundred metres long = 两百米长
数量放在 long 前面。
③ It was a long film, and the children fell asleep.
= 那部电影很长，孩子们睡着了。
a long film = 很长的电影
④ How long have you lived in this town?
= 你在这座城里住了多久？
how long = 多久
问时间的长短；问距离用 how far。
⑤ I will not be long; wait for me in the car.
= 我不会太久，在车里等我。
not be long = 不会太久
⑥ You can borrow the car as long as you bring it back by six.
= 只要你六点前还回来，车可以借给你。
as long as = 只要
so long as = 只要（同上）
⑦ He no longer works here; he left in March.
= 他不在这儿上班了，三月份走的。
no longer = 不再
也说 not … any longer。
⑧ Before long, the whole village knew the news.
= 没过多久，全村都知道了那个消息。
before long = 不久
⑨ Have you been waiting for long?
= 你等了很久吗？
for long = 很久
肯定句里说 for a long time。
⑩ Long ago, a great forest covered these hills.
= 很久以前，这些山丘上是一大片森林。
long ago = 很久以前
⑪ The baby cried all night long.
= 婴儿哭了一整夜。
all night long = 一整夜
all day long = 一整天
⑫ In the long run, it is cheaper to buy a good pair of shoes.
= 从长远看，买一双好鞋更省钱。
in the long run = 从长远看
反义 in the short run。
⑬ At long last, the letter from the university arrived.
= 大学的来信终于到了。
at long last = 终于
⑭ The long and the short of it is that we cannot afford the trip.
= 总而言之，这趟旅行我们去不起。
the long and the short of it = 总而言之
⑮ After three weeks of rain, we were longing for the sun.
= 下了三个星期的雨，我们渴望见到太阳。
long for = 渴望
这是另一个 long：动词。
⑯ She longed to see her family again.
= 她渴望再见到家人。
long to do something = 渴望做…
⑰ The longed-for letter arrived on the morning of her birthday.
= 那封盼望已久的信在她生日那天早上到了。
longed-for = 盼望已久的
⑱ The government has no long-term plan for the railways.
= 政府对铁路没有长期规划。
long-term = 长期的
反义 short-term。
⑲ He drives a long-distance lorry between Spain and Poland.
= 他开长途卡车跑西班牙到波兰一线。
long-distance = 长途的
⑳ She likes running long distances early in the morning.
= 她喜欢清早跑长距离。
long distance = 长距离
㉑ The long-range weather forecast says that August will be dry.
= 长期天气预报说八月少雨。
long-range = 长期的；远程的
a long-range missile：远程导弹。
㉒ The two families have a long-standing quarrel over a piece of land.
= 那两家人为一块地有一桩由来已久的争执。
long-standing = 由来已久的
㉓ It is the longest-running show in the history of the theatre.
= 那是该剧院历史上连演时间最长的一出戏。
long-running = 持续多年的
㉔ The accident had a long-lasting effect on his health.
= 那次事故对他的健康产生了持久的影响。
long-lasting = 持久的
㉕ Tortoises are long-lived animals that can reach a hundred and fifty.
= 陆龟是长寿的动物，能活到一百五十岁。
long-lived = 长寿的
㉖ We always keep a carton of long-life milk in the cupboard.
= 我们总在柜子里放一盒常温奶。
long-life = （英）可长期保存的
㉗ The long-serving head teacher retired after thirty years at the school.
= 那位任职多年的校长在学校干了三十年后退休了。
long-serving = 任职多年的
㉘ She married her long-time partner in a small ceremony.
= 她在一场小型仪式上嫁给了相伴多年的伴侣。
long-time = 多年的
㉙ The hospital has a ward for long-stay patients.
= 那家医院有一个长期住院病人的病房。
long-stay = 长期停留的
㉚ The long-awaited new bridge finally opened in June.
= 人们期待已久的新桥终于在六月通车。
long-awaited = 期待已久的
㉛ He found his long-lost brother through a television programme.
= 他通过一档电视节目找到了失散多年的兄弟。
long-lost = 失散多年的
只用在名词前，常带点戏谑。
㉜ After a long-drawn-out divorce, they sold the house.
= 一场拖了很久的离婚之后，他们把房子卖了。
long-drawn-out = 拖得太久的
㉝ His speech was long-winded, and half the audience left early.
= 他的讲话很啰嗦，一半听众提前走了。
long-winded = 啰嗦的
「气长」：一口气说个没完。
㉞ His long-suffering wife has put up with his singing for forty years.
= 他那位一直忍着的妻子听他唱歌听了四十年。
long-suffering = 长期忍受的
常带点幽默，说配偶、同事。
㉟ I am long-sighted and need glasses for reading.
= 我远视，看书得戴眼镜。
long-sighted = （英）远视的
㊱ Long-sightedness becomes more common as people get older.
= 人上了年纪，远视更常见。
long-sightedness = （英）远视
美式说 farsighted。
㊲ She won a silver medal in the long jump.
= 她在跳远比赛中拿了银牌。
the long jump = 跳远
跳高是 the high jump。
㊳ We are going to the coast for the long weekend.
= 我们打算趁连休的长周末去海边。
a long weekend = 连休的长周末
周末加上周五或周一。
㊴ My grandfather still listens to the cricket on long wave.
= 我祖父还在用长波听板球转播。
long wave = 长波
㊵ The children learned long division in their last year of primary school.
= 孩子们在小学最后一年学了长除法。
long division = 长除法
㊶ He wears long johns under his trousers in winter.
= 冬天他在裤子里面穿秋裤。
long johns = 秋裤
复数形式。
㊷ She ordered a long drink of orange juice and soda with ice.
= 她点了一大杯加冰的橙汁苏打。
a long drink = 大杯饮料
㊸ The team plays too many long balls and loses possession.
= 那支球队长传太多，老是丢球权。
a long ball = （足球）长传
㊹ They spent a week walking a long-distance footpath along the coast.
= 他们花了一个星期走完一条沿海的长距离步道。
a long-distance footpath = （英）长距离步道
㊺ He has longish grey hair and a short beard.
= 他留着偏长的灰发和短胡子。
longish = 偏长的
-ish：有点儿。
㊻ Fold the paper longways and cut along the line.
= 把纸纵向对折，沿线剪开。
longways = 纵向地

longboat
/ˈlɒŋbəʊt/
核心：（旧时大帆船上载的）大划艇；（维京人的）长船。
① The sailors rowed ashore in the ship's longboat to find fresh water.
= 水手们划着船上的大划艇上岸找淡水。
the ship's longboat = 船上的大划艇
② The museum has a Viking longboat that was dug out of a field.
= 博物馆有一条从田里挖出来的维京长船。
a Viking longboat = 维京长船

longbow
/ˈlɒŋbəʊ/
核心：长弓 —— 中世纪英格兰人用的手拉大弓，几乎和人一样高。
① English archers with longbows won the battle against a much larger army.
= 英格兰的长弓手打赢了那场战斗，对手的军队比他们多得多。
a longbow = 长弓
② It took years of practice to draw a longbow well.
= 要拉好长弓得练上好多年。
draw a longbow = 拉长弓
与用机括发射的 crossbow（弩）相对。

longevity
/lɒnˈdʒevɪti/

① The people of the island are famous for their longevity, and many live past a hundred.
= 那座岛上的人以长寿闻名，许多人活过一百岁。
longevity = 长寿
② Regular exercise and a simple diet can add years to your longevity.
= 经常锻炼加上饮食清淡，能让你多活好些年。
add to your longevity = 延长寿命
③ The longevity of the show surprised even its writers.
= 那档节目能播这么久，连编剧都感到意外。
the longevity of something = …的持久

longhand
/ˈlɒŋhænd/
核心：普通书写、手写（与打字、速记相对）。
① She wrote the whole novel in longhand before typing it up.
= 她先把整部小说手写出来，再打成字。
in longhand = 用手写
② The secretary took the notes in longhand because nobody had taught her shorthand.
= 那位秘书用普通写法做笔记，因为没人教过她速记。
take notes in longhand = 用普通写法做笔记
与 shorthand（速记）相对。

longing
/ˈlɒŋɪŋ/

① She felt a deep longing for the hills of her childhood.
= 她深深思念童年时的那些山丘。
a longing for something = 对…的渴望
② He gave the cake in the window a longing look.
= 他眼巴巴地看了一眼橱窗里的蛋糕。
a longing look = 渴望的眼神
③ The children looked longingly at the toys in the shop.
= 孩子们眼巴巴地望着店里的玩具。
look longingly = 眼巴巴地看

longitude
/ˈlɒndʒɪtjuːd/

① The city lies at a longitude of thirty degrees east.
= 那座城市位于东经三十度。
a longitude of thirty degrees east = 东经三十度
纬度是 latitude。
② The beetle has longitudinal stripes down its back.
= 那种甲虫背上有纵向的条纹。
longitudinal stripes = 纵向条纹
③ The longitudinal study followed the same children for twenty years.
= 那项跟踪研究对同一批孩子追踪了二十年。
a longitudinal study = 长期跟踪研究
④ Cut the stem longitudinally and look at it under the microscope.
= 把茎纵向切开，放在显微镜下观察。
longitudinally = 纵向地

loo
/luː/

loofah

look
/lʊk/
核心：看、瞧（look at）；看起来、显得（look + 形容词、look like）；（房屋）朝向；找（look for）。
名词：看一眼；神情、样子；外表；（复数 looks）相貌。感叹：Look!（听着、你看）。
短语动词：look after（照顾、照管）、look ahead（展望将来）、look around / look round（四下看看；参观；look around for = 四处找）、
look at（看；考虑、研究）、look back（回头看；回顾）、look down on（看不起）、look for（找）、look forward to（期待）、
look in（顺道看望）、look into（调查）、look on（旁观；look on as = 把…看作；look on with = 以…眼光看待）、
look out（当心）、look out for（留神找；留意）、look over（过目、检查一遍）、look through（翻阅、浏览；视而不见）、
look to（指望、依靠；look to somebody for / to do）、look up（抬头看；查阅；好转；看望）、look up to（敬仰）。
习语：have a look（看一下）、by the look of（看样子）、look somebody in the eye（直视某人）、look the other way（装没看见）。
名词 look-see（口语：看一眼）、旧词 looking glass（镜子）并入本条。
① Look at the map on the wall and tell me where we are now.
= 看看墙上的地图，告诉我我们现在在哪儿。
look at = 看
有意识地把目光投过去；see 是「看见」。
② You look tired; did you sleep badly?
= 你看上去很累，没睡好吗？
look tired = 看上去很累
系动词用法，后面接形容词。
③ It looks as if it is going to snow before we reach the top of the hill.
= 看样子我们还没到山顶就要下雪。
it looks as if… = 看样子…
口语里也说 it looks like…。
④ Our room in the hotel looks onto the harbour and the fishing boats.
= 我们的酒店房间面朝港口和渔船。
look onto something = 朝向…
⑤ Look, I am sorry, but I cannot lend you any more money.
= 听着，对不起，我不能再借钱给你了。
Look, … = 听着，…
放句首，引起注意，常带点不耐烦。
⑥ Open the bonnet and let me have a look at the engine.
= 把引擎盖打开，让我看一下发动机。
have a look = 看一下
美式多说 take a look。
⑦ She gave me an angry look and left the room.
= 她生气地瞪了我一眼，走出了房间。
an angry look = 生气的眼神
⑧ I do not like the look of those black clouds over the mountains.
= 我看山那边的乌云不大对劲。
the look of something = …的样子
⑨ He has his father's good looks and his mother's quick temper.
= 他继承了他父亲的好相貌和他母亲的急脾气。
good looks = 好相貌
这一支用复数。
⑩ By the look of the sky, we will have rain before lunch.
= 看天色，午饭前要下雨。
by the look of = 看样子
⑪ She looked me in the eye and said that she had not taken the money.
= 她直视着我的眼睛，说钱不是她拿的。
look somebody in the eye = 直视某人
⑫ The guards at the gate were paid to look the other way while the lorries drove out.
= 有人给了门口的警卫钱，让他们在卡车开出去时装没看见。
look the other way = 装没看见
⑬ Who is looking after the children while you are at work?
= 你上班的时候谁照看孩子？
look after = 照顾、照管
美式多说 take care of。
⑭ We need to look ahead and plan for the next five years.
= 我们得往前看，为今后五年作打算。
look ahead = 展望将来
⑮ We spent the morning looking around the old town.
= 我们花了一上午逛老城。
look around = 四下看看、参观
look round = （英）四下看看（同上）
⑯ She is looking around for a cheaper flat.
= 她正在四处找便宜点的公寓。
look around for = 四处找
look round for = （英）四处找（同上）
⑰ The committee will look at the plan again next month.
= 委员会下个月将再研究一次那个方案。
look at a plan = 考虑、研究方案
⑱ Looking back, I think we sold the house too cheaply.
= 回过头看，我觉得那座房子我们卖便宜了。
