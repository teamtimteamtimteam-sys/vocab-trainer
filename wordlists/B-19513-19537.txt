rounded
/ˈraʊndɪd/
核心：形容词：圆形的、带圆角的、弧形的；（人、教育）全面的、均衡的：a rounded education；（味道、声音）醇厚的、饱满的。
① Toys for babies should have rounded edges so that the children cannot cut themselves on them.
= 婴儿玩具的边应该是圆的，免得孩子划伤自己。
rounded edges = 圆角
② The school aims to give every pupil a rounded education with plenty of music, sport and art.
= 那所学校的目标是给每个学生全面的教育，音乐、体育和美术都不少。
a rounded education = 全面的教育
多修饰教育、人格、经历等，带赞许口气。
③ After five years in the barrel, the wine has a rich, rounded flavour that goes well with lamb.
= 在桶里放了五年之后，这款葡萄酒风味浓郁醇厚，很配羊肉。
a rounded flavour = 醇厚的风味

in a row = 连续

= 我们轮流划着小船过湖，到岛上去野餐。

row a boat across = 划船渡过
⑦ My neighbours had a terrible row last night, and I could hear them shouting through the wall.
= 我的邻居昨晚大吵了一架，我隔着墙都能听见他们在喊。
row = 争吵
have a row = 吵架
⑧ The government is in the middle of a row over its plans to close three local hospitals.
= 政府正因关闭三家地方医院的计划陷入一场争议。
a row over something = 关于某事的争议
⑨ She often rowed with her brother about which of them should look after their elderly mother.
= 她常和哥哥为谁来照顾年迈的母亲吵架。
row with somebody = 和某人吵架
⑩ Stop making such a terrible row in the kitchen, boys, because the baby is trying to sleep upstairs!
= 孩子们，别在厨房里那么吵了，宝宝正在楼上睡觉呢！
make a row = 吵闹

rowan
/ˈrəʊən/
核心：花楸树：一种小乔木，秋天结一簇簇鲜红的浆果。可数；浆果说 rowan berries。也作 mountain ash。
① A rowan tree grows beside the gate, and in autumn it is covered in bright red berries.
= 大门边长着一棵花楸树，秋天满树都是鲜红的浆果。
a rowan tree = 花楸树
② Birds come to the garden every October to eat the rowan berries before the first frost.
= 每年十月，鸟儿都在头一场霜降之前飞到花园里来吃花楸果。
rowan berries = 花楸果

rowback
/ˈrəʊbæk/
核心：（英式口语，多见于新闻）名词，可数：（对先前立场、承诺的）收回、退让：a rowback on a promise。
① Critics called the change a humiliating rowback on the government's promise to cut taxes for families.
= 批评者称这一改变是政府在为家庭减税的承诺上丢脸的食言。
a rowback on a promise = 在承诺上食言
② After weeks of protests, the company announced a partial rowback and kept the old prices for older customers.
= 经过几个星期的抗议，那家公司宣布部分让步，对老顾客保留原价。
a partial rowback = 部分让步
