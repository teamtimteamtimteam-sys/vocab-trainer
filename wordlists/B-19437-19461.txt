root
/ruːt/
核心：名词，可数：（植物的）根；（牙齿、头发、指甲的）根部；（问题、麻烦的）根源：the root of the problem、root cause、be at the root of something；复数 roots 指一个人的出身、根、与故乡的联系；（语言）词根。
动词：（植物、插条）生根；（猪等）用鼻子拱地找食；（口语）翻找：root around / about in something。
习语：take root 是生根，也指想法、习惯扎下根来；put down roots 是（在某地）扎根；root and branch 是彻底地、从根本上。
① We dug up the weed very carefully so that no piece of the root was left behind in the soil.
= 我们非常小心地把那棵杂草挖出来，好让土里不留下一点根。
root = 根
the root of a plant = 植物的根
② The young willow quickly took root in the wet ground beside the river and grew tall within a few years.
= 那棵小柳树在河边的湿地里很快生了根，没几年就长得很高。
take root = 生根
③ The idea of working from home took root during the long lockdown and never really went away afterwards.
= 在家办公的想法在漫长的封控期间扎下了根，此后再也没有真正消失。
an idea takes root = 想法扎下根来
这一义从植物生根引申到想法、习惯、制度在人群中站稳。
④ Engineers spent several weeks looking for the root cause of the fire before they finally blamed the old wiring.
= 工程师们花了好几个星期寻找火灾的根本原因，最后才认定是老化的电线。

⑤ The doctor told him that stress at work, not his diet, was the root of the problem with his stomach.
= 医生告诉他，他胃病问题的根源是工作压力，而不是饮食。

⑥ Jealousy was at the root of the long quarrel between the two brothers over their father's farm.

⑨ The dentist explained that the root of the tooth was badly damaged, even though the top still looked healthy.
= 牙医解释说，虽然牙齿的上半部看着还好，牙根却已经严重受损。

⑩ Teachers often show students the root of a word, such as port in export, to help them guess what it means.
= 老师常给学生指出单词的词根，比如「export」里的「port」，帮他们猜词义。

⑪ The new government promised root and branch reform of the tax system after years of scandals and waste.

⑫ She kept rooting around in her handbag for several minutes before she finally found her car keys.

⑬ Cuttings from this plant will root easily if you leave them in a glass of water on a sunny windowsill.

⑭ The pigs were rooting in the mud under the oak trees for the acorns that had fallen in the night.

rosary
/ˈrəʊzəri/
核心：（天主教）玫瑰经：按顺序反复念诵的一套祷文；也指念玫瑰经时计数用的念珠。可数。常说 say the rosary。
① Every evening my grandmother sat by the window and said the rosary quietly with her eyes closed.
= 每天傍晚，祖母都坐在窗边，闭着眼睛轻声念玫瑰经。
say the rosary = 念玫瑰经
② She held a wooden rosary in her hands and moved the beads slowly between her fingers as she prayed.
= 她手里握着一串木念珠，一边祈祷一边用手指慢慢拨动珠子。
a rosary = 一串念珠

rose
/rəʊz/
核心：名词：玫瑰、蔷薇（花或植株）；玫瑰色、淡粉红色；（喷壶、淋浴器的）莲蓬式喷头。可数，颜色义不可数。
习语：under the rose（文）是私下里、秘密地；no bed of roses 是并不轻松；come up roses 是结果一切顺利；stop and smell the roses 是放慢脚步享受生活。
（rose 也是 rise 的过去式，见 rise 条。）
① He bought her a single red rose on their first date, and she kept it pressed inside a book for years.
= 第一次约会时他给她买了一枝红玫瑰，她把它压在书里留了好多年。
rose = 玫瑰
a red rose = 一枝红玫瑰
② The park has a beautiful rose garden that is full of visitors in June when the flowers open.
= 公园里有一座漂亮的玫瑰园，六月花开时游客满园。
rose garden = 玫瑰园
③ The two ministers met under the rose, far from the cameras, to discuss a possible deal.
= 两位部长私下里见了面，远离镜头，商谈一项可能的协议。
under the rose = 私下里
④ The bridesmaids wore long dresses of pale rose that matched the small flowers in their hair.
= 伴娘们穿着淡玫瑰色的长裙，和发间的小花正好相配。
pale rose = 淡玫瑰色
⑤ Take the rose off the watering can if you want the water to come out in a single strong stream.
= 想让水成一股粗流出来，就把喷壶的喷头取下来。
the rose of a watering can = 喷壶的喷头
⑥ Life as a young doctor is no bed of roses; you work long nights and hardly ever see your friends.
= 当年轻医生并不轻松：要值长长的夜班，几乎见不到朋友。
no bed of roses = 并不轻松
⑦ Despite all our worries before the move, everything came up roses for us in the new town.
= 尽管搬家前忧心忡忡，到了新城镇以后我们一切都很顺利。
come up roses = 结果一切顺利
⑧ My doctor told me to work fewer hours and take time to stop and smell the roses now and then.
= 医生叫我少工作几个小时，时不时放慢脚步享受生活。
stop and smell the roses = 放慢脚步享受生活

roseate
/ˈrəʊziət/
核心：（文学）玫瑰色的、粉红的；（比喻，多含贬义）过分乐观的。形容词，比 rosy 书面。
① The snowy peaks turned roseate in the last light of the evening as we walked slowly back to the hut.
= 我们慢慢走回小屋时，雪峰在傍晚最后的光里染成了玫瑰色。
roseate = 玫瑰色的
② His roseate view of the economy ignored the thousands of people who had lost their jobs that year.
= 他对经济过于乐观的看法，无视了那一年失业的成千上万人。
a roseate view = 过于乐观的看法

rosebud
/ˈrəʊzbʌd/
核心：玫瑰花苞：还没开的玫瑰。可数。也作定语：a rosebud mouth 指（多形容女子）小巧红润的嘴。
① She picked a rosebud from the garden and put it in a small glass on the breakfast table.
= 她从花园里摘了一个玫瑰花苞，插在早餐桌上的一只小玻璃杯里。
a rosebud = 玫瑰花苞
② The old painting shows a young girl with pale skin, dark curls and a little rosebud mouth.
= 那幅老画画的是一个皮肤白皙、黑色卷发、樱桃小嘴的少女。
a rosebud mouth = 樱桃小嘴
