rhetoric
/ˈretərɪk/
核心：不可数。一是（贬义）华而不实的言辞、空话、花言巧语：political rhetoric；二是修辞、修辞学：有效说服人的说话、写作技巧。
① The minister's speech was full of empty rhetoric about helping the poor.
= 那位部长的讲话满是帮助穷人的空洞言辞。
empty rhetoric = 空洞的言辞
rhetoric = 华而不实的言辞
② Students in ancient Rome studied rhetoric to become lawyers and politicians.
= 古罗马的学生学修辞学，以便当律师和政治家。
study rhetoric = 学修辞学
rhetoric = 修辞学

rhetorical
/rɪˈtɒrɪkl/
核心：一是修辞的、修辞学的；二是（贬义）华而不实的、夸张的。rhetorical question 是反问句、修辞性问句：不需要回答的问题。副词 rhetorically，并入本条。
① "Who cares?" is often a rhetorical question that needs no answer.
= 「谁在乎？」常常是一个不需要回答的反问句。
a rhetorical question = 反问句
rhetorical = 修辞的
② The speech used every rhetorical trick to win over the crowd.
= 那篇讲话用尽了各种修辞手法来争取群众。
a rhetorical trick = 修辞手法
③ "Is anyone surprised?" he asked rhetorically.
= 「有谁感到意外吗？」他反问道。
ask rhetorically = 反问
rhetorically = 修辞性地

rhetorician
/ˌretəˈrɪʃn/
核心：（正式）修辞学家；（贬义）擅长辞令的人、雄辩家。可数。
① He was a skilled rhetorician who could make any audience laugh or cry.
= 他是个善于辞令的人，能让任何听众笑或哭。
a skilled rhetorician = 善于辞令的人
a rhetorician = 雄辩家
② The ancient rhetoricians taught their students how to argue in court.
= 古代的修辞学家教学生怎样在法庭上辩论。
the ancient rhetoricians = 古代的修辞学家

rheumatic
/ruːˈmætɪk/
核心：（医学）风湿病的、患风湿病的。
① My grandmother has rheumatic pains in her hands when the weather is cold.
= 天冷时我奶奶的手会犯风湿疼。
rheumatic pains = 风湿疼痛
rheumatic = 风湿病的
② The old man walked slowly on his rheumatic legs.
= 那个老人拖着患风湿的腿慢慢走着。
rheumatic legs = 患风湿的腿

rheumatism
/ˈruːmətɪzəm/
核心：风湿病：关节、肌肉疼痛僵硬的病。不可数。
① He suffers from rheumatism in his knees and cannot walk far.
= 他膝盖有风湿病，走不了远路。
suffer from rheumatism = 患风湿病
rheumatism = 风湿病
② The damp weather makes her rheumatism worse.
= 潮湿的天气让她的风湿病加重。
make rheumatism worse = 使风湿病加重

rheumatology
/ˌruːməˈtɒlədʒi/
核心：风湿病学：研究、治疗关节和肌肉疾病的医学分支。不可数。
① After ten years as a family doctor she decided to specialize in rheumatology.
= 当了十年家庭医生之后，她决定专攻风湿病学。
specialize in rheumatology = 专攻风湿病学
rheumatology = 风湿病学
② Because of the pain in his joints he was sent to the rheumatology department at the city hospital.
= 因为关节疼痛，他被转到市医院的风湿科。
the rheumatology department = 风湿科

rheumy
/ˈruːmi/
核心：（眼睛）湿润的、流泪的、浑浊的：尤指老人或病人。
① The old man looked at us with his rheumy blue eyes.
= 那个老人用他那双湿润浑浊的蓝眼睛看着我们。
rheumy eyes = 湿润浑浊的眼睛
rheumy = 流泪的
② The cold wind made the dog's eyes red and rheumy.
= 冷风吹得那条狗的眼睛又红又流泪。
red and rheumy = 又红又流泪

rhinestone

① The singer wore a white suit covered in rhinestones.
= 那位歌手穿着一套缀满人造钻石的白色西装。
covered in rhinestones = 缀满人造钻石
a rhinestone = 人造钻石
② She bought a cheap rhinestone necklace for the party.
= 她为派对买了一条便宜的人造钻石项链。
a rhinestone necklace = 人造钻石项链

rhinitis
/raɪˈnaɪtɪs/

① The doctor said that her blocked nose was caused by rhinitis.
= 医生说她鼻子堵是鼻炎引起的。
be caused by rhinitis = 由鼻炎引起
rhinitis = 鼻炎

rhino
/ˈraɪnəʊ/
核心：（口语）犀牛：rhinoceros 的截短。可数，复数 rhinos 或 rhino。
① We saw a rhino and her baby drinking at the river.
= 我们看见一头母犀牛带着小犀牛在河边喝水。
a rhino = 犀牛
② The rhinos in the park are guarded day and night against hunters.
= 公园里的犀牛日夜有人守护，防止偷猎者。
the rhinos = 犀牛

rhinoceros
/raɪˈnɒsərəs/
核心：犀牛：非洲、亚洲的一种大型动物，皮厚，鼻子上有一两只角。可数，复数 rhinoceroses 或 rhinoceros。口语简称 rhino。
① The rhinoceros is hunted for its horn, and very few are left in the wild.
= 犀牛因为角而遭到猎杀，野外剩下的已经很少了。
the rhinoceros = 犀牛
② A white rhinoceros can weigh more than two tonnes.
= 一头白犀牛的体重可以超过两吨。
a white rhinoceros = 白犀牛

rhizome
/ˈraɪzəʊm/
核心：（植物）根茎、根状茎：长在地下、横着生长的茎，能长出新芽和根。可数。
① Ginger is the rhizome of a tropical plant.
= 姜是一种热带植物的根茎。
the rhizome of a plant = 植物的根茎
a rhizome = 根茎
② The weed spreads quickly by sending out rhizomes under the ground.
= 那种杂草靠在地下伸出根茎迅速蔓延。
send out rhizomes = 伸出根茎

rhomboid

rhyme
/raɪm/
核心：名词：押韵、韵，不可数；押韵的词，可数：a rhyme for；押韵的诗、儿歌，可数：a nursery rhyme。动词：（使）押韵：rhyme with。
习语 without rhyme or reason 是毫无道理、莫名其妙。复合词 rhyming slang 是押韵俚语。
① The children learned a nursery rhyme about a little star.
= 孩子们学了一首关于小星星的儿歌。
a nursery rhyme = 儿歌
rhyme = 押韵的诗
② The poem is written in rhyme, with the last word of each line matching.
= 那首诗是押韵的，每行最后一个词都对得上。
in rhyme = 押韵地
③ "Cat" rhymes with "hat" and "mat".
= 「cat」和「hat」「mat」押韵。
rhyme with something = 与某词押韵
④ I cannot think of a rhyme for "orange".
= 我想不出跟「orange」押韵的词。
a rhyme for something = 与某词押韵的词
⑤ The firm closed its offices without rhyme or reason.
= 那家公司莫名其妙地关掉了几个办事处。
without rhyme or reason = 毫无道理
⑥ In London rhyming slang, "apples and pears" means stairs.
= 在伦敦押韵俚语里，用「apples and pears」指楼梯。
rhyming slang = 押韵俚语

rhythm
/ˈrɪðəm/
核心：可数也可不可数。一是节奏、节拍、韵律：音乐、诗歌里有规律的强弱变化：a sense of rhythm；二是（有规律的）节律、规律：the rhythm of the seasons。

① At the festival she danced barefoot on the grass to the rhythm of the drums.
= 在节庆上，她光着脚在草地上随着鼓的节奏跳舞。
the rhythm of something = 某物的节奏
rhythm = 节奏
② My brother has no sense of rhythm and cannot even clap in time to music.
= 我哥哥没有节奏感，连跟着音乐打拍子都不会。
a sense of rhythm = 节奏感
③ Farmers' lives follow the rhythm of the seasons.
= 农民的生活顺应四季的规律。
the rhythm of the seasons = 四季的规律
④ Her grandfather loved the rhythm and blues of the nineteen fifties.
= 她爷爷喜欢一九五〇年代的节奏布鲁斯。
rhythm and blues = 节奏布鲁斯
⑤ The drummer and the bass player make up the rhythm section of the band.
= 鼓手和贝斯手组成了乐队的节奏组。
the rhythm section = 节奏组
