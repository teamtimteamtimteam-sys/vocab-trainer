fugitive
核心：三支。一是逃犯、在逃者：a fugitive from justice；
二是形容词，在逃的：a fugitive prisoner；三是（正式）转瞬即逝的：a fugitive glance。
① A fugitive from justice lived in that village for eleven years.
= 一名在逃的通缉犯在那个村子住了十一年。
a fugitive from justice = 在逃的通缉犯
② Two fugitive prisoners were caught at a bus stop before midday.
= 两名越狱的囚犯中午前在公交站被抓。
a fugitive prisoner = 在逃的囚犯
③ A fugitive glance at the clock told everybody the meeting was over.
= 朝钟一瞥而过，就让所有人知道会散了。
a fugitive glance = 一瞥而过的目光

fugue

① A fugue for four voices takes longer to write than to hear.
= 一首四声部赋格，写起来比听起来费时得多。
a fugue = 赋格曲
② She teaches fugue to students who would rather write songs.
= 她教赋格，学生却更想写歌。
teach fugue = 教赋格
③ He walked out in a fugue and turned up in another county.
= 他在神游状态里走了出去，出现在另一个郡。
in a fugue = 处在神游状态

ful
核心：三支（构词成分，写作 -ful）。一是接在名词后构成形容词：careful、useful；
二是接在名词后表示"一满…的量"：a spoonful、a handful；
三是这一后缀的复数按 -fuls 走。
① Words ending in ful take one l and the adverb takes two.
= 以这个后缀结尾的词只用一个 l，变副词才用两个。
words ending in ful = 以 -ful 结尾的词
② A spoonful and a handful both use ful to mean a quantity.
= 「一满勺」「一把」这两个词里的这个后缀，表示的都是分量。
a spoonful = 一满勺
③ Four spoonfuls went in and the recipe said two.
= 下了四满勺，菜谱写的是两勺。
spoonfuls = 若干满勺

fun
核心：四支。一是乐趣、好玩的事：have fun；二是形容词，有意思的：a fun day；
三是 make fun of somebody，取笑某人；四是 for fun，闹着玩。
① They had more fun in the queue than at the thing they queued for.
= 他们排队时比排到的东西还好玩。
have fun = 玩得开心
② It was a fun day and nobody took a single photograph.
= 那天很有意思，一张照片都没人拍。
a fun day = 有意思的一天
③ They made fun of his coat until the rain started.
= 他们取笑他那件外套，直到下起雨来。
make fun of somebody = 取笑某人
④ She learned the whole thing for fun and now does it for money.
= 她当初纯是闹着玩学的，如今靠它挣钱。
for fun = 闹着玩
fun fur = 仿皮毛料
Fun fur on that coat sheds onto everybody who hugs her.
= 那件大衣上的仿皮毛料，谁抱她就掉谁一身。
fun-loving = 爱玩爱闹的
A fun-loving dog and a quiet street are a poor match.
= 一条爱玩爱闹的狗，配一条安静的街，不大合适。
fun run = 趣味跑
The fun run raised nine hundred pounds and four people took it seriously.
= 那场趣味跑筹到九百镑，有四个人当了真。

funboard
核心：三支（冲浪、帆板）。一是介于长板与短板之间的"趣味板"；
二是初学者常用的那种宽板；三是形容词性，这一类装备的。
① A funboard floats better than a short board and turns almost as well.
= 趣味板比短板浮力好，转向也差不了多少。
a funboard = 趣味板
② She learned on a funboard and sold it in the second summer.
= 她是在趣味板上学会的，第二个夏天就把板卖了。
learn on a funboard = 在趣味板上学
③ Two funboards are strapped to the roof from May to September.
= 从五月到九月，车顶上绑着两块趣味板。
funboards = 趣味板

function
核心：四支。一是功能、作用：the function of something；
二是职责：perform a function；三是（正式的）活动、场合：a school function；
四是（数学）函数：a function of x。
① The function of that lever is printed on a label nobody has read.
= 那根杆的功能印在一张没人读过的标签上。
the function of something = 某物的功能
② She performs a function that three people used to share.
= 她一个人担着从前三个人分担的职责。
perform a function = 履行职责
③ A school function on a Friday empties the staff room by four.
= 周五的校内活动，四点就让教员室空了。
a school function = 校内活动
④ Output is a function of temperature and nothing else here matters.
= 产量是温度的函数，这里别的都不要紧。
a function of something = 某物的函数
function as = 充当、起…作用
The kitchen table functions as a desk, a workbench and an ironing board.
= 厨房那张桌子充当写字台、工作台，还有熨衣板。
function key = 功能键
One function key does four things and the label mentions none.
= 一个功能键管四件事，键帽上一件也没写。
function word = 功能词
A function word carries no picture and holds the sentence together.
= 功能词本身不带画面，却把句子撑住了。
