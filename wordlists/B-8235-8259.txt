expediency
核心：（多含贬义）权宜、只图管用：眼下方便就好，不问是否合乎原则。
不可数，最常见的搭配是 political expediency。异体 expedience 少见。
① The rule was dropped for political expediency and quietly restored in June.
= 那条规矩出于政治权宜被取消，六月又悄悄恢复。
political expediency = 政治上的权宜
② Expediency explains the timetable and does not excuse it.
= 权宜之计解释得了这份时间表，却开脱不了它。
expediency = 权宜行事

expedient
核心：两支。一是形容词，权宜的、图省事的：多用 it is expedient to do something，
言下之意是管用但未必正当；二是名词，应急的权宜之计。副词 expediently。
① The committee found it expedient to postpone the vote until after the holiday.
= 委员会觉得把表决推到假期之后比较省事。
it is expedient to do something = 做某事比较权宜
② Borrowing from next year's budget is an expedient, not a plan.
= 挪用明年的预算是权宜之计，不是计划。
an expedient = 权宜之计

expediently
核心：权宜地、图方便地：修饰处理方式，暗示图的是省事，不是公正。
① The complaint was handled expediently and nobody looked at the paperwork.
= 那桩投诉图省事就打发了，没人翻过那些材料。
handle something expediently = 图省事地处理某事
② Acting expediently in March cost the department the whole of April.
= 三月里图省事，让那个部门赔上了整个四月。
act expediently = 权宜行事
构词：expedient 加 -ly

expedite
核心：（正式）加快、加急办理：让手续或流程走得快些。及物，
宾语多是 process、delivery、application 这类词，公文与商务信里常见。
① A second signature would expedite the transfer and nobody can find the registrar.
= 再补一个签名就能加快转账，可谁也找不到那位登记员。
expedite the process = 加快流程
② Paying the extra fee expedites delivery by exactly one working day.
= 多付那笔加急费，交货正好提前一个工作日。
expedite delivery = 加急发货

expedition
核心：两支。一是远征、探险、科考（也指那支队伍）：go on an expedition；
二是（正式）迅速、麻利，只见于 with all expedition 这类说法。
① The expedition set out with four sledges and came back with only two.
= 那支考察队带着四架雪橇出发，回来时只剩两架。
go on an expedition = 参加一次远征
an expedition to the interior = 深入内陆的考察
② The court asked that the papers be filed with all expedition.
= 法庭要求尽速把文件递交上去。
with all expedition = 尽速、火速

expeditious
核心：（正式）迅速而有效率的：多修饰 manner、resolution、response，
一股公文腔，日常场合说 quick。副词 expeditiously。
① The tenant asked for an expeditious repair and got a letter about the rules.
= 租客要求尽快修好，收到的却是一封讲规矩的信。
in an expeditious manner = 以迅速的方式
② An expeditious hearing suits everyone except the side that is stalling.
= 迅速开庭对谁都好，只除了那个在拖延的一方。
expeditious = 迅速高效的

expeditiously
核心：迅速地、麻利地：法律文书与公文里的常用词，多跟 deal with、handle、act 连用。
① Claims under a thousand pounds are dealt with expeditiously and without a hearing.
= 一千镑以下的索赔从速处理，也不开庭。
deal with something expeditiously = 从速处理某事
② The bank promised to act expeditiously and then took eleven weeks.
= 银行答应尽快办理，结果办了十一个星期。
act expeditiously = 迅速行事
构词：expeditious 加 -ly

expel
核心：两支。一是开除、驱逐：把学生赶出学校、把人逐出国境，
多用被动 be expelled from something；二是排出气体或液体。
变形双写 l：expelled、expelling。名词 expulsion。
① Two players were expelled from the tournament and the third was only fined.
= 有两名选手被逐出赛事，第三个只罚了款。
expel somebody from something = 把某人逐出某处
be expelled from school = 被学校开除
② The whale expels a spout of air that can be heard from the cliff top.
= 鲸鱼喷出一股气，站在崖顶都听得见。
expel air = 排出空气

expend
核心：（正式）花费、耗用：说时间、精力、金钱用在某事上，
搭配 expend something on something。比 spend 正式，多见于书面。名词 expenditure。
① They expend more energy on the rota than on the work it covers.
= 他们花在排班表上的精力，比花在班上那些活儿还多。
expend something on something = 把某物耗在某事上
② Half the grant was expended before a single wall came down.
= 一堵墙还没拆，拨款就已经用掉一半。
be expended = 被耗用掉

expendable
核心：可牺牲的、用完即弃的：说人时相当冷酷，意思是没了也不要紧；
说物则指一次性的耗材。
① The plan treats the night shift as expendable and says so in a footnote.
= 那份方案把夜班工人当成可以牺牲的，还写在脚注里。
be treated as expendable = 被当成可牺牲的
② Expendable parts are ordered by the box and never repaired.
= 易耗件是整箱订的，坏了从不修。
expendable parts = 易耗零件

expenditure
核心：（正式）支出、开销：花出去的钱，也说精力的耗费。
不可数为主，分项列举时可数；跟 income 相对。
① Public expenditure rose in every department except the one under review.
= 除了正在被审查的那个部门，各部门的公共开支都涨了。
public expenditure = 公共开支
capital expenditure = 资本支出
② The expenditure of effort was enormous and the result fitted on one page.
= 耗掉的精力极大，结果只装了一页纸。
the expenditure of effort = 精力的耗费

expense
核心：两支。一是花费、开销，不可数；二是复数 expenses，办事产生的、
可以报销的费用。要紧的是两个固定搭配：at the expense of something 是
以牺牲某事为代价，at somebody's expense 既指某人掏钱，也指拿某人取笑。
① The hall was restored at great expense and is booked eleven days a year.
= 那座礼堂花大钱修复，一年只租出去十一天。
go to the expense of doing something = 不惜花钱去做某事
spare no expense = 不惜工本
② He got the deal through at the expense of everyone who worked weekends.
= 他把那笔交易促成了，代价是所有周末加班的人。
at the expense of something = 以牺牲某事为代价
③ The joke was at his expense and he laughed longest of all.
= 那个玩笑是拿他打趣的，他自己笑得最久。
at somebody's expense = 拿某人取笑
④ Travel expenses are paid monthly and the receipts have to be stapled together.
= 差旅费按月报销，收据必须钉在一起。
travel expenses = 差旅费

expensive
核心：贵的、花钱多的：既说价格高，也说代价大，如 an expensive mistake。
反义 cheap 与 inexpensive；副词 expensively。
① The camera is expensive to buy and surprisingly cheap to run.
= 那台相机买着贵，用起来倒便宜得出奇。
be expensive to do something = 做某事花费高
② Ignoring the survey turned out to be an expensive mistake.
= 不理会那份验房报告，结果成了代价高昂的错误。
an expensive mistake = 代价高昂的错误
③ Expensive tastes are easy to acquire on somebody else's holiday.
= 花着别人的钱度假，很容易养出奢侈的口味。
expensive tastes = 奢侈的品味

expensively
核心：昂贵地、花大钱地：多修饰 dressed、furnished、educated，
常带一点「钱花了未必值」的口气。
① The lobby is expensively furnished and the lifts break every winter.
= 那个大堂装修得很花钱，电梯每年冬天都坏。
expensively furnished = 装修得很昂贵
② He was expensively educated and still cannot boil an egg.
= 他上学花了大钱，到现在连鸡蛋都不会煮。
expensively educated = 受过昂贵教育的
构词：expensive 加 -ly

experience
核心：三支。一是经验、阅历（不可数）：work experience、from experience；
二是一次经历（可数）：a memorable experience；三是动词，亲身经历、感受到，
多见于 experience pain、experience difficulty 这类正式说法。形容词 experienced。
① She has ten years in the trade and knows from experience which suppliers ring back.
= 她入行十年，凭经验就知道哪些供应商会回电话。
work experience = 工作经验
from experience = 凭经验
② In my experience the second week of a strike changes the tone of the talks.
= 依我的经验，罢工到第二周，谈判的口气就变了。
in my experience = 依我的经验
③ Sleeping in the airport was an experience she describes but never recommends.
= 在机场过夜是一次经历，她讲归讲，从来不推荐。
an experience = 一次经历
④ Patients experience the pain differently depending on how the news was broken.
= 病人对疼痛的感受各不相同，要看消息是怎么告诉他们的。
experience pain = 感到疼痛

experienced
核心：有经验的、老练的：多作定语，如 an experienced nurse；
也说 be experienced in something。反义 inexperienced。
① An experienced skipper reads the sky and leaves the forecast on the table.
= 老练的船长看天色，把天气预报撂在桌上。
an experienced teacher = 有经验的老师
② She is experienced in negotiating with landlords and hates every minute of it.
= 她很有跟房东打交道的经验，可每一分钟都讨厌。
be experienced in something = 在某方面有经验

experiential
核心：（正式、教育学）体验式的、从亲身经历得来的：最常见的说法是
experiential learning，指靠动手做、不靠听讲来学。
① The course is experiential and the first lecture happens in week four.
= 这门课是体验式的，第一场讲座排在第四周。
experiential learning = 体验式学习
② Experiential knowledge is hard to write down and easy to demonstrate.
= 亲身经历得来的知识难写下来，演示起来倒容易。
experiential knowledge = 体验得来的知识

experiment
核心：名词与动词同形。名词是实验、试验，搭配 carry out 或 conduct an experiment；
动词是做实验、试新做法，搭配 experiment with something。
名词还有 experimentation，形容词 experimental。
① The class carried out an experiment with two candles and a jam jar.
= 那个班用两支蜡烛和一个果酱瓶做了个实验。
carry out an experiment = 做实验
conduct an experiment = 进行实验
② The bakery is experimenting with rye and has stopped taking phone orders.
= 那家面包房正在试着用黑麦，已经不接电话订单了。
experiment with something = 拿某物做尝试

experimental
核心：两支。一是实验的、试验性的：还在试用阶段，没定型；
二是（艺术上）先锋的、不照常规来的。副词 experimentally。
① The drug is still experimental and is given only under a hospital licence.
= 那种药还在试验阶段，只在医院许可下才用。
an experimental treatment = 试验性疗法
on an experimental basis = 以试验的方式
② Her experimental films have no dialogue and a very loyal audience.
= 她那些先锋电影没有对白，观众倒非常忠实。
experimental music = 先锋音乐

experimentally
核心：两支。一是试验性地：先小规模试试看，成了再推开；
二是试探地：动手时小心翼翼地试一下。
① The route was run experimentally for a term and then written into the timetable.
= 那条线路试运行了一个学期，之后写进了正式时刻表。
run something experimentally = 试运行某事
② He pressed the swollen ankle experimentally and decided against the walk.
= 他试探着按了按肿起来的脚踝，决定不去走那趟路了。
press something experimentally = 试探着按一按
构词：experimental 加 -ly

experimentation
核心：（正式）反复试验（这件事）：一遍遍试出办法的过程。不可数，
常配 years of experimentation、animal experimentation。
① Years of experimentation went into a recipe that takes nine minutes to cook.
= 一道九分钟就能做好的菜，背后是多年的反复试验。
years of experimentation = 多年的反复试验
② Experimentation on animals is licensed here and reported every spring.
= 这里做动物实验须经许可，每年春天还要上报。
animal experimentation = 动物实验

experimenter
核心：实验者、爱折腾新做法的人：既说实验室里的研究人员，也说凡事爱试一试的人。可数。
① The experimenter leaves the room and the recording keeps running.
= 实验人员离开房间，录音一直开着。
an experimenter = 实验者
② He is a lifelong experimenter and a very poor keeper of notes.
= 他一辈子爱试验，做笔记却糟糕得很。
a lifelong experimenter = 一辈子爱试验的人
构词：experiment 加 -er

expert
核心：三支。一是名词，专家：an expert on something 说的是研究某题目的专家，
an expert in something 说的是某个行当里的行家，an expert at doing something
则是做某件事的能手；二是形容词，内行的、专家给的；
三是引申用法 an expert eye、an expert hand，说的是行家的眼力与手艺。
名词 expertise，副词 expertly。
① She is an expert on medieval roofs and gets called out to three counties.
= 她是研究中世纪屋顶的专家，三个郡都请她去看。
an expert on something = 某方面的专家
an expert in something = 某个行当里的行家
an expert at doing something = 做某事的行家
② The report was sent for expert advice and came back with two sentences changed.
= 那份报告送去征求专家意见，回来只改了两句话。
expert advice = 专家意见
③ It takes an expert eye to tell the copy from the original at this distance.
= 隔这么远要分出赝品和真迹，得有行家的眼力。
an expert eye = 行家的眼力

expertise
核心：专门知识、行家本领：靠长期训练练出来的那一手。不可数 ——
不说 an expertise，要说 a level of expertise 或 technical expertise。
① The firm has the expertise and not the equipment, so the work goes abroad.
= 那家公司有本事没设备，活儿只好送到国外做。
technical expertise = 技术专长
have the expertise to do something = 有做某事的本事
② Her expertise in dating timber settled an argument that had run for years.
= 她在木材断代上的专长，了结了一场吵了好些年的争论。
expertise in something = 某方面的专长

expertly
核心：熟练地、老到地：多修饰 handled、boned、taken、played，说的是手上的功夫。
① The fishmonger expertly boned the trout while answering two phone calls.
= 鱼贩一边接两个电话，一边熟练地给鳟鱼剔骨。
expertly handled = 处理得老到
② The corner was expertly taken and then headed straight over the bar.
= 那个角球开得很老到，头球却顶过了横梁。
expertly taken = 开得漂亮
构词：expert 加 -ly
