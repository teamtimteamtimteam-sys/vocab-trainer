repetitious
/ˌrepəˈtɪʃəs/
核心：（贬义）重复啰唆的、反复多次的：说了又说、写了又写，让人厌烦。副词 repetitiously；名词 repetitiousness，不可数。并入本条。
① The speech was long and repetitious, and half the audience fell asleep.
= 那篇讲话又长又啰唆，一半听众睡着了。
long and repetitious = 又长又啰唆
repetitious = 重复啰唆的
② He told the same story repetitiously at every family dinner.
= 每次家庭聚餐，他都翻来覆去地讲同一个故事。
repetitiously = 翻来覆去地
③ The editor cut the book by a third to reduce its repetitiousness.
= 编辑把那本书删去三分之一，减少重复啰唆的地方。
repetitiousness = 重复啰唆

repetitive
/rɪˈpetətɪv/
核心：重复的、单调乏味的：同样的事一遍遍地做。副词 repetitively；名词 repetitiveness，不可数。复合词 repetitive strain injury 是重复性劳损。并入本条。
① Working on the factory line was hard, repetitive work.
= 在工厂流水线上干活又累又单调。
repetitive work = 单调重复的工作
repetitive = 重复的
② The song has a repetitive beat that is easy to dance to.
= 那首歌的节拍反复重复，很好跳舞。
a repetitive beat = 反复的节拍
③ The machine repetitively stamps the same pattern onto each sheet of metal.
= 那台机器在每一块金属板上反复压出同样的花纹。
repetitively = 反复地
④ The repetitiveness of the job made him want to leave after a month.
= 工作太单调，他干了一个月就想走。
repetitiveness = 单调重复

replace
/rɪˈpleɪs/
核心：及物。一是取代、代替：replace A with B、be replaced by；二是接替（某人的职位）：replace somebody as；三是更换、换掉（坏的、旧的东西）；四是放回原处。

replace = 更换
② She replaced the old curtains with bright new ones.
= 她把旧窗帘换成了鲜亮的新窗帘。

⑤ Please replace the books on the shelf when you have finished with them.
= 书看完了请放回书架上。

⑥ We need a replacement for the broken window before the winter.
= 入冬前我们得找块玻璃换掉那扇破窗。
replacement = 替代品
