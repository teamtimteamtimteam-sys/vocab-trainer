disgorge
核心：两支。一是吐出、涌出：管道排水、人群从出口涌出来；
二是（正式）被迫交出：把不该拿的钱吐出来。及物动词。
① The pipe disgorges into the river twice a day and nobody watches it.
= 那根管子一天两次往河里排水，没人盯着。
disgorge into something = 排入某处
② The stadium disgorged twelve thousand people onto one narrow street.
= 体育场把一万两千人吐到了一条窄街上。
disgorge somebody = 把人群倾泻出来
③ The company was ordered to disgorge the profits and appealed for four years.
= 那家公司被责令吐出利润，上诉了四年。
disgorge profits = 交出（不当）利润
构词：古法语 desgorger 从喉咙吐出
注意语域：第三支是法律与监管用语，多见于金融案件。

disgrace
核心：耻辱：名词指丢脸的状态（be in disgrace），也指「丢人的东西」；
动词是「使蒙羞」。形容词 disgraced 说人「名声扫地的」，
disgraceful 说事情「丢人的」，副词 disgracefully。
习语：be in disgrace 是「正在挨罚、名声不好」，
be a disgrace to something 是「给某个群体丢脸」。
① He was in disgrace for a fortnight and the dog got the blame for a week of it.
= 他丢了两周的脸，其中一周的锅让狗背了。
be in disgrace = 正在挨罚、名声不好
② The state of that changing room is a disgrace to a club with those subscriptions.
= 收着那样会费的俱乐部，更衣室成那个样子实在丢人。
be a disgrace to something = 给某个群体丢脸
③ A disgraced official turned up as a consultant to the same department.
= 一名名声扫地的官员，转头以顾问身份回到同一个部门。
a disgraced official = 名声扫地的官员
④ It was a disgraceful way to end thirty years of service.
= 三十年的服务以那种方式收场，实在丢人。
a disgraceful way = 丢人的方式
⑤ He behaved disgracefully at the reception and apologized to the wrong person afterwards.
= 他在招待会上表现得很丢人，事后还向错的人道了歉。
behave disgracefully = 举止丢人
副词，说行为让人抬不起头。
构词：dis- 反向 加 grace 恩宠
注意用法：说给谁丢脸用 a disgrace to somebody 或 to something。

disgruntled
核心：不满而闹情绪的：觉得受了亏待，脸上带出来。形容词，
常说 a disgruntled customer、disgruntled about something。
名词 disgruntlement。
① A disgruntled former employee sent the file to three newspapers on the same day.
= 一名心怀不满的前员工同一天把那份文件寄给了三家报纸。
a disgruntled employee = 心怀不满的员工
② They were disgruntled about the change and nobody asked them beforehand.
= 他们对那次变动不满，而事先根本没人问过他们。
be disgruntled about something = 对某事心怀不满
构词：dis- 加方言 gruntle 咕哝
注意语域：略带调侃；正式说 aggrieved、dissatisfied。

disguise
核心：三支。一是伪装：换装让人认不出（in disguise）；
二是掩饰情绪、意图（disguise your feelings）；
三是习语 a blessing in disguise：因祸得福的事。
名词动词同形。
① He travelled in disguise for two months and was recognized in a chemist's shop.
= 他乔装出行两个月，却在一家药店被认了出来。
in disguise = 乔装、化了装
② She disguised her disappointment well enough to fool everybody but her sister.
= 她把失望掩饰得很好，除了她姐姐谁也没看出来。
disguise something = 掩饰某事
③ Losing that contract was a blessing in disguise and took three years to look like one.
= 丢掉那份合同其实是因祸得福，只是过了三年才看出来。
a blessing in disguise = 因祸得福的事
构词：古法语 desguisier 改换外形
注意用法：乔装说 in disguise；掩饰情绪说 disguise your feelings。

disgust
核心：厌恶、反感：强到让人生理上不舒服的那种。不可数名词，也作动词。
形容词分工：disgusted 说人（be disgusted by something），
disgusting 说事物（a disgusting smell）；
副词 disgustedly 与 disgustingly。
① She left the meeting in disgust and did not come back for the vote.
= 她厌恶地退场，投票时也没回来。
in disgust = 厌恶地
② The state of the kitchen disgusted everybody except the person responsible for it.
= 厨房的样子让所有人都反胃，除了该负责的那个人。
disgust somebody = 使某人反感
③ He was disgusted by the way the story had been reported.
= 那件事的报道方式让他反感。
be disgusted by something = 对某事反感
④ There is a disgusting smell in that corridor and three theories about it.
= 那条走廊里有股恶心的味道，还有三种关于它的说法。
a disgusting smell = 恶心的气味
⑤ The food was disgustingly good and the plates went back empty.
= 那些菜好吃得不像话，盘子都是空着回去的。
disgustingly good = 好得不像话（口语反用）
⑥ She pushed the plate away disgustedly and asked for the bill instead.
= 她厌恶地把盘子推开，直接要了账单。
push something away disgustedly = 厌恶地推开某物
副词说动作里带着反感；比 disgusted 少用。
构词：拉丁语 gustus 味觉，加 dis- 反向
注意别混：人是 disgusted，事物是 disgusting；disgustingly 有时反着用作强调。

dish
核心：四支。一是盘子、碟子；二是一道菜（the local dish）；
三是复数 the dishes 指要洗的碗碟（do the dishes）；
四是碟形的东西（a satellite dish）。
动词：dish something out 是「大量发放、随口给出」，dish up 是「上菜」，
dish the dirt on somebody 是抖料。英式口语的 dishy 说人「长得好看」。
① The dish came out of the oven too hot to put anywhere at all.
= 那只盘子从烤箱里出来时烫得没处放。
a dish = 盘子、碟子
② The local dish is one fish, two potatoes and a great deal of butter.
= 当地那道菜就是一条鱼、两个土豆和一大块黄油。
a local dish = 当地菜肴
③ He does the dishes and she cooks, and neither of them would swap.
= 他洗碗她做饭，两人谁也不肯换。
do the dishes = 洗碗
④ The satellite dish has been on that wall since before anybody moved in.
= 那面墙上的卫星天线，比住进来的任何人都早。
a satellite dish = 卫星天线
⑤ He dishes out advice to people who have not asked for any.
= 他逢人就给建议，人家根本没问。
dish something out = 大量发放、随口给出
宾语放中间放后面都行：dish out advice、dish the advice out。
⑥ She dished up at seven and ate standing at the counter.
= 她七点上菜，自己站在料理台边吃。
dish up = 上菜
⑦ Two of them dished the dirt on a third who had left the year before.
= 他们两个抖起前一年离开的第三个人的料。
dish the dirt on somebody = 抖某人的料
⑧ He is dishy in a way that photographs never catch.
= 他好看得照片拍不出来。
dishy = 长得好看的（英式口语）
⑨ They ordered a few dishes to share and finished all of them before the rice arrived.
= 他们点了几道菜分着吃，米饭还没上就全吃完了。
order a few dishes = 点几道菜
中餐与合餐的语境里最常这么说；一道菜也说 a dish。
⑩ He washes the dishes by hand and claims the machine misses the corners.
= 他坚持手洗碗碟，说洗碗机洗不到角落。
wash the dishes = 洗碗
跟 do the dishes 通用，wash 更强调动作本身。
构词：拉丁语 discus 圆盘，与 disc 同源
注意用法：说洗碗美式也说 do the dishes；英式还说 do the washing-up。

dishabille
核心：（旧，法语借词）衣衫不整：在家里没穿正式衣服的样子，
跟 déshabillé 是同一个词的两种写法。不可数，常说 in dishabille。
① She was in dishabille when the photographer arrived two hours early.
= 摄影师早到了两个钟头，她还没换好衣服。
in dishabille = 衣衫不整地
② The portrait shows him in a studied dishabille that took a valet to arrange.
= 那幅肖像画上他一副刻意的随意装束，是仆人给摆弄出来的。
a studied dishabille = 刻意做出的随意装束
构词：法语 déshabillé 脱去外衣
注意语域：旧词、文学词，今天多带戏谑。

disharmony
核心：不和谐：一是人际与群体之间的失和；二是音乐上的不和谐。
不可数，形容词 disharmonious。偏正式。
① Racial disharmony was the phrase used in the report and in none of the interviews.
= 报告里用的是「族群失和」这个说法，访谈里一次也没出现。
disharmony = 失和、不和谐
② The two wings of the building are disharmonious and the architect said so first.
= 那栋楼的两翼彼此不协调，最先这么说的是建筑师本人。
be disharmonious = 不协调的
构词：dis- 反向 加 harmony 和谐
注意别混：disharmony 偏正式；日常说 tension、friction。

dishcloth
核心：洗碗布：擦洗碗碟用的那块布。可数。
英式的 a dishcloth 洗碗、a tea towel 擦干；美式擦干那块叫 a dishtowel，
洗碗那块也叫 a dishrag。
① The dishcloth needs boiling once a week and nobody has ever done it.
= 那块洗碗布该每周煮一次，从来没人煮过。
a dishcloth = 洗碗布
② She wiped the table with a dishtowel and put it back on the oven door.
= 她用擦碗布抹了桌子，又把它挂回烤箱门上。
a dishtowel = 擦碗布（美式）
③ The dishrag by the sink is older than the sink.
= 水槽边那块抹布比水槽还老。
a dishrag = 洗碗抹布（美式）
构词：dish 加 cloth
注意用法：英式擦干碗碟那块叫 a tea towel，别跟洗碗布混。

dishearten
核心：使灰心：让人失去信心与劲头。及物动词，多用被动。
形容词 disheartened 说人灰了心，disheartening 说事情让人灰心。
① Four rejections in a month disheartened her more than the first one had.
= 一个月里四次被拒，比第一次更让她灰心。
dishearten somebody = 使某人灰心
② It is disheartening to read the same complaint in the file three years running.
= 连着三年在卷宗里读到同一条投诉，让人灰心。
be disheartening = 让人灰心的
构词：dis- 反向 加 hearten 使振作
注意别混：人是 disheartened，事情是 disheartening。

dishevelled
核心：（头发、衣着）凌乱的：一副没顾上打理的样子。形容词，
美式拼 disheveled。名词 dishevelment 少用。
① He arrived dishevelled and gave a talk that nobody remembers a word against.
= 他一身凌乱地赶到，作了个至今没人挑得出毛病的报告。
be dishevelled = 凌乱不整的
② Her dishevelled hair was the only sign that she had been awake since four.
= 头发凌乱是她从四点起就没睡的唯一迹象。
dishevelled hair = 凌乱的头发
构词：古法语 deschevelé 头发披散
注意用法：只说头发衣着，不说房间；说房间乱用 untidy。

dishonest
核心：不诚实的：说人、也说做法。形容词，副词 dishonestly，
名词 dishonesty。
比 lie 覆盖面广：隐瞒、误导、做假账都算 dishonest。
① He is not dishonest, exactly; he just answers a slightly different question.
= 说他不诚实也不准确 —— 他只是回答一个略有不同的问题。
be dishonest = 不诚实的
② The money was obtained dishonestly and repaid before anybody was charged.
= 那笔钱是靠不诚实手段拿到的，还没等有人被起诉就还了回去。
obtain something dishonestly = 以不诚实的手段取得某物
③ Dishonesty on that scale needs at least two people and a lot of patience.
= 那种规模的不诚实，至少要两个人加上极大的耐心。
dishonesty = 不诚实
构词：dis- 反向 加 honest 诚实
注意别混：dishonest 覆盖隐瞒与误导，不只是说谎。

dishonour
核心：耻辱：名词说声誉受损（bring dishonour on somebody），
动词说「使蒙羞」，也说「拒付」——a dishonoured cheque 是被退票的支票。
美式拼 dishonor。形容词 dishonourable，副词 dishonourably。
① The verdict brought dishonour on a family that had run the mill for four generations.
= 那份判决让一个经营磨坊四代的家族蒙羞。
bring dishonour on somebody = 使某人蒙羞
② He was dishonourably discharged and never used the pension he was still owed.
= 他被开除军籍，欠他的那份抚恤金一直没去领。
be dishonourably discharged = 被开除军籍
③ The cheque was dishonoured twice and the second time cost them the contract.
= 那张支票被退了两次，第二次让他们丢了合同。
a dishonoured cheque = 被退付的支票
④ It was a dishonourable way to leave a job he had once been proud of.
= 离开一份他曾引以为豪的工作，那种方式很不光彩。
a dishonourable way = 不光彩的方式
形容词，说行为有失体面；军中开除说 a dishonourable discharge。
构词：dis- 反向 加 honour 荣誉
注意用法：军中开除说 a dishonourable discharge，是固定说法。

dishpan
核心：洗碗盆：美式说法，英式多说 a washing-up bowl。可数。
旧时说 dishpan hands 指洗碗洗到手皮发红开裂。
① She filled the dishpan twice and still had the roasting tin to do.
= 她换了两盆水，烤盘还没洗。
a dishpan = 洗碗盆（美式）
② Adverts in the fifties promised to cure dishpan hands and sold a great deal of soap.
= 五十年代的广告说能治好洗碗洗坏的手，卖出了大量肥皂。
dishpan hands = 洗碗洗得发红开裂的手
构词：dish 加 pan
注意用法：英式说 a washing-up bowl。

dishware
核心：（美式）餐具：盘、碗、碟合起来的统称。不可数。
英式一般说 crockery。
① The dishware is stacked by size and nobody else in the house can find anything.
= 餐具按大小摞着，家里别人什么都找不到。
dishware = 餐具（美式）
② They registered for dishware they have used exactly twice.
= 他们登记的结婚礼物里有一套餐具，总共用过两回。
register for dishware = 登记要一套餐具作礼物
构词：dish 加 ware 器皿
注意用法：英式说 crockery；带装饰的成套瓷器说 china。

dishwasher
核心：两支。一是洗碗机；二是（餐馆里）洗碗工。可数。
洗碗水叫 dishwater，比喻里 as weak as dishwater 说茶或汤淡得像刷锅水。
① The dishwasher takes two hours and gets the glasses cleaner than anybody can.
= 那台洗碗机要跑两个钟头，杯子洗得比谁都干净。
a dishwasher = 洗碗机
② He worked as a dishwasher for one summer and can still stack anything.
= 他当过一个夏天的洗碗工，如今码放什么都在行。
a dishwasher = 洗碗工
③ The tea was as weak as dishwater and the biscuits made up for it.
= 那茶淡得像刷锅水，饼干弥补了这一点。
as weak as dishwater = 淡得像刷锅水
构词：dish 加 washer
注意用法：说机器与说人共用一个词，靠上下文分。

disillusion
核心：使幻灭：让人看清真相、不再抱不切实际的期待。及物动词与名词同形，
名词更常说 disillusionment。形容词 disillusioned 说人「不再抱幻想」。
① Two years in that department disillusioned him about the whole profession.
= 在那个部门待的两年，让他对整个行业不再抱幻想。
disillusion somebody about something = 使某人对某事幻灭
② She left disillusioned and came back four years later on her own terms.
= 她心灰意冷地离开，四年后按自己的条件回来了。
be disillusioned = 不再抱幻想的
③ Disillusionment with the process, not with the goal, is what emptied the meetings.
= 让会议空掉的是对流程的失望，不是对目标的失望。
disillusionment = 幻灭、失望
构词：dis- 反向 加 illusion 幻觉
注意别混：disillusioned 是看清了真相，disappointed 只是没达到期待。

disincentive
核心：反向激励：一项安排本意也许不坏，实际效果却是让人不去做某事。
可数，常说 a disincentive to do something。
动词 disincentivize 是「使人失去做某事的动力」，多见于政策文字。
① The rule is a disincentive to report anything at all and the numbers show it.
= 那条规定实际上让人不愿上报任何情况，数字说明了这一点。
a disincentive to do something = 让人不去做某事的因素
② Charging for the second appointment disincentivizes exactly the people who need it.
= 第二次就诊收费，恰好把最需要看病的人挡了回去。
disincentivize somebody = 让某人失去动力
构词：dis- 反向 加 incentive 激励
注意用法：常说 act as a disincentive、create a disincentive。

disinclination
核心：（正式）不情愿：不想做某事的那种心思，语气克制。不可数，
常说 a disinclination to do something。形容词 disinclined。
① There was a general disinclination to be the first person to speak at a meeting nobody had called.
= 那场没人召集的会上，大家普遍都不情愿第一个开口。
a disinclination to do something = 不情愿做某事
② She was disinclined to argue about the parking space for a fourth winter running.
= 为那个车位连着争了三个冬天，第四个冬天她不想再争了。
be disinclined to do something = 不愿做某事
构词：dis- 反向 加 inclination 意愿
注意语域：正式与含蓄；日常说 not want to、be reluctant to。

disinfect
核心：消毒：把物体表面或伤口上的病菌杀掉。及物动词。
名词 disinfection；a disinfectant 是消毒剂。
跟 sterilize 分界：sterilize 是彻底灭菌，disinfect 只是把病菌降到安全水平。
① They disinfect the handles twice a day and the door frames never.
= 他们一天给门把手消两次毒，门框却从来不管。
disinfect something = 给某物消毒
② The disinfectant smells of hospitals and works better than anything else they tried.
= 那种消毒剂一股医院味，却比他们试过的任何东西都管用。
a disinfectant = 消毒剂
③ Disinfection of the ward took four hours and had to be signed off twice.
= 病区消毒用了四个钟头，还得签两次字确认。
disinfection = 消毒
构词：dis- 去掉 加 infect 感染
注意别混：disinfect 是消毒，sterilize 是彻底灭菌。

disinformation
核心：假消息：有意散布的不实信息，目的是误导 ——
跟 misinformation 分界：后者不一定出于故意。不可数。
① The campaign was pure disinformation and took nine months to unpick.
= 那场活动纯属有意造假，用了九个月才拆解清楚。
disinformation = 蓄意散布的假消息
② A disinformation campaign works best when part of it is true.
= 假消息攻势里掺着真话时最有效。
a disinformation campaign = 假消息攻势
构词：俄语 dezinformatsiya 的仿译
注意别混：disinformation 是故意的，misinformation 可能只是弄错了。

disingenuous
核心：假装老实的：话说得像坦白，其实避重就轻、藏着东西。
形容词，副词 disingenuously。语气是批评，但比 dishonest 含蓄。
① It is disingenuous to call it a consultation when the decision was already signed.
= 决定都签了还管它叫征求意见，未免太不老实。
be disingenuous = 假装坦白的
② He answered disingenuously and technically told the truth throughout.
= 他答得避重就轻，从技术上说通篇都是实话。
answer disingenuously = 避重就轻地作答
构词：dis- 反向 加 ingenuous 天真坦率的
注意别混：disingenuous 是假坦率，dishonest 是直接不诚实。

disinherit
核心：剥夺继承权：立遗嘱把本该继承的人排除在外。及物动词，多用被动。
① He disinherited two of his children and left the house to a cousin.
= 他剥夺了两个孩子的继承权，把房子留给了一个表亲。
disinherit somebody = 剥夺某人的继承权
② She was disinherited in 1971 and the will was contested for six years.
= 她一九七一年被剥夺继承权，那份遗嘱被争讼了六年。
be disinherited = 被剥夺继承权
构词：dis- 反向 加 inherit 继承
注意别混：disinherit 是不给继承，dispossess 是把已有的产业夺走。

disinhibit
核心：（心理学与医学）解除抑制：让人放下平时的自我约束 ——
酒精、脑损伤、匿名环境都能造成。及物动词，名词 disinhibition。
① Alcohol disinhibits before it does anything else and that is most of the trouble.
= 酒精最先起的作用是解除自控，麻烦大半由此而来。
disinhibit somebody = 解除某人的自我约束
② Online disinhibition explains a great deal of what people write and none of what they mean.
= 网络上的抑制解除解释了人们写下的很多东西，却解释不了他们的本意。
disinhibition = 抑制解除
构词：dis- 反向 加 inhibit 抑制
注意语域：心理学与医学术语，日常说 lose your inhibitions。
