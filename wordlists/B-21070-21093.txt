sick
/sɪk/
核心：形容词：有病的、生病的：be sick、a sick child；恶心的、想吐的：feel sick、be sick（英式：呕吐）；厌倦的、腻烦的：be sick of something；（笑话、言行）病态的、令人反感的：a sick joke；（俚语）极好的、酷的。名词，不可数（英式口语）：呕吐物。习语：sick and tired of 是厌烦透了；call in sick 是打电话请病假；worried sick 是担心死了。
① My little brother is sick, so he can't go to school today.
= 我弟弟病了，所以今天不能去上学。
sick = 生病的
② I felt sick on the boat and had to lie down for the whole journey.
= 我在船上觉得恶心，整个行程都只好躺着。
sick = 恶心的、不舒服的
feel sick = 想吐
③ I'm sick of eating pasta every night this week; let's have something different tonight.
= 这个星期我受够了每晚都吃意面，今晚咱们换点别的吧。
be sick of = 厌烦
④ After three weeks, she was sick and tired of waiting for him to phone her back.
= 三个星期过去了，她等他回电话等得烦透了。
sick and tired of = 厌烦透了
⑤ He called in sick on Monday, but his boss saw him at the football match.
= 他星期一打电话请了病假，可他老板在足球赛上看到了他。
call in sick = 打电话请病假
⑥ Your mother was worried sick when you didn't come home last night.
= 你昨晚没回家，你妈妈担心死了。
worried sick = 担心死了
⑦ At the funeral dinner, my uncle told a sick joke, and nobody at the table laughed.
= 葬礼后的晚宴上，我叔叔讲了个恶心的笑话，桌上没一个人笑。
a sick joke = 令人反感的笑话
⑧ The airline gives every passenger a sick bag in case they feel ill during the flight.
= 航空公司给每位乘客备了一个呕吐袋，以防飞行中不舒服。
a sick bag = 呕吐袋
⑨ Workers in the new office kept getting headaches, and experts said it was sick building syndrome.
= 新办公楼里的员工老是头痛，专家说这是病态建筑综合征。
sick building syndrome = 病态建筑综合征
⑩ I had to take a sick day yesterday because I had a high temperature.
= 我昨天发高烧，只好请了一天病假。
a sick day = 一天病假
⑪ My mother used to get terrible sick headaches that kept her in bed all day.
= 我母亲以前常犯严重的偏头痛，一整天都下不了床。
a sick headache = （伴有恶心的）偏头痛
⑫ After the operation on his back, he was on sick leave from the factory for three months.
= 背部手术后，他从工厂休了三个月病假。
sick leave = 病假
⑬ The film was so violent that I found it sick-making and walked out after half an hour.
= 那部电影暴力得让我恶心，看了半小时就走了。
sick-making = （英式口语）令人作呕的
⑭ Most companies give their workers sick pay for the first few weeks of an illness.
= 大多数公司在员工生病的头几个星期照发病假工资。
sick pay = 病假工资
⑮ Just as we were leaving for the party, the baby sicked up some milk all over my shirt.
= 我们正要出门去派对，宝宝把一些奶吐在了我的衬衫上。
sick up = （英式口语）吐出
