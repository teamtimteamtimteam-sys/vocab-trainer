ruined
/ˈruːɪnd/
核心：形容词：（建筑）毁坏的、成了废墟的：a ruined castle；被毁掉的、糟蹋了的：a ruined holiday；破产的、身败名裂的。由 ruin 的过去分词而来。
① We had our picnic in the shadow of a ruined castle on top of the hill.
= 我们在山顶一座城堡废墟的阴影里野餐。
a ruined castle = 城堡废墟
② The family returned from their ruined holiday with sunburn, stomach bugs and no luggage.
= 那一家人度了个糟透了的假，晒伤了，闹肚子，行李也丢了。
a ruined holiday = 被毁掉的假期
③ After the bank failed, he was a ruined man and had to sell his house.
= 银行倒闭后，他破了产，只好把房子卖了。
a ruined man = 破了产的人

ruinous
/ˈruːɪnəs/
核心：形容词：（代价）高得惊人的、会导致破产的：ruinous costs；毁灭性的；（建筑）破败的。副词 ruinously 是极其（昂贵）地。
① The court case dragged on for years, and the legal costs were ruinous for both families.
= 那场官司拖了好几年，诉讼费让两家人都倾家荡产。
ruinous costs = 高得惊人的费用
② The war had a ruinous effect on the economy, and it took twenty years to recover.
= 战争对经济造成了毁灭性的影响，过了二十年才恢复过来。
a ruinous effect = 毁灭性的影响
③ Houses in the city centre have become ruinously expensive for young families.
= 市中心的房子对年轻家庭来说已经贵得离谱了。
ruinously expensive = 贵得离谱

rule
/ruːl/
核心：名词：规则、规定（可数）：follow the rules、break the rules、bend the rules；惯例、常规（单数）：the rule rather than the exception；统治、管辖（不可数）：under British rule、the rule of law；（旧）直尺。
动词：统治、管辖：rule a country；（法庭、官员）裁定：rule that…、rule on something；（感情等）支配：be ruled by；在纸上画（直线）。
习语：as a rule 是通常；rule of thumb 是经验法则；rule the roost 是当家作主、说了算。名词 ruler、ruling，形容词 ruled 另立条。
① All players must follow the rules, and anyone who argues with the referee will be sent off.
= 所有球员都必须守规矩，谁跟裁判争吵就会被罚下场。
follow the rules = 守规矩
② The boy was sent home because he had broken the school rules about mobile phones.
= 那个男孩违反了学校关于手机的规定，被打发回家了。
break the rules = 违反规定
③ The teacher sometimes bends the rules and lets us leave early on Friday afternoons.
= 老师有时会通融一下，让我们星期五下午早点走。
bend the rules = 通融、变通规定
④ As a rule, I go to bed at eleven and get up at seven, even at weekends.
= 我通常十一点睡、七点起，周末也一样。
as a rule = 通常
多放在句首，后面跟逗号，说的是自己或别人的习惯。
⑤ The country was under British rule for nearly two hundred years before it became independent in 1947.
= 那个国家在英国统治下将近两百年，一九四七年才独立。
under British rule = 在英国统治下
rule = 统治
⑥ The queen ruled the country for over sixty years and was loved by most of her people.
= 女王统治那个国家六十多年，深受大多数臣民爱戴。
rule a country = 统治一个国家
⑦ The judge ruled that the evidence could not be used because the police had obtained it illegally.
= 法官裁定那项证据不能采用，因为警方是非法取得的。
rule that = 裁定
⑧ In a democracy, nobody is above the law, and the rule of law protects everyone.
= 在民主社会里，没有人凌驾于法律之上，法治保护每一个人。
the rule of law = 法治
⑨ A good rule of thumb is to drink a glass of water every time you have a cup of coffee.
= 一条好用的经验法则是：每喝一杯咖啡就喝一杯水。
rule of thumb = 经验法则
⑩ In our house it is my grandmother who rules the roost, and nobody argues with her.
= 在我们家，说了算的是祖母，谁也不跟她争。
rule the roost = 当家作主、说了算
⑪ In this city, long working hours are the rule rather than the exception.
= 在这座城市里，长时间工作是常态而不是例外。
the rule rather than the exception = 常态而非例外
⑫ Don't let your heart rule your head when you are deciding which house to buy.
= 决定买哪套房子时，别让感情支配理智。
let your heart rule your head = 感情用事

rumble
/ˈrʌmbl/
核心：动词：发出隆隆声、轰隆作响：thunder rumbles、my stomach is rumbling；（车辆）隆隆驶过：rumble past；（英式口语）识破、看穿：be rumbled。名词，可数：隆隆声：a rumble of thunder；（北美旧式俚语）街头群殴。
① Thunder rumbled in the distance as we hurried to put up the tent before the storm arrived.
= 远处雷声隆隆，我们赶在暴风雨来之前忙着把帐篷搭起来。
thunder rumbles = 雷声隆隆

② My stomach rumbled loudly in the middle of the exam because I had not eaten any breakfast.
= 我没吃早饭，考试考到一半肚子咕咕叫得很响。
your stomach rumbles = 肚子咕咕叫
③ Heavy lorries rumbled past our house all night on their way to the new port.
= 重型卡车整夜从我们家门前隆隆驶过，开往新港口。
rumble past = 隆隆驶过
④ We heard the low rumble of traffic from the motorway even with all the windows closed.
= 即使窗户全关着，我们也听得见高速公路上车流低沉的隆隆声。
the rumble of traffic = 车流的隆隆声
⑤ The thieves had been selling fake tickets for months before they were finally rumbled by the police.
= 那伙小偷卖了好几个月假票，最后才被警察识破。
be rumbled = 被识破
这一义是英式口语，多用被动，指骗局、秘密被人看穿。

ruminate
/ˈruːmɪneɪt/
核心：动词，不及物：（正式）沉思、反复思考：ruminate on / about / over something；（动物）反刍。本义是反刍，引申为把一件事在脑子里翻来覆去地想。

ruminate on something = 反复思考某事

② The cows lay in the shade of the trees, ruminating quietly through the hottest part of the afternoon.
= 下午最热的时候，牛群躺在树荫下静静地反刍。
ruminate = 反刍

rye bread = 黑麦面包

ryegrass
/ˈraɪɡrɑːs/
核心：黑麦草：一种生长快的草，用来做牧草、铺草坪。不可数。
① The farmer sowed ryegrass in the field to feed his cows.
= 那个农民在地里种了黑麦草喂牛。
sow ryegrass = 种黑麦草
ryegrass = 黑麦草
② The new lawn was made with a mixture of ryegrass and other grasses.
= 新草坪用黑麦草和其他草混种而成。
a mixture of ryegrass = 黑麦草混种
