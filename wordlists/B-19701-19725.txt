safe
/seɪf/
核心：形容词，本义是「不受伤害、没有危险」：安全的：safe from、keep something safe；（人）平安的、无恙的：safe and sound；（地方、做法）不会出事的、稳妥的：a safe place、a safe bet、play it safe；（人）可靠的、可信赖的：in safe hands；（选择）保守的、不冒险的。副词 safely 是安全地、平安地；稳妥地。名词 safety 是安全（另立条）。名词 safe：保险箱、保险柜。
习语：better safe than sorry 是小心为上；to be on the safe side 是为了保险起见；safe and sound 是安然无恙。
① The castle walls kept the people safe from attack for more than two hundred years.
= 城墙保护那里的人免遭攻击两百多年。
safe = 安全的
keep somebody safe from something = 保护某人免受某事伤害
safe from 是「免受……伤害」，主语可以是人，也可以是东西、地方。
② Is it safe to swim in the river, or is the water too dirty?
= 在那条河里游泳安全吗？还是水太脏了？
it is safe to do something = 做某事是安全的
这个句型里也可加 for somebody，放在 to do 前面。
③ After three days lost in the mountains, the climbers came home safe and sound.
= 在山里迷路三天之后，登山者们安然无恙地回到了家。
safe and sound = 安然无恙
④ Keep your passport in a safe place, because you will need it again at the airport.
= 把护照放在一个稳妥的地方，到机场你还要用。
a safe place = 稳妥的地方
⑤ Take an umbrella just to be on the safe side; the sky looks very grey.
= 为了保险起见，带把伞吧，天看起来很阴。
to be on the safe side = 为了保险起见
⑥ I think it is a safe bet that prices will go up again next year.
= 我想明年物价还会涨，这几乎是十拿九稳的事。
a safe bet = 稳妥的预测、有把握的事
也说 it is a safe bet that…，指几乎可以肯定会发生。
⑦ I could take the motorway, but I will play it safe and go by train because of the snow.
= 我可以走高速公路，不过因为下雪，我还是稳妥点坐火车去。
play it safe = 稳妥行事
⑧ Don't worry about the children; they are in safe hands with their grandmother.
= 别担心孩子们，有他们奶奶照看很稳妥。
in safe hands = 有可靠的人照看
⑨ Check the door is locked before you go to bed — better safe than sorry.
= 睡觉前查一下门锁好没有，小心为上。
better safe than sorry = 小心为上
⑩ The plane landed safely in the storm, and all the passengers clapped.
= 飞机在暴风雨中安全着陆，所有乘客都鼓起掌来。
safely = 安全地
常和 arrive、land、get home 连用，表示一路平安。
⑪ I think we can safely say that the worst of the winter is over.
= 我想我们可以有把握地说，冬天最冷的时候已经过去了。
can safely say = 可以有把握地说
⑫ Safety comes first in our factory, and every worker wears a helmet and boots.
= 我们工厂安全第一，每个工人都戴头盔、穿防护靴。
safety = 安全（名词）
⑬ The thieves broke into the office at night and opened the safe with a drill.
= 窃贼夜里闯进办公室，用钻头撬开了保险柜。
a safe = 保险柜
名词 a safe 可数，指装贵重物品的铁箱、铁柜，常说 open the safe。
⑭ The witness was kept in a safe house in the country until the trial was over.
= 那位证人一直被安置在乡下的一处安全屋里，直到审判结束。
a safe house = 安全屋
⑮ The general was given safe conduct to cross the border and attend the peace talks.
= 那位将军获准安全通行，越过边境去参加和谈。
safe conduct = 安全通行（证）
⑯ She keeps her mother's jewellery in a safe deposit box at the bank.
= 她把母亲的首饰放在银行的保险箱里。
a safe deposit box = 银行保险箱
a safety deposit box = 银行保险箱（另一种说法）
⑰ If your computer keeps crashing, start it in safe mode and remove the new program.
= 如果电脑老是死机，就用安全模式启动，把新装的程序删掉。
safe mode = （电脑的）安全模式
⑱ The new house has a safe room in the basement with a steel door and its own telephone line.
= 新房子的地下室有一间避难室，装着钢门和独立的电话线。
a safe room = 避难室
⑲ The party has held this safe seat for sixty years, so the result was never in doubt.
= 那个党六十年来一直把持着这个稳赢的选区，所以结果从来没有悬念。
a safe seat = 稳操胜券的选区
⑳ The school nurse gives older students lessons about safe sex and health.
= 学校护士给高年级学生上关于安全性行为和健康的课。
safe sex = 安全性行为
㉑ The youth club wants to be a safe space where young people can talk about their problems.
= 那个青年俱乐部想成为一个安全空间，让年轻人能谈论自己的烦恼。
a safe space = 安全空间
近年流行的说法，指不受歧视、骚扰、批评的场所或氛围。

safeguard
/ˈseɪfɡɑːd/
核心：名词，可数：保护措施、防范手段：a safeguard against something。动词：保护、维护：safeguard somebody's interests、safeguard against something。比 protect 正式，多见于法律、政策。
① The new law includes safeguards against the misuse of personal information by companies.
= 新法律包含了防止公司滥用个人信息的保护措施。
a safeguard against something = 防范某事的措施
多用复数 safeguards，常见于法律条文和政策文件。
② The union's job is to safeguard the interests of its members during the talks.
= 工会的职责是在谈判中维护会员的利益。
safeguard somebody's interests = 维护某人的利益
③ Wash your hands often to safeguard against colds and flu in the winter.
= 冬天要勤洗手，预防感冒和流感。
safeguard against something = 预防某事

safety
/ˈseɪfti/
核心：名词，不可数：安全、平安：for your own safety、safety first；安全性：road safety、food safety；安全的地方：reach safety、lead somebody to safety。
作定语表示「保安全用的」，构成一大族复合词。
① For your own safety, please keep your seat belt fastened until the plane has stopped.
= 为了您自身的安全，飞机停稳前请系好安全带。
for your own safety = 为了你自己的安全
safety = 安全
② The sign at the factory gate says "Safety first" in large red letters.
= 工厂大门上的牌子用大红字写着「安全第一」。
safety first = 安全第一
③ The children learned about road safety from a police officer who visited their school.
= 一位来学校的警官给孩子们讲了道路安全。
road safety = 道路安全
safety 作定语前置于另一个名词，构成 food safety、fire safety、safety rules 等。
④ The firefighters carried the old man to safety through the thick black smoke on the third floor.
= 消防员穿过三楼浓浓的黑烟，把老人抬到了安全的地方。
carry somebody to safety = 把某人送到安全的地方
to safety 前多接表示移动的动词，说把人带到安全处。
⑤ A safe road is one you can cross safely, so the council built a crossing near the school.
= 安全的路是能安全过马路的路，所以市政在学校附近修了人行横道。
safe = 安全的（形容词）
safely = 安全地
⑥ Always wear your safety belt, even if you are only driving to the shops.
= 一定要系安全带，哪怕只是开车去商店。
a safety belt = 安全带
汽车上更常说 seat belt；safety belt 也指高空作业系的安全带。
⑦ The soldier checked that the safety catch was on before he put the gun away.
= 士兵把枪收起来之前，确认保险栓已经扣上。
the safety catch = （枪的）保险栓
⑧ When the fire started backstage, the safety curtain came down and the audience left calmly.
= 后台起火时，防火幕降了下来，观众平静地离场。
the safety curtain = （剧院的）防火幕
⑨ The shop windows are made of safety glass, which breaks into small pieces that do not cut.
= 店面的窗户用的是安全玻璃，碎了会变成小块，不会割伤人。
safety glass = 安全玻璃
⑩ We waited on the safety island in the middle of the busy road until the lights changed.
= 我们在繁忙马路中间的安全岛上等着，直到信号灯变了。
a safety island = 安全岛
⑪ The old miners used safety lamps that would not set fire to the gas underground.
= 老矿工用的是不会引燃地下瓦斯的安全矿灯。
a safety lamp = （矿工的）安全灯
⑫ Safety matches only light when you strike them on the side of the box.
= 安全火柴只有在火柴盒侧面划才点得着。
a safety match = 安全火柴
⑬ The school has introduced new safety measures, including cameras at every gate.
= 学校采取了新的安全措施，包括在每个大门装摄像头。
a safety measure = 安全措施
⑭ My grandfather shaved every morning with an old safety razor that had belonged to his father.
= 我祖父每天早上用他父亲留下的一把老式安全剃刀刮胡子。
a safety razor = 安全剃刀
⑮ For many workers, sport is a safety valve that helps them forget the stress of the job.
= 对许多上班族来说，运动是一种宣泄渠道，帮他们忘掉工作压力。
a safety valve = 安全阀；（比喻）宣泄渠道

sag
/sæɡ/
核心：动词，不及物：（中间）下垂、下陷：the roof sags、the bed sags；（价格、兴致）下跌、减弱：sales sagged。名词，单数或不可数：下垂、下陷；下跌。

= 他以前星期五常逃学，跟表哥们去钓鱼。

= 那座桥中间微微下陷，工程师们把它封了起来进行维修。
a sag = 下陷

sail
/seɪl/
核心：名词：帆（可数）：hoist / lower the sails；（单数）帆船航行：go for a sail；（风车的）翼板。动词：航行、驾船、开船：sail across、sail for；（船）启航：the ship sails at noon；驾驶（帆船）：sail a yacht；（人或物）轻快地移动、飘过：sail past；轻松通过：sail through an exam。
习语：set sail 是起航；sail close to the wind 是（做事）冒险、几近违规。名词 sailing（帆船运动）、sailor（水手）另立条。
① We hoisted the sails as soon as we left the harbour and headed out to sea.
= 一出港我们就升起帆，驶向大海。
hoist the sails = 升帆
a sail = 帆
② They sailed across the Atlantic in a small yacht and arrived in the Caribbean after four weeks.
= 他们驾一艘小游艇横渡大西洋，四个星期后到达加勒比海。
sail = 航行
不及物时主语可以是人，也可以是船；说船按时刻表开出也用 sail。
sail across something = 横渡某处
③ The ferry sails for France at eight o'clock every morning, even in winter.
= 那班渡轮每天早上八点开往法国，冬天也不例外。
sail for somewhere = 启航前往某地
④ The explorers set sail from Plymouth in the spring of 1620 and reached America two months later.
= 探险者们于一六二〇年春天从普利茅斯起航，两个月后到达美洲。
set sail = 起航
⑤ My uncle taught me to sail a small boat on the lake when I was ten.
= 我十岁时叔叔在湖上教我驾小船。
sail a boat = 驾船
⑥ She sailed through her driving test the first time, without a single mistake.
= 她第一次就轻松通过了驾照考试，一个错都没犯。
sail through = 轻松通过
宾语多是考试、面试、选举等关卡，带「毫不费力」的意味。

= 那个年轻的帆板手掉进水里好多次，才学会保持平衡。
a sailboarder = 帆板手

sailing
/ˈseɪlɪŋ/

① My parents took up sailing when they retired, and now they spend every weekend at the coast.
= 我父母退休后开始玩帆船，现在每个周末都在海边度过。

a sailing ship = （旧时的大）帆船
