parse
/pɑːz/
核心：动词，及物。本义是（语法）对（句子）作语法分析：把句子拆成各个成分，说明每个词的词性和作用；
（计算机）解析：让程序把一串文字、数据拆成它能处理的各部分；引申为细细琢磨、推敲（一句话）的含义。
① Pupils used to spend hours parsing Latin sentences on the blackboard.
= 过去的学生要花上几个钟头在黑板上分析拉丁语句子。
parse a sentence = 对句子作语法分析
② The program parses each line of the file and picks out the dates.
= 那个程序逐行解析文件，把日期挑出来。
parse a file = 解析文件
③ Journalists spent the day parsing the president's short statement for hidden meanings.
= 记者们花了一天推敲总统那份简短的声明，想找出言外之意。
parse a statement = 推敲一份声明

part
/pɑːt/
核心：本义是「整体里分出来的一块」。名词：
part of something 不带冠词，是「一部分」：Part of the roof fell in；带冠词的 a part 是可以数的一块、一段；
具体下去：（机器的）零件：spare parts；（身体的）部位；（书、戏、连续剧的）一部、一集；（配比里的）一份：one part milk to two parts water；
地区（常用复数）：in these parts；（戏里的）角色、台词；（一件事里某人的）参与、份儿：have a part in；（美式）头发的分缝。
动词从「分开」来：（人）分手、分别，介词用 from；（合着的东西）分开、向两边让开；使分开；把（头发）分缝。part with 是舍得交出、割舍。
副词：part … part … 是一半是……一半是……。
习语：take part (in) 是参加；play a part (in) 是在……中起作用；for the most part 是大体上、多半；in part 是在某种程度上、部分地；
for my part 是就我来说；on the part of somebody、on somebody's part 是由某人作出的、在某人那方面；the best part of、the better part of 是（一段时间、一个数量的）大半；
look the part 是看上去像那么回事；part and parcel of 是……不可缺少的一部分；part company 是分手、分道扬镳，也指意见不合；
take something in good part 是不见怪；a man of many parts 是多才多艺的人；want no part of 是不想沾边；part of the furniture 是待得太久、已不被人注意的人或物。
复合词：part of speech 是词类、词性；part-time 是兼职的、非全日的，也作副词，人叫 part-timer；part-way 是到中途；
part exchange 是（英式）以旧折价换新；part-work 是（英式）分册陆续出版的书刊。并入本条。
① Part of the roof fell in during the storm last night.
= 昨晚暴风雨的时候，屋顶塌了一块。
part of something = 某物的一部分
作「一部分」讲时不带冠词，不说 a part of the roof。
② We spent the early part of the evening in the garden.
= 傍晚早些时候我们是在花园里过的。
the early part of something = 某事的前一段
③ The garage could not get the parts for such an old car.
= 那家修车厂弄不到这么老的车的零件。
parts = 零件
spare parts = 备用零件
④ The cream should not be used on sensitive parts of the body.
= 这种药膏不可用在身体的敏感部位。
parts of the body = 身体的部位
⑤ The final part of the series will be shown next Sunday.
= 那部连续剧的最后一集下星期天播。
the final part = 最后一集
⑥ Mix one part vinegar with three parts oil.
= 把一份醋和三份油兑在一起。
one part vinegar = 一份醋
说配比用，各份大小相同。
⑦ We do not see many strangers in these parts.
= 我们这一带不大见得到生人。
in these parts = 在这一带
⑧ She has been offered the part of the queen in a new film.
= 有人请她在一部新片里演女王这个角色。
the part of somebody = 某人这个角色
⑨ He admitted that he had had a part in planning the robbery.
= 他承认策划那起抢劫有他一份。
have a part in something = 在某事中有份
⑩ He wears his hair with a part on the left side.
= 他的头发在左边分缝。
a part = 头发的分缝（美式）
⑪ We parted at the station and promised to write every week.
= 我们在车站分了手，说好每星期写信。
part = 分手、分别
⑫ She has never been parted from her daughter for more than a day.
= 她从没跟女儿分开超过一天。
be parted from somebody = 与某人分开
⑬ The crowd parted to let the ambulance through.
= 人群向两边让开，让救护车过去。
the crowd parted = 人群向两边让开
⑭ She parted the curtains an inch and looked out at the street.
= 她把窗帘拨开一条缝，朝街上张望。
part the curtains = 拨开窗帘
⑮ He parts his hair in the middle like a schoolboy in an old photograph.
= 他把头发从中间分开，像老照片里的小学生。

⑯ He would rather starve than part with his father's violin.
= 他宁可挨饿，也舍不得出手父亲的那把小提琴。

⑰ The building is part hotel, part museum.
= 那座楼一半是旅馆，一半是博物馆。

⑱ Over two hundred runners took part in the race along the coast.

㉑ The delay was due in part to bad weather over the Atlantic.

in part = 部分地、在某种程度上
㉒ For my part, I would be happy to stay at home this Christmas.

㉓ It was a serious mistake on the part of the hospital.

㉔ We waited for the best part of an hour before a taxi came.

㉕ In his dark suit and silver tie he certainly looked the part.

㉜ She has worked at the library so long that she is part of the furniture.

核心：三支，可数。一是微粒、极小的一点：a particle of dust；比喻用法 not a particle of 是丝毫没有；
二是（物理）粒子：构成原子的电子、质子一类；三是（语法）小品词：跟动词搭成短语动词的副词、介词，如 give up 里的 up。
particle physics 是粒子物理学，不可数。并入本条。
① Particles of dust floated in the beam of sunlight.
= 灰尘的微粒在那束阳光里飘浮。
particles of dust = 灰尘的微粒
② There is not a particle of truth in the story.
= 那个说法没有一丝一毫是真的。
not a particle of truth = 没有丝毫真实
③ The machine fires particles at each other at almost the speed of light.
= 那台机器让粒子以接近光速相撞。
a particle = 粒子
④ In "look after", the particle changes the meaning of the verb completely.
= 在「look after」里，小品词把动词的意思整个变了。
a particle = 小品词
⑤ She gave up a career in particle physics to write novels.
= 她放弃了粒子物理学的事业去写小说。
particle physics = 粒子物理学
⑥ The cheap shelves are made of particle board covered with a thin layer of oak.
= 那些便宜的架子是刨花板做的，外面贴了薄薄一层橡木。
particle board = 刨花板
美式说法，英式多说 chipboard。

particular
/pəˈtɪkjələ(r)/
核心：形容词。本义是「单单这一个的」：只作定语，指特定的、个别的那一个，不是泛指；引申为特别的、格外的：of particular interest；
作表语是讲究的、挑剔的：be particular about，口语里 I'm not particular 是我无所谓。
名词：（正式）细节、事项，可数，多用复数：particulars 是详细情况、（房产等的）书面资料。
习语：in particular 一是尤其、特别是，二是跟在 anything、nothing、anyone 后面，指具体的、特别的；in every particular 是（正式）在每个细节上。
① Is there a particular type of music that you enjoy most?
= 有没有哪一种音乐是你最喜欢的？
a particular type = 特定的一种
② I had no particular reason for choosing that hotel, apart from its low price.
= 我选那家旅馆没什么特别的理由，只是图它便宜。
no particular reason = 没什么特别的理由
③ The last chapter will be of particular interest to teachers.
= 最后一章教师会格外感兴趣。
of particular interest = 格外让人感兴趣
④ She is very particular about the way her coffee is made.
= 她对咖啡怎么煮非常讲究。
be particular about something = 对某事讲究
⑤ Give me whichever room is free, because I am not particular about the view.
= 哪间空着就给我哪间吧，看得见什么景我无所谓。
I am not particular = 我无所谓
⑥ The nurse wrote down his particulars before he saw the doctor.
= 看医生之前，护士把他的个人情况记了下来。
somebody's particulars = 某人的个人情况
⑦ The agent sent us the particulars of three houses near the school.
= 中介给我们寄来了学校附近三处房子的资料。
the particulars of something = 某物的详细资料
⑧ The whole meal was good, and the fish in particular was excellent.
= 整顿饭都好，尤其是那条鱼，好极了。
in particular = 尤其、特别是
⑨ We spent the whole weekend at home doing nothing in particular and enjoyed every minute.
= 整个周末我们都待在家里，没干什么特别的事，却过得有滋有味。
nothing in particular = 没什么特别的
⑩ Her account was correct in every particular.
= 她的叙述每个细节都准确无误。
in every particular = 在每个细节上

particularity
/pəˌtɪkjuˈlærəti/
核心：（正式）三支。一是独特性、个别性，不可数；二是细致、精确，不可数；三是特点、细节，可数，多用复数 particularities。
① The novelist captures the particularity of each street in the old town.
= 那位小说家把老城每条街的独特之处都写了出来。
the particularity of something = 某物的独特性
② She described the room with great particularity, down to the pattern on the cups.
= 她把那间屋子描述得极其细致，连杯子上的花纹都说到了。
with great particularity = 极其细致地
③ The law has to allow for the particularities of each case.
= 法律得顾及每个案子的具体情形。
the particularities of a case = 案子的具体情形

particularize
/pəˈtɪkjələraɪz/
核心：（正式）逐一列举、详细说明：把笼统的话落到具体的例子、细节上。英式也拼作 particularise。
① The complaint does not particularize the faults that the customer found.
= 那份投诉没有逐一说明顾客发现了哪些毛病。
particularize something = 详细说明某事
② He spoke of "serious errors" but refused to particularize.
= 他说有「严重错误」，却不肯具体说是哪些。
refuse to particularize = 不肯具体说

particularly
/pəˈtɪkjələli/
核心：副词：特别、尤其：一是放在形容词、副词、动词前加强程度，否定句里 not particularly 是不怎么、不太；
二是从一类里单挑出一个来强调：particularly in winter。
① The traffic through the town centre is particularly bad on Friday evenings before a holiday.
= 赶上节前的星期五晚上，穿过镇中心的交通特别糟。
particularly bad = 特别糟
② I did not particularly want to go, but I could not think of an excuse.
= 我不怎么想去，可又想不出借口。
not particularly = 不怎么、不太
③ The disease is dangerous, particularly for the very old and the very young.
= 这种病很危险，对年纪很大的和很小的尤其如此。
particularly for somebody = 对某类人尤其如此
④ When the waiter asked whether they had enjoyed the meal, he answered, "Not particularly."
= 服务员问他们饭吃得可好，他答了一句：「不怎么样。」
Not particularly. = 不怎么样。
单独作答。

particulate
/pɑːˈtɪkjələt/
核心：（术语）形容词：微粒的、微粒状的。名词 particulates：（空气里的）悬浮微粒、颗粒物，复数。
① Diesel engines give off more particulate matter than petrol engines.
= 柴油机排出的颗粒物比汽油机多。
particulate matter = 颗粒物
② The level of particulates in the city air is highest in winter.
= 那座城市空气里的悬浮颗粒物冬天最高。
particulates = 悬浮颗粒物
