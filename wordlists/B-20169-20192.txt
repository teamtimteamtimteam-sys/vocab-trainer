screw
/skruː/
核心：名词，可数：螺丝、螺钉：tighten a screw；（船的）螺旋桨；（英式俚语）狱警。动词：用螺丝固定：screw something to something；拧、旋：screw the lid on；（俚语）欺骗、敲诈：get screwed。另有一支粗俗用法，指性交，带冒犯性，这一义不配例句。
习语：have a screw loose 是（口语）脑子有毛病；put the screws on somebody 是对某人施加压力。
短语动词与复合词：screw up 是搞砸，screwed-up（另立条）是心理有问题的；screw your eyes up 是眯起眼睛；screw your face up 是皱起脸；screw around 是瞎混、胡闹；screw cap / screw top 是螺旋盖。
① He used a screwdriver to tighten the loose screw on the door handle.
= 他用螺丝刀拧紧了门把手上松动的螺丝。
a screw = 螺丝
tighten a screw = 拧紧螺丝
② We screwed the shelf to the wall so that it would not fall down.
= 我们把架子用螺丝钉在墙上，免得掉下来。
screw something to something = 用螺丝把某物固定在某处
③ Screw the lid on tightly, or the juice will leak in your bag.
= 把盖子拧紧，不然果汁会漏在你包里。
screw the lid on = 拧上盖子
④ The tourists were screwed by a taxi driver who charged them three times the normal price.
= 那些游客被一个出租车司机宰了，车费是平常的三倍。
get screwed = （俚语）被宰、被骗
⑤ Anyone who thinks the earth is flat must have a screw loose.
= 谁要是认为地球是平的，脑子一定有毛病。
have a screw loose = 脑子有毛病
⑥ The bank put the screws on him to pay back the loan by the end of the month.
= 银行向他施压，要他在月底前还清贷款。
put the screws on somebody = 对某人施加压力
⑦ I completely screwed up the job interview by arriving an hour late and forgetting my notes.
= 我晚到了一个小时，还忘了带笔记，工作面试彻底搞砸了。
screw up = 搞砸
⑧ She screwed up her eyes against the bright sunlight and looked out to sea.
= 她在刺眼的阳光下眯起眼睛，望向大海。
screw your eyes up = 眯起眼睛
⑨ The baby screwed up her face and began to cry when she tasted the lemon.
= 婴儿尝到柠檬，皱起小脸哭了起来。
screw your face up = 皱起脸
⑩ Stop screwing around in the garden and come and help me carry these boxes upstairs.
= 别在花园里瞎混了，过来帮我把这些箱子搬上楼。
screw around = 瞎混、胡闹
⑪ Wine bottles with a screw cap are easier to open than those with a cork.
= 螺旋盖的葡萄酒瓶比软木塞的好开。
a screw cap = 螺旋盖
⑫ Make sure the screw top of the jar is closed, or the jam will dry out.
= 一定要把罐子的螺旋盖拧好，不然果酱会干掉。
a screw top = 螺旋盖
