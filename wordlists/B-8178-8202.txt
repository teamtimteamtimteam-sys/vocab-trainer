export
核心：三支，动词重音在后、名词重音在前。动词是出口：export something to somewhere；
名词是出口额或出口产品，多用复数 exports；电脑上也说把文件导出。
名词还有 exportation 与 exporter。跟 import 相对。
① The country exports timber and imports almost everything made from it.
= 那个国家出口木材，用木材做成的东西却几乎全靠进口。
export something to somewhere = 把某物出口到某地
② Exports rose for three quarters and the port still closes at six.
= 出口连涨了三个季度，港口还是六点关门。
exports = 出口额、出口产品
③ You can export the table as a spreadsheet and lose all the colours.
= 你可以把表格导出成电子表格，颜色会全丢掉。
export a file = 导出文件

exportation
核心：（正式）出口、输出这件事：法律与海关文书里用，日常说 export。不可数。
① The licence covers the exportation of seed potatoes and nothing else.
= 那张许可证只管种薯的出口，别的一概不管。
the exportation of something = 某物的出口
② Exportation without a permit carries a fine and a two-year ban.
= 无证出口要罚款，还要禁运两年。
exportation = 出口、输出
构词：export 加 -ation

exporter
核心：出口商、出口国：把货卖到国外的公司或国家。可数，
常说 the largest exporter of something。跟 importer 相对。
① The firm is the largest exporter of cut flowers in the region.
= 那家公司是这一带最大的鲜花出口商。
the largest exporter of something = 某物最大的出口商
② Exporters were warned about the new paperwork in a letter nobody read.
= 出口商收到过一封信，提醒新的报关手续，可没人读。
an exporter = 出口商
构词：export 加 -er

expose
核心：三支。一是使露出、使接触到：expose somebody to something；
二是揭露丑闻或真相：expose corruption；三是（摄影）曝光。
名词 exposure，形容词 exposed。
① Stripping the plaster exposed a beam that had been charred at one end.
= 把灰泥铲掉，露出一根一头烧焦的房梁。
expose something = 使某物露出来
② Children here are exposed to three languages before they can read.
= 这里的孩子还不识字，就已经接触了三种语言。
expose somebody to something = 让某人接触某事物
③ The article exposed a scheme that had run for eleven years.
= 那篇报道揭穿了一个运作了十一年的骗局。
expose corruption = 揭露腐败

exposed
核心：两支。一是（地方）无遮挡的、任凭风吹雨打的：an exposed ridge；
二是（人）没有保障、易受打击的：feel exposed。
① The cottage sits on an exposed ridge and loses tiles every winter.
= 那座小屋建在没遮没挡的山脊上，每年冬天都掉瓦。
an exposed hillside = 无遮挡的山坡
② Without that guarantee the smaller suppliers are badly exposed.
= 没有那份担保，小供应商就很没保障。
feel exposed = 觉得无依无靠

exposition
核心：三支。一是（正式）阐述、论述：把观点讲清楚的那一段文字；
二是（正式）博览会、展览会，口语截短成 expo；
三是（音乐）奏鸣曲式里的呈示部。形容词 expository。
① The first half is a clear exposition of the argument and the second half forgets it.
= 前半部分把论点阐述得很清楚，后半部分就忘了。
a clear exposition of something = 对某题目的清晰阐述
② The Great Exposition drew nine million visitors and left one iron tower behind.
= 那届大博览会引来九百万参观者，留下一座铁塔。
an exposition = 博览会
③ The exposition comes back after the development and the audience relaxes.
= 呈示部在展开部之后再现，听众这才松了口气。
the exposition = 呈示部

expository
核心：（正式）说明性的、阐述性的：多修饰 prose、writing、passage、style ——
以把事情讲清楚为目的，跟叙事、抒情的写法相对。
① The chapter is expository and the reader picks up the plot from a footnote.
= 那一章全是说明文字，情节反倒要从脚注里捡。
expository prose = 说明性文字
② Her expository style suits a manual better than it suits a memoir.
= 她那种阐述式的笔法，写手册比写回忆录合适。
an expository passage = 一段说明性文字

expostulate
核心：（正式、旧）抗辩、规劝：带着不满跟人讲理，搭配 expostulate with somebody。
名词 expostulation。日常场合说 protest 或 argue。
① The colonel expostulated with the porter for a full minute and then paid.
= 上校跟门房理论了整整一分钟，最后还是付了钱。
expostulate with somebody = 跟某人理论
② She expostulated about the seating and was moved to a worse table.
= 她就座位安排提了意见，结果被挪到更差的一桌。
expostulate about something = 就某事抗辩

expostulation
核心：（正式、旧）抗辩、规劝的话：不满地讲理的那一番言辞。可数不可数都行。
① His expostulations were noted in the minutes and ignored in the vote.
= 他那些抗辩记进了会议记录，表决时没人理会。
expostulation = 抗辩、规劝
② A single expostulation from the back row stopped the whole ceremony.
= 后排一句抗议，让整场仪式都停了下来。
an expostulation = 一句抗辩
构词：expostulate 加 -ion

exposure
核心：四支。一是接触、受影响：exposure to something；二是（丑事的）曝光、揭露；
三是（医学）受冻、久在恶劣天气里：die of exposure；
四是（摄影）曝光，也指一张底片。
① Exposure to damp over two winters lifted the floorboards in the hall.
= 两个冬天的受潮，把门厅的地板拱了起来。
exposure to something = 接触某事物
② The exposure of the scheme cost three directors their seats.
= 那个骗局一被揭穿，三名董事丢了席位。
the exposure of something = 对某事的揭露
③ Two walkers were treated for exposure and released the same night.
= 两名徒步的人因受冻接受治疗，当晚就出了院。
die of exposure = 冻死在野外
④ A long exposure turns the crowd into a smear and keeps the building sharp.
= 长时间曝光把人群拍成一道模糊，楼倒是清晰的。
a long exposure = 长时间曝光
⑤ The exposure meter is older than the camera and she trusts it a good deal more.
= 那只测光表比相机还老，她反倒信它得多。
an exposure meter = 测光表

expound
核心：（正式）详细阐述：expound a theory，或 expound on something 就某题目大谈，
常带一点「讲得比人想听的多」的意思。
① The professor expounded a theory that the department had quietly dropped.
= 那位教授详述了一套理论，系里早就悄悄弃用了。
expound a theory = 阐述一套理论
② He will expound on the merits of the old ferry to anybody who sits down.
= 谁一坐下，他就会大谈老渡轮的种种好处。
expound on something = 就某题目大谈

express
核心：四支。一是动词，表达感情或意见：express concern、express an interest；
二是 express yourself，把心里的意思说出来；
三是快速的（形容词与名词）：an express train；四是明确的：her express wish。
副词 expressly，名词 expression。
① Several neighbours have expressed an interest in buying the strip of land.
= 有几户邻居表示有意买下那条地。
express an interest = 表示有意
express concern = 表示关切
② Teenagers who cannot express themselves in one language will try another.
= 用一种语言说不清楚的少年，会换另一种来试。
express yourself = 把自己的意思说出来
③ The express takes fifty minutes and stops nowhere useful.
= 那趟快车走五十分钟，停的站没一个顶用。
an express train = 特快列车
④ It was her express wish that the letters be burned unread.
= 那些信不许拆看就烧掉，是她明确的意愿。
her express wish = 她明确的意愿
⑤ The express lane is for eight items and the man ahead of her has nineteen.
= 快速通道限八件商品，排在她前面那位拿了十九件。
an express lane = 快速通道
副词 expressly：两支。一是明确地、白纸黑字地：expressly forbidden； 二是专门地、特意地：built expressly for something。
⑥ Cameras are expressly forbidden in the reading room and tolerated in the hall.
= 阅览室明令禁止相机，门厅里倒睁一只眼闭一只眼。
expressly forbidden = 明令禁止
⑦ The lorry was built expressly for the tunnel and fits nowhere else.
= 那辆卡车是专为这条隧道造的，别处都开不进去。
expressly for something = 专为某事

expression
核心：四支。一是表情：a puzzled expression；二是表达这件事：freedom of expression，
以及 find expression in something，说情绪在别处找到了出口；
三是说法、词语；四是（数学）表达式。形容词 expressive。
① A puzzled expression crossed his face and settled there for the afternoon.
= 一丝困惑爬上他的脸，整个下午都没下去。
a puzzled expression = 困惑的表情
② Freedom of expression is written into the charter and tested at every election.
= 表达自由写进了宪章，每逢选举都要受一次检验。
freedom of expression = 表达自由
③ Her anger found expression in the garden, which has never looked better.
= 她的怒气在花园里找到了出口，那园子从没这么好看过。
find expression in something = 在某处得到宣泄
④ The expression to the left of the equals sign simplifies to three terms.
= 等号左边那个式子可以化简成三项。
an expression = 表达式
⑤ The expression marks in that part were pencilled in by somebody else entirely.
= 那份分谱上的表情记号，是另一个人用铅笔标上去的。
an expression mark = 乐谱上的表情记号

expressionism
核心：（艺术）表现主义：不照实描摹，而是把情绪压进形与色的那个流派。
不可数；作流派名时常大写。
① German Expressionism gave the film its shadows and its crooked doorways.
= 德国表现主义给了那部电影它的阴影和歪斜的门框。
expressionism = 表现主义
② Expressionism reached the theatre a decade after it reached the canvas.
= 表现主义传到戏剧界，比传到画布上晚了十年。
German Expressionism = 德国表现主义
构词：expression 加 -ism

expressionist
核心：（艺术）表现主义的、表现主义者：形容词与名词同形，
修饰 painter、portrait、drama，也直接指那一派的人。
① The gallery hangs two expressionist portraits and a great deal of glass.
= 那家画廊挂着两幅表现主义肖像，还有一大堆玻璃制品。
an expressionist painter = 表现主义画家
② The expressionists painted what the sitter felt and not what he looked like.
= 表现主义画家画的是模特的感受，不是他的模样。
an expressionist = 表现主义者

expressionless
核心：面无表情的：脸上、声音里看不出情绪，多修饰 face、voice、stare。
① He listened expressionless while the figures were read out twice.
= 数字念了两遍，他始终面无表情地听着。
an expressionless face = 面无表情的脸
② Her expressionless voice made the good news sound like a warning.
= 她那不带起伏的声音，把好消息说得像句警告。
an expressionless voice = 毫无起伏的声音

expressive
核心：富于表情的、传情的：说脸、眼睛、演奏、文字表达力强；
正式行文里还说 be expressive of something，透出某种情绪。副词 expressively。
① She has an expressive face and a very flat speaking voice.
= 她有一张会说话的脸，说话的声音却很平。
an expressive face = 富于表情的脸
② The gesture is expressive of a patience that the letter does not have.
= 那个手势透着一种耐心，信里可没有。
be expressive of something = 透出某种情绪
副词 expressively：传神地、富于表情地：多修饰 played、shrugged、read、sang。
③ The cellist played the slow movement expressively and the hall stayed silent.
= 大提琴手把慢乐章拉得极传神，全场鸦雀无声。
play expressively = 演奏得富于表情
④ He shrugged expressively and left the question to somebody else.
= 他意味深长地耸了耸肩，把问题留给了别人。
shrug expressively = 意味深长地耸肩
构词：expressive 加 -ly

expressiveness
核心：表现力：声音、面孔、笔法能传出多少情绪。不可数。
① The expressiveness of her playing survives a very poor recording.
= 她演奏里的表现力，连那份糟糕的录音都盖不住。
the expressiveness of something = 某物的表现力
② Expressiveness matters more than accuracy in the first year of lessons.
= 头一年上课，表现力比准确要紧。
expressiveness = 表现力

expressway
核心：（美式）高速公路、快速路：英式说 motorway。可数。
① The expressway takes four lanes of traffic past the old market gate.
= 那条快速路把四个车道的车流从老市场门前带过去。
an expressway = 高速公路
② An accident on the expressway added ninety minutes to a short trip.
= 快速路上一场事故，让一趟短途多花了九十分钟。
on the expressway = 在高速公路上

expropriate
核心：（正式、法律）征用、征收：国家依法把土地或房产收走，多用被动。
名词 expropriation；日常说法是 take over。
① The state expropriated the orchard and paid for it eleven years later.
= 国家征用了那片果园，十一年后才付钱。
expropriate land = 征用土地
② Owners whose houses were expropriated still meet on the anniversary.
= 房子被征收的那些业主，每逢周年还聚一次。
be expropriated = 被征用

expropriation
核心：（正式、法律）征收、征用：把私产依法收归公有这件事。可数不可数都行。
① The expropriation of the strip along the river took four acts of parliament.
= 征收沿河那条地带，动用了四部议会法案。
the expropriation of something = 对某物的征收
② Compensation for expropriation is still calculated on the old valuation.
= 征用补偿至今按旧估价计算。
compensation for expropriation = 征用补偿
构词：expropriate 加 -ion

expulsion
核心：两支。一是开除、驱逐：expulsion from school、the expulsion of diplomats；
二是（正式）排出气体或液体。可数不可数都行。动词是 expel。
① Expulsion from school follows a hearing that most parents never attend.
= 开除学籍要经过一场听证，多数家长从不出席。
expulsion from school = 开除学籍
the expulsion of diplomats = 驱逐外交官
② The expulsion of air through that pipe makes the whole floor hum.
= 空气从那根管子排出，弄得整层楼嗡嗡响。
the expulsion of air = 空气的排出

expunge
核心：（正式）删除、抹掉：把记录或档案里的东西彻底去掉，
搭配 expunge something from something，多用被动。
① The conviction was expunged from the record and remembered by everyone anyway.
= 那项定罪从档案里抹掉了，人人还是记着。
expunge something from something = 把某物从某处抹去
② Nothing in the settlement expunges the two years he spent waiting.
= 那份和解，抹不掉他白等的那两年。
expunge = 删除、抹掉

expurgate
核心：（正式）删节：把书或剧本里被认为不妥的段落删掉，多用被动；
an expurgated edition 就是删节本，跟 unexpurgated 相对。
① The play was expurgated for schools and lost its best two scenes.
= 那出戏为进校园删了节，最好的两场没了。
be expurgated = 被删节
② Nobody says who expurgated the letters or on whose instructions.
= 那些信是谁删的、奉谁的命，没人说得出。
an expurgated edition = 删节本

exquisite
核心：两支。一是精美绝伦的：做工、细节好得挑不出毛病；
二是（正式）极强烈的：exquisite pain、exquisite pleasure。副词 exquisitely。
① The cabinet is exquisite and will not fit through any door in the house.
= 那个柜子做得精美绝伦，可这屋里哪扇门都过不去。
exquisite workmanship = 精湛的做工
② She described an exquisite pain that came and went with the weather.
= 她说那是一种钻心的疼，随天气来来去去。
exquisite pain = 钻心的剧痛
副词 exquisitely：一头修饰工艺，精美地（exquisitely carved）； 一头修饰感受与程度，极其地（exquisitely painful、exquisitely aware）。
③ The box is exquisitely carved and holds one spare pair of reading glasses.
= 那只盒子雕工精美，里面装着一副备用老花镜。
exquisitely carved = 雕工精美
④ He was exquisitely aware of how the silence in that room was working.
= 那屋里的沉默在起什么作用，他感觉得清清楚楚。
exquisitely aware = 感觉极其敏锐
构词：exquisite 加 -ly
