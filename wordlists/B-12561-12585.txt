invigilate
核心：（英）监考 —— 在考场监督考生，防止作弊。名词 invigilation 与监考人 invigilator 并入本条。美式说 proctor。
① Two teachers invigilate each exam in the main hall.
= 每场考试由两位老师在大厅监考。
invigilate an exam = 监考
② Invigilation is one of the least popular jobs in the school.
= 监考是学校里最不受欢迎的工作之一。
invigilation = 监考
③ The invigilator told us to put our pens down.
= 监考老师让我们放下笔。
an invigilator = 监考人

invigorate
核心：使精力充沛、使振作；使活跃。本义让人觉得有力气、有精神；比喻给经济、组织注入活力。
形容词 invigorating（令人精神焕发的）并入本条。
① A cold shower and a strong coffee in the morning always invigorate me before work.
= 早上上班前冲个冷水澡、喝杯浓咖啡，总能让我精神焕发。
invigorate somebody = 使某人精神焕发
② In less than a year, the young manager has invigorated the whole company with fresh ideas.
= 不到一年，那位年轻的经理就用新想法给整个公司注入了活力。
invigorate a company = 使公司充满活力
③ We went for an invigorating walk along the cliffs.
= 我们沿着悬崖散了一次让人精神一振的步。
an invigorating walk = 令人精神一振的散步

invincible
核心：不可战胜的、无敌的 —— 强大到打不败。名词 invincibility 并入本条。
① The team seemed invincible after winning twenty games in a row.
= 连赢二十场之后，那支球队似乎所向无敌。
seem invincible = 似乎无敌
② Young drivers often have a feeling of invincibility that leads to accidents.
= 年轻司机常有一种自己无所不能的感觉，这容易导致事故。
a feeling of invincibility = 无敌的感觉

inviolable
核心：（正式）不可侵犯的、不容违背的 —— 说权利、规则、边界、承诺。名词 inviolability 并入本条。
① The first article of the constitution declares that human dignity is inviolable.
= 宪法第一条宣布人的尊严不可侵犯。
be inviolable = 不可侵犯
② The peace treaty signed after the war guaranteed the inviolability of the border.
= 战后签订的和平条约保证了边界不可侵犯。
the inviolability of something = …的不可侵犯

inviolate
核心：（正式）未受侵犯的、完好无损的 —— 保持原样、没有被破坏。
① Despite the war, the old library remained inviolate.
= 尽管经历了战争，那座古老的图书馆依然完好无损。
remain inviolate = 保持完好
② He believed that the privacy of letters should be inviolate.
= 他认为信件的隐私应当不受侵犯。
be inviolate = 不受侵犯

invisible
核心：看不见的、隐形的；无形的、不引人注意的。本义是眼睛看不到（invisible to the naked eye）；
比喻被忽视、不受重视的（feel invisible）；经济上指无形的（invisible earnings，服务业收入）。
名词 invisibility 与副词 invisibly 并入本条。
① Most of the bacteria on your kitchen table are invisible to the naked eye.
= 你厨房桌子上的细菌大多是肉眼看不见的。
invisible to the naked eye = 肉眼看不见的
② Many older women say they feel invisible in shops and restaurants.
= 许多上了年纪的女性说，她们在商店和餐馆里觉得自己像透明人一样。
feel invisible = 觉得被忽视
③ Tourism brings in large invisible earnings for the island.
= 旅游业为那座岛带来了大量无形收入。
invisible earnings = 无形收入
④ In the story, a magic cloak gives the boy the power of invisibility.
= 故事里，一件魔法斗篷让男孩拥有隐身的能力。
invisibility = 隐形
⑤ The two rooms are invisibly connected by a hidden door behind the bookcase.
= 两个房间由书柜后面的一扇暗门神不知鬼不觉地连着。
invisibly = 看不见地

invitation
核心：邀请；请柬；诱因。几支：请人来参加（an invitation to a party）；写着邀请的卡片；
（比喻）招来某事的做法（an invitation to thieves）。常见 at the invitation of（应…之邀）、by invitation only（凭请柬入场）。
① Thank you for your kind invitation to dinner.
= 谢谢你盛情邀请我吃晚饭。
an invitation to something = …的邀请
② The wedding invitations were sent out three months before the big day.
= 婚礼请柬在大喜日子前三个月就发出去了。
a wedding invitation = 婚礼请柬
③ She visited the university at the invitation of the professor.
= 她应那位教授之邀访问了那所大学。
at the invitation of somebody = 应某人之邀
④ Leaving your car unlocked is an open invitation to thieves.
= 不锁车门等于公开招贼。
an open invitation to somebody = 公开招引某人
⑤ Entry to the party is by invitation only.
= 聚会凭请柬入场。
by invitation only = 凭请柬入场

invitational
核心：（美）邀请赛 —— 只有受邀者才能参加的比赛，也作形容词。
① She won the annual golf invitational at the country club.
= 她赢了乡村俱乐部一年一度的高尔夫邀请赛。
an invitational = 邀请赛
② Only the top twenty players are invited to the invitational tournament.
= 只有排名前二十的选手受邀参加这场邀请赛。
an invitational tournament = 邀请赛

invite
核心：邀请；请求；招致。几支：请人来参加活动（invite somebody to/for）；正式请人做某事（invite applications）；
（行为）引来（不好的事）（invite trouble / criticism）。一串短语动词并入本条：
invite somebody back（请回来）、invite somebody along（请一起去）、invite somebody over / round / around（请到家里）、
invite somebody in（请进屋）。名词（口语）invite = 邀请。
① They invited us to their wedding in Italy.
= 他们邀请我们去参加在意大利举行的婚礼。
invite somebody to something = 邀请某人参加…
② Let's invite a few friends over for dinner on Saturday.
= 我们星期六请几个朋友到家里吃饭吧。
invite somebody over = 请某人到家里
这一组短语动词的宾语是代词时必须放中间（invite them over）；宾语是名词时也可放后面：
invite over the neighbours、invite round the whole class、invite around a few friends、
invite in the visitors、invite along a friend、invite back the old team。
③ My neighbours invited me round for coffee on my first day in the street.
= 我搬到这条街的第一天，邻居们就请我到他们家喝咖啡。
invite somebody round = （英）请某人到家里
invite somebody around = （美）请某人到家里
④ She opened the door and invited the police officers in.
= 她打开门，请警察们进来。
invite somebody in = 请某人进屋
⑤ We're going to the beach; why don't you invite your cousin along?
= 我们要去海滩，你何不请你表弟一起来？
invite somebody along = 请某人一起去
⑥ The hotel was so good that they invited us back for a free weekend.
= 那家酒店太好了，他们还请我们回去免费住一个周末。
invite somebody back = 请某人再来
⑦ The company is inviting applications for the post of manager.
= 公司正在招聘经理一职。
invite applications = 征求申请
⑧ Leaving your bag open in a crowd is just inviting trouble.
= 在人群里敞着包，简直是自找麻烦。
invite trouble = 招惹麻烦
⑨ Thanks for the invite, but I'm busy that night.
= 谢谢邀请，可那晚我有事。
an invite = （口语）邀请

inviting
核心：诱人的、吸引人的 —— 让人想去、想做、想吃。副词 invitingly 并入本条。
① The clear blue water looked very inviting on such a hot day.
= 在这么热的天，清澈的蓝色海水看上去非常诱人。
look inviting = 看起来诱人
② The door of the bakery stood invitingly open, and the smell of bread filled the street.
= 面包店的门敞开着，很吸引人，面包的香味飘满了整条街。
invitingly = 诱人地

invocation
核心：（正式）祈求、祷告；援引 —— invoke 的名词。
① The ceremony began with an invocation to the gods of the harvest.
= 仪式以向丰收之神的祈祷开始。
an invocation to somebody = 向某人的祈求
② The minister's invocation of emergency powers was criticized by the courts.
= 部长援引紧急权力的做法受到了法院的批评。
the invocation of something = 对…的援引

invoice
核心：发票、账单 —— 列明货物或服务及应付款项的单据。也作动词：给…开发票。
① Two weeks after the work was finished, the builder sent us an invoice for the new roof.
= 完工两周后，建筑商给我们寄来了新屋顶的账单。
an invoice for something = …的发票
② For regular customers, the printing company will invoice you at the end of each month.
= 对老客户，那家印刷公司会在每月月底开发票。
invoice somebody = 给某人开发票

invoke
核心：援引、行使（法律、权利）；提及（以支持观点）；祈求（神灵）；（计算机）调用。
几支：拿法律、规则做依据（invoke a law）；引用某人某事来支持自己（invoke the memory of）；
向神灵祈求帮助；唤起（某种感觉，同 evoke，这一用法有人认为不规范）。名词 invocation 另立。
① The government invoked emergency powers to deal with the floods.
= 政府动用紧急权力应对洪灾。
invoke powers = 动用权力
② He invoked the name of his famous father to get the job.
= 他搬出了他那位著名父亲的名字来得到这份工作。
invoke somebody's name = 搬出某人的名字
③ The priest invoked God's blessing on the new ship.
= 神父为那艘新船祈求上帝赐福。
invoke a blessing = 祈求赐福
④ The program invokes a separate function to print the results.
= 程序调用一个单独的函数来打印结果。
invoke a function = 调用函数

involuntary
核心：不由自主的、无意识的；非自愿的。两支：身体不受控制的反应（an involuntary cry）；
不是自己选择的（involuntary redundancy）。副词 involuntarily 并入本条。
① She gave an involuntary shiver when she walked into the cold church.
= 她走进冰冷的教堂时，不由自主地打了个寒战。
an involuntary shiver = 不由自主的寒战
② Hundreds of workers faced involuntary redundancy when the factory closed.
= 工厂关闭时，数百名工人被迫下岗。
involuntary redundancy = 非自愿裁员
③ He smiled involuntarily when he heard her voice.
= 听到她的声音，他不由自主地笑了。
involuntarily = 不由自主地

involve
核心：包含、涉及；使参与、牵涉。几支：一件事必然包括某部分（The job involves travelling）；
让人参与进来（involve somebody in）；把人牵连进（麻烦、罪行）。involve in 并入本条；
形容词 involved、名词 involvement 另立。
① The job involves a lot of travelling, so she is rarely at home.
= 那份工作需要经常出差，所以她很少在家。
involve doing something = 需要做…
后面接 -ing，不接不定式。
② Teachers try to involve all the children in the discussion.
= 老师们设法让所有孩子都参与讨论。
involve somebody in something = 使某人参与…
③ I don't want to involve you in my family's problems.
= 我不想把你牵扯进我家里的麻烦。
involve somebody in a problem = 把某人牵扯进麻烦
④ The accident involved three cars and a lorry.
= 那起事故涉及三辆汽车和一辆卡车。
involve something = 涉及…

involved
核心：参与的、有关的；复杂的；（关系）亲密的。几支：卷入其中的（be involved in）；
过程、解释繁杂难懂（a long, involved story）；与某人有恋爱关系（be involved with somebody）；
付出很多时间精力（get involved）。
① Over a hundred people were involved in the rescue.
= 一百多人参与了营救。
be involved in something = 参与…
② She gets very involved in her work and forgets to eat.
= 她一投入工作就忘了吃饭。
get involved = 投入、参与
get involved in something = 参与…、卷入…
③ He told a long and involved story about why he was late.
= 他讲了个又长又绕的故事，说明自己为什么迟到。
an involved story = 复杂的故事
④ She is involved with a man she met on holiday.
= 她和一个度假时认识的男人好上了。
be involved with somebody = 与某人有恋爱关系

involvement
核心：参与、介入；（恋爱）关系 —— involve 的名词。
① The police are investigating his involvement in the robbery.
= 警方正在调查他与那起抢劫案的牵连。
involvement in something = 参与…
② Research shows that parental involvement in primary schools helps children do better at reading.
= 研究表明，家长参与小学事务能帮助孩子提高阅读能力。
parental involvement = 家长参与
③ Her involvement with a married man shocked her family.
= 她和一个有妇之夫的关系让家人震惊。
an involvement with somebody = 与某人的关系

invulnerable
核心：不会受伤害的、刀枪不入的；无懈可击的 —— 不可能被攻击、伤害或打败。名词 invulnerability 并入本条。
① In the legend, the hero is invulnerable except for one spot on his heel.
= 传说中，那位英雄除了脚后跟上的一处以外刀枪不入。
be invulnerable = 刀枪不入
② The party's huge majority makes it almost invulnerable at the next election.
= 那个党的巨大多数席位让它在下次选举中几乎立于不败之地。
almost invulnerable = 几乎无懈可击
③ Teenagers often have a sense of invulnerability that leads them to take risks.
= 青少年常有一种自己不会受伤的感觉，这让他们去冒险。
a sense of invulnerability = 自己不会受伤的感觉

inward
核心：内心的；向内的；输入的。几支：心里的（inward feelings）；朝里的（an inward curve）；
进入一国的（inward investment，外来投资）。也作副词，同 inwards。
副词 inwardly（在心里）与名词 inwardness（内在性、精神性）并入本条。
① She felt a sense of inward calm after making the decision.
= 做出决定后，她感到内心一片平静。
inward calm = 内心的平静
② The door opens inward, so stand back.
= 门是往里开的，往后站点。
open inward = 向内开
③ The government wants to attract more inward investment from abroad.
= 政府希望吸引更多外来投资。
inward investment = 外来投资
④ He smiled politely but inwardly he was furious.
= 他礼貌地笑着，心里却怒火中烧。
inwardly = 在心里
⑤ The poet writes about the inwardness of religious experience.
= 那位诗人写的是宗教体验的内在性。
inwardness = 内在性

inwards
核心：向内、向里 —— 副词，英式常用；美式多说 inward。
① The walls of the old barn were starting to lean inwards.
= 旧谷仓的墙开始往里歪了。
lean inwards = 向内倾斜
② The door swung inwards, and a cold wind blew into the hall.
= 门往里一荡，一股冷风灌进了门厅。
swing inwards = 向内摆动

iodide
核心：（化学）碘化物 —— 碘与另一种元素组成的化合物，如 potassium iodide（碘化钾）。
① People living near the power station were given potassium iodide tablets.
= 住在那座核电站附近的人都领到了碘化钾片。
potassium iodide = 碘化钾
② Silver iodide is sometimes sprayed into clouds to make it rain.
= 有时人们会把碘化银喷进云层里人工降雨。
silver iodide = 碘化银

iodine
核心：碘；碘酒 —— 一种化学元素，人体必需的微量元素；也指涂伤口消毒用的碘酒。
① Iodine is added to salt to prevent health problems in children.
= 盐里加了碘，以防儿童出现健康问题。
iodine = 碘
② The nurse cleaned the cut on his knee with iodine.
= 护士用碘酒清洗了他膝盖上的伤口。
iodine = 碘酒

ion
核心：（物理、化学）离子 —— 失去或得到电子而带电的原子或分子。
形容词 ionic（离子的）、动词 ionize（使电离，英式也拼 -ise）、名词 ionization 与离子发生器 ionizer 并入本条。
① Salt breaks down into sodium and chloride ions in water.
= 盐在水中分解成钠离子和氯离子。
an ion = 离子
② Sodium chloride is held together by strong ionic bonds.
= 氯化钠靠很强的离子键结合在一起。
an ionic bond = 离子键
③ High-energy radiation can ionize the air around it.
= 高能辐射能使周围的空气电离。
ionize something = 使…电离
④ The ionization of gases makes neon signs glow.
= 气体的电离让霓虹灯发光。
ionization = 电离
⑤ She bought an ionizer to clean the air in her bedroom.
= 她买了一台负离子发生器来净化卧室的空气。
an ionizer = 负离子发生器

iota
核心：极少量、一点点 —— 希腊字母表里最小的那个字母的名称，今天几乎只用在否定句里：not one iota（丝毫不）。
① His story has not changed one iota in twenty years.
= 二十年来，他的说法丝毫没变。
not one iota = 丝毫不
② There isn't an iota of truth in what the newspaper said about her.
= 报纸上关于她的说法没有一点点是真的。
not an iota of something = 一点…也没有

ipso facto
核心：（拉丁语，正式）根据该事实本身、当然地 —— 因为某个事实就自然得出某个结论。
① Being poor does not ipso facto make someone a criminal.
= 贫穷本身并不意味着一个人就是罪犯。
ipso facto = 就这一事实而言、当然
② The new rules mean that anyone over seventy is ipso facto excluded from the jury.
= 按新规定，任何七十岁以上的人都自然被排除在陪审团之外。
be ipso facto excluded = 当然被排除
