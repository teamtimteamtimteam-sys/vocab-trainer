lock
核心：锁；（运河的）船闸；一绺头发。动词：锁上；卡住、固定住。
短语动词：lock up（锁好门窗；把…关起来）、lock in（把…锁在里面；锁定）、lock out（把…锁在外面）、

习语：under lock and key（锁着、严加看管）、lock, stock and barrel（全部、一股脑儿）、lock horns（争执、较劲）。
并入本条：lockable（可上锁的）、lockbox（美：带锁的箱子）、lockdown（封锁、封控）、lockstep（美：in lockstep = 步调一致）、
lock-keeper（船闸看守人）。
① She turned the key in the lock and opened the door.
= 她把钥匙在锁里一转，打开了门。
a lock = 锁

= 我们出门前你锁后门了吗？
lock the door = 锁门
③ The boat passed through six locks on its way to the city.
= 那条船驶往城里的路上过了六道船闸。
a lock = 船闸
④ She cut off a lock of the baby's hair and kept it in a book.
= 她剪下婴儿的一绺头发，夹在一本书里。
a lock of hair = 一绺头发
⑤ The wheels locked, and the car slid across the road.
= 车轮抱死了，汽车横着滑过马路。
the wheels lock = 车轮抱死
⑥ Do not forget to lock up before you go to bed.
= 睡觉前别忘了把门窗锁好。
lock up = 锁好门窗
⑦ He was locked up for ten years for the robbery.
= 他因那起抢劫案被关了十年。
be locked up = 被关起来
⑧ The cleaner locked me in the office by mistake.
= 清洁工不小心把我锁在了办公室里。
lock in = 把…锁在里面
⑨ We locked in a low price by signing the contract early.
= 我们早早签了合同，锁定了低价。
lock in a price = 锁定价格
⑩ I locked myself out of the house and had to climb in through a window.
= 我把自己锁在了屋外，只好从窗户爬进去。
lock out = 把…锁在外面
⑪ She locks her jewellery away in a safe when she travels.

lock away = 把…锁起来收好
⑫ The missile locked onto the heat of the plane's engines.
= 导弹锁定了飞机发动机的热源。
lock onto = 锁定（目标）
⑬ The letters were kept under lock and key in the director's office.

⑭ They sold the business lock, stock and barrel to a rival.
= 他们把生意一股脑儿全卖给了一个对手。

⑰ Keep your passport in a lockable drawer.

⑱ The agent left the keys in a lockbox by the front door.

a lockbox = （美）带锁的箱子
⑲ Prices and wages have risen in lockstep for a year.
