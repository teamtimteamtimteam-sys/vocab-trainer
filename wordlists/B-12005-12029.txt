independence
/ˌɪndɪˈpendəns/
核心：独立 —— 不受别人控制、不依赖别人。两支：国家、地区脱离统治（gain independence from）；
个人经济上、生活上自立（financial independence）。形容词 independent 另立。
① The country gained independence from Britain in 1962.
= 那个国家于一九六二年脱离英国独立。
gain independence from somebody = 脱离…独立
② Leaving home at eighteen gave her a sense of independence she had never felt before.
= 十八岁离家让她有了一种从未有过的独立感。
a sense of independence = 独立感
③ Getting a job was the first step towards financial independence.
= 找到工作是迈向经济独立的第一步。
financial independence = 经济独立
④ The paper has always prided itself on its editorial independence.
= 那家报纸一向以编辑独立而自豪。
editorial independence = 编辑独立

independent
/ˌɪndɪˈpendənt/
核心：独立的；不偏不倚的；（英）私立的。几支：国家独立（an independent state）；个人自立（fiercely independent）；
不受控制、中立（an independent inquiry）；互不相关的（two independent studies）；
英式说学校、电视台「非公立的」。
副词 independently 并入本条。
① The island became an independent state after a peaceful vote.
= 那个岛经过一次和平投票成为独立国家。
an independent state = 独立国家
② At ninety my grandmother is still fiercely independent of her children and refuses any help with shopping.
= 我祖母九十岁了，还是非常要强，不靠子女，买东西不肯让人帮忙。
fiercely independent = 非常独立、要强
be independent of somebody = 不依赖某人
③ The government has ordered an independent inquiry into the accident.
= 政府已下令对这起事故进行独立调查。
an independent inquiry = 独立调查
④ Two independent studies reached exactly the same conclusion.
= 两项彼此独立的研究得出了完全相同的结论。
independent studies = 彼此独立的研究
⑤ She was educated at an independent school and then went to Oxford.
= 她在一所私立学校上学，后来进了牛津。
an independent school = （英）私立学校
⑥ Each independent clause in a long sentence could stand alone as a complete sentence.
= 长句里的每个独立分句都可以单独成为一个完整的句子。
an independent clause = 独立分句
⑦ In this experiment, temperature is the independent variable.
= 在这个实验里，温度是自变量。
an independent variable = 自变量
⑧ The two scientists made the same discovery independently of each other.
= 两位科学家各自独立地作出了同一项发现。
independently of somebody = 独立于某人

indescribable
/ˌɪndɪˈskraɪbəbəl/
核心：难以形容的 —— 好到或坏到说不出来。副词 indescribably 并入本条。
① The joy of holding her first grandchild was indescribable.
= 第一次抱孙子的喜悦难以言表。
indescribable joy = 难以形容的喜悦
② The smell in the flat after the fridge broke down was indescribably awful.
= 冰箱坏了以后，公寓里的气味难闻得没法形容。
indescribably = 难以形容地

indestructible
/ˌɪndɪˈstrʌktəbəl/
核心：毁不掉的、坚不可摧的 —— 说东西结实，也说人、精神不垮。
① These old metal toys are practically indestructible, so they get passed down the family.
= 这些老式金属玩具几乎摔不坏，所以一代代往下传。
practically indestructible = 几乎毁不掉的
② Their friendship seemed indestructible, until they fell in love with the same woman.
= 他们的友谊看似坚不可摧，直到两人爱上了同一个女人。
an indestructible friendship = 牢不可破的友谊

indeterminate
/ˌɪndɪˈtɜːmɪnət/
核心：不确定的、模糊的 —— 数量、年龄、性质说不准。名词 indeterminacy 并入本条。
① A woman of indeterminate age sat reading at the next table.
= 邻桌坐着一个看不出年纪的女人在看书。
of indeterminate age = 看不出年纪的
② He was given an indeterminate sentence and no release date.
= 他被判不定期徒刑，没有释放日期。
an indeterminate sentence = 不定期刑
③ The indeterminacy of the rules made it hard for players to know what was allowed.
= 规则含糊不清，选手们很难知道什么是允许的。
the indeterminacy of something = …的不确定

index
/ˈɪndeks/
核心：索引；指数；指标。本义是「指示」（同 indicate），由此三支：书后的索引、资料卡片目录；
经济上的指数（price index）；一般的衡量指标（an index of success）。复数 indexes 或 indices（后者多用于科学、经济）。
动词：编索引；（英）按物价指数调整（名词 indexation）。
① Look up the word in the index at the back of the book.
= 在书后的索引里查一下这个词。
the index = 索引
② The consumer price index rose by three per cent last year.
= 去年消费价格指数上涨了百分之三。
a price index = 价格指数
③ Sales of luxury cars are often treated as an index of how confident wealthy shoppers feel.
= 豪华车的销量常被当作衡量富裕消费者信心的指标。
an index of something = …的指标
④ Pensions are index-linked, so they rise with the cost of living.
= 退休金与物价指数挂钩，随生活成本上涨。
index-linked = （英）与物价指数挂钩的
index-linking = 与物价指数挂钩
indexation = 指数化
⑤ She wrote each new word on an index card and tested herself on the bus.
= 她把每个新词写在一张索引卡片上，在公交车上自己考自己。
an index card = 索引卡片
⑥ The guide pointed at the old map with his index finger to show us the route.
= 导游用食指点着那张旧地图，给我们看路线。
the index finger = 食指
⑦ Scientists have calculated the refractive indices of several new materials.
= 科学家计算出了几种新材料的折射率。
indices = index 的复数（多用于科学）
⑧ The refractive index of glass is about one and a half.
= 玻璃的折射率约为一点五。
the refractive index = 折射率

Indian
/ˈɪndiən/
核心：印度的；印度人。另一支指美洲原住民（旧称，许多人认为冒犯，今多说 Native American）。
一串以 Indian 打头的复合词并入本条；India rubber（旧，橡皮）也并在这里。
① Indian food is popular all over Britain, and every town has a curry house.
= 印度菜在全英国都很受欢迎，每个镇都有一家咖喱馆。
Indian food = 印度菜
② The museum has a collection of Indian baskets from the American Southwest.
= 那家博物馆收藏有来自美国西南部的印第安人篮子。
Indian = （旧）美洲原住民的
今天多说 Native American 或具体的部族名。
③ We had an Indian summer this year, with warm sunny days in late October.
= 今年有个小阳春，十月底还是温暖的晴天。
an Indian summer = 小阳春、深秋回暖
也比喻晚年的好时光：the Indian summer of his career。
④ The drawing was done in Indian ink and has not faded at all.
= 那幅画是用墨汁画的，一点也没褪色。
Indian ink = （英）墨汁
美式说 India ink。
⑤ The family grew Indian corn and beans on a small farm.
= 那家人在一个小农场上种玉米和豆子。
Indian corn = （美）玉米
⑥ Victorian gymnasts swung heavy Indian clubs to build their strength.
= 维多利亚时代的体操运动员挥舞沉重的体操棒来锻炼力量。
an Indian club = 体操棒
⑦ The door was painted a deep Indian red.
= 那扇门漆成了深印度红色。
Indian red = 印度红
⑧ He grew up on an Indian reservation in South Dakota.
= 他在南达科他州的一个印第安人保留地长大。
an Indian reservation = （美）印第安人保留地
⑨ She rubbed out the pencil marks with a piece of India rubber.
= 她用一块橡皮擦掉铅笔印。
India rubber = （旧）橡皮

indicate
/ˈɪndɪkeɪt/
核心：表明、显示；指出；（英）打转向灯。本义是「指出」，由此几支：
事实、迹象表明（The results indicate that…）；人用手或话指点、示意（indicate the door）；
间接地表示（She indicated she might resign）；英式开车打转向灯（indicate left）。
① Early results from the hospital trial indicate that the new drug is working in most patients.
= 医院试验的初步结果表明，新药对大多数病人有效。
indicate that = 表明…
② Without looking up from his papers, the lawyer indicated the chair by the window, and I sat down.
= 律师头也没抬，指了指窗边的椅子，我就坐下了。
indicate something = 指着、示意…
③ She has indicated that she may be willing to take the job.
= 她已表示可能愿意接受这份工作。
indicate that = 暗示、表示
④ Always check your mirror and indicate left before you pull out into the traffic.
= 驶入车流前，一定要看后视镜、打左转向灯。
indicate left = （英）打左转向灯

indication
/ˌɪndɪˈkeɪʃən/
核心：迹象、表明；暗示 —— 指向某件事的信号。常见 give an indication、there is every indication that。
医学上另指「适应症」。
① Despite the government's promises, there is every indication that food prices will continue to rise this winter.
= 尽管政府作了承诺，种种迹象表明今冬食品价格还会继续上涨。
there is every indication that = 种种迹象表明
② He gave no indication that he was unhappy with his job.
= 他一点也没流露出对工作不满的意思。
give no indication = 毫无迹象
③ Can you give us an indication of how much the repairs will cost?
= 你能大概说一下修理要花多少钱吗？
an indication of something = …的大致说法
④ Doctors list high blood pressure as the main indication for the new drug.
= 医生把高血压列为这种新药的主要适应症。
an indication for a drug = 药物的适应症

indicative
/ɪnˈdɪkətɪv/
核心：表明的、暗示的；（语法）陈述语气。两支：be indicative of（表明…、是…的迹象，正式）；
语法术语 the indicative（陈述语气，相对于虚拟与祈使）。
① His long silence at the dinner table was indicative of how angry he really was with his son.
= 他在饭桌上久久不说话，说明他其实对儿子有多生气。
be indicative of something = 表明…
② Most of the verbs in an ordinary news report are in the indicative, because they state facts.
= 普通新闻报道里的动词大多用陈述语气，因为说的是事实。
the indicative = 陈述语气

indicator
/ˈɪndɪkeɪtə(r)/
核心：指标、迹象；指示器；（英）转向灯。本义是「指示的东西」：经济指标（economic indicators）、
仪表上的指针、车上的转向灯（美式说 turn signal）。
① Unemployment is a key indicator of the state of the economy.
= 失业率是衡量经济状况的关键指标。
a key indicator = 关键指标
② The fuel indicator showed that the tank was almost empty.
= 油量表显示油箱几乎空了。
a fuel indicator = 油量表
③ The driver in front turned left without using his indicator.
= 前面的司机没打转向灯就左转了。
an indicator = （英）转向灯

indict
/ɪnˈdaɪt/
核心：（美，法律）起诉、控告 —— 正式指控某人犯有重罪。读音不发 c。
形容词 indictable（可被起诉的）与名词 indictment（起诉书；控诉）并入本条。
① The former bank manager was indicted on three charges of fraud last spring.
= 那位前银行经理去年春天被控三项欺诈罪。
be indicted on charges of something = 被控…罪
② Theft is an indictable offence and can be tried before a jury.
= 盗窃是可起诉罪，可由陪审团审判。
an indictable offence = 可起诉罪
③ The grand jury returned an indictment against the former mayor.
= 大陪审团对前市长提出了起诉。
an indictment against somebody = 对某人的起诉
④ The number of homeless families is a damning indictment of government policy.
= 无家可归的家庭数量是对政府政策的严厉控诉。
an indictment of something = 对…的控诉
比喻：一件事证明另一件事糟透了。

indifference
/ɪnˈdɪfrəns/

另有习语 a matter of indifference（无所谓的事）。形容词是 indifferent。

= 政府对农民困境的漠不关心让他们非常愤怒。

= 七点还是八点吃饭，对我来说都无所谓。
a matter of indifference = 无所谓的事

indifferent
/ɪnˈdɪfrənt/
核心：漠不关心的；平庸的、一般的。两支：对…不在乎（be indifferent to）；
质量不好不坏、偏差（an indifferent meal）。副词 indifferently 并入本条，也两支。
① Many young people seem indifferent to politics and never vote.

= 那家酒店很贵，饭菜充其量算一般。
indifferent food = 一般的饭菜
③ He shrugged indifferently when she told him she was leaving.
= 她告诉他她要走了，他漠然地耸了耸肩。
shrug indifferently = 漠然地耸肩
④ The team played indifferently all season and finished in the middle of the table.
= 那支球队整个赛季表现平平，最后排在积分榜中游。
play indifferently = 表现平平

indigenous
/ɪnˈdɪdʒənəs/

indigenous peoples = 原住民

indigent
/ˈɪndɪdʒənt/

① The court appoints a lawyer for indigent defendants who cannot pay for one.

indigestible

indigestion

indignant
/ɪnˈdɪɡnənt/

indignation

常见 righteous indignation（义愤）、express indignation at。
① The decision to close the hospital caused widespread indignation in the town.
= 关闭医院的决定在镇上激起了普遍的愤慨。

righteous indignation = 义愤

indignity

indigo

indirect
/ˌɪndɪˈrekt/
核心：间接的；迂回的；不直截了当的。几支：不是直接造成的（indirect effects）；路线绕远的（an indirect route）；
说话拐弯抹角（an indirect answer）。
副词 indirectly 与名词 indirectness 并入本条。
① The strike had an indirect effect on hundreds of small businesses.
= 那场罢工间接影响了几百家小企业。
an indirect effect = 间接影响
② We took an indirect route to avoid the traffic in the town centre.
= 为了避开市中心的拥堵，我们绕路走了。
an indirect route = 绕远的路线
③ When I asked about the money, he gave a very indirect answer.
= 我问到那笔钱时，他回答得很含糊。
an indirect answer = 拐弯抹角的回答
④ News reporters often put a politician's words into indirect speech to save space.
= 新闻记者常把政客的话改成间接引语，以节省篇幅。
indirect speech = 间接引语
⑤ Verbs such as give and send can take both a direct and an indirect object.
= 像「give」「send」这样的动词可以同时带直接宾语和间接宾语。
an indirect object = 间接宾语
⑥ Tourists are taught to ask an indirect question when they need directions from strangers.
= 游客学到的是，向陌生人问路时要用间接问句。
an indirect question = 间接问句
⑦ Value added tax is an indirect tax paid when you buy goods.
= 增值税是买东西时缴的一种间接税。
an indirect tax = 间接税
indirect taxation = 间接税制
⑧ Rising interest rates will indirectly affect the price of food.
= 利率上升会间接影响食品价格。
indirectly = 间接地
⑨ The indirectness of her criticism made it hard to know what she really thought.
= 她的批评说得很委婉，让人很难知道她到底怎么想。
the indirectness of something = …的委婉、不直接

indiscernible
/ˌɪndɪˈsɜːnəbəl/
核心：看不出的、察觉不到的 —— 太小或太模糊而无法辨认。
① The difference between the two copies is almost indiscernible to the naked eye.
= 两份复本的差别肉眼几乎看不出来。
almost indiscernible = 几乎察觉不到的
② The path was indiscernible in the dark, and they had to wait for the moon.
= 黑暗中小路完全看不清，他们只好等月亮出来。
be indiscernible = 看不清
