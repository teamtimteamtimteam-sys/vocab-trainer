juice
核心：汁、果汁；（复数）体液、肉汁；（口语）电、汽油、能量。本义是水果蔬菜里的汁（orange juice）；
肉烤出来的汁（meat juices）、消化液（digestive juices）；口语里指电力或燃料。
动词：榨汁；juice up（美，口语：使更带劲、更有趣）。
榨汁机 juicer、形容词 juiced（美，俚：兴奋的；醉的）与 juice bar（果汁吧）并入本条；juicy 另立。
① She drinks a glass of orange juice with her breakfast every morning.
= 她每天早上吃早饭时喝一杯橙汁。
orange juice = 橙汁
② Pour the meat juices from the pan over the potatoes.
= 把锅里的肉汁浇在土豆上。
meat juices = 肉汁
③ My phone has run out of juice, so I can't call a taxi.
= 我手机没电了，叫不了出租车。
run out of juice = （口语）没电、没油了
④ Juice two lemons and add the liquid to the sauce.
= 榨两个柠檬，把汁加进酱里。
juice a lemon = 榨柠檬汁
⑤ The director added car chases to juice up the film.
= 导演加了飞车追逐戏，让电影更带劲。
juice something up = （美，口语）使…更带劲
⑥ She bought an electric juicer and now makes carrot juice every day.
= 她买了台电动榨汁机，现在每天都榨胡萝卜汁。
a juicer = 榨汁机
⑦ They met for a smoothie at the juice bar next to the gym.
= 他们在健身房旁边的果汁吧见面，喝了杯冰沙。
a juice bar = 果汁吧
⑧ The crowd was really juiced before the band came on stage.
= 乐队上台前，观众都兴奋得不得了。
be juiced = （美，俚）兴奋

a juicy contract = 油水丰厚的合同

jump
核心：跳。本义是双脚离地往上或往前跃起，由此长出：
因吃惊而猛地一动（make somebody jump）；数量、价格突然猛增（jump by ten per cent）；
跳过、略过（jump a page）；插队（jump the queue）；急着下结论（jump to conclusions）；
闯（红灯）（jump the lights）；名词：一跳、猛增、（赛马、滑雪的）障碍。
短语：jump at（欣然抓住机会）、jump in（贸然插话或行动）、jump on（口语：猛烈批评）、jump out at（一下子引起注意）。
一族复合词并入本条：jump-start、jump lead（英，跨接线）、jump rope（美，跳绳）、jump shot、jump ball（篮球）、
jump cut（电影跳切）、jump jet（垂直起降喷气机）、jump-off（障碍赛加赛）、jumping jack（开合跳）、
jumping bean（跳豆）、jumping-off point（起点）。
① The children jumped up and down with excitement.
= 孩子们兴奋得蹦蹦跳跳。
jump up and down = 蹦蹦跳跳
② The cat jumped over the wall and disappeared into the garden.
= 那只猫跳过围墙，消失在花园里。
jump over something = 跳过…
③ A loud bang in the street made everyone jump.
= 街上一声巨响，把大家都吓了一跳。
make somebody jump = 把某人吓一跳
④ House prices jumped by ten per cent in a single year.
= 房价一年之内猛涨了百分之十。
jump by an amount = 猛增（多少）
⑤ He jumped the queue at the bus stop, and people shouted at him.
= 他在公交车站插队，大家都冲他嚷嚷。
jump the queue = （英）插队
⑥ Don't jump to conclusions; we don't know yet who broke the window.
= 别急着下结论，我们还不知道是谁打破了窗户。
jump to conclusions = 急着下结论
⑦ She jumped at the chance to work in Paris for a year.
= 她欣然抓住了去巴黎工作一年的机会。
jump at something = 欣然接受…
⑧ Before I could explain, he jumped in and accused me of lying.
= 我还没来得及解释，他就抢着插话，指责我撒谎。
jump in = 贸然插话
⑨ The teacher jumped on him for the smallest mistake.
= 他犯一点小错，老师就猛批他。
jump on somebody = （口语）猛烈批评某人
⑩ One mistake jumped out at me as soon as I opened the report.
= 我一打开报告，就有一处错误一下子跳进眼里。
jump out at somebody = 一下子引起某人注意
⑪ There was a sudden jump in the price of bread last month.
= 上个月面包价格突然猛涨。
a jump in something = …的猛增
⑫ The horse fell at the last jump.
= 那匹马在最后一道障碍处摔倒了。
a jump = （赛马的）障碍
⑬ The car wouldn't start, so we had to jump-start it with leads from a neighbour's car.
= 车发动不了，我们只好接上邻居车的跨接线搭电启动。
jump-start a car = 搭电启动汽车
a jump lead = （英）跨接线
比喻也说「给经济等注入一针强心剂」：jump-start the economy。
⑭ The girls were playing with a jump rope in the playground.

a jump rope = （美）跳绳
英式说 skipping rope。
⑮ He scored with a jump shot in the last second of the game.
= 他在比赛最后一秒跳投得分。
a jump shot = 跳投
a jump ball = （篮球）跳球
⑯ The director uses a jump cut to move suddenly from the kitchen to the beach.
= 导演用一个跳切，把画面突然从厨房切到海滩。

⑰ The jump jet can take off straight up from the deck of a ship.

⑱ Three riders went through to the jump-off after clear rounds.

a junk bond = 垃圾债券
⑦ Doctors dismissed the diet book as junk science.
= 医生们认为那本减肥书是伪科学。
junk science = 伪科学
⑧ Chinese junks with red sails crossed the harbour.
= 挂着红帆的中国式帆船驶过港湾。
a junk = （中国式）平底帆船

junker
核心：（美，口语）破旧的汽车。
① He drives an old junker that he bought for two hundred dollars.
= 他开着一辆花两百美元买来的破车。
an old junker = 破旧的汽车
② The yard was full of rusty junkers waiting to be scrapped.
= 院子里停满了等着报废的生锈破车。
a rusty junker = 生锈的破车

junket
核心：（口语，贬）公费旅游；乳冻甜食。
① The newspaper attacked the minister's junket to the Caribbean at public expense.
= 那家报纸抨击部长公费去加勒比海旅游。
a junket = 公费旅游
② For dessert, grandmother made junket with a little nutmeg on top.
= 祖母做了乳冻当甜点，上面撒了点肉豆蔻。
junket = 乳冻
