seldom
/ˈseldəm/
核心：副词：很少、不常：比 rarely 更书面，放在实义动词前、be 动词后。seldom if ever 是极少、几乎从不。
① My grandfather seldom watches television; he prefers to read or work in the garden.
= 我祖父很少看电视，他更喜欢看书或在花园里干活。
seldom = 很少
② The road is seldom used now that the new motorway has been built.
= 新高速公路建成后，那条路就很少有人走了。
be seldom used = 很少使用
③ She seldom if ever forgets a birthday, so she was upset when she forgot mine.
= 她几乎从不忘记谁的生日，所以忘了我的生日她很难过。
seldom if ever = 几乎从不

select
/sɪˈlekt/
核心：动词：（仔细地）挑选、选拔：select somebody for something、select from。和 choose、pick 的分别：choose 是通用的「选择」，pick 是口语的「挑」，select 正式，强调精挑细选。形容词：精选的、高级的、只供少数人的：a select group。名词 selection、形容词 selective 另立条。
① She was selected to play for the national team at the age of seventeen.
= 她十七岁就入选国家队。
select = 精挑细选（正式）
be selected for something = 入选某事
近义对照：
choose = 选择（通用）
pick = 挑（口语）
② Select a file from the list and click on the button to open it.
= 从列表中选择一个文件，点击按钮打开它。
select = 选择
③ Only a select group of people were invited to the private dinner with the prince.
= 只有少数精选的人受邀与王子共进私人晚宴。
a select group = 少数精选的人

selection
/sɪˈlekʃn/
核心：名词：选择、挑选（不可数或可数）：the selection of players；（可数）选出的人或物、精选品：a selection of cheeses；（商店的）可选货品、品种：a wide selection。
① The selection of the new team captain took the coach several weeks.
= 挑选新队长花了教练好几个星期。
selection = 选择、精选
② The restaurant offers a selection of local cheeses after the main course.
= 那家餐馆在主菜之后提供一份当地奶酪拼盘。
a selection of something = 一组精选的某物
③ The shop has a wide selection of shoes at very reasonable prices.
= 那家店的鞋品种很多，价格也很合理。
a wide selection = 品种繁多
④ The selection committee interviewed six candidates for the job of head teacher.
= 遴选委员会面试了六名校长职位的候选人。
a selection committee = 遴选委员会

selective
/sɪˈlektɪv/
核心：形容词：选择性的、有选择的：selective schools；（人）挑剔的、精心挑选的：be selective about something。副词 selectively 是有选择地。名词 selectivity 另立条。
① She is very selective about the films she watches and only goes to see a few each year.
= 她看电影很挑剔，每年只看几部。
be selective about something = 对某事很挑剔
selective = 有选择性的
② The newspaper reported the facts selectively, leaving out anything that did not support its story.
= 那家报纸有选择地报道事实，凡是不支持它说法的都略掉了。
selectively = 有选择地
③ In the 1960s, young American men had to register for selective service when they turned eighteen.
= 二十世纪六十年代，美国青年男子年满十八岁都得登记服义务兵役。
selective service = （美国）义务兵役制

selectivity
/sɪˌlekˈtɪvəti/
核心：名词，不可数：选择性；（学校的）择优录取。
① The school is famous for its selectivity and only accepts one in ten students who apply.
= 那所学校以择优录取出名，申请的学生里只收十分之一。
selectivity = 择优录取
② The selectivity of the new medicine means it attacks the disease without harming healthy cells.
= 那种新药的选择性意味着它只攻击病灶，不伤害健康细胞。
the selectivity of something = 某物的选择性

selector
/sɪˈlektə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（英式）（运动队的）选拔委员；（机器上的）选择器、选择开关。
① The selectors left the young player out of the team for the World Cup.

selenium
/səˈliːniəm/
核心：名词，不可数（化学）：硒：一种化学元素，人体必需的微量元素，也用于太阳能电池。
① Brazil nuts are rich in selenium, which the body needs in very small amounts.
= 巴西坚果富含硒，人体对它的需求量很小。
selenium = 硒
② Some farmers add selenium to the soil because the local crops contain too little of it.
= 有些农民往土里加硒，因为当地庄稼含硒太少。
add selenium = 加硒

self
/self/
核心：名词，可数或不可数，复数 selves：自我、自身、本性：your true self、your old self；（心理学）自我意识。常说 be your old self again（恢复常态）、a sense of self（自我认同感）。作构词成分 self- 表示「自己、自动」，构成一大族复合词，多数单立。
① After the long illness, it was good to see my grandfather back to his old self, laughing and telling jokes.
= 久病之后，看到祖父恢复了往日的样子，又说又笑，真好。
your old self = 往日的样子
self = 自我
② On holiday, away from work and worry, she felt she could finally be her true self.
= 度假时远离工作和烦恼，她觉得终于可以做真实的自己了。
your true self = 真实的自我
③ Teenagers need time and space to develop a strong sense of self.
= 青少年需要时间和空间来建立强烈的自我认同感。
a sense of self = 自我认同感
④ The two sisters had very different selves at home and at school.
= 那两姐妹在家里和在学校里判若两人。
selves = 自我（复数）
