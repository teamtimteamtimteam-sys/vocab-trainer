knock
核心：敲、撞、碰 —— 敲门；撞到、碰倒；（口语）批评、挑毛病。名词：敲门声；撞击；打击、挫折。
短语动词一大族：knock down（撞倒；拆除；压价）、knock out（打昏；淘汰）、knock over（碰倒）、
knock off（下班；减价；英俚：偷）、knock back（一口喝下；使受挫）、knock up（英：赶做出来）、
knock together（草草拼成）、knock around（闲逛；常在一起；粗暴对待）。
习语：knock on wood（美：但愿走运，英式说 touch wood）、knock it off（住手、别闹了）、
knock some sense into（让…清醒过来）、knock somebody's socks off（口语：让人惊艳）。
复合词并在这里：knock-on（连锁的）、knock-kneed 与 knock knees（膝内翻）、knocking copy（贬低对手的广告）、
knocking shop（英俚：妓院）、knock-knock joke（「敲敲门」笑话）、knock-down-drag-out（美：激烈不留情的）。
① Somebody knocked on the door three times while we were having dinner.
= 我们吃晚饭的时候，有人敲了三下门。
knock on the door = 敲门
英式也说 knock at the door。
② I knocked my head on the low beam as I walked into the kitchen.
= 我走进厨房时头撞在了低矮的房梁上。
knock your head on something = 头撞在…上
③ The cat jumped onto the table and knocked over a glass of milk.
= 猫跳上桌子，碰倒了一杯牛奶。
knock over = 碰倒
④ An old man was knocked down by a bus as he crossed the high street.
= 一位老人过大街时被公交车撞倒了。
knock down = 撞倒
⑤ The council plans to knock down the old cinema and build flats there.
= 市政会打算拆掉那座老电影院，在原址盖公寓。
knock down a building = 拆除建筑
⑥ I managed to knock the seller down from six hundred pounds to five hundred.
= 我成功让卖家把价钱从六百英镑压到了五百。
knock somebody down = 让某人降价
knock the price down 也常说。
⑦ The champion knocked out his opponent in the second round of the fight.
= 冠军在比赛的第二回合就把对手击昏了。
knock out = 打昏、击倒
⑧ Our team was knocked out of the cup by a club from a lower league.
= 我们队被一支低级别联赛的球队淘汰出了杯赛。
be knocked out of a competition = 被淘汰出局
⑨ We usually knock off at five on Fridays and go for a drink.
= 我们星期五通常五点收工，然后去喝一杯。
knock off = （口语）下班、收工
⑩ The manager knocked twenty pounds off the price because the box was damaged.
= 因为包装盒破了，经理把价钱减了二十英镑。
knock money off the price = 减价
⑪ Someone knocked off three laptops from the office over the weekend.
= 周末有人从办公室偷走了三台笔记本电脑。
knock off = （英，俚）偷
⑫ Knock it off, you two; I'm trying to get some sleep in here.
= 你们俩别闹了，我还想在这儿睡一会儿呢。
knock it off = 住手、别闹了
⑬ He knocked back two glasses of whisky and ordered another.
= 他一口气灌下两杯威士忌，又要了一杯。
knock back = 一口喝下
⑭ The news of the factory closing really knocked her back.
= 工厂要关门的消息着实让她受了打击。
knock somebody back = 使某人受挫、震惊
⑮ She knocked up a quick supper from the leftovers in the fridge.
= 她用冰箱里的剩菜很快弄出了一顿晚饭。
knock up = （英）赶做出来
美式俚语里 knock up 另有「使怀孕」之意，粗俗，用时小心。
⑯ He knocked together a bookshelf from some old planks in an afternoon.
= 他一个下午就用几块旧木板草草钉成了一个书架。
knock together = 草草拼成
⑰ He spent a year knocking around Europe before he went to university.
= 上大学之前，他在欧洲四处游荡了一年。
knock around = 闲逛、漂泊
⑱ She used to knock around with a group of older boys from the estate.
= 她以前常和小区里一帮年纪大些的男孩混在一起。
knock around with somebody = 常和某人在一起
knock around together = 常凑在一起
⑲ Her first husband used to knock her around when he had been drinking.
= 她的第一任丈夫喝了酒就对她动粗。
knock somebody around = 粗暴对待、殴打
⑳ It's easy to knock the plan, but nobody has offered a better one.
= 挑这个方案的毛病很容易，可谁也没拿出更好的来。
knock something = （口语）批评、挑毛病
㉑ The advert was pure knocking copy, with nothing to say about the firm's own product.
= 那则广告纯粹是贬低对手，对自家产品只字未提。
knocking copy = 贬低对手的广告文字
㉒ There was a loud knock at the door just after midnight.
= 午夜刚过，传来一阵响亮的敲门声。
a knock at the door = 敲门声
㉓ He got a nasty knock on the head when he fell off his bike.
= 他从自行车上摔下来，头上狠狠挨了一下。
a knock on the head = 头上挨的一下
㉔ Her confidence took a knock when she failed the driving test twice.
= 驾照考试两次没过，她的自信心受了打击。
take a knock = 受到打击
㉕ I haven't been ill once this winter, knock on wood.
= 今年冬天我一次病也没生过，但愿别说破了。
knock on wood = （美）但愿走运
说完吉利话敲敲木头，免得招来坏运气。
㉖ A rise in the price of oil has a knock-on effect on the cost of food.
= 油价上涨会连带影响食品的成本。
a knock-on effect = 连锁反应
英式常用。
㉗ As a child he was knock-kneed, and the doctor gave him special shoes.
= 他小时候膝内翻，医生给他配了特制的鞋。
knock-kneed = 膝内翻的
knock knees = 膝内翻
㉘ The children love knock-knock jokes and tell the same ones every evening.
= 孩子们很喜欢「敲敲门」笑话，每天晚上都讲那几个。
a knock-knock joke = 「敲敲门」笑话
以 Knock knock. — Who's there? 开头的问答式笑话。
㉙ The two candidates had a knock-down-drag-out fight over taxes on live television.
= 两位候选人在电视直播中就税收问题吵得不可开交。
a knock-down-drag-out fight = （美）激烈不留情的争斗
㉚ A few years in the army will knock some sense into that boy.
= 在军队里待几年，会让那小子清醒过来的。
knock some sense into somebody = 让某人清醒过来
㉛ The view from the top of the tower will knock your socks off.
= 塔顶的景色会让你惊艳得说不出话。
knock somebody's socks off = （口语）让人惊艳
㉜ The police closed the place down after neighbours complained that it was a knocking shop.
= 邻居投诉说那地方是妓院，警察便把它查封了。
a knocking shop = （英，俚）妓院

knockabout
核心：（英）闹哄哄的 —— 指靠摔跤、打闹逗乐的喜剧；名词：随便踢踢、打打的非正式比赛。
① The show is knockabout comedy, with actors falling off chairs and into buckets of water.
= 那出戏是打闹喜剧，演员不是从椅子上摔下来就是掉进水桶里。
knockabout comedy = 打闹喜剧
② The boys were having a knockabout with a football in the park after school.
= 放学后，男孩们在公园里随便踢着足球玩。
have a knockabout = 随便踢踢、打打球
不计分、不较真的玩法。

knocker
核心：门环 —— 装在门上用来敲门的金属环；（口语）爱挑毛病的人。
① She lifted the heavy brass knocker and let it fall twice.
= 她抬起沉重的黄铜门环，让它落下敲了两下。
a door knocker = 门环
② He ignored the knockers and went on to build a successful business.
= 他没理会那些泼冷水的人，后来把生意做得很成功。
the knockers = 爱挑毛病的人
来自 knock 的「批评」义。

knockout
核心：（拳击）击倒 —— 把对手打得起不来；（英）淘汰赛；（口语）令人惊艳的人或物。也作定语。
① The fight ended with a knockout in the fifth round.
= 那场拳赛在第五回合以击倒告终。
a knockout = 击倒
缩写 KO。
② The cup is a knockout competition, so one defeat means you are out.
= 那项杯赛是淘汰赛，所以输一场就出局。
a knockout competition = （英）淘汰赛
③ She looked an absolute knockout in the red dress at the party.
= 她在聚会上穿着那条红裙子，漂亮得惊人。
a knockout = （口语）令人惊艳的人
④ Losing their biggest customer was a knockout blow for the small firm.
= 失去最大的客户，对那家小公司是致命一击。
a knockout blow = 致命一击

knoll
核心：小圆丘 —— 低矮、圆顶的小山包。常说 a grassy knoll（长满草的小丘）。
① The church stands on a grassy knoll above the village.
= 教堂坐落在村子上方一座长满草的小丘上。
a grassy knoll = 长满草的小丘
② We climbed a small knoll to get a better view of the lake.
= 我们爬上一座小山包，想把湖看得更清楚些。
a small knoll = 小山包
k 不发音。

knot
核心：结 —— 绳结；（头发等）缠结；（木头的）节疤；一小群人；（胃里、肌肉的）紧绷感；（航海）节（速度单位）。动词：打结。
习语：tie the knot（口语：结婚）、tie yourself in knots（把自己绕糊涂）。
① He tied a knot in the rope so that it would not slip through the ring.
= 他在绳子上打了个结，免得它从环里滑出去。
tie a knot = 打结
② She brushed the knots out of her daughter's wet hair.
= 她把女儿湿头发里缠住的结梳开了。
knots in your hair = 头发里的缠结
③ The table is made of pine with dark knots in the wood.
= 那张桌子是松木的，木头上有深色的节疤。
a knot in the wood = 木头上的节疤
④ A knot of reporters was waiting outside the court.
= 一小群记者等在法院外面。
a knot of people = 一小群人
⑤ I had a knot in my stomach before the interview.
= 面试前我紧张得胃都揪成了一团。
a knot in your stomach = 胃里发紧
⑥ The ship was travelling at twenty knots when it hit the rock.
= 那艘船触礁时正以二十节的速度航行。
twenty knots = 二十节
一节是每小时一海里。
⑦ She knotted a silk scarf around her neck before going out.
= 她出门前在脖子上系了一条丝巾。
knot a scarf = 系围巾
⑧ They finally tied the knot last summer after ten years together.
= 在一起十年之后，他们终于在去年夏天结了婚。
tie the knot = （口语）结婚
⑨ He tied himself in knots trying to explain why he was late.
= 他想解释自己为什么迟到，结果越说越乱。
tie yourself in knots = 把自己绕糊涂

knotty
核心：棘手的 —— 指问题复杂难解；本义是（木头）多节疤的。
① The committee spent two hours on the knotty problem of who should pay.
= 委员会花了两个小时讨论该由谁付钱这个棘手问题。
a knotty problem = 棘手的问题
② The old shelf was made from a piece of knotty pine.
= 那个旧架子是用一块多节疤的松木做的。
knotty pine = 多节疤的松木

know
核心：知道、认识、懂得 —— 脑子里有这件事、这个人、这门本事。过去式 knew，过去分词 known。不用进行时。
句型：know (that)…、know what / where / how…、know how to do、know about（了解…的情况）、know of（听说过）。
习语：get to know（逐渐认识、了解）、let somebody know（告诉、通知）、as far as I know（据我所知）、
you never know（说不定）、know better（懂事、不至于那么傻）、in the know（知情的）、
know something inside out（了如指掌）、before you know it（转眼间）、God knows（天晓得）、
there's no knowing（没法知道）。
复合词 know-how（实际本领、技术诀窍）、know-all 与美式 know-it-all（自以为无所不知的人）并入本条。
① I know that she is angry, but I don't know why.
= 我知道她在生气，可不知道为什么。
know that… = 知道…
② Do you know where the nearest post office is?
= 你知道最近的邮局在哪儿吗？
know where… = 知道在哪儿
③ I knew the answer at once, but I was too shy to put my hand up.
= 我马上就知道答案了，可是不好意思举手。
knew = know 的过去式
④ I have known her since we were at primary school together.
= 我们一起上小学的时候我就认识她了。
have known somebody = 认识某人（已有多久）
known 是过去分词。
⑤ It took me several months to get to know my new neighbours.
= 我花了好几个月才跟新邻居熟起来。
get to know = 逐渐认识、了解
⑥ He knows the city well because he drove a taxi there for years.
= 他对那座城市很熟，因为在那儿开了好多年出租车。
know a place well = 熟悉某地
⑦ She knows a little Spanish and can order a meal without help.
= 她懂一点西班牙语，不用人帮忙就能点菜。
know a language = 懂一门语言
⑧ My grandfather never learned to swim, but he knows how to sail a boat.
= 我爷爷一直没学会游泳，却懂得怎么驾帆船。
know how to do something = 会做…
⑨ He knows a lot about old cars and repairs them at the weekend.
= 他对老爷车很在行，周末常修车。
know about something = 了解、懂…
⑩ I know of a good dentist near the station, though I've never been to her myself.
= 我听说车站附近有位不错的牙医，不过我自己没找她看过。
know of = 听说过、知道有…
比 know 浅：只知道有这么个人或东西。
⑪ You are old enough to know right from wrong.
= 你已经到了该分清是非的年纪了。
know right from wrong = 分清是非
know A from B：能把两样东西分开。
⑫ Please let me know by Friday whether you can come to the wedding.
= 请在星期五之前告诉我你能不能来参加婚礼。
let somebody know = 告诉、通知
⑬ As far as I know, the shop is still open on Sundays.
= 据我所知，那家店星期天还开门。
as far as I know = 据我所知
⑭ Take an umbrella with you; you never know what the weather will do.
= 带把伞吧，天气会怎样可说不准。
you never know = 说不定、难说
⑮ He is a doctor, so he should know better than to smoke.
= 他是医生，按说不该傻到去抽烟。
know better = 懂事、不至于那么傻
常说 know better than to do。
⑯ People in the know say that the company will be sold before Christmas.
= 知情人说，那家公司圣诞节前就会被卖掉。
in the know = 知情的
⑰ The firm has the money, but it lacks the technical know-how to build the machine.
= 那家公司有钱，但缺乏造这台机器的技术本领。
know-how = 实际本领、技术诀窍
不可数。
⑱ Nobody likes a know-all who corrects everything you say.
= 你说什么他都要纠正，这种万事通没人喜欢。
a know-all = （英）自以为无所不知的人
know-it-all = （美）自以为无所不知的人
⑲ She has worked here for twenty years and knows the business inside out.
= 她在这儿干了二十年，对这一行了如指掌。
know something inside out = 对…了如指掌
⑳ The summer holidays will be over before you know it.
= 暑假一转眼就过完了。
before you know it = 转眼间
㉑ I've never known it to snow in April in all my years here.
= 我在这儿这么多年，从没见过四月下雪。
have never known = 从未见过、经历过
多用完成时。
㉒ God knows where he has put the car keys this time.
= 天晓得他这回把车钥匙放哪儿了。
God knows = 天晓得
㉓ There's no knowing how he will react when he hears the news.
= 他听到这个消息会有什么反应，谁也说不准。
there's no knowing = 没法知道
㉔ "It's a terrible film." "I know. I walked out after an hour."
= “这电影真烂。”“可不是嘛，我看了一小时就走了。”
I know = 可不是嘛、我知道
表示同感。
㉕ He was, you know, a bit strange when I first met him.
= 我头一回见他的时候，他吧，有点儿怪。
you know = （口语填充语）你知道
说话时想词、缓一缓用的。

knowing
核心：会意的、心照不宣的 —— 表示自己知道内情。副词 knowingly 并入本条，另有「明知故犯地」一义。
① The two sisters exchanged a knowing look when their father mentioned the broken vase.
= 父亲提到那只打碎的花瓶时，姐妹俩交换了一个会意的眼神。
a knowing look = 会意的眼神
② He smiled knowingly when I said I had been working late.
= 我说我一直在加班，他心照不宣地笑了笑。
smile knowingly = 会意地笑
③ She would never knowingly sell a customer a faulty car.
= 她绝不会明知车有毛病还卖给顾客。
knowingly = 明知故犯地、故意地
法律、新闻里常见这一义。

knowledge
核心：知识、学问；知晓、了解。不可数，但可说 a knowledge of（对…的了解）。
习语：common knowledge（尽人皆知的事）、to my knowledge（据我所知）、to the best of my knowledge（就我所知）、
without somebody's knowledge（瞒着某人）。常见 general knowledge（常识）、a working knowledge of（够用的了解）。
① He has a wide knowledge of French history and often gives talks about it.
= 他对法国历史所知甚广，常常做这方面的讲座。
a knowledge of something = 对…的了解
② The quiz tests your general knowledge with questions on sport, music and science.
= 那个问答比赛用体育、音乐和科学方面的题目考你的常识。
general knowledge = 常识
③ You need a working knowledge of German to do this job.
= 做这份工作，德语得够用才行。
a working knowledge of something = 对…够用的了解
④ It is common knowledge in the village that the two brothers do not speak to each other.
= 那两兄弟互不说话，这在村里是尽人皆知的事。
common knowledge = 尽人皆知的事
⑤ To my knowledge, nobody has ever climbed that mountain in winter.
= 据我所知，还没有人在冬天登上过那座山。
to my knowledge = 据我所知
⑥ To the best of my knowledge, the letter was posted on Monday morning.
= 就我所知，那封信是星期一上午寄出的。
to the best of my knowledge = 就我所知
比 to my knowledge 更留余地。
⑦ The photographs were taken without her knowledge and sold to a newspaper.
= 那些照片是瞒着她拍的，还卖给了一家报纸。
without somebody's knowledge = 瞒着某人
⑧ She sent the letter in the full knowledge that it would cost her the job.
= 她寄出那封信时完全清楚，这会让她丢掉工作。
in the knowledge that… = 明知…
这一支是「知晓」：知道某件事这一事实。

knowledgeable
核心：知识丰富的、在行的 —— 常接 about。副词 knowledgeably 并入本条。
① The guide was very knowledgeable about the birds and plants of the island.
= 那位导游对岛上的鸟类和植物非常在行。
knowledgeable about something = 对…很在行
② She spoke knowledgeably about wine for half an hour without any notes.
= 她不看稿子，头头是道地讲了半小时葡萄酒。
speak knowledgeably = 讲得头头是道

known
核心：know 的过去分词；形容词：已知的、出了名的。常用 be known as（被称为）、be known for（以…闻名）、
be known to do（据知曾…）、make something known（把…公之于众）。
① He is a known thief, and the shopkeepers watch him closely.
= 他是个出了名的小偷，店主们都紧盯着他。
a known thief = 出了名的小偷
② There is no known cure for the disease at present.
= 这种病目前还没有已知的治法。
no known cure = 没有已知的治法
③ The town is known for its cheese and its Saturday market.
= 那座小城以奶酪和周六集市闻名。
be known for something = 以…闻名
④ The area is known as the Lake District because of its many lakes.
= 那个地区因湖泊众多而被称为湖区。
be known as = 被称为
⑤ He has been known to sleep through an entire meeting.
= 据说他曾经从头到尾睡过一整场会。
be known to do something = 据知曾做过…
⑥ She made it known that she would not stand for election again.
= 她放出话来，说自己不会再参选了。
make it known that… = 把…公之于众

knuckle
核心：指关节；（猪、小牛的）肘子、蹄膀。
短语动词：knuckle down（开始认真干）、knuckle under（屈服）。习语：a rap on the knuckles（一顿训斥）、
near the knuckle（英，口语：近乎下流）。口语复合词 knuckle sandwich（一拳打在嘴上）与
knuckle-dragger（粗野的蠢汉）并入本条。
① He hit the wall so hard that he cut his knuckles.
= 他狠狠地捶了一下墙，把指关节都蹭破了。
your knuckles = 指关节
② The restaurant is famous for its roast pork knuckle with cabbage.
= 那家餐馆以烤猪肘配卷心菜出名。
a pork knuckle = 猪肘
③ You will have to knuckle down if you want to pass the exams in June.
= 你要想通过六月的考试，就得开始下功夫了。
knuckle down = 开始认真干
常说 knuckle down to something。
④ The union refused to knuckle under to the management's demands.
= 工会拒绝向资方的要求低头。
knuckle under = 屈服
⑤ The minister got a rap on the knuckles for speaking to the press too soon.
= 那位部长因为过早向媒体开口而挨了一顿训。
a rap on the knuckles = 一顿训斥
本义是用尺子敲指关节。
⑥ Some of his jokes at the dinner were a bit near the knuckle.
= 他在晚宴上讲的笑话有几个有点儿不雅。
near the knuckle = （英，口语）近乎下流
⑦ Say that again and I'll give you a knuckle sandwich.
= 你再说一遍，我就给你嘴上来一拳。
a knuckle sandwich = （口语）一拳打在嘴上
⑧ The film shows football fans as knuckle-draggers who only want a fight.
= 那部电影把球迷演成了只想打架的粗野蠢汉。
a knuckle-dragger = （口语，贬）粗野的蠢汉
取猿猴走路时指关节拖地的样子。

knuckleduster
核心：（英）指节铜套 —— 套在指关节上、打人更狠的金属凶器。美式说 brass knuckles。
① The police found a knife and a knuckleduster in the boot of his car.
= 警察在他汽车的后备箱里搜出一把刀和一副指节铜套。
a knuckleduster = （英）指节铜套
② It is against the law to carry a knuckleduster in the street.
= 在街上携带指节铜套是违法的。
carry a knuckleduster = 携带指节铜套

knucklehead
核心：（美，口语）笨蛋、傻瓜 —— 多带点亲昵或不耐烦。
① Some knucklehead parked his truck right across our driveway.
= 不知哪个笨蛋把卡车横停在我们家车道口。
a knucklehead = （美，口语）笨蛋
② Don't be such a knucklehead; read the instructions before you plug it in.
= 别这么犯傻，插电之前先看说明书。
be a knucklehead = 犯傻

koala
核心：考拉、树袋熊 —— 澳大利亚的树栖动物，吃桉树叶，育儿袋里带崽。也说 koala bear，但它不是熊。
① The koala sleeps for up to twenty hours a day in the fork of a tree.
= 考拉每天在树杈上睡觉，最多能睡二十个小时。
a koala = 考拉
② Tourists queue for hours to have their photograph taken holding a koala bear.
= 游客排上几个小时的队，就为抱着考拉拍张照。
a koala bear = 考拉（口语叫法）
动物学上不算熊，是有袋类。

kohl
核心：眼影粉、黑色眼线粉 —— 涂在眼睛周围的黑色化妆品，中东、南亚一带用得最久。
① She lined her eyes with kohl before going on stage.
= 她上台前用黑色眼线粉描了眼线。
line your eyes with kohl = 用眼线粉描眼
② The dancer's dark eyes were ringed with thick black kohl.
= 那位舞者的黑眼睛周围涂着一圈浓浓的黑眼影。
eyes ringed with kohl = 眼周涂着眼影粉
阿拉伯语借词；读音同 coal。

kohlrabi
核心：苤蓝、球茎甘蓝 —— 卷心菜的近亲，吃的是膨大的球形茎，生吃熟吃都行。
① She peeled the kohlrabi and cut it into thin slices for the salad.
= 她把苤蓝削了皮，切成薄片拌沙拉。

② We grow kohlrabi and carrots in the vegetable garden behind the house.
= 我们在屋后的菜园里种苤蓝和胡萝卜。
grow kohlrabi = 种苤蓝

koi

= 酒店花园的池塘里满是红色和金色的锦鲤。
red and gold koi = 红色和金色的锦鲤
② He paid two thousand pounds for a single koi carp at the show.
= 他在展会上花两千英镑买了一条锦鲤。
a koi carp = 锦鲤
也说 koi carp。

kook

① The neighbours think he is a kook because he talks to his plants.
= 邻居们觉得他是个怪人，因为他跟自己养的花草说话。
a kook = （美，口语）怪人

kookaburra

the kookaburra swoops = 笑翠鸟俯冲
在澳洲野餐时常有的事。
