self-timer
/ˌself ˈtaɪmə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（相机的）自拍定时器、自动定时拍摄装置。
① We put the camera on a wall and used the self-timer so that the whole family could be in the photo.
= 我们把相机放在墙上，用自拍定时器，好让全家人都拍进照片里。
the self-timer = 自拍定时器
② Set the self-timer for ten seconds and then run and stand next to the others.
= 把自拍定时设成十秒，然后跑过去站到别人旁边。
set the self-timer = 设定自拍定时
手机相机也有这个功能，常在设置里叫「定时」。

self-titled
/ˌself ˈtaɪtld/
核心：形容词：（专辑、作品）以作者或乐队名字命名的、同名的。
① The band's first album was self-titled, simply using the name of the group.
= 那支乐队的首张专辑是同名专辑，直接用了乐队的名字。
self-titled = 同名的
② The young singer's self-titled debut sold a million copies in its first year.
= 那位年轻歌手的同名首张专辑第一年就卖了一百万张。
a self-titled debut = 同名首作
多用于音乐专辑，也用于以主角名命名的电视节目。

self-willed
/ˌself ˈwɪld/
核心：形容词（常含贬义）：任性的、固执己见的。
① She was a self-willed child who always did exactly what she wanted, whatever her parents said.
= 她是个任性的孩子，不管父母说什么，总是想干什么就干什么。
a self-willed child = 任性的孩子
self-willed = 任性的
② The self-willed old man refused to move out of his house, even when it began to fall down.
= 那个固执的老人哪怕房子开始塌了也不肯搬出去。
self-willed = 固执己见的
比 stubborn 更偏「凡事只按自己意愿」。

self-worth
/ˌself ˈwɜːθ/
核心：名词，不可数：自我价值感、自尊。
① Losing her job hurt her sense of self-worth, and she stopped going out with friends.
= 失业伤了她的自我价值感，她不再跟朋友出去了。
a sense of self-worth = 自我价值感
self-worth = 自我价值
② Volunteering at the hospital gave him a new feeling of self-worth after he retired.
= 退休后在医院做志愿者，让他重新找到了自身的价值。
a feeling of self-worth = 自我价值感
多和 sense、feeling 连用，心理学和日常都常见。

selfhood
/ˈselfhʊd/
核心：名词，不可数（正式）：自我、个性、自我意识。
① The poet writes about the struggle to find selfhood in a world that wants everyone to be the same.
= 那位诗人写的是在一个要求人人一样的世界里寻找自我的挣扎。
find selfhood = 寻找自我
selfhood = 自我
② For many young women in the novel, marriage means the loss of their selfhood.
= 对小说里许多年轻女子来说，结婚意味着失去自我。
the loss of selfhood = 失去自我
多见于文学、哲学写作，日常一般说 identity。

selfie
/ˈselfi/
核心：名词，可数（口语）：自拍照：take a selfie。
① The tourists stopped on the bridge to take a selfie with the castle behind them.
= 游客们在桥上停下，以城堡为背景自拍。
take a selfie = 自拍
a selfie = 自拍照
② Selfie sticks are banned in the museum because people kept hitting the paintings with them.
= 那座博物馆禁止使用自拍杆，因为总有人用它碰到画。
a selfie stick = 自拍杆
二〇一三年前后流行开来，现在已是日常词。

selfish
/ˈselfɪʃ/
核心：形容词：自私的、只顾自己的。副词 selfishly 是自私地。名词 selfishness 另立条。
① It was selfish of him to take the last piece of cake without asking anyone else.
= 他谁都不问就拿走最后一块蛋糕，真自私。
it is selfish of somebody to do something = 某人做某事很自私
selfish = 自私的
② She selfishly kept all the money for herself and gave nothing to her brother.
= 她自私地把钱全留给自己，一分都没给她弟弟。
selfishly = 自私地
常用 it is selfish of somebody to…句型批评某个具体行为。

selfishness
/ˈselfɪʃnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：自私。
① His selfishness ruined the holiday, because he always wanted to do only what he liked.
= 他的自私毁了那次度假，因为他总是只想做自己喜欢的事。
selfishness = 自私
② The teacher spoke to the class about selfishness after some children refused to share the toys.
= 有些孩子不肯分享玩具，老师就给全班讲了自私的问题。
talk about selfishness = 谈论自私
和它相对的是 generosity（慷慨）。

selfless
/ˈselfləs/
核心：形容词：无私的、忘我的：a selfless act。副词 selflessly 是无私地。名词 selflessness 另立条。
① The nurse was praised for her selfless work during the long winter of the epidemic.
= 那位护士因在疫情漫长的冬天里忘我工作而受到表彰。
selfless work = 忘我的工作
selfless = 无私的
② He selflessly gave up his seat on the last boat so that a young mother could leave.
= 他无私地让出了最后一条船上的座位，好让一位年轻母亲离开。
selflessly = 无私地
常带赞美，用于描述为他人做出的牺牲。

selflessness
/ˈselfləsnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：无私、忘我。
① The award recognizes the selflessness of people who give their time to help others.
= 那个奖项表彰那些奉献时间帮助他人的人的无私精神。
the selflessness of somebody = 某人的无私
selflessness = 无私
② Her selflessness was clear to everyone who saw how she cared for her sick mother.
= 看过她怎样照顾生病母亲的人，都清楚她有多无私。
somebody's selflessness = 某人的无私
和 generosity 相近，更强调把别人放在自己前面。

selfsame
/ˈselfseɪm/
核心：形容词，只作定语（正式或文学）：完全相同的、同一个的：the selfsame day。用来强调 same。
① She was born and died on the selfsame day of the year, the first of May.
= 她出生和去世恰好是一年中的同一天：五月一日。
the selfsame day = 同一天
selfsame = 完全相同的
② The selfsame people who praised him last year are now calling for him to resign.
= 去年称赞他的正是如今要求他辞职的那批人。
the selfsame people = 同一批人
比 the same 语气更重，多见于书面和文学。

sell
/sel/
核心：动词（过去式和过去分词 sold）：卖、出售：sell something to somebody、sell somebody something、sell something for £10；（商品）有销路、卖得出：sell well；推销、使接受（想法）：sell an idea to somebody。反义是 buy（买）。
习语：sell like hot cakes 是畅销、抢手；sell yourself short 是低估自己、贬低自己。
复合词：selling point 是卖点；selling price 是售价。人是 seller（另立条）。
① We sold our old car to a young couple who lived in the next street.
= 我们把旧车卖给了住在隔壁街的一对年轻夫妇。
sell = 卖
sell something to somebody = 把某物卖给某人
近义对照：
buy = 买
② The little baker on the corner sells fresh bread every morning from six o'clock.
= 街角那家小面包店每天早上六点开始卖新鲜面包。
sell something = 卖某物
③ Her first novel sold well, and it was translated into twenty languages.
= 她的第一部小说卖得很好，被译成了二十种语言。
sell well = 卖得好
④ He managed to sell the idea of a four-day week to the rest of the board.
= 他成功说服董事会其他成员接受每周工作四天的想法。
sell an idea to somebody = 使某人接受某个想法
⑤ The new phones are selling like hot cakes, and most shops have run out.
= 新手机卖得像热蛋糕一样抢手，大多数商店都卖断货了。
sell like hot cakes = 畅销
⑥ Don't sell yourself short at the interview; tell them about everything you have done.
= 面试时别低估自己，把你做过的事都告诉他们。
sell yourself short = 低估自己
⑦ The company sold off its old factories in the north to pay its debts to the bank.
= 那家公司变卖了北方的旧工厂来偿还银行的债务。
sell off = 变卖
⑧ He bought the paintings cheaply and sold them on to collectors in London.
= 他低价买进那些画，再转卖给伦敦的收藏家。
sell on = 转卖
⑨ The tickets for the band's farewell concert sold out in less than ten minutes.
= 那支乐队告别演唱会的票不到十分钟就卖光了。
sell out = 卖光
⑩ Fans accused the band of selling out when they started making pop songs for adverts.
= 那支乐队开始给广告写流行歌，歌迷指责他们为钱背叛了初心。
sell out = 背叛原则
⑪ When my grandparents retired, they sold up and moved to a small town in Spain.
= 我祖父母退休时卖掉了全部家当，搬到了西班牙的一个小镇。
sell up = 卖掉全部家产
⑫ The shop measures the sell-through of each product to decide what to order next season.
= 那家店统计每种产品的售出率，决定下一季进什么货。
sell-through = 售出率
⑬ The main selling point of the flat is its view of the river.
= 那套公寓的主要卖点是能看到河景。
a selling point = 卖点
⑭ The selling price of the house was much higher than the price they had paid ten years before.
= 那座房子的售价比他们十年前买的时候高得多。
the selling price = 售价

seller
/ˈselə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：卖方、卖主、销售者；（商品）销路……的东西：a best seller、a good seller。
① The seller agreed to lower the price because the house had been on the market for a year.

在市场上 buyers and sellers（买方与卖方）常成对说。

seltzer
/ˈseltsə(r)/

① She ordered a glass of seltzer with lime because she was driving home.

seltzer = 苏打水

selvedge

semantic
/sɪˈmæntɪk/
核心：形容词：语义的、词义的：semantic change。副词 semantically 是语义上。名词 semantics 另立条。
① The two words look similar, but there is an important semantic difference between them.
= 这两个词看着相似，但语义上有重要的差别。
a semantic difference = 语义差别
semantic = 语义的
② The teacher explained that the sentence was grammatically correct but semantically very strange.
= 老师解释说，那个句子语法正确，但语义上非常怪。
semantically = 语义上
③ Words like "red", "blue" and "green" belong to the same semantic field of colour.
= 「红」「蓝」「绿」这样的词属于颜色这个语义场。
a semantic field = 语义场

semantics
/sɪˈmæntɪks/
核心：名词，不可数：语义学；（单词、句子的）含义、意思；（贬义）咬文嚼字。形式是复数，当单数用。
① She studies semantics at university and is interested in how words change their meaning.
= 她在大学研究语义学，对词义怎样变化感兴趣。
semantics = 语义学
② Whether we call it a tax or a charge is just semantics; we still have to pay it.
= 叫它税也好、费也好，不过是咬文嚼字，我们照样得付。
just semantics = 只是咬文嚼字
贬义用法常说 it's just semantics，表示争论只在用词上。

semaphore
/ˈseməfɔː(r)/
核心：名词，不可数：旗语：手持两面旗、靠手臂位置表示字母的通信方式；（铁路的）臂板信号机。动词：打旗语。
① The scouts learned to send messages in semaphore with two red and yellow flags.
= 童子军学会了用两面红黄旗打旗语传递信息。
in semaphore = 用旗语
semaphore = 旗语
② Before radio, ships used semaphore to talk to each other at sea.
= 有无线电之前，船只在海上用旗语彼此通话。
use semaphore = 用旗语
现在多见于历史和童子军活动。

semblance

a semblance of something = 表面的样子、一点点
semblance = 表面的样子
② The teacher tried to keep some semblance of order in the noisy classroom.
= 老师设法在吵闹的教室里维持一点秩序。
a semblance of order = 一点秩序

semen

semester
