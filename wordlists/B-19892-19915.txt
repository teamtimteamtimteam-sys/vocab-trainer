satisfy
/ˈsætɪsfaɪ/
核心：动词，及物：使满意、使满足：satisfy somebody；满足（需要、欲望、好奇心）：satisfy demand、satisfy your hunger；符合、达到（要求、条件）：satisfy the requirements；使确信：satisfy somebody that…。词族：名词 satisfaction 是满意，形容词 satisfied 是感到满意的，satisfying 是令人满意的，satisfactory 是令人满意的、合格的。
① Nothing I cook ever seems to satisfy my father; he always says it needs more salt.
= 我做的菜好像从来没让我父亲满意过，他总说还得再加点盐。
satisfy = 使满意
satisfy somebody = 使某人满意
② After months of hard training, she was satisfied with the result of the race and went home happy.
= 苦练了几个月，她对比赛结果很满意，开开心心地回家了。
be satisfied with = 对…满意
satisfied = 感到满足的
③ Winning the cup gave the whole town a feeling of great satisfaction.
= 赢得奖杯让全镇人都感到非常满足。
satisfaction = 满意
④ It is very satisfying to finish a difficult puzzle after working on it for a week.
= 花了一个星期解出一道难题，非常有成就感。
satisfying = 令人满足的
⑤ The factory works day and night to satisfy the demand for the new toy before Christmas.
= 那家工厂日夜赶工，满足圣诞节前对那款新玩具的需求。
satisfy demand = 满足需求
⑥ A bowl of soup and some bread was enough to satisfy our hunger after the long walk.
= 走了那么远的路之后，一碗汤和一些面包就足以填饱肚子。
satisfy your hunger = 填饱肚子
⑦ You must satisfy all the entry requirements before the university can offer you a place.
= 你必须达到所有入学要求，大学才能给你录取名额。
satisfy the requirements = 达到要求

satisfying
/ˈsætɪsfaɪɪŋ/
核心：形容词：令人满意的、令人满足的：a satisfying meal、a satisfying job；（结局）圆满的。副词 satisfyingly 是令人满意地。
① We had a simple but satisfying meal of bread, cheese and soup by the fire.
= 我们在炉火边吃了一顿简单却很满足的饭：面包、奶酪和汤。
a satisfying meal = 令人满足的一餐
satisfying = 令人满意的
② Teaching young children is hard work, but it is one of the most satisfying jobs in the world.
= 教小孩子很辛苦，却是世界上最有成就感的工作之一。
a satisfying job = 有成就感的工作
③ The film ends satisfyingly, with the villain in prison and the family together again.
= 那部电影结局圆满：坏人进了监狱，一家人重新团聚。
satisfyingly = 令人满意地
