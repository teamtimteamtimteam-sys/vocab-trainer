prism
/ˈprɪzəm/
核心：两支，可数。一是棱镜、三棱镜：一块截面是三角形的透明玻璃，能把白光分成彩虹的各色；二是（几何）棱柱。
引申：through the prism of 是透过……的棱镜、从……的角度看。形容词 prismatic 是棱镜的、棱柱的，也指（色彩）斑斓的。并入本条。
① The teacher used a glass prism to split a beam of sunlight into its colours.
= 老师用一块玻璃棱镜把一束阳光分成了各种颜色。
a glass prism = 玻璃棱镜
② A box of matches has the shape of a rectangular prism.
= 火柴盒的形状是长方棱柱。
a rectangular prism = 长方棱柱
③ He sees every argument through the prism of his own childhood.
= 什么争论他都透过自己童年的棱镜来看。
through the prism of something = 从某事的角度看
④ The oil on the wet road shone with prismatic colours.
= 湿路面上的油渍泛着斑斓的色彩。
prismatic colours = 斑斓的色彩

prison
/ˈprɪzn/
核心：监狱，可数；坐牢、监禁，不可数：in prison、go to prison、send somebody to prison。比喻让人觉得被困住的地方、处境。
作为「坐牢」这件事不加冠词：be in prison；说那座楼、去探访才加：visit the prison。
prison officer 是狱警。并入本条。
① He spent five years in prison for his part in the robbery.
= 他因参与那起抢劫坐了五年牢。
in prison = 在坐牢
② She was sent to prison for six months for refusing to pay the fine.
= 她拒交罚款，被判入狱六个月。
be sent to prison = 被判入狱
③ The old prison by the harbour is now a museum.
= 港口边的那座旧监狱如今是博物馆。
a prison = 监狱
④ After the accident the house became a prison for her.
= 出了事故以后，那座房子对她成了一座牢笼。
a prison = 牢笼
⑤ She has been a prison visitor for twenty years and writes to six of the men.

a prison visitor = 义务探访囚犯的人
⑥ The prison officers found a mobile phone hidden in a bar of soap.
= 狱警在一块肥皂里搜出了一部藏着的手机。
a prison officer = 狱警
⑦ He spent three years in a prison camp after his plane was shot down.
= 飞机被打下来以后，他在战俘营里关了三年。
a prison camp = 战俘营、集中营

private
/ˈpraɪvət/

私营的、民办的、不归政府管的：the private sector；私事的、跟工作无关的：private life；秘密的、不愿让人知道的；
（地方）僻静的、没人打扰的；（人）不爱谈自己的、内向的。副词 privately。
名词：（陆军的）列兵、二等兵：军衔最低的士兵，可数；口语的 privates 是阴部。
习语 in private 是私下里、没有别人在场。
复合词：private eye 是私家侦探。并入本条。

a private meeting = 不公开的会晤

⑧ He is a very private man who has never given an interview.

⑭ She worked for ten years as private secretary to the chairman.

⑮ His wife hired a private detective to find out where he went on Fridays.

⑯ The country moved from state control to private enterprise in ten years.

privilege

protected by privilege = 受保密特权保护
