an ormolu clock = 镀金铜钟
② The desk is decorated with ormolu at the corners and round the drawers.
= 那张书桌的四角和抽屉边上饰有镀金铜件。
decorated with ormolu = 饰有镀金铜件

/ˈɔːnəmənt/（名）/ˈɔːnəment/（动）

④ The park has an ornamental lake with two black swans.
= 公园里有个观赏湖，养着两只黑天鹅。
ornamental = 装饰性的、观赏用的
⑤ The church is plain inside, with no ornamentation except a carved door.
