northern
/ˈnɔːðən/
核心：北方的、北部的：位于或来自一个国家、地区的北部。形容词，多作定语；地名里大写：Northern Ireland。
the northern hemisphere 是北半球；the northern lights 是北极光。
① She speaks with a strong northern accent that her pupils love to copy.
= 她说话带浓重的北方口音，学生们爱学她。
a northern accent = 北方口音
② The northern part of the island is mountainous and thinly populated.
= 岛的北部多山，人烟稀少。
the northern part = 北部
③ In the northern hemisphere the longest day falls in June.
= 在北半球，白昼最长的一天在六月。
the northern hemisphere = 北半球
④ We stood on the frozen lake and watched the northern lights until two o'clock.
= 我们站在结冰的湖上看北极光，一直看到两点钟。
the northern lights = 北极光

northerner
/ˈnɔːðənə(r)/
核心：北方人：出生或住在一个国家北部的人。可数，在英国常大写。
① As a northerner, he finds London unfriendly and far too expensive.
= 作为北方人，他觉得伦敦人情淡，物价又太贵。
a northerner = 北方人
② The firm was founded by two northerners who had never run a business.
= 那家公司是两个从没做过生意的北方人创办的。
two northerners = 两个北方人

northernmost
/ˈnɔːðənməʊst/
核心：最北的、最靠北的。形容词，只作定语。
① A white lighthouse marks the northernmost point of the mainland, an hour's drive beyond the last village.
= 一座白色灯塔标出大陆的最北端，过了最后一个村子还要开一个钟头的车。
the northernmost point = 最北端
② Students come from forty countries to study in the northernmost town in the world with a university.
= 四十个国家的学生来到世界上有大学的最北的那座城镇求学。
the northernmost town = 最北的城镇

northward
/ˈnɔːθwəd/
核心：两支。一是形容词，向北的：a northward journey；二是副词，向北，英式多写成 northwards，美式多用不带 s 的形式。
① The army began its northward march at dawn.
= 军队在黎明时开始北进。
a northward march = 北进
② The birds fly northwards in March to breed on the tundra.
= 那些鸟三月往北飞，到苔原上繁殖。
northwards = 向北
③ The road runs northward along the river for about thirty miles.
= 那条路沿河向北延伸三十英里左右。
run northward = 向北延伸

Norwegian
/nɔːˈwiːdʒən/
核心：三支，首字母大写。一是形容词，挪威的；二是名词，挪威人，可数；三是挪威语，不可数。
带 Norway 的两个动物名并入本条：Norway lobster 是挪威海螯虾，餐桌上叫 scampi 或 langoustine；
Norway rat 是褐家鼠，最常见的那种老鼠。
① The Norwegian coast is cut by hundreds of deep fjords.
= 挪威的海岸被数百条深深的峡湾切开。
the Norwegian coast = 挪威海岸
② A Norwegian was the first person to reach the South Pole.
= 头一个到达南极的是个挪威人。
a Norwegian = 挪威人
③ She learned Norwegian in a year by reading crime novels with a dictionary.
= 她靠着词典读犯罪小说，一年学会了挪威语。
learn Norwegian = 学挪威语
④ The scampi on the menu is the tail of the Norway lobster.
= 菜单上的炸虾球，用的是挪威海螯虾的尾巴。
the Norway lobster = 挪威海螯虾
⑤ The Norway rat arrived on ships and now lives in every city.
= 褐家鼠是随船来的，如今每座城市都有。
the Norway rat = 褐家鼠

nose
/nəʊz/
核心：名词四支。一是鼻子；blow your nose 是擤鼻涕，a runny nose 是流鼻涕，hold your nose 是捏住鼻子；
二是嗅觉，引申为觉察某类事物的本事：have a nose for；三是（飞机、车、船的）头部：nose cone 是火箭、飞机的鼻锥；
四是（酒的）香气。
动词一是（车、船）小心地慢慢往前探；二是（动物）用鼻子拱、嗅；
短语动词：nose about 与 nose around 是到处打探、乱翻；nose out 一是嗅出、打探出，二是以微弱优势击败。
习语很多：under your nose 是就在眼皮底下；poke your nose into 是多管闲事；turn your nose up at 是瞧不上；
look down your nose at 是看不起；follow your nose 是一直往前走、凭直觉走；pay through the nose 是花冤枉大价钱；
nose to tail 是（英式）车辆首尾相接；keep your nose clean 是不惹事；get up somebody's nose 是（英式）惹人心烦；
by a nose 是（赛马）以一鼻之差，引申为险胜；cut off your nose to spite your face 是赌气害了自己；
on the nose 是（美式）不多不少、正好；keep your nose out of 是别管闲事。
nose ring 是鼻环，nose flute 是用鼻子吹的鼻笛。并入本条。
① He broke his nose playing rugby, and it has been crooked ever since.
= 他打橄榄球把鼻子撞断了，从此鼻梁就是歪的。
break your nose = 撞断鼻子
② She took out a handkerchief and blew her nose loudly.
= 她掏出手帕，响亮地擤了擤鼻子。
blow your nose = 擤鼻涕
③ The child has had a runny nose and a cough since the weekend.
= 那孩子从周末起一直流鼻涕、咳嗽。
a runny nose = 流鼻涕
④ I held my nose and swallowed the medicine in one gulp.
= 我捏住鼻子，一口把药吞了下去。
hold your nose = 捏住鼻子
⑤ A good reporter has a nose for a story that others miss.
= 好记者对别人漏掉的新闻有灵敏的嗅觉。
have a nose for = 对…嗅觉灵敏
have a nose for something = 善于觉察某事
⑥ The nose of the plane dipped sharply, and the passengers screamed.
= 机头猛地往下一沉，乘客们尖叫起来。
the nose of a plane = 机头
⑦ The nose cone of the rocket is designed to survive great heat.
= 火箭的鼻锥设计得能耐极高的温度。
a nose cone = 鼻锥
⑧ The wine has a nose of blackcurrants and cedar.
= 那款酒有黑醋栗和雪松的香气。
the nose of a wine = 酒的香气
⑨ The taxi nosed its way through the crowd outside the stadium.
= 出租车在体育场外的人群里小心地往前挪。
nose your way through something = 小心地穿过某处
⑩ The dog nosed the door open and climbed onto the bed.
= 那条狗用鼻子拱开门，爬上了床。
nose something open = 用鼻子拱开某物
⑪ I found him nosing about in my desk when I came back from lunch.
= 我吃完午饭回来，发现他在我的书桌里乱翻。
nose about = 到处乱翻、打探
⑫ Reporters have been nosing around the village asking about the family.
= 记者们一直在村里转悠，打听那家人的事。
nose around = 到处打探
⑬ The dog can nose out a truffle buried a foot under the ground.
= 那条狗能嗅出埋在地下一英尺的松露。
nose out = 嗅出、打探出
⑭ She nosed out the favourite in the last few strides.
= 她在最后几步以微弱优势胜了热门选手。
nose out somebody = 以微弱优势击败某人
⑮ The thieves loaded the van right under the nose of the security guard.
= 那伙贼就在保安的眼皮底下把货装上了车。
under the nose of somebody = 在某人眼皮底下
under your nose = 就在眼皮底下
⑯ I wish she would stop poking her nose into other people's business.
= 但愿她别再管别人的闲事。
poke your nose into = 多管闲事
poke your nose into something = 插手某事
⑰ The children turned their noses up at the spinach.
= 孩子们对菠菜一脸嫌弃。
turn your nose up at = 瞧不上、嫌弃
⑱ He looks down his nose at anyone who did not go to university.
= 没上过大学的人，他一概看不起。
look down your nose at = 看不起
look down your nose at somebody = 看不起某人
⑲ Just follow your nose along this road until you reach the church.
= 你就顺着这条路一直往前走，走到教堂为止。
follow your nose = 一直往前走
⑳ We paid through the nose for a room with a view of the car park.
= 我们花了冤枉大价钱，住了一间看得见停车场的房间。
pay through the nose = 花冤枉大价钱

not
/nɒt/
核心：副词，构成否定。跟在助动词、情态动词、be 后面：is not、do not、cannot；口语里缩成 n't。
否定非谓语时放在它前面：not to go、not knowing。
还用来否定一个词或短语：not everyone、not many、not for long；替代整个否定从句：I hope not、I am afraid not。
固定说法：not at all 一是一点也不，二是（回答道谢）别客气；not only … but also … 是不但…而且…，放在句首要倒装；
not that 是倒不是说；not to mention 是更不用说；if not 是如果不、甚至；not even 是连…都不；
not quite 是不完全、差一点；or not 是与否；not a 加名词是一个…都没有；not really 是不太、其实没有；
not … until 是直到…才。
① She is not at home today, but you can reach her at the office.
= 她今天不在家，不过你打办公室电话找得到她。
is not = 不是、不在
② I do not understand why the door was left unlocked.
= 我不明白门为什么没锁。
do not do something = 不做某事
③ He asked me not to tell anyone until the contract was signed.
= 他让我在合同签好之前别告诉任何人。
not to do something = 不要做某事
否定不定式，放在 to 前面。
④ Not knowing the language, she pointed at the dish she wanted.
= 她不懂当地话，就指了指想要的那道菜。
not knowing = 由于不知道
⑤ Not everyone enjoys camping, especially in the rain.
= 不是人人都喜欢露营，尤其是下雨天。
not everyone = 不是人人
not many = 不多
⑥ "Will it rain this afternoon?" "I hope not."
= 「今天下午会下雨吗？」「但愿不会。」
I hope not. = 但愿不会。
I am afraid not. = 恐怕不行。
⑦ The water was not at all cold once you were in.
= 一下了水，就一点也不觉得冷了。
not at all = 一点也不
⑧ "Thank you for carrying the bags." "Not at all."
= 「谢谢你帮忙拎包。」「别客气。」
Not at all. = 别客气。
⑨ She not only wrote the play but also directed it.
= 那出戏不但是她写的，也是她导的。
not only = 不但
not only … but also … = 不但…而且…
⑩ Not only did he forget her birthday, he also forgot their anniversary.
= 他不但忘了她的生日，连结婚纪念日也忘了。
Not only did he … = 他不但…
放在句首，后面倒装。
⑪ I shall walk to the station; not that I mind the exercise.
= 我走着去车站；倒不是说我介意活动活动。
not that = 倒不是说
⑫ The flat is too small for a piano, not to mention a dog.
= 那套公寓太小，放不下钢琴，更不用说养狗了。
not to mention = 更不用说
⑬ It will take weeks, if not months, to clear the backlog.
= 清理积压的工作要好几个星期，甚至好几个月。
if not = 甚至、如果不是
⑭ He left without a word, not even a goodbye to the children.
= 他一句话没说就走了，连跟孩子们道别都没有。
not even = 连…都不
⑮ The paint is not quite dry, so mind your sleeve.
= 油漆还没全干，当心袖子。
not quite = 不完全、差一点
⑯ Tell me by Friday whether you are coming or not.
= 星期五以前告诉我你来还是不来。
or not = 与否
whether or not = 是否
⑰ Not a sound came from the room where the children were asleep.
= 孩子们睡觉的那间屋里，一点声音都没有。
not a = 一个…都没有
not a sound = 一点声音都没有
⑱ "Did you enjoy the film?" "Not really. It went on far too long."

notary

动词 notarize 是（由公证人）公证，主要是美式，并入本条。
① The contract has to be signed in front of a notary to be valid abroad.
= 那份合同要在公证人面前签字，在国外才有效。

= 那封信只有经过公证，银行才收。
be notarized = 经过公证
notarize a document = 给文件做公证

notation

notch
/nɒtʃ/
核心：名词三支，可数。一是 V 形刻痕、凹口：皮带上的扣眼也叫这个；二是等级、档次：a notch above 是高出一档，
turn something up a notch 是调高一档；三是（美式）山间的隘口。

nothing
/ˈnʌθɪŋ/
核心：代词，没有什么、没有东西。作主语时动词用单数；本身是否定，不再加否定词。
形容词、else、不定式都放在它后面：nothing new、nothing else、nothing to eat。
也指无关紧要的东西、零：It was nothing。名词指无足轻重的人或事。
固定说法很多：nothing but 是只有、只是；for nothing 一是免费，二是白白地；nothing like 一是完全不像，二是远远不；
nothing much 是没什么；have nothing to do with 是与…无关、不跟…来往；nothing of the kind 是根本不是那么回事；
nothing if not 是极其、别的不说至少；come to nothing 是落空；think nothing of 是不把…当回事；
nothing less than 是不折不扣的；nothing doing 是（口语）不行、没门；there is nothing in it 是没有那回事；
there is nothing to it 是很容易；sweet nothings 是情话。
名词 nothingness（虚无、不存在）并入本条。
① There is nothing in the fridge except a jar of mustard.
= 冰箱里除了一罐芥末，什么都没有。
nothing = 什么都没有
② I have nothing to wear to the wedding on Saturday.
= 星期六的婚礼，我没有衣服可穿。
nothing to wear = 没有可穿的
③ Nothing else matters as long as the children are safe.
= 只要孩子们平安，别的都不要紧。
nothing else = 没有别的
④ There is nothing new in the report, only what we knew already.
= 那份报告里没有什么新东西，都是我们已经知道的。
nothing new = 没什么新的
形容词放在后面。
⑤ "What is the matter?" "Nothing. I am just tired."
= 「怎么了？」「没什么，我只是累了。」
Nothing. = 没什么。
⑥ He eats nothing but toast and marmalade for breakfast.

nothing but = 只有、只是
⑦ The neighbours gave us the old bicycle for nothing.
= 邻居把那辆旧自行车白送给了我们。
for nothing = 免费
⑧ We drove all that way for nothing, because the museum was shut.
= 我们大老远开过去白跑了一趟，博物馆关着门。
for nothing = 白白地
同一个短语的另一层意思。
⑨ The film is nothing like the book it claims to be based on.
= 那部片子跟它自称改编的那本书完全不像。
nothing like = 完全不像
⑩ There is nothing like a hot bath after a long walk in the rain.
= 在雨里走了长路之后，什么也比不上洗个热水澡。
there is nothing like something = 什么也比不上某物
⑪ "What did you do at the weekend?" "Nothing much. I cleaned the car."
= 「周末干什么了？」「没干什么，洗了洗车。」
nothing much = 没什么
⑫ The delay has nothing to do with the weather, whatever the airline says.
= 不管航空公司怎么说，延误跟天气毫无关系。
have nothing to do with = 与…无关
have nothing to do with something = 与某事无关
⑬ "You promised to pay." "I said nothing of the kind."

nothing of the kind = 根本不是那么回事
⑭ She is nothing if not thorough, and checked every figure twice.
= 她别的不说，至少极其仔细，每个数字都核对了两遍。

⑮ All his plans to emigrate came to nothing when his father fell ill.

come to nothing = 落空
⑯ She thinks nothing of cycling thirty miles before breakfast.
= 早饭前骑三十英里的车，她根本不当回事。

⑰ His escape from the burning car was nothing less than a miracle.

⑱ He asked to borrow the car again, but I told him nothing doing.

⑲ There is a rumour that the shop is closing, but there is nothing in it.

now
/naʊ/
核心：副词，现在、此刻；也指「立刻」；叙述过去时指「这时」。
作名词用在介词后面：by now、until now、from now on、for now、up to now。
连词 now that 是既然、由于…已经。
口语里还用来引起注意、转换话题或缓和语气，不指时间：Now, where was I?；now then 是喂、好了。
固定说法：right now 一是此刻，二是马上；just now 一是刚才，二是此刻；now and then 与 now and again 是时不时；
any minute now 是随时、马上就；now or never 是机不可失；every now and then 是时不时。
① She used to teach in a village school and now works in a bank.
= 她从前在乡村学校教书，现在在银行工作。
now = 现在
② The doctor will see you now, if you would like to come through.
= 医生现在可以见您了，请往里走。
now = 现在、此刻
③ Stop that shouting now, or you will go straight to bed.
= 马上别嚷了，要不然你直接上床去。
now = 立刻
④ I am busy right now, but I can ring you back in ten minutes.
= 我这会儿正忙，十分钟后可以给你回电话。
right now = 就现在
⑤ Come down here right now and clear up this mess.
= 马上下来，把这一摊收拾干净。
right now = 马上
⑥ I saw her just now in the corridor, talking to the caretaker.
= 我刚才在走廊里看见她，正跟管理员说话。
just now = 刚才
⑦ They should have reached the coast by now.
= 他们这会儿应该已经到海边了。
by now = 到现在
⑧ From now on, all visitors must sign in at the front desk.
= 从现在起，所有来访者都必须在前台登记。
from now on = 从现在开始
⑨ That is enough for now, and we can finish the rest tomorrow.
= 眼下就先到这儿，剩下的明天再做完。
for now = 眼下、暂时
⑩ Up to now we have had no complaints about the new timetable.
= 到目前为止，新时刻表我们还没收到过投诉。
up to now = 到目前为止
until now = 直到现在
⑪ Now that the children have left home, the house feels far too big.
= 孩子们既然都离家了，这座房子显得太大了。
now that = 既然、由于…已经
⑫ We still see each other now and then, mostly at weddings.
= 我们时不时还见面，多半是在婚礼上。
now and then = 时不时
every now and then = 时不时
⑬ Now and again a fox crosses the garden in the middle of the day.
= 偶尔有只狐狸大白天穿过花园。
now and again = 偶尔
⑭ The taxi should be here any minute now.
= 出租车随时就到。
any minute now = 随时、马上就
⑮ If you want to ask her to dance, it is now or never.
= 你想请她跳舞的话，机不可失。
now or never = 机不可失
⑯ He was now alone in the house for the first time in forty years.
= 这时，他四十年来头一回一个人待在这座房子里。
now = 这时
叙述过去的事时用，指「故事里的此刻」。
⑰ Now, who can tell me the capital of Australia?
= 好，谁能告诉我澳大利亚的首都是哪儿？
Now, … = 好，…
引起注意，不指时间。
⑱ "Now then, what is all this shouting about?" asked the policeman.
= 警察问：「喂，嚷嚷什么呢？」
now then = 喂、好了

nowhere
/ˈnəʊweə(r)/

out of nowhere = 突然出现
⑤ I have been arguing with the bank for a month and getting nowhere.
= 我跟银行争了一个月，毫无进展。
get nowhere = 毫无进展
⑥ Shouting at the waiter will get you nowhere.
= 冲服务员嚷嚷，你什么也得不到。

come from nowhere = 名不见经传却突然出头
