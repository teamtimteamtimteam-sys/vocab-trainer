internecine
核心：（正式）内部争斗的、自相残杀的 —— 多说同一组织、政党、家族内部的激烈冲突。
① Years of internecine warfare had destroyed the old kingdom.
= 多年的自相残杀摧毁了那个古老的王国。
internecine warfare = 自相残杀的战争
② The party is weakened by internecine struggles between its leaders.
= 那个党因领导人之间的内斗而元气大伤。
internecine struggles = 内斗

internet
核心：互联网、因特网 —— 前加 the；常大写为 the Internet。也作定语：internet access、internet café。
internet dating（网上约会）与 internet service provider（互联网服务提供商，缩写「ISP」）并入本条。
① I found an old family recipe for apple cake on the internet last night.
= 昨晚我在网上找到了一个苹果蛋糕的老家传食谱。
on the internet = 在网上
② Most hotels now offer free internet access to their guests.
= 如今大多数酒店都为客人提供免费上网。
internet access = 上网
③ They met through internet dating and married a year later.
= 他们是通过网上约会认识的，一年后结了婚。
internet dating = 网上约会
④ Call your internet service provider if the connection keeps failing.
= 如果网络总是断，就给你的互联网服务提供商打电话。
an internet service provider = 互联网服务提供商

internist
核心：（美）内科医生 —— 专治成人内科疾病的医生，不做手术。
① Her internist sent her to a heart specialist after the tests.
= 检查之后，她的内科医生让她去看心脏专科医生。
an internist = 内科医生
② In the United States, adults with a long-term illness often see an internist rather than a family doctor.
= 在美国，患慢性病的成年人常去看内科医生，而不是家庭医生。
see an internist = 去看内科医生
跟 intern（实习医生）不同，internist 是正式执业的内科医生。

interoffice
核心：（美）公司内部各部门之间的 —— 多说内部邮件、备忘录。
① The memo was sent by interoffice mail to every department.
= 那份备忘录通过内部邮件发给了每个部门。
interoffice mail = 内部邮件
② An interoffice memo reminded staff about the new parking rules.
= 一份内部备忘录提醒员工注意新的停车规定。
an interoffice memo = 内部备忘录

interoperable
核心：（计算机）可互操作的、可互通的 —— 不同系统、设备能一起工作。名词 interoperability 并入本条。
① The new software is interoperable with most older systems.
= 新软件能与大多数旧系统互通。
be interoperable with something = 能与…互通
② Poor interoperability between hospital computers slows down patient care.
= 医院电脑之间互通性差，拖慢了对病人的照护。
interoperability = 互操作性

interpenetrate
核心：（正式）相互渗透、相互贯穿 —— 两种东西彼此渗入对方。名词 interpenetration 并入本条。
① In her paintings, light and shadow seem to interpenetrate.
= 在她的画里，光与影似乎相互渗透。
interpenetrate = 相互渗透
② The book explores the interpenetration of religion and politics in medieval Europe.
= 那本书探讨了中世纪欧洲宗教与政治的相互交融。
the interpenetration of A and B = A与B的相互渗透

interpersonal
核心：人际的、人与人之间的 —— 多说沟通技巧、关系。
① Working on a busy hospital ward requires good interpersonal skills and a lot of patience.
= 在繁忙的医院病房工作，需要良好的人际交往能力和极大的耐心。
interpersonal skills = 人际交往能力
② Many interpersonal problems in small offices come from poor communication between managers and staff.
= 小办公室里的许多人际问题，都源于管理者和员工之间沟通不畅。
interpersonal problems = 人际问题

interplanetary
核心：行星之间的 —— 说太空旅行、空间。
① The spacecraft is designed for interplanetary travel.
= 那艘宇宙飞船是为行星际旅行设计的。
interplanetary travel = 行星际旅行
② Interplanetary space is not completely empty but contains dust and gas.
= 行星际空间并非完全空无一物，而是有尘埃和气体。
interplanetary space = 行星际空间

interplay
核心：相互作用、相互影响 —— 两种或更多因素彼此作用。
① The film explores the interplay between love and money.
= 那部电影探讨了爱情与金钱之间的相互作用。
the interplay between A and B = A与B之间的相互作用
② The painting is famous for its interplay of light and colour.
= 那幅画以光与色的交相辉映而闻名。
the interplay of something = …的相互作用

Interpol
核心：国际刑警组织 —— 协调各国警方打击跨国犯罪的国际组织。
① Interpol issued a warrant for his arrest after he fled the country.
= 他逃出国境后，国际刑警组织发出了逮捕令。
Interpol = 国际刑警组织
② Police worked with Interpol to trace the stolen paintings.
= 警方与国际刑警组织合作追查被盗的画作。
work with Interpol = 与国际刑警组织合作

interpolate
核心：（正式）插入、插话；（数学）插值。两支：在文字或讲话中加入额外内容；
根据已知数值推算中间的值。名词 interpolation 并入本条。
① Someone had interpolated several lines into the original poem.
= 有人在原诗里窜入了几行。
interpolate something into something = 把…插入…
② The missing temperatures were interpolated from the readings on either side.
= 缺失的气温是根据前后的读数插值推算出来的。
interpolate values = 插值推算
③ Scholars believe the last chapter is a later interpolation.
= 学者们认为最后一章是后人添加的。
an interpolation = 窜入、添加的内容

interpose
核心：（正式）插入、介于…之间；插话、干预。两支：把自己或某物放到两者之间（interpose yourself between）；
在谈话中插入一句。
① The teacher interposed herself between the two boys before the fight could start.
= 老师在两个男孩之间一站，架还没打起来就被拦住了。
interpose yourself between A and B = 挡在A与B之间
② "But that's not what you said yesterday," she interposed.
= 「可你昨天不是这么说的。」她插话道。
interpose = 插话

interpret
核心：解释、阐释；理解为；口译；演绎。几支：说明意思（interpret a dream / the data）；
把某事理解成某种意思（interpret something as）；把一种语言当场译成另一种；表演者对作品的演绎。
形容词 interpretable 并入本条；interpretation、interpreter 另立。
① As a statistician, how would you interpret the results of the survey on housing?
= 作为统计学家，你会怎么解读这项住房调查的结果？
interpret results = 解读结果
② At the trial, his long silence was interpreted by the jury as a sign of guilt.
= 审判时，他长时间的沉默被陪审团理解为有罪的迹象。
interpret something as something = 把…理解为…
③ She interpreted for the minister during his visit to Beijing.
= 部长访问北京期间，她为他担任口译。
interpret for somebody = 为某人口译
④ The actor interpreted the role of the king in a completely new way.
= 那位演员以全新的方式演绎了国王这个角色。
interpret a role = 演绎角色
⑤ The old map is so faded that it is barely interpretable.
= 那张旧地图褪色得厉害，几乎看不明白。
interpretable = 可以理解的

interpretation
核心：解释、理解；演绎。可数：对同一件事的一种理解（a different interpretation）；
表演者对作品的一种处理方式。不可数：解释工作；口译。
① The law is open to interpretation, and lawyers disagree about its meaning.
= 那条法律有多种解释的余地，律师们对它的意思看法不一。
open to interpretation = 有多种解释的余地
② She gave a moving interpretation of the famous sonata.
= 她对那首著名奏鸣曲的演绎感人至深。
an interpretation of something = 对…的演绎
③ Simultaneous interpretation is provided at the conference in five languages.
= 会议提供五种语言的同声传译。
simultaneous interpretation = 同声传译

interpretative
核心：解释性的、说明性的 —— 也作 interpretive（多美式），多说博物馆展板、说明材料。
① The castle has interpretative panels explaining its history.
= 城堡里有介绍其历史的说明展板。
interpretative panels = 说明展板
② The critic takes an interpretative approach to the poems rather than a historical one.
= 那位评论家对这些诗采取阐释的方法，而不是历史考证的方法。
an interpretative approach = 阐释性的方法

interpreter
核心：口译员；解释者、演绎者；（计算机）解释程序。本义是当场翻译口语的人；
也指对作品、法律作出阐释的人。
① She spoke through an interpreter because she knew no English.
= 她不懂英语，是通过口译员说话的。
through an interpreter = 通过口译员
② He is one of the greatest interpreters of Beethoven's music.
= 他是贝多芬音乐最伟大的演绎者之一。
an interpreter of something = …的演绎者

interracial
核心：不同种族之间的 —— 多说婚姻、关系。
① Interracial marriage was illegal in some American states until 1967.
= 在美国一些州，跨种族婚姻直到一九六七年都是违法的。
interracial marriage = 跨种族婚姻
② The film tells the story of an interracial couple in the 1950s.
= 那部电影讲的是二十世纪五十年代一对跨种族夫妇的故事。
an interracial couple = 跨种族夫妇

interregnum
核心：（两任统治者之间的）空位期；过渡期 —— 旧的走了、新的还没来的一段时间。
① There was a brief interregnum after the king's death while his son was abroad.
= 国王死后，因王子在国外，出现了一段短暂的空位期。
an interregnum = 空位期
② The company went through an interregnum of six months before a new chief executive was appointed.
= 公司经历了六个月的过渡期，才任命了新的首席执行官。
an interregnum = 过渡期

interrelate
核心：（使）相互关联 —— 多用分词 interrelated（相互关联的）。名词 interrelationship 并入本条。
① The causes of the crisis are complex and interrelated.
= 危机的成因错综复杂、相互关联。
be interrelated = 相互关联
② The course shows how the different parts of the economy interrelate.
= 这门课说明了经济的各个部分是如何相互关联的。
interrelate = 相互关联
③ Scientists are studying the interrelationship between diet and disease.
= 科学家们正在研究饮食与疾病之间的相互关系。
the interrelationship between A and B = A与B之间的相互关系

interrogate
核心：审问、盘问；（计算机）查询。本义是长时间、有时带强迫地问嫌疑人问题；
技术上指向设备、数据库发出询问以取得信息。名词 interrogation 与审问者 interrogator 并入本条。
① The police interrogated the suspect for six hours before releasing him.
= 警方审问了嫌疑人六个小时，然后才放了他。
interrogate somebody = 审问某人
② The software interrogates the database every few seconds for new orders.
= 那个软件每隔几秒就查询一次数据库，看有没有新订单。
interrogate a database = 查询数据库
③ He confessed after a long and brutal interrogation.
= 经过一场漫长而残酷的审讯后，他招供了。
an interrogation = 审讯
④ His interrogators kept him awake for three days.
= 审讯者让他三天没合眼。
an interrogator = 审问者

interrogative
核心：疑问的；（语法）疑问词、疑问句。形容词说语气、神情带着询问（an interrogative look）；
语法上指 what、who、which 这类疑问词，或疑问句形式。副词 interrogatively 并入本条。
① She gave him an interrogative look when he came home so late.
= 他那么晚才回家，她用询问的眼神看着他。
an interrogative look = 询问的眼神
② Learners practise turning statements into interrogatives in the first week of the course.
= 学员在课程第一周练习把陈述句改成疑问句。
an interrogative = 疑问句、疑问词
③ He raised his eyebrows interrogatively but said nothing.
= 他询问似地扬了扬眉毛，却什么也没说。
interrogatively = 询问地

interrogatory
核心：（正式）询问的、质问的；（法律）书面质询 —— 诉讼中一方要求另一方书面回答的一组问题。
① He answered in an interrogatory tone, as if he did not believe her.
= 他用一种质问的语气回答，好像不相信她。
an interrogatory tone = 质问的语气
② The lawyers sent a list of interrogatories to the other side before the trial.
= 审判前，律师们给对方寄了一份书面质询清单。
interrogatories = 书面质询（法律）

interrupt
核心：打断、打扰；中断、阻断。两支：别人说话或做事时插进来（interrupt somebody）；
让一个过程暂时停下（interrupt a journey / the view）。名词 interruption 另立。
① Please don't interrupt me while I'm on the phone to the bank.
= 我在给银行打电话时请别打断我。
interrupt somebody = 打断某人
② The match was interrupted twice by heavy rain.
= 比赛两次因大雨中断。
be interrupted by something = 因…中断
③ Tall new buildings now interrupt the view of the mountains from the old town.
= 新建的高楼如今挡住了从老城望向群山的视线。
interrupt a view = 阻断视线

interruption
核心：打断、打扰；中断 —— interrupt 的名词。可数不可数都用。
① With the children at school, she worked on her novel for three hours without interruption.
= 孩子们上学去了，她不间断地写了三个小时小说。
without interruption = 不间断地
② I'm sorry for the interruption, but there's an urgent call for you.
= 抱歉打扰一下，有你的紧急电话。
sorry for the interruption = 抱歉打扰
③ There were several interruptions to the power supply during the storm.
= 暴风雨期间电力供应中断了几次。
an interruption to something = …的中断
