manic
/ˈmænɪk/
核心：（口语）忙乱的、狂热的、兴奋过头的；（医）躁狂的。副词 manically 并入本条。
旧称 manic depression（躁郁症，今天叫 bipolar disorder）与 manic-depressive（躁郁症的；躁郁症患者）也在这里。
① It has been a manic week at the office, with three people off sick.
= 办公室这个星期忙疯了，有三个人请了病假。
a manic week = 忙疯了的一周
口语里的夸张，不是真的病。
② The children ran around the garden with manic energy until bedtime.
= 孩子们精力过剩地在花园里疯跑，一直跑到睡觉时间。
manic energy = 过剩的精力
③ He worked manically for a month to finish the book on time.
= 他疯了似的干了一个月，按时把书写完。
work manically = 疯了似的干
④ The painter suffered from manic depression for most of his life.
= 那位画家大半辈子受躁郁症折磨。
manic depression = 躁郁症（旧称）
⑤ The doctor explained that her father was a manic-depressive and needed regular medicine.
= 医生解释说，她父亲患有躁郁症，需要按时服药。
a manic-depressive = 躁郁症患者

manicure
/ˈmænɪkjʊə(r)/
核心：修指甲、美甲（手部护理）。动词：给…修指甲。
形容词 manicured（指甲修剪整齐的；（花园、草坪）修剪得一丝不苟的）与 manicurist（美甲师）并入本条。
① She has a manicure every two weeks at the salon near her office.
= 她每两周在办公室附近的美容店做一次美甲。
have a manicure = 做美甲
拉丁语 manus（手）加 cura（护理）。
② The hotel stands in beautifully manicured gardens.
= 那家酒店坐落在修剪得一丝不苟的花园里。
manicured gardens = 修剪整齐的花园
③ She tapped the table with her perfectly manicured nails.
= 她用修剪得无可挑剔的指甲敲着桌子。
manicured nails = 修剪整齐的指甲
④ The manicurist painted each nail a different colour.
= 美甲师把每个指甲涂成了不同的颜色。
a manicurist = 美甲师
脚部护理是 pedicure。

manifest
/ˈmænɪfest/
核心：（正式）明显的、显而易见的；动词：表明、显示；（症状、情绪）表现出来（manifest itself）。
名词：（船、飞机的）货单、旅客名单。副词 manifestly 并入本条；历史用语 manifest destiny（昭昭天命）也在这里。
① His relief was manifest to everyone in the room.
= 他松了一口气，屋里的人都看得出来。
be manifest = 明显
比 obvious 正式得多。
② The workers manifested their anger by walking out of the meeting.
= 工人们以退场表示愤怒。
manifest your anger = 表示愤怒
③ The illness usually manifests itself as a rash on the arms.
= 那种病通常表现为胳膊上起皮疹。
manifest itself = 表现出来
主语多是病、问题、情绪。
④ His name was not on the ship's passenger manifest.
= 那艘船的旅客名单上没有他的名字。
a passenger manifest = 旅客名单
这一支是名词，航运、航空用语。
⑤ The decision was manifestly unfair to the younger workers.
= 那个决定对年轻工人明显不公。
manifestly unfair = 明显不公
⑥ In the nineteenth century, many Americans believed in the manifest destiny of their country to reach the Pacific.
= 十九世纪，许多美国人相信自己的国家注定要扩张到太平洋。
manifest destiny = 昭昭天命

manifestation
/ˌmænɪfeˈsteɪʃən/
核心：（正式）表现、体现；（鬼魂、神灵的）显现。
① The riots were a manifestation of anger about rising prices.
= 那些骚乱是对物价上涨的愤怒的一种表现。
a manifestation of anger = 愤怒的表现
来自动词 manifest。
② A rash is often the first manifestation of the disease.
= 皮疹往往是那种病最早的表现。
the first manifestation = 最早的表现
③ The villagers spoke of ghostly manifestations in the old church.
= 村民们说那座老教堂里有鬼魂显现。
ghostly manifestations = 鬼魂显现

manifesto
/ˌmænɪˈfestəʊ/
核心：宣言、（政党的）竞选纲领。复数 manifestos。
① The party promised lower taxes in its election manifesto.
= 那个党在竞选纲领里承诺减税。
an election manifesto = 竞选纲领
英国各党大选前都要发布。
② The young painters published a manifesto calling for a new kind of art.
= 那些年轻画家发表了一份宣言，呼吁一种新的艺术。
publish a manifesto = 发表宣言
和 manifest 同源：把主张公之于众。

manifold
/ˈmænɪfəʊld/
核心：（正式）多种多样的、许许多多的；名词：（发动机的）歧管。
① The reasons for the company's failure are manifold.
= 那家公司失败的原因是多方面的。
be manifold = 多方面的
many 加 fold（倍、重）。
② The new job brought manifold opportunities to travel and learn.
= 那份新工作带来了许许多多旅行和学习的机会。
manifold opportunities = 许许多多的机会
③ The mechanic found a crack in the exhaust manifold.
= 修车师傅在排气歧管上发现了一道裂缝。
the exhaust manifold = 排气歧管

manipulate
/məˈnɪpjʊleɪt/
核心：（贬）操纵、摆布（人、舆论、数字）；熟练地操作、摆弄；（医）推拿、正骨。
名词 manipulation、形容词 manipulative（爱摆布人的；操作的）、名词 manipulator（操纵者）并入本条。
① He knows how to manipulate people into doing what he wants.
= 他懂得怎么摆布别人去做他想让他们做的事。
manipulate somebody into doing something = 摆布某人去做…
② The company was accused of manipulating the figures to hide its losses.
= 那家公司被指篡改数字掩盖亏损。
manipulate the figures = 篡改数字
这一支含贬义：动了手脚。
③ The pilot learns to manipulate the controls without looking at them.
= 飞行员要学会不看操纵杆就能操作。
manipulate the controls = 操作控制装置
这一支是中性的：用手熟练操作。
④ The osteopath manipulated his back to ease the pain.
= 正骨师给他的后背做了推拿以缓解疼痛。
manipulate somebody's back = 给某人后背做推拿
⑤ The film is a study of the manipulation of public opinion.
= 那部电影探讨的是对舆论的操纵。
the manipulation of something = 对…的操纵
⑥ She is a manipulative woman who turns her friends against each other.
= 她是个爱摆布人的女人，让朋友们反目成仇。
manipulative = 爱摆布人的
⑦ He is a clever manipulator of the press.
= 他很会操纵新闻界。
a manipulator = 操纵者
拉丁语 manus 是「手」。

mankind
/ˌmænˈkaɪnd/

① The discovery of fire changed the history of mankind.
= 火的发现改变了人类的历史。
the history of mankind = 人类的历史
man 在这里是「人」，不专指男人。
② The doctor said that the new drug could be of great benefit to mankind.
= 那位医生说，那种新药可能造福人类。
benefit to mankind = 造福人类

manky

a manky piece of cheese = 脏兮兮的奶酪

mannequin
/ˈmænɪkɪn/

manner
/ˈmænə(r)/
核心：方式、方法；（待人的）态度、举止；（复数 manners）礼貌、规矩；（复数）风俗习惯。
习语：good manners / bad manners（有礼貌 / 没礼貌）、table manners（餐桌礼仪）、all manner of（各种各样的）、
in a manner of speaking（可以这么说）、as if to the manner born（像天生就会似的）。
形容词 mannered（举止做作的；也作后一成分，如 well-mannered 有礼貌的）并入本条。
① She answered the question in a calm and friendly manner.
= 她以平静友好的方式回答了那个问题。
in a friendly manner = 以友好的方式
比 way 正式。
② The doctor has a kind manner that puts patients at ease.
= 那位医生态度和蔼，让病人安心。
a kind manner = 和蔼的态度
这一支用单数：待人的样子。
③ It is bad manners to talk with your mouth full.
= 嘴里含着东西说话是没礼貌的。
bad manners = 没礼貌
这一支用复数。
④ The children have very good manners and always say thank you.
= 那些孩子很有礼貌，总是说谢谢。
good manners = 有礼貌
⑤ His table manners are terrible; he eats with his elbows on the table.
= 他的餐桌礼仪糟透了，吃饭时把胳膊肘支在桌上。
table manners = 餐桌礼仪
⑥ The shop sells all manner of things, from nails to wedding cakes.
= 那家店什么都卖，从钉子到结婚蛋糕。
all manner of = 各种各样的
这里 manner 是旧义「种类」。
⑦ He is, in a manner of speaking, the father of the modern computer.
= 可以这么说，他是现代计算机之父。
in a manner of speaking = 可以这么说
⑧ She took to life in the palace as if to the manner born.
= 她适应宫里的生活，就像生来如此。
to the manner born = 像天生就会似的
⑨ He is a quiet, well-mannered boy who is liked by all his teachers.
= 他是个文静有礼的男孩，老师们都喜欢他。
well-mannered = 有礼貌的
⑩ The critics found her acting stiff and mannered.
= 评论家觉得她的表演僵硬做作。
mannered = 做作的

manoeuvre
/məˈnuːvə(r)/
核心：熟练而小心的移动、操作；策略、花招；（复数）军事演习。动词：巧妙地移动、操纵；耍手腕。美式拼 maneuver。
习语 room for manoeuvre（回旋余地）。
形容词 manoeuvrable（易操纵的，美式 maneuverable）、名词 manoeuvrability、manoeuvring（耍手腕，美式 maneuvering）并入本条。
① Parking the lorry in the narrow street was a difficult manoeuvre.
= 把卡车停进那条窄街是个很难的动作。
a difficult manoeuvre = 很难的动作
读如 muh-noo-ver。
② The offer was a clever manoeuvre to win the support of the smaller parties.
= 那个提议是争取小党支持的一招妙棋。
a clever manoeuvre = 一招妙棋
③ The army is on manoeuvres in the hills this week.
= 军队这个星期在山里演习。
on manoeuvres = 在演习
这一支用复数。
④ She manoeuvred the car into a tiny space between two vans.
= 她把车巧妙地挪进了两辆货车之间的一个小空位。
manoeuvre a car = 巧妙地挪车
⑤ He manoeuvred his way into the top job within two years.
= 他耍手腕，两年之内就爬上了最高职位。
manoeuvre your way into something = 耍手腕进入…
⑥ The new rules leave the minister very little room for manoeuvre.
= 新规定让部长几乎没有回旋余地。
room for manoeuvre = 回旋余地
⑦ The small boat is light and highly manoeuvrable.
= 那条小船轻便，很好操纵。
manoeuvrable = 易操纵的
⑧ The pilot praised the manoeuvrability of the new plane.
= 飞行员称赞了新飞机的操纵性。
manoeuvrability = 操纵性
⑨ After weeks of political manoeuvring, a new leader was chosen.
= 经过几个星期的政治运作，新领袖选了出来。
political manoeuvring = 政治运作
⑩ The American newspaper reported that the driver tried to maneuver around the truck.
= 那家美国报纸报道说，司机试图绕过那辆卡车。
maneuver = manoeuvre 的美式拼法
⑪ The advert says that the bike is light, maneuverable and easy to park.
= 广告上说那辆车轻便、好操纵、好停放。
maneuverable = manoeuvrable 的美式拼法
⑫ The senator was known for skilful maneuvering behind the scenes.
= 那位参议员以幕后运作的手腕出名。
maneuvering = manoeuvring 的美式拼法
