Falstaffian
核心：（文学）福斯塔夫式的：像莎剧里那个又胖又贪杯、爱吹牛却讨人喜欢的角色。
① A Falstaffian figure at the bar told the same story to four different groups.
= 酒吧里一个福斯塔夫式的人物，把同一个故事对四拨人各讲了一遍。
a Falstaffian figure = 福斯塔夫式的人物
② His Falstaffian appetite survived two diets and one doctor.
= 他那福斯塔夫式的胃口，扛过了两次节食和一个医生。
Falstaffian = 福斯塔夫式的

falter
核心：两支。一是（声音、脚步）发颤、踉跄；二是（信心、经济、需求）动摇、走弱。
形容词 faltering。
① Her voice faltered on the second name and steadied for all the rest.
= 念到第二个名字时她声音发颤，其余的都稳住了。
somebody's voice falters = 某人声音发颤
② Demand faltered in the spring and never quite came back.
= 需求在春天走弱，之后再没完全回来。
falter = 走弱、动摇

faltering
核心：踉跄的、迟疑的、不稳的：多修饰 steps、voice、attempt、recovery。
① The faltering recovery shows up in the figures and not on the street.
= 那场步履蹒跚的复苏，只在数字里看得见，街面上看不出。
a faltering recovery = 步履蹒跚的复苏
② He took a few faltering steps and refused the stick all over again.
= 他踉跄走了几步，又一次拒绝用拐杖。
faltering steps = 蹒跚的脚步

fam
核心：（非正式，年轻人用语）「家人」：拿来称呼要好的朋友，也指自己那一伙人。
① He calls everybody fam and remembers about half of their names.
= 他管谁都叫「家人」，名字大概记得一半。
fam = 铁哥们儿、自家人
② The whole fam turned up and that flat holds six at a push.
= 那一伙人全来了，那间公寓挤挤也就装六个。
the fam = 自己那一伙人

fame
核心：名声、名气：出名这件事，不可数。
常见搭配 rise to fame、fame and fortune。形容词 famed。
① Her rise to fame took eleven years and one very short film.
= 她成名花了十一年，外加一部极短的片子。
rise to fame = 成名
② Fame and fortune arrived together and left at quite different times.
= 名与利是一起来的，走的时候却不同时。
fame and fortune = 名与利

famed
核心：（书面）著名的、以……闻名的：多说 be famed for something，比 famous 书面。
① The town is famed for its cheese and known locally for its floods.
= 那个镇以奶酪闻名，本地人记得的却是它的水患。
be famed for something = 以某物闻名
② A famed collection of maps sits in a room that nobody can find.
= 一批著名的地图藏在一个谁也找不着的房间里。
famed = 著名的

familial
核心：（正式、医学）家族的、家庭的：多说 familial ties、a familial disease。
① Familial ties decide who gets the shop and the law decides everything else.
= 谁接手那家店由家族关系定，其余一切由法律定。
familial ties = 家族纽带
② The condition is familial and skips about one generation in three.
= 那种病是家族遗传的，大约每三代跳过一代。
a familial disease = 家族遗传病

familiar
核心：三支。一是熟悉的、常见的：a familiar face；
二是 be familiar with something，对某事门儿清；
三是（贬）过分自来熟的：be too familiar。名词 familiarity。
① A familiar face at the door changes what the dog does.
= 门口出现一张熟脸，狗的反应就不一样。
a familiar face = 熟面孔
② She is familiar with the paperwork and hates every page of it.
= 那些文书她门儿清，每一页都讨厌。
be familiar with something = 熟悉某事
③ He is too familiar with the customers and the manager has noticed.
= 他跟顾客太自来熟，经理已经注意到了。
be too familiar = 过分自来熟
副词 familiarly：两支。一是熟稔地、亲昵地：greet somebody familiarly； 二是「俗称」：be familiarly known as something。
④ He greeted the porter familiarly and got the good room.
= 他跟门房打招呼很熟络，拿到了那间好房。
greet somebody familiarly = 亲昵地招呼某人
⑤ The building is familiarly known as the pepper pot.
= 那栋楼俗称「胡椒罐」。
be familiarly known as something = 俗称某名
构词：familiar 加 -ly

familiarity
核心：两支。一是熟悉、通晓：familiarity with something；二是（贬）过分随便。
谚语 familiarity breeds contempt，亲近生轻慢。
① Familiarity with the route matters more than speed on that road.
= 在那条路上，熟不熟路比开得快要紧。
familiarity with something = 对某事的熟悉
② Familiarity breeds contempt, and it breeds shortcuts as well.
= 亲近生轻慢，也生出各种偷懒的走法。
familiarity breeds contempt = 亲近生轻慢

familiarization
核心：（正式）熟悉过程、上手培训：让人先把环境或设备摸熟。
英式也拼 familiarisation。不可数。
① A day of familiarization saves a week of mistakes on the line.
= 一天的上手培训，能省下产线上一星期的差错。
familiarization = 熟悉、上手
② The familiarization visit lasts an hour and covers three floors.
= 那趟熟悉环境的参观一个钟头，走完三层楼。
a familiarization visit = 熟悉环境的参观
构词：familiarize 加 -ation

familiarize
核心：（正式）使熟悉：最常用的是 familiarize yourself with something，
先把某事摸熟。英式也拼 familiarise。
① Familiarize yourself with the exits before you unpack anything.
= 东西还没打开，先把出口摸清楚。
familiarize yourself with something = 熟悉某事
② The induction familiarizes new staff with a building they will never use.
= 那场入职培训让新员工熟悉一栋他们根本用不上的楼。
familiarize somebody with something = 让某人熟悉某事

family
核心：三支。一是家庭、家人：可当单数也可当复数；二是家族、门第；
三是（生物）科：the cat family。
习语：run in the family 是家里遗传的，start a family 是开始生儿育女，
a family tree 是家谱。
① The family sits down at seven and argues until the plates go cold.
= 一家人七点坐下吃饭，争到盘子都凉了。
a family = 家庭
② Bad knees run in the family and so does the habit of walking everywhere.
= 膝盖不好是家里遗传的，凡事靠走路也是。
run in the family = 家里遗传的
③ They started a family in a flat with one window and no lift.
= 他们在一间只有一扇窗、没有电梯的公寓里生了孩子。
start a family = 开始生儿育女
④ The family tree on the wall stops in 1911 and picks up again in 1946.
= 墙上那张家谱到 1911 年就断了，1946 年才接上。
a family tree = 家谱
⑤ Lions and domestic cats are in the same family and not the same genus.
= 狮子和家猫同科，却不同属。
the cat family = 猫科
⑥ The family doctor has known three generations of them and still gets the family name wrong.
= 那位家庭医生认识他们三代人，姓氏还是叫错。
a family doctor = 家庭医生
a family name = 姓氏
⑦ Family planning services moved into the family room at the health centre.
= 计划生育服务搬进了保健中心的家庭活动室。
family planning = 计划生育
a family room = 家庭活动室
⑧ The hotel is family-friendly, serves food family style and calls that a policy.
= 那家旅馆适合带孩子，饭菜合盘上桌，还管这叫政策。
family-friendly = 适合全家的
family style = 合盘上桌的（美式）
⑨ Family leave is generous on paper and taken by about one man in nine.
= 家庭假纸面上很慷慨，九个男的里大约一个会休。
family leave = 家庭假
⑩ The family court sits on Tuesdays and the queue starts forming at eight.
= 家事法庭周二开庭，八点就开始排队。
a family court = 家事法庭
⑪ He is a family man who works every weekend and says so proudly.
= 他自称顾家的人，周末却都在上班，还说得挺自豪。
a family man = 顾家的男人
⑫ Family values in that speech meant one thing and in the manifesto another.
= 那场讲话里的家庭价值观，跟纲领里的不是一回事。
family values = 家庭价值观
⑬ Family medicine and family practice are one specialty with two names.
= 全科医学与家庭医疗，是同一个专科的两个叫法。
family medicine = 全科医学
family practice = 家庭医疗
a family practitioner = 全科医生

famine
核心：饥荒：大范围的粮食短缺，可数不可数都行。

feast or famine = 忽多忽少、旱涝不均

famished

famous

famously

fan

= 那篇报道煽风点火，报社六月登了道歉。

= 天黑之前，搜寻的人在田野上散开。

fanatic

fanatical

fanatical support = 狂热的支持
副词 fanatically：狂热地、极端认真地：多修饰 clean、tidy、loyal、devoted。

= 那间厨房干净得吓人，隔壁办公室可不是。

fanaticism

fancier
