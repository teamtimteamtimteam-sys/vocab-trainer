forestry
核心：三支。一是林业：work in forestry；二是林学这门学问；
三是（英）负责造林的那个部门。
① Forestry pays badly and nobody in it says they would change jobs.
= 林业挣得少，干这行的却没人说想换工作。
work in forestry = 干林业
② She read forestry and spent two of the four years outdoors.
= 她读林学，四年里有两年在野外。
read forestry = 读林学
③ Forestry planted this whole ridge in a single winter.
= 林业部门一个冬天就把整道山脊种满了。
forestry plants something = 林业部门造林

foretaste
核心：三支。一是（好事或坏事的）先兆、预先体验：a foretaste of things to come；
二是提前尝到的那一点；三是先发出来的一份样品。
① Tuesday was a foretaste of things to come and nobody read it that way.
= 周二那天是后来一切的先兆，当时没人这么看。
a foretaste of things to come = 后事的先兆
② The cold snap gave us a foretaste of a winter that never arrived.
= 那阵寒潮让我们提前尝到一个后来并未到来的冬天。
a foretaste of something = 对某事的预先体验
③ One chapter was published as a foretaste of the whole book.
= 先发了一章，作为整本书的尝鲜。
a foretaste = 尝鲜的一份

foretell
核心：三支（正式或书面）。一是预言、预示：foretell the future；
二是（迹象）预告某事；三是多用于否定：impossible to foretell。
注意：三态是 foretell / foretold / foretold。
① Nobody can foretell the future and everybody in that room tried.
= 没人能预言未来，那屋里的人个个都试了。
foretell the future = 预言未来
② The cracks foretold the collapse by a good six months.
= 那些裂缝提前整整六个月预示了坍塌。
foretold the collapse = 预示了坍塌
③ The outcome was impossible to foretell from the first three rounds.
= 从头三个回合无从预料结果。
impossible to foretell = 无从预料

forethought
核心：三支。一是事先的考虑、周全的谋划：with a little forethought；
二是（法律）预谋的成分；三是替旁人着想的那份准备。
① With a little forethought the whole trip would have cost half as much.
= 稍作谋划，整趟行程本可以只花一半的钱。
with a little forethought = 稍作谋划
② The judge found no forethought and reduced the sentence accordingly.
= 法官认定并无预谋，据此减了刑。
find no forethought = 认定并无预谋
③ Somebody had the forethought to leave a torch by the door.
= 有人想得周到，在门边放了一支手电。
have the forethought to do something = 想得周到而做某事

forever
核心：四支。一是永远：last forever；二是（口语）好久好久：take forever；
三是老是、不停地：be forever doing something；四是分写的 for ever，用法相同。
① Nothing here lasts forever and the sign at the gate says otherwise.
= 这里没有什么能永远存在，门口的牌子却说不然。
last forever = 永远持续
② The kettle takes forever and the queue behind it never shortens.
= 那把水壶烧得没完，后面排的队一直不见短。
take forever = 花好久好久
③ He is forever losing gloves and never loses anything else.
= 他老是丢手套，别的东西一件不丢。
be forever doing something = 老是做某事
④ They said goodbye at the gate and meant it for ever this time.
= 他们在门口道别，这一回是真的永别。
for ever = 永远

forewarn
核心：三支。一是预先警告：forewarn somebody of something；
二是提前知会；三是分词 forewarned 常单独作形容词用。
① The office forewarned everyone of the noise and then started on a Sunday.
= 办公室提前通知了所有人会有噪音，最后还是挑了周日动工。
forewarn somebody of something = 预先警告某人某事
② She was forewarned by a friend and still arrived without an umbrella.
= 有朋友提前提醒过她，她还是没带伞就来了。
be forewarned = 事先得到提醒
③ Nobody forewarned the neighbours and the letters started on Monday.
= 没人事先知会邻居，投诉信周一就开始了。
forewarn the neighbours = 事先知会邻居

forewarning
核心：三支。一是预先的警告、预兆：without forewarning；
二是提前通知这件事；三是（复数）一连串征兆。
① The gate closed without forewarning and two people were still inside.
= 门毫无预警地关上，里头还有两个人。
without forewarning = 毫无预警地
② A week's forewarning would have been enough to move everything out.
= 提前一周通知，就足够把东西全搬走。
a week's forewarning = 提前一周的通知
③ There were forewarnings all spring and the file stayed shut.
= 整个春天都有征兆，那份卷宗一直没打开。
forewarnings = 一连串征兆

forewoman
核心：三支。一是女工头、女领班；二是陪审团的女团长；
三是车间里带班的女班长。
① The forewoman keeps the line running and never raises her voice.
= 那位女领班让生产线一直转着，从不提高嗓门。
a forewoman = 女领班
② The forewoman of the jury asked for the exhibits to be brought back.
= 陪审团女团长要求把证物再拿回来。
the forewoman of the jury = 陪审团女团长
③ She was forewoman of that shift for eleven years running.
= 她连着十一年当那个班次的女班长。
be forewoman of a shift = 当某班次的女班长

foreword
核心：三支。一是（书的）前言，多由他人执笔：write the foreword；
二是排在正文之前那几页；三是与作者自序相区别的那一篇。
① He wrote the foreword in an afternoon and it is the best part of the book.
= 那篇前言他一下午就写完了，却是全书最好的部分。
write the foreword = 写前言
② The foreword runs to nine pages and the book itself to ninety.
= 前言有九页，正文才九十页。
a nine-page foreword = 九页的前言
③ Nobody reads the foreword and every edition keeps it.
= 没人看前言，每一版却都留着。
keep the foreword = 保留前言

forfeit
核心：三支。一是（因过错而）丧失、被没收：forfeit your deposit；
二是罚金、被没收的东西；三是游戏里输了要受的罚。
① You forfeit your deposit if the keys come back late.
= 钥匙晚还，押金就没了。
forfeit your deposit = 丧失押金
② The forfeit for a late entry is the entry fee itself.
= 迟报名的罚金，就是报名费本身。
a forfeit = 罚金
③ Whoever laughs first pays a forfeit and the game starts again.
= 谁先笑谁受罚，游戏重来。
pay a forfeit = 受罚

forfeiture
核心：三支（法律）。一是财产、权利的丧失与没收：forfeiture of a licence；
二是没收令；三是被没收的那一部分。
① Forfeiture of the licence follows a second offence automatically.
= 第二次违规，执照自动被吊销。
forfeiture of a licence = 执照被吊销
② The court ordered forfeiture of the vehicle and a fine on top of it.
= 法院判决没收车辆，另加罚款。
order forfeiture = 判决没收
③ Forfeiture of the deposit was written into the third clause.
= 没收押金写在第三条里。
forfeiture of a deposit = 没收押金

forfend
核心：三支（旧、书面）。一是阻止、挡住；二是习语 heaven forfend，但愿别；
三是（更旧）护佑某人免于某事。
① Two locks forfend nothing if the window stays open all night.
= 窗户整夜开着，两把锁也挡不住什么。
forfend something = 阻挡某事
② Heaven forfend that anybody should ask who paid for it.
= 但愿别有人问这钱是谁出的。
heaven forfend = 但愿别
③ The old charm was hung there to forfend the house from fire.
= 那个旧护符挂在那儿，是为了保这屋子不着火。
forfend somebody from something = 护佑某人免于某事

forgather
核心：三支（正式，与 foregather 同义）。一是聚集、会合；
二是碰头开会；三是（人群）为某事聚起来。
① Old colleagues forgather in that pub on the first Friday of the month.
= 老同事每月第一个星期五在那家酒馆聚。
forgather in a place = 在某处相聚
② They forgathered at the bridge and waited for a boat that never came.
= 他们在桥头会合，等一条始终没来的船。
forgather at a place = 在某处会合
③ Fifty people forgathered for a lecture that had been cancelled that morning.
= 五十个人聚来听一场当天上午已经取消的讲座。
forgather for something = 为某事聚集

forge
核心：四支。一是（金属）锻造：forge iron；二是伪造：forge a signature；
三是缔结、打造（关系）：forge a partnership；四是锻炉、铁匠铺。
习语 forge ahead 大步前进、突飞猛进。
① They forge iron here the way it was done two hundred years ago.
= 这里锻铁的法子跟两百年前一样。
forge iron = 锻铁
② He forged a signature that fooled the bank and not his sister.
= 他伪造的签名骗过了银行，没骗过他姐姐。
forge a signature = 伪造签名
③ The two schools forged a partnership that outlasted both heads.
= 两所学校缔结的伙伴关系，比两位校长都长命。
forge a partnership = 缔结伙伴关系
④ The forge is cold by four and lit again before six.
= 锻炉四点前就凉了，不到六点又生起来。
a forge = 锻炉、铁匠铺
⑤ Sales forged ahead in a year when everybody else lost money.
= 在别人都亏钱的一年里，销售却大步前进。
forge ahead = 大步前进

forger
核心：三支。一是伪造文件、签名、画作的人：a skilled forger；
二是造伪币的人；三是（旧）打铁的锻工。
① A skilled forger works from the back of the paper forward.
= 高明的伪造者从纸背往正面做。
a skilled forger = 高明的伪造者
② The forger was caught by a watermark he could not have known about.
= 那个伪造者栽在一个他不可能知道的水印上。
catch a forger = 抓住伪造者
③ Two forgers ran the whole thing from a room above a launderette.
= 两个造假者在洗衣店楼上的一间屋子里操办了整件事。
a forger = 造假者

forgery
核心：三支。一是伪造品、赝品：a clumsy forgery；
二是伪造这个行为与罪名：be charged with forgery；三是（复数）一批赝品。
① A clumsy forgery hung in that hall for forty years.
= 一幅拙劣的赝品在那个厅里挂了四十年。
a clumsy forgery = 拙劣的赝品
② He was charged with forgery and acquitted on the third count only.
= 他被控伪造罪，只有第三项罪名被判无罪。
be charged with forgery = 被控伪造罪
③ The museum found nine forgeries in its own store room.
= 博物馆在自家库房里查出九件赝品。
find forgeries = 查出赝品

forget
核心：五支。一是忘记：forget a name；二是忘带、忘做：forget your keys；
三是不再想着、放下：forget about something；四是口语的 forget it，算了；
五是（感情上）把某人忘掉。
注意：三态是 forget / forgot / forgotten。
① She forgot his name twice in one conversation and used it right afterwards.
= 一次谈话里她两次忘了他的名字，之后又叫对了。
forget a name = 忘了名字
② He forgot his keys and sat on the step until half past six.
= 他忘了带钥匙，在台阶上坐到六点半。
forget your keys = 忘带钥匙
forget to do something = 忘了做某事
③ Forget about the deadline and do the part you can actually finish.
= 别想截止日期了，先做你真做得完的那部分。
forget about something = 别再想某事
④ Forget it, the shop shut an hour before we left the house.
= 算了，我们出门前一小时那家店就关了。
forget it = 算了
⑤ He has forgotten her and says so more often than necessary.
= 他已经把她忘了，而且说得比必要的次数多。
have forgotten somebody = 已经忘掉某人

forgetful
核心：三支。一是健忘的：get forgetful；二是一时没顾上的：forgetful of the time；
三是（书面）把某人某事抛在脑后的。副词 forgetfully 并入本条。
① He has got forgetful in the last year and writes everything on his hand.
= 这一年他变得健忘，什么都写在手上。
get forgetful = 变得健忘
② Forgetful of the time, she read until the library turned the lights off.
= 她读得忘了时间，直到图书馆熄灯。
forgetful of the time = 忘了时间
③ The town is forgetful of the people who paid for its park.
= 那座城把出钱修公园的人抛在了脑后。
be forgetful of somebody = 把某人忘在脑后
副词 forgetfully：心不在焉地、忘事地。
④ She forgetfully left the tap running and went out for an hour.
= 她一时忘事，没关水龙头就出门一小时。
forgetfully leave something = 忘事地留下某物

forgetfulness

① They put the missed appointment down to forgetfulness and rebooked it.

an hour of forgetfulness = 一小时的忘忧

forgettable
核心：三支。一是容易忘掉的、平淡无奇的：a forgettable film；
二是（经历）不值一记的；三是（人、表现）没留下印象的。
① A forgettable film ran for a fortnight and left no reviews behind.

③ He gave a forgettable speech and stayed in the job for years.

forgivable

二是情有可原的；三是（债务）可免除的。
① A forgivable mistake becomes something else the third time round.
= 可以原谅的错误，到第三次就变了性质。

② It is forgivable to be late for a train nobody announced.

③ The debt was forgivable and the paperwork made it impossible.

forgive
核心：四支。一是原谅：forgive somebody for something；二是免除（债务）：forgive a debt；

① She forgave him for the letter and never mentioned it again.

③ Forgive me, but the second figure cannot possibly be right.

④ They agreed to forgive and forget and then sat at separate tables.

⑤ He was forgiven long before he thought to ask.

forgiveness

forgiving

forgo
