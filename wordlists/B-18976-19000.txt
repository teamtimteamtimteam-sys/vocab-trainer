repugnance
/rɪˈpʌɡnəns/
核心：（正式）厌恶、反感、嫌恶：feel repugnance at、towards。不可数。形容词 repugnant 是令人厌恶的：be repugnant to。并入本条。
① She felt a deep repugnance at the thought of eating meat.
= 一想到吃肉，她就深感厌恶。
feel repugnance at something = 对某事感到厌恶
repugnance = 厌恶
② The idea of hurting an animal was repugnant to him.
= 伤害动物的念头让他反感。
be repugnant to somebody = 令某人反感
repugnant = 令人厌恶的

repulse
/rɪˈpʌls/
核心：及物。一是（正式）击退（进攻）；二是使厌恶、使反感，多用被动；三是拒绝（帮助、好意）。
名词 repulsion 是厌恶、反感，（物理）斥力，不可数；形容词 repulsive 是令人厌恶的、恶心的，副词 repulsively。并入本条。
① The soldiers repulsed the attack and held the town for another week.
= 士兵们击退了进攻，又守了那座城一个星期。
repulse an attack = 击退进攻
repulse = 击退
② She was repulsed by the smell of the rotting food in the bin.
= 垃圾桶里腐烂食物的气味让她恶心。
be repulsed by something = 对某物感到厌恶
③ He felt a wave of repulsion when he saw the injured animal.
= 看见那只受伤的动物，他一阵反胃。
a wave of repulsion = 一阵厌恶
repulsion = 厌恶
④ The food in the old café was so repulsive that we left without eating.
= 那家老咖啡馆的饭菜太恶心，我们没吃就走了。
repulsive = 令人厌恶的
⑤ The fat rat in the kitchen was repulsively large.
= 厨房里那只肥老鼠大得令人作呕。
repulsively = 令人厌恶地

require
/rɪˈkwaɪə(r)/
核心：及物。一是需要：require something、require doing；二是（正式）要求、规定：require somebody to do、be required to do、require that。
