matter
/ˈmætə(r)/
核心：事情、问题、情况；the matter（麻烦、毛病）；物质；（书、印刷品的）内容、材料。动词：要紧、有关系（多用于否定、疑问）。
习语：what is the matter（怎么了）、it does not matter（没关系）、no matter（不管、无论）、as a matter of fact（事实上、其实）、
a matter of（…的问题；大约）、for that matter（就此而言、说到这个）、as a matter of course（理所当然地、照例）、
to make matters worse（更糟的是）、a matter of life and death（生死攸关的事）、a matter of opinion（见仁见智的事）、
no laughing matter（不是闹着玩的）。
① I need to speak to you about a private matter before the others arrive.
= 趁别人还没来，我有件私事要跟你谈。
a private matter = 私事
② The police are looking into the matter of the missing money from the club.
= 警方正在调查俱乐部钱款失踪一事。
look into the matter = 调查这件事
③ What is the matter? You look as if you have been crying.
= 怎么了？你看着像哭过。
what is the matter = 怎么了
④ There is something the matter with the engine; it makes a strange noise on hills.
= 发动机出了点毛病，一上坡就有怪声。
something the matter with = …出了毛病
⑤ It does not matter if you are a few minutes late; we never start on time.
= 你晚几分钟没关系，我们从来不准点开始。
it does not matter = 没关系
⑥ Money matters less to her than time with her children.
= 对她来说，钱不如陪孩子的时间重要。
matter to somebody = 对某人重要
⑦ No matter how hard he tried, he could not open the door.
= 不管他怎么使劲，就是打不开那扇门。
no matter how = 不管怎么
no matter what / who / where 同此。
⑧ As a matter of fact, I have never been to Paris, though I teach French.
= 其实，我虽然教法语，却从没去过巴黎。
as a matter of fact = 事实上、其实
⑨ Learning to swim is only a matter of practice.
= 学游泳只是个练不练的问题。
a matter of = …的问题
⑩ The ambulance arrived in a matter of minutes.
= 救护车几分钟之内就到了。
in a matter of minutes = 几分钟之内
⑪ I do not like the film, or the book for that matter.
= 我不喜欢那部电影，说到这个，书我也不喜欢。
for that matter = 就此而言
⑫ The nurses check every patient's temperature as a matter of course.
= 护士照例给每位病人量体温。
as a matter of course = 照例、理所当然地
a matter of course = 理所当然的事
⑬ The car broke down and, to make matters worse, it began to snow.
= 车抛了锚，更糟的是，下起雪来了。
to make matters worse = 更糟的是
⑭ Whether the film is good is a matter of opinion.
= 那部电影好不好，见仁见智。
a matter of opinion = 见仁见智的事
⑮ Getting the child to hospital was a matter of life and death.
= 把孩子送到医院是生死攸关的事。
a matter of life and death = 生死攸关的事
⑯ Scientists study how matter behaves at very low temperatures.
= 科学家研究物质在极低温度下的表现。
matter = 物质
这一支不可数，物理学用语。
⑰ The library has a large collection of printed matter about the town.
= 那座图书馆有大量关于这座城的印刷资料。
printed matter = 印刷品
⑱ She told us in a matter-of-fact voice that she had lost her job.
= 她用平平淡淡的口气告诉我们她丢了工作。
matter-of-fact = 就事论事的、不动感情的
⑲ "The roof is leaking again," he said matter-of-factly.
= 他若无其事地说：“屋顶又漏了。”
matter-of-factly = 若无其事地
⑳ Losing your passport abroad is no laughing matter.
= 在国外丢了护照可不是闹着玩的。
no laughing matter = 不是闹着玩的
