fruitarian
核心：三支。一是只吃水果的人；二是形容词性，这种饮食的；
三是这套主张本身。
① A fruitarian eats nothing that required killing the plant.
= 只吃水果的人，凡是要弄死植物才能得到的东西都不吃。
a fruitarian = 果食者
② A fruitarian diet leaves out most of what a shop sells.
= 果食的路子，把店里卖的东西大半都排除了。
a fruitarian diet = 果食的饮食
③ Two fruitarians in that house share a kitchen with four meat eaters.
= 那所房子里两个果食者，跟四个吃肉的共用厨房。
fruitarians = 果食者

fruiterer
核心：三支（英，略旧）。一是水果商、水果店老板；二是那家水果店；
三是招牌上与蔬果店并列的那个词。
① The fruiterer on that corner has been there longer than the bank.
= 那个街角的水果商，比银行开得还久。
a fruiterer = 水果商
② She buys from the fruiterer and blames the supermarket for the price.
= 她在水果店买，却怪超市把价钱抬起来。
buy from the fruiterer = 在水果店买
③ The fruiterer's shuts at one on Wednesdays and always has.
= 那家水果店周三一点关门，一向如此。
the fruiterer's = 水果店

fruitful
核心：三支。一是富有成果的：a fruitful meeting；二是（土地）肥沃多产的；
三是（合作）有收获的。副词 fruitfully 并入本条。
① A fruitful meeting is one that ends with somebody having to do something.
= 有成果的会，是散会时有人得去做事的那种。
a fruitful meeting = 富有成果的会
② The ground is fruitful in that corner and stony everywhere else.
= 那个角落的地很肥，别处全是石头。
fruitful ground = 肥沃的土地
③ Nine fruitful years ended when the funding moved to another city.
= 九年的丰硕合作，在经费转去另一座城之后结束。
fruitful years = 硕果累累的年月
副词 fruitfully：卓有成效地。
④ They worked fruitfully together and never once agreed about method.
= 他们合作得卓有成效，方法上一次都没谈拢过。
work fruitfully = 卓有成效地合作

fruitfulness
核心：三支。一是多产、丰饶：the fruitfulness of the soil；
二是（工作、合作的）成效；三是（书面）结实累累的状态。
① The fruitfulness of that soil is the only thing keeping the farm going.
= 那片土的肥沃，是农场撑下去的唯一依靠。
the fruitfulness of the soil = 土壤的肥沃
② The fruitfulness of the partnership showed up four years later.
= 那段合作的成效，四年之后才显出来。
the fruitfulness of a partnership = 合作的成效
③ Autumn there is all fruitfulness and nobody has time to enjoy it.
= 那里的秋天满是丰饶，却没人有空享受。
fruitfulness = 丰饶

fruitiness
核心：三支。一是（酒、茶的）果味：the fruitiness of a wine；
二是气味里的果香；三是（嗓音）浑厚圆润。
① The fruitiness of that wine hides how much alcohol is in it.
= 那款酒的果味，把它的酒精度盖住了。
the fruitiness of a wine = 酒的果味
② A little fruitiness comes through after the tea has cooled.
= 茶凉下来之后，会透出一点果香。
fruitiness = 果香
③ The fruitiness of his voice carries to the back without a microphone.
= 他嗓音浑厚圆润，不用话筒也传到最后一排。
the fruitiness of a voice = 嗓音的圆润

fruition
核心：三支（正式）。一是（计划）实现、成真：come to fruition；
二是最终的成果；三是（旧）果实成熟。
① The plan came to fruition in a year when nobody was watching.
= 那个计划在一个没人留意的年份里实现了。
come to fruition = 得以实现
② Fruition took eleven years and cost two people their marriages.
= 实现用了十一年，代价是两个人的婚姻。
fruition = 实现、成事
③ Nothing that started in that room ever reached fruition.
= 在那间屋子里开的头，没有一件走到最后。
reach fruition = 走到实现的一步

fruitless
核心：三支。一是徒劳无功的：a fruitless search；二是（努力）白费的；
三是（字面，旧）不结果的。副词 fruitlessly 并入本条。
① A fruitless search of the loft took the whole of a Saturday.
= 在阁楼里徒劳地翻了一遍，搭进去整个周六。
a fruitless search = 徒劳的搜寻
② Three fruitless meetings later, somebody rang the right person.
= 三场毫无结果的会之后，终于有人打给了对的人。
fruitless meetings = 毫无结果的会
③ The old tree is fruitless and the birds still use it.
= 那棵老树不结果了，鸟照样用它。
be fruitless = 不结果
副词 fruitlessly：徒劳地。
④ She argued fruitlessly for an hour and then wrote it all down.
= 她徒劳地争了一小时，接着把这些全写了下来。
argue fruitlessly = 徒劳地争辩

③ He told a fruity joke and the table went quiet in two stages.

frustrate

frustrated
