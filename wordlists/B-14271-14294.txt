menagerie
核心：（私人养的或巡回展出的）一群野生动物、动物园；（谑）五花八门的一群。
① The king kept a menagerie of lions, bears and monkeys at the palace.
= 国王在宫里养了一群狮子、熊和猴子。
a menagerie of animals = 一群动物
② Their flat is a menagerie of cats, rabbits and a very noisy parrot.
= 他们的公寓简直是个动物园：猫、兔子，外加一只吵得要命的鹦鹉。
a menagerie = 一群五花八门的动物

mend
核心：修理、修补；（英）缝补；（伤、骨头）愈合；（关系）修复。名词 mender（修理者，多作后一成分）并入本条。
习语：on the mend（在好转、在康复）、mend your ways（改过自新）、mend fences（修复关系、言归于好）。
① He mended the broken chair with glue and two screws.
= 他用胶水和两颗螺丝修好了那把坏椅子。
mend a chair = 修椅子
英式口语常用。
② She sat by the fire mending a hole in her son's jumper.
= 她坐在炉火边，给儿子的套头衫补一个洞。
mend a hole = 补洞
③ The bone took six weeks to mend.
= 那根骨头六个星期才长好。
the bone mends = 骨头愈合
④ She has been very ill, but she is on the mend now.
= 她病得很重，不过现在正在好转。
on the mend = 在好转、在康复
⑤ The judge told the young man to mend his ways or go to prison.
= 法官告诉那个年轻人，要么改过自新，要么坐牢。
mend your ways = 改过自新
⑥ The minister flew to Paris to mend fences after the trade dispute.
= 贸易争端之后，部长飞往巴黎修复关系。
mend fences = 修复关系
⑦ The road mender filled the holes with stones and tar.
= 修路工用石子和沥青把坑填上了。
a road mender = 修路工
美式修东西多说 fix 或 repair。

menfolk

the menfolk carry = 男人们搬

menial

meningitis

① The student was rushed to hospital with suspected meningitis.

a vaccine against meningitis = 预防脑膜炎的疫苗

Mennonite

① The Mennonites of the valley still farm with horses.
= 那条山谷里的门诺派教徒还在用马耕田。

a Mennonite community = 门诺派社区

menopause

menorah

menstruate

menswear

mental
核心：思想的、精神的、心理的；脑力的；（英，俚）疯了的。副词 mentally 并入本条。
搭配：mental health（心理健康）、mental illness（精神疾病）、mental age（智力年龄）、mental arithmetic（心算）、
make a mental note（记在心里）。
① The job puts a great mental strain on the nurses.
= 那份工作给护士们带来很大的精神压力。
mental strain = 精神压力
② Regular exercise is good for your mental health.
= 经常锻炼对心理健康有好处。
mental health = 心理健康
③ One person in four will suffer from a mental illness at some time.
= 每四个人里就有一个会在某个时候患上精神疾病。
a mental illness = 精神疾病
④ I made a mental note to buy milk on the way home.
= 我在心里记下，回家路上要买牛奶。
make a mental note = 记在心里
⑤ She is good at mental arithmetic and never uses a calculator.
= 她心算很好，从来不用计算器。
mental arithmetic = 心算
⑥ The tests showed that the boy had a mental age of twelve.
= 测试表明那个男孩的智力年龄是十二岁。
a mental age = 智力年龄
⑦ By the end of the week, I was physically and mentally exhausted.
= 到了周末，我身心俱疲。
mentally exhausted = 精神上疲惫不堪
⑧ You must be mental to go swimming in this weather.
= 这种天气去游泳，你准是疯了。
be mental = （英，俚）疯了
这一支拿精神疾病开玩笑，容易冒犯人。

a village mentality = 小村子的心态

mention
核心：提到、说起；名词：提及。

now you mention it（你这么一说）。
① She did not mention her new job when we spoke on the phone.
= 我们通电话时，她没提她的新工作。
mention something = 提到…
② He mentioned that he was thinking of moving abroad.
= 他提到他在考虑搬到国外去。
mention that… = 提到…
③ The trip was expensive, not to mention tiring.
= 那趟旅行很贵，更不用说还累人。
not to mention = 更不用说
④ "Thank you for all your help." "Don't mention it."
= “谢谢你帮了这么多忙。”“不客气。”

= 当地报纸上只字未提那起事故。
no mention of something = 只字未提…
⑥ The report makes mention of several earlier studies.
= 那份报告提及了此前的几项研究。
make mention of = 提及
⑦ The dog runs to the door at the mention of a walk.
= 一提到散步，那条狗就往门口跑。
at the mention of = 一提到
⑧ Now you mention it, I did see a strange car outside last night.
= 你这么一说，我昨晚确实看见外面停着一辆陌生的车。
now you mention it = 你这么一说

mercantilism = 重商主义
