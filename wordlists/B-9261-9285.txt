forecast
核心：四支。一是天气预报：the weather forecast；二是（销售、经济等）预测：a sales forecast；
三是动词，预报、预测；四是（复数）各方给出的预测数字。
注意：过去式与过去分词可作 forecast，也可作 forecasted。
① The weather forecast promised sun and the whole village believed it.
= 天气预报说有太阳，全村都信了。
the weather forecast = 天气预报
② The sales forecast was written in March and abandoned in May.
= 销售预测三月写的，五月就作废了。
a sales forecast = 销售预测
③ Nobody forecast the closure and three people saw it coming anyway.
= 没人预测到停业，倒有三个人早看出来了。
forecast something = 预测某事
④ Both forecasts put the number near nine hundred and both were low.
= 两份预测都把数字放在九百上下，两份都偏低。
forecasts put something at = 预测把某数定在

forecaster
核心：三支。一是天气预报员：a weather forecaster；
二是（经济、市场的）预测人员；三是电视上出镜做预报的那个人。
① The weather forecaster apologised on air for the second time that week.
= 天气预报员那一周里第二次在节目上道歉。
a weather forecaster = 天气预报员
② Every forecaster in the city missed the drop by a wide margin.
= 全城的预测人员都把跌幅估偏了一大截。
a forecaster misses something = 预测人员估错
③ She trained as a forecaster and now reads the news instead.
= 她学的是预报，现在改播新闻了。
train as a forecaster = 受训当预报员

forecastle
核心：三支（航海）。一是艏楼，船首上层那部分：the forecastle deck；
二是旧时水手住的前舱；三是口语与旧日志里缩读作 fo'c'sle。
① The forecastle deck takes every wave once the sea gets up.
= 海一起浪，艏楼甲板就浪浪不落。
the forecastle deck = 艏楼甲板
② Twelve men slept in the forecastle and not one of them had a window.
= 十二个人睡在前舱，谁也没有窗户。
sleep in the forecastle = 睡在前舱
③ The mate came up from the forecastle with a lamp in one hand.
= 大副一手提着灯，从艏楼前舱上来。
come up from the fo'c'sle = 从前舱上来

foreclose
核心：三支。一是（银行）取消抵押品赎回权、收走房产：foreclose on a mortgage；
二是（正式）堵死某种可能：foreclose an option；三是使人再无选择余地。
① The bank foreclosed on the mortgage in the second year of the arrears.
= 欠款到第二年，银行就收走了抵押的房产。
foreclose on a mortgage = 收走抵押房产
② Signing that clause forecloses the option of going to court later.
= 签下那一条，日后打官司这条路就堵死了。
foreclose an option = 堵死一个选项
③ Nothing in the letter forecloses a second application.
= 信里没有一句话堵死再申请一次的可能。
foreclose something = 排除某种可能

foreclosure
核心：三支。一是取消抵押赎回权、法拍：a foreclosure notice；
二是被法拍的那处房产；三是（正式）某种可能性的关闭。
① A foreclosure notice arrived on a Tuesday and she read it standing up.
= 法拍通知周二寄到，她站着看完了。
a foreclosure notice = 法拍通知
② Half the street was foreclosures and the gardens went first.
= 半条街都是法拍房，最先荒掉的是院子。
a foreclosure = 法拍房
③ The foreclosure of that route left them one bad choice and no time.
= 那条路被堵死之后，他们只剩一个糟糕的选择，也没时间了。
the foreclosure of an option = 一个选项的关闭

forecourt
核心：三支。一是建筑物前的前庭、前院：the forecourt of a station；
二是加油站的加油区；三是车行摆展车的场地。
① The forecourt of the station fills with taxis at ten past every hour.

the forecourt of a station = 车站前庭
② He waited on the forecourt while the pump caught up with the meter.
= 他在加油区等着，看油泵追上计价表。
the forecourt of a garage = 加油站的加油区
③ Six cars sat on the forecourt with the prices in the windscreens.
= 六辆车摆在展车场上，价钱贴在挡风玻璃上。
cars on the forecourt = 展车场上的车

foredoomed
核心：三支（正式）。一是注定失败的：be foredoomed to failure；
二是（计划）一开头就没戏的；三是（人）命该如此的。
① The scheme was foredoomed to failure and took four years to fail.
= 那个方案注定失败，却用了四年才失败。
be foredoomed to failure = 注定失败
② A foredoomed attempt still taught them where the ice gives way.
= 一次注定不成的尝试，还是让他们摸清了冰在哪里会塌。
a foredoomed attempt = 注定不成的尝试
③ He talks as if everybody in that family were foredoomed.
= 他说起话来，好像那家人个个都命中注定。
be foredoomed = 命中注定

forefather
核心：三支（多用复数）。一是祖先、先辈：our forefathers；
二是某个传统、行业的开创者；三是被后人反复引述的前人。
① Our forefathers cleared this hillside and left the stones in piles.
= 我们的先辈开垦了这面山坡，把石头堆成一堆堆。
our forefathers = 我们的先辈
② The forefathers of modern surgery worked without anything for pain.
= 现代外科的开创者们，动手术时连止痛的东西都没有。
the forefathers of something = 某事的开创者
③ He quotes the forefathers whenever the argument is going badly.
= 一旦辩论落下风，他就搬出先辈来。
quote the forefathers = 搬出先辈的话

forefinger
核心：三支。一是食指：point with your forefinger；
二是（手套、指纹上的）食指部位；三是用食指做出的动作。
① She points with her forefinger and everyone looks the wrong way.
= 她用食指一指，大家全看错了方向。
point with your forefinger = 用食指指
② The forefinger of the left glove wore through inside a month.
= 左手手套的食指不到一个月就磨破了。
the forefinger of a glove = 手套的食指部位
③ He tapped the table with one forefinger until the room went quiet.
= 他用一根食指敲桌子，敲到全屋安静。
tap with a forefinger = 用食指敲

forefoot
核心：三支。一是（四足动物的）前足、前蹄：a horse's forefoot；
二是（船）艏材与龙骨相接的底部；三是人脚的前掌部分。
① The horse favours its near forefoot on hard ground.
= 在硬地上，那匹马会护着左前蹄。
a horse's forefoot = 马的前蹄
② The forefoot took the shock when the boat came off the trailer.
= 船从拖车上下来时，艏底承住了那一撞。
the forefoot of a boat = 船的艏底
③ Runners who land on the forefoot wear the shoe out at the toe.
= 用前掌着地的跑者，鞋总是脚尖先磨破。
land on the forefoot = 前掌着地

forefront
核心：三支。一是最前沿、领先的位置：at the forefront of；
二是心里最要紧的位置：in the forefront of somebody's mind；
三是队伍的最前列。
① The lab was at the forefront of the field for about six years.
= 那间实验室在这个领域领先了大约六年。
at the forefront of something = 处在某领域的前沿
② Cost stayed at the forefront of everybody's mind and nobody said it aloud.
= 成本一直摆在每个人心里最前面，没人说出口。
in the forefront of somebody's mind = 在某人心里最要紧的位置
③ She walked in the forefront of the march and carried nothing at all.
= 她走在游行队伍的最前面，什么也没拿。
in the forefront of something = 在某物的最前列

foregather
核心：三支（正式或带谐谑）。一是聚集、会合：foregather in the hall；
二是（定期）碰头；三是（人群）在某处聚起来。
① The whole family foregathers in the hall on the last Sunday of the year.
= 每年最后一个星期日，全家在门厅里聚齐。
foregather in a place = 在某处聚集
② Committee members foregather at eleven and start at half past.
= 委员们十一点碰头，十一点半才开始。
foregather at a time = 在某时碰头
③ Two hundred people foregathered outside a building that seats forty.
= 两百人聚在一栋只坐得下四十人的楼外。
foregather outside something = 聚在某处外面

forego
核心：三支（正式）。一是先于…而来、走在前面；二是放弃、不去享用：forego a fee；
三是（法律语）放弃某项权利：forego a claim。
① Everything that foregoes this chapter is background and can be skipped.
= 这一章之前的内容都是背景，可以跳过。
forego something = 先于某物
② She agreed to forego the fee and asked for the credit instead.
= 她同意不要那笔酬金，只要署名。
forego a fee = 放弃酬金
③ The buyer foregoes any claim once the keys change hands.
= 钥匙一交，买方就放弃了一切索赔权。
forego a claim = 放弃索赔权

foregoing
核心：三支（正式）。一是前面所述的：the foregoing paragraph；
二是 the foregoing，上文所说的内容；三是合同、报告里指代前文的固定用法。
① The foregoing paragraph contradicts the one before it.
= 前面那一段跟它上面那一段自相矛盾。
the foregoing paragraph = 前述段落
② The foregoing is a summary and the detail sits in the appendix.
= 上文是个提要，细节都在附录里。
the foregoing = 上文所述
③ Nothing in the foregoing affects your right to cancel.
= 上述内容均不影响你取消的权利。
nothing in the foregoing = 上述内容均不影响

foregone
核心：三支。一是 a foregone conclusion，早已注定的结局；
二是（正式）先前的、已过去的；三是被放弃掉的。
① The result was a foregone conclusion and eleven thousand people came anyway.
= 结果早已注定，还是来了一万一千人。
a foregone conclusion = 早已注定的结局
② Foregone events get tidier every time the story is told again.
= 过去的事，每讲一遍就整齐一分。
foregone events = 已过去的事
③ The fee is foregone and the paperwork still has to be signed.
= 那笔酬金已经放弃，文件照样得签。
be foregone = 被放弃

foreground
核心：三支。一是（画面、照片的）前景：in the foreground；
二是显要位置：bring something to the foreground；三是动词，突出、强调。
① Two dogs in the foreground spoil an otherwise empty beach.
= 前景里两条狗，毁了一片本来空无一人的沙滩。
in the foreground = 在前景里
② The report brings cost to the foreground and leaves safety in a footnote.
= 报告把成本摆到显要位置，把安全塞进脚注。
bring something to the foreground = 把某事摆到显要位置
③ The new edition foregrounds the letters and cuts the diaries.
= 新版突出了书信，删掉了日记。
foreground something = 突出某物

forehand

② He stands well over to the forehand and leaves half the court open.
= 他站得偏向正手一侧，让出半个球场。
to the forehand = 偏向正手一侧
③ She hit a forehand winner off a ball that had already bounced twice.
= 她用正手打出致胜球，那球其实已经弹了两下。
a forehand winner = 正手致胜球

forehead

foreign
核心：五支。一是外国的：a foreign country；二是对外的：foreign policy；
三是陌生的、不合本性的：be foreign to somebody；
四是（医学）外来的：a foreign body；五是与周围格格不入的。
① She has worked in four foreign countries and learned two of the languages.
= 她在四个外国工作过，学会了其中两门语言。
a foreign country = 外国
a foreign language = 外语
He learned a foreign language from the radio and from shop labels.
= 他靠收音机和店里的商品标签学会了一门外语。
② Foreign policy was decided in a room that held six people.
= 外交政策是在一个只容得下六个人的房间里定的。
foreign policy = 外交政策
③ Asking for help is foreign to him and always has been.
= 开口求人对他来说是件陌生事，一向如此。
be foreign to somebody = 对某人来说很陌生
④ A foreign body in the eye feels ten times its actual size.
= 眼睛里的异物，感觉比实际大十倍。
a foreign body = 异物
⑤ That tone is foreign to everything else she has ever written.
= 那种腔调，跟她写过的其他一切都格格不入。
be foreign to something = 与某物格格不入
foreign direct investment = 外商直接投资
Foreign direct investment built the factory and left the road alone.
= 外商直接投资盖起了那家工厂，路一点没管。
foreign exchange = 外汇
The foreign exchange desk closes at four and the flights land at six.
= 外汇柜台四点关门，航班六点落地。
foreign-returned = 留洋归来的
A foreign-returned engineer was given a title and no budget.
= 一位留洋归来的工程师，得了个头衔，没有预算。
