/ˈsaʊəkraʊt/

= 我们在慕尼黑一家小餐馆吃了香肠、土豆和德国酸菜。
sauerkraut = 德国酸菜
② My grandmother made her own sauerkraut in a big stone jar in the cellar.
= 我祖母在地窖里用一个大石罐自己腌德国酸菜。
make sauerkraut = 腌德国酸菜

save
/seɪv/
核心：动词，本义是「使免于损失、危险」：拯救、救：save somebody's life、save somebody from；存（钱）、攒：save money、save up for；节省、省下（时间、钱、力气）：save time；保留、留着：save a seat for somebody；（计算机）保存：save a file；（球类）扑救：save a penalty；使免去（麻烦）：save somebody the trouble。名词，可数：（足球等）扑救。介词（正式）：除……之外。
习语：save face 是保全面子；save the day 是力挽狂澜；save your breath 是别白费口舌；save your skin 是保全自己。
① The firefighters saved the family from the burning house just in time.
= 消防员及时把那一家人从着火的房子里救了出来。
save = 拯救
save somebody from something = 把某人从某处救出
② The doctor's quick action saved the boy's life after he was hit by a car.
= 男孩被车撞后，医生的果断处置救了他的命。
save somebody's life = 救某人的命
③ She saves money every month so that she can travel around Asia next year.
= 她每个月存钱，好在明年去亚洲旅行。
save money = 存钱
也说 save up；不及物时宾语可省：I am saving for a car。
④ He is saving up for a new bike and puts all his birthday money in a box.
= 他在攒钱买新自行车，把生日收到的钱全放进一个盒子里。
save up for something = 为某物攒钱
⑤ Taking the motorway instead of the old coast road will save us at least an hour.
= 走高速公路而不走那条老海滨路，至少能给我们省一个小时。
save time = 省时间
⑥ Could you save me a seat near the front? I will be ten minutes late.
= 能在前排给我留个座位吗？我会晚到十分钟。
save somebody a seat = 给某人留座位
⑦ Don't forget to save your work before you turn off the computer.
= 关电脑之前别忘了保存你的工作。
save = 保存
计算机用语，也说 save something as a new file。
⑧ The goalkeeper saved the penalty and became the hero of the match.
= 守门员扑出了那个点球，成了全场的英雄。
save a penalty = 扑出点球
⑨ He apologized in public to save face after his comments were reported in the newspapers.
= 他的言论被报纸报道后，他公开道歉以保全面子。
save face = 保全面子
⑩ Just when we thought the party would be a disaster, my uncle arrived with music and saved the day.
= 就在我们以为聚会要砸了的时候，我叔叔带着音乐来了，力挽狂澜。
save the day = 力挽狂澜
⑪ Save your breath; she has already made up her mind and will not listen.
= 别白费口舌了，她已经拿定主意，不会听的。
save your breath = 别白费口舌
⑫ He lied to the police to save his own skin, and his friend went to prison instead.
= 他为了保全自己对警察撒了谎，结果他朋友进了监狱。
save your skin = 保全自己
⑬ If you post the letter for me, it will save me the trouble of going to the post office.
= 你要是帮我寄这封信，我就省得跑邮局了。
save somebody the trouble = 省得某人麻烦
⑭ Everyone in the family came to the wedding, save my youngest brother, who was ill.
= 全家人都来参加婚礼了，只有我最小的弟弟病了没来。
save = （正式）除……之外

/sɔː/
核心：名词，可数：锯、锯子：a hand saw、an electric saw。动词（过去式 sawed，过去分词 sawn 或 sawed）：锯、锯开：saw wood、saw something in half；来回拉动（像拉锯一样）。短语动词：saw down 是锯倒；saw off 是锯掉；saw up 是锯成小块。复合词 sawn-off shotgun（英式，美式 sawed-off）是短筒猎枪。
（saw 也是 see 的过去式，见 see 条。）
① My father used an old saw to cut the branches into logs for the fire.
= 我父亲用一把旧锯子把树枝锯成生火用的木柴。
saw = 锯
名词是锯子，动词是锯；过去分词英式多用 sawn，美式多用 sawed。
a saw = 锯子
② He sawed the plank in half so that it would fit into the back of the car.
= 他把木板锯成两半，好塞进车后厢。
saw something in half = 把某物锯成两半
③ The council sawed down the old oak tree because it was dying and might fall on the road.
= 那棵老橡树快死了，可能倒在路上，市政把它锯倒了。
saw down = 锯倒
④ The farmer sawed off the broken branch and painted the cut to protect the tree.
= 农民把断枝锯掉，在锯口上刷了漆保护树木。
saw off = 锯掉
⑤ We sawed up the fallen tree and stacked the wood beside the house for the winter.
= 我们把倒下的树锯成小段，堆在屋边过冬用。
saw up = 锯成小块
⑥ The bank robbers were carrying a sawn-off shotgun, so nobody tried to stop them.
= 银行劫匪带着一把短筒猎枪，所以谁也没敢拦他们。
a sawn-off shotgun = 短筒猎枪
⑦ The violinist sawed away at the strings, and the noise was terrible.
= 那个小提琴手来回拉着琴弦，声音难听极了。
saw away at something = 来回拉动某物

sawbones
/ˈsɔːbəʊnz/
核心：名词，可数，复数也是 sawbones（旧式俚语，幽默）：外科医生、大夫。
① "Go and fetch the sawbones," shouted the captain when the sailor fell from the mast.
= 水手从桅杆上摔下来时，船长喊道：「快去把大夫叫来！」
the sawbones = （旧式俚语）大夫
② In the old western, the town sawbones pulled a bullet out of the sheriff's leg.
= 在那部老西部片里，镇上的大夫从警长腿里取出了一颗子弹。
a sawbones = 外科大夫

sawdust
/ˈsɔːdʌst/
核心：名词，不可数：锯末、木屑。
① The floor of the workshop was covered in sawdust after a day of cutting wood.
= 锯了一天木头，作坊的地上铺满了锯末。
sawdust = 锯末
② We put clean sawdust in the bottom of the rabbit's cage to keep it dry.
= 我们在兔笼底部铺上干净的木屑，让笼子保持干燥。
clean sawdust = 干净的木屑

sawhorse
/ˈsɔːhɔːs/
核心：名词，可数（北美）：锯木架：两个支架上搭横木、锯木头时用来架木料。英式说 trestle。
① He laid the long plank across two sawhorses and cut it into four pieces.
= 他把长木板横搭在两个锯木架上，锯成四段。
a sawhorse = 锯木架
② The painters used sawhorses and old doors to make a table in the garden.
= 油漆工用锯木架和旧门板在花园里搭了一张桌子。
sawhorses = 锯木架

sawmill
/ˈsɔːmɪl/
核心：名词，可数：锯木厂：用机器把原木锯成木板的工厂。
① Most of the men in the village worked at the sawmill by the river.
= 村里的男人大多在河边的锯木厂干活。
the sawmill = 锯木厂
② The logs are taken by truck to a sawmill where they are cut into planks.
= 原木用卡车运到锯木厂，在那里锯成木板。
take logs to a sawmill = 把原木运到锯木厂
