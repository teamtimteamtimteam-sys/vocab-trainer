shatter
/ˈʃætə(r)/
核心：动词：（使）粉碎、破碎：shatter into pieces；（使）破灭、毁掉（希望、信心）：shatter somebody's hopes；（使）极度震惊、悲痛。
① The glass fell off the table and shattered into a hundred tiny pieces.
= 玻璃杯从桌上掉下来，摔成了上百块碎片。
shatter into pieces = 摔得粉碎
shatter = 粉碎
② The bomb shattered every window in the street, but luckily nobody was hurt.
= 那颗炸弹震碎了整条街的每一扇窗户，所幸没人受伤。

③ The injury shattered his hopes of playing for his country at the World Cup.
= 那次受伤让他代表国家参加世界杯的希望破灭了。
shatter somebody's hopes = 使某人的希望破灭
④ Car windscreens are made of shatter-proof glass, so they do not break into sharp pieces in an accident.
= 汽车挡风玻璃用的是防碎玻璃，出事故时不会碎成尖利的碎片。
shatter-proof = 防碎的
