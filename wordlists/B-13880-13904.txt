magic
/ˈmædʒɪk/
核心：魔法、法术；魔术、戏法；魔力、魅力。形容词：有魔力的；（口语）棒极了。
习语：like magic / as if by magic（神奇地、一下子）、magic wand（魔杖；wave a magic wand = 一下子把问题解决）。
搭配：magic carpet（飞毯）、magic bullet（灵丹妙药）、magic number（关键数字）、magic mushroom（迷幻蘑菇）、
magic lantern（旧：幻灯机）、magic realism（魔幻现实主义，也说 magical realism）。
形容词 magical（有魔力的；美妙的）与副词 magically 并入本条。
① The witch used magic to turn the prince into a frog.
= 女巫用魔法把王子变成了青蛙。
use magic = 用魔法
② He did some magic for the children with a pack of cards.
= 他用一副牌给孩子们变了几个戏法。
do magic = 变戏法
③ The magic of the old city at night is hard to describe.
= 那座古城夜晚的魅力难以言表。
the magic of something = …的魅力
④ There is no magic formula for passing exams.
= 考试过关没有什么灵丹妙药。
a magic formula = 灵丹妙药
⑤ The pain disappeared like magic after I took the tablets.
= 吃了药片，疼痛神奇地消失了。
like magic = 神奇地
⑥ I cannot wave a magic wand and find you a job.
= 我没法挥一挥魔杖就给你变出一份工作来。
a magic wand = 魔杖
⑦ In the story, the boy flies over the city on a magic carpet.
= 故事里，那个男孩乘着飞毯飞过城市。
a magic carpet = 飞毯
⑧ There is no magic bullet for the country's economic problems.
= 那个国家的经济问题没有灵丹妙药。
a magic bullet = 灵丹妙药
本指只打病菌、不伤身体的药。
⑨ The party needs sixty seats, the magic number for a majority.
= 那个党需要六十席，这是过半数的关键数字。
the magic number = 关键数字
⑩ Two students fell ill after eating magic mushrooms.
= 两名学生吃了迷幻蘑菇后病倒了。
a magic mushroom = 迷幻蘑菇
⑪ Before the cinema, families watched pictures from a magic lantern.
= 有电影之前，一家人看幻灯机放的画片。
a magic lantern = （旧）幻灯机
⑫ The novel is a famous work of magic realism from South America.
= 那部小说是南美魔幻现实主义的名作。
magic realism = 魔幻现实主义
magical realism = 魔幻现实主义（同上）
⑬ It was a magical evening under the stars.
= 那是星空下一个美妙的夜晚。
magical = 美妙的；有魔力的
⑭ The stain magically disappeared when I rubbed it with lemon juice.
= 我用柠檬汁一擦，那块污渍神奇地不见了。
magically = 神奇地

⑥ The magnetic poles of the earth move a little every year.

⑦ The sailors found their way with a magnetic compass and the stars.

maim
/meɪm/
核心：使残废、使受重伤致残。
① Hundreds of people were killed or maimed by the bomb.
= 数百人被那枚炸弹炸死或炸残。
be maimed = 被弄残废
比 injure 重：留下终身残疾。
② Landmines still maim children in the fields every year.
= 地雷每年仍在田里把孩子炸残。
maim children = 使孩子致残
多用被动。

main
/meɪn/
核心：主要的、最重要的 —— 只用在名词前。名词：（输水、输气、输电的）总管、干线；（英）the mains（电源、自来水总管）。
习语 in the main（大体上）。副词 mainly（主要地）并入本条。
搭配：main road（主干道）、main street（美：小城的主街）、main course（主菜）、main line（铁路干线）、
main clause（主句）、main verb（主要动词）、（美，口语）main man（最铁的哥们儿）；
名词 mainsail（主帆）与 mainspring（主发条；主要动力）也并在这里。
① The main reason for the delay was the bad weather.
= 延误的主要原因是天气恶劣。
the main reason = 主要原因
没有比较级，不说 more main。
② The post office is on the main road through the village.
= 邮局在穿村而过的主干道上。
the main road = 主干道
③ I had fish for my main course and ice cream to follow.
= 我主菜吃的是鱼，后面还有冰淇淋。
the main course = 主菜
前菜 starter，甜点 dessert。
④ A burst water main flooded the street.
= 一根自来水总管爆了，把街道淹了。
a water main = 自来水总管
这一支是名词。
⑤ Turn the electricity off at the mains before you change the switch.
= 换开关之前先把总电源关掉。
at the mains = （英）在总开关处
⑥ The work is, in the main, well done.
= 这活儿大体上干得不错。
in the main = 大体上
⑦ The people at the meeting were mainly teachers.
= 来开会的主要是教师。
mainly = 主要地
同 mostly、chiefly。
⑧ Every shop on Main Street closes at six.
= 主街上的每家店都是六点关门。
Main Street = （美）小城的主街
也指美国小城镇居民那种保守的生活方式。
⑨ The village is on the main line from London to Edinburgh.
= 那个村子在伦敦到爱丁堡的铁路干线上。
the main line = 铁路干线
⑩ The main clause can stand alone as a sentence.
= 主句可以单独成句。
a main clause = 主句
⑪ In this sentence, the main verb comes after two auxiliary verbs.
= 这个句子里，主要动词排在两个助动词后面。
the main verb = 主要动词
⑫ He introduced the drummer as his main man.
= 他把鼓手介绍为自己最铁的哥们儿。
your main man = （美，口语）最铁的哥们儿
⑬ The crew lowered the mainsail as the wind grew stronger.
= 风越刮越大，船员们把主帆降了下来。
the mainsail = 主帆
⑭ Curiosity is the mainspring of all her research.
= 好奇心是她一切研究的原动力。
the mainspring = 主要动力
本义是钟表的主发条。
⑮ The houses in the street are all connected to the ring main.
= 那条街上的房子都接在环形干线上。
the ring main = （英式）环形电路干线

个人电脑出现之前的主力。
② Every desk in the office was connected to the mainframe in the basement.
= 办公室里的每张桌子都连着地下室里的主机。
the mainframe = 主机
