riviera
/ˌrɪviˈeərə/
核心：（海滨的）度假胜地、海滨避暑区；the Riviera 是（法国、意大利的）里维埃拉海岸。可数。
① Every summer the rich and famous sail their yachts along the French Riviera.
= 每年夏天，富人和名流都驾着游艇沿法国里维埃拉海岸航行。
the French Riviera = 法国里维埃拉海岸
② The local tourist board likes to call this stretch of coast the English riviera.
= 当地旅游局喜欢把这段海岸叫作“英格兰的海滨胜地”。
a riviera = 海滨度假胜地

rivulet
/ˈrɪvjələt/
核心：（正式）小溪、细流；（液体的）一道道细流：rivulets of。可数。
① Rivulets of rain ran down the window as we waited for the storm to pass.
= 我们等着暴风雨过去，雨水一道道顺着窗户流下来。
rivulets of rain = 一道道雨水
a rivulet = 细流
② A small rivulet ran down the hillside and joined the river in the valley.
= 一条小溪顺着山坡流下，汇入山谷里的河。
a small rivulet = 小溪

riyal
/riˈjɑːl/
核心：里亚尔：沙特阿拉伯、卡塔尔的货币单位。可数。
① The hotel room in Riyadh cost five hundred riyals a night.
= 利雅得那间旅馆客房一晚五百里亚尔。
five hundred riyals = 五百里亚尔
a riyal = 里亚尔
② He changed his dollars into riyals at the airport.
= 他在机场把美元换成了里亚尔。
change dollars into riyals = 把美元换成里亚尔

ro-ro
/ˈrəʊ rəʊ/
核心：（英式）滚装的、滚装船的：车辆可以直接开上开下的渡轮：a ro-ro ferry。是 roll-on roll-off 的缩写。
① We drove straight onto the ro-ro ferry at Dover and drove off again in Calais ninety minutes later.
= 我们在多佛直接把车开上滚装渡轮，九十分钟后又在加来开了下来。
a ro-ro ferry = 滚装渡轮
ro-ro = 滚装的
② The port has built a new ro-ro terminal so that lorries can load and unload much faster.
= 港口新建了一个滚装码头，卡车装卸快多了。
a ro-ro terminal = 滚装码头

roach
/rəʊtʃ/
核心：一是（美式口语）蟑螂，是 cockroach 的简称；二是（俚语）大麻烟屁股；三是拟鲤：欧洲一种淡水鱼。复数一般作 roaches，鱼也可作 roach。
① The old flat was full of roaches, so we called a man to come and get rid of them.
= 那套旧公寓里到处是蟑螂，我们只好请人来灭。
a roach = 蟑螂
② The police found a roach in the ashtray and asked who had been smoking cannabis.
= 警察在烟灰缸里发现一个大麻烟屁股，问是谁抽了大麻。
a roach = 大麻烟屁股
③ The boys spent the whole Saturday catching roach and perch in the canal behind the school.
= 男孩们整个星期六都在学校后面的运河里钓拟鲤和鲈鱼。
roach = 拟鲤

road
/rəʊd/
核心：路、公路、马路：by road、along the road、cross the road；（城市里的）街道，用于街名，缩写为 Rd：Station Road；（达到某目标的）道路、途径：the road to success。可数或用于固定搭配。
习语：on the road 是在路上、在旅途中，也指（乐队等）在巡回演出；hit the road 是（口语）上路、出发；down the road 是将来、以后；the end of the road 是尽头、末路；one for the road 是（口语）临走前再喝一杯；off the road 是（车）不能上路的。
① The road to the village is very narrow, so drive slowly on the bends.
= 去村子的路很窄，转弯时开慢点。
road = 路
② Look both ways before you cross the road, even when the lights are green.
= 过马路前要左右看，就算是绿灯也一样。
cross the road = 过马路
③ It is cheaper to send heavy goods by road than by air.
= 重货走公路运输比空运便宜。
by road = 走公路
④ My grandmother lives at 42 Station Road, just past the post office.
= 我奶奶住在车站路 42 号，就在邮局过去一点。
Station Road = 车站路
⑤ Hard work is the only road to success in this business.
= 在这一行，勤奋是成功的唯一途径。
the road to something = 通往某事的道路
⑥ We have been on the road since six o'clock this morning and the children are tired.
= 我们从早上六点就上路了，孩子们都累了。
on the road = 在路上、旅途中
⑦ The band will be on the road for most of the summer, playing in twenty cities.
= 乐队这个夏天大部分时间都在巡回演出，要去二十个城市。
on the road = 巡回演出
⑧ It is getting late, so I think we should hit the road before the traffic gets bad.
= 天不早了，我看我们该在堵车前上路了。
hit the road = 上路、出发
⑨ We do not need a bigger house now, but we might need one further down the road.
= 我们现在不需要大房子，但以后可能需要。
down the road = 将来、以后
⑩ After three years of losses, the company has reached the end of the road.
= 连亏三年，那家公司已经走到了尽头。
the end of the road = 尽头、末路
⑪ "Just one for the road," he said, and ordered another glass of beer.
= 他说“走之前再来一杯”，又点了一杯啤酒。
one for the road = 临走前再喝一杯
⑫ My old car has been off the road since it failed its safety test.
= 我那辆旧车自从安检没过就一直不能上路。
off the road = 不能上路

roadblock
/ˈrəʊdblɒk/

① The police set up roadblocks on all the roads out of the city after the bank robbery.
= 银行抢劫案发生后，警察在所有出城的路上设了路障。
set up a roadblock = 设路障
a roadblock = 路障
② The lack of money is the biggest roadblock to building the new hospital.
= 缺钱是建新医院的最大障碍。
a roadblock to something = 某事的障碍

roadhouse
