rout
/raʊt/
核心：名词，可数：（比赛、战斗中的）大败、溃败：a complete rout。动词，及物：彻底打败、击溃：rout the enemy；多用于体育新闻和战史。和 route 发音、意思都不同。
① The final was a complete rout, with the home team scoring six goals and conceding none.
= 决赛是一边倒的大胜，主队打进六球、一球未失。
a complete rout = 一边倒的大败
② The government's candidates were routed in the local elections, losing almost every seat.
= 执政党的候选人在地方选举中惨败，几乎丢了所有议席。
be routed = 被击溃
③ The small army routed the enemy in a surprise attack at dawn and captured their camp.
= 那支小部队在黎明发动突袭，击溃了敌军，攻占了他们的营地。
rout the enemy = 击溃敌军

route
/ruːt/
核心：名词，可数：路线、路途：plan a route；（公交、航班的）线路：a bus route；（比喻）途径、门路：the route to success；（北美）（送报、送货的）固定路线：a paper route。en route（法语来）是在途中；trade route 是商路。动词：按某路线发送、经由……转送：be routed through。美式也读 /raʊt/。
① We planned our route carefully so that we could stop at three old villages on the way to the coast.
= 我们仔细规划了路线，好在去海边的路上停三个老村子。
route = 路线
plan a route = 规划路线
② The number 12 bus route goes past the hospital and ends at the railway station.
= 十二路公交线路经过医院，终点是火车站。
a bus route = 公交线路
③ We stopped for lunch in a small market town en route to the mountains.
= 我们在去山区的途中，在一个小集镇停下来吃午饭。
en route = 在途中
en route 来自法语，也可放在名词后：passengers en route to Paris。
④ The city grew rich because it lay on an important trade route between Europe and Asia.
= 那座城市因为位于欧亚之间一条重要的商路上而富起来。
trade route = 商路
⑤ For many young people in the village, education is seen as the surest route to a better life.
= 对村里的许多年轻人来说，读书被看作通往更好生活最可靠的途径。
the route to something = 通往某事的途径
⑥ After six o'clock, all calls to the office are routed through the main desk downstairs.
= 六点以后，所有打到办公室的电话都经由楼下总台转接。
be routed through = 经由……转送
⑦ When I was twelve I had a paper route and delivered newspapers to sixty houses before school.
= 我十二岁时有一条送报路线，每天上学前给六十户人家送报。
a paper route = （北美）送报路线
⑧ Letters to the farm are addressed to a rural route number instead of a street name.
= 寄到那座农场的信写的是乡村邮路编号，而不是街名。
a rural route = （北美）乡村邮路
⑨ The team played route one football, kicking the ball long towards their tall striker every time.
= 那支球队踢长传冲吊，每次都把球大脚踢向他们的高个前锋。
route one = （英式足球）长传冲吊
也泛指最直接、最简单的办法：the route one approach。

router
/ˈruːtə(r)/（网络）/ˈraʊtə(r)/（木工）
核心：名词，可数，两个来源不同的词：一是（计算机）路由器：在网络之间转发数据的设备，读 /ˈruːtə(r)/，北美读 /ˈraʊtər/；二是（木工）刨槽机、铣刀，读 /ˈraʊtə(r)/。
① If the internet stops working, try switching the router off and on again before you call the company.
= 网断了的话，先把路由器关掉再打开，不行再给公司打电话。
a router = 路由器
② My father used a router to cut a neat groove along the edge of the wooden shelf.
= 我父亲用刨槽机沿着木架的边缘开了一道整齐的槽。
a router for wood = 刨槽机

routine
/ruːˈtiːn/
核心：名词：（日常的）惯例、常规、固定程序：a daily routine、break the routine；（舞蹈、喜剧等的）一套动作、一段固定表演：a dance routine；（计算机）例程。可数或不可数。
形容词：例行的、常规的：a routine check；（贬义）乏味的、千篇一律的。副词 routinely 是例行地、照例；经常（发生）。
① My daily routine hardly changes: I get up at seven, walk the dog and catch the train at eight.
= 我每天的日常安排几乎不变：七点起床，遛狗，八点赶火车。
a daily routine = 日常安排
routine = 例行公事
② A short holiday by the sea is a good way to break the routine of work and housework.
= 去海边度个短假，是打破工作和家务常规的好办法。
break the routine = 打破常规
③ The two girls practised their dance routine every evening for a month before the school show.
= 学校演出前，那两个女孩每天晚上练她们那套舞蹈动作，练了一个月。
a dance routine = 一套舞蹈动作
④ The police stopped the car for a routine check and found that the driver had no licence.
= 警察拦下那辆车作例行检查，发现司机没有驾照。
a routine check = 例行检查
作定语的 routine 多是中性的「常规的」，说工作乏味时才带贬义。
⑤ After ten years in the same office, he found the work routine and longed for something more exciting.
= 在同一个办公室干了十年，他觉得这份工作千篇一律，渴望做点更刺激的事。
routine = 乏味的
⑥ Passengers' bags are routinely searched at the border, so do not carry anything for a stranger.
= 在边境乘客的包照例都要检查，所以别替陌生人带任何东西。
routinely = 例行地、照例
⑦ Teachers say that children in the city routinely go to bed after midnight on school nights.
= 老师们说，城里的孩子第二天要上学的晚上也经常过了半夜才睡。
routinely = 经常

roux
/ruː/
核心：（烹饪）油面酱、面粉糊：黄油和面粉一起炒成的底料，用来给酱汁、汤勾芡。可数，复数也写 roux。来自法语。
① Start the cheese sauce by making a roux with butter and flour, then slowly add warm milk.
= 做奶酪酱先用黄油和面粉炒个油面酱，再慢慢加热牛奶。
make a roux = 炒油面酱
② If you cook the roux for longer, it turns brown and gives the soup a richer, nutty taste.
= 油面酱炒得久一点就会变成褐色，让汤带上更浓的坚果香。
a brown roux = 褐色油面酱

rove
/rəʊv/
核心：动词：（文学）漫游、流浪：rove the countryside、rove around；（目光）扫来扫去：eyes rove over / around something。不及物、及物都可。
① As a young man he roved the countryside, working on farms and sleeping in barns.
= 他年轻时四处漂泊，在农场打工，睡在谷仓里。
rove the countryside = 在乡间漫游
② During the exam, the teacher's eyes roved over the rows of desks, watching for anyone who tried to copy.
= 考试时，老师的目光在一排排课桌上扫视，留意有没有人想抄。
eyes rove over something = 目光在某处扫视

rover
/ˈrəʊvə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（文学）漫游者、流浪者；（太空）探测车：在月球或火星表面行驶的遥控车辆，如 a Mars rover。
① He was a born rover who never stayed in one town for more than a few months.
= 他天生是个漂泊的人，在哪个镇上都待不过几个月。
a rover = 漫游者
② Scientists sent a new rover to Mars to search for signs of water under the red dust.
= 科学家向火星送去一辆新的探测车，在红色尘土下寻找水的迹象。
a Mars rover = 火星车

roving
/ˈrəʊvɪŋ/

① The newspaper sent a roving reporter to talk to farmers in every part of the country.
= 那家报纸派了一名流动记者，去全国各地采访农民。
a roving reporter = 流动记者
② She left her husband because he had a roving eye and was always flirting with other women.

have a roving eye = 见异思迁

row
/rəʊ/（排、划船）/raʊ/（争吵）
核心：两个读音、两个词。
读 /rəʊ/：名词，可数：（人、物排成的）一排、一行：a row of、in a row；（剧场的）一排座位：the front row；（表格的）一行：rows and columns；in a row 也指接连、连续。动词：划（船）：row a boat；划船载送。
读 /raʊ/（英式口语）：名词，可数：争吵、吵架：have a row；（公开的）争议：a row over something；吵闹声：make a row。动词，不及物：吵架：row with somebody。
① The children sat in a row on the wooden bench outside the school, waiting for the bus to arrive.
= 孩子们在学校外面的木长椅上坐成一排，等公共汽车来。
row = 排
sit in a row = 坐成一排
② We had seats in the front row, so we could see the actors' faces clearly during the whole play.
= 我们坐在第一排，整场戏都能清清楚楚看到演员的脸。
the front row = 前排
③ There was a long row of houses along the river, all of them painted in different bright colours.
= 河边有一长排房子，每座都刷着不同的鲜艳颜色。
a row of houses = 一排房子
④ Add a new row at the bottom of the table for each customer who signs up during the month.
= 这个月每有一位顾客登记，就在表格底部加一行。
a row in a table = 表格的一行
rows and columns = 行和列
⑤ Our team has now lost five games in a row, and the fans are starting to get very angry.
= 我们队已经连输五场，球迷们开始非常愤怒了。
in a row = 连续
⑥ We took turns to row the little boat across the lake to the island where we had our picnic.
= 我们轮流划着小船过湖，到岛上去野餐。
row = 划船
row a boat across = 划船渡过
⑦ My neighbours had a terrible row last night, and I could hear them shouting through the wall.
= 我的邻居昨晚大吵了一架，我隔着墙都能听见他们在喊。
row = 争吵
have a row = 吵架
⑧ The government is in the middle of a row over its plans to close three local hospitals.
= 政府正因关闭三家地方医院的计划陷入一场争议。
a row over something = 关于某事的争议
⑨ She often rowed with her brother about which of them should look after their elderly mother.
= 她常和哥哥为谁来照顾年迈的母亲吵架。
row with somebody = 和某人吵架
⑩ Stop making such a terrible row in the kitchen, boys, because the baby is trying to sleep upstairs!
= 孩子们，别在厨房里那么吵了，宝宝正在楼上睡觉呢！
make a row = 吵闹

royal
/ˈrɔɪəl/
核心：形容词：王室的、皇家的：the royal family、a royal palace；（机构名）皇家的：the Royal Navy、Royal Air Force；（口语）盛大的、极好的：a royal welcome；（北美口语）极大的：a royal pain。名词，可数（口语）：王室成员：the royals。

② Thousands of people waited outside the royal palace to see the new king and queen on the balcony.
= 成千上万的人在王宫外等着，想看新国王和王后在阳台上露面。

③ My grandfather was a pilot in the Royal Air Force during the war and flew over Germany many times.
= 我祖父战时是皇家空军的飞行员，多次飞越德国上空。
Royal Air Force = 皇家空军
④ The newspapers are full of stories about the royals and what they wore to the wedding.
= 报纸上满是关于王室成员和他们在婚礼上穿什么的报道。
the royals = 王室成员
⑤ The team was given a royal welcome when they arrived home with the cup.
= 球队捧着奖杯回到家乡时受到了盛大的欢迎。
a royal welcome = 盛大的欢迎
⑥ Fixing the old car every weekend has been a royal pain, and I wish I had sold it years ago.
= 每个周末修那辆旧车真是件天大的麻烦事，我真希望几年前就把它卖了。

⑦ The visitors were royally entertained with music, dancing and a feast that lasted until midnight.
= 客人们受到了隆重款待，有音乐、舞蹈，还有一场一直吃到半夜的宴席。

⑧ The writer still earns royalty payments from the book she published thirty years ago.

a royal salute = 皇家礼炮

⑩ Royal tennis is an old indoor game that was played by kings of England in the sixteenth century.
= 皇家网球是一种古老的室内运动，十六世纪英格兰的国王们就玩过。
royal tennis = 皇家网球（老式室内网球）

⑪ The jam company has held a royal warrant for fifty years, so its jars show the royal coat of arms.
= 那家果酱公司持有王室供货许可五十年了，所以罐子上印着王室徽章。
a royal warrant = 王室供货许可

⑫ The royal yacht carried the queen on visits to many countries before it was turned into a museum.
= 那艘王室游艇载着女王出访过许多国家，后来改成了博物馆。
a royal yacht = 王室游艇

royalty
/ˈrɔɪəlti/
核心：名词：一是王室成员（总称，不可数）：treat somebody like royalty；二是版税、专利使用费（可数，多用复数）：royalties from a book。比喻也指某个圈子里最显赫的人：Hollywood royalty。
① The hotel staff treated us like royalty, carrying our bags and bringing us drinks by the pool.
= 酒店员工把我们当贵宾一样伺候，帮我们拎包，还把饮料送到泳池边。
treat somebody like royalty = 待某人如上宾
② The film premiere in London was attended by several members of European royalty and dozens of famous actors.
= 好几位欧洲王室成员和几十位名演员出席了那部电影在伦敦的首映礼。
members of royalty = 王室成员
③ The singer still receives royalties every time one of his old songs is played on the radio.
= 那位歌手的老歌每在电台播放一次，他都能拿到版税。
royalties = 版税
这一义多用复数，常和 receive、earn、pay 连用。
④ As the daughter of two famous actors, she was treated as Hollywood royalty from the day she was born.
= 作为两位名演员的女儿，她一出生就被当作好莱坞的显贵。
Hollywood royalty = 好莱坞显贵

rozzer
/ˈrɒzə(r)/
核心：（英式旧式俚语）警察、条子。可数，多用复数 the rozzers。
① "Run, the rozzers are coming!" shouted one of the boys as the police car turned into the street.
= 警车拐进那条街时，一个男孩大喊：「快跑，条子来了！」
the rozzers = 条子
② In the old crime films, the thieves always called the police rozzers and tried to escape over the rooftops.
= 在老犯罪片里，小偷总把警察叫作条子，还想从屋顶上逃走。
a rozzer = 条子

rub
/rʌb/
核心：动词：擦、揉、搓：rub your eyes、rub your hands together；（来回）摩擦：rub against；把（药膏等）抹上、揉进：rub cream into your skin；擦亮、擦掉：rub something clean；（鞋等）磨（脚）。名词，单数：擦、揉：give something a rub。
习语：rub shoulders with 是和（名人等）打交道、厮混；rub somebody up the wrong way 是惹恼某人；rub salt into the wound 是在伤口上撒盐；there's the rub（书面）是难就难在这里。
① She yawned and rubbed her eyes as she came downstairs for breakfast on Monday morning.
= 星期一早上她下楼吃早饭，一边打哈欠一边揉眼睛。
rub = 擦、揉
rub your eyes = 揉眼睛
② The old men stood at the bus stop, rubbing their hands together to keep warm in the frosty air.
= 老人们站在公交车站，在冰冷的空气里搓着手取暖。
rub your hands together = 搓手
③ The cat rubbed against my legs and purred until I gave it something to eat.
= 那只猫蹭着我的腿呼噜呼噜叫，直到我给它吃的。
rub against something = 在某物上蹭
不及物用法，主语是来回蹭的那一方，多说猫狗、衣领、鞋子。
④ Rub a little of the cream into your skin twice a day until the rash goes away.
= 每天两次把一点药膏揉进皮肤里，直到疹子消退。
rub cream into your skin = 把药膏揉进皮肤
结构是 rub A into / on B：A 是油、膏、粉，B 是皮肤、肉、表面。
⑤ My new boots rubbed my heels so badly that I had blisters by the end of the walk.
= 我的新靴子把脚后跟磨得很厉害，走完一趟就起了水泡。
shoes rub = 鞋磨脚
⑥ Give the table a good rub with a soft cloth, and the old wood will start to shine again.
= 用软布把桌子好好擦一擦，那老木头就又会发亮。
give something a rub = 把某物擦一擦
⑦ As a journalist in the capital, she rubbed shoulders with film stars and politicians every week.
= 作为首都的记者，她每周都和影星、政客打交道。
rub shoulders with somebody = 和某人打交道
⑧ He has a habit of rubbing people up the wrong way, so he is not very popular in the office.
= 他总是惹人恼火，所以在办公室里人缘不太好。
rub somebody up the wrong way = 惹恼某人
⑨ Losing the final was bad enough, but hearing the other team laugh at us really rubbed salt into the wound.
= 输掉决赛已经够难受了，听到对方嘲笑我们更是在伤口上撒盐。
rub salt into the wound = 在伤口上撒盐
⑩ We would love to move to the country, but there's the rub: neither of us could find work there.
= 我们很想搬到乡下去，可难就难在这里：我们俩在那儿都找不到工作。
there's the rub = 难就难在这里
这句来自莎士比亚，口气偏书面、带点戏谑。
⑪ My two brothers are very different, but they rub along well enough when they share a flat.
= 我两个哥哥很不一样，可合租一套房时也还处得来。
rub along = （英式口语）相处得还行
多说 rub along together / with somebody，指不算亲密、但能和平相处。
⑫ After the swim, his mother rubbed him down with a big towel so that he would not get cold.
= 游完泳后，他妈妈用一条大毛巾给他擦干身子，免得着凉。
rub down = 用毛巾擦干；（用砂纸）打磨
也说 rub down a door before painting it，指上漆前打磨。
⑬ Rub in the sun cream well, especially on your nose and the back of your neck.
= 把防晒霜好好揉开，尤其是鼻子和后颈。
rub in = 揉进去
也说 rub the butter into the flour，指做糕点时把黄油搓进面粉。
⑭ I know I made a mistake, so there is no need to rub it in by telling everyone in the office.
= 我知道我错了，你用不着到处跟办公室里的人说来揭我的短。
rub it in = 揭人短处
反复提起别人的失误、让人更难堪。
⑮ The smell of smoke would not rub off, even after I had washed my hands three times.
= 那股烟味怎么也擦不掉，我洗了三遍手都没用。
rub off = 擦掉；（性格、习惯）感染别人
也说 her enthusiasm rubbed off on the whole class，指热情感染了全班。
⑯ The teacher told us to rub out the wrong answers and write the correct ones in pen.
= 老师叫我们把错的答案擦掉，用钢笔写上正确的。
rub out = 擦掉
美式俚语里 rub somebody out 也指干掉某人。

rubber
/ˈrʌbə(r)/
核心：名词：橡胶（不可数）：made of rubber；（英式）橡皮擦（可数），北美多说 eraser；（北美口语）避孕套（可数）；（桥牌等）三局两胜的一盘。形容词用法（作定语）：橡胶的：rubber gloves。
① The soles of these walking boots are made of thick rubber, so they do not slip on wet rocks.
= 这双登山靴的鞋底是厚橡胶做的，在湿石头上也不打滑。
rubber = 橡胶
be made of rubber = 橡胶制成
② Always wear rubber gloves when you use strong cleaning products in the kitchen.
= 在厨房里用强效清洁剂时一定要戴橡胶手套。
rubber gloves = 橡胶手套
③ Can I borrow your rubber? I made a mistake in my drawing and need to fix it before the lesson ends.
= 能借你的橡皮用一下吗？我画错了，得在下课前改好。
a rubber = （英式）橡皮擦
④ The pharmacist kept the rubbers on a high shelf behind the counter.
= 药剂师把避孕套放在柜台后面的高架子上。
a rubber = （北美口语）避孕套
⑤ After two hours of cards we had each won one game, so we played a third to decide the rubber.
= 打了两个小时牌，我们各赢了一局，就打第三局来决出这一盘的胜负。
the rubber = （桥牌等）三局两胜的一盘
⑥ The committee was only a rubber stamp for the decisions that the president had already made.
= 那个委员会只是给总统已经做出的决定盖个橡皮图章。
a rubber stamp = 橡皮图章（只会照批不误的机构）
也作动词 rubber-stamp a plan，指不加审查地批准。

rubberized
/ˈrʌbəraɪzd/
核心：形容词：涂了橡胶的、经橡胶处理的：防水、防滑或耐磨。英式也拼作 rubberised。
① The children wore rubberized raincoats that kept them completely dry during the long walk in the rain.
= 孩子们穿着涂胶雨衣，冒雨走了很久，身上一点没湿。
a rubberized raincoat = 涂胶雨衣
② The handles of the tools have a rubberized grip, so they do not slip out of your hand when it is wet.
= 那些工具的把手有涂胶握柄，手湿的时候也不会滑脱。
a rubberized grip = 涂胶握柄
