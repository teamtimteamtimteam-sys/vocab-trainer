petition
/pəˈtɪʃn/
核心：名词，可数：请愿书：许多人签名、要求当局做某事的文书：a petition against、for；（法律）诉状、申请书：a petition for divorce；（正式）祈求。
动词：（向……）请愿、正式请求：petition for、against，petition somebody to do something；（法律）向法院申请。
提出请愿、申请的人叫 petitioner，并入本条。
① More than ten thousand people signed a petition against the closure of the hospital.
= 一万多人在反对关闭那家医院的请愿书上签了名。
a petition against something = 反对某事的请愿书
sign a petition = 在请愿书上签名
② Her husband filed a petition for divorce after two years apart.
= 分居两年后，她丈夫递了离婚诉状。
a petition for divorce = 离婚诉状
③ Local residents petitioned the council to lower the speed limit.
= 当地居民请愿，要求镇议会降低限速。
petition somebody to do something = 请愿要求某方做某事
④ The workers are petitioning for a shorter working week.
= 工人们在请愿，要求缩短每周工时。
petition for something = 为某事请愿
⑤ The court granted the petitioner custody of both children.
= 法庭把两个孩子的监护权判给了申请人。
the petitioner = 申请人、请愿人

petits pois = 嫩小豌豆
② She keeps a bag of frozen petits pois for the children's supper.

frozen petits pois = 冻嫩豌豆

petrel
/ˈpetrəl/

stormy petrel 一是一种小海燕，二是（书面）爱惹事、所到之处必起风波的人。
① Petrels followed the ship for days, skimming the tops of the waves.
= 海燕跟着船飞了好几天，贴着浪尖掠过。
a petrel = 海燕
② As a young politician he was the stormy petrel of his party.
= 年轻时从政，他是党内最爱惹事的人物。
a stormy petrel = 爱惹事的人

Petri dish
/ˈpetri dɪʃ/
核心：培养皿：实验室里用来培养细菌的带盖浅玻璃盘或塑料盘。可数；得名于发明它的德国细菌学家；也用小写。
① The students grew bacteria from their own fingers in a Petri dish.
= 学生们在培养皿里培养自己手指上的细菌。
a Petri dish = 培养皿
② A crowded school is a Petri dish for every cold that is going round.
= 拥挤的学校是各种流行感冒的培养皿。
a Petri dish for something = 滋生某物的温床
比喻用法：容易滋生某种东西的环境。

petrify
/ˈpetrɪfaɪ/
核心：两支。一是把……吓呆、吓得动弹不得，及物，多用被动；二是（使）石化：变成石头，多用分词。第三人称单数 petrifies，过去式 petrified。
形容词 petrified：吓呆了的：petrified of；石化的：petrified wood。字面是「变成石头」，吓呆是比喻。并入本条。
① The thought of speaking in front of five hundred people petrifies me.
= 一想到要在五百人面前讲话，我就吓得发呆。
petrify somebody = 把某人吓呆
② She stood petrified at the top of the ladder and could not move.
= 她站在梯子顶上吓呆了，一动不敢动。
stand petrified = 吓呆了站着
③ I am petrified of snakes, even the small ones in the zoo.
= 我怕蛇怕得要命，连动物园里的小蛇都怕。
petrified of something = 对某物怕得要命
④ The desert is littered with the trunks of petrified trees.
= 沙漠里散落着石化了的树干。
petrified trees = 石化的树
petrified wood = 硅化木

petroleum

petty
/ˈpeti/

名词 pettiness，不可数。来自法语的 petit「小」。
复合词：petty cash 是零用现金：办公室里备着付小额开支的钱；petty officer 是海军军士；petty theft 是小偷小摸。并入本条。

② He was in trouble with the police for petty crime from the age of twelve.
= 他从十二岁起就因为小偷小摸一类的事跟警察打交道。
petty crime = 轻微犯罪
petty theft = 小偷小摸
③ It was petty of her to refuse to sign his leaving card.
= 她连他的离职贺卡都不肯签名，真小气。
petty of somebody = 某人小气
④ The clerk was a petty official who enjoyed making people wait.
= 那个办事员是个爱让人等着的小官僚。
a petty official = 小官僚

⑤ She was tired of the pettiness of office quarrels about who sat where.
= 办公室里为谁坐哪儿争来争去的那种小气，她受够了。
pettiness = 小气、琐碎
⑥ The stamps and the tea are paid for out of petty cash.

⑦ His grandfather served as a petty officer on a destroyer during the war.
