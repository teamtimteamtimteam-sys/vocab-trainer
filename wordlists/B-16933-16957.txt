pictorial
/pɪkˈtɔːriəl/
核心：形容词：用图画表示的、有插图的；绘画的。多作定语。名词：画报，可数。副词 pictorially，并入本条。
① The book is a pictorial history of the railway, with a photograph on every page.
= 那本书是一部图说铁路史，每页都有照片。
a pictorial history = 图说史
② The children made a pictorial record of the school trip.
= 孩子们用图画记录了那次学校组织的出游。
a pictorial record = 图画记录
③ The results are shown pictorially in the chart on the next page.
= 结果用图在下一页的图表里表示出来了。
pictorially = 用图画地

picture
/ˈpɪktʃə(r)/
核心：本义是「画出来、照出来的样子」。名词，可数：图画、画；照片：take a picture；（电视、屏幕上的）图像、画面；
（脑子里的）印象、（用话勾出的）描述：paint a picture of；状况、局面：the overall picture；（旧式）电影，英式的 the pictures 是电影院。
be the picture of 是活脱脱是……的样子：the picture of health。
动词：想象、设想：picture somebody doing something；（多用被动）在照片、图画里显示。
习语：get the picture 是（口语）明白了；put、keep somebody in the picture 是让某人了解情况；the big picture 是全局、大局；
out of the picture 是不再参与、出局；a picture of health 是健康的化身；a picture is worth a thousand words 是一图胜千言；pretty as a picture 是美得像幅画。
复合词：picture book 是（给孩子的）图画书；picture frame 是画框、相框；picture postcard 是风景明信片，作定语指风景如画的；
picture rail 是挂画线：墙上高处钉的一条木条，用来挂画；picture window 是观景大窗；picture-perfect 是（美式）完美如画的；
picture messaging 是彩信、用手机发图片。并入本条。
① The children drew pictures of their houses with coloured pencils.
= 孩子们用彩色铅笔画了自己家的房子。
draw a picture = 画一幅画
② She took a picture of the cathedral with her phone.
= 她用手机给大教堂拍了张照。
take a picture = 拍照
③ The picture on the old television keeps jumping.
= 那台旧电视的图像老是跳。
the picture = 电视图像
④ The report paints a gloomy picture of life in the camps.
= 那份报告把营地里的生活描述得一片暗淡。
paint a picture of something = 描述某事
⑤ I have a clear picture in my mind of the house where I was born.
= 我出生的那座房子，我脑子里记得清清楚楚。
a picture in your mind = 脑子里的印象
⑥ The overall picture for farmers is better than it was a year ago.
= 农民的总体境况比一年前好了。
the overall picture = 总体状况
⑦ We used to go to the pictures every Saturday afternoon.
= 从前我们每个星期六下午都去看电影。
the pictures = 电影院（英式，旧式）
⑧ Sitting in the garden with her book, she was the picture of contentment.
= 她拿着书坐在花园里，一副心满意足的样子。
the picture of something = 活脱脱某种样子
⑨ I can still picture him standing at the gate with his suitcase.
= 我至今还能想起他拎着箱子站在院门口的样子。
picture somebody doing something = 想象某人在做某事
⑩ The minister is pictured here with the workers at the opening of the factory.
= 这张照片是部长在工厂开业时跟工人们的合影。
be pictured = 出现在照片里
⑪ You do not need to explain any more, because I get the picture.
= 不用再解释了，我明白了。
get the picture = 明白了
⑫ Keep me in the picture while I am away, and ring if anything changes.
= 我不在的时候随时让我知道情况，有变化就打电话。
in the picture = 了解情况
keep somebody in the picture = 让某人了解情况
⑬ We spend so long on details that we lose sight of the big picture.
= 我们在细节上花的工夫太多，反而看不见大局了。
the big picture = 全局、大局
⑭ With the favourite injured and out of the picture, the race is wide open.
= 夺冠热门受伤出局，这场比赛谁赢都有可能。
out of the picture = 出局、不再参与
⑮ At ninety she is still a picture of health and walks two miles a day.
= 九十岁了，她还硬朗得很，每天走两英里。
a picture of health = 健康的化身
⑯ We added a map to the report, since a picture is worth a thousand words.
= 我们给报告加了一张地图，一图胜千言嘛。
a picture is worth a thousand words = 一图胜千言
⑰ The bride looked as pretty as a picture in her mother's dress.
= 新娘穿着她母亲的礼服，美得像幅画。
pretty as a picture = 美得像幅画
⑱ She read the same picture book to her son every night for a year.
= 她每天晚上给儿子念同一本图画书，念了一年。
a picture book = 图画书
⑲ He put the photograph in a silver picture frame on the piano.
= 他把那张照片装进银相框，摆在钢琴上。
a picture frame = 相框、画框
⑳ The village is a picture postcard place of white cottages and roses.

picture messaging = 彩信

picturesque
/ˌpɪktʃəˈresk/
核心：两支。一是（地方）风景如画的：好看，尤其是带点古朴的那种；二是（语言）生动的、形象的。副词 picturesquely，并入本条。
① We stayed in a picturesque fishing village on the south coast.
= 我们住在南岸的一个风景如画的渔村里。
a picturesque village = 风景如画的村子
② The old sailor gave a picturesque account of his years at sea.
= 老水手把他在海上的那些年讲得活灵活现。
a picturesque account = 生动的讲述
③ The castle is picturesquely situated on a rock above the river.
= 那座城堡坐落在河上方的一块岩石上，景色如画。
picturesquely = 风景如画地
别跟 picaresque（流浪汉小说式的）混。

piddle
/ˈpɪdl/
核心：（旧式口语）动词：撒尿，多说孩子、小狗。名词：尿、一泡尿。美式的 piddle around 是瞎混、磨蹭。
形容词 piddling 是（口语，贬）微不足道的、小得可笑的。并入本条。
① The puppy piddled on the carpet as soon as we brought it home.
= 小狗一抱回家就在地毯上撒了泡尿。
piddle = 撒尿
② He spent the whole morning piddling around in the garage.
= 他一上午都在车库里瞎忙活。
piddle around = 瞎混、磨蹭
③ They are arguing over a piddling sum of five pounds.
= 他们在为区区五英镑争个不休。
piddling = 微不足道的

pidgin
/ˈpɪdʒɪn/
核心：洋泾浜语、混杂语：说不同语言的人为了做买卖、打交道凑出来的简化语言，词汇少、语法简单，不是任何人的母语。可数也可不可数。
pidgin English 是洋泾浜英语；口语里也指说得很蹩脚的某种语言：pidgin French。
① The traders along the coast spoke a pidgin based on English and local languages.
= 沿海的商人说的是一种以英语和当地语言为底子的混杂语。
a pidgin = 混杂语
② The sailors communicated with the islanders in pidgin English.
= 水手们用洋泾浜英语跟岛民交流。
pidgin English = 洋泾浜英语
③ I managed to order dinner in my pidgin Italian.
= 我用蹩脚的意大利语总算把晚饭点了。
pidgin Italian = 蹩脚的意大利语
一种 pidgin 成了下一代人的母语，就叫 creole。

pie
/paɪ/
核心：馅饼、派：把水果、肉、蔬菜包在酥皮里烤成的食物，英式的多是上面也盖一层皮，可数也可不可数：apple pie、a meat pie。
pie crust 是馅饼的酥皮。pie chart 是饼图、圆形分格统计图。
习语：pie in the sky 是不能兑现的空头许诺、画饼；easy as pie 是（口语）容易得很；eat humble pie 是低头认错，美式说 eat crow；
a piece、a slice、a share of the pie 是（钱、好处里的）一份；have a finger in every pie 是事事都插一手；as nice、sweet as pie 是客气得不得了。
口语的 pie-eyed 是喝得醉眼蒙眬的。并入本条。
① She baked an apple pie for the family lunch on Sunday.
= 她为星期天的家庭午饭烤了一个苹果派。
an apple pie = 苹果派
② Would you like another slice of pie with your coffee?
= 喝咖啡要不要再来一块派？
a slice of pie = 一块派
③ The filling was delicious, but the pie crust was as hard as a board.
= 馅很好吃，可馅饼皮硬得像块木板。
pie crust = 馅饼的酥皮
④ The pie chart shows how the council spends each pound of tax.
= 那张饼图显示镇议会怎样花每一英镑税款。
a pie chart = 饼图
⑤ The promise of a new hospital turned out to be pie in the sky.
= 建新医院的许诺原来是画饼。
pie in the sky = 空头许诺、画饼
⑥ Setting up the printer was easy as pie once I had read the instructions.
= 看了说明书，装那台打印机容易得很。
easy as pie = 容易得很
⑦ The newspaper had to eat humble pie and print an apology on the front page.
= 那份报纸只好低头认错，在头版登了道歉。
eat humble pie = 低头认错
⑧ Every department wants a bigger piece of the pie when the budget is drawn up.
= 一到编预算，每个部门都想多分一块。
a piece of the pie = （好处里的）一份
⑨ She has a finger in every pie in the village, from the choir to the football club.
= 村里的事她样样插一手，从唱诗班到足球俱乐部。
have a finger in every pie = 事事都插一手
⑩ He was as nice as pie to me until I asked for my money back.
= 他对我客气得不得了，直到我要他退钱。
as nice as pie = 客气得不得了
⑪ By midnight the best man was thoroughly pie-eyed.
= 到半夜，伴郎已经喝得醉眼蒙眬。
pie-eyed = 喝得醉眼蒙眬的

piebald
/ˈpaɪbɔːld/

① The girl rode a small piebald pony along the edge of the field.
= 那姑娘骑着一匹黑白花的小马沿田边走。

piece
/piːs/
核心：本义是「从整体上分出来、或拼成整体的一块」。名词，可数：
一块、一片、一段：a piece of cake、a piece of string；（一套东西里的）一件、（机器的）部件：a jigsaw with a thousand pieces；in pieces 是碎了、拆散了；
用来数不可数名词：a piece of advice、furniture、information、luck、news、paper；
（艺术、音乐、文字的）一件作品、一篇文章：a piece of music、a piece in the paper；棋子；硬币：a ten-pence piece，piece of eight 是西班牙古银币；
（美式俚语）枪。
动词只用在 piece together 里：拼合、拼凑出（事情的来龙去脉）。
习语：a piece of cake 是轻而易举的事；go to pieces、fall to pieces 是（人）垮掉、崩溃，fall to pieces 也指（东西）散架、（计划）落空；
in one piece 是完好无损、平安；give somebody a piece of your mind 是把某人数落一顿；piece by piece 是一件一件地、一点一点地；
a piece of the action 是（在赚钱、热闹的事里）分一份；say your piece 是把要说的话说完；be of a piece 是（正式）一脉相承、是一路的；
bits and pieces 是零零碎碎的东西；pick up the pieces 是收拾残局；take something to pieces 是把某物拆开。
piece rate 是计件工资的单价。并入本条。
① She cut the cake into eight pieces and gave the largest to her brother.
= 她把蛋糕切成八块，把最大的一块给了弟弟。
a piece of cake = 一块蛋糕
② He tied the gate shut with a piece of string.
= 他用一截绳子把院门拴上。
a piece of string = 一截绳子
③ The children finished a jigsaw with five hundred pieces in one afternoon.
= 孩子们一个下午就拼完了一副五百块的拼图。
a jigsaw with five hundred pieces = 五百块的拼图
④ The vase lay in pieces on the kitchen floor.
= 花瓶在厨房地板上摔成了碎片。
in pieces = 碎了
⑤ Let me give you a piece of advice before you sign anything.
= 签任何东西之前，让我给你一句忠告。
a piece of advice = 一句忠告
advice 不可数，论「条」就借 piece。
⑥ The only piece of furniture in the room was an iron bed.
= 屋里唯一的一件家具是张铁床。
a piece of furniture = 一件家具
a piece of news = 一条消息
⑦ The orchestra opened the concert with a short piece by Mozart.
= 乐队以莫扎特的一支短曲开场。
a piece of music = 一支乐曲
⑧ She wrote a piece about village schools for the Sunday paper.
= 她给星期日的报纸写了一篇谈乡村学校的文章。
a piece about something = 一篇谈某事的文章
⑨ He lost two pieces in the first ten moves of the game.
= 那盘棋头十步他就丢了两个子。
a chess piece = 棋子
⑩ The machine takes only fifty-pence pieces.
= 那台机器只收五十便士的硬币。
a fifty-pence piece = 五十便士硬币
⑪ The pirates in the story bury a chest of pieces of eight on the island.
= 故事里的海盗在岛上埋了一箱西班牙古银币。
a piece of eight = 西班牙古银币
⑫ The police slowly pieced together what had happened that night.
= 警方慢慢拼凑出那天夜里发生的事。
piece together = 拼合、拼凑出
piece something together = 把某事拼凑出来
⑬ The exam was a piece of cake, and I finished half an hour early.
= 那场考试轻而易举，我提前半个钟头就做完了。
a piece of cake = 轻而易举的事
跟第一条同形：那条是真蛋糕，这条是习语。
⑭ He went to pieces after his wife died and could not work for a year.
= 妻子去世后他整个人垮了，一年没法工作。
go to pieces = 垮掉、崩溃
⑮ The old chair fell to pieces when the mover picked it up.
= 搬运工一搬，那把旧椅子就散了架。
fall to pieces = 散架、垮掉
⑯ The parcel arrived in one piece despite the long journey.

in one piece = 完好无损
⑰ I am going to give that builder a piece of my mind when he comes back.

⑱ They took the old barn down and moved it piece by piece to the museum.

⑲ When the shop began to make money, his cousins wanted a piece of the action.

⑳ Let her say her piece before anyone else speaks.

㉑ The new law is of a piece with the government's other attacks on the unions.

bits and pieces = 零零碎碎的东西
㉓ He took the clock to pieces and could not put it together again.
= 他把钟拆了，却装不回去。
take something to pieces = 把某物拆开
㉔ The workers are paid a piece rate of forty pence for each shirt.
= 工人按件计酬，每件衬衫四十便士。

㉕ The article was a puff piece that read as if the firm had written it.
= 那篇文章是吹捧稿，读着像那家公司自己写的。
a puff piece = 吹捧的文章

pièce de résistance
/piˌes də reˈzɪstɒns/
核心：最出色的一件、压轴的东西：一桌菜里最拿得出手的那道，一场表演、一批作品里最精彩的那一件。单数；来自法语。
① The pièce de résistance of the dinner was a whole roast lamb carried in on a board.
= 那顿饭的重头戏是用木板抬上来的一整只烤羊。
the pièce de résistance = 重头戏、压轴的东西
② The collection's pièce de résistance is a clock made for a French king.
= 那批藏品里最出色的一件是给一位法国国王做的钟。
the collection's pièce de résistance = 藏品里最出色的一件

piecemeal
/ˈpiːsmiːl/
核心：（多含贬义）形容词：零敲碎打的、一点一点做的：没有通盘的计划。副词：零敲碎打地、一点一点地。
① The changes were introduced in a piecemeal fashion over ten years.
= 那些改动是十年里零敲碎打地搞起来的。
in a piecemeal fashion = 零敲碎打地
② The estate was sold off piecemeal to pay the family's debts.
= 为了还那家人的债，庄园被一块一块地卖掉了。
sell something off piecemeal = 一块一块地卖掉
-meal 是古词尾，意思是「一次一份」。

piecework
/ˈpiːswɜːk/
核心：计件工作：按做出来的件数而不是按工时付钱的活儿。不可数。做这种活的人叫 pieceworker，并入本条。
① She does piecework at home, sewing collars for a shirt factory.
= 她在家做计件活，给一家衬衫厂缝领子。
do piecework = 做计件活
② On piecework a fast picker can earn twice the daily wage.
= 按件计酬的话，摘得快的人能挣到日工资的两倍。
on piecework = 按件计酬
③ The pieceworkers are paid at the end of each week for what they have finished.
= 计件工每到周末按做完的件数领钱。
a pieceworker = 计件工

pied
/paɪd/

① A pied wagtail was running about on the lawn after flies.
= 一只白鹡鸰在草坪上跑来跑去追苍蝇。
a pied wagtail = 白鹡鸰
② In the legend the Pied Piper leads the children away from the town.
= 传说里，花衣吹笛人把孩子们从镇上引走了。
the Pied Piper = 花衣吹笛人

pied-à-terre
/ˌpjeɪd ɑː ˈteə(r)/
核心：（在城里另置的）小住处、落脚的小公寓：主人平时住在别处，只在进城时用。可数，复数 pieds-à-terre；来自法语，字面是「脚落地」。
① They live in the country and keep a small pied-à-terre in London for the theatre.
= 他们住在乡下，在伦敦留了一处小公寓，看戏时落脚。
a pied-à-terre = 落脚的小公寓

pig
/pɪɡ/
