manipulate
/məˈnɪpjʊleɪt/
核心：（贬）操纵、摆布（人、舆论、数字）；熟练地操作、摆弄；（医）推拿、正骨。
名词 manipulation、形容词 manipulative（爱摆布人的；操作的）、名词 manipulator（操纵者）并入本条。
① He knows how to manipulate people into doing what he wants.
= 他懂得怎么摆布别人去做他想让他们做的事。
manipulate somebody into doing something = 摆布某人去做…
② The company was accused of manipulating the figures to hide its losses.
= 那家公司被指篡改数字掩盖亏损。
manipulate the figures = 篡改数字
这一支含贬义：动了手脚。
③ The pilot learns to manipulate the controls without looking at them.
= 飞行员要学会不看操纵杆就能操作。
manipulate the controls = 操作控制装置
这一支是中性的：用手熟练操作。
④ The osteopath manipulated his back to ease the pain.
= 正骨师给他的后背做了推拿以缓解疼痛。
manipulate somebody's back = 给某人后背做推拿
⑤ The film is a study of the manipulation of public opinion.
= 那部电影探讨的是对舆论的操纵。
the manipulation of something = 对…的操纵
⑥ She is a manipulative woman who turns her friends against each other.
= 她是个爱摆布人的女人，让朋友们反目成仇。
manipulative = 爱摆布人的
⑦ He is a clever manipulator of the press.
= 他很会操纵新闻界。
a manipulator = 操纵者
拉丁语 manus 是「手」。

manner
/ˈmænə(r)/
核心：方式、方法；（待人的）态度、举止；（复数 manners）礼貌、规矩；（复数）风俗习惯。
习语：good manners / bad manners（有礼貌 / 没礼貌）、table manners（餐桌礼仪）、all manner of（各种各样的）、
in a manner of speaking（可以这么说）、as if to the manner born（像天生就会似的）。
形容词 mannered（举止做作的；也作后一成分，如 well-mannered 有礼貌的）并入本条。
① She answered the question in a calm and friendly manner.
= 她以平静友好的方式回答了那个问题。
in a friendly manner = 以友好的方式
比 way 正式。
② The doctor has a kind manner that puts patients at ease.
= 那位医生态度和蔼，让病人安心。
a kind manner = 和蔼的态度
这一支用单数：待人的样子。
③ It is bad manners to talk with your mouth full.
= 嘴里含着东西说话是没礼貌的。
bad manners = 没礼貌
这一支用复数。
④ The children have very good manners and always say thank you.
= 那些孩子很有礼貌，总是说谢谢。
good manners = 有礼貌
⑤ His table manners are terrible; he eats with his elbows on the table.
= 他的餐桌礼仪糟透了，吃饭时把胳膊肘支在桌上。
table manners = 餐桌礼仪
⑥ The shop sells all manner of things, from nails to wedding cakes.
= 那家店什么都卖，从钉子到结婚蛋糕。
all manner of = 各种各样的
这里 manner 是旧义「种类」。
⑦ He is, in a manner of speaking, the father of the modern computer.
= 可以这么说，他是现代计算机之父。
in a manner of speaking = 可以这么说
⑧ She took to life in the palace as if to the manner born.
= 她适应宫里的生活，就像生来如此。
to the manner born = 像天生就会似的
⑨ He is a quiet, well-mannered boy who is liked by all his teachers.
= 他是个文静有礼的男孩，老师们都喜欢他。
well-mannered = 有礼貌的
⑩ The critics found her acting stiff and mannered.
= 评论家觉得她的表演僵硬做作。
mannered = 做作的

= 那家美术馆有一间展厅陈列十六世纪意大利的矫饰主义绘画。
mannerist paintings = 矫饰主义绘画

manoeuvre

manoeuvre your way into something = 耍手腕进入…
⑥ The new rules leave the minister very little room for manoeuvre.
= 新规定让部长几乎没有回旋余地。

⑦ The small boat is light and highly manoeuvrable.

manoeuvrable = 易操纵的
⑧ The pilot praised the manoeuvrability of the new plane.
= 飞行员称赞了新飞机的操纵性。
manoeuvrability = 操纵性
⑨ After weeks of political manoeuvring, a new leader was chosen.
= 经过几个星期的政治运作，新领袖选了出来。
political manoeuvring = 政治运作
⑩ The American newspaper reported that the driver tried to maneuver around the truck.
= 那家美国报纸报道说，司机试图绕过那辆卡车。

⑪ The advert says that the bike is light, maneuverable and easy to park.
