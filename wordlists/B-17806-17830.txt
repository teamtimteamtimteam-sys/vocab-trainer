Promethean
/prəˈmiːθiən/
核心：（文学）普罗米修斯式的：像希腊神话里盗火给人的普罗米修斯那样，敢于反抗、有创造力、不惜受苦的。首字母大写。
① The poet saw the scientist as a Promethean figure who defied the gods.
= 那位诗人把科学家看成一个违抗众神的普罗米修斯式人物。
a Promethean figure = 普罗米修斯式的人物
多放在名词前。
② Building the canal through the mountains was a Promethean task.
= 开那条穿山的运河是一项艰巨无比的壮举。
a Promethean task = 艰巨无比的壮举
也指胆子大、规模大得惊人的。

prominent
/ˈprɒmɪnənt/
核心：三支。一是重要的、著名的、杰出的；二是显眼的、突出的：一眼就看得见；三是凸出的、高起的。
副词 prominently；名词 prominence 是重要、显著、出名，不可数或单数。并入本条。
① She is a prominent lawyer who has argued several famous cases.
= 她是位著名律师，办过好几桩出名的案子。
a prominent lawyer = 著名律师
play a prominent part = 起重要作用
② The notice was put up in a prominent position by the main door.
= 那张通知贴在正门旁边一个显眼的位置。
a prominent position = 显眼的位置
a prominent place = 显眼的地方
③ He has a thin face with a prominent nose and chin.
= 他脸很瘦，鼻子和下巴都很突出。
a prominent nose = 高鼻子
prominent cheekbones = 高颧骨
④ Her photograph was prominently displayed on the wall behind the desk.
= 她的照片醒目地挂在书桌后面的墙上。
be prominently displayed = 醒目地陈列着
feature prominently = 占突出的地位
⑤ The young singer came to prominence after winning a television contest.
= 那位年轻歌手赢了一场电视比赛之后出了名。
come to prominence = 出名
give prominence to something = 突出某事

promiscuous
/prəˈmɪskjuəs/
核心：两支。一是（含贬义）性关系随便的、滥交的；二是（正式）混杂的、不加选择的。
副词 promiscuously；名词 promiscuity，不可数。并入本条。
① The article claimed that young people were more promiscuous than their parents had been.
= 那篇文章声称年轻人比父辈当年更随便。
be promiscuous = 性关系随便
promiscuous behaviour = 滥交的行为
② The shop was a promiscuous jumble of books, clocks and old furniture.
= 那家店里书、钟和旧家具乱七八糟堆在一起。
a promiscuous jumble = 乱七八糟的一堆
这一支是正式用法，少见。
③ The campaign warned teenagers about the risks of sexual promiscuity.
= 那场宣传提醒青少年当心滥交的风险。
sexual promiscuity = 滥交
promiscuity = 性关系随便
④ He quotes promiscuously from poets, pop songs and old advertisements.
= 他引用起来不分青红皂白，诗人、流行歌、老广告都引。
promiscuously = 不加选择地

promise
/ˈprɒmɪs/
核心：动词：答应、许诺、保证：promise to do、promise (somebody) that、promise somebody something；使很可能、预示：promise to be。
名词：诺言、许诺，可数：make、keep、break a promise；（会有出息、会成功的）迹象、希望，不可数：show promise。
习语：promise the moon、promise the earth 是开空头支票、许下办不到的诺；I can't promise anything 是我不敢打包票；
I promise you 是（口语）我跟你保证，用来加强语气；the Promised Land 是（圣经里上帝许给以色列人的）应许之地，也比喻盼望中的好地方。
分词 promising 作形容词：有希望的、有前途的，副词 promisingly。并入本条。
① She promised to write to her mother every week from the camp.
= 她答应每个星期从营地给母亲写信。
promise to do something = 答应做某事
② He promised me that he would never mention the matter again.
= 他向我保证再也不提这件事。
promise somebody that… = 向某人保证……
promise that… = 保证……
③ I have promised the children a trip to the zoo on Saturday.
= 我答应了孩子们星期六带他们去动物园。
promise somebody something = 答应给某人某物
promise something to somebody = 把某物许给某人
④ "I will be back before dark, I promise," she called from the gate.
= 「我天黑前一定回来，我保证。」她在院门口喊道。
I promise = 我保证
放在句末、句中都行。
⑤ It promises to be an exciting match, with both sides at full strength.
= 双方都是全部主力上场，看来会是一场精彩的比赛。
promise to be something = 看来会是……
主语是事，不是人。
⑥ The dark clouds in the west promised rain before the evening.
= 西边的乌云预示着傍晚前有雨。
promise rain = 预示有雨
promise something = 预示某事
⑦ He made a promise to his father that he would finish his studies.
= 他向父亲许下诺言，一定把书念完。
make a promise = 许下诺言
a promise to do something = 做某事的诺言
⑧ She kept her promise and came back for the dog a year later.
= 她说话算数，一年后回来接那条狗。
keep a promise = 守信、说话算数
keep your promise = 守信
⑨ He broke his promise and told the newspapers everything.
= 他食言了，把什么都告诉了报纸。
break a promise = 食言
break your promise = 食言
⑩ The boy shows great promise as a pianist and practises four hours a day.
= 那个男孩很有钢琴家的苗子，一天练四个小时。
show promise = 有出息的苗头
a young player of promise = 有前途的年轻球员
⑪ The spring sunshine brought the promise of a good harvest.
= 春天的阳光带来了丰收的希望。
the promise of something = 某事的希望
这一支不可数。
⑫ The candidates promised the moon and delivered nothing once they were elected.
= 候选人许得天花乱坠，当选之后什么也没兑现。
promise the moon = 开空头支票
promise the earth = 许下办不到的诺
⑬ I will try to get you a ticket, but I can't promise anything.
= 我尽量给你弄张票，但不敢打包票。
I can't promise anything = 我不敢打包票
话说在前头的说法。
⑭ You will not regret it, I promise you, if you take the job.
= 我跟你保证，接下这份工作你不会后悔的。
I promise you = 我跟你保证
是加强语气，不是真的在许诺。
⑮ To the people on the crowded ships, America was the Promised Land.
= 对挤在船上的那些人来说，美国就是应许之地。
the Promised Land = 应许之地
首字母大写。
⑯ She is one of the most promising young writers in the country.
= 她是国内最有前途的年轻作家之一。
promising = 有前途的
a promising start = 良好的开端
⑰ The season began promisingly, with three wins in the first four games.
= 那个赛季开局不错，头四场赢了三场。
promisingly = 有希望地
begin promisingly = 开局不错

promo

a promo = 宣传片

a promo copy = 赠阅本

promontory

① The lighthouse stands on a rocky promontory at the mouth of the bay.

a rocky promontory = 多石的岬角

= 从那个海角上，我们看得见整条海岸线。
from the promontory = 从海角上
比 headland 书面一些。

promote

promotion
/prəˈməʊʃn/

prompt
/prɒmpt/

名词，可数：（给演员的）提词；（电脑屏幕上的）提示符、提示。副词：（英式口语）准时地、整：at six o'clock prompt。

= 请准时，大巴不等任何人。
be prompt = 准时

prompt somebody to do something = 促使某人做某事
④ The minister's remarks prompted an angry response from the unions.
= 部长的话引起了工会愤怒的反应。
prompt a response = 引起反应
prompt something = 引起某事
⑤ "And then what happened?" he prompted, when she fell silent.
= 她不作声了，他提了一句：「后来呢？」
prompt = 提醒人往下说
prompt somebody = 提示某人
⑥ She forgot her lines twice and had to be prompted from the wings.
= 她忘了两次词，只好由人在侧幕提词。
be prompted = 由人提词
prompt an actor = 给演员提词
⑦ The actor dried up completely and needed a prompt.
= 那位演员彻底忘了词，得有人提一下。
a prompt = 提词
give somebody a prompt = 给某人提词
⑧ Type your password at the prompt and press the return key.
= 在提示符处输入密码，再按回车键。
at the prompt = 在提示符处
a prompt = 提示符、提示
⑨ The meeting will begin at ten o'clock prompt.
= 会议十点整开始。
at ten o'clock prompt = 十点整
这样用是英式口语，放在钟点后面。
⑩ She arrived promptly at nine and left on the stroke of five.
= 她九点准时到，五点一到就走。
promptly at nine = 九点准时

⑪ He sat down in the armchair and promptly fell asleep.
= 他在扶手椅上一坐下，立刻就睡着了。
promptly = 立刻、马上

⑫ The staff were praised for the promptness with which they dealt with the fire.
= 员工处置火情迅速，受到了表扬。
promptness = 迅速

⑬ The prompter sat hidden in a box at the front of the stage.
= 提词员坐在舞台前沿的一个小间里，观众看不见。
a prompter = 提词员
⑭ He tidied his room without any prompting from his parents.
= 没等父母催，他自己把房间收拾了。
without prompting = 不用人催
at somebody's prompting = 在某人的催促下

a pronounced limp = 明显的跛
③ The difference between the two groups was most pronounced among the youngest children.
= 两组之间的差别在最小的孩子当中最明显。
be pronounced = 明显

pronouncement
/prəˈnaʊnsmənt/

= 教皇在复活节讲话里就那个问题表了态。

pronto
/ˈprɒntəʊ/

是从西班牙语来的口语词。

pronunciation
/prəˌnʌnsiˈeɪʃn/
核心：两支。一是（一个词、一种语言的）发音、读法，可数也可不可数；二是（一个人的）发音：说得准不准，单数或不可数。
拼写里是 -nun-，不是 -noun-；读音也是 /nʌn/。
① The dictionary gives the British and the American pronunciation of every word.
= 那本词典每个词都标出英式和美式的读音。
the pronunciation of a word = 一个词的读音
a guide to pronunciation = 发音指南
② There are two pronunciations of "either", and both are correct.
= 「either」有两种读法，都对。
two pronunciations = 两种读法
这样用可数。
③ Her grammar is good, but her pronunciation needs a lot of work.
= 她语法不错，可是发音还得下大功夫。
her pronunciation = 她的发音
improve your pronunciation = 改进发音
④ The old newsreaders on the radio all spoke with received pronunciation.
= 过去电台的新闻播音员说的都是英国标准发音。
received pronunciation = （英国的）标准发音
缩写 RP。

proof
/pruːf/
核心：名词：证据、证明，不可数：proof of、proof that；（数学的）证明、（验算的）过程，可数；（印刷的）校样，可数，多用复数；
（烈酒的）标准酒精度，不可数。形容词：proof against 是（正式）抵挡得住……的；也接在名词后构成复合词：waterproof、bulletproof。
动词：给（布料）做防水处理；也等于 proofread。
习语：the proof of the pudding (is in the eating) 是东西好不好要试了才知道；living proof 是活生生的证明；
proof positive 是确证、铁证。
① The police have no proof that he was in the house that night.
= 警方没有证据证明他那天夜里在那栋房子里。
proof that… = ……的证据
have no proof = 没有证据
② You will need to bring proof of your age, such as a passport.
= 你得带上年龄证明，比如护照。
proof of something = 某事的证明
proof of identity = 身份证明
③ The teacher asked her to write out the proof on the board.
= 老师让她把证明过程写在黑板上。
a proof = （数学的）证明
这一支可数。
④ The author is checking the proofs of her new novel this week.
= 那位作家这个星期在看她新小说的校样。
the proofs = 校样
check the proofs = 看校样
⑤ The rum is seventy per cent proof and should be drunk with care.
= 那种朗姆酒标准酒精度七十，得悠着点喝。
seventy per cent proof = 标准酒精度七十
英美的算法不一样。
⑥ No lock is proof against a thief with enough time and the right tools.
= 贼只要有足够的时间和合适的工具，没有哪把锁挡得住。
be proof against something = 抵挡得住某物
这一支是正式用法。
⑦ The windows of the car are bulletproof and cannot be opened.
= 那辆车的窗玻璃是防弹的，打不开。
bulletproof = 防弹的
childproof = 小孩弄不开的
⑧ The plan looks good on paper, but the proof of the pudding is in the eating.
= 那个计划纸面上看着不错，可行不行得试了才知道。
the proof of the pudding = 好不好试了才知道
全句是 the proof of the pudding is in the eating。
⑨ She is living proof that you can start a new career at sixty.
= 她就是活生生的证明：六十岁照样可以开始新的事业。
living proof = 活生生的证明
be living proof of something = 是某事活生生的证明
⑩ The fingerprints on the glass were proof positive that he had been there.
= 玻璃杯上的指纹是他到过那里的铁证。
proof positive = 确证、铁证
形容词放在后面，是固定说法。

proofread
/ˈpruːfriːd/
核心：校对：把稿子、校样读一遍，找出并改正错字。及物也可不及物；过去式、过去分词 proofread，读 /ˈpruːfred/。
做这件事的人叫 proofreader。并入本条。
① Always proofread your essay before you hand it in to the teacher.
= 作文交给老师之前，一定要校对一遍。
proofread an essay = 校对作文
proofread something = 校对某物
② The letter had clearly not been proofread, for it was full of mistakes.
= 那封信显然没校对过，满篇都是错。
be proofread = 经过校对
过去分词拼法不变，读音变。
③ She works at home as a proofreader for a firm that publishes law books.
= 她在家给一家出法律书的公司做校对。
a proofreader = 校对员
work as a proofreader = 做校对

prop
/prɒp/
核心：名词，可数：支柱、支撑物；比喻靠山、精神支柱；（戏剧、电影的）道具，多用复数；（橄榄球）支柱前锋；（口语）螺旋桨：propeller 的截短。
动词：把……靠在、支在：prop something against、on；用东西把……撑住：prop the door open。过去式 propped。
（口语）props 是应得的称赞、敬意：give somebody props。
① The roof of the old mine was held up by wooden props.
= 那座旧矿井的顶是用木头支柱撑着的。
a wooden prop = 木头支柱
a pit prop = 坑木
② After his wife died, his work became his only prop.
= 妻子死后，工作成了他唯一的精神支柱。
a prop = 靠山、精神支柱
这是比喻的用法。
③ She is in charge of the props for the school play this term.
= 这学期学校那出戏的道具归她管。
the props = 道具
a stage prop = 舞台道具
④ He propped his bicycle against the wall and went into the shop.
= 他把自行车靠在墙上，进了商店。
prop something against something = 把某物靠在某物上
prop something on something = 把某物支在某物上
⑤ She propped the door open with a chair to let the air in.
= 她用一把椅子把门撑开，好让空气进来。
prop the door open = 把门撑开
prop something open = 把某物撑开
⑥ He propped himself up on one elbow and looked at the clock.
= 他用一只胳膊肘撑起身子，看了看钟。
prop yourself up = 撑起身子
prop up = 撑住、支起
⑦ The government spent millions to prop up the failing steel industry.

⑧ You have to give her props for finishing the race with a broken toe.

give somebody props = 佩服某人、称赞某人
这是美式口语。

propaganda
/ˌprɒpəˈɡændə/
核心：（多含贬义）宣传、鼓吹：为了让人接受一种政治主张而散布的、常常片面或不实的说法。不可数。
搞宣传的人叫 propagandist，可数；动词 propagandize 是（正式，含贬义）搞宣传，英式也拼作 propagandise。并入本条。
① The film was a piece of enemy propaganda made to frighten the troops.
= 那部片子是敌方的宣传品，拍来吓唬士兵的。
enemy propaganda = 敌方的宣传
a piece of propaganda = 一件宣传品
② The party ran a propaganda campaign in the newspapers before the vote.
= 投票之前，那个党在报上搞了一场宣传攻势。
a propaganda campaign = 宣传攻势
这个词带贬义：片面、不尽不实。
③ He worked as a propagandist for the regime throughout the war.
= 整个战争期间他都在替那个政权搞宣传。
a propagandist = 搞宣传的人
④ The teachers were accused of propagandizing in the classroom.
= 那些教师被指责在课堂上搞宣传。
propagandize = 搞宣传
这个动词少见，带贬义。

propagate
/ˈprɒpəɡeɪt/
核心：三支。一是（正式）传播、宣传（思想、信仰、消息），及物；二是繁殖（植物）、（植物）繁殖，及物也可不及物；
三是（物理）（波、声、光）传播。名词 propagation，不可数；繁殖幼苗用的保温育苗箱叫 propagator。并入本条。
① The group uses the internet to propagate its ideas among the young.
= 那个团体利用互联网在年轻人当中传播自己的主张。
propagate ideas = 传播主张
propagate a belief = 传播一种信仰
② The plant is easy to propagate from cuttings taken in the spring.
= 那种植物用春天剪的插条很容易繁殖。
propagate a plant = 繁殖一种植物
propagate from cuttings = 用插条繁殖
③ Sound waves propagate more quickly through water than through air.
= 声波在水里比在空气里传得快。
waves propagate = 波传播
这是物理学里的说法。
④ The book explains the propagation of roses by grafting.
= 那本书讲怎样用嫁接繁殖玫瑰。
the propagation of something = 某物的繁殖、传播
⑤ She raises her tomato seedlings in a heated propagator on the windowsill.
= 她在窗台上一个带加热的育苗箱里育番茄苗。
a propagator = 育苗箱
