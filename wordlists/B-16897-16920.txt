phrasing
/ˈfreɪzɪŋ/
核心：两支，不可数。一是措辞、表达的方式；二是（音乐）乐句的划分与处理：把一段旋律分成一句一句来奏、来唱的方式。
① The phrasing of the question made it hard to answer with a simple yes or no.
= 那个问题的问法让人很难用一个「是」或「不是」来回答。
the phrasing of something = 某话的措辞
② The singer was admired for her delicate phrasing of the old songs.
= 那位歌手处理老歌的乐句细腻，受人称道。
delicate phrasing = 细腻的乐句处理

phreaking
/ˈfriːkɪŋ/
核心：（口语）盗打电话、侵入电话系统：用电子手段骗过电话网，免费打电话或窃取服务。不可数。干这事的人叫 phreaker，并入本条。
① In the seventies phreaking was done with a toy whistle that copied the tone of the exchange.
= 七十年代，盗打电话靠的是一只能模仿交换机音调的玩具哨子。
phreaking = 盗打电话
② Several famous programmers began as phreakers in their teens.
= 好几位有名的程序员十几岁时是从盗打电话起家的。
a phreaker = 盗打电话的人
由 phone 和 freak 拼成。

phrenology
/frəˈnɒlədʒi/
核心：颅相学：十九世纪流行的一种学说，以为摸头骨的形状就能判断一个人的性格与才能，如今已被否定。不可数。
相信、从事这一套的人叫 phrenologist，并入本条。
① Phrenology was taken seriously by doctors and judges in the eighteen thirties.
= 一八三〇年代，医生和法官都把颅相学当回事。
phrenology = 颅相学
② The museum has a china head marked with the regions used in phrenology.
= 博物馆里有一个瓷头，上面标着颅相学用的各个区域。
used in phrenology = 颅相学用的
③ A travelling phrenologist felt the bumps on her skull and promised her a long life.
= 一个走江湖的颅相师摸了摸她头骨上的隆起，许她长命百岁。
a phrenologist = 颅相师

phwoah
/fwɔː/

① "Phwoah, look at him!" shouted one of the girls from the bus.
= 公共汽车上有个姑娘喊：「哇噻，瞧他！」
Phwoah! = 哇噻！
② The tabloid printed "Phwoah!" in huge letters above the photograph.
= 那份小报在照片上方用大字印了个「哇噻！」
print "Phwoah!" = 印上「哇噻！」
只见于小报和玩笑话。

phylum

① Insects, spiders and crabs all belong to the same phylum.
= 昆虫、蜘蛛和螃蟹都属于同一个门。
the same phylum = 同一个门

= 动物界分成大约三十五个门。
phyla = 门（复数）
植物的同一级叫 division。

physical
/ˈfɪzɪkl/
核心：本义是「属于身体、属于实物」，跟精神的、抽象的相对。形容词：身体的、肉体的：physical health、physical exercise；
物质的、有形的、实际存在的，只作定语：the physical world、physical evidence；自然规律的、物理的，只作定语：a physical impossibility；
（运动、人）动作粗野的、身体碰撞多的：get physical 是动起手来；也指爱用身体接触表达感情的；性的、肉体上的：physical attraction。
名词：体格检查，可数，也说 physical examination。
副词 physically：身体上；实际上、根本：physically impossible。名词 physicality 是身体性、体力上的冲击力，不可数。
复合词：physical education 是体育（课），简称 PE，美式口语说 phys. ed.；physical training 是体能训练；
physical therapy 是（美式）理疗，做理疗的叫 physical therapist；physical geography 是自然地理；physical science 是自然科学里研究无生命物质的那部分：物理、化学等。并入本条。
① Regular physical exercise is as important as a good diet.
= 经常锻炼身体跟吃得好一样要紧。
physical exercise = 体育锻炼
physical health = 身体健康
② He is in good physical condition for a man of seventy.
= 就一个七十岁的人来说，他身体状况不错。
physical condition = 身体状况
③ The police found no physical evidence that anyone had broken in.
= 警方没找到有人闯入的实物证据。
physical evidence = 实物证据
指摸得着的，跟证词相对。
④ The shop has closed its physical stores and now sells only on the Internet.
= 那家店关掉了实体店，如今只在网上卖。
physical stores = 实体店
⑤ It is a physical impossibility to be in two places at once.
= 一个人同时在两个地方，这在物理上根本不可能。
a physical impossibility = 物理上根本不可能的事
⑥ Rugby is a very physical game, and injuries are common.
= 橄榄球是身体碰撞很多的运动，受伤是常事。
a physical game = 身体碰撞多的运动
⑦ The argument got physical, and a waiter had to pull the two men apart.
= 争吵动起了手，一个服务员只好把两人拉开。
get physical = 动起手来
⑧ She comes from a very physical family in which everyone hugs everyone.
= 她家里人都爱搂搂抱抱，见谁抱谁。
a physical family = 爱用身体接触表达感情的一家人
⑨ There was a strong physical attraction between them from the first meeting.
= 两人头一回见面，彼此就有强烈的肉体吸引。
physical attraction = 肉体上的吸引
⑩ All new pilots must pass a physical before they are allowed to fly.
= 新飞行员都得通过体检才准上天。
a physical = 体格检查
⑪ She felt physically sick when she saw the bill.
= 她一看到账单，真觉得要吐。
physically sick = 身体上觉得恶心
⑫ It is physically impossible to finish the job in two days.
= 两天干完那个活，根本办不到。
physically impossible = 根本办不到
⑬ Critics praised the physicality of his performance as the boxer.
= 评论家称赞他演拳击手时那股身体的冲击力。
physicality = 身体性、体力上的冲击力
⑭ The children have physical education twice a week in the hall.
= 孩子们每星期在礼堂上两节体育课。
physical education = 体育（课）
phys. ed. = 体育课（美式口语）
⑮ The recruits begin each day with an hour of physical training.
= 新兵每天以一个钟头的体能训练开始。
physical training = 体能训练
⑯ After the operation he needed six weeks of physical therapy.
= 手术后他需要做六个星期的理疗。
physical therapy = 理疗
a physical therapist = 理疗师
⑰ The first half of the course covers physical geography: rivers, mountains and climate.
= 课程的前一半讲自然地理：河流、山脉和气候。
physical geography = 自然地理
⑱ She preferred the physical sciences to biology at school.
= 上学时，比起生物她更喜欢物理、化学这类学科。
the physical sciences = 物理、化学一类学科
a physical science = 一门研究无生命物质的学科

physician
/fɪˈzɪʃn/
核心：医生，尤指内科医生：跟 surgeon（外科医生）相对。可数；美式的正式说法，英式里显得旧，日常都说 doctor。
美国的 physician's assistant 是医师助理：受过训练、在医生指导下看病的人。并入本条。
① You should consult your physician before starting the diet.
= 开始那套节食办法之前，应当先问问你的医生。
consult your physician = 请教医生
② Her grandfather was physician to the royal family for twenty years.
= 她爷爷给王室当了二十年御医。

③ In the country clinic most patients are seen by a physician's assistant.
= 在那家乡村诊所，多数病人是由医师助理看的。
a physician's assistant = 医师助理

physiology
/ˌfɪziˈɒlədʒi/
核心：两支，不可数。一是生理学：研究活的生物体及其各部分怎样运转的学问；二是（某种生物、某个器官的）生理机能。
形容词 physiological，副词 physiologically；研究者叫 physiologist。并入本条。
① She won a prize for her research in the physiology of the heart.
= 她凭心脏生理方面的研究得了奖。
the physiology of the heart = 心脏生理
② The book compares the physiology of birds with that of reptiles.
= 那本书把鸟类的生理机能跟爬行动物的作比较。
the physiology of birds = 鸟类的生理机能
③ Fear produces physiological changes such as a faster heartbeat.
= 恐惧会引起心跳加快之类的生理变化。
physiological changes = 生理变化
④ The two species look alike but are physiologically very different.
= 那两个物种样子相似，生理上却大不相同。
physiologically = 生理上
⑤ A sports physiologist tested the runners on a treadmill.
= 一位运动生理学家在跑步机上给那些跑步选手做了测试。
a physiologist = 生理学家
别跟 psychology（心理学）混。

/ˌfɪziəʊˈθerəpi/

理疗师叫 physiotherapist。并入本条。
① He needed three months of physiotherapy after breaking his leg.
= 他摔断腿以后做了三个月理疗。
three months of physiotherapy = 三个月理疗
② The hospital offers physiotherapy to patients who have had a stroke.
= 那家医院给中风的病人做理疗。
offer physiotherapy = 提供理疗
③ The physiotherapist showed her some exercises to strengthen her back.
= 理疗师教了她几个加强背部力量的动作。
a physiotherapist = 理疗师

pi
/paɪ/

begin pianissimo = 以极弱开头

piano
