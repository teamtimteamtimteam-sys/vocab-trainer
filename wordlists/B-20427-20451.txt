self-control
/ˌself kənˈtrəʊl/
核心：名词，不可数：自制力、自我控制：lose your self-control、show self-control。
① It took a lot of self-control not to shout at the rude customer in front of everyone.
= 当着大家的面忍住不冲那个无礼的顾客嚷嚷，需要很强的自制力。
self-control = 自制力
② He lost his self-control and hit the man who had insulted his wife.
= 他失去了自制，打了那个侮辱他妻子的人。
lose your self-control = 失去自制

/ˌself kənˈtrəʊld/

be self-controlled = 有自制力
