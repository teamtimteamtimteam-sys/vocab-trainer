seismologist
/saɪzˈmɒlədʒɪst/
核心：名词，可数：地震学家。
① Seismologists say that a large earthquake could hit the city at any time in the next fifty years.
= 地震学家说，未来五十年里那座城市随时可能发生大地震。
seismologists = 地震学家
② The seismologist explained how the movement of the rocks deep underground causes earthquakes.
= 那位地震学家解释了地下深处岩石的移动是如何引发地震的。
a seismologist = 地震学家

seismology
/saɪzˈmɒlədʒi/

① She studied seismology at university because she grew up in a city that often had earthquakes.
= 她在大学学地震学，因为她从小生活在一座经常发生地震的城市。
study seismology = 学地震学
seismology = 地震学
② Advances in seismology have made it easier to build houses that can survive earthquakes.
= 地震学的进步让人们更容易建造能抗震的房屋。
advances in seismology = 地震学的进步

seize
/siːz/
核心：动词：（突然用力）抓住、夺取：seize somebody by the arm；（武力）夺取、占领：seize power、seize control；（警方、海关）没收、查获：seize drugs；抓住（机会）：seize the opportunity；（情绪）突然侵袭：be seized by panic。和 hold 的分别：hold 是持续地握着，seize 是一下子用力抓住。
习语：seize the day 是及时行乐、抓住今天。短语动词：seize on / seize upon 是抓住（机会、借口、错误）不放；seize up 是（机器、肌肉）卡住、僵住。名词 seizure 另立条。
① The man seized her bag and ran off down the street before she could shout.
= 那个男人一把抓过她的包，没等她喊出声就顺着街跑掉了。
seize = 抓住
seize = 突然用力抓住
近义对照：
hold = 握住（持续）
② The army seized power in the middle of the night and closed the airport.
= 军队在半夜夺取了政权，关闭了机场。
seize power = 夺取政权
③ Customs officers seized a large amount of drugs hidden in the back of a lorry.
= 海关人员查获了藏在一辆卡车后部的大量毒品。
seize drugs = 查获毒品
④ When she was offered a job in Paris, she seized the opportunity at once.
= 有人给她提供一份在巴黎的工作，她马上抓住了这个机会。
seize the opportunity = 抓住机会
⑤ Don't wait until you retire to travel; seize the day and go now.
= 别等退休了才去旅行，抓住今天，现在就去。
seize the day = 及时行乐
⑥ The newspapers seized on the minister's mistake and wrote about it for a week.
= 报纸抓住那位部长的失误不放，连写了一个星期。
seize on something = 抓住某事不放
⑦ The opposition seized upon the figures as proof that the government had failed.
= 反对党抓住那些数字，把它们当作政府失败的证据。
seize upon something = 抓住某事（作为把柄）
⑧ The old engine seized up halfway up the hill, and we had to call for help.
= 那台旧发动机爬坡爬到一半卡死了，我们只好打电话求救。
seize up = （机器、肌肉）卡住、僵住

seldom
/ˈseldəm/
核心：副词：很少、不常：比 rarely 更书面，放在实义动词前、be 动词后。seldom if ever 是极少、几乎从不。
① My grandfather seldom watches television; he prefers to read or work in the garden.
= 我祖父很少看电视，他更喜欢看书或在花园里干活。
seldom = 很少
② The road is seldom used now that the new motorway has been built.
= 新高速公路建成后，那条路就很少有人走了。
be seldom used = 很少使用
③ She seldom if ever forgets a birthday, so she was upset when she forgot mine.
= 她几乎从不忘记谁的生日，所以忘了我的生日她很难过。
seldom if ever = 几乎从不

select
/sɪˈlekt/
核心：动词：（仔细地）挑选、选拔：select somebody for something、select from。和 choose、pick 的分别：choose 是通用的「选择」，pick 是口语的「挑」，select 正式，强调精挑细选。形容词：精选的、高级的、只供少数人的：a select group。名词 selection、形容词 selective 另立条。
① She was selected to play for the national team at the age of seventeen.
= 她十七岁就入选国家队。
select = 精挑细选（正式）
be selected for something = 入选某事
近义对照：
choose = 选择（通用）
pick = 挑（口语）
② Select a file from the list and click on the button to open it.
= 从列表中选择一个文件，点击按钮打开它。
select = 选择
③ Only a select group of people were invited to the private dinner with the prince.
= 只有少数精选的人受邀与王子共进私人晚宴。
a select group = 少数精选的人
