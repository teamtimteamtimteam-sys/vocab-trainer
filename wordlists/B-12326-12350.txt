instigate
/ˈɪnstɪɡeɪt/
核心：发起、促成（调查、改革）；煽动（暴乱）。中性时是「启动」，贬义时是「挑起」。
名词 instigation（at somebody's instigation = 在某人的发起／唆使下）与发起人 instigator 并入本条。
① After the train crash, the transport minister instigated an inquiry into safety on the line.
= 火车相撞之后，交通部长就这条线路的安全问题展开了调查。
instigate an inquiry = 发起调查
② The young man was accused of instigating the riot outside the football ground.
= 那个年轻人被指控在球场外煽动暴乱。
instigate a riot = 煽动暴乱
③ The changes were made at the instigation of the new director.
= 这些改变是在新总监的推动下进行的。
at the instigation of somebody = 在某人的发起下
④ Police are studying video footage to find the instigators of Saturday night's violence.
= 警方正在查看录像，寻找周六晚上暴力事件的挑起者。
an instigator = 发起者、挑唆者

instil
/ɪnˈstɪl/
核心：逐渐灌输、培养（观念、品质）—— instil something in/into somebody。美式拼 instill。
① Good parents try to instil a sense of responsibility in their children.
= 好父母会设法培养孩子的责任感。
instil something in somebody = 向某人灌输…
② The new coach instilled confidence into the young team.
= 新教练给这支年轻的球队注入了信心。
instil confidence = 灌注信心

instinct
/ˈɪnstɪŋkt/
核心：本能、天性；直觉。两支：动物与生俱来的行为（an instinct for survival）；
人不经思考的判断（trust your instincts、gut instinct）。by instinct 是「凭本能」。
① Birds build nests by instinct, without being taught.
= 鸟凭本能筑巢，不用人教。
by instinct = 凭本能
② My first instinct was to run, but I stayed where I was.
= 我的第一反应是跑，可我还是待在原地没动。
your first instinct = 第一反应
③ She has an instinct for knowing what customers want.
= 她天生就知道顾客要什么。
an instinct for something = 对…的天赋
④ Trust your gut instinct; if the deal feels wrong, walk away.
= 相信你的直觉，要是觉得这笔交易不对劲，就别做。
gut instinct = 直觉

instinctive
/ɪnˈstɪŋktɪv/
核心：本能的、直觉的 —— 不经思考就做出的。副词 instinctively 并入本条。
① When the car skidded, her instinctive reaction was to protect the child in the back seat.
= 车子打滑时，她的本能反应是护住后座的孩子。
an instinctive reaction = 本能反应
② When the ball flew towards him, he instinctively put his hands up to protect his face.
= 球朝他飞来时，他本能地抬起双手护住脸。
instinctively = 本能地

instinctual
/ɪnˈstɪŋktʃuəl/
核心：（正式）本能的 —— 同 instinctive，多见于心理学、生物学文字。
① Fear of snakes may be partly instinctual in humans.
= 人怕蛇可能部分出于本能。
be instinctual = 出于本能
② Psychologists study the instinctual drives that shape human behaviour.
= 心理学家研究塑造人类行为的本能驱力。
instinctual drives = 本能驱力

institute
/ˈɪnstɪtjuːt/
核心：学院、研究所、协会 —— 为某项专门目的（研究、教育、职业）设立的机构，常作名称。
动词（正式）：设立、制定（规则）；提起（诉讼）。
① After her degree, she took a research job at the Institute of Marine Biology in Plymouth.
= 拿到学位后，她在普利茅斯的海洋生物研究所找了份研究工作。
an institute = 研究所、学院
② The company instituted a new policy on working from home.
= 公司制定了一项新的居家办公政策。
institute a policy = 制定政策
③ After their son died, the family instituted legal proceedings against the hospital.
= 儿子去世后，那家人对医院提起了诉讼。
institute proceedings = 提起诉讼

institution
/ˌɪnstɪˈtjuːʃən/
核心：机构；（社会）制度、惯例；（贬）收容所。几支：大型组织，如银行、大学、医院（financial institutions）；
社会里长期形成的制度或习俗（the institution of marriage）；养老院、精神病院等收容机构（常带冷冰冰的意味）；
（谑）某人或某物成了「老字号」。形容词 institutional、副词 institutionally 与动词 institutionalize（使制度化；
把人送进收容机构）、形容词 institutionalized（制度化的；在机构里住久了失去自理能力的）、
名词 institutionalization 都并入本条。
① Banks and other financial institutions are being asked to lend more to small firms.
= 银行和其他金融机构被要求多向小企业放贷。
a financial institution = 金融机构
② Some people think the institution of marriage is out of date.
= 有些人认为婚姻制度已经过时了。
the institution of marriage = 婚姻制度
③ She spent most of her childhood in institutions after her parents died.
= 父母去世后，她童年的大部分时间都在收容机构里度过。
an institution = 收容机构
④ The café has been a local institution for fifty years.
= 那家咖啡馆五十年来一直是当地的老字号。
a local institution = 当地的老字号
谑用：一个人或店铺成了大家熟悉的固定存在。
⑤ The report found evidence of institutional racism in the police force.
= 报告发现警队里存在制度性的种族歧视。
institutional racism = 制度性种族歧视
⑥ The use of bribes had become institutionalized in the ministry.
= 行贿在那个部里已经成了制度化的惯例。
become institutionalized = 变得制度化
⑦ After twenty years in prison, he was completely institutionalized and could not cope outside.
= 坐了二十年牢，他已经完全被体制化了，出狱后应付不了外面的生活。
institutionalized = 被体制化的
⑧ Campaigners want to end the institutionalization of elderly people who could live at home.
= 活动人士希望结束把能在家生活的老人送进收容机构的做法。
the institutionalization of somebody = 把某人送进收容机构
⑨ The two banks are institutionally separate but share the same owners.
= 那两家银行在机构上是分开的，但属于同一批所有者。
institutionally = 在机构上
⑩ The court ordered that he be institutionalized for his own safety.
= 为了他自身的安全，法院下令把他送进收容机构。
institutionalize somebody = 把某人送进收容机构

instruct
/ɪnˈstrʌkt/
核心：指示、命令；教、指导；（法律）委托（律师）。几支：正式地要人做某事（instruct somebody to do）；
教授技能（instruct somebody in something）；英式法律里委托律师办案。
① The police instructed the crowd to move back from the building.
= 警察命令人群从大楼前往后退。
instruct somebody to do something = 指示某人做…
② She instructs beginners in the basics of rock climbing.
= 她教初学者攀岩的基本技术。
instruct somebody in something = 教某人…
③ The family instructed a solicitor to deal with the will.
= 那家人委托了一位律师处理遗嘱。
instruct a solicitor = （英）委托律师

instruction
/ɪnˈstrʌkʃən/
核心：指示、命令；（复数）说明书、使用指南；教学、传授。几支：
一条命令（on somebody's instructions = 奉某人之命）；产品的操作说明（follow the instructions）；
不可数的教导（instruction in something）；计算机的指令。
① Read the instructions carefully before you use the machine.
= 使用机器前请仔细阅读说明书。
the instructions = 使用说明
② The soldiers were acting on the instructions of their commander.
= 士兵们是奉指挥官之命行事的。
on the instructions of somebody = 奉某人之命
③ The school offers instruction in several foreign languages.
= 那所学校开设几门外语的课程。
instruction in something = …的教学
不可数，正式。
④ The program crashed because of a faulty instruction in the code.
= 程序崩溃是因为代码里有一条错误的指令。
an instruction = （计算机）指令

instructional

instructional materials = 教学资料

instructive
/ɪnˈstrʌktɪv/
核心：有教益的、给人启发的 —— 能让人学到东西的。副词 instructively 并入本条。
① It is instructive to compare the two reports.
= 比较这两份报告很有启发。

instructor
/ɪnˈstrʌktə(r)/
核心：教练、教员 —— 教某项实用技能的人：驾驶、游泳、滑雪、健身；美式也指大学讲师。

instrument
/ˈɪnstrəmənt/
核心：乐器；器械、仪器；手段、工具。本义是「工具」：做精细工作的器械（surgical instruments）；
测量用的仪表（the plane's instruments）；演奏的乐器（play an instrument）；
比喻达到目的的手段，或被人利用的人（an instrument of policy）。
① At her school every child learns to play at least one musical instrument.

= 几十年来，利率一直是央行政策的主要工具。
an instrument of something = …的工具、手段

instrumental

insufferable

insufficient
