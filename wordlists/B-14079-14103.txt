marriage
核心：婚姻、婚姻生活；结婚、婚礼；（比喻）紧密的结合。
习语与搭配：by marriage（姻亲关系上的）、a marriage of convenience（权宜婚姻；权宜的结合）、
marriage licence（结婚许可证，美式 marriage license）、marriage counselling（婚姻咨询）、a proposal of marriage（求婚）。
形容词 marriageable（旧：到了适婚年龄的）并入本条。
① They have had a long and happy marriage.
= 他们的婚姻长久而幸福。
a happy marriage = 幸福的婚姻
② Her first marriage ended in divorce after three years.
= 她的第一段婚姻三年后以离婚告终。
her first marriage = 她的第一段婚姻
③ The marriage took place in a small church by the sea.
= 婚礼在海边的一座小教堂举行。
the marriage takes place = 婚礼举行
这一支指仪式，日常多说 wedding。
④ He is my uncle by marriage, the husband of my mother's sister.
= 他是我的姨父，我母亲妹妹的丈夫，算是姻亲。
by marriage = 姻亲关系上的
⑤ The two parties formed a marriage of convenience to keep the government in power.
= 两党为保住政权结成了权宜的联盟。
a marriage of convenience = 权宜的结合
本指为拿居留权、财产而结的婚。
⑥ She accepted his proposal of marriage on the bridge where they had first met.
= 在他们初次相遇的那座桥上，她答应了他的求婚。
a proposal of marriage = 求婚
⑦ They collected their marriage licence from the town hall on Friday.
= 他们星期五从市政厅领了结婚许可证。
a marriage licence = 结婚许可证
⑧ The couple went to marriage counselling for six months.
= 那对夫妇做了六个月的婚姻咨询。
marriage counselling = 婚姻咨询
⑨ In the old novel, a mother has five daughters of marriageable age.
= 在那部老小说里，一位母亲有五个到了婚龄的女儿。
of marriageable age = 到了婚龄的
⑩ The film is a perfect marriage of music and pictures.
= 那部电影是音乐与画面的完美结合。
a marriage of A and B = 两者的结合
⑪ You need to show your marriage certificate when you apply for the visa.
= 申请那种签证时要出示结婚证。
a marriage certificate = 结婚证
⑫ Thousands of people marched through the city in support of marriage equality.
= 成千上万的人在城里游行，支持婚姻平权。
marriage equality = 婚姻平权
指同性伴侣享有同等的结婚权利。
⑬ The couple went for marriage guidance before deciding to separate.
= 那对夫妇先去做了婚姻辅导，才决定分居。
marriage guidance = （英）婚姻辅导

married
核心：已婚的；婚姻的；married to（与…结了婚；全身心扑在…上）。常说 get married（结婚）。
① She is married with two children and lives in a village near Leeds.
= 她已婚，有两个孩子，住在利兹附近的一个村子里。
be married = 已婚
② They got married in a small church last June.
= 他们去年六月在一座小教堂结了婚。
get married = 结婚
③ He is married to a doctor from Poland whom he met while working abroad.
= 他娶了一位波兰来的医生，是在国外工作时认识的。
be married to somebody = 与某人结了婚
介词用 to，不用 with。
④ After forty years alone, he enjoys married life more than he ever expected.
= 独身四十年之后，他比预想的更享受婚姻生活。
married life = 婚姻生活
⑤ My brother is married to his job and never takes a holiday.
= 我哥哥一心扑在工作上，从不休假。
be married to your job = 一心扑在工作上

marrow
核心：骨髓（也说 bone marrow）；（英）西葫芦一类的长形大瓜。习语 to the marrow（彻骨、彻底）。
marrowbone（带髓的大骨）与 marrowfat pea（大粒豌豆）并入本条。
① The boy needs a bone marrow transplant from a brother or sister.
= 那个男孩需要兄弟姐妹的骨髓移植。
bone marrow = 骨髓
② He grows marrows the size of footballs in his garden.
= 他在园子里种出足球那么大的西葫芦。
a marrow = （英）西葫芦
③ The wind off the sea chilled us to the marrow.
= 海上吹来的风让我们冷到骨头里。
to the marrow = 彻骨
④ She made soup from a marrowbone and a few carrots.
= 她用一根带髓的大骨和几根胡萝卜炖了汤。
a marrowbone = 带髓的大骨
⑤ Mushy peas are made from dried marrowfat peas soaked overnight.
= 豌豆泥是用泡了一夜的干大粒豌豆做的。
marrowfat peas = 大粒豌豆
a marrowfat pea = 一粒大粒豌豆

marry
核心：和…结婚、娶、嫁；（牧师、官员）为…主持婚礼；（比喻）把…结合起来。
短语动词：marry into（嫁入、通过结婚进入）、marry off（把（子女）嫁出去）、marry up（英：对得上、配得起来）。
习语 marry money（嫁给有钱人）。
① She married a man she had met at university.
= 她嫁给了一个大学里认识的男人。
marry somebody = 和某人结婚
直接接宾语，不加 with。
② They married young, at nineteen, and had four children before they were thirty.
= 他们结婚早，十九岁就成了家，三十岁前生了四个孩子。
marry young = 早婚
③ They were married by the priest who had baptized them both.
= 给他们主持婚礼的，正是当年给他们俩施洗的那位神父。
be married by a priest = 由神父主持婚礼
④ She married into a family of farmers in the next valley.
= 她嫁进了邻近山谷的一户务农人家。
marry into = 嫁入
⑤ The old man was eager to marry off his three daughters.
= 那位老人急着把三个女儿嫁出去。
marry off = 把…嫁出去
⑥ The two halves of the torn map do not marry up at the river.
= 那张撕开的地图两半在河那儿对不上。
marry up = （英）对得上
⑦ Her mother always told her to marry money, but she chose a poor teacher.
= 她母亲总叫她嫁个有钱人，她却选了一个穷教师。
marry money = 嫁给有钱人
⑧ The building marries old stone with modern glass.
= 那座建筑把古老的石材和现代的玻璃结合在一起。
marry A with B = 把两者结合起来

③ The field is too marshy to grow wheat.
= 那块地太泥泞，种不了小麦。

④ Bubbles of marsh gas rose to the surface of the black water.
= 一串串沼气泡冒上了黑水的水面。
marsh gas = 沼气
