instance
核心：例子、实例；情况。最常见的是 for instance（例如）。另有 in the first instance（首先、第一步）、
in this instance（在这个情况下）。动词 instance（正式）：举…为例。
① Many fruits, for instance oranges and kiwis, are rich in vitamin C.
= 很多水果，例如橙子和猕猴桃，都富含维生素C。
for instance = 例如
② There have been several instances of cars being stolen in the area.
= 这一带发生过好几起汽车被盗的事。
an instance of something = …的实例
③ In the first instance, please contact your local office.
= 请首先联系你所在地的办事处。
in the first instance = 首先、第一步
④ Normally we don't refund tickets, but in this instance we will make an exception.
= 通常我们不退票，但这一次我们破个例。
in this instance = 在这个情况下

instant
核心：立即的、即刻的；速溶的、即食的。形容词：马上发生的（an instant success）；
食品做起来很快（instant coffee）。名词：刹那、瞬间（for an instant、the instant = 一…就）。
instant message / instant messaging / instant messenger（即时通讯）与 instant replay（美，即时回放）并入本条；
副词 instantly 另立。
① The book was an instant success and sold out in a week.
= 那本书一炮而红，一周就卖光了。
an instant success = 一炮而红
② I only had time for a cup of instant coffee.
= 我只来得及喝一杯速溶咖啡。
instant coffee = 速溶咖啡
③ For an instant she thought she saw her father in the crowd.
= 有那么一刹那，她以为在人群里看到了父亲。
for an instant = 一刹那
④ The instant I saw him, I knew something was wrong.
= 我一看到他，就知道出事了。
the instant = 一…就
连词用法，同 the moment、as soon as。
⑤ Come here this instant!
= 马上给我过来！
this instant = 立刻
口语，多是大人训孩子。
⑥ The team uses instant messaging to share files during the day.
= 团队白天用即时通讯软件共享文件。
instant messaging = 即时通讯
an instant message = 一条即时消息
an instant messenger = 即时通讯软件
⑦ The instant replay showed that the ball had crossed the line.
= 即时回放显示球已经越过了线。
an instant replay = （美）即时回放
英式说 action replay。

instantaneous
核心：瞬间的、即刻的 —— 几乎不花时间就发生。副词 instantaneously 并入本条。
① The effect of the painkiller was almost instantaneous, and she fell asleep within minutes.
= 那种止痛药几乎立刻见效，她几分钟内就睡着了。
almost instantaneous = 几乎即刻的
② Thanks to satellites, messages are sent instantaneously to phones all over the world.
= 有了卫星，信息瞬间就能发送到全世界的手机上。
instantaneously = 瞬间

instantly
核心：立刻、马上 —— 比 immediately 更强调「一瞬间」。也常说一眼就认出（instantly recognizable）。
① The driver was killed instantly when the lorry hit his car.
= 卡车撞上他的车，司机当场死亡。
be killed instantly = 当场死亡
② Her face is instantly recognizable from her films.
= 她的脸因那些电影而一眼就能认出来。
instantly recognizable = 一眼就能认出的

instead
核心：代替、反而 —— 副词，放句末或句首：不做前面那件事，改做这件。instead of 后接名词或 -ing（instead of doing）。
① We didn't go to the beach; we stayed at home and watched films instead.
= 我们没去海滩，而是待在家里看电影。
instead = 反而、改为
② Can I have tea instead of coffee?
= 我能要茶而不是咖啡吗？
instead of something = 而不是…
③ Instead of complaining, why don't you try to fix it?
= 与其抱怨，你为什么不试着修好它？
instead of doing something = 不做…而
后面接 -ing，不接不定式。

instep
核心：脚背；鞋面（脚背部分）—— 脚踝和脚趾之间拱起的部分。
① The ball hit him on the instep and flew into the net.
= 球打在他的脚背上，飞进了网里。
the instep = 脚背
② These shoes are too tight across the instep.
= 这双鞋脚背那儿太紧了。
tight across the instep = 脚背处太紧

instigate
核心：发起、促成（调查、改革）；煽动（暴乱）。中性时是「启动」，贬义时是「挑起」。
名词 instigation（at somebody's instigation = 在某人的发起／唆使下）与发起人 instigator 并入本条。
① After the train crash, the transport minister instigated an inquiry into safety on the line.
= 火车相撞之后，交通部长就这条线路的安全问题展开了调查。
instigate an inquiry = 发起调查
② The young man was accused of instigating the riot outside the football ground.
= 那个年轻人被指控在球场外煽动暴乱。
instigate a riot = 煽动暴乱
③ The changes were made at the instigation of the new director.
= 这些改变是在新总监的推动下进行的。
at the instigation of somebody = 在某人的发起下
④ Police are studying video footage to find the instigators of Saturday night's violence.
= 警方正在查看录像，寻找周六晚上暴力事件的挑起者。
an instigator = 发起者、挑唆者

instil
核心：逐渐灌输、培养（观念、品质）—— instil something in/into somebody。美式拼 instill。
① Good parents try to instil a sense of responsibility in their children.
= 好父母会设法培养孩子的责任感。
instil something in somebody = 向某人灌输…
② The new coach instilled confidence into the young team.
= 新教练给这支年轻的球队注入了信心。
instil confidence = 灌注信心

instinct
核心：本能、天性；直觉。两支：动物与生俱来的行为（an instinct for survival）；
人不经思考的判断（trust your instincts、gut instinct）。by instinct 是「凭本能」。
① Birds build nests by instinct, without being taught.
= 鸟凭本能筑巢，不用人教。
by instinct = 凭本能
② My first instinct was to run, but I stayed where I was.
= 我的第一反应是跑，可我还是待在原地没动。
your first instinct = 第一反应
③ She has an instinct for knowing what customers want.
= 她天生就知道顾客要什么。
an instinct for something = 对…的天赋
④ Trust your gut instinct; if the deal feels wrong, walk away.
= 相信你的直觉，要是觉得这笔交易不对劲，就别做。
gut instinct = 直觉

instinctive
核心：本能的、直觉的 —— 不经思考就做出的。副词 instinctively 并入本条。
① When the car skidded, her instinctive reaction was to protect the child in the back seat.
= 车子打滑时，她的本能反应是护住后座的孩子。
an instinctive reaction = 本能反应
② When the ball flew towards him, he instinctively put his hands up to protect his face.
= 球朝他飞来时，他本能地抬起双手护住脸。
instinctively = 本能地

instinctual
核心：（正式）本能的 —— 同 instinctive，多见于心理学、生物学文字。
① Fear of snakes may be partly instinctual in humans.
= 人怕蛇可能部分出于本能。
be instinctual = 出于本能
② Psychologists study the instinctual drives that shape human behaviour.
= 心理学家研究塑造人类行为的本能驱力。
instinctual drives = 本能驱力

institute
核心：学院、研究所、协会 —— 为某项专门目的（研究、教育、职业）设立的机构，常作名称。
动词（正式）：设立、制定（规则）；提起（诉讼）。
① After her degree, she took a research job at the Institute of Marine Biology in Plymouth.
= 拿到学位后，她在普利茅斯的海洋生物研究所找了份研究工作。
an institute = 研究所、学院
② The company instituted a new policy on working from home.
= 公司制定了一项新的居家办公政策。
institute a policy = 制定政策
③ After their son died, the family instituted legal proceedings against the hospital.
= 儿子去世后，那家人对医院提起了诉讼。
institute proceedings = 提起诉讼

institution
核心：机构；（社会）制度、惯例；（贬）收容所。几支：大型组织，如银行、大学、医院（financial institutions）；
社会里长期形成的制度或习俗（the institution of marriage）；养老院、精神病院等收容机构（常带冷冰冰的意味）；
（谑）某人或某物成了「老字号」。形容词 institutional、副词 institutionally 与动词 institutionalize（使制度化；
把人送进收容机构）、形容词 institutionalized（制度化的；在机构里住久了失去自理能力的）、
名词 institutionalization 都并入本条。
① Banks and other financial institutions are being asked to lend more to small firms.
= 银行和其他金融机构被要求多向小企业放贷。
a financial institution = 金融机构
② Some people think the institution of marriage is out of date.
= 有些人认为婚姻制度已经过时了。
the institution of marriage = 婚姻制度
③ She spent most of her childhood in institutions after her parents died.
= 父母去世后，她童年的大部分时间都在收容机构里度过。
an institution = 收容机构
④ The café has been a local institution for fifty years.
= 那家咖啡馆五十年来一直是当地的老字号。
a local institution = 当地的老字号
谑用：一个人或店铺成了大家熟悉的固定存在。
⑤ The report found evidence of institutional racism in the police force.
= 报告发现警队里存在制度性的种族歧视。
institutional racism = 制度性种族歧视
⑥ The use of bribes had become institutionalized in the ministry.
= 行贿在那个部里已经成了制度化的惯例。
become institutionalized = 变得制度化
⑦ After twenty years in prison, he was completely institutionalized and could not cope outside.
= 坐了二十年牢，他已经完全被体制化了，出狱后应付不了外面的生活。
institutionalized = 被体制化的
⑧ Campaigners want to end the institutionalization of elderly people who could live at home.
= 活动人士希望结束把能在家生活的老人送进收容机构的做法。
the institutionalization of somebody = 把某人送进收容机构
⑨ The two banks are institutionally separate but share the same owners.
= 那两家银行在机构上是分开的，但属于同一批所有者。
institutionally = 在机构上
⑩ The court ordered that he be institutionalized for his own safety.
= 为了他自身的安全，法院下令把他送进收容机构。
institutionalize somebody = 把某人送进收容机构

instruct
核心：指示、命令；教、指导；（法律）委托（律师）。几支：正式地要人做某事（instruct somebody to do）；
教授技能（instruct somebody in something）；英式法律里委托律师办案。
① The police instructed the crowd to move back from the building.
= 警察命令人群从大楼前往后退。
instruct somebody to do something = 指示某人做…
② She instructs beginners in the basics of rock climbing.
= 她教初学者攀岩的基本技术。
instruct somebody in something = 教某人…
③ The family instructed a solicitor to deal with the will.
= 那家人委托了一位律师处理遗嘱。
instruct a solicitor = （英）委托律师

instruction
核心：指示、命令；（复数）说明书、使用指南；教学、传授。几支：
一条命令（on somebody's instructions = 奉某人之命）；产品的操作说明（follow the instructions）；
不可数的教导（instruction in something）；计算机的指令。
① Read the instructions carefully before you use the machine.
= 使用机器前请仔细阅读说明书。
the instructions = 使用说明
② The soldiers were acting on the instructions of their commander.
= 士兵们是奉指挥官之命行事的。
on the instructions of somebody = 奉某人之命
③ The school offers instruction in several foreign languages.
= 那所学校开设几门外语的课程。
instruction in something = …的教学
不可数，正式。
④ The program crashed because of a faulty instruction in the code.
= 程序崩溃是因为代码里有一条错误的指令。
an instruction = （计算机）指令

instructional
核心：教学的、指导性的 —— 多作定语，说视频、材料、课程是用来教的。
① The company produces short instructional videos to help new staff use the booking system.
= 公司制作了简短的教学视频，帮助新员工使用预订系统。
an instructional video = 教学视频
② For the evening course, instructional materials are available in five languages at the front desk.
= 夜校课程的教学资料在前台有五种语言的版本可取。
instructional materials = 教学资料

instructive
核心：有教益的、给人启发的 —— 能让人学到东西的。副词 instructively 并入本条。
① It is instructive to compare the two reports.
= 比较这两份报告很有启发。
it is instructive to do something = 做…很有启发
② The failure of the project was instructive for everyone involved.
= 项目的失败让所有参与者都受益匪浅。
be instructive for somebody = 对某人有教益
③ The book explains instructively how the ancient city was built.
= 那本书很有教益地讲解了古城是怎样建起来的。
instructively = 有教益地

instructor
核心：教练、教员 —— 教某项实用技能的人：驾驶、游泳、滑雪、健身；美式也指大学讲师。
① My driving instructor made me practise parking for an hour.
= 我的驾驶教练让我练了一个小时停车。
a driving instructor = 驾驶教练
② He works as a ski instructor in the Alps every winter.
= 每年冬天他都在阿尔卑斯山当滑雪教练。
a ski instructor = 滑雪教练

instrument
核心：乐器；器械、仪器；手段、工具。本义是「工具」：做精细工作的器械（surgical instruments）；
测量用的仪表（the plane's instruments）；演奏的乐器（play an instrument）；
比喻达到目的的手段，或被人利用的人（an instrument of policy）。
① At her school every child learns to play at least one musical instrument.
= 在她的学校，每个孩子都至少要学会演奏一种乐器。
a musical instrument = 乐器
② The surgeon laid out her instruments before the operation.
= 手术前，外科医生把器械摆了出来。
surgical instruments = 手术器械
③ In the thick fog over the mountains, the pilot had to rely entirely on his instruments.
= 在山区上空的浓雾里，飞行员只能完全依靠仪表。
the instruments = 仪表
④ For decades, interest rates have been the main instrument of the central bank's policy.
= 几十年来，利率一直是央行政策的主要工具。
an instrument of something = …的工具、手段

instrumental
核心：起重要作用的；器乐的。两支：在促成某事上起关键作用（be instrumental in doing）；
只用乐器、没有人声的（instrumental music）。也作名词：器乐曲。副词 instrumentally 并入本条。
① She was instrumental in setting up the new children's hospital.
= 她在创建新的儿童医院中起了关键作用。
be instrumental in doing something = 在做…中起关键作用
② The album has three songs and seven instrumentals.
= 那张专辑有三首歌和七首器乐曲。
an instrumental = 器乐曲
③ The film uses mostly instrumental music, with no words at all.
= 那部电影用的大多是纯器乐音乐，一句歌词都没有。
instrumental music = 器乐
④ The piece was arranged instrumentally for a small string group.
= 那首曲子被改编成了小型弦乐组的器乐版。
instrumentally = 在器乐方面

instrumentalist
核心：乐器演奏者 —— 相对于歌手而言。
① The band has four singers and six instrumentalists.
= 那个乐队有四名歌手和六名乐器演奏者。
an instrumentalist = 乐器演奏者
② She is a talented instrumentalist who plays both violin and piano.
= 她是一位才华横溢的演奏者，小提琴和钢琴都会。
a talented instrumentalist = 才华横溢的演奏者

instrumentation
核心：（一套）仪器、仪表；（音乐）配器、乐器编配。
① The new car has digital instrumentation instead of old-fashioned dials.
= 那款新车用的是数字仪表，不是老式表盘。
digital instrumentation = 数字仪表
② The composer changed the instrumentation for the second performance.
= 作曲家在第二场演出时改动了配器。
the instrumentation of a piece = 乐曲的配器

insubordinate
核心：（正式）不服从的、违抗上级的 —— 多说士兵、员工。名词 insubordination 并入本条。
① The soldier was punished for being insubordinate to an officer.
= 那名士兵因违抗军官而受到惩罚。
be insubordinate to somebody = 违抗某人
② She was dismissed for gross insubordination after refusing to follow orders.
= 她拒不服从命令，因严重抗命被解雇。
insubordination = 抗命、不服从

insubstantial
核心：不牢固的、单薄的；空洞的、缺乏实质的。两支：东西不结实、分量轻；论据、证据站不住。
① The tent looked too insubstantial to survive a storm.
= 那顶帐篷看上去太单薄，经不起暴风雨。
insubstantial = 单薄的
② The evidence against him was insubstantial, and the case was dropped.
= 对他不利的证据站不住脚，案子被撤销了。
insubstantial evidence = 站不住脚的证据
