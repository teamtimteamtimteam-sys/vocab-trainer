nil
/nɪl/
核心：两支，不可数。一是（英式）体育比分里的零：two nil 是二比零，美式多说 nothing 或 zero；
二是无、零：多说可能性、数量。
① Our team won three nil, with two goals in the first ten minutes.
= 我们队三比零赢了，头十分钟就进了两个球。
three nil = 三比零
② The chances of finding the ring in the sea are virtually nil.
= 在海里找到那枚戒指的可能性几乎为零。
virtually nil = 几乎为零
③ The patient was kept nil by mouth for twelve hours before the operation.
= 手术前十二小时，病人禁食禁水。
nil by mouth = 禁食禁水
医院里的说法。

nimble

nimbostratus = 雨层云

nimbus
/ˈnɪmbəs/

nip
/nɪp/

nitrogen
/ˈnaɪtrədʒən/

② Beans and clover put nitrogen back into the soil through their roots.
= 豆类和三叶草通过根部把氮还回土壤里。

③ The samples are frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored for decades.
