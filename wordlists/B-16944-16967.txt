pied
/paɪd/
核心：（鸟、兽）黑白相间的、两色相杂的。只作定语，多用在鸟名里；the Pied Piper 是传说里穿花衣的吹笛人。
① A pied wagtail was running about on the lawn after flies.
= 一只白鹡鸰在草坪上跑来跑去追苍蝇。
a pied wagtail = 白鹡鸰
② In the legend the Pied Piper leads the children away from the town.
= 传说里，花衣吹笛人把孩子们从镇上引走了。
the Pied Piper = 花衣吹笛人
跟 magpie（喜鹊）的 pie 同源。

pied-à-terre
/ˌpjeɪd ɑː ˈteə(r)/
核心：（在城里另置的）小住处、落脚的小公寓：主人平时住在别处，只在进城时用。可数，复数 pieds-à-terre；来自法语，字面是「脚落地」。
① They live in the country and keep a small pied-à-terre in London for the theatre.
= 他们住在乡下，在伦敦留了一处小公寓，看戏时落脚。
a pied-à-terre = 落脚的小公寓
② The flat is too small for a family but perfect as a pied-à-terre.
= 那套公寓住一家人太小，当个落脚处正合适。
as a pied-à-terre = 当落脚处

pier
/pɪə(r)/
核心：三支，可数。一是（伸进海里的）长堤、栈桥：英国海滨的多建有游乐场、小卖部；二是码头、突堤：停船上下客货的；
三是（建筑）桥墩、支柱、窗间墙。
① We walked to the end of the pier and watched the fishermen.
= 我们走到栈桥尽头，看人钓鱼。
the end of the pier = 栈桥尽头
② The ferry leaves from the pier at the bottom of the main street.
= 渡轮从大街尽头的码头开出。
from the pier = 从码头
③ The bridge rests on six stone piers built in the fourteenth century.
= 那座桥架在六个十四世纪修的石墩上。
a stone pier = 石墩
读音跟 peer 一样。

pierce
/pɪəs/
核心：动词。本义是（尖东西）刺穿、扎透：及物，或跟 through；特指给（耳朵、鼻子）穿孔戴首饰：have your ears pierced；
引申为（光、声音）穿透、划破；（书面）深深刺痛（人的心）；突破（防线）。
① The nail pierced the tyre and let the air out slowly.
= 钉子扎穿了轮胎，气慢慢漏了。
pierce the tyre = 扎穿轮胎
② She had her ears pierced on her sixteenth birthday.
= 她十六岁生日那天扎了耳洞。
have your ears pierced = 扎耳洞
③ A scream pierced the silence of the sleeping house.
= 一声尖叫划破了沉睡的房子里的寂静。
pierce the silence = 划破寂静
④ The headlights could hardly pierce the fog on the mountain road.
= 山路上的雾，车头灯几乎照不透。
pierce the fog = 穿透雾
⑤ Her words pierced him to the heart, though he said nothing.
= 她的话深深刺痛了他的心，尽管他什么也没说。
pierce somebody to the heart = 深深刺痛某人的心
⑥ The cavalry pierced the enemy line at its weakest point.
= 骑兵从敌军防线最薄弱的地方突破了。
pierce the line = 突破防线

piercing
/ˈpɪəsɪŋ/

③ A piercing wind blew off the sea all afternoon.
= 整个下午，一股刺骨的风从海上吹来。
a piercing wind = 刺骨的风
④ The baby screamed piercingly every time the door banged.

piercingly = 尖厉地
⑤ He has three piercings in one ear and a ring in his nose.
= 他一只耳朵上穿了三个孔，鼻子上还有个环。
a piercing = 穿的孔
body piercing = 身体穿孔

Pierrot
/ˈpɪərəʊ/

① A Pierrot with a painted tear on his cheek handed out leaflets at the fair.
= 游乐会上，一个脸颊画着一滴泪的皮埃罗在发传单。

② She went to the fancy-dress ball as a Pierrot in white silk.

as a Pierrot = 扮成皮埃罗

pietà
/ˌpiːeˈtɑː/
核心：圣母怜子像：画、雕塑的一种题材，圣母马利亚抱着耶稣的遗体。可数；来自意大利语「怜悯」。
① The chapel contains a marble pietà carved in the fifteenth century.
= 小礼拜堂里有一尊十五世纪雕的大理石圣母怜子像。
a marble pietà = 大理石圣母怜子像
② Visitors queue for hours to see the famous pietà behind its glass screen.
= 游客排上几个钟头的队，就为看玻璃屏后面那尊著名的圣母怜子像。
the famous pietà = 著名的圣母怜子像

piety
/ˈpaɪəti/
核心：虔诚：对神深深的敬畏，以及合乎宗教要求的言行。不可数。形容词是 pious；反义是 impiety。
① The old woman was known in the village for her piety and her sharp tongue.
= 那位老太太在村里出名的，一是虔诚，二是嘴厉害。
somebody's piety = 某人的虔诚
② He went to church every day out of habit rather than piety.
= 他天天去教堂，是出于习惯，不是出于虔诚。
out of piety = 出于虔诚
filial piety = 孝道

piffle
/ˈpɪfl/
核心：（旧式口语）胡说、废话。不可数。形容词 piffling 是（口语，贬）微不足道的、小得可笑的。并入本条。
① "Piffle!" said my aunt when the salesman promised that the paint would last for ever.
= 推销员保证那油漆永远不掉，我姑妈说：「胡扯！」
Piffle! = 胡扯！
② The speech was an hour of piffle about the wisdom of the common man.
= 那篇讲话是一个钟头的废话，说什么平民百姓的智慧。
an hour of piffle = 一个钟头的废话
③ They quarrelled for a week over a piffling amount of money.
= 他们为一点小得可笑的钱吵了一个星期。
piffling = 微不足道的

pig
/pɪɡ/
核心：名词，可数：猪：美式也说 hog；小猪叫 piglet。口语里骂人：贪吃的人、邋遢的人、讨厌的家伙；（俚语，冒犯）警察。
动词：pig out (on) 是（口语）大吃特吃、胡吃海塞。过去式 pigged。
习语：pigs might fly 是（英式）除非太阳从西边出来，美式说 when pigs fly；a pig in a poke 是没看清就买下的东西；
make a pig of yourself 是吃得太多、吃相难看；make a pig's ear of 是（英式口语）把……弄得一团糟。
派生与复合词：piggery 是养猪场；pigswill 是泔水、猪食，也指难吃的东西；pig iron 是生铁；pig-ignorant 是（口语）无知透顶的；
pig-headed 是固执的、死不回头的，名词 pig-headedness；pig-sticking 是骑马用长矛猎野猪。并入本条。
① The farmer keeps a few pigs in a field behind the barn.
= 那个农民在谷仓后面的田里养着几头猪。
a pig = 猪
② The sow lay on her side while ten piglets fought for milk.
= 母猪侧躺着，十只小猪抢着吃奶。
a piglet = 小猪
③ You greedy pig, you have eaten the whole box of chocolates!
= 你这馋鬼，一整盒巧克力都让你吃光了！
a greedy pig = 馋鬼
④ We pigged out on pizza and ice cream after the exams.
= 考完试，我们胡吃了一顿比萨和冰淇淋。
pig out = 大吃特吃
pig out on something = 大吃某物
⑤ "He says he will pay you back next week." "Yes, and pigs might fly."
= 「他说下星期还你钱。」「是啊，除非太阳从西边出来。」
pigs might fly = 除非太阳从西边出来
⑥ Never buy a used car without driving it, or you may get a pig in a poke.
= 买二手车一定要先试开，不然可能买个不知好歹的东西。
a pig in a poke = 没看清就买下的东西
poke 是古词「口袋」：隔着口袋买猪。
⑦ I made a pig of myself at the wedding and could hardly move afterwards.
= 我在婚礼上吃得太多，过后几乎动不了。
make a pig of yourself = 吃得太多
⑧ The builders made a pig's ear of the kitchen floor, and it had to be laid again.
= 建筑工把厨房地板铺得一团糟，只好重铺。
a pig's ear = 一团糟
make a pig's ear of something = 把某事弄得一团糟
⑨ You can smell the piggery from the road when the wind is in the east.
= 刮东风的时候，在路上就闻得到养猪场的味儿。
a piggery = 养猪场
⑩ The soldiers complained that the stew was no better than pigswill.
= 士兵们抱怨那锅炖菜跟泔水差不多。
pigswill = 泔水、猪食
⑪ The furnace turns iron ore into pig iron, which is then made into steel.
= 高炉把铁矿石炼成生铁，再拿去炼钢。
pig iron = 生铁
⑫ The remark was pig-ignorant, and everyone at the table knew it.
= 那句话无知透顶，桌上人人都听得出来。
pig-ignorant = 无知透顶的
⑬ He is too pig-headed to admit that he took the wrong road.
= 他太固执，不肯承认自己走错了路。
pig-headed = 固执的
pig-headedness = 固执
⑭ The officers in the old photograph are setting out for a day's pig-sticking.
= 那张老照片上的军官们正要出发去猎一天野猪。
pig-sticking = 骑马用长矛猎野猪

pigeon
/ˈpɪdʒɪn/

习语 put、set the cat among the pigeons 是（英式）惹出乱子、捅马蜂窝；be somebody's pigeon 是（英式，旧）是某人的事、归某人管。

③ Her letter to the newspaper really put the cat among the pigeons.
= 她给报纸写的那封信可真捅了马蜂窝。
the cat among the pigeons = 惹出的乱子
put the cat among the pigeons = 捅马蜂窝
④ The boy is slightly pigeon-toed and wears special shoes.

pigeon-toed = 内八字的

pigeonhole
/ˈpɪdʒɪnhəʊl/

动词：把……归入某一类、给……贴标签，多含「太武断」的意思；也指把（计划、建议）搁置起来。

pigeonhole a report = 把报告搁置起来
本指鸽棚上让鸽子进出的小洞。

pigment

pilchard
/ˈpɪltʃəd/

① He opened a tin of pilchards in tomato sauce for his supper.
= 他开了一罐番茄汁沙丁鱼当晚饭。
a tin of pilchards = 一罐沙丁鱼
② The fishermen of the village once lived on the pilchard that filled the bay each summer.

the pilchard = 沙丁鱼

pile
/paɪl/
核心：名词，可数：一堆、一摞：a pile of books；口语里 a pile of、piles of 是一大堆、许多；（口语）一大笔钱：make a pile 是发大财；
（正式，或玩笑）大宅、大建筑；（打进地里撑住建筑的）桩；也指（地毯、绒布的）绒面，单数。
动词：堆、摞：pile A on B、pile B with A；（人）一窝蜂地挤进、挤出：pile in、pile into、pile out。
pile up 是堆起来、积压；车祸里几辆车撞成一堆叫 a pile-up。pile on 是越积越多；pile it on 是（口语）说得过火、夸大其词。
习语：the bottom、the top of the pile 是（社会、单位里的）最底层、最顶层。
piledriver 是打桩机；复数的 piles 是（口语）痔疮。并入本条。
① She left a pile of dirty clothes on the bathroom floor.
= 她在浴室地板上留下一堆脏衣服。
a pile of clothes = 一堆衣服
② I have piles of work to finish before the holiday.
= 放假前我有一大堆活要干完。
piles of work = 一大堆活
a pile of work = 一堆活
③ He made a pile selling land to the railway company.
= 他靠把地卖给铁路公司发了大财。
make a pile = 发大财
④ The family has lived in the same draughty pile for three hundred years.
= 那家人在同一座透风的大宅里住了三百年。
a pile = 大宅
⑤ The houses on the marsh are built on wooden piles.
= 沼泽地上的房子建在木桩上。
a pile = 桩
⑥ The carpet has a thick pile that hides every footprint.
= 那块地毯绒面很厚，脚印都看不出来。
a thick pile = 厚绒面
⑦ He piled the plates in the sink and went to bed.
= 他把盘子摞在水槽里，就上床了。
pile plates = 摞盘子
⑧ Her desk was piled high with unanswered letters.

be piled high with something = 高高地堆着某物
⑨ The children piled into the back of the car, all talking at once.
= 孩子们一窝蜂地挤进汽车后座，七嘴八舌地说着话。
pile into something = 一窝蜂地挤进某处

pile up = 堆起来、积压

pile it on = 说得过火、夸大其词
⑭ Cleaners and porters are at the bottom of the pile in every hospital.
= 在哪家医院，清洁工和护工都处在最底层。
the bottom of the pile = 最底层
