shall
/ʃəl/（弱）/ʃæl/（强）
核心：情态动词：（用于 I、we 的疑问句）表示提议、征求意见：Shall I…?、Shall we…?；（书面、英式）将要、会（用于 I、we）；（正式、法律文件）必须、应当。否定缩略式 shan't；古旧第二人称单数 thou shalt。
① Shall I open the window for you? It's very hot in here with all these people.
= 要我帮你开窗吗？这里人这么多，太热了。
shall = 用于提议
Shall I…? = 要我…吗？
② Shall we go to the cinema tonight, or would you rather stay at home?
= 我们今晚去看电影好吗，还是你更想待在家里？
Shall we…? = 我们…好吗？
③ We shall arrive in Paris at about six o'clock, if the train is on time.
= 如果火车准点，我们大约六点到巴黎。
shall = 将要
④ I shan't be home for dinner tonight, because I have to work late at the office.
= 我今晚不回家吃晚饭了，因为得在办公室加班。
shan't = shall not 的缩略式
⑤ The contract says that the tenant shall pay the rent on the first day of each month.
= 合同规定租户必须在每月一日交房租。
shall = 必须（法律文件）
⑥ "Thou shalt not steal" is one of the Ten Commandments in the Bible.
= "不可偷盗"是《圣经》十诫之一。
thou shalt not = 你不可（古旧）
