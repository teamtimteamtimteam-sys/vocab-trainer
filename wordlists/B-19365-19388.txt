roadway
/ˈrəʊdweɪ/
核心：车行道、路面：道路上供车辆行驶的部分。可数，多用单数。
① A fallen tree was blocking the roadway, so all the cars had to turn back.
= 一棵倒下的树挡住了车道，所有车只好掉头。
the roadway = 车行道
② Children must not play on the roadway in front of the school.
= 孩子们不许在学校门前的车道上玩耍。
play on the roadway = 在车道上玩耍

roadworks
/ˈrəʊdwɜːks/
核心：（英式）道路施工、修路：修路造成的路段封闭或改道。复数。
① There are roadworks on the main road into town, so expect long delays this morning.
= 进城的主路在修路，今早会堵很久。
roadworks = 道路施工
② The roadworks outside our house have been going on for six months and the noise is terrible.
= 我们家门外的修路工程已经搞了六个月，吵得要命。
the roadworks have been going on = 修路一直在进行

roadworthiness
/ˈrəʊdwɜːðinəs/
核心：（车辆）适合上路的状况、车况合格。不可数。
① Every car older than three years must have a yearly test to check its roadworthiness.
= 车龄超过三年的车每年都要检测，查看车况是否合格。
check the roadworthiness of a car = 检查车况是否合格
roadworthiness = 适合上路的状况
② The police stopped the lorry because they had doubts about its roadworthiness.
= 警察拦下那辆卡车，因为怀疑它车况不合格。
doubts about its roadworthiness = 怀疑车况不合格

roadworthy
/ˈrəʊdwɜːði/
核心：（车辆）适合上路的、车况良好的。
① The garage told us that the old van was no longer roadworthy and should be sold for parts.

roar
/rɔː(r)/
核心：动词：（狮子等）吼叫、咆哮；（人）大声喊叫、怒吼：roar at somebody；（机器、风、火）轰鸣、呼啸；哈哈大笑：roar with laughter；（车辆）轰鸣着疾驶：roar past / off。

⑤ A group of motorbikes roared past the house at two o'clock in the morning.
= 凌晨两点，一群摩托车轰鸣着从房子旁边驶过。

⑥ We could hardly hear each other above the roar of the traffic in the city centre.
= 市中心车流轰鸣，我们几乎听不见对方说话。
the roar of the traffic = 车流的轰鸣声
⑦ There was a great roar from the crowd when the home team scored in the last minute.
= 主队在最后一分钟进球时，人群中爆发出一阵欢呼。
a roar from the crowd = 人群的欢呼声
