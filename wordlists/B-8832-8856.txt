firebug
核心：（非正式）纵火犯：爱放火的人。可数。
① A firebug in the district set nine bins alight in one month.
= 那个区有个纵火的，一个月点了九个垃圾桶。
a firebug = 纵火犯
catch a firebug = 抓住纵火犯
② The firebug was caught by a camera that had not worked for a year.
= 那个纵火犯，被一台坏了一年的摄像头拍下来了。

firecracker
核心：两支。一是爆竹、鞭炮；二是（非正式）活力四射的人。可数。
① A firecracker went off in the yard and the dog is still under the bed.
= 院子里响了个爆竹，狗到现在还在床底下。
a firecracker = 爆竹
② She is a firecracker on stage and very quiet at the table afterwards.
= 她在台上活力四射，散场后在饭桌上安静得很。
a firecracker = 活力四射的人

firefight
核心：（军事）交火：双方持枪对射的战斗。可数。
① The firefight lasted forty minutes and neither side moved a single metre.
= 那场交火打了四十分钟，双方谁也没挪出一米。
a firefight = 交火
② Two firefights in one week emptied the village of everybody who could leave.
= 一个星期里两次交火，能走的人都走光了。
be caught in a firefight = 卷入交火

firefighter
核心：消防员：如今通用的说法，取代了旧称 fireman。可数。
① Firefighters got there in six minutes and the road took four of them.
= 消防员六分钟就到，其中四分钟花在路上。
a firefighter = 消防员
a retained firefighter = 兼职消防员
② Two firefighters carried the ladder that four people had brought.
= 四个人抬来的那架梯子，两名消防员就扛走了。

firefighting
核心：两支。一是灭火这件事；二是引申的四处救火、疲于应付。不可数。
① Firefighting equipment is checked in April and needed in August.
= 灭火器材四月检查，八月用得上。
firefighting equipment = 灭火器材
② The whole job is firefighting and the plan gets written in December.
= 那份工作整天都在救火，计划要到十二月才写得出来。
firefighting = 疲于应付

fireman
核心：两支。一是（旧称）消防员：如今通用 firefighter，这个词多见于旧文；
二是蒸汽机车上的司炉。可数，复数 firemen。
① The fireman on that engine shovelled two tons of coal in a single shift.
= 那台机车上的司炉，一个班要铲两吨煤。
a fireman = 司炉（蒸汽机车上的）
② A fireman in that photograph is holding a hose that needs four men.
= 照片里那位消防员，端着一根要四个人才拿得住的水带。
a fireman = 消防员（旧称）

firing
核心：三支。一是射击：firing practice；二是解雇：the firing of three managers；
三是（陶瓷的）烧制。不可数为主。习语 in the firing line 是首当其冲。
① Firing practice happens at dawn and the sheep have got used to it.
= 打靶训练在天亮时进行，羊群已经习惯了。
firing practice = 打靶训练
in the firing line = 首当其冲
② The firing of three managers in one week emptied the car park by six.
= 一个星期解雇三名经理，六点停车场就空了。
the firing of somebody = 对某人的解雇
③ The second firing sets the glaze and the first only hardens the clay.
= 第二次烧制定住釉面，头一次只是把泥坯烧硬。
a firing = 一次烧制

firkin
核心：（英式，酿造）小桶：约九加仑的啤酒桶。可数。
① A firkin lasts that pub a night and a half.
= 一小桶啤酒，那家酒馆一晚半就卖完。
a firkin = 九加仑小桶
a firkin of ale = 一小桶麦芽啤酒
② Two firkins came off the lorry and one of them was already leaking.
= 两只小桶从货车上卸下来，其中一只已经在漏了。

firm
核心：四支。一是结实的、硬实的：a firm mattress；二是坚定的：be firm with somebody；
三是（日期、安排）定死的：a firm date；四是名词，公司：a law firm。副词 firmly。
① A firm mattress and a soft pillow is the compromise that household reached.
= 硬床垫配软枕头，是那家人达成的折中。
a firm mattress = 硬床垫
② You have to be firm with a puppy and gentle about how you do it.
= 对小狗要坚定，态度还得温和。
be firm with somebody = 对某人态度坚定
③ There is no firm date yet and the invitations are already printed.
= 日子还没定死，请柬倒已经印好了。
a firm date = 确定的日期
④ The law firm has nine partners and one working lift.
= 那家律所有九位合伙人，一部还能用的电梯。
a law firm = 律师事务所
副词 firmly：牢牢地、坚决地，多修饰 shut、held、believed。
⑤ The door was firmly shut and the key was in the other coat.
= 门关得严严实实，钥匙在另一件外套里。
firmly shut = 关得严实
⑥ She firmly believes the second version and has read only the first.
= 她坚信第二个版本，读过的却只有第一个。
firmly believe = 坚信

firmament
核心：（书面、旧）苍穹、天空；引申指某个领域的「星空」。不可数。
① Stars filled the firmament and the streetlight filled everything else.
= 星星填满苍穹，路灯填满了别的一切。
the firmament = 苍穹
② A new name in the literary firmament lasts about two seasons.
= 文坛星空里的一个新名字，大约亮两季。
the literary firmament = 文坛星空

firmness
核心：两支。一是（质地的）硬实；二是（态度的）坚定。不可数。
① The firmness of that mattress is why he sleeps at his mother's.
= 那张床垫太硬，所以他都去母亲家睡。
the firmness of something = 某物的软硬程度
② Firmness at the first meeting saves three meetings later on.
= 头一次会上态度坚定，能省下后面三次会。
firmness = 坚定

firmware
核心：（计算机）固件：写死在设备里的那层程序，介于硬件与软件之间。不可数。
① A firmware update took the camera offline for a day and fixed nothing.
= 一次固件更新让那台相机停用一天，什么也没修好。
a firmware update = 固件更新
② The firmware in that box is older than the phone that talks to it.
= 那个盒子里的固件，比跟它通信的手机还老。
firmware = 固件

first
核心：五支。一是第一的、最先的：the first time；二是副词，首先；
三是名词，第一次、第一名；四是（英式）一等荣誉学位：get a first；
五是一串习语：at first、first of all、first come, first served。副词 firstly 用于列举。
① The first time she drove that road she went the wrong way twice.
= 她头一回开那条路，走错了两次。
the first time = 第一次
② At first the noise was funny and by Thursday it was not.
= 起初那声音还好笑，到周四就不好笑了。
at first = 起初
③ First of all, nobody asked; second, nobody minded.
= 首先，没人问过；其次，没人在意。
first of all = 首先
④ Seats are first come, first served and the queue starts at seven.
= 座位先到先得，七点就开始排队。
first come, first served = 先到先得
⑤ She got a first and now works in a shop she actually likes.
= 她拿了一等荣誉学位，如今在一家自己真心喜欢的店里上班。
get a first = 拿到一等荣誉学位
副词 firstly：列举时的「第一」，跟 secondly 成套，用在正式行文里。
⑥ Firstly the roof, secondly the boiler, and thirdly the money.
= 第一是屋顶，第二是锅炉，第三是钱。
firstly = 第一（列举用语）

firstborn
核心：长子、长女：家里第一个出生的孩子。可数，也作定语。
① The firstborn gets the photographs and the second gets the hand-me-downs.
= 老大占了照片，老二捡旧衣裳。
the firstborn = 长子、长女
② Their firstborn was named after a street and the second after a saint.
= 他们的老大用一条街的名字命名，老二用了一位圣人的。
a firstborn son = 长子

firth
核心：（苏格兰）河口湾：河流入海处那片宽阔水域，多见于地名。可数。
① The firth is grey in every photograph and blue about four days a year.
= 那道河口湾在每张照片里都是灰的，一年大约有四天是蓝的。
a firth = 河口湾
cross the firth = 横渡河口湾
② A bridge across the firth took nine years and two different designs.
= 跨那道河口湾的桥，修了九年，换过两版设计。

fiscal
核心：（正式）财政的、税收的：fiscal policy、the fiscal year。副词 fiscally。
① Fiscal policy moves slowly and everybody feels it at the same moment.
= 财政政策动得慢，人人却在同一刻感觉到。
fiscal policy = 财政政策
② The fiscal year ends in April and the paperwork ends in June.
= 财政年度四月结束，文书到六月才完。
the fiscal year = 财政年度
副词 fiscally：在财政上，多说 fiscally neutral、fiscally responsible。
③ The plan is fiscally neutral and politically expensive.
= 那个方案财政上不增负担，政治上代价高昂。
fiscally neutral = 财政上中性的
④ A fiscally responsible budget still needs somebody to vote for it.
= 一份财政上稳健的预算，也还得有人投票通过。
fiscally responsible = 财政上稳健的

fish
核心：四支。一是鱼：单复数同形，说不同种类时用 fishes；二是鱼肉：fish and chips；
三是动词，钓鱼、捕鱼；四是习语：a fish out of water 是浑身不自在的人。
① Three fish came out of that pond in a summer and two went back in.
= 那口塘一夏天钓上来三条鱼，放回去两条。
a fish = 一条鱼
② Fish and chips on a Friday is a habit older than that shop.
= 周五吃炸鱼薯条，这习惯比那家店还老。
fish and chips = 炸鱼薯条
③ He fishes the same stretch of river every Sunday and catches almost nothing.
= 他每个周日钓同一段河，几乎什么也钓不着。
go fishing = 去钓鱼
④ She was a fish out of water at that dinner and left before the pudding.
= 那顿饭上她浑身不自在，甜点还没上就走了。
a fish out of water = 格格不入的人

fishbowl
核心：两支。一是鱼缸；二是引申，毫无隐私可言的处境。可数。
① The fishbowl on the counter holds one fish and a great deal of gravel.
= 柜台上那只鱼缸养着一条鱼，铺了一大堆石子。
a fishbowl = 鱼缸
② Living in a fishbowl is the part of the job nobody warns you about.
= 活得像在鱼缸里，是这份工作中没人事先提醒你的那一部分。
live in a fishbowl = 活在众目睽睽之下

fishcake
核心：鱼饼：鱼肉拌土豆泥做成饼，裹粉煎或炸。可数。
① Fishcakes are what happens to yesterday's fish and today's potatoes.
= 鱼饼就是昨天的鱼加今天的土豆的去处。
a fishcake = 鱼饼
a salmon fishcake = 三文鱼饼
② Two fishcakes and a wedge of lemon is that whole lunch menu.
= 两个鱼饼加一角柠檬，就是那份午餐菜单的全部。

fisher
核心：两支。一是（正式或旧）打鱼的人：如今多说 fisherman 或 angler；
二是（北美）渔貂，一种鼬科动物。可数。
① The fisher in that story catches one fish and then lets it talk.
= 那个故事里的渔人钓上一条鱼，还让它开口说话。
a fisher = 打鱼的人
② A fisher is a weasel the size of a cat and twice as unwelcome.
= 渔貂是一种猫那么大的鼬，讨人嫌的程度是猫的两倍。
a fisher = 渔貂

fisherman
核心：渔民、钓鱼的人：复数 fishermen；说女性可用 fisherwoman。可数。
① The fisherman on that boat mends nets faster than he catches anything.
= 那条船上的渔民，补网比打鱼快。
a fisherman = 渔民
a deep-sea fisherman = 远洋渔民
② Two fishermen share a licence and never fish the same tide.
= 两个渔民合用一张执照，从不在同一个潮汛出海。

fisherwoman
核心：女渔民、钓鱼的女人：复数 fisherwomen。可数。
① The fisherwoman at the end of the pier out-fishes everybody with better gear.
= 栈桥尽头那位女钓者，比装备更好的人钓得都多。
a fisherwoman = 女渔民
a local fisherwoman = 本地的女渔民
② Two fisherwomen run that boat and hire help only in August.
= 两位女渔民经营着那条船，只在八月雇帮手。

fishery
核心：两支。一是渔场、渔业区；二是渔业这个行当。可数。
① The fishery closed for two seasons and the stock came back in three.
= 那片渔场关了两季，鱼群三季之后才回来。
a fishery = 渔场
② Fishery policy is decided a long way from any actual boat.
= 渔业政策，是在离任何一条真船都很远的地方定下的。
fishery policy = 渔业政策

fisheye
核心：（摄影）鱼眼的：a fisheye lens 指视角极广、画面外凸的镜头。
① A fisheye lens makes a small room look like a stadium.
= 鱼眼镜头能把一间小屋拍得像体育场。
a fisheye lens = 鱼眼镜头
② The fisheye view bends every straight line in that building.
= 鱼眼视角把那栋楼里的每条直线都拗弯了。
a fisheye view = 鱼眼视角

fishing
核心：钓鱼、捕鱼：不可数 —— go fishing、a fishing rod、fishing rights。
① Fishing on that stretch needs a permit and a great deal of patience.
= 在那一段钓鱼，要有许可证，还要极大的耐心。
a fishing rod = 钓竿
② Fishing rights on the river were sold in 1911 and never bought back.
= 那条河的捕鱼权 1911 年卖掉了，再没买回来。
fishing rights = 捕鱼权
