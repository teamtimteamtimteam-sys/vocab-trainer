mews
/mjuːz/
核心：（英）马厩巷 —— 由旧马厩改成住宅的小街或院落。单复数同形。
① They live in a quiet mews behind the main road.
= 他们住在主路后面一条安静的马厩巷里。
a mews = 马厩巷
② The mews house has a garage on the ground floor, where the horses were once kept.
= 那座马厩改建的住宅一楼有个车库，从前是养马的地方。
a mews house = 马厩改建的住宅
伦敦市中心这样的房子如今很贵。

Mexican
/ˈmeksɪkən/
核心：墨西哥的；墨西哥人；也指与墨西哥有关的事物。
① We had dinner at a Mexican restaurant near the station.
= 我们在车站附近的一家墨西哥餐馆吃了晚饭。
a Mexican restaurant = 墨西哥餐馆
② Many Mexicans cross the border every day to work.
= 许多墨西哥人每天过境上班。
a Mexican = 墨西哥人
③ The crowd did a Mexican wave all the way around the stadium.
= 观众绕着整个体育场掀起了人浪。
a Mexican wave = （英）人浪
因一九八六年墨西哥世界杯而得名；美式只说 the wave。

mezzanine
/ˈmezəniːn/
核心：夹层 —— 两层楼之间的半层；（美）剧院的二楼前排座。
① The shop has a small cafe on the mezzanine above the bookshelves.
= 那家店在书架上方的夹层里有一个小咖啡座。
on the mezzanine = 在夹层
② We bought two tickets in the front row of the mezzanine.
= 我们买了两张二楼前排的票。
the mezzanine = （美）剧院二楼前排
意大利语「中间的」。

mi
/miː/
核心：（音乐）唱名 mi —— 大调音阶的第三个音。也拼 me。
① The children sang doh, ray, mi and then stopped to breathe.
= 孩子们唱了「哆、来、咪」，然后停下来换气。
mi = 唱名「咪」
② The tune begins on mi and climbs slowly to the top of the scale.
= 那支曲子从「咪」起音，慢慢爬到音阶的顶端。
begin on mi = 从「咪」起音

miasma
/miˈæzmə/
核心：（文）瘴气、污浊难闻的气体；（比喻）乌烟瘴气的氛围。
① A miasma of smoke and sweat hung over the crowded bar.
= 拥挤的酒吧里弥漫着一股烟味和汗味混在一起的浊气。
a miasma of smoke = 一股烟雾浊气
② The company was lost in a miasma of rumour and fear.
= 那家公司陷在谣言和恐惧的乌烟瘴气里。
a miasma of rumour = 谣言四起的乌烟瘴气
旧时以为疟疾等病是瘴气引起的。

mic
/mɪk/
核心：（口语）麦克风 —— microphone 的简称，读如 mike，也拼 mike。
① The singer walked to the front of the stage and picked up the mic.
= 歌手走到台前，拿起了麦克风。
pick up the mic = 拿起麦克风
② Check that your mic is switched off before you start talking to your neighbour.
= 跟旁边的人说话之前，先看看麦克风关了没有。
your mic = 你的麦克风
③ The reporter held a mike in front of the winner's face.
= 记者把麦克风举到冠军面前。
a mike = 麦克风（另一种拼法）
④ She finished her speech with a joke about the boss; it was a real mic drop.
= 她用一个拿老板开涮的笑话结束了讲话，收尾收得真漂亮。
a mic drop = 漂亮的收尾

Michaelmas
/ˈmɪkəlməs/
核心：米迦勒节 —— 九月二十九日的基督教节日；在英国也指这一时节。
① The rent on the farm was paid twice a year, at Easter and at Michaelmas.
= 那座农场的地租一年交两次，复活节一次，米迦勒节一次。
at Michaelmas = 在米迦勒节
② New students arrive in Oxford at the start of Michaelmas term.
= 新生在秋季学期开始时来到牛津。
Michaelmas term = 秋季学期
③ Purple Michaelmas daisies flower in the garden until the first frost.
= 花园里紫色的紫菀一直开到头一场霜。
a Michaelmas daisy = 紫菀

mickey
/ˈmɪki/
核心：take the mickey (out of)（英，口语：取笑、逗弄）。
① His friends take the mickey out of him because of his new haircut.
= 朋友们拿他的新发型取笑他。
take the mickey out of somebody = 取笑某人
② Are you taking the mickey, or do you really want me to pay?
= 你是在逗我，还是真要我付钱？
take the mickey = 逗弄、开玩笑
善意的调侃居多。

micro
/ˈmaɪkrəʊ/
核心：极小的、微型的；前缀 micro-：微、小（microchip、microscope）；百万分之一。与 macro 相对。
① The company began as a micro business run from a kitchen table.
= 那家公司起初是在厨房餐桌上经营的微型企业。
a micro business = 微型企业
② The report studies the economy at the micro level of single families.
= 那份报告从单个家庭这一微观层面研究经济。
at the micro level = 在微观层面
希腊语「小」。

microbe
/ˈmaɪkrəʊb/
核心：微生物、细菌 —— 尤指致病的。
① Washing your hands removes most of the microbes that cause illness.
= 洗手能去掉大部分致病的微生物。
microbes = 微生物
日常口语也说 germs。
② The soil is full of microbes that break down dead leaves.
= 土壤里满是分解落叶的微生物。
full of microbes = 满是微生物

microbiology
/ˌmaɪkrəʊbaɪˈɒlədʒi/
核心：微生物学。名词 microbiologist（微生物学家）与形容词 microbiological 并入本条。
① She has a degree in microbiology and works in a hospital laboratory.
= 她有微生物学学位，在一家医院的实验室工作。
a degree in microbiology = 微生物学学位
② A microbiologist tested the water from the village well.
= 一位微生物学家检测了村里井水的水样。
a microbiologist = 微生物学家
-ist 表示「研究…的人」。
③ The food must pass microbiological tests before it can be sold.
= 那种食品必须通过微生物检测才能出售。
microbiological tests = 微生物检测

microblog
/ˈmaɪkrəʊblɒɡ/
核心：微博 —— 发短消息的社交网站或账号；动词：发微博。名词 microblogging 并入本条。
① The singer has thirty million followers on her microblog.
= 那位歌手的微博有三千万粉丝。
a microblog = 微博
② He microblogs about football several times a day from the bus on his way to work.
= 他每天在上班的公交车上发好几条关于足球的微博。
microblog about something = 发关于…的微博
③ Microblogging changed the way news travels from one country to another in minutes.
= 微博改变了新闻传播的方式，几分钟就能从一国传到另一国。
microblogging = 发微博（这种活动）

microchip
/ˈmaɪkrəʊtʃɪp/
核心：微芯片、集成电路块；动词：给（宠物）植入芯片。
① A single microchip can hold millions of tiny switches.
= 一块微芯片里能装下几百万个微小的开关。
a microchip = 微芯片
口语只说 chip。
② All dogs must be microchipped so that lost ones can be returned to their owners.
= 所有的狗都必须植入芯片，好让走失的狗能回到主人身边。
be microchipped = 植入芯片

microclimate
/ˌmaɪkrəʊˈklaɪmət/
核心：小气候 —— 一小块地方（山谷、城市、花园）与周围不同的气候。
① The valley has a warm microclimate that suits grapes and peaches.
= 那条山谷有温暖的小气候，适合种葡萄和桃子。
a warm microclimate = 温暖的小气候
② Tall buildings create a microclimate of strong winds at street level.
= 高楼在街面上造成了多强风的小气候。
create a microclimate = 造成小气候

microcosm
/ˈmaɪkrəʊkɒzəm/
核心：缩影、小天地 —— 能反映整体面貌的小样本。常说 a microcosm of、in microcosm。
① The village school is a microcosm of the whole country.
= 那所乡村学校是整个国家的缩影。
a microcosm of something = …的缩影
② The family's quarrels show, in microcosm, the troubles of the whole region.
= 那家人的争吵以小见大，显出了整个地区的问题。
in microcosm = 以小见大地
与 macrocosm 相对。

microelectronics
/ˌmaɪkrəʊɪˌlekˈtrɒnɪks/
核心：微电子学、微电子技术。形容词 microelectronic 并入本条。
① The region around the university has become a centre for microelectronics and software.
= 那所大学周边的地区成了微电子和软件产业的中心。
a centre for microelectronics = 微电子产业中心
② The cheap plastic watch is controlled by a tiny microelectronic circuit and a battery.
= 那块便宜的塑料表由一个微小的微电子电路和一粒电池控制。
a microelectronic circuit = 微电子电路

microfibre
/ˈmaɪkrəʊfaɪbə(r)/

① Clean the screen with a dry microfibre cloth.
= 用一块干的超细纤维布擦屏幕。
a microfibre cloth = 超细纤维布
② The jacket is made of light microfibre that dries in minutes.
= 那件外套是轻薄的超细纤维做的，几分钟就干。
made of microfibre = 超细纤维做的

microfilm
/ˈmaɪkrəʊfɪlm/

= 那座图书馆把旧报纸存在缩微胶卷上。
on microfilm = 存在缩微胶卷上
② The spy microfilmed the secret plans and hid the film in a pen.
= 那个间谍把秘密计划拍成缩微胶卷，藏在一支钢笔里。
microfilm the plans = 把计划拍成缩微胶卷

microfinance

microlight

micromanage

microorganism
/ˌmaɪkrəʊˈɔːɡənɪzəm/
核心：微生物 —— 肉眼看不见的生物，如细菌、病毒、真菌。
① Yeast is a microorganism that turns the sugar in grape juice into alcohol.
= 酵母是一种把葡萄汁里的糖变成酒精的微生物。
a microorganism = 微生物
复数常见。
② Heating the milk for a few seconds kills harmful microorganisms without spoiling the taste.
= 把牛奶加热几秒钟，就能杀死有害的微生物而不破坏味道。
harmful microorganisms = 有害的微生物
比 microbe 更学术。

microphone

= 记者把话筒伸到部长面前。
a microphone = 话筒

microplastics = 微塑料
② Washing clothes made of plastic fibres sends microplastics into the sea.
= 洗塑料纤维做的衣服会把微塑料排进大海。
send microplastics into the sea = 把微塑料排进大海
