frolic
核心：三支。一是嬉戏、欢闹：frolic in the snow；
二是名词，一场欢闹；三是（旧）轻松逗趣的作品。
注意：动词变形是 frolic / frolicked / frolicking。
① The dogs frolic in the snow for ten minutes and then want the door.
= 狗在雪里撒欢十分钟，就想进门。
frolic in the snow = 在雪里撒欢
frolic about = 四处嬉闹
Two lambs frolic about the field and the ewes ignore them entirely.
= 两只羊羔在田里四处嬉闹，母羊压根不理。
② The whole afternoon was a frolic and the bill came later.
= 整个下午都在疯玩，账单是后来才到的。
a frolic = 一场欢闹
③ The play is a frolic and the second act is twenty minutes too long.
= 那出戏是个轻松逗趣的本子，第二幕长了二十分钟。
a frolic = 逗趣的轻喜剧

a frolicsome puppy = 爱闹的小狗
② The evening was frolicsome until somebody mentioned the bill.

③ A frolicsome tune plays while the credits go up.
