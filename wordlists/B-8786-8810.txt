finalization
核心：（正式）定案、最后敲定：英式也拼 finalisation。不可数。
① Finalization of the accounts waits on one signature and one holiday.
= 账目定案，就等一个签名和一个人休完假。
finalization = 定案
② The finalization of the contract took three weeks longer than the negotiation.
= 合同的最后敲定，比谈判本身多花了三个星期。
the finalization of something = 某事的最后敲定

finalize
核心：把……定下来、敲定：英式也拼 finalise，多说 finalize the details。
① Finalize the seating plan before the invitations go out, not after.
= 请柬发出去之前把座位表定下来，别等发完再定。
finalize the details = 敲定细节
② The route was finalized in March and then changed twice in April.
= 那条路线三月定了案，四月又改了两回。
be finalized = 被最终定下

finally
核心：三支。一是终于、总算：等了很久之后，带一点松口气的意味；
二是列举时的「最后一点」，跟 firstly、secondly 成套；三是一锤定音地、最终地。
① The parcel finally arrived on a day when nobody was in.
= 那个包裹总算到了，偏偏那天家里没人。
finally = 终于、总算
② Finally, and briefly, the roof still leaks.
= 最后一点，简单说：屋顶还在漏。
finally = 最后（列举用语）
③ The matter was finally settled by a letter that nobody kept.
= 那件事最终是靠一封没人留存的信了结的。
be finally settled = 最终了结

finance
核心：三支。一是财政、金融：public finance，不可数；二是资金：raise finance；
三是动词，出资：finance a project。复数 finances 指某人的财务状况。
① Public finance is dull until the year the money runs out.
= 公共财政很枯燥，直到钱花光的那一年。
public finance = 公共财政
personal finance = 个人理财
② They raised finance from three banks and spent it on one building.
= 他们从三家银行筹到资金，全花在了一栋楼上。
raise finance = 筹措资金
③ Her finances recovered a good deal faster than her patience did.
= 她的财务状况恢复得比她的耐心快得多。
somebody's finances = 某人的财务状况
④ The finance company lends at a rate the shop never mentions out loud.
= 那家财务公司放贷的利率，店里从来不明说。
a finance company = 财务公司

financial
核心：财政的、金融的、财务的：the financial year、financial difficulties。
副词 financially。
① The financial year ends in March and the panic starts in February.
= 财政年度三月结束，恐慌二月就开始了。
the financial year = 财政年度
② Financial difficulties closed the shop and the landlord kept the sign.
= 财务困难让那家店关了门，招牌被房东留下了。
financial difficulties = 财务困难
副词 financially：在财务上、经济上，多说 financially viable、financially independent。
③ The scheme is financially viable and politically impossible.
= 那个方案财务上可行，政治上没门。
financially viable = 财务上可行的
④ She has been financially independent since she was nineteen.
= 她从十九岁起就在经济上独立了。
financially independent = 经济独立的
⑤ Financial aid covers the fees and does not cover the bus fare.
= 助学金能覆盖学费，覆盖不了车费。
financial aid = 助学金、财政援助

financials
核心：（商业）财务报表：一家公司那套账目数字，作复数。
① The financials look tidy and the footnotes are where the year really is.
= 那套财务报表看着规整，这一年的实情都在脚注里。
the financials = 财务报表
② Nobody reads the financials until somebody wants to buy the company.
= 财务报表没人看，直到有人想买下这家公司。
publish the financials = 公布财务报表

financier
核心：（正式）金融家、出资人：靠资本运作为业的人。可数。
① A financier bought the pier and left it exactly as it was.
= 一位金融家买下那座栈桥，原样放着没动。
a financier = 金融家
② Two financiers backed the scheme and only one of them read it first.
= 两位出资人支持那个方案，事先读过的只有一位。

financing
核心：融资、资金安排：不可数 —— arrange financing。
① Financing for the bridge took longer to arrange than the bridge took to build.
= 那座桥的融资谈下来，比造桥还费时间。
arrange financing = 安排融资
② The financing falls apart if any one bank changes its mind.
= 只要有一家银行改主意，那笔融资就散了。
financing = 融资

finch
核心：雀类小鸟：喙短而厚、专吃种子的一类小鸟，金翅雀就是其中一种。可数。
① A finch takes the sunflower seeds and leaves the rest of the mix.
= 那只雀专挑葵花籽吃，混合料里别的一概不碰。
a finch = 雀类小鸟
② Finches turn up in twos and quarrel over a single perch.
= 雀鸟总是成对地来，为一根站杆吵个不停。

find
核心：五支。一是找到、发现：find a seat；二是发觉、觉得：find it hard to do something；
三是（法律）裁定：find somebody guilty；四是名词，发现物：a lucky find；
五是短语动词 find out。习语 find your feet 是适应下来。
过去式与过去分词都是 found。
① Find a seat near the door and you can leave when the speeches start.
= 找个靠门的座位，一开始致辞你就能溜。
find a seat = 找个座位
② She finds it hard to say no and very easy to look busy.
= 她很难说出一个「不」字，装忙倒容易得很。
find it hard to do something = 觉得做某事很难
③ The jury found him guilty on one count and disagreed on the rest.
= 陪审团裁定他一项罪名成立，其余的没能一致。
find somebody guilty = 裁定某人有罪
④ The bowl was a lucky find in a box of broken things.
= 那只碗是在一箱破烂里捡到的宝。
a lucky find = 捡到的宝
⑤ It took her a term to find her feet and a year to find out why.
= 她用一个学期适应下来，用一年弄明白为什么。
find your feet = 适应下来
find out = 弄清楚
⑥ The court found for the tenant and found against the agency twice.
= 法庭判租客胜诉，两次判中介败诉。
find for somebody = 判某人胜诉
find against somebody = 判某人败诉

finder
核心：拾得者、找到东西的人；也指望远镜上的寻星镜。可数。
习语 finders keepers 是「谁捡到归谁」，多是孩子话。
① The finder handed the wallet in and left no name at the desk.
= 捡到钱包的人交了上去，在前台没留名字。
a finder = 拾得者
② Finders keepers works in a playground and nowhere at all in law.
= 「谁捡到归谁」在操场上说得通，在法律里哪儿都说不通。
finders keepers = 谁捡到归谁

finding
核心：两支。一是（调查、研究的）结论，多用复数 findings；
二是（法律）裁定：a finding of negligence。可数。
① The findings fill nine pages and the summary contradicts two of them.
= 那些结论写了九页，摘要跟其中两条自相矛盾。
the findings = 调查结论
publish the findings = 公布结论
② A finding of negligence changed what the insurer was willing to pay.
= 一项过失认定，改变了保险公司愿意赔的数目。
a finding of negligence = 过失认定

fine
核心：五支。一是好的、不错的：a fine day；二是细的、精细的：fine sand；
三是罚款（名词与动词）：pay a fine；四是（勉强）还行；
五是习语：cut it fine 是时间掐得太紧，a fine line 是一线之差。副词 finely。
① It turned into a fine day and the forecast never admitted it.
= 后来天气很好，天气预报始终没承认。
a fine day = 好天气
② Fine sand gets into the camera and the camera never quite recovers.
= 细沙钻进相机，相机就再没彻底好过。
fine sand = 细沙
③ The fine was forty pounds and the parking would have cost two.
= 罚款四十镑，本来停车费两镑。
pay a fine = 交罚款
④ They cut it fine and made the ferry with ninety seconds to spare.
= 他们时间掐得太紧，赶上渡轮时只剩九十秒。
cut it fine = 时间掐得太紧
⑤ There is a fine line between confident and unbearable.
= 自信与讨人厌之间，只有一线之差。
a fine line = 一线之差
副词 finely：一是细细地：finely chopped；二是精确地：finely balanced。
⑥ The onion needs to be finely chopped and the garlic does not.
= 洋葱要切得极细，蒜就不必。
finely chopped = 切得极细的
⑦ The argument is finely balanced and one letter tipped it over.
= 那场争论势均力敌，一封信就把它压偏了。
finely balanced = 势均力敌的
⑧ They went through the accounts with a fine-tooth comb and fine-tuned nothing.
= 他们拿细齿梳把账目梳了一遍，一处也没微调。
a fine-tooth comb = 细齿梳（细查）
fine-tune = 微调
fine-tuning = 微调（名词）

fineness
核心：两支。一是精细、细密；二是（金银的）成色、纯度。不可数。
① The fineness of the mesh decides what gets through and what stays.
= 网眼的细密程度，决定什么过得去、什么留下。
the fineness of something = 某物的细密
② Fineness is stamped on the inside of the ring in three tiny numbers.
= 纯度用三个小数字打在戒指内圈上。
fineness = 成色、纯度

finery
核心：华服、盛装：郑重场合穿戴的漂亮衣饰。不可数。
① The whole family turned up in finery and stood in the rain for an hour.
= 全家人盛装到场，在雨里站了一个钟头。
finery = 盛装
② Her finery came out of a suitcase that lives on top of the wardrobe.
= 她那身华服，是从衣柜顶上那只箱子里翻出来的。
in all your finery = 盛装打扮地

fines herbes
核心：（法餐）细香草碎：欧芹、细香葱、龙蒿与山萝卜切碎的混合香草。作复数。
① Fines herbes go in at the very end and lose everything if they boil.
= 细香草最后才下锅，一煮就什么味道都没了。
fines herbes = 法式细香草碎
② An omelette with fines herbes is the test that kitchen sets its cooks.
= 一份细香草蛋卷，是那间厨房考厨子的题。
an omelette with fines herbes = 细香草蛋卷

finesse
核心：两支。一是（正式）手法的精妙圆熟：handle something with finesse；
二是动词，巧妙地绕开或处理。不可数。
① She handled the complaint with a finesse that the letter did not deserve.
= 她处理那桩投诉的手法之圆熟，那封信其实不配。
with finesse = 手法圆熟地
② He finessed the question and answered a different one rather well.
= 他把那个问题巧妙绕开，另答了一个，答得还挺好。
finesse something = 巧妙地绕开某事

finger
核心：三支加一串习语。一是手指；二是条状的食物：fish fingers；三是动词，用手指摸。
习语：keep your fingers crossed 是祈求好运，put your finger on 是说准症结，
point the finger at 是把矛头指向某人。
① She broke a finger in a door and typed with nine of them for a month.
= 她的手指被门夹断，用剩下九根打了一个月的字。
a finger = 手指
② Keep your fingers crossed until the second envelope arrives.
= 第二个信封到之前，先祈祷着。
keep your fingers crossed = 祈求好运
cross one's fingers = 交叉手指祈求好运
③ Nobody could put a finger on what had changed about that room.
= 那间屋子究竟哪儿变了，谁也说不上来。
put your finger on something = 说准某事的症结
④ The report points the finger at a department that no longer exists.
= 那份报告把矛头指向一个已经不存在的部门。
point the finger at somebody = 指认某人
⑤ Fish fingers and peas is what that oven was bought for.
= 那台烤箱买来就是为了烤鱼柳配豌豆。
fish fingers = 鱼柳条
⑥ Finger food and a finger bowl belong to two different kinds of evening.
= 手抓小食和洗手盅，属于两种不同的晚宴。
finger food = 手抓小食
a finger bowl = 洗手盅
⑦ Finger-painting covers the child, the table and one entire wall.
= 手指画会涂满孩子、桌子，还有整整一面墙。
finger-painting = 手指画
⑧ The report is finger-pointing dressed up as analysis.
= 那份报告是相互指责，装成了分析。
finger-pointing = 相互指责

fingerboard
核心：（乐器）指板：小提琴、吉他上按弦的那块木板。可数。
① The fingerboard is worn where four generations have put the same finger.
= 指板上磨出一处凹痕，四代人都把同一根手指按在那儿。
a fingerboard = 指板
② A warped fingerboard makes every note above the fifth fret sour.
= 指板一变形，五品以上的音全走调。
a warped fingerboard = 变形的指板

fingering
核心：（音乐）指法：乐谱上标着哪个音该用哪根手指。不可数。
① The fingering in that edition suits a hand that nobody actually has.
= 那个版本上的指法，适合一双谁也没有的手。
fingering = 指法
② She pencils her own fingering over the printed one and rubs it out yearly.
= 她在印好的指法上用铅笔另标一套，每年擦掉重来。
mark the fingering = 标出指法

fingerless
核心：无指的：几乎只用在 fingerless gloves 上，就是露指手套。
① Fingerless gloves keep the palms warm and the fingers ready.
= 露指手套让手心暖着，手指随时能用。
fingerless gloves = 露指手套
② A fingerless mitten sounds like a compromise and works like one.
= 露指的连指手套听着像折中，用起来也是。
fingerless = 无指的

fingermark
核心：（英式）指印、手指留下的污痕：多说玻璃与门上的那种。可数。
① Fingermarks on the glass show up only when the sun is low.
= 玻璃上的手指印，只有太阳低的时候才看得见。
a fingermark = 指印污痕
② She cleaned the door twice and left two fingermarks doing it.
= 她把门擦了两遍，擦的时候又留下两个指印。
leave a fingermark = 留下指印

fingernail
核心：手指甲。可数。
① A broken fingernail ended the recital and nobody blamed her for it.
= 一片指甲断了，独奏会就此中止，没人怪她。
a fingernail = 手指甲
② He bites his fingernails in meetings and denies it in the corridor.
= 他开会时咬指甲，出了会议室就不认。
bite your fingernails = 咬指甲

fingerprint
核心：指纹；也作动词，给人采指纹。可数。名词 fingerprinting。
① A fingerprint on the inside of the window took the case somewhere new.
= 窗户内侧的一枚指纹，把案子引向了新的方向。
a fingerprint = 指纹
② Everybody entering the site is fingerprinted and photographed twice.
= 进那片场地的人都要采指纹，还要拍两张照。
be fingerprinted = 被采指纹

fingerprinting
核心：采集指纹这件事：不可数；genetic fingerprinting 指基因图谱鉴定。
① Fingerprinting takes four minutes and the paperwork takes forty.
= 采指纹四分钟，文书要四十分钟。
fingerprinting = 采集指纹
② Genetic fingerprinting settled a question that had run for thirty years.
= 基因图谱鉴定，了结了一个拖了三十年的问题。
genetic fingerprinting = 基因图谱鉴定
