intense
/ɪnˈtens/
核心：强烈的、剧烈的；紧张的；（人）热切认真的。几支：程度很高（intense heat / pressure）；
活动集中而激烈（intense competition）；人对事物投入、认真到让人有压力（an intense young man）。
副词 intensely 并入本条。跟 intensive 分清：intense 说程度强；intensive 说集中投入（intensive course）。
① The heat from the fire was so intense that firefighters could not get close.
= 火的热度太强，消防员无法靠近。
intense heat = 酷热
② There is intense competition for places at the university.
= 那所大学的招生名额竞争非常激烈。
intense competition = 激烈的竞争
③ He is a very intense young man who rarely smiles.
= 他是个非常较真的年轻人，很少笑。
an intense person = 热切认真的人
说人时常带「让人有压力」的意味。
④ She disliked him intensely from the first day they met.
= 从认识的第一天起她就极其讨厌他。
intensely = 强烈地、极其

intensifier
/ɪnˈtensɪfaɪə(r)/
核心：（语法）强调词 —— 加强程度的副词，如 very、extremely、really。
① Good writers use intensifiers such as very and extremely sparingly, or they lose their force.
= 好的作者会少用「very」「extremely」这类强调词，用多了就没力度了。
an intensifier = 强调词
② Teenagers often use new intensifiers that their parents find strange.
= 青少年常用一些父母觉得奇怪的新强调词。
new intensifiers = 新的强调词

intensify
/ɪnˈtensɪfaɪ/
核心：加强、加剧 —— 使变得更强烈、更严重；也可不及物：局势加剧。名词 intensification 并入本条。
① The police have intensified their search for the missing child.
= 警方加大了对失踪儿童的搜寻力度。
intensify a search = 加大搜寻力度
② The storm intensified as it moved towards the coast.
= 暴风雨在向海岸移动时加强了。
intensify = 加剧
不及物用法，说局势、天气、冲突变得更严重。
③ The intensification of farming has destroyed many hedges and ponds.
= 农业的集约化破坏了许多树篱和池塘。
the intensification of something = …的加强、集约化

intensity
/ɪnˈtensɪti/
核心：强度；强烈、紧张。可数不可数都用：光、声、感情、活动的强烈程度。
① The intensity of the light can be adjusted with a small switch.
= 光的强度可以用一个小开关调节。
the intensity of something = …的强度
② She was surprised by the intensity of her own feelings.
= 她被自己感情之强烈吓了一跳。
the intensity of feelings = 感情的强烈
③ The fighting continued with increasing intensity through the night.
= 战斗持续了一整夜，越来越激烈。
with increasing intensity = 越来越激烈地

intensive
/ɪnˈtensɪv/
核心：集中的、密集的；（农业）集约的。说在短时间内投入大量精力、资源（an intensive course）；
农业上用大量肥料、机器在小块地上高产（intensive farming）。
intensive care（重症监护）与副词 intensively 并入本条。
① She did an intensive course in Spanish before moving to Madrid.
= 搬到马德里之前，她上了一个西班牙语强化班。
an intensive course = 强化课程
② Intensive farming has increased yields but harmed wildlife.
= 集约化农业提高了产量，却伤害了野生动物。
intensive farming = 集约化农业
③ After the accident he spent two weeks in intensive care.
= 事故之后，他在重症监护室待了两周。
intensive care = 重症监护
④ The area has been intensively studied by archaeologists.
= 考古学家对那个地区做过深入细致的研究。
intensively = 集中地、深入地

intent
/ɪnˈtent/
核心：意图、目的（正式）；专注的。名词：法律上的意图（with intent to）；to all intents and purposes（实际上）。
形容词：专心的、目不转睛的（an intent look）；be intent on doing（决心做…、一心要…）。
副词 intently（专心地）并入本条。
① He was charged with possession of a weapon with intent to cause harm.
= 他被控持有武器并意图伤人。
with intent to do something = 意图做…
法律用语。
② To all intents and purposes, the war was over by Christmas, though the treaty was signed months later.
= 实际上，到圣诞节战争就已经结束了，虽然和约几个月后才签。
to all intents and purposes = 实际上、事实上
③ All through dinner, his sister seemed intent on making him angry in front of the guests.
= 整顿晚饭，他姐姐似乎一心要当着客人的面惹他生气。
be intent on doing something = 一心要做…
④ He had an intent expression on his face as he read the letter.
= 读信时，他脸上一副全神贯注的表情。
an intent expression = 专注的表情
⑤ The children listened intently as the old man told his story about the war.
= 老人讲打仗的故事时，孩子们听得聚精会神。
listen intently = 专心地听
副词 intently，对应形容词「专注的」这一支。
⑥ She stared intently at the screen, waiting for the exam results to appear.
= 她目不转睛地盯着屏幕，等着考试成绩出来。
stare intently = 目不转睛地盯着

intention
/ɪnˈtenʃən/
核心：意图、打算 —— 心里想要做的事。常见 have no intention of doing（完全不打算）、
with the intention of doing（为了…）、good intentions（好意）。
① I have no intention of selling the house, whatever price they offer.
= 不管他们出什么价，我都不打算卖这房子。
have no intention of doing something = 完全不打算做…
② He moved to London with the intention of finding work as an actor.
= 他搬到伦敦，想找份演员的工作。
with the intention of doing something = 为了做…
③ The plan failed badly, but everyone agreed that her intentions were good.
= 计划彻底失败了，但大家都认为她的本意是好的。
good intentions = 好意
④ It was never my intention to hurt anyone.
= 我从没想要伤害任何人。
it was never my intention to do something = 我从没打算做…

intentional
/ɪnˈtenʃənəl/
核心：故意的、有意的 —— 不是偶然发生的。副词 intentionally 并入本条。
① The damage to the car was not accidental; it was intentional.
= 车子的损坏不是意外，是故意的。
be intentional = 是故意的
② He intentionally left the door open so the cat could come in.
= 他故意没关门，好让猫进来。
intentionally = 故意地

inter
/ɪnˈtɜː(r)/
核心：（正式）埋葬 —— 把死者葬入墓中，多见于正式文字。过去式 interred。
拉丁短语 inter alia（尤其、除了别的以外，正式）并入本条。
① The poet was interred in the village churchyard where he had played as a boy.
= 那位诗人被安葬在他小时候玩耍过的村教堂墓地里。
be interred = 被安葬
比 bury 正式，多见于讣告与历史。
② The report discusses, inter alia, the cost of the new bridge and its effect on traffic.
= 报告讨论了诸多问题，其中包括新桥的造价及其对交通的影响。
inter alia = 尤其、除了别的以外（正式）

interact
/ˌɪntəˈrækt/
核心：相互作用；交流、互动。两支：人与人之间交往沟通（interact with）；
事物之间相互影响（drugs interact）。名词 interaction、形容词 interactive 另立。
① Teachers want the children to interact with each other more in class.
= 老师们希望孩子们在课堂上多互动。
interact with somebody = 与某人交流
② The two drugs can interact and cause serious side effects.
= 这两种药会相互作用，产生严重的副作用。
interact = 相互作用

interaction
/ˌɪntəˈrækʃən/
核心：互动、交流；相互作用。可数不可数都用。
① The course encourages interaction between students from different countries.
= 这门课鼓励来自不同国家的学生互相交流。
interaction between people = 人与人之间的交流
② Scientists study the interaction of the drug with other medicines.
= 科学家研究这种药与其他药物的相互作用。
the interaction of A with B = A与B的相互作用

interactive
/ˌɪntəˈræktɪv/
核心：互动的、交互式的 —— 人与人之间有来有往的；（电脑、电视）使用者能与之交互的。
副词 interactively、名词 interactivity 与 interactive whiteboard（交互式电子白板）并入本条。
① The museum has interactive displays that children can touch and play with.
= 博物馆有互动展品，孩子们可以摸一摸、玩一玩。
interactive displays = 互动展品
② Lessons are more interactive now, with students working in small groups.
= 现在的课堂互动更多，学生分小组学习。
interactive lessons = 互动课堂
③ The teacher wrote on the interactive whiteboard and saved the notes for the class.
= 老师在交互式电子白板上写字，并为全班保存了笔记。
an interactive whiteboard = 交互式电子白板
④ The website lets users explore the old city interactively.
= 那个网站让用户以互动的方式探索古城。
interactively = 以互动方式
⑤ The new version of the game offers more interactivity.
= 这款游戏的新版本互动性更强。
interactivity = 互动性

interbreed
/ˌɪntəˈbriːd/
核心：（使）杂交 —— 不同品种或物种的动植物交配繁殖。过去式 interbred。
① Wolves and dogs can interbreed, and their puppies are often hard to train.
= 狼和狗可以杂交，生下的小狗往往很难训练。
interbreed = 杂交
② Farmers interbred the local sheep with a hardier breed from the mountains.
= 农民们让本地羊与一种来自山区、更耐寒的品种杂交。
interbreed A with B = 让A与B杂交

intercede
/ˌɪntəˈsiːd/
核心：（正式）说情、调停 —— 为别人向有权者求情（intercede with somebody for/on behalf of somebody），
或在争执双方之间调解。名词 intercession（说情、调停；宗教上的代祷）并入本条。
① The bishop interceded with the king on behalf of the prisoners.
= 主教代表囚犯们向国王求情。
intercede with somebody on behalf of somebody = 代某人向某人求情
② The UN was asked to intercede in the border dispute between the two countries.
= 联合国被请求调停两国之间的边界争端。
intercede in a dispute = 调停争端
③ Thanks to the intercession of a local priest, the boy was released from prison.
= 多亏当地一位神父说情，那男孩被放出了监狱。
the intercession of somebody = 某人的说情
④ The congregation offered prayers of intercession for the sick.
= 会众为病人作代祷。
prayers of intercession = 代祷

intercept
/ˌɪntəˈsept/
核心：拦截、截获 —— 在人、东西、信息到达目的地之前把它截住。
名词 interception（拦截；截获）与拦截者、拦截机 interceptor 并入本条。
① Police intercepted the lorry at the border and found drugs hidden in the cargo.
= 警方在边境拦下那辆卡车，在货物里发现了藏着的毒品。
intercept a vehicle = 拦截车辆
② The defender intercepted the pass and ran the length of the pitch to score.
= 那名后卫截住传球，带球跑了全场破门得分。
intercept a pass = 截断传球
③ Security services are accused of the illegal interception of private emails.
= 安全部门被指控非法截取私人邮件。
the interception of something = 对…的截取
④ Two fighter interceptors were sent up to follow the unknown plane.
= 两架截击机起飞，跟踪那架不明飞机。
an interceptor = 截击机、拦截者

interchange
/ˈɪntətʃeɪndʒ/（名）/ˌɪntəˈtʃeɪndʒ/（动）
核心：交换、交流；（高速公路的）立交道口。名词重音在前：想法、信息的互换（an interchange of ideas）；
公路上的立体交叉枢纽。动词重音在后：互换、交替。
形容词 interchangeable（可互换的）、副词 interchangeably 与名词 interchangeability 并入本条。
① The conference allowed a useful interchange of ideas between scientists and farmers.
= 那次会议让科学家和农民进行了有益的思想交流。
an interchange of ideas = 思想交流
② There was a long traffic jam at the motorway interchange.
= 高速公路立交道口堵了很长的车。
a motorway interchange = 高速公路立交道口
③ The parts are interchangeable, so you can use either battery in the camera.
= 这些部件可以互换，所以两块电池哪块都能用在相机上。
be interchangeable = 可互换
④ In everyday speech many people use the two words interchangeably.
= 日常说话时，很多人把这两个说法混着用。
use something interchangeably = 交替使用
⑤ The interchangeability of the parts made repairs much cheaper.
= 零件可互换，让修理便宜了很多。
the interchangeability of something = …的可互换性

intercity
/ˌɪntəˈsɪti/
核心：城际的 —— 城市之间的（交通），多作定语：intercity trains、intercity buses。
① The intercity train from London to Edinburgh takes about four and a half hours.
= 从伦敦到爱丁堡的城际列车大约要四个半小时。
an intercity train = 城际列车
② Cheap intercity buses have taken many passengers away from the railways.
= 廉价的城际大巴从铁路那里抢走了许多乘客。
intercity buses = 城际大巴

intercollegiate
/ˌɪntəkəˈliːdʒiət/
核心：（美）大学之间的、校际的 —— 多说体育比赛、辩论赛。
① She played in the national intercollegiate tennis championship.
= 她参加了全国大学生网球锦标赛。
an intercollegiate championship = 大学校际锦标赛
② The university has a long history of success in intercollegiate debate.
= 那所大学在校际辩论赛中一向成绩出色。

intercom
/ˈɪntəkɒm/
核心：对讲机、内部通话系统 —— 楼门口、办公室之间用的通话装置。
① A voice on the intercom told us to take the lift to the fifth floor.
= 对讲机里有个声音让我们坐电梯上五楼。
on the intercom = 通过对讲机

over the intercom = 通过内部通话系统

intercommunication
/ˌɪntəkəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃən/
核心：（正式）相互交流、互相通讯 —— 不可数。
① Good intercommunication between departments prevents costly mistakes.
= 部门之间良好的相互沟通能避免代价高昂的错误。
intercommunication between departments = 部门之间的相互沟通
② The old ships had no system of intercommunication between the bridge and the engine room.
= 旧式船只的驾驶台和机舱之间没有相互通话的系统。
a system of intercommunication = 相互通话系统

interconnect
/ˌɪntəkəˈnekt/
核心：（使）互相连接、互相联系 —— 常用被动或分词 interconnected。名词 interconnection 并入本条。
① The two computer networks are interconnected, so staff can share files easily.
= 两个电脑网络互相连通，员工可以轻松共享文件。
be interconnected = 互相连接
② Poverty, poor health and bad housing are closely interconnected.
= 贫困、健康状况差和住房条件差彼此密切相关。
closely interconnected = 密切相关的
③ The report shows the interconnections between climate change and food prices.
= 报告揭示了气候变化与食品价格之间的相互关联。
an interconnection between A and B = A与B之间的关联
可数，多用复数。

intercontinental

intercourse
