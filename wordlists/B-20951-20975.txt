shooting
/ˈʃuːtɪŋ/
核心：名词：枪击、开枪（可数）：a shooting；射击运动、狩猎（不可数）；（电影的）拍摄（不可数）。形容词：（疼痛）一阵阵剧烈的：a shooting pain。
① There was a shooting outside the nightclub last night, and two men were taken to hospital.
= 昨晚夜总会外发生了一起枪击事件，两名男子被送进医院。
a shooting = 枪击事件
shooting = 枪击
② The shooting of the film was delayed for a month because the main actor broke his leg.
= 主演摔断了腿，那部电影的拍摄推迟了一个月。
the shooting of a film = 电影的拍摄
③ I felt a shooting pain down my leg when I lifted the heavy box.
= 我搬那只重箱子时，腿上一阵剧痛。
a shooting pain = 一阵剧痛
这一支只作定语，形容突然窜起的刺痛。
④ We made a wish when we saw a shooting star over the sea.
= 我们看到海上空划过一颗流星时许了个愿。
a shooting star = 流星
⑤ At the fair, my father won a teddy bear at the shooting gallery.
= 在游乐会上，我父亲在射击游戏摊赢了一只泰迪熊。
a shooting gallery = 射击游戏摊
⑥ The farmer sat on his shooting stick at the edge of the field to watch the race.
= 农场主坐在田边他那根折凳手杖上看比赛。
a shooting stick = （可当凳子的）折凳手杖
⑦ He plays as a shooting guard and is the fastest player in the team.
= 他打得分后卫，是队里速度最快的球员。
a shooting guard = （篮球）得分后卫
⑧ We had to move the whole shooting match, furniture, books and all, to the new house in one day.
= 我们得在一天之内把所有东西，家具、书什么的，统统搬到新房子去。
the whole shooting match = （口语）所有的一切
⑨ My uncle goes skeet shooting at the club every Sunday, firing at clay targets thrown into the air.
= 我叔叔每个星期天都去俱乐部打飞碟，射击抛向空中的泥靶。
skeet shooting = 飞碟射击（双向）

shop
/ʃɒp/
核心：名词，可数：商店、店铺：a shop、go to the shops；（工厂的）车间、作坊：a repair shop。动词（shopped, shopped）：购物、买东西：shop for something、go shopping；（英式口语）告发。习语：talk shop 是谈论工作上的事；all over the shop 是到处、乱七八糟。
① There is a small shop at the end of our street that sells bread and newspapers.
= 我们街尽头有一家小店，卖面包和报纸。
shop = 商店
② We go shopping every Saturday morning at the market in the town centre.
= 我们每个星期六早上都去镇中心的市场购物。
go shopping = 去购物
③ She is shopping for a new coat, but she hasn't found one she likes yet.
= 她在买新外套，可还没找到喜欢的。
shop for something = 选购某物
go shopping 多指逛街买东西，买日常用品多说 do the shopping。
④ My father works in the repair shop at the bus station, fixing engines.
= 我父亲在汽车站的修理车间修发动机。
a repair shop = 修理车间
⑤ Whenever the two doctors meet at a party, they end up talking shop all evening.
= 那两位医生只要在聚会上碰到，整晚都在聊工作。
talk shop = 谈工作上的事
多带不满的口气，嫌别人在社交场合还谈工作。
⑥ After the storm, the branches were all over the shop and the garden looked terrible.
= 暴风雨过后，树枝散得到处都是，花园一片狼藉。
all over the shop = 到处都是
⑦ It is worth shopping around before you buy a new phone, because prices vary a lot.
= 买新手机前货比三家是值得的，因为价格差别很大。
shop around = 货比三家
后面可接 for something：shop around for a cheaper flight。
⑧ My grandmother never buys shop-bought cakes for our birthdays; she bakes everything herself.
= 我们过生日时，奶奶从不买店里卖的现成蛋糕，什么都自己烤。
shop-bought = 店里买的（非自制的）
⑨ The jumper was shop-soiled, so they sold it to me for half price.
= 那件毛衣在店里放旧了，所以他们半价卖给了我。
shop-soiled = （商品）在店里放旧了的
⑩ The shop steward told the workers that the union would fight the pay cut.
= 工会代表告诉工人们，工会将反对减薪。
a shop steward = 工会车间代表
⑪ I hate parties where everyone just stands in a corner and shop talk goes on all night.
= 我讨厌那种大家站在角落里、整晚都在谈工作的聚会。
shop talk = 谈工作的话
⑫ The children pressed their noses against the shop window to look at the toys.
= 孩子们把鼻子贴在商店橱窗上看里面的玩具。
a shop window = 商店橱窗
⑬ After forty years of selling bread in the village, the old baker finally decided to shut up shop and retire.
= 在村里卖了四十年面包之后，那位老面包师终于决定关门歇业、退休了。
shut up shop = 关门歇业、停业
多用于英式口语，也可指一天的营业结束。

= 新来的老师留着深色短发，戴一副挂着链子的大圆眼镜。
short = 短的
② My brother is quite short, but he is the best basketball player in his class.
= 我弟弟个子挺矮，但他是班上篮球打得最好的。
short = 矮的
short = 短、矮
③ We are a bit short of milk, so can you buy some on the way home?
= 我们牛奶不太够了，你回家路上能买点吗？

short of = 缺少
④ We ran short of water on the second day of the walk and had to ask at a farm.
= 徒步第二天我们水不够了，只好去一个农场要。

⑤ It is only a short walk from the station to the hotel.

⑥ Everyone in the office calls him Tom, which is short for Thomas, his grandfather's name.
= 办公室里大家都叫他汤姆，这是托马斯的简称，托马斯是他祖父的名字。

⑧ It rained, the hotel was dirty and I lost my passport; in short, the holiday was a disaster.

the long and the short of it = 总而言之

= 他看见那条蛇横在小路上，猛地停住了。
stop short = 突然停下
⑭ The new rules are only a short-term solution, and the problem will come back next year.
= 新规定只是权宜之计，明年问题还会再来。
short-term = 短期的
