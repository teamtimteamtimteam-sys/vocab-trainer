slavish
/ˈsleɪvɪʃ/

① The new building is a slavish copy of an old castle, with no ideas of its own.
= 那座新建筑是对一座古堡的生搬硬套，毫无自己的创意。
a slavish copy = 生搬硬套的复制
slavish = 盲从的
② He slavishly follows every instruction his boss gives him, even the silly ones.
= 老板的每个指示他都盲目照办，连那些愚蠢的也一样。
slavishly = 盲从地
