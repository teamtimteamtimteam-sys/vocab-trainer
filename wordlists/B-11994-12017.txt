indeed
核心：确实、真的 —— 副词，用来肯定、加强语气。几个位置：
一是加强肯定的回答（Yes, indeed）；二是放在 very + 形容词之后加强（very good indeed，英式）；
三是承接上文、补充更强的一点（= 事实上，其实）；四是表示惊讶或讽刺（Indeed?）；
五是跟 if 连用：if indeed（如果真的…的话）。
① "Did you see the match?" "I did indeed, and what a finish it was!"
= 「你看比赛了吗？」「当然看了，最后那段太精彩了！」
I did indeed = 确实如此
加强肯定的回答。
② Thank you very much indeed for all your help with the move.
= 非常感谢你帮忙搬家。
very much indeed = 非常（英）
放在 very + 形容词或副词之后，英式特有的加强法。
③ The plan was risky; indeed, some thought it was impossible.
= 那个计划很冒险，事实上，有些人认为根本不可能。
indeed = 事实上、其实
承接上文，引出一个更进一步的说法。
④ "He says he's too busy to help." "Busy indeed! He's been watching TV all day."
= 「他说他太忙没空帮忙。」「忙？他看了一整天电视！」
busy indeed = 还真是忙（讽刺）
重复对方的词再加 indeed，表示不信或讥讽。
⑤ The talks will resume next month, if indeed they resume at all.
= 谈判将于下月恢复 —— 如果真能恢复的话。
if indeed = 如果真的…的话

indefatigable
核心：（正式）不知疲倦的、孜孜不倦的 —— 多用来称赞人工作、奔走的劲头。副词 indefatigably 并入本条。
① She was an indefatigable campaigner for women's rights for over forty years.
= 四十多年来，她一直是孜孜不倦的女权运动者。
an indefatigable campaigner = 不知疲倦的活动家
② The volunteers worked indefatigably through the night to fill sandbags.
= 志愿者们通宵不知疲倦地装沙袋。
indefatigably = 不知疲倦地

indefensible
核心：站不住脚的、无法辩护的；（军事）守不住的。前一支说行为、观点；后一支说阵地。
① The company's decision to cut the nurses' pay was morally indefensible.
= 公司削减护士工资的决定在道义上站不住脚。
morally indefensible = 在道义上站不住脚
② The general knew the town was indefensible and ordered his troops to withdraw.
= 将军知道那个镇守不住，就下令部队撤退。
an indefensible position = 守不住的阵地

indefinable
核心：难以确切描述的、说不出的 —— 多说感觉、气质。副词 indefinably 并入本条。
① There was an indefinable sadness about the old house.
= 那座老房子透着一种说不出的哀伤。
an indefinable sadness = 说不出的哀伤
② The room felt indefinably different, though nothing seemed to have moved.
= 那间屋子感觉哪里不一样了，可又说不出来，东西好像都没动过。
indefinably = 说不出地

indefinite
核心：无限期的；不确定的、模糊的。两支：时间没有定下终点（an indefinite period）；
（数量、意思）不明确。语法上：the indefinite article（不定冠词 a/an）、indefinite pronoun（不定代词）并入本条。
副词 indefinitely（无限期地）并入本条。
① The workers are on indefinite strike until the company agrees to talks.
= 工人们在无限期罢工，直到公司同意谈判为止。
an indefinite strike = 无限期罢工
② She is staying with us for an indefinite period while her flat is repaired.
= 她的公寓在修，她会在我们家住一段时间，不定多久。
for an indefinite period = 无限期地、不定期地
③ In English you use the indefinite article before a noun the listener has not heard about yet.
= 英语里，听话人头一回听说的名词前面要用不定冠词。
the indefinite article = 不定冠词
④ Learners often mix up indefinite pronouns such as someone and anyone in negative sentences.
= 学英语的人在否定句里常把「someone」「anyone」这类不定代词弄混。
an indefinite pronoun = 不定代词
⑤ The concert has been postponed indefinitely because the singer is ill.
= 歌手病了，音乐会无限期推迟。
postpone something indefinitely = 无限期推迟…

indelible
核心：擦不掉的；难以忘怀的。本义说墨水、笔迹、污渍洗不掉；比喻说印象、记忆、影响抹不去。
副词 indelibly 并入本条。
① Voters had their fingers marked with indelible ink so they could not vote twice.
= 选民的手指被涂上擦不掉的墨水，防止重复投票。
indelible ink = 擦不掉的墨水
② The war left an indelible mark on everyone who lived through it.
= 那场战争在每个经历过它的人身上都留下了无法磨灭的印记。
an indelible mark = 无法磨灭的印记
③ The sight of the burning city was indelibly printed on her memory.
= 城市燃烧的景象深深地印在了她的记忆里。
indelibly = 不可磨灭地

indelicate
核心：（正式）不得体的、粗俗的 —— 话题、问题、举止让人尴尬，多涉及钱、身体、私事。
名词 indelicacy 并入本条。
① It seemed indelicate to ask the widow how much the funeral had cost.
= 问那位遗孀葬礼花了多少钱，似乎不大得体。
an indelicate question = 不得体的问题
② She apologized for the indelicacy of her remark about his weight.
= 她为自己拿他的体重说事的冒失话道了歉。
the indelicacy of something = …的不得体

indemnify
核心：（法律）保障、赔偿 —— 保证对可能的损失给予补偿（indemnify somebody against something），
或为已发生的损失赔偿。名词 indemnification 并入本条。
① The contract indemnifies the builder against any claims for damage to neighbouring houses.
= 合同保障建筑商不承担任何邻居房屋受损的索赔。
indemnify somebody against something = 保障某人免于…
② The airline agreed to indemnify passengers for their lost luggage.
= 航空公司同意赔偿乘客丢失的行李。
indemnify somebody for something = 就…赔偿某人
③ The company is seeking indemnification from its insurers for the fire.
= 公司正向保险公司就那场火灾寻求赔偿。
indemnification = 赔偿、补偿

indemnity
核心：（法律）保障、赔偿（金）—— 不可数时指免于损失的保障；可数时指一笔赔偿金。
① The policy provides indemnity against loss or damage to the building.
= 这份保单保障建筑物的损失或损坏。
indemnity against something = 对…的保障
② The defeated country was forced to pay a huge indemnity to the victors.
= 战败国被迫向战胜国支付巨额赔款。
pay an indemnity = 支付赔款

indent
核心：缩进；订购。三支：排版上把一行开头往里缩（indent the first line）；
（英）正式订购、申请物资（indent for supplies）；名词指缩进或（英）订单。
形容词 indented（缩进的；边缘凹凸不平的）与名词 indentation（缩进；凹痕）并入本条。
① Indent the first line of each paragraph by one centimetre.
= 每段第一行缩进一厘米。
indent a line = 缩进一行
② The hospital indented for new beds, but the order took six months to arrive.
= 医院申领了新床，可订货六个月才到。
indent for something = （英）申领、订购…
③ The coast is deeply indented, with hundreds of small bays and inlets.
= 海岸线曲折，有几百个小海湾和小港湾。
a deeply indented coastline = 曲折的海岸线
④ His ring had left a small indentation on the table.
= 他的戒指在桌面上留下了一个小凹痕。
an indentation = 凹痕
⑤ Use the tab key to create an indentation at the start of the paragraph.
= 用跳格键在段首缩进。
create an indentation = 缩进

indenture
核心：（旧）契约；学徒合同 —— 旧时雇主与学徒、契约劳工签订的合同，约定服务若干年。
形容词 indentured（契约束缚的）并入本条，多说殖民时期的契约劳工。
① At fourteen he signed an indenture to serve a printer for seven years.
= 十四岁时，他签了一份契约，给一个印刷商当七年学徒。
sign an indenture = 签订学徒契约
② Thousands of indentured labourers were shipped to the sugar plantations.
= 成千上万的契约劳工被运到了甘蔗种植园。
indentured labourers = 契约劳工

independence
核心：独立 —— 不受别人控制、不依赖别人。两支：国家、地区脱离统治（gain independence from）；
个人经济上、生活上自立（financial independence）。形容词 independent 另立。
① The country gained independence from Britain in 1962.
= 那个国家于一九六二年脱离英国独立。
gain independence from somebody = 脱离…独立
② Leaving home at eighteen gave her a sense of independence she had never felt before.
= 十八岁离家让她有了一种从未有过的独立感。
a sense of independence = 独立感
③ Getting a job was the first step towards financial independence.
= 找到工作是迈向经济独立的第一步。
financial independence = 经济独立
④ The paper has always prided itself on its editorial independence.
= 那家报纸一向以编辑独立而自豪。
editorial independence = 编辑独立

independent
核心：独立的；不偏不倚的；（英）私立的。几支：国家独立（an independent state）；个人自立（fiercely independent）；
不受控制、中立（an independent inquiry）；互不相关的（two independent studies）；
英式说学校、电视台「非公立的」（an independent school）。
语法 independent clause（独立分句）、统计学 independent variable（自变量）并入本条；副词 independently 并入本条。
① The island became an independent state after a peaceful vote.
= 那个岛经过一次和平投票成为独立国家。
an independent state = 独立国家
② At ninety my grandmother is still fiercely independent of her children and refuses any help with shopping.
= 我祖母九十岁了，还是非常要强，不靠子女，买东西不肯让人帮忙。
fiercely independent = 非常独立、要强
be independent of somebody = 不依赖某人
③ The government has ordered an independent inquiry into the accident.
= 政府已下令对这起事故进行独立调查。
an independent inquiry = 独立调查
④ Two independent studies reached exactly the same conclusion.
= 两项彼此独立的研究得出了完全相同的结论。
independent studies = 彼此独立的研究
⑤ She was educated at an independent school and then went to Oxford.
= 她在一所私立学校上学，后来进了牛津。
an independent school = （英）私立学校
⑥ Each independent clause in a long sentence could stand alone as a complete sentence.
= 长句里的每个独立分句都可以单独成为一个完整的句子。
an independent clause = 独立分句
⑦ In this experiment, temperature is the independent variable.
= 在这个实验里，温度是自变量。
an independent variable = 自变量
⑧ The two scientists made the same discovery independently of each other.
= 两位科学家各自独立地作出了同一项发现。
independently of somebody = 独立于某人

indescribable
核心：难以形容的 —— 好到或坏到说不出来。副词 indescribably 并入本条。
① The joy of holding her first grandchild was indescribable.
= 第一次抱孙子的喜悦难以言表。
indescribable joy = 难以形容的喜悦
② The smell in the flat after the fridge broke down was indescribably awful.
= 冰箱坏了以后，公寓里的气味难闻得没法形容。
indescribably = 难以形容地

indestructible
核心：毁不掉的、坚不可摧的 —— 说东西结实，也说人、精神不垮。
① These old metal toys are practically indestructible, so they get passed down the family.
= 这些老式金属玩具几乎摔不坏，所以一代代往下传。
practically indestructible = 几乎毁不掉的
② Their friendship seemed indestructible, until they fell in love with the same woman.
= 他们的友谊看似坚不可摧，直到两人爱上了同一个女人。
an indestructible friendship = 牢不可破的友谊

indeterminate
核心：不确定的、模糊的 —— 数量、年龄、性质说不准。名词 indeterminacy 并入本条。
① A woman of indeterminate age sat reading at the next table.
= 邻桌坐着一个看不出年纪的女人在看书。
of indeterminate age = 看不出年纪的
② He was given an indeterminate sentence and no release date.
= 他被判不定期徒刑，没有释放日期。
an indeterminate sentence = 不定期刑
③ The indeterminacy of the rules made it hard for players to know what was allowed.
= 规则含糊不清，选手们很难知道什么是允许的。
the indeterminacy of something = …的不确定

index
核心：索引；指数；指标。本义是「指示」（同 indicate），由此三支：书后的索引、资料卡片目录；
经济上的指数（price index）；一般的衡量指标（an index of success）。复数 indexes 或 indices（后者多用于科学、经济）。
动词：编索引；（英）按物价指数调整（index-linked 与数据化的 indexation）。
index card（索引卡片）、index finger（食指）、index-linked、index-linking 并入本条。
① Look up the word in the index at the back of the book.
= 在书后的索引里查一下这个词。
the index = 索引
② The consumer price index rose by three per cent last year.
= 去年消费价格指数上涨了百分之三。
a price index = 价格指数
③ Sales of luxury cars are often treated as an index of how confident wealthy shoppers feel.
= 豪华车的销量常被当作衡量富裕消费者信心的指标。
an index of something = …的指标
④ Pensions are index-linked, so they rise with the cost of living.
= 退休金与物价指数挂钩，随生活成本上涨。
index-linked = （英）与物价指数挂钩的
index-linking = 与物价指数挂钩
indexation = 指数化
⑤ She wrote each new word on an index card and tested herself on the bus.
= 她把每个新词写在一张索引卡片上，在公交车上自己考自己。
an index card = 索引卡片
⑥ The guide pointed at the old map with his index finger to show us the route.
= 导游用食指点着那张旧地图，给我们看路线。
the index finger = 食指
⑦ Scientists have calculated the refractive indices of several new materials.
= 科学家计算出了几种新材料的折射率。
indices = index 的复数（多用于科学）

Indian
核心：印度的；印度人。另一支指美洲原住民（旧称，许多人认为冒犯，今多说 Native American）。
一串以 Indian 打头的复合词并入本条：Indian summer（深秋回暖；晚年的好时光）、Indian ink（英，墨汁）、
Indian corn（美，玉米）、Indian club（体操棒）、Indian red（印度红，一种红褐色）、Indian reservation
（美，印第安人保留地）；India rubber（旧，橡皮）也并在这里。
① Indian food is popular all over Britain, and every town has a curry house.
= 印度菜在全英国都很受欢迎，每个镇都有一家咖喱馆。
Indian food = 印度菜
② The museum has a collection of Indian baskets from the American Southwest.
= 那家博物馆收藏有来自美国西南部的印第安人篮子。
Indian = （旧）美洲原住民的
今天多说 Native American 或具体的部族名。
③ We had an Indian summer this year, with warm sunny days in late October.
= 今年有个小阳春，十月底还是温暖的晴天。
an Indian summer = 小阳春、深秋回暖
也比喻晚年的好时光：the Indian summer of his career。
④ The drawing was done in Indian ink and has not faded at all.
= 那幅画是用墨汁画的，一点也没褪色。
Indian ink = （英）墨汁
美式说 India ink。
⑤ The family grew Indian corn and beans on a small farm.
= 那家人在一个小农场上种玉米和豆子。
Indian corn = （美）玉米
⑥ Victorian gymnasts swung heavy Indian clubs to build their strength.
= 维多利亚时代的体操运动员挥舞沉重的体操棒来锻炼力量。
an Indian club = 体操棒
⑦ The door was painted a deep Indian red.
= 那扇门漆成了深印度红色。
Indian red = 印度红
⑧ He grew up on an Indian reservation in South Dakota.
= 他在南达科他州的一个印第安人保留地长大。
an Indian reservation = （美）印第安人保留地
⑨ She rubbed out the pencil marks with a piece of India rubber.
= 她用一块橡皮擦掉铅笔印。
India rubber = （旧）橡皮

indicate
核心：表明、显示；指出；（英）打转向灯。本义是「指出」，由此几支：
事实、迹象表明（The results indicate that…）；人用手或话指点、示意（indicate the door）；
间接地表示（She indicated she might resign）；英式开车打转向灯（indicate left）。
① Early results from the hospital trial indicate that the new drug is working in most patients.
= 医院试验的初步结果表明，新药对大多数病人有效。
indicate that = 表明…
② Without looking up from his papers, the lawyer indicated the chair by the window, and I sat down.
= 律师头也没抬，指了指窗边的椅子，我就坐下了。
indicate something = 指着、示意…
③ She has indicated that she may be willing to take the job.
= 她已表示可能愿意接受这份工作。
indicate that = 暗示、表示
④ Always check your mirror and indicate left before you pull out into the traffic.
= 驶入车流前，一定要看后视镜、打左转向灯。
indicate left = （英）打左转向灯

indication
核心：迹象、表明；暗示 —— 指向某件事的信号。常见 give an indication、there is every indication that。
医学上另指「适应症」。
① Despite the government's promises, there is every indication that food prices will continue to rise this winter.
= 尽管政府作了承诺，种种迹象表明今冬食品价格还会继续上涨。
there is every indication that = 种种迹象表明
② He gave no indication that he was unhappy with his job.
= 他一点也没流露出对工作不满的意思。
give no indication = 毫无迹象
③ Can you give us an indication of how much the repairs will cost?
= 你能大概说一下修理要花多少钱吗？
an indication of something = …的大致说法
④ Doctors list high blood pressure as the main indication for the new drug.
= 医生把高血压列为这种新药的主要适应症。
an indication for a drug = 药物的适应症

indicative
核心：表明的、暗示的；（语法）陈述语气。两支：be indicative of（表明…、是…的迹象，正式）；
语法术语 the indicative（陈述语气，相对于虚拟与祈使）。
① His long silence at the dinner table was indicative of how angry he really was with his son.
= 他在饭桌上久久不说话，说明他其实对儿子有多生气。
be indicative of something = 表明…
② Most of the verbs in an ordinary news report are in the indicative, because they state facts.
= 普通新闻报道里的动词大多用陈述语气，因为说的是事实。
the indicative = 陈述语气

indicator
核心：指标、迹象；指示器；（英）转向灯。本义是「指示的东西」：经济指标（economic indicators）、
仪表上的指针、车上的转向灯（美式说 turn signal）。
① Unemployment is a key indicator of the state of the economy.
= 失业率是衡量经济状况的关键指标。
a key indicator = 关键指标
② The fuel indicator showed that the tank was almost empty.
= 油量表显示油箱几乎空了。
a fuel indicator = 油量表
③ The driver in front turned left without using his indicator.
= 前面的司机没打转向灯就左转了。
an indicator = （英）转向灯

indict
核心：（美，法律）起诉、控告 —— 正式指控某人犯有重罪。读音不发 c。
形容词 indictable（可被起诉的）与名词 indictment（起诉书；控诉）并入本条。
① The former bank manager was indicted on three charges of fraud last spring.
= 那位前银行经理去年春天被控三项欺诈罪。
be indicted on charges of something = 被控…罪
② Theft is an indictable offence and can be tried before a jury.
= 盗窃是可起诉罪，可由陪审团审判。
an indictable offence = 可起诉罪
③ The grand jury returned an indictment against the former mayor.
= 大陪审团对前市长提出了起诉。
an indictment against somebody = 对某人的起诉
④ The number of homeless families is a damning indictment of government policy.
= 无家可归的家庭数量是对政府政策的严厉控诉。
an indictment of something = 对…的控诉
比喻：一件事证明另一件事糟透了。

indie
核心：（口语）独立的 —— 由 independent 截短而来，多说不属于大公司的唱片公司、乐队、电影、游戏。
也作名词。indie music（独立音乐）并入本条。
① The band signed to a small indie label before they became famous.
= 那支乐队出名之前签的是一家小型独立唱片公司。
an indie label = 独立唱片公司
② He listens mostly to indie music and hates the charts.
= 他主要听独立音乐，讨厌排行榜上的歌。
indie music = 独立音乐
③ The festival shows indie films that would never reach the big cinemas.
= 那个电影节放映进不了大影院的独立电影。
indie films = 独立电影
