insidious
核心：暗中为害的、潜伏的 —— 坏事悄悄地、慢慢地发展，等发现时已很严重。多说疾病、影响、危险。
副词 insidiously 并入本条。
① High blood pressure is an insidious disease because it often has no symptoms.
= 高血压是一种隐匿的疾病，因为它常常没有症状。
an insidious disease = 隐匿的疾病
② Advertising has an insidious effect on how children see themselves.
= 广告在不知不觉中影响着孩子对自己的看法。
an insidious effect = 潜移默化的坏影响
③ The damp had spread insidiously through the walls of the old house.
= 潮气已经悄悄地渗透了老房子的墙壁。
spread insidiously = 悄悄蔓延

insight
核心：洞察力；深刻的见解。不可数时指看透事物本质的能力；可数时指一个具体的深刻认识（an insight into）。
形容词 insightful 另立。
① The book gives a fascinating insight into life in a medieval village.
= 那本书让人深入了解了中世纪村庄的生活，引人入胜。
an insight into something = 对…的深入了解
② She is a writer of great insight and humour.
= 她是一位富有洞察力和幽默感的作家。
great insight = 很强的洞察力
③ Talking to the patients gave the doctors new insights into the disease.
= 跟病人交谈让医生们对这种病有了新的认识。
new insights = 新的认识

insightful
核心：有洞察力的、见解深刻的 —— 说评论、分析、人。
① Her insightful comments helped the team see what had gone wrong.
= 她见解深刻的评论帮助团队看清了问题出在哪里。
insightful comments = 见解深刻的评论
② He is an insightful critic of modern architecture.
= 他是一位对现代建筑很有洞见的评论家。
an insightful critic = 有洞见的评论家

insignia
核心：徽章、标志 —— 表示军衔、职位、组织的标记。单复同形（也有人用 insignias）。
① The pilot's uniform carried the insignia of the air force.
= 那名飞行员的制服上佩戴着空军的徽章。
the insignia of something = …的徽章
② The general removed his insignia before meeting the enemy commander.
= 将军在会见敌方指挥官之前摘下了自己的军衔标志。
remove your insignia = 摘下徽章

insignificant
核心：微不足道的、不重要的 —— 小到或少到不值一提；人觉得自己渺小。
名词 insignificance 与副词 insignificantly 并入本条。
① The difference in price between the two phones is insignificant.
= 两款手机的价差微不足道。
be insignificant = 微不足道
② Standing under the huge old trees, she felt small and insignificant.
= 站在那些巨大的古树下，她觉得自己渺小而微不足道。
feel insignificant = 觉得自己渺小
③ His problems faded into insignificance when he heard about her illness.
= 听说她病了，他自己的那些烦心事就显得无足轻重了。
fade into insignificance = 相形之下无足轻重
④ Profits rose only insignificantly last year.
= 去年利润增长得微乎其微。
insignificantly = 微不足道地

insincere
核心：不真诚的、虚伪的 —— 嘴上说一套，心里想一套。副词 insincerely 与名词 insincerity 并入本条。
① Read out on television the next morning, his apology sounded insincere, and nobody believed him.
= 第二天早上他在电视上念的道歉听起来很不真诚，没人相信他。
sound insincere = 听起来不真诚
② Our new neighbour smiled insincerely and said she was delighted to see us at her party.
= 新邻居虚情假意地笑着，说很高兴我们来参加她的派对。
smile insincerely = 虚情假意地笑
③ Voters are tired of the insincerity of politicians.
= 选民们厌倦了政客的虚伪。
the insincerity of somebody = 某人的虚伪

insinuate
核心：暗示、影射（坏事）；（正式）使（自己）巧妙地挤进。两支：不直说而暗指某人不好（insinuate that）；
慢慢地、不引人注意地让自己进入某个位置（insinuate yourself into）。名词 insinuation 并入本条。
① Are you insinuating that I only got the promotion because I play golf with the boss?
= 你是在暗示我升职只是因为跟老板打高尔夫吗？
insinuate that = 暗示…（不好的事）
② He insinuated himself into the director's circle of friends.
= 他想方设法挤进了董事的朋友圈子。
insinuate yourself into something = 设法挤进…
③ She was hurt by the insinuation that she had only got the job because of her father.
= 有人暗示她是靠父亲才得到那份工作，这让她很受伤。
an insinuation that = …的暗示、影射

insipid
核心：淡而无味的；枯燥乏味的。本义说食物没味道；比喻说人、作品平淡无趣。
① The hospital soup was watery and insipid, so she added half a bottle of pepper sauce.
= 医院的汤又稀又没味，她加了半瓶辣椒酱。
insipid food = 淡而无味的食物
② Despite a strong cast and beautiful scenery, the film is let down by its insipid script.
= 尽管演员阵容强大、景色优美，那部电影还是被乏味的剧本拖累了。
an insipid script = 乏味的剧本

insist
核心：坚持、坚决要求；坚称。两支：坚决要求做某事（insist on something / on doing / that）；
不管别人信不信，坚持说某事是真的（insist that）。insist upon 更正式。
① She insisted on paying for the meal, although it was my birthday.
= 虽然是我生日，她还是坚持要付饭钱。
insist on doing something = 坚持要做…
② The doctor insisted that he stay in bed for a week.
= 医生坚决要求他卧床一周。
insist that somebody do something = 坚决要求某人做…
后面的从句常用原形动词（虚拟），也可用 should。
③ He insists that he was at home all evening, but nobody saw him.
= 他坚称自己整晚都在家，可没人看见他。
insist that = 坚称…
④ The school insists upon a high standard of behaviour.
= 学校对行为举止要求很严。
insist upon something = 坚持要求…（正式）
⑤ "Have another piece of cake." "Well, if you insist."
= 「再吃一块蛋糕吧。」「好吧，既然你一定要我吃。」
if you insist = 既然你坚持
口语里接受劝让时的客套话。

insistence
核心：坚持、坚决要求 —— insist 的名词，不可数。常见 at somebody's insistence（在某人的坚持下）。
① At her father's insistence, she applied to study law.
= 在父亲的坚持下，她申请了法律专业。
at somebody's insistence = 在某人的坚持下
② His insistence on doing everything himself slowed the project down.
= 他坚持凡事亲力亲为，拖慢了项目进度。
insistence on something = 对…的坚持

insistent
核心：坚持的、执意的；持续不断的。两支：人再三要求（be insistent that）；声音、要求不停地重复（an insistent knocking）。
副词 insistently 并入本条。
① Our host was insistent that we should stay for dinner, even though it was already late.
= 虽然天已经晚了，主人还是执意要我们留下吃晚饭。
be insistent that = 执意要…
② The insistent ringing of the phone finally woke him up.
= 电话铃声响个不停，终于把他吵醒了。
insistent ringing = 响个不停的铃声
③ In the queue for the ice cream, the child tugged insistently at her mother's sleeve.
= 排队买冰淇淋时，那孩子一个劲地拽着妈妈的袖子。
insistently = 执意地、一个劲地

insobriety
核心：（正式）醉酒、酗酒 —— sobriety（清醒、不喝酒）的反面，多见于正式或旧式文字。
① The captain was dismissed for insobriety while on duty.
= 船长因值勤时醉酒被解职。
insobriety = 醉酒
② The report blamed the accident on the driver's insobriety.
= 报告把事故归咎于司机醉酒。
somebody's insobriety = 某人的醉酒状态

insofar as
核心：（正式）在…范围内、就…而言 —— 连词，限定说法适用的程度。英式也写成 in so far as。
① The plan was a success insofar as it saved money.
= 就省钱而言，这个计划是成功的。
insofar as = 就…而言
② I will help you insofar as I can, but I cannot promise anything.
= 我会尽我所能帮你，但我什么也不能保证。
insofar as I can = 在我力所能及的范围内

insole
核心：鞋垫；（鞋的）内底 —— 放在鞋子里、脚踩着的那层。
① He bought gel insoles to make his old boots more comfortable.
= 他买了凝胶鞋垫，让那双旧靴子穿起来更舒服。
an insole = 鞋垫
② The insole of the shoe had worn through after a year.
= 一年后那双鞋的内底就磨穿了。
the insole of a shoe = 鞋的内底

insolent
核心：傲慢无礼的、放肆的 —— 对应该尊敬的人不尊敬，故意冒犯。
名词 insolence 与副词 insolently 并入本条。
① The boy was sent home for being insolent to his teacher.
= 那男孩因对老师出言不逊被遣送回家。
be insolent to somebody = 对某人无礼
② The old hotel manager would not tolerate insolence from his staff, however busy they were.
= 那位老酒店经理不能容忍员工无礼，不管他们多忙。
insolence = 无礼
③ The young guard stared insolently at the visitors and took his time checking their passes.
= 年轻的门卫傲慢地盯着访客，慢吞吞地查看他们的通行证。
insolently = 傲慢无礼地

insoluble
核心：不能溶解的；无法解决的。两支：化学上不溶于水；比喻问题没法解决（美式后一支多说 insolvable）。
① Chalk and sand are insoluble in water, so they sink to the bottom of the glass.
= 粉笔和沙子不溶于水，所以会沉到杯底。
be insoluble in something = 不溶于…
② For years the problem of traffic on the old bridge seemed insoluble to the town council.
= 多年来，镇议会一直觉得旧桥上的交通问题无法解决。
an insoluble problem = 无法解决的问题

insolvent
核心：（公司、个人）无力偿债的、破产的 —— 法律、财务用语。名词 insolvency 并入本条。
① The company was declared insolvent after its biggest customer went bankrupt.
= 最大的客户破产后，那家公司被宣告无力偿债。
be declared insolvent = 被宣告无力偿债
比 bankrupt 更偏财务状态，bankrupt 更偏法律程序的结果。
② Rising costs have pushed many small firms into insolvency.
= 成本上涨把许多小公司推向了破产。
insolvency = 无力偿债、破产

insomnia
核心：失眠（症）—— 长期难以入睡或睡不好。不可数。
① Stress at work gave her insomnia, and she rarely slept more than four hours.
= 工作压力让她失眠，她很少能睡超过四个小时。
suffer from insomnia = 患失眠症
② The doctor suggested cutting out coffee to cure his insomnia.
= 医生建议他戒掉咖啡来治失眠。
cure insomnia = 治失眠

insomniac
核心：失眠者 —— 长期失眠的人。也作形容词。
① As an insomniac, he often wrote his best songs at three in the morning.
= 他是个失眠者，最好的歌常常是凌晨三点写出来的。
an insomniac = 失眠者
② Late-night radio has a loyal audience of insomniacs and night workers.
= 深夜电台有一批忠实听众，都是失眠的人和上夜班的人。
insomniacs = 失眠的人

insouciant
核心：（正式）漫不经心的、无忧无虑的 —— 对本该在意的事显得满不在乎。名词 insouciance 并入本条。
① He gave an insouciant shrug when asked about the missing money.
= 被问到那笔不见了的钱，他满不在乎地耸了耸肩。
an insouciant shrug = 满不在乎地耸肩
② She treated the whole crisis with remarkable insouciance.
= 她对整场危机表现得异常淡定。
with insouciance = 满不在乎地

inspect
核心：检查、视察 —— 仔细查看以发现问题；官员正式视察学校、工厂、部队。
① The mechanic inspected the brakes and said they needed replacing.
= 机修工检查了刹车，说需要更换。
inspect something = 检查…
② Government officials inspect every school at least once every four years.
= 政府官员至少每四年视察一次每所学校。
inspect a school = 视察学校
③ The queen inspected the troops before the parade began.
= 游行开始前，女王检阅了部队。
inspect the troops = 检阅部队

inspection
核心：检查、视察 —— inspect 的名词。on closer inspection（仔细一看）是常用短语。
① The inspection found several safety problems in the factory.
= 检查发现工厂里有几处安全问题。
an inspection = 检查、视察
② Visitors can see the documents on inspection at the town hall.
= 访客可以在市政厅查阅这些文件。
on inspection = 供查阅
③ The ring looked like gold, but on closer inspection it was brass.
= 那枚戒指看着像金的，仔细一看却是黄铜的。
on closer inspection = 仔细一看

inspector
核心：检查员、督察；（英）警督。几支：负责检查的官员（a health inspector）；
英国警察的一个级别（Detective Inspector）；检票员（ticket inspector）。
inspector of taxes（英，税务稽查员）并入本条。
① A health inspector visited the restaurant and closed the kitchen.
= 一位卫生检查员来到餐馆，关停了厨房。
a health inspector = 卫生检查员
② Inspector Morgan led the investigation into the robbery.
= 摩根督察负责调查那起抢劫案。
Inspector = （英）督察（警衔）
③ The ticket inspector fined him for travelling without a ticket.
= 查票员因他无票乘车罚了他款。
a ticket inspector = 查票员
④ He received a letter from the inspector of taxes about his unpaid bills.
= 他收到税务稽查员寄来的一封信，谈他欠缴的税款。
an inspector of taxes = （英）税务稽查员

inspectorate
核心：（英）检查团、督察机构 —— 负责检查某类机构（学校、监狱）的官方部门。
① The schools inspectorate criticized the college for poor teaching.
= 学校督察机构批评那所学院教学质量差。
the schools inspectorate = 学校督察机构
② The prisons inspectorate publishes a report on every prison it visits.
= 监狱督察机构每视察一所监狱就发布一份报告。
the prisons inspectorate = 监狱督察机构
