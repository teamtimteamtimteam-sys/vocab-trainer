praxis
/ˈpræksɪs/
核心：（哲学，正式）实践：把一种理论、一门学问实际用起来、做出来，跟 theory 相对。不可数。
① The essay argues that theory is worthless unless it leads to praxis.
= 那篇文章认为理论若不通向实践就毫无价值。
praxis = 实践
书面词，哲学、教育学里常见。
② The course joins the study of teaching to the praxis of the classroom.
= 那门课把教学研究跟课堂实践结合起来。
the praxis of the classroom = 课堂实践
来自希腊语「做」。

pray
/preɪ/
核心：动词：祈祷、祷告：pray to 加神、pray for 加人或事、pray that；引申为极力盼望：pray for rain。
副词：（旧，正式）请：用在请求、提问前面，如今多带讽刺。
① They knelt down and prayed to God for help.
= 他们跪下来向上帝祈求帮助。
pray to God = 向上帝祈祷
pray for somebody = 为某人祈祷
② The whole village prayed for the safe return of the fishing boats.
= 全村人都为渔船平安归来祈祷。
pray for something = 为某事祈祷
③ We are praying that the weather will hold until the harvest is in.
= 我们巴望着天气能撑到庄稼收完。
pray that = 巴望、祈祷
这一支不一定跟宗教有关。
④ And what, pray, do you think you are doing in my garden?
= 请问，你以为你在我的花园里干什么？
pray = 请（旧式，带讽刺）

prayer
/preə(r)/
核心：名词：祷词、祈祷文，可数：say a prayer；祈祷（这件事），不可数：kneel in prayer；prayers 是祈祷仪式；也指心里极盼的事。
习语：not have a prayer 是（口语）毫无成功的希望；on a wing and a prayer 是全凭侥幸、勉强撑着；the answer to somebody's prayers 是某人求之不得的人、物。
形容词 prayerful 是虔诚祈祷的。
复合词：prayer book 是祈祷书；prayer meeting 是祷告会；prayer rug、prayer mat 是（穆斯林的）礼拜毯；prayer wheel 是（藏传佛教的）转经筒。并入本条。
① The children say their prayers before they go to bed.
= 孩子们睡前做祷告。
say your prayers = 做祷告
a prayer for something = 为某事作的祷告
② The old woman knelt in prayer before the altar.
= 老太太跪在祭坛前祈祷。
in prayer = 在祈祷
这一支不可数。
③ The school begins each day with prayers in the hall.
= 那所学校每天以礼堂里的祈祷开始。
prayers = 祈祷仪式
morning prayers = 晨祷
④ The team does not have a prayer against the champions.
= 那支球队对上冠军毫无胜算。
not have a prayer = 毫无成功的希望
⑤ The pilot brought the damaged plane home on a wing and a prayer.
= 飞行员全凭侥幸把受损的飞机开了回来。
on a wing and a prayer = 全凭侥幸
⑥ A flat near the station was the answer to our prayers.
= 车站附近的一套公寓正是我们求之不得的。
the answer to somebody's prayers = 某人求之不得的东西
⑦ The service ended with a prayerful silence of two minutes.
= 礼拜以两分钟虔诚的默祷结束。
prayerful = 虔诚祈祷的
⑧ She carried her grandmother's prayer book to church every Sunday.
= 她每个星期天带着奶奶的祈祷书去教堂。
a prayer book = 祈祷书
⑨ The women hold a prayer meeting in the hall on Wednesday evenings.
= 妇女们星期三晚上在礼堂里开祷告会。
a prayer meeting = 祷告会
⑩ He unrolled his prayer rug and turned towards the east.
= 他铺开礼拜毯，转身朝向东方。
a prayer rug = 礼拜毯
⑪ The pilgrims turned the prayer wheels as they walked round the temple.
= 朝圣的人绕着寺庙走，一边转动转经筒。
a prayer wheel = 转经筒

preach
/priːtʃ/
核心：动词：布道、讲道：（神职人员）在礼拜上宣讲教义；宣扬、鼓吹（一种主张、做法）；（贬）说教、唠唠叨叨地教训人：preach at、to。
习语：preach to the converted 是对已经信了的人传道、白费口舌；practise what you preach 是言行一致。
派生：preacher 是传道人、牧师；preachy 是（口语，贬）爱说教的。并入本条。
① The vicar preached on forgiveness to a church that was almost empty.
= 牧师对着几乎空无一人的教堂讲了一篇关于宽恕的道。
preach on something = 就某题目布道
preach a sermon = 讲道
② She preaches the benefits of a diet without meat or sugar.
= 她宣扬不吃肉、不吃糖的饮食的好处。
preach the benefits of something = 宣扬某事的好处
③ I wish he would stop preaching at me about my smoking.
= 我真希望他别再为我抽烟的事教训我。
preach at somebody = 教训某人
这一支带贬义：说教。
④ Telling teachers that books matter is preaching to the converted.
= 跟教师讲书很重要，是对着信徒传道。
preach to the converted = 对已经信了的人传道
⑤ The preacher spoke for an hour without looking at his notes.
= 那位传道人讲了一个钟头，没看一眼笔记。
a preacher = 传道人
多指新教里讲道有感染力的人。
⑥ The film is spoiled by a preachy ending about the evils of money.
= 那部片子被一个说教味很重、讲金钱之恶的结尾糟蹋了。
preachy = 爱说教的

preamble
/priˈæmbl/
核心：（正式）序言、前言、开场白：正文之前说明目的、缘由的那一段。可数也可不可数；without preamble 是开门见山地。
① The preamble to the treaty sets out the aims of the two governments.
= 条约的序言阐明了两国政府的目标。
the preamble to a treaty = 条约的序言
宪法、法律文件开头的那段也叫这个。
② She told me without preamble that the firm was closing.
= 她开门见山地告诉我公司要关门了。
without preamble = 开门见山地

prearranged
/ˌpriːəˈreɪndʒd/
核心：预先安排好的、事先约定的。
① At a prearranged signal the lights went out and the band began to play.
= 一个事先约好的信号一发，灯灭了，乐队奏起来。
a prearranged signal = 事先约好的信号
也写作 pre-arranged。
② The two men met at a prearranged spot behind the station.
= 两人在车站后面一个预先约定的地点碰了头。
a prearranged spot = 预先约定的地点
pre- 是「在……之前」。

prebuttal
/priˈbʌtl/
核心：（口语）预先反驳：政客在对手还没开口批评之前，抢先作出的回应。可数；由 pre- 和 rebuttal 拼成。
① The minister issued a prebuttal an hour before the report was published.
= 报告发表前一个钟头，部长就发了一份抢先的反驳。
a prebuttal = 预先的反驳
新闻、政治评论里的新词。
② The campaign team spent the morning preparing prebuttals to the rival's speech.
= 竞选班子一上午都在准备对对手演讲的预先反驳。
prepare a prebuttal = 准备预先的反驳

precancerous
/ˌpriːˈkænsərəs/
核心：（医学）癌前的：（细胞、病变）不处理就可能变成癌的。
① The test found precancerous cells that were removed the same week.
= 检查发现了癌前细胞，当周就切除了。
precancerous cells = 癌前细胞
a precancerous condition = 癌前病变
② The doctor explained that the growth was precancerous and should not be left.
= 医生解释说那个肿物是癌前病变，不能放着不管。
be precancerous = 是癌前的

precariat
/prɪˈkeəriət/
核心：不稳定无产者：没有固定工作、没有保障、靠零工和短期合同过活的阶层。单数；由 precarious 和 proletariat 拼成。
① The book describes a growing precariat of drivers and cleaners with no fixed hours.
= 那本书写的是一个日益壮大、由没有固定工时的司机和清洁工组成的不稳定阶层。
a growing precariat = 日益壮大的不稳定阶层
社会学的新词，带批评意味。
② Many graduates now join the precariat instead of finding a career.
= 许多大学毕业生如今找不到一份事业，沦入不稳定阶层。
join the precariat = 沦入不稳定阶层

precarious
/prɪˈkeəriəs/
核心：两支。一是（处境）不稳定的、没保障的、岌岌可危的；二是（位置）不稳固的、随时会掉、会倒的。
副词 precariously；名词 precariousness，不可数。并入本条。
① He earns a precarious living as a street musician.
= 他当街头乐手，过着朝不保夕的日子。
a precarious living = 朝不保夕的生计
a precarious existence = 朝不保夕的生活
② The ladder was in a precarious position on the edge of the roof.
= 梯子搁在屋檐边上，位置很悬。
a precarious position = 很悬的位置
也用来说处境。
③ The vase was balanced precariously on a pile of books.
= 那只花瓶摇摇欲坠地架在一摞书上。
precariously = 摇摇欲坠地
④ The strike showed the precariousness of the government's majority.
= 那场罢工显出政府的多数席位有多不牢靠。
precariousness = 不稳、岌岌可危

precast
/ˌpriːˈkɑːst/
核心：（混凝土构件）预制的：先在工厂里浇成一块块，再运到工地拼装。多作定语。
① The walls of the car park are made of precast concrete panels.
= 那座停车场的墙是预制混凝土板拼的。
precast concrete = 预制混凝土
cast 是「浇铸」。
② The bridge was built in a month from precast sections.
= 那座桥是用预制的构件一个月搭起来的。
precast sections = 预制构件

precaution
/prɪˈkɔːʃn/
核心：预防措施、防备：为免出事预先做的事。可数，多用复数：take precautions、as a precaution。
形容词 precautionary 是预防性的。并入本条。
① You should take sensible precautions against theft when you travel alone in a crowded city.
= 一个人在拥挤的城市里出行，应当采取合理的防盗措施。
take precautions against something = 采取防备某事的措施
take the precaution of doing something = 为保险起见做某事
② After the fall she was kept in hospital overnight as a precaution, although no bones were broken.
= 摔了那一跤以后，虽然没骨折，为了稳妥起见她还是被留院观察了一夜。
as a precaution = 为了稳妥起见
③ The school was closed for a day as a precautionary measure after two pupils fell ill.
= 两名学生病倒之后，作为预防措施，学校停课一天。
a precautionary measure = 预防措施

precede
/prɪˈsiːd/
核心：（正式）在……之前发生、出现；走在……前面；precede A with B 是在甲之前先来个乙。及物。
形容词 preceding 只作定语：在前的、前面的：the preceding chapter。并入本条。
① A long and uncomfortable silence preceded her answer to the lawyer's last question.
= 她回答律师最后一个问题之前，有一阵长长的、令人难受的沉默。
precede something = 在某事之前发生
反义是 follow。
② The mayor entered the crowded hall, preceded by two guards in red coats carrying silver staffs.
= 市长走进挤满人的大厅，前面有两名穿红外套、手持银杖的卫兵开路。
be preceded by somebody = 前面有某人走着
③ He preceded his long speech on taxes with a joke about the weather that nobody understood.
= 讲那篇关于税的长话之前，他先说了个谁也没听懂的天气笑话。
precede A with B = 在甲之前先来乙
④ The reasons for the change of plan were explained at length in the preceding chapter.
= 改变计划的原因在前一章已经详细讲过。
the preceding chapter = 前一章
别跟 proceed（继续进行）混。

precedence
/ˈpresɪdəns/
核心：优先、优先权：因为更重要而排在前面。不可数：take precedence over、give precedence to；in order of precedence 是按尊卑先后。
① The safety of the passengers takes precedence over everything else.
= 乘客的安全高于一切。
take precedence over something = 优先于某事
give precedence to something = 让某事优先
② The guests were seated in order of precedence.
= 客人按尊卑先后入座。
in order of precedence = 按尊卑先后

precedent
/ˈpresɪdənt/
核心：先例、前例：可以拿来当依据的、以前有过的事；（法律）判例。可数也可不可数：set a precedent、without precedent。
① The ruling sets a precedent for other cases of the same kind.
= 那项裁决为同类的其他案件立下了先例。
set a precedent = 立下先例
create a precedent = 开先例
② The floods were without precedent in the history of the town.
= 那场洪水在该镇历史上前所未有。
without precedent = 前所未有
形容词是 unprecedented。
③ There is no precedent for a president resigning in his first year.
= 总统上任头一年就辞职，没有先例。
a precedent for something = 某事的先例

precept
/ˈpriːsept/
核心：（正式）戒律、准则、箴言：教人怎样立身处世的一条规矩。可数也可不可数。
① The children were taught the precept that honesty is the best policy.
= 孩子们受的教训是：诚实为上。
a precept = 准则、箴言
书面词，日常说 rule、principle。
② He taught more by example than by precept.
= 他言传少，身教多。
by precept = 靠言传

precinct
/ˈpriːsɪŋkt/
核心：三支，可数。一是（英式）（城里专作某种用途的）区域：a shopping precinct 是步行购物区；二是（美式）警区、选区，也指警区的分局；
三是 precincts：（大教堂、学院等）围墙以内的地方、周围的场地，复数。
① The new shopping precinct has no cars and plenty of seats.
= 新建的步行购物区不通汽车，座位很多。
a shopping precinct = 步行购物区
a pedestrian precinct = 步行街区
② The detective had worked in the same precinct for twenty years.
= 那位侦探在同一个警区干了二十年。
a police precinct = 警区
③ Smoking is forbidden within the precincts of the hospital.
= 医院院内禁止吸烟。
the precincts of a place = 某处的院内

precious
/ˈpreʃəs/
核心：形容词：贵重的、值钱的：precious metals、precious stone 是宝石；宝贵的、珍贵的：因为稀少、要紧而不能浪费；珍爱的、心爱的；
（贬）矫揉造作的、过分讲究的；（口语，只作定语）表示不屑、恼火：your precious car。
副词：（口语）precious little、precious few 是少得可怜。名词：（口语）宝贝，用作称呼。
派生：preciousness、preciosity 是矫揉造作，后者是书面词。并入本条。
① The thieves took gold, silver and other precious metals from the safe.
= 窃贼从保险柜里拿走了金、银和别的贵金属。
precious metals = 贵金属
指金、银、铂这几种。
② The ring is set with a single precious stone.
= 那枚戒指镶着一颗宝石。
a precious stone = 宝石
③ Clean water is a precious resource in the desert.
= 在沙漠里，干净的水是宝贵的资源。
a precious resource = 宝贵的资源
precious time = 宝贵的时间
④ We wasted two precious hours waiting for the bus.
= 我们等公共汽车白白耗了两个钟头的宝贵时间。
precious hours = 宝贵的时间
⑤ The photographs are very precious to her, because they are all she has of her father.
= 那些照片对她非常珍贵，她父亲留下的就只有这些。
precious to somebody = 对某人珍贵
⑥ His poems are a little too precious for my taste.
= 他的诗对我的口味来说有点太矫情。
be precious = 矫揉造作
⑦ I never touched your precious car!
= 你那辆宝贝车我碰都没碰！
your precious car = 你那辆宝贝车
⑧ There is precious little evidence that the scheme has worked.
= 能证明那个方案奏效的证据少得可怜。
precious little = 少得可怜
这里是副词，只跟 little、few 连用。
⑨ Precious few of the passengers knew how to open the emergency door.
= 知道怎么开紧急出口的乘客没几个。
precious few = 没几个
⑩ Critics complained about the preciousness of the dialogue.
= 评论家抱怨对白太矫情。
preciousness = 矫揉造作
preciosity = 矫揉造作

precipice
/ˈpresəpɪs/
核心：悬崖、峭壁：山、岩石极陡的一面。可数；比喻危急的关头：on the edge of a precipice。
① The path runs along the edge of a precipice with no fence.
= 那条小路沿着一道没有护栏的悬崖边走。
a precipice = 悬崖
比 cliff 更强调陡、险。
② The country stood on the edge of a precipice in the last days of the crisis.
= 危机的最后几天，那个国家站在悬崖边上。
on the edge of a precipice = 在悬崖边上、危在旦夕

precipitate
/prɪˈsɪpɪteɪt/（动）/prɪˈsɪpɪtət/（形、名）
核心：动词：（正式）使（坏事）突然发生、加速（危机）的到来；precipitate somebody、something into 是使……猛然陷入；（化学）使沉淀。
形容词：（正式）仓促的、鲁莽的。副词 precipitately。名词：（化学）沉淀物，可数。
① The minister's resignation precipitated a crisis in the government.
= 那位部长的辞职引发了政府的一场危机。
precipitate a crisis = 引发危机
宾语多是坏事。
② The murder precipitated the country into civil war.
= 那起谋杀让国家猛然陷入内战。
precipitate something into something = 使某物猛然陷入某种状态
③ We must not take any precipitate action before the facts are known.
= 事实弄清之前，我们不可贸然行动。
precipitate action = 贸然的行动
形容词末音节读 /ət/。
④ He left the meeting precipitately and did not come back.
= 他匆匆离开了会场，没再回来。
precipitately = 仓促地
⑤ A white precipitate formed at the bottom of the tube.
= 试管底部形成了白色沉淀。
a precipitate = 沉淀物

precipitation
/prɪˌsɪpɪˈteɪʃn/
核心：三支，不可数。一是（气象）降水：雨、雪、冰雹的总称，也指降水量；二是（化学）沉淀；三是（正式）仓促、鲁莽。
① The region has an annual precipitation of less than ten centimetres.
= 那个地区的年降水量不到十厘米。
annual precipitation = 年降水量
气象预报的术语，日常说 rain、snow。
② Precipitation will fall as snow on the hills tonight.
= 今晚山上的降水将以雪的形式落下。
precipitation = 降水
③ He acted with a precipitation that he later regretted.
= 他当时做得很仓促，后来后悔了。
with precipitation = 仓促地

precipitous
/prɪˈsɪpɪtəs/
核心：（正式）三支。一是险峻的、陡峭的；二是（变化）急剧的、骤然的；三是仓促的、贸然的。副词 precipitously，并入本条。
① The castle stands on a precipitous rock above the river.
= 那座城堡耸立在河上方一块险峻的岩石上。
a precipitous rock = 险峻的岩石
从 precipice 来。
② There has been a precipitous fall in the number of visitors.
= 游客人数急剧下降。
a precipitous fall = 急剧的下降
③ The land drops precipitously to the sea on the western side.
= 西面的地势陡然落向大海。
precipitously = 陡然地、急剧地

precis
/ˈpreɪsiː/
核心：摘要、梗概：把一篇文章、一次讲话的要点压成短短的一段。可数，复数不变，读作 /ˈpreɪsiːz/；也写作 précis。动词：写……的摘要。
① The pupils had to write a precis of the article in a hundred words.
= 学生们得用一百个词写出那篇文章的摘要。
a precis of something = 某物的摘要
② She precised the report for the minister in a single page.
= 她把那份报告给部长压成了一页摘要。
precis a report = 给报告写摘要
来自法语，末尾的 s 不发音。

precise
/prɪˈsaɪs/
核心：三支。一是准确的、精确的；二是恰好的、正是那个的，只作定语：at that precise moment；三是（人）严谨的、一丝不苟的。
to be precise 是确切地说。副词 precisely：精确地；恰恰、正是；用来表示完全同意：Precisely.
① Can you give me a more precise idea of the cost before I speak to the bank?
= 我跟银行谈之前，你能就花费给我个更确切的数吗？
a precise idea = 确切的概念
② At that precise moment the telephone rang, and the cat jumped off the table in fright.
= 就在那一刻，电话响了，猫吓得从桌上跳了下去。
at that precise moment = 就在那一刻
③ He is very precise in everything he does, from folding his shirts to filling in his tax form.
= 他做什么都一丝不苟，从叠衬衫到填税表。
be precise = 严谨、一丝不苟
④ There were about forty people there, or thirty-eight to be precise.
= 当时大约有四十人，确切地说是三十八人。
to be precise = 确切地说
⑤ The meeting will begin at two o'clock precisely, and the doors will be closed at five past.
= 会议两点整开始，两点零五分关门。
at two o'clock precisely = 两点整
⑥ That is precisely what I have been trying to tell you for the last half hour.
= 这半个钟头我一直想跟你说的正是这个。
precisely what = 正是……的
⑦ When I asked whether the bank had refused the loan after all, she answered, "Precisely."
= 我问银行到头来是不是拒了那笔贷款，她答道：「正是。」
Precisely. = 正是。

precision
/prɪˈsɪʒn/
核心：精确、准确、精密。不可数：with precision；常作定语：precision instruments 是精密仪器。
① The surgeon cut with great precision round the nerve that controls the movement of the hand.
= 那位外科医生十分精准地绕着控制手部活动的神经下刀。
with precision = 精准地
② The small factory outside the town makes precision instruments for hospitals all over Europe.
= 城外的那家小工厂给全欧洲的医院生产精密仪器。
precision instruments = 精密仪器
③ The forty dancers moved across the stage with military precision and never once collided.
= 四十名舞者像军人一样整齐划一地穿过舞台，一次也没撞上。
military precision = 军人般的整齐划一

preclude
/prɪˈkluːd/
核心：（正式）排除、妨碍、使不可能：preclude something、preclude somebody from doing。及物。
① Lack of time precludes any further discussion.
= 时间不够，没法再往下讨论。
preclude something = 使某事不可能
② His injury precluded him from playing for the rest of the season.
= 他的伤让他这个赛季余下的比赛都打不了了。
preclude somebody from doing something = 使某人不能做某事

precocious
/prɪˈkəʊʃəs/
核心：（孩子）早熟的、早慧的：某些本事发展得比同龄人早；有时含贬义：人小鬼大、老成得让人不舒服。
副词 precociously；名词 precocity，不可数，也说 precociousness。并入本条。
① She was a precocious child who could read at the age of three.
= 她是个早慧的孩子，三岁就能阅读。
a precocious child = 早慧的孩子
② He showed a precocious talent for the violin and gave his first concert at the age of nine.
= 他早早显出了拉小提琴的才能，九岁就开了头一场音乐会。
a precocious talent = 早露的才能
③ The boy was precociously clever and corrected his teachers' spelling in front of the whole class.
= 那男孩聪明得早，会当着全班的面纠正老师的拼写。
precociously = 早熟地
④ The precocity of the young composer astonished the court.
= 那位年轻作曲家的早慧让宫廷上下惊叹。
precocity = 早熟、早慧

precognition
/ˌpriːkɒɡˈnɪʃn/
核心：（正式）预知、先知：事情发生之前就知道它会发生的本事，科学解释不了。不可数。
① She claimed to have had a moment of precognition in the car a minute before the accident.
= 她声称出事前一分钟在车里有过一瞬间的预知。
a moment of precognition = 一瞬间的预知
② No carefully controlled experiment has ever shown that precognition exists outside stories and films.
= 没有哪个严格控制的实验证明过，预知在故事和电影之外确实存在。
precognition = 预知

preconceived
/ˌpriːkənˈsiːvd/
核心：（看法、想法）事先形成的、先入为主的：还没了解实情就有了的。只作定语。名词 preconception 是先入之见、成见，可数，多用复数。并入本条。
① She arrived in the village with no preconceived ideas about country people.
= 她来到那个村子的时候，对乡下人没有先入为主的看法。
preconceived ideas = 先入为主的看法
pre- 是「预先」，conceive 是「形成想法」。
② The jury was told to put aside any preconceived notions about the accused.
= 陪审团被告知要抛开对被告的一切成见。
preconceived notions = 成见
③ The film challenges our preconceptions about life in a small prison.
= 那部片子挑战了我们对小监狱里生活的先入之见。
preconceptions about something = 对某事的先入之见
a preconception = 一个先入之见

precondition
/ˌpriːkənˈdɪʃn/
核心：先决条件、前提：一件事要发生，先得满足的条件，介词用 for、of。可数。
① A ceasefire is a precondition for any talks between the two sides.
= 停火是双方进行任何会谈的先决条件。
a precondition for something = 某事的先决条件
谈判、合同里常见。
② The bank made the sale of the old house a precondition of the loan.
= 银行把卖掉老房子定为放贷的前提。
a precondition of something = 某事的前提
也说 prerequisite。

preconscious
/ˌpriːˈkɒnʃəs/
核心：（心理学）前意识的：眼下不在意识里、一想就能想起来的（念头、记忆）。也作名词：the preconscious 是前意识，单数。
① The name was stored in his preconscious mind and came back as soon as he saw her face.
= 那个名字存在他的前意识里，一见她的脸就想了起来。
the preconscious mind = 前意识
介于 conscious（意识）和 unconscious（无意识）之间。
② The lecture explained the difference between preconscious memories and those that are truly buried.
= 那场讲座讲了前意识的记忆跟真正被埋起来的记忆有什么不同。
preconscious memories = 前意识的记忆

precooked
/ˌpriːˈkʊkt/
核心：预先煮熟的：事先做好、吃的时候只要热一热的。
① The canteen serves precooked meals that are heated up in a microwave.
= 那家食堂供应预先做好、用微波炉加热的饭菜。
precooked meals = 预制的熟食
也写作 pre-cooked。
② Use precooked rice if you have only ten minutes to make the dish.
= 要是只有十分钟做这道菜，就用预先煮熟的米饭。
precooked rice = 预先煮熟的米饭

precursor
/priˈkɜːsə(r)/
核心：（正式）先驱、前身、先兆：在同类的人、事物出现之前就有的、为它开了路的那一个，介词用 of、to。可数。
① The machine was a precursor of the modern computer.
= 那台机器是现代计算机的前身。
a precursor of something = 某物的前身
字面是「跑在前面的」。
② The small strikes in the spring were a precursor to the unrest of the summer.
= 春天的那几次小罢工是夏天动荡的先兆。
a precursor to something = 某事的先兆
说事件时用 to 更多。

predate
/ˌpriːˈdeɪt/
核心：（在时间上）早于、先于：比……更早出现、建造。及物。反义是 postdate。
① The church predates the castle by at least two hundred years.
= 那座教堂比城堡至少早两百年。
predate something = 早于某物
也写作 pre-date，跟动物的捕食无关。
② Some of the customs of the village predate the arrival of the Romans.
= 那个村子的一些习俗比罗马人到来还早。
predate the Romans = 早于罗马人到来

predator
/ˈpredətə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是捕食性动物、食肉动物：靠捕杀别的动物为食的；二是（贬）掠夺者：利用别人的弱点为自己谋利、害人的人、公司。
派生：predatory 是捕食性的、掠夺成性的；predation 是（术语）捕食，不可数。
predatory pricing 是掠夺性定价：故意压低价钱把对手挤垮；predatory lending 是掠夺性放贷：以苛刻条件借钱给还不起的人。并入本条。
① The young birds are safe from predators on the cliff.
= 雏鸟在悬崖上不怕捕食的动物。
a predator = 捕食性动物
被它捕食的叫 prey。
② The firm was bought by a corporate predator who sold its factories one by one.
= 那家公司被一个企业掠夺者买下，工厂被一家一家地卖掉。
a corporate predator = 企业掠夺者
a sexual predator = 性侵惯犯
③ The owl is a predatory bird that hunts at night.
= 猫头鹰是夜里捕猎的猛禽。
a predatory bird = 猛禽
也说 a bird of prey。
④ Predation by foxes has reduced the number of rabbits on the island.
= 狐狸的捕食让岛上的兔子少了。
predation = 捕食
⑤ The airline was fined for predatory pricing on the routes of its smaller rival.
= 那家航空公司因为在小对手的航线上搞掠夺性定价被罚了款。
predatory pricing = 掠夺性定价
⑥ The new law is meant to protect poor families from predatory lending.
= 新法律是为了保护穷苦人家不受掠夺性放贷之害。
predatory lending = 掠夺性放贷

predawn
/ˌpriːˈdɔːn/
核心：黎明前的。只作定语；美式用得多。
① The fishermen set out in the predawn darkness and were back by noon.
= 渔民们在黎明前的黑暗里出发，中午就回来了。
the predawn darkness = 黎明前的黑暗
英式多说 before dawn。
② The police arrested four men in a predawn raid on the flat.
= 警察在黎明前突袭那套公寓，抓了四个人。
a predawn raid = 黎明前的突袭

predecease
/ˌpriːdɪˈsiːs/
核心：（法律，正式）先于……死去。及物。
① The will says that the house should go to his nephew if his wife predeceases him.
= 遗嘱里写明，妻子万一先他而去，房子就归他侄子。
predecease somebody = 先于某人死去
遗嘱、法律文书用语。
② She had no children, and both her brothers predeceased her, so the farm passed to a distant cousin.
= 她没有孩子，两个兄弟又都先她去世，农场就落到了一个远房表亲手里。
predeceased her = 先她去世

predecessor
/ˈpriːdɪsesə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是前任：在某人之前担任同一职位的人；二是（被取代的）前一个东西、前身。反义是 successor。
① The new manager is far more popular than her predecessor.
= 新经理比她的前任受欢迎得多。
somebody's predecessor = 某人的前任
接任的人叫 successor。
② The latest model is lighter and cheaper than its predecessor.
= 最新的型号比前一代轻，也便宜。
its predecessor = 它的前身

predestination
/ˌpriːdestɪˈneɪʃn/
核心：（宗教）宿命论、预定论：认为一切、包括谁得救谁沉沦，都由神早早定下。不可数。
形容词 predestined 是命中注定的：predestined to do。并入本条。
① The preacher believed firmly in the doctrine of predestination.
= 那位传道人坚信预定论的教义。
the doctrine of predestination = 预定论的教义
pre- 加 destine「注定」。
② The two reformers quarrelled bitterly about predestination and free will.
= 那两位改革家为预定论和自由意志吵得很凶。
predestination = 预定论
③ It seemed that the two friends were predestined to become rivals.
= 那两个朋友好像命中注定要成为对手。
predestined to do something = 命中注定要做某事
日常话里不带宗教意味。

predetermine
/ˌpriːdɪˈtɜːmɪn/
核心：（正式）预先决定、事先安排：让一件事的结果早早定下。及物，多用被动。分词 predetermined 作形容词：预先定好的。并入本条。
① The sex of the child is predetermined at the moment of conception.
= 孩子的性别在受孕的那一刻就定下了。
be predetermined = 预先定下
多用被动。
② Critics say that the result of the inquiry was predetermined by the choice of its chairman.
= 批评的人说，选谁当主席，那场调查的结果就已经定了。
be predetermined by something = 由某事预先决定
③ The guards changed places at a predetermined signal.
= 一个预先定好的信号一发，卫兵们就换了岗。
a predetermined signal = 预先定好的信号
a predetermined time = 预先定好的时间

predeterminer
/ˌpriːdɪˈtɜːmɪnə(r)/
核心：（语法）前位限定词：放在冠词等限定词前面的词，如 all the books 里的 all、both my hands 里的 both。可数。
① In "half the cake", the word "half" is a predeterminer.
= 在「half the cake」里，「half」是前位限定词。
a predeterminer = 前位限定词
常见的有 all、both、half、such、what。
② The exercise asks pupils to underline every predeterminer in the paragraph.
= 那道练习要学生把段落里的前位限定词都划出来。
every predeterminer = 每个前位限定词

predicament
/prɪˈdɪkəmənt/
核心：困境、窘境：尴尬、难办、不知如何是好的处境。可数。
① We found ourselves in an awkward predicament when the last bus had gone.
= 末班车一走，我们就陷入了尴尬的境地。
in a predicament = 身处困境
比 difficulty 多一层「左右为难」。
② The loss of the key left him in a real predicament.
= 钥匙一丢，他真是进退两难。
a real predicament = 真正的困境

predicate
/ˈpredɪkət/（名）/ˈpredɪkeɪt/（动）
核心：名词：（语法）谓语：句子里说明主语怎么样、做什么的那部分，包括动词，可数。
动词：（正式）be predicated on 是以……为基础、取决于。
语法上的 predicative 是表语的、作表语用的：（形容词）放在 be、seem 等动词后面，跟 attributive（作定语的）相对；副词 predicatively。并入本条。
① In "The dog barked loudly", the predicate is "barked loudly".
= 在「The dog barked loudly」里，谓语是「barked loudly」。
the predicate = 谓语
名词末音节读 /ət/，动词读 /eɪt/。
② The whole plan is predicated on the assumption that prices will rise.
= 整个计划建立在物价会上涨的假定上。
be predicated on something = 以某事为基础
③ "Asleep" is a predicative adjective and cannot be used before a noun.
= 「asleep」是只作表语的形容词，不能用在名词前。
a predicative adjective = 作表语的形容词
afraid、alive、asleep、alone 都是这一类。
④ The word "afraid" is nearly always used predicatively.
= 「afraid」这个词几乎总是作表语用。
predicatively = 作表语地

predict
/prɪˈdɪkt/
核心：预言、预测、预报：说出将来会发生什么：predict something、predict that、predict what。及物。
派生：predictive 是预测的、有预测力的：predictive text 是（手机的）联想输入；predictor 是预示某事的东西、指标。并入本条。
① Nobody could have predicted the result of the election.
= 那场选举的结果谁也预料不到。
predict the result = 预测结果
字面是「先说出来」。
② The report predicts that sales will double within five years.
= 那份报告预测销量五年内翻一番。
predict that = 预测
③ It is impossible to predict what the weather will do in the mountains.
= 山里的天气会怎样，没法预料。
predict what = 预测……会怎样
④ The phone's predictive text changed "home" into "good".
= 手机的联想输入把「home」改成了「good」。
predictive text = 联想输入
这一支是手机上的用法。
⑤ A child's vocabulary at five is a good predictor of success at school.
= 孩子五岁时的词汇量很能预示日后学业的好坏。
a predictor of something = 预示某事的指标

predictable
/prɪˈdɪktəbl/
核心：两支。一是可以预料的、料得到的；二是（贬）老一套的、毫无新意的。反义是 unpredictable。
副词 predictably；名词 predictability，不可数。并入本条。
① The result of the vote was entirely predictable.
= 表决的结果完全在意料之中。
be predictable = 在意料之中
entirely predictable = 完全在意料之中
② The film has a predictable ending in which the hero gets the girl.
= 那部片子的结尾毫无新意：主人公抱得美人归。
a predictable ending = 毫无新意的结尾
这一支带贬义。
③ Predictably, the trains were late on the first day of snow.
= 不出所料，下雪的头一天火车晚点了。
predictably = 不出所料
④ Children need a certain predictability in their daily lives.
= 孩子的日常生活需要有一定的规律可循。
predictability = 可预见性、有规律

prediction
/prɪˈdɪkʃn/
核心：预言、预测、预报。可数也可不可数：make a prediction、a prediction about、of。
① The weatherman made a prediction of heavy snow for the weekend.
= 气象员预报周末有大雪。
make a prediction = 作出预测
a prediction of something = 对某事的预报
② Her predictions about the price of oil turned out to be correct.
= 她对油价的预测后来证明是对的。
predictions about something = 对某事的预测

predigested
/ˌpriːdaɪˈdʒestɪd/
核心：（贬）（信息、材料）被人事先嚼碎、简化过的：容易懂，却不用自己动脑。
① The textbook offers predigested summaries instead of the original documents.
= 那本教科书给的是嚼碎了的摘要，不是原始文献。
predigested summaries = 嚼碎了的摘要
本义是替人先消化过的食物。
② Viewers are fed predigested opinions by the evening news.
= 晚间新闻把现成的观点喂给观众。
predigested opinions = 现成的观点

predilection
/ˌpriːdɪˈlekʃn/
核心：（正式）偏爱、嗜好：对某样东西特别喜欢，介词用 for。可数，多用单数。
① He has a predilection for long words that nobody else understands.
= 他偏爱别人都听不懂的大词。
a predilection for something = 对某物的偏爱
书面词，日常说 liking、fondness。
② Her predilection for bright colours shows in every room of the house.
= 她对鲜艳颜色的偏爱，房子的每个房间里都看得出来。
somebody's predilection for something = 某人对某物的偏爱

predispose
/ˌpriːdɪˈspəʊz/
核心：（正式）两支，及物。一是使……预先倾向于（某种看法、做法）：predispose somebody to、towards；二是使易患（某种病）：predispose somebody to。
名词 predisposition：倾向、（患病的）易感体质，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① His childhood in the mountains predisposed him to like quiet places.
= 在山里度过的童年让他天生就喜欢安静的地方。
predispose somebody to do something = 使某人倾向于做某事
主语多是经历、出身。
② Smoking predisposes people to infections of the chest.
= 吸烟让人容易得胸部感染。
predispose somebody to something = 使某人易患某病
③ Some families have a genetic predisposition to heart disease.
= 有些家族有易患心脏病的遗传体质。
a predisposition to something = 易患某病的体质
a genetic predisposition = 遗传倾向

predominant
/prɪˈdɒmɪnənt/
核心：两支。一是最显著的、最突出的；二是占主导地位的、占优势的。副词 predominantly 是主要地、绝大多数；名词 predominance，不可数或单数。并入本条。
① Blue is the predominant colour in all the paintings that she made during her years by the sea.
= 她住在海边那些年画的画里，蓝色都是主色。
the predominant colour = 主色
比 main 正式。
② Farming is still the predominant way of life in the valley.
= 务农仍是那个山谷里最主要的生活方式。
the predominant way of life = 最主要的生活方式
③ The audience at the concert was predominantly female and under thirty, with a few puzzled fathers.

核心：（医学）术前用药：手术麻醉之前先给病人用的镇静药。不可数；口语缩成 premed。
① The nurse gave him his premedication an hour before the operation.
= 手术前一个钟头，护士给他用了术前药。
premedication = 术前用药
② The premedication made her drowsy before she reached the theatre.
= 还没到手术室，术前药就让她昏昏欲睡了。
the premedication = 术前药

premeditated
/ˌpriːˈmedɪteɪtɪd/
核心：（罪行、坏事）预谋的、蓄意的：事先打算好的，不是一时冲动。名词 premeditation，不可数。并入本条。
① The judge said that the attack was premeditated and showed no mercy.
= 法官说那次袭击是预谋的，量刑毫不留情。
be premeditated = 是预谋的
② He was found guilty of premeditated murder.
= 他被判蓄意谋杀罪成立。
premeditated murder = 蓄意谋杀
③ The lawyer argued that there had been no premeditation, only a moment of panic.
= 律师辩称并无预谋，只是一时惊慌。
premeditation = 预谋

premenstrual
/ˌpriːˈmenstruəl/
核心：经前的：月经来之前那几天的。只作定语。premenstrual syndrome 是经前综合征：经前的烦躁、乏力、身上不舒服，简称 PMS；英式也说 premenstrual tension。
① She suffers from premenstrual syndrome for a few days every month.
= 她每个月都有几天受经前综合征之苦。
premenstrual syndrome = 经前综合征
② The doctor suggested exercise as a way of easing premenstrual tension.
= 医生建议用锻炼来缓解经前的紧张不适。
premenstrual tension = 经前紧张

premier
/ˈpremiə(r)/
核心：名词：总理、首相：一些国家政府的首脑，新闻里也这样称英国首相；加拿大、澳大利亚的省长、州长。可数。
形容词，只作定语：首要的、最重要的、第一流的。名词 premiership 是总理的职位、任期；英格兰足球的顶级联赛叫 the Premier League。并入本条。
① The two premiers signed the trade agreement at a ceremony in the capital.
= 两国总理在首都的一个仪式上签了贸易协定。
a premier = 总理
② The town is the premier holiday resort on the south coast.
= 那座城是南岸首屈一指的度假胜地。
the premier resort = 首屈一指的度假地
③ The country grew rich during the ten years of his premiership.
= 在他当总理的十年里，那个国家富了起来。
his premiership = 他的总理任期
④ The club was promoted to the Premier League after forty years in the lower divisions.
= 那家俱乐部在低级别联赛待了四十年，终于升入英超。
the Premier League = 英格兰超级联赛

premiere
/ˈpremieə(r)/
核心：名词：（电影、戏剧的）首映、首演，可数。动词：首映、首演，及物或不及物。来自法语，也写作 première。
① The stars arrived for the premiere in a line of black cars.
= 明星们坐着一长串黑色轿车来出席首映式。
a premiere = 首映、首演
② The world premiere of the opera will take place in Vienna next spring.
= 那部歌剧的全球首演将于明年春天在维也纳举行。
the world premiere = 全球首演
③ The play premiered in a tiny theatre above a pub before it moved to the capital.
= 那出戏先在一家酒吧楼上的小剧场首演，后来才进了首都。
premiere = 首演

premise
/ˈpremɪs/
核心：名词：（正式）前提、假定：推理、论证所依据的那个说法，可数，英式也拼作 premiss。
动词：be premised on 是以……为前提。
复数的 premises 是另一回事：（一家店、一家公司占用的）房屋连同地皮、营业场所：on the premises 是在店内、在场所内。并入本条。
① The argument rests on the false premise that prices will go on rising.
= 那个论证建立在物价会一直上涨这个错误的前提上。
a false premise = 错误的前提
② The whole scheme is premised on the idea that people will pay more for quiet.
= 整个方案的前提是人们愿意为清静多掏钱。
be premised on something = 以某事为前提
③ The firm has moved to larger premises on the edge of the town.
= 那家公司搬到了城边更大的场所。
larger premises = 更大的场所
④ Alcohol may not be drunk on the premises.
= 店内不得饮酒。
on the premises = 在店内、在场所内
premises 永远是复数，动词也用复数。

premium
/ˈpriːmiəm/
核心：名词，可数：保险费：定期缴给保险公司的钱；额外的费用、加价、溢价：pay a premium for。
形容词，只作定语：高价的、优质的：premium prices、premium beer。
习语：at a premium 是奇缺、紧俏，也指以高于正常的价钱；put、place a premium on 是高度重视、看重。
① The premiums on my car insurance have gone up again this year.
= 我的汽车保险费今年又涨了。
an insurance premium = 保险费
② Buyers are willing to pay a premium for a house near a good school.
= 买房的人愿意为靠近好学校的房子多掏钱。
pay a premium for something = 为某物多掏钱
③ The shop charges premium prices for fruit flown in from abroad.
= 那家店对从国外空运来的水果收高价。
premium prices = 高价
④ Space is at a premium in the crowded old town.
= 在拥挤的老城里，空间十分紧俏。
at a premium = 奇缺、紧俏
⑤ The school puts a premium on good manners and neat handwriting.
= 那所学校很看重礼貌和工整的字迹。
put a premium on = 高度重视
put a premium on something = 看重某事

premodifier
/ˌpriːˈmɒdɪfaɪə(r)/
核心：（语法）前置修饰语：放在被修饰的名词前面的词、短语，如 a very old stone bridge 里的 very old stone。可数。
① In "the tall thin man", the adjectives "tall" and "thin" are premodifiers of the noun.
= 在「the tall thin man」里，形容词「tall」和「thin」是那个名词的前置修饰语。
a premodifier = 前置修饰语
② English allows a long string of premodifiers before a single noun.
= 英语允许一个名词前面排上长长一串前置修饰语。
a string of premodifiers = 一串前置修饰语
放在后面的叫 postmodifier。

premolar
/ˌpriːˈməʊlə(r)/

① The dentist had to take out one of her upper premolars to make room for the others.
= 牙医只好拔掉她一颗上前磨牙，给别的牙腾地方。
a premolar = 前磨牙
② The child's first premolars came through at the age of ten.
= 那孩子的第一前磨牙是十岁时长出来的。
the first premolars = 第一前磨牙

premonition
/ˌpriːməˈnɪʃn/
核心：（不祥的）预感：觉得要出事，多半是坏事，介词用 of、that。可数。形容词 premonitory 是预兆的、预警的，正式。并入本条。
① She had a premonition of danger as soon as she stepped on to the bridge.
= 她一踏上那座桥，就预感到有危险。
a premonition of danger = 有危险的预感
② He had a sudden premonition that he would never see the house again.
= 他忽然预感到自己再也见不到那座房子了。
a premonition that = ……的预感
③ The headaches were a premonitory sign of the illness that followed.
= 那些头疼是后来那场病的预兆。
a premonitory sign = 预兆

prenatal

prenatal classes = 产前课

preoccupation
/priˌɒkjuˈpeɪʃn/
核心：两支。一是心事、老惦记着的事：占满了一个人心思的那件事，可数；二是全神贯注、心事重重（的状态），不可数或单数：preoccupation with。
① His main preoccupation at the moment is finding a job before the baby is born.
= 他眼下最大的心事是赶在孩子出生前找到工作。
a main preoccupation = 最大的心事
② She spoke slowly, with an air of preoccupation.
= 她说得很慢，一副心事重重的样子。
an air of preoccupation = 心事重重的样子
③ The newspapers criticized the government's preoccupation with its own image.
= 报纸批评政府一门心思只顾自己的形象。
preoccupation with something = 一门心思顾着某事

preoccupy
/priˈɒkjupaɪ/
核心：使全神贯注、占满……的心思：让人老想着一件事，顾不上别的。及物；过去式 preoccupied。
分词 preoccupied 作形容词：心事重重的、心不在焉的：preoccupied with。并入本条。
① The question of money has preoccupied him ever since he lost his job.
= 自从丢了工作，钱的问题就一直占着他的心思。
preoccupy somebody = 占满某人的心思
② She was too preoccupied with her own troubles to notice that he was ill.
= 她光顾着自己的烦心事，没注意到他病了。
preoccupied with something = 一心想着某事
③ He gave me a preoccupied nod and went on reading the letter.
= 他心不在焉地冲我点了点头，接着看那封信。
a preoccupied nod = 心不在焉的点头

prep
/prep/
核心：几个截短的口语词。名词，不可数：（英式）（寄宿学校的）家庭作业、自习时间；（美式）准备工作。
动词：（美式）为……作准备：给（病人）做术前准备、预备（食材）；也指作准备。过去式 prepped。

prep = 准备工作

④ He was sent away to prep school at the age of eight.
= 他八岁就被送去上预备学校。
prep school = 预备学校

prepaid

preparation
/ˌprepəˈreɪʃn/
核心：三支。一是准备、预备（这件事、这个过程），不可数：in preparation for；二是 preparations：（为某事作的）各项准备工作，复数：make preparations；
三是（特制的）配制品、制剂：药、化妆品、食品，可数。
① The team trained in the mountains in preparation for the long race.
= 为准备那场长距离比赛，那支队伍在山里训练。
in preparation for something = 为准备某事
② Careful preparation is the secret of a good speech.
= 认真准备是讲好话的诀窍。
careful preparation = 认真准备
③ The preparations for the wedding took nearly a year.
= 婚礼的各项准备花了将近一年。
preparations for something = 为某事作的准备工作
④ The chemist sells a preparation for dry skin made from oats.
= 药店卖一种用燕麦做的治皮肤干燥的制剂。
a preparation = 制剂

preparatory
/prɪˈpærətri/
核心：（正式）预备的、准备性的：为后面的事作准备的。只作定语；preparatory to 是在……之前、为……作准备。
preparatory school 是预备学校：在英国是为七到十三岁孩子办的私立小学，在美国是为上大学作准备的私立中学。并入本条。
① The committee held a preparatory meeting to agree on the agenda.
= 委员会开了一次预备会议，商定议程。
a preparatory meeting = 预备会议
② He cleared his desk preparatory to leaving the firm.
= 他收拾好办公桌，准备离开公司。
preparatory to doing something = 在做某事之前、为做某事作准备
③ Both brothers went to the same preparatory school in the hills.
= 兄弟俩上的是山里的同一所预备学校。
a preparatory school = 预备学校

prepare
/prɪˈpeə(r)/
核心：动词：准备、预备：把东西弄好备用：prepare a room、a report；做（饭）、调制；使（自己、别人）作好准备：prepare for、prepare to do、prepare somebody for。
习语 prepare the ground (for) 是为……铺路、打基础。
分词 prepared 作形容词：有准备的：be prepared for；愿意的：be prepared to do；事先准备好的：a prepared statement。名词 preparedness 是有备（的状态），不可数。并入本条。
① The nurse prepared a room for the new patient.
= 护士给新来的病人收拾出一间屋子。
prepare a room = 收拾出一间屋子
② She spent the whole afternoon preparing a meal for twelve people.
= 她花了一下午给十二个人做饭。
prepare a meal = 做饭
③ The students are preparing for their final examinations in June.
= 学生们在为六月的期末考试作准备。
prepare for something = 为某事作准备
④ The army was preparing to cross the river at dawn.
= 军队准备在拂晓渡河。
prepare to do something = 准备做某事
⑤ Nothing had prepared her for the shock of seeing the empty house.
= 看到那座空房子时的震惊，她事先毫无准备。
prepare somebody for something = 使某人对某事有准备
⑥ The early talks prepared the ground for a treaty the following year.
= 早期的会谈为第二年的条约铺了路。
prepare the ground = 铺路、打基础
⑦ We were not prepared for such a cold night in the mountains.
= 山里的夜晚这么冷，我们没有准备。
be prepared for something = 对某事有准备
⑧ I am not prepared to pay that much for a second-hand car.
= 我不愿意为一辆二手车出那么多钱。
be prepared to do something = 愿意做某事
⑨ The minister read a prepared statement and refused to answer questions.
= 部长念了一份事先准备好的声明，不肯回答提问。
a prepared statement = 事先准备好的声明
⑩ The floods exposed the country's lack of preparedness for disaster.
= 那场洪水暴露出该国对灾害毫无准备。
preparedness = 有备

preposterous

④ The houses in that street are preposterously expensive.

① The last movement of the sonata is marked presto.
= 那首奏鸣曲的末乐章标着「急板」。
be marked presto = 标着「急板」
② You just add water, stir once, and hey presto, the soup is ready.
= 加上水，搅一下，变！汤就好了。
hey presto = 变！一下子就成了
来自意大利语「快」。

presumably
/prɪˈzjuːməbli/
核心：副词：想必、大概、据推测：说话人觉得多半是这样，却不敢肯定。
① Presumably the letter was lost in the post, since it never arrived.
= 那封信一直没到，想必是寄丢了。
presumably = 想必
② You have presumably read the report, so I shall not repeat it.
= 那份报告你想必读过了，我就不重复了。
have presumably done something = 想必已经做了某事
比 probably 多一层「我是这么推断的」。

presume
/prɪˈzjuːm/
核心：动词：推测、假定、以为：没有确证就认定：presume that、be presumed dead、innocent；（正式）擅自、冒昧地做：presume to do。
presume on、upon 是（正式）利用（别人的好意、交情）得寸进尺。
① I presume that you have already eaten, since it is past nine.
= 已经九点多了，我想你吃过了。
presume that = 推测
比 assume 更有根据；口语里 I presume 也用来求证。
② The three climbers are missing and presumed dead.
= 那三名登山者失踪，据推定已经遇难。
be presumed dead = 被推定死亡
③ In law a person is presumed innocent until proved guilty.
= 法律上，一个人被证明有罪之前推定为无罪。
be presumed innocent = 被推定无罪
法律用语。
④ I would not presume to tell you how to run your own business.
= 我可不敢冒昧地教你怎么经营自己的生意。
presume to do something = 冒昧地做某事
⑤ I do not wish to presume on your kindness by staying another week.
= 我不想仗着您好心再多住一个星期。
presume on something = 利用某事得寸进尺
presume upon something = 利用某事得寸进尺

presumption
/prɪˈzʌmpʃn/
核心：两支。一是推测、假定、推定：a presumption that、of，可数：the presumption of innocence 是无罪推定；二是（正式）冒昧、放肆、自以为是，不可数。
① The whole plan for the outdoor concert rests on the presumption that the weather will hold.
= 露天音乐会的整个计划，前提是天气会一直好下去。
a presumption that = ……的假定
a presumption of something = 对某事的推定
② The presumption of innocence is the basis of a fair trial.
= 无罪推定是公正审判的基础。
the presumption of innocence = 无罪推定
③ She was angered by his presumption in opening her letters and answering one of them himself.
= 他竟敢拆她的信，还自作主张回了一封，那份放肆让她很生气。
somebody's presumption = 某人的放肆

presumptive
/prɪˈzʌmptɪv/
核心：（正式，术语）推定的、假定的：根据现有的情况认为多半如此。多作定语；the heir presumptive 是假定继承人，放在名词后面。
① The doctors made a presumptive diagnosis of malaria before the tests came back.
= 化验结果出来之前，医生们初步推断是疟疾。
a presumptive diagnosis = 推定的诊断
② As the king had no children, his brother was the heir presumptive.
= 国王没有子女，他的弟弟是假定继承人。
the heir presumptive = 假定继承人

presumptuous
/prɪˈzʌmptʃuəs/
核心：冒昧的、放肆的、自以为是的：做了没资格做、没人请你做的事。不用在名词前居多。别跟 presumptive 混。
① It would be presumptuous of me to comment on a subject I have never studied.
= 一个我从没研究过的题目，让我来评论就太冒昧了。
presumptuous of somebody = 某人冒昧
说人越了本分。
② I hope it is not presumptuous to ask how much you paid for the house.
= 希望我问一句您买那座房子花了多少钱不算冒昧。
it is presumptuous to do something = 做某事冒昧

presuppose
/ˌpriːsəˈpəʊz/
核心：（正式）两支，及物。一是预先假定：还没证实就先认定是真的；二是以……为前提：有甲必先有乙。名词 presupposition，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The question presupposes that the witness was in the house that night.
= 那个问题预先假定证人那天夜里在那座房子里。
presuppose that = 预先假定
比 assume 正式。
② A good harvest presupposes rain at the right time.
= 好收成的前提是雨下得是时候。
presuppose something = 以某事为前提
③ The whole theory rests on a presupposition that has never been tested.
= 整个理论建立在一个从没检验过的预设上。
a presupposition = 预设、前提

pretence
/prɪˈtens/
核心：三支。一是假装、伪装、做样子：a pretence of、that，不可数或单数：make a pretence of、keep up the pretence；二是借口，可数：under false pretences 是以欺骗的手段；
三是（正式）自称、标榜：pretence to，不可数。美式拼作 pretense。
① She made no pretence of being pleased to see us.
= 她一点没装出见到我们很高兴的样子。
make no pretence of doing something = 毫不假装做某事
a pretence of something = 装出来的某种样子
② He kept up the pretence that he was still working for a year after he lost his job.
= 丢了工作以后，他装作还在上班，装了一年。
keep up the pretence = 把样子装下去
③ He was accused of obtaining money under false pretences.
= 他被控以欺骗手段取得钱财。
under false pretences = 以欺骗的手段
法律上常见，总用复数。
④ I make no pretence to any knowledge of music.
= 我不敢自称懂音乐。
make no pretence to something = 不自称有某物
⑤ In the American novel the detective makes a pretense of reading the paper while he listens.
= 在那部美国小说里，侦探一边假装看报，一边听着。
pretense = 假装（美式拼法）

pretend
/prɪˈtend/
核心：动词：假装、装作：pretend to do、pretend that；（孩子）装扮着玩、假扮：pretend to be；（多用于否定、疑问）自称、妄称：pretend to。
形容词，只作定语：（儿语）假装的、闹着玩的。
① She pretended to be asleep when her mother looked into the room.
= 母亲往屋里看的时候，她假装睡着了。
pretend to do something = 假装做某事
不说 pretend doing。
② He pretended that he had not heard the question.
= 他装作没听见那个问题。
pretend that = 装作
③ The children pretended to be pirates and made the dog walk the plank.
= 孩子们扮海盗，让狗去走跳板。
pretend to be somebody = 假扮某人
孩子玩耍时也说 Let's pretend.
④ I do not pretend to understand how the machine works.
= 我不敢说自己懂那台机器是怎么运转的。
not pretend to do something = 不敢自称做得到某事
⑤ The little girl poured pretend tea into the dolls' cups.
= 小女孩往洋娃娃的杯子里倒假装的茶。
pretend tea = 假装的茶

pretender
/prɪˈtendə(r)/
核心：觊觎者、自称有权继承（王位、头衔）的人：介词用 to。可数。
① The pretender to the throne landed on the coast with a few hundred men.
= 那个觊觎王位的人带着几百人在海岸登陆。
a pretender to the throne = 觊觎王位的人
不是「假装的人」：是「提出要求的人」。
② Two young pretenders are challenging the champion for his title this season.
= 这个赛季有两个年轻的挑战者要夺那位冠军的头衔。
a young pretender = 年轻的挑战者

pretension
/prɪˈtenʃn/
核心：两支。一是自命不凡、装腔作势，不可数，也用复数；二是自称、抱负：自以为有（某种才能、地位），可数，多用复数：pretensions to。
① The little café by the harbour serves good plain food without any pretension or fancy prices.
= 港口边的那家小咖啡馆做的是实实在在的家常菜，不装腔作势，价钱也不花哨。
without pretension = 不装腔作势
反义的形容词是 unpretentious。
② He is a simple farmer with no pretensions to learning, but he knows every bird in the valley.
= 他是个朴实的农民，从不以有学问自居，可山谷里的鸟他样样认得。
pretensions to something = 以某事自居
③ The family's social pretensions amused the neighbours, who remembered when they had kept a fish stall.
= 那家人自以为高人一等的做派让邻居们好笑：谁都记得他们摆鱼摊的时候。
social pretensions = 自以为高人一等的做派

pretentious
/prɪˈtenʃəs/
核心：（贬）自命不凡的、装腔作势的、卖弄的：想显得比实际更重要、更有学问、更有品位。反义是 unpretentious。
副词 pretentiously；名词 pretentiousness，不可数。并入本条。
① The menu is written in a pretentious mixture of English and French.
= 那份菜单用英语夹法语写成，装腔作势。
a pretentious mixture = 装腔作势的混搭
② I found the film pretentious and far too long.
= 我觉得那部片子故作高深，而且长得过分。
be pretentious = 故作高深
说人、说作品都行。
③ The house is pretentiously named "The Palace".
= 那座房子起了个装腔作势的名字，叫「宫殿」。
pretentiously = 装腔作势地
④ She laughed at the pretentiousness of the wine list.
= 她笑那份酒单装模作样。
pretentiousness = 装腔作势

preterm
/ˌpriːˈtɜːm/
核心：（医学）早产的、足月以前的。形容词、副词。
① The unit cares for preterm babies who weigh less than a kilogram.
= 那个科室照料体重不足一公斤的早产儿。
preterm babies = 早产儿
term 在这里是「足月」。
② Twins are often born preterm and need extra care for a few weeks.
= 双胞胎常常早产，头几个星期需要特别照料。
be born preterm = 早产

preternatural
/ˌpriːtəˈnætʃrəl/
核心：（正式）异乎寻常的、超出常情的：不像是自然会有的。只作定语。副词 preternaturally，并入本条。
① The child showed a preternatural calm as the doctor explained the operation.
= 医生讲解手术的时候，那孩子显出异乎寻常的镇定。
a preternatural calm = 异乎寻常的镇定
preter- 是「超出」。
② The dog has a preternatural ability to know when its master is coming home.
= 那条狗有一种异乎寻常的本事，知道主人什么时候回家。
a preternatural ability = 异乎寻常的本事
③ The street was preternaturally quiet for a Saturday night.
= 作为一个星期六晚上，那条街静得出奇。
preternaturally = 异乎寻常地

pretest
/ˈpriːtest/
核心：名词：预备性的测验、摸底测试：正式开始之前先测一次，可数。动词：预先测试。
① The students took a pretest on the first morning to show what they already knew.
= 学生们头一个上午做了摸底测试，看已经会些什么。
a pretest = 摸底测试
学完以后再测的那次叫 post-test。
② The questions were pretested on a small group before the survey began.
= 调查开始之前，那些问题先在一小群人里试过。
be pretested = 预先试过

pretext
/ˈpriːtekst/
核心：借口、托词：为掩盖真正的原因编出来的理由：a pretext for、on the pretext of、under the pretext that。可数。
① He left the party early on the pretext of having a train to catch.
= 他借口要赶火车，早早离开了聚会。
on the pretext of doing something = 以做某事为借口
比 excuse 更强调「编出来的」。
② The incident was used as a pretext for sending in the army.
= 那起事件被拿来当出兵的借口。
a pretext for something = 某事的借口
③ She came into the office under the pretext that she had left her umbrella.
= 她借口落了雨伞，进了办公室。
under the pretext that = 借口……

pretrial
/ˌpriːˈtraɪəl/
核心：（法律）审判前的、开庭前的。只作定语。
① The two men were held in pretrial detention for eleven months.
= 那两个人在审前羁押中被关了十一个月。
pretrial detention = 审前羁押
也写作 pre-trial。
② The judge will decide at a pretrial hearing which evidence may be used.
= 法官将在庭前听证会上决定哪些证据可以用。
a pretrial hearing = 庭前听证会

prettify
/ˈprɪtɪfaɪ/
核心：（多含贬义）把……弄得好看：尤指弄得俗气、失了本色，或粉饰不好看的真相。及物；第三人称单数 prettifies，过去式 prettified。
① The council has prettified the old market with plastic flowers and coloured lights.
= 镇议会用塑料花和彩灯把老集市打扮了一番。
prettify the market = 把集市打扮得好看
② The film prettifies the life of a miner and leaves out the dust and the danger.
= 那部片子美化了矿工的生活，粉尘和危险只字不提。
prettify the life of somebody = 美化某人的生活

pretty
/ˈprɪti/
核心：形容词：（女子、孩子）漂亮的、秀气的；（地方、东西）好看的、悦目的：小巧精致的那种好看，不是 beautiful 的那种美。
副词：（口语）相当、很：pretty good；pretty much、pretty well、pretty nearly 是几乎、差不多。
习语：not a pretty sight 是不好看、不堪入目；be sitting pretty 是处境优越、稳占上风；a pretty penny 是一大笔钱；not just a pretty face 是不光长得好看、还有真本事；
pretty as a picture 是美得像幅画。
派生：prettily 是漂亮地、优雅地；prettiness 是漂亮，不可数。并入本条。
① She has a pretty little daughter with dark curls.
= 她有个漂亮的小女儿，一头深色鬈发。
a pretty daughter = 漂亮的女儿
说成年男子一般不用 pretty。
② We stayed in a pretty village with a stream and a stone bridge.
= 我们住在一个有小溪、有石桥的漂亮村子里。
a pretty village = 漂亮的村子
③ The exam was pretty difficult, but I think I passed.
= 考试相当难，不过我想我过了。
pretty difficult = 相当难
口语里的程度副词，比 very 弱、比 fairly 强。
④ The two phones are pretty much the same inside.
= 那两款手机里面几乎一样。
pretty much = 几乎、差不多
⑤ The work is pretty well finished, apart from the painting.
= 活儿差不多干完了，就剩刷漆。
pretty well = 几乎、差不多
⑥ After a week without washing, the kitchen was not a pretty sight.
= 一个星期没洗刷，那间厨房不堪入目。
not a pretty sight = 不堪入目
⑦ With the mortgage paid off and two pensions, they are sitting pretty.
= 房贷还清了，又有两份养老金，他们的日子稳稳当当。
sitting pretty = 处境优越
多用进行时。
⑧ She fixed the engine herself to prove that she was not just a pretty face.
= 她自己把发动机修好了，证明她不是只有一张好看的脸。
not just a pretty face = 不光长得好看
⑨ The table was prettily laid with flowers and blue china.
= 桌子布置得很漂亮，摆着花和蓝瓷餐具。
prettily = 漂亮地
⑩ The prettiness of the cottage hid a damp and draughty interior.
= 那座小屋外表漂亮，里面却又潮又透风。
prettiness = 漂亮

pretzel
/ˈpretsl/
核心：椒盐脆饼、扭结饼：一种烤得又硬又脆的咸饼干，形状像打了个结，也有做成小棍的；德国和美国常见。可数。
① He ate a bag of salted pretzels with his beer.

prevail
/prɪˈveɪl/
核心：（正式）动词，不及物：盛行、流行、普遍存在：prevail in、among；占上风、获胜、压倒：prevail over、against。
prevail on、upon somebody to do something 是劝说、说动某人做某事。
形容词 prevailing 只作定语：盛行的、普遍的：the prevailing wind 是盛行风、常刮的那种风。并入本条。
① The belief that the earth was flat prevailed for many centuries.
= 地球是平的这种看法盛行了许多世纪。
prevail = 盛行
② A mood of quiet confidence prevails among the players before the final.
= 决赛之前，球员当中弥漫着一种沉着自信的情绪。
prevail among somebody = 在某类人当中普遍存在
③ In the end common sense prevailed over anger, and the two men shook hands.
= 最后理智压倒了怒气，两人握了手。
prevail over something = 压倒某物
④ We prevailed on her to stay for another week and finish the painting.
= 我们说动她再留一个星期，把画画完。
prevail on to do = 说动……去做
prevail upon to do = 说动……去做
⑤ The trees on the cliff all lean away from the prevailing wind.
= 悬崖上的树全都背着盛行风歪着长。
the prevailing wind = 盛行风
⑥ His views were out of step with the prevailing mood of the country.
= 他的看法跟全国普遍的情绪合不上拍。
the prevailing mood = 普遍的情绪

prevalent
/ˈprevələnt/
核心：流行的、普遍的、常见的：在某个地方、某个时候、某群人里到处都有，介词用 in、among。名词 prevalence，不可数。并入本条。
① The disease is more prevalent in the villages near the swamp than in the hills.
= 那种病在沼泽附近的村子里比在山上常见。
prevalent in a place = 在某地常见
② Smoking is still prevalent among men of that generation.
= 抽烟在那一代男人当中仍然很普遍。
prevalent among somebody = 在某类人当中普遍
③ The report measures the prevalence of asthma in children under ten.
= 那份报告统计十岁以下儿童哮喘的患病情况。
the prevalence of something = 某事的流行程度
重音在第一个音节，跟 prevail 不同。

prevaricate
/prɪˈværɪkeɪt/
核心：（正式）支吾搪塞、含糊其词：为了不说实话、不作决定而绕弯子。不及物。名词 prevarication，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The minister prevaricated when he was asked whether taxes would rise.
= 问到会不会加税，部长支吾搪塞。
prevaricate = 支吾搪塞
② Stop prevaricating and tell me whether you took the money or not.
= 别绕弯子了，告诉我钱你到底拿没拿。
stop prevaricating = 别绕弯子
③ After months of prevarication the council finally agreed to repair the bridge.
= 推诿了几个月，镇议会终于同意修桥。
prevarication = 推诿、搪塞

prevent
/prɪˈvent/
核心：阻止、防止、预防：不让一件事发生，不让某人做某事。及物：prevent something、prevent somebody (from) doing something。
形容词 preventable 是可以预防的、可以避免的。并入本条。
① The guard rail was put up to prevent accidents on the steep path.
= 装那道护栏是为了防止陡路上出事故。
prevent accidents = 防止事故
② Nothing would prevent him from speaking out against the plan.
= 什么也拦不住他站出来反对那个计划。
prevent somebody from doing something = 阻止某人做某事
③ The rain prevented us from finishing the roof before dark.
= 下雨，我们没能在天黑前把屋顶盖完。
prevent somebody from doing something = 使某人做不成某事
英式里 from 可以省：prevent him speaking。
④ Many of these deaths are preventable with clean water and simple medicines.
= 这些死亡当中有许多靠干净的水和简单的药品就能避免。
preventable = 可以预防的

prevention
/prɪˈvenʃn/
核心：预防、防止。不可数：the prevention of。习语 prevention is better than cure 是预防胜于治疗，美式说 an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure。
