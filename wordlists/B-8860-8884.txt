fishmonger
核心：（英式）鱼贩，也指鱼店：卖鱼的人或那家铺子。可数。
① The fishmonger closes at two and knows what everybody wants by name.
= 那家鱼店两点关门，谁要什么，老板都叫得出名字。
a fishmonger = 鱼贩
the fishmonger's = 鱼店
② Two fishmongers in that street became one and then became a café.
= 那条街上两家鱼店并成一家，后来变成了咖啡馆。

fishnet
核心：两支。一是渔网；二是网眼织物：fishnet tights 就是网袜。
① Fishnet tights and a winter coat is a combination that town understands.
= 网袜配冬大衣，那个镇上的人懂这套搭配。
fishnet tights = 网袜
② An old fishnet hangs on the wall and holds up the whole display.
= 一张旧渔网挂在墙上，整面陈列都靠它撑着。
a fishnet = 渔网

fishtail
核心：两支。一是（车）尾部甩摆：后轮打滑时车尾左右扫；
二是鱼尾状的：a fishtail skirt、a fishtail parka。名词与动词同形。
① The van fishtailed on the ice and stopped facing the way it had come.
= 那辆货车在冰面上甩了尾，停下时正对着来路。
fishtail = 车尾甩摆
② A fishtail skirt is easy to walk in and impossible to sit down in.
= 鱼尾裙走路方便，坐下就没法坐了。
a fishtail skirt = 鱼尾裙

fishwife
核心：两支。一是（旧）卖鱼的女人；二是（贬）泼辣骂街的女人：
说话像 fishwife 是嗓门大、话难听。复数 fishwives。
① The fishwife on that corner outsold the shop behind her for thirty years.
= 街角那位卖鱼的女人，三十年里卖得比她身后那家店还多。
a fishwife = 卖鱼的女人
② He said she screeched like a fishwife and lost the argument on the spot.
= 他说她嚷得像个泼妇，当场就把那场争论输掉了。
screech like a fishwife = 像泼妇一样尖声嚷嚷

fishy
核心：两支。一是有鱼味的：a fishy smell；二是（非正式）可疑的：
something fishy 指事情不对劲。
① A fishy smell in the corridor turned out to be the lift motor.
= 走廊里那股鱼腥味，原来是电梯电机发出来的。
a fishy smell = 鱼腥味
② There is something fishy about an invoice with no address on it.
= 一张不写地址的发票，怎么看都不对劲。
something fishy = 可疑的事

fissile
核心：两支。一是（物理）易裂变的：能发生核裂变，如铀 235；
二是（地质）易劈开成薄片的。
① Fissile material is counted, weighed and argued over at every inspection.
= 易裂变材料每次检查都要清点、称重，还要吵上一架。
fissile material = 易裂变材料
② The slate is fissile and splits into sheets a millimetre thick.
= 那种板岩容易劈开，能剥成一毫米厚的薄片。
fissile = 易劈开的

fission
核心：两支。一是（物理）核裂变：nuclear fission，跟聚变 fusion 相对；
二是（生物）分裂生殖。不可数。
① Nuclear fission splits a heavy atom and fusion joins two light ones.
= 核裂变把重原子劈开，聚变把两个轻原子并起来。
nuclear fission = 核裂变
② Bacteria multiply by fission and do it faster when it is warm.
= 细菌靠分裂繁殖，天一暖分得更快。
fission = 分裂生殖

fissure
核心：（正式）裂隙、裂缝：岩石或皮肤上的窄长裂口；引申指组织内部的裂痕。可数。
① A fissure in the rock takes the rope and holds a climber all afternoon.
= 岩石上一道裂隙夹住绳子，整个下午吊着一名攀登者。
a fissure = 裂隙
② The fissure inside the party widened over one paragraph of a manifesto.
= 党内的裂痕，因纲领里的一段话而扩大。
a fissure in something = 某物内部的裂痕

fissured
核心：布满裂纹的：说岩石、树皮，也说皮肤皲裂。
① The bark is deeply fissured and the tree is younger than it looks.
= 那树皮裂纹很深，树本身比看上去年轻。
deeply fissured = 深深皲裂的
② A fissured landscape swallows the rain and gives none of it back.
= 布满裂隙的地貌把雨水吞下去，一点也不还回来。
fissured = 布满裂纹的

fist
核心：拳头。可数。习语：make a good fist of something 是把某事办得像样，
hand over fist 是（赚钱）大把大把地。
① He made a fist and thought better of it before anybody noticed.
= 他握起拳头，没等人看见就改了主意。
make a fist = 握拳
② She made a good fist of a job she had never been trained for.
= 一份从没受过训练的活儿，她干得相当像样。
make a good fist of something = 把某事办得像样
③ That stall makes money hand over fist for six weeks a year.
= 那个摊子一年有六个星期赚得盆满钵满。
hand over fist = 大把大把地
④ A fist bump replaced the handshake and the fist pump replaced the cheer.
= 碰拳取代了握手，挥拳庆祝取代了欢呼。
a fist bump = 碰拳
a fist pump = 挥拳庆祝
fist-pumping = 挥拳庆祝的

fistful
核心：一把（的量）：攥在手里那么多，多说 a fistful of something。可数。
① He arrived with a fistful of receipts and no order to any of them.
= 他攥着一把收据来，一张都没排过序。
a fistful of something = 一把某物
② A fistful of coins bought a newspaper and a bus ride in 1979.
= 1979 年，一把硬币能买一份报纸加一趟公交。
a fistful of coins = 一把硬币

fisticuffs
核心：（旧或戏谑）拳斗、动手打架：作复数，多用在轻描淡写的说法里。
① The argument stopped short of fisticuffs and went on by letter instead.
= 那场争执没到动手的地步，改用写信继续。
come to fisticuffs = 动起手来
② Fisticuffs in the car park cost both of them their season tickets.
= 停车场里一场拳脚，让两人都丢了季票。
fisticuffs = 拳斗

fistula
核心：（医学）瘘管：两个器官或体腔之间长出来的、本不该有的通道。
可数，复数 fistulas 或 fistulae。
① One fistula healed on its own and the second one needed surgery.
= 一处瘘管自己长好了，第二处得动手术。
a fistula = 瘘管
② Fistula repair is routine surgery in one country and rare in another.
= 瘘管修补在一个国家是常规手术，在另一个国家很少见。
fistula repair = 瘘管修补

fit
核心：五支。一是（尺寸）合身：the coat fits；二是健康结实的：keep fit；
三是适合的：fit for purpose；四是名词，（病或情绪的）发作：a coughing fit；
五是习语：see fit 是认为合适，fit in 是融入。
过去式与过去分词英式用 fitted、美式用 fit。
① The coat fits everywhere except across the shoulders.
= 那件大衣哪儿都合身，就是肩那儿不行。
fit somebody = 合某人的身
② She keeps fit by cycling to a gym she never actually goes into.
= 她靠骑车去一家从来不进门的健身房来保持体能。
keep fit = 保持健康
③ The building is not fit for purpose and the sign says it opened in 2019.
= 那栋楼不堪使用，牌子上写着 2019 年启用。
fit for purpose = 合用的
④ A coughing fit ended the speech four minutes early.
= 一阵咳嗽，让那场讲话提前四分钟结束。
a coughing fit = 一阵咳嗽
⑤ Do as you see fit and tell me about it afterwards.
= 你看着办，事后跟我说一声。
see fit = 认为合适
⑥ He fits in everywhere and belongs nowhere in particular.
= 他到哪儿都合得来，哪儿都不算是他的地方。
fit in = 融入
⑦ They fitted out the van in a week and it fits into the garage by nine millimetres.
= 他们一个星期就把货车改装好，进车库只多出九毫米余地。
fit out = 装备、改装
fit into something = 塞进某处
⑧ He was fitted up for something he could not possibly have done.
= 他被人栽赃了一件他绝无可能做的事。
fit somebody up = 栽赃某人
fit up = 栽赃、诬陷

fitful
核心：断断续续的、时有时无的：多说 sleep、progress、rain。副词 fitfully。
① Fitful sleep in a hot room is worse than no sleep in a cold one.
= 在热屋子里睡得断断续续，比在冷屋子里干脆不睡还难受。
fitful sleep = 断断续续的睡眠
② Progress has been fitful and the deadline has not moved at all.
= 进展一直断断续续，截止日期却纹丝没动。
fitful progress = 时断时续的进展
副词 fitfully：断断续续地，多修饰 slept、burned、rained。
③ The lamp burned fitfully and went out whenever the door opened.
= 那盏灯忽明忽暗，一开门就灭。
burn fitfully = 忽明忽暗地燃烧
④ She slept fitfully and got up before the alarm went off anyway.
= 她睡得断断续续，闹钟没响就起来了。
sleep fitfully = 断断续续地睡

fitment
核心：（英式）固定装置、配件：装在墙上、车里或厨房里的那类东西。可数。
① The kitchen fitments came in nine boxes and one of them held the handles.
= 那套厨房配件装了九个箱子，把手全在其中一箱里。
a fitment = 固定装置
② Every fitment on that list costs extra and none of them is optional.
= 那张单子上每样配件都要另加钱，一样都不能不要。
kitchen fitments = 厨房固定配件

fitness
核心：两支。一是健康、体能：physical fitness；
二是（正式）适合、胜任：fitness for something。不可数。
① Physical fitness fades in three weeks and takes three months to build.
= 体能三个星期就掉，练回来要三个月。
physical fitness = 身体素质
② Her fitness for the post was never in doubt and the interview took an hour.
= 她能否胜任那个职位从没人怀疑，面试还是谈了一个钟头。
fitness for something = 对某事的胜任
③ The fitness centre sells a fitness tracker that counts stairs as running.
= 那家健身中心卖一种运动手环，把爬楼算成跑步。
a fitness centre = 健身中心
a fitness tracker = 运动手环

fitted
核心：三支。一是合身的、量身做的：a fitted jacket；
二是（家具）嵌入式的：a fitted kitchen；三是装有某物的：be fitted with something。
① A fitted jacket hides nothing and forgives even less.
= 修身外套什么都藏不住，更谈不上遮丑。
a fitted jacket = 修身外套
② The flat has a fitted kitchen and a bathroom you can just turn round in.
= 那套房有整体厨房，浴室勉强转得开身。
a fitted kitchen = 整体厨房
③ Every room is fitted with a smoke alarm and two of them actually work.
= 每个房间都装了烟雾报警器，真能用的有两个。
be fitted with something = 装有某物
④ The fitted carpet came with the flat and predates the lease by two decades.
= 那块满铺地毯是随房子来的，比租约早二十年。
a fitted carpet = 满铺地毯

fitter
核心：（英式）安装工、装配工；也指量体裁衣的师傅。可数。
① The fitter came twice and the boiler worked on the third visit.
= 安装工来了两趟，第三趟锅炉才好使。
a fitter = 安装工
② A fitter measured her for the dress and took forty minutes over one sleeve.
= 裁缝师傅给她量那条裙子，光一只袖子就量了四十分钟。
a fitter = 量体裁衣的师傅

fitting

三是形容词，恰如其分的：a fitting tribute。

five

fivefold

fiver

fives

fix
核心：五支。一是修理：fix a leak；二是固定、安上：fix something to something；
三是安排、定下：fix a date；四是（贬）做手脚、内定；
五是名词：in a fix 是陷入困境，a quick fix 是权宜之计。
① He fixed the leak with something that was never meant for pipes.

② Fix the shelf to the wall before you put anything heavy on it.
= 放重东西之前，先把架子固定到墙上。
fix something to something = 把某物固定在某处
③ They fixed a date and then moved it twice before the invitations went out.
= 他们定了日子，请柬发出去之前又改了两回。
fix a date = 定下日期
④ The match was fixed and the only surprise was who admitted it.
= 那场比赛是做了手脚的，唯一意外的是谁承认了。
be fixed = 被做了手脚
⑤ We are in a fix and a quick fix will only make it worse.
= 我们陷在困境里，权宜之计只会让事情更糟。
in a fix = 陷入困境
a quick fix = 权宜之计
⑥ They fixed on a date and fixed up the spare room in one afternoon.
= 他们定下日子，一个下午就把空房收拾停当。
fix on something = 定下某事
fix something up = 把某物收拾停当
fix up = 收拾停当、安排好
