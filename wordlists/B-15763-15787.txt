operative
/ˈɒpərətɪv/
核心：名词两支，可数。一是（正式）技工、操作工；二是特工、情报人员，也指替政党等暗中办事的人。
形容词一是正在运转的、有效的、已生效的，作表语；二是手术的，只作定语；the operative word 是关键的那个词。
① The factory employs sixty machine operatives on each shift.
= 那家工厂每班雇六十名机器操作工。
a machine operative = 机器操作工
② The novel is about an intelligence operative who is sent to Berlin.
= 那部小说写的是一名被派往柏林的情报人员。
an intelligence operative = 情报人员
③ The new rules will become operative at midnight.
= 新规定午夜起生效。
become operative = 生效
④ He was late again, and "again" is the operative word.
= 他又迟到了，「又」这个字是关键。
the operative word = 关键的那个词

operator
/ˈɒpəreɪtə(r)/
核心：四支，可数。一是操作（机器、设备）的人：a crane operator；二是电话接线员；
三是经营某类业务的公司或个人：a tour operator；四是（口语，常含贬义）精明圆滑、善于钻营的人：a smooth operator。
数学上指运算符。
① The crane operator sits in a cabin sixty metres above the ground.
= 起重机司机坐在离地六十米的驾驶室里。
a crane operator = 起重机司机
② Dial zero and ask the operator to put you through to the hospital.
= 拨零，请接线员给你接医院。
the operator = 接线员
③ The tour operator went bankrupt and left two thousand people abroad.
= 那家旅行社破产了，把两千人撂在了国外。
a tour operator = 旅行社
④ He is a smooth operator who has talked his way into three jobs.
= 他是个圆滑的角色，靠一张嘴混进了三份工作。
a smooth operator = 圆滑的角色

= 那支曲子出自一部如今没人演的维也纳轻歌剧。
a Viennese operetta = 维也纳轻歌剧

ophthalmology
/ˌɒfθælˈmɒlədʒi/
核心：眼科学：研究和治疗眼病的医学分支。不可数。拼写注意 ph 后面还有一个 th。
眼科医生叫 ophthalmologist；形容词 ophthalmic 是眼科的，英国的 ophthalmic optician 是有资格验光并检查眼病的验光师。并入本条。
① She trained in ophthalmology and now runs a clinic for children.

an ophthalmologist = 眼科医生
③ The hospital has opened a new ophthalmic ward with forty beds.
= 那家医院新开了一个有四十张床位的眼科病房。
ophthalmic = 眼科的
④ The ophthalmic optician sent him to a specialist after the sight test.
= 验完视力，验光师把他转给了专科医生。
an ophthalmic optician = 验光师

opiate
/ˈəʊpiət/

① The patient was given an opiate to control the pain after the operation.

an opiate = 麻痹人的东西

opine
/əʊˈpaɪn/
核心：（正式，常带讽刺）发表意见、认为：多接 that 从句或引语。及物动词。
① The critic opined that the film was an hour too long.
= 那位评论家认为，那部片子长了一个钟头。
opine that… = 认为…
② "The young have no manners," he opined from the depths of his armchair.

somebody opined = 某人发表高见

opinion
/əˈpɪnjən/
核心：三支。一是（个人的）看法、意见，可数：介词用 about、on、of；in my opinion 是依我看；
have a high、low opinion of 是对…评价高、评价低；be of the opinion that 是（正式）认为；
二是（一群人的）舆论、普遍看法，不可数：public opinion；a matter of opinion 是见仁见智的事；
三是专家的意见、诊断，可数：a second opinion 是另请一位专家的意见。
形容词 opinionated 是固执己见、好发议论的，含贬义；opinion poll 是民意调查。并入本条。
① What is your opinion of the new timetable?
= 你对新时刻表怎么看？
opinion = 意见
your opinion of something = 你对某事的看法
② In my opinion, the house is worth far less than they are asking.
= 依我看，那座房子远不值他们要的价。
in my opinion = 依我看
③ She has a low opinion of politicians and a high opinion of dogs.
= 她对政客评价很低，对狗评价很高。
have a low opinion of somebody = 对某人评价低
④ The committee is of the opinion that the plan should be dropped.
= 委员会认为那项计划应当放弃。
be of the opinion = 认为
be of the opinion that… = 认为…
⑤ Public opinion has turned against the war since the spring.
= 自春天以来，舆论已转而反对那场战争。
public opinion = 舆论
⑥ Whether the film is better than the book is a matter of opinion.
= 那部片子是不是比原著好，见仁见智。
a matter of opinion = 见仁见智的事
⑦ Opinion is divided on whether the bridge should be rebuilt.
= 那座桥该不该重建，意见不一。
opinion is divided = 意见不一
⑧ Before agreeing to surgery, she asked for a second opinion.
= 同意手术之前，她要求再请一位医生看看。
a second opinion = 另一位专家的意见
⑨ Her uncle is loud, opinionated and wrong about almost everything.
= 她叔叔嗓门大，固执己见，几乎什么事都说错。
opinionated = 固执己见的
⑩ The latest opinion poll puts the two parties level.
= 最新的民意调查显示两党持平。
an opinion poll = 民意调查

opium

opossum

opponent
/əˈpəʊnənt/

① She beat her opponent in straight sets in under an hour and went directly to the airport.
= 她不到一个钟头就直落两盘击败了对手，随即直奔机场。
your opponent = 对手

opportune
/ˈɒpətjuːn/
核心：（正式）适时的、恰好的：多说时机、时刻：an opportune moment。形容词，反义 inopportune。副词 opportunely 并入本条。
① She waited for an opportune moment to raise the question of pay.

an opportune moment = 合适的时机

opportunism
/ˌɒpəˈtjuːnɪzəm/
核心：机会主义：不顾原则、只看眼前有利可图就抓住机会的做法。不可数，含贬义。
这样做的人叫 opportunist，也作形容词；opportunistic 是机会主义的，也指（犯罪、感染）趁机而发的。并入本条。
① The opposition accused the minister of political opportunism.
= 反对党指责那位大臣搞政治机会主义。
political opportunism = 政治机会主义
② He is an opportunist who changes sides whenever the wind shifts.
= 他是个机会主义者，风向一变就换边站。
an opportunist = 机会主义者
③ The police described the burglary as opportunistic, since a window had been left open.
= 警方说那起入室盗窃是临时起意，因为有扇窗户没关。
opportunistic = 趁机而为的

opportunity
/ˌɒpəˈtjuːnɪti/
核心：机会、时机：适合做某事的有利情形。可数也可不可数，复数 opportunities；后面接 to do、for doing 或 of doing。
跟 chance 的分别：两个词常可通用，这个词更正式；chance 还有「可能性」的意思，这个词没有。
常搭 have、get an opportunity；miss an opportunity；take the opportunity 是趁机；at the first opportunity 是一有机会；
equal opportunities 是（就业等方面的）机会均等。
经济学上 opportunity cost 是机会成本，并入本条。
① The course gives students the opportunity to work abroad for a year.
= 那门课程给学生到国外工作一年的机会。
opportunity = 机会
the opportunity to do something = 做某事的机会
② She took the opportunity to thank everyone who had helped.
= 她趁机感谢了每个帮过忙的人。
take the opportunity = 趁机
③ I shall ring him at the first opportunity.
= 我一有机会就给他打电话。
at the first opportunity = 一有机会
④ He missed the opportunity to buy the house when it was cheap.
= 那座房子便宜的时候，他错过了买下它的机会。
miss an opportunity = 错过机会
⑤ There are few opportunities for young people in the village.
= 村里留给年轻人的机会不多。
opportunities for somebody = 给某人的机会
⑥ The firm has a strict policy on equal opportunities.
= 那家公司在机会均等方面有严格的规定。
equal opportunities = 机会均等
⑦ It was a golden opportunity, and he let it slip.
= 那是个千载难逢的机会，他却让它溜走了。
a golden opportunity = 千载难逢的机会
⑧ The opportunity cost of a year at college is a year's wages.
= 上一年大学的机会成本，是一年的工资。
opportunity cost = 机会成本

oppose
/əˈpəʊz/
核心：反对、反抗、与…对抗：不赞成并设法阻止；也指在比赛、选举中与某人对垒。及物动词，后面直接接宾语或动名词，不加介词。
形容词 opposed：be opposed to 是反对；as opposed to 是与…相对、而不是。opposing 作定语指对立的、对方的。
opposable 只见于 an opposable thumb：能与其余手指对握的拇指。并入本条。

oppose doing something = 反对做某事
③ He will be opposed in the election by a candidate half his age.
= 在选举中跟他对垒的，将是一位年纪只有他一半的候选人。
be opposed by somebody = 与某人对垒
④ I am strongly opposed to any increase in the fees.
= 我坚决反对学费有任何上涨。
be opposed to = 反对
⑤ The course teaches spoken, as opposed to written, Arabic.
= 那门课教的是口头阿拉伯语，而不是书面的。
as opposed to = 与…相对、而不是
⑥ The two brothers found themselves on opposing sides in the debate.
= 兄弟俩发现自己在辩论中站到了对立的两边。
opposing sides = 对立的两方
⑦ Humans and apes have an opposable thumb that lets them grip a branch.
= 人和猿都有能与其余手指对握的拇指，所以抓得住树枝。
an opposable thumb = 可对握的拇指

opposite
/ˈɒpəzɪt/
核心：形容词两支。一是对面的、另一边的：on the opposite side of the road；二是完全相反的、截然不同的：in the opposite direction。
介词与副词：在…对面：the bank opposite the station、live opposite；美式常说 across from。
名词：对立面、反义词、相反的人或事：the opposite of；just the opposite 是恰恰相反。
the opposite sex 是异性；opposite number 是（另一机构、另一国里）职位相当的人、对等人物。
① There is a chemist on the opposite side of the street.
= 街对面有一家药店。
opposite = 对面的
the opposite side = 对面
② The two cars drove off in opposite directions.
= 两辆车朝相反的方向开走了。
in opposite directions = 朝相反的方向
③ The medicine had the opposite effect and kept him awake all night.
= 那药起了反作用，害得他一夜没睡着。
the opposite effect = 相反的效果
④ The post office is opposite the church, next to the baker's.
= 邮局在教堂对面，挨着面包店。
opposite = 正对面
opposite the church = 在教堂对面
⑤ An old couple lives opposite and waves to the children every morning.
= 对面住着一对老夫妇，每天早上跟孩子们招手。
live opposite = 住在对面
⑥ "Generous" is the opposite of "mean".
= 「慷慨」是「吝啬」的反义词。
the opposite of something = 某事物的反面
⑦ I thought that she would be angry, but she was just the opposite.
= 我以为她会生气，结果恰恰相反。
just the opposite = 恰恰相反
⑧ At fourteen he was too shy to speak to the opposite sex.
= 十四岁的时候，他腼腆得不敢跟异性说话。
the opposite sex = 异性
⑨ The minister flew to Paris to meet her opposite number in the French government.
= 那位大臣飞往巴黎，会见法国政府里跟她对等的官员。
opposite number = 职位对等的人

opposition
/ˌɒpəˈzɪʃən/
核心：三支。一是反对、抵抗：介词用 to，不可数：strong opposition、meet opposition；in opposition to 是反对、与…相对；
二是 the opposition，对手、对方：比赛里的另一队、生意上的对头；

③ She voted in opposition to her own party for the first time.

in opposition to something = 反对某事
④ The team studied films of the opposition for a week before the final.
= 决赛前，那支球队研究了一个星期对手的录像。

⑤ The leader of the Opposition replied to the prime minister's speech.

⑥ The party spent eighteen years in opposition before it won again.

⑦ The teacher described the boy as bright but oppositional.

oppress
/əˈpres/
核心：两支，多用被动。一是压迫：掌权者不公正、残酷地对待一群人；二是使人心里沉重、压抑：热、忧虑、环境。
名词 oppression；压迫者是 oppressor；形容词 oppressed 是受压迫的，the oppressed 指受压迫的人；
oppressive 一是压迫人的、暴虐的，二是（天气）闷热难耐的、（气氛）压抑的；副词 oppressively，名词 oppressiveness。并入本条。
① The regime oppressed the minority in the north for forty years.
= 那个政权压迫北方的少数族群长达四十年。
oppress somebody = 压迫某人
② She was oppressed by the silence of the empty house.
= 空房子里的寂静让她心头沉重。
be oppressed by something = 被某事压得心头沉重
③ They fled the country to escape political oppression.
= 他们为逃避政治压迫而离开了那个国家。
oppression = 压迫
④ The poem asks the oppressor to look his victims in the face.
= 那首诗要压迫者正视受害者的脸。
an oppressor = 压迫者
⑤ The charity speaks for the poor and the oppressed.
= 那家慈善机构替穷人和受压迫的人说话。
the oppressed = 受压迫的人
⑥ The country lived under oppressive laws that banned public meetings.
= 该国生活在禁止公众集会的暴虐法律之下。
oppressive laws = 暴虐的法律
⑦ The heat in the valley was oppressive by eleven in the morning.
= 到上午十一点，山谷里已经热得让人喘不过气。
oppressive = 闷热难耐的
⑧ It was oppressively hot in the courtroom, and two jurors fell asleep.
= 法庭里闷热难当，有两名陪审员睡着了。
oppressively hot = 闷热难当
⑨ The oppressiveness of the long silence made her want to laugh.
= 那阵长久的沉默压抑得她想笑。
oppressiveness = 压抑

opprobrium
/əˈprəʊbriəm/
核心：（正式）公众的谴责、骂名：因做了丑事而遭到的严厉批评与鄙视。不可数。
形容词 opprobrious 是辱骂的、带侮辱的，并入本条。
① The bank earned public opprobrium by paying bonuses in a year of losses.
= 那家银行在亏损的年份发奖金，招来了公众的谴责。
public opprobrium = 公众的谴责
② He was sacked for using opprobrious language to a customer.
= 他因为对一位顾客恶语相向被解雇了。
opprobrious language = 辱骂的话

opt
/ɒpt/
核心：选择、决定要：从几种可能里挑一种：opt for 加名词，opt to do something。不及物动词。
短语动词 opt out 是选择不参加、退出，介词用 of；opt in 是选择加入。
名词 opt-out 指退出的权利或做法，opting out 指退出这种行为。
① Most of the students opted for the shorter course.
= 多数学生选了短一些的那门课。
opt for something = 选择某物
② She opted to stay at home and look after her father.
= 她选择留在家里照顾父亲。
opt to do something = 选择做某事
③ Workers may opt out of the pension scheme by signing a form.
= 员工签一张表就可以退出养老金计划。
opt out = 选择退出
opt out of something = 退出某事
④ Customers have to opt in before the firm may send them advertising.
= 顾客得先自己选择加入，公司才可以给他们发广告。
opt in = 选择加入
⑤ The country negotiated an opt-out from the rules on working hours.
= 该国通过谈判，争取到了不受工时规定约束的豁免。
an opt-out = 退出的权利
⑥ Opting out is easy on paper and takes three phone calls in practice.

optic
/ˈɒptɪk/
核心：两支。一是形容词，眼睛的、视觉的，只作定语，技术用语：the optic nerve 是视神经；
二是名词，（英式）装在倒置酒瓶上的定量出酒器，酒吧里用，原是商标名，可数。
① The tumour was pressing on the optic nerve and blurring her sight.
= 那个肿瘤压迫着视神经，让她视力模糊。
the optic nerve = 视神经
② The barman pushed the glass up against the optic and measured out a whisky.
= 酒保把杯子往定量器上一顶，量出一份威士忌。
an optic = 定量出酒器

optical
/ˈɒptɪkəl/
核心：三支，多作定语。一是视觉的、视力的：an optical illusion 是视错觉；二是光学的、帮助看的：optical instruments；
三是（技术上）用光来读取、传输信息的：optical fibre 是光纤，optical character recognition 是光学字符识别，缩写 OCR。
副词 optically 并入本条。
① The lines look bent, but that is only an optical illusion.
= 那些线看上去是弯的，其实只是视错觉。
an optical illusion = 视错觉
② The firm makes microscopes and other optical instruments.
= 那家公司生产显微镜和别的光学仪器。
optical instruments = 光学仪器
③ The village was connected by optical fibre last year, and house prices rose.
= 那座村子去年通了光纤，房价涨了。
optical fibre = 光纤
④ The library scans old newspapers and turns them into text by optical character recognition.
= 图书馆把旧报纸扫描下来，用光学字符识别转成文字。
optical character recognition = 光学字符识别
⑤ The glass must be optically perfect, without a single bubble.
= 那块玻璃在光学上必须完美，一个气泡都不能有。
optically = 在光学上

optician
/ɒpˈtɪʃən/

① The optician tested each eye in turn and said that she needed glasses for driving at night.
= 验光师把两只眼睛轮流查了一遍，说她夜里开车需要戴眼镜。
the optician = 验光师
② I have an appointment at the optician's on Thursday morning to collect the new reading glasses.
= 我星期四上午约了去眼镜店，取那副新配的老花镜。
at the optician's = 在眼镜店

optics
/ˈɒptɪks/
核心：两支。一是光学：研究光与视觉的物理分支，不可数，动词用单数；二是（主要是美式，新闻用语）一件事给公众的观感。
① Newton's work on optics showed that white light is a mixture of colours.
= 牛顿的光学研究表明，白光是多种颜色的混合。
optics = 光学
② The optics of flying to a climate meeting in a private jet were terrible.
= 坐私人飞机去开气候会议，给人的观感糟透了。
the optics of something = 某事给公众的观感

optimal
/ˈɒptɪməl/

① The plants are kept at the optimal temperature for growth.
= 那些植物保持在最适合生长的温度。

② The optimal time to plant the bulbs is early October.

the optimal time = 最佳时间
③ The engine performs optimally at about two thousand revolutions a minute.
= 那台发动机在每分钟两千转左右表现最佳。
optimally = 最佳地

optimism
/ˈɒptɪmɪzəm/
核心：乐观、乐观主义：相信事情会往好处发展的心态，介词用 about。不可数，反义 pessimism。
cautious optimism 是谨慎乐观。乐观的人叫 optimist；形容词 optimistic，介词用 about，副词 optimistically。并入本条。
① There is cautious optimism that the talks will succeed.
= 人们对谈判成功持谨慎乐观的态度。
cautious optimism = 谨慎乐观
② She faced the operation with her usual optimism and packed two novels for the weeks of recovery.
= 她带着一贯的乐观面对那次手术，还为术后休养的几个星期带了两本小说。
optimism = 乐观
③ He is an optimist who buys a lottery ticket every Friday and plans how to spend the prize.
= 他是个乐天派，每个星期五买一张彩票，还盘算着奖金怎么花。
an optimist = 乐天派
④ I am optimistic about our chances of finishing before the winter.
= 对我们入冬前完工的可能性，我是乐观的。

⑤ The report forecasts, rather optimistically, that sales will double.
= 那份报告颇为乐观地预测销量会翻一番。
optimistically = 乐观地
