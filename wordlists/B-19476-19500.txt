rostrum
/ˈrɒstrəm/
核心：讲台、指挥台、领奖台：比地面高出一截、供人站上去讲话、指挥或领奖的台子。可数，复数 rostrums 或 rostra。
① The conductor stepped onto the rostrum and raised his baton, and the whole hall fell silent.
= 指挥走上指挥台，举起指挥棒，整个大厅安静下来。
the conductor's rostrum = 指挥台
② The minister walked slowly to the rostrum to deliver her speech to the party conference.
= 那位部长慢慢走上讲台，向党代会发表讲话。
walk to the rostrum = 走上讲台
③ The young swimmer stood on the top step of the rostrum while her national anthem played.
= 国歌奏响时，那位年轻的游泳选手站在领奖台的最高一级上。
on the rostrum = 在领奖台上
领奖台义英式常用 rostrum，北美多说 podium。

rosy
/ˈrəʊzi/
核心：粉红的、红润的（脸色、天色）；（比喻）乐观的、美好的：a rosy future、paint a rosy picture。形容词，比较级 rosier、最高级 rosiest。
① The children came in from the snow with rosy cheeks and wet gloves, asking for hot chocolate.
= 孩子们从雪地里进来，脸蛋红扑扑的，手套湿透了，嚷着要喝热巧克力。
rosy cheeks = 红润的脸颊
② The bank painted a rosy picture of the economy, but many families were still struggling to pay their bills.
= 银行把经济描绘得一片大好，可许多家庭还在为付账单发愁。
paint a rosy picture = 描绘美好前景
③ Their future looked rosy after both of them found good jobs in the same city.
= 两人都在同一座城市找到好工作之后，他们的前景看起来一片光明。
look rosy = 前景看好
比喻义常用于 future、picture、outlook，否定说 not look so rosy。

rot
/rɒt/
核心：动词：（有机物）腐烂、烂掉：rot away；使腐烂；（比喻）被弃置、衰败：be left to rot。名词，不可数：腐烂、腐朽；the rot 指每况愈下的势头：the rot sets in（情况开始变糟）、stop the rot（扭转颓势）；（英式旧式口语）胡说八道：talk rot。
形容词 rotten 是腐烂的（另立条）。
① The fallen apples were left on the grass all winter and slowly began to rot in the rain.
= 掉下来的苹果整个冬天都留在草地上，在雨里慢慢烂掉了。
rot = 腐烂
东西自己烂掉用不及物；糖蛀坏牙这类「使腐烂」用及物。
② If you drink sugary drinks all day, they will rot your teeth long before you are old.
= 要是整天喝含糖饮料，不等你老，牙就会被蛀坏。
rot your teeth = 蛀坏牙齿
③ The wooden posts of the old fence had rotted away, and the whole thing fell down in the storm.
= 旧篱笆的木桩已经烂透了，暴风雨一来整道篱笆都倒了。
rot away = 烂透
④ We found that the timber under the bathroom floor was soft with rot and had to be replaced.
= 我们发现浴室地板下的木料已经朽软，只好换掉。
rot = 腐朽
⑤ The team won their first five games, but the rot set in after their captain broke his leg.
= 那支球队前五场都赢了，可队长摔断腿以后就开始走下坡路。
the rot sets in = 情况开始变糟
这一支总带定冠词 the rot，指一旦开了头就难以停下的颓势。
⑥ The new manager was brought in to stop the rot after the shop had lost money for three straight years.
= 那家店连续三年亏损之后，请来了新经理扭转颓势。
stop the rot = 扭转颓势
⑦ Some prisoners were left to rot in jail for years without ever seeing a lawyer or a judge.
= 有些囚犯被关在牢里任其烂掉，好多年都没见过律师或法官。
be left to rot = 被弃置不管
⑧ My grandfather thought most modern art was nonsense and said the critics were talking utter rot.
= 我祖父认为大多数现代艺术都是胡闹，说那些评论家是在胡说八道。
talk rot = 胡说八道
这一义已显老派，今天多说 rubbish 或 nonsense。
⑨ Nobody wanted to eat the rotten tomatoes that had been forgotten at the back of the fridge.
= 没人想吃那些被忘在冰箱最里头、已经烂掉的西红柿。
rotten = 腐烂的

rota
/ˈrəʊtə/
核心：（英式）值班表、轮值表：规定谁在什么时候做什么的名单。可数。常说 on a rota、a cleaning rota、draw up a rota。北美多用 roster 表达同一意思。
① The four students who share the flat have a cleaning rota pinned to the kitchen door.
= 合租那套公寓的四个学生在厨房门上钉了一张打扫轮值表。
a cleaning rota = 打扫轮值表
② The nurses work on a rota, so each of them has one weekend off in every three.
= 护士们轮班工作，所以每人每三个周末能休一个。
work on a rota = 轮班工作
③ Before the summer holidays, the head teacher drew up a rota of parents to water the school garden.
= 暑假前，校长排了一张家长轮值表，安排大家来给学校菜园浇水。
draw up a rota = 排轮值表

rotary
/ˈrəʊtəri/
核心：形容词：旋转的、转动的；靠旋转工作的：a rotary engine、a rotary lawnmower。名词，可数（北美）：环形交叉路口、环岛。
① Old telephones had a rotary dial, and you had to turn it with your finger for every number.
= 老式电话机有个转盘拨号盘，每拨一个号码都要用手指转一下。
a rotary dial = 转盘拨号盘
② The machine changes the up-and-down movement of the pistons into rotary motion that turns the wheels.
= 那台机器把活塞的上下运动转换成带动车轮的旋转运动。
rotary motion = 旋转运动
③ Go straight through the first rotary and take the second exit at the next one to reach the beach.
= 直穿第一个环岛，到下一个环岛走第二个出口，就能到海滩。
a rotary = （北美）环岛
这一义多见于美国东北部，北美别处多说 traffic circle。

rotate
/rəʊˈteɪt/
核心：动词：（绕轴）旋转、转动：rotate around / on an axis；使旋转；轮换、轮流担任：rotate jobs、rotate crops；（照片、图形）旋转。名词 rotation。
① The earth rotates once every twenty-four hours, which is why we have day and night.
= 地球每二十四小时自转一周，所以才有昼夜。
rotate = 旋转
② Rotate the handle slowly to the left until you hear a click and the door opens.
= 把把手慢慢向左转，直到听见咔嗒一声，门就开了。
rotate the handle = 转动把手
③ The workers on the line rotate jobs every two hours so that nobody gets too bored or tired.
= 流水线上的工人每两个小时轮换一次岗位，免得谁太闷太累。
rotate jobs = 轮换岗位
轮换义的主语是人，宾语是岗位、职务、值班。
④ Farmers rotate their crops each year to keep the soil healthy and stop diseases from spreading.
= 农民每年轮种作物，以保持土壤肥力、防止病害蔓延。
rotate crops = 轮种作物
⑤ The rotation of the wheel was so fast that you could not see the spokes any more.
= 轮子转得太快，连辐条都看不见了。
rotation = 旋转、轮换
⑥ Tap the arrow to rotate the photo so that the picture is the right way up.
= 点一下箭头把照片旋转过来，让画面正过来。
rotate a photo = 旋转照片
电脑、手机界面里的「旋转」也用这个词。

rotating
/rəʊˈteɪtɪŋ/
核心：形容词：旋转的、转动的：rotating blades；轮换的、轮流担任的：rotating shifts、a rotating presidency。
① Keep your fingers well away from the rotating blades of the food mixer while it is switched on.
= 食物搅拌机开着的时候，手指要远离旋转的刀片。
rotating blades = 旋转的刀片
② The factory runs twenty-four hours a day, and the staff work rotating shifts of eight hours.
= 那家工厂全天二十四小时运转，员工按八小时一班轮换上班。
rotating shifts = 轮班
指早、中、晚班轮流上；固定班次说 fixed shifts。
③ The countries in the group share a rotating presidency, so a different leader is in charge every six months.
= 该组织的成员国轮流担任主席，每六个月换一个领导人主事。
a rotating presidency = 轮值主席

rotation
/rəʊˈteɪʃn/
核心：名词：旋转、转动；（绕轴的）一周：one full rotation；轮换、轮流：in rotation、crop rotation；（体育）球员轮换。可数或不可数。动词是 rotate。
① The rotation of the earth on its axis gives us day and night.
= 地球绕轴自转，给我们带来了昼夜。
the rotation of the earth = 地球自转
② The wheel makes one full rotation every second, and a small light flashes each time.
= 那个轮子每秒转一整圈，每转一圈小灯就闪一下。
one full rotation = 转一整圈
③ The three teachers take the morning assembly in rotation, so each of them does it twice a week.
= 三位老师轮流主持晨会，所以每人每周主持两次。
in rotation = 轮流
④ Crop rotation helps farmers keep their fields healthy without using too many chemicals.
= 轮作能帮农民保持田地肥力，而不必用太多化学药剂。
crop rotation = 轮作
名词词组说 crop rotation，动词说法是 rotate crops。
⑤ The coach kept the two young goalkeepers in rotation so that both of them got enough games.
= 教练让两名年轻门将轮换上场，好让两人都有足够的比赛机会。
keep players in rotation = 让球员轮换上场

rotational
/rəʊˈteɪʃənl/
核心：形容词：旋转的；轮换的。多见于科技、农业：rotational speed、rotational grazing。
① The engineers measured the rotational speed of the turbine every hour during the test.
= 测试期间，工程师们每小时测一次涡轮的转速。
rotational speed = 转速
② The farm uses rotational grazing, moving the cows to a fresh field every few days.
= 那座农场实行轮牧，每隔几天就把牛赶到一块新草场。
rotational grazing = 轮牧

rote
/rəʊt/
核心：名词，不可数：死记硬背、机械重复。几乎只用于 by rote 和定语 rote learning；带贬义，暗示记住了却不理解。
① At my old school we learned poems by rote and recited them in front of the whole class every Friday.
= 在我原来那所学校，我们靠死记硬背学诗，每周五在全班面前背诵。
learn something by rote = 死记硬背
by rote 前面常是 learn、memorize、recite 这类动词。
② Many teachers now believe that rote learning of dates is less useful than understanding why events happened.
= 现在许多老师认为，死背年代不如理解事情为什么发生有用。
rote learning = 死记硬背式的学习

roti
/ˈrəʊti/
核心：（南亚、加勒比）烤饼：不发酵的面饼，在平锅上烙熟；在加勒比也指裹着咖喱馅的卷饼。可数或不可数。
① My mother made a pile of warm roti and we ate them with spicy vegetable curry.
= 我妈妈烙了一大摞热烤饼，我们配着辣蔬菜咖喱吃。
roti = 烤饼
② In Trinidad we bought a chicken roti from a street stall and ate it on the beach.
= 在特立尼达，我们从路边摊买了一份鸡肉咖喱卷饼，拿到海滩上吃。
a chicken roti = 鸡肉咖喱卷饼

rotisserie
/rəʊˈtɪsəri/
核心：烤肉叉、旋转烤架：让肉串在火前慢慢转动烤熟的装置；也指专卖这种烤肉的店。可数。作定语：rotisserie chicken。
① The supermarket sells hot rotisserie chicken, which is perfect when you are too tired to cook.

a rotisserie = 旋转烤架

rotor
/ˈrəʊtə(r)/
核心：转子、旋翼：机器里旋转的部件，尤指直升机顶上的旋翼。可数。常说 rotor blades。
① The helicopter's rotor blades began to turn, blowing dust and leaves across the field.
= 直升机的旋翼开始转动，把尘土和树叶吹得满场飞。

② The mechanic said the rotor inside the electric motor was worn out and needed to be replaced.

the rotor of a motor = 电动机转子

rotten
/ˈrɒtn/
核心：形容词：腐烂的、腐朽的；（口语）糟糕的、差劲的：rotten weather；（口语）不舒服的、内疚的：feel rotten；卑鄙的、不厚道的：a rotten thing to do；腐败的：rotten to the core。副词用法（口语）：spoil somebody rotten 是把某人宠坏。
① The floorboards in the old house were so rotten that my foot went straight through one of them.
= 那座老房子的地板烂得厉害，我一脚就踩穿了一块。
rotten = 腐朽的
② We had rotten weather for the whole holiday and spent most days playing cards inside.
= 整个假期天气都糟透了，我们大部分时间都在屋里打牌。
rotten weather = 糟糕的天气
口语里 rotten 常只是加重语气的「糟透了」，和腐烂无关。
③ I felt rotten all weekend with a cold and could not even get out of bed.
= 我整个周末都感冒，难受得连床都下不了。
feel rotten = 感觉很不舒服
④ I felt rotten about forgetting her birthday, so I sent her flowers the next morning.
= 我忘了她的生日，心里很过意不去，第二天一早就给她送了花。
feel rotten about something = 为某事内疚
⑤ It was a rotten thing to do, reading her diary and telling all her friends what was in it.
= 偷看她的日记还告诉她所有朋友里面写了什么，这事做得太缺德了。
a rotten thing to do = 缺德的事
⑥ The report showed that the whole police department was rotten to the core.
= 报告显示，整个警察部门已经腐败透顶。
rotten to the core = 腐败透顶
⑦ Their grandparents spoil them rotten, buying them sweets and toys every time they visit.
= 祖父母把孩子们宠坏了，每次去都给他们买糖买玩具。
spoil somebody rotten = 把某人宠坏
这里 rotten 作副词，基本只跟 spoil 连用。
⑧ The teacher warned that one rotten apple in the group could get the whole class into trouble.
= 老师警告说，一个害群之马就能让全班惹上麻烦。
a rotten apple = 害群之马
从「一个烂苹果坏了一筐」引申而来，也说 a bad apple。

rottenness
/ˈrɒtnnəs/

Rottweiler
/ˈrɒtwaɪlə(r)/

rotund
/rəʊˈtʌnd/

② The actor's rotund voice filled the theatre even when he spoke quite softly.

rotunda

rotundity

= 她脸上敷了厚厚的粉，抹了胭脂，像橱窗里的洋娃娃。
rouged cheeks = 抹了胭脂的脸颊

rough
/rʌf/
核心：形容词，本义是「表面不平滑」：（表面）粗糙的、坑洼不平的；由「不平」延伸到天气和水面：（海、天气）风浪大的、恶劣的；延伸到人和行为：粗暴的、粗野的：rough play，（地区）治安差的：a rough area；延伸到做工：未经细加工的、粗略的：a rough idea、a rough estimate、a rough draft；延伸到身体和处境：（英式口语）不舒服的：feel rough，艰难的：have a rough time；（声音）沙哑的。
名词：the rough（高尔夫）球道外的长草区；in rough 是打草稿。副词：play rough 是玩得粗野；sleep rough（英式）是露宿街头。动词：rough it 是将就着过苦日子。
副词 roughly：大约；粗暴地；roughly speaking 是大体上说。习语：take the rough with the smooth 是好坏都得接受。
① The bark of the old tree felt rough under my hands as I climbed up to the first branch.
= 我往第一根树枝上爬的时候，手底下老树的树皮摸着很粗糙。
rough = 粗糙的
反义是 smooth；摸上去的粗糙，也用来说皮肤、布料、纸张。
② The car shook badly as we drove along the rough mountain track to the farmhouse.
= 我们沿着坑洼不平的山间土路开往农舍，车颠得厉害。
a rough track = 坑洼不平的土路
③ The sea was too rough for the small ferry, so all the boats to the island were cancelled that day.
= 海上风浪太大，小渡轮开不了，那天去岛上的船全都停了。
a rough sea = 风浪大的海面
从「表面不平」引申到水面起伏，再到整个天气恶劣。
rough weather = 恶劣天气
④ The referee warned both teams that he would not accept any more rough play in the second half.
= 裁判警告两队，下半场他不会再容忍任何粗野动作。
rough play = 粗野动作
说人和动作时 rough 偏「下手重」，不一定有恶意；有意伤人用 violent。
⑤ My parents did not want me to walk home alone through that rough part of town after dark.
= 我父母不愿意我天黑以后独自穿过城里那片治安差的地方回家。
a rough area = 治安差的地区
⑥ Can you give me a rough idea of how much the repairs to the roof are going to cost?
= 你能给我说个大概，修屋顶要花多少钱吗？
a rough idea = 大致的概念
「粗略」这一支和 exact、precise 相对，常配 idea、guess、estimate、sketch。
⑦ At a rough estimate, about two hundred people came to the meeting in the village hall last night.
= 粗略估计，昨晚大约有两百人来村礼堂开会。
at a rough estimate = 粗略估计
⑧ I wrote a rough draft of the speech on the train and then corrected it at home in the evening.
= 我在火车上写了讲稿的草稿，晚上回家再改。
a rough draft = 草稿
⑨ I felt really rough this morning after only three hours of sleep, so I took the day off work.
= 只睡了三个小时，我今天早上觉得很不舒服，就请了一天假。
feel rough = （英式口语）身体不舒服
多指熬夜、喝酒、感冒之后的难受，不说重病。
⑩ She had a rough time at her new school at first, but things got better once she made some friends.
= 她刚到新学校时日子很难过，交了几个朋友以后才好起来。
have a rough time = 日子难过
⑪ His voice was rough after he had spent the whole afternoon shouting at the football match.
= 他在足球赛上喊了一下午，嗓子都哑了。
a rough voice = 沙哑的嗓音
⑫ His first shot went into the rough, and it took him three more shots to reach the green.
= 他第一杆把球打进了长草区，又打了三杆才上果岭。
the rough = （高尔夫）长草区
⑬ I always write my essays in rough first and then copy them out neatly for the teacher.
= 我写文章总是先打草稿，再工工整整地誊给老师。
in rough = 打草稿
⑭ The older boys played rough, and my little brother came home with a cut on his knee.
= 大男孩们玩得很野，我弟弟回家时膝盖上划了个口子。
play rough = 玩得粗野
这里 rough 作副词，只和 play、treat 等少数动词连用。
⑮ Hundreds of people sleep rough in the city centre every night, even in the middle of winter.
= 每天晚上都有好几百人露宿市中心街头，隆冬时节也一样。
sleep rough = 露宿街头
⑯ We roughed it for a week in the mountains, with no electricity and only a cold stream for washing.
= 我们在山里将就着过了一个星期，没有电，只有一条冰凉的小溪可以洗漱。
rough it = 将就着过苦日子
⑰ Every job has good days and bad days, and you have to learn to take the rough with the smooth.
= 每份工作都有好日子和坏日子，你得学会好坏都接受。
take the rough with the smooth = 好坏都得接受
⑱ The journey from London to Edinburgh takes roughly four and a half hours by train.
= 从伦敦坐火车到爱丁堡大约要四个半小时。
roughly = 大约、粗暴地
「大约」义放在数字前，和 about、approximately 同义，口语里很常用。
⑲ The guard pushed him roughly through the door and locked it behind him without saying a word.
= 看守一言不发地把他粗暴地推进门，在他身后锁上了门。
push somebody roughly = 粗暴地推某人
⑳ Roughly speaking, half of the students in the class come from homes where English is not spoken.
= 大体上说，班上有一半学生家里不说英语。
roughly speaking = 大体上说

roughage
/ˈrʌfɪdʒ/
核心：名词，不可数：（食物中的）粗纤维，比 fibre 口语化、旧式；（喂牲口的）粗饲料，如干草、秸秆。
① The doctor told him to eat more fruit, vegetables and brown bread to get enough roughage in his diet.
= 医生叫他多吃水果、蔬菜和全麦面包，让饮食里有足够的粗纤维。
roughage = 粗纤维
② In winter the farmer feeds his cows on hay and other roughage that he stored in the barn.
= 冬天，农场主拿存在谷仓里的干草和其他粗饲料喂牛。
roughage for cattle = 牲口的粗饲料
