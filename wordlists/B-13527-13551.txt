lift
/lɪft/
核心：举起、抬起、提起；（雾、情绪）消散、好转；解除（禁令、限制）；（口语）偷、抄袭。
名词：（英）电梯；搭便车；提升、鼓舞。
短语动词 lift off（火箭升空）。习语：give somebody a lift（让某人搭车；让某人精神一振）、not lift a finger（一点忙都不帮）。
① He lifted the box onto the table with both hands.
= 他用双手把箱子举到桌子上。
lift something onto the table = 把…举到桌上
② She lifted her eyes from the book and smiled at me.
= 她从书上抬起眼睛，对我笑了笑。
lift your eyes = 抬起眼睛
③ The fog lifted by ten, and the planes began to take off.
= 雾在十点钟散了，飞机开始起飞。
the fog lifts = 雾散了
这一支不及物：自己升起、消散。
④ The government has lifted the ban on travel to the island.
= 政府解除了前往该岛的旅行禁令。
lift a ban = 解除禁令
lift a siege / sanctions 同此。
⑤ The good news lifted everyone's spirits.
= 那个好消息让大家精神一振。
lift somebody's spirits = 让某人精神一振
⑥ Whole paragraphs of the essay had been lifted from a website.
= 那篇文章里整段整段是从一个网站上抄来的。
lift something from a website = 从网站上抄…
口语；shoplifting（商店行窃）里的 lift 也是这个意思。
⑦ The rocket lifted off at dawn from the base in the desert.
= 火箭拂晓时从沙漠里的基地升空。
lift off = （火箭）升空
⑧ Take the lift to the fifth floor and turn right.
= 坐电梯到六楼，然后右转。
take the lift = （英）坐电梯
美式说 elevator。英式的 fifth floor 是六楼。
⑨ Could you give me a lift to the station?
= 你能捎我去车站吗？
give somebody a lift = 让某人搭车
⑩ Winning the prize gave her a real lift.
= 得奖让她大受鼓舞。
give somebody a lift = 让某人精神一振
同一个短语的另一个意思。
⑪ He never lifts a finger to help with the housework.
= 家务活他从来一点忙都不帮。
not lift a finger = 一点忙都不帮

ligament
/ˈlɪɡəmənt/
核心：韧带 —— 把骨头在关节处连在一起的坚韧组织。
① He tore a ligament in his knee and missed the rest of the season.
= 他撕裂了膝盖的一条韧带，赛季剩下的比赛都没能上场。
tear a ligament = 撕裂韧带
② The ligaments of the ankle hold the bones of the foot in place.
= 脚踝的韧带把脚骨固定在位。
the ligaments of the ankle = 脚踝的韧带
连接肌肉和骨头的叫 tendon（肌腱）。

ligature
/ˈlɪɡətʃə(r)/
核心：（正式）绑扎用的绳、带；（医）结扎线；（印刷）连字，如 æ。
① The doctor found marks on the neck that had been made by a ligature.
= 医生在脖子上发现了绳索勒出的痕迹。
a ligature = 绑扎用的绳、带
法医报告里常见。
② The surgeon tied a ligature around the blood vessel.
= 外科医生在血管上扎了一道结扎线。
tie a ligature = 扎结扎线
③ Old books often print the letters f and i as a ligature.
= 老书常把字母 f 和 i 印成一个连字。
a ligature = 连字

light
/laɪt/
核心：两个不同的词，拼法相同。
其一：光、光线；灯；（点烟的）火。动词：点燃；照亮。形容词：明亮的；（颜色）浅的。过去式 lit，也用 lighted（多作定语）。
短语动词：light up（点亮；点烟；（脸）亮起来）、light on / light upon（文：偶然发现）。
习语：come to light（暴露、为人所知）、bring to light（揭露）、shed light on（阐明）、in the light of（鉴于）、
see the light（恍然大悟）、set light to（点着、放火烧）、the light at the end of the tunnel（隧道尽头的光、希望）。
搭配：light bulb（灯泡）、light year（光年）、light show（灯光秀）、light meter（测光表）、light pen（光笔）、
light stick（荧光棒）、light pollution（光污染）、light-coloured（浅色的）。
其二：轻的；少量的、轻微的；轻松的；（食物）清淡的；（睡眠）浅的。名词 lightness（轻；明亮）并入本条。
习语 make light of（不当回事）。
搭配：light aircraft（轻型飞机）、light industry（轻工业）、light railway（轻轨）、light ale（淡色麦酒）、
light cream（美：稀奶油）；形容词 light-hearted（轻松愉快的，副词 light-heartedly）、light-headed（头晕的）、
light-fingered（爱小偷小摸的）、light-footed（脚步轻快的）。
① Light from the street lamp came in through the curtains.
= 路灯的光透过窗帘照进来。
light from the lamp = 灯光
这一支不可数。
② Turn off the lights when you leave the room.
= 离开房间时把灯关掉。
turn off the lights = 关灯
这一支可数：一盏灯。
③ Wait until the lights turn green before you cross.
= 等绿灯亮了再过马路。
the lights = 红绿灯
traffic lights 的简称。
④ Have you got a light? I have left my matches at home.
= 你有火吗？我把火柴落在家里了。
a light = （点烟的）火
⑤ He lit a candle and carried it up the stairs.
= 他点了一支蜡烛，端着上了楼。
lit = light 的过去式
light a candle = 点蜡烛
⑥ The stage was lit by a single lamp.
= 舞台只由一盏灯照着。
be lit by something = 被…照亮
这一支是「照亮」，不是「点燃」。
⑦ She carried a lighted candle into the dark room.
= 她端着一支点着的蜡烛走进黑屋子。
a lighted candle = 点着的蜡烛
作定语多用 lighted。
⑧ It gets light at about five in summer.
= 夏天五点左右天就亮了。
get light = 天亮
反义是 get dark。
⑨ She painted the walls light blue.
= 她把墙刷成了浅蓝色。
light blue = 浅蓝色
反义是 dark blue。
⑩ Her face lit up when she saw the present.
= 她看见礼物，脸一下子亮了起来。
light up = （脸）亮起来
⑪ Fireworks lit up the sky over the harbour.
= 烟花照亮了港口上空。
light up the sky = 照亮天空
及物用法。
⑫ He sat down and lit up a cigarette.
= 他坐下来，点了一支烟。
light up a cigarette = 点烟
⑬ New facts about the case came to light last week.
= 上周，这个案子的新情况浮出了水面。
come to light = 暴露、为人所知
⑭ The inquiry brought to light serious failures at the hospital.
= 那场调查揭露出医院的严重失职。
bring to light = 揭露
⑮ The letters shed light on the writer's early life.
= 那些信让人对作家的早年生活有了了解。
shed light on = 阐明、使人了解
也说 throw / cast light on。
⑯ In the light of the new evidence, the police reopened the case.
= 鉴于新证据，警方重新立案。
in the light of = 鉴于
美式说 in light of。
⑰ He finally saw the light and gave up smoking.
= 他终于想通了，把烟戒了。
see the light = 恍然大悟、想通了
⑱ Someone set light to the old barn during the night.
= 夜里有人放火烧了那座旧谷仓。
set light to = 点着、放火烧
英式说法；美式说 set fire to。
⑲ After months of losses, we can see the light at the end of the tunnel.
= 亏了好几个月，我们总算看到了隧道尽头的光。
the light at the end of the tunnel = 困境将尽的希望
⑳ While tidying the attic, she lit upon a box of old letters.
= 整理阁楼时，她偶然发现了一盒旧信。
light upon = （文）偶然发现
light on = 偶然发现（同上）
㉑ The light bulb in the hall needs changing.
= 门厅的灯泡该换了。
a light bulb = 灯泡
漫画里脑袋上亮起灯泡：有了主意。
㉒ The star is four light years from the earth.
= 那颗星离地球四光年。
a light year = 光年
是距离，不是时间。
㉓ The concert ended with a light show over the river.
= 音乐会以河面上的一场灯光秀结束。
a light show = 灯光秀
㉔ The photographer checked the light meter before each shot.
= 摄影师每拍一张之前都看一下测光表。
a light meter = 测光表
㉕ Early computers let you draw on the screen with a light pen.
= 早期的计算机能让你用光笔在屏幕上画画。
a light pen = 光笔
触摸屏出现以前的东西。
㉖ The fans waved light sticks in time to the music.
= 歌迷们随着音乐的节拍挥舞荧光棒。
a light stick = 荧光棒
㉗ Light pollution makes it hard to see the stars from the city.
= 光污染让人在城里很难看见星星。
light pollution = 光污染
㉘ Wear light-coloured clothes so that drivers can see you at night.
= 穿浅色衣服，夜里司机才看得见你。
light-coloured = 浅色的
㉙ The suitcase is light enough for a child to carry.
= 那只手提箱轻得连孩子都拎得动。
be light = 轻
这是另一个 light：「轻」。
㉚ Traffic was light on the motorway this morning.
= 今天早上高速公路上车不多。
light traffic = 车流稀少
这一支是「量少」：light rain（小雨）同此。
㉛ We had a light lunch of soup and bread.
= 我们午饭吃得清淡，就是汤和面包。
a light lunch = 清淡的午饭
㉜ I took a novel with me for some light reading on the train.
= 我带了本小说，在火车上轻松地读读。
light reading = 轻松的读物
不费脑子、消遣用的。
㉝ She is a light sleeper who wakes at the slightest noise.
= 她睡得浅，一点动静就醒。
a light sleeper = 睡得浅的人
㉞ He made light of his injury and finished the match.
= 他没把伤当回事，踢完了比赛。
make light of = 不当回事
㉟ They flew to the island in a light aircraft with four seats.
= 他们坐一架四座的轻型飞机飞往那座岛。
a light aircraft = 轻型飞机
㊱ The town's light industry makes shoes and clothes.
= 那座城的轻工业生产鞋和衣服。
light industry = 轻工业
㊲ A light railway carries tourists up the valley to the lake.
= 一条轻轨把游客沿山谷送到湖边。
a light railway = 轻轨
轨道窄、车厢小的铁路。
㊳ He ordered half a pint of light ale.
= 他点了半品脱淡色麦酒。
light ale = 淡色麦酒
㊴ Pour a little light cream over the strawberries.
= 往草莓上浇一点稀奶油。
light cream = （美）稀奶油
㊵ It is a light-hearted film that the whole family can enjoy.
= 那是一部轻松愉快的电影，全家都能看。
light-hearted = 轻松愉快的
㊶ They talked light-heartedly about their plans for the summer.
= 他们轻松愉快地聊着夏天的打算。
light-heartedly = 轻松愉快地
㊷ I felt light-headed after standing in the sun for an hour.
= 在太阳底下站了一个小时，我觉得头晕。
light-headed = 头晕的
不是「头脑轻松」，是发飘、要晕。
㊸ Watch your wallet; some of the people in this market are light-fingered.
= 看好你的钱包，这个市场上有些人手脚不干净。
light-fingered = 爱小偷小摸的

㊹ The light-footed dancer crossed the stage without a sound.
= 那位脚步轻盈的舞者悄无声息地穿过舞台。
light-footed = 脚步轻快的
㊺ The lightness of the new bike makes it easy to carry up the stairs.
= 新自行车很轻，拎上楼很方便。
lightness = 轻
㊻ Check that your reversing light works before the car's test.
= 汽车年检前，检查一下倒车灯亮不亮。
a reversing light = （英式）倒车灯

lightning
/ˈlaɪtnɪŋ/

① A flash of lightning lit up the whole valley.

a flash of lightning = 一道闪电

= 暴风雨中，那棵老橡树被雷劈了。

= 那只猫闪电般一动，逮住了鸟。

④ The goalkeeper made a save with lightning speed.
= 守门员以闪电般的速度把球扑了出去。
lightning speed = 闪电般的速度
⑤ A lightning conductor runs down the side of the church tower.
= 一根避雷针顺着教堂塔楼的侧面通下来。
a lightning conductor = （英）避雷针
⑥ The minister has become a lightning rod for anger about the new tax.
= 那位部长成了人们对新税的怒火集中的对象。
a lightning rod = （美）避雷针；招来批评的人
⑦ The fire was started by a lightning strike.
= 那场火是雷击引起的。

like
/laɪk/
核心：两个词。其一（动词）：喜欢；想要（would like）。名词 likes（喜好）。
其二（介词、连词）：像、如同；比如；（口语）好像。形容词（正式）：相似的。名词：the like（诸如此类的东西）。
口语里还当填充语和引语标记用（I was like, …）。
句型与习语：like doing / like to do（喜欢做）、would like（想要，比 want 客气）、if you like（如果你愿意）、
look like（看起来像）、feel like（想要；摸起来像）、what is it like（…怎么样）、and the like（诸如此类）、
the likes of（像…这样的人）、like-minded（志趣相投的）、like father, like son（有其父必有其子）。
① She likes walking in the hills with her two dogs at the weekend.
= 她周末喜欢带着两条狗去山里走走。
like doing something = 喜欢做…
② I like to get up early and read before breakfast.
= 我喜欢早起，早饭前看会儿书。
like to do something = 喜欢（习惯于）做…
③ I would like a cup of tea and a piece of cake, please.
= 请给我一杯茶和一块蛋糕。
would like = 想要
④ Would you like to come to dinner on Friday and meet my parents?
= 你星期五愿意来吃晚饭、见见我父母吗？
would you like to… = 你愿意…吗
⑤ We can walk to the station along the river path, if you like.
= 你要是愿意，我们可以沿着河边小路走去车站。
if you like = 如果你愿意
⑥ He looks like his father in the old wedding photograph when he smiles.
= 他笑起来像结婚旧照上的他父亲。
look like = 看起来像
⑦ It looks like rain over the hills, so take a coat and an umbrella.
= 山那边看样子要下雨，带件外套和一把伞吧。
it looks like rain = 看样子要下雨
⑧ I feel like a walk after sitting at the desk all day.
= 在桌前坐了一整天，我想去走走。

⑨ What is the new maths teacher like? Is she strict about homework?
= 新来的数学老师怎么样？对作业要求严吗？
what is it like = …怎么样
问的是特点，不是「喜欢什么」。
⑩ She sings like an angel, but she is too shy to join the church choir.
= 她唱得像天使一样，却害羞得不肯加入教堂唱诗班。
like an angel = 像天使一样
这是介词：像。
⑪ I enjoy team sports like football and hockey.
= 我喜欢足球、曲棍球之类的团体运动。
like football = 比如足球
⑫ It is just like him to forget his own birthday.
= 连自己的生日都忘，这正是他的作风。
it is just like somebody to… = …正是某人的作风
⑬ We grew up in the same street, and nobody understands her like I do.
= 我们在同一条街上长大，没有人像我这样了解她。
like I do = 像我这样
这是连词，口语；正式写作用 as。
⑭ He has worked here for a week, and he acts like he owns the place.
= 他才来一个星期，那副做派就好像这地方是他的。
like = （口语）好像
正式说 as if。
⑮ He told me the ticket cost two hundred pounds, and I was like, "Are you serious?"
= 他跟我说那张票要两百英镑，我就说：“你当真？”
I was like = （口语）我就说
年轻人口语里引出话语、反应。
⑯ We are all different, with our own likes and dislikes.
= 我们各不相同，各有各的好恶。
likes and dislikes = 好恶
⑰ The little shop beside the school sells pens, notebooks and the like.
= 学校旁边那家小店卖钢笔、笔记本之类的东西。
and the like = 诸如此类
⑱ A hotel like that is not for the likes of us.
= 那样的酒店不是给我们这种人住的。
the likes of = 像…这样的人
⑲ She joined a club to meet like-minded people.
= 她加入了一个俱乐部，想结识志趣相投的人。
like-minded = 志趣相投的

like father, like son = 有其父必有其子
