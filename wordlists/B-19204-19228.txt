revoke
/rɪˈvəʊk/
核心：（正式）撤销、废除、吊销（法律、决定、许可证）。及物。名词 revocation 见另条。
① The council revoked the licence of the bar after several fights.
= 打了几次架之后，镇议会吊销了那家酒吧的执照。
revoke a licence = 吊销执照
revoke = 撤销
② The government revoked the law that had banned meetings in the square.
= 政府废除了禁止在广场集会的那项法律。
revoke a law = 废除法律

revolt
/rɪˈvəʊlt/
核心：名词，可数也可不可数：反抗、叛乱、起义：in revolt、a revolt against。动词：反抗、叛乱、造反：revolt against；（使）厌恶、（使）反感，及物。
分词 revolting 作形容词：令人作呕的，见另条。
① The people rose in revolt against the cruel king.
= 人民起来反抗残暴的国王。
rise in revolt = 起来反抗
revolt = 反抗
② The farmers revolted against the new tax on land.
= 农民们起来反抗新的土地税。
revolt against something = 反抗某事
③ The way he treated the animals revolted everyone who saw it.
= 他对待动物的方式让每个看见的人都很反感。
revolt somebody = 使某人反感
④ The smell from the bins was absolutely revolting.
= 垃圾桶里的气味简直令人作呕。
revolting = 令人作呕的

revolting
/rɪˈvəʊltɪŋ/
核心：令人作呕的、令人厌恶的、讨厌的。副词 revoltingly，并入本条。
① The soup at the station café was cold and greasy and tasted revolting.
= 车站咖啡馆的汤又凉又油，味道令人作呕。
taste revolting = 味道令人作呕
revolting = 令人作呕的
② The old house was revoltingly dirty when we moved in, with rubbish in every room.
= 我们搬进去的时候，那座老房子脏得令人恶心，每个房间都有垃圾。
revoltingly dirty = 脏得令人恶心

revolution
/ˌrevəˈluːʃn/
核心：可数也可不可数。一是革命：推翻政府、改变政治制度；二是（方法、思想的）巨大变革、革命：the industrial revolution；三是旋转、转一圈：revolutions per minute。
① The revolution of seventeen eighty-nine changed France for ever.
= 一七八九年的革命永远改变了法国。
the revolution = 革命
revolution = 革命
② The computer has caused a revolution in the way we work.
= 电脑给我们的工作方式带来了一场革命。
a revolution in something = 某方面的巨大变革
③ The wheel makes three thousand revolutions per minute.
= 那个轮子每分钟转三千转。
revolutions per minute = 每分钟转数

revolutionary
/ˌrevəˈluːʃənəri/
核心：形容词：革命的、革命性的；彻底变革的、全新的：a revolutionary idea。名词，可数：革命者、革命家。
① The revolutionary army marched into the capital in the spring.
= 革命军春天开进了首都。
the revolutionary army = 革命军
revolutionary = 革命的
② The new drug is a revolutionary treatment for the disease.
= 那种新药是治那种病的革命性疗法。
a revolutionary treatment = 革命性疗法
③ As a young man he was a revolutionary who wanted to change the world.
= 他年轻时是个想改变世界的革命者。
a revolutionary = 革命者

revolutionize
/ˌrevəˈluːʃənaɪz/
核心：彻底改变、使发生革命性变化。及物。英式也拼 revolutionise。
① In the last twenty years the internet has revolutionized the way people shop and pay their bills.
= 近二十年来，互联网彻底改变了人们购物和付账的方式。
revolutionize the way… = 彻底改变……的方式
revolutionize = 彻底改变
② The invention of the petrol engine revolutionized farming in the early twentieth century.
= 汽油发动机的发明在二十世纪初给农业带来了革命性的变化。
revolutionize farming = 彻底改变农业

revolve

① The earth revolves around the sun once a year.
= 地球每年绕太阳转一圈。
revolve around something = 绕某物旋转
revolve = 旋转
② Her whole life revolves around her children and her garden.
= 她的整个生活都围着孩子和园子转。

③ The theatre has a revolving stage that turns between the scenes.
= 那家剧院有一个在换场时转动的旋转舞台。
a revolving stage = 旋转舞台
revolving = 旋转的
④ His whole life seemed to revolve round football.
= 他的整个生活似乎都围着足球转。
revolve round something = 围着某事转

reward
/rɪˈwɔːd/
核心：名词，可数也可不可数：报酬、回报、奖赏：as a reward for、in reward for；（找回失物、抓到罪犯的）赏金、悬赏：offer a reward。
动词：奖赏、酬谢、回报：reward somebody for、reward somebody with。形容词 rewarding 见另条。

= 当护士很辛苦，但回报很大。
the rewards = 回报
reward = 因付出得到的回报
③ The police are offering a reward for information about the robbery.
= 警方悬赏征集有关那起抢劫的线索。
offer a reward = 悬赏
④ She won first prize in the singing competition, and her hard work was rewarded at last.
= 她在歌唱比赛中得了一等奖，辛苦终于有了回报。
prize = 比赛赢的奖
⑤ The writer received an award for her first novel, a fair reward for ten years of work.
= 那位作家的第一部小说获了奖，是对她十年心血应得的回报。
award = 评选颁发的奖
⑥ The firm rewarded its workers with a bonus at Christmas.
= 那家公司圣诞节给员工发奖金作为奖励。
reward somebody with something = 用某物奖励某人
⑦ She was rewarded for her patience when the shy bird finally came out.
= 那只怕人的鸟终于出来了，她的耐心得到了回报。
be rewarded for something = 某事得到回报
⑧ Teaching young children is very rewarding.

rewarding = 有回报的
