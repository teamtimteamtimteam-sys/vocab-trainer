reverse
/rɪˈvɜːs/
核心：动词：使反转、颠倒（顺序、位置）；彻底改变、推翻（决定、政策）；（车）倒车、倒退：reverse into；（英式）reverse the charges 是打对方付费电话。

① She reversed the car into the narrow space between two vans.

= 名字是倒着念的，从最后一个开始。

④ The film tells the story in reverse, from the death of the hero to his birth.
= 那部片子倒着讲故事，从主人公之死讲到他出生。
in reverse = 倒着
⑤ He put the car into reverse and slowly backed out of the garage.
= 他挂上倒挡，慢慢把车倒出了车库。
put the car into reverse = 挂倒挡
⑥ The test was meant to make him calm, but it had the reverse effect.
= 那次测试本来是想让他平静，结果却适得其反。
the reverse effect = 相反的效果
⑦ The coin has the queen's head on one side and a ship on the reverse.
= 那枚硬币一面是女王头像，背面是一艘船。

⑧ Some people say that giving jobs only to women is reverse discrimination.
