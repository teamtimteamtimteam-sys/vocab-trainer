intuitive
/ˌɪnˈtjuːˈɪtɪv/
核心：直觉的；（设计）易懂好用的。两支：凭直觉的、有直觉力的（an intuitive understanding）；
软件、界面不用学就会用（an intuitive interface）。副词 intuitively 并入本条。
① She has an intuitive understanding of how children think.
= 她对孩子怎么想有一种直觉上的理解。
an intuitive understanding = 直觉上的理解
② The new phone is so intuitive that my grandmother learned to use it in a day.
= 新手机简单易用，我奶奶一天就学会了。
intuitive = 易懂好用的
③ He knew intuitively that the plan would fail.
= 他凭直觉就知道那个计划会失败。
intuitively = 凭直觉地

Inuit
/ˈɪnjuɪt/
核心：因纽特人 —— 生活在北极地区（加拿大、格陵兰、阿拉斯加）的原住民；也作形容词。
许多因纽特人不愿被称为 Eskimo，Inuit 是今天通用的称呼。
① Many Inuit still hunt seals and fish through holes in the ice.
= 许多因纽特人至今仍在冰上凿洞捕海豹、捕鱼。
the Inuit = 因纽特人
② The museum has a large collection of Inuit art and carvings.
= 那家博物馆收藏了大量因纽特艺术品和雕刻。
Inuit art = 因纽特艺术

Inuktitut
/ɪˈnʊktɪtʊt/
核心：因纽特语 —— 加拿大东部因纽特人的语言，在努纳武特地区是官方语言之一。
① Road signs in the town are written in both Inuktitut and English.
= 那个镇上的路标用因纽特语和英语双语书写。
Inuktitut = 因纽特语
② Children in the region now learn Inuktitut at school.
= 那个地区的孩子们现在在学校学因纽特语。
learn Inuktitut = 学因纽特语

inundate
/ˈɪnʌndeɪt/
核心：淹没；使应接不暇 —— 本义是洪水淹没；比喻大量的信件、请求像潮水般涌来（be inundated with）。
名词 inundation 并入本条。
① After the TV programme, the charity was inundated with offers of help.
= 电视节目播出后，那家慈善机构收到了潮水般的援助提议。
be inundated with something = 被…淹没、应接不暇
② The river burst its banks and inundated the fields for miles around.
= 河水决堤，淹没了方圆数英里的田地。
inundate something = 淹没…
③ The whole valley was lost to inundation when the dam was built.
= 水坝建成后，整个山谷被淹没了。
inundation = 淹没

inure
/ɪˈnjʊə(r)/
核心：（正式）使习惯于（困难、痛苦）—— 多用被动 be inured to something：已经习惯到不再觉得难受。
① Years of living in the city had inured her to the noise of the traffic.
= 多年城市生活让她对车流噪音已经习以为常。
inure somebody to something = 使某人习惯于…
② Nurses become inured to the sight of blood.
= 护士们对见血已经习以为常。
be inured to something = 对…习以为常
③ Years of working in the busy hospital had inured the nurse to the sight of blood.
= 在繁忙的医院工作多年，那位护士早已对血见怪不怪了。
inure to = 使习惯于（多用被动：be inured to something）

invade
/ɪnˈveɪd/
核心：侵略、侵入；涌入；侵犯。几支：军队进入别国领土（invade a country）；
大批人涌进某地（fans invaded the pitch）；侵扰（隐私）；疾病、细胞侵入身体。侵略者 invader 并入本条。
① The army invaded the country from the north in the spring of 1940.
= 一九四〇年春天，那支军队从北面入侵了那个国家。
invade a country = 入侵一个国家
② Hundreds of fans invaded the pitch after the final whistle.
= 终场哨响后，成百上千的球迷冲进了球场。
invade the pitch = 冲进球场
③ The photographers invaded her privacy by following her on holiday.
= 摄影记者一路跟着她去度假，侵犯了她的隐私。
invade somebody's privacy = 侵犯某人的隐私
④ The castle was built to defend the town against invaders from the sea.
= 那座城堡是为了抵御从海上来的侵略者而建的。
an invader = 侵略者

invalid
/ɪnˈvælɪd/（无效的）/ˈɪnvəlɪd/（病弱者）
核心：两个词同形。形容词（重音在后）：无效的、作废的（an invalid passport）；
（论点）站不住脚的。名词（重音在前）：（旧，现多嫌冒犯）病弱者、残疾人。
名词 invalidity（无效；（英）残疾）并入本条。
① Your ticket is invalid because it is for yesterday's train.
= 你的票无效，因为那是昨天那班车的。
an invalid ticket = 无效车票
② The court declared the election result invalid.
= 法院宣布选举结果无效。
declare something invalid = 宣布…无效
③ She spent twenty years caring for her father, who was an invalid.
= 她花了二十年照顾病弱的父亲。
an invalid = 病弱者（旧）
今天多说 a person with a disability 或 a sick person。
④ The lawyer argued for the invalidity of the contract.
= 律师论证那份合同无效。
the invalidity of something = …的无效

invalidate
/ɪnˈvælɪdeɪt/
核心：使无效、使作废；证明（论点）站不住脚。名词 invalidation 并入本条。
① Any handwritten change to the signed document will invalidate it in the eyes of the court.
= 对签过字的文件做任何手写改动，在法庭看来都会使它失效。
invalidate something = 使…无效
多用于法律、合同、证件等正式语境；主语常是某个行为或事实，而不是人。
② New evidence from the ancient bones invalidated the old theory about the first farmers.
= 那些古骨提供的新证据，推翻了关于最早农民的旧理论。
invalidate a theory = 推翻一个理论
③ The invalidation of the election result by the court led to weeks of angry protests.
= 法院宣布选举结果无效，引发了几个星期的愤怒抗议。
the invalidation of something = …的作废

invaluable
/ɪnˈvæljʊbəl/
核心：极其宝贵的、无价的 —— 注意不是 valuable 的反面，而是「贵重得无法估价」。
① Her experience of working abroad proved invaluable when the company expanded.
= 公司扩张时，她在国外工作的经验证明极其宝贵。
prove invaluable = 证明极有价值
② The internet is an invaluable source of information for students.
= 互联网对学生来说是极其宝贵的信息来源。
an invaluable source = 极宝贵的来源

invariable
/ɪnˈveəriəbəl/
核心：不变的、始终如一的 —— 从不改变的。副词 invariably（总是、一贯）最常用，并入本条。
① Her invariable answer to every question was "Ask your father."
= 无论问她什么，她的回答总是「去问你爸爸」。
an invariable answer = 一成不变的回答
② Whatever the weather, my uncle invariably arrives late for family dinners and leaves early.
= 不管天气怎样，我叔叔参加家宴总是迟到早退。
invariably = 总是、一贯

invariant
/ɪnˈveəriənt/
核心：（数学、科学）不变的；不变量 —— 在变换中保持不变的量或性质。
① The speed of light is invariant, whatever the speed of the observer.
= 无论观察者速度如何，光速都是不变的。
be invariant = 保持不变
② Mathematicians look for invariants that stay the same when a shape is stretched.
= 数学家寻找在图形被拉伸时保持不变的量。
an invariant = 不变量

invasion
/ɪnˈveɪʒən/
核心：侵略、入侵；涌入；侵犯。invade 的名词，几支对应：军事入侵（the invasion of）；
大批涌入（an invasion of tourists）；侵犯（an invasion of privacy）。
① The invasion began at dawn with an attack on the coast.
= 入侵在黎明时分以对海岸的攻击开始。
an invasion = 入侵
② Every summer the village suffers an invasion of tourists.
= 每年夏天，那个村子都会遭到游客的大举涌入。
an invasion of tourists = 游客的涌入
③ Reading someone's diary is an invasion of privacy.
= 看别人的日记是侵犯隐私。
an invasion of privacy = 侵犯隐私

invasive
/ɪnˈveɪsɪv/
核心：侵入的；（医学）侵入性的、创伤性的；（物种）入侵的。几支：
医学上需要切开身体的（invasive surgery）；疾病扩散的；外来物种泛滥成灾的（invasive species）。
① The new treatment is less invasive than traditional surgery.
= 新疗法的创伤比传统手术小。
invasive surgery = 侵入性手术
② Invasive plants from other countries are crowding out native species.
= 来自别国的入侵植物正在排挤本地物种。
invasive species = 入侵物种
③ Many people find the new security checks invasive.
= 许多人觉得新的安检措施侵扰隐私。
invasive = 侵扰的
④ A non-invasive scan can detect the blockage without any need for surgery.
= 一次无创的扫描就能查出堵塞，不必动手术。
non-invasive = 无创的

invective
/ɪnˈvektɪv/
核心：（正式）痛骂、抨击、谩骂 —— 不可数，指一连串激烈的辱骂言辞。
① The politician let loose a stream of invective against his opponents.
= 那位政客对他的对手们发出了一连串的抨击。
a stream of invective = 一连串的谩骂
② The article is full of personal invective and contains few facts.
= 那篇文章满是人身攻击，却没几条事实。
personal invective = 人身攻击

inveigh
/ɪnˈveɪ/
核心：（正式）痛斥、猛烈抨击 —— 几乎只用 inveigh against something。
① The preacher inveighed against the evils of gambling.
= 传教士痛斥赌博之恶。
inveigh against something = 痛斥…
② Critics inveighed against the new law as an attack on free speech.
= 批评者猛烈抨击那项新法律，称其是对言论自由的攻击。
inveigh against a law = 抨击一项法律

inveigle
/ɪnˈveɪɡəl/
核心：（正式）哄骗、诱使 —— 用花言巧语让人做某事或得到某物（inveigle somebody into doing）。
① He inveigled his aunt into lending him money for a business that never existed.
= 他哄骗姑妈借钱给他，去做一桩根本不存在的生意。
inveigle somebody into doing something = 哄骗某人做…
② She inveigled her way into the director's office by pretending to be a journalist.
= 她假装记者，混进了董事的办公室。
inveigle your way into somewhere = 设法混进某处

invent
/ɪnˈvent/
核心：发明；编造、捏造。两支：创造出以前没有的东西（invent a machine）；
编出假的故事、借口（invent an excuse）。名词 invention、inventor 另立。
① Alexander Graham Bell is usually said to have invented the telephone.
= 人们通常说是贝尔发明了电话。
invent something = 发明…
② He invented an excuse about a sick grandmother to leave work early.
= 他编了个奶奶生病的借口，提前下了班。
invent an excuse = 编造借口

invention
/ɪnˈvenʃən/
核心：发明；发明物；编造；创造力。几支：发明这件事（the invention of printing）；一项具体的发明（a clever invention）；
虚构的故事（pure invention）；创造性。
① The invention of the printing press changed the world.
= 印刷机的发明改变了世界。
the invention of something = …的发明
② His latest invention is a bicycle that folds up into a small bag.
= 他最新的发明是一辆可以折进小包里的自行车。
an invention = 发明物
③ The story about his time in the army was pure invention.
= 他那些当兵的故事纯属编造。
pure invention = 纯属编造

inventive
/ɪnˈventɪv/
核心：有创造力的、善于发明的；新颖的。副词 inventively 与名词 inventiveness 并入本条。
① She is an inventive cook who never follows a recipe exactly.
= 她是个很有创意的厨师，从不照搬食谱。
an inventive cook = 有创意的厨师
既可形容人，也可形容做法、想法本身，表示新颖巧妙。
② The old warehouse has been inventively turned into a theatre.
= 那座旧仓库被别出心裁地改成了剧院。
inventively = 别出心裁地
③ Children often show great inventiveness in their games.
= 孩子们在游戏中常常表现出很强的创造力。
inventiveness = 创造力

inventor
/ɪnˈventə(r)/
核心：发明者、发明家 —— 发明了某种东西的人。
① The inventor of the washing machine in the museum never made any money from it.
= 博物馆里那台洗衣机的发明者从来没靠它赚过钱。
the inventor of something = …的发明者
② As a child she wanted to be an inventor and filled the garage with strange machines.
= 她小时候想当发明家，把车库塞满了奇怪的机器。
an inventor = 发明家

inventory
/ˈɪnvəntəri/
核心：清单、存货清单；存货。两支：一份详细列出物品的清单（make an inventory）；
（美）店里、仓库里的全部存货（英式多说 stock）。
① The landlord made an inventory of all the furniture before we moved in.
= 我们搬进去之前，房东把所有家具列了一份清单。
make an inventory of something = 列出…的清单
② The shop is selling off its old inventory at half price.
= 那家店正在半价清仓旧存货。
inventory = （美）存货

inverse
/ˌɪnˈvɜːs/
核心：相反的、反向的；反面、逆 —— 数学上指反比（in inverse proportion）、逆运算；一般说正好倒过来的关系。
副词 inversely 并入本条。
① The number of mistakes rose in inverse proportion to the time the students were given.
= 给学生的时间越少，错误就越多，二者成反比。
in inverse proportion to something = 与…成反比
② Adding is the inverse of subtracting, so one can undo the other.
= 加法是减法的逆运算，所以一个能抵消另一个。
the inverse of something = …的逆、反面
③ The price of the old bonds moves inversely to interest rates.
= 那些旧债券的价格与利率反向变动。
inversely = 相反地、成反比地

inversion
/ɪnˈvɜːʒən/
核心：倒置、颠倒；（语法）倒装；（气象）逆温。本义是把顺序、位置倒过来。
① The inversion of the usual roles meant that the children cooked dinner for their parents.
= 角色一颠倒，就成了孩子们给父母做晚饭。
the inversion of something = …的颠倒
② In questions such as "Are you ready?", English uses inversion of the subject and the verb.
= 在「Are you ready?」这类问句里，英语把主语和动词倒装。
inversion = 倒装
③ A temperature inversion trapped the smoke over the city for three days.
= 逆温现象把烟雾困在城市上空三天。
a temperature inversion = 逆温

invert
/ɪnˈvɜːt/
核心：倒置、颠倒 —— 把东西上下或前后翻过来。形容词 inverted（倒置的）并入本条；
inverted commas（英，引号）与 inverted snobbery（反向势利，看不起上层的东西以显示自己接地气）也并在这里。
① Invert the cake onto a plate as soon as it comes out of the oven.
= 蛋糕一出炉就把它倒扣在盘子上。
invert something = 把…倒过来
② The picture in the camera is inverted, so the photographer sees everything upside down.
= 相机里的图像是倒过来的，所以摄影师看到的一切都是颠倒的。
inverted = 倒置的
③ Put the title of the poem in inverted commas.
= 把诗的题目放在引号里。
inverted commas = （英）引号
口语里也说 in inverted commas，表示「所谓的」。
④ His refusal to eat in expensive restaurants is a kind of inverted snobbery.
= 他拒绝去高档餐馆吃饭，是一种反向势利。
inverted snobbery = 反向势利

invertebrate
/ɪnˈvɜːtɪbrət/
核心：无脊椎动物 —— 没有脊骨的动物，如虫、蜗牛、水母。也作形容词。
① Most of the animals living in the pond are invertebrates such as snails and insects.
= 池塘里的动物大多是蜗牛、昆虫之类的无脊椎动物。
an invertebrate = 无脊椎动物
② Scientists counted over two hundred invertebrate species in a single tree.
= 科学家在一棵树上就数出了两百多种无脊椎动物。
invertebrate species = 无脊椎动物物种
