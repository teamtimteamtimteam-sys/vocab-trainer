interminable
核心：没完没了的、冗长乏味的 —— 长得让人烦。副词 interminably 并入本条。
① We sat through an interminable speech about the history of the company.
= 我们硬着头皮听完了一场关于公司历史的冗长讲话。
an interminable speech = 冗长的讲话
② The meeting dragged on interminably, and nothing was decided.
= 会议没完没了地拖着，什么也没决定。
drag on interminably = 没完没了地拖着

intermingle
核心：（使）混合、混杂 —— 人、东西混在一起。常接 with。
① At the party, the students intermingled with the teachers and their families.
= 聚会上，学生们和老师及其家人打成一片。
intermingle with somebody = 与某人混在一起
② The smells of coffee and fresh bread intermingled in the morning air.
= 清晨的空气中，咖啡和新鲜面包的香味混在一起。
intermingle = 混合

intermission
核心：（演出的）幕间休息、中场休息；间歇 —— 美式常用；英式多用另一个表示「间隔」的词。
① We queued for ice cream in the foyer during the intermission of the musical.
= 音乐剧中场休息时，我们在门厅里排队买冰淇淋。
during the intermission = 中场休息时
② The rain fell without intermission for three days, and the river burst its banks.
= 雨接连下了三天没停过，河水漫过了堤岸。
without intermission = 不间断地

intermittent
核心：断断续续的、间歇的 —— 时有时无、不连续。副词 intermittently 并入本条。
① The forecast for the weekend is for sunshine and intermittent showers in the west.
= 周末的天气预报说，西部地区晴，间有阵雨。
intermittent showers = 间歇性阵雨
② Up in the mountains the phone signal is intermittent, so hikers should carry a map.
= 在山里手机信号时有时无，所以徒步者应该带张地图。
be intermittent = 时有时无
③ She worked intermittently as a waitress while she was a student.
= 上学期间，她断断续续地当过服务员。
intermittently = 断断续续地

intermix
核心：（使）混合 —— 同 intermingle，多说不同成分混在一起。
① The two languages intermix in the everyday speech of the border towns.
= 边境小镇的日常口语里，两种语言混杂在一起。
intermix = 混杂
② Intermix the dry ingredients before adding the eggs.
= 加鸡蛋之前，先把干料混合均匀。
intermix ingredients = 混合原料

intern
核心：两个来路不同的意思。名词（重音在前）：（美）实习医生；实习生（an intern at a law firm）；
动词（重音在后）：（战时）拘留、扣押（敌国侨民、战俘）。
被拘留者 internee、拘留 internment 与实习期 internship 并入本条。
① She spent the summer as an intern at a newspaper in New York.
= 她那个夏天在纽约一家报社当实习生。
an intern = 实习生
② During the war, thousands of foreign nationals were interned in camps.
= 战争期间，成千上万的外国侨民被关进了拘留营。
be interned = 被拘留
③ The internees were held on an island for the rest of the war.
= 被拘留者在一座岛上一直被关到战争结束。
an internee = 被拘留者
④ The internment of civilians without trial is now against international law.
= 未经审判拘留平民如今违反国际法。
internment = 拘留
⑤ He did a six-month internship with a software company after graduating.
= 毕业后，他在一家软件公司实习了半年。
an internship = 实习期

internal
核心：内部的；体内的；国内的。几支：在一个东西、组织里面的（internal walls、an internal inquiry）；
身体内部的（internal injuries）；国家内部的（internal affairs）；心里的（internal conflict）。
副词 internally、动词 internalize（使内化，英式也拼 -ise）与名词 internalization 并入本条。
internal market（内部市场）、internal medicine（内科学）、internal rhyme（行内韵）、
internal-combustion engine（内燃机）也并在这里。
① The police have launched an internal inquiry into the missing money.
= 警方已就失踪的钱展开内部调查。
an internal inquiry = 内部调查
② The driver suffered internal injuries and was rushed to hospital.
= 司机受了内伤，被紧急送往医院。
internal injuries = 内伤
③ The government warned other countries not to interfere in its internal affairs.
= 政府警告别国不要干涉其内政。
internal affairs = 内政
④ The medicine is for external use only and must not be taken internally.
= 此药仅限外用，切勿内服。
internally = 内部地；内服地
⑤ Children internalize their parents' values long before they can explain them.
= 孩子们早在能说出道理之前，就已经把父母的价值观内化了。
internalize something = 使…内化
⑥ The internalization of rules is a key part of growing up.
= 把规则内化是成长的关键部分。
the internalization of something = …的内化
⑦ The European Union created an internal market with no barriers to trade.
= 欧盟建立了一个没有贸易壁垒的内部市场。
an internal market = 内部市场
⑧ She trained as a doctor of internal medicine.
= 她受训成为一名内科医生。
internal medicine = 内科学
⑨ Children's songs often use internal rhyme to make each line easy to remember.
= 儿歌常用行内韵，让每一行都好记。
internal rhyme = 行内韵
⑩ The internal-combustion engine changed the way people travelled.
= 内燃机改变了人们的出行方式。
an internal-combustion engine = 内燃机

international
核心：国际的、国与国之间的 —— 涉及两个或更多国家的。也作名词（英）：国际比赛；参加国际比赛的运动员。
副词 internationally、名词 internationalism（国际主义）、internationalist（国际主义者）、
动词 internationalize（使国际化，英式也拼 -ise）与名词 internationalization 并入本条。
① The city hosts an international film festival every September.
= 那座城市每年九月举办国际电影节。
an international festival = 国际节日
② The two countries signed an international agreement on fishing rights.
= 两国签署了一项关于捕鱼权的国际协议。
an international agreement = 国际协议
③ He has played in more than fifty internationals for Wales.
= 他代表威尔士参加过五十多场国际比赛。
an international = （英）国际比赛
④ Her paintings are now internationally famous.
= 她的画如今已国际知名。
internationally famous = 国际知名的
⑤ After the war, internationalism grew as nations tried to prevent another conflict.
= 战后，国际主义思潮兴起，各国努力防止再次发生冲突。
internationalism = 国际主义
⑥ As a lifelong internationalist, she believed in cooperation between all countries.
= 她是个终生的国际主义者，信奉各国之间的合作。
an internationalist = 国际主义者
⑦ The university wants to internationalize its courses and attract more foreign students.
= 那所大学想让课程国际化，吸引更多外国学生。
internationalize something = 使…国际化
⑧ The internationalization of the company began with an office in Tokyo.
= 公司的国际化始于在东京开设的一个办事处。
the internationalization of something = …的国际化

internecine
核心：（正式）内部争斗的、自相残杀的 —— 多说同一组织、政党、家族内部的激烈冲突。
① Years of internecine warfare had destroyed the old kingdom.
= 多年的自相残杀摧毁了那个古老的王国。
internecine warfare = 自相残杀的战争
② The party is weakened by internecine struggles between its leaders.
= 那个党因领导人之间的内斗而元气大伤。
internecine struggles = 内斗

internet
核心：互联网、因特网 —— 前加 the；常大写为 the Internet。也作定语：internet access、internet café。
internet dating（网上约会）与 internet service provider（互联网服务提供商，缩写「ISP」）并入本条。
① I found an old family recipe for apple cake on the internet last night.
= 昨晚我在网上找到了一个苹果蛋糕的老家传食谱。
on the internet = 在网上
② Most hotels now offer free internet access to their guests.
= 如今大多数酒店都为客人提供免费上网。
internet access = 上网
③ They met through internet dating and married a year later.
= 他们是通过网上约会认识的，一年后结了婚。
internet dating = 网上约会
④ Call your internet service provider if the connection keeps failing.
= 如果网络总是断，就给你的互联网服务提供商打电话。
an internet service provider = 互联网服务提供商

internist
核心：（美）内科医生 —— 专治成人内科疾病的医生，不做手术。
① Her internist sent her to a heart specialist after the tests.
= 检查之后，她的内科医生让她去看心脏专科医生。
an internist = 内科医生
② In the United States, adults with a long-term illness often see an internist rather than a family doctor.
= 在美国，患慢性病的成年人常去看内科医生，而不是家庭医生。
see an internist = 去看内科医生
跟 intern（实习医生）不同，internist 是正式执业的内科医生。

interoffice
核心：（美）公司内部各部门之间的 —— 多说内部邮件、备忘录。
① The memo was sent by interoffice mail to every department.
= 那份备忘录通过内部邮件发给了每个部门。
interoffice mail = 内部邮件
② An interoffice memo reminded staff about the new parking rules.
= 一份内部备忘录提醒员工注意新的停车规定。
an interoffice memo = 内部备忘录

interoperable
核心：（计算机）可互操作的、可互通的 —— 不同系统、设备能一起工作。名词 interoperability 并入本条。
① The new software is interoperable with most older systems.
= 新软件能与大多数旧系统互通。
be interoperable with something = 能与…互通
② Poor interoperability between hospital computers slows down patient care.
= 医院电脑之间互通性差，拖慢了对病人的照护。
interoperability = 互操作性

interpenetrate
核心：（正式）相互渗透、相互贯穿 —— 两种东西彼此渗入对方。名词 interpenetration 并入本条。
① In her paintings, light and shadow seem to interpenetrate.
= 在她的画里，光与影似乎相互渗透。
interpenetrate = 相互渗透
② The book explores the interpenetration of religion and politics in medieval Europe.
= 那本书探讨了中世纪欧洲宗教与政治的相互交融。
the interpenetration of A and B = A与B的相互渗透

interpersonal
核心：人际的、人与人之间的 —— 多说沟通技巧、关系。
① Working on a busy hospital ward requires good interpersonal skills and a lot of patience.
= 在繁忙的医院病房工作，需要良好的人际交往能力和极大的耐心。
interpersonal skills = 人际交往能力
② Many interpersonal problems in small offices come from poor communication between managers and staff.
= 小办公室里的许多人际问题，都源于管理者和员工之间沟通不畅。
interpersonal problems = 人际问题

interplanetary
核心：行星之间的 —— 说太空旅行、空间。
① The spacecraft is designed for interplanetary travel.
= 那艘宇宙飞船是为行星际旅行设计的。
interplanetary travel = 行星际旅行
② Interplanetary space is not completely empty but contains dust and gas.
= 行星际空间并非完全空无一物，而是有尘埃和气体。
interplanetary space = 行星际空间

interplay
核心：相互作用、相互影响 —— 两种或更多因素彼此作用。
① The film explores the interplay between love and money.
= 那部电影探讨了爱情与金钱之间的相互作用。
the interplay between A and B = A与B之间的相互作用
② The painting is famous for its interplay of light and colour.
= 那幅画以光与色的交相辉映而闻名。
the interplay of something = …的相互作用

Interpol
核心：国际刑警组织 —— 协调各国警方打击跨国犯罪的国际组织。
① Interpol issued a warrant for his arrest after he fled the country.
= 他逃出国境后，国际刑警组织发出了逮捕令。
Interpol = 国际刑警组织
② Police worked with Interpol to trace the stolen paintings.
= 警方与国际刑警组织合作追查被盗的画作。
work with Interpol = 与国际刑警组织合作

interpolate
核心：（正式）插入、插话；（数学）插值。两支：在文字或讲话中加入额外内容；
根据已知数值推算中间的值。名词 interpolation 并入本条。
① Someone had interpolated several lines into the original poem.
= 有人在原诗里窜入了几行。
interpolate something into something = 把…插入…
② The missing temperatures were interpolated from the readings on either side.
= 缺失的气温是根据前后的读数插值推算出来的。
interpolate values = 插值推算
③ Scholars believe the last chapter is a later interpolation.
= 学者们认为最后一章是后人添加的。
an interpolation = 窜入、添加的内容

interpose
核心：（正式）插入、介于…之间；插话、干预。两支：把自己或某物放到两者之间（interpose yourself between）；
在谈话中插入一句。
① The teacher interposed herself between the two boys before the fight could start.
= 老师在两个男孩之间一站，架还没打起来就被拦住了。
interpose yourself between A and B = 挡在A与B之间
② "But that's not what you said yesterday," she interposed.
= 「可你昨天不是这么说的。」她插话道。
interpose = 插话

interpret
核心：解释、阐释；理解为；口译；演绎。几支：说明意思（interpret a dream / the data）；
把某事理解成某种意思（interpret something as）；把一种语言当场译成另一种；表演者对作品的演绎。
形容词 interpretable 并入本条；interpretation、interpreter 另立。
① As a statistician, how would you interpret the results of the survey on housing?
= 作为统计学家，你会怎么解读这项住房调查的结果？
interpret results = 解读结果
② At the trial, his long silence was interpreted by the jury as a sign of guilt.
= 审判时，他长时间的沉默被陪审团理解为有罪的迹象。
interpret something as something = 把…理解为…
③ She interpreted for the minister during his visit to Beijing.
= 部长访问北京期间，她为他担任口译。
interpret for somebody = 为某人口译
④ The actor interpreted the role of the king in a completely new way.
= 那位演员以全新的方式演绎了国王这个角色。
interpret a role = 演绎角色
⑤ The old map is so faded that it is barely interpretable.
= 那张旧地图褪色得厉害，几乎看不明白。
interpretable = 可以理解的

interpretation
核心：解释、理解；演绎。可数：对同一件事的一种理解（a different interpretation）；
表演者对作品的一种处理方式。不可数：解释工作；口译。
① The law is open to interpretation, and lawyers disagree about its meaning.
= 那条法律有多种解释的余地，律师们对它的意思看法不一。
open to interpretation = 有多种解释的余地
② She gave a moving interpretation of the famous sonata.
= 她对那首著名奏鸣曲的演绎感人至深。
an interpretation of something = 对…的演绎
③ Simultaneous interpretation is provided at the conference in five languages.
= 会议提供五种语言的同声传译。
simultaneous interpretation = 同声传译

interpretative
核心：解释性的、说明性的 —— 也作 interpretive（多美式），多说博物馆展板、说明材料。
① The castle has interpretative panels explaining its history.
= 城堡里有介绍其历史的说明展板。
interpretative panels = 说明展板
② The critic takes an interpretative approach to the poems rather than a historical one.
= 那位评论家对这些诗采取阐释的方法，而不是历史考证的方法。
an interpretative approach = 阐释性的方法

interpreter
核心：口译员；解释者、演绎者；（计算机）解释程序。本义是当场翻译口语的人；
也指对作品、法律作出阐释的人。
① She spoke through an interpreter because she knew no English.
= 她不懂英语，是通过口译员说话的。
through an interpreter = 通过口译员
② He is one of the greatest interpreters of Beethoven's music.
= 他是贝多芬音乐最伟大的演绎者之一。
an interpreter of something = …的演绎者

interracial
核心：不同种族之间的 —— 多说婚姻、关系。
① Interracial marriage was illegal in some American states until 1967.
= 在美国一些州，跨种族婚姻直到一九六七年都是违法的。
interracial marriage = 跨种族婚姻
② The film tells the story of an interracial couple in the 1950s.
= 那部电影讲的是二十世纪五十年代一对跨种族夫妇的故事。
an interracial couple = 跨种族夫妇
