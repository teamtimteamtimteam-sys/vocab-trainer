Olympian
/əˈlɪmpiən/
核心：两支，首字母大写。一是名词，奥运选手，可数；二是形容词，像神一样超然的、高高在上的：出自希腊众神居住的奥林匹斯山；
也指极大的、非凡的：an Olympian effort。
① The school was opened by an Olympian who had learned to swim in its pool.
= 给学校剪彩的是一位奥运选手，她就是在这所学校的泳池里学会游泳的。
an Olympian = 奥运选手
② The professor watched the argument with Olympian detachment.
= 那位教授带着一种居高临下的超然，旁观那场争论。
Olympian detachment = 居高临下的超然
③ It took an Olympian effort to clear the hall before midnight.
= 费了九牛二虎之力，才在午夜前把大厅清理干净。
an Olympian effort = 九牛二虎之力

Olympic
/əˈlɪmpɪk/
核心：奥运会的、奥林匹克的。形容词，只作定语，首字母大写。
the Olympic Games 是奥运会，也说 the Olympics，动词用复数；the Winter Olympics 是冬奥会。
① She won an Olympic gold medal in the high jump at the age of nineteen.
= 她十九岁拿下了跳高的奥运金牌。
an Olympic gold medal = 奥运金牌
② The Olympic Games are held every four years in a different city.
= 奥运会每四年在不同的城市举行一次。
the Olympic Games = 奥运会
③ He hopes to swim for his country at the next Olympics.
= 他希望在下一届奥运会上代表国家参加游泳比赛。
the Olympics = 奥运会
④ The Olympic flame was carried through the town by a local teacher.
= 奥运圣火由当地的一位教师传递着穿过了镇子。
the Olympic flame = 奥运圣火

Omani
/əʊˈmɑːni/
核心：两支，首字母大写。一是形容词，阿曼的；二是名词，阿曼人，可数。
① The Omani coast has been a centre of the frankincense trade for thousands of years.
= 阿曼的海岸几千年来一直是乳香贸易的中心。
the Omani coast = 阿曼海岸
② Many Omanis work in the oil industry or for the government.
= 许多阿曼人在石油业或政府部门工作。
an Omani = 阿曼人

ombudsman
/ˈɒmbʊdzmən/
核心：申诉专员、监察专员：受理并调查公众对政府部门、银行、公用事业等机构投诉的独立官员。可数，来自瑞典语，复数 ombudsmen。
① She complained to the ombudsman when the bank refused to refund the charge.
= 银行拒绝退还那笔费用，她就向申诉专员投诉。
complain to the ombudsman = 向申诉专员投诉
英国许多行业各设专门的申诉专员，如银行业、能源业。
② The ombudsman found that the council had lost the file and ordered it to pay compensation.
= 申诉专员认定市政会弄丢了档案，责令其赔偿。
the ombudsman = 申诉专员

omega
/ˈəʊmɪɡə/
核心：希腊字母表的最后一个字母，大写 Ω，小写 ω。可数。因为排在最末，引申指终结：the alpha and omega 是始与终、全部要义。
① The symbol for the ohm is the capital letter omega.
= 欧姆的符号是大写的希腊字母 Ω。
the letter omega = 希腊字母 Ω
② For that coach, fitness is the alpha and omega of the game.
= 在那位教练看来，体能是这项运动的全部要义。
the alpha and omega = 始与终、全部要义
③ Oily fish such as sardines are a good source of Omega-3.
= 沙丁鱼这类油性鱼是欧米伽 3 脂肪酸的好来源。
Omega-3 = 欧米伽 3 脂肪酸
Omega-3 多见于食品、营养话题，也常写作小写开头。

omelette
/ˈɒmlət/
核心：煎蛋卷、奄列：把蛋液搅匀后煎成的一张饼，常包上奶酪、火腿、蘑菇再对折。可数，美式拼作 omelet。
谚语 you cannot make an omelette without breaking eggs 是「不打破鸡蛋做不成蛋卷」：要成事总得付出点代价。
① He made a cheese omelette and ate it straight from the pan.
= 他做了个奶酪煎蛋卷，直接就着锅吃了。
a cheese omelette = 奶酪煎蛋卷
② A plain omelette takes three eggs and about two minutes.
= 一个原味煎蛋卷要三个鸡蛋，大约两分钟。
a plain omelette = 原味煎蛋卷
③ The minister defended the job cuts by saying that you cannot make an omelette without breaking eggs.
= 那位大臣为裁员辩护，说不打破鸡蛋做不成蛋卷。
you cannot make an omelette without breaking eggs = 要成事总得付出代价

omen
/ˈəʊmən/
核心：预兆、征兆：被看作预示将来吉凶的事，介词用 of、for。可数。常搭 a good omen、a bad omen、an ill omen。
① The sailors took the calm sea as a good omen for the voyage.
= 水手们把风平浪静看作这趟航行的吉兆。
a good omen = 吉兆
这个词本身不分吉凶，所以常加形容词说明好坏。
② A broken mirror is thought by some people to be an omen of bad luck.
= 有些人认为，镜子打碎是倒霉的预兆。
an omen of something = 某事的预兆
③ The early frost was a bad omen for the grape harvest.
= 早来的霜对葡萄的收成是个凶兆。
a bad omen for something = 对某事的凶兆

omicron
/əʊˈmaɪkrɒn/
核心：希腊字母表的第十五个字母，大写 Ο，小写 ο，相当于短音的 o。可数。二〇二一年起也因新冠病毒的一个变异株以它命名而广为人知。
① The letter omicron looks exactly like a Roman O.
= 这个希腊字母看上去跟拉丁字母 O 一模一样。
the letter omicron = 希腊字母 ο
② The Omicron variant spread faster than any earlier form of the virus.
= 奥密克戎变异株比那种病毒此前的任何一种形态都传播得快。
the Omicron variant = 奥密克戎变异株

ominous
/ˈɒmɪnəs/
核心：不祥的、预示要出事的：让人觉得有坏事要来。形容词。常搭 an ominous silence、ominous clouds。
副词 ominously 并入本条。
① There was an ominous silence at the other end of the line.
= 电话那头是一阵不祥的沉默。
an ominous silence = 不祥的沉默
多用来描写气氛、声音、天色，常见于叙述文字。
② Ominous black clouds were gathering over the hills to the west.
= 西边的山丘上空，不祥的乌云正在聚集。
ominous clouds = 不祥的乌云
③ The engine coughed ominously and then stopped altogether.
= 发动机不祥地咳了几声，然后彻底熄了火。
ominously = 不祥地

omit
/əˈmɪt/
核心：两支，正式。一是省略、删去、漏掉：介词用 from，可以是故意，也可以是疏忽；二是 omit to do something，没有做、忘了做。
过去式 omitted，t 要双写。名词 omission 指省略、遗漏，也指漏掉的东西，并入本条。
① Her name was omitted from the list of prize winners by mistake.
= 她的名字被误从获奖名单上漏掉了。
omit = 省略
be omitted from something = 被从某处漏掉
② You may omit the second paragraph if the letter is too long.
= 信要是太长，第二段可以删去。
omit something = 删去某物
③ He omitted to mention that the car had been in an accident.
= 他没提那辆车出过事故。
omit to do something = 没有做某事
④ The omission of the date makes the document useless in court.
= 文件上漏了日期，拿到法庭上就没用了。
omission = 遗漏
the omission of something = 漏掉某物
⑤ There are several serious omissions in the report.
= 那份报告有好几处严重的遗漏。
an omission = 一处遗漏

omnibus
/ˈɒmnɪbəs/
核心：三支，可数。一是（英式）把一周里播过的几集连起来重播的节目：the omnibus edition；

= 他买了一本收四部侦探小说的合订本，留着假期看。
an omnibus = 合订本
③ In the old photograph a horse-drawn omnibus is crossing the bridge.
= 在那张旧照片上，一辆马拉的公共马车正在过桥。
a horse-drawn omnibus = 公共马车
④ The changes were hidden in an omnibus bill of four hundred pages.
= 那些改动藏在一份四百页的综合法案里。
an omnibus bill = 综合法案

omnipotent
/ɒmˈnɪpətənt/
核心：全能的、无所不能的、权力无限的。形容词，正式，多说神、君主、国家。名词 omnipotence 并入本条。
① The poem asks why an omnipotent God allows the innocent to suffer.

omnipotence = 全能、无上权力

omnipresent
/ˌɒmnɪˈprezənt/

omnipresent cameras = 无处不在的摄像头

omniscient
/ɒmˈnɪsiənt/

omniscience = 全知

omnivore

on
/ɒn/
核心：介词兼副词，底子是「接触着表面、附着在上面」，是 off 的反义。
介词的主要用法：一是在…上面（接触着）：on the table、on the wall、on the ceiling；
二是在某一天、某个日期：on Monday、on 5 May、on my birthday；三是乘坐（公共交通工具）：on the bus、on a plane；
四是通过某种媒介：on television、on the phone、on the internet；五是关于：a book on birds；
六是处于某种状态、正在进行：on fire、on holiday、on sale、on duty、on strike；七是靠…为生、以…为食：live on rice；
八是属于（委员会、团队）：on the committee；九是一…就：on arrival、on hearing the news；
十是身上带着：have no money on me；十一是由…付钱（口语）：the drinks are on me；十二是方位：on the left。
副词的主要用法：一是穿着、戴着：put your coat on；二是继续下去：go on、carry on；三是开着、通着：the lights are on；
四是在上演、在进行：What's on?；五是往前、往后（时间）：from now on、later on。
固定说法：on and on 是没完没了；on and off 是断断续续；be on about 是（英式口语）唠叨的是；it is not on 是（英式口语）这可不行。
① She left the keys on the kitchen table beside the fruit bowl.
= 她把钥匙留在厨房桌上，果盘旁边。
on the table = 在桌上
② There is a spider on the ceiling above your head.
= 你头顶的天花板上有只蜘蛛。
on the ceiling = 在天花板上
只要接触着表面就用它，不一定在上方。
③ The shop is closed on Sundays and on the first of January.
= 那家店星期天和元旦不开门。
on Sundays = 每逢星期天
④ I read most of the book on the train to Manchester.
= 那本书我大半是在去曼彻斯特的火车上读的。
on the train = 在火车上
⑤ I heard the news on the radio while I was driving home.
= 我是开车回家的时候从收音机里听到那条消息的。
on the radio = 在收音机里
on the phone = 在电话里
⑥ She has written three books on the history of the railway.
= 她写过三本讲铁路史的书。
a book on something = 讲某事的书
⑦ The kitchen was on fire by the time the neighbours noticed the smoke.
= 等邻居看见烟的时候，厨房已经着火了。
on fire = 着火
⑧ The whole family is on holiday in Wales until the end of the month.
= 全家人在威尔士度假，月底才回来。
on holiday = 在度假
on duty = 在值班
⑨ The drivers have been on strike for a week over pay.
= 司机们为了工资已经罢工一个星期。
on strike = 在罢工
⑩ The islanders live mainly on fish, rice and coconuts.
= 岛民主要靠鱼、米饭和椰子过活。
live on something = 靠某物过活
⑪ She has served on the committee for eleven years.
= 她在那个委员会里干了十一年。
on the committee = 在委员会里
⑫ Visitors must sign the book on arrival at the front desk.
= 来访者一到前台就必须登记签名。
on arrival = 一到就
on doing something = 一做某事就
⑬ I have no change on me, so could you pay for the parking?
= 我身上没带零钱，你能付一下停车费吗？
have something on you = 身上带着某物
⑭ Put your wallet away, because lunch is on me today.
= 把钱包收起来，今天午饭我请。
be on somebody = 由某人付钱
⑮ The post office is on the left, just after the bridge.
= 邮局在左手边，刚过桥就是。
on the left = 在左边
⑯ Put your coat on, because it is freezing outside.
= 把大衣穿上，外面冷得要命。
put something on = 穿上某物
这里是副词。
⑰ He went on talking long after everyone had stopped listening.
= 大家早就不听了，他还在往下说。
go on doing something = 继续做某事
⑱ Somebody has left the lights on in the garage.
= 有人没关车库的灯。
leave the lights on = 没关灯
⑲ What is on at the cinema this weekend?
= 这个周末电影院上演什么？
What is on? = 上演什么？
⑳ From now on, the gate will be locked at eight every evening.
= 从现在起，大门每晚八点上锁。
from now on = 从现在起
㉑ We can talk about the money later on, when the children are in bed.
= 钱的事晚些时候再说，等孩子们上了床。
later on = 晚些时候
㉒ The speech went on and on until half the audience had left.
= 那篇讲话没完没了，一直讲到走了一半听众。
on and on = 没完没了
㉓ It has been raining on and off since Tuesday.
= 从星期二起，雨一直断断续续地下。
on and off = 断断续续
㉔ I have no idea what he is on about half the time.
= 他唠叨些什么，我多半听不明白。
be on about = 唠叨的是
㉕ You cannot leave the bill to her again, because it is just not on.
= 你不能又把账单留给她，这可不行。
it is not on = 这可不行
英式口语，说某种做法不像话。
㉖ They have had an on-off friendship for twenty years.
= 他们时好时坏地做了二十年朋友。
on-off = 时断时续的
㉗ The captain apologised for his on-field behaviour after the match.
= 赛后，队长为自己在场上的举止道了歉。
on-field = 赛场上的
㉘ The two stars have a real on-screen chemistry that carries the film.
= 那两位明星在银幕上很来电，撑起了整部片子。
on-screen = 银幕上的
㉙ There is free on-street parking after six in the evening.
= 晚上六点以后街边可以免费停车。
on-street = 街边的
㉚ Traffic was backed up for a mile at the on-ramp to the bridge.
= 上桥的入口匝道那儿，车堵了一英里。
an on-ramp = 入口匝道
㉛ The shop sells cheap copies of whatever is on-trend this month.
= 这个月流行什么，那家店就卖什么的廉价仿品。
on-trend = 合潮流的

onanism
/ˈəʊnənɪzəm/

onanism = 手淫
② The pamphlet of 1760 warned young men against the dangers of onanism.
= 一七六〇年的那本小册子警告年轻人当心手淫的危害。
the dangers of onanism = 手淫的危害

onboard
/ˈɒnbɔːd/
核心：形容词，装在车、船、飞机上的、随机随车的，只作定语：an onboard computer；作副词时多分开写成 on board。
动词是（商业用语）让新员工、新客户入职上手，名词 onboarding 并入本条。
① The car's onboard computer warns the driver when a tyre is losing pressure.
= 那辆车的车载电脑会在轮胎漏气时提醒司机。
an onboard computer = 车载电脑
② The ferry has an onboard shop that opens once the ship leaves the harbour.
= 那艘渡轮上有个小卖部，船一离港就开门。
an onboard shop = 船上的小卖部
③ The firm takes two weeks to onboard each new member of staff.
= 那家公司让每位新员工入职上手要花两个星期。
onboard somebody = 让某人入职上手
④ Her onboarding consisted of a password and a map of the building.
= 她的入职培训就是一个密码和一张大楼平面图。
onboarding = 入职培训

once
/wʌns/
核心：副词两支。一是一次：once a week、once or twice；二是曾经、从前：she once lived here。
连词：一旦、一…就：Once you have signed, you cannot change your mind。
名词：the once、this once 指「这一回」：just this once、for once。
固定说法很多：at once 一是立刻，二是同时；all at once 一是突然，二是一下子全都；
once again 与 once more 是再一次；once in a while 是偶尔；once in a blue moon 是千载难逢；
once and for all 是一劳永逸地；once upon a time 是（童话开头）从前；
谚语 once bitten, twice shy 是一朝被蛇咬，十年怕井绳。
① The window cleaner comes once a month and leaves the bill in the letterbox.
= 擦窗工每月来一次，把账单留在信箱里。
once a month = 每月一次
once a week = 每周一次
② I have only met her once, at a wedding in the summer.
= 我只见过她一次，是在夏天的一场婚礼上。
once = 一次
③ She has been late once or twice, but never without ringing.
= 她迟到过一两回，不过从没不打招呼。
once or twice = 一两回
④ This quiet street was once the busiest market in the city.
= 这条僻静的街，曾经是城里最热闹的集市。
once = 曾经
⑤ Once you have tried fresh pasta, you will never buy the dried kind again.
= 你一旦尝过新鲜面条，就再也不会买干面了。
once = 一旦
这里是连词。
⑥ Come here at once, and bring the first-aid box with you.
= 马上过来，把急救箱带上。
at once = 立刻
⑦ Do not all talk at once, or I shall hear nobody.
= 别大家同时说，要不然我谁的话也听不清。
at once = 同时
⑧ All at once the lights went out and the music stopped.
= 突然，灯灭了，音乐也停了。
all at once = 突然
⑨ The train was late once again, for the fourth time that week.
= 火车又晚点了，那个星期已是第四回。
once again = 再一次
⑩ Let us go through the plan once more before the others arrive.
= 趁别人还没到，咱们把计划再过一遍。
once more = 再一次
⑪ We still go to the cinema once in a while, when a good film comes.
= 有好片子的时候，我们偶尔还去电影院。
once in a while = 偶尔
⑫ He rings his mother once in a blue moon and wonders why she is cross.
= 他难得给母亲打一回电话，还纳闷她为什么不高兴。
once in a blue moon = 千载难逢、极少
⑬ The court's ruling settled the question once and for all.
= 法院的裁决一劳永逸地了结了那个问题。
once and for all = 一劳永逸地
⑭ Once upon a time there was a miller who had three sons.
= 从前，有个磨坊主，他有三个儿子。
once upon a time = 从前
⑮ You may stay up late just this once, because it is your birthday.
= 就这一回，你可以晚睡，因为今天是你的生日。
just this once = 就这一回
⑯ For once, the bus arrived early and I had time for a coffee.
= 公共汽车破天荒地早到了，我还有时间喝杯咖啡。
for once = 破天荒地、难得一回
⑰ He lost money on shares in his twenties and has kept it in the bank ever since: once bitten, twice shy.
= 他二十多岁炒股亏了钱，从此把钱一直存在银行里：一朝被蛇咬，十年怕井绳。
once bitten, twice shy = 一朝被蛇咬，十年怕井绳
⑱ The mechanic gave the engine a quick once-over and found nothing wrong.
= 修车工把发动机草草查了一遍，没发现毛病。
a once-over = 粗略的检查

oncology
/ɒŋˈkɒlədʒi/
核心：肿瘤学、肿瘤科：研究和治疗肿瘤、癌症的医学分支。不可数。肿瘤科医生叫 oncologist，并入本条。
① She was referred to the oncology department after the scan.
= 扫描之后，她被转到了肿瘤科。
the oncology department = 肿瘤科
② The oncologist explained the choice between surgery and six weeks of treatment.
= 肿瘤科医生讲解了手术与六周治疗这两种选择。
an oncologist = 肿瘤科医生

oncoming
/ˈɒnkʌmɪŋ/
核心：迎面而来的、即将来临的。形容词，只作定语：oncoming traffic 是对面来的车。
① He swerved to avoid a dog and almost hit an oncoming lorry.
= 他为了躲一条狗猛打方向盘，差点撞上迎面开来的卡车。
an oncoming lorry = 迎面开来的卡车
说车辆时最常见，多见于交通事故报道。
② Dip your headlights for oncoming traffic on country roads.
= 在乡间公路上，遇到对面来车要把大灯调成近光。
oncoming traffic = 对面来的车
③ The squirrels were busy storing nuts for the oncoming winter.
= 松鼠们忙着为即将到来的冬天储存坚果。
the oncoming winter = 即将到来的冬天

one
/wʌn/
核心：数词、限定词、代词都用。读音跟 won 相同。
数词：一；也指一点钟、一岁。作限定词时比 a 更强调「只一个」：one thing is certain。
跟时间词连用指不确定的某一个：one day（将来或过去的某一天）、one morning。
代词用法：一是替代前面提过的可数名词，免得重复：the red one、which one、the ones on the left；
二是 one of 加复数名词：one of my friends；三是（正式）泛指任何人、包括说话人自己：One should always check，
所有格 one's，反身形式 oneself；口语里多用 you。
成对的说法：one … the other、one … another；one after another 与 one by one 是一个接一个。
习语：one or two 是一两个、几个；for one thing 是首先、一来；the one and only 是独一无二的；
one and the same 是同一个；at one with 是与…一致、融为一体；one up on 是比…略胜一筹；
be a one for 是（英式口语）特别喜欢。
① There is only one biscuit left in the tin.
= 罐子里只剩一块饼干了。
one biscuit = 一块饼干
② The meeting starts at one, so we have time for a sandwich.
= 会一点开，我们来得及吃个三明治。
at one = 在一点钟
③ One thing is certain: the bridge will be closed all summer.
= 有一点是肯定的：那座桥整个夏天都要封闭。
one thing is certain = 有一点是肯定的
④ One day I shall learn to sail, but this is not the year for it.
= 总有一天我要学帆船，不过今年还不是时候。
one day = 总有一天、有一天
⑤ One morning in March a letter arrived that changed everything.
= 三月里的一个早晨，来了一封信，一切都变了。
one morning = 一天早晨
⑥ I prefer the blue shirt to the red one.
= 比起那件红衬衫，我更喜欢蓝的。
the red one = 红的那个
替代前面提过的名词，免得重复。
⑦ "Which seats are ours?" "The ones by the window."
= 「哪些座位是我们的？」「靠窗的那几个。」
the ones = 那几个
which one = 哪一个
⑧ One of my colleagues has just had twins.
= 我有个同事刚生了双胞胎。
one of somebody = 某些人里的一个
⑨ One should always read a contract before signing it.
= 签合同之前，人总该先读一遍。
One should… = 人应该…
很正式；口语里说 you。
⑩ One must look after one's health, as my grandmother used to say.
= 我奶奶常说，人得照顾好自己的身体。
one's = 自己的
⑪ She has two brothers: one is a farmer and the other teaches music.
= 她有两个哥哥，一个务农，另一个教音乐。
one … the other = 一个…另一个
三个以上里说「一个…另一个」用 one … another；the other 只用于两个中的另一个。
⑫ The neighbours help one another with the harvest every autumn.
= 每年秋天，邻居们互相帮着收庄稼。
one another = 互相
⑬ The guests arrived one after another until the hall was full.
= 客人一个接一个地到，直到大厅坐满。
one after another = 一个接一个
⑭ The children came up one by one to collect their prizes.
= 孩子们一个一个走上来领奖。
one by one = 一个一个地
one by one 强调逐个、依次；one after another 强调接连不断。
⑮ I have one or two questions before we sign.
= 签字之前，我有几个问题。
one or two = 一两个、几个
⑯ I cannot come: for one thing, I have no way of getting there.
= 我去不了：首先，我没法到那儿。
for one thing = 首先、一来
⑰ Please welcome the one and only Ella Marsh.
= 让我们欢迎独一无二的埃拉·马什。
the one and only = 独一无二的
⑱ It turned out that the caller and the thief were one and the same person.
= 原来打电话的人和那个贼是同一个人。
one and the same = 同一个
⑲ On this point the minister is at one with her critics.
= 在这一点上，那位大臣跟批评她的人意见一致。
at one with = 与…一致
⑳ He always has to be one up on his brother, whether in cars or holidays.
= 不管是车还是度假，他总得比弟弟略胜一筹。
one up on = 比…略胜一筹
㉑ Their dinner parties are exercises in one-upmanship.
= 他们的晚宴是处处要压人一头的比拼。
one-upmanship = 处处占上风的做派
㉒ She is a great one for lists and writes three before breakfast.
= 她特别爱列清单，早饭前就写三张。
be a one for something = 特别喜欢某事
㉓ The concert is a one-off and will not be repeated.
= 那场音乐会只此一场，不会再演。
a one-off = 只此一次的事
㉔ Each pupil has a one-to-one lesson with the teacher every week.
= 每个学生每周跟老师上一节一对一的课。
one-to-one = 一对一的
one-on-one = 一对一的
㉕ The match was completely one-sided and finished seven nil.
= 那场比赛完全一边倒，以七比零收场。
one-sided = 一边倒的、片面的
㉖ The street is one-way, so you will have to drive round the block.
= 那条街是单行道，你得绕着街区开一圈。
one-way = 单行的、单程的
㉗ The detectives watched the interview through a one-way mirror.
= 探员们透过单面镜观看问讯。
a one-way mirror = 单面镜
㉘ The hall is run by a one-time boxer who now teaches dancing.
= 管那座礼堂的是个当年的拳击手，如今教跳舞。
one-time = 从前的
㉙ The council has opened a one-stop shop for advice on housing and benefits.
= 市政会开了一个一站式的窗口，提供住房和补助方面的咨询。
one-stop = 一站式的
㉚ She packed a one-piece swimsuit and a pair of goggles.
= 她带了一件连体泳衣和一副泳镜。
one-piece = 连体的
㉛ The villain is one-dimensional and has no reason for anything he does.
= 那个反派扁平单薄，做什么都没有缘由。
one-dimensional = 扁平单薄的
㉜ We stayed in a one-star hotel with a shared bathroom.
= 我们住的是一家一星级旅馆，浴室共用。
one-star = 一星级的
㉝ The grant is a one-shot payment and will not be renewed.
= 那笔拨款只发一次，不会续发。
one-shot = 只此一次的
㉞ The team's quick one-touch passing wore the defenders down.
= 那支球队快速的一脚出球把对方后卫拖垮了。
one-touch = 一脚出球的、一键的
㉟ He runs the shop as a one-man band and has not had a holiday in years.
= 那家店他一个人撑着，好多年没休过假了。
a one-man band = 单枪匹马干的人
one-man = 一个人的
㊱ She tours with a one-woman show about her years as a nurse.
= 她带着一出讲自己护士生涯的独角戏四处巡演。
one-woman = 一个女人撑起来的
㊲ The comedian is famous for one-liners that take a second to sink in.
= 那位喜剧演员以要愣一下才听懂的一句话俏皮话出名。
a one-liner = 一句话的俏皮话
㊳ The band played a one-night stand in every town along the coast.
= 那支乐队在沿海的每座城镇各演一场。
a one-night stand = 只演一场的演出、一夜情
㊴ The scheme offers free school meals to children from one-parent families.
= 那项计划给单亲家庭的孩子提供免费校餐。
a one-parent family = 单亲家庭
㊵ He has a one-track mind and can talk about nothing but fishing.
= 他一根筋，除了钓鱼什么都不会聊。
a one-track mind = 一根筋
㊶ A one-size-fits-all policy will not work for schools as different as these.
= 对差别这么大的学校，一刀切的政策行不通。
one-size-fits-all = 一刀切的
㊷ Critics called the striker a one-trick pony who could only shoot from distance.
= 评论说那名前锋只会一招，就是远射。
a one-trick pony = 只会一招的人
㊸ One-day cricket brought new crowds to a game that used to last five days.
= 一日制板球给这项从前要打五天的运动带来了新观众。
one-day cricket = 一日制板球

oneness
/ˈwʌnnəs/
核心：一体、合一、融为一体的感觉：介词用 with。不可数，书面。
① Walking alone on the moor gave her a sense of oneness with the land.
= 独自走在荒原上，她有一种与大地融为一体的感觉。
a sense of oneness with something = 与某物融为一体的感觉
② The preacher spoke of the oneness of all people before God.
= 那位牧师讲到，在上帝面前人人同为一体。
the oneness of something = 某事物的一体
