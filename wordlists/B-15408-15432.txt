noon
/nuːn/
核心：正午、中午十二点。不可数，不加冠词。介词用 at、by、before、around：at noon。
high noon 一是正午时分，二是（比喻）决定性的时刻、对决。
习语 morning, noon and night 是从早到晚、没日没夜。
旧式、文学的说法 noonday（多作定语：the noonday sun）与 noontide 并入本条。
① The ferry leaves at noon and reaches the island in time for a late lunch.
= 渡船正午开，到岛上正好赶上一顿迟午饭。
at noon = 在正午
② The work must be finished by noon on Friday.
= 那项工作必须在星期五中午之前完成。
by noon = 中午之前
③ The talks have reached high noon, and one side must give way.
= 谈判到了决定性的时刻，总得有一方让步。
high noon = 决定性的时刻
④ She thinks about that boy morning, noon and night.
= 她从早到晚想着那个男孩。
morning, noon and night = 从早到晚
⑤ The walkers rested under a tree to escape the noonday sun.
= 徒步的人在树下歇脚，躲开正午的太阳。
the noonday sun = 正午的太阳
noonday = 正午（旧式）
⑥ By noontide the mist had lifted, and the whole valley lay in sunshine.
= 到了正午，雾散了，整条山谷沐浴在阳光里。
noontide = 正午（文学）

noose
/nuːs/
核心：两支，可数。一是绳套、套索：一头打了活结、越拉越紧的绳圈，尤指绞刑的绞索；二是（比喻）越收越紧的束缚、绝境。
① The cowboy dropped a noose over the calf's head at the first throw.
= 那个牛仔头一下就把套索甩到了小牛头上。
a noose = 套索
② The condemned man walked to the noose without a word.
= 那个死囚一言不发地走向绞索。
the noose = 绞索
③ The debt was a noose round the company's neck that tightened every month.
= 那笔债是套在公司脖子上的绞索，一个月比一个月紧。
a noose round somebody's neck = 套在某人脖子上的绞索

nope
/nəʊp/
核心：两支，口语。一是 no 的随意说法，只用于回答，语气干脆；二是（网络用语）表示坚决不干、看一眼就想躲。只见于对话与聊天。
① "Have you finished your homework?" "Nope. I have not even started."
= 「作业做完了吗？」「没。我还没开始呢。」
Nope. = 没、不。
② "Want to hold the spider?" "Nope, nope, nope," she said, backing out of the room.
= 「想托一下这只蜘蛛吗？」「不不不。」她说着退出了房间。
nope = 坚决不

nor
/nɔː(r)/
核心：连词。一是 neither … nor …，既不…也不…；二是接在否定句后面，引出又一个否定：「也不」，后面要倒装：
Nor do I、nor did she；三是（正式）用在句首，承接上文再添一条否定。
跟 or 的分别：前面有 neither 或另一句否定句打头时用它；一句之内的 no、never 后面通常接 or。
① He can neither read nor write, yet he runs a successful business.
= 他既不会读也不会写，却把生意做得很成功。
neither … nor … = 既不…也不…
② "I have never been to Wales." "Nor have I, though I live an hour away."
= 「我从没去过威尔士。」「我也没去过，尽管我住的地方离那儿才一个钟头。」
Nor have I. = 我也没有。
后面主语和助动词倒装。
③ She did not reply to the letter, nor did she return any of his calls.
= 她没有回那封信，也没有回他的任何一个电话。
nor did she = 她也没有
④ The house is far from cheap. Nor is it close to the station.
= 那座房子一点都不便宜，离车站也远。
Nor is it … = 它也不…
放在句首，偏正式。

a nor'easter is forecast = 预报有东北风暴

Nordic

Nordic skiing = 北欧滑雪
④ Every morning a group of pensioners goes Nordic walking round the lake.
= 每天早上，一群退休老人绕着湖持杖健走。
Nordic walking = 持杖健走
⑤ The channel shows Nordic noir on Saturday nights, with subtitles.
= 那个频道星期六晚上放带字幕的北欧黑色犯罪剧。
Nordic noir = 北欧黑色犯罪作品

norm
/nɔːm/
核心：三支，可数。一是 the norm，常态、通常的情形：be the norm 是很普遍；
二是 norms，复数，（社会的）行为规范、准则；三是（技术、教育）标准水平、定额。
① Working from home has become the norm in many offices.
= 在许多办公室，在家办公已成常态。
the norm = 常态
② Families with one child are now the norm rather than the exception.
= 独生子女家庭如今是常态，不是例外。
the norm rather than the exception = 是常态而非例外
③ Every society has norms about how close to stand to a stranger.
= 跟陌生人站多近，每个社会都有不成文的规矩。
social norms = 社会规范
④ The child's reading is well above the norm for her age.
= 那孩子的阅读水平远高于同龄人的标准。
above the norm = 高于标准水平

normal
/ˈnɔːməl/
核心：形容词两支。一是正常的、通常的、合乎常规的：it is normal to do something、perfectly normal、in the normal way；
二是（身心）正常的、健康的。
名词是常态、正常水平，不可数：back to normal、above normal、below normal；the new normal 是「新常态」。
副词 normally 一是通常、一般情况下，二是正常地。
名词 normality 是正常状态，美式也说 normalcy；动词 normalize 是使正常化、恢复正常，名词 normalization。
统计学上 normal distribution 是正态分布。并入本条。
① It is normal to feel tired for a few days after a long flight.
= 长途飞行之后累上几天是正常的。
normal = 正常的
it is normal to do something = 做某事是正常的
② Under normal circumstances the journey takes about forty minutes.
= 在正常情况下，那段路大约要四十分钟。
under normal circumstances = 在正常情况下
③ The shop is open during normal working hours and closed on Sundays.
= 那家店在正常上班时间营业，星期天不开。
normal working hours = 正常上班时间
④ The baby's weight is perfectly normal for her age.
= 那个宝宝的体重，在她这个月龄完全正常。
perfectly normal = 完全正常
⑤ Things are slowly getting back to normal after the flood.
= 洪水过后，一切正慢慢恢复正常。
back to normal = 恢复正常
⑥ Rainfall this month has been well above normal.
= 这个月的降雨量远高于正常水平。
above normal = 高于正常水平
below normal = 低于正常水平
⑦ Queues at the border are the new normal, and drivers pack sandwiches.
= 边境排长队成了新常态，司机们都自带三明治。
the new normal = 新常态
⑧ I normally cycle to work, but today I took the bus.
= 我通常骑车上班，不过今天坐了公共汽车。
normally = 通常
⑨ Her heart is beating normally, and she should go home tomorrow.
= 她的心跳正常，明天应该可以回家。
normally = 正常地
⑩ The town longs for a return to normality after a month of power cuts.
= 停了一个月的电，镇上的人盼着恢复常态。
a return to normality = 恢复常态
⑪ The two countries agreed to normalize relations and reopen their embassies.
= 两国同意实现关系正常化，重开使馆。
normalize relations = 实现关系正常化
⑫ The normalization of trade took a further three years.
= 贸易正常化又花了三年。
normalization = 正常化
⑬ The heights of adult men follow a normal distribution.
= 成年男子的身高呈正态分布。
a normal distribution = 正态分布

north
/nɔːθ/
核心：四个方位词之一，名词、形容词、副词三种用法，缩写 N。
名词：北、北方，通常带 the：in the north 是在北部（范围之内），to the north of 是在…以北（范围之外）；
the North 大写时指一国的北部地区，在英国指英格兰北部，在美国指北方各州；也指北半球的富国。
形容词：北部的、朝北的、（风）从北面来的：the north coast、a north wind；地名里大写：North America。
副词：向北、在北面：drive north、face north；up north 是（口语）在北方、到北方去。
true north 是真北，magnetic north 是磁北；the North Pole 是北极。
中间方位写作 north-east、north-west，再细分有 north-north-east、north-north-west；north-south 指南北向的、南北之间的。
north-country 作定语指英格兰北部的。并入本条。
① The wind is blowing from the north and bringing snow with it.
= 风从北边刮来，还带着雪。
from the north = 从北边
② They live in the north of Italy, close to the Swiss border.
= 他们住在意大利北部，靠近瑞士边境。
in the north of = 在…的北部
③ The lake lies ten miles to the north of the town.
= 那个湖在镇子以北十英里。
to the north of = 在…以北
在范围之外用 to，在范围之内用 in。
④ House prices in the North are far lower than in London.
= 英格兰北部的房价比伦敦低得多。
the North = 北部地区
⑤ We walked along the north coast for three days without seeing a town.
= 我们沿着北海岸走了三天，没见到一座城镇。
the north coast = 北海岸
⑥ A bitter north wind kept everyone indoors.
= 刺骨的北风把大家都留在了屋里。
a north wind = 北风
⑦ The geese fly north in spring and return in the autumn.
= 大雁春天往北飞，秋天回来。
fly north = 往北飞
⑧ The kitchen faces north and gets hardly any sun.
= 厨房朝北，几乎晒不到太阳。
face north = 朝北
⑨ His sister moved up north to be near her grandchildren.
= 他姐姐搬到北边去，好离孙辈近些。
up north = 在北方、到北方去
⑩ The compass points to magnetic north, which is a little way from true north.
= 指南针指向磁北，磁北跟真北差一点。
magnetic north = 磁北
true north = 真北
⑪ The first explorers reached the North Pole on foot, pulling their own sledges.
= 头一批探险者徒步到达北极，自己拉着雪橇。
the North Pole = 北极
⑫ The storm is moving north-east at twenty miles an hour.
= 那场风暴正以每小时二十英里的速度向东北移动。
north-east = 东北
north-west = 西北
⑬ The pilot turned the plane a few degrees to north-north-west.
= 飞行员把飞机往北偏西北方向转了几度。
north-north-west = 北偏西北
north-north-east = 北偏东北
⑭ The main north-south railway line runs through the valley.
= 南北向的铁路干线从那条山谷穿过。
north-south = 南北向的
⑮ He never lost his north-country accent, even after forty years in London.
= 他在伦敦住了四十年，北方口音始终没丢。
north-country = 英格兰北部的

not
/nɒt/
核心：副词，构成否定。跟在助动词、情态动词、be 后面：is not、do not、cannot；口语里缩成 n't。
否定非谓语时放在它前面：not to go、not knowing。
还用来否定一个词或短语：not everyone、not many、not for long；替代整个否定从句：I hope not、I am afraid not。
固定说法：not at all 一是一点也不，二是（回答道谢）别客气；not only … but also … 是不但…而且…，放在句首要倒装；
not that 是倒不是说；not to mention 是更不用说；if not 是如果不、甚至；not even 是连…都不；
not quite 是不完全、差一点；or not 是与否；not a 加名词是一个…都没有；not really 是不太、其实没有；
not … until 是直到…才。
① She is not at home today, but you can reach her at the office.
= 她今天不在家，不过你打办公室电话找得到她。
is not = 不是、不在
② I do not understand why the door was left unlocked.
= 我不明白门为什么没锁。
do not do something = 不做某事
③ He asked me not to tell anyone until the contract was signed.
= 他让我在合同签好之前别告诉任何人。
not to do something = 不要做某事
否定不定式，放在 to 前面。
④ Not knowing the language, she pointed at the dish she wanted.
= 她不懂当地话，就指了指想要的那道菜。
not knowing = 由于不知道
⑤ Not everyone enjoys camping, especially in the rain.
= 不是人人都喜欢露营，尤其是下雨天。
not everyone = 不是人人
not many = 不多
⑥ "Will it rain this afternoon?" "I hope not."
= 「今天下午会下雨吗？」「但愿不会。」
I hope not. = 但愿不会。
I am afraid not. = 恐怕不行。
⑦ The water was not at all cold once you were in.
= 一下了水，就一点也不觉得冷了。
not at all = 一点也不
⑧ "Thank you for carrying the bags." "Not at all."
= 「谢谢你帮忙拎包。」「别客气。」
Not at all. = 别客气。
⑨ She not only wrote the play but also directed it.
= 那出戏不但是她写的，也是她导的。
not only = 不但
not only … but also … = 不但…而且…
⑩ Not only did he forget her birthday, he also forgot their anniversary.
= 他不但忘了她的生日，连结婚纪念日也忘了。
Not only did he … = 他不但…
放在句首，后面倒装。
⑪ I shall walk to the station; not that I mind the exercise.
= 我走着去车站；倒不是说我介意活动活动。
not that = 倒不是说
⑫ The flat is too small for a piano, not to mention a dog.
= 那套公寓太小，放不下钢琴，更不用说养狗了。
not to mention = 更不用说
⑬ It will take weeks, if not months, to clear the backlog.
= 清理积压的工作要好几个星期，甚至好几个月。
if not = 甚至、如果不是
⑭ He left without a word, not even a goodbye to the children.
= 他一句话没说就走了，连跟孩子们道别都没有。
not even = 连…都不
⑮ The paint is not quite dry, so mind your sleeve.
= 油漆还没全干，当心袖子。
not quite = 不完全、差一点
⑯ Tell me by Friday whether you are coming or not.
= 星期五以前告诉我你来还是不来。
or not = 与否
whether or not = 是否
⑰ Not a sound came from the room where the children were asleep.
= 孩子们睡觉的那间屋里，一点声音都没有。
not a = 一个…都没有
not a sound = 一点声音都没有
⑱ "Did you enjoy the film?" "Not really. It went on far too long."
= 「那部片子你喜欢吗？」「不太喜欢。太长了。」
Not really. = 不太、其实没有。
⑲ The letter did not arrive until the following Tuesday.
= 那封信直到第二个星期二才到。
not … until = 直到…才

nothing
/ˈnʌθɪŋ/
核心：代词，没有什么、没有东西。作主语时动词用单数；本身是否定，不再加否定词。
形容词、else、不定式都放在它后面：nothing new、nothing else、nothing to eat。
也指无关紧要的东西、零：It was nothing。名词指无足轻重的人或事。
固定说法很多：nothing but 是只有、只是；for nothing 一是免费，二是白白地；nothing like 一是完全不像，二是远远不；
nothing much 是没什么；have nothing to do with 是与…无关、不跟…来往；nothing of the kind 是根本不是那么回事；
nothing if not 是极其、别的不说至少；come to nothing 是落空；think nothing of 是不把…当回事；
nothing less than 是不折不扣的；nothing doing 是（口语）不行、没门；there is nothing in it 是没有那回事；
there is nothing to it 是很容易；sweet nothings 是情话。
名词 nothingness（虚无、不存在）并入本条。
① There is nothing in the fridge except a jar of mustard.
= 冰箱里除了一罐芥末，什么都没有。
nothing = 什么都没有
② I have nothing to wear to the wedding on Saturday.
= 星期六的婚礼，我没有衣服可穿。
nothing to wear = 没有可穿的
③ Nothing else matters as long as the children are safe.
= 只要孩子们平安，别的都不要紧。
nothing else = 没有别的
④ There is nothing new in the report, only what we knew already.
= 那份报告里没有什么新东西，都是我们已经知道的。
nothing new = 没什么新的
形容词放在后面。
⑤ "What is the matter?" "Nothing. I am just tired."
= 「怎么了？」「没什么，我只是累了。」
Nothing. = 没什么。
⑥ He eats nothing but toast and marmalade for breakfast.
= 他早饭只吃烤面包抹橘子酱。
nothing but = 只有、只是
⑦ The neighbours gave us the old bicycle for nothing.
= 邻居把那辆旧自行车白送给了我们。
for nothing = 免费
⑧ We drove all that way for nothing, because the museum was shut.
= 我们大老远开过去白跑了一趟，博物馆关着门。
for nothing = 白白地
同一个短语的另一层意思。
⑨ The film is nothing like the book it claims to be based on.
= 那部片子跟它自称改编的那本书完全不像。
nothing like = 完全不像
⑩ There is nothing like a hot bath after a long walk in the rain.
= 在雨里走了长路之后，什么也比不上洗个热水澡。
there is nothing like something = 什么也比不上某物
⑪ "What did you do at the weekend?" "Nothing much. I cleaned the car."
= 「周末干什么了？」「没干什么，洗了洗车。」
nothing much = 没什么
⑫ The delay has nothing to do with the weather, whatever the airline says.
= 不管航空公司怎么说，延误跟天气毫无关系。
have nothing to do with = 与…无关
have nothing to do with something = 与某事无关
⑬ "You promised to pay." "I said nothing of the kind."
= 「你答应过要付钱的。」「我根本没说过那种话。」
nothing of the kind = 根本不是那么回事
⑭ She is nothing if not thorough, and checked every figure twice.
= 她别的不说，至少极其仔细，每个数字都核对了两遍。
nothing if not = 极其
⑮ All his plans to emigrate came to nothing when his father fell ill.
= 他父亲一病，他移居国外的计划全落了空。
come to nothing = 落空
⑯ She thinks nothing of cycling thirty miles before breakfast.
= 早饭前骑三十英里的车，她根本不当回事。
think nothing of = 不把…当回事
think nothing of doing something = 做某事不当回事
⑰ His escape from the burning car was nothing less than a miracle.
= 他从着火的车里逃出来，不折不扣是个奇迹。
nothing less than = 不折不扣的
⑱ He asked to borrow the car again, but I told him nothing doing.
= 他又来借车，我跟他说没门。
nothing doing = 不行、没门
⑲ There is a rumour that the shop is closing, but there is nothing in it.
= 有传言说那家店要关门，其实没有那回事。
there is nothing in it = 没有那回事
⑳ You will soon learn to change a tyre, because there is nothing to it.
= 换轮胎你很快就学会了，一点都不难。
there is nothing to it = 很容易
㉑ He whispered sweet nothings in her ear all through the film.
= 整场电影，他都在她耳边说悄悄情话。
sweet nothings = 情话
㉒ She treats him as a nothing, although he pays half the bills.
= 她把他当个无足轻重的人，尽管账单有一半是他付的。

㉓ The poem stares into the nothingness that waits at the end of every life.
= 那首诗凝视着等在每个人生命尽头的虚无。
nothingness = 虚无、不存在

now
/naʊ/
核心：副词，现在、此刻；也指「立刻」；叙述过去时指「这时」。
作名词用在介词后面：by now、until now、from now on、for now、up to now。
连词 now that 是既然、由于…已经。
口语里还用来引起注意、转换话题或缓和语气，不指时间：Now, where was I?；now then 是喂、好了。
固定说法：right now 一是此刻，二是马上；just now 一是刚才，二是此刻；now and then 与 now and again 是时不时；
any minute now 是随时、马上就；now or never 是机不可失；every now and then 是时不时。
① She used to teach in a village school and now works in a bank.
= 她从前在乡村学校教书，现在在银行工作。
now = 现在
② The doctor will see you now, if you would like to come through.
= 医生现在可以见您了，请往里走。
now = 现在、此刻
③ Stop that shouting now, or you will go straight to bed.
= 马上别嚷了，要不然你直接上床去。
now = 立刻
④ I am busy right now, but I can ring you back in ten minutes.
= 我这会儿正忙，十分钟后可以给你回电话。
right now = 就现在
⑤ Come down here right now and clear up this mess.
= 马上下来，把这一摊收拾干净。
right now = 马上
⑥ I saw her just now in the corridor, talking to the caretaker.
= 我刚才在走廊里看见她，正跟管理员说话。
just now = 刚才
⑦ They should have reached the coast by now.
= 他们这会儿应该已经到海边了。
by now = 到现在
⑧ From now on, all visitors must sign in at the front desk.
= 从现在起，所有来访者都必须在前台登记。
from now on = 从现在开始
⑨ That is enough for now, and we can finish the rest tomorrow.
= 眼下就先到这儿，剩下的明天再做完。
for now = 眼下、暂时
⑩ Up to now we have had no complaints about the new timetable.
= 到目前为止，新时刻表我们还没收到过投诉。
up to now = 到目前为止
until now = 直到现在
⑪ Now that the children have left home, the house feels far too big.
= 孩子们既然都离家了，这座房子显得太大了。
now that = 既然、由于…已经
⑫ We still see each other now and then, mostly at weddings.
= 我们时不时还见面，多半是在婚礼上。
now and then = 时不时
every now and then = 时不时
⑬ Now and again a fox crosses the garden in the middle of the day.
= 偶尔有只狐狸大白天穿过花园。
now and again = 偶尔
⑭ The taxi should be here any minute now.
= 出租车随时就到。
any minute now = 随时、马上就
⑮ If you want to ask her to dance, it is now or never.
= 你想请她跳舞的话，机不可失。
now or never = 机不可失
⑯ He was now alone in the house for the first time in forty years.
= 这时，他四十年来头一回一个人待在这座房子里。
now = 这时
叙述过去的事时用，指「故事里的此刻」。
⑰ Now, who can tell me the capital of Australia?
= 好，谁能告诉我澳大利亚的首都是哪儿？
Now, … = 好，…
引起注意，不指时间。
⑱ "Now then, what is all this shouting about?" asked the policeman.
= 警察问：「喂，嚷嚷什么呢？」
now then = 喂、好了

nowhere
/ˈnəʊweə(r)/
核心：副词，哪儿都不、没有地方。本身是否定，不再加否定词。后面可以接 else、不定式：nowhere else、nowhere to sit。
也作名词：没有名气的地方、不存在的地方。

⑤ I have been arguing with the bank for a month and getting nowhere.
= 我跟银行争了一个月，毫无进展。
get nowhere = 毫无进展
⑥ Shouting at the waiter will get you nowhere.
= 冲服务员嚷嚷，你什么也得不到。
