self-rule
/ˌself ˈruːl/
核心：名词，不可数：自治：一个国家或地区自己管理自己。
① After a hundred years of foreign control, the country finally won self-rule in 1960.
= 经过一百年的外国统治，那个国家终于在一九六〇年赢得了自治。
win self-rule = 赢得自治
self-rule = 自治
② The islanders voted for self-rule, but they wanted to keep close ties with the mainland.
= 岛民们投票赞成自治，但希望和大陆保持密切联系。
vote for self-rule = 投票赞成自治

self-sacrifice
/ˌself ˈsækrɪfaɪs/
核心：名词，不可数：自我牺牲：为了别人放弃自己的利益。
① Her parents' self-sacrifice made it possible for her to study medicine at a good university.
= 她父母的自我牺牲让她得以在一所好大学学医。
self-sacrifice = 自我牺牲
② The film celebrates the self-sacrifice of the nurses who worked through the war.
= 那部电影颂扬了在战争中坚持工作的护士们的自我牺牲。
the self-sacrifice of somebody = 某人的自我牺牲

self-sacrificing
/ˌself ˈsækrɪfaɪsɪŋ/
核心：形容词：自我牺牲的、舍己为人的。
① She was a kind, self-sacrificing woman who always put her family before herself.
= 她是个善良、舍己为人的女人，总是把家人放在自己前面。
self-sacrificing = 舍己为人的
② The self-sacrificing doctor gave up her holidays to work in the poorest parts of the city.
= 那位舍己为人的医生放弃了假期，到城里最穷的地方工作。
a self-sacrificing doctor = 舍己为人的医生

self-satisfaction

= 没有自满的余地，因为球队只赢了一分。
no room for self-satisfaction = 没有自满的余地

self-satisfied

① He had a self-satisfied smile on his face after he won the argument.
= 他吵赢之后，脸上带着沾沾自喜的笑容。
a self-satisfied smile = 沾沾自喜的笑容
self-satisfied = 自满的
② The government report strongly criticized the self-satisfied attitude of the company's managers.
= 那份政府报告严厉批评了公司管理层自满的态度。
a self-satisfied attitude = 自满的态度

self-seeking
/ˌself ˈsiːkɪŋ/
核心：形容词（贬义）：追逐私利的、自私的。名词，不可数：追逐私利。
① The voters were tired of self-seeking politicians who cared only about their own careers.
= 选民厌倦了那些只关心自己仕途、追逐私利的政客。
self-seeking politicians = 追逐私利的政客
self-seeking = 追逐私利的
② The charity was damaged by the self-seeking of a few of its leaders.
= 那家慈善机构因少数领导人追逐私利而受损。
the self-seeking of somebody = 某人的追逐私利

self-selected
/ˌself sɪˈlektɪd/
核心：形容词：（样本、人群）自选的、自愿加入的（而非随机抽取）。
① The survey used a self-selected group of people who chose to answer the questions online.
= 那项调查用的是一组自愿在网上答题的人。
a self-selected group = 自选的群体
self-selected = 自选的
② Results from a self-selected sample may not show what the whole population thinks.
= 自选样本得出的结果未必代表全体人口的想法。
a self-selected sample = 自选样本

self-selecting
/ˌself sɪˈlektɪŋ/
核心：形容词：自行选择的、自愿参与的：self-selecting groups。
① The class for advanced students is self-selecting, because only the keenest choose to join it.
= 那个高级班是自愿参加的，因为只有最积极的学生才会选。
self-selecting = 自愿参与的
② Online polls are self-selecting, so they often attract people with very strong opinions.
= 网上投票是自愿参加的，所以常常吸引观点非常强烈的人。
self-selecting polls = 自愿参与的投票

self-selection
/ˌself sɪˈlekʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：自我选择：（研究中）参与者自行决定是否参加，可能造成偏差。
① Self-selection is a problem in the study, because only healthy people chose to take part.
= 自我选择是那项研究的一个问题，因为只有健康的人选择参加。
self-selection = 自我选择
② The researchers tried to avoid self-selection by choosing people at random from the phone book.
= 研究人员从电话簿里随机挑选参与者，设法避免自我选择偏差。
avoid self-selection = 避免自我选择偏差

self-service
/ˌself ˈsɜːvɪs/

① We had lunch in a self-service restaurant where you carry your own tray to the table.
= 我们在一家自助餐厅吃了午饭，那里要自己端着托盘到桌边。
a self-service restaurant = 自助餐厅
self-service = 自助式的
② Most petrol stations now are self-service, so you fill the car yourself and pay inside.
= 现在大多数加油站都是自助的，你自己加油，然后进去付钱。
be self-service = 是自助的

be self-serving = 只为自己谋利

self-sufficiency

= 那座农场以完全自给自足为目标，粮食全靠自己种，电也自己发。
complete self-sufficiency = 完全自给自足

self-sufficient
/ˌself səˈfɪʃnt/

① The island is self-sufficient in vegetables, but it has to bring in meat and fuel by boat.
= 那座岛蔬菜能自给自足，但肉和燃料得靠船运进来。
be self-sufficient in something = 某方面自给自足
self-sufficient = 自给自足的
② Living alone in the mountains made her very self-sufficient and good at fixing things.
= 独自住在山里让她变得非常自立，也很会修东西。
very self-sufficient = 非常自立
说人时有时略带「不需要别人、难以亲近」的意味。

self-supporting
/ˌself səˈpɔːtɪŋ/

① The small theatre is now self-supporting and no longer needs money from the council.
= 那家小剧院如今自负盈亏，不再需要市政拨款了。

self-sustaining growth = 可持续的自主增长
多用于经济、生态、项目，强调不再需要外部投入。
