fire
核心：五支。一是火、火灾：put out a fire；二是（英式）取暖炉具：an electric fire；
三是开火射击；四是解雇：be fired；
五是一串习语：catch fire、set fire to、on fire、under fire、open fire、play with fire。
① It took nine minutes to put out a fire that had started in a bin.
= 一场从垃圾桶烧起来的火，扑了九分钟。
put out a fire = 扑灭火
make a fire = 生火
a house fire = 住宅火灾
② The curtains caught fire and the smoke alarm stayed silent throughout.
= 窗帘着了火，烟雾报警器全程一声没响。
catch fire = 着火
③ Somebody set fire to the shed and left the door open behind them.
= 有人放火烧了那间棚子，走的时候门还敞着。
set fire to something = 放火烧某物
④ The whole hillside was on fire by four and out by midnight.
= 到四点整面山坡都在烧，午夜前扑灭。
on fire = 着火、在燃烧
⑤ The minister came under fire for a sentence she had not written.
= 那位大臣因一句不是她写的话挨了炮轰。
come under fire = 受到抨击
⑥ They opened fire on an empty ridge and the report says as much.
= 他们朝一道空无一人的山脊开了火，报告里也是这么写的。
open fire = 开火
⑦ Two people were fired and the third resigned before the meeting started.
= 有两个人被解雇，第三个在开会前就辞了。
be fired = 被解雇
⑧ Lending your name to that scheme is playing with fire.
= 把名字借给那个方案，是玩火。
play with fire = 玩火
⑨ The fire department sent two fire engines and a fire truck that would not start.
= 消防局派来两辆消防车，还有一辆打不着火的云梯车。
the fire department = 消防局（美式）
the fire service = 消防部门（英式）
a fire engine = 消防车
a fire truck = 云梯消防车（美式）
⑩ The fire chief runs three stations and the fire company at the harbour runs itself.
= 消防长官管着三个站，港口那支消防队自己管自己。
a fire chief = 消防长官
a fire company = 消防队
a fire station = 消防站
a firehouse = 消防站（美式）
⑪ A fire hose froze to the fire hydrant and took two men to free.
= 一根消防水带冻在消火栓上，两个人才弄开。
a fire hose = 消防水带
a fire hydrant = 消火栓
⑫ The fire escape ends a floor above the ground and the building is a fire trap.
= 那道太平梯到二楼就断了，整栋楼就是个火灾陷阱。
a fire escape = 太平梯
a fire trap = 极易失火的房子
⑬ The door is fireproof, the curtain is fire-retardant, and the fireguard is missing.
= 门是防火的，帘子是阻燃的，炉前围栏却不见了。
fireproof = 防火的
fire-retardant = 阻燃的
a fireguard = 炉前围栏
a fire screen = 炉前挡板
⑭ Fire practice happens twice a year and the fire line gets repainted after each one.
= 消防演习一年两次，每次之后那条消防警戒线都要重画。
fire practice = 消防演习
a fire line = 消防警戒线
⑮ The fire-raiser was caught in April and fire-raising in that county carries seven years.
= 那名纵火者四月落网，在那个郡纵火要判七年。
a fire-raiser = 纵火者
fire-raising = 纵火
⑯ The fire-eater works for tips and buys a firelighter every Saturday.
= 那位吞火艺人靠打赏过活，每周六买一块引火块。
a fire-eater = 吞火艺人
a firelighter = 引火块
a fire starter = 引火物
⑰ Firewood stacked against that wall dries in a summer and burns in a week.
= 靠那面墙码着的柴火，一个夏天晾干，一个星期烧完。
firewood = 柴火
⑱ Firelight from the fireplace reaches the fireside chair and stops there.
= 壁炉的火光照到炉边那把椅子，就到那儿为止。
a fireplace = 壁炉
firelight = 火光
the fireside = 炉边
⑲ A firestorm of criticism followed a decision that had taken nine minutes.
= 一个只花了九分钟的决定，引来铺天盖地的批评。
a firestorm = 铺天盖地的风暴
⑳ The firewall blocks that site at work and the same site opens at home.
= 防火墙在单位挡住那个网站，同一个网站在家却打得开。
a firewall = 防火墙
㉑ Fire ants arrived with the topsoil and now own the entire lawn.
= 火蚁是随表土来的，如今整片草坪都归它们。
fire ants = 火蚁
㉒ The shop is having a fire sale and the fireworks are going at half price.
= 那家店在清仓甩卖，烟花半价出。
a fire sale = 清仓甩卖
a firework = 烟花
firepower = 火力
㉓ One glass of that firewater ended the evening for two of them.
= 那种烈酒一杯下肚，他们里有两个的晚上就到此为止。
firewater = 烈酒（俗称）
㉔ Fire away — the meeting has nine minutes left and no agenda at all.
= 你尽管问 —— 这个会还剩九分钟，连议程都没有。
fire away = 尽管问、开始吧
㉕ He fired off three emails and then fired up the printer that always jams.
= 他连发三封邮件，又启动了那台老卡纸的打印机。
fire off = 迅速发出
fire up = 启动、点着
㉖ A firefly in that jar lasted one night and taught nine children something.
= 罐子里那只萤火虫活了一夜，教会九个孩子一件事。
a firefly = 萤火虫
㉗ A fireside chat on the radio reached more people than any speech in that hall.
= 电台里的炉边谈话，比那间礼堂里任何演讲传得都远。
a fireside chat = 炉边谈话

firearm

① A firearms certificate takes eleven weeks and two referees to obtain.
= 办一张持枪证要十一个星期，还要两位担保人。
a firearms certificate = 持枪证
② The museum keeps the firearm in a case with no label at all.
= 博物馆把那支枪放在一个完全没有标签的柜子里。
a firearm = 枪支

fireball
核心：三支。一是火球：爆炸时腾起的那团火；二是（天文）火流星；
三是（非正式）精力过人的人。可数。
① The fireball took out the windows and the fire itself did far less damage.
= 那团火球震碎了窗户，火本身造成的损失反倒小得多。
a fireball = 火球
② A fireball crossed the sky at nine and the switchboard lit up.
= 九点有颗火流星划过天空，总机电话立刻被打爆。
a fireball = 火流星

fireballer
核心：（棒球，美式）快球投手：球速极快的投手。可数。
① The fireballer throws hard for four innings and has nothing after that.
= 那位快球投手能猛投四局，之后就没东西了。
a fireballer = 快球投手
a young fireballer = 年轻的快球投手
② Every scout wants a fireballer and every coach wants a second pitch.
= 球探个个想要快球投手，教练个个想要第二种球路。

firebomb
核心：燃烧弹：名词与动词同形。可数。
① A firebomb through the letterbox failed to light and the family moved anyway.
= 一枚从信箱塞进来的燃烧弹没点着，那家人还是搬走了。
a firebomb = 燃烧弹
② The depot was firebombed twice in the same summer.
= 那座仓库同一个夏天被投了两次燃烧弹。
be firebombed = 遭燃烧弹袭击

firebrand
核心：煽动者、鼓动家：说话激烈、专会点火的人。可数，褒贬都能用。
① The firebrand of 1968 chairs the pensions committee now.
= 1968 年那位鼓动家，如今主持养老金委员会。
a firebrand = 煽动者
② A firebrand speech filled the hall and emptied the negotiating table.
= 一场激烈的演说挤满了会场，清空了谈判桌。
a firebrand speech = 煽动性的演说

firebreak
核心：防火隔离带：林中特意清出的空带，用来挡住火势。可数。
① The firebreak held on the north side and gave way on the east.
= 那条防火带在北面守住了，东面破了口。
a firebreak = 防火隔离带
② Cutting a firebreak takes two days and can save a whole forest.
= 开一条防火带要两天，能救下整片林子。
cut a firebreak = 开辟防火带

firebrick
核心：耐火砖：炉膛与烟囱里用的耐高温砖。可数。
① The firebricks at the back of the stove outlast everything around them.
= 炉子后壁那些耐火砖，比周围一切都耐用。
a firebrick = 耐火砖
line something with firebrick = 用耐火砖砌衬
② Two cracked firebricks let the heat straight into the wall behind.
= 两块开裂的耐火砖，让热量直接透进后面的墙。

firebug
核心：（非正式）纵火犯：爱放火的人。可数。
① A firebug in the district set nine bins alight in one month.
= 那个区有个纵火的，一个月点了九个垃圾桶。
a firebug = 纵火犯
catch a firebug = 抓住纵火犯
② The firebug was caught by a camera that had not worked for a year.
= 那个纵火犯，被一台坏了一年的摄像头拍下来了。

firecracker
核心：两支。一是爆竹、鞭炮；二是（非正式）活力四射的人。可数。
① A firecracker went off in the yard and the dog is still under the bed.
= 院子里响了个爆竹，狗到现在还在床底下。
a firecracker = 爆竹
② She is a firecracker on stage and very quiet at the table afterwards.

firefight

firefighter
核心：消防员：如今通用的说法，取代了旧称 fireman。可数。
① Firefighters got there in six minutes and the road took four of them.
= 消防员六分钟就到，其中四分钟花在路上。
a firefighter = 消防员
a retained firefighter = 兼职消防员
② Two firefighters carried the ladder that four people had brought.
= 四个人抬来的那架梯子，两名消防员就扛走了。

firefighting
核心：两支。一是灭火这件事；二是引申的四处救火、疲于应付。不可数。
① Firefighting equipment is checked in April and needed in August.
= 灭火器材四月检查，八月用得上。
firefighting equipment = 灭火器材
② The whole job is firefighting and the plan gets written in December.
= 那份工作整天都在救火，计划要到十二月才写得出来。
firefighting = 疲于应付

fireman
核心：两支。一是（旧称）消防员：如今通用 firefighter，这个词多见于旧文；
二是蒸汽机车上的司炉。可数，复数 firemen。
① The fireman on that engine shovelled two tons of coal in a single shift.
= 那台机车上的司炉，一个班要铲两吨煤。
a fireman = 司炉（蒸汽机车上的）
② A fireman in that photograph is holding a hose that needs four men.
= 照片里那位消防员，端着一根要四个人才拿得住的水带。
a fireman = 消防员（旧称）

firing

② The firing of three managers in one week emptied the car park by six.

③ The second firing sets the glaze and the first only hardens the clay.
= 第二次烧制定住釉面，头一次只是把泥坯烧硬。
a firing = 一次烧制
④ The firing squad in that film is eleven men and one blank round.
= 那部电影里的行刑队，十一个人，一发空包弹。
a firing squad = 行刑队

firkin

= 两只小桶从货车上卸下来，其中一只已经在漏了。

firm
核心：四支。一是结实的、硬实的：a firm mattress；二是坚定的：be firm with somebody；
三是（日期、安排）定死的：a firm date；四是名词，公司：a law firm。副词 firmly。
① A firm mattress and a soft pillow is the compromise that household reached.
= 硬床垫配软枕头，是那家人达成的折中。
a firm mattress = 硬床垫
② You have to be firm with a puppy and gentle about how you do it.
= 对小狗要坚定，态度还得温和。
be firm with somebody = 对某人态度坚定
③ There is no firm date yet and the invitations are already printed.
= 日子还没定死，请柬倒已经印好了。
a firm date = 确定的日期
④ The law firm has nine partners and one working lift.
= 那家律所有九位合伙人，一部还能用的电梯。
a law firm = 律师事务所
副词 firmly：牢牢地、坚决地，多修饰 shut、held、believed。
⑤ The door was firmly shut and the key was in the other coat.
= 门关得严严实实，钥匙在另一件外套里。
firmly shut = 关得严实
⑥ She firmly believes the second version and has read only the first.
= 她坚信第二个版本，读过的却只有第一个。
firmly believe = 坚信
⑦ Prices firm up in spring and soften again by the middle of August.
= 价格春天走稳，到八月中又松动。
firm up = 走稳、确定下来

firmness

firmware

first
核心：五支。一是第一的、最先的：the first time；二是副词，首先；
三是名词，第一次、第一名；四是（英式）一等荣誉学位：get a first；
五是一串习语：at first、first of all、first come, first served。副词 firstly 用于列举。
① The first time she drove that road she went the wrong way twice.
= 她头一回开那条路，走错了两次。
the first time = 第一次
② At first the noise was funny and by Thursday it was not.
= 起初那声音还好笑，到周四就不好笑了。
at first = 起初
③ First of all, nobody asked; second, nobody minded.
= 首先，没人问过；其次，没人在意。
first of all = 首先
④ Seats are first come, first served and the queue starts at seven.
= 座位先到先得，七点就开始排队。
first come, first served = 先到先得
⑤ She got a first and now works in a shop she actually likes.
= 她拿了一等荣誉学位，如今在一家自己真心喜欢的店里上班。
get a first = 拿到一等荣誉学位
副词 firstly：列举时的「第一」，跟 secondly 成套，用在正式行文里。
⑥ Firstly the roof, secondly the boiler, and thirdly the money.
= 第一是屋顶，第二是锅炉，第三是钱。

⑦ First aid was given by a first-aider who had qualified the week before.

㉒ The first finger on that hand is shorter than the second and always has been.
= 那只手的食指比中指短，一向如此。
the first finger = 食指
㉓ A first-person shooter sold nine million copies and taught nobody to shoot.
= 一款第一人称射击游戏卖了九百万份，没教会谁开枪。
a first-person shooter = 第一人称射击游戏
㉔ The first down came in the third minute and the crowd sat back down.
= 第一次进攻推进出现在第三分钟，观众又坐了回去。
a first down = （美式足球）首攻成功

firstborn
核心：长子、长女：家里第一个出生的孩子。可数，也作定语。
① The firstborn gets the photographs and the second gets the hand-me-downs.
= 老大占了照片，老二捡旧衣裳。
the firstborn = 长子、长女
② Their firstborn was named after a street and the second after a saint.
= 他们的老大用一条街的名字命名，老二用了一位圣人的。
a firstborn son = 长子

firth
核心：（苏格兰）河口湾：河流入海处那片宽阔水域，多见于地名。可数。
① The firth is grey in every photograph and blue about four days a year.
= 那道河口湾在每张照片里都是灰的，一年大约有四天是蓝的。
a firth = 河口湾
cross the firth = 横渡河口湾
② A bridge across the firth took nine years and two different designs.
= 跨那道河口湾的桥，修了九年，换过两版设计。

fiscal
核心：（正式）财政的、税收的：fiscal policy、the fiscal year。副词 fiscally。
① Fiscal policy moves slowly and everybody feels it at the same moment.
= 财政政策动得慢，人人却在同一刻感觉到。
fiscal policy = 财政政策
② The fiscal year ends in April and the paperwork ends in June.
= 财政年度四月结束，文书到六月才完。
the fiscal year = 财政年度
副词 fiscally：在财政上，多说 fiscally neutral、fiscally responsible。
③ The plan is fiscally neutral and politically expensive.
= 那个方案财政上不增负担，政治上代价高昂。
fiscally neutral = 财政上中性的
④ A fiscally responsible budget still needs somebody to vote for it.
= 一份财政上稳健的预算，也还得有人投票通过。
fiscally responsible = 财政上稳健的
⑤ The fiscal cliff arrived on schedule and nobody had packed for it.
= 财政悬崖如期而至，谁都没为它做准备。
a fiscal cliff = 财政悬崖
