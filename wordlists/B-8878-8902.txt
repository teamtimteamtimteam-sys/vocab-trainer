a fitter = 安装工
② A fitter measured her for the dress and took forty minutes over one sleeve.
= 裁缝师傅给她量那条裙子，光一只袖子就量了四十分钟。
a fitter = 量体裁衣的师傅

fitting

三是形容词，恰如其分的：a fitting tribute。
① She went for a fitting and the dress came back a different colour.
= 她去试穿，裙子回来时换了个颜色。

② The light fittings are original and the wiring behind them is not.
= 那些灯具是原装的，后面的线路可不是。
light fittings = 灯具配件
③ A fitting tribute would have been a bench and they built an arch.
= 恰如其分的纪念本该是一条长椅，他们造了一座拱门。
a fitting tribute = 恰如其分的纪念
④ The fitting room has one hook and a mirror that leans forward.
= 那间试衣间只有一个挂钩，还有一面往前倾的镜子。
a fitting room = 试衣间

five

fives

fix
核心：五支。一是修理：fix a leak；二是固定、安上：fix something to something；
三是安排、定下：fix a date；四是（贬）做手脚、内定；
五是名词：in a fix 是陷入困境，a quick fix 是权宜之计。
① He fixed the leak with something that was never meant for pipes.
= 他用一样根本不是给管子用的东西把漏补上了。
fix a leak = 修好漏水
② Fix the shelf to the wall before you put anything heavy on it.
= 放重东西之前，先把架子固定到墙上。
fix something to something = 把某物固定在某处
③ They fixed a date and then moved it twice before the invitations went out.
= 他们定了日子，请柬发出去之前又改了两回。
fix a date = 定下日期
④ The match was fixed and the only surprise was who admitted it.
= 那场比赛是做了手脚的，唯一意外的是谁承认了。
be fixed = 被做了手脚
⑤ We are in a fix and a quick fix will only make it worse.
= 我们陷在困境里，权宜之计只会让事情更糟。
in a fix = 陷入困境
a quick fix = 权宜之计
A quick fix held for two winters and cost four times as much.
= 那个权宜之计撑了两个冬天，花的钱是四倍。
⑥ They fixed on a date and fixed up the spare room in one afternoon.
= 他们定下日子，一个下午就把空房收拾停当。
fix on something = 定下某事
fix something up = 把某物收拾停当
She fixed the old bicycle up and gave it to the neighbour's son.
= 她把那辆旧自行车收拾停当，送给了邻居家的儿子。
fix up = 收拾停当、安排好
They fixed up to meet in March and neither wrote it down.
= 他们安排好三月见面，两个人都没记下来。

fixable
核心：修得好的、有办法解决的：多用在否定或对比里。
① The gearbox is fixable and the bodywork is not worth the paint.

= 一个本来修得好的问题，撂上一年就修不好了。
a fixable problem = 有办法解决的问题

fixated

① He is fixated on a rule that was withdrawn back in 2011.

② A committee fixated on process produces minutes and nothing else.

fixated = 执迷的

fixation
核心：（贬或心理学）执念、固恋：a fixation with something 或 on something。可数。
① His fixation with the timetable outlasted the service it described.
= 他对那份时刻表的执念，比时刻表上那条线还长命。
a fixation with something = 对某事的执念
② A childhood fixation on trains became a career and then a hobby again.
= 童年对火车的迷恋成了职业，后来又变回爱好。
a fixation on something = 对某事的固恋

fixative
核心：两支。一是（美术）定画液：喷在炭笔或粉彩画上防蹭；
二是香水与标本用的固定剂。可数不可数都行。

fixed
核心：三支。一是固定的、不变的：a fixed price；
二是（表情、目光）僵住不动的：a fixed smile；三是安装牢固的。副词 fixedly。
① A fixed price sounds simple until the extras appear on the second page.
= 固定价格听着简单，直到第二页冒出一堆附加项。
a fixed price = 固定价格
② She kept a fixed smile through nine speeches and one power cut.

③ He stared fixedly at the wall behind her and answered every question.
= 他直勾勾地盯着她身后那面墙，把每个问题都答了。

④ The cat looked fixedly at the cupboard until somebody opened it.
= 那只猫死盯着柜子，直到有人把它打开。

⑤ Fixed costs stay put, fixed assets depreciate, and fixed-term contracts end.
= 固定成本不动，固定资产折旧，定期合同到期。
fixed costs = 固定成本
fixed assets = 固定资产

= 那张资产负债表上的固定资产，包括一条谁也没见过的船。
fixed-term = 定期的
A fixed-term contract ends on a date the tenant keeps forgetting.

⑥ A fixed-line phone survives the power cut that takes out everything else.

fixed-line = 固定线路的
⑦ Fixed-wing aircraft need a runway and helicopters need somewhere flat.
= 固定翼飞机要跑道，直升机只要一块平地。
