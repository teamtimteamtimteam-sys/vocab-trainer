reversal
/rɪˈvɜːsl/

① The new government announced a complete reversal of the old policy on schools.
= 新政府宣布彻底改变过去的学校政策。
a reversal of something = 某事的彻底转变
reversal = 逆转
② The team suffered a series of reversals and dropped to the bottom of the league.
= 那支球队接连失利，跌到了联赛垫底。
suffer a reversal = 遭受挫折
③ The appeal court ordered a reversal of the judge's decision.
= 上诉法院下令推翻那个法官的判决。
a reversal of a decision = 推翻判决
