习语 redeeming feature 是（缺点很多的人或物身上的）可取之处。并入本条。
① The film is long and slow, but the music redeems it.
= 那部片子又长又慢，但音乐弥补了不足。
redeem something = 弥补某物
redeem = 弥补
② He missed an easy goal but redeemed himself with a brilliant save.
= 他错过了一个好进的球，但又靠一次精彩的扑救挽回了面子。
redeem yourself = 挽回面子
③ The only redeeming feature of the hotel was its view of the sea.
= 那家旅馆唯一的可取之处是能看到大海。
redeeming feature = 可取之处
④ Christians believe that Christ came to redeem the world from sin.
= 基督徒相信基督降世是为了把世人从罪中救赎出来。
redeem somebody from sin = 把某人从罪中救赎出来
⑤ You can redeem the vouchers at any of our shops.
= 那些代金券在我们任何一家店都能兑换。
redeem a voucher = 兑换代金券
⑥ She went back to the pawnshop to redeem her grandmother's ring.
= 她回到当铺，把祖母的戒指赎了回来。
redeem something = 赎回某物
⑦ The new government has failed to redeem its promises on schools.
= 新政府没能兑现它在学校问题上的承诺。
redeem a promise = 履行诺言
⑧ The bonds are redeemable after ten years.
= 那些债券十年后可以兑现。
redeemable = 可兑换的、可赎回的
⑨ The novel is about a criminal's search for redemption.
= 那部小说讲一个罪犯寻求救赎的故事。
redemption = 救赎、赎回
⑩ Some people think that the old building is beyond redemption and should be pulled down.
= 有人认为那栋老楼已经无可救药，该拆掉。
beyond redemption = 无可救药
⑪ The film has a redemptive ending in which the father and son are reunited.
= 那部片子有个救赎式的结尾：父子团聚了。
a redemptive ending = 救赎式的结尾

redefine
/ˌriːdɪˈfaɪn/
核心：重新定义、改变（某事物的）性质或界限。及物。名词 redefinition，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The internet has redefined the way people shop and talk to each other.
= 互联网重新定义了人们购物和交流的方式。
redefine the way… = 重新定义……的方式
redefine = 重新定义
② The new law involves a redefinition of what counts as a small business.
= 新法律涉及重新界定什么算小企业。
a redefinition of something = 对某事的重新定义

redeploy
/ˌriːdɪˈplɔɪ/
核心：重新部署、调派（军队、人员、资源）。及物。名词 redeployment，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The troops were redeployed to the south after the fighting ended in the north.
= 北方的战斗结束后，部队被调往南方。
be redeployed to somewhere = 被调往某处
redeploy = 重新部署
② Instead of sacking staff, the firm will redeploy them to its new offices.
= 那家公司不裁员，而是把员工调到新办公室。
redeploy staff = 调派员工
③ The redeployment of nurses has eased the pressure on the hospital.
= 护士的重新调配缓解了医院的压力。
the redeployment of somebody = 某些人的重新调配

redesign
/ˌriːdɪˈzaɪn/
核心：动词：重新设计、改进设计。名词，可数也可不可数：重新设计。
① The car has been completely redesigned for the new year.
= 那款车为新的一年彻底重新设计了。
be redesigned = 被重新设计
redesign = 重新设计
② The redesign of the website made it much easier to use.
= 网站重新设计以后好用多了。
the redesign of something = 某物的重新设计
