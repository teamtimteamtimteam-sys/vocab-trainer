hubris
核心：（正式）傲慢、狂妄 —— 来自希腊悲剧：自视过高、终招惩罚的那种骄傲。不可数。
① Building the tallest tower in the world was an act of pure hubris, and it was never finished.
= 修建世界最高塔纯属狂妄之举，结果一直没有完工。
an act of hubris = 狂妄之举
② The company's collapse was blamed on the hubris of its founder.
= 公司的倒闭被归咎于创始人的傲慢。
the hubris of somebody = 某人的傲慢

huckleberry
核心：越橘 —— 北美一种小浆果，深紫色，像蓝莓。可数。
① They picked huckleberries on the mountain and made jam that night.
= 他们在山上采了越橘，当晚就做了果酱。
huckleberries = 越橘
② A single huckleberry bush can feed a bear for an afternoon.
= 一丛越橘足够一头熊吃上一个下午。
a huckleberry bush = 越橘丛

huckster
核心：（贬义）叫卖小贩；兜售夸大其词东西的推销者 —— 带「强卖、夸大」的意味。
① A huckster at the fair was selling a cure for everything from baldness to backache.
= 集市上一个小贩在叫卖一种从秃头到腰疼什么都能治的药。
a huckster = 叫卖小贩、兜售者（贬）
② Critics called him a political huckster who would say anything for a vote.
= 批评者说他是个政治掮客，为了拉票什么都说得出口。
a political huckster = 政治掮客

huddle
核心：挤作一团 —— 因为冷、怕或要商量而紧紧聚在一起；也作名词：a huddle（一小堆人）。
形容词 huddled（缩成一团的、挤在一起的）并入本条。
① The children huddled together under a blanket until the storm passed.
= 孩子们挤在一条毯子底下，直到暴风雨过去。
huddle together = 挤作一团
② The players went into a huddle before the last play of the game.
= 比赛最后一个回合之前，球员们围成一圈商量。
go into a huddle = 围成一圈商量
③ She sat huddled in a corner, with her coat pulled tight around her.
= 她蜷缩在角落里，紧紧裹着外套。
huddled = 缩成一团的

hue
核心：（正式或文学）色彩、色调；比喻「类型、派别」（people of every political hue）。
习语 hue and cry：一片喧嚷的抗议声。
① The sky turned a deep orange hue as the sun went down behind the hills.
= 太阳落到山后时，天空变成了深橙色。
a hue = 色彩、色调
② Politicians of every hue turned up for the funeral.
= 各党各派的政客都来参加了葬礼。
of every hue = 各种派别的
③ The plan to close the hospital raised a hue and cry across the county.
= 关闭医院的计划在全郡引起一片抗议声。
a hue and cry = 一片喧嚷的抗议声

huff
核心：名词 in a huff：（因为小事）生闷气、赌气。动词 huff：呼哧呼哧地喘；不满地说。
习语 huff and puff：气喘吁吁；（比喻）大声抱怨却不行动。
形容词 huffy（爱生气的、气呼呼的）与副词 huffily 并入本条。
① He left in a huff when nobody laughed at his joke.
= 没人笑他的笑话，他气呼呼地走了。
in a huff = 生闷气、赌气
② The minister huffed and puffed about the report but changed nothing.
= 部长对那份报告大发牢骚，却什么也没改。
huff and puff = 大声抱怨却不行动
③ She gets huffy if you ask about her age.
= 你要是问她年纪，她就不高兴。
huffy = 气呼呼的、爱生气的
④ "I'm perfectly capable of doing it myself," he said huffily.
= 「我完全可以自己来。」他气呼呼地说。
huffily = 气呼呼地

hug
核心：本义是拥抱 —— 用双臂抱紧；名词 a hug。
由「紧贴」长出：贴着…走 —— hug the coast、hug the kerb（沿着海岸、贴着路边）；
衣服紧贴身体：a dress that hugs her figure。
① She gave her grandson a big hug at the airport.
= 她在机场给了孙子一个大大的拥抱。
give somebody a hug = 拥抱某人
② He hugged the parcel to his chest and ran through the rain.
= 他把包裹紧紧抱在胸前，冒雨跑了过去。
hug something = 紧紧抱住某物
③ The road hugs the coast for twenty miles before turning inland.
= 那条路紧贴着海岸延伸二十英里，然后转向内陆。
hug the coast = 沿着海岸
④ Her father gave her a bear hug that lifted her off the ground.
= 她父亲给了她一个熊抱，把她整个人抱离了地面。
a bear hug = 熊抱、紧紧的拥抱

huge
核心：巨大的、极大的 —— 说体积、数量、程度都行（A 表教过）。口语里也说「非常成功的」：
The band is huge in Japan。副词 hugely（非常、极其）并入本条。
① They live in a huge house with nine bedrooms and a swimming pool.
= 他们住在一栋有九间卧室和一个游泳池的大宅里。
huge = 巨大的
② The film cost a huge amount of money and made very little back.
= 那部电影花了一大笔钱，却几乎没赚回来什么。
a huge amount of something = 大量的…
③ The band was never famous here, but it's huge in Japan.
= 那支乐队在这里从没出过名，在日本却红得发紫。
be huge = 非常成功、红极一时（口语）
④ Her first novel was hugely successful and sold a million copies.
= 她的第一部小说非常成功，卖了一百万册。
hugely successful = 极其成功的

Huguenot
核心：胡格诺派教徒 —— 十六、十七世纪法国的新教徒，许多人因受迫害逃往英国等地。首字母大写。
① The street was built by Huguenot silk weavers who had fled from France.
= 那条街是由逃离法国的胡格诺派丝织工建起来的。
Huguenot = 胡格诺派的
② Her family are descended from a Huguenot who arrived in London in 1685.
= 她家是一位一六八五年来到伦敦的胡格诺派教徒的后代。
a Huguenot = 胡格诺派教徒

huh
核心：（口语）感叹词 —— 表示疑问、惊讶、没听清，或放在句末寻求对方同意（「是吧？」）。
① "Huh? What did you say? The music's too loud in here and I can't hear a single word."
= 「啊？你说什么？这里音乐太吵，我一个字都听不见。」
huh = 啊？（没听清、疑问）
② Pretty good for a first attempt at baking bread without a recipe, huh?
= 第一次不看菜谱烤面包就这样，挺不错的吧？
something, huh? = …是吧？（寻求认同）
③ "He's getting married next spring." "Huh. I never thought he would settle down with anyone."
= 「他明年春天要结婚了。」「哼，我从没想到他会跟谁安定下来。」
huh = 哼（表示意外或不以为然）

hula
核心：草裙舞 —— 夏威夷传统舞蹈，用手势讲故事。可数，也常带 the：dance the hula。
① The dancers performed a slow hula, their hands telling the story of the islands.
= 舞者跳着缓慢的草裙舞，用手势讲述岛屿的故事。
a hula = 草裙舞
② They learned to dance the hula on their honeymoon in Hawaii.
= 他们在夏威夷度蜜月时学会了跳草裙舞。
dance the hula = 跳草裙舞

hulk
核心：一是废船的船体、报废的庞然大物（the rusting hulk of a ship）；
二是笨重的大块头 —— 高大粗壮的人。形容词 hulking（庞大笨重的）并入本条。
① The rusting hulk of an old ferry lay on the beach for years.
= 一艘旧渡轮锈蚀的船体在海滩上躺了好多年。
the hulk of something = …的残骸
② A great hulk of a man stood in the doorway and blocked out the light.
= 一个大块头站在门口，把光都挡住了。
a hulk of a man = 大块头
③ A hulking figure appeared out of the fog.
= 一个庞大的身影从雾里冒了出来。
hulking = 庞大笨重的

hull
核心：两个词：一是船体 —— 船的主体外壳；二是（豆、谷物的）外壳、荚，动词是剥壳、去蒂。
① The storm cracked the hull, and water began to pour in.
= 风暴把船体撞裂了，水开始涌进来。
the hull = 船体
② She hulled the strawberries and put them in a bowl with cream.
= 她把草莓去了蒂，放进碗里配奶油。
hull strawberries = 给草莓去蒂
③ The hulls of the rice are used as fuel in the villages.
= 稻壳在村子里被用作燃料。
hulls = 谷壳、外壳

hullabaloo
核心：（口语）喧闹、大吵大闹；一场风波 —— 为一件事闹得沸沸扬扬。单数。
① There was a huge hullabaloo when the club changed the colour of its shirts.
= 俱乐部换了球衣颜色，引起一场轩然大波。
a hullabaloo = 喧闹、风波
② What's all the hullabaloo about? It's only a parking ticket.
= 闹什么呀？不过是一张停车罚单。
all the hullabaloo about something = 为某事闹得沸沸扬扬

hullo
核心：（英式，偏旧）你好 —— hello 的另一种拼法；也表示惊讶「咦」。
① "Hullo, is anybody there?" she called up the stairs.
= 「喂，有人在吗？」她朝楼上喊道。
hullo = 你好、喂（英式，旧）
② Hullo, what's this? Somebody has left a bag on the seat.
= 咦，这是什么？有人把包落在座位上了。
hullo = 咦（表示惊讶）

hum
核心：拟声而来 —— 低沉持续的嗡嗡声。动词两支：一是（虫、机器）嗡嗡响；二是闭着嘴哼（曲子）。
口语 be humming 是「热闹繁忙、一片兴旺」；英式 hum and haw 是「支支吾吾」（那一条收在 haw）。
① The fridge hums all night, and I can hear it from the bedroom.
= 冰箱整夜嗡嗡响，我在卧室里都听得见。
hum = 嗡嗡响
② She hummed a tune to herself while she did the washing-up.
= 她一边洗碗一边自己哼着一支曲子。
hum a tune = 哼曲子
③ There was a low hum of conversation in the restaurant before the speeches.
= 讲话开始前，餐厅里一片低低的交谈声。
a hum of something = …的嗡嗡声
④ By nine the market was humming, with queues at every stall.
= 到九点，集市已经一片繁忙，每个摊位前都排着队。
be humming = 热闹繁忙（口语）

human
核心：人的、人类的；名词是「人」（a human、humans，A 表教过）。
形容词两支：一是属于人类的（the human body、the human race）；
二是有人情味的、有人性弱点的 —— be only human（人非圣贤）、the human touch。
跟 humane 的分界：human 只说「人的」，humane 说「仁慈的、人道的」，各立词条。
一串复合词并入本条：human error、human nature、human rights、human resources、
human capital、human geography、human interest、a human shield、human trafficking、human smuggling。
① The human body is about sixty per cent water.
= 人体大约百分之六十是水。
the human body = 人体
② Scientists say the human race has been on the planet for about 300,000 years.
= 科学家说人类在地球上已经存在了约三十万年。
the human race = 人类
③ Dogs can hear sounds that humans cannot.
= 狗能听见人听不见的声音。
humans = 人、人类（名词）
④ Don't be too hard on him; he's only human and he was exhausted.
= 别对他太苛刻，他也是人，而且累坏了。
be only human = 人非圣贤、是人都会这样
⑤ What the office needs is a manager with the human touch.
= 这个办公室需要的是一个有人情味的经理。
the human touch = 人情味
⑥ Investigators blamed the crash on human error, not a mechanical fault.
= 调查人员把事故归咎于人为失误，而不是机械故障。
human error = 人为失误
⑦ It's human nature to want what you can't have.
= 得不到的才想要，这是人的本性。
human nature = 人性、人的本性
⑧ The report accuses the government of serious human rights abuses.
= 报告指控该国政府严重侵犯人权。
human rights = 人权
⑨ She works in human resources and handles all the new contracts.
= 她在人力资源部工作，负责所有新合同。
human resources = 人力资源（部）
⑩ Economists argue that education builds human capital as much as roads build physical capital.
= 经济学家认为，教育积累人力资本，就像修路积累实物资本一样。
human capital = 人力资本
⑪ She studied human geography, the study of how people use and shape places.
= 她学的是人文地理学，研究人如何利用、塑造地方。
human geography = 人文地理学
⑫ The paper ran a human interest story about a dog that found its way home across three counties.
= 报纸登了一篇温情报道，讲一条狗穿过三个郡找到了回家的路。
a human interest story = 温情报道、人情味新闻
⑬ The rebels used villagers as a human shield to stop the air strikes.
= 叛军把村民当作人肉盾牌，阻止空袭。
a human shield = 人肉盾牌
⑭ Police arrested a human trafficker who had brought forty people across the border in a lorry.
= 警方逮捕了一名人口贩子，他用一辆卡车把四十个人偷运过境。
a human trafficker = 人口贩子
human trafficking = 人口贩卖
⑮ Human smuggling across the Channel has risen sharply this year.
= 今年经英吉利海峡偷运人口的活动急剧增加。
human smuggling = 偷运人口
跟人口贩卖的分别：偷运是收钱帮人非法越境，贩卖是强迫、剥削被运的人。
⑯ A human smuggler charged each family five thousand euros for the crossing.
= 一个蛇头向每个家庭收取五千欧元的偷渡费。
a human smuggler = 蛇头

humane
核心：仁慈的、人道的 —— 尽量减少痛苦的。反义是 inhumane。副词 humanely 并入本条。
跟 human 只差一个 e，意思不同：human 是「人的」。
① The charity campaigns for the humane treatment of farm animals.
= 那家慈善机构倡导人道对待农场动物。
humane treatment = 人道对待
② The judge was known as a humane man who always listened to the accused.
= 那位法官以仁慈著称，总是认真听取被告的陈述。
a humane man = 仁慈的人
③ The animals were killed humanely and quickly.
= 那些动物被人道而迅速地宰杀了。
humanely = 人道地

humanism
核心：人文主义 —— 以人的价值、理性为中心，而非以神为中心的思想。不可数。
持这种主张的人叫 humanist，形容词 humanistic，都并入本条；
文艺复兴时 humanist 还指研究古希腊罗马文化的学者。
① Renaissance humanism brought Greek and Latin texts back into the universities.
= 文艺复兴时期的人文主义把希腊、拉丁文献重新带回了大学。
humanism = 人文主义
② As a humanist, she wanted a funeral with poems instead of prayers.
= 作为一个人文主义者，她希望葬礼上读诗而不是祷告。
a humanist = 人文主义者
③ The school takes a humanistic approach, putting the child's own interests first.
= 那所学校采取人本主义的做法，把孩子自己的兴趣放在首位。
a humanistic approach = 人本主义的方法

humanitarian
核心：人道主义的 —— 以减轻人类苦难为宗旨的；名词指人道主义者。
最常见的是 humanitarian aid（人道主义援助）、a humanitarian crisis（人道主义危机）。
名词 humanitarianism（人道主义）并入本条。
① Humanitarian aid reached the flooded villages a week after the disaster.
= 灾后一周，人道主义援助才送到被淹的村子。
humanitarian aid = 人道主义援助
② The war has created a humanitarian crisis on the border.
= 那场战争在边境上造成了一场人道主义危机。
a humanitarian crisis = 人道主义危机
③ She was honoured as a humanitarian who had spent forty years in refugee camps.
= 她在难民营里度过了四十年，作为一位人道主义者受到表彰。
a humanitarian = 人道主义者
④ The prize recognises humanitarianism in medicine.
= 这个奖表彰医学领域的人道主义精神。
humanitarianism = 人道主义

humanity
核心：三支：一是人类（总称，不可数）；二是人性、仁慈 —— 与 inhumanity 相对；
三是复数 the humanities：人文学科（文学、历史、哲学等）。
① The discovery could change the future of humanity.
= 这项发现可能改变人类的未来。
humanity = 人类
② The nurses treated every prisoner with humanity and patience.
= 护士们以仁慈和耐心对待每一个囚犯。
treat somebody with humanity = 仁慈地对待某人
③ She studied the humanities at university and now works in publishing.
= 她大学学的是人文学科，现在在出版社工作。
the humanities = 人文学科
④ The trial was about crimes against humanity committed during the war.
= 那场审判针对的是战争期间犯下的危害人类罪。
crimes against humanity = 危害人类罪

humanize
核心：使人性化、使更有人情味 —— 让制度、地方、形象更贴近人、更亲切。英式也拼 humanise。
① Plants and soft lighting helped to humanize the huge open-plan office.
= 绿植和柔和的灯光让那间巨大的开放式办公室多了几分人情味。
humanize something = 使某物人性化
② The documentary humanized a politician most people had only seen shouting.
= 那部纪录片让一位大多数人只见过他大喊大叫的政客显得有血有肉。
humanize somebody = 让某人显得有人情味

humankind
核心：人类 —— 比 mankind 中性，不带性别色彩。不可数。
① The invention of writing changed the history of humankind.
= 文字的发明改变了人类的历史。
humankind = 人类
② Climate change is the greatest threat humankind has faced.
= 气候变化是人类面临过的最大威胁。
the threat humankind faces = 人类面临的威胁

humanly
核心：几乎只用在 humanly possible 里：「人力所能及的」—— 常说 everything humanly possible、
as soon as humanly possible。
① The doctors did everything humanly possible to save him.
= 医生们尽了一切人力所能及的努力去救他。
everything humanly possible = 一切人力所能及的
② I'll get the report to you as soon as humanly possible.
= 我会尽快把报告交给你。
as soon as humanly possible = 尽可能快

humanoid
核心：类人的；类人机器人、类人生物 —— 外形像人的机器或（科幻里的）生物。
① The company unveiled a humanoid robot that could walk up stairs.
= 那家公司发布了一款能上楼梯的类人机器人。
a humanoid robot = 类人机器人
② In the film, a race of humanoids lives beneath the ice of a distant moon.
= 在那部电影里，一族类人生物生活在一颗遥远卫星的冰层之下。
humanoids = 类人生物
