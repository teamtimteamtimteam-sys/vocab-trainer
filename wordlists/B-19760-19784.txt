① Add a little salt to the water before you put the pasta in.
= 下意面之前往水里加一点盐。
salt = 盐（不可数）

sugar = 糖（不可数）
两者都不可数，「一点」说 a little salt、a pinch of salt，不说 a salt。
② This soup needs more salt and pepper; it tastes of nothing at all.

⑤ Nobody knew that the old man had salted away thousands of pounds under his bed.
= 谁也不知道那位老人在床底下偷偷攒了几千英镑。

/sælˈveɪʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：（宗教）拯救、救赎；（某人或某事的）救星、出路：be somebody's salvation。
① The preacher told the crowd that they could find salvation through faith and prayer.
= 传道人告诉人群，他们可以通过信仰和祈祷得到救赎。
salvation = 救赎
② After he lost his job, gardening was his salvation and kept him busy every day.
= 他失业以后，园艺成了他的救星，让他每天都有事可干。
be somebody's salvation = 是某人的救星

salve
/sælv/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：药膏、油膏：a lip salve。动词：减轻（内疚等）、使良心好受些：salve your conscience。
① She rubbed a little salve on her dry lips before she went out into the wind.
= 她出门迎着风之前，在干裂的嘴唇上抹了点润唇膏。
a lip salve = 润唇膏
salve = 药膏
② He gave a large sum to charity to salve his conscience after years of cheating his customers.
= 他多年欺骗顾客之后，捐了一大笔钱给慈善机构，好让良心好受些。
salve your conscience = 使良心好受些

= 高尔夫锦标赛的冠军获得了一个刻字托盘和一张支票。
