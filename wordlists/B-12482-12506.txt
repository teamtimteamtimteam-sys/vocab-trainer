intervention
/ˌɪntəˈvenʃən/
核心：干预、介入 —— 为改变局面而采取的行动，多说政府、军队、医疗。
名词 interventionism（干预主义）与 interventionist（干预主义的；干预主义者）并入本条。
① Military intervention may only make the conflict worse.
= 军事干预可能只会让冲突更糟。
military intervention = 军事干预
② Early intervention by teachers can help children who are falling behind.
= 老师尽早介入可以帮助掉队的孩子。
early intervention = 尽早干预
③ Critics of interventionism argue that countries should solve their own problems.
= 反对干预主义的人认为，各国应该自己解决自己的问题。
interventionism = 干预主义
④ The new president is more interventionist in foreign policy than the last.
= 新总统在外交政策上比上一任更倾向于干预。
interventionist = 干预主义的
⑤ The government followed a policy of non-intervention in the civil war across the border.
= 对边境那边的内战，政府奉行不干涉政策。
non-intervention = 不干涉

interview
/ˈɪntəvjuː/
核心：面试；采访；（警方的）问话。名词与动词同形。三支：求职、入学时的面试（a job interview）；
记者对名人的采访（give an interview）；警方对证人、嫌疑人的问话。
被面试者、受访者 interviewee、面试官或采访者 interviewer 与动名词 interviewing（面试工作）并入本条。
① She has a job interview at a bank on Monday morning.
= 她星期一上午在一家银行有个求职面试。
a job interview = 求职面试
② The singer rarely gives interviews and never talks about her family.
= 那位歌手很少接受采访，从不谈论家人。
give an interview = 接受采访
③ Police want to interview anyone who saw the accident.
= 警方想询问任何目击事故的人。
interview somebody = 询问、面谈某人
④ He was interviewed for the post of head teacher but did not get it.
= 他参加了校长职位的面试，但没有获选。
be interviewed for something = 参加…的面试
⑤ The interviewee was nervous and kept looking at the door.
= 被面试者很紧张，一直盯着门看。
an interviewee = 被面试者、受访者
⑥ A good interviewer knows when to stay silent and let people talk.
= 好的采访者知道什么时候该沉默，让对方说。
an interviewer = 采访者、面试官
⑦ The manager spends most of September interviewing for new staff.
= 经理九月的大部分时间都在为招新员工面试。
interviewing = 面试工作

interwar
/ˌɪntəˈwɔː(r)/
核心：两次世界大战之间的 —— 指一九一八年至一九三九年那段时期。
① The house was built in the interwar years, between 1918 and 1939.
= 那座房子建于两次大战之间的年代，也就是一九一八到一九三九年间。
the interwar years = 两次大战之间的年代
② Interwar Europe was marked by economic crisis and political extremism.
= 两次大战之间的欧洲以经济危机和政治极端主义为特征。
interwar Europe = 两次大战之间的欧洲

interweave
/ɪntəˈwiːv/
核心：（使）交织、交错 —— 把线编在一起；比喻不同主题、故事交织。过去式 interwove，过去分词 interwoven。
① The basket was made by interweaving strips of willow.
= 那个篮子是用柳条交织编成的。
interweave strips = 把条子交织在一起
② The novel interweaves the stories of two families over a hundred years.
= 那部小说把两个家族百年间的故事交织在一起。
interweave stories = 使故事交织

interwork
/ˌɪntəˈwɜːk/
核心：（计算机）互通、协同工作 —— 不同系统、网络之间能相互配合。
① The two phone networks can now interwork, so customers can send messages between them.
= 两个电话网络现在可以互通，客户能在两网之间发送信息。
interwork = 互通
② The new devices are designed to interwork with older equipment.
= 新设备的设计能与旧设备协同工作。
interwork with something = 与…协同工作

intestate
/ɪnˈtesteɪt/
核心：（法律）未留遗嘱的 —— 多用 die intestate（死时未立遗嘱）。名词 intestacy（未立遗嘱死亡的情况）并入本条。
① Her uncle died intestate, and the family argued about his house for years.
= 她叔叔死时没立遗嘱，家里人为他的房子争了好几年。
die intestate = 死时未立遗嘱

= 按照无遗嘱继承的规定，遗产首先归配偶所有。
the rules of intestacy = 无遗嘱继承规则

intestine
/ɪnˈtestɪn/
核心：肠 —— 消化道里胃以下的部分，常用复数 intestines；分 the small intestine（小肠）与 the large intestine（大肠）。
形容词 intestinal（肠的）与 intestinal fortitude（美，谑，勇气、胆量）并入本条。
① Food passes from the stomach into the small intestine, where most of it is absorbed.
= 食物从胃进入小肠，大部分在那里被吸收。
the small intestine = 小肠
② The surgeon removed a blocked section of the large intestine.
= 外科医生切除了大肠上堵塞的一段。
the large intestine = 大肠
③ He was treated for an intestinal infection after drinking dirty water on holiday.
= 他度假时喝了不干净的水，因肠道感染接受了治疗。
an intestinal infection = 肠道感染
④ It took real intestinal fortitude to stand up to the boss in that meeting.
= 在那次会上顶撞老板，真需要点胆量。
intestinal fortitude = （美，谑）胆量

intimacy
/ˈɪntɪməsi/

= 那家小剧院有一种大音乐厅比不上的亲切感。
an intimacy = 亲切感、私密感
说地方小而让人觉得亲近。

= 那对夫妻在餐桌上互相说着亲昵的悄悄话。
intimacies = 亲昵的言行

intimate
/ˈɪntɪmət/（形）/ˈɪntɪmeɪt/（动）

个人私事（intimate details）；（委婉）有性关系的（be intimate with somebody）；了解得透彻（an intimate knowledge）；
地方小而温馨（an intimate restaurant）。名词：密友。动词（末音节读音不同）：（正式）暗示、透露。
副词 intimately 并入本条；名词 intimation 另立。
① They have been intimate friends since their first day at school.
= 她们从上学第一天起就是亲密的朋友。

= 那本杂志刊登了那对夫妇婚姻的私密细节。
intimate details = 私密细节
③ The guide has an intimate knowledge of the mountains and every path across them.
= 那位向导对这片山区和每一条山路都了如指掌。
an intimate knowledge of something = 对…的透彻了解
④ The restaurant is small and intimate, with only eight tables.
= 那家餐馆小而温馨，只有八张桌子。
an intimate restaurant = 温馨的小餐馆
⑤ The minister was accused of being intimate with his secretary.
= 那位部长被指与秘书有染。
be intimate with somebody = 与某人有性关系（委婉）
⑥ Only a few of her intimates knew that she was ill.
= 只有她的几个密友知道她病了。
an intimate = 密友
名词，正式。
⑦ He intimated that he would be willing to resign if the report was critical.
= 他暗示说，如果报告有批评意见，他愿意辞职。
intimate that = 暗示…
动词，正式，末音节读 /meɪt/。
⑧ The two problems are intimately connected.
= 这两个问题密切相关。
intimately connected = 密切相关

/ɪnˈtɪmɪdeɪt/

into
/ˈɪntə/（弱读）/ˈɪntuː/（强读）
核心：介词，本义是「从外面进到里面」—— in 表示在里面的位置，into 表示进入的动作。
由「进入」长出许多用法：变成另一种状态（turn into、translate into）；撞上（crash into）；
朝着某方向（look into the sun）；时间上进入某段（well into the night）；
调查、研究（an inquiry into）；（口语）热衷于（be into something）；
（数学）除（three into twelve goes four）；强迫、诱使某人做某事（talk somebody into）。
① She walked into the kitchen and switched on the light.
= 她走进厨房，打开了灯。
walk into somewhere = 走进某处
② The caterpillar will turn into a butterfly in a few weeks.
= 几周后那条毛毛虫会变成一只蝴蝶。
turn into something = 变成…
③ The book has been translated into more than forty languages.
= 那本书已被译成四十多种语言。
translate something into something = 把…译成…
④ The van skidded on the ice and crashed into a wall.
= 面包车在冰上打滑，撞上了一堵墙。
crash into something = 撞上…
⑤ Don't look directly into the sun, even with sunglasses on.
= 即使戴着墨镜也别直视太阳。
look into the sun = 直视太阳
⑥ They talked well into the night and missed the last bus home.
= 他们一直聊到深夜，错过了回家的末班车。
well into the night = 直到深夜
⑦ The government has ordered an inquiry into the causes of the fire.
= 政府已下令调查那场火灾的起因。
an inquiry into something = 对…的调查
⑧ The police are looking into the complaints about the landlord.
= 警方正在调查针对那个房东的投诉。
look into something = 调查…
⑨ My son is really into football at the moment and plays every weekend.
= 我儿子现在特别迷足球，每个周末都去踢。
be into something = （口语）热衷于…
⑩ The teacher asked the class how many times three into twelve goes.
= 老师问全班，十二除以三等于几。
A into B goes C = B除以A等于C
⑪ His friends talked him into buying the expensive car.
= 他的朋友们劝他买下了那辆贵车。
talk somebody into doing something = 说服某人做…
⑫ The company got into debt after two bad years.
= 连续两年不景气之后，公司负债累累。
get into debt = 负债
