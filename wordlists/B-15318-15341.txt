nightlife
/ˈnaɪtlaɪf/
核心：夜生活：一个地方晚上可去的酒吧、夜店、演出等娱乐。不可数。
① The town is famous for its beaches by day and its nightlife after dark.
= 那座城白天以海滩出名，天黑以后以夜生活出名。
nightlife = 夜生活
② There is not much nightlife in the village apart from one pub and a chip shop.
= 村里没什么夜生活，只有一家酒馆和一家炸薯条店。
not much nightlife = 没什么夜生活

nightly
/ˈnaɪtli/
核心：两支。一是形容词，每夜的、每晚的，只作定语；二是副词，每夜、每晚：twice nightly 是每晚两场。
① The nightly news bulletin was moved from nine o'clock to ten.
= 每晚的新闻简报从九点挪到了十点。
the nightly news = 每晚的新闻
② The show is performed twice nightly, with an extra matinee on Saturdays.
= 那场演出每晚两场，星期六加演一场日场。
twice nightly = 每晚两场
③ Foxes visit the garden nightly and turn over the bins.
= 狐狸每天夜里来花园，把垃圾桶翻倒。
visit nightly = 每夜都来

nightmare
/ˈnaɪtmeə(r)/
核心：两支，可数。一是噩梦：have a nightmare；二是（口语）可怕的经历、极难应付的事或人：a nightmare journey；
somebody's worst nightmare 是某人最怕发生的事；a nightmare scenario 是最坏的情形。
形容词 nightmarish（噩梦般的）并入本条。
① He still has nightmares about the night the boat went down.
= 那条船沉没的那一夜，他至今还会梦见，吓醒过来。
have a nightmare = 做噩梦
② The child woke screaming from a nightmare and would not go back to bed.
= 那孩子从噩梦里尖叫着醒来，不肯回床上去。
wake from a nightmare = 从噩梦中醒来
③ The journey home was a nightmare, with three cancelled trains.
= 回家的那趟路简直是场噩梦，三班火车取消了。

④ Filling in the tax form is a nightmare for anyone who is self-employed.
= 对自己当老板的人来说，填税表是件要命的事。
a nightmare for somebody = 对某人是要命的事
⑤ Losing the only copy of the manuscript is every writer's worst nightmare.
= 丢掉手稿的唯一一份，是每个写作者最怕的事。
somebody's worst nightmare = 某人最怕发生的事
⑥ The nightmare scenario is that both engines fail over the sea.
= 最坏的情形是，两台发动机在海上同时失灵。
a nightmare scenario = 最坏的情形
⑦ They made a nightmarish crossing of the mountains in a blizzard.
= 他们在暴风雪中翻越了那片山，那趟路像噩梦一样。
a nightmarish crossing = 噩梦般的翻越
nightmarish = 噩梦般的
