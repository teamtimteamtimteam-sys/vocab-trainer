repairer
/rɪˈpeərə(r)/
核心：修理工、修理人：以修理某物为业的人。可数。常构成复合词：a watch repairer。
① She took her grandfather's old clock to a repairer in the market town.
= 她把爷爷的旧钟拿到集镇上的一个修理匠那里。
a repairer = 修理工
② He works as a shoe repairer in a small shop near the station.
= 他在车站附近的一家小店里当修鞋匠。
a shoe repairer = 修鞋匠

repairman
/rɪˈpeəmæn/
核心：（尤其美式）修理工、维修员：上门修理电器、机器的人。可数，复数 repairmen。
① The repairman came to fix the washing machine but could not find the fault.
= 修理工来修洗衣机，却找不出毛病。
the repairman = 修理工
② We had to wait three days for a repairman to come and look at the heating.
= 我们等了三天，才有修理工来看暖气。
wait for a repairman = 等修理工上门

reparable
/ˈrepərəbl/
核心：（正式）可补救的、可挽回的、可修复的：多说损害。反义 irreparable。
① The damage to their friendship was serious but reparable with time.
= 他们的友谊受了不轻的伤，但假以时日还可以修复。
be reparable = 可以修复
reparable = 可补救的
② The doctors said that the harm to his eyes was reparable by surgery.
= 医生们说他眼睛受的损伤可以通过手术修复。
reparable by surgery = 可通过手术修复

reparation
/ˌrepəˈreɪʃn/
核心：（正式）补偿、赔偿：make reparation for；reparations 是（战败国付的）战争赔款，复数。不可数也可数。
① The firm made reparation to the families whose homes had been flooded.
= 那家公司向房子被淹的人家作了赔偿。
make reparation to somebody = 向某人赔偿
reparation = 赔偿
② After the war the defeated country had to pay huge reparations.
= 战后，那个战败国不得不支付巨额战争赔款。
pay reparations = 支付战争赔款

repartee

= 那出戏里满是两个年轻恋人之间机智的对白。
witty repartee = 机智的对白
repartee = 妙语如珠的对话
② He was famous at the club for his quick repartee and his long stories.
= 他在俱乐部以反应敏捷的妙语和长篇故事出名。
quick repartee = 敏捷的应答

repast

① After the long walk we sat down to a simple repast of bread and cheese.

a light repast = 便餐

repatriate
/riːˈpætrieɪt/
核心：及物。一是遣返、把……送回本国：repatriate somebody to；二是把（利润、资金）调回本国。名词 repatriation，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The refugees were repatriated when the war in their country ended.
= 他们国家的战争结束后，那些难民被遣返了。
be repatriated = 被遣返
repatriate = 遣返
② The firm repatriates most of its profits to its head office in Tokyo.
= 那家公司把大部分利润调回东京总部。

③ The government has arranged the repatriation of the bodies of the soldiers.
= 政府安排把士兵们的遗体运回国。
the repatriation of something = 把某物运回本国

repay
/rɪˈpeɪ/
核心：及物，过去式、过去分词 repaid。一是偿还、归还（借的钱）：repay a loan、repay somebody；二是报答、回报：repay somebody for something；
三是（正式）值得、对……有回报：repay study。形容词 repayable 是应偿还的；名词 repayment 是偿还、还款，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① It took him ten years to repay the loan from the bank.
= 他花了十年才还清银行的贷款。
repay a loan = 还贷款
repay = 偿还
repaid = 已偿还（过去式、过去分词）
② How can I ever repay you for all your kindness?
= 你对我那么好，我怎么报答你才好？
repay somebody for something = 为某事报答某人
③ The old letters would repay careful study by anyone interested in the war.
= 对那场战争感兴趣的人，仔细研究那些旧信件会大有收获。
repay study = 值得研究
④ The loan is repayable over five years.
= 那笔贷款分五年还清。
be repayable = 应偿还
⑤ He fell behind with his repayments when he lost his job.
= 他失业后还款就跟不上了。
repayments = 还款
repayment = 偿还

repeal
/rɪˈpiːl/
核心：动词：（正式）废除、撤销（法律）。名词：废除、撤销，不可数。
① The new government promised to repeal the unpopular tax on food.
= 新政府承诺废除那项不得人心的食品税。
repeal a tax = 废除一项税
repeal = 废除
② The protesters called for the repeal of the law on public meetings.
= 抗议者要求废除那项关于公众集会的法律。

repeat
/rɪˈpiːt/
核心：动词：重复、重说、重做：repeat something、repeat yourself；（跟着）复述、跟读；（事情）再次发生、重演；（课程）重修、留级。
名词，可数：重复、重演；（电视、广播的）重播节目：a repeat of。名词 repetition 是重复，见另条；副词 repeatedly 是反复地。
① Could you repeat the question, please, because I did not hear it?
= 请把问题再说一遍好吗？我没听见。
repeat the question = 重复问题
repeat = 重复
② The teacher said each word slowly, and the children repeated it after her.
= 老师慢慢说出每个词，孩子们跟着她念。
repeat something after somebody = 跟着某人念
③ The old man keeps repeating himself, telling the same stories every day.
= 那个老人老是说车轱辘话，天天讲同样的故事。
repeat yourself = 重复自己说过的话
④ The police do not want the violence of last year to be repeated.
= 警方不希望去年的暴力事件重演。
be repeated = 重演
⑤ We do not want a repeat of the trouble we had at the last match.
= 我们不希望上一场比赛的麻烦再出现。
a repeat of something = 某事的重演
⑥ There is nothing on television tonight except repeats of old comedies.
= 今晚电视上除了老喜剧的重播没什么可看的。
repeats = 重播节目
⑦ Learning a language takes a lot of repetition of the same words.
= 学语言需要把同样的词反复说很多遍。
repetition = 重复（名词）
⑧ He was warned repeatedly about his behaviour but would not listen.
= 他一再被警告注意行为，却就是不听。
repeatedly = 反复地

repeatable
/rɪˈpiːtəbl/
核心：可重复的、能再做一次的；（话）说得出口的、可以转述的：多用于否定。
① A good experiment must be repeatable by other scientists.

not repeatable = 说不出口的

repeated

repeatedly = 一再地

repel

replace
/rɪˈpleɪs/
核心：及物。一是取代、代替：replace A with B、be replaced by；二是接替（某人的职位）：replace somebody as；三是更换、换掉（坏的、旧的东西）；四是放回原处。
注意：replace A with B 是用乙换掉甲；substitute B for A 也是用乙代替甲，两者语序相反。
① The old bridge will be replaced by a new one next year.
= 那座旧桥明年将由一座新桥取代。
be replaced by something = 被某物取代
replace = 更换
② She replaced the old curtains with bright new ones.
= 她把旧窗帘换成了鲜亮的新窗帘。
replace A with B = 用 B 换掉 A
③ Instead of replacing the butter with oil, you can substitute yoghurt for the cream.
= 与其用油换掉黄油，不如用酸奶代替奶油。
substitute B for A = 用 B 代替 A
④ He replaced his father as head of the family firm.
= 他接替父亲成为家族企业的掌门人。
replace somebody as = 接替某人
⑤ Please replace the books on the shelf when you have finished with them.
= 书看完了请放回书架上。
replace something = 把某物放回原处
⑥ We need a replacement for the broken window before the winter.
= 入冬前我们得找块玻璃换掉那扇破窗。
replacement = 替代品
