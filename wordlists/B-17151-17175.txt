pledge
/pledʒ/
核心：名词，可数：郑重的保证、誓言：a pledge of support、a pledge to do；（答应捐出的）一笔认捐款；抵押品、信物：as a pledge of。
动词：郑重保证、许诺（给予）：pledge support、pledge to do；使（某人、自己）发誓：pledge yourself to；认捐；把……抵押出去。
the pledge 是（旧）戒酒誓约：sign、take the pledge 是发誓戒酒。
① The government has given a pledge to build ten new hospitals.
= 政府保证新建十所医院。
a pledge to do something = 做某事的保证
make a pledge = 作出保证
② Listeners rang in with pledges of money for the flood victims.
= 听众打电话来为水灾灾民认捐。
pledges of money = 认捐的钱
③ He left his watch as a pledge that he would return with the rent.
= 他留下手表作抵押，保证会带着房租回来。
as a pledge = 作为抵押、信物
④ The two countries pledged their support for the peace plan.
= 两国保证支持那项和平计划。
pledge support = 保证支持
⑤ The company has pledged to cut its use of plastic by half.
= 那家公司承诺把塑料用量减一半。
pledge to do something = 承诺做某事
⑥ The members pledged themselves to secrecy.
= 成员们发誓保密。
pledge yourself to something = 发誓做到某事
⑦ His grandfather signed the pledge at twenty and never touched a drop again.
= 他爷爷二十岁发誓戒酒，从此滴酒不沾。
sign the pledge = 发誓戒酒
take the pledge = 发誓戒酒

plenary
/ˈpliːnəri/
核心：（正式）形容词，只作定语：（会议）全体出席的；（权力）完全的、不受限制的。名词：全体会议，可数，复数 plenaries。
① The vote will be taken at a plenary session of the conference on Friday.
= 表决将在星期五大会的全体会议上进行。
a plenary session = 全体会议
② The envoy was given plenary powers to sign the treaty.
= 那位特使被授予签署条约的全权。
plenary powers = 全权
③ The final plenary lasted four hours and settled nothing.
= 最后一次全体会议开了四个钟头，什么也没定下来。
a plenary = 全体会议

plenipotentiary
/ˌplenɪpəˈtenʃəri/
核心：（术语）全权代表、全权大使：有全权代表本国政府在外国办事、签约的人。可数，复数 plenipotentiaries；也作形容词。
① The treaty was signed in the palace by plenipotentiaries of the five governments after a month of talks.
= 谈了一个月之后，五国政府的全权代表在宫里签署了那项条约。
a plenipotentiary = 全权代表
② He was sent to the emperor's court as ambassador plenipotentiary with the power to end the war.
= 他被派往皇帝的朝廷担任全权大使，有权议和停战。
ambassador plenipotentiary = 全权大使
字面是「全部的权力」。

plenitude
/ˈplenɪtjuːd/
核心：（正式）丰富、充裕、大量。单数或不可数：a plenitude of。
① The valley offers a plenitude of fruit in the late summer.

plenty
/ˈplenti/
核心：代词：大量、许多、足够而有余：plenty of 加可数名词复数或不可数名词；单用：That's plenty。
副词：（口语）plenty big enough 是足够大；（美式口语）很、非常。名词：（正式）富足，不可数：a time of plenty。
① There is plenty of time, so there is no need to run.
= 时间多得是，用不着跑。
plenty of time = 时间多得是
② "Would you like some more soup?" "No, thank you, I have had plenty."
= 「再来点汤吗？」「不了，谢谢，我吃得够多了。」
have had plenty = 够多了
③ The box is plenty big enough for all your books.
= 那只箱子装你所有的书绰绰有余。
plenty big enough = 绰绰有余
④ We talked plenty about the old days on the drive home.
= 开车回家的路上，我们聊了好多从前的事。
plenty = 很多地（美式口语）
⑤ The children grew up in years of plenty and never knew hunger.
= 孩子们在富足的年月里长大，从不知道饿是什么。
years of plenty = 富足的年月
肯定句里说 plenty of；否定、疑问里多说 enough、much、many。

pleonasm

plethora

pleura

pleurisy = 胸膜炎

plod

plodding = 慢吞吞的、沉闷的

plot
/plɒt/
核心：名词，可数：阴谋、密谋：a plot to do、a plot against；（小说、戏、电影的）情节；一小块地：a vegetable plot、a burial plot。

a plot twist = 情节的转折
③ They bought a small plot of land on the edge of the village.
= 他们在村边买了一小块地。
a plot of land = 一块地
a vegetable plot = 菜地
④ The officers were accused of plotting against the president.
= 那些军官被控密谋反对总统。
plot against somebody = 密谋反对某人
plot to do something = 密谋做某事
⑤ The navigator plotted the ship's course on the chart.
= 领航员在海图上标出了船的航线。
plot a course = 标出航线
⑥ She plotted the temperatures on a graph and joined the points.
= 她把各个气温标在图上，把点连起来。

⑦ Now the gardener says that he saw two visitors, so the plot thickens.
= 这会儿园丁又说看见了两个来客，事情更蹊跷了。
the plot thickens = 事情更蹊跷了
⑧ The manager seems to have lost the plot and changes the team every week.

⑨ The plotters met in the back room of a café near the palace.
= 密谋的人在王宫附近一家咖啡馆的后屋碰头。

⑩ The film is exciting, but nobody explains the plot hole about the missing key.

plough
/plaʊ/
核心：名词：犁：牲口、拖拉机拉着翻地的农具，可数。动词：犁（地）、耕。美式拼作 plow。
短语动词：plough into 是（车）猛撞上；plough on 是硬着头皮干下去；plough through 是费力地穿过、啃完（一大堆东西）；
plough up 是把……翻起来、碾得坑坑洼洼；plough back、plough back in 是把（利润）再投进去。
习语：plough a lonely furrow 是（英式，文学）单枪匹马、独自干自己的。
派生与复合词：ploughman 是犁地的人，美式拼作 plowman；ploughman's lunch 是（英国酒吧里的）农夫午餐：面包、奶酪加腌菜，也只说 a ploughman's；
ploughshare 是犁头、犁铧：beat swords into ploughshares 是铸剑为犁。并入本条。
① The museum has a wooden plough that was pulled by two oxen.
= 博物馆里有一张由两头牛拉的木犁。
a plough = 犁
② The farmer ploughed the top field before the frost came.
= 霜冻到来之前，那个农民把坡上那块地犁了。
plough a field = 犁地
③ The lorry skidded on the ice and ploughed into a line of parked cars.
= 卡车在冰上打滑，撞上了一排停着的汽车。
plough into = 猛撞上
plough into something = 猛撞上某物
④ He knew that nobody was listening, but he ploughed on with his speech.
= 他知道没人在听，可还是硬着头皮讲下去。
plough on = 硬着头皮干下去
⑤ I ploughed through three hundred pages of the report over the weekend.
