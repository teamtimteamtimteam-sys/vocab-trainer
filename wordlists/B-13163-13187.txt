labour
/ˈleɪbə(r)/
核心：劳动、（尤指）体力活；劳动力、工人（总称）；分娩。动词：辛苦干活；吃力地行进。美式拼 labor。
英国的工党叫 Labour。名词 labourer（体力劳动者）、形容词 laboured（吃力的；不自然的）、labouring（干体力活的）并入本条。
习语：a labour of love（心甘情愿做的苦差）、labour the point（把一点说个没完）、labour under（受…之苦；抱着错误想法）。
搭配：labour force（劳动力）、labour market（劳动力市场）、labour camp（劳改营）、labour-intensive（劳动密集的）、
labour-saving（省力的）、（美）labor union（工会）。
① The price includes the cost of parts and labour.
= 这个价钱包括零件和人工费。
parts and labour = 零件和人工
② After ten years of hard manual labour, his hands were rough and scarred.
= 干了十年繁重的体力活，他的双手又粗糙又满是疤。
manual labour = 体力劳动
③ The factory moved abroad in search of cheap labour.
= 那家工厂为找廉价劳动力搬到了国外。
cheap labour = 廉价劳动力
这一支指「工人」这个总体。
④ Women now make up nearly half of the labour force.
= 如今妇女占了劳动力的将近一半。
the labour force = 劳动力
⑤ Young people are finding it hard to enter the labour market.
= 年轻人发现进入劳动力市场很难。
the labour market = 劳动力市场
⑥ She was in labour for twelve hours before the baby was born.
= 她阵痛了十二个小时孩子才出生。
in labour = 在分娩中
go into labour：开始阵痛。
⑦ Labour won the election with a majority of sixty seats.
= 工党以六十席的多数赢得了大选。
Labour = （英国）工党
⑧ He laboured in the fields from dawn until it was too dark to see.
= 他在田里从天亮一直干到天黑得看不见。
labour in the fields = 在田里干活
⑨ The old bus laboured up the steep hill with smoke pouring from the back.
= 那辆旧公交车吃力地爬上陡坡，后面直冒烟。
labour up a hill = 吃力地爬坡
⑩ Restoring the old boat was a labour of love that took him six years.
= 修复那条旧船是他心甘情愿干的苦差，花了他六年。
a labour of love = 心甘情愿做的苦差
⑪ I understand what you mean, so there is no need to labour the point.
= 我明白你的意思，不用翻来覆去地说了。
labour the point = 把一点说个没完
⑫ He was still labouring under the belief that the job was his.
= 他还一直误以为那份工作是他的。
labour under = 抱着（错误想法）；受…之苦
常说 labour under a misapprehension / delusion。
⑬ His grandfather worked as a farm labourer all his life.
= 他祖父当了一辈子农场雇工。
a labourer = 体力劳动者
a laborer = （美式拼法）体力劳动者
⑭ Her breathing was slow and laboured after the long climb.
= 爬了那么久，她的呼吸又慢又吃力。
laboured breathing = 吃力的呼吸
labored breathing = （美式拼法）吃力的呼吸
也指文章、笑话「不自然、硬挤出来的」。
⑮ He left school at fourteen and took a labouring job on a building site.
= 他十四岁离开学校，在建筑工地上找了份体力活。
a labouring job = 体力活
a laboring job = （美式拼法）体力活
⑯ Picking fruit by hand is slow, labour-intensive work.
= 用手摘水果是又慢又耗人工的活儿。
labour-intensive = 劳动密集的
⑰ The kitchen is full of labour-saving machines that she never uses.
= 厨房里摆满了省力的机器，她却从来不用。
labour-saving = 省力的
⑱ Thousands of prisoners died in the labour camps during the winter.
= 那年冬天，成千上万的囚犯死在劳改营里。

⑲ The workers at the car plant voted to join a labor union.

laconic
/ləˈkɒnɪk/
核心：话少的、简短的 —— 寥寥几个字，常带点干巴巴的幽默。副词 laconically 并入本条。
① His laconic reply to the long letter was a single word: no.
= 对那封长信，他的回复简短到只有一个词：不。
a laconic reply = 简短的回复
② "It could be worse," she said laconically, looking at the flooded kitchen.
= 她望着被水淹了的厨房，淡淡地说：“还不算最糟。”
say laconically = 淡淡地、简短地说
得名于古希腊的拉科尼亚：斯巴达人以话少出名。

lacquer
/ˈlækə(r)/

① The box is covered with many layers of black lacquer.
= 那只盒子上涂了许多层黑漆。
black lacquer = 黑漆
② She sprayed her hair with lacquer to keep it in place in the wind.
= 她往头发上喷了发胶，好让它在风里不乱。
hair lacquer = （旧）发胶
③ The table was lacquered in red and decorated with gold birds.
= 那张桌子漆成红色，上面饰有金色的鸟。
be lacquered = 上了漆
