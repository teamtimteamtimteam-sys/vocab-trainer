lateral
核心：侧面的、横向的 —— 朝向或位于旁边的。副词 laterally 并入本条。

= 那种植物从主茎上长出侧枝。
lateral shoots = 侧枝
② She made a lateral move to another department at the same salary.
= 她平调到了另一个部门，工资不变。
a lateral move = 平调
不升不降，横着走。
③ The crab moves laterally across the sand.
= 螃蟹在沙子上横着爬。
move laterally = 横着移动
④ The puzzle cannot be solved by logic alone and needs some lateral thinking.
= 那道谜题光靠逻辑解不开，得用点横向思维。
lateral thinking = 横向思维

the latest about something = …的最新进展

latex
核心：乳胶 —— 橡胶树等植物流出的白色汁液；也指人工合成的类似材料，用来做手套、油漆、床垫。
① The nurse put on a pair of latex gloves before cleaning the wound.
= 护士戴上一副乳胶手套，然后清洗伤口。

collect the latex = 收集胶乳
这一支是本义：树上流出来的汁液。

lath

lathe

Latin

④ She tried to laugh off the mistake, but her face was red.
= 她想把那个差错一笑带过，脸却红了。
laugh off = 一笑置之
⑤ When he saw my haircut, he burst out laughing.
= 他一看见我的发型，就突然大笑起来。
burst out laughing = 突然大笑起来
⑥ She gave a nervous laugh and looked at the floor.
= 她紧张地笑了一声，看着地板。
give a laugh = 笑一声
⑦ We dressed up as pirates for a laugh.
= 我们为了好玩，打扮成了海盗。
for a laugh = 为了好玩
⑧ Your uncle is a real laugh once he starts telling stories.

be a laugh = （英，口语）有趣的人或事
⑨ Losing your passport abroad is no laughing matter.

no laughing matter = 不是闹着玩的
⑩ They said her shop would fail, but she had the last laugh.

have the last laugh = 笑到最后

a laughing stock = 笑柄
⑫ The dentist gave him laughing gas before taking the tooth out.

⑬ The comedy uses a laugh track instead of a live audience.

a laugh track = （美）罐头笑声
⑭ It is a laugh-out-loud comedy that kept the whole cinema shaking.
= 那是一部让人笑出声的喜剧，整个影院都笑得前仰后合。
laugh-out-loud = 让人笑出声的

⑮ The photograph shows three laughing children on a beach.
= 照片上是海滩上三个笑着的孩子。
laughing children = 笑着的孩子

laughingly = 笑着
⑰ The room was laughingly called a hotel suite.

launderette
核心：（英）自助洗衣店 —— 投币用洗衣机和烘干机的店。美式说 laundromat。
① Students without a washing machine take their clothes to the launderette.
= 没有洗衣机的学生把衣服拿到自助洗衣店去洗。
the launderette = （英）自助洗衣店
② She read a magazine in the launderette while her washing went round.
= 衣服在机器里转的时候，她在自助洗衣店里看杂志。
in the launderette = 在自助洗衣店里
也拼 laundrette。

laundry
核心：要洗或刚洗好的衣物；洗衣店、洗衣房。a laundry list（一长串、冗长的清单）并入本条。
① There is a pile of dirty laundry on the bathroom floor.
= 浴室地板上有一堆脏衣服。
dirty laundry = 脏衣服
也比喻不可外扬的家丑。
② I do the laundry on Saturday mornings.
= 我星期六上午洗衣服。
do the laundry = 洗衣服
③ The hospital sends its sheets to a laundry in the next town.
= 那家医院把床单送到邻镇的一家洗衣店去洗。
a laundry = 洗衣店
④ The union arrived with a laundry list of complaints about pay and hours.
= 工会带着一长串关于工资和工时的不满来了。
a laundry list = 一长串、冗长的清单

laureate
核心：获奖者 —— 尤指获重大奖项或荣誉的人。常见 a Nobel laureate（诺贝尔奖得主）、the Poet Laureate（桂冠诗人）。
① The lecture will be given by a Nobel laureate in physics.
= 那场讲座将由一位诺贝尔物理学奖得主主讲。
a Nobel laureate = 诺贝尔奖得主
② The Poet Laureate wrote a poem for the royal wedding.
= 桂冠诗人为王室婚礼写了一首诗。
the Poet Laureate = 桂冠诗人
古时用月桂枝叶编冠授给优胜者，故名。

laurel
核心：月桂树 —— 叶子常绿发亮；（复数）荣誉、殊荣。
习语：rest on your laurels（躺在功劳簿上）、look to your laurels（小心被人超过）。
a laurel wreath（月桂花环）并入本条。
① A laurel hedge hides the house from the road.
= 一道月桂树篱把房子挡住，从路上看不见。
a laurel hedge = 月桂树篱
② She won the prize last year, but she is not resting on her laurels.
= 她去年得了奖，但并没有躺在功劳簿上。
rest on your laurels = 躺在功劳簿上
③ The champion will have to look to his laurels now that the young players are improving.
= 年轻选手在进步，冠军得小心别被超过了。
look to your laurels = 小心被人超过
④ The winner of the race was crowned with a laurel wreath.
= 赛跑的优胜者被戴上了月桂花环。
a laurel wreath = 月桂花环
古希腊、罗马授给胜利者的。
