preemie
/ˈpriːmi/
核心：（美式口语）早产儿。可数；也拼作 preemy；premature baby 的昵称。
① Their son was a preemie who spent his first six weeks in hospital.
= 他们的儿子是早产儿，头六个星期是在医院过的。
a preemie = 早产儿
英式口语没有对应的简称，说 premature baby。
② The nurses on the ward care for preemies who weigh less than a bag of sugar.
= 那个病房的护士照料的是体重还不到一袋糖的早产儿。
care for preemies = 照料早产儿

preen
/priːn/
核心：动词：（鸟）用嘴整理（羽毛）；（贬）（人）精心打扮、对着镜子臭美：preen yourself；沾沾自喜：preen yourself on。
① The swan sat on the bank preening its feathers in the sun.
= 天鹅蹲在岸上，在阳光里用嘴梳理羽毛。
preen its feathers = 梳理羽毛
说人是从鸟的动作引申来的。
② He spends half an hour preening himself in front of the mirror every morning.
= 他每天早上要在镜子前臭美半个钟头。
preen yourself = 精心打扮
③ The minister preened himself on the success of a plan that was not his own.
= 那位部长为一个并不是他自己的计划的成功沾沾自喜。
preen yourself on something = 为某事沾沾自喜

preface
/ˈprefəs/
核心：名词：序言、前言：书的正文之前作者写的说明，可数。
动词：给……作序；在（讲话）前面先说、先做：preface A with B、by doing。形容词 prefatory 是序言的、开场的，只作定语。并入本条。
① In the preface the author thanks the villagers who told her their stories.
= 作者在序言里感谢给她讲故事的村民。
the preface = 序言
多是作者自己写的；别人写的叫 foreword。
② He prefaced his remarks with an apology for arriving late.
= 他先为迟到道了个歉，才开始讲话。

③ After a few prefatory words from the chairman the debate began.
= 主席说了几句开场白，辩论就开始了。
prefatory = 序言的、开场的
读音是 /ˈprefəs/，不读 face。

prefer
/prɪˈfɜː(r)/
核心：更喜欢、宁愿：两样里更中意其中一样。及物，不用进行时；过去式 preferred。
句型：prefer A to B；prefer to do、prefer doing；prefer somebody to do；would prefer to do；would prefer it if。
（法律）prefer charges against 是对……提出指控。
① I prefer a pot of weak tea to strong coffee first thing in the morning.
= 一大早，比起浓咖啡我更爱喝一壶淡茶。
prefer A to B = 比起乙更喜欢甲
介词用 to，不用 than。
② She prefers to work at night when the house is quiet.
= 她宁愿夜里家里安静的时候干活。
prefer to do something = 宁愿做某事
③ He prefers walking the two miles to the office to taking the crowded bus.
= 那两英里路，比起挤公共汽车，他更愿意走着去办公室。
prefer doing A to doing B = 比起做乙更愿意做甲
④ I would prefer you not to mention this to anyone in the office until the contract is signed.
= 合同签下来之前，我希望你别跟办公室里任何人提这件事。
would prefer somebody not to do something = 希望某人别做某事
⑤ The builder said that he would prefer it if we paid in cash at the end of each week.
= 建筑工说，我们要是每到周末付现金，他会更乐意。
would prefer it if = 希望
⑥ The police decided not to prefer charges against the boy.
= 警方决定不对那男孩提出指控。
prefer charges = 提出指控

preferably = 最好

preference
/ˈprefrəns/

in preference to something = 优先于某物

/ˈpriːfɪks/

prejudice
/ˈpredʒudɪs/

④ Any delay could prejudice the child's chances of recovery.
= 任何拖延都可能损害孩子康复的机会。
prejudice somebody's chances = 损害某人的机会
⑤ The offer is made without prejudice to our client's rights.
= 提出这项提议，不损害我方当事人的权利。
without prejudice = 不损害合法权利地
⑥ He is deeply prejudiced against anyone with a foreign accent.
= 他对口音带外国腔的人成见很深。
prejudiced against somebody = 对某人有偏见
⑦ The judge ruled that the evidence was prejudicial to a fair trial.
= 法官裁定那项证据有碍公正审判。
prejudicial to something = 有损于某事
