genealogist
核心：三支。一是系谱学者、家谱研究者；二是替人查祖先的人；
三是（业余）热衷查家族史的人。
① A genealogist found nine people with a claim to the same house.
= 一位系谱研究者查出九个人对同一栋房子有主张。
a genealogist = 系谱研究者
② She hired a genealogist and got a name and a date and nothing else.
= 她雇了个家谱研究者，只得到一个名字和一个日期。
hire a genealogist = 雇家谱研究者
③ Every family has one genealogist and nobody asks for the results.
= 每家都有一个热衷查家谱的人，没人问结果。
a genealogist = 查家族史的人

genealogy
核心：三支。一是家谱、世系：trace a genealogy；二是系谱学这门学问；
三是（引申）某个观念的来龙去脉。
① Tracing a genealogy in that county means reading four hands of writing.
= 在那个郡查家谱，等于读四种笔迹。
trace a genealogy = 追查家谱
② She read genealogy at evening classes for nine years.
= 她在夜校学了九年系谱学。
read genealogy = 学系谱学
③ The genealogy of that word ends in a language nobody speaks.
= 那个词的来龙去脉，止于一门没人说的语言。
the genealogy of something = 某物的源流

genera
核心：三支（生物学，genus 的复数）。一是若干属；
二是分类表里属这一层级；三是同科之下的各属。
① Two genera in that family look identical until you count the legs.
= 那个科里的两个属，不数腿就看不出分别。
genera = 属（复数）
② The key separates the genera and leaves the species to somebody else.
= 那份检索表分到属为止，种留给别人去分。
separate the genera = 区分各属
③ Four new genera were named in one paper and three were merged later.
= 一篇论文里命名了四个新属，后来三个被并掉。
new genera = 新属

general
核心：五支。一是总体的、普遍的：a general rule；二是笼统的、不具体的；
三是总的、全面的：general knowledge；四是（军衔）将军；
五是习语 in general，一般来说。副词 generally 并入本条。
① As a general rule they answer letters and never answer the phone.
= 一般而言，他们回信，从不接电话。
as a general rule = 按一般规矩
general knowledge = 常识
② The answer was general enough to fit any of the four questions.
= 那个回答笼统得四个问题都套得上。
be general = 笼统
③ A general election was called in October and held in one day.
= 十月宣布大选，一天之内投完。
a general election = 大选
④ The general arrived by helicopter and left before the speeches.
= 将军坐直升机来，讲话还没开始就走了。
a general = 将军
⑤ In general the trains run and in that weather they do not.
= 一般来说火车是通的，那种天气就不通。
in general = 一般来说
副词 generally：通常、大体上。
⑥ It generally rains on that day and generally nobody plans for it.
= 那一天通常下雨，也通常没人为此做准备。
generally = 通常

generalissimo
核心：三支。一是大元帅，统领全军的最高统帅；
二是（历史）某些政权里那个头衔；三是（引申，贬）说一不二的当权者。
① A generalissimo commands every branch and answers to one person.
= 大元帅统辖各军种，只对一个人负责。
a generalissimo = 大元帅
② The title of generalissimo was created for him and died with him.
= 大元帅这个头衔是为他设的，也随他消失。
the title of generalissimo = 大元帅头衔
③ The department has a generalissimo and nine people who agree with him.
= 那个部门有个说一不二的头儿，外加九个附和的人。
a generalissimo = 说一不二的当权者

generalist
核心：三支。一是通才、什么都懂一点的人；二是（医学）全科医生；
三是形容词性，通才路线的。
① A generalist in that team knows enough to ask the right specialist.
= 队里那位通才懂得够多，知道该去问哪个专家。
a generalist = 通才
② The generalist sees the patient first and decides where they go next.
= 全科医生先看，再决定病人往哪儿转。
a generalist = 全科医生
③ A generalist training takes longer and closes fewer doors.
= 通才式的培养耗时更长，关上的门更少。
a generalist training = 通才式培养

generality
核心：三支。一是笼统的说法、泛泛之论：speak in generalities；
二是（正式）大多数、大部分：the generality of something；三是普遍性。
① He speaks in generalities and never names a single figure.
= 他净说空泛的话，一个数字都不提。
speak in generalities = 泛泛而谈
② The generality of people in that survey had never used the service.
= 那次调查里的大多数人，从没用过那项服务。
the generality of people = 大多数人
③ The generality of the rule is what makes it useless in practice.
= 那条规则太普泛，用起来才没用。
the generality of a rule = 规则的普泛

generalization
核心：三支。一是概括、归纳：a broad generalization；
二是（贬）以偏概全的说法；三是（学术）把结论推广到更大范围。
① A broad generalization at the start of that book undoes the rest of it.
= 那本书开头一个大而化之的概括，把后面全毁了。
a broad generalization = 大而化之的概括
② It is a generalization and the four exceptions are in the footnotes.
= 那是个以偏概全的说法，四个例外都在脚注里。
be a generalization = 以偏概全
③ The generalization from nine cases to a whole country failed twice.
= 从九个案例推及全国，这种推广失败过两次。
generalization from something = 由某物作出的推广

generalize
核心：三支。一是概括、归纳：generalize from something；
二是（贬）以偏概全；三是（正式）把结论推广开。也拼作 generalise。
① You cannot generalize from four people who all live on the same street.
= 四个住同一条街的人，概括不出什么来。
generalize from something = 由某事归纳
② She generalizes about drivers and drives worse than any of them.
= 她对司机一概而论，自己开得比谁都差。
generalize about something = 对某事一概而论
③ The result generalizes to any surface with the same property.
= 那个结论可以推广到任何有同样性质的曲面。
generalize to something = 推广到某范围

generalized
核心：三支。一是普遍的、全身性的：generalized pain；
二是笼统的、不具体的：a generalized statement；三是（数学）广义的。
① Generalized pain is harder to place than pain in one spot.
= 全身性的疼痛，比一处疼更难定位。
generalized pain = 全身性疼痛
② A generalized statement about the whole trade got him four letters.
= 一句对整个行当的笼统断言，给他招来四封信。
a generalized statement = 笼统的断言
③ The generalized form covers the earlier result as one special case.
= 广义的形式把先前的结论当成一个特例包含进去。
the generalized form = 广义形式
