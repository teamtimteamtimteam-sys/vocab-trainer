scuttlebutt
/ˈskʌtlbʌt/
核心：名词，不可数（北美口语）：小道消息、流言。原指船上的饮水桶，水手们在桶边闲聊。
① The scuttlebutt in the office is that the boss is going to retire next year.
= 办公室里的小道消息说老板明年要退休了。
the scuttlebutt = 小道消息
② Don't believe all the scuttlebutt you hear around the school about the new teacher.
= 别全信你在学校里听到的关于那个新老师的流言。
believe the scuttlebutt = 相信小道消息

scuzzy
/ˈskʌzi/
核心：形容词（北美俚语）：肮脏的、邋遢的、令人恶心的。比较级 scuzzier。
① We spent the night in a scuzzy motel with dirty sheets and a broken television.
= 我们在一家肮脏的汽车旅馆过了一夜，床单脏兮兮的，电视也坏了。
a scuzzy motel = 肮脏的汽车旅馆
scuzzy = 肮脏的
② The bar was dark and scuzzy, but the music was the best in town.
= 那家酒吧又暗又邋遢，可音乐是镇上最好的。
dark and scuzzy = 又暗又邋遢

scythe
/saɪð/
核心：名词，可数：长柄大镰刀：割草或收割庄稼用。动词：用大镰刀割；（比喻）（像镰刀一样）横扫、穿过。
① The old farmer cut the long grass in the orchard with a scythe, swinging it slowly from side to side.
= 老农用长柄大镰刀割果园里的长草，慢慢左右挥动。
a scythe = 长柄大镰刀
② In the last minute, the winger scythed through the defence and scored a brilliant goal.
= 最后一分钟，那名边锋像镰刀一样切入防线，打进了一记漂亮的进球。
scythe through something = 横扫穿过某处

sea
/siː/
核心：名词：海、海洋（可数或不可数，常作 the sea）：swim in the sea、by the sea；（专名）……海：the Black Sea；（单数）（海面的）状况：a calm / rough sea；a sea of something 是大量的、一大片。和 ocean 的分别：ocean 指大洋，比 sea 更大。
习语：at sea 是在海上航行（不加 the），也指茫然不知所措；by sea 是走海路；go to sea 是去当水手。
① The children ran across the sand and jumped into the sea, shouting with excitement.
= 孩子们跑过沙滩跳进海里，兴奋地大喊大叫。
sea = 海
泛指大海时说 the sea；作定语表示「海上的、海里的」，构成一大族复合词。
② My grandparents live in a little white house by the sea in Cornwall.
= 我祖父母住在康沃尔海边的一座小白房子里。
by the sea = 在海边
近义对照：
ocean = 大洋（比海更大）
注意带 the；去掉 the 的 by sea 是「走海路」，意思完全不同。
③ The ship had been at sea for three months when the sailors finally saw land.
= 那艘船在海上航行了三个月，水手们才终于看到陆地。
at sea = 在海上（不加 the）
加了 the 的 at the sea 是「在海边」，意思不同。
④ I am completely at sea with this new computer program; can you show me how it works?
= 这个新电脑程序我完全摸不着头脑，你能教我怎么用吗？
be at sea = 茫然不知所措
⑤ The heavy furniture was sent to Australia by sea, while we flew there by plane.
= 笨重的家具走海路运到澳大利亚，我们自己坐飞机去。
by sea = 走海路
⑥ The sea was so rough that half the passengers on the ferry were sick.
= 海浪太大，渡轮上一半的乘客都吐了。
a rough sea = 风浪大的海面
⑦ When I looked out from the stage, I saw a sea of faces staring back at me.
= 我从台上往下看，只见黑压压一片面孔盯着我。
a sea of something = 一大片、大量
⑧ The town is only two metres above sea level, so it often floods in winter.
= 那座小镇只比海平面高两米，所以冬天常发洪水。
sea level = 海平面
说海拔高度用 above sea level，不加冠词。
⑨ A group of sea lions lay on the rocks in the sun, barking at the passing boats.
= 一群海狮躺在岩石上晒太阳，冲着经过的船只叫。
a sea lion = 海狮
⑩ The sea cow feeds on underwater plants in warm shallow water near the coast.
= 海牛在近海温暖的浅水里以水下植物为食。
a sea cow = 海牛
⑪ In the market we saw dried sea cucumber, which is very expensive in some countries.
= 我们在集市上看到了干海参，在有些国家它非常昂贵。
sea cucumber = 海参
⑫ Be careful not to step on a sea urchin when you walk among the rocks.
= 在礁石间走的时候小心别踩到海胆。
a sea urchin = 海胆
⑬ A sea turtle came up onto the beach at night to lay her eggs in the sand.
= 一只海龟夜里爬上海滩，在沙里产卵。
a sea turtle = 海龟
⑭ Most of the sea fish sold in the market were caught that morning by local boats.
= 市场上卖的海鱼大多是当地渔船当天早上捕到的。
sea fish = 海鱼
⑮ Sprinkle a little sea salt on the tomatoes before you serve them.
= 上桌前在番茄上撒一点海盐。
sea salt = 海盐
⑯ The doctor told my grandfather that the fresh sea air would be good for his chest.
= 医生告诉我祖父，新鲜的海边空气对他的肺有好处。
sea air = 海边的空气
⑰ A cool sea breeze blew across the beach in the late afternoon.
= 傍晚时分，凉爽的海风吹过海滩。
a sea breeze = 海风
⑱ A thick sea fret rolled in from the water, and we could not see the end of the pier.
= 浓浓的海雾从海面涌来，我们连码头的尽头都看不见了。
a sea fret = （英式）海雾
⑲ She painted the bedroom walls a soft sea green and put white curtains at the window.
= 她把卧室的墙刷成了柔和的海绿色，窗上挂了白窗帘。
sea green = 海绿色
⑳ The narrow strait is one of the busiest sea lanes in the world.
= 那条狭窄的海峡是世界上最繁忙的航道之一。
a sea lane = 航道
㉑ The little island is about twenty sea miles from the mainland, or two hours by ferry.
= 那座小岛离大陆约二十海里，坐渡轮要两个小时。
a sea mile = 海里
㉒ In the eighteenth century, Britain was the greatest sea power in the world.
= 十八世纪时，英国是世界上最强大的海上强国。
a sea power = 海上强国
㉓ The town built a new sea wall to protect the houses from winter storms.
= 那座小镇修了一道新防波堤，保护房屋免受冬季风暴的侵袭。
a sea wall = 防波堤
㉔ The old sea captain had sailed around the world six times before he retired.
= 那位老船长退休前环游世界航行过六次。
a sea captain = 船长
㉕ My grandfather was an old sea dog who loved to tell stories about storms and pirates.
= 我祖父是个老水手，爱讲风暴和海盗的故事。
an old sea dog = 老水手
㉖ The men in the pub sang a sea shanty about sailors who were far from home.
= 酒馆里的男人们唱了一首关于远离家乡的水手的号子。
a sea shanty = 水手号子
㉗ The children learned an old sea chantey that sailors sang while they pulled the ropes.
= 孩子们学了一首水手拉绳时唱的老号子。
a sea chantey = 水手号子（sea shanty 的另一种说法）
㉘ It took me three days on the boat to get my sea legs, but after that I felt fine.
= 我在船上过了三天才适应了颠簸，之后就没事了。
get your sea legs = 适应海上颠簸
㉙ There has been a sea change in the way people shop since the internet arrived.
= 自从有了互联网，人们的购物方式发生了巨大变化。
a sea change = 巨变
出自莎士比亚，指彻底而深刻的转变，比 a big change 正式。

/siːmd/
核心：形容词：有接缝的：seamed stockings；（脸）布满皱纹的、沟壑纵横的。
① The old fisherman's face was brown and seamed from years of wind and sun.
= 老渔夫的脸被多年的风吹日晒弄得黝黑，布满了皱纹。
a seamed face = 布满皱纹的脸
seamed = 布满皱纹的
② She wore seamed stockings with a black line running up the back of each leg.
= 她穿着有后缝的长筒袜，每条腿背面都有一道黑线。
seamed stockings = 有后缝的长筒袜

seamless
/ˈsiːmləs/
核心：形容词：无缝的、浑然一体的；（过程）顺畅的、天衣无缝的：a seamless transition。副词 seamlessly 是天衣无缝地、顺畅地。
① The change from the old computer system to the new one was seamless, and nobody noticed any problems.
= 从旧电脑系统换到新系统非常顺畅，谁都没察觉到任何问题。
seamless = 顺畅的
a seamless change = 顺畅的转换
② The new building fits seamlessly into the old street, as if it had always been there.
= 那栋新楼和老街浑然一体，好像一直就在那儿。
seamlessly = 天衣无缝地
