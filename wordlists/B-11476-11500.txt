Hun
核心：匈人 —— 四、五世纪入侵欧洲的游牧民族，首字母大写，多用复数 the Huns。
一战、二战时英国人也用它蔑称德国人，带冒犯色彩，今天只在历史语境里读得到。
① The Huns swept across Europe under Attila in the fifth century.
= 五世纪时，匈人在阿提拉率领下横扫欧洲。
the Huns = 匈人
② Old wartime posters warned that the Hun was at the gate.
= 旧时的战时海报警告说「德国佬已经打到门口了」。
the Hun = 德国佬（旧，蔑称）

hunch
核心：名词是直觉、预感 —— have a hunch that…、act on a hunch；
动词是弓起背、缩起肩膀（hunch over something）。形容词 hunched（弓着背的）并入本条。
① I had a hunch she would turn up in the end, and she did.
= 我预感她最后会来，她果然来了。
have a hunch that = 预感、有直觉
② The detective acted on a hunch and searched the garden shed.
= 那个侦探凭直觉行事，搜查了花园里的小棚子。
act on a hunch = 凭直觉行事
③ He hunched over his desk, writing furiously until midnight.
= 他弓着背伏在书桌上，奋笔疾书到半夜。
hunch over something = 弓着背伏在…上
④ She sat hunched against the cold at the bus stop.
= 她在公交站缩着身子抵挡寒冷。
hunched = 弓着背的、缩成一团的

hunchback
核心：（旧，冒犯）驼背的人；驼背 —— 今天指人时视为不礼貌，医学上说 kyphosis。
形容词 hunchbacked 并入本条。
① In the old novel the bell-ringer is a lonely hunchback who lives in the tower.
= 在那部旧小说里，敲钟人是个住在钟楼里的孤独驼子。
a hunchback = 驼背的人（旧，冒犯）
② The painting shows a hunchbacked old woman carrying firewood.
= 那幅画画的是一个背着柴、驼着背的老妇人。
hunchbacked = 驼背的

hundred
核心：百。前面要加 a 或数字：a hundred、two hundred；数字后面不加 s（three hundred people）。
复数 hundreds 表示「好几百、许许多多」：hundreds of people。
序数词 hundredth（第一百；百分之一）并入本条。
英式的 hundreds and thousands 是撒在蛋糕上的彩色糖粒，也收在这里。
① The hall holds about a hundred people, so we'll need a bigger room.
= 大厅大约能坐一百人，所以我们需要一个更大的房间。
a hundred = 一百
② Three hundred families lost their homes in the flood.
= 三百户人家在洪水中失去了家园。
three hundred = 三百
数字后面的 hundred 不加 s。
③ Hundreds of people queued all night for tickets.
= 几百人通宵排队买票。
hundreds of something = 好几百、许许多多
④ The town celebrated its hundredth birthday with a parade.
= 小镇举行游行，庆祝建镇一百周年。
the hundredth = 第一百
⑤ The race was decided by a hundredth of a second.
= 那场比赛的胜负只差百分之一秒。
a hundredth = 百分之一
⑥ The cake was covered in hundreds and thousands for the children's party.
= 为了孩子们的派对，蛋糕上撒满了彩色糖粒。
hundreds and thousands = 彩色糖粒（英式）

hundredweight
核心：英担 —— 重量单位，英制为 112 磅，美制为 100 磅。复数不变。
① The farmer sold three hundredweight of potatoes at the market.
= 农夫在集市上卖了三英担土豆。
a hundredweight = 英担
② Sacks of coal were delivered by the hundredweight every winter.
= 每年冬天，成袋的煤都按英担送来。
by the hundredweight = 按英担

hung
核心：hang 的过去式与过去分词（「吊死」义用 hanged）。单立的是它另有的形容词用法：
a hung parliament（悬浮议会，没有一党过半）、a hung jury（无法达成一致的陪审团）；
口语 be hung up on / about something（对某事耿耿于怀、放不下）。
① The election produced a hung parliament, and talks went on for weeks.
= 选举产生了一个悬浮议会，谈判持续了好几个星期。
a hung parliament = 悬浮议会
② The jury was hung after three days, so the trial had to be held again.
= 陪审团三天都没能达成一致，审判只好重来。
a hung jury = 无法达成一致的陪审团
③ He's still hung up on his ex-girlfriend, two years after they split.
= 分手两年了，他还对前女友念念不忘。
be hung up on somebody = 对某人念念不忘
④ They hung the paintings in the hall before the guests arrived.
= 客人到之前，他们把画挂在了大厅里。
hung = 挂（hang 的过去式）

Hungarian

① They ordered a Hungarian goulash and a bottle of red wine.

Hungarian = 匈牙利的
② She is learning Hungarian, which she says is harder than Japanese.

Hungarian = 匈牙利语
③ A Hungarian won the chess tournament for the third year running.

a Hungarian = 匈牙利人

hunger
核心：饥饿（A 表教过）；饥荒、饥饿状态；比喻「渴望」—— a hunger for something。
动词 hunger for / after something（渴望，书面）。
复合词 hunger strike（绝食抗议）与 hunger striker 并入本条。
① Thousands of children still die of hunger every year.
= 每年仍有成千上万的儿童死于饥饿。
die of hunger = 饿死
② By the evening they were weak with hunger.
= 到了傍晚，他们饿得浑身无力。
be weak with hunger = 饿得无力
③ Her hunger for knowledge kept her reading late into the night.
= 她对知识的渴望让她每晚读到深夜。
a hunger for something = 对…的渴望
④ He hungered for recognition all his life and never got it.
= 他一辈子渴望得到认可，却始终没有得到。
hunger for something = 渴望某物
⑤ The old poet hungered after the fame his younger rival enjoyed.
= 那位老诗人渴望得到他年轻对手所享有的名声。
hunger after something = 渴望某物（书面）
⑥ Twenty prisoners went on hunger strike to protest against conditions in the jail.
= 二十名囚犯绝食抗议监狱条件。
go on hunger strike = 绝食抗议
⑦ A hunger striker was taken to hospital after forty days without food.
= 一名绝食者四十天没进食，被送进了医院。
a hunger striker = 绝食抗议者

hungover
核心：宿醉的 —— 前一晚喝多了，第二天头疼、恶心。只作表语。
① He turned up to work hungover and fell asleep in the meeting.
= 他宿醉未醒地来上班，开会时睡着了。
be hungover = 宿醉
② Half the wedding guests looked hungover at breakfast the next morning.
= 第二天早饭时，一半的婚礼宾客看上去都是宿醉的样子。
look hungover = 看上去宿醉未醒

hungry
核心：饿的（A 表教过）；比喻渴望的 —— hungry for something。
go hungry 是「挨饿」。副词 hungrily（饥饿地；渴望地）并入本条。
① I'm hungry, so let's stop for lunch at the next village.
= 我饿了，到下一个村子停下来吃午饭吧。
be hungry = 饿
② Many families go hungry at the end of the month when the money runs out.
= 许多家庭到了月底钱用完了就得挨饿。
go hungry = 挨饿
③ The young players are hungry for success and train every day.
= 年轻球员渴望成功，每天都在训练。
be hungry for something = 渴望某物
④ The dog watched hungrily as we ate our sandwiches.
= 我们吃三明治时，那条狗眼巴巴地看着。
hungrily = 饥饿地、眼巴巴地

hunk
核心：一是一大块（面包、奶酪、肉）；二是（口语）健壮性感的男人。
① He cut a hunk of bread and a piece of cheese for his lunch.
= 他切了一大块面包和一块奶酪当午饭。
a hunk of something = 一大块…
② The new lifeguard is a real hunk, and the pool has never been so busy.
= 新来的救生员是个猛男，游泳池从来没这么热闹过。
a hunk = 健壮性感的男人（口语）

hunker
核心：几乎只用在 hunker down 里：一是蹲下；二是（为了躲避或熬过难关）缩在一处、坚守不出；
三是埋头准备（hunker down for a long winter）。
名词复数 hunkers 只用在 on your hunkers（蹲着）里。
① He hunkered down beside the fire to warm his hands.
= 他在火边蹲下来烤手。
hunker down = 蹲下
② The government hunkered down and waited for the scandal to pass.
= 政府低调应对，等着丑闻过去。
hunker down = 缩起来、熬过去
③ The children sat on their hunkers, watching the ants carry crumbs.
= 孩子们蹲着，看蚂蚁搬面包屑。
on your hunkers = 蹲着

hunky
核心：（口语）健壮性感的 —— 说男人。美式的 hunky-dory（一切顺利、好得很）并入本条。
① A hunky actor was cast as the new detective.
= 一位健壮性感的演员被选来演新的侦探。
hunky = 健壮性感的
② Everything seemed hunky-dory until the bank called about the loan.
= 一切看上去都好得很，直到银行打电话来说贷款的事。
hunky-dory = 一切顺利、好得很（口语）

hunt
核心：本义是打猎 —— 追捕并猎杀野兽；由此长出「搜寻、寻找」：hunt for something、
job-hunting（找工作，A 表教过）；以及「追捕（人）」：hunt down（追捕到）、a manhunt。
名词 a hunt：一次打猎、一场搜寻；英式里 the hunt 还指猎狐的一伙人。
短语：hunt out（翻找出来）、hunt-and-peck（一指禅打字）。
① They go hunting in the hills every autumn and come back with nothing.
= 他们每年秋天都去山里打猎，每次都空手而归。
go hunting = 去打猎
② Lions usually hunt at night and rest in the heat of the day.
= 狮子通常夜里捕猎，白天最热时休息。
hunt = 捕猎
③ I've been hunting for my car keys all morning.
= 我找车钥匙找了一上午。
hunt for something = 寻找某物
找得再仔细些就说 hunt high and low for something（翻箱倒柜地找）。
④ He spent six months job-hunting before a small firm took him on.
= 他找了六个月工作，才被一家小公司录用。
job-hunting = 找工作
⑤ The police hunted down the gang within a week of the robbery.
= 抢劫发生一周内，警方就把那伙人追捕归案了。
hunt down somebody = 追捕到某人
⑥ She hunted out her old school photos for the reunion.
= 为了同学聚会，她把旧的学校照片翻了出来。
hunt out something = 翻找出某物
⑦ The hunt for the missing boy went on through the night.
= 搜寻失踪男孩的行动持续了一整夜。
the hunt for somebody = 对某人的搜寻
⑧ He types by hunt-and-peck, one finger at a time, and is surprisingly fast.
= 他用一指禅打字，一次一根手指，却快得出奇。
hunt-and-peck = 一指禅打字

hunted

hunter

hunting

hurdle

④ The plan fell at the first hurdle when the bank refused the loan.

⑤ She was a promising hurdler before a knee injury ended her career.
= 她曾是个前途看好的跨栏运动员，后来一次膝伤结束了她的运动生涯。
a hurdler = 跨栏运动员
⑥ Hurdling needs speed and perfect timing more than strength.
= 跨栏靠的是速度和完美的节奏，而不是力量。
hurdling = 跨栏运动
