fascist
核心：法西斯主义者、法西斯的：名词与形容词同形；
小写时也拿来骂人专横。可数。
① A fascist party won nine seats and lost all of them four years later.
= 一个法西斯政党赢了九个席位，四年后全丢光。
a fascist party = 法西斯政党
② She called the new dress code fascist and went on wearing what she liked.
= 她说新的着装规定专横，照旧穿自己爱穿的。
a fascist = 法西斯分子

fashion
核心：三支。一是时尚、流行：in fashion、out of fashion，不可数；
二是时装业：the fashion industry；三是（正式）方式，
after a fashion 是「勉强算是」。形容词 fashionable。
① Wide collars are back in fashion and the old photographs are suddenly useful.
= 宽领子又流行起来，那些老照片忽然有了用处。
in fashion = 流行
out of fashion = 过时
② The fashion industry runs about six months ahead of the weather.
= 时装业比天气大约提前半年。
the fashion industry = 时装业
③ He fixed it after a fashion and told everybody not to touch it.
= 他勉强算是修好了，还叮嘱谁都别碰。
after a fashion = 勉强算是
④ The fashion designer sat through her own fashion show without once looking up.
= 那位时装设计师坐看自己的时装秀，一次也没抬头。
a fashion designer = 时装设计师
a fashion show = 时装秀
⑤ A fashion-conscious teenager and a fashion victim are two stops apart.
= 讲究穿着的少年，跟盲目追潮流的人，隔着两站地。
fashion-conscious = 讲究穿着的
a fashion victim = 盲目追潮流的人
⑥ The coat is fashion-forward and makes a fashion statement nobody asked for.
= 那件大衣走在潮流前面，摆出一个没人想看的时尚宣言。
fashion-forward = 引领潮流的
a fashion statement = 时尚宣言

fashionable
核心：时髦的、时兴的：说衣着、说地段，也说某种观点一时受宠。
反义 unfashionable，副词 fashionably。
① The street became fashionable and the bakery became a gallery.
= 那条街时髦起来，面包房变成了画廊。
fashionable = 时髦的
② It is fashionable to blame the software and nobody reads the log.
= 如今流行怪软件，日志倒没人看。
it is fashionable to do something = 眼下流行做某事
副词 fashionably：时髦地：多说 fashionably dressed； fashionably late 指故意晚到一会儿，算是一种派头。
③ She arrived fashionably late and found the buffet already cleared away.
= 她时髦地晚到了一会儿，自助餐已经撤了。
fashionably late = 故意晚到
④ The room is fashionably bare and completely impossible to work in.
= 那房间空得很时髦，根本没法在里头干活。
fashionably dressed = 穿得时髦
构词：fashionable 加 -ly

fashionista
核心：（非正式，常带调侃）时尚达人：紧跟潮流、也确实懂门道的人。可数。
① The fashionistas in the front row leave before the last three looks.
= 前排那些时尚达人，最后三套造型还没出来就走了。
a fashionista = 时尚达人
② Every office has one fashionista and one person in the same jumper all year.
= 每间办公室都有一个时尚达人，还有一个一年到头同一件毛衣的人。

fast
核心：四支。一是快的、快地：a fast train、run fast；
二是牢固地、紧紧地：hold fast、stuck fast；三是（钟表）走快了；
四是禁食、斋戒（动词与名词）：break your fast。
习语 fast asleep 是睡得很沉，play fast and loose 是玩弄手段、出尔反尔。
① The fast train stops twice and the slow one stops eleven times.
= 快车停两站，慢车停十一站。
a fast train = 快车
② The lid is stuck fast and the tin is older than the cupboard.
= 那个盖子卡死了，罐头比柜子还老。
stuck fast = 卡死了
hold fast = 抓牢
③ The kitchen clock is ten minutes fast and nobody bothers to adjust it.
= 厨房那只钟快十分钟，谁也懒得去调。
be ten minutes fast = 快十分钟
④ They fast from dawn to sunset and break the fast with dates and water.
= 他们从破晓斋戒到日落，用椰枣和水开斋。
break your fast = 开斋
⑤ The baby was fast asleep before the car had left the drive.
= 车还没开出车道，孩子就睡熟了。
fast asleep = 睡得很沉
⑥ The company played fast and loose with the deadlines and lost the contract.
= 那家公司拿交期玩花样，把合同弄丢了。
play fast and loose = 玩弄手段、出尔反尔
⑦ A fast bowler needs a long run-up and a very forgiving back.
= 快速投球手要有长助跑，还要有一副经得起折腾的腰。
a fast bowler = 板球里的快速投球手
⑧ The fast break in the third quarter turned an eight-point gap into two.
= 第三节的一次快攻，把八分的差距变成了两分。
a fast break = 篮球里的快攻
⑨ Fast food is cheap at the counter and expensive a long time later.
= 快餐在柜台上便宜，很久以后才见代价。
fast food = 快餐
⑩ She was put on the fast track and handed the work nobody else wanted.
= 她被放进快速晋升通道，接手的是别人都不愿干的活。
the fast track = 快速晋升通道
the fast lane = 快车道
⑪ Fast forward two years and the fast-paced series already has three spin-offs.
= 快进两年，那部节奏明快的剧集已经有了三部衍生剧。
fast forward = 快进
fast-paced = 节奏明快的
⑫ A fast breeder makes more fuel than it burns and more paperwork than either.
= 快中子增殖堆产出的燃料比烧掉的多，产出的文书比这两样还多。
a fast breeder = 快中子增殖反应堆

fastball
核心：（棒球）快速球：投得最快、走得最直的那种球。可数。
① His fastball is down to eighty-eight and his control is better than ever.
= 他的快速球降到八十八英里，控球却比以往都好。
a fastball = 快速球
② She hit the fastball and watched two outfielders run the wrong way.
= 她打中那记快速球，看着两名外野手往错的方向跑。
throw a fastball = 投出快速球

fasten
核心：三支。一是扣紧、系上：fasten your seat belt；二是闩牢门窗；
三是 fasten on something，盯住某一点不放。名词 fastener 与 fastening。
① Fasten your seat belt before the sign goes off, not after it.
= 安全带要在指示灯灭之前系好，不是灭了才系。
fasten your seat belt = 系好安全带
② The gate fastens with a loop of blue rope and always has.
= 那道门是用一圈蓝绳系住的，一向如此。
fasten a gate = 把门闩好
③ He fastened on the one figure in the report that nobody could source.
= 他抓住报告里唯一一个查不到出处的数字不放。
fasten on something = 盯住某点不放

fastener
核心：扣件：把两片东西连起来的小物件，扣子、按扣、拉链都算。可数。
① The fastener on that bag has outlived two shoulder straps.
= 那只包上的扣件，比两条背带活得都长。
a fastener = 扣件
② Every fastener on the coat is a different size and all of them still work.
= 那件大衣上每个扣件尺寸都不一样，倒是都还能用。
a zip fastener = 拉链
a press fastener = 按扣

fastening
核心：（英式）扣合装置：门窗上的插销、衣物上的搭扣与拉链。可数。
① The window fastening is painted shut and the window itself is not.
= 那扇窗的插销被漆死了，窗本身倒没事。
a window fastening = 窗户插销
② The dress has a fastening at the back that needs a second pair of hands.
= 那条裙子背后的扣合，非得有第二双手帮忙。
a fastening = 扣合装置

fastidious
核心：（可褒可贬）一丝不苟的、挑剔的：对细节、整洁、用词极其讲究。
副词 fastidiously，名词 fastidiousness。
① He is fastidious about commas and remarkably careless about deadlines.
= 他对逗号一丝不苟，对交期倒马虎得很。
be fastidious about something = 对某事讲究挑剔
② A fastidious editor saved that book from three separate lawsuits.
= 一位一丝不苟的编辑，让那本书躲过了三场官司。
a fastidious editor = 一丝不苟的编辑
副词 fastidiously：一丝不苟地、讲究地：多修饰 tidy、clean、dressed、arranged。
③ The workshop is fastidiously tidy and the office next door is chaos.
= 那间工场收拾得一丝不苟，隔壁办公室一片狼藉。
fastidiously tidy = 一丝不苟地整洁
④ She fastidiously separated the receipts by month and then by colour.
= 她一丝不苟地把收据先按月份、再按颜色分开。
fastidiously separate something = 一丝不苟地把某物分开
构词：fastidious 加 -ly

fastidiousness
核心：一丝不苟、讲究到极点：不可数。褒时是细致，贬时是吹毛求疵。
① His fastidiousness about the accounts is why the audit took only two days.
= 他在账目上的一丝不苟，正是审计只用两天的缘故。
fastidiousness = 一丝不苟
fastidiousness about detail = 对细节的讲究
② Fastidiousness in a kitchen is a virtue and in a hurry is a problem.
= 厨房里讲究是美德，赶时间的时候就成了麻烦。
构词：fastidious 加 -ness

fastness
核心：两支。一是（书面）要塞、易守难攻的据点：a mountain fastness；
二是（染料的）牢度：colour fastness 指不易褪色。
① The mountain fastness held out for a winter and fell to hunger.
= 那座山中要塞守了一整个冬天，最后败给了饥饿。
a mountain fastness = 山中要塞
② Colour fastness is tested by washing the same sample forty times.
= 色牢度是把同一块样品洗四十次测出来的。
colour fastness = 色牢度

fat
核心：四支。一是胖的：说人时当心，客气些用 overweight；
二是脂肪、油脂，不可数：low in fat；三是厚厚的、鼓鼓的：a fat envelope；
四是（口语）大笔的：a fat fee。习语 chew the fat 是闲聊，
a fat lot of good 是反话，「一点用没有」。
① The label says low in fat and high in almost everything else.
= 标签上写着低脂，别的几乎样样都高。
low in fat = 低脂的
② A fat envelope arrived and turned out to hold forty pages of terms.
= 来了个鼓鼓的信封，打开是四十页条款。
a fat envelope = 鼓鼓的信封
③ They chewed the fat on the doorstep for an hour and a half.
= 他们在门口闲聊了一个半钟头。
chew the fat = 闲聊
④ A fat lot of good that advice did anybody.
= 那条建议对谁都没起一点用。
a fat lot of good = 一点用没有
⑤ The cat is fat, unrepentant and asleep on the only warm chair.
= 那只猫又胖又毫无悔意，睡在唯一暖和的椅子上。
fat = 胖的
⑥ Two fat cats in the boardroom voted themselves a rise and the staff a freeze.
= 董事会里两位肥猫，给自己投了加薪，给员工投了冻薪。
a fat cat = 肥猫（贬称高薪权贵）
⑦ A fat finger on the keypad turned four thousand into forty thousand.
= 键盘上手指按错一个键，四千变成了四万。
a fat finger = 手指按错键
fat-fingered = 按错键的
⑧ The yoghurt is fat-free and sweeter than the one that is not.
= 那种酸奶是脱脂的，比不脱脂的还甜。
fat-free = 脱脂的
⑨ Fat shaming drives people out of the gym that the fat camp sends them to.
= 对肥胖的羞辱，把人从减肥营送去的那家健身房里赶了出来。
fat shaming = 对肥胖的羞辱
a fat camp = 减肥营

fatal
核心：两支。一是致命的：a fatal accident、a fatal dose；
二是后果灾难性的：a fatal mistake。副词 fatally。
① A fatal accident on that bend closed the road for six hours.
= 那个弯道上一场致命事故，把路封了六个钟头。
a fatal accident = 致命事故
② Signing before reading was the fatal mistake and everybody made it.
= 没读就签字才是致命的错，人人都犯了。
a fatal mistake = 致命的错误
副词 fatally：致命地：多说 fatally injured、fatally flawed。
③ Two people were fatally injured and the report took a year to appear.
= 两人伤重不治，那份报告过了一年才出来。
fatally injured = 伤重不治
④ The design is fatally flawed and the first three worked perfectly well.
= 那个设计有致命缺陷，头三台却好好的。
fatally flawed = 有致命缺陷
构词：fatal 加 -ly

fatalism
核心：宿命论：认定一切早已注定、努力也改不了的想法。不可数。
① Fatalism in that department passes for realism.
= 那个部门里的宿命论，被当成了现实主义。
fatalism = 宿命论
② Years of bad harvests bred a fatalism that no scheme could shift.
= 连年歉收养出一种宿命论，什么方案都撼不动。
a mood of fatalism = 一种听天由命的情绪

fatalist
核心：宿命论者：相信事情早已定好、争也无用的人。可数。
① A fatalist plants nothing and complains about the harvest anyway.
= 宿命论者什么都不种，照样抱怨收成。
a fatalist = 宿命论者
a born fatalist = 天生的宿命论者
② He is a fatalist about the weather and a planner about everything else.
= 对天气他信命，别的事都要计划。

fatalistic
核心：宿命论的、听天由命的：说态度与口气。副词 fatalistically。
① Her fatalistic view of the ferry timetable turns out to be entirely correct.
= 她对那份渡轮时刻表听天由命的看法，结果完全正确。
a fatalistic view = 听天由命的看法
② The crew grew fatalistic about the engine and started carrying more tools.
= 船员对那台发动机认了命，工具倒带得更多了。
be fatalistic about something = 对某事听天由命
副词 fatalistically：听天由命地：多修饰 shrugged、accepted、predicted。
③ He shrugged fatalistically and booked the later flight anyway.
= 他听天由命地耸耸肩，还是订了晚一班的航班。
shrug fatalistically = 听天由命地耸肩
accept something fatalistically = 听天由命地接受某事
④ She fatalistically accepted the third delay and opened a book.
= 第三次延误她认了命，翻开一本书。
构词：fatalistic 加 -ly

fatality
核心：两支。一是死亡事故中的死者，多用复数：road fatalities；
二是（正式）致命性：the fatality rate。多见于统计与新闻。
① Road fatalities fell for six years and rose again with the new junction.
= 道路交通死亡人数降了六年，新路口一开又涨。
road fatalities = 道路交通死亡人数
② The fatality rate depends on how quickly the ambulance gets there.
= 病死率取决于救护车多快能赶到。
the fatality rate = 病死率

fatberg
核心：（新词，英式）下水道油脂块：油脂、湿巾与垃圾在下水道里结成的巨块。可数。
① The fatberg under that street weighed more than a double-decker bus.
= 那条街底下的油脂块，比一辆双层巴士还重。
a fatberg = 下水道油脂块
form a fatberg = 结成下水道油块
② Clearing a fatberg takes three weeks and a very short-handled shovel.
= 清掉一块下水道油块要三个星期，还得配一把短柄铲。
构词：fat 加 iceberg 的后半截

fate
核心：三支。一是命运、天意，不可数；二是（人或物的）下场、结局，可数：
the fate of the building；三是习语：tempt fate 是说得太满招祸，
seal somebody's fate 是定下某人的结局。形容词 fated 与 fateful。
① The fate of the building was decided at a meeting nobody attended.
= 那栋楼的命运，是在一场没人出席的会上定下来的。
the fate of something = 某物的下场
② She refused to book the return leg, not wanting to tempt fate.
= 她不肯先订回程，怕说得太满招来晦气。
tempt fate = 说得太满招祸
③ One missing signature sealed the company's fate.
= 一个漏掉的签名，定了那家公司的结局。
seal somebody's fate = 定下某人的结局

fated
核心：注定的：多说 be fated to do something；
合成词 ill-fated 指命途多舛的。
① The ill-fated voyage is remembered for one photograph and no cargo at all.
= 那趟命途多舛的航行，留下的是一张照片，货一点没剩。
ill-fated = 命途多舛的
② They were fated to meet twice and to work together exactly once.
= 他们注定要见两次面，一起共事只有一回。
be fated to do something = 注定要做某事

fateful
核心：（书面）决定命运的、影响深远的：多说 a fateful decision、that fateful day。
① The fateful decision was taken in ten minutes and argued about for years.
= 那个决定命运的决议十分钟就作了，之后争论了好些年。
a fateful decision = 决定命运的决定
② On that fateful morning the ferry left twenty minutes early.
= 就在那个改变了一切的早晨，渡轮提前二十分钟开走了。
that fateful day = 那决定命运的一天

father
核心：三支。一是父亲；二是（正式）创始人、鼻祖：the father of modern surgery；
三是天主教与圣公会里对神父的称呼：Father Michael。
动词用法是「生育」。名词 fatherhood，形容词 fatherly。
① Her father taught her to drive on a beach at six in the morning.
= 她父亲早上六点在海滩上教她开车。
a father = 父亲
become a father = 当上父亲
② He is called the father of modern surgery and hated the operating theatre.
= 他被称作现代外科之父，却厌恶手术室。
the father of something = 某事物的鼻祖
③ Father Michael takes the early service and mends the roof himself.
= 迈克尔神父主持早祷，屋顶也是自己修。
Father = 神父（称呼）
④ Her father-in-law became a father figure to half the street.
= 她公公成了半条街孩子的父亲式人物。
a father-in-law = 岳父、公公
a father figure = 父亲般的长者
⑤ The father of the chapel called the meeting and the print run stopped.
= 印刷厂的工会车间代表召集了会议，印刷线就停了。
the father of the chapel = 英式印刷业的工会车间代表
⑥ Father's Day falls on a Sunday and the phone lines are busy all afternoon.
= 父亲节赶在星期天，电话线整个下午都占着。
Father's Day = 父亲节

fatherhood
核心：为人父、父亲这个身份。不可数。
① Fatherhood arrived with a car seat and eleven hours of instructions.
= 当上父亲这件事，是随一把安全座椅和十一小时的说明一起来的。
fatherhood = 为人父
② He took to fatherhood better than to any job he had ever had.
= 他做父亲比做过的任何一份工作都上手。
take to fatherhood = 适应做父亲
构词：father 加 -hood

fatherland
核心：祖国：多用在德语背景的语境里，跟 motherland 相对；
用这个词常带一股民族主义腔调，语域要留心。
① Songs about the fatherland were sung at the gate and nowhere else.
= 那些歌颂祖国的歌只在大门口唱，别处不唱。
the fatherland = 祖国
② He left the fatherland at nineteen and wrote about it for sixty years.
= 他十九岁离开祖国，写了它六十年。
leave the fatherland = 离开祖国
