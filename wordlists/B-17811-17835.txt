promontory
/ˈprɒməntri/
核心：岬、海角：伸进海里或湖里的一块高而窄的陆地。可数，复数 promontories。
① The lighthouse stands on a rocky promontory at the mouth of the bay.
= 灯塔立在海湾口一块多石的岬角上。
a rocky promontory = 多石的岬角
a promontory = 岬、海角
② From the promontory we could see the whole length of the coast.
= 从那个海角上，我们看得见整条海岸线。
from the promontory = 从海角上
比 headland 书面一些。

promote
/prəˈməʊt/
核心：及物。一是促进、推动、增进：promote growth；二是提升、晋升，多用被动：be promoted to，反义 demote；
三是促销、宣传、推广（产品、活动）；四是（体育）让（球队）升级，多用被动，反义 relegate。
① The organization works to promote understanding between the two countries.
= 那个组织致力于增进两国之间的了解。
promote understanding = 增进了解
promote growth = 促进增长
② She was promoted to head of department after only three years.
= 她只干了三年就升了系主任。
be promoted to something = 升为某职
promote somebody = 提升某人
③ He was promoted from sergeant to captain during the war.
= 战争期间他从中士升到了上尉。
be promoted from A to B = 从甲升到乙
职位前不加冠词。
④ The singer is touring the country to promote her new album.
= 那位歌手正在全国巡回，宣传她的新专辑。
promote an album = 宣传专辑
promote a product = 推销产品
⑤ The firm promotes the drink as a healthy alternative to coffee.
= 那家公司把这种饮料当作代替咖啡的健康之选来推销。

⑥ The club was promoted to the first division at the end of the season.
= 赛季结束时，那家俱乐部升入了甲级。
be promoted to the first division = 升入甲级

prompt
/prɒmpt/
核心：形容词：迅速的、立刻的、不耽搁的；（人）准时的，不放在名词前。
动词：促使、引起：prompt somebody to do；（用提问、提醒）鼓励……往下说；给（演员）提词。
名词，可数：（给演员的）提词；（电脑屏幕上的）提示符、提示。副词：（英式口语）准时地、整：at six o'clock prompt。

prompt somebody to do something = 促使某人做某事
④ The minister's remarks prompted an angry response from the unions.
= 部长的话引起了工会愤怒的反应。
prompt a response = 引起反应
prompt something = 引起某事
⑤ "And then what happened?" he prompted, when she fell silent.
= 她不作声了，他提了一句：「后来呢？」
prompt = 提醒人往下说
prompt somebody = 提示某人
⑥ She forgot her lines twice and had to be prompted from the wings.

be prompted = 由人提词
prompt an actor = 给演员提词
⑦ The actor dried up completely and needed a prompt.

a prompt = 提词
give somebody a prompt = 给某人提词
⑧ Type your password at the prompt and press the return key.
= 在提示符处输入密码，再按回车键。
at the prompt = 在提示符处
a prompt = 提示符、提示
⑨ The meeting will begin at ten o'clock prompt.
= 会议十点整开始。
at ten o'clock prompt = 十点整
这样用是英式口语，放在钟点后面。
⑩ She arrived promptly at nine and left on the stroke of five.
= 她九点准时到，五点一到就走。
promptly at nine = 九点准时

⑪ He sat down in the armchair and promptly fell asleep.
= 他在扶手椅上一坐下，立刻就睡着了。

⑫ The staff were praised for the promptness with which they dealt with the fire.
= 员工处置火情迅速，受到了表扬。
promptness = 迅速
with promptness = 迅速地
⑬ The prompter sat hidden in a box at the front of the stage.
= 提词员坐在舞台前沿的一个小间里，观众看不见。
a prompter = 提词员
⑭ He tidied his room without any prompting from his parents.
= 没等父母催，他自己把房间收拾了。
without prompting = 不用人催
at somebody's prompting = 在某人的催促下

prop up an industry = 扶持一个行业
prop up a regime = 支撑一个政权
⑧ You have to give her props for finishing the race with a broken toe.
= 她断了一根脚趾还跑完了全程，这一点你不能不佩服。
give somebody props = 佩服某人、称赞某人
这是美式口语。

propaganda
/ˌprɒpəˈɡændə/
核心：（多含贬义）宣传、鼓吹：为了让人接受一种政治主张而散布的、常常片面或不实的说法。不可数。
搞宣传的人叫 propagandist，可数；动词 propagandize 是（正式，含贬义）搞宣传，英式也拼作 propagandise。并入本条。
① The film was a piece of enemy propaganda made to frighten the troops.
= 那部片子是敌方的宣传品，拍来吓唬士兵的。
enemy propaganda = 敌方的宣传
a piece of propaganda = 一件宣传品
② The party ran a propaganda campaign in the newspapers before the vote.
= 投票之前，那个党在报上搞了一场宣传攻势。
a propaganda campaign = 宣传攻势
这个词带贬义：片面、不尽不实。
③ He worked as a propagandist for the regime throughout the war.
= 整个战争期间他都在替那个政权搞宣传。
a propagandist = 搞宣传的人
④ The teachers were accused of propagandizing in the classroom.
= 那些教师被指责在课堂上搞宣传。
propagandize = 搞宣传
这个动词少见，带贬义。

propagate
/ˈprɒpəɡeɪt/
核心：三支。一是（正式）传播、宣传（思想、信仰、消息），及物；二是繁殖（植物）、（植物）繁殖，及物也可不及物；
三是（物理）（波、声、光）传播。名词 propagation，不可数；繁殖幼苗用的保温育苗箱叫 propagator。并入本条。
① The group uses the internet to propagate its ideas among the young.
= 那个团体利用互联网在年轻人当中传播自己的主张。
propagate ideas = 传播主张
propagate a belief = 传播一种信仰
② The plant is easy to propagate from cuttings taken in the spring.
= 那种植物用春天剪的插条很容易繁殖。
propagate a plant = 繁殖一种植物
propagate from cuttings = 用插条繁殖
③ Sound waves propagate more quickly through water than through air.
= 声波在水里比在空气里传得快。
waves propagate = 波传播
这是物理学里的说法。
④ The book explains the propagation of roses by grafting.
= 那本书讲怎样用嫁接繁殖玫瑰。
the propagation of something = 某物的繁殖、传播
⑤ She raises her tomato seedlings in a heated propagator on the windowsill.
= 她在窗台上一个带加热的育苗箱里育番茄苗。
a propagator = 育苗箱

propane
/ˈprəʊpeɪn/
核心：丙烷：一种无色的气体，压成液体装在罐子里，用来烧饭、取暖。不可数。
① The stove in the caravan runs on propane from a cylinder outside.
= 房车里的炉子烧的是车外一个罐子里的丙烷。
run on propane = 烧丙烷
propane = 丙烷
② We took a small propane heater to keep the tent warm at night.
= 我们带了一个小丙烷取暖器，夜里给帐篷取暖。
a propane heater = 丙烷取暖器
a propane cylinder = 丙烷罐

propel
/prəˈpel/
核心：两支，及物，多用被动。一是推进、推动：让……往前走；二是驱使、促使、把（某人）推到（某种境地）：propel somebody into、towards。
过去式 propelled。-propelled 构成形容词：jet-propelled。
① The boat is propelled by a small engine at the back.
= 那条船由船尾的一台小发动机推进。
be propelled by something = 由某物推进
propel a boat = 推动船
② He took her by the arm and propelled her gently towards the door.
= 他抓住她的胳膊，轻轻把她往门口推。
propel somebody towards something = 把某人往某处推
③ The success of the film propelled her to fame almost overnight.
= 那部片子的成功让她几乎一夜成名。
propel somebody to fame = 把某人推上成名的路
propel somebody into something = 把某人推进某种境地
④ The army relied on rocket-propelled grenades to stop the tanks.
= 那支军队靠火箭推进的榴弹挡坦克。
rocket-propelled = 火箭推进的
jet-propelled = 喷气推进的

propellant
/prəˈpelənt/
核心：两支，可数也可不可数。一是（喷雾罐里把东西压出来的）喷射剂、推进气体；二是（火箭、枪炮的）推进剂、发射药。
① The spray uses compressed air as a propellant in place of harmful gases.
= 那种喷雾用压缩空气当喷射剂，代替有害的气体。
a propellant = 喷射剂
use something as a propellant = 用某物当喷射剂
② The rocket carries four hundred tonnes of liquid propellant in its tanks.
= 那枚火箭的燃料箱里装着四百吨液体推进剂。
liquid propellant = 液体推进剂
solid propellant = 固体推进剂

propeller
/prəˈpelə(r)/
核心：螺旋桨：装在船尾或飞机上、靠转动的叶片把船或飞机往前推的装置。可数。口语简称 prop。
① The fishing net had wrapped itself round the propeller of the boat.
= 渔网缠在了船的螺旋桨上。
the propeller of a boat = 船的螺旋桨
a propeller = 螺旋桨
② It was a small plane with a single propeller on its nose.
= 那是一架机头只有一个螺旋桨的小飞机。
a single propeller = 单螺旋桨
a propeller blade = 螺旋桨的叶片

propensity
/prəˈpensəti/
核心：（正式）（天生的）倾向、习性：propensity for、propensity to do。可数，复数 propensities。
① He has a propensity for getting into arguments with strangers on trains.
= 他有个毛病，爱在火车上跟陌生人争起来。
a propensity for something = 好某事的倾向
a propensity for doing something = 好做某事的倾向
② The dog showed a propensity to chase anything that moved.
= 那条狗见什么动就爱追什么。
a propensity to do something = 爱做某事的倾向
比 tendency 正式，多指天性。

proper
/ˈprɒpə(r)/
核心：形容词：（英式口语）像样的、真正的、名副其实的，只放在名词前：a proper meal；正确的、恰当的、合适的；
（社会上、道德上）得体的、规矩的、正派的；放在名词后：严格意义上的、本身：the city proper；
（英式口语）十足的、彻底的：a proper fool；proper to 是（正式）为……所特有的。
副词 properly：正确地、恰当地、好好地；得体地；（英式口语）十足地、真正地。习语 properly speaking 是严格说来。并入本条。
（语法）proper noun、proper name 是专有名词。
① We have not had a proper holiday since the children were born.
= 自打孩子出生，我们就没正经度过一次假。
a proper holiday = 像样的假期
a proper meal = 一顿像样的饭
② Please put the books back in their proper place on the shelf.
= 请把书放回书架上原来的位置。
the proper place = 该放的位置
the proper way to do something = 做某事的正确办法
③ It is not considered proper to arrive at a funeral in bright colours.
= 穿着鲜艳的衣服去参加葬礼，是不合规矩的。
it is not proper to do something = 做某事不合规矩
right and proper = 理所应当
④ Her grandmother was a very proper lady who never raised her voice.
= 她奶奶是位规规矩矩的老太太，从不高声说话。
a very proper lady = 规规矩矩的女士
prim and proper = 一本正经
⑤ They live in a village outside the city proper, twenty minutes by bus.
= 他们住在市区以外的一个村子里，坐公共汽车二十分钟。
the city proper = 市区本身
这一支放在名词后面。
⑥ I felt a proper fool standing there in the rain with no umbrella.
= 我没带伞站在雨里，觉得自己真是个大傻瓜。
a proper fool = 十足的傻瓜
这样用是英式口语。
⑦ The teacher showed the dignity proper to her position.
= 那位老师显出了与她的身份相称的庄重。
proper to something = 为某物所特有的、与某物相称的
这一支是正式用法。
⑧ The machine will not work unless it is properly connected.
= 那台机器接得不对就开不动。
properly connected = 接得对
do something properly = 把某事做对、做好
⑨ You cannot eat properly on the money they pay you.
= 靠他们给的那点钱，你吃不上像样的饭。
eat properly = 好好吃饭
sleep properly = 好好睡觉
⑩ He does not know how to behave properly at the table.
= 他不知道在饭桌上该怎么守规矩。
behave properly = 举止得体
dress properly = 穿得得体
⑪ Properly speaking, a tomato is a fruit and not a vegetable.
= 严格说来，番茄是水果，不是蔬菜。
properly speaking = 严格说来
⑫ "London" and "Mary" are proper nouns and begin with a capital letter.
= 「London」和「Mary」是专有名词，首字母大写。
a proper noun = 专有名词
a proper name = 专有名词
