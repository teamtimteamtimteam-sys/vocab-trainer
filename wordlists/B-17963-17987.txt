psychopath
/ˈsaɪkəpæθ/
核心：精神变态者、心理变态的人：有严重的精神障碍，行为凶暴、不顾别人死活、毫无愧疚的人。可数。口语简称 psycho。
形容词 psychopathic，并入本条。
① The film is about a psychopath who kills a stranger on a train.
= 那部片子讲一个精神变态者在火车上杀了一个陌生人。
a psychopath = 精神变态者
a dangerous psychopath = 危险的精神变态者
② The doctors described the prisoner as a psychopath who felt no guilt at all.
= 医生们说那名犯人是个毫无愧疚之心的精神变态者。
be described as a psychopath = 被说成精神变态者
③ The report said that he had a psychopathic personality and was a danger to others.
= 报告说他有精神变态的人格，对别人有危险。
psychopathic = 精神变态的
a psychopathic personality = 精神变态的人格

psychopathology
/ˌsaɪkəʊpəˈθɒlədʒi/
核心：精神病理学：研究精神疾病的成因和表现的学问；也指一个人的精神病症。不可数。
① The course in psychopathology covers the main disorders of mood and thought.
= 精神病理学这门课讲情绪和思维的几种主要障碍。
psychopathology = 精神病理学
a course in psychopathology = 精神病理学课
② The biography tries to explain the psychopathology of the dictator.
= 那本传记想解释那个独裁者的精神病症。
the psychopathology of somebody = 某人的精神病症
是专业用词。

psychosexual
/ˌsaɪkəʊˈsekʃuəl/
核心：性心理的：跟性的心理方面有关的。只放在名词前。
① The clinic offers counselling for couples with psychosexual problems.
= 那家诊所给有性心理问题的夫妻提供咨询。
psychosexual problems = 性心理问题
psychosexual = 性心理的
② Freud described five stages of psychosexual development in the child.
= 弗洛伊德讲过儿童性心理发展的五个阶段。
psychosexual development = 性心理发展
是专业用词。

psychosis
/saɪˈkəʊsɪs/
核心：精神病：一类严重的精神疾病，病人跟现实脱节，有幻觉、妄想。可数也可不可数，复数 psychoses。
① The patient was suffering from a psychosis and believed that he was being followed.
= 那个病人患了精神病，认定有人跟踪他。
suffer from a psychosis = 患精神病
a psychosis = 精神病
② Heavy use of the drug can lead to psychosis in some young people.
= 大量使用那种药会让一些年轻人得精神病。
lead to psychosis = 导致精神病
比 neurosis（神经症）严重得多。

psychosomatic
/ˌsaɪkəʊsəˈmætɪk/
核心：（病、症状）身心的、由心理引起的：身体上真的不舒服，根子却在焦虑、压力这些心理因素上。
① The doctor thought that her headaches were psychosomatic and caused by worry.
= 医生认为她的头疼是心因性的，是愁出来的。
be psychosomatic = 是心因性的
a psychosomatic illness = 身心疾病
② Psychosomatic symptoms are real to the patient, even if no disease is found.
= 即使查不出病，心因性症状对病人来说也是实实在在的。
psychosomatic symptoms = 心因性症状
不是说病人在装病。

psychotherapy
/ˌsaɪkəʊˈθerəpi/
核心：心理治疗：靠谈话、而不是靠药物来治精神、情绪上的毛病。不可数。也简称 therapy。做这种治疗的人叫 psychotherapist。并入本条。
① She had six months of psychotherapy after the death of her son.
= 儿子死后，她做了半年心理治疗。
psychotherapy = 心理治疗
have psychotherapy = 做心理治疗
② The doctor suggested group psychotherapy as an alternative to tablets.
= 医生建议用集体心理治疗代替吃药。
group psychotherapy = 集体心理治疗
③ He sees a psychotherapist once a week to talk about his fear of crowds.
= 他一个星期见一次心理治疗师，谈自己怕人多的毛病。
a psychotherapist = 心理治疗师
see a psychotherapist = 看心理治疗师

psychotic
/saɪˈkɒtɪk/
核心：形容词：精神病的、患精神病的。名词，可数：精神病患者。
① He was admitted to hospital after a psychotic episode at work.
= 他在班上精神病发作了一次，之后住进了医院。
a psychotic episode = 一次精神病发作
a psychotic illness = 精神病
② The drug is used to calm patients who have become psychotic.
= 那种药用来让发了精神病的病人安静下来。
become psychotic = 发精神病
③ The ward was built to hold forty psychotics in locked rooms.
= 那个病区当初是建来把四十名精神病患者关在上锁的房间里的。
a psychotic = 精神病患者
这样用名词如今显得不够尊重。

psychotropic
/ˌsaɪkəˈtrɒpɪk/
核心：（药物）作用于精神的、影响心理状态的。多放在名词前。也作名词，可数。
① The patient had been taking psychotropic drugs for several years.
= 那个病人吃了好几年精神药物。
psychotropic drugs = 精神药物
psychotropic = 作用于精神的
② Psychotropic medication should never be stopped suddenly without a doctor's advice.
= 没有医嘱，精神类药物绝不可突然停掉。
psychotropic medication = 精神类药物
跟 psychoactive 意思相近，多指治病用的药。

ptarmigan
/ˈtɑːmɪɡən/
核心：雷鸟：一种生活在北方高山、寒带的松鸡类鸟，羽毛冬天变白。可数。字母 p 不发音。
① A ptarmigan in its white winter feathers is almost invisible against the snow.
= 披着白色冬羽的雷鸟在雪地里几乎看不出来。
a ptarmigan = 雷鸟
② We saw a pair of ptarmigan among the rocks near the top of the mountain.
= 我们在山顶附近的岩石间看见了一对雷鸟。
a pair of ptarmigan = 一对雷鸟
复数也常不加 s。

pterodactyl
/ˌterəˈdæktɪl/
核心：翼手龙：恐龙时代一种会飞的爬行动物，早已灭绝。可数。字母 p 不发音。
① The museum has the skeleton of a pterodactyl hanging from the ceiling.
= 那家博物馆的天花板上吊着一副翼手龙的骨架。
a pterodactyl = 翼手龙
the skeleton of a pterodactyl = 翼手龙的骨架
② The boy drew a pterodactyl with huge wings flying over a volcano.
= 那个男孩画了一只张着大翅膀飞过火山的翼手龙。
a pterodactyl with huge wings = 翅膀巨大的翼手龙
严格的学名用词是 pterosaur（翼龙）。

pub
/pʌb/
核心：（英国、爱尔兰的）酒馆、酒吧：卖啤酒等饮料、常常也供饭的店，是街坊聚会的地方。可数。
英式 pub crawl 是一晚上连串几家酒馆喝酒；pub quiz 是酒馆里办的知识竞猜；pub lunch 是在酒馆吃的午饭。
① They went to the pub for a drink after the match on Saturday.
= 星期六比赛完了，他们去酒馆喝了一杯。
go to the pub = 去酒馆
a pub = 酒馆
② We stopped for a pub lunch in a village on the way to the coast.
= 去海边的路上，我们在一个村子的酒馆里停下来吃了午饭。
a pub lunch = 在酒馆吃的午饭
the local pub = 家门口的酒馆
③ The students went on a pub crawl through the old town on the last night of term.
= 学期最后一晚，学生们在老城里一家接一家地串酒馆。
a pub crawl = 连串几家酒馆喝酒
go on a pub crawl = 去串酒馆
④ Our team came second in the pub quiz on Thursday night.
= 星期四晚上酒馆的知识竞猜，我们队得了第二。
a pub quiz = 酒馆里的知识竞猜

puberty
/ˈpjuːbəti/

= 大多数女孩在十到十四岁之间进入青春期。
reach puberty = 进入青春期
puberty = 青春期
② A boy's voice breaks and grows deeper at puberty.
= 男孩到青春期会变声，嗓音变低。
at puberty = 在青春期
the onset of puberty = 青春期的开始

pubescent

① The magazine is read mainly by pubescent girls of twelve or thirteen.

pubic
/ˈpjuːbɪk/
核心：阴部的、耻骨的：跟生殖器周围那一带有关的。只放在名词前。
相关名词：pubis 是耻骨，可数；pubes 是阴部、阴毛，读 /ˈpjuːbiːz/。并入本条。
① Pubic hair begins to grow at the start of puberty.
= 阴毛在青春期开始时长出来。
pubic hair = 阴毛
pubic = 阴部的
② The X-ray showed a crack in the pubic bone after the fall.
= X 光片显示那一跤把耻骨摔裂了。
the pubic bone = 耻骨
③ The two halves of the pubis meet at the front of the pelvis.
= 耻骨的左右两半在骨盆前面合拢。
the pubis = 耻骨
④ The surgeon made a small cut just above the pubes.
= 外科医生在阴部上方切了一个小口。
the pubes = 阴部
这是解剖学用词。

public
/ˈpʌblɪk/
核心：形容词：公众的、大众的，只放在名词前：public opinion；公共的、公用的：供大家用的：a public library；政府的、公家的、国家办的：public money；
公开的、当众的：a public apology；众所周知的；人多眼杂的：反义都是 private。
名词：the public 是公众、民众，单数，动词单复数都行；（某一类的）一群人、（作家、艺人的）读者、观众：the reading public。
副词 publicly：公开地、当众；由公家地。并入本条。
习语：in public 是当众、公开地；go public 是把事情公开，也指（公司）上市；in the public eye 是常在公众视线里、是公众人物；
public enemy number one 是头号公敌；make something public 是把某事公之于众。
复合词：public holiday 是法定假日；public opinion 是舆论；public transport 是公共交通；public relations 是公关；
public sector 是公营部门；public school 在英国是（收费的）私立中学，在美国、苏格兰是公立学校；public speaking 是当众演讲。
① The government had to change its plans because of public opinion.
= 迫于舆论，政府不得不改了计划。
public opinion = 舆论
public interest = 公众的关注、公众利益
② The town has a public library, a public park and two public swimming pools.
= 那座城有一家公共图书馆、一座公园和两个公共游泳池。
a public library = 公共图书馆
a public place = 公共场所
③ The new hospital was built entirely with public money.
= 那家新医院全是用公款盖的。
public money = 公款
public spending = 公共开支
④ The minister made a public apology on television that evening.
= 部长当晚在电视上公开道了歉。
a public apology = 公开道歉
a public statement = 公开声明
⑤ The affair became public knowledge when a letter was printed in the paper.
= 报上登出一封信，那件事便尽人皆知了。
public knowledge = 尽人皆知的事
make something public = 把某事公之于众
⑥ Let us go somewhere a little less public to talk about this.
= 我们找个人少点的地方谈这件事吧。
less public = 人少些、不那么显眼
这一支说的是地方人多眼杂。
⑦ The palace is open to the public every day except Monday.
= 那座宫殿除星期一外每天对公众开放。
the public = 公众
be open to the public = 对公众开放
⑧ The general public knows very little about how the law is made.
= 一般民众对法律是怎么制定的知道得很少。
the general public = 一般民众
a member of the public = 一位民众
⑨ The novelist has never forgotten what she owes to her reading public.
= 那位小说家从没忘记自己欠读者的情。
the reading public = 读者大众
her public = 她的读者、观众
⑩ She was too shy to sing in public until she was nearly thirty.
= 她太害羞，快三十岁了才敢当众唱歌。
in public = 当众、公开地
反过来是 in private。
⑪ The nurse decided to go public with what she had seen in the ward.
= 那位护士决定把她在病房里看到的事公开出去。
go public = 把事情公开
go public with something = 把某事公开
⑫ The firm went public two years ago, and its shares have doubled in value.
= 那家公司两年前上市，股价已经翻了一番。
a company goes public = 公司上市
这一支是商业用法。
⑬ She has been in the public eye ever since she won the gold medal.
= 自打拿了金牌，她就一直在公众的视线里。
in the public eye = 在公众的视线里、是公众人物
⑭ After the robbery the newspapers called him public enemy number one.
= 那次抢劫之后，报纸管他叫头号公敌。
public enemy number one = 头号公敌
也说 public enemy No. 1。
⑮ Monday is a public holiday, so the banks and the post office will be shut.
= 星期一是法定假日，银行和邮局都关门。
a public holiday = 法定假日
⑯ The village is hard to reach by public transport, with only two buses a day.
= 那个村子坐公共交通很难到，一天只有两班车。
public transport = 公共交通
by public transport = 坐公共交通
⑰ She works in public relations for a large firm that makes soft drinks.
= 她在一家做汽水的大公司搞公关。
public relations = 公关
缩写作 PR。
⑱ Teachers and nurses in the public sector were given a rise of two per cent.
= 公营部门的教师和护士涨了百分之二的工资。
the public sector = 公营部门
反过来是 the private sector。
⑲ He was sent away to a public school in the country at the age of thirteen.
= 他十三岁被送到乡下一所私立中学寄宿。
a public school = （英国）私立中学；（美国、苏格兰）公立学校
英美意思正相反，要看是哪国的文字。
⑳ The course helps people to overcome their fear of public speaking.
= 那门课帮人克服对当众讲话的恐惧。
public speaking = 当众演讲
㉑ He has never publicly admitted that the decision was a mistake.
= 他从没公开承认那个决定是个错误。
publicly = 公开地、当众
publicly admit something = 公开承认某事
㉒ The railways in that country are publicly owned and cheap to use.
= 那个国家的铁路归公家所有，坐起来便宜。
publicly owned = 归公家所有的
publicly funded = 由公家出钱的
㉓ She worked for twenty years as a correspondent covering public affairs in Washington.
= 她在华盛顿当了二十年记者，专跑公共事务。
public affairs = 公共事务

㉔ The coal mines were run by a public corporation until they were sold.

㉖ Authors receive a small payment under public lending right each time their books are borrowed.
= 按公共借阅权，作者的书每被借一次就能拿到一小笔钱。
public lending right = 公共借阅权

㉗ She listens to public radio in the car on the way to work.
= 她开车上班时听公共电台。
public radio = （美国）公共电台
不靠商业广告维持的电台。
㉘ Teachers, nurses and police officers are all public servants.
= 教师、护士和警察都是公职人员。
a public servant = 公职人员
㉙ Keeping the parks clean is a public service that everyone takes for granted.
= 保持公园整洁是一项人人都视为理所当然的公共服务。
a public service = 公共服务

㉚ Every department had to cut its budget in the latest public spending round.
= 在最近一轮公共开支预算里，各部门都得削减预算。
a public spending round = （英国）一轮公共开支预算
㉛ It was public spirit, not money, that made her clean the beach every Sunday.
= 她每个星期天去打扫海滩，是出于公德心，不是为了钱。

㉜ A public-spirited neighbour paid for new swings in the playground.
= 一位热心公益的邻居出钱给游乐场换了新秋千。

㉝ The nature programme was first shown on public television in nineteen seventy.

㉞ Most people in the city get to work by public transit.

㉟ Water, gas and electricity are run as public utilities in many countries.

a public utility = 公用事业
㊱ The new dam was one of the great public works of the decade.

publication
/ˌpʌblɪˈkeɪʃn/

= 印刷厂罢工，她头一部小说的出版推迟了。
the publication of something = 某物的出版

② The library takes more than two hundred scientific publications from abroad.
= 那家图书馆订了两百多种国外的科学刊物。
a publication = 出版物、刊物

③ The judge banned the publication of the names of the children involved.
= 法官禁止公布涉案儿童的名字。
the publication of names = 名字的公布

publicist

publicity
/pʌbˈlɪsəti/
核心：两支，不可数。一是（媒体的）关注、报道、名声：good、bad publicity；二是宣传、推广：宣传这件事，也指宣传品。
publicity stunt 是为了引人注意搞的噱头。
① The trial received a great deal of publicity in the national newspapers.
= 那场审判在全国性的报纸上受到了大量报道。
receive publicity = 受到报道
a great deal of publicity = 大量报道
② The accident was bad publicity for a firm that sells safety equipment.
= 对一家卖安全设备的公司来说，那起事故是很糟的名声。
bad publicity = 坏名声
good publicity = 好名声
③ The band spent very little on publicity, and the hall was half empty.
= 那支乐队在宣传上没花什么钱，大厅空了一半。

publish
/ˈpʌblɪʃ/
核心：及物。一是出版、发行（书、杂志、唱片）；二是（报纸、杂志）刊登、登载；三是（作者）发表（作品），及物也可不及物；
四是（在网上）发布；五是（正式）公布、公开（消息）。
① The firm publishes about forty new books for children every year.
= 那家公司每年出四十来本新的儿童书。
publish a book = 出版一本书
be published = 出版
② The dictionary was first published in nineteen forty-eight and is now in its tenth edition.
= 那本词典初版于一九四八年，现在出到第十版。
be first published = 初版
③ The newspaper refused to publish her letter about the new road.
= 那份报纸不肯登她那封讲新公路的信。
publish a letter = 刊登一封信
④ He has published widely on the history of medicine in China.
= 他发表过很多关于中国医学史的著述。
publish widely = 发表过很多著述
publish a paper = 发表论文
⑤ The results of the survey will be published on the website next week.
= 调查结果下星期在网站上发布。
be published on a website = 在网站上发布
⑥ The government publishes figures for unemployment every month.
= 政府每个月公布失业数字。
publish figures = 公布数字
这一支是「公布」，不一定印成书。
⑦ The editor decided to publish and be damned, whatever the minister might say.
= 不管部长会说什么，编辑决定豁出去照登不误。
publish and be damned = 照登不误、不怕后果
表示不顾威胁也要公开。
