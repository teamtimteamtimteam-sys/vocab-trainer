self-deprecating
/ˌself ˈdeprəkeɪtɪŋ/
核心：形容词：自嘲的、谦逊自贬的：self-deprecating humour。
① The famous actor won the audience over with his self-deprecating jokes about his age.
= 那位名演员拿自己的年龄自嘲开玩笑，赢得了观众的好感。
self-deprecating jokes = 自嘲的笑话
self-deprecating = 自嘲的
② She has a charming self-deprecating manner and never boasts about her success.
= 她举止谦逊可爱，从不夸耀自己的成就。
a self-deprecating manner = 谦逊自贬的举止

a note of self-deprecation = 几分自嘲

self-destruct
/ˌself dɪˈstrʌkt/
核心：动词，不及物：（机器、武器）自毁、自动销毁；（比喻）（人）自我毁灭。形容词：自毁的：a self-destruct button。
① In the film, the spy's message self-destructs five seconds after he reads it.
= 那部电影里，间谍读完消息五秒后，消息就自动销毁了。
self-destruct = 自动销毁
② The team seemed to self-destruct in the second half, losing four goals in fifteen minutes.
= 那支球队下半场好像自己垮了，十五分钟内丢了四个球。
self-destruct = （比喻）自己垮掉
