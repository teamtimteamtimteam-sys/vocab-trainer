responsible
/rɪˈspɒnsəbl/
核心：形容词。一是有责任的、负责的：be responsible for；二是（对坏事）应负责任的、该怪的：be responsible for；三是有责任心的、可靠的、懂事的；

= 新老师负责一年级的孩子。
be responsible for = 对…负责
responsible = 负责的
② The police say that the same gang was responsible for three robberies.
= 警方说那三起抢劫是同一伙人干的。

③ She is a very responsible girl and can be trusted with the house keys.
= 她是个很懂事的女孩，可以把家门钥匙交给她。

④ He has a very responsible job in a big bank.

= 经理对董事会负责。
be responsible to somebody = 向某人负责
⑥ Please drive responsibly and do not drink before you get in the car.
= 请负责任地开车，上车前别喝酒。

⑦ The government must take responsibility for the state of the hospitals.

take responsibility = 承担责任
responsibility = 责任

/rest/
核心：名词：休息、歇息，可数也可不可数：have a rest、take a rest；the rest 是剩余部分、其余的人或物：the rest of；（东西的）支架、托架：an arm rest；（音乐）休止符。
动词：休息、歇息；（使）靠在、搁在：rest something on、against；rest on 是基于、依赖于；（正式）（事情）由……决定：rest with。
习语：at rest 是静止的、安息的；come to rest 是停下来；lay something to rest 是平息（谣言、担心），也指安葬；the rest is history 是后面的事大家都知道了。

rest = 休息
⑥ She rested her head on his shoulder and closed her eyes.

⑦ The case against him rests on the word of a single witness.

⑧ The final decision rests with the head of the school.

⑨ The ball rolled down the hill and came to rest against a tree.

⑩ The water in the lake was at rest, and not a leaf moved.

at rest = 静止的、安息的
⑪ The report should lay to rest any fears about the safety of the bridge.
= 那份报告应能打消人们对那座桥安全的担心。

⑫ She sang at a small club, a producer heard her, and the rest is history.
= 她在一家小俱乐部唱歌，一位制作人听到了，后来的事大家都知道了。
the rest is history = 后面的事大家都知道了

= 我们在高速公路的休息站停下来喝咖啡、吃三明治。
a rest stop = 休息站
⑭ His whole argument rests upon one old letter.
= 他整个论点就凭一封旧信。
rest upon = 依靠、基于
