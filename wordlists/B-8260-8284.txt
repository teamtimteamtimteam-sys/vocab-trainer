expiate
核心：（正式、书面）赎罪、补过：拿行动去抵消自己犯下的过错。
及物，宾语多是 sin、guilt、crime。名词 expiation。
① He spent thirty years trying to expiate a decision made in one afternoon.
= 一个下午做的决定，他花了三十年去赎。
expiate a sin = 赎罪
② Nothing in the settlement expiates the delay, and the family says so plainly.
= 那笔和解金抵不了拖延的过错，那家人也把话挑明了。
expiate guilt = 补赎罪愆

expiation
核心：（正式、宗教）赎罪、补赎：用苦行或善举去抵过错这件事。不可数。
① The pilgrimage began as expiation and turned into an annual holiday.
= 那趟朝圣起初是为赎罪，后来变成了一年一度的假期。
expiation = 赎罪
② Expiation of the debt took two harvests and most of the herd.
= 抵清那笔债，花了两季收成，还搭上了大半群牲口。
expiation of something = 对某事的补赎
构词：expiate 加 -ion

expiration
核心：两支。一是（多为美式，正式）期满、到期：合同或证件失效；
二是（医学）呼气，跟吸气相对。英式日常说 expiry。
① On the expiration of the lease the shop reverts to the family trust.
= 租约期满，那家店就归还给家族信托。
the expiration of a contract = 合同期满
an expiration date = 有效期限
② Each expiration is measured through a tube and printed onto a paper slip.
= 每一次呼气都通过一根管子测量，打印在小纸条上。
expiration = 呼气

expire
核心：两支。一是（证件、合同、期限）到期失效：the passport has expired；
二是（书面、委婉）咽气、去世。名词是 expiry 与 expiration，形容词 expired。
① Her passport expired in March and the appointment is not until June.
= 她的护照三月就到期了，预约却排到六月。
expire = 到期失效
a licence expires = 执照到期
② The old man expired at home with the curtains open and the radio on.
= 老人在家中咽了气，窗帘开着，收音机响着。
expire = 咽气、去世

expired
核心：过期的、失效的：多作定语，如 an expired ticket；
美式口语里也说 be expired，英式则说 has expired。
① The guard waved through an expired pass and lost his job over it.
= 门卫放行了一张过期的通行证，为这事丢了工作。
an expired passport = 过期的护照
② Expired medicines are collected by the pharmacy twice a year.
= 过期药品由药房每年回收两次。
expired medicines = 过期药品

expiry
核心：（英式）期满、到期：证件或合同失效这件事，不可数。
最常用的说法是 the expiry date；美式多说 expiration。
① The expiry of the contract caught both sides without a plan.
= 合同到期，两边都没备下一步的打算。
the expiry of something = 某物的期满
② The expiry date on the packet has been rubbed off by the freezer.
= 包装上的保质期日期让冰柜蹭掉了。
the expiry date = 保质期、有效期

explain
核心：三支。一是解释、说明：explain something to somebody ——
介词用 to，不能说 explain somebody something；二是说明原因、解释得通；
三是两个习语：explain yourself 是把自己的所作所为交代清楚，
explain away 是把不利的事轻描淡写地圆过去。名词 explanation。
① Could you explain the charge to me before I sign anything at all?
= 我签任何字之前，你能不能把这笔费用解释给我听？
explain something to somebody = 向某人解释某事
② One late lorry explains the queue and not the missing paperwork.
= 一辆晚到的货车解释得了排队，解释不了缺的那些单据。
explain something = 说明某事的原因
③ He was asked to explain himself and talked instead about the weather.
= 有人要他交代清楚，他却扯起了天气。
explain yourself = 把自己的行为交代清楚
④ The manager tried to explain away three missing invoices and made it worse.
= 经理想把三张不见的发票搪塞过去，反倒越描越黑。
explain away = 搪塞过去、圆过去

explainer
核心：（新闻业用语）解释性报道：把复杂的事拆开讲清楚的文章或短片；
也指展馆、部门里负责讲解的人。可数。
① The paper ran an explainer on the new tariffs and doubled its weekend traffic.
= 那家报纸发了一篇关税解读，周末流量翻了一番。
an explainer = 一篇解释性报道
② She is the department's explainer and gets sent on every school visit.
= 她是部里负责讲解的人，每次进校园都派她去。
the department's explainer = 部门里负责讲解的人

explanation
核心：解释、说明：可数时指给出的那个说法，an explanation for something 或
an explanation of something；不可数时指解释这个行为，如 by way of explanation。
① The only explanation for the missing hour is a clock that nobody reset.
= 那消失的一个钟头，唯一说得通的解释是有只钟没人调过。
an explanation for something = 对某事的解释
② His explanation of how the lock works took longer than picking it.
= 他讲那把锁怎么使，比撬开它还费时间。
an explanation of something = 对某物的说明
③ By way of explanation he simply pointed at the flooded cellar.
= 作为解释，他只是指了指淹了水的地窖。
by way of explanation = 作为解释

explanatory
核心：解释性的、说明用的：修饰 note、leaflet、booklet、text，
指专为把事情讲明白而写的那类东西。
① An explanatory note at the foot of the bill runs to four lines of jargon.
= 账单底下那条说明，写了四行行话。
an explanatory note = 说明性注释
② The museum hands out an explanatory leaflet that most visitors leave behind.
= 博物馆发一份说明单张，多数参观者转手就丢下了。
an explanatory leaflet = 说明单张

expletive
核心：（正式）粗话、脏字：书面报道里用来指人脱口骂出的那个词。可数，
常见说法 deleted expletive、an expletive-laden rant。
① The transcript replaces every expletive with a dash and loses half the meeting.
= 那份记录把每句粗话都换成破折号，会议的意思丢了一半。
an expletive = 一句粗话
② He muttered an expletive at the printer and then apologised to the intern.
= 他冲着打印机低声骂了一句，随后跟实习生道了歉。
mutter an expletive = 低声骂一句粗话

explicable
核心：（正式）解释得通的、说得出缘由的：多用在否定或对比里，
常配 explicable in terms of something。反义 inexplicable 反而更常用。
① The gap in the accounts is explicable and the missing key is not.
= 账目上那个缺口说得通，那把不见的钥匙说不通。
explicable = 解释得通的
② The delay is explicable in terms of the weather and nothing else.
= 那次延误只能从天气上解释，别的都讲不通。
explicable in terms of something = 可以用某因素解释

explicate
核心：（正式、学术）阐释、详加说明：把文本或理论一层层剖开讲，
比 explain 学究气重得多。及物，宾语多是 text、theory、passage。名词 explication。
① The lecture explicates three lines of the poem and skips the rest.
= 那堂课把诗里的三行阐释了一遍，其余的跳过去了。
explicate a text = 阐释文本
② Nobody has explicated the rule well enough for the staff to apply it.
= 那条规则谁也没讲透，员工没法照着办。
explicate something = 详加阐释某物

explication
核心：（正式、学术）阐释、解析：把文本或概念讲开的那篇东西，也指这个过程。
最常见的说法是 explication of the text。
① The edition prints the poem on the left and the explication on the right.
= 那个版本左页印诗，右页印解析。
explication of a text = 文本解析
② Her explication of the treaty runs to sixty pages and settles nothing.
= 她对那份条约的解析写了六十页，什么也没定下来。
explication = 阐释、解析
构词：explicate 加 -ion

explicit
核心：三支。一是明确的、把话说透的：跟 implicit 相对；
二是说人直言不讳：be explicit about something；
三是说内容露骨：sexually explicit。副词 explicitly，名词 explicitness。
① The instructions are explicit about the order and silent about the timing.
= 那些指示对顺序说得明明白白，对时间却只字不提。
explicit instructions = 明确的指示
be explicit about something = 把某事说明白
② The film carries a warning because two of the scenes are sexually explicit.
= 那部电影带警示标识，因为有两场戏很露骨。
sexually explicit = 露骨的

explicitly
核心：明确地、白纸黑字地：多修饰 states、says、forbids、asks，
用来强调话是写死的、不留余地。跟 implicitly 相对。
① The lease explicitly forbids subletting and says nothing about lodgers.
= 租约明文禁止转租，对合住的人却没提。
explicitly forbid something = 明文禁止某事
② She explicitly asked for the figures in writing and got a phone call.
= 她明确要求把数字写下来，等来的是一通电话。
explicitly state something = 明确说明某事
构词：explicit 加 -ly

explicitness
核心：（正式）直白、明确的程度：既说条文写得多死，也说内容有多露骨。不可数。
① The explicitness of the warning surprised the people who drafted it.
= 那条警示写得那么直白，连起草的人都吃了一惊。
the explicitness of something = 某物的直白程度
② Explicitness in the contract saved both sides a year in court.
= 合同里把话写明白，给双方省下了一年官司。
explicitness = 明确、直白

explode
核心：三支。一是爆炸；二是（人）发作、（数量）激增；
三是习语 explode a myth，用事实戳破一个流行说法。
名词 explosion，形容词 explosive。
① The boiler exploded at four in the morning and took the back wall with it.
= 锅炉凌晨四点炸了，把后墙也带塌了。
explode = 爆炸
② The town's population exploded in the ten years after the bridge opened.
= 桥通车之后的十年里，那个镇的人口暴涨。
explode = 激增、暴涨
③ Three winters of records explode a myth that gets repeated every autumn.
= 三个冬天的记录，戳破了一个每年秋天都被重复的说法。
explode a myth = 戳破一个流行说法

exploded
核心：（技术）分解图式的：几乎只用在 an exploded view 与 an exploded diagram 里，
指把零件拉开画的示意图，好让人看清怎么装。
① The manual opens with an exploded view of the gearbox and no words at all.
= 那本手册开篇是变速箱的分解图，一个字都没有。
an exploded view = 分解图
② The exploded diagram shows nine screws and the box contains eight.
= 分解图上画着九颗螺丝，盒子里只有八颗。
an exploded diagram = 分解示意图

exploit
核心：三支。一是（贬）剥削人；二是（中性）充分利用、开发资源与机会；
三是名词，了不起的事迹，多用复数 exploits，重音在前。名词 exploitation。
① The agency exploits drivers who cannot read the contract they sign.
= 那家中介剥削那些看不懂合同就签字的司机。
exploit somebody = 剥削某人
② They exploited the gap in the timetable and ran two extra services.
= 他们抓住时刻表里的空当，加开了两班车。
exploit an opportunity = 抓住机会
exploit a resource = 开发一种资源
③ His wartime exploits fill two chapters and one cautious footnote.
= 他战时的事迹占了两章，外加一条谨慎的脚注。
exploits = 事迹、功绩

exploitation
核心：两支。一是（贬）剥削：the exploitation of migrant workers；
二是（中性）开发利用：the exploitation of natural resources。不可数。
① The report documents the exploitation of migrant workers on three sites.
= 那份报告记录了三个工地上对外来工人的剥削。
the exploitation of somebody = 对某人的剥削
② Commercial exploitation of the seabed needs a licence and a full survey.
= 商业开发海床需要许可证，还得先做一次完整勘测。
the exploitation of natural resources = 自然资源的开发

exploitative
核心：（贬）剥削性的：说条件、做法、关系占人便宜、不公平。
美式偶尔拼作 exploitive。
① The hours are legal, the rates are exploitative, and nobody stays past March.
= 工时合法，工钱却是剥削，没人待得过三月。
exploitative practices = 剥削性的做法
② An exploitative contract can survive a court and still lose every worker.
= 一份剥削性的合同也许打得赢官司，工人却一个都留不住。
exploitative = 剥削性的

exploiter
核心：（贬）剥削者：靠占别人便宜赚钱的人或公司。可数。
① The pamphlet names three exploiters and gets two of the addresses wrong.
= 那本小册子点了三个剥削者的名，其中两个地址还写错了。
an exploiter = 剥削者
② Exploiters of cheap labour move on as soon as the wage floor rises.
= 靠廉价劳力发财的人，工资底线一涨就换地方。
an exploiter of cheap labour = 靠廉价劳力发财的人
构词：exploit 加 -er

exploration
核心：两支。一是勘探、探险：oil exploration、polar exploration；
二是（正式）对题目、想法的探讨：an exploration of grief。
① Oil exploration in the bay stopped when the survey ship was recalled.
= 勘测船一被召回，海湾里的石油勘探就停了。
oil exploration = 石油勘探
polar exploration = 极地探险
② The book is an exploration of grief written entirely in short chapters.
= 那本书探讨的是悲伤，通篇由短章组成。
an exploration of something = 对某题目的探讨

exploratory
核心：探索性的、试探性的：多修饰 talks、surgery、drilling ——
先探探情况，不下定论。
① The two sides held exploratory talks and agreed only on the next date.
= 双方进行了试探性会谈，商定的只有下次的日期。
exploratory talks = 试探性会谈
② Exploratory surgery found nothing and the pain went on for months.
= 探查手术什么也没查出来，疼痛又持续了好几个月。
exploratory surgery = 探查手术
