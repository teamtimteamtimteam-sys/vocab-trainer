lump
核心：（不成形的）块、团；肿块、包；（英，口语）笨重的人。动词：把…混为一谈、归在一起（lump together）。
习语：a lump in your throat（哽咽）、like it or lump it（口语：不喜欢也得忍着）。
搭配 lump sum（一次性付的一笔钱）与形容词 lumpish（笨重迟钝的）并入本条。
① She put two lumps of sugar in her tea.
= 她往茶里放了两块方糖。
a lump of sugar = 一块方糖
② He has a lump on his head where the ball hit him.
= 他头上被球砸中的地方起了个包。
a lump on your head = 头上的包
③ She found a lump in her breast and went to the doctor at once.
= 她发现乳房里有个肿块，马上去看了医生。
a lump = 肿块
④ I had a lump in my throat as I watched the children leave.
= 看着孩子们离开，我喉头哽住了。
a lump in your throat = 哽咽
⑤ You cannot lump all young people together as lazy.
= 你不能把所有年轻人都混为一谈，说他们懒。
lump together = 混为一谈
⑥ The bus leaves at six, so you will have to like it or lump it.
= 大巴六点开，你不喜欢也得忍着。
like it or lump it = 不喜欢也得忍着
⑦ He took his pension as a lump sum and bought a boat.
= 他把养老金一次性取出来，买了一条船。
a lump sum = 一次性付的一笔钱
⑧ The lumpish boy at the back of the class never said a word.
= 坐在教室后面的那个笨重迟钝的男孩从来一声不吭。
lumpish = 笨重迟钝的

lumpectomy
核心：（医）乳房肿块切除术 —— 只切除肿块，保留乳房。
① She had a lumpectomy followed by six weeks of treatment.
= 她做了肿块切除术，之后又治疗了六个星期。
have a lumpectomy = 做肿块切除术
② The surgeon said that a lumpectomy would be enough to remove the growth.
= 外科医生说，做肿块切除术就足以去除那个肿物。
a lumpectomy = 乳房肿块切除术
-ectomy 表示「切除术」。

a lumpy mattress = 疙疙瘩瘩的床垫

① It would be lunacy to sail in weather like this.

it would be lunacy to do something = …简直是疯了
② Selling the house now, when prices are so low, is sheer lunacy.

lunar
核心：月球的、月亮的；按月亮运行计算的。
搭配：lunar month（太阴月）、lunar year（太阴年）、lunar cycle（月相周期）、lunar day（太阴日）、lunar calendar（阴历）。
① The astronauts collected rocks from the lunar surface.
= 宇航员从月球表面采集了岩石。
the lunar surface = 月球表面
拉丁语 luna 是月亮。
② The festival falls on the first day of the lunar year.
= 那个节日在阴历新年的头一天。
the lunar year = 太阴年、阴历年
③ A lunar month lasts about twenty-nine and a half days.
= 一个太阴月大约二十九天半。
a lunar month = 太阴月
④ The fishermen plan their work around the lunar cycle.
= 渔民按月相周期安排活计。
the lunar cycle = 月相周期
⑤ A lunar day is about fifty minutes longer than a day measured by the sun.
= 一个太阴日比按太阳计算的一天长五十分钟左右。
a lunar day = 太阴日
⑥ Many Asian countries keep a lunar calendar for traditional holidays.
= 许多亚洲国家的传统节日用阴历。
a lunar calendar = 阴历
与 solar（太阳的）相对。

⑤ The offer sounds wonderful, but there is no such thing as a free lunch.

⑥ The shops in the town centre are crowded at lunchtime with office workers.

⑧ He packs a sandwich and an apple in his lunch box every morning.

⑨ The pupils eat in the lunchroom on the ground floor.

⑩ The lunch lady always gave him an extra spoonful of potatoes.
= 食堂阿姨总是多给他一勺土豆。
a lunch lady = （美，口语）学校食堂的阿姨
⑪ She made sandwiches with lunch meat and cheese.
= 她用冷切肉和奶酪做了三明治。
lunch meat = （美）冷切肉、午餐肉
