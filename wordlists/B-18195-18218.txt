quicken
/ˈkwɪkən/
核心：两支。一是（使）加快：脚步、心跳、速度变快，及物也可不及物；二是（文学）（使）活跃起来、激起（兴趣、感情）。
① She quickened her pace when she heard footsteps behind her.
= 听见身后有脚步声，她加快了步子。
quicken your pace = 加快步子
quicken = 加快
② His pulse quickened as the plane began to land.
= 飞机开始降落时，他的脉搏加快了。

③ The story of the old ship quickened the boy's interest in the sea.
= 那艘老船的故事激起了男孩对大海的兴趣。
quicken somebody's interest = 激起某人的兴趣
这一支是文学用法。

quiet
/ˈkwaɪət/

④ She is a quiet girl who prefers books to parties.

⑤ After years in the city they wanted a quiet life in the country.
