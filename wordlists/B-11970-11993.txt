incorrect
核心：不正确的、错误的；（旧）不得体的。in- 加 correct。副词 incorrectly 与名词 incorrectness 并入本条。
① Two of the answers on your test were incorrect, but you still passed easily.
= 你卷子上有两道题答错了，不过还是轻松及格了。
an incorrect answer = 错误的答案
② The letter never arrived because the address had been written incorrectly.
= 地址写错了，那封信一直没寄到。
incorrectly = 错误地
③ The newspaper admitted the incorrectness of its story and printed an apology.
= 那家报纸承认报道有误，刊登了道歉。
the incorrectness of something = …的错误

incorrigible
核心：无可救药的、屡教不改的 —— 坏习惯改不了。常带幽默或宠溺的口吻：an incorrigible flirt。
副词 incorrigibly 并入本条。
① My grandfather is an incorrigible flirt, even at ninety.
= 我祖父九十岁了，还是改不了爱调情的毛病。
an incorrigible flirt = 屡教不改的调情高手
② She is incorrigibly optimistic and believes every plan will work.
= 她乐观得无可救药，相信每个计划都会成功。
incorrigibly = 无可救药地

incorruptible
核心：不可收买的、廉洁的；不会腐烂的。前一支说官员、法官；后一支说物质。名词 incorruptibility 并入本条。
① The new judge had a reputation for being completely incorruptible.
= 那位新法官以绝对廉洁著称。
be incorruptible = 廉洁不可收买
② Gold was prized in ancient times partly because it was incorruptible and never rusted.
= 金子在古代受珍视，部分原因是它不腐不锈。
an incorruptible metal = 不朽的金属
③ Voters trusted her because of her reputation for incorruptibility.
= 选民信任她，因为她素有廉洁之名。
incorruptibility = 廉洁

increase
核心：增加、增长 —— 动词重音在后，名词重音在前。数量、程度、大小变大。
动词可及物（increase prices）也可不及物（prices increased）；名词常接 in（an increase in prices）。
习语 on the increase（在增加）。形容词 increased（增加了的）并入本条；副词 increasingly 另立。
① The population of the town has increased from five thousand to over twelve thousand.
= 那个镇的人口从五千增加到了一万二千多。
increase from A to B = 从A增加到B
② The company plans to increase prices by ten per cent next month.
= 那家公司计划下个月把价格提高百分之十。
increase something by = 把…增加（多少）
③ There has been a sharp increase in the number of people working from home.
= 在家工作的人数急剧增加。
an increase in something = …的增加
④ Crime in the city centre is on the increase again after two quiet years.
= 平静了两年之后，市中心的犯罪又在增加。
on the increase = 在增加
⑤ Smokers face an increased risk of heart disease and cancer.
= 吸烟者患心脏病和癌症的风险更高。
an increased risk = 增加的风险

increasingly
核心：越来越、日益 —— 修饰形容词或整个句子，表示程度在不断加深。
① It is becoming increasingly difficult to find cheap flats in the city.
= 在这座城市找便宜公寓越来越难了。
increasingly difficult = 越来越难
② Increasingly, young people are choosing to rent rather than buy a home.
= 越来越多的年轻人选择租房而不是买房。
increasingly = 越来越多地（句首）

incredible
核心：难以置信的；（口语）极好的、惊人的。两支：本义是「让人不信」；口语里多是赞叹「太棒了」。
副词 incredibly 并入本条，口语里常用作「非常」。
① Her story about being chased by a bear sounded incredible, but it was true.
= 她说自己被熊追过，听着难以置信，却是真的。
sound incredible = 听起来难以置信
② The view from the top of the tower is incredible, especially at sunset.
= 塔顶的景色太棒了，日落时尤其好看。
incredible = 极好的（口语）
③ The exam was incredibly difficult, and half the class failed.
= 那场考试难得出奇，全班一半人没及格。
incredibly = 非常、极其

incredulous
核心：不相信的、表示怀疑的 —— 说人的表情、语气：不敢相信听到的事。跟 incredible 分清：
incredible 是「让人难以相信的」（说事），incredulous 是「不肯相信的」（说人）。
名词 incredulity 与副词 incredulously 并入本条。
① She gave him an incredulous look when he said he had never heard of the band.
= 他说从没听说过那支乐队时，她一脸不信地看着他。
an incredulous look = 不相信的表情
② "You paid how much for that old bike?" her father asked incredulously.
= 「那辆旧自行车你花了多少钱？」她父亲不敢相信地问。
incredulously = 不敢相信地
③ The news that the factory would close was met with incredulity by the workers.
= 工厂要关门的消息让工人们难以置信。
be met with incredulity = 让人难以置信

increment
核心：增量、增长的一份 —— 一次增加的量，尤指工资按级递增的那一级（an annual increment）。
形容词 incremental（逐步增加的、渐进的）与副词 incrementally 并入本条。
① Staff receive an annual salary increment on the first of April each year.
= 员工每年四月一日获得一次年度工资增额。
an annual increment = 年度增额
② The volume can be adjusted in small increments using the button on the side.
= 用侧面的按钮可以一点一点地调节音量。
in small increments = 以小幅度递增
③ The new manager prefers incremental changes to a sudden reorganization of the whole office.
= 新经理更喜欢逐步改变，而不是把整个办公室突然重组。
incremental changes = 渐进的改变
④ Prices have risen incrementally over the past ten years, so few people noticed.
= 过去十年物价一点一点地上涨，所以没几个人注意到。
incrementally = 逐步地

incriminate
核心：使显得有罪、牵连（某人）入罪 —— 证据、证词让人看起来犯了罪。incriminate yourself 是「自证其罪」。
形容词 incriminating（使人显得有罪的）与名词 incrimination 并入本条。
① The letters found in his desk incriminated him in the fraud.
= 在他书桌里找到的那些信证明他跟诈骗案有关。
incriminate somebody in something = 使某人牵连进…
② She refused to answer on the grounds that she might incriminate herself.
= 她拒绝回答，理由是回答可能会自证其罪。
incriminate yourself = 自证其罪
③ Police searched the flat for incriminating evidence but found nothing useful.
= 警方搜查公寓，想找能定罪的证据，却一无所获。
incriminating evidence = 可以定罪的证据
④ The fear of incrimination kept several witnesses silent during the trial.
= 害怕被牵连，好几个证人在审判中都不开口。
the fear of incrimination = 害怕被牵连入罪

incrustation
核心：外壳、硬壳、结垢 —— 表面结上的一层硬东西（盐、水垢、珠宝镶嵌）。也写 encrustation。
① A thick incrustation of salt covered the rocks along the shore.
= 岸边的岩石上结了一层厚厚的盐壳。
an incrustation of salt = 一层盐壳
② The old kettle was full of white incrustations from the hard water.
= 那把旧水壶里全是硬水结的白色水垢。
incrustations = 水垢、结垢

incubate
核心：孵（蛋）；培养（细菌）；（病）潜伏。本义是鸟伏在蛋上保温；引申为实验室里保温培养，
以及疾病在体内「孵」着还没发作；还比喻酝酿想法、扶持新企业。
名词 incubation 与 incubator（孵化器、早产儿保育箱、创业孵化器）并入本条。
① The female bird incubates the eggs for about three weeks while the male finds food.
= 雌鸟孵蛋约三周，雄鸟负责找食物。
incubate eggs = 孵蛋
② The samples were incubated at thirty-seven degrees for two days.
= 样本在三十七度下培养了两天。
incubate samples = 培养样本
③ The virus has an incubation period of up to fourteen days.
= 这种病毒的潜伏期最长可达十四天。
an incubation period = 潜伏期
④ The premature baby spent her first month in an incubator.
= 那个早产的女婴头一个月是在保育箱里度过的。
an incubator = 早产儿保育箱
⑤ The university runs a business incubator that helps students turn ideas into companies.
= 那所大学办了一个创业孵化器，帮学生把想法变成公司。
a business incubator = 创业孵化器

incubus
核心：梦魇、压在心头的重负；（传说）梦淫妖。本义是中世纪传说里夜里压在睡者身上的男妖；
今天多比喻让人喘不过气的心事、负担。复数 incubi 或 incubuses。
① The debt hung over the family like an incubus for twenty years.
= 那笔债像梦魇一样压在那家人头上二十年。
like an incubus = 像梦魇一样
② In medieval stories, an incubus visits women in their sleep.
= 在中世纪的故事里，梦淫妖会在女人睡着时来访。
an incubus = （传说）梦淫妖

inculcate
核心：（正式）反复灌输、谆谆教诲 —— 把观念、习惯一点一点地、反复地教进人心里。
句型两种：inculcate something in/into somebody、inculcate somebody with something。名词 inculcation 并入本条。
① Good teachers try to inculcate a love of reading in their pupils.
= 好老师设法让学生养成热爱阅读的习惯。
inculcate something in somebody = 向某人灌输…
② The school inculcated its students with a strong sense of duty.
= 那所学校向学生灌输了强烈的责任感。
inculcate somebody with something = 向某人灌输…
③ The inculcation of good habits should start at a very young age.
= 良好习惯的培养应从很小的时候开始。
the inculcation of something = …的灌输、培养

incumbent
核心：现任的；在职者 —— 名词指现任官员、在位者（the incumbent），形容词指现任的（the incumbent president）。
另有正式的「有责任的」：it is incumbent on/upon somebody to do something。名词 incumbency（任期、在职）并入本条。
① The incumbent president is expected to win a second term easily.
= 现任总统预计会轻松赢得第二个任期。
the incumbent president = 现任总统
② The incumbent lost her seat to a young challenger by just forty votes.
= 那位在任议员仅以四十票之差把席位输给了一位年轻的挑战者。
the incumbent = 现任者、在任者
③ It is incumbent on all of us to report anything suspicious to the police.
= 我们大家都有责任向警方报告任何可疑情况。
be incumbent on somebody to do something = 某人有责任做…
④ During his incumbency the school doubled in size.
= 在他任职期间，学校的规模扩大了一倍。
somebody's incumbency = 某人的任期

incur
核心：招致、遭受 —— 因自己的行为而引来（不好的东西）：费用、债务、损失、罚款、怒气。
过去式 incurred，双写 r。
① The company incurred heavy losses when the factory had to close for six months.
= 工厂被迫关闭半年，公司蒙受了重大损失。
incur losses = 蒙受损失
② You may incur extra charges if you return the car late.
= 还车晚了可能会产生额外费用。
incur charges = 产生费用
③ He incurred the anger of his colleagues by taking credit for their work.
= 他把同事的功劳揽到自己身上，惹恼了大家。
incur somebody's anger = 惹某人发怒

incurable
核心：无法治愈的；（谑）改不了的。前一支说病；后一支说人的习性：an incurable romantic。
副词 incurably 并入本条。
① He was diagnosed with an incurable disease but lived for another fifteen years.
= 他被诊断出一种不治之症，却又活了十五年。
an incurable disease = 不治之症
② She is an incurable romantic who still believes in love at first sight.
= 她是个不可救药的浪漫主义者，至今还相信一见钟情。
an incurable romantic = 不可救药的浪漫主义者
③ Some of the patients on the ward are incurably ill and need care rather than treatment.
= 病房里有些病人已无法治愈，需要的是照护而不是治疗。
incurably ill = 病得无法治愈

incurious
核心：（正式）不好奇的、漠不关心的 —— 对新事物、周围的事没兴趣去了解。副词 incuriously 并入本条。
① The guards were incurious about the prisoners and never asked why they were there.
= 看守们对囚犯毫无好奇心，从不问他们为什么被关在那里。
be incurious about something = 对…不感兴趣
② The cat looked incuriously at the new dog and went back to sleep.
= 那只猫漠然地看了看新来的狗，又接着睡了。
incuriously = 漠然地

incursion
核心：（正式）侵犯、突袭；侵入 —— 本义是短时间攻入别国领土；比喻「闯入」一个新领域（an incursion into politics）。
① Border villages suffered repeated incursions by armed groups from across the river.
= 边境村庄屡次遭到河对岸武装团伙的袭扰。
an incursion into a territory = 侵入某领土
② The actor's brief incursion into politics ended after one election.
= 那位演员涉足政坛的短暂尝试一次选举后就结束了。
an incursion into something = 涉足…

indebted
核心：感激的；负债的。两支：受了别人的帮助而心怀感激（be indebted to somebody for something）；
欠钱的（heavily indebted countries）。名词 indebtedness 并入本条，也两支。
① I am deeply indebted to my parents for their support during my studies.
= 我非常感激父母在我求学期间的支持。
be indebted to somebody for something = 为…感激某人
② Many heavily indebted farmers were forced to sell their land.
= 许多负债累累的农民被迫卖地。
heavily indebted = 负债累累的
③ The author acknowledges her indebtedness to the work of earlier historians.
= 作者承认受益于前辈历史学家的研究。
indebtedness to somebody = 对某人的感激、受益
④ The level of household indebtedness has risen sharply since the crisis.
= 自那次危机以来，家庭负债水平急剧上升。
household indebtedness = 家庭负债

indecent
核心：下流的、猥亵的；不得体的。两支：跟性有关的不雅（indecent photographs）；
（非正式）不合适、过分的（with indecent haste，迫不及待得不像话）。
法律术语 indecent assault（猥亵罪）、indecent exposure（露阴罪）并入本条；
名词 indecency 与副词 indecently 并入本条。
① He was arrested for sending indecent photographs to a colleague.
= 他因给一位同事发送下流照片而被捕。
indecent photographs = 下流照片
② They sold their father's house with indecent haste, only a week after the funeral.
= 葬礼才过一周，他们就急不可耐地把父亲的房子卖了。
with indecent haste = 迫不及待得不像话
③ The man was charged with indecent assault after the attack on the bus.
= 那名男子在公交车上作案后被控猥亵罪。
indecent assault = 猥亵罪
④ Police arrested a man for indecent exposure in the park.
= 警方以露阴罪逮捕了公园里的一名男子。
indecent exposure = 露阴罪
⑤ The comedian's jokes crossed the line into indecency, and several viewers complained.
= 那位喜剧演员的笑话越界成了下流话，好几名观众投诉了。
indecency = 下流、猥亵
⑥ She was indecently rich, even by the standards of the neighbourhood.
= 就算按那一带的标准，她也富得离谱。
indecently = 过分地、离谱地

indecipherable
核心：无法辨认的、难以破解的 —— 字迹、密码、声音看不懂或听不清。
① The doctor's signature at the bottom of the prescription was completely indecipherable.
= 处方底部医生的签名完全认不出来。
an indecipherable signature = 认不出的签名
② The message was written in an indecipherable code that took experts months to break.
= 那条信息是用一种难以破译的密码写的，专家花了几个月才破解。
an indecipherable code = 难以破译的密码

indecision
核心：犹豫不决、优柔寡断 —— 拿不定主意的状态。不可数。形容词是 indecisive。
① After weeks of indecision, she finally accepted the job in Madrid.
= 犹豫了几个星期之后，她终于接受了马德里的那份工作。
weeks of indecision = 几周的犹豫不决
② The government's indecision over the new airport has cost millions.
= 政府在新机场问题上的举棋不定已经耗费了数百万。
indecision over something = 在…上的举棋不定

indecisive
核心：优柔寡断的；非决定性的。两支：人拿不定主意；结果（战斗、选举）没分出胜负。
副词 indecisively 与名词 indecisiveness 并入本条。
① He is so indecisive that it takes him twenty minutes to order a coffee.
= 他优柔寡断到点杯咖啡都要二十分钟。
be indecisive = 优柔寡断
② The election was indecisive, and no party won a majority.
= 选举没分出胜负，没有哪个政党赢得多数席位。
an indecisive result = 不分胜负的结果
③ She hovered indecisively at the door, unsure whether to go in.
= 她在门口犹犹豫豫地徘徊，拿不准要不要进去。
indecisively = 犹豫不决地
④ His indecisiveness frustrated everyone on the team.
= 他的优柔寡断让团队里每个人都很恼火。
indecisiveness = 优柔寡断

indecorous
核心：（正式）不得体的、有失体面的 —— 违反礼节、不合身份。
① It would be indecorous to laugh during the ceremony, however long the speeches are.
= 无论讲话多长，仪式上笑出声都是有失体面的。
it would be indecorous to do something = 做…是有失体面的
② The bishop was shocked by the indecorous behaviour of the wedding guests.
= 主教被婚礼宾客们不得体的举止惊到了。
indecorous behaviour = 不得体的行为
