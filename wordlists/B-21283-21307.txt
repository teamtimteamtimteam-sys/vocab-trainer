核心：名词，不可数：护肤、皮肤护理。
① She spends a lot of money on skincare products, especially creams for her face.
= 她在护肤品上花很多钱，尤其是脸霜。
skincare products = 护肤品
skincare = 护肤
② A good skincare routine is to wash your face twice a day and use sun cream.
= 好的护肤习惯是一天洗两次脸，并涂防晒霜。
a skincare routine = 护肤习惯
