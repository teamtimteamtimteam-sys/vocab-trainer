fortissimo
核心：三支（音乐）。一是极强，记作 ff：play fortissimo；
二是标着这个记号的那一段；三是（引申）声势极大地。
① The last four bars are played fortissimo and the hall cannot take it.
= 最后四小节奏得极强，那个厅承不住。
play fortissimo = 极强地演奏
② A fortissimo passage after ten minutes of silence wakes everybody.
= 十分钟静默之后来一段极强的乐句，谁都醒了。
a fortissimo passage = 极强的乐段
③ The whole campaign opened fortissimo and went quiet in a fortnight.
= 整场造势声势极大地开场，两周就没声了。
open fortissimo = 声势极大地开场

foundling
核心：三支（旧）。一是弃婴、捡来的孩子：a foundling；
二是收养弃婴的机构：a foundling hospital；三是（引申）来历不明的东西。
① A foundling was left at that door twice in the same winter.
= 同一个冬天，那道门口被放下过两个弃婴。
a foundling = 弃婴
② The foundling hospital took in two hundred children a year.
= 那家育婴堂一年收两百个孩子。
a foundling hospital = 育婴堂
③ The painting is a foundling and no gallery will say where it came from.
= 那幅画来历不明，没有画廊肯说它从哪来。
a foundling = 来历不明的东西

foundry
核心：三支。一是铸造厂：work in a foundry；二是铸造车间；
三是（半导体）代工厂。
① He worked in a foundry for thirty years and never wore ear protection.
= 他在铸造厂干了三十年，从没戴过护耳。
work in a foundry = 在铸造厂工作
② The foundry pours on Tuesdays and the whole street knows it.
= 铸造车间周二浇铸，整条街都知道。
the foundry pours = 车间浇铸
③ The chips are designed here and made at a foundry six thousand miles away.
= 芯片在这里设计，在六千英里外的代工厂制造。
a chip foundry = 芯片代工厂
