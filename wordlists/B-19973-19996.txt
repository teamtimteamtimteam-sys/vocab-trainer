scarce
/skeəs/
核心：形容词：稀少的、短缺的、不足的：Food was scarce.、scarce resources。习语 make yourself scarce 是（为避开麻烦）溜走、躲开。副词 scarcely：几乎不、简直不：scarcely ever、scarcely … when 是刚……就；（文）勉强。名词 scarcity 是短缺、稀缺（另立条）。
① Water is scarce in the desert, so people store every drop of rain they can.
= 沙漠里水很稀缺，所以人们把能接到的每一滴雨水都存起来。
scarce = 稀缺的
② During the war, food was scarce and many families grew vegetables in their gardens.
= 战争期间食物短缺，许多家庭在自家园子里种菜。
food is scarce = 食物短缺
近义对照：
scarcity = 稀缺
③ When my father came home in a bad mood, we children made ourselves scarce.
= 我爸心情不好回到家时，我们几个孩子就赶紧躲开。
make oneself scarce = 溜走
④ I could scarcely believe it when they told me I had won the first prize.
= 他们告诉我我得了一等奖时，我简直不敢相信。
scarcely = 几乎不
⑤ We had scarcely sat down to dinner on Christmas Day when the telephone rang in the hall.
= 圣诞节那天我们刚坐下吃晚饭，门厅里的电话就响了。
scarcely … when = 刚……就
这一结构较书面，when 前的动作刚完成，后一个动作就紧接着发生。
⑥ My grandmother scarcely ever leaves the village now that she is in her nineties.
= 我祖母九十多岁了，现在几乎从不离开村子。
scarcely ever = 几乎从不
