expanse
核心：（书面）一大片：说水面、天空、土地一眼望不到边，几乎总跟 of 连用。
可数，前面常配 vast、wide、open。
① A vast expanse of wheat runs from the road to a line of poplars.
= 从公路一直到那排白杨，是一大片望不到头的麦田。
a vast expanse of something = 一大片……
② The window gives onto an expanse of grey water with one boat moored in it.
= 窗外是一片灰色的水面，中间泊着一条船。
an expanse of water = 一片水面

expansion
核心：两支。一是扩张、扩大：规模、数量、领土的增长，常配 rapid、economic；
二是物理上的膨胀，如 thermal expansion。不可数为主，指某一次扩张时可数。
① Rapid expansion left the company with three offices and no filing system.
= 迅猛扩张之后，公司有了三个办事处，却没有一套档案制度。
rapid expansion = 迅猛扩张
economic expansion = 经济扩张
② Thermal expansion opens the joints on the bridge by a few millimetres.
= 热膨胀让桥上的伸缩缝张开了几毫米。
thermal expansion = 热膨胀
构词：expand 加 -sion

expansionary
核心：（经济）扩张性的：指有意刺激增长的财政与货币政策，跟紧缩的相对。
几乎只修饰 policy、budget、phase 这类词。
① The budget is expansionary and the central bank has said so twice this month.
= 这份预算是扩张性的，央行这个月已经说过两回。
expansionary fiscal policy = 扩张性财政政策
② Expansionary policy pulls unemployment down and pushes the rents up.
= 扩张性政策把失业率压下去，把房租推上来。
expansionary policy = 扩张性政策

expansionism
核心：（贬）扩张主义：以扩大领土或势力范围为目标的那套主张。不可数，
多见于政治评论；说人则用 expansionist。
① Nineteenth-century expansionism is taught here as settlement and elsewhere as conquest.
= 十九世纪的扩张主义，这里当拓殖来教，别处当征服来教。
expansionism = 扩张主义
② Territorial expansionism turns expensive long before it turns popular.
= 领土扩张主义早在得人心之前，就已经很烧钱了。
territorial expansionism = 领土扩张主义
构词：expansion 加 -ism

expansionist
核心：扩张主义的、扩张主义者：形容词与名词同形，多含贬义。
形容词修饰 policy、wing、ambition；名词说人。
① The party's expansionist wing lost the vote and kept the newspaper.
= 该党主张扩张的那一翼输了表决，倒是保住了报纸。
expansionist policies = 扩张主义政策
② Expansionists inside the ministry argued for a second port on the north coast.
= 部里的扩张派主张在北岸再修一个港口。
an expansionist = 扩张主义者

expansive
核心：两支。一是开阔的、大范围的：说视野、手势、理解的宽窄；
二是说人谈兴大发，吃饱喝足之后话多起来，in expansive mood。
副词 expansively，名词 expansiveness。
① The terrace has an expansive view of the estuary and no shade at all.
= 那片露台望得见开阔的河口，却一点遮阴也没有。
an expansive view = 开阔的视野
② After two glasses he grew expansive about his years at sea.
= 两杯下肚，他谈兴大发，讲起自己出海的那些年。
in expansive mood = 谈兴正浓
③ The report takes an expansive view of what counts as a household.
= 那份报告对什么算一户人家，取的是宽泛的理解。
an expansive definition = 宽泛的定义
副词 expansively：两支。一是大幅度地：多跟 gesture、wave 连用，形容手一挥的架势； 二是兴致高地、滔滔不绝地，说的是讲话时的那副样子。
④ He waved expansively at the fields and mentioned none of the debts.
= 他朝田野大手一挥，一句债务也没提。
gesture expansively = 大幅度地做手势
⑤ She talked expansively about next season and briefly about this one.
= 她大谈下个赛季，对本赛季只说了几句。
talk expansively = 滔滔不绝地讲
构词：expansive 加 -ly

expansiveness
核心：两支。一是辽阔、开阔：地方或视野的宽广；
二是热情健谈的劲头，说人放开了谈的那种状态。不可数。
① The sheer expansiveness of the plain is hard to photograph from a car window.
= 那片平原到底有多辽阔，从车窗里很难拍出来。
the sheer expansiveness of something = 某物的辽阔
② His expansiveness at dinner surprised colleagues who had only ever emailed him.
= 他饭桌上那股健谈劲头，让只跟他通过邮件的同事很意外。
expansiveness = 热情健谈

expansivity
核心：（物理）膨胀率：材料受热之后胀多少，专业行文里写作 thermal expansivity。
不可数。日常说法是 expansion。
① Steel and glass differ in expansivity, so the frames crack in a hard frost.
= 钢和玻璃的膨胀率不一样，重霜天里窗框就会开裂。
expansivity = 膨胀率
② Low expansivity is why the dish survives both the oven and the cold tap.
= 正因为膨胀率低，那只盘子进烤箱、冲冷水都没事。
thermal expansivity = 热膨胀率

expat
核心：（非正式）侨居国外的人：expatriate 的截短形，现在比全称还常用。
也当形容词，说 expat workers、the expat community。
① The bar fills with expats on Fridays and with students the rest of the week.
= 那家酒吧周五挤满了外派侨民，别的日子都是学生。
the expat community = 侨民圈子
② She has been an expat for eleven years and still votes by post.
= 她在国外住了十一年，投票还是靠邮寄。
an expat = 侨居国外的人

expatiate
核心：（正式，常带调侃）长篇大论、详说：几乎只用 expatiate on 或 upon something，
言下之意是讲得比听众想听的多。
① The chairman expatiated on the history of the site until the caterers arrived.
= 主席就那块地的来历长篇大论，一直讲到送餐的人来了。
expatiate on something = 就某事长篇大论
② Given half a chance he will expatiate upon the merits of the older engine.
= 只要给一点机会，他就会大谈老款发动机的种种好处。
expatiate upon something = 详论某事

expatriate
核心：三支。一是名词，侨居国外的人，尤指外派去工作的，口语截短成 expat；
二是形容词，expatriate staff、expatriate community；
三是（正式）动词，把人逐出国、或自己放弃本国国籍，多用被动。
① The company hires local staff and two expatriates who rotate every three years.
= 公司雇本地员工，另有两名外派人员，每三年轮换一次。
an expatriate = 侨居国外的人
② Expatriate teachers fill the timetable that the island cannot staff itself.
= 外籍教师顶上了本岛自己排不满的课表。
expatriate staff = 外派员工
③ Those who were expatriated in 1946 were later told they could apply to return.
= 1946 年被逐出国的那些人，后来被告知可以申请回来。
be expatriated = 被逐出国境

expect
核心：四支。一是预料、料想会发生：expect that、expect to do something；
二是等着某人某物到来；三是要求、指望某人做某事，带点应尽义务的意思；
四是 be expecting，委婉说怀孕了。反问句 what do you expect 是摊手的口气。
① We expect the results on Friday, so nobody has booked anything for the weekend.
= 我们预计周五出结果，所以周末谁都没安排别的事。
expect something = 预料某事
expect to do something = 预计要做某事
② She is expecting a parcel and has left a note for the courier on the door.
= 她在等一个包裹，门上给快递员留了张条。
be expecting somebody = 在等某人来
③ The school expects parents to read every letter it sends home.
= 学校指望家长把发回家的每一封信都读一遍。
expect somebody to do something = 指望某人做某事
④ They are expecting again, which is why the spare room is being painted.
= 他们又要添孩子了，那间空房才在粉刷。
be expecting = 有喜了
⑤ He turned up late and blamed the traffic — what do you expect from a Friday?
= 他迟到了，怪路上堵 —— 周五还能指望什么呢？
what do you expect = 你还指望什么呢

expectancy
核心：两支。一是期待：等着好事发生的那股气氛，an air of expectancy；
二是统计上的预期值，最常见的说法是 life expectancy 平均寿命。不可数。
① An air of expectancy filled the hall before either candidate had spoken.
= 两位候选人还没开口，大厅里就弥漫着一股期待。
an air of expectancy = 一片期待的气氛
② Life expectancy here rose by nine years and then stopped rising.
= 这里的平均寿命涨了九岁，之后就不再涨了。
life expectancy = 平均预期寿命

expectant
核心：两支。一是满怀期待的：expectant faces，脸上写着等好事；
二是固定说法 an expectant mother，指孕妇。副词 expectantly。
① Forty expectant faces turned towards the door and the wrong man walked in.
= 四十张满怀期待的脸转向门口，进来的却是别人。
expectant faces = 一张张期待的脸
② Expectant mothers are seen in the side clinic and never wait long.
= 孕妇在旁边那间诊室看诊，从来不用等太久。
an expectant mother = 孕妇
副词 expectantly：期待地、眼巴巴地：多修饰 look、wait、glance，说的是等回应的那副神情。
③ The dog sat expectantly by the cupboard where the leads are kept.
= 那条狗满怀期待地坐在放牵引绳的柜子旁边。
wait expectantly = 满怀期待地等着
④ She looked at him expectantly and he went on reading the menu.
= 她眼巴巴地看着他，他继续读他的菜单。
look expectantly at somebody = 期待地看着某人
构词：expectant 加 -ly

expectation
核心：期望、预期：既指「以为会发生」，也指「要求别人达到的标准」，
后一支多用复数 expectations。常见搭配 live up to expectations、
exceed expectations，以及 contrary to expectation。
① Contrary to expectation, the older bridge took the flood and the new one did not.
= 出乎意料的是，扛住洪水的是那座老桥，不是新桥。
contrary to expectation = 出乎意料
② The film exceeded expectations at home and disappointed everyone abroad.
= 那部电影在国内超出预期，在国外让所有人失望。
exceed expectations = 超出预期
③ Nobody can live up to expectations that were never said out loud.
= 从没说出口的期望，谁也达不到。
live up to expectations = 不负期望
④ Expectation of life in those tables is worked out for a whole cohort, not for you.
= 那些表上的预期寿命，是按整代人算的，不是按你算的。
expectation of life = 预期寿命

expected
核心：预期的、意料之中的：多作定语，如 the expected outcome；
副词化的 as expected 是「果然」。跟 unexpected 相对。
① The expected crowd never came and the stewards folded the barriers away.
= 预计的人潮一直没来，工作人员把围栏收了起来。
the expected outcome = 预期的结果
② As expected, the cheapest quote came with the longest delivery time.
= 果然，报价最低的那家交货期最长。
as expected = 果然、不出所料

expectorant
核心：（医学）祛痰药：帮人把痰咳出来的药，也作形容词修饰 syrup、mixture。可数。
① The pharmacist recommended an expectorant and told him to drink more water.
= 药师给他推荐了一种祛痰药，还叫他多喝水。
an expectorant = 一种祛痰药
② Expectorant syrups taste of aniseed and are cheaper than the branded pills.
= 祛痰糖浆一股茴香味，比那些名牌药片便宜。
an expectorant syrup = 祛痰糖浆

expectorate
核心：（正式、医学）咳痰、吐痰：日常说 spit，只有告示牌与病历上写这个词。
名词 expectoration。
① A sign on the platform still asks passengers not to expectorate.
= 站台上还挂着一块牌子，请乘客不要随地吐痰。
expectorate = 吐痰、咳出痰
② Patients are asked to expectorate into a pot before the sample is sealed.
= 采样封存之前，医生请病人把痰咳进小盒里。
expectorate into something = 把痰咳进某容器

expectoration
核心：（医学）咳痰：既指咳的这个动作，也指咳出来的那口痰。不可数。
① Expectoration at night is the symptom the doctor asked about twice.
= 夜里咳痰，是医生问了两遍的那个症状。
expectoration = 咳痰
② Blood in the expectoration sent him back to the clinic that same afternoon.
= 痰里带血，让他当天下午又回了诊所。
blood in the expectoration = 痰中带血
构词：expectorate 加 -ion

expediency
核心：（多含贬义）权宜、只图管用：眼下方便就好，不问是否合乎原则。
不可数，最常见的搭配是 political expediency。异体 expedience 少见。
① The rule was dropped for political expediency and quietly restored in June.
= 那条规矩出于政治权宜被取消，六月又悄悄恢复。
political expediency = 政治上的权宜
② Expediency explains the timetable and does not excuse it.
= 权宜之计解释得了这份时间表，却开脱不了它。
expediency = 权宜行事

expedient
核心：两支。一是形容词，权宜的、图省事的：多用 it is expedient to do something，
言下之意是管用但未必正当；二是名词，应急的权宜之计。副词 expediently。
① The committee found it expedient to postpone the vote until after the holiday.
= 委员会觉得把表决推到假期之后比较省事。
it is expedient to do something = 做某事比较权宜
② Borrowing from next year's budget is an expedient, not a plan.
= 挪用明年的预算是权宜之计，不是计划。
an expedient = 权宜之计
副词 expediently：权宜地、图方便地：修饰处理方式，暗示图的是省事，不是公正。
③ The complaint was handled expediently and nobody looked at the paperwork.
= 那桩投诉图省事就打发了，没人翻过那些材料。
handle something expediently = 图省事地处理某事
④ Acting expediently in March cost the department the whole of April.
= 三月里图省事，让那个部门赔上了整个四月。
act expediently = 权宜行事
构词：expedient 加 -ly

expedite
核心：（正式）加快、加急办理：让手续或流程走得快些。及物，
宾语多是 process、delivery、application 这类词，公文与商务信里常见。
① A second signature would expedite the transfer and nobody can find the registrar.
= 再补一个签名就能加快转账，可谁也找不到那位登记员。
expedite the process = 加快流程
② Paying the extra fee expedites delivery by exactly one working day.
= 多付那笔加急费，交货正好提前一个工作日。
expedite delivery = 加急发货

expedition
核心：两支。一是远征、探险、科考（也指那支队伍）：go on an expedition；
二是（正式）迅速、麻利，只见于 with all expedition 这类说法。
① The expedition set out with four sledges and came back with only two.
= 那支考察队带着四架雪橇出发，回来时只剩两架。
go on an expedition = 参加一次远征
an expedition to the interior = 深入内陆的考察
② The court asked that the papers be filed with all expedition.
= 法庭要求尽速把文件递交上去。
with all expedition = 尽速、火速

expeditious
核心：（正式）迅速而有效率的：多修饰 manner、resolution、response，
一股公文腔，日常场合说 quick。副词 expeditiously。
① The tenant asked for an expeditious repair and got a letter about the rules.
= 租客要求尽快修好，收到的却是一封讲规矩的信。
in an expeditious manner = 以迅速的方式
② An expeditious hearing suits everyone except the side that is stalling.
= 迅速开庭对谁都好，只除了那个在拖延的一方。
expeditious = 迅速高效的
副词 expeditiously：迅速地、麻利地：法律文书与公文里的常用词，多跟 deal with、handle、act 连用。
③ Claims under a thousand pounds are dealt with expeditiously and without a hearing.
= 一千镑以下的索赔从速处理，也不开庭。
deal with something expeditiously = 从速处理某事
④ The bank promised to act expeditiously and then took eleven weeks.
= 银行答应尽快办理，结果办了十一个星期。
act expeditiously = 迅速行事
构词：expeditious 加 -ly

expel
核心：两支。一是开除、驱逐：把学生赶出学校、把人逐出国境，
多用被动 be expelled from something；二是排出气体或液体。
变形双写 l：expelled、expelling。名词 expulsion。
① Two players were expelled from the tournament and the third was only fined.
= 有两名选手被逐出赛事，第三个只罚了款。
expel somebody from something = 把某人逐出某处
be expelled from school = 被学校开除
② The whale expels a spout of air that can be heard from the cliff top.
= 鲸鱼喷出一股气，站在崖顶都听得见。
expel air = 排出空气
