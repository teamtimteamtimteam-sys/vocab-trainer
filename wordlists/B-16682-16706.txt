in the perineum = 在会阴处

period
/ˈpɪəriəd/
核心：本义是「一段时间」。名词，可数：一段时间、时期：a period of、over a period of；
（一个人一生里、一国历史上的）时期、时代；（地质）纪；（学校里的）一节课；（妇女的）月经、例假；
（美式）句号，英式说 full stop；由此，说完一句话再加一个 period，表示「就这样，没什么可商量的」，英式说 full stop。
形容词，只作定语：某个历史时期（风格）的：period costume、period furniture。
① The work must be finished within a period of six months.
= 那项工作须在六个月的期限内完成。
a period of six months = 六个月的期限
② The patient was watched over a period of several weeks.
= 那位病人被观察了好几个星期。
over a period of time = 在一段时间里
③ Most of her paintings from this period are of the sea.
= 她这一时期的画多半画的是海。
from this period = 这一时期的
说一位艺术家、一个国家历史上的某个阶段。
④ The rocks were formed during the Jurassic period.
= 那些岩石形成于侏罗纪。
the Jurassic period = 侏罗纪
⑤ We have two periods of history on Tuesday afternoons.
= 我们星期二下午有两节历史课。
a period of history = 一节历史课
⑥ Her periods stopped when she began training for the marathon.
= 她开始练马拉松以后，月经就停了。
somebody's period = 某人的月经
⑦ Remember to put a period at the end of every sentence.
= 记住每句话的末尾要加句号。
a period = 句号（美式）
⑧ You are not going out tonight, period.
= 你今晚不许出去，没什么可说的。
period = 就这样、没的商量
⑨ The actors wore period costume and spoke in the language of the time.
= 演员们穿着那个时代的服装，说的也是当时的话。
period costume = 那个时代的服装
⑩ The house is full of period pieces collected by her grandfather.
= 那座房子里满是她爷爷收集的古董。
a period piece = 古董、有时代特色的旧物

③ Mailing lists are updated periodically to remove old addresses.
= 邮寄名单定期更新，删掉旧地址。
periodically = 定期地、时不时地
④ Gold appears near the bottom of the periodic table.
= 金排在元素周期表靠下的位置。
the periodic table = 元素周期表

peripheral

a peripheral figure = 边缘人物
③ The new estates were built in the peripheral areas of the city.

④ She saw a movement in her peripheral vision and turned her head.

peripheral vision = 余光、周边视觉
⑤ The shop sells monitors, printers and other peripherals.
= 那家店卖显示器、打印机和别的外围设备。
a peripheral = 外围设备
⑥ He was only peripherally involved in the planning of the event.
= 那次活动的筹划，他只是沾了点边。
peripherally = 次要地、沾边地

periphrasis
/pəˈrɪfrəsɪs/

二是（语法）迂回形式：用另加的词而不是词尾变化来表达语法意思，如用 more 构成比较级、用 did 构成过去时强调。

① "Passed away" is a familiar periphrasis for "died".
= 「过世」是「死」的一种常见的委婉迂回说法。
a periphrasis for something = 某说法的迂回表达
② The report hides its bad news in clouds of periphrasis.
= 那份报告把坏消息藏在一大堆绕弯子的话里。
clouds of periphrasis = 一大堆绕弯子的话
③ "More pleasant" is a periphrastic form, while "pleasanter" is not.
= 「more pleasant」是迂回形式，「pleasanter」不是。
a periphrastic form = 迂回形式

perish
/ˈperɪʃ/
核心：动词，不及物，两支。一是（书面）丧生、死去，尤指死于非命；也指毁灭、消亡；二是（英式）（橡胶、皮革）老化、腐烂、变脆。
习语 perish the thought 是（口语）但愿不会、想都别想。
派生：perishable 是（食物）易腐的，名词 perishables 是易腐食品，复数；
英式口语的 perished 是冻坏了的，perishing 是冷得要命的，也用来泄愤：该死的；perisher 是（旧式）讨厌鬼、小淘气。并入本条。
① Three hundred people perished when the ferry sank in the storm.
= 渡轮在暴风雨里沉没，三百人丧生。
perish = 丧生
② Whole villages perished in the famine and were never rebuilt.
= 整村整村的人死于那场饥荒，村子再没重建。
perish in the famine = 死于饥荒
③ The rubber seal on the door had perished and let in the rain.
= 门上的橡胶密封条老化了，雨漏了进来。
the rubber has perished = 橡胶老化了
④ If the hotel is full, perish the thought, we can sleep in the car.
= 万一旅馆满了，但愿不会，我们可以睡在车里。
perish the thought = 但愿不会
⑤ Milk, fish and other perishable goods are carried in cooled lorries.
= 牛奶、鱼和别的易腐货物用冷藏车运送。
perishable = 易腐的
⑥ Put the perishables in the fridge as soon as you get home.
= 一到家就把易腐的食品放进冰箱。
perishables = 易腐食品
⑦ Come in and sit by the fire, because you must be perished.
= 进来坐到炉火边吧，你准冻坏了。
be perished = 冻坏了
⑧ It is perishing outside, so wear your thick coat.
= 外面冷得要命，穿上厚大衣。
perishing = 冷得要命的
⑨ I have had enough of this perishing rain.
= 这该死的雨我受够了。
this perishing rain = 这该死的雨
英式口语，旧式，用来泄愤。
⑩ "Come here, you little perisher!" shouted the gardener.
= 园丁喊道：「过来，你这小淘气！」
a little perisher = 小淘气
⑪ Young lecturers live under the rule of publish or perish.
= 年轻讲师活在「不发表就出局」这条规矩底下。
publish or perish = 不发表就出局
说学术界的人不出论文就保不住位子。

permeate
/ˈpɜːmieɪt/
核心：（正式）两支。一是（液体、气体、气味）渗入、弥漫：及物，或跟 through、into；二是（想法、情绪、风气）弥漫、充满、渗透到。
派生：permeable 是可渗透的、透水的，介词用 to，反义是 impermeable；permeability 是渗透性，不可数；permeation 是渗透，不可数。并入本条。
① The smell of fresh bread permeated the whole house by breakfast time.
= 到吃早饭的时候，新烤面包的香味已经弥漫了整座房子。
permeate the house = 弥漫整座房子
及物：气味、液体作主语，地方作宾语。
② Rainwater permeates through the soil and collects in the rock below.
= 雨水渗过土壤，积聚在下面的岩层里。
permeate through something = 渗过某物
③ A sense of failure permeates every chapter of his last novel.
= 他最后一部小说的每一章都透着一种失败感。
permeate every chapter = 贯穿每一章
说情绪、想法时多是这种比喻用法。
④ The membrane is permeable to water but not to salt.
= 那层膜水透得过，盐透不过。
permeable to something = 某物透得过
a permeable membrane = 透性膜
⑤ The engineers measured the permeability of the rock before drilling the well.
= 打井之前，工程师测了岩石的渗透性。
permeability = 渗透性
⑥ The paint slows the permeation of moisture into the wall.
= 那种涂料能减缓潮气往墙里渗。
permeation = 渗透

permissible
/pəˈmɪsəbl/
核心：（正式）许可的、可以允许的：规则、法律不禁止的。
① The level of lead in the water was twice the permissible limit.
= 水里的铅含量是许可限度的两倍。
the permissible limit = 许可的限度
permissible levels = 许可的水平
② It is not permissible for an employer to ask about a candidate's religion.
= 雇主询问应聘者的宗教信仰是不允许的。
it is not permissible to do something = 做某事是不允许的
比 allowed 正式，见于规章。

permission
/pəˈmɪʃn/
核心：许可、准许：有权的人说「可以」。不可数：ask (for) permission、give permission、with、without somebody's permission；
可数的 permissions 是（正式）书面的许可、授权，多指引用、转载别人作品的许可。
planning permission 是（英国）建筑规划许可。
① You must ask permission before you take photographs inside the church.
= 在教堂里拍照得先征得许可。
ask permission = 征求许可
ask for permission = 征求许可
② The headmaster gave the pupils permission to leave an hour early.
= 校长准许学生们提前一个钟头走。
give somebody permission to do something = 准许某人做某事
不可数，不说 a permission。
③ He took the car without his father's permission.
= 他没经父亲同意就把车开走了。
without somebody's permission = 未经某人同意
with your permission = 您要是同意的话
④ The publisher is responsible for obtaining permissions for all the photographs.
= 所有照片的使用许可都由出版社负责取得。
obtain permissions = 取得使用许可
⑤ They were refused planning permission for a second floor.
= 他们加盖二楼的规划申请没批下来。
planning permission = 建筑规划许可
