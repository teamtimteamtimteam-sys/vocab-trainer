shred
/ʃred/
核心：名词，可数：碎片、细条：tear something to shreds；（用于否定）一点点、丝毫：not a shred of evidence。动词（shredded, shredded）：切碎、撕碎；用碎纸机销毁（文件）。
① The dog got into the kitchen and tore the newspaper to shreds.
= 那条狗溜进厨房，把报纸撕成了碎片。
tear something to shreds = 把某物撕成碎片
shred = 碎片
② The police had not a shred of evidence that he was at the house that night.
= 警方没有丝毫证据证明他那晚在那所房子里。
not a shred of something = 一点某物也没有
③ Shred the cabbage finely and mix it with carrots for the salad.
= 把卷心菜切成细丝，和胡萝卜拌成沙拉。
shred = 切碎
④ The company shredded all the documents in the office the night before the police arrived.
= 那家公司在警察到达前一晚，把办公室里所有文件都用碎纸机销毁了。
shred documents = 销毁文件

shredder
/ˈʃredə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：碎纸机；（厨房用的）切丝器。
① Put the old bank letters through the shredder so that nobody can read your details.
= 把旧银行信件放进碎纸机里，这样谁也看不到你的信息。
the shredder = 碎纸机
shredder = 碎纸机
② She used a vegetable shredder to cut the carrots into thin strips.
= 她用蔬菜切丝器把胡萝卜切成细丝。
a vegetable shredder = 蔬菜切丝器

shrew
/ʃruː/
核心：名词，可数：鼩鼱（一种像老鼠的小动物，鼻子尖长）；（旧，贬义）泼妇、悍妇。
① The cat brought a tiny shrew into the house and dropped it on the kitchen floor.
= 猫把一只小鼩鼱叼进屋，扔在厨房地板上。
a shrew = 鼩鼱
shrew = 鼩鼱
② In Shakespeare's play, the shrew is a bad-tempered woman who does not want to marry.
= 在莎士比亚的那出戏里，悍妇是个脾气暴躁、不想嫁人的女人。
the shrew = 悍妇
「泼妇」一义带性别偏见，现代少用。

shrewd
/ʃruːd/
核心：形容词：精明的、敏锐的、有眼光的：a shrewd businessman、a shrewd guess。副词 shrewdly 是精明地。名词 shrewdness 另立条。
① My grandmother was a shrewd businesswoman who bought land when it was cheap.
= 我奶奶是个精明的女商人，在地价便宜的时候买进了土地。
a shrewd businesswoman = 精明的女商人
shrewd = 精明的
② I had a shrewd idea that he was lying, because he would not look at me.
= 我心里很清楚他在撒谎，因为他不敢看我。
a shrewd idea = 心里有数的猜测
③ She shrewdly sold all her shares in the company just before the price fell.
= 她很精明地在股价下跌前卖出了手中那家公司的全部股票。
shrewdly = 精明地

shrewdness
/ˈʃruːdnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：精明、敏锐。
① Everyone admired the shrewdness of the old farmer, who always knew when to sell his cattle.
= 大家都佩服那位老农的精明，他总知道什么时候该卖牛。
the shrewdness of somebody = 某人的精明
shrewdness = 精明
② Her political shrewdness helped her to win three elections in a row.
= 她的政治敏锐帮她连赢了三次选举。
political shrewdness = 政治敏锐

shrewish
/ˈʃruːɪʃ/
核心：形容词（旧，贬义）：（女人）泼辣的、爱骂人的。
① In the old novel, the farmer's shrewish wife shouted at him from morning to night.
= 在那部老小说里，农夫那个泼辣的妻子从早到晚冲他嚷嚷。
a shrewish wife = 泼辣的妻子
shrewish = 泼辣的
② Modern readers often find the play's picture of a shrewish woman unfair.
= 现代读者常觉得那出戏对泼辣女人的刻画不公平。
a shrewish woman = 泼辣的女人
带性别偏见的旧词，多见于文学作品。

shriek

① The children shrieked with laughter when the clown fell into the water.
= 小丑掉进水里时，孩子们笑得尖叫起来。
shriek with laughter = 尖声大笑
shriek = 尖叫
② We heard a shriek from the bathroom and ran upstairs to see what was wrong.
= 我们听到浴室里一声尖叫，赶紧跑上楼看出了什么事。
a shriek = 一声尖叫
③ When she found her little brother reading her diary, she shrieked at him to get out.
= 她发现弟弟在偷看她的日记，就尖声冲他喊，叫他出去。
shriek at somebody = 冲某人尖叫

shrill
/ʃrɪl/
核心：形容词：（声音）尖利的、刺耳的：a shrill voice、a shrill whistle；（要求、批评）尖锐的、激烈的。副词 shrilly 是尖声地。名词 shrillness 另立条。
① The teacher blew a shrill whistle, and all the children stopped running.
= 老师吹了一声尖利的哨子，所有孩子都停下不跑了。
a shrill whistle = 尖利的哨声
shrill = 尖利的
② Since the scandal, the newspapers have made shrill demands for the minister to resign at once.
= 丑闻曝光以来，各家报纸激烈地要求那位部长立即辞职。
shrill demands = 激烈的要求
③ Late at night, the phone rang shrilly in the empty office, but nobody was there to answer it.
= 深夜，电话在空荡荡的办公室里尖声响着，却没人接。
shrilly = 尖声地

shrillness
/ˈʃrɪlnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：（声音的）尖利、刺耳；（言辞的）尖锐、激烈。
① At three in the morning, the shrillness of the fire alarm woke everyone in the building.
= 凌晨三点，刺耳的火警声惊醒了楼里所有人。
the shrillness of something = 某声音的尖利
shrillness = 刺耳
② The shrillness of the debate surprised viewers, who expected a calm discussion.
= 辩论的火药味让本以为会是平和讨论的观众吃了一惊。
the shrillness of the debate = 辩论的激烈

shrink
