robotic
/rəʊˈbɒtɪk/
核心：一是机器人的、自动化的：a robotic arm；二是（贬义）（人的动作、说话）机械的、呆板的。
① A robotic arm lifts the heavy car doors and fixes them into place.
= 一只机械臂把沉重的车门举起来，装到位。
a robotic arm = 机械臂
robotic = 机器人的
② The actor's voice sounded flat and robotic, as if he was reading from a list.
= 那演员的声音平板机械，像在念清单。
flat and robotic = 平板机械

advances in robotics = 机器人技术的进步

robust
/rəʊˈbʌst/
核心：一是（人）强健的、健壮的；（东西）结实的、耐用的；二是（制度、经济）稳健的、强劲的；（论点、行动）坚定有力的；三是（食物、酒）味浓的。副词 robustly，并入本条。
① My grandfather was a robust man who worked on the farm until he was eighty.
= 我爷爷身体强健，一直在农场干到八十岁。
robust = 强健的
② We need a robust box that will not break if it is dropped in the post.
= 我们需要一个结实的箱子，在邮寄途中摔了也不会坏。
a robust box = 结实的箱子
说东西结实时 robust 偏「经摔耐用」，比 strong 更强调禁得起粗用。
③ The country has a robust economy, and unemployment is very low.
= 那个国家经济强劲，失业率很低。
a robust economy = 强劲的经济
这一支常修饰 economy、growth、system、evidence，指经得起冲击、不轻易垮。
④ The minister gave a robust defence of the new policy in parliament.
= 部长在议会里为新政策作了坚定有力的辩护。
a robust defence = 坚定有力的辩护
⑤ This robust red wine goes well with beef and strong cheese.
= 这款浓郁的红葡萄酒配牛肉和味重的奶酪很好。
a robust wine = 浓郁的葡萄酒
⑥ The government robustly denied that it had known about the plan.
= 政府坚决否认事先知道那个计划。
robustly = 坚决地、有力地

robustness

/rɒk/
核心：名词：岩石、岩层，不可数；（海上、地上的）大石头、礁石，可数；（美式）石子、石块，可数；（英式）硬棒棒糖：a stick of rock，不可数；摇滚乐，不可数；（口语）钻石。

a rock = 礁石

= 男孩们往湖里扔石块，比谁溅起的水花大。

④ We bought a stick of pink rock with the name of the town written all the way through it.
= 我们买了一根粉红色的硬棒糖，里面从头到尾都印着镇名。

⑤ He listens to rock all day, but his sister prefers classical music.

⑧ The news of the minister's secret payments rocked the government.

⑨ Their marriage has been on the rocks since he lost his job last year.

⑩ He ordered a whisky on the rocks and sat down at the bar.
= 他点了一杯加冰威士忌，在吧台边坐下。
on the rocks = 加冰块的

= 我遇到种种麻烦，我的老朋友始终坚定可靠地支持我。

⑫ Everyone was happy with the way things were, so nobody wanted to rock the boat.
= 大家都满意现状，所以谁也不想捣乱。
rock the boat = 打破现状
⑬ I am caught between a rock and a hard place: if I tell the truth I lose my job, and if I lie I lose my friend.

roiling = 翻腾的
② Roiling black clouds filled the sky as the storm came closer.
= 暴风雨逼近，翻滚的乌云布满了天空。
roiling clouds = 翻滚的云

roistering
/ˈrɔɪstərɪŋ/
核心：（旧式）（喝酒）喧闹作乐的、狂欢的。也作名词，不可数：喧闹作乐。
① The roistering sailors sang and shouted in the harbour inn until the landlord threw them out.
= 喧闹作乐的水手在港口客栈里又唱又叫，最后被店主轰了出去。
roistering sailors = 喧闹作乐的水手
roistering = 喧闹作乐的
② The neighbours complained about the roistering that went on in the flat every weekend.
= 邻居们抱怨那套公寓每个周末都在喧闹作乐。
the roistering = 喧闹作乐

role
/rəʊl/
核心：一是作用、职能、角色：play a role in、a key role；二是（戏剧、电影中的）角色：play the role of、a leading role。可数。
复合词 role model 是榜样、模范；role reversal 是角色互换。
① Parents play a role in their children's success at school, but teachers matter too.
= 父母在孩子的学业成功中起作用，但老师也很重要。
play a role = 起作用
② The role of a referee is to make sure that both teams follow the rules.
= 裁判的职责是确保两队都遵守规则。
role = 角色
③ Tourism plays a key role in the economy of the island, and most families depend on it.
= 旅游业在那个岛的经济中起关键作用，大多数家庭都靠它生活。
key role = 关键作用
④ She has been offered the leading role in a new film about a famous scientist.
= 有人请她在一部讲著名科学家的新电影里演主角。
a leading role = 主角
⑤ He played the role of the king in the school play and forgot half his lines.
= 他在学校话剧里演国王，忘了一半台词。
play the role of somebody = 扮演某人
⑥ My older sister was my role model when I was growing up, and I copied everything she did.
= 我小时候姐姐是我的榜样，她做什么我就学什么。
role model = 榜样
⑦ When the father stayed at home and the mother went out to work, the children found the role reversal strange at first.
= 父亲在家带孩子、母亲出去上班，孩子们起初觉得这种角色互换很奇怪。
role reversal = 角色互换
