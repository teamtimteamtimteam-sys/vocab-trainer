rowan
/ˈrəʊən/
核心：花楸树：一种小乔木，秋天结一簇簇鲜红的浆果。可数；浆果说 rowan berries。也作 mountain ash。
① A rowan tree grows beside the gate, and in autumn it is covered in bright red berries.
= 大门边长着一棵花楸树，秋天满树都是鲜红的浆果。
a rowan tree = 花楸树
② Birds come to the garden every October to eat the rowan berries before the first frost.
= 每年十月，鸟儿都在头一场霜降之前飞到花园里来吃花楸果。
rowan berries = 花楸果

rowback
/ˈrəʊbæk/
核心：（英式口语，多见于新闻）名词，可数：（对先前立场、承诺的）收回、退让：a rowback on a promise。
① Critics called the change a humiliating rowback on the government's promise to cut taxes for families.
= 批评者称这一改变是政府在为家庭减税的承诺上丢脸的食言。
a rowback on a promise = 在承诺上食言
② After weeks of protests, the company announced a partial rowback and kept the old prices for older customers.
= 经过几个星期的抗议，那家公司宣布部分让步，对老顾客保留原价。
a partial rowback = 部分让步

rowboat
/ˈrəʊbəʊt/
核心：（北美）划艇：靠桨划的小船。可数。英式另有说法。
① We rented a rowboat for an hour and paddled slowly around the lake looking at the ducks.
= 我们租了一条划艇划一个小时，慢慢绕着湖划，看鸭子。
rent a rowboat = 租划艇
② An old wooden rowboat was tied to the dock, half full of rainwater and leaves.
= 码头上拴着一条旧木划艇，里面积了半船雨水和树叶。
a wooden rowboat = 木划艇

rowdiness
/ˈraʊdinəs/
核心：名词，不可数：吵闹、喧闹粗野。由 rowdy 加 -ness 构成。
① The landlord warned the students that any more rowdiness after midnight would mean the end of their lease.
= 房东警告那些学生，半夜以后再吵闹，就终止他们的租约。
rowdiness = 吵闹
② The rowdiness of the crowd outside the stadium kept the police busy until the early morning.
= 球场外人群喧闹粗野，警察一直忙到凌晨。
the rowdiness of a crowd = 人群的喧闹

rowdy
/ˈraʊdi/
核心：形容词：吵闹粗野的、闹哄哄的：a rowdy crowd、rowdy behaviour。比较级 rowdier。名词（旧式）：粗野吵闹的人。副词 rowdily 是吵吵嚷嚷地。
① A rowdy group of fans sang and shouted on the train all the way home after the match.
= 比赛结束后，一群吵吵闹闹的球迷在回家的火车上一路唱一路喊。
a rowdy crowd = 吵闹的人群
rowdy = 吵闹粗野的
② The pub was closed for a month after several nights of rowdy behaviour and fights in the street.
= 连续几个晚上有人吵闹粗野、在街上打架之后，那家酒馆被勒令停业一个月。
rowdy behaviour = 吵闹粗野的行为
多说一群人、聚会、酒馆里的场面，比 noisy 多一层粗野、快要失控的意思。
③ The guests were singing rowdily in the garden when the neighbours finally called the police.
= 客人们在花园里吵吵嚷嚷地唱歌，邻居终于报了警。
rowdily = 吵吵嚷嚷地

rowdyism
/ˈraʊdiɪzəm/
核心：名词，不可数（旧式）：粗野吵闹的行为。比 rowdiness 更强调成风的捣乱。
① The town council promised to deal with the rowdyism that broke out every Saturday night in the main square.
= 镇议会承诺治理每周六晚上在主广场上发生的吵闹捣乱。
rowdyism = 粗野吵闹的行为
② Newspapers at the time blamed the rowdyism at football grounds on drink and boredom.
= 当时的报纸把球场上的闹事归咎于酗酒和无聊。
rowdyism at football grounds = 球场闹事

rower
/ˈrəʊə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：划船的人、桨手，尤指赛艇运动员。
① The rowers trained on the river every morning at six, even when it was cold and dark.
= 赛艇运动员每天早上六点在河上训练，天冷天黑也不例外。
rowers = 赛艇运动员
② The two old fishermen were strong rowers who could cross the bay in under an hour.
= 那两个老渔民划船很有力气，一个小时不到就能横渡海湾。
a strong rower = 有力气的桨手

rowing

① We went rowing on the lake after breakfast, and my arms ached for the rest of the day.
= 早饭后我们去湖上划船，我胳膊酸了一整天。

② Rowing is a hard sport because you have to train for hours with the rest of your crew.

rowing = 赛艇运动

rowlock
/ˈrɒlək/
核心：（英式）桨架、桨叉：船舷上架桨的叉形或环形支架。可数。北美说 oarlock。
① One of the rowlocks broke halfway across the river, so we had to paddle back with a single oar.
= 过河过到一半，一个桨架坏了，我们只好用一支桨划回去。
a rowlock = 桨架
② He slid the oars into the rowlocks and pushed the boat away from the shore.
= 他把桨插进桨架，把船推离了岸边。
slide the oars into the rowlocks = 把桨插进桨架

royal
/ˈrɔɪəl/
核心：形容词：王室的、皇家的：the royal family、a royal palace；（机构名）皇家的：the Royal Navy、Royal Air Force；（口语）盛大的、极好的：a royal welcome；（北美口语）极大的：a royal pain。名词，可数（口语）：王室成员：the royals。
名词 royalty 指王室成员（总称）和版税（另立条）；副词 royally 是盛大地、隆重地，口语里也表示「极其」。
① The royal family usually spends the summer at their castle in Scotland.
= 王室一家通常在苏格兰的城堡里过夏天。
royal = 王室的
the royal family = 王室
指国王一家时 royal 只作定语，不说 the family is royal。
② Thousands of people waited outside the royal palace to see the new king and queen on the balcony.
= 成千上万的人在王宫外等着，想看新国王和王后在阳台上露面。
a royal palace = 王宫
③ My grandfather was a pilot in the Royal Air Force during the war and flew over Germany many times.
= 我祖父战时是皇家空军的飞行员，多次飞越德国上空。
Royal Air Force = 皇家空军
④ The newspapers are full of stories about the royals and what they wore to the wedding.
= 报纸上满是关于王室成员和他们在婚礼上穿什么的报道。
the royals = 王室成员
⑤ The team was given a royal welcome when they arrived home with the cup.
= 球队捧着奖杯回到家乡时受到了盛大的欢迎。
a royal welcome = 盛大的欢迎
⑥ Fixing the old car every weekend has been a royal pain, and I wish I had sold it years ago.
= 每个周末修那辆旧车真是件天大的麻烦事，我真希望几年前就把它卖了。
a royal pain = （北美口语）天大的麻烦
⑦ The visitors were royally entertained with music, dancing and a feast that lasted until midnight.
= 客人们受到了隆重款待，有音乐、舞蹈，还有一场一直吃到半夜的宴席。
be royally entertained = 受到隆重款待
royally = 盛大地、极其
⑧ The writer still earns royalty payments from the book she published thirty years ago.
= 那位作家至今还从她三十年前出的那本书拿版税。
royalty = 王室成员、版税
⑨ When the prince was born, the guns fired a royal salute from the walls of the castle.
= 王子出生时，礼炮从城堡的城墙上鸣放了皇家礼炮。
a royal salute = 皇家礼炮
通常是二十一响，用于王室的庆典。
⑩ Royal tennis is an old indoor game that was played by kings of England in the sixteenth century.
= 皇家网球是一种古老的室内运动，十六世纪英格兰的国王们就玩过。
royal tennis = 皇家网球（老式室内网球）
也叫 real tennis，与现代草地网球不同。
⑪ The jam company has held a royal warrant for fifty years, so its jars show the royal coat of arms.
= 那家果酱公司持有王室供货许可五十年了，所以罐子上印着王室徽章。
a royal warrant = 王室供货许可
获得许可的商家可以在包装上使用王室徽章。
⑫ The royal yacht carried the queen on visits to many countries before it was turned into a museum.
= 那艘王室游艇载着女王出访过许多国家，后来改成了博物馆。
a royal yacht = 王室游艇

royalist
/ˈrɔɪəlɪst/
核心：名词，可数：保王派、君主制拥护者；（英国历史）内战中支持国王的保王党人，与 Roundhead 相对。形容词：保王的。
① Even after the revolution, a small group of royalists hoped that the old king would one day return.
= 即使在革命之后，仍有一小批保王派盼着老国王有一天能回来。
a royalist = 保王派
② The royalist army lost the battle and the king had to escape to France in disguise.
= 保王党的军队打了败仗，国王只好乔装逃往法国。
the royalist army = 保王党的军队

royalty
/ˈrɔɪəlti/
核心：名词：一是王室成员（总称，不可数）：treat somebody like royalty；二是版税、专利使用费（可数，多用复数）：royalties from a book。比喻也指某个圈子里最显赫的人：Hollywood royalty。
① The hotel staff treated us like royalty, carrying our bags and bringing us drinks by the pool.
= 酒店员工把我们当贵宾一样伺候，帮我们拎包，还把饮料送到泳池边。
treat somebody like royalty = 待某人如上宾
② The film premiere in London was attended by several members of European royalty and dozens of famous actors.
= 好几位欧洲王室成员和几十位名演员出席了那部电影在伦敦的首映礼。
members of royalty = 王室成员
③ The singer still receives royalties every time one of his old songs is played on the radio.
= 那位歌手的老歌每在电台播放一次，他都能拿到版税。
royalties = 版税
这一义多用复数，常和 receive、earn、pay 连用。
④ As the daughter of two famous actors, she was treated as Hollywood royalty from the day she was born.
= 作为两位名演员的女儿，她一出生就被当作好莱坞的显贵。
Hollywood royalty = 好莱坞显贵

rozzer

a rozzer = 条子

rub
/rʌb/
核心：动词：擦、揉、搓：rub your eyes、rub your hands together；（来回）摩擦：rub against；把（药膏等）抹上、揉进：rub cream into your skin；擦亮、擦掉：rub something clean；（鞋等）磨（脚）。名词，单数：擦、揉：give something a rub。
习语：rub shoulders with 是和（名人等）打交道、厮混；rub somebody up the wrong way 是惹恼某人；rub salt into the wound 是在伤口上撒盐；there's the rub（书面）是难就难在这里。
① She yawned and rubbed her eyes as she came downstairs for breakfast on Monday morning.
= 星期一早上她下楼吃早饭，一边打哈欠一边揉眼睛。
rub = 擦、揉
rub your eyes = 揉眼睛
② The old men stood at the bus stop, rubbing their hands together to keep warm in the frosty air.
= 老人们站在公交车站，在冰冷的空气里搓着手取暖。
rub your hands together = 搓手
③ The cat rubbed against my legs and purred until I gave it something to eat.
= 那只猫蹭着我的腿呼噜呼噜叫，直到我给它吃的。
rub against something = 在某物上蹭
不及物用法，主语是来回蹭的那一方，多说猫狗、衣领、鞋子。
④ Rub a little of the cream into your skin twice a day until the rash goes away.
= 每天两次把一点药膏揉进皮肤里，直到疹子消退。
rub cream into your skin = 把药膏揉进皮肤
结构是 rub A into / on B：A 是油、膏、粉，B 是皮肤、肉、表面。
⑤ My new boots rubbed my heels so badly that I had blisters by the end of the walk.
= 我的新靴子把脚后跟磨得很厉害，走完一趟就起了水泡。
shoes rub = 鞋磨脚
⑥ Give the table a good rub with a soft cloth, and the old wood will start to shine again.
= 用软布把桌子好好擦一擦，那老木头就又会发亮。
give something a rub = 把某物擦一擦
⑦ As a journalist in the capital, she rubbed shoulders with film stars and politicians every week.
= 作为首都的记者，她每周都和影星、政客打交道。
rub shoulders with somebody = 和某人打交道
⑧ He has a habit of rubbing people up the wrong way, so he is not very popular in the office.
= 他总是惹人恼火，所以在办公室里人缘不太好。
rub somebody up the wrong way = 惹恼某人
⑨ Losing the final was bad enough, but hearing the other team laugh at us really rubbed salt into the wound.
= 输掉决赛已经够难受了，听到对方嘲笑我们更是在伤口上撒盐。
rub salt into the wound = 在伤口上撒盐
⑩ We would love to move to the country, but there's the rub: neither of us could find work there.
= 我们很想搬到乡下去，可难就难在这里：我们俩在那儿都找不到工作。
there's the rub = 难就难在这里
这句来自莎士比亚，口气偏书面、带点戏谑。
⑪ My two brothers are very different, but they rub along well enough when they share a flat.
= 我两个哥哥很不一样，可合租一套房时也还处得来。
rub along = （英式口语）相处得还行
多说 rub along together / with somebody，指不算亲密、但能和平相处。
⑫ After the swim, his mother rubbed him down with a big towel so that he would not get cold.
= 游完泳后，他妈妈用一条大毛巾给他擦干身子，免得着凉。
rub down = 用毛巾擦干；（用砂纸）打磨
也说 rub down a door before painting it，指上漆前打磨。
⑬ Rub in the sun cream well, especially on your nose and the back of your neck.
= 把防晒霜好好揉开，尤其是鼻子和后颈。
rub in = 揉进去
也说 rub the butter into the flour，指做糕点时把黄油搓进面粉。
⑭ I know I made a mistake, so there is no need to rub it in by telling everyone in the office.
= 我知道我错了，你用不着到处跟办公室里的人说来揭我的短。
rub it in = 揭人短处
反复提起别人的失误、让人更难堪。
⑮ The smell of smoke would not rub off, even after I had washed my hands three times.
= 那股烟味怎么也擦不掉，我洗了三遍手都没用。
rub off = 擦掉；（性格、习惯）感染别人
也说 her enthusiasm rubbed off on the whole class，指热情感染了全班。
⑯ The teacher told us to rub out the wrong answers and write the correct ones in pen.
= 老师叫我们把错的答案擦掉，用钢笔写上正确的。
rub out = 擦掉
美式俚语里 rub somebody out 也指干掉某人。

rubber
/ˈrʌbə(r)/
核心：名词：橡胶（不可数）：made of rubber；（英式）橡皮擦（可数），北美多说 eraser；（北美口语）避孕套（可数）；（桥牌等）三局两胜的一盘。形容词用法（作定语）：橡胶的：rubber gloves。

rubber = 橡胶
be made of rubber = 橡胶制成
② Always wear rubber gloves when you use strong cleaning products in the kitchen.
= 在厨房里用强效清洁剂时一定要戴橡胶手套。
rubber gloves = 橡胶手套
③ Can I borrow your rubber? I made a mistake in my drawing and need to fix it before the lesson ends.
= 能借你的橡皮用一下吗？我画错了，得在下课前改好。

④ The pharmacist kept the rubbers on a high shelf behind the counter.
= 药剂师把避孕套放在柜台后面的高架子上。

rubberneck
/ˈrʌbənek/
核心：（口语，贬义）动词：伸长脖子看热闹，尤指开车经过事故现场时减速围观。名词，可数：看热闹的人。

= 高速公路两侧的车都开得很慢，因为司机们都在伸着脖子看事故。
rubberneck = 伸长脖子看热闹
② A crowd of rubbernecks gathered outside the burning house and got in the way of the fire engines.
= 一群看热闹的人聚在着火的房子外面，挡了消防车的路。
a rubberneck = 看热闹的人

rubbernecker
/ˈrʌbənekə(r)/

① The police put up screens around the crash so that rubberneckers would stop slowing down to look.
= 警察在撞车现场周围竖起屏障，好让围观的司机不再减速张望。
rubberneckers = 围观的人
② One rubbernecker was so busy staring at the accident that he drove into the car in front of him.
= 有个看热闹的司机光顾着盯着事故看，撞上了前面的车。
a rubbernecker = 看热闹的人

rubbery

rubbery legs = 发软的腿
③ The toy is made of a soft rubbery plastic that is safe for babies to chew.

rubbing
/ˈrʌbɪŋ/
核心：名词：一是摩擦、揉搓（不可数）；二是拓印、拓片（可数）：把纸铺在浮雕、碑文上用蜡笔等擦出图案：a brass rubbing。
① Years of rubbing against the rocks had worn a deep groove in the rope.
= 多年在岩石上摩擦，绳子上磨出了一道深槽。
rubbing = 摩擦
② The children made rubbings of the old gravestones by laying paper over them and using crayons.

rubbish
/ˈrʌbɪʃ/
核心：名词，不可数（英式）：垃圾、废物：throw away rubbish、take the rubbish out，北美多说 garbage、trash；（口语）废话、胡说：talk rubbish；（口语）蹩脚的东西：the film was rubbish。形容词（英式口语）：差劲的、不擅长的：be rubbish at something。动词（英式口语）：贬斥、说得一钱不值：rubbish an idea。
① Please take the rubbish out before you go to bed, because it is collected early tomorrow morning.
= 睡觉前请把垃圾拿出去，明天一大早就有人来收。
rubbish = 垃圾
take the rubbish out = 倒垃圾
② The beach was covered in plastic bottles and other rubbish after the holiday weekend.
= 假日周末过后，海滩上满是塑料瓶和别的垃圾。
plastic rubbish = 塑料垃圾
③ Don't talk rubbish — you know perfectly well that you were the one who lost the keys.
= 别胡说了，你心里明明清楚弄丢钥匙的就是你。
talk rubbish = 胡说八道
也可单说 Rubbish! 表示「胡扯！」，是英式口语里很常见的反驳。
④ We left the cinema after half an hour because the film was absolute rubbish.
= 半个小时后我们就离开了电影院，因为那部电影烂透了。

⑤ I am rubbish at maths, so my sister always helps me with my homework in the evenings.
= 我数学很差，所以姐姐晚上总帮我做作业。

⑥ The minister rubbished the report and said it was based on old and unreliable figures.
= 那位部长把报告批得一钱不值，说它依据的是陈旧不可靠的数字。
rubbish something = 贬斥某事
⑦ He screwed up the letter and threw it into the rubbish bin under his desk without reading it.
= 他看都没看就把信揉成一团，扔进了桌子底下的垃圾桶。
rubbish bin = 垃圾桶
（英式）；北美说 trash can、garbage can。

rubble
/ˈrʌbl/

② The doctor said the red spots on the child's face were caused by rubella and would soon disappear.
= 医生说孩子脸上的红点是风疹引起的，很快就会消退。
rubella = 风疹

= 那幅画画的是三个丰腴的女子在夏日天空下的绿野里跳舞。
Rubenesque women = 丰腴的女子
