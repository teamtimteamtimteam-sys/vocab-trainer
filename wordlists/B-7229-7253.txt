elder
核心：三支。一是（家里、两人中）年长的：my elder sister，只作定语；
二是名词「长者、长辈」，教会里的 elder 是长老；三是接骨木（树）。
注意别混：不说 elder than，比较要用 older than。
① My elder sister remembers the house and I only remember the garden.
= 我姐姐记得那栋房子，我只记得那个园子。
an elder sister = 姐姐
② Village elders decided the boundary and nobody has moved it since.
= 村里的长者定下那条界，之后没人挪过。
village elders = 村中长者
③ The elder by the gate flowers in June and feeds every bird in August.
= 门边那棵接骨木六月开花，八月喂饱所有的鸟。
an elder = 接骨木
④ Elder abuse is under-reported because the person reporting it usually lives there.
= 虐待老人的事很少被举报，因为能举报的人多半就住在那家里。
elder abuse = 虐待老人
⑤ The elder statesman was wheeled out for one paragraph and a photograph.
= 那位政坛元老被请出来，就为了一段话和一张照片。
an elder statesman = 政坛元老

elderberry
核心：接骨木果：接骨木上一串串的小黑果，多拿来做酒、糖浆与果酱；
生的不能吃，要煮熟。可数，复数 elderberries。
① Elderberries stain everything they touch, including the pan.
= 接骨木果碰到什么染什么，连锅也不放过。
elderberries = 接骨木果
② She makes elderberry syrup every September and gives most of it away.
= 她每年九月熬接骨木糖浆，多半都送人了。
elderberry syrup = 接骨木糖浆

eldercare
核心：（美式）老年照护：照料老年人的服务与安排，英式多说 care for the elderly。不可数。
① Eldercare costs more than childcare and gets a fraction of the attention.
= 老年照护比育儿花钱多，得到的关注却只有一小部分。
eldercare = 老年照护
② The firm added eldercare leave and half the staff used it within a year.
= 那家公司加了照护老人的假，一年之内有一半员工用了。
eldercare leave = 照护老人的假

elderflower
核心：接骨木花：接骨木初夏开的成簇白花，香气浓，用来做糖浆、气泡饮与利口酒。不可数。
① Elderflower is picked on a dry morning and used the same day.
= 接骨木花要挑干燥的早上采，当天就得用掉。
elderflower = 接骨木花
② Elderflower cordial tastes of the two weeks in which it can be made.
= 接骨木花糖浆的味道，就是能做它的那两个星期的味道。
elderflower cordial = 接骨木花糖浆

elderly
核心：两支。一是（委婉）年长的、上了年纪的：比 old 客气；
二是 the elderly，指老年人这一群体，作复数看待。
① An elderly neighbour keeps the spare key and knows everyone's timetable.
= 一位上了年纪的邻居保管着备用钥匙，谁几点出门都清楚。
an elderly neighbour = 上了年纪的邻居
elderly = 年长的、上了年纪的
② Services for the elderly were cut in March and demand rose in the same month.
= 面向老年人的服务三月被砍，需求也在同一个月涨了。
the elderly = 老年人（群体）
注意语域：the elderly 有把人一概而论之嫌，写作时多用 older people。

eldest
核心：最年长的：三个或三个以上里最大的那个，多说家里的孩子；
也作名词：the eldest of four。只作定语与名词，两者相比要用 oldest。
① The eldest daughter runs the farm and the other three visit at Christmas.
= 大女儿管着农场，另外三个圣诞才来。
the eldest daughter = 大女儿
② She is the eldest of four and the only one who left.
= 她是四个里最大的，也是唯一走掉的那个。
the eldest of four = 四个里最年长的

eldritch
核心：（文学）诡异的、瘆人的：说声音或景象透着一股不自然的邪气，
如今多见于恐怖小说与仿古文体。
① An eldritch cry came off the moor and the dogs would not go out.
= 荒原上传来一声瘆人的怪叫，狗死活不肯出门。
an eldritch cry = 瘆人的怪叫
② The story piles up eldritch adjectives and forgets to frighten anyone.
= 那篇小说堆了一大堆诡异的形容词，却忘了吓着人。
eldritch = 诡异瘆人的

elect
核心：三支。一是选举、推选：elect somebody to something；
二是（正式）选择去做：elect to do something；
三是后置形容词：the president elect 指已当选、尚未就职的。
名词有 election、elector、electorate。
① They elected her chair on the second ballot and nobody else stood.
= 第二轮投票选了她当主席，也没有别人参选。
elect somebody chair = 选某人当主席
elect somebody to something = 选某人进入某机构
② He elected to take the earlier flight and missed the whole argument.
= 他选择坐早一班飞机，整场争吵都躲开了。
elect to do something = 选择做某事
③ The president elect said nothing at all for six weeks.
= 那位候任总统整整六个星期一言不发。
the president elect = 候任总统

electability
核心：可当选性、选得上的把握：说候选人在选民那里的胜算。不可数。
① The party talks about electability and means somebody else's candidate.
= 那个党嘴上说胜算，心里指的是别人的人选。
electability = 可当选性
② Her electability rose the year she stopped explaining herself.
= 她不再替自己辩解的那一年，胜算反倒升了。
somebody's electability = 某人的当选把握

electable
核心：选得上的、有当选希望的：说候选人能被选民接受。
① He is electable in two regions and unheard of in the other four.
= 他在两个大区选得上，在另外四个没人听说过。
electable = 有当选希望的
② The safest candidate is not always the most electable one.
= 最稳妥的候选人，未必是最选得上的那个。
the most electable candidate = 最有当选希望的人选
构词：elect 加 -able

election
核心：两支。一是选举：一次投票选人的过程，可数；二是当选这件事。
a general election 是大选，a by-election 是补选。
① The election was called in July and decided almost nothing.
= 那场选举七月宣布举行，几乎什么也没定下来。
an election = 选举
a general election = 大选
② Her election as leader surprised even the people who had counted the votes.
= 她当选党首，连数票的人都意外。
somebody's election as something = 某人当选为…

electioneering
核心：（常含贬义）竞选拉票：为选举奔走造势 ——
说某件事是 electioneering，常暗指那是做给选票看的。不可数。
① Six weeks of electioneering produced two promises and one poster campaign.
= 六个星期的竞选活动，产出两个承诺和一轮海报战。
electioneering = 竞选拉票
② The visit was pure electioneering and the timetable proved it.
= 那次视察纯粹是拉票，日程表就是证据。
pure electioneering = 十足的作秀拉票

elective
核心：三支。一是选举产生的：an elective office 是民选职位；
二是（医疗）择期的：elective surgery 不是急救手术；
三是（美式）名词「选修课」。
① An elective office looks different the day after the count.
= 民选的职位，点票次日看着就不是一回事了。
an elective office = 民选职位
② Elective surgery was postponed for four months and the lists never recovered.
= 择期手术推迟了四个月，排队名单再没缓过来。
elective surgery = 择期手术
③ She took two electives in a subject she had never studied before.
= 她在一门从没学过的科目里选了两门选修课。
an elective = 选修课（美式）

elector
核心：两支。一是选民、有投票权的人；二是（美国）选举人：选举人团里投票的那些人。可数。
① Every elector on that register was written to twice.
= 名册上的每一位选民都收到过两封信。
an elector = 选民
② The electors meet in December and confirm what November decided.
= 选举人十二月开会，确认十一月已经定下的结果。
electors = 选举人（美国选举人团成员）

electoral
核心：选举的：跟选举与选民有关，只作定语：the electoral system、electoral law。
① The electoral system rewards the party that comes second everywhere.
= 那套选举制度，反倒便宜了处处屈居第二的那个党。
the electoral system = 选举制度
electoral reform = 选举改革
② Electoral law says nothing at all about that kind of advertisement.
= 选举法对那类广告只字未提。
electoral law = 选举法
③ The electoral college can pick the candidate who came second in votes.
= 选举人团有可能选出票数居次的那位候选人。
the electoral college = 选举人团
④ Your name has to be on the electoral register four weeks before polling day.
= 名字得在投票日前四周就上到选民登记册上。
the electoral register = 选民登记册
an electoral vote = 选举人票

electorally
核心：在选举上、从选票上看：修饰整句或形容词，
常见搭配是 electorally risky、electorally impossible。
① The policy is sound and electorally impossible in three regions.
= 那项政策本身站得住，可在三个大区选票上通不过。
electorally impossible = 选票上办不到的
② Electorally, the smaller party gained more from losing than from winning.
= 从选票上看，那个小党输比赢得到的还多。
electorally = 在选举上
构词：electoral 加 -ly

electorate
核心：全体选民：一地有投票权的人的总和，作单数或复数看待都行。可数。
① The electorate is older than it was and votes more reliably.
= 选民比从前年长，投票也更稳。
the electorate = 全体选民
② A quarter of the electorate has never voted in a local election.
= 有四分之一的选民，从没在地方选举里投过票。
a quarter of the electorate = 四分之一的选民

electric
核心：三支。一是用电的、带电的：an electric kettle；
二是（气氛）紧张而热烈的；三是名词复数 electrics，指车或房子的电路系统。
注意别混：electric 说具体的用电物件，electrical 偏「与电有关的」，electronic 是电子的。
① The electric kettle boils in ninety seconds and trips the fuse in three.
= 那把电水壶九十秒烧开，三秒就跳闸。
an electric kettle = 电水壶
electric light = 电灯
② The atmosphere in the hall was electric until the microphone failed.
= 大厅里的气氛热烈得要爆，直到话筒坏掉。
be electric = 气氛紧张而热烈
③ Electric cars are quiet enough that the law now requires a noise.
= 电动车安静到法律如今要求它们发出声响。
an electric car = 电动汽车
an electric bike = 电动自行车
④ The electric blanket goes on at nine and off before anyone gets into bed.
= 电热毯九点打开，还没人上床就关掉。
an electric blanket = 电热毯
⑤ She painted the shed electric blue and the whole lane talks about it.
= 她把那间棚子刷成亮蓝色，整条巷子都在议论。
electric blue = 亮蓝色
⑥ Two states retired the electric chair in the same year.
= 有两个州在同一年停用了电椅。
the electric chair = 电椅
⑦ An electric eel stuns its prey and its own jaw at the same time.
= 电鳗放电既击晕猎物，也震到自己的下颌。
an electric eel = 电鳗
⑧ The electric fence keeps the cattle in and teaches the dog once.
= 那道电围栏把牛拦住，也让狗一次就学乖。
an electric fence = 电围栏
⑨ He shaves with an electric razor on the train and nobody objects.
= 他在火车上用电动剃须刀刮胡子，也没人说什么。
an electric razor = 电动剃须刀
⑩ The electric shock threw him across the room and left one small mark.
= 那下电击把他弹到屋子那头，只留下一个小印子。
an electric shock = 电击

electrical
核心：与电有关的：说系统、设备、行业与专业。
electrical engineering 是电气工程，an electrical fault 是电路故障。副词 electrically。
① An electrical fault started the fire and the wiring was forty years old.
= 火是电路故障引起的，那些线已经用了四十年。
an electrical fault = 电路故障
electrical equipment = 电气设备
② She read electrical engineering and now works in insurance.
= 她读的是电气工程，如今做保险。
electrical engineering = 电气工程
③ An electrical storm took out the power and left the phones working.
= 一场雷暴把电断了，电话倒还通着。
an electrical storm = 雷暴

electrically
核心：用电地、靠电力地：说驱动或操作的方式。
① The gates are electrically operated and open at exactly half past eight.
= 那些门是电动的，八点半准时开。
be electrically operated = 电动操作的
② The seat adjusts electrically and jams in the one position nobody wants.
= 座椅靠电动调节，偏偏卡在谁也不想要的那个位置。
adjust electrically = 电动调节
构词：electrical 加 -ly

electrician
核心：电工：安装与检修电路、电气设备的人。可数。
① The electrician found the fault in nine minutes and charged for an hour.
= 电工九分钟就找到了故障，收了一个钟头的钱。
an electrician = 电工
call an electrician = 叫电工来
② A qualified electrician has to sign off the work before it is covered.
= 得有持证电工签字验收，保险才认。
a qualified electrician = 持证电工

electricity
核心：两支。一是电：作为能量与供应的电；二是（比喻）令人兴奋的气氛。不可数。
① Electricity went off at four and came back after the news.
= 四点停电，新闻播完才来。
electricity = 电
an electricity bill = 电费单
② There was an electricity in the room that the recording does not pick up.
= 屋里那股兴奋劲儿，录音里听不出来。
electricity = （比喻）令人兴奋的气氛

electrics
核心：（英式）电路系统：车子或房子里整套的电气部分。复数形式。
① The electrics failed in the rain and the engine was perfectly fine.
= 雨里电路出了毛病，发动机倒好好的。
the electrics = 电路系统
② Rewiring the electrics cost more than the roof did.
= 重新布线花的钱比修屋顶还多。
rewire the electrics = 重新布线

electrification
核心：两支。一是电气化：把铁路或地区改成电力驱动、通上电；
二是通电、带电的过程。不可数。
① Electrification of that line stopped at the county boundary for nine years.
= 那条线的电气化在郡界上停了九年。
the electrification of something = 某物的电气化
② Rural electrification changed the working day before it changed anything else.
= 农村通电，最先改变的是一天怎么干活。
rural electrification = 农村电气化

electrify
核心：三支。一是使电气化：给铁路、地区改电力；二是给…通电、使带电；
三是（比喻）使震动、使振奋。过去式 electrified。
① They electrified the line in stages and ran diesels on the rest.
= 他们分段把线路电气化，其余路段仍跑柴油机车。
electrify a line = 把线路电气化
② The fence is electrified and the sheep learned it in one afternoon.
= 那道围栏通了电，羊一个下午就学乖了。
be electrified = 通了电的
③ Her first three sentences electrified a room that had been half asleep.
= 她开头三句话，把半睡半醒的一屋子人震住了。
electrify somebody = 使某人震动、振奋
