frilly
核心：三支。一是花边繁复的：a frilly blouse；二是（贬）过分花哨的；
三是（文字）堆砌辞藻的。
① A frilly blouse hangs in that wardrobe and has never been worn.
= 那个衣柜里挂着一件花边衬衫，从没穿过。
a frilly blouse = 花边衬衫
② The room is frilly in a way that costs a great deal to keep clean.
= 那间屋子花哨得很，打扫起来很费钱。
be frilly = 花哨繁复
③ His prose is frilly and the argument underneath is three lines long.
= 他的文字堆砌辞藻，底下的论点只有三行。
frilly prose = 辞藻堆砌的文字

fringe
核心：四支。一是（英）刘海：cut your fringe；二是（衣物、地毯的）流苏；
三是边缘、外围：on the fringe of something；四是（团体、剧场的）边缘、非主流。
习语 a fringe benefit 是附加福利。
① She cuts her own fringe and blames the mirror twice a year.
= 她自己剪刘海，一年怪两次镜子。
cut your fringe = 剪刘海
② The rug has a fringe that the vacuum cleaner eats every time.
= 那块地毯的流苏，每次都被吸尘器吃掉。
a fringe = 流苏
③ They live on the fringe of the town and pay town rates.
= 他们住在镇子边缘，交的是镇里的税。
on the fringe of something = 在某处的边缘
④ A fringe benefit of that job is a car that nobody else wants.
= 那份工作的附加福利，是一辆没人要的车。
a fringe benefit = 附加福利
fringe medicine = 非主流医学
Fringe medicine cures a cold in seven days and so does nothing.
= 非主流医学七天治好感冒，什么也不做也是七天。
fringe theatre = 边缘剧场
Fringe theatre puts nine people in a room above a pub.
= 边缘剧场把九个人塞进酒馆楼上的一间屋子。
