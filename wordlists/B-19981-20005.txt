scarab
/ˈskærəb/
核心：名词，可数：圣甲虫：古埃及人视为神圣的一种甲虫；圣甲虫形的护身符、宝石。
① The ancient Egyptians believed that the scarab rolled the sun across the sky every day.
= 古埃及人相信圣甲虫每天推着太阳穿过天空。
the scarab = 圣甲虫
② She wore a blue stone scarab on a gold chain that her father had bought in Cairo.
= 她戴着一枚蓝色石头雕的圣甲虫护身符，挂在她父亲在开罗买的金链子上。
a stone scarab = 石雕圣甲虫护身符

scarce
/skeəs/
核心：形容词：稀少的、短缺的、不足的：Food was scarce.、scarce resources。习语 make yourself scarce 是（为避开麻烦）溜走、躲开。副词 scarcely：几乎不、简直不：scarcely ever、scarcely … when 是刚……就；（文）勉强。名词 scarcity 是短缺、稀缺（另立条）。
① Water is scarce in the desert, so people store every drop of rain they can.
= 沙漠里水很稀缺，所以人们把能接到的每一滴雨水都存起来。
scarce = 稀缺的
② During the war, food was scarce and many families grew vegetables in their gardens.
= 战争期间食物短缺，许多家庭在自家园子里种菜。
food is scarce = 食物短缺
近义对照：
scarcity = 稀缺
③ When my father came home in a bad mood, we children made ourselves scarce.
= 我爸心情不好回到家时，我们几个孩子就赶紧躲开。
make oneself scarce = 溜走
④ I could scarcely believe it when they told me I had won the first prize.
= 他们告诉我我得了一等奖时，我简直不敢相信。
scarcely = 几乎不
⑤ We had scarcely sat down to dinner on Christmas Day when the telephone rang in the hall.
= 圣诞节那天我们刚坐下吃晚饭，门厅里的电话就响了。
scarcely … when = 刚……就
这一结构较书面，when 前的动作刚完成，后一个动作就紧接着发生。
⑥ My grandmother scarcely ever leaves the village now that she is in her nineties.
= 我祖母九十多岁了，现在几乎从不离开村子。
scarcely ever = 几乎从不

scare
/skeə(r)/
核心：动词：使害怕、吓唬：scare somebody；害怕、受惊：scare easily；吓跑、吓走；scare somebody into doing something 是吓得某人去做某事。名词：惊吓（单数）：give somebody a scare；（公众的）恐慌（可数）：a health scare、a bomb scare。形容词 scared（另立条）是害怕的；scary 是吓人的（另立条）。复合词 scaredy-cat（口语）是胆小鬼。
① The loud bang from the street outside scared the baby, and she started to cry.
= 外面街上那声巨响吓着了宝宝，她哭了起来。
scare = 吓
scare somebody = 吓着某人
多指突然一下的惊吓，口语常用；被吓到的人作宾语。
② She is scared of spiders and screams whenever she sees one in the bath.
= 她怕蜘蛛，每次在浴缸里看到一只就尖叫。
be scared of = 害怕
③ The film was so scary that my little brother watched it from behind the sofa.
= 那部电影太吓人了，我弟弟躲在沙发后面看。
scary = 吓人的
④ The farmer put a scarecrow in the field to scare away the birds.
= 农民在地里立了个稻草人，把鸟吓跑。
scare away = 吓跑
⑤ The dogs barking in the garden scared off the burglars before they could get in.
= 花园里的狗叫声在窃贼进门前就把他们吓跑了。
scare off = 吓走
⑥ We managed to scare up enough money for the train tickets by emptying our piggy banks.
= 我们把存钱罐倒空，好不容易凑够了火车票钱。
scare up = （北美口语）设法凑出
⑦ The advertisements try to scare people into buying expensive locks and alarms.
= 那些广告想吓唬人们去买昂贵的锁和警报器。
scare into doing = 吓得某人去做某事
⑧ You gave me a scare when you jumped out from behind the door!
= 你从门后跳出来，吓了我一跳！
give somebody a scare = 吓某人一跳
⑨ The airport was closed for three hours because of a bomb scare.
= 由于有炸弹恐慌，机场关闭了三个小时。
a bomb scare = 炸弹恐慌
⑩ The journalist put the word "experts" in scare quotes to show that she did not trust them.
= 那位记者给「专家」一词加上了讽刺性的引号，表示她不信任他们。
scare quotes = 表示讽刺、保留的引号
⑪ Don't be such a scaredy-cat; the old dog next door will not bite you.
= 别那么胆小，隔壁那条老狗不会咬你的。
a scaredy-cat = （口语）胆小鬼
