negligee
/ˈneɡlɪʒeɪ/
核心：两支，可数，来自法语，也写作 negligée。一是女式薄睡袍：轻薄、带花边的那种；二是泛指这类贴身的家居衣着。
① She came down to breakfast in a silk negligee and slippers.
= 她穿着一件丝绸薄睡袍和拖鞋下楼吃早饭。
a silk negligee = 丝绸薄睡袍
② The shop window displayed a lace negligee at a price that made her laugh.
= 橱窗里摆着一件蕾丝睡袍，那价钱让她笑出了声。
a lace negligee = 蕾丝睡袍

negligence
/ˈneɡlɪdʒəns/
核心：疏忽、过失：没有尽到应有的小心，不可数。法律上指因此造成他人损害而要担责的行为：medical negligence。
形容词 negligent 一是疏忽的、失职的，二是（书面）漫不经心、随意的；副词 negligently。并入本条。
① The accident was caused by negligence on the part of the driver.
= 那起事故是司机一方的疏忽造成的。
negligence on the part of somebody = 某人一方的疏忽
② The family is suing the hospital for medical negligence.
= 那家人正以医疗过失为由起诉医院。
medical negligence = 医疗过失
③ The court found the school negligent in failing to supervise the pool.
= 法院认定，学校没有派人看管泳池，属于失职。
be negligent in doing something = 在做某事上失职
④ He waved a negligent hand towards the chairs and went on reading.
= 他漫不经心地朝椅子挥了挥手，接着看他的书。
a negligent hand = 漫不经心的一挥手
这一支书面，指随意、不上心的样子。
⑤ The guard had negligently left the gate unlocked overnight.
= 那个门卫疏忽大意，让大门一夜没锁。
negligently leave something = 疏忽大意地留下某物

negligible
/ˈneɡlɪdʒəbəl/
核心：两支。一是微不足道的、小到可以忽略的：多说数量、影响、风险、成本；二是（否定形式）not negligible，不容小看的。
① The cost of running the lamp for a year is negligible.
= 那盏灯开一年的花费微不足道。
be negligible = 微不足道
② The change had a negligible effect on the time it takes to cross the city.
= 那项改动对穿城所需的时间影响小到可以忽略。
a negligible effect = 可以忽略的影响
③ The risk to swimmers is small but not negligible.
= 对游泳的人来说，风险不大，但也不容小看。
not negligible = 不容小看

negotiate
/nɪˈɡəʊʃieɪt/
核心：三支。一是谈判、协商：不及物，介词 with 接对方、for 或 over 接要谈的事；二是通过谈判达成，及物：negotiate a deal；
三是顺利通过（难走的路段、障碍）。
形容词 negotiable 一是可以商量的，二是（票据）可转让的，三是（道路）走得通的；
名词 negotiation 常用复数：be in negotiations、under negotiation；谈判的人叫 negotiator。并入本条。
① The union is negotiating with the management for a shorter working week.
= 工会正与资方谈判，争取缩短每周工时。
negotiate with somebody for something = 为某事与某人谈判
② The two sides have agreed to negotiate over the future of the port.
= 双方同意就港口的前途举行谈判。
negotiate over something = 就某事谈判
③ She negotiated a pay rise of eight per cent for the whole department.
= 她为整个部门谈下了百分之八的加薪。
negotiate a pay rise = 谈成加薪
negotiate a deal = 谈成一笔交易
④ The lorry could not negotiate the sharp bend and had to reverse.
= 那辆卡车拐不过那个急弯，只好倒车。
negotiate a bend = 拐过弯道
这一支指小心地通过难走的地方。
⑤ The price is negotiable if you are willing to pay in cash.
= 你要是愿意付现金，价钱可以商量。
be negotiable = 可以商量
⑥ The track is only negotiable by tractor after heavy rain.
= 大雨过后，那条土路只有拖拉机走得通。
negotiable by something = 只有某种车走得通
⑦ The contract is still under negotiation and may not be signed this month.
= 合同还在谈，这个月不一定签得下来。
under negotiation = 在谈判中
⑧ Negotiations between the airline and its pilots broke down on Friday.
= 航空公司与飞行员之间的谈判星期五破裂了。
negotiations break down = 谈判破裂
be in negotiations with somebody = 正与某人谈判
⑨ She is a tough negotiator who has never lost her temper at the table.
= 她是个强硬的谈判者，在谈判桌上从没发过脾气。
a tough negotiator = 强硬的谈判者

negritude

① The poem celebrates negritude at a time when African culture was dismissed in the schools.

② He wrote his thesis on the poets of the Negritude movement in Paris.
= 他的论文写的是巴黎「黑人性」运动的诗人。
the Negritude movement = 「黑人性」运动

Negro
/ˈniːɡrəʊ/

指女性的 Negress 更旧，冒犯意味更重。Negro spiritual 是黑人灵歌，如今多只说 spiritual。并入本条。

the Negro Leagues = 黑人棒球联盟（历史名称）
Negro = 黑人（旧称，现视为冒犯）
② The painting long catalogued as Portrait of a Negress was given a different title by the museum in 2019.
= 那幅画在目录里长期题作《一位黑人女子的肖像》，二〇一九年博物馆给它换了标题。
Negress = 黑人女性（旧称，冒犯）
③ The choir ended the concert with a Negro spiritual first written down in the 1860s.
= 合唱团以一首十九世纪六十年代最早记谱的黑人灵歌结束了音乐会。
a Negro spiritual = 黑人灵歌

neigh

neighbour
/ˈneɪbə(r)/

二是相邻的人、物、国家：坐在旁边的人、邻国；三是（书面）同胞、身边的人：love your neighbour。
动词是（正式）与…相邻。形容词 neighbouring（邻近的，只作定语）并入本条。美式拼作 neighbor。
① Our next-door neighbours feed the cat whenever we go away.
= 我们一出门，隔壁邻居就帮忙喂猫。
a next-door neighbour = 隔壁邻居
neighbour = 邻居
② She has been a good neighbour to the old couple across the road.
= 对马路对面那对老夫妇，她一直是个好邻居。
a good neighbour = 好邻居
③ He turned to his neighbour at dinner and asked what the speech had been about.
= 吃饭时他转向邻座，问那篇讲话讲的是什么。
your neighbour at dinner = 饭桌上的邻座
④ The country has signed trade agreements with all four of its neighbours.
= 该国已同四个邻国全部签了贸易协定。
a country's neighbours = 邻国
⑤ The priest reminded them of the duty to love your neighbour.
= 神父提醒他们，有爱人如己的本分。
love your neighbour = 爱人如己
⑥ The farm neighbours a military airfield, and the cows no longer look up.

neighbour something = 与某物相邻
⑦ Children come to the school by bus from the neighbouring villages.
= 孩子们从邻近的村子坐公共汽车来上学。
neighbouring villages = 邻近的村子
neighbouring = 邻近的

neighbourhood
/ˈneɪbəhʊd/
核心：三支。一是街区、居民区：城镇里的一片地方，也指住在那儿的人，可数；二是附近、周围一带，单数：
in the neighbourhood；三是 in the neighbourhood of，（数目）大约。
neighbourhood watch 是居民自己组织的邻里守望，防盗用，并入本条。美式拼作 neighborhood。
① They grew up in a poor neighbourhood on the east side of the city.
= 他们在城东一个贫穷的街区长大。
a poor neighbourhood = 贫穷的街区
neighbourhood = 街区
② The whole neighbourhood turned out to look for the missing child.

the whole neighbourhood = 整个街区的人
③ Is there a decent bakery anywhere in the neighbourhood?

in the neighbourhood = 在附近
④ The repairs will cost in the neighbourhood of five thousand pounds.

in the neighbourhood of = 大约
⑤ Burglaries fell by a third after the street set up a neighbourhood watch.

a neighbourhood watch = 邻里守望

neighbourly
/ˈneɪbəli/

① It was neighbourly of her to take in the parcels while we were away.

neither
/ˈnaɪðə(r)/（常见）/ˈniːðə(r)/（也读）
核心：只管两个人或两样东西，三个以上用 none。四种用法。
一是限定词，接单数名词：neither answer is right。二是代词：neither of 加复数名词或代词，正式文体动词用单数，口语里也用复数。
三是 neither … nor …，既不…也不…：动词跟靠近它的那个主语一致。
四是副词，放在句首表示「也不」，后面倒装：Neither do I；口语里说 Me neither。
① Neither answer is correct, so try the sum again.
= 两个答案都不对，再算一遍吧。
neither + 单数名词 = 两个都不
neither = 两者都不
② Neither of my parents drives, so we went everywhere by bus.
= 我父母都不开车，所以我们去哪儿都坐公共汽车。
neither of somebody = 两个人都不
③ "Tea or coffee?" "Neither, thank you. A glass of water will do."
= 「茶还是咖啡？」「都不要，谢谢。来杯水就行。」
Neither, thank you. = 都不要，谢谢。
④ The hotel is neither cheap nor clean, and the lift is broken.
= 那家旅馆既不便宜也不干净，电梯还是坏的。
neither … nor … = 既不…也不…
⑤ Neither the manager nor his assistants were told about the inspection.
= 经理和他的几个助手，事先都没人告诉他们要检查。
neither A nor B = 甲和乙都不
动词跟靠近它的 assistants 一致，所以用复数。
⑥ "I cannot swim." "Neither can I, so let us stay in the boat."
= 「我不会游泳。」「我也不会，那咱们就待在船上吧。」
Neither can I. = 我也不会。
句首表示「也不」，后面主语和助动词要倒装。
⑦ "I do not like the look of that sky." "Me neither."
= 「我看这天色不妙。」「我也觉得。」
Me neither. = 我也不。
口语说法。

nelson

nematode
/ˈnemətəʊd/

① Gardeners water nematodes into the soil to kill slugs without poison.
= 园丁把线虫随水浇进土里杀鼻涕虫，不用毒药。
nematodes = 线虫
② The potato crop was ruined by a nematode that attacks the roots.
= 一种专攻根部的线虫毁了那一季的土豆。
a nematode = 一种线虫

nemesis

nemesis for something = 对某事的报应

neon
/ˈniːɒn/
核心：两支。一是氖：一种惰性气体，通电后发出橙红色的光，化学符号 Ne，不可数；
二是霓虹灯、霓虹灯光，不可数，常作定语：a neon sign、neon lights；也说颜色鲜亮得刺眼的：neon pink。
① Neon glows orange-red when an electric current passes through it.
= 氖通上电流，会发出橙红色的光。
neon = 氖
② A neon sign above the door flashed the name of the bar in blue.
= 门上方的霓虹招牌用蓝光一闪一闪地打出酒吧的名字。
a neon sign = 霓虹招牌
③ The neon lights of the main street were reflected in the wet pavement.
= 主街的霓虹灯光倒映在湿漉漉的人行道上。
neon lights = 霓虹灯
④ The runners wore neon yellow vests so that drivers could see them at dusk.
= 跑步的人穿着荧光黄的背心，好让司机在黄昏时看得见他们。
neon yellow = 荧光黄

neonatal
/ˌniːəʊˈneɪtəl/
核心：新生儿的：指出生后头四个星期的婴儿，医学用语，只作定语。
名词 neonate 是新生儿，术语，日常说 newborn baby 的地方医学上用它。并入本条。
① The twins spent their first three weeks in the neonatal unit.
= 那对双胞胎头三个星期是在新生儿病房度过的。
a neonatal unit = 新生儿病房
② Better neonatal care has halved the death rate among premature babies.
= 新生儿护理的改善，使早产儿的死亡率减了一半。
neonatal care = 新生儿护理
③ The drug is safe for adults but has not been tested on neonates.
= 那种药对成人是安全的，但没有在新生儿身上试验过。
a neonate = 新生儿

neophyte
/ˈniːəfaɪt/
核心：两支，正式，可数。一是新手、初学者，介词用 at、in；二是（宗教）新入教的人、新进修道院的人。
① The course is designed for complete neophytes who have never held a camera.
= 那门课是为从没拿过相机的纯新手开的。
a complete neophyte = 纯新手
② As a political neophyte, she was surprised by how little the debates changed.
= 她在政界是个新手，没想到辩论能改变的东西那么少。
a political neophyte = 政界新手
③ The neophytes rise at four and spend the first hour in silent prayer.
= 新入会的修士四点起床，头一个钟头默祷。
the neophytes = 新入会的修士
