proceed
/prəˈsiːd/
核心：（正式）动词，不及物：继续进行、接着做：proceed with；接着（去做另一件事）：proceed to do；（向某个方向）前进、行进：proceed to、along。
proceed from 是（正式）出自、起因于；proceed against 是（法律）起诉。别跟 precede（在……之前）混。
① The work is proceeding according to plan in spite of the rain.
= 尽管下雨，工程仍在按计划进行。
proceed according to plan = 按计划进行
② The committee decided not to proceed with the sale of the land.
= 委员会决定不再继续卖那块地。
proceed with something = 继续进行某事
③ He sat down at the piano and proceeded to play for an hour.
= 他在钢琴前坐下，接着就弹了一个钟头。
proceed to do something = 接着做某事
④ Passengers for Rome should proceed to gate twelve.
= 去罗马的旅客请前往十二号登机口。
proceed to a place = 前往某处
⑤ Most of the firm's troubles proceed from a single bad decision.
= 那家公司的麻烦大多出自一个错误的决定。
proceed from = 出自、起因于
proceed from something = 出自某事
⑥ The council has decided to proceed against the owner of the building.
= 镇议会决定起诉那座楼的业主。
proceed against somebody = 起诉某人

proceeding
/prəˈsiːdɪŋ/
核心：（正式）名词，多用复数 proceedings：（法律）诉讼、诉讼程序：divorce proceedings；（一次会议、典礼的）进程、活动；（学会、会议的）记录、会刊。
① She has started divorce proceedings against her husband.
= 她已经对丈夫提起离婚诉讼。
divorce proceedings = 离婚诉讼
② The mayor opened the proceedings with a short speech of welcome.
= 市长以一篇简短的欢迎词宣布活动开始。
open the proceedings = 宣布活动开始
③ The paper was printed in the proceedings of the conference.
= 那篇论文印在大会的会刊里。
the proceedings of a conference = 大会的会刊

proceeds
/ˈprəʊsiːdz/
核心：（卖东西、办活动得来的）收入、收益。复数名词：the proceeds of、from。重音在前，跟动词 proceed 不同。
① The proceeds of the concert will go to the children's hospital.
= 那场音乐会的收入将捐给儿童医院。
the proceeds of something = 某事的收入
② She bought a small flat with the proceeds from the sale of the farm.
= 她用卖农场得的钱买了一套小公寓。
the proceeds from the sale = 卖得的钱

process
/ˈprəʊses/
核心：名词，可数：过程、进程：一连串自然发生的变化：the ageing process；（为达到某个结果做的）一系列步骤、程序；（工业上的）制作方法、工序。
动词：加工、处理（原料、食品）；办理、审核（文件、申请）；（计算机）处理（数据）；冲洗（胶卷）。名词 processing，不可数。
习语：in the process 是在这个过程中、同时；be in the process of doing something 是正在做某事。
另有一个不及物动词，读 /prəˈses/：（正式）列队缓缓行进。
① Learning a language is a slow process that cannot be hurried.
= 学一门语言是个急不得的慢过程。
a slow process = 慢过程
② The peace process broke down after the attack on the border.
= 边境遭袭后，和平进程中断了。
the peace process = 和平进程
③ The factory uses a new process for making glass from sand.
= 那家工厂用一种新工艺把沙子制成玻璃。
a process for doing something = 做某事的工艺
④ Most of the fish is processed and tinned on the boat.
= 大部分鱼是在船上加工装罐的。
be processed = 被加工
⑤ It takes three weeks to process an application for a passport.
= 办理一份护照申请要三个星期。
process an application = 办理申请
⑥ The computer processes the figures in a fraction of a second.
= 电脑不到一秒钟就把那些数字处理完了。
process figures = 处理数字
⑦ I spilled the coffee and ruined my shirt in the process.
= 我把咖啡洒了，顺带毁了衬衫。
in the process = 在这个过程中、同时
⑧ We are in the process of moving the office to a new building.
= 我们正在把办公室搬到一座新楼去。
in the process of = 正在
be in the process of doing something = 正在做某事
⑨ The new system has cut the processing time for each form from a week to a day.
= 新系统把每张表格的处理时间从一个星期缩到了一天。
processing = 处理、加工
processing time = 处理时间
⑩ The bishops processed slowly down the aisle of the cathedral.
= 主教们沿着大教堂的过道缓缓列队行进。
process = 列队行进
这一支重音在第二个音节。

procession
/prəˈseʃn/

形容词 processional 是列队行进的、行列用的。并入本条。

③ A procession of visitors came to the hospital during the first week.

④ The choir sang a processional hymn as the bride entered.

processional = 列队行进用的

proclaim
/prəˈkleɪm/

proclamation

proclivity

sexual proclivities = 性癖好

Procrustean

productive
/prəˈdʌktɪv/
核心：三支。一是多产的、产量高的、效率高的；二是富有成效的、有收获的；三是（正式）productive of：产生、引起（某种结果）的。
副词 productively。反义是 unproductive。并入本条。
① The land in the valley is the most productive in the region.
= 山谷里的地是那个地区产量最高的。
productive land = 产量高的地
② We had a very productive meeting and settled all four questions.
= 我们开了一个很有成效的会，把四个问题都解决了。
a productive meeting = 有成效的会
③ The long delay was productive of nothing but bad temper.
= 那次长时间的延误除了让人生气，什么也没带来。
productive of something = 产生某事
④ She learned to use the time on the train productively.
= 她学会了把火车上的时间用得有成效。
productively = 有成效地

productivity
/ˌprɒdʌkˈtɪvəti/
核心：生产率、生产效率：一定的时间、人力里能生产出多少。不可数。
① The new machines have raised productivity by a fifth.
= 新机器把生产率提高了五分之一。
raise productivity = 提高生产率
② Wages can rise only if productivity improves as well.
= 只有生产率也提高，工资才能涨。
productivity improves = 生产率提高

prof
/prɒf/
核心：（口语）教授：professor 的截短。可数。写在姓名前的 Prof. 是 Professor 的缩写。
① The old prof lectured for an hour without looking at his notes.
= 老教授讲了一个钟头课，没看一眼讲稿。
a prof = 教授
② The letter was signed by Prof. Jones of the history department.
= 那封信的落款是历史系的琼斯教授。
Prof. Jones = 琼斯教授
