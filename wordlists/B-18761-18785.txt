regulate
/ˈreɡjuleɪt/
核心：及物。一是（用规则、法律）管理、控制、调节：regulate the banks；二是调节、校准（机器、温度、速度）。
① In many countries the government regulates the price of gas and electricity for homes.
= 在许多国家，政府管控家用煤气和电的价格。
regulate prices = 管控价格
regulate = 管理、控制
② After the crisis a new body was set up to regulate the banks and protect savers.
= 那场危机之后，成立了一个新机构来监管银行、保护储户。
regulate the banks = 监管银行
③ The thermostat on the wall regulates the temperature in every room of the house.
= 墙上的恒温器调节屋里每个房间的温度。
regulate the temperature = 调节温度

① The new regulations ban smoking in all public buildings.
= 新规定禁止在所有公共建筑内吸烟。
regulations = 规章
safety regulations = 安全规定
② Under the regulations, every worker must wear a helmet on the site.
= 按照规定，每个工人在工地上都得戴安全帽。
under the regulations = 按照规定
③ The regulation of the banks is the job of the central bank.
= 监管银行是中央银行的职责。
the regulation of something = 对某事的管理
④ The soldiers wore regulation boots and caps.
= 士兵们穿着标准的靴子，戴着标准的帽子。
