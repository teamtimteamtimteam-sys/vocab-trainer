saleroom
/ˈseɪlruːm/
核心：名词，可数（英式）：拍卖场、拍卖行：进行拍卖的房间或地方。美式多说 salesroom。
① The old clock was sold in a London saleroom for much more than anyone expected.
= 那座老钟在伦敦一家拍卖行卖出的价钱比所有人预想的都高。
a saleroom = 拍卖行
② Dealers filled the saleroom on the morning of the auction, hoping for a bargain.
= 拍卖那天上午，交易商们挤满了拍卖场，希望捡个便宜。
the saleroom = 拍卖场

salesgirl
/ˈseɪlzɡɜːl/
核心：名词，可数（旧式）：女售货员。现在多说 sales assistant 或 salesperson。
① The salesgirl wrapped the scarf in tissue paper and put it in a small blue bag.
= 女售货员用薄纸把围巾包好，放进一个蓝色小袋子里。
a salesgirl = 女售货员
② My grandmother worked as a salesgirl in a big department store when she was sixteen.
= 我祖母十六岁时在一家大百货商店当女售货员。
work as a salesgirl = 当女售货员

salesman
/ˈseɪlzmən/
核心：名词，可数，复数 salesmen：推销员、销售员；（男）售货员。
① The car salesman tried to persuade us to buy a much more expensive model.
= 那个汽车推销员想说服我们买一款贵得多的车型。
a car salesman = 汽车推销员
② My father was a travelling salesman who drove all over the country selling books.
= 我父亲以前是个旅行推销员，开车跑遍全国卖书。
a travelling salesman = 旅行推销员

salesmanship
/ˈseɪlzmənʃɪp/
核心：名词，不可数：推销术、推销技巧。
① It took a lot of salesmanship to persuade the bank to lend us the money.
= 说服银行借钱给我们费了不少口舌。
salesmanship = 推销本事
② Good salesmanship is about listening to customers, not just talking to them.
= 好的推销术在于倾听顾客，而不只是跟他们说话。
good salesmanship = 高明的推销术

salesperson
/ˈseɪlzpɜːsn/
核心：名词，可数，复数 salespeople：销售员、售货员。不分性别的说法，取代 salesman、saleswoman。
① A friendly salesperson helped me choose a new laptop and showed me how to set it up.
= 一位友善的售货员帮我挑了台新笔记本电脑，还教我怎么设置。
a salesperson = 售货员
② The company is looking for experienced salespeople to sell its software to schools.
= 那家公司在招有经验的销售员，向学校推销它的软件。
salespeople = 销售员

salesroom
/ˈseɪlzruːm/
核心：名词，可数：（尤北美）拍卖场；商品陈列室。
① The furniture was sold in a salesroom in New York to a collector from Paris.
= 那件家具在纽约的一家拍卖场卖给了一位巴黎的收藏家。
a salesroom = 拍卖场
② The car company opened a new salesroom where customers can try the cars before they buy them.
= 那家汽车公司新开了一个展厅，顾客买车前可以先试车。
a car salesroom = 汽车展厅

saleswoman
/ˈseɪlzwʊmən/
核心：名词，可数，复数 saleswomen：女推销员、女售货员。
① The saleswoman in the shoe shop measured my feet and brought out six pairs to try.
= 鞋店的女售货员量了我的脚，拿出六双让我试。
a saleswoman = 女售货员
② She became the company's best saleswoman after only two years in the job.
= 她入职才两年就成了公司最出色的女推销员。
the best saleswoman = 最出色的女推销员

salience
/ˈseɪliəns/
核心：名词，不可数（正式）：显著、突出；重要性。由 salient 变来，多见于学术和政策写作。
① The report increased the political salience of climate change, and every party now talks about it.
= 那份报告让气候变化在政治上更受重视，如今每个党都在谈论它。
the salience of an issue = 某问题的重要性
② The study looks at the salience of different colours in advertising and which ones people notice first.
= 那项研究考察广告中不同颜色的醒目程度，以及人们先注意到哪些颜色。
salience = 显著、醒目

salient
/ˈseɪliənt/
核心：形容词（正式）：最重要的、最显著的：the salient points、salient features。名词，可数（军事）：突出部：伸进敌方阵地的一块阵地。
① The teacher summarized the salient points of the chapter on the board before the exam.
= 考试前，老师在黑板上概括了那一章的要点。
the salient points = 要点
正式用语，口语里说 the main points、the key points。
salient = 最显著的
② One salient feature of the new design is the large window that fills the whole south wall.
= 新设计的一个显著特点是占满整面南墙的大窗户。
a salient feature = 显著特点
③ The army held the salient for three months before it was forced back across the river.
= 那支军队守住突出部三个月，才被迫退回河对岸。
a salient = （军事）突出部

saline
/ˈseɪlaɪn/
核心：形容词：含盐的、咸的：a saline solution。名词，不可数：盐水、生理盐水：医院里用来输液、冲洗伤口的。
① The nurse cleaned the cut on my leg with a saline solution before she put on a bandage.
= 护士先用盐水清洗我腿上的伤口，再包上绷带。
a saline solution = 盐水溶液
saline = 含盐的
② The patient was given saline through a drip because he had lost so much water.
= 病人失水太多，医生给他输了生理盐水。
saline = 生理盐水

salinity
/səˈlɪnəti/
核心：名词，不可数：盐度、含盐量：水或土壤里含盐的多少。
① Scientists measured the salinity of the water at different depths in the lake.
= 科学家测量了湖里不同深度的水的盐度。
the salinity of water = 水的盐度
② Rising salinity in the soil has made it impossible to grow rice in some coastal areas.
= 土壤含盐量上升，使一些沿海地区种不了水稻。
salinity = 含盐量

saliva
/səˈlaɪvə/
核心：名词，不可数：唾液、口水。比 spit 正式，多见于医学和科学。
① The smell of fresh bread made saliva fill my mouth even before I saw the bakery.
= 还没看到面包店，新鲜面包的香味就让我满嘴口水。
saliva = 唾液
② The doctor took a sample of saliva to test for the virus.
= 医生取了一份唾液样本检测病毒。
a sample of saliva = 唾液样本

salivary
/səˈlaɪvəri/
核心：形容词：唾液的、分泌唾液的：salivary glands（唾液腺）。
① The doctor felt the swollen salivary glands under the boy's jaw and said he had mumps.
= 医生摸了摸男孩下巴底下肿起的唾液腺，说他得了腮腺炎。
salivary glands = 唾液腺
② Chewing gum increases salivary flow and can help to protect your teeth after a meal.
= 嚼口香糖能增加唾液分泌，饭后有助于保护牙齿。
salivary flow = 唾液分泌

salivate
/ˈsælɪveɪt/
核心：动词，不及物：分泌唾液、流口水；（比喻，常贬义）垂涎、眼馋：salivate over / at something。
① The dog began to salivate as soon as it heard the sound of its food bowl.
= 那条狗一听到食盆的声音就开始流口水。
salivate = 流口水
② The football clubs are salivating over the young player, and he will probably move for a huge fee.
= 各家足球俱乐部都对那名年轻球员垂涎三尺，他多半会以天价转会。
salivate over something = 对某物垂涎
比喻用法常带贬义，指对钱、东西、机会的贪婪渴望。

salivation
/ˌsælɪˈveɪʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：分泌唾液、流涎。
① Some medicines cause too much salivation, so patients have to swallow more often.
= 有些药会导致唾液分泌过多，所以病人得更频繁地吞咽。
salivation = 唾液分泌
② In his famous experiment, the scientist measured the dogs' salivation when they heard a bell.
= 在那个著名实验里，科学家测量狗听到铃声时的唾液分泌量。
measure salivation = 测量唾液分泌

sallow
/ˈsæləʊ/
核心：形容词：（脸色）蜡黄的、病黄的。名词，可数：（植物）黄华柳：一种灌木状的柳树。
① After months in hospital, his face was thin and sallow, and his eyes looked tired.
= 住院几个月后，他脸颊消瘦，脸色蜡黄，眼神疲惫。
a sallow face = 蜡黄的脸
sallow = 蜡黄的
② Sallows grow along the edge of the river, and their soft yellow flowers appear in early spring.
= 河边长着黄华柳，早春时开出柔软的黄花。
a sallow = 黄华柳

sally
/ˈsæli/
核心：名词，可数：（被围困者的）突围出击；（旧式）俏皮话、妙语；短途出行。动词：sally forth / out（幽默或文学）是出发、兴冲冲地出去。
① The soldiers made a sally from the castle at night and captured the enemy's guns.
= 士兵们夜里从城堡里突围出击，缴获了敌人的炮。
make a sally = 突围出击
② My uncle's witty sallies about the neighbours kept everyone at the table laughing all through dinner.
= 我叔叔拿邻居开的那些俏皮话，让饭桌上的人整顿晚饭都笑个不停。
a witty sally = 俏皮话
③ After breakfast we sallied forth to explore the old town with a map and an umbrella.
= 早饭后，我们带着地图和雨伞兴冲冲地出发去逛老城。
sally forth = 出发、兴冲冲地出去
④ The children sallied out into the snow as soon as their mother opened the door.
= 妈妈一开门，孩子们就冲到雪地里去了。
sally out = 冲出去

salmon
/ˈsæmən/
核心：名词，可数或不可数，复数也是 salmon：鲑鱼、三文鱼；（作食物）鲑鱼肉，不可数；鲑肉色、橙粉色：salmon pink。字母 l 不发音。

= 每年秋天，鲑鱼逆流而上游几百英里去产卵。
salmon = 鲑鱼
② We had grilled salmon with new potatoes and green beans for dinner on Friday.
= 星期五晚饭我们吃了烤三文鱼，配新土豆和四季豆。
grilled salmon = 烤三文鱼
③ She painted the walls of the baby's bedroom a soft salmon pink and hung white curtains at the window.
= 她把婴儿房的墙刷成了柔和的橙粉色，窗上挂了白窗帘。
salmon pink = 鲑肉色、橙粉色

salmonella
/ˌsælməˈnelə/
核心：名词，不可数：沙门氏菌；沙门氏菌食物中毒。常见于没做熟的鸡肉、鸡蛋。
① Cook the chicken all the way through, because undercooked meat can carry salmonella.
= 鸡肉要彻底煮熟，因为没做熟的肉可能带沙门氏菌。
salmonella = 沙门氏菌
② Twenty guests at the wedding went down with salmonella after eating the egg salad.
= 婚礼上二十位客人吃了鸡蛋沙拉后得了沙门氏菌食物中毒。
go down with salmonella = 得沙门氏菌食物中毒

salon
/ˈsælɒn/
核心：名词，可数：（美发、美容、时装的）店、沙龙：a hair salon、a beauty salon；（旧时富人家的）客厅、会客厅；（文人雅士的）沙龙聚会。来自法语。
① She works at a hair salon in town and cuts about twenty people's hair every day.
= 她在镇上一家美发店工作，每天给二十个左右的人剪头发。
a hair salon = 美发店
比 hairdresser's 显得讲究一些，店名里很常见。
② My sister booked a day at a beauty salon as a birthday present for our mother.
= 我姐姐在一家美容院给妈妈订了一天的护理，作生日礼物。
a beauty salon = 美容院
③ In eighteenth-century Paris, writers and thinkers met in the salons of rich women to discuss new ideas.
= 在十八世纪的巴黎，作家和思想家在富家太太的沙龙里聚会，讨论新思想。
a salon = （文人）沙龙

saloon

① He sold his sports car and bought a big family saloon with plenty of room for the children.
= 他卖掉跑车，买了一辆宽敞的家用轿车，孩子们坐着很舒服。
a saloon = （英式）轿车
② In the old western, the cowboys rode into town and went straight to the saloon for a drink.
= 那部老西部片里，牛仔们骑马进城，直奔酒馆去喝酒。
a saloon = （美国西部的）酒馆
③ Passengers sat in the ship's saloon, playing cards and drinking tea as the sea grew rough.
= 海浪渐大，乘客们坐在客轮大厅里打牌喝茶。
the ship's saloon = 客轮大厅

salsify

② My grandfather grew salsify in his vegetable garden, and we dug up the long thin roots in winter.

grow salsify = 种婆罗门参
