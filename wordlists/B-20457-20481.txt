self-fulfilling
/ˌself fʊlˈfɪlɪŋ/
核心：形容词：自我应验的：多用于 a self-fulfilling prophecy（自我应验的预言：因为相信而成真）。
① If you keep telling yourself you will fail, it can become a self-fulfilling prophecy.
= 你要是一直对自己说会失败，这可能会变成自我应验的预言。
a self-fulfilling prophecy = 自我应验的预言
self-fulfilling = 自我应验的
② Fear of a crisis can be self-fulfilling, as people take their money out of the banks.
= 对危机的恐惧可能会自我应验，因为人们会把钱从银行取出来。
be self-fulfilling = 自我应验

self-fulfilment
/ˌself fʊlˈfɪlmənt/
核心：名词，不可数：自我实现、实现自我价值。美式拼作 self-fulfillment。
① She found self-fulfilment in teaching rather than in making a lot of money.
= 她在教书中实现了自我价值，而不是在赚大钱中。
find self-fulfilment in something = 在某事中实现自我价值
self-fulfilment = 自我实现
② For him, a job is not just about money; it is also about self-fulfilment.
= 对他来说，工作不只是为了钱，也是为了实现自我。
be about self-fulfilment = 为了实现自我

self-governing
/ˌself ˈɡʌvənɪŋ/
核心：形容词：自治的：a self-governing region。
① The island is a self-governing territory with its own parliament and laws.
= 那座岛是一个自治领地，有自己的议会和法律。
a self-governing territory = 自治领地
self-governing = 自治的
② The school became self-governing and no longer had to follow the local council's rules.
= 那所学校实行了自治，不再需要遵守地方议会的规定。
become self-governing = 实行自治

self-government
/ˌself ˈɡʌvənmənt/
核心：名词，不可数：自治、自治政府。
① The mountain region was finally granted self-government after many years of protests.
= 经过多年的抗议，那个山区终于获准自治。
be granted self-government = 获准自治
self-government = 自治
② Self-government means that local people make their own decisions about schools and roads.
= 自治意味着当地人自己决定学校和道路的事。
self-government means = 自治意味着

self-harm
/ˌself ˈhɑːm/
核心：名词，不可数：自残、自我伤害：尤指故意割伤自己。动词：自残。
① The charity runs a helpline for young people who are struggling with self-harm.
= 那家慈善机构为受自残困扰的年轻人开设了一条求助热线。
self-harm = 自残
② Teachers are trained to notice the signs when a student may be self-harming.
= 老师们受过培训，能察觉学生可能在自残的迹象。
self-harm = 自残（动词）

self-help
/ˌself ˈhelp/
核心：名词，不可数：自助、自救：靠自己（而不靠别人）解决问题。作定语：self-help books、a self-help group。
① The shop has a whole section of self-help books about confidence, money and happiness.
= 那家书店有一整个区专卖关于自信、理财和幸福的自助类书籍。
self-help books = 自助类书籍
self-help = 自助
② She joined a self-help group for people who had lost their jobs, and they met every week.
= 她加入了一个失业者互助小组，他们每周聚一次。
a self-help group = 互助小组

self-identification
/ˌself aɪˌdentɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：自我认同：把自己看作属于某一群体或某种身份。
① Language is an important part of self-identification for many minority groups in the country.
= 对这个国家的许多少数群体来说，语言是自我认同的重要部分。
self-identification = 自我认同
② The survey relies on self-identification, so people choose the group they feel they belong to.
= 那项调查依据自我认同，所以人们自己选择觉得所属的群体。
rely on self-identification = 依据自我认同

self-identify
/ˌself aɪˈdentɪfaɪ/
核心：动词，不及物：自我认定为、认同自己是：self-identify as something。
① About a third of the students self-identify as religious, according to the university survey.
= 据大学的调查，大约三分之一的学生认为自己信教。
self-identify as something = 自我认定为某种身份
self-identify = 自我认定
② In the new census, people can self-identify as belonging to more than one group.
= 在新的人口普查里，人们可以自我认定属于不止一个群体。
self-identify as belonging to = 自认属于

form your self-identity = 形成自我身份

a self-indulgent film = 自我陶醉的电影
