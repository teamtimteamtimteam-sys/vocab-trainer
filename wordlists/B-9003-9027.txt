fleshly
核心：（书面或旧）肉体的、肉欲的：跟精神的相对，多见于宗教与文学。
① Fleshly appetites are what that sermon is about and what fills the church.
= 那篇布道讲的是肉体的欲望，也正是它把教堂坐满的缘由。
fleshly appetites = 肉体的欲望
② The rule forbids fleshly comforts and says nothing at all about coffee.
= 那条戒律禁绝肉体享受，对咖啡却只字未提。
fleshly = 肉体的

fleshpots
核心：（书面或谑）声色场所、安逸享乐之地：作复数，常带调侃。
① The fleshpots of that city are two streets and a car park.
= 那座城所谓的销金窟，不过两条街加一个停车场。
the fleshpots = 声色场所
② He left the fleshpots for a cottage and was back within a year.
= 他抛下那些享乐去处搬进小屋，不到一年就回来了。
leave the fleshpots = 离开安逸享乐之地

fleshy
核心：两支。一是肉多的、丰腴的；二是（植物）肉质的：fleshy leaves。
① Fleshy leaves store water and rot the moment they are overwatered.
= 肉质叶子储水，浇多了立刻就烂。
fleshy leaves = 肉质的叶子
② A fleshy face in that portrait belongs to the man who paid for it.
= 那幅肖像里丰腴的脸，属于付钱定画的那个人。
fleshy = 肉多的

fleur-de-lis
核心：（纹章）鸢尾花饰：法国王室纹章上那个三瓣图案。可数，复数 fleurs-de-lis。
① A fleur-de-lis on the gate is the only thing left of the old estate.
= 门上一个鸢尾花饰，是那座老庄园留下的唯一痕迹。
a fleur-de-lis = 鸢尾花饰
② The flag carries three fleurs-de-lis and no explanation whatsoever.
= 那面旗上有三个鸢尾花饰，没有任何说明。

flex
核心：三支。一是屈伸、活动（肌肉、关节）：flex your fingers；
二是（英式）电线：a length of flex；三是（非正式）炫耀。
① Flex your fingers before you play and not while you are playing.
= 弹之前活动手指，别弹着的时候活动。
flex your fingers = 活动手指
② The flex on that lamp is older than the lamp and has been mended twice.
= 那盏灯的电线比灯还老，修过两回。
a length of flex = 一段电线
③ Posting the receipt was a flex and everybody read it that way.
= 把收据发出来是在炫耀，人人都这么看。
a flex = 炫耀之举

flexibility
核心：两支。一是（身体的）柔韧；二是（安排、制度上的）灵活余地。不可数。
① Flexibility goes first and comes back slowest of everything you lose.
= 柔韧性最先丢，也是丢掉的东西里回来最慢的。
flexibility = 柔韧性
② The contract offers flexibility on dates and none at all on money.
= 那份合同在日期上留了余地，在钱上一点没有。
flexibility on something = 在某事上的灵活余地

flexible
核心：三支。一是易弯曲的：a flexible pipe；二是（人、安排）灵活的：flexible hours；
三是（态度）不死板的。副词 flexibly。
① A flexible pipe goes round the corner that defeated the copper one.
= 铜管拐不过去的那个弯，软管拐得过去。
a flexible pipe = 软管
② Flexible hours suit everybody except the person doing the rota.
= 弹性工时人人满意，除了排班的那一个。
flexible hours = 弹性工时
flexible working hours = 弹性工作时间
Flexible working hours mean the first person in opens up every day.
= 所谓弹性工作时间，就是最早到的那个人天天开门。
副词 flexibly：灵活地，多修饰 applied、interpreted、worked。
③ The rule is applied flexibly and nobody will say where the line is.
= 那条规矩执行得很灵活，界线在哪儿谁也不肯说。
be applied flexibly = 灵活执行
④ They work flexibly and meet in person once a quarter.
= 他们弹性办公，一个季度当面见一次。
work flexibly = 弹性工作
flexible friend = （戏称）信用卡
He reached for the flexible friend and the machine declined it twice.
= 他掏出信用卡，机器拒了两回。

flexion
核心：（解剖）屈曲：关节向内弯的动作，跟伸展 extension 相对。不可数。
另有拼法 flection。
① Flexion at the elbow was full and extension was ten degrees short.
= 肘关节屈曲到位，伸展差十度。
flexion = 屈曲
② The exercise works on flexion and the pain is somewhere else entirely.
= 那个动作练的是屈曲，疼的地方完全在别处。
flexion of a joint = 关节的屈曲

flexitarian
核心：弹性素食者：以素为主、偶尔吃肉的人；也作形容词。可数。
① A flexitarian eats meat twice a month and explains it four times a week.
= 弹性素食者一个月吃两回肉，一周要解释四回。
a flexitarian = 弹性素食者
② The menu is flexitarian and the kitchen is mostly vegetarian anyway.
= 那份菜单是弹性素食的，厨房本来也基本是素的。
a flexitarian diet = 弹性素食饮食

flexitime
核心：（英式）弹性工作时间：核心时段必到，两头自己安排。美式说 flextime。不可数。
① Flexitime means the office is full at eleven and empty by four.
= 弹性工时的结果是：十一点办公室满员，四点就空了。
flexitime = 弹性工作时间
② She works flexitime and banks the hours for the school holidays.
= 她上弹性工时，把工时攒着留给学校放假时用。
work flexitime = 实行弹性工时

flexor
核心：（解剖）屈肌：让关节弯曲的那类肌肉，跟伸肌 extensor 相对。可数。
① The flexors in the forearm do the gripping and get the cramp.
= 前臂的屈肌负责抓握，抽筋的也是它们。
a flexor = 屈肌
② Strengthening the flexors without the extensors makes things worse.
= 只练屈肌不练伸肌，反而更糟。
flexor muscles = 屈肌群

flibbertigibbet
核心：（旧、谑）没定性的人：说话轻浮、心思跳来跳去的人。可数。
① The flibbertigibbet in that novel turns out to be the only honest character.
= 那本小说里那个没定性的角色，最后是唯一诚实的人。
a flibbertigibbet = 没定性的人
② Her aunt called her a flibbertigibbet and left her the house.
= 她姨妈说她是个没定性的丫头，却把房子留给了她。
call somebody a flibbertigibbet = 说某人没定性

flic
核心：（俚语，源自法语）警察、条子：多见于翻译小说与字幕。可数。
① Two flics on the corner watched the market and bought nothing.
= 街角两名警察盯着集市，什么也没买。
a flic = 警察（俚）
② A flic in that story is never the person who solves it.
= 那个故事里的条子，从来不是破案的那个。
a flic = 条子

flick
核心：三支。一是轻弹、轻甩：flick a switch；二是快速翻动：flick through something；
三是（非正式）电影。
① Flick the switch twice and the light decides for itself.
= 把开关拨两下，灯自己决定亮不亮。
flick a switch = 拨开关
② She flicked through the report and found the one number that mattered.
= 她把那份报告飞快翻了一遍，找出了唯一要紧的数字。
flick through something = 快速翻阅某物
③ They watched a flick that nobody has mentioned since.
= 他们看了部片子，此后谁也没再提过。
a flick = 电影（非正式）
flick knife = 弹簧刀
A flick knife in a coat pocket is an offence on its own.
= 大衣口袋里揣一把弹簧刀，这本身就是犯法。

flicker
核心：两支。一是（光）闪烁不定：the candle flickered；
二是引申，（情绪、希望）一闪：a flicker of hope。名词与动词同形。
① The candle flickered every time the door opened and never went out.
= 每次开门那支蜡烛都晃一下，却始终没灭。
flicker = 闪烁不定
② A flicker of hope arrived with the second letter and left with the third.
= 一丝希望随第二封信来，随第三封信走。
a flicker of hope = 一丝希望

flier
核心：三支。一是宣传单页，也拼 flyer；二是飞行者、常坐飞机的人；
三是习语 take a flier，冒险一试。可数。
① A flier through every door on that street brought in two customers.
= 那条街家家门缝塞了一张传单，招来两个顾客。
a flier = 宣传单页
② A frequent flier gets a lounge and a longer queue at security.
= 常旅客能进休息室，安检的队却更长。
a frequent flier = 常旅客

flight
核心：五支。一是航班：a flight to Rome；二是飞行；三是一段楼梯：a flight of stairs；
四是（正式）逃离：flight from something；五是习语 take flight，起飞或逃走。
① The flight to Rome leaves at six and boards at half past four.
= 飞罗马那班六点起飞，四点半登机。
a flight = 航班
② Two flights of stairs is the reason she chose the ground floor.
= 两段楼梯，就是她选一楼的原因。
a flight of stairs = 一段楼梯
③ Birds take flight before anybody hears what frightened them.
= 鸟先飞了起来，人才听见是什么惊了它们。
take flight = 起飞、逃走
④ The flight from the countryside filled two cities and emptied nine villages.
= 那场逃离乡村的迁徙填满两座城，掏空九个村子。
flight from something = 从某处的逃离
flight crew = 机组人员
The flight crew had been awake longer than the passengers had.
= 机组人员醒着的时间，比乘客还长。
flight deck = 驾驶舱；航母飞行甲板
Two of them sat on the flight deck and neither touched the controls.
= 他们俩坐在驾驶舱里，谁也没碰操纵装置。
flight jacket = 飞行夹克
A flight jacket from that decade sells for more than the aircraft parts.
= 那个年代的飞行夹克，卖得比飞机零件还贵。
flight lieutenant = （英空军）中尉
A flight lieutenant signed the log and the squadron never saw him again.
= 一位中尉在日志上签了字，中队再没见过他。
flight officer = 飞行军官
The flight officer files the paperwork that keeps everybody else flying.
= 飞行军官办的那些文书，让别人飞得起来。
flight path = 航线、飞行路径
A new flight path put nine thousand people under the approach.
= 一条新航线，把九千人划到了进近航道底下。
flight recorder = 黑匣子
The flight recorder was found in four metres of water on Thursday.
= 黑匣子是周四在四米深的水里找到的。
flight sergeant = （英空军）军士长
A flight sergeant ran that hangar and two officers pretended otherwise.
= 那个机库是一位军士长在管，有两名军官装作不是这样。
flight simulator = 飞行模拟器
Four hours in a flight simulator costs less than nine minutes of fuel.
= 在飞行模拟器上待四个钟头，比烧九分钟的油还便宜。
flight suit = 飞行服
A flight suit has fourteen pockets and nowhere to put a sandwich.
= 飞行服有十四个口袋，却没一个放得下三明治。

flightless
核心：不会飞的：几乎只修饰 birds。
① Flightless birds do well on islands and badly the moment a cat arrives.
= 不会飞的鸟在岛上过得很好，猫一到就糟。
flightless birds = 不会飞的鸟
② The chick is flightless for eleven weeks and eats the whole time.
= 那只雏鸟十一个星期不会飞，一直在吃。
be flightless = 不会飞

flighty
核心：（贬）见异思迁的、心思飘忽的：说人没定性。
① He calls her flighty and she has had the same job for eleven years.
= 他说她心思飘忽，她那份工作干了十一年。
flighty = 见异思迁的
② A flighty reputation is a good deal easier to earn than to lose.
= 「没定性」的名声，得来容易得多，甩掉难。
a flighty reputation = 没定性的名声

flimflam
核心：（非正式）胡扯、蒙人的花言巧语；也指小骗局。不可数为主。
① The pitch was flimflam and the contract underneath it was worse.
= 那番推销纯属胡扯，底下那份合同更糟。
flimflam = 胡扯、蒙人的话
② A bit of flimflam sells the first box and never the second.
= 花言巧语能卖出头一箱，第二箱就不行了。
a piece of flimflam = 一套蒙人的话

flimsiness
核心：两支。一是（材料）单薄不结实；二是（论证、借口）站不住脚。不可数。
① The flimsiness of the box explains why the mugs arrived in pieces.
= 那个箱子太单薄，杯子碎着到货也就不奇怪了。
the flimsiness of something = 某物的单薄
② The flimsiness of the evidence did not stop the story running for a week.
= 证据站不住脚，那条报道照样跑了一个星期。
flimsiness = 站不住脚

flimsy
核心：三支。一是（材料）单薄易破的：a flimsy box；二是（借口、证据）站不住脚的；
三是（衣服）轻薄的。副词 flimsily。
① A flimsy box and a heavy book make a bad parcel.
= 单薄的箱子配一本厚书，就是个糟糕的包裹。
a flimsy box = 单薄的箱子
② A flimsy excuse repeated confidently gets accepted more often than it should.
= 一个站不住的借口说得笃定，被接受的次数比该有的多。
a flimsy excuse = 站不住脚的借口
副词 flimsily：单薄地、草率地，多修饰 built、constructed、dressed。
③ The shed is flimsily built and has stood for thirty years.
= 那间棚子搭得单薄，却立了三十年。
be flimsily built = 搭得单薄
④ She was flimsily dressed for a hill that still had snow on it.
= 山上还有雪，她穿得那么单薄。
be flimsily dressed = 穿得单薄

flinch
核心：两支。一是（因痛或惊）瑟缩、退一下：flinch at something；
二是引申，回避：not flinch from something。
① He flinched at the needle and told the nurse it was the light.
= 针一扎他就瑟缩了一下，跟护士说是灯晃的。
flinch at something = 见某物而瑟缩
② The report does not flinch from naming the department involved.
= 那份报告没有回避，把涉事的部门点了名。
not flinch from something = 不回避某事
flinch from doing = 畏缩不敢做某事
She never flinched from saying what the accounts actually showed.
= 账目上到底是什么，她从不畏缩着不说。

fling
核心：三支。一是猛扔、甩：fling something；二是名词，一段短暂的恋情：have a fling；
三是名词，尽兴玩一场。过去式与过去分词都是 flung。
① He flung the keys at the sofa and they landed behind it.
= 他把钥匙朝沙发一扔，落到了沙发后头。
fling something = 猛扔某物
② They had a fling one summer and send cards at Christmas.
= 他们有过一个夏天的短暂恋情，如今圣诞互寄贺卡。
have a fling = 有过一段短暂恋情
fling off = 甩掉、脱掉
He flung off a wet coat and knocked the lamp over doing it.
= 他一把甩掉湿外套，顺手把灯碰倒了。
fling out = 扔出去、撵出去
They flung out three boxes of paper and regretted one of them.
= 他们扔出去三箱纸，其中一箱后来后悔了。
fling yourself at = 扑向、倒贴
She flung herself at a job that had already been promised elsewhere.
= 她一头扑向一份早已许给别人的工作。
fling yourself into = 一头扎进
He flung himself into the rebuilding and slept in the church hall.
= 他一头扎进重建，就睡在教堂礼堂里。

flint
核心：两支。一是燧石：又硬又脆的石头，敲击生火，也是旧石器时代的工具材料；
二是打火机里的火石。可数不可数都行。形容词 flinty。
① A flint in that field came out of the plough and went into a museum.
= 那块地里翻出一块燧石，从犁下直接进了博物馆。
a flint = 燧石
② The flint in the lighter lasts a good deal longer than the gas does.
= 打火机里的火石，比气耐用得多。
a flint = 打火石
