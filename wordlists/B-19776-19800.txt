salutary
/ˈsæljətri/
核心：形容词（正式）：（不愉快的经历）有益的、给人教训的：a salutary lesson、a salutary reminder。
① Failing his first exam was a salutary lesson, and he worked much harder after that.
= 第一次考试不及格给了他一个有益的教训，此后他用功多了。
a salutary lesson = 有益的教训
说的总是不愉快、却让人长记性的经历。
② The accident was a salutary reminder of how dangerous the mountains can be in winter.
= 那次事故警示人们，冬天的山区有多危险。
a salutary reminder = 警示

salutation
/ˌsæljuˈteɪʃn/
核心：名词（正式）：问候、致意（可数或不可数）；（信开头的）称呼语：如 Dear Sir。
① He raised his hat in salutation as he passed the old lady in the street.
= 他在街上经过那位老太太时，举帽向她致意。
in salutation = 以示问候
② The teacher reminded us to begin a formal letter with a proper salutation such as "Dear Madam".
= 老师提醒我们，正式信件要用恰当的称呼开头，比如「尊敬的女士」。
a salutation = （信的）称呼语

salutatorian
/səˌluːtəˈtɔːriən/
核心：名词，可数（美国）：（毕业典礼上）致开幕辞的学生：通常是成绩第二名的毕业生；第一名叫 valedictorian。
① As salutatorian, she gave a short speech to welcome the families at the start of the graduation.
= 作为毕业典礼的开幕致辞人，她在典礼开始时发表了简短讲话欢迎家长们。
the salutatorian = 致开幕辞的毕业生
② He was salutatorian of his high school class and went on to study medicine at a top university.
= 他是高中毕业班的第二名，后来去一所顶尖大学读医学。
be salutatorian = 是毕业班第二名

salute
/səˈluːt/
核心：动词：敬礼、行礼：salute an officer；（正式）向……致敬、赞扬：salute somebody's courage。名词：敬礼（可数）：give a salute；礼炮（可数）：a 21-gun salute；致敬（可数或不可数）：in salute。
① The young soldier stood very straight and saluted as the general walked past the line of tents.
= 将军走过那排帐篷时，年轻的士兵笔直地立正敬礼。
salute = 敬礼
军人、警察的举手礼；比喻「致敬」时多见于讲话和新闻。
salute somebody = 向某人敬礼
② We salute the courage of the firefighters who saved so many lives last night.
= 我们向昨晚救了那么多人的消防员的勇气致敬。
salute somebody's courage = 向某人的勇气致敬
③ The captain gave a smart salute and then turned to face his men.
= 上尉干脆利落地敬了个礼，然后转身面对他的士兵。
give a salute = 敬礼
④ The guns by the river fired a 21-gun salute to welcome the visiting president to the capital.
= 河边的礼炮鸣放二十一响，欢迎来访的总统抵达首都。
a 21-gun salute = 二十一响礼炮
⑤ The crowd raised their glasses in salute to the oldest man in the village on his hundredth birthday.
= 人群举杯向村里最年长的人致敬，祝贺他百岁生日。
in salute = 以示致敬

salvage
/ˈsælvɪdʒ/
核心：动词：（从事故、火灾、沉船中）抢救、打捞（财物）：salvage something from；挽回、补救（局面、名声）：salvage a situation。名词，不可数：抢救、打捞；抢救出的物品。
① Divers were able to salvage some of the gold coins from the ship that sank two hundred years ago.
= 潜水员从两百年前沉没的那艘船里打捞出了一些金币。
salvage something from something = 从某处抢救出某物
salvage = 抢救、打捞
宾语是从灾难中救出来的财物；救人一般不用 salvage，用 rescue。
② The team scored twice in the last ten minutes and managed to salvage a draw.
= 球队在最后十分钟进了两球，勉强挽回一场平局。
salvage a draw = 挽回一场平局
③ After the scandal, the minister tried to salvage his reputation by apologizing on national television.

salvage your reputation = 挽回名声
④ A salvage team worked for weeks to raise the wreck from the bottom of the harbour.
= 一支打捞队干了几个星期，把沉船从港底捞了上来。
a salvage team = 打捞队
⑤ We found an old iron gate at a salvage yard and used it at the entrance to our garden.
= 我们在一家旧货回收场找到一扇旧铁门，装在了花园入口。
a salvage yard = 旧货回收场

same
/seɪm/
核心：形容词、代词，前面几乎总带 the：同一个的、相同的、一样的：the same as、the same … that；（代词）同样的事物：do the same。副词：the same 是同样地：think the same。习语很多：all the same 是尽管如此；at the same time 是同时，也用来引出相反的一面；by the same token 是同理；same here 是我也是；one and the same 是同一个；in the same boat 是处境相同。名词 sameness 另立条。
① My sister and I go to the same school, but we are in different classes.
= 我和姐姐在同一所学校上学，但在不同的班。
the same = 同一个的
same 几乎总带 the；「和……一样」说 the same as，不说 same with。
② Your phone is the same as mine, except that yours is red and mine is black.
= 你的手机和我的一样，只不过你的是红色、我的是黑色。
the same as = 和…一样
③ He ordered fish and chips, and I decided to have the same.
= 他点了炸鱼薯条，我决定也要一样的。
have the same = 要一样的
这里 same 是代词，the 不能省。
④ I know it was not your fault, but thank you all the same for saying sorry.
= 我知道那不是你的错，不过还是谢谢你道歉。
all the same = 尽管如此
常放句末，口气缓和，意思接近 nevertheless；也说 just the same。
婉拒时常说 thanks all the same（还是谢谢你）。
⑤ She was angry with her brother, but at the same time she understood why he had lied.
= 她生她哥哥的气，可同时也明白他为什么撒谎。
at the same time = 同时；不过
⑥ He is a hard worker, and by the same token he expects everyone else to work hard too.
= 他工作卖力，同理也要求别人都卖力。
by the same token = 同理
⑦ "I am so tired I could sleep for a week." "Same here."
= 「我累得能睡上一个星期。」「我也是。」
same here = 我也是
⑧ The writer and the famous detective turned out to be one and the same person.
= 原来那位作家和那个有名的侦探是同一个人。
one and the same = 同一个
⑨ Don't worry about losing your job; half the people in this town are in the same boat.
= 别为丢了工作发愁，这个镇上一半的人处境都一样。
in the same boat = 处境相同
⑩ The country made same-sex marriage legal in 2015, and many couples married on the first day.
= 那个国家二〇一五年使同性婚姻合法化，许多伴侣在第一天就结了婚。
same-sex = 同性的
