② The wind freshened after four and the smaller boats came in.
= 四点后风变大了，小船都回来了。
the wind freshens = 风变强
③ She went to freshen up and came back wearing a different coat.
= 她去梳洗一下，回来换了件外套。
freshen up = 梳洗一下
freshen yourself up = 梳洗一下
She freshened herself up in a station toilet and went straight in.
= 她在车站厕所里梳洗了一下，直接就进去了。
