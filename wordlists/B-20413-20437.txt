self-centred
/ˌself ˈsentəd/
核心：形容词（贬义）：以自我为中心的、自私的。美式拼作 self-centered。
① He is so self-centred that he only ever talks about himself and his problems.
= 他太以自我为中心了，只会谈他自己和他的问题。
self-centred = 以自我为中心的
② Small children are naturally self-centred, but they learn to think of others as they grow.
= 小孩子天生以自我为中心，长大了才学会替别人着想。
naturally self-centred = 天生以自我为中心
