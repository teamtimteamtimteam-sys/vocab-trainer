rumple
/ˈrʌmpl/
核心：动词：弄皱、弄乱（衣服、头发、床单）：rumple somebody's hair。名词 rumple 不常用；形容词多用过去分词 rumpled：皱巴巴的、乱糟糟的。
① He rumpled his son's hair and told him that he had played well, even though the team had lost.
= 他揉乱儿子的头发，跟他说虽然球队输了，他踢得很好。
rumple somebody's hair = 揉乱某人的头发
② She arrived at the meeting in a rumpled suit after sleeping on the night train.
= 她坐了一夜的卧铺火车，穿着皱巴巴的西装来开会。
a rumpled suit = 皱巴巴的西装

rumpus
/ˈrʌmpəs/
核心：（口语）喧闹、吵闹；激烈的争吵：cause a rumpus、kick up a rumpus。可数，多用单数。
① The children were making such a rumpus upstairs that I could not hear the telephone.
= 孩子们在楼上闹得那么凶，我连电话都听不见。
make a rumpus = 吵闹
② The decision to cut the school's music lessons caused a rumpus among the parents.
= 削减学校音乐课的决定在家长中引起了一场轩然大波。
cause a rumpus = 引起轩然大波
口语色彩重，比 row、argument 轻松，常带点戏谑。

rumpy pumpy
/ˌrʌmpi ˈpʌmpi/
核心：（英式口语，幽默）性爱、云雨之事。不可数。多见于小报和喜剧的调侃口吻。
① The tabloids were full of stories about rumpy pumpy between the footballer and a television presenter.
= 小报上满是那名足球运动员和一位电视主持人风流韵事的报道。
rumpy pumpy = 风流韵事
② The comedy is a silly tale of rumpy pumpy in a country hotel, with lovers hiding in every cupboard.
= 那部喜剧讲的是乡间旅馆里一段荒唐的风流事，每个衣柜里都藏着情人。
a tale of rumpy pumpy = 风流故事

run
/rʌn/
核心：本义是「快速地向前移动」，过去式 ran、过去分词 run。由此长出四大支：
一、人和动物的移动：跑、奔跑：run fast、run for the bus；跑（比赛）：run a marathon；（口语）开车送：run somebody home。
二、东西的运转与延续：（机器）运转：an engine runs；（车辆）按班次运行：buses run every ten minutes；（道路、河流）延伸：the road runs beside the river；（戏剧、合同）持续、上演：run for three years；运行（程序）、进行（检查）：run tests；（报纸）刊登：run a story；竞选：run for president。
三、液体的流动：（液体）流：water runs；放（水）：run a bath；流鼻涕：your nose is running；（颜色）掉色、洇开：colours run。
四、经营与掌控：经营、管理：run a business、run the show。
名词：跑步：go for a run；一连串：a run of bad luck；（银行）挤兑：a bank run；（板球、棒球）一分；连续上演的场次：a successful run；（丝袜）脱丝；（家禽的）圈栏：a chicken run；滑雪道：a ski run。
习语：in the long run 是从长远看；on the run 是在逃；make a run for it 是拔腿就跑；something runs in the family 是家族遗传；run wild / run riot 是撒野、闹事；run short 是快用完；run dry 是干涸；run its course 是自然发展到结束；run late 是晚点；run a temperature 是发烧；in / out of the running 是有 / 没有获胜希望。
① As soon as the bell rang for the end of the school day, the children ran as fast as they could towards the gate.
= 放学铃一响，孩子们就拼命朝校门跑去。
run = 跑
② My sister ran her first marathon last spring and finished in just under four hours.
= 我姐姐去年春天跑了她的第一个马拉松，用时不到四个小时。
run a marathon = 跑马拉松
及物用法，宾语是赛程或距离：run a race、run ten kilometres。
③ I had to run for the bus this morning because my alarm clock did not go off.
= 我今天早上闹钟没响，只好跑着去赶公交车。
run for the bus = 跑着赶公交车
④ Her parents run a small hotel by the sea and work seven days a week in the summer.
= 她父母在海边经营一家小旅馆，夏天一周干七天。
run = 经营、管理
run a hotel = 经营旅馆
「经营」这一支是把「跑」引申为「让事情转起来」，宾语是店、公司、学校、项目。
⑤ The manager thinks he is in charge, but everyone knows that his assistant really runs the show.
= 经理以为是他说了算，可谁都知道真正主事的是他的助理。
run the show = 主事、说了算
⑥ The engine was still running when the police arrived and found the car empty by the side of the road.
= 警察赶到时，那辆车停在路边、里面没人，发动机还在转。
an engine runs = 发动机运转
机器义是「转动不停」，也说 leave the engine running。
⑦ The buses run every ten minutes during the day but only once an hour after midnight.
= 白天公交车每十分钟一班，半夜以后一小时才一班。
buses run = 公交车运行
说班次、线路时用一般现在时，强调按时刻表固定运行。
⑧ The old railway line runs beside the river for about forty miles before it turns towards the hills.
= 那条老铁路沿着河延伸大约四十英里，然后转向山里。
a road runs beside something = 道路沿某处延伸
⑨ The musical ran for three years in London before it moved to New York.
= 那部音乐剧在伦敦连演三年，之后才移师纽约。
a play runs = 连续上演
「延续」这一支也用于合同、展览、签证：run until、run for。
⑩ The doctors ran some tests on his heart but could not find anything wrong with it.
= 医生给他的心脏做了些检查，却没查出什么毛病。
run tests = 做检查
⑪ Several newspapers ran the story on their front pages the next morning, with photos of the fire.
= 第二天早上好几家报纸都在头版刊登了这条新闻，还配了火灾的照片。
run a story = 刊登报道
⑫ She has decided to run for president at the next election, even though she has little money.
= 她决定在下次选举中竞选总统，尽管她没什么钱。
run for president = 竞选总统
英式也常说 stand for，美式多用 run for。
⑬ Water ran from the broken pipe all night and flooded the kitchen floor before anyone woke up.
= 破水管里的水流了一整夜，大家还没醒，厨房地板就淹了。
run = （液体）流
water runs = 水流
「流」这一支的主语是液体，也可以是流着液体的东西：the tap is running。
⑭ Shall I run you a hot bath while you finish your dinner and read your letters?
= 你吃完饭、看信的时候，我给你放一缸热洗澡水好吗？
run a bath = 放洗澡水
⑮ My nose has been running all day, and I think I am getting a cold.
= 我一整天都在流鼻涕，我想是要感冒了。
your nose runs = 流鼻涕
⑯ I washed my new red shirt with the white sheets, and the colour ran and turned everything pink.
= 我把新红衬衫和白床单一起洗了，结果掉色，全都染成了粉红色。
colours run = 掉色
洗衣标签上常见 colours may run，提醒深浅分开洗。
⑰ Can I run you home after the party, or is your brother coming to collect you?
= 聚会结束后我开车送你回家吧，还是你哥哥会来接你？
run somebody home = 开车送某人回家
⑱ Red hair runs in the family; my mother, my aunt and my grandfather all had it.
= 红头发是家族遗传：我母亲、姑姑和祖父都是红头发。
something runs in the family = 家族遗传
⑲ The children were allowed to run wild in the woods behind the house all summer long.
= 整个夏天，孩子们都可以在屋后的树林里撒野。
run wild = 撒野
也说植物疯长：the garden had run wild。
⑳ Angry fans ran riot in the city centre after the match, breaking shop windows and setting fire to cars.
= 赛后愤怒的球迷在市中心闹事，砸商店橱窗、烧汽车。
run riot = 闹事、撒野
㉑ We ran short of milk on the camping trip and had to drink our tea black for two days.
= 露营途中我们的牛奶快用完了，只好喝了两天不加奶的茶。
run short of something = 某物快用完
指「还有一点但不够了」，主语也可以是东西：time is running short。
㉒ The village well ran dry during the long hot summer, so people had to carry water from the river.
= 漫长炎热的夏天里村里的井干了，人们只好从河里挑水。
run dry = 干涸
㉓ The doctor said there was no medicine for the virus and that it would simply have to run its course.
= 医生说这种病毒没药可治，只能等它自己好。
run its course = 自然发展到结束
㉔ The trains are running late because of the snow, so we will probably miss the start of the film.
= 火车因为下雪晚点了，我们多半赶不上电影开头了。
run late = 晚点
㉕ The baby is running a temperature, so I am taking her to see the doctor this afternoon.
= 宝宝在发烧，所以我今天下午带她去看医生。
run a temperature = 发烧
㉖ Whatever the weather is like, I go for a run before work every morning, usually twice around the park.
= 不管天气怎样，我每天早上上班前都去跑步，通常绕公园跑两圈。
go for a run = 去跑步
㉗ When the guard looked away for a moment, the prisoner made a run for it across the field.
= 看守一转眼的工夫，那个犯人就拔腿穿过田野逃跑了。
make a run for it = 拔腿就跑
㉘ The two men who robbed the bank were on the run for three weeks before the police caught them.
= 抢银行的那两个人在逃三个星期后才被警察抓住。
on the run = 在逃
口语里也指忙得团团转：I have been on the run all day。
㉙ Buying good shoes is cheaper in the long run, because cheap ones fall apart after a few months.
= 从长远看买好鞋更省钱，因为便宜的鞋几个月就坏了。
in the long run = 从长远看
反过来说眼前、短期是 in the short run。
㉚ The team has had a run of bad luck this season, with three players injured in the same week.
= 这个赛季球队霉运连连，同一个星期就有三名球员受伤。
a run of bad luck = 一连串霉运
㉛ News that the bank was in trouble started a bank run, as thousands of people tried to take out their savings.
= 那家银行有麻烦的消息传出后引发了挤兑，成千上万的人想把存款取出来。
a bank run = 银行挤兑
名词 run 的「蜂拥」义，也指人们争相抛售某种货币。
㉜ He scored a hundred runs in the cricket match and was named player of the day.
= 他在那场板球赛里得了一百分，被评为当天的最佳球员。
score runs = （板球、棒球）得分
㉝ Three candidates are still in the running for the job of head teacher at the village school.
= 村小学校长的职位还有三名候选人有希望。
in the running = 有希望获胜
㉞ After losing to Spain last night, our team is now out of the running for the cup.
= 昨晚输给西班牙之后，我们队已经没有夺杯的希望了。
out of the running = 没有希望获胜
㉟ The show enjoyed a successful run of two years in London and then went on a tour of the whole country.
= 那部剧在伦敦成功连演两年，之后又去全国巡演。
a successful run = 连演成功
㊱ Just before she was due to go on stage, she noticed that her new tights had a long run.
= 她就要上台时，发现新丝袜脱了一长道丝。
tights have a run = 丝袜脱丝
㊲ We keep six hens in a chicken run behind the shed and collect the eggs every morning.
= 我们在棚屋后面的鸡圈里养了六只母鸡，每天早上去捡蛋。
a chicken run = 鸡圈
㊳ Because of the high winds, all the ski runs were closed on the north side of the mountain.
= 因为风大，山北坡的滑雪道全都关了。
a ski run = 滑雪道
㊴ I ran across an old friend from school while I was waiting for a train in Manchester.
= 我在曼彻斯特等火车时偶然碰到了一个老同学。
run across = 偶然碰到、偶然发现
宾语可以是人，也可以是东西：run across an old letter。
㊵ Run along now, children, and let the grown-ups talk in peace for a while.
= 孩子们，现在去玩吧，让大人们安安静静地说一会儿话。
run along = （旧式口语）走开、去玩吧
多是大人打发孩子的话，对成年人说显得居高临下。
㊶ Her parents were worried because she had started to run around with a group of older boys.
= 她父母很担心，因为她开始跟一伙年纪大些的男孩混在一起。
run around with = 和……鬼混
口语，带不赞成的口气，常指和坏朋友混在一起。
㊷ The dog barked and ran at the postman, but it stopped as soon as he held out his hand.
= 那条狗冲着邮递员叫着扑过去，可他一伸手它就停住了。
run at = 向……冲过去
也指（比率）达到：inflation is running at five per cent。
㊸ Can I run a few ideas by you before I write the final report for the manager?
= 给经理写最终报告之前，我能先跟你说说我的几个想法吗？
run by = 把……说给某人听（征求意见）
结构是 run something by somebody，和 run something past somebody 同义。
㊹ Can I run these ideas past you before the meeting, just to hear what you honestly think?
= 开会前我能不能把这些想法先说给你听听，听听你的真实意见？
run past = 先给某人过目、征求意见
结构是 run something past somebody，口语常用。
㊺ The old man was run down by a car as he crossed the road outside the post office.
= 那位老人在邮局外面过马路时被一辆车撞倒了。
run down = 撞倒；（电池）耗尽；贬低
也说 the battery has run down、stop running down your own work。
㊻ The police ran him in for being drunk and shouting at people in the street late at night.
= 他深夜喝醉了在街上冲人嚷嚷，警察把他抓了进去。
run in = （口语）拘捕；磨合（新车）
英式也说 run in a new car，指新车头几百英里慢开磨合。
㊼ Guess who I ran into at the supermarket this morning? My old maths teacher from primary school!
= 你猜我今天早上在超市碰见谁了？我小学的数学老师！
run into = 偶然遇见；撞上；遇到（困难）
也说 run into trouble / problems，以及 the car ran into a tree。
㊽ The secretary ran off fifty copies of the timetable and put one in every teacher's pigeonhole.
= 秘书印了五十份时间表，在每位老师的信件格里各放了一份。
run off = 复印、印出；跑掉
也指逃跑：the thief ran off down the street。
㊾ The club treasurer ran off with all the money that the members had raised for the new roof.
= 俱乐部的财务卷走了会员们为修新屋顶筹的全部钱款。
run off with = 卷走（钱财）；和……私奔
也说 he ran off with his best friend's wife。
㊿ The meeting ran on for three hours, and by the end half the people in the room were asleep.
= 会一直开了三个小时，到最后屋里一半的人都睡着了。
run on = 持续得比预期久；（机器）以……为动力
也说 the bus runs on electricity，指车辆用什么作动力。
❶ The teacher marked several run-on sentences in my essay and told me to use more full stops.
= 老师在我的作文里标出了好几处串句，叫我多用句号。
a run-on sentence = 串句、流水句（该断不断的句子）
语法用语，指两个独立分句之间没有恰当的标点或连词。
❷ We ran out of petrol on the motorway and had to walk two miles to the nearest garage.
= 我们在高速公路上汽油用光了，只好走两英里去最近的加油站。
run out = 用完、耗尽；（期限）到期
也说 time is running out、my passport runs out next month。
❸ He ran out on his wife and three children and was never seen in the town again.
= 他抛下妻子和三个孩子跑了，从此再没在镇上露过面。
run out on = 抛弃（某人）
口语，指在别人需要时扔下他们不管。
❹ Be careful when you reverse; last week my neighbour ran over our cat in the drive.
= 倒车的时候小心点；上星期我邻居在车道上轧了我们家的猫。
run over = （车）轧过；超时；溢出
也说 the meeting ran over by twenty minutes、the bath ran over。
❺ Let's run through the plan once more before the guests arrive, so that nobody forgets anything.
= 客人到之前我们再把计划过一遍，免得谁忘了什么。
run through = 把……过一遍、排练；（想法）掠过
也说 a thought ran through my mind、run through a song。
❻ The cost of repairing the old church roof could run to more than a million pounds.
= 修那座老教堂屋顶的费用可能高达一百多万英镑。
run to = （数量）达到；（钱）够支付
也说 our budget won't run to a new car，指钱不够买。
❼ She ran up a huge bill on her credit card during her three weeks in New York.
= 她在纽约那三个星期，信用卡上欠下了一大笔账。
run up = 欠下（账、债）；赶制（衣服）
也说 run up a flag，指把旗子升起来。
❽ The newspaper decided to run with the story, even though some of the facts had not been checked.
= 尽管有些事实还没核实，那家报纸还是决定刊发这条新闻。
run with = 采纳、着手推进（想法、计划）
也说 let's run with your idea，指就照你的主意办。

runabout
/ˈrʌnəbaʊt/
核心：（口语）小型汽车、小汽艇：适合短途来回跑的轻便车船。可数。
① We bought a little second-hand runabout for driving to the shops and taking the children to school.
= 我们买了一辆二手小车，用来去商店、接送孩子上学。
a runabout = 代步小车
② The family keeps a small runabout at the harbour for fishing trips around the bay.
= 那家人在港口放了一条小汽艇，用来在海湾里出去钓鱼。
a runabout = 小汽艇

runaround
/ˈrʌnəraʊnd/
核心：名词，单数（口语）：推诿、搪塞、兜圈子：几乎只用于 give somebody the runaround 和 get the runaround。
① I phoned the insurance company six times, but they kept giving me the runaround and never paid.
= 我给保险公司打了六次电话，可他们一直推诿搪塞，始终没赔钱。
give somebody the runaround = 对某人推诿搪塞
带口语色彩，多用来抱怨公司、机关。
② If you get the runaround from the shop, write to the head office and ask for your money back.
= 如果商店跟你兜圈子，就写信给总公司要求退款。
get the runaround = 被人推诿

runaway
/ˈrʌnəweɪ/
核心：名词，可数：离家出走的人（尤指孩子）、逃跑者。形容词，只作定语：失控的：a runaway horse、a runaway train；（成功、胜利）轻而易举的、一边倒的：a runaway success、a runaway victory；（物价）飞涨的：runaway inflation。
① The charity helps young runaways who are sleeping in doorways and train stations in the capital.
= 那家慈善机构帮助在首都的门洞和火车站里过夜的离家出走的少年。
a runaway = 离家出走的孩子
② A runaway lorry crashed into three parked cars at the bottom of the hill.
= 一辆失控的卡车在山脚下撞上了三辆停着的车。
a runaway lorry = 失控的卡车
③ Her first novel was a runaway success and was translated into twenty languages within two years.
= 她的第一部小说一炮而红，两年内被译成了二十种语言。
a runaway success = 一举成功
作定语时 runaway 都有「停不下来、势头失控」的意思，好坏两面都能用。
④ Runaway inflation meant that prices in the shops doubled almost every month.
= 失控的通货膨胀使商店里的物价几乎每个月翻一番。
runaway inflation = 失控的通货膨胀

rundown
/ˈrʌndaʊn/
核心：名词，单数：概要、简要汇报：give somebody a rundown of / on something；（英式）（企业、行业的）缩减、裁撤：the rundown of an industry。
① Before the meeting, my assistant gave me a quick rundown of what had happened while I was away.
= 开会前，助理把我不在时发生的事给我简要汇报了一下。
give somebody a rundown of something = 向某人简要汇报某事
② The government was blamed for the rundown of the coal industry in the north during the 1980s.
= 政府被指责要为二十世纪八十年代北方煤炭业的萎缩负责。
the rundown of an industry = 某行业的缩减

rune
/ruːn/
核心：名词，可数：如尼字母：古代北欧日耳曼人刻在石头、木头上的字母；（有魔力的）符文。read the runes（英式）是从迹象推测（局势）。
① Archaeologists found a large stone covered in runes on a hillside in Sweden.
= 考古学家在瑞典的一处山坡上发现了一块刻满如尼字母的大石头。
runes = 如尼字母
② The minister read the runes and resigned before the party could force him out.
= 那位部长看出了苗头，在党把他赶下台之前就辞职了。
read the runes = 从迹象推测局势

rung
/rʌŋ/
核心：名词，可数：（梯子的）横档；（比喻）（组织、社会中的）等级、阶层：the bottom rung of the ladder。（rung 也是 ring 的过去分词，见 ring 条。）
① One of the rungs of the old ladder broke, and he fell into the bushes below.
= 旧梯子的一根横档断了，他摔进了下面的灌木丛里。
a rung of a ladder = 梯子的横档
② Everyone has to start on the bottom rung of the ladder in this company, even the boss's son.
= 在这家公司，人人都得从最底层干起，老板的儿子也不例外。
the bottom rung of the ladder = 最底层

runic
/ˈruːnɪk/
核心：形容词：如尼字母的、用如尼字母写的：runic inscriptions；神秘的、像符文的。
① The museum has a collection of runic inscriptions carved into stones more than a thousand years ago.
= 那座博物馆收藏了一批一千多年前刻在石头上的如尼文铭文。
runic inscriptions = 如尼文铭文
② The old sword has strange runic symbols along its blade that nobody has been able to read.
= 那把古剑的剑身上刻着奇怪的符文，至今没人能读懂。
runic symbols = 符文

runnel
/ˈrʌnl/
核心：名词，可数（文学）：小溪、细流；（路边的）小沟。
① A tiny runnel of clear water trickled over the rocks and into the deep pool below the old mill.
= 一道细细的清水淌过岩石，流进老磨坊下面的深潭。
a runnel of water = 一道细流
② After the storm, runnels of muddy water flowed down both sides of the village street.
= 暴风雨过后，村里街道两边淌着一道道泥水。
runnels = 细流

runner
/ˈrʌnə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：跑步的人、赛跑者：a long-distance runner；（赛马）参赛的马；（送信、跑腿的）信差；走私者：a drug runner；（雪橇、抽屉下的）滑条、滑轨；（植物的）匍匐茎；长条桌布、窄长地毯：a table runner。（英式俚语）do a runner 是溜号、不付钱就跑。
① She is a good long-distance runner and hopes to compete in the Olympics one day.
= 她是个优秀的长跑运动员，希望有一天能参加奥运会。
a long-distance runner = 长跑运动员
② There were twelve runners in the big race, and my uncle's horse came in third.
= 那场大赛有十二匹马参赛，我叔叔的马跑了第三。
runners = 参赛的马
③ The police arrested two drug runners at the airport with bags full of cocaine.
= 警察在机场逮捕了两名运毒者，他们的包里装满了可卡因。
a drug runner = 运毒者
④ The children pulled the sledge up the hill, and its metal runners left two straight lines in the snow.
= 孩子们把雪橇拉上山，金属滑条在雪地上留下两道直线。
the runners of a sledge = 雪橇的滑条
⑤ The strawberry plants send out long runners, and each one grows into a new plant.
= 草莓苗会长出长长的匍匐茎，每一根都能长成一棵新苗。
runners = 匍匐茎
⑥ She put a long linen runner down the middle of the dining table for the dinner party.
= 为了晚宴，她在餐桌中间铺了一条长长的亚麻桌旗。
a table runner = 桌旗
⑦ Two young men ate a huge meal and then did a runner without paying the bill.
= 两个年轻人吃了一大顿，然后没付账就溜了。
do a runner = 溜号、不付钱就跑
⑧ She won the gold medal, and her younger sister was the runner-up just one second behind her.
= 她拿了金牌，她妹妹以仅一秒之差屈居第二。
a runner-up = 亚军、第二名
复数是 runners-up；也泛指没得第一的获奖者：the runners-up each received a book。

running
/ˈrʌnɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：跑步、赛跑：go running；管理、经营：the running of something。形容词：流动的：running water；连续的：three days running；持续不断的：a running battle、a running joke；边做边说的：a running commentary。
① My father took up running when he was fifty and now goes out every morning before breakfast.
= 我父亲五十岁开始跑步，现在每天早饭前都出去跑。
go running = 去跑步
running = 跑步
② She is responsible for the day-to-day running of the hotel, while her husband looks after the money.
= 她负责旅馆的日常经营，她丈夫管钱。
the running of something = 某事的经营管理
③ The old cottage has no running water, so we carry buckets from the stream behind it.
= 那座老农舍没有自来水，我们从屋后的小溪里提水。
running water = 自来水
字面是「流动的水」，指通到屋里的自来水，也指河溪的活水。
④ It has rained for five days running, and the river is close to bursting its banks.
= 已经连续下了五天雨，河水快要漫过堤岸了。
five days running = 连续五天
⑤ The neighbours have been fighting a running battle over the noise from the farm for years.
= 为农场的噪音，邻居们已经争了好几年。
a running battle = 持续不断的争斗
⑥ My uncle's terrible cooking has become a running joke in our family.
= 我叔叔糟糕的厨艺成了我们家的一个老笑话。
a running joke = 老笑话
⑦ My grandfather gave us a running commentary on the football match from his armchair.
= 我祖父坐在扶手椅上，一边看球一边给我们讲个不停。
a running commentary = 实况解说
⑧ The garage carried out some running repairs on the van so that we could finish the journey home.
= 汽修厂给面包车做了些临时修理，好让我们把回家的路走完。
running repairs = （小修小补的）临时修理
英式用语，指为了能继续用而做的小修，不是大修。
⑨ The argument over the garden fence had become a running sore between the two families.
= 花园篱笆的争执已经成了两家之间一块久治不愈的心病。
a running sore = 流脓的疮；（比喻）久拖不决的麻烦
比喻义多用于长期得不到解决的纠纷、问题。
⑩ Children who learn to read at home before school get a running start over the rest of the class.
= 上学前就在家学会认字的孩子，比班上其他人起步更有优势。
a running start = 助跑起步；（比喻）先发优势
美式用语，英式也说 a head start。
⑪ The film has a running time of just over two hours, so we should be home by eleven.
= 那部电影片长两个多小时，所以我们十一点前应该能到家。
running time = （电影、节目的）片长、时长
也指车辆、机器的运行时间。
⑫ Keep a running total of what you spend on the holiday so that you do not go over your budget.
= 把度假的花销随时累计记下来，免得超出预算。
a running total = 累计总数
随着每一笔新增而不断更新的总数。

runny
/ˈrʌni/

① I like my eggs with runny yellow centres so that I can dip my toast into them.
= 我喜欢蛋黄是流心的，好拿面包蘸着吃。

runny = 稀的

runt
/rʌnt/
核心：名词，可数：（一窝动物中）最小最弱的那只：the runt of the litter；（贬义，冒犯）矮小瘦弱的人、小不点。
① We chose the runt of the litter, a tiny puppy that the other dogs kept pushing away from the food.

rupture
/ˈrʌptʃə(r)/
核心：名词：（管道、器官、容器的）破裂（可数或不可数）：the rupture of a pipe；（关系的）决裂（可数）；（旧，口语）疝气。动词：（使）破裂、断裂；（使）决裂。比 break 正式，多见于医学和新闻。
① The doctors operated at once because they were afraid of a rupture of the main artery.
= 医生们马上动了手术，因为担心主动脉破裂。
the rupture of something = 某物的破裂
② The heavy rain caused a gas pipe to rupture under the main street of the town.
= 大雨导致镇上主街下的一根燃气管道破裂。
a pipe ruptures = 管道破裂
比 burst 正式，多见于新闻、工程和医学报告。
③ The argument over the will led to a rupture between the two sisters that lasted twenty years.
= 为遗嘱发生的争执导致姐妹俩决裂，二十年不相往来。
a rupture between people = 人与人的决裂
④ The footballer ruptured a tendon in his ankle and will miss the rest of the season.
= 那名足球运动员脚踝的一根肌腱断裂，将缺席本赛季余下的比赛。
rupture a tendon = 肌腱断裂

rural

rural = 农村的

= 政府承诺为乡村发展投入更多资金，包括修新路、建新学校。
rural development = 乡村发展

= 在伦敦住了三十年后，我父母搬进一座小屋，过上了安静的乡村生活。
rural life = 乡村生活

ruse
/ruːz/

① The phone call about a prize was just a ruse to get people to give their bank details.
= 那通中奖电话只是个骗局，为的是套取人们的银行信息。
a ruse to do something = 为某目的的诡计
比 trick 正式，常见于新闻和侦探故事。
② The prisoners used a clever ruse to escape, pretending to be ill so the guards would open the door.

a clever ruse = 巧妙的花招

rush
/rʌʃ/
核心：动词：冲、奔、赶：rush to the hospital；匆忙做、仓促行事：rush into something；催促：rush somebody；赶紧送：rush somebody to hospital；（体育）冲锋、冲抢。
名词：匆忙、急忙（单数）：in a rush、be in no rush；（人或物的）涌动、蜂拥：a rush for tickets；（感情的）涌起：a rush of excitement；繁忙时段：rush hour、the Christmas rush；灯心草（可数）。形容词 rushed 是仓促的（另立条）。
① When she heard the news, she rushed to the hospital to be with her son.
= 一听到消息，她就赶到医院陪儿子。
rush = 冲、赶
② I can't stop to talk now because I'm in a rush to catch the last train home.
= 我现在不能停下来聊，我正急着赶最后一班火车回家。
in a rush = 匆忙
也说 be in a rush to do something；否定说 be in no rush。
③ Please don't rush me; I want to read the contract carefully before I sign anything.
= 请别催我，我要仔细读完合同再签字。
rush = 催促
rush somebody = 催促某人
④ They met in March and got married in May, but nobody thought they were rushing into it.
= 他们三月认识、五月结婚，可谁也不觉得他们太仓促。
rush into = 仓促做某事
多用于结婚、买房、签约这类大事，带「没想清楚」的责备意味。
⑤ The injured climber was rushed to hospital by helicopter after falling on the mountain.
= 那名在山上摔伤的登山者被直升机紧急送往医院。
rush somebody to hospital = 把某人紧急送往医院
⑥ The traffic in the city centre is terrible during the morning rush hour, so I go to work by bike.
= 早高峰时段市中心交通糟透了，所以我骑车上班。
rush hour = 交通高峰
早高峰、晚高峰分别说 the morning / evening rush hour。
⑦ There was a rush for tickets as soon as the band announced its last concert.
= 那支乐队一宣布最后一场演唱会，人们就蜂拥抢票。
a rush for something = 对某物的抢购
⑧ She felt a sudden rush of excitement as the plane took off for her first trip abroad.
= 飞机起飞、她第一次出国时，突然感到一阵兴奋。
a rush of excitement = 一阵兴奋
⑨ There is no rush; you can give the book back to me whenever you have finished it.
= 不着急，你看完了随时还我就行。
there is no rush = 不着急
⑩ The shop takes on extra staff every year to deal with the Christmas rush.
= 那家店每年都加雇人手，应付圣诞节前的购物高峰。
the Christmas rush = 圣诞购物高峰
⑪ The old chairs had seats made of rushes that had been cut from the edge of the river.
= 那些旧椅子的座面是用河边割来的灯心草编的。
rushes = 灯心草
⑫ The company rushed out a new version of the phone after customers complained about the battery.
= 顾客抱怨电池问题后，那家公司匆忙推出了新版手机。
rush out = 匆忙推出（产品、书等）
也指人匆忙跑出去：she rushed out of the room。
⑬ The government rushed the new law through parliament in just two days before the summer holiday.
= 政府赶在暑假前，只用两天就让新法律在议会匆匆通过了。
rush through = 使（法案等）匆忙通过；匆匆做完
也说 rush through your homework，指草草赶完作业。

rushed
