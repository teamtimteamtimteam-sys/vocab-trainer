sainted
/ˈseɪntɪd/
核心：形容词：被封为圣徒的；（幽默、旧式）（已故的人）神圣的、可敬的：my sainted mother。
① The church holds the bones of a sainted bishop who died more than a thousand years ago.
= 那座教堂供奉着一位一千多年前去世、被封为圣徒的主教的遗骨。
a sainted bishop = 被封圣的主教
② "My sainted mother would never have let me leave the house dressed like that," he joked.
= 「要是我那位可敬的老妈在，绝不会让我穿成这样出门。」他开玩笑说。
my sainted mother = 我那位可敬的母亲

sainthood
/ˈseɪnthʊd/
核心：名词，不可数：圣徒身份、圣徒地位。
① The church is considering the nun for sainthood because of her years of work with the poor.
= 那位修女多年为穷人工作，教会正考虑封她为圣徒。
consider somebody for sainthood = 考虑封某人为圣徒
② Being married to him for forty years should be enough to qualify her for sainthood, her friends joked.
= 朋友们开玩笑说，跟他过了四十年，她都够格封圣了。
qualify for sainthood = 够格封圣

saintliness
/ˈseɪntlinəs/
核心：名词，不可数：圣洁、德行高尚。由 saintly 变 y 为 i 加 -ness 构成。
① People spoke of the old priest's saintliness and the help he had given to every family in the town.
= 人们说起那位老神父的圣洁，说他帮过镇上的每一户人家。
saintliness = 圣洁
② Her saintliness made the rest of us feel rather selfish and lazy.
= 她的高尚德行让我们其他人都觉得自己有点自私懒惰。
somebody's saintliness = 某人的高尚德行

saintly
/ˈseɪntli/
核心：形容词：圣徒般的、圣洁的、德行高尚的：a saintly old man。比较级 saintlier。
① My grandmother was a saintly woman who never said an unkind word about anyone.
= 我祖母是个圣洁的女人，从没说过谁一句不好。
a saintly woman = 圣洁的女人
② He looked very saintly singing in the choir, but at school he was always in trouble.
= 他在唱诗班里唱歌时一副乖巧圣洁的样子，在学校里却总惹麻烦。
look saintly = 看上去很乖巧圣洁

sake
/seɪk/
核心：名词：（for the sake of）为了……的缘故、为了……的利益：for the sake of somebody / something、for your own sake；为……而……：talk for the sake of talking。（读 /ˈsɑːki/）日本清酒。
习语：for goodness' sake / for heaven's sake 是看在老天的分上（表示不耐烦）；for old times' sake 是看在老交情的分上、为了怀旧。
① They stayed together for the sake of the children, even though they were not happy.
= 他们为了孩子没有分开，尽管两人过得并不快乐。
for the sake of somebody = 为了某人
② You really should stop smoking for your own sake, if not for the sake of your family.
= 就算不为家人，为了你自己你也真该把烟戒了。
for your own sake = 为了你自己
③ He seems to argue just for the sake of arguing, even when he agrees with you.
= 他好像是为了争而争，哪怕他其实同意你的看法。
for the sake of doing something = 为……而……
④ For goodness' sake, turn that music down — I am trying to sleep!
= 看在老天的分上，把音乐关小点——我在睡觉呢！
for goodness' sake = 看在老天的分上
⑤ We went back to the little café where we first met, just for old times' sake.
= 为了怀旧，我们回到了初次见面的那家小咖啡馆。
for old times' sake = 为了怀旧
⑥ We drank warm sake and ate sushi at a small Japanese restaurant near the station.
= 我们在车站附近一家日本小餐馆喝温清酒、吃寿司。
sake = 日本清酒
这一义读 /ˈsɑːki/，和前面几义是不同来源的两个词。

salaam

① The old man bowed and made a deep salaam as the prince walked past.
= 王子走过时，老人躬身深深行了个额手礼。
make a salaam = 行额手礼
② He greeted his guests with a salaam, touching his forehead with his right hand.
= 他用右手触额，向客人行额手礼致意。
greet somebody with a salaam = 向某人行额手礼

salacious
/səˈleɪʃəs/
核心：形容词（贬义）：（故事、图片、言谈）诲淫的、下流的、过分渲染性的：salacious details、salacious gossip。多用来批评小报和八卦。
① The newspaper printed every salacious detail of the actor's private life, and his family were very upset.
= 那家报纸把那位演员私生活的每个下流细节都登了出来，他的家人非常难过。
salacious details = 下流的细节
② The book was full of salacious gossip about the royal court, and it sold thousands of copies.
= 那本书满是关于宫廷的桃色八卦，卖了好几千册。
salacious gossip = 桃色八卦

salaciousness
/səˈleɪʃəsnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：下流、诲淫。由 salacious 加 -ness 构成。
① Critics complained about the salaciousness of the television show and said it was not suitable for families.
= 评论家抱怨那档电视节目内容下流，说不适合全家观看。
the salaciousness of something = 某物的下流
② The salaciousness of the stories in the magazine shocked many of its older readers.
= 杂志上那些故事的低俗让许多年长读者大为震惊。
salaciousness = 低俗、下流

salad
/ˈsæləd/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：沙拉、色拉：生菜等生吃的蔬菜，或拌好的冷盘：a green salad、potato salad、fruit salad。习语 your salad days 是（旧式）少不更事的青葱岁月。

salad = 沙拉

泛指沙拉时不可数；说一份、一盘时可数，如本句 a salad。
② For dessert my mother served a big bowl of fruit salad with fresh cream.
= 我妈妈端上一大碗水果沙拉配鲜奶油当甜点。
fruit salad = 水果沙拉
③ Mix the olive oil, lemon juice and a little salt to make a simple salad dressing.
= 把橄榄油、柠檬汁和少许盐拌在一起，就是一份简单的沙拉酱。
salad dressing = 沙拉酱
④ My grandfather still puts salad cream on everything, even though the rest of us prefer mayonnaise.
= 我祖父还是什么都抹沙拉奶油，虽然我们其他人都更爱蛋黄酱。
salad cream = （英式）沙拉奶油
⑤ He often talks about his salad days in London, when he was young and had no money.
= 他常聊起在伦敦的青葱岁月，那时他年轻，一贫如洗。
your salad days = 少不更事的青葱岁月
⑥ The government was accused of salami slicing, cutting the health budget a little each year so that nobody noticed.
= 政府被指玩切香肠战术，每年把医疗预算削一点，让谁都察觉不到。
salami slicing = 切香肠式的逐步削减

salaried
/ˈsælərid/

salary
/ˈsæləri/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：薪水、薪金：按月或按年发的固定收入，多指白领、专业人员：earn a salary、a salary of、a salary increase。和 wage 的分别：wage 按小时、按日或按周算，多指体力劳动；income 是所有收入的总称。
① Her salary is paid into her bank account on the last day of every month.
= 她的薪水每月最后一天打进她的银行账户。
salary = 薪水
salary = 月薪年薪（按月发）
近义对照：
wage = 工资（按时按日发）
income = 收入（总称）
② He earns a good salary as an engineer, but he works very long hours.
= 他当工程师，薪水不错，可工作时间很长。
earn a good salary = 薪水不错
③ The job offers a starting salary of thirty thousand pounds a year.
= 那份工作的起薪是一年三万英镑。
a starting salary = 起薪
说薪水多少用 a salary of；年薪说 an annual salary。
④ The teachers are asking for a salary increase because prices have gone up so much.
= 物价涨了那么多，教师们要求加薪。
a salary increase = 加薪

sale
/seɪl/
核心：名词：出售、卖（不可数或可数）：the sale of、for sale、on sale；（可数）一笔交易；（复数 sales）销售额、销量：sales rose；（复数 sales）销售部门、销售工作：work in sales；（可数）减价、促销：a sale、the January sales；拍卖（会）。
for sale 是待售；on sale 英式指正在出售、上市，美式也指打折中。形容词 saleable 是好卖的（另立条）。
① The house next to ours has been for sale for six months, but nobody has bought it yet.
= 我们隔壁的房子挂牌出售六个月了，还没人买。
for sale = 出售中
for sale = 待售
② The new phone goes on sale next Friday, and people are already waiting outside the shop.
= 新手机下周五开售，已经有人在店外排队了。
on sale = 打折中（英式）/ 出售中（美式）
③ I bought these boots on sale for half price in the shop near the station.
= 这双靴子是我在车站附近的店里打折时半价买的。
on sale = 打折中（英式）
④ The sale of the old factory brought in enough money to build a new school.
= 卖掉那座老工厂的钱足够建一所新学校。
the sale of something = 某物的出售
⑤ Sales of electric cars have doubled in the last two years as petrol prices have gone up.
= 随着汽油价格上涨，过去两年电动汽车的销量翻了一番。
sales = 销量、销售额
⑥ She has worked in sales for ten years and travels all over the country to meet customers.
= 她做销售十年了，跑遍全国去见客户。
work in sales = 做销售
这一义总用复数 sales，前面不加冠词，也说 a job in sales、the sales team。
⑦ I bought a new coat in the January sales for less than half the normal price.
= 我在一月份的大减价里买了件新大衣，不到原价的一半。
the sales = 大减价
⑧ The church holds a sale of work every Christmas to raise money for the roof.
= 那座教堂每年圣诞节都办一次手工义卖，为修屋顶筹钱。
a sale of work = （英式）手工义卖
⑨ The painting was sold at a sale of old masters in London for two million pounds.
= 那幅画在伦敦一场古典大师作品拍卖会上以两百万英镑售出。
a sale = 拍卖会

/ˈseɪliənt/

② One salient feature of the new design is the large window that fills the whole south wall.
= 新设计的一个显著特点是占满整面南墙的大窗户。
a salient feature = 显著特点
③ The army held the salient for three months before it was forced back across the river.
= 那支军队守住突出部三个月，才被迫退回河对岸。
a salient = （军事）突出部
