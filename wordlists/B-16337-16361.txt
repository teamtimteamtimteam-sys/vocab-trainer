a slice of parkin = 一片燕麦姜饼

parking
/ˈpɑːkɪŋ/

复合词：parking space 是一个停车位；parking lot 是（美式）停车场，英式说 car park；parking garage 是（美式）多层停车楼；
parking meter 是停车计时收费表；parking ticket 是违章停车罚单；parking brake 是（美式）手刹、驻车制动器，英式说 handbrake。并入本条。
① There is no parking in this street between eight and six.
= 这条街八点到六点之间禁止停车。

② The hotel has free parking for forty cars behind the building.
= 那家旅馆在楼后面有四十个免费车位。

③ I drove round for twenty minutes looking for a parking space.
= 我开着车转了二十分钟找车位。
a parking space = 停车位
④ The parking lot at the mall was almost empty on a weekday morning.
= 工作日的上午，商场的停车场几乎是空的。
a parking lot = 停车场
⑤ He left the car on the top floor of the parking garage.
= 他把车停在了停车楼的顶层。
a parking garage = 停车楼
⑥ She had no coins for the parking meter and had to ask a stranger for change.
= 她没有硬币投停车计时表，只好找陌生人换零钱。
a parking meter = 停车计时收费表
⑦ I got a parking ticket for leaving the car outside the bank for five minutes.
= 我把车在银行外面停了五分钟，就吃了一张罚单。
a parking ticket = 违章停车罚单
⑧ He forgot to put on the parking brake, and the truck rolled down the hill.

Parkinson's disease
/ˈpɑːkɪnsnz dɪziːz/
核心：帕金森病：一种神经系统的慢性病，病人肌肉僵硬、手脚发抖、动作迟缓。不可数；也省作 Parkinson's，得名于最早描述它的英国医生。
① Her father was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease in his early sixties.
= 她父亲六十出头时被诊断出帕金森病。
Parkinson's disease = 帕金森病
② He has had Parkinson's for ten years and can no longer hold a pen steady.
= 他得帕金森病十年了，笔已经拿不稳。
have Parkinson's = 得帕金森病

/ˈpɑːkɪnsnz lɔː/

= 照帕金森定律，一天就能写完的报告，委员会花了一个月。
Parkinson's law = 帕金森定律
② The manager quoted Parkinson's law whenever anyone asked for a later deadline.
= 只要有人要求把期限推后，经理就搬出帕金森定律。
quote Parkinson's law = 搬出帕金森定律

parliament
