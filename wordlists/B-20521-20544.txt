self-selection
/ˌself sɪˈlekʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：自我选择：（研究中）参与者自行决定是否参加，可能造成偏差。
① Self-selection is a problem in the study, because only healthy people chose to take part.
= 自我选择是那项研究的一个问题，因为只有健康的人选择参加。
self-selection = 自我选择
② The researchers tried to avoid self-selection by choosing people at random from the phone book.
= 研究人员从电话簿里随机挑选参与者，设法避免自我选择偏差。
avoid self-selection = 避免自我选择偏差

self-service
/ˌself ˈsɜːvɪs/
核心：形容词：自助式的：a self-service restaurant、self-service checkouts。名词，不可数：自助（服务）。
① We had lunch in a self-service restaurant where you carry your own tray to the table.
= 我们在一家自助餐厅吃了午饭，那里要自己端着托盘到桌边。
a self-service restaurant = 自助餐厅
self-service = 自助式的
② Most petrol stations now are self-service, so you fill the car yourself and pay inside.
= 现在大多数加油站都是自助的，你自己加油，然后进去付钱。
be self-service = 是自助的

self-serving

be self-serving = 只为自己谋利

sell
/sel/
核心：动词（过去式和过去分词 sold）：卖、出售：sell something to somebody、sell somebody something、sell something for £10；（商品）有销路、卖得出：sell well；推销、使接受（想法）：sell an idea to somebody。反义是 buy（买）。
习语：sell like hot cakes 是畅销、抢手；sell yourself short 是低估自己、贬低自己。
短语动词与复合词：sell off 是廉价出售、变卖；sell on 是转卖；sell out 是卖光，也指（为利益）背叛原则；sell up 是（英式）卖掉全部家产；sell-through 是（零售的）售出率；selling point 是卖点；selling price 是售价。人是 seller（另立条）。
① We sold our old car to a young couple who lived in the next street.
= 我们把旧车卖给了住在隔壁街的一对年轻夫妇。
sell = 卖
sell something to somebody = 把某物卖给某人
近义对照：
buy = 买
② The little baker on the corner sells fresh bread every morning from six o'clock.
= 街角那家小面包店每天早上六点开始卖新鲜面包。
sell something = 卖某物
③ Her first novel sold well, and it was translated into twenty languages.
= 她的第一部小说卖得很好，被译成了二十种语言。
sell well = 卖得好
④ He managed to sell the idea of a four-day week to the rest of the board.
= 他成功说服董事会其他成员接受每周工作四天的想法。
sell an idea to somebody = 使某人接受某个想法
⑤ The new phones are selling like hot cakes, and most shops have run out.
= 新手机卖得像热蛋糕一样抢手，大多数商店都卖断货了。
sell like hot cakes = 畅销
⑥ Don't sell yourself short at the interview; tell them about everything you have done.
= 面试时别低估自己，把你做过的事都告诉他们。
sell yourself short = 低估自己
⑦ The company sold off its old factories in the north to pay its debts to the bank.
= 那家公司变卖了北方的旧工厂来偿还银行的债务。
sell off = 变卖
⑧ He bought the paintings cheaply and sold them on to collectors in London.
= 他低价买进那些画，再转卖给伦敦的收藏家。
sell on = 转卖
⑨ The tickets for the band's farewell concert sold out in less than ten minutes.
= 那支乐队告别演唱会的票不到十分钟就卖光了。
sell out = 卖光
⑩ Fans accused the band of selling out when they started making pop songs for adverts.
= 那支乐队开始给广告写流行歌，歌迷指责他们为钱背叛了初心。
sell out = 背叛原则
⑪ When my grandparents retired, they sold up and moved to a small town in Spain.
= 我祖父母退休时卖掉了全部家当，搬到了西班牙的一个小镇。
sell up = 卖掉全部家产
⑫ The shop measures the sell-through of each product to decide what to order next season.
= 那家店统计每种产品的售出率，决定下一季进什么货。
sell-through = 售出率
⑬ The main selling point of the flat is its view of the river.
= 那套公寓的主要卖点是能看到河景。
a selling point = 卖点
⑭ The selling price of the house was much higher than the price they had paid ten years before.
= 那座房子的售价比他们十年前买的时候高得多。
the selling price = 售价
