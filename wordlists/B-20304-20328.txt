secluded
/sɪˈkluːdɪd/
核心：形容词：（地方）僻静的、与世隔绝的：a secluded beach；（生活）隐居的。
① We found a secluded beach where there was nobody else, only birds and the sound of the waves.
= 我们找到一处僻静的海滩，那里一个人也没有，只有鸟和海浪声。
a secluded beach = 僻静的海滩
secluded = 僻静的
② After her husband died, she lived a secluded life in a small cottage in the hills.
= 丈夫去世后，她在山里的一座小屋里过着隐居的生活。
a secluded life = 隐居的生活

seclusion
/sɪˈkluːʒn/
核心：名词，不可数：隔绝、隐居；僻静：in seclusion。
① The old actor lived in seclusion for the last twenty years of his life.
= 那位老演员在人生的最后二十年过着隐居生活。
in seclusion = 隐居
seclusion = 隐居
② The hotel offers peace and seclusion, far away from the noise of the city.
= 那家酒店远离城市喧嚣，提供宁静与清幽。
peace and seclusion = 宁静与清幽

second
/ˈsekənd/（第二、秒）/sɪˈkɒnd/（借调）
核心：一、序数词、形容词、副词：第二（的）；第二位、第二名：come second；次于……的：second only to；副词 secondly 是第二、其次（列举时）。
二、名词，可数：秒；（口语）一会儿、片刻：in a second、a split second；（复数）（口语）第二份饭菜：have seconds；（复数）次品；（决斗、拳击中的）助手。
三、动词：附议、支持（提议）；（英式，读 /sɪˈkɒnd/）借调、暂调（到别的部门）：be seconded to。
习语：second to none 是首屈一指；on second thoughts 是转念一想。
① There are sixty seconds in a minute and sixty minutes in an hour.
= 一分钟有六十秒，一小时有六十分钟。
second = 秒
口语里 a second 常只是「一会儿」，和 a minute 一样不按字面计时。
② She finished second in the school race, just a few metres behind her older sister.
= 她在学校赛跑中得了第二名，只比她姐姐落后几米。
second = 第二
come second = 得第二名
作名次时常不加 the：come second、finish second。
③ I'll be with you in a second; I just need to finish this email.
= 我马上就来，只要把这封邮件写完。
in a second = 马上
④ It all happened in a split second, and nobody saw who threw the stone.
= 一切都发生在一瞬间，谁也没看见是谁扔的石头。
a split second = 一瞬间
⑤ The hotel's restaurant is second to none, and people come from miles away to eat there.
= 那家酒店的餐厅首屈一指，人们从老远的地方赶来吃饭。
second to none = 首屈一指
多作表语，用来称赞服务、质量等。
⑥ I was going to drive, but on second thoughts I will take the train because of the snow.
= 我本来要开车去，可转念一想，因为下雪还是坐火车吧。
on second thoughts = 转念一想
⑦ After years of practice, playing the piano has become second nature to her.
= 多年练习之后，弹钢琴对她来说已经习以为常。
second nature = 第二天性
⑧ Firstly, the house is too small; secondly, it is too far from the station.
= 第一，那房子太小；第二，离车站太远。
secondly = 第二、其次
⑨ My grandmother's dinner was so good that everybody at the table asked for seconds.
= 祖母做的晚饭太好吃了，桌上每个人都要了第二份。
have seconds = 再来一份
⑩ "I propose that we finish the meeting now and go home." "I'll second that, it's late."

⑪ She was seconded to the London office for six months to help with the new project.

be seconded to = 被借调到

= 和父母住在一起不是最好的选择，可我们只负担得起这个。

= 我花二十英镑买了一辆二手自行车，到现在还好好的。

/ˈsekʃənl/
核心：形容词：（社会中）某一部分人的、局部的：sectional interests；组合式的、可拆装的：a sectional sofa；剖面的：a sectional drawing。
① The new law protects sectional interests rather than the good of the whole country.
= 那项新法律保护的是局部利益，而不是全国的利益。
sectional interests = 局部利益
sectional = 局部的
② We bought a big sectional sofa that can be arranged in different ways.
= 我们买了一张大组合沙发，可以用不同方式摆放。
a sectional sofa = 组合沙发

sector
/ˈsektə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（经济的）部门、领域、行业：the public / private sector、the service sector；（城市、地区的）区域、地带；（数学）扇形。形容词 sectoral 另立条。
① She left her job in the private sector to work for a hospital run by the government.
= 她辞去了私营部门的工作，去一家政府办的医院上班。
the private sector = 私营部门
a sector = 部门、行业
② Jobs in the public sector are usually safer but less well paid.
= 公共部门的工作通常更稳定，但工资较低。
the public sector = 公共部门
③ The army divided the city into four sectors, each controlled by a different country.
= 军队把那座城市分成四个区，各由一个国家控制。
a sector of a city = 城市的一个区
④ Cut the circle into eight equal sectors, like slices of a cake.
= 把那个圆分成八个相等的扇形，像切蛋糕一样。
a sector = 扇形

sectoral
/ˈsektərəl/
核心：形容词：（经济）部门的、行业的、分领域的。
① The report gives a sectoral breakdown of jobs, showing how many people work in farming, industry and services.
= 那份报告按行业细分了就业情况，显示多少人从事农业、工业和服务业。
a sectoral breakdown = 按行业细分
sectoral = 部门的、行业的
② The government signed sectoral agreements with each industry about wages and training.
= 政府就工资和培训问题与各个行业签订了分领域的协议。
sectoral agreements = 分领域的协议

secular
/ˈsekjələ(r)/
核心：形容词：世俗的、非宗教的：a secular state、secular music；（神职人员）在俗的。
① France is a secular state, so religion is not taught in its public schools.
= 法国是世俗国家，所以公立学校不教宗教。
a secular state = 世俗国家
secular = 世俗的
② The village choir sings both religious and secular music at its Christmas concerts.
= 村里的合唱团在圣诞音乐会上既唱宗教音乐，也唱世俗音乐。
secular music = 世俗音乐

secularism
/ˈsekjələrɪzəm/
核心：名词，不可数：世俗主义：主张宗教与国家、教育分离的观点。
① The new constitution was based on secularism and equal rights for people of all religions.
= 新宪法以世俗主义和各宗教信徒权利平等为基础。
secularism = 世俗主义
② The debate about secularism in schools has divided the country for years.
= 关于学校世俗主义的争论多年来让那个国家意见分裂。
secularism in schools = 学校里的世俗主义

secularist
/ˈsekjələrɪst/
核心：名词，可数：世俗主义者。形容词：世俗主义的。
① The secularists argued that the state should not pay for religious schools.
= 世俗主义者主张，国家不应该为宗教学校出钱。
secularists = 世俗主义者
② She belongs to a secularist group that campaigns against prayers in parliament.
= 她属于一个世俗主义团体，该团体反对在议会里祈祷。
a secularist group = 世俗主义团体
