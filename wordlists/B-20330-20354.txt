see off = 为（某人）送行（常拆开用：see somebody off）
多在车站、机场，宾语是人，代词要放在 see 和 off 中间。
㉑ My secretary will see you out when you are ready to leave.
= 你准备走的时候，我的秘书会送你出去。
see somebody out = 送某人出门
㉒ An agent will meet you at the house so that you can see over it before you decide.
= 中介会在那座房子跟你碰头，让你先看看房再决定。
see over something = 察看某处
㉓ She can see through his lies at once, so she refuses to lend him any more money.
= 她一眼就能看穿他的谎言，所以不肯再借钱给他。
see through somebody = 看穿某人
㉔ Can you see to the children's dinner while I take the dog for a walk?
= 我去遛狗的时候，你能照料一下孩子们的晚饭吗？
see to something = 照料、处理某事
㉕ The manager will see to it that you are paid by the end of the week.
= 经理会确保你在周末前拿到工钱。
see to it that = 确保
㉖ The children took turns on the see-saw in the park until it got dark.
= 孩子们在公园里轮流玩跷跷板，一直玩到天黑。
a see-saw = 跷跷板
㉗ The old man said he wanted to see out his last years in the village where he was born.
= 那位老人说，他想在出生的村子里度过最后的岁月。
see out = 度过（剩余的时间）

seed
/siːd/
核心：名词：种子、籽（可数或不可数）：plant seeds、sunflower seeds；（比喻）根源、开端：the seeds of something；（体育）种子选手：the top seed。动词：播种；（植物）结籽；（体育）把……列为种子选手：be seeded；去籽。
习语：go to seed / run to seed 是（植物）结籽而不再长叶，比喻（人、地方）走下坡路、衰败。
① Plant the seeds in small pots and keep them in a warm place until they start to grow.
= 把种子种在小花盆里，放在温暖的地方，直到发芽。
seed = 种子
plant seeds = 播种
② Every winter morning the children fed sunflower seeds to the birds in the garden.
= 冬天每天早上，孩子们都在花园里用葵花籽喂鸟。
sunflower seed = 葵花籽
③ The seeds of the war were sown many years before the first shot was fired.
= 那场战争的根源早在第一枪打响的许多年前就埋下了。
the seeds of something = 某事的根源
④ After he lost his job, he let himself go to seed and stopped looking after his house.
= 他失业后就开始颓废，不再打理房子。
go to seed = 走下坡路
⑤ The young company needs seed money from investors to build its first product.
= 那家初创公司需要投资人的启动资金来做出第一款产品。
seed money = 启动资金
⑥ She was the top seed in the tennis tournament but lost in the second round.
= 她是那次网球锦标赛的头号种子，却在第二轮就输了。
the top seed = 头号种子
⑦ My grandmother always made a seed cake with caraway seeds for Sunday tea.
= 我祖母星期天喝下午茶时总要做一个加葛缕子籽的香籽蛋糕。
a seed cake = 香籽蛋糕
⑧ The hard seed coat protects the seed until it is planted in wet soil.
= 坚硬的种皮保护着种子，直到它被种进湿润的土里。
the seed coat = 种皮
⑨ Cutting money for schools is like eating your seed corn; it saves money now but hurts the future.
= 削减学校经费就像把留种的粮食吃掉，眼下省了钱，却损害了将来。
seed corn = 留种的谷物；为将来的投资
⑩ My grandmother's old necklace was made of hundreds of tiny seed pearls.
= 我祖母那条旧项链是用几百颗小粒珍珠串成的。
a seed pearl = 小粒珍珠

seedbed
/ˈsiːdbed/
核心：名词，可数：苗床：为播种准备的细土地块；（比喻）（思想、人才等的）温床、发源地。
① Rake the soil until it is fine and flat to make a good seedbed for the carrots.
= 把土耙细耙平，给胡萝卜做一块好苗床。
a seedbed = 苗床
② The small university became a seedbed for new ideas in science and art.
= 那所小大学成了科学和艺术新思想的温床。
a seedbed for something = 某物的温床
