/ˈsklɪərə/

= 医生注意到病人眼睛的巩膜有点发黄。
the sclera = 巩膜
② The sclera protects the inside of the eye and gives the eyeball its shape.
= 巩膜保护眼球内部，并让眼球保持形状。
the sclera of the eye = 眼睛的巩膜

sclerosis

score
/skɔː(r)/

（比赛的）比分、得分（可数）：the final score、keep score；（考试的）分数（可数）：a test score；（音乐）乐谱、（电影、戏剧的）配乐（可数）：the film score；（旧式）二十：a score of、scores of 是许多；（刻出的）划痕、刻痕（可数）。
动词：得分、进球：score a goal；（考试）得……分；获得（成功）：score a success；（在表面）刻划：score a line；为（乐曲）配器。
习语：settle a score 是算旧账、报复；on that score 是在那一点上；know the score 是心里有数、了解实情；keep score 是记分。
复合词与短语动词：score draw（英式足球）是有进球的平局；score off somebody（英式）是驳倒某人、占上风；score out / score through 是划掉（字）；score sheet 是记分单。
① The final score was three goals to one, and the home fans sang all the way back to the station.
= 最终比分是三比一，主队球迷一路唱着歌走回车站。

② She scored a goal in the last minute and won the cup for her team.
= 她在最后一分钟进了一球，为球队赢得了奖杯。

③ His test score was the highest in the class, so the teacher asked him to help the others.
= 他的考试分数是全班最高的，所以老师请他帮助别的同学。

④ My little brother kept score while we played cards in the garden.

⑤ The film score was written by a young composer who had never worked in cinema before.

⑥ Scores of people came out into the streets to welcome the team home.
= 许多人涌上街头欢迎球队回家。

⑦ He has waited ten years to settle a score with the man who cheated his father.
= 他等了十年，要跟那个欺骗过他父亲的人算账。

⑧ The hotel is close to the beach, so you need not worry on that score.
= 那家酒店离海滩很近，在这一点上你不用担心。
on that score = 在那一点上
⑨ You don't need to explain — I know the score, and I won't tell anyone.
= 你不用解释了，我心里有数，不会告诉任何人的。
know the score = 心里有数
⑩ Score a line down the middle of the card with a knife before you fold it.
= 对折卡片之前，先用小刀在中间划一道线。
score a line = 划一道线
⑪ The match ended in a score draw, two goals each, after a wild second half.
= 下半场打得激烈，比赛最后以二比二的有进球平局结束。

⑫ The two politicians spent the whole debate trying to score off each other.

⑬ She scored out the wrong word and wrote the correct one above it.

= 老师把整段都划掉，在页边写了「太长了！」
