fallout
核心：两支。一是（核爆之后的）放射性尘埃：radioactive fallout；
二是引申开来的余波、后果：the political fallout。不可数。
① Radioactive fallout drifted east and the sheep were tested for years afterwards.
= 放射性尘埃向东飘散，那些羊此后被检测了好多年。
radioactive fallout = 放射性尘埃
② The political fallout lasted a good deal longer than the policy it came from.
= 政治余波持续的时间，比引发它的那项政策长得多。
the political fallout = 政治余波

fallow
核心：两支。一是（田地）休耕的：lie fallow；
二是引申，（创作、生意）停滞不出活的：a fallow period。
① The field lies fallow this year and the weeds are doing extremely well.
= 那块地今年休耕，野草长得极好。
lie fallow = 休耕
② A fallow period of two years produced the book that pays the mortgage.
= 两年的停滞期，倒产出了那本还房贷的书。
a fallow period = 停滞期

false
核心：四支。一是不真实的、错的：a false statement；二是假的、人造的：false teeth；
三是虚伪的：a false smile；四是一串搭配：a false alarm、a false economy、
under false pretences。副词 falsely，名词 falsehood 与 falsity。
① A false statement on that form is a criminal offence and the box is tiny.
= 那张表上填不实信息属刑事犯罪，可那个格子小得可怜。
a false statement = 不实陈述
② His false teeth click when he is thinking and stop when he speaks.
= 他的假牙在他思考时咔咔响，一开口就不响了。
false teeth = 假牙
③ It was a false alarm and the whole street stood outside for an hour.
= 那是一场虚惊，整条街的人在外头站了一个钟头。
a false alarm = 虚惊一场
④ Buying the cheaper boots turned out to be a false economy.
= 买那双便宜靴子，结果是省小钱吃大亏。
a false economy = 省小钱吃大亏

falsehood
核心：（正式）谎言、虚假：可数时指一句谎话，不可数时指虚假这件事，跟 truth 相对。
① A falsehood repeated in three papers has become a fact by Friday.
= 一句谎话被三家报纸重复，到周五就成了事实。
a falsehood = 一句谎言
② Truth and falsehood are printed in exactly the same typeface.
= 真话与假话，印出来用的是同一种字体。
falsehood = 虚假

falsely
核心：两支。一是错误地、不实地：falsely accused；二是虚伪地：smile falsely。
① He was falsely accused and cleared eleven months later.
= 他被诬告，十一个月之后才洗清。
be falsely accused = 被诬告
② She smiled falsely at the camera and properly at her sister.
= 她冲镜头假笑，冲妹妹才是真笑。
smile falsely = 假笑
构词：false 加 -ly

falsettist
核心：（音乐）假声男歌手：用假声演唱高声部的男歌手。可数。
① The falsettist takes the top line and the choir holds everything under it.
= 那位假声歌手唱最高声部，合唱队托住底下所有声部。
a falsettist = 假声男歌手
② Two falsettists share the part and neither will sing the last night.
= 两位假声歌手分唱那个角色，末场谁都不肯上。
构词：falsetto 加 -ist

falsetto
核心：（音乐）假声：男声唱到自然音域之上的那种发声；也作形容词与副词。
不可数为主。
① He sings the chorus in falsetto and drops the verses an octave.
= 副歌他用假声唱，主歌降八度。
in falsetto = 用假声
② A falsetto voice carries over a crowd better than a shout does.
= 假声比喊叫更能穿过人群。
a falsetto voice = 假声嗓音

falsies
核心：（非正式）垫胸的假衬垫：缝在内衣或戏服里的那种。作复数。
① The costume came with falsies sewn into the lining.
= 那套戏服的衬里缝着假衬垫。
falsies = 垫胸假衬垫
② The dressing room keeps spare falsies in the same drawer as the pins.
= 化妆间把备用衬垫和别针放在同一个抽屉里。
spare falsies = 备用的假衬垫

falsification
核心：两支。一是伪造、篡改（记录、数据）；二是（科学哲学）证伪。不可数。
① Falsification of the log book cost the captain his licence.
= 篡改航海日志，让那位船长丢了执照。
the falsification of records = 记录的篡改
② Falsification, not proof, is what a good theory has to survive.
= 好理论要经得住的是证伪，不是证明。
falsification = 证伪
构词：falsify 加 -ation

falsify
核心：两支。一是伪造、篡改（文件、账目）：falsify records；
二是（科学哲学）证伪：拿证据推翻一个说法。名词 falsification。
① He falsified the mileage and the garage turned out to keep its own record.
= 他改了里程数，修车行原来自己另有一份记录。
falsify records = 篡改记录
② One clear counter-example falsifies the rule and nobody wants to find it.
= 一个明白的反例就能推翻那条规律，可谁也不想找到它。
falsify a theory = 证伪一个理论

falsity
核心：（正式）虚假、不实：说陈述本身是假的。不可数。
① The falsity of the claim took one phone call to establish.
= 那个说法是假的，打一通电话就查实了。
the falsity of something = 某说法的虚假
② Falsity in an insurance form voids the policy from the start.
= 保险表格上有不实之处，保单从头就作废。
falsity = 不实

Falstaffian
核心：（文学）福斯塔夫式的：像莎剧里那个又胖又贪杯、爱吹牛却讨人喜欢的角色。
① A Falstaffian figure at the bar told the same story to four different groups.
= 酒吧里一个福斯塔夫式的人物，把同一个故事对四拨人各讲了一遍。
a Falstaffian figure = 福斯塔夫式的人物
② His Falstaffian appetite survived two diets and one doctor.
= 他那福斯塔夫式的胃口，扛过了两次节食和一个医生。
Falstaffian = 福斯塔夫式的

falter
核心：两支。一是（声音、脚步）发颤、踉跄；二是（信心、经济、需求）动摇、走弱。
形容词 faltering。
① Her voice faltered on the second name and steadied for all the rest.
= 念到第二个名字时她声音发颤，其余的都稳住了。
somebody's voice falters = 某人声音发颤
② Demand faltered in the spring and never quite came back.
= 需求在春天走弱，之后再没完全回来。
falter = 走弱、动摇

faltering
核心：踉跄的、迟疑的、不稳的：多修饰 steps、voice、attempt、recovery。
① The faltering recovery shows up in the figures and not on the street.
= 那场步履蹒跚的复苏，只在数字里看得见，街面上看不出。
a faltering recovery = 步履蹒跚的复苏
② He took a few faltering steps and refused the stick all over again.
= 他踉跄走了几步，又一次拒绝用拐杖。
faltering steps = 蹒跚的脚步

fam
核心：（非正式，年轻人用语）「家人」：拿来称呼要好的朋友，也指自己那一伙人。
① He calls everybody fam and remembers about half of their names.
= 他管谁都叫「家人」，名字大概记得一半。
fam = 铁哥们儿、自家人
② The whole fam turned up and that flat holds six at a push.
= 那一伙人全来了，那间公寓挤挤也就装六个。
the fam = 自己那一伙人

fame
核心：名声、名气：出名这件事，不可数。
常见搭配 rise to fame、fame and fortune。形容词 famed。
① Her rise to fame took eleven years and one very short film.
= 她成名花了十一年，外加一部极短的片子。
rise to fame = 成名
② Fame and fortune arrived together and left at quite different times.
= 名与利是一起来的，走的时候却不同时。
fame and fortune = 名与利

famed
核心：（书面）著名的、以……闻名的：多说 be famed for something，比 famous 书面。
① The town is famed for its cheese and known locally for its floods.
= 那个镇以奶酪闻名，本地人记得的却是它的水患。
be famed for something = 以某物闻名
② A famed collection of maps sits in a room that nobody can find.
= 一批著名的地图藏在一个谁也找不着的房间里。
famed = 著名的

familial
核心：（正式、医学）家族的、家庭的：多说 familial ties、a familial disease。
① Familial ties decide who gets the shop and the law decides everything else.
= 谁接手那家店由家族关系定，其余一切由法律定。
familial ties = 家族纽带
② The condition is familial and skips about one generation in three.
= 那种病是家族遗传的，大约每三代跳过一代。
a familial disease = 家族遗传病

familiar
核心：三支。一是熟悉的、常见的：a familiar face；
二是 be familiar with something，对某事门儿清；
三是（贬）过分自来熟的：be too familiar。名词 familiarity。
① A familiar face at the door changes what the dog does.
= 门口出现一张熟脸，狗的反应就不一样。
a familiar face = 熟面孔
② She is familiar with the paperwork and hates every page of it.
= 那些文书她门儿清，每一页都讨厌。
be familiar with something = 熟悉某事
③ He is too familiar with the customers and the manager has noticed.
= 他跟顾客太自来熟，经理已经注意到了。
be too familiar = 过分自来熟

familiarity
核心：两支。一是熟悉、通晓：familiarity with something；二是（贬）过分随便。
谚语 familiarity breeds contempt，亲近生轻慢。
① Familiarity with the route matters more than speed on that road.
= 在那条路上，熟不熟路比开得快要紧。
familiarity with something = 对某事的熟悉
② Familiarity breeds contempt, and it breeds shortcuts as well.
= 亲近生轻慢，也生出各种偷懒的走法。
familiarity breeds contempt = 亲近生轻慢

familiarization
核心：（正式）熟悉过程、上手培训：让人先把环境或设备摸熟。
英式也拼 familiarisation。不可数。
① A day of familiarization saves a week of mistakes on the line.
= 一天的上手培训，能省下产线上一星期的差错。
familiarization = 熟悉、上手
② The familiarization visit lasts an hour and covers three floors.
= 那趟熟悉环境的参观一个钟头，走完三层楼。
a familiarization visit = 熟悉环境的参观
构词：familiarize 加 -ation

familiarize
核心：（正式）使熟悉：最常用的是 familiarize yourself with something，
先把某事摸熟。英式也拼 familiarise。
① Familiarize yourself with the exits before you unpack anything.
= 东西还没打开，先把出口摸清楚。
familiarize yourself with something = 熟悉某事
② The induction familiarizes new staff with a building they will never use.
= 那场入职培训让新员工熟悉一栋他们根本用不上的楼。
familiarize somebody with something = 让某人熟悉某事

familiarly
核心：两支。一是熟稔地、亲昵地：greet somebody familiarly；
二是「俗称」：be familiarly known as something。
① He greeted the porter familiarly and got the good room.
= 他跟门房打招呼很熟络，拿到了那间好房。
greet somebody familiarly = 亲昵地招呼某人
② The building is familiarly known as the pepper pot.
= 那栋楼俗称「胡椒罐」。
be familiarly known as something = 俗称某名
构词：familiar 加 -ly

family
核心：三支。一是家庭、家人：可当单数也可当复数；二是家族、门第；
三是（生物）科：the cat family。
习语：run in the family 是家里遗传的，start a family 是开始生儿育女，
a family tree 是家谱。
① The family sits down at seven and argues until the plates go cold.
= 一家人七点坐下吃饭，争到盘子都凉了。
a family = 家庭
② Bad knees run in the family and so does the habit of walking everywhere.
= 膝盖不好是家里遗传的，凡事靠走路也是。
run in the family = 家里遗传的
③ They started a family in a flat with one window and no lift.
= 他们在一间只有一扇窗、没有电梯的公寓里生了孩子。
start a family = 开始生儿育女
④ The family tree on the wall stops in 1911 and picks up again in 1946.
= 墙上那张家谱到 1911 年就断了，1946 年才接上。
a family tree = 家谱
⑤ Lions and domestic cats are in the same family and not the same genus.
= 狮子和家猫同科，却不同属。
the cat family = 猫科
