question
/ˈkwestʃən/
核心：名词：问题、提问，可数：ask a question；（需要讨论、解决的）问题、事情，可数：the question of；疑问、怀疑，不可数：there is no question that；
（考试的）试题，可数。动词：问、讯问、盘问：question somebody about；怀疑、质疑：question whether。
习语：out of the question 是不可能的、谈不上；beyond question 是毫无疑问；in question 是正在谈论的、所说的，也指有疑问的；
call something into question 是对某事提出质疑；beg the question 是回避要害、让人不禁要问；pop the question 是（口语）求婚；
without question 是毫无疑问、毫无异议地；there is no question of 是不可能有……；a question of 是……的问题、关乎……。
派生：questioner 是提问的人；questioning 是讯问，不可数，也作形容词：怀疑的、探询的，副词 questioningly。
① Can I ask you a question about the maths homework before the lesson starts?
= 上课前我能问你一个关于数学作业的问题吗？
ask a question = 问问题
question = 问题（需要回答的）

answer a question = 回答问题
problem = 问题（需要解决的麻烦）
③ The question of who will pay for the repairs has not been settled.
= 修理费由谁出的问题还没定下来。
the question of something = 某事的问题
raise a question = 提出问题
④ There is no question that she is the best player in the team.
= 毫无疑问，她是队里最好的球员。
there is no question that… = 毫无疑问……
there is some question about something = 某事有些疑问
⑤ I could answer only three of the questions in the history examination this morning.
= 今天上午的历史考卷，我只答得出三道题。
a question = 试题
⑥ The police questioned him about where he had been that night.
= 警方盘问他那天夜里去了哪里。
question somebody about something = 就某事盘问某人
be questioned by the police = 被警方讯问
⑦ Nobody at the meeting questioned whether the figures in the annual report were correct.
= 会上谁也没质疑年度报告里的那些数字对不对。
question whether… = 怀疑是否……
question somebody's motives = 怀疑某人的动机
⑧ With the roof leaking and the car broken, a holiday abroad is out of the question this year.
= 屋顶漏雨、车也坏了，今年出国度假是不可能了。
out of the question = 不可能的、谈不上
⑨ Her honesty is beyond question, for she once returned a wallet full of cash to a stranger.
= 她的诚实毋庸置疑，她曾把一个装满现金的钱包还给了陌生人。
beyond question = 毫无疑问
⑩ On the night in question he was at home with his wife.
= 在所说的那天夜里，他跟妻子在家。
in question = 正在谈论的、所说的
the person in question = 所说的那个人
⑪ The new evidence calls into question everything we thought we knew.
= 新证据让我们原以为知道的一切都成了问题。
call something into question = 对某事提出质疑
⑫ Saying that the plan will work because it is a good plan simply begs the question.
= 说那个计划行得通是因为它是个好计划，等于什么也没说。
beg the question = 回避要害
口语里也指「让人不禁要问」。
⑬ He popped the question on the beach at sunset, with a ring hidden in a shell.
= 他在日落时分的海滩上求了婚，戒指藏在一只贝壳里。
pop the question = 求婚
⑭ She was, without question, the finest teacher I ever had.
= 毫无疑问，她是我遇到过的最好的老师。
without question = 毫无疑问
⑮ There is no question of the factory closing this year.
= 那家工厂今年不可能关门。
there is no question of something = 不可能有某事
⑯ Finishing the bridge is not a question of money but of finding enough skilled workers.
= 把桥修完不是钱的问题，而是能不能找到足够的熟练工人。
a question of something = 某事的问题、关乎某事
⑰ The teacher reminded the class to put a question mark at the end of every direct question.
= 老师提醒全班，每个直接问句末尾都要打问号。
a question mark = 问号
a question mark over something = 某事的疑问
⑱ The question master read out the scores at the end of each round.
= 每一轮结束时，问答节目主持人都念一遍得分。
a question master = （英式）问答节目主持人
⑲ "Isn't it?" in "It's cold, isn't it?" is a question tag.
= 「It's cold, isn't it?」里的「isn't it?」是附加疑问句。
a question tag = 附加疑问句
⑳ The questioner sat down without waiting for an answer.
= 提问的人没等回答就坐下了。
a questioner = 提问的人
㉑ The suspect was taken to the police station for questioning.
= 嫌疑人被带到警察局接受讯问。
for questioning = 接受讯问
questioning = 讯问
㉒ She gave him a questioning look across the table and waited for him to explain.
= 她隔着桌子探询地看了他一眼，等他解释。
a questioning look = 探询的目光
㉓ He looked at me questioningly, as if he did not believe me.
= 他疑惑地看着我，好像不信我的话。
questioningly = 疑惑地、探询地
㉔ "She asked me where I lived" is an example of a reported question.
= 「She asked me where I lived」是间接疑问句的一个例子。
a reported question = （语法）间接疑问句
