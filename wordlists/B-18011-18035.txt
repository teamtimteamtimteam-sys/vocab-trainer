punctilious
/pʌŋkˈtɪliəs/
核心：（正式）一丝不苟的、拘泥细节的、礼数周到的：处处留心把事情做得合规矩。常说 punctilious about、in。
副词 punctiliously；名词 punctiliousness，不可数。并入本条。
① He was punctilious about paying his debts on the day they fell due.
= 他还债一丝不苟，到期当天必还。
be punctilious about something = 对某事一丝不苟
be punctilious in doing something = 做某事一丝不苟
② She is a punctilious hostess who writes every place card by hand.
= 她是位礼数周到的女主人，每张座位卡都亲手写。
a punctilious hostess = 礼数周到的女主人
③ The clerk punctiliously recorded every penny that passed through his hands.
= 那个职员把经手的每一个便士都记得一丝不苟。
punctiliously = 一丝不苟地
④ His punctiliousness over small matters of dress amused the younger officers.
= 他对衣着上的小事一丝不苟，年轻军官觉得好笑。
punctiliousness = 一丝不苟、拘泥细节

punctuate
/ˈpʌŋktʃueɪt/
核心：两支。一是 punctuate something with、by：不时打断，多用被动；二是给（文字）加标点，及物也可不及物。
① Her speech was punctuated by bursts of applause from the back of the hall.
= 她的讲话不时被大厅后面爆发的掌声打断。
be punctuated by something = 不时被某事打断
be punctuated with something = 夹杂着某物
② The silence of the night was punctuated by the bark of a distant dog.
= 远处的狗叫不时划破夜的寂静。
punctuate the silence = 划破寂静
③ The children are learning to punctuate their sentences correctly.
= 孩子们在学着给句子正确地加标点。
punctuate a sentence = 给句子加标点
punctuate correctly = 标点加得对
