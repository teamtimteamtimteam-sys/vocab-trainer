intercede
核心：（正式）说情、调停 —— 为别人向有权者求情（intercede with somebody for/on behalf of somebody），
或在争执双方之间调解。名词 intercession（说情、调停；宗教上的代祷）并入本条。
① The bishop interceded with the king on behalf of the prisoners.
= 主教代表囚犯们向国王求情。
intercede with somebody on behalf of somebody = 代某人向某人求情
② The UN was asked to intercede in the border dispute between the two countries.
= 联合国被请求调停两国之间的边界争端。
intercede in a dispute = 调停争端
③ Thanks to the intercession of a local priest, the boy was released from prison.
= 多亏当地一位神父说情，那男孩被放出了监狱。
the intercession of somebody = 某人的说情
④ The congregation offered prayers of intercession for the sick.
= 会众为病人作代祷。
prayers of intercession = 代祷

intercept
核心：拦截、截获 —— 在人、东西、信息到达目的地之前把它截住。
名词 interception（拦截；截获）与拦截者、拦截机 interceptor 并入本条。
① Police intercepted the lorry at the border and found drugs hidden in the cargo.
= 警方在边境拦下那辆卡车，在货物里发现了藏着的毒品。
intercept a vehicle = 拦截车辆
② The defender intercepted the pass and ran the length of the pitch to score.
= 那名后卫截住传球，带球跑了全场破门得分。
intercept a pass = 截断传球
③ Security services are accused of the illegal interception of private emails.
= 安全部门被指控非法截取私人邮件。
the interception of something = 对…的截取
④ Two fighter interceptors were sent up to follow the unknown plane.
= 两架截击机起飞，跟踪那架不明飞机。
an interceptor = 截击机、拦截者

interchange
核心：交换、交流；（高速公路的）立交道口。名词重音在前：想法、信息的互换（an interchange of ideas）；
公路上的立体交叉枢纽。动词重音在后：互换、交替。
形容词 interchangeable（可互换的）、副词 interchangeably 与名词 interchangeability 并入本条。
① The conference allowed a useful interchange of ideas between scientists and farmers.
= 那次会议让科学家和农民进行了有益的思想交流。
an interchange of ideas = 思想交流
② There was a long traffic jam at the motorway interchange.
= 高速公路立交道口堵了很长的车。
a motorway interchange = 高速公路立交道口
③ The parts are interchangeable, so you can use either battery in the camera.
= 这些部件可以互换，所以两块电池哪块都能用在相机上。
be interchangeable = 可互换
④ In everyday speech many people use the two words interchangeably.
= 日常说话时，很多人把这两个说法混着用。
use something interchangeably = 交替使用
⑤ The interchangeability of the parts made repairs much cheaper.
= 零件可互换，让修理便宜了很多。
the interchangeability of something = …的可互换性

intercity
核心：城际的 —— 城市之间的（交通），多作定语：intercity trains、intercity buses。
① The intercity train from London to Edinburgh takes about four and a half hours.
= 从伦敦到爱丁堡的城际列车大约要四个半小时。
an intercity train = 城际列车
② Cheap intercity buses have taken many passengers away from the railways.
= 廉价的城际大巴从铁路那里抢走了许多乘客。
intercity buses = 城际大巴

intercollegiate
核心：（美）大学之间的、校际的 —— 多说体育比赛、辩论赛。
① She played in the national intercollegiate tennis championship.
= 她参加了全国大学生网球锦标赛。
an intercollegiate championship = 大学校际锦标赛
② The university has a long history of success in intercollegiate debate.
= 那所大学在校际辩论赛中一向成绩出色。
intercollegiate debate = 校际辩论

intercom
核心：对讲机、内部通话系统 —— 楼门口、办公室之间用的通话装置。
① A voice on the intercom told us to take the lift to the fifth floor.
= 对讲机里有个声音让我们坐电梯上五楼。
on the intercom = 通过对讲机
② The pilot spoke to the passengers over the intercom.
= 机长通过广播系统对乘客讲话。
over the intercom = 通过内部通话系统

intercommunication
核心：（正式）相互交流、互相通讯 —— 不可数。
① Good intercommunication between departments prevents costly mistakes.
= 部门之间良好的相互沟通能避免代价高昂的错误。
intercommunication between departments = 部门之间的相互沟通
② The old ships had no system of intercommunication between the bridge and the engine room.
= 旧式船只的驾驶台和机舱之间没有相互通话的系统。
a system of intercommunication = 相互通话系统

interconnect
核心：（使）互相连接、互相联系 —— 常用被动或分词 interconnected。名词 interconnection 并入本条。
① The two computer networks are interconnected, so staff can share files easily.
= 两个电脑网络互相连通，员工可以轻松共享文件。
be interconnected = 互相连接
② Poverty, poor health and bad housing are closely interconnected.
= 贫困、健康状况差和住房条件差彼此密切相关。
closely interconnected = 密切相关的
③ The report shows the interconnections between climate change and food prices.
= 报告揭示了气候变化与食品价格之间的相互关联。
an interconnection between A and B = A与B之间的关联
可数，多用复数。

intercontinental
核心：洲际的 —— 在大洲之间的，多说航班、导弹。
① The airport handles more than fifty intercontinental flights a day.
= 那座机场每天有五十多个洲际航班起降。
intercontinental flights = 洲际航班
② The country is suspected of developing intercontinental missiles.
= 那个国家被怀疑正在研制洲际导弹。
intercontinental missiles = 洲际导弹

intercostal
核心：（解剖）肋间的 —— 肋骨之间的（肌肉、神经）。
① He pulled an intercostal muscle while lifting a heavy box.
= 他搬重箱子时拉伤了肋间肌。
an intercostal muscle = 肋间肌
② The doctor injected a local anaesthetic into the intercostal nerves.
= 医生往肋间神经里注射了局部麻醉药。
intercostal nerves = 肋间神经

intercourse
核心：性交（正式）；（旧）交往、交流。今天单用几乎总是指 sexual intercourse；
「交往」义只见于旧式文字（social intercourse）。
① The disease can be passed on through sexual intercourse.
= 这种病可以通过性交传播。
sexual intercourse = 性交
正式、医学用语。
② In the small village, social intercourse took place mainly after church on Sundays.
= 在那个小村子里，社交往来主要在星期天做完礼拜之后进行。
social intercourse = 社交往来（旧）

intercultural
核心：跨文化的、不同文化之间的 —— 多说交流、理解、培训。
① The company offers intercultural training to staff who work abroad.
= 公司为驻外员工提供跨文化培训。
intercultural training = 跨文化培训
② Learning a language is also an exercise in intercultural understanding.
= 学一门语言也是在练习跨文化理解。
intercultural understanding = 跨文化理解

intercut
核心：（电影）交叉剪接 —— 把两组不同场景的镜头穿插剪接在一起。过去式 intercut。
① The director intercut scenes of the wedding with scenes of the robbery.
= 导演把婚礼的镜头和抢劫的镜头交叉剪接在一起。
intercut A with B = 把A与B交叉剪接
② The documentary is intercut with old family photographs.
= 那部纪录片里穿插着一些老家庭照片。
be intercut with something = 穿插着…

interdenominational
核心：各教派之间的、跨宗派的 —— 不同基督教派别共同参与的。
① An interdenominational service was held for the victims of the flood.
= 为洪水遇难者举行了一场跨教派的礼拜。
an interdenominational service = 跨教派礼拜
② The school is interdenominational and welcomes children from all churches.
= 那所学校不分宗派，欢迎各教会的孩子。
be interdenominational = 不分宗派

interdepartmental
核心：各部门之间的 —— 说政府、公司、大学里不同部门之间的会议、合作、竞争。
① An interdepartmental committee was set up to deal with the crisis.
= 为应对危机成立了一个跨部门委员会。
an interdepartmental committee = 跨部门委员会
② Interdepartmental rivalry slowed down the whole project.
= 部门之间的明争暗斗拖慢了整个项目。
interdepartmental rivalry = 部门间的竞争

interdependent
核心：相互依存的、相互依赖的 —— 彼此离不开。名词 interdependence 并入本条。
① The economies of the two countries have become closely interdependent.
= 两国的经济已经变得紧密依存。
be interdependent = 相互依存
② The course teaches children about the interdependence of plants and animals.
= 这门课教孩子们认识动植物之间的相互依存。
the interdependence of something = …的相互依存

interdict
核心：（正式，法律）禁止、禁令；（美，军事）阻断（补给线）。名词 interdiction（禁止；阻断）并入本条。
① The court issued an interdict preventing the sale of the land.
= 法院下达禁令，禁止出售那块土地。
an interdict = 禁令
苏格兰法律里常用，相当于英格兰的 injunction。
② Warships were sent to interdict the supply of weapons to the rebels.
= 军舰被派去阻断向叛军运送武器。
interdict supplies = 阻断补给
③ The coast guard is responsible for the interdiction of drug shipments.
= 海岸警卫队负责拦截毒品运输。
the interdiction of something = 对…的拦截、阻断

interdisciplinary
核心：跨学科的 —— 涉及两个或更多学科的。名词 interdisciplinarity 并入本条。
① The university offers an interdisciplinary course in science and philosophy.
= 那所大学开设一门科学与哲学的跨学科课程。
an interdisciplinary course = 跨学科课程
② Funding bodies now encourage interdisciplinarity in research projects.
= 资助机构如今鼓励研究项目的跨学科性。
interdisciplinarity = 跨学科性

interest
核心：兴趣；利息；利益；股份。本义是「与…有关、对…有份」（拉丁语 interesse），由此长出几支：
心里对某事有关心、想知道的感觉 —— 兴趣（take an interest in），以及让人感兴趣的东西（an interest = 爱好）；
钱上有份 —— 借钱付出、存钱得到的利息（interest rates）；以及在企业里的股份、权益（an interest in a company）；
事情对某人有好处 —— 利益（in somebody's interest、in the interest(s) of）；
有共同利益的一群人（business interests、an interest group）。
动词 interest：使感兴趣。interest in、interest-free（无息的）、interest group（利益集团）、
conflict of interest（利益冲突）并入本条；形容词 interested、interesting 另立。
① She has always had a great interest in history and old buildings.
= 她一向对历史和古建筑很感兴趣。
an interest in something = 对…的兴趣
have interest in something = 对…有兴趣
② He began to take an interest in politics when he was at university.
= 他上大学时开始关心政治。
take an interest in something = 开始关心…
③ Her interests include sailing, painting and cooking for friends.
= 她的爱好包括航海、画画和给朋友做饭。
somebody's interests = 某人的爱好
可数复数，指具体的爱好。
④ The house is of great historical interest and is open to visitors in summer.
= 那座房子很有历史价值，夏天对游客开放。
of interest = 值得关注的、有价值的
⑤ The bank charges seven per cent interest on the loan.
= 银行对这笔贷款收取百分之七的利息。
interest on a loan = 贷款利息
⑥ The central bank raised interest rates for the third time this year.
= 央行今年第三次加息。
interest rates = 利率
⑦ The shop offers interest-free credit on all furniture for a year.
= 那家店的所有家具都提供一年免息分期付款。
interest-free = 无息的
⑧ It is in your own interest to tell the police everything you know.
= 把你知道的都告诉警察，这对你自己有好处。
in somebody's interest = 符合某人的利益
⑨ In the interests of safety, please keep your seat belt fastened.
= 为了安全起见，请系好安全带。
in the interests of something = 为了…起见
in the interest of something = 为了…起见
单复数两种写法都常见，意思相同。
⑩ The judge stepped down from the case because of a conflict of interest.
= 由于存在利益冲突，法官退出了那个案子。
a conflict of interest = 利益冲突
⑪ Powerful interest groups spent millions trying to change the law.
= 强大的利益集团花了几百万想改变那项法律。
an interest group = 利益集团
⑫ The family still has a controlling interest in the company.
= 那个家族仍然持有公司的控股权。
an interest in a company = 在公司的股份
⑬ He paid back the favour with interest, inviting us to his villa for a week.
= 他加倍回报了那份人情，请我们去他的别墅住了一周。
with interest = 加倍地
比喻：还的比借的多。
⑭ Can I interest you in a cup of coffee before you go?
= 你走之前要不要来杯咖啡？
interest somebody in something = 使某人对…有兴趣
动词用法，常见于客气的邀请或推销。

interested
核心：感兴趣的；有利害关系的。两支：想了解、想参与（be interested in / to do）；
跟结果有利益关系的（interested parties）。反义 uninterested（不感兴趣）与 disinterested（无私的、不偏不倚的）要分清。
① Are you interested in joining our book club, which meets in the library on Tuesdays?
= 我们的读书会每周二在图书馆聚会，你有兴趣加入吗？
be interested in doing something = 对做…有兴趣
② As an engineer, I would be very interested to know how you solved the problem with the pump.
= 作为工程师，我很想知道你是怎么解决水泵那个问题的。
be interested to do something = 很想…
③ All interested parties will be invited to comment on the plans.
= 所有利益相关方都将受邀对计划发表意见。
interested parties = 利益相关方
第二支，正式用语。

interesting
核心：有趣的、引起兴趣的 —— 让人想了解、想关注。副词 interestingly（有趣的是）并入本条，
常放句首评论整件事。跟 interested 分清：interesting 说东西让人感兴趣，interested 说人感兴趣。
① It was a really interesting film, and we talked about it all the way home.
= 那部电影真的很有意思，我们一路聊着回家。
an interesting film = 有趣的电影
② It will be interesting to see whether the new manager can turn things around.
= 新经理能否扭转局面，值得关注。
it will be interesting to see = 值得关注的是…
③ Interestingly, the youngest children scored highest in the test.
= 有意思的是，最小的孩子考试得分最高。
interestingly = 有趣的是

interface
核心：界面；接口；（学科、领域的）交接处。本义是两个东西接触的面；
计算机上指人与机器交互的界面（user interface），或设备之间的连接口；比喻两个领域相交的地方。
也作动词：连接、交流。
① The new app has a simple interface that even children can use.
= 新应用的界面很简单，连孩子都会用。
a user interface = 用户界面
② The printer connects to the computer through a standard interface.
= 打印机通过标准接口与电脑连接。
an interface = 接口
③ Her research sits at the interface between biology and computing.
= 她的研究处在生物学与计算机科学的交叉点上。
the interface between A and B = A与B的交叉点
④ The software interfaces with the bank's own systems.
= 那个软件与银行自己的系统对接。
interface with something = 与…对接

interfaith
核心：不同宗教之间的、跨信仰的 —— 多作定语：interfaith dialogue、interfaith service。
① The city held an interfaith service for the victims of the attack.
= 那座城市为袭击事件的遇难者举行了一场跨宗教的悼念仪式。
an interfaith service = 跨宗教仪式
② Religious leaders met for an interfaith dialogue on climate change.
= 宗教领袖们聚在一起，就气候变化进行跨宗教对话。
interfaith dialogue = 跨宗教对话

interfere
核心：干涉、干预；妨碍。两支：插手别人的事（interfere in）；
阻碍事情顺利进行（interfere with）。interfere with 另有两个意思：乱动别人的东西；（英，委婉）猥亵。
形容词 interfering（爱管闲事的）并入本条。
① I wish my mother would stop interfering in my private life.
= 我希望我妈别再干涉我的私生活了。
interfere in something = 干涉…
② Late-night phone calls interfere with my sleep.
= 深夜的电话影响我睡觉。
interfere with something = 妨碍…
③ Somebody had interfered with the brakes before the crash.
= 车祸之前有人动过刹车。
interfere with something = 乱动…（使其坏掉）
④ The teacher was charged with interfering with a child.
= 那名教师被控猥亵儿童。
interfere with somebody = （英，委婉）猥亵某人
⑤ Her interfering neighbour reported every visitor to the landlord.
= 她那个爱管闲事的邻居把每个来访的人都报告给了房东。
an interfering neighbour = 爱管闲事的邻居
