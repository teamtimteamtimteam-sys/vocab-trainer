sailboarding
/ˈseɪlbɔːdɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：帆板运动。也叫 windsurfing。
① Sailboarding is popular on the coast because the wind is strong and steady in the afternoons.
= 帆板运动在海边很流行，因为下午的风又大又稳。
sailboarding = 帆板运动
② We went sailboarding every day of our holiday and came home with very sore arms.
= 度假期间我们天天去玩帆板，回家时胳膊酸疼得厉害。
go sailboarding = 去玩帆板

sailboat
/ˈseɪlbəʊt/
核心：名词，可数（北美）：帆船。英式多说 sailing boat。
① A small white sailboat moved slowly across the lake in the evening breeze.
= 一艘白色小帆船在傍晚的微风里缓缓驶过湖面。
a sailboat = 帆船
② They saved for years to buy a sailboat and now spend every summer on the water.
= 他们攒了好几年钱买了一艘帆船，现在每年夏天都在水上度过。
buy a sailboat = 买帆船

sailcloth
/ˈseɪlklɒθ/
核心：名词，不可数：帆布：做船帆、帐篷、包的结实厚布。
① The old bag is made of heavy sailcloth and has lasted more than twenty years.
= 那只旧包是用厚帆布做的，已经用了二十多年。
sailcloth = 帆布
② The tent was made from sailcloth that the fishermen had treated with oil to keep out the rain.
= 那顶帐篷是用渔民上过油、能挡雨的帆布做的。
made from sailcloth = 帆布做的

sailing
/ˈseɪlɪŋ/
核心：名词：帆船运动、驾船（不可数）：go sailing；（客轮、渡轮的）班次、航班（可数）：the next sailing。习语 plain sailing 是一帆风顺、容易的事。复合词 sailing boat（英式）是帆船，sailing ship 是帆船（大型的、旧时的）。
① My parents took up sailing when they retired, and now they spend every weekend at the coast.
= 我父母退休后开始玩帆船，现在每个周末都在海边度过。
sailing = 帆船运动
go sailing = 去驾帆船
② The storm cancelled the first two sailings, so we had to wait for the afternoon ferry.
= 暴风雨取消了头两班船，我们只好等下午的渡轮。
a sailing = 一班船
③ Once we found a good builder, the rest of the work on the house was plain sailing.
= 一找到靠谱的施工队，房子剩下的活就一帆风顺了。
plain sailing = 一帆风顺
④ We hired a small sailing boat for the afternoon and took turns at the tiller.
= 我们租了一下午的小帆船，轮流掌舵。
a sailing boat = （英式）帆船
⑤ The museum has a model of the great sailing ship that carried tea from China in the 1860s.
= 博物馆里有一艘大帆船的模型，十九世纪六十年代它从中国运茶。
a sailing ship = （旧时的大）帆船

work as a sailmaker = 当帆匠

sailmaking

sailor
/ˈseɪlə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：水手、海员；水兵；驾帆船的人：a keen sailor。be a good / bad sailor 是不晕船 / 容易晕船。复合词 sailor suit 是（儿童穿的）水手服。

be a bad sailor = 容易晕船

③ The little boy in the old photograph is wearing a white sailor suit and a straw hat.
= 老照片里那个小男孩穿着一身白色水手服，戴着草帽。
a sailor suit = 水手服

saint
/seɪnt/
核心：名词，可数：圣徒、圣人：被教会尊为圣的人，名字前缩写为 St；（口语）圣人般的人、大好人：be a saint。saint's day 是圣徒纪念日。
① The church is named after a saint who lived in the town in the seventh century.
= 那座教堂以七世纪住在镇上的一位圣徒命名。
a saint = 圣徒
② You have been a saint, looking after my children all week while I was in hospital.
= 我住院那一个星期，你一直帮我照看孩子，真是个大好人。
be a saint = 是个大好人
③ The village holds a market on its saint's day every year in the middle of June.
= 那个村子每年六月中旬的圣徒纪念日都有集市。
a saint's day = 圣徒纪念日
④ It would take the patience of a saint to teach that class of noisy teenagers.
= 要教那一班吵闹的青少年，得有圣人般的耐心。
the patience of a saint = 圣人般的耐心

sainted

① The church holds the bones of a sainted bishop who died more than a thousand years ago.
= 那座教堂供奉着一位一千多年前去世、被封为圣徒的主教的遗骨。

② "My sainted mother would never have let me leave the house dressed like that," he joked.

my sainted mother = 我那位可敬的母亲

sainthood

qualify for sainthood = 够格封圣

saintliness

sake
/seɪk/
核心：名词：（for the sake of）为了……的缘故、为了……的利益：for the sake of somebody / something、for your own sake；为……而……：talk for the sake of talking。（读 /ˈsɑːki/）日本清酒。
习语：for goodness' sake / for heaven's sake 是看在老天的分上（表示不耐烦）；for old times' sake 是看在老交情的分上、为了怀旧。
① They stayed together for the sake of the children, even though they were not happy.
= 他们为了孩子没有分开，尽管两人过得并不快乐。
for the sake of somebody = 为了某人
② You really should stop smoking for your own sake, if not for the sake of your family.
= 就算不为家人，为了你自己你也真该把烟戒了。
for your own sake = 为了你自己
③ He seems to argue just for the sake of arguing, even when he agrees with you.
= 他好像是为了争而争，哪怕他其实同意你的看法。
for the sake of doing something = 为……而……
④ For goodness' sake, turn that music down — I am trying to sleep!
= 看在老天的分上，把音乐关小点——我在睡觉呢！
for goodness' sake = 看在老天的分上
⑤ We went back to the little café where we first met, just for old times' sake.
= 为了怀旧，我们回到了初次见面的那家小咖啡馆。
for old times' sake = 为了怀旧
⑥ We drank warm sake and ate sushi at a small Japanese restaurant near the station.
= 我们在车站附近一家日本小餐馆喝温清酒、吃寿司。
sake = 日本清酒
这一义读 /ˈsɑːki/，和前面几义是不同来源的两个词。

salary
/ˈsæləri/

wage = 工资（按时按日发）
income = 收入（总称）
② He earns a good salary as an engineer, but he works very long hours.
= 他当工程师，薪水不错，可工作时间很长。

= 那份工作的起薪是一年三万英镑。
a starting salary = 起薪

④ The teachers are asking for a salary increase because prices have gone up so much.
= 物价涨了那么多，教师们要求加薪。
a salary increase = 加薪

sale

③ I bought these boots on sale for half price in the shop near the station.

④ The sale of the old factory brought in enough money to build a new school.

⑤ Sales of electric cars have doubled in the last two years as petrol prices have gone up.

⑦ I bought a new coat in the January sales for less than half the normal price.

⑧ The church holds a sale of work every Christmas to raise money for the roof.
= 那座教堂每年圣诞节都办一次手工义卖，为修屋顶筹钱。

⑨ The painting was sold at a sale of old masters in London for two million pounds.
