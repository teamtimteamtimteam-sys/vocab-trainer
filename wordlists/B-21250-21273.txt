sitting
/ˈsɪtɪŋ/
核心：名词，可数：（议会、法庭的）一次会议、开庭；（用餐的）一轮、一批：the first sitting；（当模特的）一次坐着让人画或拍。形容词：在任的、现任的：a sitting MP。习语：at one sitting 是一口气、一次坐着完成。
① The hotel serves dinner in two sittings, at seven and at half past eight.
= 那家酒店分两轮供应晚餐，七点一轮，八点半一轮。
two sittings = 两轮（用餐）
sitting = 一轮
② I read the whole book at one sitting because I couldn't put it down.
= 我一口气读完了整本书，因为放不下来。
at one sitting = 一口气
③ In the election, the sitting MP lost his seat to a young teacher from the village.
= 选举中，那位现任议员把席位输给了村里的一个年轻教师。
the sitting MP = 现任议员
④ On winter evenings, we sat in the sitting room and watched the fire.
= 冬天的晚上，我们坐在起居室里看着炉火。
the sitting room = （英式）起居室
也叫 living room、lounge。
⑤ The landlord could not sell the house, because it had a sitting tenant who had lived there for forty years.
= 房东卖不掉那所房子，因为里面有一位住了四十年的现住租户。
a sitting tenant = （英式）现住租户

situate
/ˈsɪtʃueɪt/

① The writer tries to situate the story in the history of the town.
= 那位作家试图把那个故事放到小镇的历史背景中去讲。

② The architects situated the new school close to the park so that children could play outside.
= 建筑师们把新学校建在公园附近，好让孩子们能在外面玩。
situate a building = 给建筑选址

/ˈsɪtʃueɪtɪd/

① The hotel is situated on a hill above the town, with views of the sea.
= 那家酒店坐落在小镇上方的一座山上，能看到大海。

be well situated to do something = 很有条件做某事

situation
/ˌsɪtʃuˈeɪʃn/
核心：名词，可数：情况、形势、处境：the current situation、in a difficult situation；（书面）位置、地点；（旧）职位：Situations Vacant（招聘栏）。
① The situation in the hospital is getting worse because there are not enough nurses.
= 由于护士不够，医院的情况越来越糟。
situation = 情况
② The government will meet tomorrow morning to discuss the current situation in the north.
= 政府明天上午开会讨论北部目前的局势。
current situation = 目前情况
situation 指整体局面，常和 difficult、economic、political 连用。
③ The new manager handled the situation very calmly and nobody lost their job.
= 新经理非常冷静地处理了局面，没有人丢掉工作。
handle a situation = 处理局面
④ She found herself in a difficult situation, with no money and nowhere to sleep.
= 她陷入了困境，身无分文，无处过夜。
situation = 整体局面
近义对照：
condition = 状态、条件
⑤ The house has a beautiful situation on the edge of a lake.
= 那座房子位于湖边，地理位置极佳。
a beautiful situation = 极佳的位置
⑥ The old situation comedy about a family running a hotel is still shown on television every Saturday evening.
= 那部讲一家人开旅馆的老情景喜剧，现在每个星期六晚上还在电视上播。
a situation comedy = 情景喜剧（简称 sitcom）
