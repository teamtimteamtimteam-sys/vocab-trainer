③ Congress raised the debt ceiling.

the debt ceiling = 债务上限
④ He hit the ceiling when he heard.

⑤ A ceiling rose hides the wiring.

a ceiling rose = 天花板上的圆形灯座（英式）

celandine

celebrant

celebrate
核心：三支。一是庆祝：为好事办活动、乐一乐。二是举行
仪式：celebrate Mass 是主持弥撒。三是赞颂：文章或纪念碑

受赞誉的；celebratory 是庆祝性的。
① They celebrated her birthday.

② The priest celebrated Mass.
= 神父主持了弥撒。

celebratory = 庆祝性的
⑦ They celebrated by opening champagne.
= 他们开香槟庆祝。
celebrate by doing = 以……来庆祝
构词：拉丁语 celebrare 举行、聚众
注意别混：celebrated 是著名的，不是被庆祝的。

celeriac
