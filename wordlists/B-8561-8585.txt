February
核心：二月：一年里最短的月份，闰年有二十九天。缩写 Feb.。
注意读音：中间那个 r 常被吞掉。
① February is short and still manages to feel longer than March.
= 二月很短，感觉却比三月还长。
in February = 在二月
② The garden does nothing in February and everything in the first week of April.
= 那个园子二月里毫无动静，四月头一周就全冒出来了。
in early February = 二月初

fecal
核心：（美式拼法）粪便的：英式写 faecal，用法完全一样。
① Fecal bacteria in the samples closed two wells and one campsite.
= 样本里的粪便细菌，让两口井和一处营地关闭。
fecal bacteria = 粪便细菌
② A fecal sample travels to the lab in a sealed container.
= 粪便样本装在密封容器里送去化验室。
a fecal sample = 粪便样本

feces
核心：（美式拼法）粪便：英式写 faeces，作复数。
① Bird feces on the statue take a specialist and a scaffold to remove.
= 雕像上的鸟粪，得请专人搭架子才清得掉。
feces = 粪便
② The report counts feces in the water and stops short of naming a source.
= 那份报告统计了水里的粪便含量，却没点出来源。
bird feces = 鸟粪

feckless
核心：（贬）不中用的、没担当的：说人做事没条理、靠不住。名词 fecklessness。
① A feckless landlord and a patient tenant kept that house standing.
= 一个不中用的房东加一个有耐心的租客，让那栋房子撑住了。
feckless = 不中用的
② He is feckless with money and meticulous with his record collection.
= 他花钱没个数，唱片收藏倒一丝不苟。
be feckless with money = 花钱没数

fecklessness
核心：（贬）不中用、没担当：做事没条理、靠不住的那种性情。不可数。
① The fecklessness of the agency cost the tenants a winter without heating.
= 那家中介的不中用，让租客过了一个没暖气的冬天。
fecklessness = 不中用、没担当
② Fecklessness in a young man was a joke and in a father is not.
= 一个年轻人不着调是笑话，做了父亲就不是了。
somebody's fecklessness = 某人的没担当

fecund
核心：（正式）多产的、肥沃的：说土地、说人，也说想象力。名词 fecundity。
① The soil is fecund and the drainage is the reason nobody ever farmed it.
= 那片土壤肥沃，没人耕它是因为排水不行。
fecund soil = 肥沃的土壤
② A fecund imagination is worth two filing cabinets to a company like that.
= 对那样一家公司来说，一副多产的想象力抵得上两个文件柜。
a fecund imagination = 丰饶的想象力

fecundity
核心：（正式）多产、繁殖力、肥沃。不可数。
① The fecundity of that pair of rabbits is a matter of record.
= 那对兔子的繁殖力是有案可查的。
fecundity = 繁殖力
② Fecundity of soil is measured in what it grows, not in how it looks.
= 土壤肥不肥，看它长出什么，不看它长什么样。
the fecundity of something = 某物的肥沃

fed
核心：两支，都跟 feed 的过去式无关。一是（美式，非正式）联邦探员；
二是大写 the Fed，指美国联邦储备系统。可数。
① Two feds in identical coats stood at the end of the corridor.
= 两名联邦探员穿着一模一样的大衣，站在走廊尽头。
a fed = 联邦探员（美式）
② The Fed raised rates and every mortgage in the country noticed.
= 美联储一加息，全国每一笔房贷都感觉到了。
the Fed = 美国联邦储备系统
③ He is fed up with the timetable and says so to anybody who sits down.
= 他受够了那份时刻表，谁一坐下他就说。
be fed up with something = 受够了某事
fed up = 厌烦透了
By March everybody was fed up and the rota changed in June.
= 到三月人人都厌烦透了，排班表六月才改。

federal
核心：联邦的：一是国家层面的，跟州、省相对：federal law；
二是由多个邦组成的：a federal system。副词 federally。
① Federal law beats state law and the argument goes on regardless.
= 联邦法律高于州法律，争论照样不停。
federal law = 联邦法律
the federal government = 联邦政府
The federal government paid for the bridge and the county named it.
= 那座桥是联邦政府出的钱，名字是郡里起的。
② A federal system leaves each state its own school year.
= 联邦制让每个州保有自己的学年安排。
a federal system = 联邦制
副词 federally：在联邦层面上，多说 federally funded、federally regulated。
③ The programme is federally funded and locally resented.
= 那个项目由联邦出钱，在地方上招人嫌。
federally funded = 联邦出资的
④ Federally regulated industries file the same form eleven times.
= 受联邦监管的行业，同一张表要报十一遍。
federally regulated = 受联邦监管的

federalism
核心：联邦制：把权力分给中央与各邦的那一套安排。不可数。
① Federalism looks tidy on a diagram and messy in a courtroom.
= 联邦制画成图表很整齐，进了法庭就乱。
federalism = 联邦制
② Arguments about federalism turn out to be arguments about who pays.
= 关于联邦制的争论，说到底是谁掏钱的争论。

federalist
核心：联邦主义者、主张联邦制的：名词与形容词同形。
① The federalist wing lost the vote and kept the party's newspaper.
= 主张联邦制的那一翼输了表决，倒保住了党报。
a federalist = 联邦主义者
② Federalist arguments carry further in a big country than in a small one.
= 联邦主义的说法，在大国比在小国传得远。
federalist = 联邦主义的

federate
核心：（正式）结成联邦、联合起来：多说国家、工会与协会。名词 federation。
① The three unions federated in 1948 and argued about the name until 1951.
= 那三家工会 1948 年联合起来，名字一直吵到 1951 年。
federate = 结成联邦
② Small colleges federate to share a library and one very good lawyer.
= 小学院联合起来，共用一个图书馆和一位很好的律师。
federate to do something = 联合起来做某事

federation
核心：两支。一是联邦（国家）：a federation of states；
二是（团体的）联合会：a federation of growers。可数。
① The federation has six members and one of them pays for everything.
= 那个联邦有六个成员，其中一个掏全部的钱。
a federation = 联邦
② The federation of growers sets the price and blames the weather.
= 种植者联合会定价，出了事怪天气。
a federation of something = 某某联合会

fedora
核心：软呢帽：帽顶有凹槽、帽檐柔软的那种毡帽。可数。
① The fedora came back into fashion and went out again within a season.
= 软呢帽又流行起来，一季不到又过气了。
a fedora = 软呢帽
② He wears a fedora indoors and nobody has yet told him not to.
= 他在室内也戴着软呢帽，还没人跟他说别戴。
wear a fedora = 戴软呢帽

fee
核心：费用：为专业服务或某项权利付的钱。可数 ——
a legal fee、an entrance fee、a flat fee（一口价）。
① The legal fee is charged by the hour and the meter starts at the handshake.
= 律师费按小时算，握手那一刻就开始计。
a legal fee = 律师费
an entrance fee = 入场费
The entrance fee doubled and the queue got longer all the same.
= 入场费涨了一倍，队伍照样排得更长。
② They charge a flat fee and keep the surprise in the postage.
= 他们收一口价，惊喜留在邮费里。
a flat fee = 一口价的费用

feeble
核心：两支。一是虚弱的、无力的：a feeble voice；
二是（借口、论证）站不住脚的：a feeble excuse。副词 feebly，名词 feebleness。
① His voice is feeble on the telephone and perfectly clear across a room.
= 他在电话里声音微弱，隔着一间屋倒清清楚楚。
a feeble voice = 微弱的声音
② A feeble excuse delivered confidently beats a good one mumbled.
= 一个站不住的借口说得笃定，胜过一个好借口含含糊糊。
a feeble excuse = 站不住脚的借口
副词 feebly：一是有气无力地；二是（辩解、抗议）无力地。
③ The lamp glowed feebly and went out the moment the door shut.
= 那盏灯有气无力地亮着，门一关就灭了。
glow feebly = 微弱地发亮
④ He protested feebly and then signed exactly where he was told.
= 他无力地抗议了一句，还是照人家指的地方签了字。
protest feebly = 无力地抗议
⑤ The letter calls the rule feeble-minded and gets the name of it wrong.
= 那封信说那条规矩蠢笨，却把它的名字写错了。
feeble-minded = 蠢笨的（旧时用来说智力障碍，如今冒犯，别用）

feebleness
核心：虚弱、无力：说身体，也说论证与借口的软弱。不可数。
① The feebleness of the light made everybody read standing up.
= 光线太弱，人人只好站着读。
the feebleness of something = 某物的微弱
② The feebleness of the defence was the strongest thing about that case.
= 辩方的无力，反倒是那桩案子里最有力的部分。
feebleness = 无力

feed
核心：四支。一是喂、给……吃：feed the cat；二是以……为食、靠……供给：
feed on something；三是名词，饲料：cattle feed；四是（数据）推送：a news feed。
过去式与过去分词都是 fed。
① Feed the cat before you unpack anything or you will not be allowed to.
= 先喂猫再拆行李，不然它不会让你拆。
feed the cat = 喂猫
② The stove feeds on wood that has been drying for two years.
= 那只炉子烧的是晾了两年的柴。
feed on something = 以某物为食
③ Cattle feed went up twice and the milk price stayed exactly where it was.
= 饲料涨了两回，牛奶价格纹丝不动。
cattle feed = 牛饲料
④ The news feed refreshes every ten seconds whether anything happened or not.
= 那个新闻推送每十秒刷新一次，不管有没有事发生。
a news feed = 新闻推送
⑤ His mother fed him up for a fortnight and sent him back visibly heavier.
= 他母亲给他补了两个星期身子，送他回去时明显重了。
feed somebody up = 给某人补身子
feed up = 把人养胖、补身子
Grandmothers exist to feed up anybody who arrives looking tired.
= 祖母的存在，就是为了把一切看着疲惫的人补起来。
⑥ The dog has been off her feed since the fireworks and the vet is unbothered.
= 自打放过烟花，那条狗就没胃口，兽医倒不当回事。
off your feed = 没胃口
⑦ They feed back what the survey says and leave out what it does not.
= 他们把调查说了的反馈回去，没说的就略掉。
feed back = 把意见反馈回去
⑧ Two streams feed into the reservoir and one of them dries up in August.
= 两条溪流汇入水库，其中一条八月就干了。
feed into something = 汇入某物
⑨ The rumour feeds off silence and dies the day somebody answers it.
= 那个流言靠沉默活着，有人回应的当天就死了。
feed off something = 靠某物滋长
⑩ Everything feeds through to the price about six months later.
= 什么变化都要过大约半年才传导到价格上。
feed through = 传导过来
⑪ Her feed reader collects nine hundred items a day and she reads four.
= 她的订阅阅读器一天收九百条，她读四条。
a feed reader = 订阅阅读器

feedback
核心：两支。一是反馈意见：give feedback、positive feedback；
二是（音响）啸叫，那种刺耳的尖啸。不可数。
① The feedback was positive, detailed and eleven months late.
= 那份反馈意见正面、细致，晚了十一个月。
give feedback = 给出反馈
positive feedback = 正面反馈
Positive feedback arrives by email and the other kind arrives in person.
= 正面反馈是发邮件来的，另一种是当面来的。
② Feedback from the speaker cleared the front two rows in a second.
= 音箱一啸叫，前两排的人一秒钟就躲开了。
feedback = 啸叫

feedbag
核心：（美式）饲料袋：套在马嘴上的那种，英式说 nosebag。可数。
① The horse gets a feedbag at noon and starts looking for it at eleven.
= 那匹马中午才有饲料袋，十一点就开始找。
a feedbag = 饲料袋
② Two feedbags hang by the door and only one of them is ever used.
= 门边挂着两个饲料袋，一直在用的只有一个。

feeder
核心：三支。一是喂食器：a bird feeder；二是进食的人或动物：a fussy feeder；
三是支线、支流：a feeder road、a feeder school。
① The bird feeder empties in a day and the squirrels take the credit.
= 那个喂鸟器一天就空，功劳归松鼠。
a bird feeder = 喂鸟器
② He was a fussy feeder at two and eats absolutely anything now.
= 他两岁时挑食，如今什么都吃。
a fussy feeder = 挑食的小孩
③ The feeder road joins the motorway exactly where nobody expects it.
= 那条支线公路并入高速的地方，谁都想不到。
a feeder road = 支线公路
a feeder school = 对口输送生源的小学
A feeder school sends thirty children a year and knows every one.
= 一所对口输送生源的小学，一年送去三十个孩子，个个都认得。

feeding
核心：喂养、进食：不可数；也作定语，feeding time、a feeding frenzy。
① Feeding time at that zoo draws a bigger crowd than the animals do.
= 那家动物园的喂食时间，招来的人比动物本身还多。
feeding time = 喂食时间
② The auction turned into a feeding frenzy in the last four minutes.
= 那场拍卖在最后四分钟变成了一场哄抢。
a feeding frenzy = 疯抢、哄抢
③ The feeding bottle goes into the steriliser and comes out warm.
= 奶瓶放进消毒器，出来时是温的。
a feeding bottle = 奶瓶

feedstuff
核心：（农业）饲料：喂牲畜的东西，多用复数 feedstuffs。
① Feedstuffs are tested twice a year and the results are posted in the barn.
= 饲料一年检测两次，结果贴在谷仓里。
feedstuffs = 饲料
② The price of feedstuff decides what a farm can keep through the winter.
= 饲料价格决定一座农场冬天养得住什么。
feedstuff = 饲料

feel
核心：多支。一是感觉到、觉得：feel cold、feel sick；二是摸、触：feel the cloth；
三是认为：feel that…；四是名词，手感与氛围：the feel of something。
习语：feel like 是想要、也是摸着像，feel free 是请随意，
feel your age 是觉出自己上了年纪，get the feel of 是把某物摸熟。
过去式与过去分词都是 felt。
① She felt sick on the ferry and perfectly fine ten minutes after it docked.
= 她在渡轮上想吐，靠岸十分钟后就好端端的了。
feel sick = 想吐
feel cold = 觉得冷
He feels cold in June and sleeps with the window open in January.
= 他六月还觉得冷，一月却开着窗睡。
② Feel the cloth before you so much as look at the price.
= 先摸摸料子，再去看价钱。
feel something = 摸某物
③ I feel that we have had this meeting before, and twice.
= 我觉得这个会我们开过了，还开过两回。
feel that = 觉得……
④ Do you feel like a walk, or does that sound too much like work?
= 你想去散个步吗，还是听着太像干活？
feel like something = 想要某物
feel like doing something = 想做某事
Nobody feels like cooking on the night the clocks go back.
= 调回冬令时的那个晚上，谁也不想做饭。
⑤ Feel free to open a window and to close it again.
= 想开窗尽管开，想关也随你。
feel free to do something = 请随意做某事
⑥ Two flights of stairs are enough to make him feel his age.
= 两层楼梯就够让他觉出自己上了年纪。
feel your age = 感到自己老了
⑦ The feel of the place changes the moment the lights come on.
= 那地方的氛围，灯一开就变了。
the feel of something = 某物的手感、氛围
get the feel of something = 把某物摸熟
Take a week to get the feel of the machine before anything matters.
= 趁还没要紧事，花一周把那台机器摸熟。
⑧ She feels for anybody who has to ring that number twice.
= 谁要是得给那个号码打两遍电话，她都同情。
feel for somebody = 同情某人
⑨ The ending is feel-good and the ninety minutes before it are not.
= 那个结局暖心，前面九十分钟可不是。
feel-good = 让人心情舒畅的
⑩ He does not feel up to the drive and will not say so to anybody.
= 他觉得撑不住那段车程，又不肯跟人说。
feel up to something = 觉得应付得了某事
⑪ Being felt up on a crowded train is neither a compliment nor a joke.
= 在挤满人的车厢里被人动手动脚，既不是恭维也不是玩笑。
feel somebody up = 对某人动手动脚（猥亵）
feel up = 咸猪手（俚，贬）
Two passengers reported being felt up and the operator finally moved the cameras.
= 有两名乘客报告遭人动手动脚，运营方这才把摄像头挪了位置。

feeler
核心：两支。一是（昆虫的）触角；二是试探：put out feelers 是放风探口风。
① The moth's feelers move before any other part of it does.

a feeler = 触角
② They put out feelers about a merger and got three polite refusals.
= 他们放风试探合并的事，收到三个客气的拒绝。
