occupant
/ˈɒkjʊpənt/
核心：两支，可数，正式。一是（房屋、房间的）住户、使用者；二是（车、座位里的）乘坐者。
名词 occupancy 是占用、居住，也指占用期、入住率，不可数，并入本条。
① The previous occupant of the flat left a piano that nobody can move.
= 那套公寓的前任住户留下一架谁也挪不动的钢琴。
the previous occupant = 前任住户
② All four occupants of the car escaped before it caught fire.
= 车里的四个人都在起火之前逃了出来。
the occupants of the car = 车里的人
③ The house will be ready for occupancy at the end of the month.
= 那座房子月底就可以入住。
ready for occupancy = 可以入住
④ Hotel occupancy on the island falls below twenty per cent in winter.
= 冬天，岛上旅馆的入住率跌到百分之二十以下。
hotel occupancy = 旅馆入住率

occupation
/ˌɒkjʊˈpeɪʃən/
核心：三支。一是职业：正式说法，多用于表格、公文，可数；二是（军队对别国的）占领，也指占领时期，不可数；
三是（对房屋、土地的）占用、居住，不可数；也指消遣、用来打发时间的事，可数。
形容词 occupational 是职业的、与工作有关的：an occupational hazard 是职业上免不了的风险；副词 occupationally。
occupational therapy 是职业治疗、作业疗法，做这种治疗的人是 occupational therapist。并入本条。
① Please state your name, address and occupation at the top of the form.
= 请在表格顶端写明姓名、地址和职业。
occupation = 职业
state your occupation = 写明职业
② The town was under foreign occupation for four years.
= 那座城被外国占领了四年。
occupation = 占领
under occupation = 被占领
③ The new flats will be ready for occupation in the spring.
= 新公寓春天就可以入住。
ready for occupation = 可以入住
④ Gardening is her favourite occupation now that she has retired.
= 她退休了，如今最喜欢的消遣是侍弄花园。
a favourite occupation = 最喜欢的消遣
⑤ Sore knees are an occupational hazard for anyone who lays carpets.
= 膝盖疼是铺地毯的人免不了的职业风险。
an occupational hazard = 职业风险
occupational = 职业的
⑥ The disease is occupationally acquired and is seen mainly in miners.
= 那种病是因职业而得的，主要见于矿工。
occupationally = 因职业
⑦ After the stroke he had six months of occupational therapy to learn to dress himself.
= 中风之后，他做了六个月的职业治疗，重新学会自己穿衣。
occupational therapy = 职业治疗
⑧ The occupational therapist showed her how to hold a pen again.
= 职业治疗师教她重新握笔。
an occupational therapist = 职业治疗师

occupy
/ˈɒkjʊpaɪ/
核心：五支。一是占用、占据（空间、时间）；二是居住在、使用（房屋）；三是（军队）占领，（抗议者）占据；
四是使忙碌、使无暇他顾：occupy yourself with、be occupied with；occupy somebody's mind 是占据某人的心思；五是担任（职位）。
形容词 occupied 一是有人使用的（厕所、座位），二是被占领的，三是忙着的；
名词 occupier 是（英式）房屋的占用人、住户，也指占领者。并入本条。
① The bed occupies most of the room, and the door hardly opens.
= 那张床占了大半间屋，门都快打不开了。
occupy = 占用
occupy most of the room = 占了大半间屋
② The family has occupied the same farmhouse for five generations.
= 那家人在同一座农舍里住了五代。
occupy a house = 住在一座房子里
③ Enemy troops occupied the capital within a week.
= 敌军不到一个星期就占领了首都。
occupy the capital = 占领首都
④ Students occupied the main hall for three days in protest at the fees.
= 学生们占据礼堂三天，抗议学费。
occupy a building = 占据一栋楼
⑤ She occupied herself with a crossword while she waited for the results.
= 等结果的时候，她做填字游戏打发时间。
occupy yourself with something = 忙着做某事
⑥ He is fully occupied with the accounts until the end of the month.
= 月底之前，他都在全力忙账目。
be occupied with = 忙于
⑦ The problem of the leaking roof occupied her mind all night.
= 屋顶漏水的问题在她脑子里转了一整夜。
occupy somebody's mind = 占据某人的心思
⑧ He has occupied the post of treasurer since the club was founded.
= 自俱乐部成立起，他就担任司库一职。
occupy a post = 担任职位
⑨ The sign on the toilet door said that it was occupied.
= 厕所门上的牌子显示里面有人。
occupied = 有人使用的
⑩ Life in the occupied zone grew harder with every month.
= 占领区里的日子一个月比一个月难熬。
the occupied zone = 占领区
⑪ The letter was addressed simply to the occupier.
= 那封信的收信人只写了「住户」两个字。
the occupier = 住户、占用人

occur
/əˈkɜː(r)/
核心：三支，偏正式。一是发生：多说事故、变化、没有计划的事；二是出现、存在：见于某处、某类事物之中，介词用 in、among；
三是（想法）浮现在脑子里，后接 to：it occurs to somebody that…、it never occurred to me to…。
过去式 occurred，现在分词 occurring，r 都要双写。不用被动。
名词 occurrence 指发生的事，也指发生的频率，并入本条。
① The accident occurred at about half past eight on a wet road.
= 事故发生在八点半左右，当时路面是湿的。
occur = 发生
② Errors of this kind occur most often at the end of a long shift.
= 这类差错最常发生在长班快结束的时候。
errors occur = 出现差错
③ The mineral occurs naturally in the rocks along the coast.
= 那种矿物天然存在于沿海的岩石里。
occur naturally = 天然存在
④ The disease occurs mainly among children under five.
= 那种病主要见于五岁以下的儿童。
occur among somebody = 见于某类人当中
⑤ It occurred to me that we had left the back door unlocked.
= 我突然想到，我们没锁后门。
occur to = 浮现在…脑子里
it occurs to somebody that = 某人想到
⑥ It never occurred to her to ask how much the room would cost.
= 她压根没想到要问问那个房间多少钱。
it never occurred to somebody to do something = 某人压根没想到要做某事
⑦ Power cuts are a daily occurrence in that part of the city.
= 在城里那一带，停电是天天有的事。
a daily occurrence = 天天有的事
⑧ The study looks at the occurrence of asthma near busy roads.
= 那项研究考察的是繁忙道路附近哮喘的发病情况。
the occurrence of something = 某事的发生率

ocean
/ˈəʊʃən/
核心：两支。一是大洋：地球上覆盖大部分表面的那片咸水，the ocean；也指其中某一个：the Pacific Ocean。
美式里日常说「海」也用这个词，英式说 the sea。跟 sea 的分别：这个词大得多，sea 多指靠近陆地、范围较小的海。
二是 oceans of，（口语）大量的。
习语 a drop in the ocean 是沧海一粟、杯水车薪。
① The Pacific Ocean covers almost a third of the surface of the planet.
= 太平洋几乎占了地球表面的三分之一。
ocean = 大洋
the Pacific Ocean = 太平洋
② Strange fish live at the bottom of the ocean, where no light reaches.
= 在光照不到的洋底，生活着奇形怪状的鱼。
the bottom of the ocean = 洋底
③ The children ran down the beach and jumped into the ocean.
= 孩子们跑下沙滩，跳进了海里。
jump into the ocean = 跳进海里
美式说法，英式说 the sea。
④ There is no hurry, because we have oceans of time before the train.
= 不用急，离开车还有大把的时间。
oceans of time = 大把的时间
⑤ The money raised so far is a drop in the ocean compared with what is needed.
= 到现在筹到的钱，跟所需要的相比只是杯水车薪。
a drop in the ocean = 杯水车薪
⑥ The harbour is too shallow for ocean-going ships.
= 那座港口太浅，远洋轮船进不来。
ocean-going = 远洋的
⑦ The deepest ocean trench lies almost eleven kilometres below the surface.
= 最深的海沟在海面以下将近十一公里。
an ocean trench = 海沟

oceanarium

an oceanarium = 海洋馆

a trip to the oceanarium = 去海洋馆的出游

oceanfront
/ˈəʊʃənfrʌnt/

① They rented an oceanfront apartment with a balcony over the beach.

on the oceanfront = 在海边

Oceanian
/ˌəʊsiˈɑːniən/
核心：两支，首字母大写。一是形容词，大洋洲的：指澳大利亚、新西兰与太平洋诸岛；二是名词，大洋洲人，可数。
① The museum has a gallery of Oceanian art, with canoes and carved masks.

oceanic
/ˌəʊʃiˈænɪk/
核心：海洋的、大洋的：生活在大洋里的、与大洋有关的。形容词，多作定语，技术用语。气候上指海洋性的：温差小、湿润。
① The albatross is an oceanic bird that comes ashore only to breed.
= 信天翁是一种大洋鸟类，只在繁殖时才上岸。
an oceanic bird = 大洋鸟类
② The west coast has an oceanic climate, with mild winters and cool summers.
= 西海岸是海洋性气候，冬天温和，夏天凉爽。
an oceanic climate = 海洋性气候

= 那位海洋学家把一台摄像机放到四公里深的海底。
an oceanographer = 海洋学家

octane

octave

odd
/ɒd/
核心：形容词五支。一是奇怪的、反常的：it is odd that…；二是（数）奇数的，跟 even 相对；

五是放在整数后面，表示「…多」：thirty-odd years。
习语 the odd one out 是与众不同的那一个、多出来的那一个。
副词 oddly 是古怪地，oddly enough 是说来也怪；名词 oddness。
① It is odd that nobody has answered the door, because the lights are on.
= 灯亮着却没人应门，真奇怪。
odd = 奇怪的
it is odd that… = …真奇怪
② There was something odd about the way he avoided the question.
= 他回避那个问题的样子有点不对劲。
something odd about something = 某事有点不对劲
③ The houses with odd numbers are on the left side of the street.

odd = 奇数的
odd numbers = 奇数
④ The drawer was full of odd socks and a single glove.

an odd sock = 不成对的袜子
⑤ He does not smoke, but he enjoys the odd cigar at Christmas.
= 他不抽烟，不过圣诞节时会偶尔抽支雪茄。
the odd cigar = 偶尔的一支雪茄
⑥ He earns a little by doing odd jobs for the neighbours.
= 他给邻居们干点零活，挣一点钱。
odd jobs = 零活
⑦ They have lived in the same house for thirty-odd years.

thirty-odd years = 三十多年
⑧ Which of these four shapes is the odd one out?

⑨ She was the only adult at the table and felt the odd one out.

feel the odd one out = 觉得自己格格不入
⑩ He has been behaving oddly ever since the letter arrived.

oddly = 古怪地
⑪ Oddly enough, the cheapest seats had the best view.

oddball
/ˈɒdbɔːl/
核心：两支，口语。一是名词，怪人、举止古怪的人，可数；二是形容词，古怪的，只作定语。口气多半带着好感。
① Her uncle is a harmless oddball who collects street signs.
= 她叔叔是个没有恶意的怪人，爱收集路牌。
an oddball = 怪人
② The film is an oddball comedy about a postman who cannot read.
= 那部片子是一出古怪的喜剧，讲一个不识字的邮递员。
an oddball comedy = 古怪的喜剧

oddity
/ˈɒdɪti/
核心：两支。一是怪人、怪事、怪东西，可数，复数 oddities；二是古怪、反常这种性质，不可数。
① The three-wheeled car is an oddity that draws a crowd wherever it stops.
= 那辆三轮汽车是个稀罕物，停在哪儿都围上一群人。
an oddity = 稀罕物、怪东西
② One of the oddities of English is that "cleave" has two opposite meanings.
= 英语的怪事之一，是有一个动词兼有两个相反的意思。
one of the oddities of something = 某事物的怪处之一
③ She was struck by the oddity of eating breakfast in the dark.
= 摸着黑吃早饭，她觉得很怪。
the oddity of something = 某事的古怪
