insult
/ɪnˈsʌlt/（动）/ˈɪnsʌlt/（名）
核心：侮辱、辱骂 —— 动词重音在后，名词重音在前。
动词：说或做冒犯人的事；名词：一句冒犯的话、一个冒犯的举动（shout insults、an insult to）。
习语 add insult to injury（雪上加霜）已在 injury 条讲；形容词 insulting 另立。
① He insulted the referee and was sent off.
= 他辱骂裁判，被罚下场。
insult somebody = 侮辱某人
② The fans shouted insults at the players as they left the pitch.
= 球员们离场时，球迷冲他们大声辱骂。
shout insults = 大声辱骂
③ The offer of such a small pay rise was an insult to the nurses.
= 只提供那么一点点加薪，是对护士们的侮辱。
an insult to somebody = 对某人的侮辱
④ I hope you won't take it as an insult if I suggest some changes.
= 我提几点修改意见，希望你别觉得受了冒犯。
take something as an insult = 把…看作侮辱

insulting
/ɪnˈsʌltɪŋ/
核心：侮辱性的、无礼的 —— 冒犯人的言行。常接 to。
① His remarks about women were deeply insulting.
= 他对女性的评论极具侮辱性。
deeply insulting = 极具侮辱性的
② The low price was insulting to the farmers who had worked all year.
= 那么低的价格对辛苦了一整年的农民是一种侮辱。
be insulting to somebody = 对某人是侮辱

insuperable
/ɪnˈsuːpərəbəl/
核心：（正式）无法克服的 —— 说困难、障碍大到解决不了。跟 insurmountable 同义。
① The cost of the project proved an insuperable obstacle.
= 项目的费用成了一个无法克服的障碍。
an insuperable obstacle = 无法克服的障碍
② The difficulties of building a bridge there seemed insuperable to the engineers of the time.
= 在那里架桥的困难，在当时的工程师看来似乎无法克服。
insuperable difficulties = 无法克服的困难

insupportable
/ˌɪnsəˈpɔːtəbəl/
核心：（正式）无法忍受的；站不住脚的。两支：痛苦、处境让人受不了；说法、观点无法支持。
① The pain became insupportable, and she called the doctor at midnight.
= 疼痛变得难以忍受，她半夜给医生打了电话。
insupportable pain = 难以忍受的疼痛
② The minister's claim that prices had fallen was simply insupportable.
= 部长声称物价已经下降，这种说法根本站不住脚。
an insupportable claim = 站不住脚的说法

insurance
/ɪnˈʃɔːrəns/
核心：保险；保险费；保险业；（比喻）防范措施。几支：为防损失而买的保障（car insurance、take out insurance）；
为此付的钱；保险这一行；为以防万一而做的准备（as insurance against）。
① You can't drive the car until you have arranged insurance.
= 你办好保险之前不能开这辆车。
arrange insurance = 办理保险
② They took out travel insurance before their trip to Mexico.
= 去墨西哥之前，他们买了旅行保险。
take out insurance = 买保险
③ She works in insurance, dealing with claims after floods.
= 她在保险业工作，处理洪灾之后的理赔。
work in insurance = 在保险业工作
④ He kept some cash at home as insurance against a bank failure.
= 他在家里放了些现金，以防银行倒闭。
as insurance against something = 以防…
比喻用法：以防万一的准备。
⑤ Check your insurance policy to see if it covers damage from storms.
= 查查你的保单，看看是否涵盖风暴造成的损失。
an insurance policy = 保单
⑥ An insurance adjuster came to look at the damage to the roof.
= 一位理赔员来查看了屋顶的损坏情况。
an insurance adjuster = （美）理赔员
英式说 loss adjuster。

insure
/ɪnˈʃɔː(r)/
核心：给…保险、投保；（美）确保。两支：为东西或生命买保险（insure something against something）；
美式里也写作「确保」，同 ensure。形容词 insured（已投保的）与保险公司 insurer 并入本条。
① The house is insured against fire and flood.
= 那座房子投保了火灾险和水灾险。
insure something against something = 为…投保…险
② The painting was insured for two million pounds.
= 那幅画投保了两百万英镑。
insure something for an amount = 为…投保若干金额
③ The insured driver must report any accident within seven days.
= 投保的司机必须在七天内报告任何事故。
the insured = 被保险人
④ The insurer refused to pay because the car had been left unlocked.
= 保险公司拒绝赔付，因为车没锁。
an insurer = 保险公司
⑤ Please insure that all windows are closed before you leave.
= 离开前请确保所有窗户都关好了。
insure that = （美）确保
英式在这一支只用 ensure。

insurgency
/ɪnˈsɜːdʒənsi/
核心：叛乱、暴动 —— 反政府武装在一个地区进行的长期武装斗争。
① The army has been fighting an insurgency in the north for ten years.
= 军队在北部与叛乱武装作战已有十年。
an insurgency = 叛乱
② The government's new policy aims to end the insurgency through talks.
= 政府的新政策旨在通过谈判结束叛乱。
end an insurgency = 结束叛乱

insurgent
/ɪnˈsɜːdʒənt/
核心：叛乱者、起义者 —— 武装反抗政府的人。也作形容词：叛乱的。
① Insurgents attacked a police station on the edge of the city.
= 叛乱分子袭击了城市边缘的一个警察局。
insurgents = 叛乱分子
② The insurgent forces took control of three towns.
= 叛军控制了三个城镇。
insurgent forces = 叛军

insurmountable
/ˌɪnsəˈmaʊntəbəl/
核心：无法克服的、不可逾越的 —— 障碍、问题太大，没法解决。
① Lack of money is not an insurmountable problem; we can apply for a grant.
= 缺钱不是解决不了的问题，我们可以申请补助。
an insurmountable problem = 无法解决的问题
② The team faced an insurmountable lead after the first half.
= 上半场结束时，对方的领先优势已经无法追赶。
an insurmountable lead = 无法追赶的领先优势

insurrection
/ˌɪnsəˈrekʃən/
核心：起义、暴动 —— 民众武装反抗政府。形容词 insurrectionary（起义的）并入本条。
① Within a week the army had crushed the insurrection and arrested its leaders.
= 不到一周，军队就镇压了那场起义，逮捕了起义领袖。
crush an insurrection = 镇压起义
② The police arrested twelve members of an insurrectionary group hiding in the hills.
= 警方逮捕了藏在山里的一个起义组织的十二名成员。
an insurrectionary group = 起义组织

intact
/ɪnˈtækt/
核心：完整无损的、未受损伤的 —— 经历了事故、灾难或时间后依然完好。只作表语或后置。
① Most of the town was destroyed, but the old church survived the earthquake intact.
= 小镇大部分被毁，但那座古老的教堂在地震中完好无损。
survive intact = 完好无损
② Despite losing the election badly, his reputation for honesty remained intact.
= 尽管选举惨败，他诚实的名声丝毫未损。
remain intact = 完好无损

intaglio
/ɪnˈtɑːliəʊ/
核心：凹雕、阴刻；凹版印刷 —— 在石头、金属上刻出凹下去的图案；也指用这种方法印的画。
① The ring has an intaglio of a lion's head cut into the stone.
= 那枚戒指的宝石上刻着一个凹雕的狮头。
an intaglio = 凹雕
② Banknotes are often printed by intaglio to make them hard to copy.
= 钞票常用凹版印刷，使它们难以仿造。
print by intaglio = 用凹版印刷

intake
/ˈɪnteɪk/
核心：摄入量；吸收（新人）的数量；进口。几支：吃进喝进的量（alcohol intake）；
一批新招的学生或员工（this year's intake）；水、空气的入口（an air intake）；an intake of breath 是倒吸一口气。
① Doctors advised him to reduce his intake of salt.
= 医生建议他减少盐的摄入量。
somebody's intake of something = 某人…的摄入量
② The school's intake has doubled since it moved to the new building.
= 学校搬进新楼后，招生人数翻了一番。
the intake = 招收人数
③ There was a sharp intake of breath when the price was announced.
= 价格一宣布，大家都倒吸了一口气。
an intake of breath = 倒吸一口气
④ Leaves had blocked the engine's air intake.
= 树叶堵住了发动机的进气口。
an air intake = 进气口

intangible
/ɪnˈtændʒəbəl/
核心：无形的、难以捉摸的 —— 摸不到、说不清，却确实存在的；商业上指无形资产。也作名词复数 intangibles。
① There is an intangible sense of peace in the old monastery.
= 那座古老的修道院里有一种难以言喻的宁静感。
an intangible sense of something = 一种说不清的…
这一义多用来描写气氛、感受，带点书面色彩，口语里更常直接说「说不清」。
② A company's reputation is one of its most important intangible assets.
= 公司的声誉是它最重要的无形资产之一。
intangible assets = 无形资产
③ When choosing a job, consider the intangibles, such as how friendly the people are.
= 选择工作时要考虑一些无形的因素，比如同事友不友好。
intangibles = 无形因素

intarsia
/ɪnˈtɑːsiə/
核心：（毛衣编织的）嵌花；（木工的）镶嵌细工 —— 用不同颜色的毛线或木片拼出图案的技法。
① She knitted a jumper with an intarsia pattern of red and white stars.
= 她织了一件带红白星星嵌花图案的毛衣。
an intarsia pattern = 嵌花图案
② The old cabinet is decorated with fine intarsia in several kinds of wood.
= 那个旧柜子上装饰着用几种木料做的精美镶嵌细工。
intarsia = 镶嵌细工

integer
/ˈɪntɪdʒə(r)/
核心：（数学）整数 —— 不带小数或分数的数，包括正数、负数和零。
① In this part of the test the answer must be an integer, so fractions will be marked wrong.
= 这部分考试的答案必须是整数，填分数会被判错。
an integer = 整数
② The booking form only accepts positive integers in the box for the number of guests.
= 预订表格上填客人数目的那一栏只接受正整数。
a positive integer = 正整数

integral
/ˈɪntɪɡrəl/
核心：构成整体所必需的、不可或缺的；（数学）积分。形容词：是整体的一部分、离不开（be integral to）；
数学上的名词：积分。副词 integrally 并入本条。
① At the village school, music and drama are an integral part of the teaching.
= 在那所乡村学校，音乐和戏剧是教学中不可或缺的一部分。
an integral part of something = …不可或缺的部分
② With no factories and little farming, tourism is integral to the island's economy.
= 岛上没有工厂，农业也很少，旅游业对经济至关重要。
be integral to something = 对…不可或缺
③ Students learn to calculate the integral of simple functions.
= 学生们学习计算简单函数的积分。
an integral = 积分
④ The farm and the cheese shop are integrally linked, since all the milk comes from the same cows.
= 农场和奶酪店紧密相连，因为所有牛奶都出自同一群牛。
integrally linked = 紧密相连

integrate
/ˈɪntɪɡreɪt/
核心：（使）融入、结合；（使）一体化。两支：把几部分合成一个整体（integrate A with/into B）；
让人融入一个群体或社会（integrate into a community）。形容词 integrated（综合的、一体化的）并入本条，
integrated circuit（集成电路）也并在这里。
① The new system integrates voice, video and data.
= 新系统集语音、视频和数据于一体。
integrate something = 把…整合
② Many immigrants find it hard to integrate into their new community.
= 许多移民觉得难以融入新的社区。
integrate into something = 融入…
③ The city has an integrated transport system of buses, trams and trains.
= 那座城市有一个公交、电车和火车一体化的交通系统。
an integrated system = 一体化系统
④ The invention of the integrated circuit made small computers possible.
= 集成电路的发明使小型电脑成为可能。
an integrated circuit = 集成电路

integration
/ˌɪntɪˈɡreɪʃən/
核心：结合、整合；融入、融合；（种族）一体化、取消隔离。
① The integration of the two companies took more than a year.
= 两家公司的整合用了一年多时间。
the integration of something = …的整合
② The government wants to encourage the integration of refugees into society.
= 政府希望促进难民融入社会。
integration into society = 融入社会
③ The civil rights movement fought for racial integration in schools.
= 民权运动为学校中的种族融合而斗争。
racial integration = 种族融合

integrity
/ɪnˈteɡrɪti/
核心：正直、诚实；完整、完好。两支：人的品格坚持原则、不受收买（a man of integrity）；
事物保持完整、不被破坏（territorial integrity）。
① She is a woman of great integrity who would never take a bribe.
= 她是一个非常正直的人，绝不会收受贿赂。
a person of integrity = 正直的人
② The scandal damaged the integrity of the whole election.
= 那起丑闻损害了整个选举的公正性。
the integrity of something = …的公正、完整
③ Engineers checked the structural integrity of the bridge after the earthquake.
= 地震之后，工程师检查了桥梁结构是否完好。
structural integrity = 结构完整性

intellect
/ˈɪntəlekt/
核心：智力、理解力；才智出众的人。不可数时指思考、理解的能力；可数时指聪明人。
① Even her critics admitted that the old judge was a woman of formidable intellect.
= 连批评她的人都承认，那位老法官是个才智过人的女性。
formidable intellect = 过人的才智
② Einstein is often described as one of the great intellects of the twentieth century.
= 爱因斯坦常被称为二十世纪最杰出的才智之士之一。
an intellect = 才智出众的人

intellectual
/ˌɪntəˈlektʃuəl/
核心：智力的、理智的；知识分子。形容词：跟思考、知识有关的（intellectual development）；
（人）有学问、爱思考的。名词：知识分子。副词 intellectually、名词 intellectualism（唯理智论；重理智）
与动词 intellectualize（从理智上分析）并入本条。
① Reading to children helps their intellectual development.
= 给孩子读书有助于他们的智力发展。
intellectual development = 智力发展
② He is a leading intellectual and writes regularly for the newspapers.
= 他是一位知名知识分子，经常给报纸写文章。
an intellectual = 知识分子
③ The film is intellectually challenging but not much fun.
= 那部电影在智力上很有挑战，但不太好看。
intellectually = 在智力上
④ Several critics attacked the novel's cold intellectualism and lack of real feeling.
= 几位评论家抨击那部小说冷冰冰的唯理智倾向，缺乏真情实感。
intellectualism = 唯理智论、重理智
⑤ He tends to intellectualize his feelings rather than express them.
= 他倾向于从理智上分析自己的感情，而不是表达出来。
intellectualize something = 从理智上分析…
⑥ The company took legal action to protect its intellectual property.
= 那家公司采取法律行动保护自己的知识产权。
intellectual property = 知识产权

intelligence
/ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/
核心：智力、智慧；情报；情报机构。两支：理解、学习、思考的能力（human intelligence、artificial intelligence）；
（尤指军事、政治上的）秘密信息，以及搜集这些信息的机构（military intelligence）。
① Children of average intelligence can easily learn to play chess.
= 智力一般的孩子也能轻松学会下国际象棋。
average intelligence = 一般的智力
② Some people worry that artificial intelligence will replace millions of jobs.
= 有些人担心人工智能会取代数以百万计的工作岗位。
artificial intelligence = 人工智能
③ Intelligence reports suggested that an attack on the capital was likely.
= 情报报告显示，首都很可能遭到袭击。
intelligence reports = 情报报告
④ He spent thirty years working for military intelligence and never talked about it.
= 他在军事情报部门工作了三十年，从来不谈这件事。
military intelligence = 军事情报部门
⑤ An intelligence quotient of around one hundred is considered average.
= 智商在一百左右被认为是一般水平。
intelligence quotient = 智商
⑥ All applicants for the police training course had to take an intelligence test first.
= 所有报考警察培训课程的人都得先做一次智力测验。
an intelligence test = 智力测验

intelligent
/ɪnˈtelɪdʒənt/
核心：聪明的、有才智的；（机器）智能的。说人理解快、会思考；也说机器、系统能自行判断。
副词 intelligently 并入本条。跟 clever 的分别：intelligent 偏思维能力；clever 更口语，也可指机灵、耍小聪明。
① She is a highly intelligent student who reads everything she can find.
= 她是个非常聪明的学生，能找到的书全都读。
highly intelligent = 非常聪明的
② Dolphins are among the most intelligent animals in the sea.
= 海豚是海洋中最聪明的动物之一。
intelligent animals = 聪明的动物
③ The new intelligent heating system learns when you are usually at home.
= 新的智能供暖系统会记住你通常什么时候在家。
an intelligent system = 智能系统
④ He spoke intelligently about the problem and offered practical solutions.
= 他对这个问题谈得很有见地，还提出了切实可行的办法。
intelligently = 聪明地、有见地地
⑤ Supporters of intelligent design argue that living things are too complex to have evolved by chance.
= 「智能设计论」的支持者认为，生物复杂得不可能是偶然进化来的。
intelligent design = 智能设计论
一种主张生命由某种智能设计者创造的观点，科学界普遍不接受。

intelligentsia
/ɪnˌtelɪˈdʒentsiə/
核心：知识界、知识阶层 —— 一个社会里的知识分子群体（作家、学者、艺术家），集合名词，前加 the。
① The country's intelligentsia met in the cafés of the capital to discuss politics.
= 那个国家的知识界在首都的咖啡馆里聚会，讨论政治。
the intelligentsia = 知识界
② The new government arrested many members of the intelligentsia.
= 新政府逮捕了许多知识界人士。
members of the intelligentsia = 知识界人士
