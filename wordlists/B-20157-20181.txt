scratchy
/ˈskrætʃi/
核心：形容词：（布料）扎人的、粗糙刺痒的；（声音、录音）沙沙作响的、刺耳的；（嗓子）发痒的。比较级 scratchier。
① I can't wear this wool jumper because it feels so scratchy against my skin.
= 我穿不了这件羊毛衫，贴着皮肤太扎人了。
scratchy = 扎人的
② We listened to a scratchy old record of my grandfather singing in 1950.
= 我们听了一张沙沙作响的老唱片，是我祖父一九五〇年唱的歌。
a scratchy record = 沙沙作响的唱片
③ My throat feels scratchy, so I think I am getting a cold.
= 我嗓子发痒，我想是要感冒了。
a scratchy throat = 嗓子发痒

/skrɔːl/
核心：动词：潦草地写、乱涂：scrawl something on something。名词，可数或单数：潦草的字迹。

= 有人在公交候车亭的墙上乱涂了些下流话。
scrawl something on something = 在某处乱写某物
scrawl = 潦草地写
② I could not read the doctor's scrawl on the prescription, so I asked the pharmacist.
= 我认不出处方上医生潦草的字，就去问药剂师。
somebody's scrawl = 某人潦草的字迹

scream
/skriːm/
核心：动词：尖叫、惊叫：scream with fear / pain / laughter；大声喊叫：scream at somebody；（风、机器）发出尖啸声。名词：尖叫声（可数）；（口语，单数）非常好笑的人或事：be a scream。scream out 是尖声喊出。副词 screamingly 是极其（好笑等）（另立条）。
① My sister screamed when she saw a huge black spider in the bath this morning.

③ Please don't scream at me like that; I am only trying to help you with the move.
= 请别那样冲我嚷嚷，我只是想帮你搬家。

④ We heard a scream from the house next door and called the police.

⑥ "Help me, please!" the little girl screamed out from the bottom of the old well.

scream out = 尖声喊出

scrimmage
/ˈskrɪmɪdʒ/
核心：名词，可数：混战、扭打；（美式橄榄球）争球；（北美）（训练中的）队内练习赛。动词：混战、争抢。
① There was a scrimmage at the door as everyone tried to get out of the hot room at the same time.
= 大家同时想从闷热的房间里出去，门口挤成一团。
a scrimmage = 混战、扭打
② The coach divided the players into two teams for a scrimmage at the end of practice.
= 训练结束时，教练把球员分成两队打一场队内练习赛。
a scrimmage = 队内练习赛

scrimp
/skrɪmp/
核心：动词，不及物：节省、省吃俭用：scrimp and save 是省吃俭用、精打细算。
① My parents scrimped and saved for years so that we could go to a good university.
= 我父母省吃俭用了好多年，好让我们能上一所好大学。
scrimp and save = 省吃俭用
scrimp = 节省
② Don't scrimp on the shoes; cheap ones will hurt your feet on a long walk.
= 鞋子上别省钱，便宜的鞋走远路会磨脚。
scrimp on something = 在某物上省钱
