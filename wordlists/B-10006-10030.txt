general
核心：五支。一是总体的、普遍的：a general rule；二是笼统的、不具体的；
三是总的、全面的：general knowledge；四是（军衔）将军；
五是习语 in general，一般来说。副词 generally 并入本条。
① As a general rule they answer letters and never answer the phone.
= 一般而言，他们回信，从不接电话。
as a general rule = 按一般规矩
general knowledge = 常识
The general knowledge round is where every quiz team falls apart.
= 常识那一轮，是每支答题队伍散架的地方。
② The answer was general enough to fit any of the four questions.
= 那个回答笼统得四个问题都套得上。
be general = 笼统
③ A general election was called in October and held in one day.
= 十月宣布大选，一天之内投完。
a general election = 大选
④ The general arrived by helicopter and left before the speeches.
= 将军坐直升机来，讲话还没开始就走了。
a general = 将军
⑤ In general the trains run and in that weather they do not.
= 一般来说火车是通的，那种天气就不通。
in general = 一般来说
副词 generally：通常、大体上。
⑥ It generally rains on that day and generally nobody plans for it.
= 那一天通常下雨，也通常没人为此做准备。
generally = 通常

generalissimo
核心：三支。一是大元帅，统领全军的最高统帅；
二是（历史）某些政权里那个头衔；三是（引申，贬）说一不二的当权者。
① A generalissimo commands every branch and answers to one person.
= 大元帅统辖各军种，只对一个人负责。
a generalissimo = 大元帅
② The title of generalissimo was created for him and died with him.
= 大元帅这个头衔是为他设的，也随他消失。
the title of generalissimo = 大元帅头衔
③ The department has a generalissimo and nine people who agree with him.
= 那个部门有个说一不二的头儿，外加九个附和的人。
a generalissimo = 说一不二的当权者

generalist
核心：三支。一是通才、什么都懂一点的人；二是（医学）全科医生；
三是形容词性，通才路线的。
① A generalist in that team knows enough to ask the right specialist.
= 队里那位通才懂得够多，知道该去问哪个专家。
a generalist = 通才
② The generalist sees the patient first and decides where they go next.
= 全科医生先看，再决定病人往哪儿转。
a generalist = 全科医生
③ A generalist training takes longer and closes fewer doors.
= 通才式的培养耗时更长，关上的门更少。
a generalist training = 通才式培养

generality
核心：三支。一是笼统的说法、泛泛之论：speak in generalities；
二是（正式）大多数、大部分：the generality of something；三是普遍性。
① He speaks in generalities and never names a single figure.
= 他净说空泛的话，一个数字都不提。
speak in generalities = 泛泛而谈
② The generality of people in that survey had never used the service.
= 那次调查里的大多数人，从没用过那项服务。
the generality of people = 大多数人
③ The generality of the rule is what makes it useless in practice.
= 那条规则太普泛，用起来才没用。
the generality of a rule = 规则的普泛

generalization
核心：三支。一是概括、归纳：a broad generalization；
二是（贬）以偏概全的说法；三是（学术）把结论推广到更大范围。
① A broad generalization at the start of that book undoes the rest of it.
= 那本书开头一个大而化之的概括，把后面全毁了。
a broad generalization = 大而化之的概括
② It is a generalization and the four exceptions are in the footnotes.
= 那是个以偏概全的说法，四个例外都在脚注里。
be a generalization = 以偏概全
③ The generalization from nine cases to a whole country failed twice.
= 从九个案例推及全国，这种推广失败过两次。
generalization from something = 由某物作出的推广

generalize
核心：三支。一是概括、归纳：generalize from something；
二是（贬）以偏概全；三是（正式）把结论推广开。也拼作 generalise。
① You cannot generalize from four people who all live on the same street.
= 四个住同一条街的人，概括不出什么来。
generalize from something = 由某事归纳
② She generalizes about drivers and drives worse than any of them.
= 她对司机一概而论，自己开得比谁都差。
generalize about something = 对某事一概而论
③ The result generalizes to any surface with the same property.
= 那个结论可以推广到任何有同样性质的曲面。
generalize to something = 推广到某范围

generalized
核心：三支。一是普遍的、全身性的：generalized pain；
二是笼统的、不具体的：a generalized statement；三是（数学）广义的。
① Generalized pain is harder to place than pain in one spot.
= 全身性的疼痛，比一处疼更难定位。
generalized pain = 全身性疼痛
② A generalized statement about the whole trade got him four letters.
= 一句对整个行当的笼统断言，给他招来四封信。
a generalized statement = 笼统的断言
③ The generalized form covers the earlier result as one special case.
= 广义的形式把先前的结论当成一个特例包含进去。
the generalized form = 广义形式

generalship
核心：三支。一是将才、统帅之能：fine generalship；
二是（引申）调度指挥的本事；三是将军这一职位与任期。
① Fine generalship saved an army that had already lost the ground.
= 出色的统帅之能，救下了一支已经失地的军队。
fine generalship = 出色的统帅之能
② The campaign was won by generalship and not by numbers.
= 那场战役靠的是调度指挥，不是人数。
generalship = 指挥才能
③ His generalship lasted four years and two of them are in every textbook.
= 他任将四年，其中两年写进了每本教科书。
somebody's generalship = 某人的将任

generate
核心：三支。一是产生、引起（兴趣、收入、热量）：generate interest；
二是发电：generate electricity；三是（计算机）生成。
① The advertisement generated interest from people who could not afford it.
= 那则广告引来的兴趣，来自买不起的人。
generate interest = 引起兴趣
② The turbine generates electricity for about nine hundred houses.
= 那台涡轮机发的电，够九百来户人家用。
generate electricity = 发电
③ The program generates a report nobody has ever read to the end.
= 那个程序生成的报告，没人从头看到尾过。
generate a report = 生成报告

generation
核心：四支。一是一代人：the younger generation；
二是（技术的）代：a third generation machine；
三是（产生的）过程：the generation of heat；四是从一代传到下一代。
① The younger generation in that village has three people in it.
= 那个村子里的年轻一代，一共三个人。
the younger generation = 年轻一代
② A third generation machine does the same work and takes half the space.
= 第三代机器干同样的活，占一半地方。
a third generation machine = 第三代机器
③ The generation of heat in that box is the whole problem.
= 那个箱子里发热，就是全部的毛病所在。
the generation of heat = 热的产生
④ The recipe went from generation to generation and lost an ingredient each time.
= 那个方子一代代传下来，每传一次少一样料。
from generation to generation = 一代代地

generational
核心：三支。一是代际的、一代人的：a generational divide；
二是（变化）需要一代人时间的；三是（差异）出于年龄层的。
① A generational divide in that office runs along who answers the phone.
= 那间办公室的代际差异，划在谁接电话上。
a generational divide = 代际分歧
② Rebuilding the trade is a generational job and the budget is annual.
= 重振这个行当是一代人的事，预算却是一年一定。
a generational job = 一代人的工程
③ The difference is generational and both sides call it a difference of principle.
= 那种分歧其实是代际的，双方都说是原则之争。
be generational = 属于代际差异

generative
核心：三支。一是能生成的、有生成力的：a generative process；
二是（语言学）生成的：generative grammar；三是（技术）生成式的。
① A generative process makes more of itself out of what it has already made.
= 生成性的过程，会拿已经做出的东西再造出更多。
a generative process = 生成性的过程
② Generative grammar asks what a speaker could say and not what they did say.
= 生成语法问的是说话人可能说什么，而不是实际说了什么。
generative grammar = 生成语法
③ The generative model writes four drafts and none of them is checked.
= 那个生成模型写了四稿，一稿都没核对过。
a generative model = 生成式模型

generic

generous
