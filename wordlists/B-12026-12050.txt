indignity
核心：侮辱、有失尊严的事 —— 让人觉得丢脸、没面子的对待。可数不可数都用。
① He suffered the indignity of being searched in front of his colleagues.
= 他当着同事的面被搜身，受了这番侮辱。
the indignity of doing something = …的屈辱
② Old age brings many small indignities, such as needing help to dress.
= 年老会带来许多小小的难堪，比如穿衣都要人帮忙。
small indignities = 小小的难堪

indigo
核心：靛蓝色；靛青（染料）。一种深蓝紫色，也指从植物提取的这种染料。
① The sky turned from pale blue to deep indigo as the sun went down.
= 太阳落山时，天空从浅蓝变成了深靛蓝。
deep indigo = 深靛蓝
② The cloth was dyed with indigo, and the colour lasted for years.
= 那块布是用靛青染的，颜色好多年都不褪。
dye something with indigo = 用靛青染…

indirect
核心：间接的；迂回的；不直截了当的。几支：不是直接造成的（indirect effects）；路线绕远的（an indirect route）；
说话拐弯抹角（an indirect answer）。语法与财税上的一串术语并入本条：indirect speech（间接引语）、
indirect object（间接宾语）、indirect question（间接问句）、indirect tax / indirect taxation（间接税）。
副词 indirectly 与名词 indirectness 并入本条。
① The strike had an indirect effect on hundreds of small businesses.
= 那场罢工间接影响了几百家小企业。
an indirect effect = 间接影响
② We took an indirect route to avoid the traffic in the town centre.
= 为了避开市中心的拥堵，我们绕路走了。
an indirect route = 绕远的路线
③ When I asked about the money, he gave a very indirect answer.
= 我问到那笔钱时，他回答得很含糊。
an indirect answer = 拐弯抹角的回答
④ News reporters often put a politician's words into indirect speech to save space.
= 新闻记者常把政客的话改成间接引语，以节省篇幅。
indirect speech = 间接引语
⑤ Verbs such as give and send can take both a direct and an indirect object.
= 像「give」「send」这样的动词可以同时带直接宾语和间接宾语。
an indirect object = 间接宾语
⑥ Tourists are taught to ask an indirect question when they need directions from strangers.
= 游客学到的是，向陌生人问路时要用间接问句。
an indirect question = 间接问句
⑦ Value added tax is an indirect tax paid when you buy goods.
= 增值税是买东西时缴的一种间接税。
an indirect tax = 间接税
indirect taxation = 间接税制
⑧ Rising interest rates will indirectly affect the price of food.
= 利率上升会间接影响食品价格。
indirectly = 间接地
⑨ The indirectness of her criticism made it hard to know what she really thought.
= 她的批评说得很委婉，让人很难知道她到底怎么想。
the indirectness of something = …的委婉、不直接

indiscernible
核心：看不出的、察觉不到的 —— 太小或太模糊而无法辨认。
① The difference between the two copies is almost indiscernible to the naked eye.
= 两份复本的差别肉眼几乎看不出来。
almost indiscernible = 几乎察觉不到的
② The path was indiscernible in the dark, and they had to wait for the moon.
= 黑暗中小路完全看不清，他们只好等月亮出来。
be indiscernible = 看不清

indiscipline
核心：（正式）无纪律、目无纪律 —— 不遵守规矩的状态。不可数，多说学校、军队、球队。
① The coach blamed the defeat on indiscipline, after two players were sent off.
= 两名球员被罚下后，教练把失利归咎于缺乏纪律。
indiscipline = 无纪律
② Indiscipline in the classroom makes it impossible for anyone to learn.
= 课堂上没有纪律，谁都学不进去。
indiscipline in the classroom = 课堂上的无纪律

indiscreet
核心：不慎重的、口无遮拦的 —— 不该说的说了，不该让人知道的暴露了。副词 indiscreetly 并入本条；名词 indiscretion 另立。
① It was indiscreet of her to discuss the merger in a crowded restaurant.
= 她在满是人的餐馆里讨论合并的事，太不谨慎了。
it was indiscreet of somebody to do something = 某人做…太不谨慎
② He spoke indiscreetly about his boss to a journalist and lost his job.
= 他跟记者口无遮拦地谈论老板，结果丢了工作。
speak indiscreetly = 口无遮拦地说

indiscretion
核心：言行失检；不检点的行为 —— 不可数时指不谨慎；可数时指一件失检的事，常委婉指婚外情等。
① The minister's indiscretion at a private dinner, secretly recorded by a guest, cost him his job.
= 那位部长在私人晚宴上言行失检，被一位客人偷偷录了音，结果丢了官。
indiscretion = 言行失检
② He admitted to a youthful indiscretion but said it was many years ago.
= 他承认年轻时有过一次不检点，但说那是很多年前的事了。
a youthful indiscretion = 年轻时的荒唐事

indiscriminate
核心：不加区别的、胡乱的 —— 不分对象、不管后果，多说暴力、使用（indiscriminate bombing）。
副词 indiscriminately 并入本条。
① Beekeepers blame the indiscriminate use of pesticides on nearby farms for the loss of their bees.
= 养蜂人把蜜蜂死亡归咎于附近农场滥用杀虫剂。
the indiscriminate use of something = 滥用…
② Witnesses said the soldiers fired indiscriminately into the crowd of unarmed protesters.
= 目击者说，士兵们朝手无寸铁的抗议人群胡乱开枪。
fire indiscriminately = 胡乱开枪

indispensable
核心：必不可少的 —— 缺了就不行。常接 to/for。
① A good dictionary is indispensable for anyone learning a language.
= 对学语言的人来说，一本好词典必不可少。
be indispensable for somebody = 对某人必不可少
② She made herself indispensable to the company by learning every job in the office.
= 她学会了办公室里的每项工作，让自己成了公司离不开的人。
make yourself indispensable = 让自己不可或缺

indisposed
核心：（正式）身体不适的；不愿意的。前一支是委婉说法（尤其用来解释缺席）；后一支接不定式。
名词 indisposition（小病、不适）并入本条。
① The singer was indisposed, so her understudy performed the role that evening.
= 那位歌手身体不适，当晚由她的替角演出。
be indisposed = 身体不适（委婉）
② The committee seemed indisposed to accept any of our suggestions.
= 委员会似乎不愿接受我们的任何建议。
be indisposed to do something = 不愿做…
③ A slight indisposition kept her at home for a few days.
= 一点小病让她在家待了几天。
a slight indisposition = 小病、微恙

indisputable
核心：无可争辩的、不容置疑的 —— 事实、证据明摆着。副词 indisputably 并入本条。
① After a century of careful temperature records, it is indisputable that the climate is changing.
= 有了一个世纪的精确气温记录，气候正在变化已是不容置疑的事实。
it is indisputable that = …是无可争辩的
② With forty goals this season, she is indisputably the best player in the team.
= 这个赛季进了四十个球，她无疑是队里最好的球员。
indisputably = 无可争辩地

indissoluble
核心：（正式）牢不可破的、不能解除的 —— 多说婚姻、联系、纽带。副词 indissolubly 并入本条。
① The church teaches that marriage is an indissoluble union.
= 教会认为婚姻是不可解除的结合。
an indissoluble union = 不可解除的结合
② Her name is indissolubly linked with the fight for women's votes.
= 她的名字与争取妇女选举权的斗争紧紧联系在一起。
be indissolubly linked with something = 与…紧紧联系在一起

indistinct
核心：模糊的、不清楚的 —— 看不清、听不清、记不清。副词 indistinctly 并入本条。
① The voices in the next room were indistinct, but they sounded angry.
= 隔壁房间的声音听不清，但听着像在吵架。
indistinct voices = 模糊的声音
② Through the fog we could see the shape of the castle only indistinctly.
= 透过雾，我们只能模模糊糊地看到城堡的轮廓。
indistinctly = 模糊地

indistinguishable
核心：无法区分的、难以辨别的 —— 两样东西像到分不出来，常接 from。
① The fake notes printed in the cellar were almost indistinguishable from real ones, even to bank staff.
= 地下室里印的假钞几乎跟真钞分不出来，连银行职员都看不出。
be indistinguishable from something = 与…难以区分
② In the dark hallway, the twins in their matching coats were completely indistinguishable.
= 在昏暗的走廊里，穿着同样外套的双胞胎完全分不清谁是谁。
be indistinguishable = 无法区分

individual
核心：来自拉丁语「不可分的」—— 一个人、一样东西作为不可再分的单位。形容词两支：
单独的、各自的（each individual case）；个人的、独特的（an individual style）。
名词：个人（相对于集体）；（口语，常带点意味）某个人、家伙（a strange individual）。
副词 individually 并入本条；individualism、individuality 等另立。
① Each individual student will get a written report at the end of term.
= 每个学生期末都会拿到一份书面评语。
each individual = 每个单独的
② The teacher tries to give individual attention to every child in the class.
= 那位老师尽量给班上每个孩子个别关照。
individual attention = 个别关照
③ She dresses in a very individual style that nobody else would dare to copy.
= 她穿衣风格很独特，别人都不敢模仿。
an individual style = 独特的风格
④ The rights of the individual must be balanced against the needs of society.
= 个人的权利必须与社会的需要相平衡。
the individual = 个人（相对于社会）
⑤ Some very strange individual has been leaving notes on my car.
= 有个很古怪的家伙一直往我车上留纸条。
a strange individual = 古怪的家伙
口语里说「某人」常带不以为然。
⑥ The chocolates are individually wrapped, so they make a good present.
= 巧克力是单独包装的，适合送人。
individually wrapped = 单独包装的

individualism
核心：个人主义 —— 强调个人自由、独立与自我判断的思想，也指行事独来独往、不随大流。
中文「个人主义」常带贬义，英文 individualism 多是中性或褒义。
① American culture is often said to value individualism above community.
= 人们常说美国文化把个人主义看得比集体更重。
value individualism = 推崇个人主义
② Her individualism made her a brilliant artist but a difficult colleague.
= 她特立独行，这让她成了出色的艺术家，却也是个难相处的同事。
somebody's individualism = 某人的特立独行

individualist
核心：个人主义者；特立独行的人。形容词 individualistic（个人主义的；独特的）并入本条。
① He was a born individualist who never joined any club or party.
= 他天生特立独行，从不加入任何俱乐部或党派。
an individualist = 特立独行的人
② The team's individualistic style of play made them exciting but unreliable.
= 那支球队各自为战的打法让他们好看却不稳定。
individualistic = 个人主义的、各自为战的

individuality
核心：个性、独特性 —— 让一个人或东西与众不同的特质。不可数。
① The school uniform leaves little room for individuality.
= 校服几乎不给个性留余地。
room for individuality = 个性的空间
② Each of the old houses in the street has its own individuality.
= 那条街上每座老房子都有自己的特色。
have its own individuality = 有自己的特色

individualize
核心：使个性化、按个人需要调整 —— 英式也拼 -ise。形容词 individualized（个性化的）
与名词 individualization 并入本条。
① In small classes, teachers can individualize lessons for pupils with special needs or unusual talents.
= 在小班里，老师可以为有特殊需要或特殊才能的学生量身调整课程。
individualize lessons = 使课程个性化
② After the first scan, each patient receives an individualized treatment plan from the hospital.
= 第一次扫描后，每位病人都会从医院拿到一份个性化的治疗方案。
an individualized plan = 个性化方案
③ The individualization of learning is easier with small classes.
= 小班更容易实现个性化教学。
the individualization of something = …的个性化

indivisible
核心：不可分割的；（数学）不能整除的。名词 indivisibility 与副词 indivisibly 并入本条。
① Freedom and responsibility are indivisible; you cannot have one without the other.
= 自由与责任不可分割，没有一个就没有另一个。
be indivisible = 不可分割
② Any odd number, such as seven or fifteen, is indivisible by two.
= 任何奇数，比如七或十五，都不能被二整除。
be indivisible by a number = 不能被某数整除
③ After centuries of trade and war, the two countries' histories are indivisibly linked.
= 经过几百年的贸易与战争，两国的历史紧密相连、无法分割。
indivisibly = 不可分割地
④ The first article of the constitution declares the indivisibility of the nation and its territory.
= 宪法第一条宣布国家及其领土不可分割。
the indivisibility of something = …的不可分割

indoctrinate
核心：（贬）灌输（思想）、洗脑 —— 让人不加批判地接受一套观念。常用 indoctrinate somebody with/into。
名词 indoctrination 并入本条。
① The children were indoctrinated with the party's ideas from the age of five.
= 那些孩子从五岁起就被灌输那个党的思想。
indoctrinate somebody with something = 向某人灌输…
② Critics accused the school of political indoctrination.
= 批评者指责那所学校搞政治洗脑。
political indoctrination = 政治灌输

indolent
核心：（正式）懒惰的、懒散的 —— 不爱动、不爱做事。名词 indolence 并入本条。
① The heat made everyone indolent, and nobody wanted to work after lunch.
= 天热得让人犯懒，午饭后谁都不想干活。
be indolent = 懒散
② His teachers blamed his poor results on indolence rather than a lack of ability.
= 老师们把他成绩差归咎于懒惰，而不是能力不足。
indolence = 懒惰

indomitable
核心：（正式，褒）不屈不挠的、不可征服的 —— 说精神、意志、人。
① Her indomitable spirit kept her going through years of illness.
= 她不屈不挠的精神支撑她熬过了多年的病痛。
an indomitable spirit = 不屈不挠的精神
② The old farmer was indomitable and rebuilt his barn three times after floods.
= 那位老农不屈不挠，洪水过后三次重建了他的谷仓。
be indomitable = 不屈不挠

Indonesian
核心：印度尼西亚的；印尼人；印尼语（官方语言，也叫 Bahasa Indonesia）。
① Indonesian food is spicy, and rice is served with almost every meal.
= 印尼菜很辣，几乎每顿饭都配米饭。
Indonesian food = 印尼菜
② She learned Indonesian while working in Jakarta for three years.
= 她在雅加达工作三年期间学会了印尼语。
Indonesian = 印尼语

indoor
核心：室内的 —— 只放在名词前：indoor swimming pool、indoor sports。副词是 indoors。反义 outdoor。
① The hotel has an indoor swimming pool, so we could swim even in the rain.
= 酒店有室内游泳池，下雨天也能游泳。
an indoor swimming pool = 室内游泳池
② Badminton is mainly an indoor sport because the wind blows the shuttle away.
= 羽毛球主要是室内运动，因为风会把球吹跑。
an indoor sport = 室内运动
