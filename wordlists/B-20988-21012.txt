should
/ʃəd/（弱）/ʃʊd/（强）
核心：情态动词：（表示义务、正确做法）应该：You should…；（表示建议）最好、应该（语气比 must 柔和）；（表示推测）应该会、大概：They should be here soon；should have done 是本该做而没做；（英式，正式）万一：If you should need help…；（用于某些从句）I suggest that he should…。否定 should not / shouldn't。
① You should always wear a helmet when you ride your bike on the main road.
= 在大路上骑自行车时，你应该一直戴着头盔。
should = 应该
后接动词原形，不加 to。
② If you come to this little harbour restaurant, you should try the fish; it's the best in town.
= 要是来这家港口小餐馆，你应该尝尝这里的鱼，是全镇最好的。
should = 建议（较柔）
近义对照：
must = 必须（强制）
③ Standing wet at the bus stop, I knew I should have listened to my mother and taken an umbrella.
= 浑身湿透地站在公交站，我知道我本该听妈妈的话带把伞的。
should have done = 本该做而没做
④ The train left at nine, so they should be here by half past ten.
= 火车九点开的，所以他们应该十点半前到。
should be = 应该会（推测）
⑤ If you should need any help with your luggage, please call reception.
= 万一您搬行李需要帮忙，请给前台打电话。
if you should = 万一
正式、礼貌的说法，多见于通知和服务用语。
⑥ You shouldn't eat so many sweets before dinner, or you won't want your vegetables.
= 晚饭前你不该吃那么多糖，不然你就不想吃蔬菜了。
shouldn't = 不应该

shoulder
/ˈʃəʊldə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：肩膀、肩：on your shoulders；（衣服的）肩部；（动物的）前腿上部、前肩肉：shoulder of lamb；（美式）（公路的）路肩。动词，及物：担负、承担（责任、费用）：shoulder the blame；用肩扛；用肩膀推、挤。习语：a shoulder to cry on 是可以倾诉的人；shoulder to shoulder 是肩并肩、并肩地；give somebody the cold shoulder 是冷落某人；rub shoulders with 是与（名人）交往。
① He carried his little daughter on his shoulders so that she could see the parade.
= 他把小女儿扛在肩上，好让她看游行。
shoulder = 肩膀
on your shoulders = 在肩上
比喻也说承担重担：on your shoulders 指责任压在身上。
② When I asked him where he had been, he just shrugged his shoulders.
= 我问他去哪儿了，他只是耸了耸肩。
shrug one's shoulders = 耸肩
③ On the cold morning of the parade, the soldiers stood shoulder to shoulder at the palace gate.
= 阅兵那天寒冷的早晨，士兵们在宫门口肩并肩地站着。
shoulder to shoulder = 肩并肩
④ After her divorce, her best friend was always there as a shoulder to cry on.
= 她离婚后，最好的朋友一直在身边听她倾诉。
a shoulder to cry on = 可以倾诉的人
⑤ Since the argument, my neighbour has been giving me the cold shoulder.
= 自从那次吵架后，我的邻居一直对我不理不睬。
give somebody the cold shoulder = 冷落某人
⑥ At the film festival party in Cannes, she rubbed shoulders with famous actors and singers.
= 在戛纳电影节的派对上，她和著名演员、歌手们打成一片。
rub shoulders with somebody = 与某人交往
⑦ The parents will have to shoulder most of the cost of the school trip.
= 学校旅行的大部分费用要由家长承担。
shoulder the cost = 承担费用
常与 blame、responsibility、burden 连用。
⑧ My mother roasted a shoulder of lamb with garlic and rosemary for Sunday lunch.
= 星期天午饭我妈妈用大蒜和迷迭香烤了一块羊肩肉。
a shoulder of lamb = 羊肩肉
⑨ She carries a small leather shoulder bag with her phone and keys in it.
= 她背着一个小皮挎包，里面装着手机和钥匙。
a shoulder bag = 挎包
⑩ In the 1980s, women's jackets often had big shoulder pads that made them look very square.
= 二十世纪八十年代，女式夹克常常有大垫肩，看上去方方正正的。
shoulder pads = 垫肩
⑪ One of the shoulder straps on my backpack broke when I was climbing the hill.
= 我爬山时背包的一根肩带断了。
a shoulder strap = 肩带
⑫ Be careful when you type your PIN at the cash machine, because shoulder surfing is common in busy streets.
= 在取款机输密码时要小心，因为热闹街头常有人偷窥。
shoulder surfing = （从背后）偷看别人输密码
⑬ The water in the flooded street was shoulder-high, so the firemen used a boat.
= 被淹的街道里水深齐肩，所以消防员用了船。
shoulder-high = 齐肩高的
⑭ The new nurse has shoulder-length brown hair that she ties back when she is at work.
= 新来的护士留着齐肩的棕色头发，上班时扎起来。
shoulder-length = 齐肩的

shout
/ʃaʊt/

= 请别当着大家的面冲我喊，我听得清清楚楚。

shout = 大喊（音量大）
近义对照：

whisper = 低语
② The swimmer was shouting for help, so the lifeguard jumped into the water.
= 那个游泳的人在大声呼救，救生员就跳进了水里。

⑤ When the minister tried to speak, the angry crowd shouted him down.
= 部长想讲话时，愤怒的人群把他的声音压了下去。
shout down = 用喊声压倒（常拆开用：shout somebody down）
⑥ The teacher asked a question, and three children shouted out the answer at the same time.
= 老师提了个问题，三个孩子同时大声喊出了答案。

⑦ What started as a small argument about the bill turned into a shouting match in the middle of the restaurant.

a shouting match = 对骂、大吵

① We went to see a show in London and had dinner afterwards.
= 我们去伦敦看了一场演出，之后吃了晚饭。

= 你有空的时候，能给我看看你在希腊度假的照片吗？

④ When we arrived at the restaurant, the waiter showed us to a table by the window.

show your face = 露面
⑪ The lead actor was ill, but the show must go on, so his understudy played the part.
= 主演病了，但演出必须继续，于是由替补演员上场。
the show must go on = 演出必须继续
⑫ We waited outside the cinema for an hour, but he never showed up.
= 我们在电影院外面等了一个小时，可他始终没露面。
show up = 露面
口语，多指迟到或爽约的场合；正式说法用 arrive、appear。

= 那个小男孩为了在女孩们面前炫耀，一次次从墙上跳下来。
