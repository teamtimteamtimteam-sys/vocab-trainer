noon
/nuːn/
核心：正午、中午十二点。不可数，不加冠词。介词用 at、by、before、around：at noon。
high noon 一是正午时分，二是（比喻）决定性的时刻、对决。
习语 morning, noon and night 是从早到晚、没日没夜。
旧式、文学的说法 noonday（多作定语：the noonday sun）与 noontide 并入本条。
① The ferry leaves at noon and reaches the island in time for a late lunch.
= 渡船正午开，到岛上正好赶上一顿迟午饭。
at noon = 在正午
② The work must be finished by noon on Friday.
= 那项工作必须在星期五中午之前完成。
by noon = 中午之前
③ The talks have reached high noon, and one side must give way.
= 谈判到了决定性的时刻，总得有一方让步。
high noon = 决定性的时刻
④ She thinks about that boy morning, noon and night.
= 她从早到晚想着那个男孩。
morning, noon and night = 从早到晚
⑤ The walkers rested under a tree to escape the noonday sun.
= 徒步的人在树下歇脚，躲开正午的太阳。
the noonday sun = 正午的太阳
noonday = 正午（旧式）
⑥ By noontide the mist had lifted, and the whole valley lay in sunshine.
= 到了正午，雾散了，整条山谷沐浴在阳光里。
noontide = 正午（文学）

noose
/nuːs/
核心：两支，可数。一是绳套、套索：一头打了活结、越拉越紧的绳圈，尤指绞刑的绞索；二是（比喻）越收越紧的束缚、绝境。
① The cowboy dropped a noose over the calf's head at the first throw.
= 那个牛仔头一下就把套索甩到了小牛头上。
a noose = 套索
② The condemned man walked to the noose without a word.
= 那个死囚一言不发地走向绞索。
the noose = 绞索
③ The debt was a noose round the company's neck that tightened every month.
= 那笔债是套在公司脖子上的绞索，一个月比一个月紧。
a noose round somebody's neck = 套在某人脖子上的绞索

nope
/nəʊp/
核心：两支，口语。一是 no 的随意说法，只用于回答，语气干脆；二是（网络用语）表示坚决不干、看一眼就想躲。只见于对话与聊天。
① "Have you finished your homework?" "Nope. I have not even started."
= 「作业做完了吗？」「没。我还没开始呢。」
Nope. = 没、不。
② "Want to hold the spider?" "Nope, nope, nope," she said, backing out of the room.
= 「想托一下这只蜘蛛吗？」「不不不。」她说着退出了房间。
nope = 坚决不

nor
/nɔː(r)/
核心：连词。一是 neither … nor …，既不…也不…；二是接在否定句后面，引出又一个否定：「也不」，后面要倒装：
Nor do I、nor did she；三是（正式）用在句首，承接上文再添一条否定。
跟 or 的分别：前面有 neither 或另一句否定句打头时用它；一句之内的 no、never 后面通常接 or。
① He can neither read nor write, yet he runs a successful business.
= 他既不会读也不会写，却把生意做得很成功。
neither … nor … = 既不…也不…
② "I have never been to Wales." "Nor have I, though I live an hour away."
= 「我从没去过威尔士。」「我也没去过，尽管我住的地方离那儿才一个钟头。」
Nor have I. = 我也没有。
后面主语和助动词倒装。
③ She did not reply to the letter, nor did she return any of his calls.
= 她没有回那封信，也没有回他的任何一个电话。
nor did she = 她也没有
④ The house is far from cheap. Nor is it close to the station.
= 那座房子一点都不便宜，离车站也远。
Nor is it … = 它也不…
放在句首，偏正式。

a nor'easter is forecast = 预报有东北风暴

Nordic

= 一次受伤让她告别了高山雪道，之后她改练越野滑雪。
Nordic skiing = 北欧滑雪
④ Every morning a group of pensioners goes Nordic walking round the lake.
= 每天早上，一群退休老人绕着湖持杖健走。
Nordic walking = 持杖健走
⑤ The channel shows Nordic noir on Saturday nights, with subtitles.
= 那个频道星期六晚上放带字幕的北欧黑色犯罪剧。
Nordic noir = 北欧黑色犯罪作品

norm
/nɔːm/
核心：三支，可数。一是 the norm，常态、通常的情形：be the norm 是很普遍；
二是 norms，复数，（社会的）行为规范、准则；三是（技术、教育）标准水平、定额。
① Working from home has become the norm in many offices.
= 在许多办公室，在家办公已成常态。
the norm = 常态
② Families with one child are now the norm rather than the exception.
= 独生子女家庭如今是常态，不是例外。
the norm rather than the exception = 是常态而非例外
③ Every society has norms about how close to stand to a stranger.
= 跟陌生人站多近，每个社会都有不成文的规矩。
social norms = 社会规范
④ The child's reading is well above the norm for her age.
= 那孩子的阅读水平远高于同龄人的标准。
above the norm = 高于标准水平

normal
/ˈnɔːməl/
核心：形容词两支。一是正常的、通常的、合乎常规的：it is normal to do something、perfectly normal、in the normal way；
二是（身心）正常的、健康的。
名词是常态、正常水平，不可数：back to normal、above normal、below normal；the new normal 是「新常态」。
副词 normally 一是通常、一般情况下，二是正常地。
名词 normality 是正常状态，美式也说 normalcy；动词 normalize 是使正常化、恢复正常，名词 normalization。
统计学上 normal distribution 是正态分布。并入本条。
① It is normal to feel tired for a few days after a long flight.
= 长途飞行之后累上几天是正常的。
normal = 正常的
it is normal to do something = 做某事是正常的
② Under normal circumstances the journey takes about forty minutes.
= 在正常情况下，那段路大约要四十分钟。
under normal circumstances = 在正常情况下
③ The shop is open during normal working hours and closed on Sundays.
= 那家店在正常上班时间营业，星期天不开。
normal working hours = 正常上班时间
④ The baby's weight is perfectly normal for her age.
= 那个宝宝的体重，在她这个月龄完全正常。
perfectly normal = 完全正常
⑤ Things are slowly getting back to normal after the flood.
= 洪水过后，一切正慢慢恢复正常。
back to normal = 恢复正常
⑥ Rainfall this month has been well above normal.
= 这个月的降雨量远高于正常水平。
above normal = 高于正常水平
below normal = 低于正常水平
⑦ Queues at the border are the new normal, and drivers pack sandwiches.
= 边境排长队成了新常态，司机们都自带三明治。
the new normal = 新常态
⑧ I normally cycle to work, but today I took the bus.
= 我通常骑车上班，不过今天坐了公共汽车。
normally = 通常
⑨ Her heart is beating normally, and she should go home tomorrow.
= 她的心跳正常，明天应该可以回家。
normally = 正常地
⑩ The town longs for a return to normality after a month of power cuts.
= 停了一个月的电，镇上的人盼着恢复常态。
a return to normality = 恢复常态
⑪ The two countries agreed to normalize relations and reopen their embassies.
= 两国同意实现关系正常化，重开使馆。
normalize relations = 实现关系正常化
⑫ The normalization of trade took a further three years.
= 贸易正常化又花了三年。
normalization = 正常化
⑬ The heights of adult men follow a normal distribution.
= 成年男子的身高呈正态分布。
a normal distribution = 正态分布

Norman
/ˈnɔːmən/

normative
/ˈnɔːmətɪv/

north
/nɔːθ/
核心：四个方位词之一，名词、形容词、副词三种用法，缩写 N。
名词：北、北方，通常带 the：in the north 是在北部（范围之内），to the north of 是在…以北（范围之外）；
the North 大写时指一国的北部地区，在英国指英格兰北部，在美国指北方各州；也指北半球的富国。
形容词：北部的、朝北的、（风）从北面来的：the north coast、a north wind；地名里大写：North America。
副词：向北、在北面：drive north、face north；up north 是（口语）在北方、到北方去。
true north 是真北，magnetic north 是磁北；the North Pole 是北极。
中间方位写作 north-east、north-west，再细分有 north-north-east、north-north-west；north-south 指南北向的、南北之间的。
north-country 作定语指英格兰北部的。并入本条。
① The wind is blowing from the north and bringing snow with it.
= 风从北边刮来，还带着雪。
from the north = 从北边
② They live in the north of Italy, close to the Swiss border.
= 他们住在意大利北部，靠近瑞士边境。
in the north of = 在…的北部
③ The lake lies ten miles to the north of the town.
= 那个湖在镇子以北十英里。
to the north of = 在…以北
在范围之外用 to，在范围之内用 in。
④ House prices in the North are far lower than in London.
= 英格兰北部的房价比伦敦低得多。
the North = 北部地区
⑤ We walked along the north coast for three days without seeing a town.
= 我们沿着北海岸走了三天，没见到一座城镇。
the north coast = 北海岸
⑥ A bitter north wind kept everyone indoors.
= 刺骨的北风把大家都留在了屋里。
a north wind = 北风
⑦ The geese fly north in spring and return in the autumn.
= 大雁春天往北飞，秋天回来。
fly north = 往北飞
⑧ The kitchen faces north and gets hardly any sun.
= 厨房朝北，几乎晒不到太阳。
face north = 朝北
⑨ His sister moved up north to be near her grandchildren.
= 他姐姐搬到北边去，好离孙辈近些。
up north = 在北方、到北方去
⑩ The compass points to magnetic north, which is a little way from true north.
= 指南针指向磁北，磁北跟真北差一点。
magnetic north = 磁北
true north = 真北
⑪ The first explorers reached the North Pole on foot, pulling their own sledges.
= 头一批探险者徒步到达北极，自己拉着雪橇。
the North Pole = 北极
⑫ The storm is moving north-east at twenty miles an hour.
= 那场风暴正以每小时二十英里的速度向东北移动。
north-east = 东北
north-west = 西北
⑬ The pilot turned the plane a few degrees to north-north-west.
= 飞行员把飞机往北偏西北方向转了几度。
north-north-west = 北偏西北
north-north-east = 北偏东北
⑭ The main north-south railway line runs through the valley.
= 南北向的铁路干线从那条山谷穿过。
north-south = 南北向的
⑮ He never lost his north-country accent, even after forty years in London.
= 他在伦敦住了四十年，北方口音始终没丢。
north-country = 英格兰北部的
⑯ A north-easterly wind brought sleet to the coast, while a north-westerly gale battered the islands.
= 东北风给海岸带来雨夹雪，西北大风则猛袭那些岛屿。
north-easterly = 东北的、从东北吹来的
north-westerly = 西北的、从西北吹来的
⑰ Rainfall is far heavier in the north-western hills than on the north-eastern plain.
= 西北部山区的降雨比东北部平原多得多。
north-western = 西北部的
north-eastern = 东北部的
⑱ The main road runs north-eastwards to the port, and a branch turns north-westwards to the mines.
= 主路向东北通往港口，一条支路折向西北通往矿区。
north-eastwards = 向东北
north-westwards = 向西北
⑲ The glacier is retreating in a north-eastward direction, and the river has taken a north-westward course.
= 冰川正朝东北方向退缩，河流则改走了偏西北的河道。
north-eastward = 向东北的
north-westward = 向西北的

northward
/ˈnɔːθwəd/
核心：两支。一是形容词，向北的：a northward journey；二是副词，向北，英式多写成 northwards，美式多用不带 s 的形式。
① The army began its northward march at dawn.
= 军队在黎明时开始北进。
a northward march = 北进
② The birds fly northwards in March to breed on the tundra.
= 那些鸟三月往北飞，到苔原上繁殖。
northwards = 向北
③ The road runs northward along the river for about thirty miles.
= 那条路沿河向北延伸三十英里左右。
run northward = 向北延伸

Norwegian
/nɔːˈwiːdʒən/
核心：三支，首字母大写。一是形容词，挪威的；二是名词，挪威人，可数；三是挪威语，不可数。
带 Norway 的两个动物名并入本条：Norway lobster 是挪威海螯虾，餐桌上叫 scampi 或 langoustine；
Norway rat 是褐家鼠，最常见的那种老鼠。
① The Norwegian coast is cut by hundreds of deep fjords.
= 挪威的海岸被数百条深深的峡湾切开。
the Norwegian coast = 挪威海岸
② A Norwegian was the first person to reach the South Pole.
= 头一个到达南极的是个挪威人。
a Norwegian = 挪威人
③ She learned Norwegian in a year by reading crime novels with a dictionary.
= 她靠着词典读犯罪小说，一年学会了挪威语。
learn Norwegian = 学挪威语
④ The scampi on the menu is the tail of the Norway lobster.
= 菜单上的炸虾球，用的是挪威海螯虾的尾巴。
the Norway lobster = 挪威海螯虾
⑤ The Norway rat arrived on ships and now lives in every city.
= 褐家鼠是随船来的，如今每座城市都有。
the Norway rat = 褐家鼠

nose
/nəʊz/
核心：名词四支。一是鼻子；blow your nose 是擤鼻涕，a runny nose 是流鼻涕，hold your nose 是捏住鼻子；
二是嗅觉，引申为觉察某类事物的本事：have a nose for；三是（飞机、车、船的）头部：nose cone 是火箭、飞机的鼻锥；
四是（酒的）香气。
动词一是（车、船）小心地慢慢往前探；二是（动物）用鼻子拱、嗅；
短语动词：nose about 与 nose around 是到处打探、乱翻；nose out 一是嗅出、打探出，二是以微弱优势击败。
习语很多：under your nose 是就在眼皮底下；poke your nose into 是多管闲事；turn your nose up at 是瞧不上；
look down your nose at 是看不起；follow your nose 是一直往前走、凭直觉走；pay through the nose 是花冤枉大价钱；
nose to tail 是（英式）车辆首尾相接；keep your nose clean 是不惹事；get up somebody's nose 是（英式）惹人心烦；
by a nose 是（赛马）以一鼻之差，引申为险胜；cut off your nose to spite your face 是赌气害了自己；
on the nose 是（美式）不多不少、正好；keep your nose out of 是别管闲事。
nose ring 是鼻环，nose flute 是用鼻子吹的鼻笛。并入本条。
① He broke his nose playing rugby, and it has been crooked ever since.
= 他打橄榄球把鼻子撞断了，从此鼻梁就是歪的。
break your nose = 撞断鼻子
② She took out a handkerchief and blew her nose loudly.
= 她掏出手帕，响亮地擤了擤鼻子。
blow your nose = 擤鼻涕
③ The child has had a runny nose and a cough since the weekend.
= 那孩子从周末起一直流鼻涕、咳嗽。
a runny nose = 流鼻涕
④ I held my nose and swallowed the medicine in one gulp.
= 我捏住鼻子，一口把药吞了下去。
hold your nose = 捏住鼻子
⑤ A good reporter has a nose for a story that others miss.
= 好记者对别人漏掉的新闻有灵敏的嗅觉。
have a nose for = 对…嗅觉灵敏
have a nose for something = 善于觉察某事
⑥ The nose of the plane dipped sharply, and the passengers screamed.
= 机头猛地往下一沉，乘客们尖叫起来。
the nose of a plane = 机头
⑦ The nose cone of the rocket is designed to survive great heat.
= 火箭的鼻锥设计得能耐极高的温度。
a nose cone = 鼻锥
⑧ The wine has a nose of blackcurrants and cedar.
= 那款酒有黑醋栗和雪松的香气。
the nose of a wine = 酒的香气
⑨ The taxi nosed its way through the crowd outside the stadium.
= 出租车在体育场外的人群里小心地往前挪。
nose your way through something = 小心地穿过某处
⑩ The dog nosed the door open and climbed onto the bed.
= 那条狗用鼻子拱开门，爬上了床。
nose something open = 用鼻子拱开某物
⑪ I found him nosing about in my desk when I came back from lunch.
= 我吃完午饭回来，发现他在我的书桌里乱翻。
nose about = 到处乱翻、打探
⑫ Reporters have been nosing around the village asking about the family.
= 记者们一直在村里转悠，打听那家人的事。
nose around = 到处打探
⑬ The dog can nose out a truffle buried a foot under the ground.
= 那条狗能嗅出埋在地下一英尺的松露。
nose out = 嗅出、打探出
⑭ She nosed out the favourite in the last few strides.
= 她在最后几步以微弱优势胜了热门选手。
nose out somebody = 以微弱优势击败某人
⑮ The thieves loaded the van right under the nose of the security guard.
= 那伙贼就在保安的眼皮底下把货装上了车。
under the nose of somebody = 在某人眼皮底下
under your nose = 就在眼皮底下
⑯ I wish she would stop poking her nose into other people's business.
= 但愿她别再管别人的闲事。
poke your nose into = 多管闲事
poke your nose into something = 插手某事
⑰ The children turned their noses up at the spinach.
= 孩子们对菠菜一脸嫌弃。
turn your nose up at = 瞧不上、嫌弃
⑱ He looks down his nose at anyone who did not go to university.
= 没上过大学的人，他一概看不起。
look down your nose at = 看不起
look down your nose at somebody = 看不起某人
⑲ Just follow your nose along this road until you reach the church.
= 你就顺着这条路一直往前走，走到教堂为止。
follow your nose = 一直往前走
⑳ We paid through the nose for a room with a view of the car park.
= 我们花了冤枉大价钱，住了一间看得见停车场的房间。
pay through the nose = 花冤枉大价钱
㉑ Traffic was nose to tail on the coast road all afternoon.
= 整个下午，海滨公路上的车首尾相接。
nose to tail = 首尾相接
㉒ Since he came out of prison he has kept his nose clean.
= 出狱以后，他一直安分守己。
keep your nose clean = 不惹事
㉓ His habit of humming really gets up my nose.
= 他爱哼曲子的习惯真让我心烦。
get up somebody's nose = 惹某人心烦
㉔ The grey horse won by a nose after a photograph was examined.
= 查看了终点照片之后，判那匹灰马以一鼻之差获胜。
by a nose = 以一鼻之差
㉕ Refusing the money out of pride would be cutting off your nose to spite your face.
= 为了面子不要那笔钱，是赌气害了自己。
cut off your nose to spite your face = 赌气害了自己
㉖ Her guess of forty-two was right on the nose.
= 她猜四十二，不多不少正好。
on the nose = 不多不少、正好
㉗ Keep your nose out of my affairs and look after your own.
= 少管我的事，管好你自己的。
keep your nose out of something = 别管某事
㉘ She came home from her travels with a nose ring and a tattoo.
= 她旅行回来，多了一个鼻环和一处文身。
a nose ring = 鼻环
㉙ A musician from the islands played a tune on a bamboo nose flute.
= 一位来自那些岛屿的乐手用竹鼻笛吹了一支曲子。
a nose flute = 鼻笛
㉚ The old soldier in the painting has a proud face, grey eyes and a long Roman nose.
= 画里那位老兵神情高傲，灰眼睛，长着一个高高的鹰钩鼻。
a Roman nose = 鹰钩鼻、高鼻梁

nosebag
/ˈnəʊzbæɡ/
核心：马料袋：装着饲料、套在马头上让它边站边吃的袋子。可数，英式，美式叫 feed bag。
① The carter hung a nosebag on the horse while he unloaded the coal.
= 车夫卸煤的时候，给马套上了料袋。
a nosebag = 马料袋
② The old pony stood in the yard with its nosebag on, chewing steadily.
= 那匹老矮马套着料袋站在院子里，不紧不慢地嚼着。
with its nosebag on = 套着料袋

noseband
/ˈnəʊzbænd/
核心：鼻革：马笼头上横过马鼻梁的那条皮带。可数，马具用语。
① Check that the noseband is not so tight that the horse cannot open its mouth.
= 检查一下鼻革，别紧得让马张不开嘴。
the noseband = 鼻革
② She fitted a padded noseband to stop the leather rubbing.
= 她装了一条带衬垫的鼻革，免得皮子磨马。
a padded noseband = 带衬垫的鼻革

nosebleed
/ˈnəʊzbliːd/
核心：鼻出血、流鼻血。可数，常搭 have a nosebleed。
① He had a nosebleed in the middle of the exam and was allowed extra time.
= 他考试考到一半流了鼻血，获准延长时间。
have a nosebleed = 流鼻血
② Pinch the soft part of the nose for ten minutes to stop a nosebleed.
= 捏住鼻子的软处十分钟，就能止住鼻血。
stop a nosebleed = 止鼻血
