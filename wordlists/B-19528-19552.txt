routine
/ruːˈtiːn/
核心：名词：（日常的）惯例、常规、固定程序：a daily routine、break the routine；（舞蹈、喜剧等的）一套动作、一段固定表演：a dance routine；（计算机）例程。可数或不可数。
形容词：例行的、常规的：a routine check；（贬义）乏味的、千篇一律的。副词 routinely 是例行地、照例；经常（发生）。
① My daily routine hardly changes: I get up at seven, walk the dog and catch the train at eight.
= 我每天的日常安排几乎不变：七点起床，遛狗，八点赶火车。
a daily routine = 日常安排
routine = 例行公事
② A short holiday by the sea is a good way to break the routine of work and housework.
= 去海边度个短假，是打破工作和家务常规的好办法。
break the routine = 打破常规
③ The two girls practised their dance routine every evening for a month before the school show.
= 学校演出前，那两个女孩每天晚上练她们那套舞蹈动作，练了一个月。
a dance routine = 一套舞蹈动作
④ The police stopped the car for a routine check and found that the driver had no licence.
= 警察拦下那辆车作例行检查，发现司机没有驾照。
a routine check = 例行检查
作定语的 routine 多是中性的「常规的」，说工作乏味时才带贬义。
⑤ After ten years in the same office, he found the work routine and longed for something more exciting.
= 在同一个办公室干了十年，他觉得这份工作千篇一律，渴望做点更刺激的事。
routine = 乏味的
⑥ Passengers' bags are routinely searched at the border, so do not carry anything for a stranger.
= 在边境乘客的包照例都要检查，所以别替陌生人带任何东西。
routinely = 例行地、照例
⑦ Teachers say that children in the city routinely go to bed after midnight on school nights.
= 老师们说，城里的孩子第二天要上学的晚上也经常过了半夜才睡。
routinely = 经常

roux
/ruː/
核心：（烹饪）油面酱、面粉糊：黄油和面粉一起炒成的底料，用来给酱汁、汤勾芡。可数，复数也写 roux。来自法语。
① Start the cheese sauce by making a roux with butter and flour, then slowly add warm milk.
= 做奶酪酱先用黄油和面粉炒个油面酱，再慢慢加热牛奶。
make a roux = 炒油面酱
② If you cook the roux for longer, it turns brown and gives the soup a richer, nutty taste.
= 油面酱炒得久一点就会变成褐色，让汤带上更浓的坚果香。
a brown roux = 褐色油面酱

rove
/rəʊv/
核心：动词：（文学）漫游、流浪：rove the countryside、rove around；（目光）扫来扫去：eyes rove over / around something。不及物、及物都可。
① As a young man he roved the countryside, working on farms and sleeping in barns.
= 他年轻时四处漂泊，在农场打工，睡在谷仓里。
rove the countryside = 在乡间漫游
② During the exam, the teacher's eyes roved over the rows of desks, watching for anyone who tried to copy.
= 考试时，老师的目光在一排排课桌上扫视，留意有没有人想抄。
eyes rove over something = 目光在某处扫视

rover
/ˈrəʊvə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：（文学）漫游者、流浪者；（太空）探测车：在月球或火星表面行驶的遥控车辆，如 a Mars rover。
① He was a born rover who never stayed in one town for more than a few months.
= 他天生是个漂泊的人，在哪个镇上都待不过几个月。
a rover = 漫游者
② Scientists sent a new rover to Mars to search for signs of water under the red dust.
= 科学家向火星送去一辆新的探测车，在红色尘土下寻找水的迹象。
a Mars rover = 火星车

roving
/ˈrəʊvɪŋ/

row
/rəʊ/（排、划船）/raʊ/（争吵）
核心：两个读音、两个词。
读 /rəʊ/：名词，可数：（人、物排成的）一排、一行：a row of、in a row；（剧场的）一排座位：the front row；（表格的）一行：rows and columns；in a row 也指接连、连续。动词：划（船）：row a boat；划船载送。
读 /raʊ/（英式口语）：名词，可数：争吵、吵架：have a row；（公开的）争议：a row over something；吵闹声：make a row。动词，不及物：吵架：row with somebody。
① The children sat in a row on the wooden bench outside the school, waiting for the bus to arrive.
= 孩子们在学校外面的木长椅上坐成一排，等公共汽车来。
row = 排
sit in a row = 坐成一排
② We had seats in the front row, so we could see the actors' faces clearly during the whole play.
= 我们坐在第一排，整场戏都能清清楚楚看到演员的脸。
the front row = 前排
③ There was a long row of houses along the river, all of them painted in different bright colours.
= 河边有一长排房子，每座都刷着不同的鲜艳颜色。
a row of houses = 一排房子
④ Add a new row at the bottom of the table for each customer who signs up during the month.
= 这个月每有一位顾客登记，就在表格底部加一行。
a row in a table = 表格的一行
rows and columns = 行和列
⑤ Our team has now lost five games in a row, and the fans are starting to get very angry.
= 我们队已经连输五场，球迷们开始非常愤怒了。
in a row = 连续
⑥ We took turns to row the little boat across the lake to the island where we had our picnic.
= 我们轮流划着小船过湖，到岛上去野餐。
row = 划船
row a boat across = 划船渡过
⑦ My neighbours had a terrible row last night, and I could hear them shouting through the wall.
= 我的邻居昨晚大吵了一架，我隔着墙都能听见他们在喊。
row = 争吵
have a row = 吵架
⑧ The government is in the middle of a row over its plans to close three local hospitals.
= 政府正因关闭三家地方医院的计划陷入一场争议。
a row over something = 关于某事的争议
⑨ She often rowed with her brother about which of them should look after their elderly mother.
= 她常和哥哥为谁来照顾年迈的母亲吵架。
row with somebody = 和某人吵架
⑩ Stop making such a terrible row in the kitchen, boys, because the baby is trying to sleep upstairs!
= 孩子们，别在厨房里那么吵了，宝宝正在楼上睡觉呢！
make a row = 吵闹

royal

= 我祖父战时是皇家空军的飞行员，多次飞越德国上空。

④ The newspapers are full of stories about the royals and what they wore to the wedding.
= 报纸上满是关于王室成员和他们在婚礼上穿什么的报道。
the royals = 王室成员
⑤ The team was given a royal welcome when they arrived home with the cup.
= 球队捧着奖杯回到家乡时受到了盛大的欢迎。

⑥ Fixing the old car every weekend has been a royal pain, and I wish I had sold it years ago.

⑦ The visitors were royally entertained with music, dancing and a feast that lasted until midnight.
= 客人们受到了隆重款待，有音乐、舞蹈，还有一场一直吃到半夜的宴席。

royally = 盛大地、极其
⑧ The writer still earns royalty payments from the book she published thirty years ago.
= 那位作家至今还从她三十年前出的那本书拿版税。

rub
/rʌb/
核心：动词：擦、揉、搓：rub your eyes、rub your hands together；（来回）摩擦：rub against；把（药膏等）抹上、揉进：rub cream into your skin；擦亮、擦掉：rub something clean；（鞋等）磨（脚）。名词，单数：擦、揉：give something a rub。
习语：rub shoulders with 是和（名人等）打交道、厮混；rub somebody up the wrong way 是惹恼某人；rub salt into the wound 是在伤口上撒盐；there's the rub（书面）是难就难在这里。
① She yawned and rubbed her eyes as she came downstairs for breakfast on Monday morning.
= 星期一早上她下楼吃早饭，一边打哈欠一边揉眼睛。

rub your eyes = 揉眼睛
② The old men stood at the bus stop, rubbing their hands together to keep warm in the frosty air.
= 老人们站在公交车站，在冰冷的空气里搓着手取暖。

= 那只猫蹭着我的腿呼噜呼噜叫，直到我给它吃的。
rub against something = 在某物上蹭

④ Rub a little of the cream into your skin twice a day until the rash goes away.
= 每天两次把一点药膏揉进皮肤里，直到疹子消退。
rub cream into your skin = 把药膏揉进皮肤
结构是 rub A into / on B：A 是油、膏、粉，B 是皮肤、肉、表面。
⑤ My new boots rubbed my heels so badly that I had blisters by the end of the walk.
= 我的新靴子把脚后跟磨得很厉害，走完一趟就起了水泡。

⑥ Give the table a good rub with a soft cloth, and the old wood will start to shine again.
= 用软布把桌子好好擦一擦，那老木头就又会发亮。
give something a rub = 把某物擦一擦
⑦ As a journalist in the capital, she rubbed shoulders with film stars and politicians every week.
= 作为首都的记者，她每周都和影星、政客打交道。
rub shoulders with somebody = 和某人打交道
⑧ He has a habit of rubbing people up the wrong way, so he is not very popular in the office.
= 他总是惹人恼火，所以在办公室里人缘不太好。
rub somebody up the wrong way = 惹恼某人
⑨ Losing the final was bad enough, but hearing the other team laugh at us really rubbed salt into the wound.
= 输掉决赛已经够难受了，听到对方嘲笑我们更是在伤口上撒盐。
rub salt into the wound = 在伤口上撒盐
⑩ We would love to move to the country, but there's the rub: neither of us could find work there.
= 我们很想搬到乡下去，可难就难在这里：我们俩在那儿都找不到工作。
there's the rub = 难就难在这里
这句来自莎士比亚，口气偏书面、带点戏谑。

rubber

= 这双登山靴的鞋底是厚橡胶做的，在湿石头上也不打滑。
rubber = 橡胶
be made of rubber = 橡胶制成
② Always wear rubber gloves when you use strong cleaning products in the kitchen.
= 在厨房里用强效清洁剂时一定要戴橡胶手套。
rubber gloves = 橡胶手套
③ Can I borrow your rubber? I made a mistake in my drawing and need to fix it before the lesson ends.
= 能借你的橡皮用一下吗？我画错了，得在下课前改好。
a rubber = （英式）橡皮擦

= 药剂师把避孕套放在柜台后面的高架子上。
a rubber = （北美口语）避孕套

the rubber = （桥牌等）三局两胜的一盘

rubberized
/ˈrʌbəraɪzd/
核心：形容词：涂了橡胶的、经橡胶处理的：防水、防滑或耐磨。英式也拼作 rubberised。
① The children wore rubberized raincoats that kept them completely dry during the long walk in the rain.
= 孩子们穿着涂胶雨衣，冒雨走了很久，身上一点没湿。
a rubberized raincoat = 涂胶雨衣
② The handles of the tools have a rubberized grip, so they do not slip out of your hand when it is wet.
= 那些工具的把手有涂胶握柄，手湿的时候也不会滑脱。
a rubberized grip = 涂胶握柄

rubberneck
/ˈrʌbənek/
核心：（口语，贬义）动词：伸长脖子看热闹，尤指开车经过事故现场时减速围观。名词，可数：看热闹的人。
① Traffic was moving slowly on both sides of the motorway because drivers were rubbernecking at the accident.
= 高速公路两侧的车都开得很慢，因为司机们都在伸着脖子看事故。
rubberneck = 伸长脖子看热闹
② A crowd of rubbernecks gathered outside the burning house and got in the way of the fire engines.
= 一群看热闹的人聚在着火的房子外面，挡了消防车的路。
a rubberneck = 看热闹的人

rubbernecker
/ˈrʌbənekə(r)/
核心：（口语，贬义）看热闹的人，尤指开车时减速围观事故的司机。可数。由 rubberneck 加 -er 构成。
① The police put up screens around the crash so that rubberneckers would stop slowing down to look.
= 警察在撞车现场周围竖起屏障，好让围观的司机不再减速张望。
rubberneckers = 围观的人
② One rubbernecker was so busy staring at the accident that he drove into the car in front of him.
= 有个看热闹的司机光顾着盯着事故看，撞上了前面的车。
a rubbernecker = 看热闹的人

rubbery
/ˈrʌbəri/
核心：形容词：像橡胶的、有弹性的；（食物）老得嚼不动的：rubbery squid；（腿）发软的：rubbery legs。
① The squid had been cooked for too long and was so rubbery that nobody could chew it.
= 鱿鱼煮得太久了，老得像橡皮，谁都嚼不动。
rubbery = 嚼不动的
② After finishing the whole marathon, my legs felt rubbery and I could hardly climb the stairs.
= 跑完全程马拉松后，我两腿发软，几乎上不了楼梯。
rubbery legs = 发软的腿
③ The toy is made of a soft rubbery plastic that is safe for babies to chew.
= 那个玩具是用一种软软的、像橡胶一样的塑料做的，婴儿啃了也安全。
rubbery plastic = 像橡胶的塑料
