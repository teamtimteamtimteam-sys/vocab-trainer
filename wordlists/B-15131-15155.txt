nautical
/ˈnɔːtɪkəl/
核心：航海的、与船和水手有关的。多作定语：nautical terms（航海用语）、a nautical chart（海图）。
也说带海洋风的装饰与衣着。nautical mile 是海里，约一千八百五十二米，并入本条。
① The novel is full of nautical terms that send most readers to the dictionary.
= 那部小说里满是航海用语，多数读者得去查词典。
nautical terms = 航海用语
② The captain spread a nautical chart on the table and marked the reef in red.
= 船长把一张海图摊在桌上，用红笔标出了那片礁石。
a nautical chart = 海图
③ The café has a nautical theme, with ropes, lanterns and a ship's wheel on the wall.
= 那家咖啡馆走的是航海风，墙上挂着缆绳、提灯和一只舵轮。
a nautical theme = 航海风
④ The lighthouse can be seen from twenty nautical miles away on a clear night.
= 晴朗的夜晚，二十海里之外就能看见那座灯塔。
a nautical mile = 海里

nautilus
/ˈnɔːtɪləs/
核心：两支，可数。一是鹦鹉螺：生活在热带海洋里的一种软体动物，外壳呈螺旋形，里面分成一个个小室；
二是它的壳，因螺线匀称常被拿来作图案与比喻。复数 nautiluses 或 nautili。
① The nautilus rises and sinks by changing the amount of gas in its shell.
= 鹦鹉螺靠改变壳里气体的多少来上浮和下沉。
a nautilus = 鹦鹉螺
② A nautilus shell cut in half shows a perfect spiral of small chambers.
= 把鹦鹉螺的壳对半切开，能看到由一个个小室排成的完美螺线。
a nautilus shell = 鹦鹉螺壳

navigate
/ˈnævɪɡeɪt/
核心：四支。一是导航、认路：靠地图、仪器确定路线，开车时指坐在副驾上指路；二是驾船、驾机航行于，或小心地穿过难走的地方；
三是在复杂的制度、局面里找到路子；四是浏览网站、在页面之间找路。
形容词 navigable 一是（河道）可通航的，二是（网站）好找路的；名词 navigability。并入本条。
① I will drive if you navigate, because I cannot read a map and steer at once.
= 你来看路我来开，我没法一边看地图一边握方向盘。
navigate = 看图指路
② Early sailors navigated by the stars and the colour of the water.
= 早期的水手靠星星和海水的颜色导航。
navigate by the stars = 靠星星导航
③ The pilot navigated the tanker through the narrow channel at high tide.
= 领航员趁涨潮把那艘油轮带过了狭窄的航道。
navigate a ship through something = 驾船穿过某处
④ We navigated our way through the crowd to the far side of the square.
= 我们从人群里一路穿过去，到了广场的另一头。
navigate your way through something = 一路穿过某处
⑤ The leaflet helps patients to navigate the benefits system after leaving hospital.
= 那份小册子帮助病人出院以后摸清福利制度的门道。
navigate a system = 摸清一套制度的门道
⑥ The site is easy to navigate, with a clear menu at the top of every page.
= 那个网站很好找路，每一页顶上都有清楚的菜单。
easy to navigate = 好找路的
⑦ The river is navigable for sixty miles above the port.
= 那条河从港口往上六十英里都可以通航。
be navigable = 可通航的
⑧ The dam improved the navigability of the lower river during the dry season.
= 那座水坝改善了下游河段在旱季的通航条件。
the navigability of a river = 河道的通航条件

near
/nɪə(r)/
核心：介词、副词、形容词、动词四种用法，底子都是「离得不远」。比较级 nearer，最高级 nearest。
介词：空间上靠近，时间上接近，也说数量、状态接近（near tears、near the end）；也可以说 near to，意思相同。
形容词：近的；作定语时多用最高级（the nearest bank），原级多见于 in the near future、a near relative；
还指差一点就是的（a near disaster）、几乎一样的（the nearest thing to）。
副词：come near、draw near 是走近、临近；nowhere near 是远远不；near enough 是差不多；
加在形容词前构成复合词：near-perfect、near-impossible。动词是（正式）接近、靠近。
习语：a near miss 是险些相撞、差一点成功；a near thing 是险胜、险些出事；so near and yet so far 是功亏一篑。
名词 nearness 并入本条；near-death experience 是濒死体验，也并入。
① They bought a cottage near the sea and walk on the beach every morning.
= 他们在海边买了座小屋，每天早上去沙滩散步。
near the sea = 靠近海
② Is there a post office near here that stays open on Saturdays?
= 这附近有没有星期六照常营业的邮局？
near here = 这附近
③ Do not sit too near to the fire in that woollen dress.
= 穿着那条羊毛裙，别坐得离火太近。
near to something = 离某物近
④ The roads are always busy near the end of the month.
= 快到月底的时候，路上总是很堵。
near the end of something = 快到某事的结尾
⑤ She was near to tears when the last train pulled out without her.
= 末班车没等她就开走了，她快要哭出来。
near to tears = 快要哭出来
⑥ Where is the nearest chemist that is open at this hour?
= 这个钟点还开着的药店，最近的在哪儿？
the nearest something = 最近的…
⑦ The firm plans to open a second factory in the near future.
= 那家公司打算在不久的将来开第二家工厂。
in the near future = 在不久的将来
⑧ Only near relatives were invited to the funeral.
= 只有近亲受邀参加葬礼。
a near relative = 近亲
⑨ The opening night was a near disaster, with half the lights failing.
= 首演之夜差一点砸了，一半的灯都不亮。
a near disaster = 差一点就是灾难
⑩ This cabin is the nearest thing to a home that he has ever had.
= 这间小木屋，是他有过的最接近家的地方。
the nearest thing to something = 最接近某物的东西
⑪ The children fell silent as the day of the exam drew near.
= 考试的日子一天天临近，孩子们安静了下来。
draw near = 临近
⑫ Do not come any nearer, because the ice is thin by the bank.
= 别再走近了，岸边的冰很薄。
come near = 走近
⑬ The work is nowhere near finished, and the guests arrive on Friday.
= 活儿远远没干完，客人星期五就到。
nowhere near = 远远不
⑭ There were five hundred people there, or near enough.
= 那儿有五百人，或者说差不多这个数。
near enough = 差不多
⑮ The two paintings are near-identical, and only an expert can tell them apart.
= 那两幅画几乎一模一样，只有行家分得出来。
near-identical = 几乎一模一样的
near-perfect = 近乎完美的
⑯ The ship was nearing the harbour when the engine failed.
= 那艘船快到港口的时候，发动机坏了。
near something = 接近某处
这里是动词，偏正式。
⑰ She is nearing retirement and has started to hand over her cases.
= 她快退休了，已经开始移交手上的案子。
near retirement = 临近退休
⑱ Two planes had a near miss over the airport in thick cloud.
= 两架飞机在浓云里险些在机场上空相撞。
a near miss = 险些相撞
⑲ We caught the ferry in the end, but it was a near thing.
= 我们最后赶上了渡船，可是好险。
a near thing = 好险的事
⑳ He led the race until the final bend: so near and yet so far.
= 他一路领跑到最后一个弯道，真是功亏一篑。
so near and yet so far = 功亏一篑
㉑ The nearness of the school was the main reason they chose the house.
= 离学校近，是他们挑中那座房子的主要原因。
the nearness of something = 离某物近
㉒ He described a near-death experience in which he floated above the operating table.
= 他讲述了一次濒死体验：他飘在手术台的上方。
a near-death experience = 濒死体验

nearby
/ˌnɪəˈbaɪ/
核心：两支。一是形容词，附近的，只作定语，放在名词前；二是副词，在附近，放在动词后或句末。
跟 near 的分别：near 后面要接地点，这个词后面不接宾语。
① The injured walkers were taken to a nearby hospital by helicopter.
= 受伤的徒步者被直升机送到了附近的一家医院。
a nearby hospital = 附近的医院
② Her parents live nearby and collect the children from school twice a week.
= 她父母住在附近，每周两次去学校接孩子。
live nearby = 住在附近
这里是副词，后面不接宾语。

nearly
/ˈnɪəli/
核心：副词，几乎、差不多：差一点但还没到。跟 almost 大体通用，但不能放在 no、nobody、nothing、never 前面
（要说 almost nothing）。not nearly 是远远不、差得远：not nearly enough。
very nearly 与 pretty nearly 是加强的说法。
① It took nearly three hours to drive forty miles in the snow.
= 在雪里开四十英里，花了将近三个钟头。
nearly three hours = 将近三个钟头
② I nearly missed the flight because the taxi went to the wrong terminal.
= 出租车开错了航站楼，我差点误了航班。
nearly miss something = 差点错过某事
③ She very nearly drowned as a child and has feared deep water ever since.
= 她小时候差一点淹死，从此怕深水。
very nearly = 差一点就
④ There is not nearly enough bread for twenty people.
= 给二十个人吃，面包远远不够。
not nearly enough = 远远不够
not nearly = 远远不
⑤ The bottle is nearly empty, so put olive oil on the shopping list.
= 瓶子快空了，把橄榄油记到购物单上吧。
nearly empty = 快空了

nearside
/ˈnɪəsaɪd/
核心：两支，英式，驾驶用语。一是形容词，（车辆）靠路边一侧的，在英国即左侧，只作定语；
二是名词，the nearside，车的这一侧。反义 offside。
① The nearside mirror was knocked off by a van parked too close to the kerb.
= 靠路边那一侧的后视镜，被一辆紧贴路沿停着的货车撞掉了。

= 车子靠路边的那一侧有一道长长的划痕。
the nearside = 靠路边的一侧

nearsighted
/ˌnɪəˈsaɪtɪd/
核心：两支，主要是美式，英式说 short-sighted。一是近视的；二是（比喻）目光短浅的。
名词 nearsightedness 并入本条。
① He is so nearsighted that he cannot recognise his own wife across the street.
= 他近视得厉害，隔着一条街认不出自己的妻子。
be nearsighted = 近视的
② Cutting the training budget was a nearsighted way to save money.
= 砍掉培训预算，是一种目光短浅的省钱办法。
a nearsighted way = 目光短浅的办法
③ Her nearsightedness was not noticed until she failed to read the board at school.
= 直到她在学校看不清黑板，才有人发现她近视。
nearsightedness = 近视

neat
/niːt/
核心：五支。一是整洁的、整齐的：neat and tidy；二是（人）爱整洁的、有条理的；三是（做法、说法）简洁巧妙的；
四是（酒）不掺水、不加冰的，英式说法，美式说 straight；五是（美式口语）很棒的。
副词 neatly 与名词 neatness 并入本条。
① She keeps her desk neat and tidy, with every pencil pointing the same way.
= 她把书桌收拾得整整齐齐，每支铅笔都朝一个方向。
neat and tidy = 整整齐齐
② He has small, neat handwriting that fits forty lines on a page.
= 他的字又小又工整，一页能写四十行。
neat handwriting = 工整的字
③ He is a neat worker who sweeps up before he leaves the site.
= 他干活利落，离开工地前总要扫干净。
a neat worker = 干活利落的人
④ It was a neat solution to a problem that had defeated three engineers.
= 那是个巧妙的办法，解决了难倒三位工程师的问题。
a neat solution = 巧妙的解决办法
⑤ He drinks his whisky neat and frowns at anyone who adds ice.
= 他喝威士忌什么都不加，谁往里加冰他就皱眉头。
drink whisky neat = 喝纯的威士忌
这一支是英式说法。
⑥ "We are going to the lake tomorrow." "That sounds really neat."
= 「我们明天去湖边。」「听起来真不错。」
neat = 很棒的（美式口语）
⑦ The shirts were neatly folded and stacked according to colour.
= 衬衫叠得整整齐齐，按颜色码放。
neatly = 整齐地
neatly folded = 叠得整整齐齐
⑧ The title neatly sums up the argument of the whole book.
= 那个书名把全书的论点概括得恰到好处。
neatly sum up = 概括得恰到好处
⑨ The teacher gave extra marks for the neatness of the diagrams.
= 老师因为图画得整洁，多给了分。
the neatness of something = 某物的整洁

neaten
/ˈniːtən/
核心：把…弄整齐、理一理。及物动词，常说 neaten something up。对象多是头发、衣服、纸张、边角。

= 开会之前，她对着电梯里的镜子理了理头发。
neaten your hair = 理一理头发
② He neatened up the pile of papers and squared it against the edge of the desk.
= 他把那叠文件理齐，靠着桌边码正。
neaten something up = 把某物理整齐
