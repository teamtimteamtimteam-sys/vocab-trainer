footage
核心：三支。一是影片素材、片段：archive footage；
二是按英尺算的长度、面积：square footage；三是（旧）胶片的英尺数。
① Archive footage of the flood runs behind the presenter for two minutes.
= 洪水的档案影像在主持人身后放了两分钟。
archive footage = 档案影像
② Security footage shows the van arriving twice on the same night.
= 监控录像显示那辆货车同一晚来过两次。
security footage = 监控录像
③ The square footage on the advertisement was measured generously.
= 广告上的面积英尺数量得很宽松。
square footage = 建筑面积

football
核心：四支。一是足球运动（美国指橄榄球）：play football；二是那只球本身；
三是被各方踢来踢去的议题：a political football；四是一场足球赛。
① They play football on a pitch that floods every winter.
= 他们在一块一到冬天就淹水的球场上踢球。
play football = 踢足球
② The football went over the fence and into the garden with the dog.
= 球飞过围栏，落进了有狗的那个院子。
a football = 足球
③ School funding became a political football and stayed one for years.
= 学校经费成了各方踢来踢去的政治皮球，一踢就是好几年。
a political football = 政治皮球
④ The football is on at three and the whole street goes quiet.
= 三点开球，整条街都安静下来。
the football is on = 有球赛
football boot = 足球鞋
football pools = 足球博彩

footballer
核心：三支。一是足球运动员：a professional footballer；
二是以踢球为业这个身份；三是青训、业余队里也这么称呼。
① A professional footballer here earns less than the club's press officer.
= 这里的职业球员挣得比俱乐部的新闻官还少。
a professional footballer = 职业足球运动员
② She was a footballer before her knee went and a coach afterwards.
= 膝盖伤了之前她是球员，之后当了教练。
be a footballer = 当足球运动员
③ Two young footballers were signed on the same afternoon.
= 两名年轻球员在同一个下午签了约。
sign a footballer = 签下球员

footballing
核心：三支（只作定语）。一是足球方面的：a footballing nation；
二是（人）踢球的：somebody's footballing days；三是打法上的：a footballing philosophy。
① A footballing nation of that size producing one good keeper is odd.
= 那么大的足球国家只出一个好门将，实在奇怪。
a footballing nation = 足球国家
② His footballing days ended in a car park in 1994.
= 他踢球的年月 1994 年结束在一个停车场里。
somebody's footballing days = 某人踢球的年月
③ The club has a footballing philosophy and no money to carry it out.
= 那家俱乐部有一套踢法理念，却没钱去实现。
a footballing philosophy = 踢法理念
