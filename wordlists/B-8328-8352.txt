exploded
核心：（技术）分解图式的：几乎只用在 an exploded view 与 an exploded diagram 里，
指把零件拉开画的示意图，好让人看清怎么装。
① The manual opens with an exploded view of the gearbox and no words at all.
= 那本手册开篇是变速箱的分解图，一个字都没有。
an exploded view = 分解图
② The exploded diagram shows nine screws and the box contains eight.
= 分解图上画着九颗螺丝，盒子里只有八颗。
an exploded diagram = 分解示意图

exploit
核心：三支。一是（贬）剥削人；二是（中性）充分利用、开发资源与机会；
三是名词，了不起的事迹，多用复数 exploits，重音在前。名词 exploitation。
① The agency exploits drivers who cannot read the contract they sign.
= 那家中介剥削那些看不懂合同就签字的司机。
exploit somebody = 剥削某人
② They exploited the gap in the timetable and ran two extra services.
= 他们抓住时刻表里的空当，加开了两班车。
exploit an opportunity = 抓住机会
exploit a resource = 开发一种资源
③ His wartime exploits fill two chapters and one cautious footnote.
= 他战时的事迹占了两章，外加一条谨慎的脚注。
exploits = 事迹、功绩

exploitation
核心：两支。一是（贬）剥削：the exploitation of migrant workers；
二是（中性）开发利用：the exploitation of natural resources。不可数。
① The report documents the exploitation of migrant workers on three sites.
= 那份报告记录了三个工地上对外来工人的剥削。
the exploitation of somebody = 对某人的剥削
② Commercial exploitation of the seabed needs a licence and a full survey.
= 商业开发海床需要许可证，还得先做一次完整勘测。
the exploitation of natural resources = 自然资源的开发

exploitative
核心：（贬）剥削性的：说条件、做法、关系占人便宜、不公平。
美式偶尔拼作 exploitive。
① The hours are legal, the rates are exploitative, and nobody stays past March.
= 工时合法，工钱却是剥削，没人待得过三月。
exploitative practices = 剥削性的做法
② An exploitative contract can survive a court and still lose every worker.
= 一份剥削性的合同也许打得赢官司，工人却一个都留不住。
exploitative = 剥削性的

exploiter
核心：（贬）剥削者：靠占别人便宜赚钱的人或公司。可数。
① The pamphlet names three exploiters and gets two of the addresses wrong.
= 那本小册子点了三个剥削者的名，其中两个地址还写错了。
an exploiter = 剥削者
② Exploiters of cheap labour move on as soon as the wage floor rises.
= 靠廉价劳力发财的人，工资底线一涨就换地方。
an exploiter of cheap labour = 靠廉价劳力发财的人
构词：exploit 加 -er

exploration
核心：两支。一是勘探、探险：oil exploration、polar exploration；
二是（正式）对题目、想法的探讨：an exploration of grief。
① Oil exploration in the bay stopped when the survey ship was recalled.
= 勘测船一被召回，海湾里的石油勘探就停了。
oil exploration = 石油勘探
polar exploration = 极地探险
② The book is an exploration of grief written entirely in short chapters.
= 那本书探讨的是悲伤，通篇由短章组成。
an exploration of something = 对某题目的探讨

exploratory
核心：探索性的、试探性的：多修饰 talks、surgery、drilling ——
先探探情况，不下定论。
① The two sides held exploratory talks and agreed only on the next date.
= 双方进行了试探性会谈，商定的只有下次的日期。
exploratory talks = 试探性会谈
② Exploratory surgery found nothing and the pain went on for months.
= 探查手术什么也没查出来，疼痛又持续了好几个月。
exploratory surgery = 探查手术

explore
核心：三支。一是勘察、到处走看；二是探讨、研究（想法、可能性）：
explore the possibility of doing something；三是习语 explore every avenue，
把每条路子都试一遍。名词 exploration 与 explorer。
① They spent the morning exploring the coast path and missed the only bus.
= 他们一上午都在探那条海岸小路，错过了唯一一班车。
explore a place = 勘察某地
② The council is exploring the possibility of buying the building outright.
= 议会在探讨把那栋楼整个买下来的可能。
explore the possibility of doing something = 探讨做某事的可能
③ The family says it will explore every avenue before selling the farm.
= 那家人说，卖掉农场之前会把每条路子都试一遍。
explore every avenue = 想尽一切办法

explorer
核心：探险家、勘探者：到没人去过的地方查看的人。可数。
① The explorer's diary lists the dogs by name and the men by number.
= 那位探险家的日记里，狗按名字记，人按编号记。
an explorer = 探险家
② Early explorers of the river drew maps that the ferrymen still argue about.
= 早年勘察那条河的人画的地图，摆渡人至今还在争。
an explorer of somewhere = 某地的勘探者
构词：explore 加 -r

explosion
核心：三支。一是爆炸：a gas explosion；二是激增：a population explosion；
三是感情的爆发：an explosion of anger。三支都可数。
① A gas explosion took out two shopfronts and neither of them was occupied.
= 一场煤气爆炸掀掉两间店面，两间当时都没人。
a gas explosion = 煤气爆炸
② The population explosion of the 1960s built every school in this street.
= 六十年代的人口激增，才有了这条街上所有的学校。
a population explosion = 人口激增
③ His explosion of temper cleared the room in under a minute.
= 他那顿脾气一发，不到一分钟屋里就空了。
an explosion of anger = 一顿火气爆发

explosive
核心：三支。一是名词，炸药：plastic explosive；二是形容词，爆炸的：
an explosive device；三是引申，一触即发的、迅猛的：an explosive situation、
explosive growth。副词 explosively。
① The dogs are trained to find explosives and to ignore everything else.
= 那些狗受过训，只找炸药，别的一概不理。
plastic explosive = 塑胶炸药
② An explosive device was made safe on the platform before the first train.
= 头班车之前，站台上一个爆炸装置被拆掉了引信。
an explosive device = 爆炸装置
③ The report landed in an explosive week and got two paragraphs.
= 那份报告赶在火药味十足的一周发布，只占了两段。
an explosive situation = 一触即发的局面

explosively
核心：两支。一是猛地一下、爆开似的：说动作又快又猛；
二是迅猛地：多跟 grow、rise 连用。
① The bottle opened explosively and covered the ceiling in foam.
= 那只瓶子猛地一开，泡沫溅了满天花板。
open explosively = 猛地爆开
② Demand grew explosively for two years and then fell off a cliff.
= 需求猛涨了两年，接着断崖式下跌。
grow explosively = 迅猛增长
构词：explosive 加 -ly

expo
核心：（非正式）博览会：exposition 的截短形，多指大型展销会或世博会。可数。
① The trade expo fills two halls and books out every hotel within a mile.
= 那场贸易博览会占满两个展厅，一英里内的旅馆全被订光。
a trade expo = 贸易博览会
② She met her business partner at an expo that neither of them wanted to attend.
= 她是在一场两人都不想去的博览会上认识生意伙伴的。
at an expo = 在博览会上

exponent
核心：三支。一是倡导者、代表人物：a leading exponent of something；
二是技艺的能手、演绎者：an exponent of the tango；
三是（数学）指数，写在右上角的那个小数字。
① She is a leading exponent of the plain-English school of drafting.
= 她是主张用大白话起草文书那一派的代表人物。
a leading exponent of something = 某主张的主要代表
② The best exponents of the style make the difficult bars sound easy.
= 这一风格最好的演绎者，能把难的小节弹得轻轻松松。
an exponent of something = 某技艺的能手
③ A small exponent above the bracket changes the whole calculation.
= 括号右上角一个小小的指数，能把整道运算都改掉。
an exponent = 指数

exponential
核心：两支。一是（数学）指数的：an exponential function；
二是日常用法，说增长快得吓人：exponential growth。副词 exponentially。
① Exponential growth looks flat for a while and then leaves the page.
= 指数式增长一开始看着挺平，随后就冲出了纸面。
exponential growth = 指数式增长
② The curve is exponential and the axis had to be redrawn twice.
= 那条曲线是指数型的，坐标轴改画了两回。
an exponential function = 指数函数

exponentially
核心：呈指数地、急剧地：多修饰 grow、rise、fall，
日常用来强调「越变越快」，未必真是数学意义上的指数。
① Storage costs fell exponentially and nobody deletes anything any more.
= 存储成本急剧下降，如今谁也不删东西了。
fall exponentially = 急剧下降
② The queue grows exponentially once the second till closes.
= 第二个收银台一关，队伍就成倍地长。
grow exponentially = 呈指数增长
构词：exponential 加 -ly

export
核心：三支，动词重音在后、名词重音在前。动词是出口：export something to somewhere；
名词是出口额或出口产品，多用复数 exports；电脑上也说把文件导出。
名词还有 exportation 与 exporter。跟 import 相对。
① The country exports timber and imports almost everything made from it.
= 那个国家出口木材，用木材做成的东西却几乎全靠进口。
export something to somewhere = 把某物出口到某地
② Exports rose for three quarters and the port still closes at six.
= 出口连涨了三个季度，港口还是六点关门。
exports = 出口额、出口产品
③ You can export the table as a spreadsheet and lose all the colours.
= 你可以把表格导出成电子表格，颜色会全丢掉。
export a file = 导出文件

exportation
核心：（正式）出口、输出这件事：法律与海关文书里用，日常说 export。不可数。
① The licence covers the exportation of seed potatoes and nothing else.
= 那张许可证只管种薯的出口，别的一概不管。
the exportation of something = 某物的出口
② Exportation without a permit carries a fine and a two-year ban.
= 无证出口要罚款，还要禁运两年。
exportation = 出口、输出
构词：export 加 -ation

exporter
核心：出口商、出口国：把货卖到国外的公司或国家。可数，
常说 the largest exporter of something。跟 importer 相对。
① The firm is the largest exporter of cut flowers in the region.
= 那家公司是这一带最大的鲜花出口商。
the largest exporter of something = 某物最大的出口商
② Exporters were warned about the new paperwork in a letter nobody read.
= 出口商收到过一封信，提醒新的报关手续，可没人读。
an exporter = 出口商
构词：export 加 -er

expose
核心：三支。一是使露出、使接触到：expose somebody to something；
二是揭露丑闻或真相：expose corruption；三是（摄影）曝光。
名词 exposure，形容词 exposed。
① Stripping the plaster exposed a beam that had been charred at one end.
= 把灰泥铲掉，露出一根一头烧焦的房梁。
expose something = 使某物露出来
② Children here are exposed to three languages before they can read.
= 这里的孩子还不识字，就已经接触了三种语言。
expose somebody to something = 让某人接触某事物
③ The article exposed a scheme that had run for eleven years.
= 那篇报道揭穿了一个运作了十一年的骗局。
expose corruption = 揭露腐败

exposed
核心：两支。一是（地方）无遮挡的、任凭风吹雨打的：an exposed ridge；
二是（人）没有保障、易受打击的：feel exposed。
① The cottage sits on an exposed ridge and loses tiles every winter.
= 那座小屋建在没遮没挡的山脊上，每年冬天都掉瓦。
an exposed hillside = 无遮挡的山坡
② Without that guarantee the smaller suppliers are badly exposed.
= 没有那份担保，小供应商就很没保障。
feel exposed = 觉得无依无靠

exposition
核心：三支。一是（正式）阐述、论述：把观点讲清楚的那一段文字；
二是（正式）博览会、展览会，口语截短成 expo；
三是（音乐）奏鸣曲式里的呈示部。形容词 expository。
① The first half is a clear exposition of the argument and the second half forgets it.
= 前半部分把论点阐述得很清楚，后半部分就忘了。
a clear exposition of something = 对某题目的清晰阐述
② The Great Exposition drew nine million visitors and left one iron tower behind.
= 那届大博览会引来九百万参观者，留下一座铁塔。
an exposition = 博览会
③ The exposition comes back after the development and the audience relaxes.
= 呈示部在展开部之后再现，听众这才松了口气。
the exposition = 呈示部

expository
核心：（正式）说明性的、阐述性的：多修饰 prose、writing、passage、style ——
以把事情讲清楚为目的，跟叙事、抒情的写法相对。
① The chapter is expository and the reader picks up the plot from a footnote.
= 那一章全是说明文字，情节反倒要从脚注里捡。
expository prose = 说明性文字
② Her expository style suits a manual better than it suits a memoir.
= 她那种阐述式的笔法，写手册比写回忆录合适。
an expository passage = 一段说明性文字

expostulate
核心：（正式、旧）抗辩、规劝：带着不满跟人讲理，搭配 expostulate with somebody。
名词 expostulation。日常场合说 protest 或 argue。
① The colonel expostulated with the porter for a full minute and then paid.
= 上校跟门房理论了整整一分钟，最后还是付了钱。
expostulate with somebody = 跟某人理论
② She expostulated about the seating and was moved to a worse table.
= 她就座位安排提了意见，结果被挪到更差的一桌。
expostulate about something = 就某事抗辩

expostulation
核心：（正式、旧）抗辩、规劝的话：不满地讲理的那一番言辞。可数不可数都行。
① His expostulations were noted in the minutes and ignored in the vote.
= 他那些抗辩记进了会议记录，表决时没人理会。
expostulation = 抗辩、规劝
② A single expostulation from the back row stopped the whole ceremony.
= 后排一句抗议，让整场仪式都停了下来。
an expostulation = 一句抗辩
构词：expostulate 加 -ion
