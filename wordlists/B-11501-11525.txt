hurricane
/ˈhʌrɪkeɪn/
核心：飓风 —— 大西洋、东太平洋上风速极高的热带风暴；在西太平洋同样的风暴叫 typhoon。
常带名字：Hurricane Katrina。复合词 hurricane lamp（防风灯）并入本条。
① The hurricane destroyed half the houses on the island.
= 那场飓风摧毁了岛上一半的房子。
a hurricane = 飓风
② Hurricane season runs from June to November, and the hotels are cheap.
= 飓风季从六月持续到十一月，这时酒店很便宜。
hurricane season = 飓风季
③ When the power failed, they lit a hurricane lamp and played cards.
= 停电后，他们点起一盏防风灯打牌。
a hurricane lamp = 防风灯
④ She burst into the room like a hurricane and knocked over a lamp.
= 她像一阵旋风似的冲进房间，撞倒了一盏灯。
like a hurricane = 像一阵旋风（比喻）

hurried
/ˈhʌrid/

= 我们匆匆吃了早饭，跑去赶火车。
a hurried breakfast = 匆忙的早饭
② He wrote a hurried note to his mother before he left for the airport.
= 去机场前，他匆匆给母亲写了一张便条。
a hurried note = 匆匆写的便条
③ She hurriedly hid the letter as her husband came in.
= 丈夫进来时，她急忙把信藏了起来。
hurriedly = 急忙地

hurry
/ˈhʌri/
核心：赶快、匆忙 —— 动词、名词同形（A 表教过）。
动词：hurry（赶快）、hurry somebody（催）、hurry up（快点）、hurry on（赶紧接着说／做）；
名词：in a hurry（匆忙）、in no hurry（不着急）、What's the hurry?（急什么？）。
跟 rush 的分界：rush 更急、更带「仓促、草率」的意思。
① If you hurry, you'll still catch the last bus from the end of the high street.
= 你要是抓紧，还能赶上主街尽头的末班车。
hurry = 赶快
② Hurry up, the film starts in ten minutes and we still have to buy the tickets!
= 快点，电影十分钟后就开始了，我们还得买票呢！
hurry up = 快点
③ Don't hurry me, I need time to think about it.
= 别催我，我需要时间考虑。
hurry somebody = 催促某人
④ She hurried out of the room without saying goodbye.
= 她没说再见就匆匆离开了房间。
hurry out of somewhere = 匆匆离开某处
⑤ Sorry, I can't stop to chat, I'm in a hurry to get to the bank before it closes.
= 对不起，我不能停下来聊，我得赶在银行关门前过去。
be in a hurry = 赶时间、匆忙
⑥ Take your time over the menu, we're in no hurry and the kitchen is open late.
= 菜单慢慢看，我们不着急，厨房开到很晚。
be in no hurry = 不着急
⑦ What's the hurry? The shop doesn't close until nine.
= 急什么？商店九点才关门。
What's the hurry? = 急什么？
⑧ She hurried on before anyone could interrupt her.
= 她赶紧接着往下说，不让任何人打断。
hurry on = 赶紧接着说（做）下去

hurt
/hɜːt/
核心：过去式、过去分词都是 hurt。四支（A 表教过前两支）：
一、弄伤、使受伤（hurt yourself、get hurt）；
二、疼 —— 主语是身体部位：My back hurts；
三、伤害感情、使难过：hurt somebody's feelings、be hurt、feel hurt；
四、损害、对…不利：The scandal hurt his chances。
口语 it won't hurt (somebody) to do something：做某事没坏处。
形容词 hurtful（伤人的）与副词 hurtfully 并入本条。
① He fell off the ladder and hurt his back.
= 他从梯子上摔下来，伤了背。
hurt something = 弄伤（身体部位）
② Nobody was badly hurt in the accident, but the car was a write-off.
= 事故中没人受重伤，但车报废了。
be hurt = 受伤
③ My feet hurt after walking round the museum all day.
= 在博物馆里走了一整天，我脚疼。
something hurts = …疼
④ I didn't mean to hurt your feelings, I was only joking.
= 我不是有意伤你的感情，我只是开个玩笑。
hurt somebody's feelings = 伤某人的感情
⑤ She felt hurt that nobody had invited her to the wedding.
= 没人请她参加婚礼，她觉得很受伤。
feel hurt = 感到受伤、难过
⑥ The scandal hurt his chances of becoming party leader.
= 那桩丑闻损害了他当党魁的机会。
hurt somebody's chances = 损害某人的机会
⑦ It wouldn't hurt you to say thank you once in a while.
= 你偶尔说声谢谢也没什么坏处。
it would not hurt to do something = 做某事没坏处
⑧ His comments about her weight were hurtful and completely unnecessary.
= 他对她体重的评论很伤人，而且完全没必要。
hurtful = 伤人的
⑨ "You never listen," she said hurtfully, and left the room.
= 「你从来都不听。」她伤人地说了一句，离开了房间。
hurtfully = 伤人地

hush
/hʌʃ/
核心：名词是一片寂静（a hush fell over…）；动词和感叹词是「嘘、别出声」。
形容词 hushed（压低的、寂静的）并入本条：in a hushed voice。
短语动词 hush up：掩盖、捂住（丑闻）；复合词 hush money（封口费）、
hush-hush（口语，秘密的）；美式 hush puppies 是一种炸玉米小丸子；
摇篮曲开头的 hush-a-bye baby 也收在这里。
① A hush fell over the room as the judge came in.
= 法官进来时，屋里顿时一片寂静。
a hush falls over something = …一片寂静
② "Hush, you'll wake the baby," she whispered.
= 「嘘，你会把孩子吵醒的。」她低声说。
hush = 嘘、别出声
③ They spoke in hushed voices outside the hospital room.
= 他们在病房外压低声音说话。
in a hushed voice = 压低声音
④ The company tried to hush up the accident, but a worker told the newspapers.
= 公司想把事故捂住，但一名工人告诉了报社。
hush up something = 掩盖、捂住某事
⑤ He was paid hush money to keep quiet about what he had seen.
= 他拿了封口费，对他看到的事闭口不言。
hush money = 封口费
⑥ The plans are all very hush-hush, and nobody outside the board knows.
= 那些计划都非常保密，董事会以外没人知道。
hush-hush = 秘密的（口语）
⑦ The fish came with a basket of hush puppies and a pot of coleslaw.
= 鱼配着一篮炸玉米丸子和一罐凉拌卷心菜。
hush puppies = 炸玉米小丸子（美式）
⑧ Her grandmother still sings hush-a-bye baby to every new child in the family.
= 家里每添一个孩子，她祖母还是会唱那首「宝宝快睡」的摇篮曲。
hush-a-bye baby = 宝宝快睡（摇篮曲）
