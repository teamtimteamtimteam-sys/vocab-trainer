scintilla
/sɪnˈtɪlə/
核心：名词，单数（正式）：一点点、丝毫：多用于否定：not a scintilla of evidence / truth。
① There is not a scintilla of evidence that he was anywhere near the house that night.
= 没有丝毫证据表明他那天晚上到过那座房子附近。
not a scintilla of something = 没有丝毫某物
a scintilla = 一点点
② The minister's speech did not contain a scintilla of truth, according to the opposition.
= 据反对党说，那位部长的讲话没有半点真话。
a scintilla of truth = 半点真话

scintillating
/ˈsɪntɪleɪtɪŋ/
核心：形容词：（谈话、表演）妙趣横生的、才华横溢的：scintillating conversation；（光）闪烁的。
① The dinner was wonderful, with good food and scintillating conversation until midnight.
= 那顿晚宴好极了，菜肴可口，谈话妙趣横生，一直聊到午夜。
scintillating conversation = 妙趣横生的谈话
scintillating = 妙趣横生的
② The young pianist gave a scintillating performance that brought the audience to its feet.
= 那位年轻钢琴家的演奏才华横溢，观众全体起立。
a scintillating performance = 才华横溢的演奏

scion
/ˈsaɪən/

① He is a scion of one of the oldest banking families in Europe.
= 他是欧洲最古老的银行世家之一的后裔。
a scion of a family = 名门后裔
a scion = 后裔、子弟

= 早春时从老苹果树上剪一根接穗，嫁接到年轻的根株上。
a scion = （嫁接用的）接穗

scissor
/ˈsɪzə(r)/
核心：动词（较少用）：用剪刀剪：scissor something out of a newspaper；（腿）像剪刀一样交叉开合。作定语（单数形）：剪刀式的：scissor hold 是（摔跤）剪刀式锁腿，scissor kick 是（游泳、足球）剪刀腿、剪式踢。
① She scissored the recipe out of the magazine and stuck it on the fridge.
= 她把食谱从杂志上剪下来，贴在冰箱上。
scissor something out = 把某物剪下来
② The wrestler held his opponent in a scissor hold until the referee stopped the fight.
= 那名摔跤手用剪刀式锁腿制住对手，直到裁判叫停。
a scissor hold = （摔跤）剪刀式锁腿
③ The striker scored with a spectacular scissor kick from the edge of the box.
= 那名前锋在禁区边缘一记精彩的剪刀脚破门。
a scissor kick = 剪刀脚、剪式踢

scissors
/ˈsɪzəz/
核心：名词，复数：剪刀：a pair of scissors。数一把要说 a pair of scissors，后接复数动词：The scissors are on the table。
① Can you pass me a pair of scissors? I need to cut this paper in half.
= 你能递给我一把剪刀吗？我得把这张纸剪成两半。
a pair of scissors = 一把剪刀
② The scissors are in the top drawer, next to the tape and the glue.
= 剪刀在最上面的抽屉里，挨着胶带和胶水。
the scissors are = 剪刀在……
scissors 形式是复数，动词也用复数。

sclerosis
/skləˈrəʊsɪs/

= 她姑姑患多发性硬化症二十年了，现在坐轮椅。

= 那种病会导致动脉硬化，使血液流动更困难。
sclerosis of the arteries = 动脉硬化

sclerotic
/skləˈrɒtɪk/

① The doctor said that the patient's arteries were sclerotic and needed treatment.
= 医生说病人的动脉已经硬化，需要治疗。
sclerotic arteries = 硬化的动脉
sclerotic = 硬化的
② The new leader promised to reform the sclerotic old system of government.
= 新领导人承诺改革那套僵化的旧政府体制。
a sclerotic system = 僵化的体制

scope
/skəʊp/

= 新经理想扩大公司的业务范围，把网上销售也包括进去。

③ There is still a lot of scope for improvement in the way the hospital treats its patients.
= 医院对待病人的方式还有很大的改进余地。

④ The job gives you plenty of scope to use your own ideas.
= 这份工作给你很大的空间发挥自己的想法。
scope to do something = 做某事的空间
