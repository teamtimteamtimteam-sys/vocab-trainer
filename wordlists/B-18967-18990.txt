reproduce
/ˌriːprəˈdjuːs/
核心：一是复制、翻印、复写（图片、文字）；二是再现、重现（声音、效果）：使再次出现；三是（动植物）繁殖、生殖，不及物。
形容词 reproducible 是可复制的、可再现的；名词 reproduction 是复制品、繁殖，可数也可不可数；形容词 reproductive 是生殖的、繁殖的。并入本条。
① The photograph has been reproduced in newspapers all over the world.
= 那张照片被世界各地的报纸转载了。
be reproduced = 被复制、转载
reproduce = 复制
② The new speakers reproduce the sound of the orchestra very clearly.
= 新音箱把乐队的声音再现得非常清晰。
reproduce the sound = 再现声音
③ Some plants reproduce by sending out runners along the ground.
= 有些植物靠沿地面伸出匍匐茎来繁殖。
reproduce = 繁殖
④ The results of the test must be reproducible in another laboratory.
= 测试结果必须能在另一个实验室里再现。
be reproducible = 可再现
reproducible = 可再现的
⑤ The painting on the wall is only a cheap reproduction of the original.
= 墙上那幅画不过是原作的廉价复制品。
a reproduction of something = 某物的复制品
reproduction = 复制品
⑥ The book explains the reproduction of fish and frogs in simple words.
= 那本书用简单的语言讲鱼和青蛙的繁殖。
the reproduction of something = 某物的繁殖
⑦ The doctor examined the woman's reproductive system.
= 医生检查了那个女人的生殖系统。
the reproductive system = 生殖系统
reproductive = 生殖的
