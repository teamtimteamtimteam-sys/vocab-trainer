① The boy shinnied up the flagpole in the school yard to get his kite back.
= 那个男孩顺着校园里的旗杆爬上去，把风筝拿了回来。
shinny up something = 爬上某物
shinny = 攀爬
② When the fire started, the workers shinnied down a rope from the roof of the warehouse.
= 起火时，工人们顺着绳子从仓库屋顶滑了下来。
shinny down something = 顺着某物滑下
③ After school the children played shinny on the frozen pond with sticks and an old tin can.
= 放学后孩子们在结冰的池塘上用棍子和一个旧铁罐玩简易曲棍球。
play shinny = 玩简易曲棍球
