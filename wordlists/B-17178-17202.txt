pluck
/plʌk/
核心：动词：拔（鸡、鸭的毛）；拔、修（眉毛）；（书面）采、摘（花、果）；拨（琴弦），美式说 pick；
猛地拉、拽；把……（从危险、默默无闻中）救出、提拔出来：be plucked from。
习语 pluck up (the) courage 是鼓起勇气；pluck something out of the air 是信口说出（一个数字、名字）。
名词：（旧式口语）胆量、勇气，不可数。形容词 plucky 是有胆量的、敢拼的，副词 pluckily。并入本条。
① She sat on the back step plucking a chicken for the pot.
= 她坐在后门台阶上给一只要下锅的鸡拔毛。
pluck a chicken = 给鸡拔毛
② She plucks her eyebrows into a thin line every Saturday.
= 她每个星期六把眉毛修成细细的一条。
pluck your eyebrows = 修眉毛
③ He plucked an apple from the tree as he walked past.
= 他走过时从树上摘了个苹果。
pluck an apple = 摘苹果
④ The harpist plucked the strings so softly that we had to lean forward.
= 竖琴手拨弦拨得那么轻，我们得探身去听。
pluck the strings = 拨弦
⑤ The child plucked at her mother's sleeve to get her attention.
= 那孩子扯了扯母亲的袖子，想让她注意自己。
pluck at = 轻轻地扯
pluck at something = 轻轻地扯某物
⑥ Three sailors were plucked from the sea by a helicopter.
= 三名水手被直升机从海里救了上来。
be plucked from the sea = 从海里被救起
⑦ It took him a week to pluck up the courage to ask her to dance.
= 他花了一个星期才鼓起勇气请她跳舞。
pluck up courage = 鼓起勇气
pluck up the courage to do something = 鼓起勇气做某事
⑧ The figure of two million was simply plucked out of the air.
= 两百万这个数完全是信口说的。
pluck something out of the air = 信口说出某事
⑨ It took real pluck to stand up and contradict the chairman.
= 站起来反驳主席，真得有胆量。
pluck = 胆量
⑩ The plucky little team held the champions to a draw.
= 那支敢打敢拼的小球队逼平了冠军。
plucky = 有胆量的、敢拼的
pluckily = 勇敢地

plug
/plʌɡ/

（发动机的）火花塞，同 spark plug；（口语）（在节目、文章里顺带做的）宣传、推荐。
动词：把（洞）堵上、塞住；填补（空缺、漏洞）：plug a gap；（口语）趁机宣传。过去式 plugged。
习语 pull the plug (on) 是终止（一项计划）、停掉（资金），本指拔掉插头。
复合词：plughole 是（英式）（浴缸、水槽的）排水孔：go down the plughole 是泡汤、白费。并入本条。
① The kettle will not work because the plug has come out of the wall.
= 水壶不工作，是因为插头从墙上掉了。
a plug = 插头
② She pulled out the plug and let the bath water run away.
= 她拔掉塞子，把洗澡水放掉。
pull out the plug = 拔掉塞子
③ The mechanic cleaned the plugs and the engine started at once.
= 修车工把火花塞擦干净，发动机马上就着了。
a spark plug = 火花塞
④ The author managed to get in a plug for his new book during the interview.
= 作者在采访中设法给自己的新书做了个宣传。
a plug for something = 给某物做的宣传
⑤ He plugged the hole in the pipe with a piece of rag.
= 他用一块破布把管子上的洞堵住。
plug a hole = 堵洞
⑥ The government borrowed more money to plug the gap in its budget.

⑦ She spent the whole programme plugging her latest film.

⑧ Is the printer plugged in and switched on?

plug in = 插上电源
plug something in = 给某物插上电源
⑨ You can plug the guitar straight into the computer.

plug into = 接到、接入
plug something into something = 把某物接到某物上
⑩ She has been plugging away at her thesis for five years.
= 她埋头写论文已经写了五年。
plug away = 埋头苦干
plug away at something = 埋头苦干某事
⑪ The bank pulled the plug on the project when costs doubled.
= 花费翻了一番，银行就停了那个项目的资金。
pull the plug = 终止、停掉
pull the plug on something = 终止某事
⑫ A year of work went down the plughole when the client changed his mind.
= 客户一变卦，一年的工夫全泡了汤。
the plughole = 排水孔
go down the plughole = 泡汤、白费
⑬ They live in a plug-ugly block of flats beside the motorway.
= 他们住在高速公路旁一栋丑得要命的公寓楼里。
plug-ugly = 丑得要命的

plumb

plus
/plʌs/
核心：介词：加、加上：Two plus two is four；外加、还有。名词，可数，复数 pluses：加号，也说 plus sign；（口语）好处、有利条件。
形容词：零上的、正的；（放在数字后）以上、多：twenty plus；（成绩）略高于：B plus；只作定语：有利的。
连词：（口语）而且、再说。
习语：plus or minus 是上下相差、误差在……之内；on the plus side 是从好的方面说。
① Two plus five is seven.
= 二加五等于七。
two plus five = 二加五
② The bill came to forty pounds plus service.
= 账单是四十英镑，外加服务费。
plus service = 外加服务费
③ He put a plus where he should have written a minus.
= 该写减号的地方他写了个加号。
a plus = 加号
④ The garden is a big plus for a family with young children.
= 对有小孩的人家来说，那个花园是一大好处。
a big plus = 一大好处
⑤ The temperature rose to plus three in the afternoon.
= 下午气温升到零上三度。
plus three = 零上三度
⑥ The work will cost ten thousand pounds plus.
= 那项工程要花一万英镑以上。
ten thousand pounds plus = 一万英镑以上
⑦ She got a B plus for her essay on the causes of the war.
= 她那篇谈战争起因的文章得了个 B 加。
B plus = B 加
⑧ The flat is too small, plus it is a long way from the station.
= 那套公寓太小，再说离车站也远。
plus = 而且、再说
⑨ The survey is accurate to plus or minus three per cent.
= 那项调查的误差在正负百分之三以内。
plus or minus = 上下相差、正负
plus-minus = 正负
⑩ On the plus side, the new job is only ten minutes from home.
= 从好的方面说，新工作离家只有十分钟。
on the plus side = 从好的方面说
⑪ The invitation says that each guest may bring a plus-one.
= 请柬上说每位客人可以带一位同伴。
a plus-one = 可带的同伴
⑫ The store has opened a new department for plus size clothing.
= 那家商场新开了一个大码服装部。
plus size = 大码的
⑬ The golfers in the old photograph are wearing plus fours and flat caps.
= 老照片里打高尔夫的人穿着灯笼裤，戴着鸭舌帽。
plus fours = 灯笼裤
