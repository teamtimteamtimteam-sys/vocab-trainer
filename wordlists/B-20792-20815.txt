/ˈʃeɪmfl/

① It is shameful that so many old people live alone without enough heating in winter.
= 那么多老人冬天独自生活、连暖气都不够，真是可耻。
shameful = 可耻的
It is shameful that… = …是可耻的
② The family kept his time in prison a shameful secret for many years.
= 一家人多年来把他坐过牢当成一个不光彩的秘密。
a shameful secret = 不光彩的秘密
③ The workers were shamefully treated, and some of them were not paid for months.
= 工人们受到可耻的对待，有些人几个月都没拿到工钱。
shamefully = 可耻地
