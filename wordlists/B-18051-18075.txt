pupil
/ˈpjuːpl/
核心：三支，可数。一是（英式）（中小学的）学生；二是（跟名师学艺的）弟子、门生；三是瞳孔：眼睛中间那个黑色的圆孔。
（英国）pupillage 是见习大律师的实习期，不可数，也可数。并入本条。
① The school has four hundred pupils between the ages of five and eleven.
= 那所学校有四百名五到十一岁的学生。
a pupil = 学生
a pupil at a school = 某校的学生
② The painter was a pupil of Rembrandt and worked in his studio for six years.
= 那位画家是伦勃朗的弟子，在他的画室里干了六年。
a pupil of somebody = 某人的弟子
③ The pupils of your eyes grow larger in the dark to let in more light.
= 在暗处，瞳孔会放大，好多进些光。
the pupils of your eyes = 瞳孔
a pupil = 瞳孔
④ She did her pupillage in a set of chambers in London.
= 她在伦敦的一家大律师事务所做的见习。
pupillage = 见习大律师的实习期
美式说 student，不说 pupil。

操纵木偶的人叫 puppeteer；木偶戏这门艺术叫 puppetry，不可数。并入本条。
① The children watched a puppet show in the park on Sunday afternoon.

a puppet show = 木偶戏
a puppet = 木偶

= 她用一只旧袜子和两颗扣子做了个布袋偶。

a string puppet = 提线木偶
③ The prince was only a puppet of the generals who had put him on the throne.
= 那位亲王不过是把他扶上王位的那些将军的傀儡。
a puppet of somebody = 某人的傀儡
a puppet government = 傀儡政府
④ The puppeteer stood hidden behind a black curtain above the little stage.
= 操纵木偶的人藏在小戏台上方的黑幕后面。

puppetry = 木偶戏艺术

purchase

pure

= 那枚戒指是纯金的，重二十克。
pure gold = 纯金
pure wool = 纯羊毛

= 那眼泉水很纯净，可以直接喝。
pure water = 纯净的水
pure air = 洁净的空气
③ We met again by pure chance at an airport ten years later.
= 十年后我们纯属偶然地在机场又碰上了。
by pure chance = 纯属偶然
pure luck = 纯粹是运气
④ The boy sang the solo in a high, pure voice.
= 那个男孩用清亮的高音唱了独唱。
a pure voice = 清亮的嗓音
a pure white = 纯白
⑤ The saint is said to have been pure in thought and deed.
= 据说那位圣徒心思行为都清白无瑕。
pure in thought = 心思清白
pure in heart = 心地纯洁
⑥ She gave up pure mathematics for a job in a bank.
= 她放下纯数学，去银行上了班。
pure mathematics = 纯数学
pure science = 纯科学
⑦ It was jealousy, pure and simple, that made him write that letter.
= 让他写那封信的，就是嫉妒，不折不扣的嫉妒。

⑧ The dogs are pure Labradors, with papers to prove it.
= 那些狗是纯种的拉布拉多，有证书为凭。
a pure Labrador = 纯种拉布拉多
名词是 purity。
⑨ The farmer breeds pure-bred cattle that win prizes at every show.
= 那个农民养的纯种牛每次展会都得奖。
pure-bred = 纯种的
也说 purebred。

③ I could not follow every word, but the purport of the speech was clear.
= 我没能字字听懂，但那篇讲话的大意是清楚的。
the purport of something = 某事的大意
这一支是名词，重音在前。
④ The police are examining the purported confession found in his room.
= 警方正在查验在他房间里找到的那份据称是供词的东西。
purported = 据称的、所谓的
⑤ The painting was purportedly bought in Paris before the war.
= 那幅画据称是战前在巴黎买的。
purportedly = 据称

purpose
/ˈpɜːpəs/
核心：名词：目的、意图、用途，可数：the purpose of、for the purpose of；purposes 是（某种）需要、用途，复数：for tax purposes；
（人生的）意义、目标，不可数：a sense of purpose；决心、意志，不可数。
习语：on purpose 是故意地；to no purpose 是（正式）白白地、毫无结果；serve a purpose 是有用、派得上用场；
for all practical purposes 是实际上、事实上，也说 to all intents and purposes；at cross purposes 是各说各的、互相误会；
for the purposes of 是为了……、就……而言；to little purpose 是没什么用。
派生：purposeful 是有目标的、坚定的，副词 purposefully，名词 purposefulness；purposeless 是没有目的的、无意义的；
purposely 是故意地；purposive 是（正式）有目的的。并入本条。
① The purpose of the meeting is to choose a new chairman.
= 开这个会的目的是选一位新主席。
the purpose of something = 某事的目的
a purpose = 目的、用途
② The building is no longer used for its original purpose.
= 那栋楼已经不作原先的用途了。
its original purpose = 原先的用途
for this purpose = 为此
③ He went to Paris for the purpose of buying paintings for the gallery.
= 他去巴黎是为了给画廊买画。
for the purpose of doing something = 为了做某事
④ The house is treated as an office for tax purposes.
= 为报税起见，那栋房子算作办公室。
for tax purposes = 为报税起见
for the purposes of something = 为了某事、就某事而言
⑤ Since she retired, she has lost her sense of purpose.
= 退休以后，她觉得日子没了奔头。
a sense of purpose = 目标感、奔头
give somebody a purpose = 给某人一个奔头
⑥ She walked into the room with an air of great purpose.
= 她一副打定主意的样子走进屋来。
with purpose = 坚决地
这一支说的是决心，不可数。
⑦ He broke the window on purpose to get into the house.
= 他是故意砸窗户好进屋的。
on purpose = 故意地
反过来是 by accident。
⑧ We argued with the manager for an hour, but to no purpose.
= 我们跟经理争了一个小时，却毫无结果。
to no purpose = 白白地、毫无结果
to little purpose = 没什么用
⑨ The old shed is ugly, but it serves a purpose in the winter.
= 那间旧棚子难看，可是冬天派得上用场。
serve a purpose = 有用、派得上用场
serve no purpose = 毫无用处
⑩ The two towns have grown together and are, for all practical purposes, one city.
= 那两座城已经连成一片，实际上就是一座城。
for all practical purposes = 实际上、事实上
to all intents and purposes = 实际上
⑪ We were talking at cross purposes, for I meant the son and she meant the father.
= 我们各说各的：我说的是儿子，她说的是父亲。
at cross purposes = 各说各的、互相误会
⑫ She walked with a purposeful stride towards the manager's office.
= 她迈着坚定的步子朝经理办公室走去。
purposeful = 有目标的、坚定的
a purposeful stride = 坚定的步子
⑬ He moved purposefully through the crowd towards the stage.
= 他目标明确地穿过人群朝舞台走去。
purposefully = 目标明确地、坚定地
⑭ There was a new purposefulness about the team after the change of manager.
= 换了主教练之后，那支球队有了一股新的劲头。
purposefulness = 目标明确、坚定
⑮ The film is full of purposeless violence that adds nothing to the story.
= 那部片子里满是无谓的暴力，对故事毫无帮助。
purposeless = 没有目的的、无谓的
⑯ He purposely left the door unlocked so that she could get in.
= 他故意没锁门，好让她进得来。
purposely = 故意地
⑰ The study treats play as a purposive activity and not as a waste of time.
= 那项研究把玩耍看成有目的的活动，而不是浪费时间。
purposive = 有目的的
这是正式、学术用词。

purr
/pɜː(r)/
核心：动词：（猫）发出呼噜声；（机器）发出低沉平稳的轰鸣；（人）轻柔而满足地说。名词，可数：呼噜声、低沉的轰鸣。
① The cat curled up on my knee and began to purr.
= 猫蜷在我膝盖上，打起了呼噜。
a cat purrs = 猫打呼噜
purr = 发出呼噜声
② The big car purred along the empty road towards the coast.
= 那辆大车低声轰鸣着，沿空荡荡的公路朝海边开去。
purr along the road = 低声轰鸣着沿路开
an engine purrs = 发动机平稳地响
③ "What a lovely surprise," she purred, taking the flowers from him.
= 「真是个惊喜。」她接过他手里的花，娇声说道。
purr = 轻柔而满足地说
④ I could hear the purr of the cat from the other side of the room.
= 我在屋子另一头都听得见猫的呼噜声。
the purr of a cat = 猫的呼噜声
the purr of an engine = 发动机低沉的声音

purse
/pɜːs/
核心：名词，可数：（英式）（女用的）钱包：装硬币、钞票的小包；（美式）女用手提包，英式叫 handbag；（一个机构能花的）资金、财力，单数：the public purse；
（拳击等比赛的）奖金。动词：purse your lips 是噘起嘴、抿紧嘴，表示不赞成。
习语 hold the purse strings 是掌管钱财、管着钱袋子。
① She took a few coins out of her purse and gave them to the child.
= 她从钱包里掏出几枚硬币给了孩子。
a purse = （英式）钱包；（美式）女用手提包
take something out of your purse = 从钱包里掏出某物
② She put her keys in her purse and slung it over her shoulder.
= 她把钥匙放进手提包，把包往肩上一挎。
put something in your purse = 把某物放进手提包
这一支是美式的意思。
③ The repairs to the bridge will be paid for out of the public purse.
= 修那座桥的钱由公款出。
the public purse = 公款、国库
这一支用单数。
④ The two boxers will fight for a purse of five million dollars.
= 那两名拳击手要争夺五百万美元的奖金。
a purse of five million dollars = 五百万美元的奖金
⑤ The old lady pursed her lips and said nothing when she saw the mess.
= 老太太看见那一片狼藉，抿紧嘴唇，什么也没说。
purse your lips = 噘起嘴、抿紧嘴
表示不赞成、不高兴。
⑥ In that family it is the grandmother who holds the purse strings.
= 那一家管钱的是奶奶。
hold the purse strings = 掌管钱财
tighten the purse strings = 紧缩开支

purser
/ˈpɜːsə(r)/

= 事务长把舱房钥匙和一张全船平面图交给了我们。
the purser = 事务长
② Passengers may leave their valuables with the purser during the voyage.
= 航行期间乘客可以把贵重物品交事务长保管。
