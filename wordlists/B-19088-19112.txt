restrict
/rɪˈstrɪkt/
核心：及物。一是限制、限定（数量、范围、程度）：restrict something to；二是约束、限制（行动、自由）：restrict somebody's movement。
分词 restricted 作形容词，见另条；名词 restriction 见另条。
① The new law restricts the sale of alcohol to people over eighteen.
= 新法律规定只能把酒卖给十八岁以上的人。
restrict something to somebody = 把某物限定于某人
restrict to = 限制在
② The town has restricted parking in the narrow streets of the centre.
= 那座城限制在市中心的窄街上停车。
restrict = 限制
③ The injury restricted his movement, and he could not play for a month.
= 那次受伤限制了他的行动，他一个月不能上场。
restrict somebody's movement = 限制某人的行动
④ There are strict restrictions on the number of fish you can catch.
= 对能捕捞的鱼的数量有严格的限制。
restriction = 限制
⑤ Entry to the building is restricted to staff only.
= 那栋楼只限员工进入。
restricted = 受限的
