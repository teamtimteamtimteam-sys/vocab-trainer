fire
核心：五支。一是火、火灾：put out a fire；二是（英式）取暖炉具：an electric fire；
三是开火射击；四是解雇：be fired；
五是一串习语：catch fire、set fire to、on fire、under fire、open fire、play with fire。
① It took nine minutes to put out a fire that had started in a bin.
= 一场从垃圾桶烧起来的火，扑了九分钟。
put out a fire = 扑灭火
make a fire = 生火
They made a fire on the beach and the tide put it out.
= 他们在海滩上生了火，涨潮把它浇灭了。
a house fire = 住宅火灾
A house fire two doors down emptied the whole terrace at three.
= 隔两个门的一场住宅火灾，凌晨三点把整排房子的人都腾了出来。
② The curtains caught fire and the smoke alarm stayed silent throughout.
= 窗帘着了火，烟雾报警器全程一声没响。
catch fire = 着火
③ Somebody set fire to the shed and left the door open behind them.
= 有人放火烧了那间棚子，走的时候门还敞着。
set fire to something = 放火烧某物
④ The whole hillside was on fire by four and out by midnight.
= 到四点整面山坡都在烧，午夜前扑灭。
on fire = 着火、在燃烧
⑤ The minister came under fire for a sentence she had not written.
= 那位大臣因一句不是她写的话挨了炮轰。
come under fire = 受到抨击
⑥ They opened fire on an empty ridge and the report says as much.
= 他们朝一道空无一人的山脊开了火，报告里也是这么写的。
open fire = 开火
⑦ Two people were fired and the third resigned before the meeting started.
= 有两个人被解雇，第三个在开会前就辞了。
be fired = 被解雇
⑧ Lending your name to that scheme is playing with fire.
= 把名字借给那个方案，是玩火。
play with fire = 玩火
⑨ The fire department sent two fire engines and a fire truck that would not start.
= 消防局派来两辆消防车，还有一辆打不着火的云梯车。
the fire department = 消防局（美式）
the fire service = 消防部门（英式）
The fire service was called to a chip pan and stayed for tea.
= 消防部门为一锅炸薯条的油被叫了来，留下喝了杯茶。
a fire engine = 消防车
A fire engine cannot turn into that lane and never could.
= 消防车拐不进那条小巷，从来就拐不进。
a fire truck = 云梯消防车（美式）
A fire truck reaches the ninth floor and the building has eleven.
= 云梯消防车够得到九层，那栋楼有十一层。
⑩ The fire chief runs three stations and the fire company at the harbour runs itself.
= 消防长官管着三个站，港口那支消防队自己管自己。
a fire chief = 消防长官
a fire company = 消防队
The village fire company is nine volunteers and one very old pump.
= 村里那支消防队，是九名志愿者加一台很旧的水泵。
a fire station = 消防站
The fire station opens its doors each August and the children come.
= 消防站每年八月开放，孩子们都来。
a firehouse = 消防站（美式）
A firehouse dog is a tradition that outlived the horses it started with.
= 消防站养狗是个传统，比当初拉车的马活得还久。
⑪ A fire hose froze to the fire hydrant and took two men to free.
= 一根消防水带冻在消火栓上，两个人才弄开。
a fire hose = 消防水带
a fire hydrant = 消火栓
Parking across a fire hydrant costs more than parking anywhere else.
= 横在消火栓前停车，罚得比停在任何别处都重。
⑫ The fire escape ends a floor above the ground and the building is a fire trap.
= 那道太平梯到二楼就断了，整栋楼就是个火灾陷阱。
a fire escape = 太平梯
a fire trap = 极易失火的房子
The club was a fire trap for eleven years and nobody closed it.
= 那家俱乐部当了十一年的火灾陷阱，也没人把它关掉。
⑬ The door is fireproof, the curtain is fire-retardant, and the fireguard is missing.
= 门是防火的，帘子是阻燃的，炉前围栏却不见了。
fireproof = 防火的
fire-retardant = 阻燃的
The costumes are fire-retardant and smell of it for three washes.
= 那些戏服是阻燃的，洗三回还带着那股味。
a fireguard = 炉前围栏
A fireguard goes up the week the baby starts crawling.
= 孩子开始爬的那一周，炉前围栏就装上了。
a fire screen = 炉前挡板
A fire screen keeps the sparks in and the heat mostly out.
= 炉前挡板把火星挡在里头，热气大半也挡在了外头。
⑭ Fire practice happens twice a year and the fire line gets repainted after each one.
= 消防演习一年两次，每次之后那条消防警戒线都要重画。
fire practice = 消防演习
a fire line = 消防警戒线
The fire line held on three sides and gave way on the fourth.
= 消防警戒线三面守住了，第四面没守住。
⑮ The fire-raiser was caught in April and fire-raising in that county carries seven years.
= 那名纵火者四月落网，在那个郡纵火要判七年。
a fire-raiser = 纵火者
fire-raising = 纵火
Two barns went up in a month and fire-raising was never proved.
= 一个月里烧了两座谷仓，纵火始终没能证实。
⑯ The fire-eater works for tips and buys a firelighter every Saturday.
= 那位吞火艺人靠打赏过活，每周六买一块引火块。
a fire-eater = 吞火艺人
a firelighter = 引火块
One firelighter and a dry newspaper beat any amount of blowing.
= 一块引火块加一张干报纸，比怎么吹都管用。
a fire starter = 引火物
A fire starter made of wax and sawdust lights in the rain.
= 蜡加木屑做的引火物，下雨天也点得着。
⑰ Firewood stacked against that wall dries in a summer and burns in a week.
= 靠那面墙码着的柴火，一个夏天晾干，一个星期烧完。
firewood = 柴火
⑱ Firelight from the fireplace reaches the fireside chair and stops there.
= 壁炉的火光照到炉边那把椅子，就到那儿为止。
a fireplace = 壁炉
firelight = 火光
Nobody looks their age by firelight and everybody knows it.
= 火光里谁都不显岁数，人人都清楚这一点。
the fireside = 炉边
Two chairs at the fireside and neither of them faces the television.
= 炉边两把椅子，哪把都不对着电视。
⑲ A firestorm of criticism followed a decision that had taken nine minutes.
= 一个只花了九分钟的决定，引来铺天盖地的批评。
a firestorm = 铺天盖地的风暴
⑳ The firewall blocks that site at work and the same site opens at home.
= 防火墙在单位挡住那个网站，同一个网站在家却打得开。
a firewall = 防火墙
㉑ Fire ants arrived with the topsoil and now own the entire lawn.
= 火蚁是随表土来的，如今整片草坪都归它们。
fire ants = 火蚁
㉒ The shop is having a fire sale and the fireworks are going at half price.
= 那家店在清仓甩卖，烟花半价出。
a fire sale = 清仓甩卖
a firework = 烟花
A single firework went off in November and frightened the whole street.
= 十一月里一枚烟花炸响，把整条街都吓着了。
firepower = 火力
Firepower settles the first hour and supply settles everything after that.
= 火力决定头一个钟头，之后全看补给。
㉓ One glass of that firewater ended the evening for two of them.
= 那种烈酒一杯下肚，他们里有两个的晚上就到此为止。
firewater = 烈酒（俗称）
㉔ Fire away — the meeting has nine minutes left and no agenda at all.
= 你尽管问 —— 这个会还剩九分钟，连议程都没有。
fire away = 尽管问、开始吧
㉕ He fired off three emails and then fired up the printer that always jams.
= 他连发三封邮件，又启动了那台老卡纸的打印机。
fire off = 迅速发出
fire up = 启动、点着
She fires up the boiler in October and nurses it until April.
= 她十月把锅炉点着，一路伺候到四月。
㉖ A firefly in that jar lasted one night and taught nine children something.
= 罐子里那只萤火虫活了一夜，教会九个孩子一件事。
a firefly = 萤火虫
㉗ A fireside chat on the radio reached more people than any speech in that hall.
= 电台里的炉边谈话，比那间礼堂里任何演讲传得都远。
a fireside chat = 炉边谈话
falsely shouting fire in a crowded theater = 在挤满人的剧院里谎喊失火（言论自由的经典界限）
Somebody always cites falsely shouting fire in a crowded theater by the second hour.
= 到第二个钟头，总有人搬出「在挤满人的剧院里谎喊失火」这一条。

③ There is no firm date yet and the invitations are already printed.

= 那家律所有九位合伙人，一部还能用的电梯。

first

① The first time she drove that road she went the wrong way twice.
= 她头一回开那条路，走错了两次。
the first time = 第一次
② At first the noise was funny and by Thursday it was not.
= 起初那声音还好笑，到周四就不好笑了。
at first = 起初
③ First of all, nobody asked; second, nobody minded.
= 首先，没人问过；其次，没人在意。
first of all = 首先
④ Seats are first come, first served and the queue starts at seven.
= 座位先到先得，七点就开始排队。
