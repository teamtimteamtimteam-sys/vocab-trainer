expensive
核心：贵的、花钱多的：既说价格高，也说代价大，如 an expensive mistake。
反义 cheap 与 inexpensive；副词 expensively。
① The camera is expensive to buy and surprisingly cheap to run.
= 那台相机买着贵，用起来倒便宜得出奇。
be expensive to do something = 做某事花费高
② Ignoring the survey turned out to be an expensive mistake.
= 不理会那份验房报告，结果成了代价高昂的错误。
an expensive mistake = 代价高昂的错误
③ Expensive tastes are easy to acquire on somebody else's holiday.
= 花着别人的钱度假，很容易养出奢侈的口味。
expensive tastes = 奢侈的品味

expensively
核心：昂贵地、花大钱地：多修饰 dressed、furnished、educated，
常带一点「钱花了未必值」的口气。
① The lobby is expensively furnished and the lifts break every winter.
= 那个大堂装修得很花钱，电梯每年冬天都坏。
expensively furnished = 装修得很昂贵
② He was expensively educated and still cannot boil an egg.
= 他上学花了大钱，到现在连鸡蛋都不会煮。
expensively educated = 受过昂贵教育的
构词：expensive 加 -ly

experience
核心：三支。一是经验、阅历（不可数）：work experience、from experience；
二是一次经历（可数）：a memorable experience；三是动词，亲身经历、感受到，
多见于 experience pain、experience difficulty 这类正式说法。形容词 experienced。
① She has ten years in the trade and knows from experience which suppliers ring back.
= 她入行十年，凭经验就知道哪些供应商会回电话。
work experience = 工作经验
from experience = 凭经验
② In my experience the second week of a strike changes the tone of the talks.
= 依我的经验，罢工到第二周，谈判的口气就变了。
in my experience = 依我的经验
③ Sleeping in the airport was an experience she describes but never recommends.
= 在机场过夜是一次经历，她讲归讲，从来不推荐。
an experience = 一次经历
④ Patients experience the pain differently depending on how the news was broken.
= 病人对疼痛的感受各不相同，要看消息是怎么告诉他们的。
experience pain = 感到疼痛

experienced
核心：有经验的、老练的：多作定语，如 an experienced nurse；
也说 be experienced in something。反义 inexperienced。
① An experienced skipper reads the sky and leaves the forecast on the table.
= 老练的船长看天色，把天气预报撂在桌上。
an experienced teacher = 有经验的老师
② She is experienced in negotiating with landlords and hates every minute of it.
= 她很有跟房东打交道的经验，可每一分钟都讨厌。
be experienced in something = 在某方面有经验

experiential
核心：（正式、教育学）体验式的、从亲身经历得来的：最常见的说法是
experiential learning，指靠动手做、不靠听讲来学。
① The course is experiential and the first lecture happens in week four.
= 这门课是体验式的，第一场讲座排在第四周。
experiential learning = 体验式学习
② Experiential knowledge is hard to write down and easy to demonstrate.
= 亲身经历得来的知识难写下来，演示起来倒容易。
experiential knowledge = 体验得来的知识

experiment
核心：名词与动词同形。名词是实验、试验，搭配 carry out 或 conduct an experiment；
动词是做实验、试新做法，搭配 experiment with something。
名词还有 experimentation，形容词 experimental。
① The class carried out an experiment with two candles and a jam jar.
= 那个班用两支蜡烛和一个果酱瓶做了个实验。
carry out an experiment = 做实验
conduct an experiment = 进行实验
② The bakery is experimenting with rye and has stopped taking phone orders.
= 那家面包房正在试着用黑麦，已经不接电话订单了。
experiment with something = 拿某物做尝试

experimental
核心：两支。一是实验的、试验性的：还在试用阶段，没定型；
二是（艺术上）先锋的、不照常规来的。副词 experimentally。
① The drug is still experimental and is given only under a hospital licence.
= 那种药还在试验阶段，只在医院许可下才用。
an experimental treatment = 试验性疗法
on an experimental basis = 以试验的方式
② Her experimental films have no dialogue and a very loyal audience.
= 她那些先锋电影没有对白，观众倒非常忠实。
experimental music = 先锋音乐

experimentally
核心：两支。一是试验性地：先小规模试试看，成了再推开；
二是试探地：动手时小心翼翼地试一下。
① The route was run experimentally for a term and then written into the timetable.
= 那条线路试运行了一个学期，之后写进了正式时刻表。
run something experimentally = 试运行某事
② He pressed the swollen ankle experimentally and decided against the walk.
= 他试探着按了按肿起来的脚踝，决定不去走那趟路了。
press something experimentally = 试探着按一按
构词：experimental 加 -ly

experimentation
核心：（正式）反复试验（这件事）：一遍遍试出办法的过程。不可数，
常配 years of experimentation、animal experimentation。
① Years of experimentation went into a recipe that takes nine minutes to cook.
= 一道九分钟就能做好的菜，背后是多年的反复试验。
years of experimentation = 多年的反复试验
② Experimentation on animals is licensed here and reported every spring.
= 这里做动物实验须经许可，每年春天还要上报。
animal experimentation = 动物实验

experimenter
核心：实验者、爱折腾新做法的人：既说实验室里的研究人员，也说凡事爱试一试的人。可数。
① The experimenter leaves the room and the recording keeps running.
= 实验人员离开房间，录音一直开着。
an experimenter = 实验者
② He is a lifelong experimenter and a very poor keeper of notes.
= 他一辈子爱试验，做笔记却糟糕得很。
a lifelong experimenter = 一辈子爱试验的人
构词：experiment 加 -er

expert
核心：三支。一是名词，专家：an expert on something 说的是研究某题目的专家，
an expert in something 说的是某个行当里的行家，an expert at doing something
则是做某件事的能手；二是形容词，内行的、专家给的；
三是引申用法 an expert eye、an expert hand，说的是行家的眼力与手艺。
名词 expertise，副词 expertly。
① She is an expert on medieval roofs and gets called out to three counties.
= 她是研究中世纪屋顶的专家，三个郡都请她去看。
an expert on something = 某方面的专家
an expert in something = 某个行当里的行家
an expert at doing something = 做某事的行家
② The report was sent for expert advice and came back with two sentences changed.
= 那份报告送去征求专家意见，回来只改了两句话。
expert advice = 专家意见
③ It takes an expert eye to tell the copy from the original at this distance.
= 隔这么远要分出赝品和真迹，得有行家的眼力。
an expert eye = 行家的眼力

expertise
核心：专门知识、行家本领：靠长期训练练出来的那一手。不可数 ——
不说 an expertise，要说 a level of expertise 或 technical expertise。
① The firm has the expertise and not the equipment, so the work goes abroad.
= 那家公司有本事没设备，活儿只好送到国外做。
technical expertise = 技术专长
have the expertise to do something = 有做某事的本事
② Her expertise in dating timber settled an argument that had run for years.
= 她在木材断代上的专长，了结了一场吵了好些年的争论。
expertise in something = 某方面的专长

expertly
核心：熟练地、老到地：多修饰 handled、boned、taken、played，说的是手上的功夫。
① The fishmonger expertly boned the trout while answering two phone calls.
= 鱼贩一边接两个电话，一边熟练地给鳟鱼剔骨。
expertly handled = 处理得老到
② The corner was expertly taken and then headed straight over the bar.
= 那个角球开得很老到，头球却顶过了横梁。
expertly taken = 开得漂亮
构词：expert 加 -ly

expiate
核心：（正式、书面）赎罪、补过：拿行动去抵消自己犯下的过错。
及物，宾语多是 sin、guilt、crime。名词 expiation。
① He spent thirty years trying to expiate a decision made in one afternoon.
= 一个下午做的决定，他花了三十年去赎。
expiate a sin = 赎罪
② Nothing in the settlement expiates the delay, and the family says so plainly.
= 那笔和解金抵不了拖延的过错，那家人也把话挑明了。
expiate guilt = 补赎罪愆

expiation
核心：（正式、宗教）赎罪、补赎：用苦行或善举去抵过错这件事。不可数。
① The pilgrimage began as expiation and turned into an annual holiday.
= 那趟朝圣起初是为赎罪，后来变成了一年一度的假期。
expiation = 赎罪
② Expiation of the debt took two harvests and most of the herd.
= 抵清那笔债，花了两季收成，还搭上了大半群牲口。
expiation of something = 对某事的补赎
构词：expiate 加 -ion

expiration
核心：两支。一是（多为美式，正式）期满、到期：合同或证件失效；
二是（医学）呼气，跟吸气相对。英式日常说 expiry。
① On the expiration of the lease the shop reverts to the family trust.
= 租约期满，那家店就归还给家族信托。
the expiration of a contract = 合同期满
an expiration date = 有效期限
② Each expiration is measured through a tube and printed onto a paper slip.
= 每一次呼气都通过一根管子测量，打印在小纸条上。
expiration = 呼气

expire
核心：两支。一是（证件、合同、期限）到期失效：the passport has expired；
二是（书面、委婉）咽气、去世。名词是 expiry 与 expiration，形容词 expired。
① Her passport expired in March and the appointment is not until June.
= 她的护照三月就到期了，预约却排到六月。
expire = 到期失效
a licence expires = 执照到期
② The old man expired at home with the curtains open and the radio on.
= 老人在家中咽了气，窗帘开着，收音机响着。
expire = 咽气、去世

expired
核心：过期的、失效的：多作定语，如 an expired ticket；
美式口语里也说 be expired，英式则说 has expired。
① The guard waved through an expired pass and lost his job over it.
= 门卫放行了一张过期的通行证，为这事丢了工作。
an expired passport = 过期的护照
② Expired medicines are collected by the pharmacy twice a year.
= 过期药品由药房每年回收两次。
expired medicines = 过期药品

expiry
核心：（英式）期满、到期：证件或合同失效这件事，不可数。
最常用的说法是 the expiry date；美式多说 expiration。
① The expiry of the contract caught both sides without a plan.
= 合同到期，两边都没备下一步的打算。
the expiry of something = 某物的期满
② The expiry date on the packet has been rubbed off by the freezer.
= 包装上的保质期日期让冰柜蹭掉了。
the expiry date = 保质期、有效期

explain
核心：三支。一是解释、说明：explain something to somebody ——
介词用 to，不能说 explain somebody something；二是说明原因、解释得通；
三是两个习语：explain yourself 是把自己的所作所为交代清楚，
explain away 是把不利的事轻描淡写地圆过去。名词 explanation。
① Could you explain the charge to me before I sign anything at all?
= 我签任何字之前，你能不能把这笔费用解释给我听？
explain something to somebody = 向某人解释某事
② One late lorry explains the queue and not the missing paperwork.
= 一辆晚到的货车解释得了排队，解释不了缺的那些单据。
explain something = 说明某事的原因
③ He was asked to explain himself and talked instead about the weather.
= 有人要他交代清楚，他却扯起了天气。
explain yourself = 把自己的行为交代清楚
④ The manager tried to explain away three missing invoices and made it worse.
= 经理想把三张不见的发票搪塞过去，反倒越描越黑。
explain away = 搪塞过去、圆过去

explainer
核心：（新闻业用语）解释性报道：把复杂的事拆开讲清楚的文章或短片；
也指展馆、部门里负责讲解的人。可数。
① The paper ran an explainer on the new tariffs and doubled its weekend traffic.
= 那家报纸发了一篇关税解读，周末流量翻了一番。
an explainer = 一篇解释性报道
② She is the department's explainer and gets sent on every school visit.
= 她是部里负责讲解的人，每次进校园都派她去。
the department's explainer = 部门里负责讲解的人

explanation
核心：解释、说明：可数时指给出的那个说法，an explanation for something 或
an explanation of something；不可数时指解释这个行为，如 by way of explanation。
① The only explanation for the missing hour is a clock that nobody reset.
= 那消失的一个钟头，唯一说得通的解释是有只钟没人调过。
an explanation for something = 对某事的解释
② His explanation of how the lock works took longer than picking it.
= 他讲那把锁怎么使，比撬开它还费时间。
an explanation of something = 对某物的说明
③ By way of explanation he simply pointed at the flooded cellar.
= 作为解释，他只是指了指淹了水的地窖。
by way of explanation = 作为解释

explanatory
核心：解释性的、说明用的：修饰 note、leaflet、booklet、text，
指专为把事情讲明白而写的那类东西。
① An explanatory note at the foot of the bill runs to four lines of jargon.
= 账单底下那条说明，写了四行行话。
an explanatory note = 说明性注释
② The museum hands out an explanatory leaflet that most visitors leave behind.
= 博物馆发一份说明单张，多数参观者转手就丢下了。
an explanatory leaflet = 说明单张

expletive
核心：（正式）粗话、脏字：书面报道里用来指人脱口骂出的那个词。可数，
常见说法 deleted expletive、an expletive-laden rant。
① The transcript replaces every expletive with a dash and loses half the meeting.
= 那份记录把每句粗话都换成破折号，会议的意思丢了一半。
an expletive = 一句粗话
② He muttered an expletive at the printer and then apologised to the intern.
= 他冲着打印机低声骂了一句，随后跟实习生道了歉。
mutter an expletive = 低声骂一句粗话
