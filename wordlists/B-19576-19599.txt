rudiments
/ˈruːdɪmənts/
核心：名词，复数（正式）：基础、基本原理：the rudiments of a subject；雏形、萌芽。常说 learn the rudiments of。
① My uncle taught me the rudiments of sailing during one summer on the lake when I was ten.
= 我十岁那年夏天，叔叔在湖上教了我驾帆船的基本功。
the rudiments of something = 某事的基础
② The children learn the rudiments of grammar in their first two years at school.
= 孩子们上学头两年学语法的基础知识。
learn the rudiments = 学基础知识

rue
/ruː/
核心：动词（旧式或文学）：后悔、懊悔：rue the day 是后悔那一天、追悔莫及。名词，不可数：芸香：一种叶子味苦的草本植物。形容词 rueful 是懊悔的（另立条）。
① He will rue the day he decided to sell the family farm to those developers.
= 他会后悔当初决定把家里的农场卖给那些开发商的。
rue the day = 后悔那一天、追悔莫及
② She rued her decision to leave her coat at home as the snow began to fall more heavily.
= 雪越下越大，她懊悔自己把外套留在了家里。
rue something = 懊悔某事
③ The old herb garden grows rue, sage and thyme in neat beds beside the kitchen door.
= 那个老香草园在厨房门边整齐的苗床里种着芸香、鼠尾草和百里香。
rue = 芸香

rueful
/ˈruːfl/
核心：形容词：（带点自嘲的）懊悔的、无奈的：a rueful smile。副词 ruefully 是懊悔地、苦笑着。
① He gave a rueful smile and admitted that he had forgotten to book the tickets.
= 他无奈地笑了笑，承认自己忘了订票。
a rueful smile = 无奈的苦笑
常修饰 smile、laugh、grin、look，带「自认倒霉」的味道。
rueful = 懊悔的
② "I suppose I should have listened to you," she said ruefully, looking at the broken window.
= 「我想我当初该听你的。」她看着那扇破窗，懊悔地说。
ruefully = 懊悔地

ruff
/rʌf/

① In the old portrait, the merchant wears a black coat and a stiff white ruff around his neck.
= 那幅老肖像里，商人穿着黑外套，脖子上围着一圈挺括的白色轮状皱领。
a ruff = 轮状皱领
② The male bird raises the ruff of feathers around its neck to attract a female.
= 雄鸟竖起颈部那圈翎毛来吸引雌鸟。
a ruff of feathers = 一圈颈羽

a little ruffian = 小坏蛋

ruffle
/ˈrʌfl/
核心：动词：弄乱、弄皱（头发、羽毛、水面）：ruffle somebody's hair；使恼火、使不安：ruffle somebody、ruffle feathers。名词，可数：（衣服上的）褶边、荷叶边。形容词 ruffled 是凌乱的、恼火的（另立条）。
① Her father ruffled her hair and told her not to worry about the exam.

= 好像什么都惹不恼她；哪怕别人都在嚷，她也很镇定。

④ The new manager ruffled a few feathers when he changed the way the office worked.
= 新经理改变了办公室的工作方式，惹恼了一些人。
ruffle feathers = 惹恼人
从鸟受惊竖起羽毛引申而来。
⑤ She wore a white blouse with a ruffle down the front and at the end of each sleeve.
= 她穿着一件白衬衫，前襟和袖口都有荷叶边。
a ruffle = 荷叶边

rug

rugged

ruin
/ˈruːɪn/
核心：动词：毁坏、彻底毁掉：ruin a holiday、ruin somebody's life；使破产、使身败名裂：be ruined。和可以修好的 damage 不同，ruin 指毁得无法挽回。
名词：毁灭、毁坏（不可数）：fall into ruin；破产（不可数）：face ruin；废墟、遗迹（可数，多用复数）：the ruins of a castle、in ruins；（某人）毁灭的根源：be the ruin of somebody。
习语：go to rack and ruin 是（因无人照管）破败不堪。名词 ruination、形容词 ruined、ruinous 另立条。
① The rain ruined our picnic, so we ate our sandwiches in the car instead.
= 雨毁了我们的野餐，我们只好在车里吃三明治。
ruin = 毁坏
② One careless mistake can ruin your whole career if you work in a bank.
= 在银行工作，一次粗心的失误就能彻底毁掉你的整个职业生涯。
ruin = 彻底毁掉
近义对照：
damage = 损坏（可修）
③ Many farmers were ruined when the floods destroyed their crops two years in a row.
= 洪水连续两年毁了庄稼，许多农民因此破产。
be ruined = 破产
④ After the earthquake, the old part of the city was left in ruins.
= 地震过后，老城区成了一片废墟。
in ruins = 成废墟
⑤ Every summer, tourists climb the hill to visit the ruins of the castle.
= 每年夏天，游客都爬上山去参观那座城堡的遗迹。
the ruins of a castle = 城堡遗迹
⑥ The small family company faced financial ruin after losing the long court case against its supplier.
= 那家小家族企业和供应商打了很久的官司，输了以后面临破产。
financial ruin = 破产
⑦ Gambling was the ruin of him; he lost his house, his family and all his friends.
= 赌博毁了他；他输掉了房子、家庭和所有朋友。
be the ruin of somebody = 是某人毁灭的根源
⑧ Nobody has lived in the farmhouse for years, and it has gone to rack and ruin.
= 那座农舍多年没人住，已经破败不堪。
go to rack and ruin = 破败不堪

rule
/ruːl/
核心：名词：规则、规定（可数）：follow the rules、break the rules、bend the rules；惯例、常规（单数）：the rule rather than the exception；统治、管辖（不可数）：under British rule、the rule of law；（旧）直尺。
动词：统治、管辖：rule a country；（法庭、官员）裁定：rule that…、rule on something；（感情等）支配：be ruled by；在纸上画（直线）。
习语：as a rule 是通常；rule of thumb 是经验法则；rule the roost 是当家作主、说了算。名词 ruler、ruling，形容词 ruled 另立条。
① All players must follow the rules, and anyone who argues with the referee will be sent off.
= 所有球员都必须守规矩，谁跟裁判争吵就会被罚下场。
follow the rules = 守规矩
② The boy was sent home because he had broken the school rules about mobile phones.
= 那个男孩违反了学校关于手机的规定，被打发回家了。
break the rules = 违反规定
③ The teacher sometimes bends the rules and lets us leave early on Friday afternoons.
= 老师有时会通融一下，让我们星期五下午早点走。
bend the rules = 通融、变通规定
④ As a rule, I go to bed at eleven and get up at seven, even at weekends.
= 我通常十一点睡、七点起，周末也一样。
as a rule = 通常
多放在句首，后面跟逗号，说的是自己或别人的习惯。
⑤ The country was under British rule for nearly two hundred years before it became independent in 1947.
= 那个国家在英国统治下将近两百年，一九四七年才独立。
under British rule = 在英国统治下
rule = 统治
⑥ The queen ruled the country for over sixty years and was loved by most of her people.
= 女王统治那个国家六十多年，深受大多数臣民爱戴。
rule a country = 统治一个国家
⑦ The judge ruled that the evidence could not be used because the police had obtained it illegally.
= 法官裁定那项证据不能采用，因为警方是非法取得的。
rule that = 裁定
⑧ In a democracy, nobody is above the law, and the rule of law protects everyone.
= 在民主社会里，没有人凌驾于法律之上，法治保护每一个人。
the rule of law = 法治
⑨ A good rule of thumb is to drink a glass of water every time you have a cup of coffee.
= 一条好用的经验法则是：每喝一杯咖啡就喝一杯水。
rule of thumb = 经验法则
⑩ In our house it is my grandmother who rules the roost, and nobody argues with her.
= 在我们家，说了算的是祖母，谁也不跟她争。
rule the roost = 当家作主、说了算
⑪ In this city, long working hours are the rule rather than the exception.
= 在这座城市里，长时间工作是常态而不是例外。
the rule rather than the exception = 常态而非例外
⑫ Don't let your heart rule your head when you are deciding which house to buy.
= 决定买哪套房子时，别让感情支配理智。
let your heart rule your head = 感情用事
⑬ The teacher showed us how to rule off the end of each piece of work with a straight line.
= 老师教我们每做完一份作业就在末尾用直尺画一道线隔开。
rule off = （在纸上）画线隔开
老派的学校用语，指写完一段后画横线收尾。
⑭ The police have not ruled out the possibility that the fire was started on purpose.
= 警方没有排除火是有人故意放的可能性。
rule out = 排除（可能性）
常和 possibility、option、chance 搭配，多用否定：not rule out。
⑮ His knee injury will rule him out of the next three games, and possibly the rest of the season.
= 他的膝伤让他无法参加接下来的三场比赛，甚至可能缺席本赛季余下的比赛。
rule out of = 使不能参加
结构是 rule somebody out of something，多见于体育新闻。

③ It's a rum business, this; nobody seems to know where the money from the village fund has gone.
= 这事真蹊跷；村里那笔基金的钱去了哪儿，好像谁都不知道。
a rum business = 蹊跷事
这一义已显老派，只在英式口语里偶尔听到。

rumba
/ˈrʌmbə/
核心：伦巴舞：节奏感强、源自古巴的舞蹈；也指它的舞曲。可数，也可不可数。也拼作 rhumba。
① My grandparents still dance the rumba together every Saturday night at the village hall.
= 我祖父母至今每个星期六晚上还一起在村礼堂跳伦巴。
dance the rumba = 跳伦巴
rhumba = 伦巴舞（rumba 的另一种拼法）
② The band played a slow rumba, and the couples moved closer together on the dance floor.
= 乐队奏起一支慢伦巴，舞池里的一对对舞伴贴得更近了。
a slow rumba = 慢伦巴舞曲

rumble
/ˈrʌmbl/
核心：动词：发出隆隆声、轰隆作响：thunder rumbles、my stomach is rumbling；（车辆）隆隆驶过：rumble past；（英式口语）识破、看穿：be rumbled。名词，可数：隆隆声：a rumble of thunder；（北美旧式俚语）街头群殴。
① Thunder rumbled in the distance as we hurried to put up the tent before the storm arrived.
= 远处雷声隆隆，我们赶在暴风雨来之前忙着把帐篷搭起来。
thunder rumbles = 雷声隆隆
本义就是这种低沉、连续、像在远处滚动的声音，雷声、车流、肚子饿都能用。
② My stomach rumbled loudly in the middle of the exam because I had not eaten any breakfast.
= 我没吃早饭，考试考到一半肚子咕咕叫得很响。
your stomach rumbles = 肚子咕咕叫
③ Heavy lorries rumbled past our house all night on their way to the new port.
= 重型卡车整夜从我们家门前隆隆驶过，开往新港口。
rumble past = 隆隆驶过
④ We heard the low rumble of traffic from the motorway even with all the windows closed.
= 即使窗户全关着，我们也听得见高速公路上车流低沉的隆隆声。
the rumble of traffic = 车流的隆隆声
⑤ The thieves had been selling fake tickets for months before they were finally rumbled by the police.
= 那伙小偷卖了好几个月假票，最后才被警察识破。
be rumbled = 被识破
这一义是英式口语，多用被动，指骗局、秘密被人看穿。
⑥ The argument about the new airport has rumbled on for years without any decision being made.
= 关于新机场的争论已经拖了好几年，一直没有定论。
rumble on = （争论、纠纷）持续不断、拖下去
多用于英式新闻，带「迟迟不解决」的意味。
⑦ The car shook as its wheels went over the rumble strip at the edge of the motorway.
= 车轮压过高速公路边缘的减速震动带时，车身抖了一下。
a rumble strip = 减速震动带
路面上一道道凸起的条纹，车压上去会震动、发出隆隆声，提醒司机。

rumbling
/ˈrʌmblɪŋ/
核心：名词，可数：隆隆声；（多用复数）（不满的）传言、牢骚：rumblings of discontent；（事情的）先兆。
① We could hear the rumbling of the trains through the walls of our cheap little flat by the railway.
= 我们住在铁路边那套便宜的小公寓里，隔着墙都能听见火车的隆隆声。
the rumbling of trains = 火车的隆隆声
② There have been rumblings of discontent among the nurses about their long hours and low pay.
= 护士们对工时长、薪水低已经有了不满的牢骚。
rumblings of discontent = 不满的声音
③ The first rumblings of the crisis were heard in the summer, months before the banks collapsed.
= 早在银行倒闭前几个月的夏天，危机的先兆就已经出现了。
the first rumblings of something = 某事的先兆
