multifarious
/ˌmʌltiˈfeəriəs/
核心：（正式）各式各样的、五花八门的：种类极多。书面语，常修饰 activities、duties。
① The vicar somehow finds time for his multifarious duties in three parishes.
= 那位牧师不知怎么总能抽出时间，应付三个教区里五花八门的职责。
multifarious duties = 五花八门的职责
② The museum reflects the multifarious interests of the man who founded it.
= 那家博物馆反映了创办人各式各样的兴趣。
multifarious interests = 各式各样的兴趣

multifunctional
/ˌmʌltiˈfʌŋkʃənəl/
核心：多功能的：一样东西能派好几种用场。
① The office has a multifunctional printer that also scans and sends faxes.
= 办公室里有一台多功能打印机，还能扫描、发传真。
a multifunctional printer = 多功能打印机
② The village hall is a multifunctional space used for concerts, classes and weddings.
= 村礼堂是个多功能的场地，办音乐会、上课、办婚礼都用它。
a multifunctional space = 多功能的场地

multigrade
/ˈmʌltiɡreɪd/
核心：两个行业里的用语。说机油：多级的，冷热天都适用；说班级（尤美）：复式的，几个年级的学生合在一个班里上课。
① The manual recommends a multigrade oil for use all year round.
= 说明书建议全年使用多级机油。
a multigrade oil = 多级机油
② She teaches a multigrade class of twenty children aged from six to ten.
= 她教一个复式班，二十个孩子，从六岁到十岁都有。
a multigrade class = 复式班

multigrain
/ˈmʌltiɡreɪn/
核心：杂粮的：用好几种谷物做的。说面包、饼干、早餐麦片。只放在名词前。
① He has two slices of multigrain toast with honey for breakfast.
= 他早餐吃两片抹蜂蜜的杂粮吐司。
multigrain toast = 杂粮吐司
② The bakery sells a multigrain loaf made with oats, barley and rye.
= 那家面包店卖一种用燕麦、大麦和黑麦做的杂粮面包。
a multigrain loaf = 杂粮面包

multigym
/ˈmʌltidʒɪm/
核心：（英）多功能健身器：一台机器上能做好几种力量练习；也指摆着这类器械的健身房。
① He bought a second-hand multigym and set it up in the garage.
= 他买了一台二手的多功能健身器，装在车库里。
a multigym = 多功能健身器
② The hotel has a small pool and a multigym in the basement.
= 那家酒店的地下室有一个小泳池和一间器械健身房。
a multigym in the basement = 地下室的器械健身房

multilateral
/ˌmʌltiˈlætərəl/
核心：多边的：有三个以上的国家或团体参加的。跟 bilateral（双边的）、unilateral（单边的）相对。
名词 multilateralism（多边主义）并入本条。
① The four countries signed a multilateral agreement on fishing rights.
= 四国签署了一项关于捕鱼权的多边协定。
a multilateral agreement = 多边协定
② The minister called for multilateral talks rather than action by a single state.
= 那位部长呼吁举行多边会谈，而不是由一国单独行动。
multilateral talks = 多边会谈
③ The speech was a strong defence of multilateralism in world trade.
= 那篇讲话有力地捍卫了世界贸易中的多边主义。
multilateralism = 多边主义

multilingual
/ˌmʌltiˈlɪŋɡwəl/
核心：使用多种语言的；（人）会说多种语言的；（文件、词典）用几种语言写成的。
① Switzerland is a multilingual country with four national languages.
= 瑞士是个使用多种语言的国家，有四种国语。
a multilingual country = 使用多种语言的国家
② The firm needs multilingual staff who can deal with customers across Europe.
= 那家公司需要会多种语言的员工，好接待欧洲各地的客户。
multilingual staff = 会多种语言的员工
③ The airport has multilingual signs in English, French and Arabic.
= 机场的指示牌用英语、法语和阿拉伯语三种文字写成。
multilingual signs = 多语种指示牌

multimedia
/ˌmʌltɪˈmiːdiə/
核心：多媒体的：把文字、声音、图像、视频结合在一起的。多作定语；也作不可数名词。
① The museum has a multimedia display about the history of the port.
= 博物馆里有一个讲港口历史的多媒体展示。
a multimedia display = 多媒体展示
② She gave a multimedia presentation with film clips and recorded interviews.
= 她做了一场带电影片段和采访录音的多媒体演示。
a multimedia presentation = 多媒体演示
③ The course teaches students to use multimedia in the classroom.
= 那门课教学生在课堂上使用多媒体。
use multimedia = 使用多媒体
这里是名词。

multimillionaire
/ˌmʌltiˌmɪljəˈneə(r)/
核心：千万富翁、大富豪：财产值好几百万（英镑、美元）的人。
① He became a multimillionaire by the age of thirty after selling his software company.
= 他卖掉自己的软件公司，不到三十岁就成了千万富翁。
become a multimillionaire = 成为千万富翁
② The club was bought by a multimillionaire who had made his money in property.
= 那家俱乐部被一位靠房地产发家的大富豪买下了。
a multimillionaire = 大富豪

multinational
/ˌmʌltɪˈnæʃənəl/
核心：跨国的、多国的：在许多国家经营的，或由多国人员组成的。名词指跨国公司。
① She works for a multinational company with offices in forty countries.
= 她在一家跨国公司工作，公司在四十个国家设有办事处。
a multinational company = 跨国公司
② A multinational force was sent to keep the peace along the border.
= 一支多国部队被派去维持边境沿线的和平。
a multinational force = 多国部队
③ Small farmers cannot compete with the big food multinationals.
= 小农户竞争不过那些大型食品跨国公司。
a multinational = 跨国公司
名词，可数。

multipack
/ˈmʌltipæk/
核心：多件装：几件同样的商品捆在一起卖的一包。
① Crisps are cheaper if you buy them in a multipack of twelve bags.
= 薯片买十二袋的多件装更便宜。
a multipack of twelve = 十二件装
② The label says that cans from a multipack must not be sold separately.
= 标签上写着，多件装里的罐子不得拆开单卖。
from a multipack = 从多件装里拿出来的

multiparty
/ˌmʌltiˈpɑːti/
核心：多党的：有几个政党参加的。只放在名词前。
① The country held its first multiparty elections for thirty years.
= 那个国家举行了三十年来的第一次多党选举。
multiparty elections = 多党选举
② The new constitution guarantees a multiparty system and a free press.
= 新宪法保障多党制和新闻自由。
a multiparty system = 多党制

multiplayer
/ˌmʌltiˈpleɪə(r)/
核心：（电子游戏）多人的：供几个人同时玩的。只放在名词前。
① The boys spend their evenings in a multiplayer game with friends in three countries.
= 那几个男孩晚上都在跟三个国家的朋友一起打一款多人游戏。
a multiplayer game = 多人游戏
② The new version adds an online multiplayer mode for up to eight people.
= 新版本增加了最多八人的在线多人模式。
a multiplayer mode = 多人模式

multiple
/ˈmʌltɪpəl/
核心：形容词：多个的、多重的，只放在名词前，比 many 正式，强调「不止一个、种类不一」。
名词：数学上的倍数；英式商业里指连锁店。
multiple-choice、multiple sclerosis、multiple personality disorder 并入本条。
① The driver was taken to hospital with multiple injuries.
= 那名司机多处受伤，被送进医院。
multiple injuries = 多处受伤
② Make multiple copies of the file and keep them in different places.
= 把那个文件多备份几份，存在不同的地方。
multiple copies = 多份副本
③ The form may be completed by multiple users at the same time.
= 那份表格可以由多个用户同时填写。
multiple users = 多个用户
正式文字和技术说明里的用法。
④ Twelve is a multiple of both three and four.
= 十二既是三的倍数，也是四的倍数。
a multiple of three = 三的倍数
⑤ The small shops in the town cannot match the prices of the multiples.
= 镇上的小店拼不过连锁店的价钱。
the multiples = 连锁店
英式商业用语。
⑥ The first part of the exam is a multiple-choice test of fifty questions.
= 考试的第一部分是五十道选择题。
a multiple-choice test = 选择题测验
⑦ She was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis when she was thirty-two.
= 她三十二岁时被诊断出患有多发性硬化症。
multiple sclerosis = 多发性硬化症
常缩写成 MS。
⑧ The film is about a woman with multiple personality disorder.
= 那部影片讲一个患多重人格障碍的女人。
multiple personality disorder = 多重人格障碍

multiplex
/ˈmʌltɪpleks/
核心：多厅影院：一栋楼里有好几个放映厅。也说 multiplex cinema。
① A new multiplex with twelve screens has opened on the edge of town.
= 城边新开了一家有十二个放映厅的多厅影院。
a multiplex = 多厅影院
② The old cinema in the high street closed when the multiplex arrived.
= 多厅影院一来，商业街上的老电影院就关门了。
the multiplex arrives = 多厅影院开张

multiplication
/ˌmʌltɪplɪˈkeɪʃən/
核心：乘法；也指数量的大量增加、繁殖。不可数。
multiplication table（乘法表）并入本条，英式也叫 times table。
① The children are learning multiplication and division this term.
= 孩子们这学期在学乘法和除法。
multiplication and division = 乘法和除法
② The multiplication of rules has made the system almost impossible to understand.
= 规章越来越多，那套制度几乎没人弄得懂了。
the multiplication of something = …的大量增加
正式用法。
③ He could recite the multiplication tables up to twelve by the age of six.
= 他六岁就能背到十二的乘法表。
a multiplication table = 乘法表

multiplicity
/ˌmʌltɪˈplɪsɪti/
核心：（正式）多种多样、大量。固定说 a multiplicity of，后接复数名词。
① The city has a multiplicity of small theatres, most of them above pubs.
= 那座城市有各式各样的小剧场，大多开在酒馆楼上。
a multiplicity of something = 各式各样的…
② The delay was caused by a multiplicity of small errors rather than one large one.
= 延误是许许多多小差错造成的，而不是一个大错。
a multiplicity of errors = 许许多多的差错

multiplier
/ˈmʌltɪplaɪə(r)/
核心：乘数：乘法里用来去乘的那个数。经济学上指乘数效应的那个倍数；也指把效果放大的东西。
① In the sum six times four, the number four is the multiplier.
= 在六乘四这道题里，四是乘数。
the multiplier = 乘数
② Economists argue about the size of the multiplier for public spending.
= 经济学家们对公共支出的乘数有多大争论不休。
the multiplier for spending = 支出的乘数
③ A good teacher acts as a multiplier, because her pupils go on to teach others.
= 好老师的作用会成倍放大，因为她的学生会再去教别人。
act as a multiplier = 起成倍放大的作用

multiply
/ˈmʌltɪplaɪ/
核心：乘：multiply A by B；A 和 B 相乘说 multiply A and B together。
引申为成倍增加，及物不及物都行；说动物、细菌是繁殖。
① Multiply the length by the width to find the area of the room.
= 用长乘以宽，求出房间的面积。
multiply A by B = 用甲乘以乙
介词用 by。
② Children learn to multiply before they learn to divide.
= 孩子先学乘法，再学除法。
learn to multiply = 学乘法
③ Our problems have multiplied since the new manager arrived.
= 新经理来了以后，我们的麻烦成倍增加。
problems multiply = 麻烦成倍增加
④ Cigarette smoking multiplies the risk of heart disease.
= 吸烟使患心脏病的风险成倍增加。
multiply the risk = 使风险成倍增加
⑤ Bacteria multiply quickly in warm food left out of the fridge.
= 细菌在没放进冰箱的温热食物里繁殖得很快。
bacteria multiply = 细菌繁殖

multiprocessor

multiprocessor machines = 多处理器机器

multipurpose

① He carries a multipurpose tool with a knife, a saw and a pair of pliers.

multiracial

multitask
/ˌmʌltiˈtɑːsk/

multitrack
/ˈmʌltitræk/

① The band made the album on a multitrack recorder in a rented cottage.
= 那支乐队在一栋租来的小屋里，用一台多轨录音机做出了那张专辑。
a multitrack recorder = 多轨录音机
② Multitrack recording allows a singer to add her voice after the band has gone home.
= 多轨录音让歌手可以等乐队走了以后再把自己的声音加进去。
multitrack recording = 多轨录音
