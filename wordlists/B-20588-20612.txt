senator
/ˈsenətə(r)/
核心：名词，可数（常大写 Senator）：参议员；（古罗马的）元老院议员。缩写 Sen.。
① The senator for California spoke against the plan in a long speech.
= 加利福尼亚州的参议员发表长篇演讲反对那项计划。
a senator = 参议员
② She was elected to the Senate at thirty-five and became the youngest senator in the country.
= 她三十五岁当选参议员，成了全国最年轻的参议员。
the youngest senator = 最年轻的参议员
美国每州两名参议员，任期六年。

① The senatorial election in the state is expected to be very close this year.
= 预计今年该州的参议员选举会非常激烈。
a senatorial election = 参议员选举
senatorial = 参议员的
② At the dinner he spoke in a slow, senatorial voice that made everyone at the table listen.
= 晚宴上，他用一种缓慢庄重、参议员般的语调说话，桌上每个人都在听。
a senatorial voice = 参议员般庄重的语调
引申义形容人说话庄重、有派头。

send

send = 寄、发送
② I sent the documents to the bank by email, but they say they have not received them.
= 我用电子邮件把文件发给了银行，可他们说没收到。

⑩ I sent off my application for the job on Friday and got a reply on Monday.

⑫ If any letters come for me after I move, please send them on to my new address.
= 我搬走后要是有寄给我的信，请转寄到我的新地址。

⑬ The company sent out invitations to all its customers for the opening of the new shop.
= 那家公司给所有顾客发出了新店开业的请柬。

⑭ We were too tired to cook, so we sent out for a pizza.

/ˈsiːnaɪl/

= 我祖父九十多岁时有点老糊涂了，常常忘记我们的名字。

= 镇上那家老医院有一个专门收治老年痴呆患者的病房。
senile dementia = 老年痴呆
医学上现在多说 dementia，不带 senile。

sensible = 明智的
② She is very sensitive about her weight, so please don't mention it when we have dinner with her.
= 她对自己的体重很敏感，所以别提这个。
be sensitive about something = 对某事敏感
③ The chemist told me this cream is good for people with sensitive skin who burn easily in summer.
= 这种面霜适合皮肤敏感的人用。
sensitive skin = 敏感肌肤
④ Religion is a sensitive subject, so be careful what you say at dinner.
= 宗教是个敏感话题，所以晚饭时说话要小心。
a sensitive subject = 敏感话题
⑤ In the quiet hospital room, the doctor spoke sensitively to the family about their father's illness.
= 医生体贴地跟那一家人谈了他们父亲的病情。
sensitively = 体贴地

sensitivity
/ˌsensəˈtɪvəti/
核心：名词：体贴、善解人意（不可数）；敏感（不可数或可数）：sensitivity to light；（话题的）敏感性（不可数）；（仪器的）灵敏度。
① After the accident, the nurse dealt with the frightened children in the waiting room with great sensitivity.
= 那位护士非常体贴地对待那些受惊的孩子。
with sensitivity = 体贴地
sensitivity = 体贴
② Some people have a sensitivity to bright light and need to wear dark glasses.
= 有些人对强光敏感，得戴墨镜。
a sensitivity to something = 对某物敏感
③ Because of the sensitivity of the subject, the meeting was held in private.
= 由于话题敏感，那次会议是闭门举行的。
the sensitivity of a subject = 话题的敏感性

sensitization
/ˌsensətaɪˈzeɪʃn/
核心：名词，不可数：致敏、敏化（医学）；（对某问题的）唤起意识、提高认识。英式也拼作 sensitisation。
① Repeated contact with the chemical can lead to sensitization and a skin rash.
= 反复接触那种化学品可能导致过敏，出现皮疹。
sensitization = 致敏
② The campaign aims at the sensitization of young people to the dangers of drugs.
= 那场宣传活动旨在让年轻人认识到毒品的危害。
the sensitization of somebody to something = 使某人认识到某事

sensitize
/ˈsensətaɪz/
核心：动词，及物：使认识到、使敏感：sensitize somebody to something；（医学）使致敏。英式也拼作 sensitise。
① The film helped to sensitize the public to the problems of homeless people.
= 那部电影帮助公众认识到无家可归者的困境。
sensitize somebody to something = 使某人认识到某事
sensitize = 使认识到
② Some people become sensitized to certain foods and react badly when they eat them.
= 有些人对某些食物变得过敏，吃了就有不良反应。
become sensitized to something = 对某物变得过敏
