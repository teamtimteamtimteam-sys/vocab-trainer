= 徒步第二天我们水不够了，只好去一个农场要。

⑥ Everyone in the office calls him Tom, which is short for Thomas, his grandfather's name.
= 办公室里大家都叫他汤姆，这是托马斯的简称，托马斯是他祖父的名字。

= 他看见那条蛇横在小路上，猛地停住了。

shorthand
/ˈʃɔːthænd/
核心：名词，不可数：速记（法）：in shorthand；（比喻）简略的说法：shorthand for something。
① My grandmother was a secretary and could write shorthand faster than most people can talk.
= 我奶奶当过秘书，速记写得比大多数人说话还快。
write shorthand = 速记
shorthand = 速记
② "The big day" is shorthand for their wedding, and everyone in the family knows it.
= "大日子"是他们婚礼的简略说法，家里人人都知道。
shorthand for something = 某事的简略说法

shorthorn
/ˈʃɔːthɔːn/
核心：名词，可数：短角牛（一种英国肉乳兼用牛）。
① The farmer keeps a small herd of red shorthorns on the hills behind the village.
= 那个农场主在村后山上养了一小群红色短角牛。
shorthorns = 短角牛
shorthorn = 短角牛
② Shorthorn cattle were brought to Australia from England in the nineteenth century.
= 短角牛是十九世纪从英国引进澳大利亚的。
shorthorn cattle = 短角牛

shortlist
/ˈʃɔːtlɪst/
核心：名词，可数：（候选人、作品的）入围名单、候选名单：be on the shortlist。动词，及物（常用被动）：把…列入候选名单：be shortlisted for something。
① Her novel is on the shortlist for the country's biggest book prize.
= 她的小说进入了全国最大图书奖的入围名单。
be on the shortlist = 入围
shortlist = 候选名单
② Three people have been shortlisted for the job, and they will be interviewed next week.
= 有三个人入围了那个职位，下周面试。
be shortlisted for something = 入围某事

shortly
/ˈʃɔːtli/
核心：副词：不久、很快：shortly after / before something；（说话）简短而不耐烦地、冷淡地。
① The doctor will be with you shortly, so please take a seat in the waiting room.
= 医生马上就来，请在候诊室坐一下。
shortly = 不久
② We sold the farm and moved to the city shortly after our son was born.
= 我们儿子出生后不久，我们就卖掉农场搬到了城里。
shortly after something = 某事后不久
③ "I'm busy," she said shortly, and closed the door before I could ask anything else.
= 她冷冷地说了句"我很忙"，没等我再问就关上了门。
say shortly = 冷淡地说
注意 shortly 不表示「简短地」，那个意思用 briefly。

shortness
/ˈʃɔːtnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：短；矮；（时间的）短暂；（物资的）不足。shortness of breath 是气短、呼吸困难。
① The doctor asked whether she had noticed any shortness of breath when she climbed the stairs.
= 医生问她爬楼梯时有没有觉得气短。
shortness of breath = 气短
shortness = 短
② Because of the shortness of the summer, the farmers have to plant their crops very early.
= 由于夏天短，农民得很早就播种。
the shortness of something = 某事的短暂
