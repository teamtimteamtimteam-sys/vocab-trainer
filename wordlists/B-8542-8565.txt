faze
核心：（非正式）使慌乱、难住：多用否定，nothing fazes somebody。
① Nothing fazes the woman who runs that desk at four in the morning.
= 凌晨四点守着那张台子的女士，什么都难不倒她。
nothing fazes somebody = 什么都难不倒某人
② The question fazed him for a second and he answered it rather well.
= 那个问题让他愣了一秒，答得倒相当漂亮。
faze somebody = 使某人一时慌乱

fealty
核心：（旧、历史）效忠：封臣对领主的忠诚义务，如今多用作比喻。不可数。
最常见的搭配是 swear fealty to somebody。
① The barons swore fealty in the morning and started plotting by supper.
= 众男爵上午宣誓效忠，晚饭前就开始密谋了。
swear fealty to somebody = 向某人宣誓效忠
② His fealty to the old brand survived three changes of recipe.
= 他对那个老牌子的忠心，扛过了三次配方更改。
fealty = 效忠

fear
核心：三支。一是恐惧、害怕：fear of heights；二是担忧：fears for somebody；
三是动词，害怕、担心：fear the worst、fear for somebody。
习语：for fear of 是唯恐，no fear 是英式口语里的「才不呢」。
形容词 fearful 与 fearless。
① Her fear of heights begins somewhere around the third rung of the ladder.
= 她的恐高，大约从梯子第三级开始。
fear of heights = 恐高
② Fears for the crew grew when the second radio failed as well.
= 第二台电台也坏了之后，对船员的担忧更重了。
fears for somebody = 对某人安危的担忧
fear for somebody = 为某人的安危担心
③ They fear the worst and keep the kettle on anyway.
= 他们做了最坏的打算，水壶照样烧着。
fear the worst = 做最坏的打算
④ He said nothing at all for fear of making it worse.
= 他一句话没说，唯恐越描越黑。
for fear of doing something = 唯恐做出某事
⑤ Would he ever do it again? No fear.
= 他还会再干一次吗？才不呢。
no fear = 才不呢（英式口语）

fearful
核心：两支。一是害怕的、担忧的：be fearful of something；
二是（略旧，口语）可怕的、糟糕的：a fearful mess。副词 fearfully。
① Parents are fearful of a road that has had one accident in nine years.
= 家长们怕那条路，九年里它只出过一次事故。
be fearful of something = 害怕某事
② The kitchen was in a fearful mess and the cake came out perfect.
= 厨房乱得一塌糊涂，蛋糕倒是做得完美。
a fearful mess = 一塌糊涂
副词 fearfully：一是提心吊胆地；二是（略旧）非常地，等于 very。
③ She looked fearfully at the door and went on talking.
= 她提心吊胆地瞟了一眼门，接着往下说。
look fearfully at something = 提心吊胆地看着某物
④ That place is fearfully expensive and everybody goes twice a year.
= 那地方贵得吓人，人人一年还去两趟。
fearfully expensive = 贵得吓人

fearfulness
核心：（正式）畏惧、胆怯：怕这怕那的性情或状态。不可数。
① Fearfulness in a new dog fades faster in a house with children.
= 新来的狗那份胆怯，在有孩子的家里消得快些。
fearfulness = 畏惧、胆怯
② His fearfulness about flying costs him two days on every trip.
= 他怕坐飞机，每趟出行都要多搭上两天。
fearfulness about something = 对某事的畏惧

fearless
核心：无所畏惧的：说人、说报道、说决定。副词 fearlessly，名词 fearlessness。
① A fearless reporter and a careless one look much the same for about a year.
= 无所畏惧的记者跟粗心大意的记者，头一年看着差不多。
a fearless reporter = 无所畏惧的记者
② She is fearless on a ladder and frightened of the dark.
= 她上梯子毫不含糊，却怕黑。
be fearless = 无所畏惧
副词 fearlessly：无所畏惧地，多修饰 spoke、faced、tackled、reported。
③ He tackled the question fearlessly and got two of the numbers wrong.
= 他毫无惧色地接下那个问题，两个数字却报错了。
tackle something fearlessly = 无所畏惧地应对某事
④ The paper reported the case fearlessly and paid for the lawyers itself.
= 那家报纸无所畏惧地报道了那桩案子，律师费自己掏。
report something fearlessly = 无所畏惧地报道某事

fearlessness
核心：无畏：什么都不怕的那份性情。不可数。
① Her fearlessness in the water started with one very cold lesson.
= 她在水里的无畏，是从一堂冻人的课开始的。
fearlessness = 无畏
② Fearlessness and inexperience are hard to tell apart from the bank.
= 站在岸上看，无畏和没经验很难分得清。

fearsome
核心：（书面）可怕的、令人生畏的：说外表、名声与对手。
① The dog has a fearsome bark and no interest in following it up.
= 那条狗叫得吓人，却没兴趣真扑上来。
a fearsome bark = 吓人的吠声
② She has a fearsome reputation and answers her own emails.
= 她名声让人生畏，邮件倒是自己回。
a fearsome reputation = 令人生畏的名声

feasibility
核心：可行性：办不办得成。不可数；最常见的说法是 a feasibility study。
① The feasibility study cost more than the bridge it advised against.
= 那份可行性研究的花费，比它建议别修的那座桥还多。
a feasibility study = 可行性研究
② Feasibility here depends on the ferry timetable and on nothing else.
= 这里可行不可行，全看渡轮班次。
feasibility = 可行性

feasible
核心：可行的、办得到的：说方案与日程，比 possible 更强调实际做得成。
① The plan is feasible in summer and impossible from October onwards.
= 那个方案夏天可行，十月起就没门。
feasible = 可行的
② It is feasible to finish by Friday if nobody asks for a meeting.
= 只要没人提开会，周五做完是办得到的。
it is feasible to do something = 做某事是可行的

feast
核心：三支。一是盛宴、大餐：a wedding feast；二是（宗教）节庆日：a feast day；
三是动词，尽情享用：feast on something。习语 feast your eyes on 是大饱眼福。
① The wedding feast ran to nine courses and one very short speech.
= 那场婚宴上了九道菜，讲话只有短短一段。
a wedding feast = 婚宴
② The feast day falls in November and the village cooks for two days.
= 那个节庆日在十一月，村里要连做两天饭。
a feast day = 节庆日
③ They feasted on bread, cheese and three kinds of pickle.
= 他们就着面包、奶酪和三种腌菜大吃了一顿。
feast on something = 尽情享用某物
④ Feast your eyes on the ceiling and try to ignore the carpet.
= 天花板尽管看个够，地毯就尽量别看。
feast your eyes on something = 大饱眼福

feat
核心：壮举、了不起的本事：a remarkable feat、a feat of engineering。可数。
① Getting nine people into that car was a feat and also a summons.
= 把九个人塞进那辆车是个壮举，也换来一张传票。
a feat = 壮举
② The bridge is a feat of engineering and a nuisance to walk across.
= 那座桥是工程上的壮举，走起来却折腾。
a feat of engineering = 工程壮举

feather
核心：羽毛，可数。习语三条：as light as a feather 轻如鸿毛，
a feather in your cap 是值得夸耀的一笔，feather your nest 是中饱私囊。
形容词 feathery 与 feathered。
① A feather came out of the pillow and stayed in the room for a week.
= 一根羽毛从枕头里钻出来，在屋里飘了一个星期。
a feather = 羽毛
as light as a feather = 轻如鸿毛
② The prize is a feather in her cap and worth nothing at the bank.
= 那个奖是她履历上的一笔光彩，在银行里一文不值。
a feather in your cap = 值得夸耀的一笔
③ Three directors feathered their nests and the fourth took the minutes.
= 三名董事中饱私囊，第四位在记会议纪要。
feather your nest = 中饱私囊

feathered
核心：有羽毛的、插羽饰的：多作定语；
打趣说鸟儿整体时说 our feathered friends。
① A feathered hat blocked the view of everybody behind row four.
= 一顶插羽毛的帽子，挡住了第四排以后所有人的视线。
a feathered hat = 插羽毛的帽子
② The garden is busy with our feathered friends and one very fat cat.
= 园子里鸟儿忙个不停，还有一只很肥的猫。
our feathered friends = 鸟儿（打趣的说法）

featherweight
核心：两支。一是（拳击）羽量级：体重级别之一；二是引申，无足轻重的人或物。可数。
① The featherweight fought above his division and won on points.
= 那位羽量级拳手越级出战，靠点数赢了。
a featherweight = 羽量级选手
② The report is a featherweight and the appendix is where the work is.
= 那份报告分量很轻，真东西都在附录里。
a featherweight = 无足轻重的东西

feathery
核心：羽毛般的、轻软的：说质地、说叶片、说雪。
① The fennel has feathery leaves and a smell that fills the whole garden.
= 茴香叶子细软如羽，香气弥漫整个园子。
feathery leaves = 羽状的叶子
② Feathery snow settled on the wall and was gone by ten o'clock.
= 羽毛般的雪落在墙头上，十点就没了。
feathery snow = 羽毛般的雪

feature
核心：四支。一是特色、特征：a key feature；二是（面部）五官：somebody's features；
三是（报刊的）特写：a feature article；四是动词，以……为卖点、由……主演。
① The key feature of the flat is a window that opens onto a wall.
= 那套公寓的主要特色，是一扇朝着墙开的窗。
a key feature = 主要特色
② Her features soften in photographs and sharpen in person.
= 她的五官在照片里柔和，真人则显得锋利。
somebody's features = 某人的五官
③ The feature ran to four pages and the news it came from ran to two lines.
= 那篇特写写了四页，引出它的新闻只有两行。
a feature article = 特写文章
④ The film features two actors who have never actually met.
= 那部片子由两位从没真正见过面的演员主演。
feature somebody = 由某人主演

featureless
核心：毫无特色的、平淡无奇的：说风景、建筑，也说文字。
① The plain looks featureless until you have walked it twice.
= 那片平原乍看毫无特色，走过两回就不一样了。
featureless = 毫无特色的
② A featureless corridor connects two buildings that hate each other.
= 一条毫无特色的走廊，连着两栋互相看不顺眼的楼。
a featureless corridor = 毫无特色的走廊

febrile
核心：两支。一是（医学）发热的：a febrile illness；
二是（书面）狂热不安的：a febrile atmosphere。
① A febrile child is checked every hour and the chart says so.
= 发热的孩子每小时查一次，记录单上写着。
a febrile illness = 发热性疾病
② The febrile atmosphere before the vote made sensible people shout.
= 表决前那种狂热不安的气氛，连明白人都喊起来了。
a febrile atmosphere = 狂热不安的气氛

February
核心：二月：一年里最短的月份，闰年有二十九天。缩写 Feb.。
注意读音：中间那个 r 常被吞掉。
① February is short and still manages to feel longer than March.
= 二月很短，感觉却比三月还长。
in February = 在二月
② The garden does nothing in February and everything in the first week of April.
= 那个园子二月里毫无动静，四月头一周就全冒出来了。
in early February = 二月初

fecal
核心：（美式拼法）粪便的：英式写 faecal，用法完全一样。
① Fecal bacteria in the samples closed two wells and one campsite.
= 样本里的粪便细菌，让两口井和一处营地关闭。
fecal bacteria = 粪便细菌
② A fecal sample travels to the lab in a sealed container.
= 粪便样本装在密封容器里送去化验室。
a fecal sample = 粪便样本

feces
核心：（美式拼法）粪便：英式写 faeces，作复数。
① Bird feces on the statue take a specialist and a scaffold to remove.
= 雕像上的鸟粪，得请专人搭架子才清得掉。
feces = 粪便
② The report counts feces in the water and stops short of naming a source.
= 那份报告统计了水里的粪便含量，却没点出来源。
bird feces = 鸟粪

feckless
核心：（贬）不中用的、没担当的：说人做事没条理、靠不住。名词 fecklessness。
① A feckless landlord and a patient tenant kept that house standing.
= 一个不中用的房东加一个有耐心的租客，让那栋房子撑住了。
feckless = 不中用的
② He is feckless with money and meticulous with his record collection.
= 他花钱没个数，唱片收藏倒一丝不苟。
be feckless with money = 花钱没数

fecklessness
核心：（贬）不中用、没担当：做事没条理、靠不住的那种性情。不可数。
① The fecklessness of the agency cost the tenants a winter without heating.
= 那家中介的不中用，让租客过了一个没暖气的冬天。
fecklessness = 不中用、没担当
② Fecklessness in a young man was a joke and in a father is not.
= 一个年轻人不着调是笑话，做了父亲就不是了。
somebody's fecklessness = 某人的没担当
