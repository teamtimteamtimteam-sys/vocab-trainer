firefight
核心：（军事）交火：双方持枪对射的战斗。可数。
① The firefight lasted forty minutes and neither side moved a single metre.
= 那场交火打了四十分钟，双方谁也没挪出一米。
a firefight = 交火
② Two firefights in one week emptied the village of everybody who could leave.
= 一个星期里两次交火，能走的人都走光了。
be caught in a firefight = 卷入交火

firefighter
核心：消防员：如今通用的说法，取代了旧称 fireman。可数。
① Firefighters got there in six minutes and the road took four of them.
= 消防员六分钟就到，其中四分钟花在路上。
a firefighter = 消防员
a retained firefighter = 兼职消防员
A retained firefighter leaves the shop mid-sale when the pager goes.
= 兼职消防员传呼机一响，生意做到一半就得走。
② Two firefighters carried the ladder that four people had brought.
= 四个人抬来的那架梯子，两名消防员就扛走了。

firefighting
核心：两支。一是灭火这件事；二是引申的四处救火、疲于应付。不可数。
① Firefighting equipment is checked in April and needed in August.
= 灭火器材四月检查，八月用得上。
firefighting equipment = 灭火器材
② The whole job is firefighting and the plan gets written in December.
= 那份工作整天都在救火，计划要到十二月才写得出来。
firefighting = 疲于应付

fireman
核心：两支。一是（旧称）消防员：如今通用 firefighter，这个词多见于旧文；
二是蒸汽机车上的司炉。可数，复数 firemen。
① The fireman on that engine shovelled two tons of coal in a single shift.
= 那台机车上的司炉，一个班要铲两吨煤。
a fireman = 司炉（蒸汽机车上的）
② A fireman in that photograph is holding a hose that needs four men.
= 照片里那位消防员，端着一根要四个人才拿得住的水带。
a fireman = 消防员（旧称）

firing
核心：三支。一是射击：firing practice；二是解雇：the firing of three managers；
三是（陶瓷的）烧制。不可数为主。习语 in the firing line 是首当其冲。
① Firing practice happens at dawn and the sheep have got used to it.
= 打靶训练在天亮时进行，羊群已经习惯了。
firing practice = 打靶训练
in the firing line = 首当其冲
The counter staff are in the firing line for decisions made upstairs.
= 楼上做的决定，首当其冲的是柜台上的人。
② The firing of three managers in one week emptied the car park by six.
= 一个星期解雇三名经理，六点停车场就空了。
the firing of somebody = 对某人的解雇
③ The second firing sets the glaze and the first only hardens the clay.
= 第二次烧制定住釉面，头一次只是把泥坯烧硬。
a firing = 一次烧制
④ The firing squad in that film is eleven men and one blank round.
= 那部电影里的行刑队，十一个人，一发空包弹。
a firing squad = 行刑队

firkin
核心：（英式，酿造）小桶：约九加仑的啤酒桶。可数。
① A firkin lasts that pub a night and a half.
= 一小桶啤酒，那家酒馆一晚半就卖完。
a firkin = 九加仑小桶
a firkin of ale = 一小桶麦芽啤酒
A firkin of ale arrived for the wake and was gone by nine.
= 守灵宴上送来一小桶麦芽啤酒，九点就没了。
② Two firkins came off the lorry and one of them was already leaking.
= 两只小桶从货车上卸下来，其中一只已经在漏了。

firm
核心：四支。一是结实的、硬实的：a firm mattress；二是坚定的：be firm with somebody；
三是（日期、安排）定死的：a firm date；四是名词，公司：a law firm。副词 firmly。
① A firm mattress and a soft pillow is the compromise that household reached.
= 硬床垫配软枕头，是那家人达成的折中。
a firm mattress = 硬床垫
② You have to be firm with a puppy and gentle about how you do it.
= 对小狗要坚定，态度还得温和。
be firm with somebody = 对某人态度坚定
③ There is no firm date yet and the invitations are already printed.
= 日子还没定死，请柬倒已经印好了。
a firm date = 确定的日期
④ The law firm has nine partners and one working lift.
= 那家律所有九位合伙人，一部还能用的电梯。
a law firm = 律师事务所
副词 firmly：牢牢地、坚决地，多修饰 shut、held、believed。
⑤ The door was firmly shut and the key was in the other coat.
= 门关得严严实实，钥匙在另一件外套里。
firmly shut = 关得严实
⑥ She firmly believes the second version and has read only the first.
= 她坚信第二个版本，读过的却只有第一个。
firmly believe = 坚信
⑦ Prices firm up in spring and soften again by the middle of August.
= 价格春天走稳，到八月中又松动。
firm up = 走稳、确定下来

firmament
核心：（书面、旧）苍穹、天空；引申指某个领域的「星空」。不可数。
① Stars filled the firmament and the streetlight filled everything else.
= 星星填满苍穹，路灯填满了别的一切。
the firmament = 苍穹
② A new name in the literary firmament lasts about two seasons.
= 文坛星空里的一个新名字，大约亮两季。
the literary firmament = 文坛星空

firmness
核心：两支。一是（质地的）硬实；二是（态度的）坚定。不可数。
① The firmness of that mattress is why he sleeps at his mother's.
= 那张床垫太硬，所以他都去母亲家睡。
the firmness of something = 某物的软硬程度
② Firmness at the first meeting saves three meetings later on.
= 头一次会上态度坚定，能省下后面三次会。
firmness = 坚定

firmware
核心：（计算机）固件：写死在设备里的那层程序，介于硬件与软件之间。不可数。
① A firmware update took the camera offline for a day and fixed nothing.
= 一次固件更新让那台相机停用一天，什么也没修好。
a firmware update = 固件更新
② The firmware in that box is older than the phone that talks to it.
= 那个盒子里的固件，比跟它通信的手机还老。
firmware = 固件

first
核心：五支。一是第一的、最先的：the first time；二是副词，首先；
三是名词，第一次、第一名；四是（英式）一等荣誉学位：get a first；
五是一串习语：at first、first of all、first come, first served。副词 firstly 用于列举。
① The first time she drove that road she went the wrong way twice.
= 她头一回开那条路，走错了两次。
the first time = 第一次
② At first the noise was funny and by Thursday it was not.
= 起初那声音还好笑，到周四就不好笑了。
at first = 起初
③ First of all, nobody asked; second, nobody minded.
= 首先，没人问过；其次，没人在意。
first of all = 首先
④ Seats are first come, first served and the queue starts at seven.
= 座位先到先得，七点就开始排队。
first come, first served = 先到先得
⑤ She got a first and now works in a shop she actually likes.
= 她拿了一等荣誉学位，如今在一家自己真心喜欢的店里上班。
get a first = 拿到一等荣誉学位
副词 firstly：列举时的「第一」，跟 secondly 成套，用在正式行文里。
⑥ Firstly the roof, secondly the boiler, and thirdly the money.
= 第一是屋顶，第二是锅炉，第三是钱。
firstly = 第一（列举用语）
⑦ First aid was given by a first-aider who had qualified the week before.
= 急救是由一位上星期才拿到证的急救员做的。
first aid = 急救
a first-aider = 急救员
A first-aider on every floor is the rule and two floors have none.
= 规定是每层楼一名急救员，有两层一个也没有。
a first responder = 首批到场的急救人员
A first responder on a bicycle beats the ambulance twice a week.
= 骑自行车的首批到场急救人员，一周有两回比救护车先到。
⑧ The first floor in Britain is the second floor in America and the lift knows neither.
= 英国说的「first floor」是美国的二楼，电梯两边都不认。
the first floor = （英式）二楼、（美式）一楼
a first balcony = 剧场二层前排包厢
The first balcony costs twice the stalls and hears rather less.
= 剧场二层前排包厢的票价是正厅两倍，听得反倒差些。
⑨ Her first name is on the badge and nobody uses it.
= 她的名字印在胸牌上，没人用。
a first name = 名字
a first language = 母语

= 她的母语，在那个镇上只有九个人说。
⑩ The first night went well and the first-timer in row three cried twice.

a first night = 首演之夜
a first-timer = 头一回做某事的人
A first-timer at that market pays double and learns by Saturday.
= 头一回逛那个集市的人要多付一倍钱，到周六就学乖了。
a first-time buyer = 首次购房者
A first-time buyer needs a deposit that takes nine years to save.
= 首次购房者要攒的首付，得攒九年。
⑪ A first offender gets a warning and a first-degree charge gets a lawyer.

a first offender = 初犯者
a first degree = 一级罪；学士学位
He took a first degree at forty and a second one at fifty.
= 他四十岁读了学士，五十岁又读了一个。
first blood = 首次得手
First blood went to the smaller team and the score stayed level.
= 首次得手的是实力较弱的那队，比分随后一直咬平。
⑫ At first light the first mate was already on deck and the first officer was not.
= 天刚亮，大副已经在甲板上，二副还没有。

a first mate = 大副
The first mate keeps the log and the captain signs it blind.
= 航海日志由大副记，船长闭着眼就签。

The first officer flew the leg home and said nothing about it.

A first lieutenant runs the platoon and carries the blame upward.

fisherwoman
核心：女渔民、钓鱼的女人：复数 fisherwomen。可数。
① The fisherwoman at the end of the pier out-fishes everybody with better gear.
= 栈桥尽头那位女钓者，比装备更好的人钓得都多。
a fisherwoman = 女渔民
a local fisherwoman = 本地的女渔民
A local fisherwoman showed the visitors where not to stand.
= 一位本地的女渔民，指给游客看哪儿站不得。
② Two fisherwomen run that boat and hire help only in August.
= 两位女渔民经营着那条船，只在八月雇帮手。

fishery
核心：两支。一是渔场、渔业区；二是渔业这个行当。可数。
① The fishery closed for two seasons and the stock came back in three.
= 那片渔场关了两季，鱼群三季之后才回来。
a fishery = 渔场
② Fishery policy is decided a long way from any actual boat.
= 渔业政策，是在离任何一条真船都很远的地方定下的。
fishery policy = 渔业政策

fisheye
核心：（摄影）鱼眼的：a fisheye lens 指视角极广、画面外凸的镜头。
① A fisheye lens makes a small room look like a stadium.
= 鱼眼镜头能把一间小屋拍得像体育场。
a fisheye lens = 鱼眼镜头
② The fisheye view bends every straight line in that building.
= 鱼眼视角把那栋楼里的每条直线都拗弯了。
a fisheye view = 鱼眼视角

fishing
核心：钓鱼、捕鱼：不可数 —— go fishing、a fishing rod、fishing rights。
① Fishing on that stretch needs a permit and a great deal of patience.
= 在那一段钓鱼，要有许可证，还要极大的耐心。

② Fishing rights on the river were sold in 1911 and never bought back.
= 那条河的捕鱼权 1911 年卖掉了，再没买回来。
fishing rights = 捕鱼权
③ The fishing tackle fills a shed and the fishing line is always the wrong weight.
= 渔具塞满一间棚子，鱼线的号数总是不对。
fishing tackle = 渔具
a fishing line = 鱼线
A fishing line in the trees takes longer to free than to replace.
= 鱼线挂在树上，解下来比换一根还费事。
④ The inquiry turned into a fishing expedition and found one parking fine.
= 那场调查变成了漫无目标的搜罗，只查出一张停车罚单。
a fishing expedition = 漫无目标的搜罗

fishmeal = 鱼粉

= 风向一转，鱼粉的味道就飘进村里。

fishmonger
核心：（英式）鱼贩，也指鱼店：卖鱼的人或那家铺子。可数。
① The fishmonger closes at two and knows what everybody wants by name.
= 那家鱼店两点关门，谁要什么，老板都叫得出名字。
a fishmonger = 鱼贩
the fishmonger's = 鱼店
She goes to the fishmonger's on Fridays and the butcher's on Tuesdays.
= 她周五去鱼店，周二去肉铺。

= 那条街上两家鱼店并成一家，后来变成了咖啡馆。
