核心：（漆、纸、照片）哑光的、无光泽的。美式也拼 matte。
① We painted the bedroom walls with white matt paint that hides the old cracks.
= 我们用白色哑光漆刷了卧室的墙，遮住了旧裂缝。
matt paint = 哑光漆
② The wedding photographs were printed with a matt finish so that they do not shine under the lamp.
= 婚礼的照片印成了哑光面，在灯下不反光。
a matt finish = 哑光面
与 gloss（亮光）相对。
