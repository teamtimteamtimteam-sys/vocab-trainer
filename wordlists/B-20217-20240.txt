scuffed
/skʌft/
核心：形容词：（鞋、地板、家具）磨损的、有刮痕的。
① His shoes were scuffed and dirty after the long walk through the woods.
= 在树林里走了很长的路后，他的鞋又磨损又脏。
scuffed shoes = 磨损的鞋
scuffed = 磨损的
② The old piano was badly scuffed, but it still had a beautiful sound.
= 那架旧钢琴刮痕累累，可音色仍然很美。
badly scuffed = 刮痕累累

scuffle
/ˈskʌfl/
核心：名词，可数：扭打、混战：a scuffle broke out。动词，不及物：扭打、混战：scuffle with somebody。比 fight 规模小、时间短。
① A scuffle broke out between two fans outside the stadium, but the police stopped it quickly.
= 体育场外两名球迷扭打起来，不过警察很快就制止了。
a scuffle broke out = 发生扭打
a scuffle = 扭打
② Protesters scuffled with police as they tried to get into the building.
= 抗议者想冲进大楼时，与警察发生了推搡扭打。
scuffle with somebody = 与某人扭打

scuffling
/ˈskʌflɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：扭打；（小动物发出的）窸窣声、刮擦声。
① We heard a scuffling in the roof at night and realized that mice had got in.
= 夜里我们听到屋顶上有窸窣声，才知道老鼠钻进去了。
a scuffling = 窸窣声
② There was some scuffling at the back of the hall, and two men were asked to leave.
= 礼堂后面发生了一些推搡，有两个人被请了出去。
some scuffling = 一些推搡扭打

scull
/skʌl/
核心：名词，可数：（双桨划艇用的）短桨；单人（或双人）双桨赛艇。动词：用短桨划（船）。
① He rowed the little boat across the lake with a pair of light wooden sculls.
= 他用一对轻便的木制短桨把小船划过了湖。
a pair of sculls = 一对短桨
② Every Sunday she sculled slowly down the river in the early morning mist.
= 每个星期天清晨的薄雾里，她都划着双桨赛艇顺河慢慢而下。
scull down a river = 划桨顺河而下

sculler
/ˈskʌlə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：双桨赛艇运动员、划双桨船的人。
① The young sculler trained on the river every morning at six before going to school.
= 那位年轻的双桨赛艇运动员每天早上六点上学前都在河上训练。
a sculler = 双桨赛艇运动员
② She became the fastest sculler in the country and won a gold medal at the Olympics.
= 她成了全国最快的双桨赛艇手，还在奥运会上拿了金牌。
the fastest sculler = 最快的双桨赛艇手

scullery
/ˈskʌləri/
核心：名词，可数：（旧时大宅厨房旁的）洗涤间：洗碗、洗菜的小房间。
① In the old house, the maids washed the dishes in a cold scullery next to the kitchen.
= 在那座老宅里，女仆们在厨房旁边冰冷的洗涤间里洗碗。
a scullery = 洗涤间
② We turned the old scullery into a small laundry room with a washing machine.
= 我们把旧洗涤间改成了放洗衣机的小洗衣房。
turn a scullery into something = 把洗涤间改成某物

sculling
/ˈskʌlɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：双桨划船运动、单人双桨赛艇。
① Sculling is harder than it looks, because you have to use both arms at exactly the same time.
= 双桨划船比看上去难，因为两只胳膊得完全同时用力。
sculling = 双桨划船
② She took up sculling at university and now races for her local club.
= 她上大学时开始玩双桨赛艇，现在代表当地俱乐部比赛。
take up sculling = 开始玩双桨赛艇

sculpt
/skʌlpt/
核心：动词：雕刻、雕塑：sculpt a figure in stone；（比喻）塑造、使成形。
① The artist sculpted a beautiful horse out of a single block of white stone.
= 那位艺术家用一整块白石雕刻出了一匹漂亮的马。
sculpt something out of something = 用某物雕出某物
sculpt = 雕刻
② The wind and waves have sculpted the cliffs into strange shapes over thousands of years.
= 几千年来，风浪把悬崖塑造成了奇形怪状。
sculpt something into something = 把某物塑造成某形状

sculptor
/ˈskʌlptə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：雕塑家、雕刻家。
① The sculptor spent three years working on the huge statue in the city square.
= 那位雕塑家花了三年时间创作城市广场上的那座巨大雕像。
a sculptor = 雕塑家
② The young sculptor works mainly with metal and old pieces of machinery.
= 那位年轻雕塑家主要用金属和旧机器零件创作。
work as a sculptor = 当雕塑家

sculptress
/ˈskʌlptrəs/
核心：名词，可数（旧式）：女雕塑家。今天一般不分性别，都说 sculptor。
① The museum has a whole room of works by a famous sculptress from the nineteenth century.
= 那座博物馆有一整间展厅陈列一位十九世纪著名女雕塑家的作品。
a sculptress = 女雕塑家
② She was one of the first women to be called a sculptress in an age when few women made art.
= 在很少有女性从事艺术的年代，她是最早被称为女雕塑家的人之一。
be called a sculptress = 被称为女雕塑家

sculptural
/ˈskʌlptʃərəl/
核心：形容词：雕塑的、雕塑般的：sculptural forms。
① The new building has a sculptural shape, with curved white walls that look like waves.
= 那座新楼造型如雕塑一般，弯曲的白墙像波浪。
a sculptural shape = 雕塑般的造型
sculptural = 雕塑般的
② The modern garden behind the gallery is full of sculptural plants with large dramatic leaves.
= 美术馆后面那个现代花园里满是叶子硕大、造型夸张的雕塑感植物。
sculptural plants = 雕塑感植物

sculpture
/ˈskʌlptʃə(r)/
核心：名词：雕塑作品、雕像（可数或不可数）；雕塑艺术（不可数）。动词：雕刻。人是 sculptor（雕塑家）。
① The park is full of modern sculptures made of stone, metal and glass.
= 公园里满是用石头、金属和玻璃做的现代雕塑作品。
sculpture = 雕塑
sculptures = 雕塑作品
② She studied sculpture at art college before she became a famous painter.
= 她成为名画家之前，在美术学院学的是雕塑。
study sculpture = 学雕塑
③ The sculptor who made the statue in the square also made the one in front of the station.
= 广场上那座雕像的雕塑家，也做了车站前面那座。
sculptor = 雕塑家

sculptured
/ˈskʌlptʃəd/
核心：形容词：雕刻的、雕塑装饰的；（面容、身材）线条分明的、轮廓清晰的。
① The church door is decorated with sculptured figures of angels and saints.
= 那座教堂的大门上装饰着天使和圣徒的雕像。
sculptured figures = 雕像
sculptured = 雕刻的
② The young model is famous for her sculptured cheekbones and bright green eyes.
= 那位年轻模特以轮廓分明的颧骨和明亮的绿眼睛出名。
sculptured cheekbones = 轮廓分明的颧骨

scum
/skʌm/
核心：名词，不可数：（液体表面的）浮渣、泡沫：a layer of scum；（俚语，贬义）人渣、败类。
① A layer of green scum covered the surface of the old pond behind the factory.
= 工厂后面那个旧池塘的水面上漂着一层绿色浮渣。
a layer of scum = 一层浮渣
scum = 浮渣
② The people who sold the fake medicine to sick children are the scum of the earth.
= 把假药卖给生病孩子的那些人是社会败类。
the scum of the earth = 社会败类

scumbag
/ˈskʌmbæɡ/
核心：名词，可数（俚语，冒犯）：卑鄙小人、混蛋。
① Some scumbag stole my bike from outside the hospital while I was visiting my mother.
= 我去医院看我妈的时候，不知哪个混蛋从门外偷走了我的自行车。
some scumbag = 某个混蛋
a scumbag = 卑鄙小人
② The detective called the drug dealer a scumbag and threw him into the police car.
= 侦探骂那个毒贩是个人渣，把他扔进了警车。
call somebody a scumbag = 骂某人是人渣

scummy
/ˈskʌmi/
核心：形容词：（水）有浮渣的、肮脏的；（口语）卑鄙的、下流的、令人恶心的。比较级 scummier。
① The water in the old swimming pool was green and scummy, so nobody wanted to go in.
= 那个旧游泳池的水又绿又脏，谁都不想下去。
scummy water = 有浮渣的脏水
scummy = 肮脏的
② It was a scummy thing to do, taking money from a blind old man.
= 拿一个失明老人的钱，这事干得太卑鄙了。
a scummy thing to do = 卑鄙的事

scupper
/ˈskʌpə(r)/
核心：动词，及物（英式口语）：破坏、使（计划）落空：scupper a plan；（故意）凿沉（船）。名词，可数：（船的）排水孔。
① The bad weather scuppered our plans for a barbecue in the garden.
= 坏天气让我们在花园里烧烤的计划泡了汤。
scupper a plan = 使计划落空
scupper = 破坏
② The crew scuppered the ship so that the enemy could not capture it.
= 船员们凿沉了那艘船，免得被敌人缴获。
scupper a ship = 凿沉船
③ Water poured off the deck through the scuppers as the waves crashed over the boat.
= 海浪砸上船时，水从排水孔里哗哗流出甲板。
the scuppers = 排水孔

scurrilous
/ˈskʌrələs/

① Last week the newspaper printed scurrilous rumours about the minister's private life and family.
= 上周那家报纸刊登了中伤部长私生活和家人的谣言。
scurrilous rumours = 中伤人的谣言
scurrilous = 恶语中伤的
② He was scurrilously attacked in an anonymous letter that was sent to all his customers.
= 他在一封寄给他所有客户的匿名信里遭到了恶意中伤。
scurrilously = 恶意中伤地

scurry
/ˈskʌri/

① A mouse scurried across the kitchen floor and disappeared under the cupboard.
= 一只老鼠窜过厨房地板，钻进柜子底下不见了。
scurry across something = 窜过某处
scurry = 急匆匆地跑
② Office workers scurried along the street, trying to get out of the rain.
= 上班族们沿着街道急匆匆地跑，想躲雨。
scurry along = 急匆匆地走

scurvy
/ˈskɜːvi/

① In the past, sailors on long voyages often died of scurvy because they had no fresh fruit.
= 过去，远航的水手常常因为没有新鲜水果而死于坏血病。
die of scurvy = 死于坏血病
scurvy = 坏血病
② Captains began to carry limes on their ships to protect the crew from scurvy.
= 船长们开始在船上带酸橙，防止船员得坏血病。
protect somebody from scurvy = 防止某人得坏血病

scuttle

scuttle across something = 急促地窜过某处
scuttle = 急促地跑
② Strong opposition from local people finally scuttled the plans for a new airport.
= 当地人的强烈反对最终让新建机场的计划泡了汤。
scuttle a plan = 使计划失败
③ My grandmother filled the coal scuttle every morning and carried it to the fire.
= 我祖母每天早上把煤斗装满，提到壁炉边。
a coal scuttle = 煤斗

scuttlebutt

= 办公室里的小道消息说老板明年要退休了。

② Don't believe all the scuttlebutt you hear around the school about the new teacher.
= 别全信你在学校里听到的关于那个新老师的流言。
believe the scuttlebutt = 相信小道消息

= 那家酒吧又暗又邋遢，可音乐是镇上最好的。
dark and scuzzy = 又暗又邋遢

scythe

scythe through something = 横扫穿过某处
