sensation
/senˈseɪʃn/
核心：名词：（身体的）感觉、知觉（可数或不可数）：a burning sensation、lose sensation；（说不清的）感觉（可数）：have the sensation that…；轰动、引起轰动的人或事（可数，常单数）：cause a sensation、an overnight sensation。形容词 sensational 另立条。
① I had a strange burning sensation in my throat after drinking the hot sauce.
= 喝了那辣酱之后，我喉咙里有一种奇怪的灼烧感。
a burning sensation = 灼烧感
sensation = 感觉
② After the accident, he lost all sensation in his left leg for several weeks.
= 事故后，他左腿有好几个星期完全没了知觉。
lose sensation = 失去知觉
③ I had the strange sensation that someone was watching me from the trees.
= 我有一种奇怪的感觉，好像有人在树林里盯着我。
have the sensation that = 有……的感觉
④ The young singer caused a sensation when she appeared on television for the first time.
= 那位年轻歌手第一次上电视就引起了轰动。
cause a sensation = 引起轰动

sensational
/senˈseɪʃənl/
核心：形容词：轰动的、耸人听闻的（常贬义，指新闻）：sensational stories；（口语）极好的、精彩的：a sensational goal。副词 sensationally 是轰动地、极其。
① The newspaper printed sensational stories about the actor's private life to sell more copies.
= 那家报纸刊登关于那位演员私生活的耸人听闻的故事，好多卖几份。
sensational stories = 耸人听闻的故事
sensational = 耸人听闻的
② He scored a sensational goal from forty yards in the last minute.
= 他在最后一分钟从四十码外打进了一记精彩绝伦的进球。
a sensational goal = 精彩的进球
③ The little village team sensationally beat the champions in the cup final last May.
= 去年五月，那支小村队在杯赛决赛中爆冷击败了冠军队。
sensationally = 轰动地

sensationalism
/senˈseɪʃənəlɪzəm/
核心：名词，不可数（贬义）：（新闻的）煽情手法、哗众取宠。
① The television channel was accused of sensationalism in its reports about the crime.
= 那家电视台被指在报道那起罪案时哗众取宠。
sensationalism = 哗众取宠
② Good journalists avoid sensationalism and report the facts calmly, even when the story is shocking.
= 好记者不搞煽情，即使新闻令人震惊，也冷静地报道事实。
avoid sensationalism = 不搞煽情
常用来批评小报和娱乐新闻。

sensationalist
/senˈseɪʃənəlɪst/
核心：形容词（贬义）：哗众取宠的、渲染耸人听闻的。名词，可数：哗众取宠的人。
① The sensationalist headlines in the papers frightened people more than the actual news did.
= 报纸上那些耸人听闻的标题比新闻本身更让人害怕。
sensationalist headlines = 耸人听闻的标题
sensationalist = 哗众取宠的
② He is a sensationalist who will say anything to get attention on television.
= 他是个哗众取宠的人，为了在电视上博关注什么话都说得出口。
a sensationalist = 哗众取宠的人
多修饰 press、media、headlines、reporting。

sensationalize
/senˈseɪʃənəlaɪz/
核心：动词，及物（贬义）：渲染、夸大（以引起轰动）。英式也拼作 sensationalise。
① The magazine sensationalized the story to make it seem much worse than it really was.
= 那份杂志渲染那个故事，把它说得比实际糟糕得多。
sensationalize a story = 渲染故事
sensationalize = 渲染、夸大
② The police asked the newspapers not to sensationalize the crime, out of respect for the family.
= 出于对家属的尊重，警方请报纸不要渲染那起罪案。
sensationalize a crime = 渲染罪案
宾语多是 story、news、crime。

sense
/sens/
核心：名词：感官、官能（可数）：the five senses、a sense of smell；（单数）感觉、意识：a sense of humour、a sense of direction、a sense of guilt；（不可数）判断力、道理：common sense、there is no sense in doing something；（可数）意义、含义：in a sense、in every sense；（复数 senses）理智：come to your senses。动词：（凭直觉）感觉到、意识到：sense danger、sense that…。
习语：make sense 是讲得通、有道理；in a sense 是在某种意义上；common sense 是常识、判断力；come to your senses 是醒悟过来；a sense of humour 是幽默感。
① Dogs have a much better sense of smell than people do, so they can find lost children.
= 狗的嗅觉比人灵敏得多，所以能找到走失的孩子。
a sense of smell = 嗅觉
② Being part of the team gave him a sense of belonging for the first time in his life.
= 加入团队让他平生第一次有了归属感。
a sense of = …感
③ The instructions do not make sense; I have read them three times and still do not understand.
= 这说明书讲不通，我读了三遍还是不明白。
make sense = 讲得通
④ In a sense, losing the job was the best thing that ever happened to her.
= 从某种意义上说，丢了那份工作是她遇到的最好的事。
in a sense = 在某种意义上
⑤ Use your common sense and take an umbrella if the sky looks grey.
= 动动脑子，天色发灰就带把伞。
common sense = 常识、判断力
⑥ He was going to give up university, but he came to his senses and finished his degree.
= 他本来打算退学，可后来醒悟过来，读完了学位。
come to your senses = 醒悟过来
⑦ My father has a great sense of humour and can make anyone laugh.
= 我父亲很有幽默感，能把谁都逗笑。
a sense of humour = 幽默感
⑧ There is no sense in waiting any longer; the last bus has already gone.
= 再等也没有意义了，末班车已经走了。
there is no sense in doing something = 做某事没有意义
⑨ The horse sensed danger and refused to go any further along the path.
= 那匹马察觉到危险，不肯再沿着小路往前走。
sense danger = 察觉到危险
⑩ She sensed that something was wrong as soon as she walked into the house.
= 她一进屋就感觉到有点不对劲。
sense that = 感觉到

sensibility
/ˌsensəˈbɪləti/
核心：名词：（对艺术、美的）感受力、鉴赏力（不可数或可数）：artistic sensibility；（复数 sensibilities）（易受伤害的）感情、情感：offend somebody's sensibilities。
① The young painter showed great artistic sensibility in his use of colour and light.
= 那位年轻画家在用色和用光上表现出很强的艺术感受力。
artistic sensibility = 艺术感受力
sensibility = 感受力
② The film was cut so that it would not offend the sensibilities of older viewers.
= 那部电影做了删减，以免冒犯年长观众的感情。
offend somebody's sensibilities = 冒犯某人的感情
注意和 sensitivity、sensible（明智的）区分。

/ˈsensəbl/
