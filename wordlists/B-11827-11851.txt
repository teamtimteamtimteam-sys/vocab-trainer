Impressionism
/ɪmˈpreʃənɪzəm/
核心：印象派 —— 十九世纪后期兴起于法国的绘画流派，注重捕捉光与色的瞬间印象；也用于音乐。
名称来自莫奈一幅画的标题，最初是评论家的嘲讽。首字母大写。
① The exhibition traces the history of Impressionism from its first shows in Paris.
= 那个展览追溯了印象派从巴黎首批展出开始的历史。
Impressionism = 印象派
② Debussy's music is often linked with Impressionism in painting.
= 德彪西的音乐常被与绘画中的印象派联系在一起。
Impressionism in music = 音乐中的印象派

impressionist
/ɪmˈpreʃənɪst/
核心：两个意思，来自两个源头：一是印象派画家（多大写 Impressionist）；二是模仿名人的表演者 ——
来自 do an impression of。形容词 impressionistic（印象派的；凭印象的、不精确的）并入本条。
① Monet is probably the most famous of the French Impressionists.
= 莫奈可能是法国印象派画家里最有名的一位。
an Impressionist = 印象派画家
② The impressionist on the comedy show can do the voices of fifty celebrities.
= 那个喜剧节目里的模仿演员能学五十个名人的声音。
an impressionist = 模仿秀演员
③ His account of the battle is impressionistic rather than accurate.
= 他对那场战斗的叙述只是凭印象，而不准确。
impressionistic = 凭印象的、不精确的

impressive
/ɪmˈpresɪv/
核心：令人印象深刻的、了不起的 —— 从 impress 来，说规模、成绩、表现、外观。
副词 impressively 并入本条。
① Winning five gold medals at her first Olympics is an impressive achievement.
= 在第一届奥运会上就拿下五枚金牌，是个了不起的成就。
an impressive achievement = 了不起的成就
② The cathedral is even more impressive when it is lit up at night.
= 那座大教堂夜里亮灯时更加壮观。
be even more impressive = 更加壮观
③ The children behaved impressively well during the long ceremony.
= 在漫长的仪式中，孩子们表现得出奇地好。
impressively = 令人佩服地

imprimatur
/ˌɪmprɪˈmɑːtə(r)/
核心：（正式）认可、批准 —— 本义是天主教会准许出版的许可（拉丁语「准予印行」），
今天泛指权威方面的认可。
① The project went ahead with the imprimatur of the prime minister himself.
= 那个项目在首相本人的认可下推进了。
the imprimatur of somebody = 某人的认可
② The book carries the imprimatur of the bishop, so it can be used in Catholic schools.
= 那本书有主教的出版许可，所以可以在天主教学校使用。
carry the imprimatur = 获得出版许可

imprint
/ɪmˈprɪnt/（动）/ˈɪmprɪnt/（名）
核心：印记、痕迹 —— 压出来的印（名词重音在前）；比喻深刻的影响；出版上指出版社的品牌、印记。
动词（重音在后）：印上；使铭记（be imprinted on your memory）。
① The fossil shows the clear imprint of a leaf in the stone.
= 那块化石上清晰地留着一片叶子的印痕。
the imprint of something = …的印痕
② The war left a deep imprint on the whole generation.
= 那场战争在整整一代人身上留下了深深的烙印。
leave an imprint on somebody = 在某人身上留下烙印
③ The scene was imprinted on her memory for the rest of her life.
= 那个场景她一辈子都记得清清楚楚。
be imprinted on your memory = 铭刻在记忆里
④ The novel was published under a small imprint belonging to a large publishing group.
= 那部小说是以一家大出版集团旗下一个小品牌的名义出版的。
a publishing imprint = 出版品牌

imprison
/ɪmˈprɪzən/
核心：监禁、关押 —— 把人关进监狱；比喻使人受困（imprisoned by fear）。名词 imprisonment 并入本条。
① He was imprisoned for ten years for his part in the robbery.
= 他因参与那起抢劫被判入狱十年。
be imprisoned for something = 因…被监禁
② She felt imprisoned by the routine of her job and the small town.
= 她觉得自己被那份工作的例行公事和那个小镇困住了。
feel imprisoned = 觉得被困住
③ The judge sentenced him to life imprisonment for the murder.
= 法官以谋杀罪判他终身监禁。
life imprisonment = 终身监禁

improbable
/ɪmˈprɒbəbəl/
核心：不大可能的；离奇的 —— im- 加 probable。两支：不太可能发生（it is improbable that）；
出人意料、令人难以置信（an improbable friendship）。名词 improbability 与副词 improbably 并入本条。
① It is highly improbable that anyone will find the ring after so many years.
= 这么多年过去了，有人找到那枚戒指的可能性极小。
it is improbable that = …不大可能
② The film tells the improbable story of a cat that travelled across Europe on trains.
= 那部电影讲的是一只猫坐火车穿越欧洲的离奇故事。
an improbable story = 离奇的故事
③ Despite the improbability of success, they decided to try.
= 尽管成功的可能性很小，他们还是决定试一试。
the improbability of something = …的不大可能
④ The team, improbably, reached the final with only ten players.
= 那支球队只剩十个人，却出人意料地进了决赛。
improbably = 出人意料地

impromptu
/ɪmˈprɒmptuː/
核心：即兴的、临时的 —— 事先没准备、当场就做。多说讲话、聚会、演出。
也作名词：即兴曲（音乐）。
① When the band failed to turn up, the guests held an impromptu concert in the kitchen.
= 乐队没来，客人们就在厨房里即兴开了场音乐会。
an impromptu concert = 即兴音乐会
② The minister gave an impromptu press conference on the steps of the court.
= 部长在法院台阶上临时开了个记者会。
an impromptu press conference = 临时记者会
③ She played one of Schubert's impromptus at the end of the recital.
= 独奏会最后，她弹了一首舒伯特的即兴曲。
an impromptu = 即兴曲

improper
/ɪmˈprɒpə(r)/
核心：不当的、不合适的 —— im- 加 proper。三支：不合规矩、不诚实（improper use of funds，常指违规违法）；
不得体、有伤风化（improper suggestions，旧时说性方面）；数学上的「假分数」（improper fraction，分子大于分母）。
副词 improperly 并入本条。
① The inquiry found no evidence of improper conduct by the police.
= 调查没有发现警方有不当行为的证据。
improper conduct = 不当行为
多指违反规定，甚至违法。
② The judge said it would be improper to comment on the case while it was still being heard.
= 法官说，案件还在审理，发表评论是不恰当的。
it would be improper to do something = 做…是不恰当的
③ He was sacked for making improper suggestions to a junior colleague.
= 他因向一位年轻同事提出不轨的要求而被解雇。
improper suggestions = 不轨的要求
④ Seven over four is an improper fraction because the top number is bigger.
= 四分之七是个假分数，因为分子比分母大。
an improper fraction = 假分数
⑤ The documents had been improperly obtained and could not be used as evidence.
= 那些文件是以不正当手段取得的，不能作为证据。
improperly = 不正当地、不当地

impropriety
/ˌɪmprəˈpraɪəti/
核心：（正式）不得体、不正当的行为 —— improper 的名词。可数时指一件具体的不当之事，
常见于政治、财务丑闻的报道（financial impropriety）。
① The minister denied any impropriety in awarding the contract to his brother's firm.
= 部长否认把合同给他哥哥的公司有任何不当之处。
deny any impropriety = 否认有任何不当之处
② An audit uncovered serious financial improprieties at the charity.
= 一次审计揭出那家慈善机构有严重的财务违规行为。
financial impropriety = 财务违规

improv
/ˈɪmprɒv/
核心：（非正式）即兴表演 —— 由 improvisation 截短而来，多指即兴喜剧、即兴戏剧。
不可数，也作定语：an improv class。
① She took an improv class to feel less nervous about speaking in public.
= 她上了一门即兴表演课，好让自己在公开场合说话时不那么紧张。
an improv class = 即兴表演课
② The show is pure improv, and the actors ask the audience for ideas at the start.
= 那场演出完全是即兴的，演员们一开场就请观众出点子。
improv = 即兴表演

improve
/ɪmˈpruːv/
核心：变好、改进 —— 可及物（improve something）也可不及物（things improved）。
另有两个常用搭配：improve on/upon（胜过、改进已有的东西）；improve yourself（自我提升）。
名词 improvement 另立一条。
① Her English improved a lot after she spent a year in London.
= 在伦敦待了一年后，她的英语进步很大。
improve = 变好、进步
不及物：主语自己变好。
② The council has promised to improve the roads before winter.
= 市政会承诺入冬前改善道路状况。
improve something = 改善某物
③ It will be hard to improve on last year's results.
= 要超过去年的成绩会很难。
improve on something = 改进、胜过…
④ The new model improves upon the original design in almost every way.
= 新款几乎在各方面都比原先的设计更好。
improve upon something = 在…基础上改进
upon 比 on 更正式，意思相同。
⑤ He went to evening classes to improve himself and find a better job.
= 他去上夜校提升自己，想找份更好的工作。
improve yourself = 提升自己

improvement
/ɪmˈpruːvmənt/
核心：改进、改善 —— improve 的名词。不可数指「变好」这回事；可数指具体的改进之处
（an improvement on/in）。复数 improvements 还常指房屋的装修改造（home improvements）。
① There has been a big improvement in her health since she stopped smoking.
= 自从戒烟以后，她的健康状况大有好转。
an improvement in something = …方面的改善
② The new system is a great improvement on the old one.
= 新系统比旧系统强多了。
an improvement on something = 比…有所改进
on 后面接比较的对象；in 后面接改善的方面。
③ Your essay is good, but there is still room for improvement.
= 你的文章不错，但还有改进的余地。
room for improvement = 改进的余地
④ They spent their savings on home improvements such as a new kitchen.
= 他们把积蓄花在了翻修房子上，比如装了个新厨房。
home improvements = 家居装修

improvident
/ɪmˈprɒvɪdənt/
核心：（正式）不为将来打算的、挥霍的 —— im- 加 provident（有远见的、节俭的）。
名词 improvidence 并入本条。
① An improvident young man, he spent his inheritance in two years and had nothing left.
= 他是个不为将来打算的年轻人，两年就把遗产花光了。
an improvident young man = 挥霍无度的年轻人
② The family's poverty was partly the result of their father's improvidence.
= 那家人的贫穷，部分是父亲不懂节俭造成的。
somebody's improvidence = 某人的不知节俭

improvise
/ˈɪmprəvaɪz/
核心：即兴创作；临时凑合。两支：没准备就当场说、演、奏（improvise a speech）；
手边缺东西时用别的凑合（improvise a bed from cushions）。名词 improvisation 并入本条。
① The actor forgot his lines and had to improvise for the rest of the scene.
= 演员忘了台词，那场戏剩下的部分只好即兴发挥。
improvise = 即兴表演
② With no tent, they improvised a shelter from branches and a sheet of plastic.
= 没有帐篷，他们用树枝和一块塑料布临时搭了个棚子。
improvise something from something = 用…临时凑合出…
③ Jazz depends heavily on improvisation, so no two performances are the same.
= 爵士乐很大程度上靠即兴，所以没有两场演出是一样的。
improvisation = 即兴创作

imprudent
/ɪmˈpruːdənt/
核心：（正式）不明智的、轻率的 —— im- 加 prudent（谨慎的）。多说财务、法律上的决定。
名词 imprudence 与副词 imprudently 并入本条。
① It would be imprudent to invest all your savings in a single company.
= 把所有积蓄都投进一家公司是不明智的。
it would be imprudent to do something = 做…是不明智的
② He regretted the imprudence of signing the contract without reading it.
= 他后悔自己没看就签了合同，太轻率了。
the imprudence of doing something = 做…的轻率
③ The bank had lent imprudently to people who could never repay.
= 那家银行轻率地把钱借给了根本还不起的人。
lend imprudently = 轻率放贷

impudent
/ˈɪmpjʊdənt/
核心：（正式）无礼的、放肆的 —— 对应该尊敬的人不尊敬，多说孩子、年轻人。比 rude 旧式。
名词 impudence 并入本条。
① During the school assembly an impudent boy in the front row stuck his tongue out at the headmaster.
= 全校集会时，前排一个放肆的男孩冲着校长吐舌头。
an impudent boy = 放肆的男孩
② On her second day in the office she had the impudence to tell her boss how to run the department.
= 上班第二天，她居然放肆到告诉老板该怎么管理部门。
have the impudence to do something = 竟敢做…

impugn
/ɪmˈpjuːn/
核心：（正式）质疑、指责 —— 公开对某人的诚信、动机、名誉提出怀疑。多见于法律与政治语言。
① Nobody is impugning your honesty; we just need to check the figures.
= 没人在质疑你的诚信，我们只是需要核对一下数字。
impugn somebody's honesty = 质疑某人的诚信
② The lawyer tried to impugn the motives of the main witness.
= 律师试图质疑主要证人的动机。
impugn somebody's motives = 质疑某人的动机

impulse
/ˈɪmpʌls/
核心：拉丁语「推动」。本质是一股推力，按推的是什么分三支：心里突然的冲动（an impulse to do、on impulse）；
促使事情发生的动力（give an impulse to trade）；物理、生理上的脉冲（nerve impulses）。
impulse buy / impulse buying（冲动购物）并入本条；形容词 impulsive 另立。
① Halfway through the meeting she felt a sudden impulse to call her mother.
= 会开到一半，她突然有一股冲动想给妈妈打电话。
an impulse to do something = 做…的冲动
② He bought the car on impulse and regretted it the next morning.
= 他一时冲动买了那辆车，第二天早上就后悔了。
on impulse = 一时冲动
③ Supermarkets put chocolate near the tills to encourage impulse buying.
= 超市把巧克力摆在收银台附近，诱导人们冲动购物。
impulse buying = 冲动购物
④ The sunglasses were an impulse buy at the airport.
= 那副墨镜是在机场一时冲动买的。
an impulse buy = 冲动买下的东西
⑤ Nerve impulses carry messages from the brain to the muscles.
= 神经脉冲把信息从大脑传到肌肉。
a nerve impulse = 神经脉冲
第三支：物理、生理学上的脉冲，可数。
⑥ The new trade agreement gave fresh impulse to the region's economy.
= 新的贸易协定给那个地区的经济注入了新的动力。
give impulse to something = 给…以推动（正式）

impulsion
/ɪmˈpʌlʃən/
核心：（正式）冲动、强烈的欲望；推动力 —— 跟 impulse 同源，但更正式，也更强调「被推着」的感觉。
① He felt an impulsion to confess everything to the police.
= 他有一股强烈的冲动，想向警方和盘托出。
an impulsion to do something = 做…的强烈冲动
② The main impulsion for reform came from the younger members of the party.
= 推动改革的主要力量来自党内的年轻党员。
the impulsion for something = …的推动力

impulsive
/ɪmˈpʌlsɪv/
核心：冲动的、凭一时冲动行事的 —— 不经思考就做，可说人也可说行为。跟 impetuous 近义，
impulsive 偏「一念之间」。副词 impulsively 与名词 impulsiveness 并入本条。
① He is so impulsive that he once booked a holiday during a coffee break.
= 他冲动得很，有一次喝杯咖啡的工夫就订了个假期。
be impulsive = 冲动
② In an impulsive moment the night before her exams, she cut off all her hair.
= 考试前一晚，她一时冲动把头发全剪了。
an impulsive moment = 一时冲动
③ She impulsively hugged the stranger who had found her dog.
= 她一冲动就拥抱了那个帮她找到狗的陌生人。
impulsively = 冲动地
④ His impulsiveness got him into trouble at school more than once.
= 他的冲动不止一次让他在学校惹上麻烦。
somebody's impulsiveness = 某人的冲动

impunity
/ɪmˈpjuːnɪti/
核心：不受惩罚 —— 几乎只用在 with impunity（不受惩罚地、肆无忌惮地）里。
① Gangs operated with impunity in the port because the police had been bribed.
= 因为警方被收买了，帮派在港口肆无忌惮地活动。
with impunity = 不受惩罚地
② The report criticized the culture of impunity that protected senior officers.
= 报告批评了那种庇护高级军官、让他们逍遥法外的风气。
a culture of impunity = 有罪不罚的风气

impure
/ɪmˈpjʊə(r)/
核心：不纯的；不纯洁的。两支：（物质）掺了杂质的；（旧，道德上）不纯洁的，多指性方面的念头。
名词 impurity 另立。
① The drugs sold on the street are often impure and dangerous.
= 街头卖的毒品常常掺了杂质，很危险。
impure drugs = 掺杂的毒品
② The priest warned the young men against impure thoughts.
= 神父告诫那些年轻人不要有淫邪的念头。
impure thoughts = 不纯洁的念头（旧）

impurity
/ɪmˈpjʊərɪti/
核心：杂质；不纯。可数时指具体的杂质（多用复数）；不可数时指不纯的状态。
① The water is filtered to remove impurities before it reaches your tap.
= 水在流到你家龙头之前要经过过滤，去除杂质。
remove impurities = 去除杂质
② Tests showed a high level of impurity in the metal.
= 检测显示那种金属杂质含量很高。
a high level of impurity = 杂质含量高

impute
/ɪmˈpjuːt/
核心：（正式）归咎于、把（罪责、动机）归于 —— impute something to somebody。
名词 imputation（归咎、指责）并入本条。
① Without any real evidence, it would be unfair to impute selfish motives to a woman who gave away half her salary.
= 没有任何真凭实据，就把自私的动机加到一个捐出一半工资的女人身上，是不公平的。
impute something to somebody = 把…归于某人
宾语较长时可以把介词短语提前：impute to her motives she never had。
② The senator was furious at the imputation in the newspaper that he had lied to the committee.
= 报纸暗指参议员对委员会撒了谎，他对此大为愤怒。
the imputation that = …的指责
