layabout
核心：（英，口语，旧）游手好闲的人、懒汉。
① Her father called him a layabout who had never done a day's work.
= 她父亲说他是个游手好闲的家伙，一天活也没干过。

= 一伙游手好闲的年轻人整天在那家店外面晃荡。
young layabouts = 游手好闲的年轻人
字面是「到处躺着的人」。

① She put the winter coat on layaway and paid a little every week.
= 她付了订金把那件冬大衣留下，每周付一点。

offer layaway = 提供预付留货
没有信用卡的年代很常见。

layer
核心：层 —— 一层东西盖在另一层上；（机构、体系里的）层级。动词：把…分层叠放。layer cake（夹层蛋糕）并入本条。

lea

lead
核心：两个不同的词，拼法相同。
其一读如 leed：带领、领路；通向；导致（lead to）；领先；过（某种生活）；领导。过去式、过去分词 led。
名词：领先地位（in the lead、take the lead）；榜样、带头；线索；主角；（英）牵狗的绳子；（英）电线。
短语动词：lead to（导致；通向）、lead up to（是…的前奏；把话引向）、lead on（哄骗、让人抱空想）、
lead off（开头、打头阵）、lead with（把…放在头条；以…开头）。
习语：lead the way（带路；领先）、lead astray（带坏、引入歧途）。
搭配：lead story（头条新闻）、lead time（从下单到交货的周期）、lead-in（开场白、引子）、lead guitar（主音吉他）。
其二读如 led：铅 —— 一种重而软的灰色金属；铅笔芯。
形容词 leaded（含铅的；镶铅条的）与 lead-free（无铅的）、lead shot（铅弹丸）、leaded light（铅条镶嵌的小窗格）并入本条。
① The guide led us through the forest to the waterfall.
= 向导带我们穿过森林去看瀑布。
lead somebody to a place = 带某人去某处
led 是过去式。
② This path leads to the beach.
= 这条小路通向海滩。
lead to a place = 通向某处
③ Eating too much sugar can lead to serious health problems.
= 吃糖太多会导致严重的健康问题。
lead to = 导致
④ You lead the way, and we will follow in the other car.
= 你在前面带路，我们开另一辆车跟着。
lead the way = 带路
也比喻在某方面走在前头。
⑤ She was leading by two points when the rain stopped the match.
= 下雨中断比赛时，她领先两分。
lead by two points = 领先两分
⑥ He has led a quiet life in the village since he retired.
= 退休以后，他在村里过着平静的生活。
lead a quiet life = 过平静的生活
⑦ She was chosen to lead the team on the new project.
= 她被选中带领团队做那个新项目。
lead a team = 带领团队
⑧ What led you to believe that the shop was closing?
= 是什么让你以为那家店要关门？
lead somebody to do something = 使某人去做…
⑨ He fell in with older boys who led him astray.
= 他结交了一些年纪大的男孩，被他们带坏了。
lead astray = 带坏、引入歧途
⑩ In the weeks leading up to the election, the newspapers wrote about nothing else.
= 选举前的几个星期，报纸上别的什么都不写。
lead up to = 是…的前奏、临近
⑪ He talked about the weather for a while, and I wondered what he was leading up to.
= 他聊了一会儿天气，我琢磨他到底想把话引到哪儿去。
lead up to something = 把话引向…
⑫ She thought he loved her, but he was only leading her on.
= 她以为他爱她，可他只是在哄她。
lead on = 哄骗、让人抱空想
⑬ The chairman led off with a short welcome to the new members.
= 主席先说了几句欢迎新成员的话作开场。
lead off = 开头、打头阵
⑭ Every newspaper led with the story of the missing child.
= 各家报纸都把那个失踪儿童的事放在头条。
lead with = 把…放在头条
⑮ Our team took the lead ten minutes before the end.
= 我们队在终场前十分钟取得了领先。
take the lead = 取得领先；带头
⑯ The Kenyan runner has been in the lead since the first lap.
= 那位肯尼亚选手从第一圈起就一直领先。
in the lead = 领先
⑰ If we recycle more at home, the children will follow our lead.
= 我们在家多做回收，孩子们就会跟着学。

⑱ The police have several leads but have made no arrests.

a lead = 线索
⑲ She plays the lead in a new film about a jazz singer.
= 她在一部讲爵士歌手的新片里演主角。
play the lead = 演主角
⑳ Dogs must be kept on a lead in the park.
= 在公园里，狗必须用绳子牵着。
on a lead = （英）用绳子牵着
美式说 leash。
㉑ The fire was the lead story on every news programme.

㉒ There is a lead time of six weeks between ordering the sofa and getting it.

lead-free = 无铅的
㉙ The old cottage has small leaded lights in every window.

leaf

① A single yellow leaf fell from the tree onto the path.
= 一片黄叶从树上飘落到小路上。
a leaf = 一片叶子
② The children collected autumn leaves and stuck them in a book.
= 孩子们捡了秋天的落叶，贴在本子里。
leaves = leaf 的复数
③ The trees come into leaf in April.
= 那些树四月长叶。

leak
核心：漏 —— （容器、屋顶）漏水漏气；（液体、气体）漏出来；泄露（机密）。名词：漏洞、裂缝；泄漏；泄密。
短语动词 leak out（消息走漏）。名词 leakage（泄漏、漏出量）与形容词 leaky（漏的）并入本条。
① The roof leaks every time it rains hard.
= 一下大雨，屋顶就漏。
the roof leaks = 屋顶漏水
② Oil was leaking from the engine onto the garage floor.

③ Someone in the office leaked the report to a newspaper.
= 办公室里有人把那份报告泄露给了一家报纸。
leak something to somebody = 把…泄露给某人
④ News of the sale leaked out before the company was ready to announce it.
= 公司还没准备好宣布，出售的消息就走漏了。
leak out = （消息）走漏
⑤ There is a leak in the pipe under the kitchen sink.
= 厨房水槽下面的管子有个漏洞。
a leak in the pipe = 管子上的漏洞
⑥ A gas leak forced fifty families to leave their homes overnight.
= 一次煤气泄漏迫使五十户人家连夜离家。
a gas leak = 煤气泄漏
⑦ The minister ordered an inquiry into the leak of the secret letters.
= 部长下令调查那些密信泄露一事。

⑧ The company loses a fifth of its water through leakage from old pipes.
= 那家公司五分之一的水因旧管道渗漏而损失掉。
leakage = 泄漏、漏出量

⑨ We crossed the lake in a leaky old boat and got our feet wet.
= 我们坐一条漏水的旧船过湖，脚都湿了。

lean
核心：倾斜、探身、弯腰；倚、靠（lean against / on）。过去式 leaned，英式也用 leant。
形容词：（人）精瘦的；（肉）瘦的；（时期）不景气的、歉收的；（机构）精简的。
短语动词：lean on（依靠；口语：施压）、lean towards（倾向于，美式多写 lean toward）、lean to（偏向）。
名词 leaning（倾向、偏好）与 leanness（精瘦）并入本条。
① She leaned out of the window to wave goodbye.
= 她探出窗外挥手告别。
lean out of the window = 探出窗外
② He leaned forward to hear what the old man was saying.
= 他往前探了探身子，好听清老人在说什么。
lean forward = 往前探身
③ She leaned her bicycle against the wall of the shop.
= 她把自行车靠在商店的墙上。
lean something against the wall = 把…靠在墙上
④ The tower leans slightly to one side.
= 那座塔略微向一边倾斜。
lean to one side = 向一边倾斜
⑤ He has always leaned on his older sister for advice.
= 他一向依赖姐姐给他出主意。
lean on = 依靠
⑥ The bank is leaning on him to pay back the loan this month.
= 银行正逼他这个月还贷款。
lean on somebody = （口语）向某人施压
同一个短语的另一面：靠上去压人。
⑦ I am leaning towards the blue one, but I have not decided yet.
= 我倾向于要蓝色的那个，不过还没定。
lean towards = 倾向于
lean toward = 倾向于（美式写法）
⑧ The paper leans to the right in its politics.
= 那家报纸政治立场偏右。
lean to = 偏向
⑨ He is a tall, lean man with a quiet voice.
= 他是个又高又精瘦的男人，说话声音很轻。
a lean man = 精瘦的男人
是好话：瘦而结实。
⑩ Use lean meat and cut off any fat before cooking.
= 用瘦肉，下锅前把肥的都切掉。
lean meat = 瘦肉
⑪ The company survived the lean years after the war by selling land.
= 那家公司靠卖地熬过了战后的萧条年月。
lean years = 萧条的年月
⑫ He has strong leanings towards the left of the party.
= 他明显倾向于党内的左派。
leanings towards something = 对…的倾向

⑬ The leanness of the runners showed how hard they had trained.
= 那些跑步选手一个个精瘦，可见训练多刻苦。
leanness = 精瘦

核心：跳、跃 —— 跳得高或远；猛地一动；（数量、价格）猛增。过去式 leapt 或 leaped。名词：跳跃；飞跃、剧增。

leap at = 迫不及待地抓住

your heart leaps = 心头一喜
⑥ He cleared the stream in a single leap.

in a single leap = 一跃
⑦ There has been a big leap in the number of people working from home.

a leap of faith = 放手一搏、姑且一信
⑪ She was born on 29 February in a leap year.
= 她生在闰年的二月二十九日。
