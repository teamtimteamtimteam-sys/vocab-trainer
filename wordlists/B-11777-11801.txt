imperious
/ɪmˈpɪəriəs/
核心：（正式）专横的、傲慢的 —— 像发号施令的人一样，期待别人服从。来自 imperial 同一个词根。
副词 imperiously 并入本条。
① With an imperious wave of her hand, she sent the waiter away.
= 她傲慢地一挥手，把服务员打发走了。
an imperious gesture = 傲慢的手势
② "Bring me the file at once," he said imperiously.
= 「马上把文件给我拿来。」他专横地说。
imperiously = 专横地

imperishable
/ɪmˈperɪʃəbəl/
核心：（文）不朽的、不会消亡的；（食物）不易腐烂的。前一支多说名声、记忆、美；后一支说储存食物。
① The poet hoped his verses would win him imperishable fame.
= 那位诗人希望他的诗能为他赢得不朽的名声。
imperishable fame = 不朽的名声
② Sailors took imperishable foods such as biscuits and salted meat on long voyages.
= 水手们远航时带饼干、咸肉这类不易腐坏的食物。
imperishable foods = 不易腐坏的食物

impermanent
/ɪmˈpɜːmənənt/
核心：（正式）不持久的、暂时的 —— im- 加 permanent。名词 impermanence（无常）并入本条，
多见于哲学、宗教讨论。
① Many of the jobs in the tourist industry are low-paid and impermanent.
= 旅游业的很多工作薪水低、不稳定。
impermanent jobs = 不稳定的工作
② Watching the old house being pulled down made her think about the impermanence of all things.
= 看着那座老房子被拆掉，她想到了万物的无常。
the impermanence of something = …的无常

impermeable
/ɪmˈpɜːmiəbəl/
核心：不透水的、不渗透的 —— im- 加 permeable。多说材料、岩石、薄膜；比喻也可说对观念不为所动。
① The tent is made of an impermeable fabric that keeps out the heaviest rain.
= 那顶帐篷用不透水的面料做成，最大的雨也渗不进来。
an impermeable fabric = 不透水的面料
② Rainwater collects in the valley because it cannot drain through the layer of impermeable rock below.
= 雨水聚在山谷里，因为下面那层不透水的岩石让它渗不下去。
impermeable rock = 不透水岩层

impermissible
/ˌɪmpəˈmɪsəbəl/
核心：（正式）不允许的、不可接受的 —— im- 加 permissible。多见于法律与伦理讨论。
① The court ruled that the evidence was impermissible because it had been obtained illegally.
= 法院裁定那份证据不能采用，因为它是非法取得的。
impermissible evidence = 不可采纳的证据
② Some philosophers argue that lying is impermissible even to save a life.
= 一些哲学家认为，即使为了救命，说谎也是不被允许的。
morally impermissible = 道德上不允许的

impersonal
/ɪmˈpɜːsənəl/
核心：不带个人感情的；冷冰冰的。两支：中性的「客观、不针对个人」（an impersonal letter）；
贬义的「缺乏人情味」（large impersonal hospitals）。语法上另有 impersonal pronoun（非人称代词），
如 it rains 里的 it。名词 impersonality 与副词 impersonally 并入本条。
① Big city hospitals can feel cold and impersonal to elderly patients.
= 大城市的医院对老年病人来说可能显得冰冷、没有人情味。
cold and impersonal = 冷冰冰、没有人情味的
② Try to keep your comments impersonal and focus on the work, not the person.
= 尽量让你的评论客观些，针对工作，不针对人。
keep something impersonal = 保持客观、不针对个人
③ English needs an impersonal pronoun in sentences about the weather, while Spanish can leave it out.
= 英语说天气的句子需要一个非人称代词，西班牙语却可以省掉。
an impersonal pronoun = 非人称代词
④ Workers complained about the impersonality of the new management.
= 工人们抱怨新管理层缺乏人情味。
the impersonality of something = …的缺乏人情味
⑤ The letter was addressed impersonally to "the occupier".
= 那封信冷冰冰地写给「住户」。
impersonally = 不针对个人地

impersonate
/ɪmˈpɜːsəneɪt/
核心：假冒；模仿 —— 装成另一个人。两支：为欺骗而假冒（违法）；为逗乐而模仿（表演）。
名词 impersonation 与 impersonator 并入本条。
① He was arrested for impersonating a police officer to get into the stadium without a ticket.
= 他为了不买票混进体育场，假冒警察，结果被捕了。
impersonate somebody = 假冒某人
② The comedian impersonates famous politicians on his weekly show.
= 那位喜剧演员在他的周播节目里模仿知名政客。
impersonate somebody = 模仿某人（逗乐）
③ She does a brilliant impersonation of the headmaster.
= 她模仿校长模仿得惟妙惟肖。
an impersonation of somebody = 对某人的模仿
④ They hired an Elvis impersonator to sing at the wedding, and the grandparents loved him.
= 他们请了一位模仿猫王的歌手在婚礼上唱歌，爷爷奶奶们喜欢极了。
an impersonator = 模仿者

impertinent
/ɪmˈpɜːtɪnənt/
核心：无礼的、冒失的 —— 对长辈或上级不尊重，多指问题问得不得体。
名词 impertinence 与副词 impertinently 并入本条。
① It was impertinent of the new student to ask the elderly professor about her salary in front of everyone.
= 那个新来的学生当着众人的面问年长的教授挣多少钱，太冒失了。
it is impertinent of somebody to do something = 某人做…很无礼
② The next morning he wrote the head teacher a letter apologizing for his impertinence in class.
= 第二天早上，他给校长写了封信，为自己在课堂上的无礼道歉。
somebody's impertinence = 某人的无礼
③ "Who says I have to tidy my room?" the boy asked impertinently, still staring at his phone.
= 「谁说我非得收拾房间？」那男孩眼睛还盯着手机，没大没小地问。
impertinently = 无礼地

imperturbable
/ˌɪmpəˈtɜːbəbəl/
核心：（正式）沉着冷静的、泰然自若的 —— 什么都扰乱不了他。名词 imperturbability 与副词 imperturbably 并入本条。
① The imperturbable pilot calmly landed the plane with one engine on fire.
= 那位沉着冷静的飞行员在一台发动机着火的情况下平稳地把飞机降落了。
an imperturbable pilot = 沉着冷静的飞行员
② Her imperturbability in a crisis made her the obvious choice to lead the team.
= 她临危不乱，是领导团队的不二人选。
imperturbability = 沉着冷静
③ He smiled imperturbably as the angry crowd booed him all the way to his car.
= 愤怒的人群一路嘘他到车旁，他却泰然自若地微笑着。
imperturbably = 泰然自若地

impervious
/ɪmˈpɜːviəs/
核心：不受影响的；不透（水、气）的。两支：字面上不渗透（impervious to water）；比喻上对批评、劝说、
痛苦无动于衷（impervious to criticism）。两支都接 to。
① The boots are made of a material that is impervious to water, even after hours in the snow.
= 那双靴子的材料不透水，在雪里走几个小时也不会湿。
be impervious to water = 不透水
② He seemed impervious to criticism and carried on exactly as before.
= 他对批评似乎无动于衷，照旧我行我素。
be impervious to criticism = 对批评无动于衷
比喻义：像防水布挡住水一样挡住外界的影响。

impetigo
/ˌɪmpɪˈtaɪɡəʊ/
核心：（医学）脓疱病 —— 一种传染性的皮肤病，多见于儿童。不可数。
① Several children at the nursery caught impetigo and had to stay at home for a few days.
= 托儿所里有好几个孩子染上了脓疱病，只好在家待了几天。
catch impetigo = 染上脓疱病
② The doctor prescribed an antibiotic cream to clear up the impetigo on her face.
= 医生开了一种抗生素药膏，治好她脸上的脓疱病。
clear up impetigo = 治好脓疱病

impetuous
/ɪmˈpetʃuəs/
核心：冲动的、鲁莽的 —— 不经考虑就行动，常带「急性子、年轻气盛」的意味。
跟 impulsive 近义，impetuous 更强调急躁。名词 impetuosity 与副词 impetuously 并入本条。
① In an impetuous moment he quit his job and bought a ticket to Brazil.
= 他一时冲动辞了工作，买了一张去巴西的机票。
an impetuous decision = 冲动的决定
② The coach warned his young players against their impetuosity in front of goal.
= 教练提醒年轻球员在球门前别毛躁。
impetuosity = 急躁、鲁莽
③ She impetuously agreed to marry him after knowing him for only a week.
= 她才认识他一个星期，就冲动地答应嫁给他。
impetuously = 冲动地

impetus
/ˈɪmpɪtəs/
核心：推动力、动力 —— 物理上指运动的冲力，比喻指促使事情发展的力量。
常见搭配：give (a) new impetus to、provide the impetus for、gain impetus。
① The scandal gave new impetus to the campaign for stricter rules on lobbying.
= 那桩丑闻给推动更严格游说规定的运动注入了新的动力。
give new impetus to something = 给…注入新的动力
② The main impetus for the change came from the students themselves.
= 推动这项变革的主要力量来自学生本身。
the impetus for something = …的推动力
③ The cart rolled on under its own impetus after the horse had stopped.
= 马停下后，车子还靠自己的冲力往前滚。
under its own impetus = 靠自身冲力
字面义：物理上的冲力，现在少用。

impinge
/ɪmˈpɪndʒ/
核心：（正式）产生影响、侵犯 —— 多用 impinge on/upon：对…有（不好的）影响，侵犯（权利、自由、隐私）。
① Long working hours began to impinge on his family life.
= 长时间工作开始影响他的家庭生活。
impinge on something = 影响…
② Critics say the new law impinges upon the right to free speech.
= 批评者说新法律侵犯了言论自由的权利。
impinge upon a right = 侵犯某项权利

impious
/ˈɪmpiəs/
核心：（正式）不敬神的、亵渎的 —— im- 加 pious（虔诚的）。名词 impiety 并入本条。
① Many in the village considered it impious to work on a holy day.
= 村里很多人认为在圣日干活是不敬神的。
it is impious to do something = 做…是亵渎的
② He was accused of impiety for mocking the priests in his plays.
= 他因在剧作中嘲讽神父而被指控亵渎神明。
be accused of impiety = 被指控亵渎神明

implacable
/ɪmˈplækəbəl/
核心：（正式）无法平息的、不能和解的 —— 说仇恨、敌人、反对。副词 implacably 并入本条。
① The two families remained implacable enemies for three generations.
= 那两家人三代都是不共戴天的仇敌。
an implacable enemy = 不共戴天的仇敌
② The union was implacably opposed to any cuts in pay.
= 工会坚决反对任何减薪。
be implacably opposed to something = 坚决反对…

implant
/ɪmˈplɑːnt/（动）/ˈɪmplɑːnt/（名）
核心：植入 —— 动词重音在后，名词重音在前。两支：医学上把人造器官、装置植入体内（名词 an implant 就是植入物）；
比喻把观念深深植入人心。名词 implantation（植入；受精卵着床）并入本条。
① Surgeons implanted a tiny device to control his heartbeat.
= 外科医生给他植入了一个控制心跳的微型装置。
implant a device = 植入装置
② She had a dental implant fitted after losing a tooth in the accident.
= 她在事故中掉了一颗牙，后来装了一颗种植牙。
a dental implant = 种植牙
breast implants = 隆胸假体
③ The idea had been implanted in her mind as a child and never went away.
= 这个念头在她小时候就植入了脑海，再也没有消失。
implant an idea in somebody's mind = 把观念植入某人脑中
④ Implantation of the embryo usually happens about a week after fertilization.
= 胚胎着床通常发生在受精后一周左右。
implantation = 着床；植入

implausible
/ɪmˈplɔːzəbəl/
核心：难以置信的、不像真的 —— im- 加 plausible。多说解释、借口、情节。
名词 implausibility 与副词 implausibly 并入本条。
① His explanation for being late was so implausible that nobody believed him.
= 他对迟到的解释太离谱，没人相信他。
an implausible explanation = 难以置信的解释
② The film's plot requires an implausible number of coincidences.
= 那部电影的情节需要一连串难以置信的巧合。
an implausible plot = 不可信的情节
③ Critics pointed out the implausibility of the ending.
= 评论家指出了结局的不合情理。
the implausibility of something = …的不可信
④ The hero survives the fall implausibly, without even a scratch.
= 主角从高处跌落后竟然毫发无损地活了下来，令人难以置信。
implausibly = 令人难以置信地

implement
/ˈɪmplɪment/（动）/ˈɪmplɪmənt/（名）
核心：两个词性、两个意思。动词（末音节读得完整，名词末音节弱读）是实施、执行（计划、决定、政策），这是最常用的一支；
名词是工具、器具，尤指农具、园艺工具。名词 implementation 另立。
① The government has promised to implement the new tax rules by next April.
= 政府承诺在明年四月前实施新的税收规定。
implement rules = 实施规定
② It is one thing to make a plan and another to implement it.
= 定计划是一回事，执行又是另一回事。
implement a plan = 执行计划
③ The museum displays farming implements used in the village two hundred years ago.
= 博物馆展出两百年前村里用的农具。
farming implements = 农具
名词：比 tool 正式，多说简单的手工器具。

implementation
/ˌɪmplɪmenˈteɪʃən/
核心：实施、执行 —— implement 动词的名词。另在计算机上指（某种设计、标准的）具体实现。
① The implementation of the new system has been delayed by a year.
= 新系统的实施推迟了一年。
the implementation of something = …的实施
② There are several implementations of the language, and they do not all behave the same way.
= 这门编程语言有好几种实现，它们的表现不尽相同。
an implementation = （计算机）实现

implicate
/ˈɪmplɪkeɪt/
核心：牵连、表明（某人）与罪行有关 —— 常用 implicate somebody in something、be implicated in。
另可说「被认为是…的原因」（be implicated in a disease）。名词 implication 另立。
① The documents implicate two senior officials in the bribery scandal.
= 那些文件表明两名高级官员与贿赂丑闻有牵连。
implicate somebody in something = 表明某人与…有牵连
② Smoking has been implicated in a wide range of diseases.
= 吸烟被认为与多种疾病有关。
be implicated in a disease = 被认为与某病有关

implication
/ˌɪmplɪˈkeɪʃən/
核心：三支，分属两个动词：imply 的名词 —— 言外之意、暗示（by implication）；
可能的影响、后果（implications for，多用复数）；implicate 的名词 —— 牵连（implication in a crime）。
① The implication of his remark was that I had lied.
= 他那番话的言外之意是我撒了谎。
the implication of something = …的言外之意
② By implication, the report criticizes the minister without naming him.
= 那份报告没点名，却间接批评了部长。
by implication = 间接地、暗含地
③ The decision will have serious implications for the whole industry.
= 这个决定将对整个行业产生严重影响。
implications for something = 对…的影响
第二支：还没显现、但可推知的后果。
④ His implication in the fraud, though never proved in court, ended his political career.
= 他牵涉进那起诈骗案，虽然法庭上始终没有证实，政治生涯却就此结束。
somebody's implication in something = 某人与…的牵连

implicit
/ɪmˈplɪsɪt/
核心：含蓄的、未明说的 —— 跟 explicit（明确的）相对。另一支：完全的、无保留的（implicit trust），
只用在 faith、trust、confidence 前面。副词 implicitly 并入本条。
① Beneath the polite words of her letter there was an implicit threat to go to the newspapers.
= 她那封信措辞客气，底下却暗含着要去找报社的威胁。
an implicit threat = 暗含的威胁
② Criticism of the government was implicit in everything he wrote.
= 他写的每样东西都暗含着对政府的批评。
be implicit in something = 暗含于…
③ She had implicit trust in her doctor and never questioned his advice.
= 她绝对信任她的医生，从不质疑他的建议。
implicit trust = 绝对的信任
第二支只搭 trust、faith、confidence。
④ The contract implicitly allows the company to change the prices.
= 合同暗含允许公司调整价格。
implicitly = 含蓄地、暗中

implode
/ɪmˈpləʊd/
核心：内爆、向内塌陷 —— 跟 explode 相对；比喻指（组织、计划）从内部崩溃。
名词 implosion 并入本条。
① The screen of the old television imploded with a loud bang and scattered glass across the carpet.
= 那台旧电视的屏幕砰的一声内爆了，玻璃碴撒了一地毯。
implode = 内爆
② The party imploded after its leader resigned in disgrace.
= 领导人丢脸辞职后，那个政党从内部分崩离析。
implode = 从内部崩溃
③ The implosion of the banking sector led to a deep recession.
= 银行业的崩溃导致了一场严重的衰退。
the implosion of something = …的崩溃

implore
/ɪmˈplɔː(r)/
核心：（正式）恳求、哀求 —— 比 beg 更正式，带强烈情感。常见 implore somebody to do something。
形容词 imploring（哀求的）并入本条。
① She implored him to stay, but he had already packed his bags.
= 她苦苦哀求他留下，可他已经收拾好行李了。
implore somebody to do something = 恳求某人做…
② The child gave his mother an imploring look as they walked past the toy shop.
= 走过玩具店时，孩子向妈妈投去哀求的目光。
an imploring look = 哀求的目光
