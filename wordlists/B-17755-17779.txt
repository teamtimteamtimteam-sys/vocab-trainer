prod
/prɒd/
核心：动词：（用手指、棍子）戳、捅：prod somebody、prod at something；催促、督促、提醒：prod somebody into doing。过去式 prodded。
名词，可数：一戳、一捅；一次催促、提醒；（赶牲口的）刺棒：a cattle prod。名词 prodding 是催促，不可数。并入本条。
① She prodded him in the back to make him move forward.
= 她在他背上捅了一下，让他往前走。
prod somebody in the back = 在某人背上捅一下
② The child prodded at the fish on his plate with a fork.
= 那孩子用叉子戳着盘子里的鱼。
prod at something = 戳某物
③ The newspapers finally prodded the council into mending the road.
= 报纸终于把镇议会催得动手修路了。
prod somebody into doing something = 催得某人做某事
④ He gave the sleeping dog a gentle prod with his foot.
= 他用脚轻轻捅了一下睡着的狗。
a gentle prod = 轻轻一捅
⑤ I will write the letter tonight, but I may need a prod if I forget.
= 那封信我今晚写，万一忘了，得有人提醒我一下。
need a prod = 需要人提醒
⑥ After much prodding from his wife, he went to see the doctor.
= 妻子催了又催，他才去看了医生。
prodding = 催促

动词：（正式）得益、获利：profit from、by；对……有好处。
习语 at a profit 是赚了钱地、高于成本地。
派生与复合词：profitable 是有利润的、有益的，副词 profitably，名词 profitability；profitless 是无利可图的、无益的；
profit margin 是利润率；profit-sharing 是分红制；profit-taking 是（股市上）获利回吐；profit warning 是盈利预警。并入本条。
① The firm made a profit of two million pounds last year.
= 那家公司去年盈利两百万英镑。
make a profit = 盈利
② There is little profit in arguing with a man who will not listen.
= 跟一个不肯听的人争，没什么好处。
profit in doing something = 做某事的好处
③ She sold the house at a profit two years after she bought it.
= 买下那座房子两年后，她赚了钱把它卖了。
at a profit = 赚了钱地
④ Small farmers have not profited from the rise in food prices.
= 小农户没有从粮价上涨里得到好处。
profit from = 从……得益
profit from something = 从某事得益
⑤ The shop became profitable in its third year.
= 那家店第三年开始赚钱。
profitable = 有利润的
profitably = 有利润地
⑥ The bank is worried about the profitability of its branches in small towns.
= 那家银行担心小城镇分行的盈利能力。
profitability = 盈利能力
⑦ It was a profitless argument that left everyone tired and angry.
= 那是一场毫无益处的争论，弄得人人又累又气。
profitless = 无益的、无利可图的
⑧ The supermarkets work on a very small profit margin.
= 超市的利润率很低。
a profit margin = 利润率
⑨ The firm runs a profit-sharing scheme for all its staff.
= 那家公司对全体员工实行分红制。
profit-sharing = 分红制
⑩ Shares fell slightly as a result of profit-taking after a week of gains.
= 涨了一个星期之后，有人获利回吐，股价略有下跌。
profit-taking = 获利回吐
⑪ The company's shares dropped by a fifth after it issued a profit warning.
= 那家公司发出盈利预警后，股价跌了五分之一。
a profit warning = 盈利预警
⑫ The accountant asked to see the profit and loss account for the last three years.
= 会计要看近三年的损益表。
a profit and loss account = 损益表
列出一段时间里的收入、开支和盈亏。
⑬ The railway has never been a profit-making business and depends on public money.
= 那条铁路从来不是赚钱的买卖，靠公家的钱维持。
profit-making = 赚钱的、营利的
反过来是 non-profit-making（非营利的）。

profiteer
/ˌprɒfɪˈtɪə(r)/
核心：（贬）名词：牟取暴利的人、奸商：趁战争、短缺的时候抬价发财的人，可数。动词：牟取暴利。这种行为叫 profiteering，不可数。并入本条。
① The profiteers sold bread at ten times its normal price during the siege.
= 围城期间，奸商把面包卖到平时十倍的价钱。
a profiteer = 奸商
② The firm was accused of profiteering from the shortage of medicines.
= 那家公司被指责趁药品短缺牟取暴利。
profiteer from something = 趁某事牟取暴利
③ The government passed a law against profiteering in wartime.
= 政府通过了一项禁止战时牟取暴利的法律。
profiteering = 牟取暴利

profiterole
/prəˈfɪtərəʊl/
核心：泡芙、奶油小空心饼：一种小圆酥皮点心，里面填奶油，上面浇巧克力酱。可数。
① She ordered profiteroles with hot chocolate sauce for pudding.
= 甜点她点了浇热巧克力酱的泡芙。
profiteroles = 泡芙
② The wedding cake was a tower of profiteroles held together with caramel.
= 那个婚礼蛋糕是用焦糖粘起来的一座泡芙塔。
a tower of profiteroles = 泡芙塔

profligate
/ˈprɒflɪɡət/
核心：（正式，贬）挥霍无度的、恣意浪费的：花钱、用东西毫无节制；也指放荡的。名词 profligacy，不可数。并入本条。
① The report attacks the profligate spending of the last government.
= 那份报告抨击上届政府挥霍无度的开支。
profligate spending = 挥霍无度的开支
② We cannot go on being so profligate with water in a dry country.
= 在一个干旱的国家，我们不能再这样浪费水了。
profligate with something = 对某物浪费无度
③ The profligacy of the young king emptied the treasury in five years.
= 年轻的国王挥霍无度，五年就把国库花空了。
profligacy = 挥霍无度

profound
/prəˈfaʊnd/
核心：三支。一是（感受、影响）极大的、深切的、深远的；二是（见解、学问）深刻的、渊博的、有深度的；三是（医学、正式）严重的、彻底的：profound deafness。
副词 profoundly；名词 profundity 是深刻、深度，不可数，也指深奥的话，可数。并入本条。
① The death of his father had a profound effect on the boy.
= 父亲的去世对那男孩影响极深。
a profound effect = 极深的影响
② The book offers a profound understanding of how children learn.
= 那本书对孩子怎样学习有深刻的理解。
a profound understanding = 深刻的理解
③ The child was born with a profound loss of hearing in both ears.
= 那孩子生下来两只耳朵就重度失聪。
a profound loss of hearing = 重度失聪
④ I was profoundly moved by the letters that the soldiers wrote home.
= 士兵们写回家的那些信深深打动了我。
profoundly = 深深地
⑤ Readers admire the profundity of her last essays.
= 读者钦佩她最后几篇文章的深度。
profundity = 深刻、深度

profuse
/prəˈfjuːs/
核心：大量的、极其丰富的、源源不断的：多说流血、出汗、道歉、道谢。副词 profusely，并入本条。
① The patient was brought in with profuse bleeding from a cut on the head.
= 那位病人送来时，头上的一道伤口大量出血。
profuse bleeding = 大量出血
② She offered profuse apologies for keeping us waiting in the rain.
= 让我们在雨里等，她一再道歉。
profuse apologies = 一再的道歉
③ He was sweating profusely after carrying the piano up three floors.
= 把钢琴抬上三楼以后，他大汗淋漓。
sweat profusely = 大汗淋漓
