school
/skuːl/
核心：名词，本义是「教人的地方」：学校（可数）：a primary school、a secondary school；上学、上课（不可数，不加冠词）：go to school、after school、leave school；（单数）全校师生：the whole school；（北美）大学（不可数）；（大学的）学院、系（可数）：the school of medicine；（艺术、思想的）流派、学派（可数）：school of thought；（鱼、海豚的）群（可数）：a school of fish。动词（正式）：训练、教育：be schooled in something。
和 the 的分别：go to school 指去上学；go to the school 指为别的事去那栋楼。
习语：school of thought 是（某种）观点、学派；of the old school 是老派的、守旧的。
复合词：school age 是学龄；school-leaving age 是（法定）离校年龄；school-leaver（英式）是中学毕业生；school board 是学校董事会，school council 是学生会（英式）；school day 是上课日；school district 是（美国）学区；school friend 是同学、学校里的朋友；school uniform 是校服。
① The new school has a big library, a swimming pool and a garden where the children grow vegetables.
= 那所新学校有一个大图书馆、一个游泳池和一个孩子们种菜的园子。
school = 学校（建筑）
a school = 一所学校
② My little sister starts school next September, and she is very excited about it.
= 我妹妹明年九月开始上学，她特别兴奋。
school = 上学这件事
这一义不可数、不加冠词：at school、to school、after school。
start school = 开始上学
③ We usually play football in the park after school until it gets dark.
= 我们通常放学后在公园里踢足球，一直踢到天黑。
after school = 放学后
④ He left school at sixteen and went to work in his father's garage.
= 他十六岁就离开学校，去他父亲的修车厂干活。
leave school = 离开学校、毕业
⑤ The whole school came out into the playground to watch the fire engines.
= 全校师生都跑到操场上看消防车。
the whole school = 全校师生
⑥ She went to medical school in Chicago and now works as a doctor in a small town.
= 她在芝加哥上的医学院，现在在一个小镇上当医生。
medical school = 医学院
⑦ There are two schools of thought about how children should learn to read.
= 关于孩子们该怎样学阅读，有两派观点。
school of thought = 学派、观点
常说 there are two schools of thought about…，引出两种看法。
⑧ My grandfather was a gentleman of the old school, who always opened the door for women.
= 我祖父是个老派绅士，总给女士开门。
of the old school = 老派的
⑨ A school of fish swam past us, flashing silver in the clear blue water.
= 一群鱼从我们身边游过，在清澈的蓝色海水里闪着银光。
a school of fish = 一群鱼
⑩ The young prince was schooled in history, languages and good manners from the age of five.
= 小王子五岁起就接受历史、语言和礼仪的教育。
be schooled in something = 接受某方面的训练
⑪ All children of school age must go to school, even if their families move around a lot.
= 所有学龄儿童都必须上学，即使他们的家庭经常搬迁。
school age = 学龄
⑫ The government wants to raise the school-leaving age from sixteen to eighteen.
= 政府想把法定离校年龄从十六岁提高到十八岁。
the school-leaving age = （法定）离校年龄
⑬ Many school-leavers find it hard to get a job without any experience.
= 许多中学毕业生没有经验，很难找到工作。
a school-leaver = （英式）中学毕业生
⑭ The school board voted to build a new sports hall next to the old gym.
= 学校董事会投票决定在旧体育馆旁边建一座新的运动馆。
the school board = 学校董事会
⑮ The pupils elected two members from each class to the school council.
= 学生们从每个班选出两名代表进入学生会。
the school council = （英式）学生会
⑯ The school day starts at half past eight and finishes at a quarter past three.
= 学校每天八点半上课，三点一刻放学。
the school day = 上课日、每天上课时间
⑰ We moved to a new house so that our children could go to a better school district.
= 我们搬了新家，好让孩子们进一个更好的学区。
a school district = （美国）学区
⑱ I met an old school friend at the station whom I had not seen for twenty years.
= 我在车站碰见一位二十年没见的老同学。
a school friend = 同学、学校里的朋友
⑲ The children wear a dark blue school uniform with a white shirt and a red tie.
= 孩子们穿深蓝色校服，配白衬衫和红领带。
a school uniform = 校服

= 「about」的第一个音是中央元音，它是英语里最常见的元音。
a schwa = 中央元音
② Learners often pronounce every vowel fully, but native speakers turn many of them into a schwa.
= 学习者常把每个元音都读足，而母语者会把其中很多读成弱读元音。
turn a vowel into a schwa = 把元音弱读成中央元音
