reflection
/rɪˈflekʃn/
核心：可数也可不可数。一是（镜子、水面里的）映像、倒影；二是（光、热、声的）反射；三是反映、体现：a reflection of；四是深思、反思，不可数：on reflection 是经过仔细考虑、再一想；
五是（写下来的）想法、感想，可数；六是 a reflection on 是（对某人能力、品质的）不良影响、非议。
① She stopped and looked at her reflection in the shop window before the interview.
= 面试之前，她停下来看了看自己在商店橱窗里的映像。
somebody's reflection = 某人的映像
reflection = 倒影、反思
② The reflection of the sun on the snow hurt our eyes.
= 雪地上反射的阳光刺得我们眼睛疼。
the reflection of something = 某物的反射
③ The high crime rate is a reflection of the poverty in the area.
= 犯罪率高反映了那一带的贫困。
a reflection of something = 某事的反映
④ On reflection, I think you were right to refuse the job.
= 再想想，我觉得你拒绝那份工作是对的。
on reflection = 再一想
⑤ The book is a collection of her reflections on life in the country.
= 那本书汇集了她对乡村生活的感想。
reflections on something = 对某事的感想
⑥ The team's failure in the final is no reflection on your ability as a coach.
= 球队决赛失利，并不说明你当教练的能力不行。
a reflection on something = 对某事的不良影响

reflective
/rɪˈflektɪv/
核心：两支。一是沉思的、深思的、若有所思的；二是反光的、反射的：reflective clothing。副词 reflectively；名词 reflectivity 是反射率，不可数。并入本条。
① After the funeral he was in a reflective mood and said very little at dinner.
= 葬礼之后他若有所思，晚饭时没说几句话。
in a reflective mood = 若有所思
reflective = 沉思的
② Cyclists on country roads should wear reflective clothing when they ride at night.
= 在乡间公路上骑车的人，夜里骑行时应该穿反光服。
reflective clothing = 反光服
reflective = 反光的
③ She sat in her grandfather's old chair and stared reflectively at the fire.
= 她坐在爷爷的旧椅子上，若有所思地盯着炉火。
reflectively = 若有所思地
④ Fresh snow has a very high reflectivity, which is why skiers wear dark glasses.
= 新雪的反射率很高，所以滑雪的人戴墨镜。
reflectivity = 反射率

reflector
/rɪˈflektə(r)/
核心：反光器、反光板、反射镜：反射光、热、声的东西，如自行车上的反光片。可数。
① There is a red reflector on the back of the bicycle.
= 自行车后面有一块红色反光片。
a reflector = 反光片

= 摄影师用一块白色反光板给新娘的脸补光。
a white reflector = 白色反光板

reflex
/ˈriːfleks/
核心：名词，可数：反射、反射作用：身体不经思考的自动反应：the knee reflex；reflexes 是反应能力，复数：quick reflexes。形容词：反射的。
① The doctor tapped my knee to test my reflexes.
= 医生敲了敲我的膝盖，检查我的反射。
test somebody's reflexes = 检查某人的反射
a reflex = 反射
② A goalkeeper needs very quick reflexes.
= 守门员需要非常快的反应。
quick reflexes = 反应快
③ Shutting your eyes when something comes near them is a reflex action.
= 东西靠近眼睛时闭眼是一种反射动作。
a reflex action = 反射动作

reflexive
/rɪˈfleksɪv/
核心：两支。一是（语法）反身的：reflexive pronoun 是反身代词，如 myself；reflexive verb 是反身动词；二是（行为）本能反应的、不假思索的。
① In the sentence "She hurt herself on the stairs", the word "herself" is a reflexive pronoun.
= 在「She hurt herself on the stairs」这句里，「herself」是反身代词。
a reflexive pronoun = 反身代词
reflexive = 反身的
② Whatever his children asked for, his reflexive answer was always a firm "no".
= 不管孩子们要什么，他不假思索的回答总是一个坚决的「不」。
a reflexive answer = 不假思索的回答

② The reflexologist pressed the soles of my feet with her thumbs.
= 那位足疗师用拇指按我的脚底。
a reflexologist = 足疗师
