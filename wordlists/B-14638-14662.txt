molehill
/ˈməʊlhɪl/
核心：鼹鼠丘：鼹鼠打洞时拱到地面上的小土堆。
最常见于习语 make a mountain out of a molehill：小题大做，把小事说成大事。
① The football pitch was covered in molehills after the long wet winter.
= 漫长潮湿的冬天过后，足球场上到处是鼹鼠拱出的土堆。
be covered in molehills = 到处是鼹鼠丘
② He tripped over a molehill in the dark and twisted his ankle.
= 他摸黑被一个鼹鼠丘绊倒，崴了脚。
a molehill = 鼹鼠丘
③ It is only a scratch on the door, so stop making a mountain out of a molehill.
= 不过是门上的一道划痕，别小题大做了。
make a mountain out of a molehill = 小题大做

moleskin
/ˈməʊlskɪn/
核心：厚实的斜纹棉布，表面起一层短绒，摸上去像鼹鼠皮，多用来做工装裤。不可数，常作定语。
① The gamekeeper wore a pair of moleskin trousers and heavy leather boots.
= 猎场看守人穿着一条厚绒棉布裤和一双沉重的皮靴。
moleskin trousers = 厚绒棉布裤
② Moleskin is warm and hard-wearing, which makes it popular with farmers.
= 厚绒棉布暖和又耐磨，很受农民喜欢。
moleskin = 厚绒棉布

molest
/məˈlest/
核心：猥亵、性侵犯，尤指对儿童，现在几乎只用这个意思；旧义是骚扰、攻击、打扰（人或动物）。
名词 molestation、molester 并入本条。
① The coach was jailed for molesting two boys in his care.
= 那名教练因猥亵两个由他照管的男孩而入狱。
molest a child = 猥亵儿童
② The notice warns visitors not to molest the birds during the nesting season.
= 告示提醒游客在筑巢季节不要惊扰鸟类。
molest the birds = 惊扰鸟类
旧用法，现在只见于告示和正式文字。
③ He was charged with the molestation of a girl of twelve.
= 他被控猥亵一名十二岁的女孩。
the molestation of a child = 对儿童的猥亵
④ A convicted child molester is not allowed to work in any school.
= 被定过罪的猥亵儿童犯不得在任何学校工作。
a child molester = 猥亵儿童犯

moll
/mɒl/
核心：（旧，俚语）黑帮分子的情妇、女伴。几乎只见于 a gangster's moll。
① She plays a gangster's moll who betrays her lover to the police.
= 她演一个黑帮分子的情妇，向警方出卖了自己的情人。
a gangster's moll = 黑帮分子的情妇
② In the old films the moll always wears furs and smokes through a long holder.
= 老电影里，黑帮的女人总是穿着皮草，叼着长烟嘴抽烟。
the moll = 黑帮的女人

mollify
/ˈmɒlɪfaɪ/
核心：安抚、使消气：让正在生气的人平静下来。正式些。
① The manager tried to mollify the angry customers by offering them a free meal.
= 经理想用免费请一顿饭的办法，安抚那些愤怒的顾客。
mollify somebody by doing something = 用某种办法安抚某人
② She was not mollified by his apology and refused to speak to him for a week.
= 他的道歉没能让她消气，她一个星期不肯跟他说话。
be mollified by something = 因…而消气

mollusc
/ˈmɒləsk/
核心：软体动物：蜗牛、蛞蝓、贻贝、章鱼这一大类身体柔软、多数带壳的动物。美式拼 mollusk。
① The oyster is a mollusc that can change sex several times in its life.
= 牡蛎是一种一生中能几次变换性别的软体动物。
a mollusc = 软体动物
② The beach was covered with the shells of molluscs washed up by the storm.
= 海滩上满是风暴冲上来的软体动物的壳。
the shells of molluscs = 软体动物的壳

molly
/ˈmɒli/
核心：（非正式）摇头丸一类毒品的俗称，指它的粉末或晶体。不可数，也大写。
① Two students were taken to hospital after taking molly at a music festival.
= 两名学生在音乐节上服用摇头丸后被送进医院。
take molly = 服用摇头丸
② The police found a bag of molly hidden in the lining of his jacket.
= 警察在他夹克的衬里中搜出了一包摇头丸。
a bag of molly = 一包摇头丸

mollycoddle
/ˈmɒlikɒdəl/
核心：（贬义）娇惯、过分呵护，把人护得什么苦都吃不了。口气比 spoil 更嫌弃。
① She was mollycoddled as a child and never learned to cook a meal for herself.
= 她从小被娇惯，连给自己做顿饭都没学会。
be mollycoddled as a child = 从小被娇惯
② The coach refuses to mollycoddle his young players and trains them in all weathers.
= 那位教练不肯娇惯年轻球员，什么天气都照练不误。
mollycoddle somebody = 娇惯某人

molten
/ˈməʊltən/
核心：熔化的、熔融的。原是 melt 的旧过去分词，现在只作形容词，而且只说高温才化的东西：金属、岩石、玻璃。
黄油、巧克力、雪化了用 melted。
① Molten lava poured down the side of the volcano and reached the village by dawn.
= 熔岩顺着火山的山坡倾泻而下，天亮前流到了村子。
molten lava = 熔岩
② The workers poured the molten metal into moulds shaped like bells.
= 工人们把熔化的金属浇进钟形的模子里。
molten metal = 熔化的金属

molybdenum
/məˈlɪbdənəm/
核心：钼：一种银白色的金属元素，主要用来炼合金钢。不可数。读音重音在第二个音节。
① A little molybdenum is added to the steel to make it stronger at high temperatures.
= 钢里加一点钼，高温下强度更高。
add molybdenum to steel = 往钢里加钼
② The town grew up around a molybdenum mine that closed thirty years ago.
= 那座小镇是围着一座钼矿发展起来的，矿三十年前就关了。
a molybdenum mine = 钼矿

mom
/mɒm/
核心：（美，非正式）妈妈，英式说 mum。当称呼用时常大写。
更亲昵、孩子气的说法是 mommy 和 momma，并入本条。
mommy track 指为照顾孩子而放慢的职业道路。
① My mom drove me to practice every morning before she went to work.
= 我妈每天早上先开车送我去训练，然后才去上班。
my mom = 我妈
② Can I stay up late tonight, Mom, since there is no school tomorrow?
= 妈，今晚我能晚点睡吗？明天不上学。
Mom = 妈（称呼）
③ She gave up her job to be a stay-at-home mom until the twins started school.
= 她辞了工作在家当全职妈妈，直到双胞胎上学。
a stay-at-home mom = 全职妈妈
④ The little boy cried for his mommy when the lights went out.
= 灯一灭，小男孩就哭着要妈妈。
mommy = 妈咪（幼儿用语）
⑤ His momma taught him to say grace before every meal.
= 他妈妈教他每顿饭前都要祷告。
momma = 妈（美国南方等地的说法）
⑥ The supermarket drove every mom-and-pop store in the neighbourhood out of business.
= 那家超市把街坊里的夫妻小店全挤垮了。
a mom-and-pop store = 夫妻小店
⑦ She refused to be put on the mommy track after her first child was born.
= 生了第一个孩子以后，她不肯被划进那条专给妈妈们走的慢车道。
the mommy track = 妈妈慢车道

moment
/ˈməʊmənt/
核心：底子是「极短的一段时间」，短到近乎一个点。由此两支：一是「一会儿」，说时间短；
二是「那个时刻」，说时间上的一个点，再引申为「时机」和某人露脸的「高光时刻」。
用它搭出的时间状语极多：at the moment 是现在，for the moment 是暂时，in a moment 是马上，
at any moment 是随时，at the last moment 是到最后关头。the moment 还能当连词用，等于 as soon as。
正式用法里另有「重要性」一义：of great moment。
① Could you wait a moment while I look for the right key?
= 你能稍等一下吗？我找找是哪把钥匙。
wait a moment = 稍等一下
② She hesitated for a moment and then signed her name at the bottom of the page.
= 她犹豫了片刻，然后在那页纸的底部签了名。
for a moment = 片刻
③ I will be with you in a moment, as soon as I have finished this call.
= 我马上就来，打完这个电话就好。
in a moment = 马上
④ At that very moment the lights went out and somebody screamed.
= 就在那一刻，灯灭了，有人尖叫起来。
at that very moment = 就在那一刻
这一支说的是时间上的一个点，不是长短。
⑤ He is busy at the moment, but he can ring you back after lunch.
= 他现在正忙，午饭后可以给你回电话。
at the moment = 现在、此刻
英式常用；美式多说 right now。
⑥ Let us leave that question for the moment and return to it tomorrow.
= 那个问题我们暂时先放一放，明天再谈。
for the moment = 暂时
⑦ The roof could collapse at any moment, so nobody is allowed inside the building.
= 屋顶随时可能塌，所以谁也不许进楼。
at any moment = 随时
⑧ They changed the venue at the last moment, and half of the guests went to the wrong hotel.
= 他们到最后关头换了地点，一半客人去错了酒店。
at the last moment = 到最后关头
⑨ The moment that I saw his face, I knew that something had gone wrong.
= 我一看到他的脸色，就知道出事了。
the moment that… = 一…就…
当连词用，that 常省去。
⑩ You have chosen a bad moment to ask for a pay rise, because the firm has just lost a contract.
= 你挑了个糟糕的时机提加薪，公司刚丢了一份合同。
a bad moment to do something = 做某事的糟糕时机
从「时刻」到「合不合适的时刻」。
⑪ Winning the cup in front of her home crowd was the proudest moment of her career.
= 在主场观众面前夺杯，是她职业生涯中最骄傲的时刻。
the proudest moment of your career = 职业生涯中最骄傲的时刻
⑫ The moment of truth came when the engineers switched the machine on for the first time.
= 工程师们第一次开动机器时，见分晓的时刻到了。
the moment of truth = 见分晓的时刻
⑬ We decided on the spur of the moment to drive to the coast and watch the sunrise.
= 我们一时兴起，决定开车去海边看日出。
on the spur of the moment = 一时兴起
⑭ I did not believe for a moment that he would actually pay the money back.
= 我压根儿没相信过他真的会还钱。
not for a moment = 压根儿不
跟第二条字面相近，放进否定句就成了强调。
⑮ The film is far too long, but it has its moments, especially in the final scene.
= 那部片子太长了，不过也有精彩之处，尤其是最后一场戏。
have its moments = 也有精彩之处
⑯ The young chef is the man of the moment, and his restaurant is booked up until June.
= 那位年轻厨师是眼下的红人，他的餐馆订位已经排到六月。
of the moment = 眼下最红的
⑰ One moment, please, and I will put you through to the right department.
= 请稍等，我给您转到相关部门。
one moment, please = 请稍等
⑱ The committee had to decide matters of great moment in a single afternoon.
= 委员会得在一个下午之内决定几件极其重大的事。
of great moment = 极其重大的
正式用法，这里是「分量」而不是时间。

momentary
/ˈməʊməntəri/
核心：短暂的、片刻的：只持续一瞬就过去。副词 momentarily 并入本条，英美意思不同：
英式是「片刻地」，美式还能表示「马上、随时」。
① There was a momentary silence before the whole room burst into applause.
= 短暂的沉默之后，全场爆发出掌声。
a momentary silence = 短暂的沉默
② A momentary lapse of concentration at the wheel can cost a driver his life.
= 开车时一瞬间走神，就可能送掉司机的命。
a momentary lapse of concentration = 一瞬间走神
③ He paused momentarily at the door and then walked in without knocking.
= 他在门口停了一下，然后没敲门就走了进去。
pause momentarily = 停顿片刻
④ The captain announced that the plane would be landing momentarily.
= 机长广播说飞机马上就要降落。
be landing momentarily = 马上降落
美式用法；英式听众会理解成「只降落一小会儿」。

momentous
/məˈmentəs/
核心：重大的、意义深远的：说决定、事件、日子，影响会延续很久。来自 moment 的「重要性」那一义。
注意别混：momentary 是「短暂的」。
① Moving the capital to the coast was a momentous decision that changed the country's history.
= 把首都迁到沿海是个重大的决定，改变了那个国家的历史。
a momentous decision = 重大的决定
② Nobody in the crowd realized that they were witnessing a momentous event.
= 人群里谁也没意识到自己正在见证一个重大事件。
a momentous event = 重大事件

momentum
/məˈmentəm/
核心：势头、冲劲：一件事一旦动起来、越走越快的那股力。搭配 gain / gather momentum、lose momentum。
物理上是动量，质量乘速度。不可数。
① The campaign gained momentum after a famous actor gave it his support.
= 一位著名演员表示支持之后，那场运动声势渐起。
gain momentum = 势头增强
② The peace talks lost momentum when the two leaders left for their summer holidays.
= 两位领导人一去度暑假，和谈就失去了势头。
lose momentum = 失去势头
③ The lorry's momentum carried it across the junction even after the driver had braked.
= 司机踩了刹车以后，卡车的惯性仍带着它冲过了路口。
the momentum of the lorry = 卡车的冲力
这是本义：运动着的东西自己带的那股劲。

monarch
/ˈmɒnək/
核心：君主：国王、女王、皇帝一类世袭的国家元首。正式用词，不分男女。
① She became the longest-reigning monarch in the country's history.
= 她成了那个国家历史上在位时间最长的君主。
the longest-reigning monarch = 在位最久的君主
② The portrait shows the young monarch on the day of his coronation.
= 那幅肖像画的是年轻的君主加冕当天的样子。
the young monarch = 年轻的君主
③ Every autumn the monarch butterfly flies thousands of kilometres south to Mexico.
= 每年秋天，黑脉金斑蝶南飞数千公里到墨西哥。
a monarch butterfly = 黑脉金斑蝶

monarchy
/ˈmɒnəki/
核心：君主制；实行君主制的国家；the monarchy 指王室这个制度和家族本身。
名词兼形容词 monarchist（拥护君主制的人）与形容词 monarchical 并入本条。
① Many people believe that the monarchy should be replaced by an elected president.
= 许多人认为应该用民选总统取代君主制。
the monarchy = 君主制、王室
② Sweden is a constitutional monarchy in which the king has no political power.
= 瑞典是君主立宪制国家，国王没有政治权力。
a constitutional monarchy = 君主立宪制国家
可数用法，指一个国家。
③ Her father was a lifelong monarchist who stood up whenever the anthem was played.
= 她父亲一辈子拥护君主制，每逢奏国歌必定起立。
a monarchist = 拥护君主制的人
④ The revolution swept away the old monarchical system within a few months.
= 革命在几个月之内就扫除了旧的君主制度。
a monarchical system = 君主制度

monastery
/ˈmɒnəstəri/
核心：修道院，尤指男修士住的；女修士住的叫 convent。来自希腊语的「独居」。
① The monks at the monastery rise at four and work in the fields until noon.
= 那座修道院的修士四点起床，在田里干到中午。
the monks at the monastery = 修道院的修士
② He spent a year in a Buddhist monastery in the mountains before returning to his job.
= 他在山里的一座佛寺住了一年，然后才回去上班。
a Buddhist monastery = 佛寺

monastic
/məˈnæstɪk/
核心：修道院的、修士的；引申为清苦、与世隔绝的，像修士过的日子。名词 monasticism（修道生活、修道制度）并入本条。
① He gave up his career in the city to enter the monastic life.
= 他放弃了在城里的事业，过起了修道生活。
the monastic life = 修道生活
② The scholar led an almost monastic existence, seeing nobody but his housekeeper.
= 那位学者过着近乎修士的日子，除了管家谁也不见。
a monastic existence = 修士般清苦的生活
引申义：没进修道院，日子却过成了那样。
③ The book traces the spread of monasticism across medieval Europe.
= 那本书追溯了修道制度在中世纪欧洲的传播。
monasticism = 修道制度

Monday
/ˈmʌndi/
核心：星期一。首字母永远大写，缩写 Mon。介词用 on；on Mondays 是每逢星期一；
英式 on Monday week 是下下个星期一。字面是「月亮日」。
① The museum is closed on Monday, so we will have to go on Tuesday instead.
= 博物馆星期一闭馆，我们只好改到星期二去。
on Monday = 在星期一
② She goes swimming on Mondays and plays tennis at the weekend.
= 她每逢星期一去游泳，周末打网球。
on Mondays = 每逢星期一
③ I never arrange meetings for Monday morning, when nobody is properly awake.
= 我从不把会安排在星期一早上，那时候谁都没睡醒。
Monday morning = 星期一早上
④ It is easy to be a Monday morning quarterback when you did not have to make the decision.
= 用不着自己拿主意的时候，当事后诸葛亮是很容易的。
a Monday morning quarterback = 事后诸葛亮

Monégasque
/ˌmɒneɪˈɡæsk/
核心：摩纳哥的；摩纳哥人。国名是 Monaco，这个形容词借自法语，所以长得不像国名。
① The race was won by a Monégasque driver on the streets where he grew up.
= 一位摩纳哥车手在自己长大的街道上赢了那场比赛。
a Monégasque driver = 摩纳哥车手
② Only a small part of the population is Monégasque by birth.
= 那里的人口只有一小部分是土生土长的摩纳哥人。
be Monégasque by birth = 生来就是摩纳哥人

monetary
/ˈmʌnɪtəri/
核心：货币的、金融的，尤指一国的货币总量与政策；也指「用钱衡量的」。只放在名词前。
比 financial 窄：financial 管一切钱财上的事，monetary 专管货币本身。
名词 monetarism（货币主义）与 monetarist（货币主义者）并入本条。
① The central bank tightened monetary policy by raising interest rates twice in a year.
= 央行一年内两次加息，收紧了货币政策。
monetary policy = 货币政策
② The stolen ring has little monetary value, but it belonged to her grandmother.
= 那枚被偷的戒指值不了几个钱，却是她祖母留下的。
monetary value = 金钱上的价值
③ The government of the 1980s was strongly influenced by monetarism.
= 二十世纪八十年代的那届政府深受货币主义的影响。
monetarism = 货币主义
④ The minister was a convinced monetarist who believed in controlling the supply of cash.
= 那位部长是坚定的货币主义者，主张控制现金的供应。
a monetarist = 货币主义者

monetize
/ˈmʌnɪtaɪz/
核心：从（网站、内容、技能）上赚到钱、使它变现；本义是把某物定为货币、换算成钱。英式也拼 -ise。
名词 monetization 并入本条。
① She monetized her cooking videos by selling advertising space beside them.
= 她在自己的烹饪视频旁边卖广告位，把视频变成了收入。
monetize your videos = 靠视频赚钱
② The company has millions of users but has never found a way to monetize them.
= 那家公司有几百万用户，却始终没找到从他们身上赚钱的办法。
monetize the users = 把用户变成收入
③ The monetization of the website began with a small charge for old articles.
= 那个网站的变现是从对旧文章收一点费开始的。
the monetization of something = …的变现

money
/ˈmʌni/
核心：钱。不可数，不说「一个钱」「许多个钱」，说 some money、a lot of money。三层意思：
挣来花去的钱；拿在手里的钞票硬币；一个人的财产、财富。法律文字里才有复数，写 moneys 或 monies，指一笔笔款项。
配它的动词最要紧：make、earn 是挣，spend、waste 是花，save 是攒，borrow、lend、owe 是借与欠。
习语多半拿它当「力量」或「赌注」：money talks、put your money where your mouth is。
以它打头的复合词并入本条。
① She earns good money as a plumber, far more than her brother the teacher.
= 她当水管工挣得不少，比她当老师的哥哥多得多。
earn good money = 挣得不少
② He spends most of his money on records and second-hand books.
= 他的钱大多花在唱片和旧书上。
spend money on something = 把钱花在…上
介词用 on。
③ We are saving money for a deposit on a flat near the station.
= 我们在攒钱，准备付车站附近一套公寓的首付。
save money for something = 为…攒钱
攒钱的目的用 for；save money on something 则是「在某项开支上省钱」。
④ The café did not make any money in its first two years.
= 那家咖啡馆头两年一分钱没赚到。
make money = 赚钱
主语可以是人，也可以是生意；挣工资用 earn，做买卖得利用 make。
⑤ She had to borrow money from her parents to pay the fine.
= 她只好向父母借钱交罚款。
borrow money from somebody = 向某人借钱
方向相反的是 lend money to somebody；欠着没还是 owe somebody money。
⑥ He counted the money twice and put it in a brown envelope.
= 他把钱点了两遍，装进一只牛皮纸信封。
count the money = 点钱
这里是摸得着的钞票和硬币。
⑦ You can change money at the airport, but the rate is better in town.
= 机场可以换外币，不过城里的汇率更好。
change money = 兑换外币
这里换的是币种；把大钞换成零钱说 change a note。
⑧ The family made its money in shipping more than a century ago.
= 那个家族一百多年前靠航运发了家。
make your money in something = 靠…发家
这一层说的是家产。
⑨ He married money and never needed to work again.
= 他娶了个有钱人家的女儿，从此不用再工作。
marry money = 跟有钱人结婚
money 在这里指「有钱的人家」，带点讥讽。
⑩ She came into money when an aunt whom she had barely known died.
= 一个她几乎不认识的姑妈去世，她继承了一笔钱。
come into money = 继承一笔钱
come into 专说继承得来，不是挣来的。
⑪ All moneys received by the society are paid into a separate account.
= 协会收到的一切款项都存入一个单独的账户。
moneys = 款项
只有法律和正式文字里才用复数。
⑫ The set lunch is excellent value for money at twelve pounds.
= 十二英镑的午间套餐非常划算。
value for money = 物有所值
英式常用，前面加 good、excellent、poor 说划算不划算。
⑬ The concert lasted three hours, so the audience certainly got its money's worth.
= 音乐会演了三个小时，观众的票钱肯定花得值。
get your money's worth = 钱花得值
所有格 money's 不能省。
⑭ The children earn their pocket money by washing the car on Saturdays.
= 孩子们每周六洗车挣零花钱。
pocket money = 零花钱
英式指父母按周给孩子的钱；美式说 allowance。
⑮ If you are not satisfied, return the kettle and ask for your money back.
= 如果不满意，可以把水壶退回来，要求退款。
ask for your money back = 要求退款
⑯ The mattress comes with a money-back guarantee if you return it within thirty days.
= 那款床垫有退款保证，三十天内可以退。
a money-back guarantee = 退款保证
⑰ I cannot buy you another phone; do you think that I am made of money?
= 我不能再给你买一部手机了，你以为我是印钞票的吗？
be made of money = 有花不完的钱
多用在反问和否定里。
⑱ Tourists with money to burn fill the jewellers' shops along the harbour.
= 钱多得花不完的游客挤满了港口沿街的珠宝店。
have money to burn = 钱多得花不完
⑲ If this horse wins, we shall be in the money for the rest of the year.
= 这匹马要是赢了，我们今年剩下的日子就不愁钱了。
be in the money = 发财了
⑳ For my money, the second film is far better than the first.
= 依我看，第二部比第一部好得多。
for my money = 依我看
字面是「要我下注的话」。
㉑ The planning rules were quietly changed for the developer, which shows that money talks.
= 规划条例悄悄为那个开发商改了，可见有钱好办事。
money talks = 有钱好办事
㉒ If the minister believes in the scheme, he should put his money where his mouth is.
= 那位部长要是真相信这个方案，就该拿出真金白银来。
put your money where your mouth is = 别光说，拿出实际行动
㉓ I would put money on her winning the election by a wide margin.
= 我敢打赌她会以大比分赢得选举。
put money on something = 敢打赌…
本义是下注，口语里用来表示十拿九稳。
㉔ Being paid to taste chocolate all day sounds like money for old rope.
= 整天尝巧克力还有钱拿，听上去简直是白捡的钱。
money for old rope = 轻松到手的钱
英式口语。
㉕ The council has thrown money at the problem for years without solving it.
= 市政会多年来一味往这个问题上砸钱，却没有解决。
throw money at something = 光靠砸钱解决
带批评：只花钱，不动脑子。
㉖ Repairing that old van yet again would be throwing good money after bad.
= 那辆旧货车再修下去，就是往无底洞里填钱。
throw good money after bad = 往无底洞里填钱
㉗ Her forecast of the election result turned out to be right on the money.
= 她对选举结果的预测分毫不差。
on the money = 分毫不差
美式口语。
㉘ Money is no object for a client who wants a swimming pool on the roof.
= 对一个想在屋顶上修游泳池的客户来说，钱不是问题。
money is no object = 钱不是问题
object 在这里是「要顾虑的事」，固定说法，不加冠词。
㉙ The little girl dropped the coin into her money box and shook it happily.
= 小女孩把硬币投进储蓄罐，高兴地摇了摇。
a money box = 储蓄罐
㉚ He sent his rent by money order because the landlord would not accept cheques.
= 房东不收支票，他就用汇票寄房租。
a money order = 汇票
㉛ A money transfer to an overseas account can take three working days.
= 向海外账户汇款可能要三个工作日。
a money transfer = 汇款
㉜ Interest rates on the money market rose sharply after the announcement.
= 消息公布后，货币市场上的利率急剧上升。
the money market = 货币市场
㉝ The central bank tried to control inflation by reducing the money supply.
= 央行想通过减少货币供应量来控制通货膨胀。
the money supply = 货币供应量
㉞ The bank was fined for failing to report suspected money laundering.
= 那家银行因没有上报涉嫌洗钱的交易而被罚款。
money laundering = 洗钱
把来路不正的钱「洗」成看似合法的收入。
㉟ The gang used a money launderer who owned a chain of car washes.
= 那个团伙用的洗钱人名下有一串连锁洗车店。
a money launderer = 洗钱的人
㊱ The magazine prints a page of money-saving tips for students every autumn.
= 那本杂志每年秋天登一页给学生的省钱窍门。
money-saving = 省钱的
㊲ The museum café has turned out to be a real money-spinner.
= 博物馆的咖啡厅没想到成了一棵摇钱树。
a money-spinner = 很赚钱的东西
英式口语。
㊳ He described his landlord as a money-grubbing old man who never repaired anything.
= 他说他的房东是个贪财的老头，什么都不给修。
money-grubbing = 贪财的
㊴ Do not sell the painting to that money-grubber, who will only sell it on for a profit.
= 别把画卖给那个财迷，他只会转手倒卖赚钱。
a money-grubber = 财迷
㊵ Ask old money bags over there to pay for the drinks, since he has just sold his company.
= 叫那边那位阔佬付酒钱吧，他刚把公司卖了。
money bags = 阔佬
玩笑话，当称呼或外号用。
㊶ A money spider ran across her sleeve, and she refused to brush it off.
= 一只小蜘蛛爬过她的袖子，她不肯把它掸掉。
a money spider = 钱蛛（据说带来财运的小蜘蛛）
㊷ After weeks of travelling, the foreign notes began to feel like Monopoly money.
= 旅行了几个星期，那些外国钞票用起来越来越像玩具钱。
Monopoly money = 玩具钞票、不像真钱的钱
㊸ He paid for the car in ready money and drove it away at once.
= 他用现金买下那辆车，立刻开走了。
ready money = 现金

moneyed
/ˈmʌnid/
核心：（正式）有钱的、富有的。只放在名词前，最常见的搭配是 the moneyed classes。
① The resort was built for the moneyed classes of the nineteenth century.
= 那处度假地是为十九世纪的有钱阶层修建的。
the moneyed classes = 有钱阶层
monied = 有钱的（moneyed 的另一种拼法）
② She came from a moneyed family and had never had to think about the rent.
= 她出身富裕人家，从来不用为房租操心。
a moneyed family = 富裕人家
