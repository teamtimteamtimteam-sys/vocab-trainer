production
/prəˈdʌkʃn/
核心：名词：生产、制造、产量，不可数：in production、go into production；（自然的）产生、分泌，不可数；
（电影、戏剧、节目的）制作，不可数，也指上演的一出戏、摄制的一部片子，可数。
习语：on production of 是（正式）出示……后；make a production of 是把一件小事弄得小题大做。
复合词：production line 是生产线；production values 是（电影、演出的）制作水准；production number 是（歌舞片里场面大的）歌舞段落。并入本条。
① The production of steel has fallen by a third in ten years.
= 钢产量十年里下降了三分之一。
the production of steel = 钢的生产
② The new model will go into production early next year.
= 新型号明年初投产。
go into production = 投产
in production = 在生产中
③ The drug slows the production of acid in the stomach.
= 那种药减缓胃酸的分泌。
the production of acid = 酸的分泌
④ The school's production of the comedy sold out on all three nights.
= 学校排的那出喜剧三个晚上的票都卖光了。
a production of a play = 一出戏的演出
⑤ Discounts are given on production of a student card.
= 出示学生证可以打折。
on production of = 出示……后
on production of something = 出示某物后
⑥ He makes a production of every small repair in the house.
= 家里每一处小修小补，他都弄得兴师动众。
make a production of something = 把某事弄得小题大做
⑦ She worked on the production line for thirty years.
= 她在生产线上干了三十年。
a production line = 生产线
⑧ The film has a weak story but very high production values.
= 那部片子故事薄弱，制作水准却很高。
production values = 制作水准
⑨ The show ends with a big production number for the whole cast.
= 那场演出以全体演员的一大段歌舞收尾。
a production number = 大场面的歌舞段落
⑩ The film spent two years in pre-production before a single scene was shot.
= 那部片子光前期筹备就花了两年，一场戏都还没拍。
pre-production = （影视的）前期筹备
拍完之后的剪辑、配音叫 post-production。

productive
/prəˈdʌktɪv/

副词 productively。反义是 unproductive。并入本条。
① The land in the valley is the most productive in the region.
= 山谷里的地是那个地区产量最高的。
productive land = 产量高的地
② We had a very productive meeting and settled all four questions.

③ The long delay was productive of nothing but bad temper.

④ She learned to use the time on the train productively.
= 她学会了把火车上的时间用得有成效。
productively = 有成效地

= 只有生产率也提高，工资才能涨。
productivity improves = 生产率提高

professional

派生：professionalism 是专业水准、职业操守，不可数；professionalize 是使职业化，名词 professionalization。并入本条。

a professional footballer = 职业足球运动员
④ The clinic is staffed by doctors, nurses and other health professionals.
= 那家诊所有医生、护士和别的医疗专业人员。
health professionals = 医疗专业人员
⑤ The race is open to both amateurs and professionals.
= 那场比赛业余选手和职业选手都可以参加。

⑥ She sings professionally with a small opera company.

⑦ The nurses were praised for their professionalism during the emergency.

⑧ The sport was professionalized in the nineteen nineties.
= 那项运动是一九九〇年代职业化的。

professionalization = 职业化
⑨ The defender was sent off for a professional foul on the edge of the penalty area.
= 那名后卫在禁区边上故意犯规，被罚下了场。

profit
/ˈprɒfɪt/
核心：名词：利润、盈利：赚的钱超过花的钱的那部分，可数也可不可数：make a profit；（正式）好处、益处，不可数。
动词：（正式）得益、获利：profit from、by；对……有好处。
习语 at a profit 是赚了钱地、高于成本地。
派生与复合词：profitable 是有利润的、有益的，副词 profitably，名词 profitability；profitless 是无利可图的、无益的；
profit margin 是利润率；profit-sharing 是分红制；profit-taking 是（股市上）获利回吐；profit warning 是盈利预警。并入本条。
① The firm made a profit of two million pounds last year.
= 那家公司去年盈利两百万英镑。
make a profit = 盈利
② There is little profit in arguing with a man who will not listen.
= 跟一个不肯听的人争，没什么好处。
profit in doing something = 做某事的好处
③ She sold the house at a profit two years after she bought it.
= 买下那座房子两年后，她赚了钱把它卖了。
at a profit = 赚了钱地
④ Small farmers have not profited from the rise in food prices.
= 小农户没有从粮价上涨里得到好处。
profit from = 从……得益
profit from something = 从某事得益
⑤ The shop became profitable in its third year.
= 那家店第三年开始赚钱。
profitable = 有利润的
profitably = 有利润地
⑥ The bank is worried about the profitability of its branches in small towns.
= 那家银行担心小城镇分行的盈利能力。
profitability = 盈利能力
⑦ It was a profitless argument that left everyone tired and angry.
= 那是一场毫无益处的争论，弄得人人又累又气。
profitless = 无益的、无利可图的
⑧ The supermarkets work on a very small profit margin.
= 超市的利润率很低。
a profit margin = 利润率
⑨ The firm runs a profit-sharing scheme for all its staff.
= 那家公司对全体员工实行分红制。
profit-sharing = 分红制
⑩ Shares fell slightly as a result of profit-taking after a week of gains.
= 涨了一个星期之后，有人获利回吐，股价略有下跌。
profit-taking = 获利回吐
⑪ The company's shares dropped by a fifth after it issued a profit warning.
= 那家公司发出盈利预警后，股价跌了五分之一。
a profit warning = 盈利预警
⑫ The accountant asked to see the profit and loss account for the last three years.
= 会计要看近三年的损益表。
a profit and loss account = 损益表

⑬ The railway has never been a profit-making business and depends on public money.
= 那条铁路从来不是赚钱的买卖，靠公家的钱维持。
profit-making = 赚钱的、营利的
反过来是 non-profit-making（非营利的）。

= 草原上的野马是所有现代马种的祖先。
the progenitor of something = 某物的祖先
a progenitor = 祖先、始祖
② She is regarded as the progenitor of the modern detective novel.
= 她被看作现代侦探小说的开山鼻祖。
the progenitor of a movement = 一场运动的创始人
多指开创者，口气比 founder 书面。

progeny
/ˈprɒdʒəni/
核心：（正式，或玩笑）子女、后代、（动植物的）幼仔、后裔。复数名词。别跟 prodigy（神童）混。
① He appeared at the gate with his wife and all six of his progeny.
= 他带着妻子和六个子女出现在院门口。
his progeny = 他的子女
这样用多带玩笑口气。
② The progeny of the two plants had flowers of a quite different colour.
= 那两株植物的后代开的花颜色完全不同。
the progeny of something = 某物的后代
produce progeny = 繁殖后代

progesterone
/prəˈdʒestərəʊn/
核心：孕酮、黄体酮：女性、雌性动物体内的一种激素，让子宫为怀孕作好准备。不可数。
① The level of progesterone in the blood rises sharply after the egg is released.
= 排卵之后，血液里孕酮的水平急剧上升。
the level of progesterone = 孕酮的水平
progesterone = 孕酮、黄体酮
② The pill contains a small dose of progesterone and no other hormone.
= 那种药片含小剂量的孕酮，不含别的激素。
a dose of progesterone = 一剂孕酮
常跟 oestrogen（雌激素）并提。

prognosis
/prɒɡˈnəʊsɪs/
核心：两支，可数，复数 prognoses。一是（医学）预后：医生对病情会怎样发展的判断；二是（正式）（对事情前景的）预测。
形容词 prognostic；prognostication 是（正式）预言、预测，可数也可不可数。并入本条。
① The doctors say that the prognosis is good if the growth is removed at once.
= 医生说，那个肿物马上切除的话，预后良好。
the prognosis is good = 预后良好
a poor prognosis = 预后不良
② The bank gave a gloomy prognosis for the economy next year.
= 那家银行对明年的经济作了悲观的预测。
a prognosis for something = 对某事前景的预测
a gloomy prognosis = 悲观的预测
③ The size of the tumour is an important prognostic factor.
= 肿瘤的大小是重要的预后因素。
prognostic = 预后的
a prognostic factor = 预后因素
④ None of the experts' prognostications about the election came true.
= 专家们对那场选举的预言一个也没应验。
a prognostication = 预言
口气略带嘲讽：说得煞有介事的预言。
别跟 diagnosis 混：diagnosis 是「得的是什么病」，prognosis 是「往后会怎样」。

programme
/ˈprəʊɡræm/
核心：名词，可数：（电视、广播的）节目；计划、方案、规划：一系列有安排的活动：a training programme、a programme of reform；
（演出、典礼的）节目单、程序册；课程、大纲；（机器按步骤运行的）程序：a wash programme。
动词：为……安排（时间、活动），多用被动；预先设定（机器）让它自动做：programme something to do。
美式拼作 program。电脑的「程序」和「编程」英美都拼 program，过去式 programmed，美式也作 programed。
派生：programmer 是程序员；programming 是编程，也指（电台、电视台的）节目编排；programmable 是可编程的；
programmatic 是（正式）纲领性的、按计划的，副词 programmatically，在计算机里指通过程序地。并入本条。
① We watched a programme about wolves on television last night.
= 昨晚我们在电视上看了一个讲狼的节目。
a programme about something = 关于某事的节目
a television programme = 电视节目
② The firm runs a training programme for school leavers every autumn.
= 那家公司每年秋天给中学毕业生办培训计划。
a training programme = 培训计划
run a programme = 办一个计划
③ The government has announced a programme of reform for the railways.
= 政府宣布了一套铁路改革方案。
a programme of reform = 改革方案
a programme of something = 一套……的方案
④ I bought a programme at the door to see who was singing.
= 我在门口买了份节目单，看都有谁唱。
a programme = 节目单
a theatre programme = 戏院的节目单
⑤ What is the programme for tomorrow morning?
= 明天上午怎么安排？
the programme for tomorrow = 明天的安排
口语：问「今天有什么安排」就说 What's the programme for today?
⑥ Use the short wash programme for clothes that are only slightly dirty.
= 只是稍微有点脏的衣服，用短洗涤程序。
a wash programme = 洗涤程序
指洗衣机、洗碗机设好的一套步骤。
⑦ The final section of the motorway is programmed for completion next year.
= 那条高速公路的最后一段定于明年完工。
be programmed for something = 被安排在某时
这样用多是被动。
⑧ I have programmed the heating to come on at six in the morning.
= 我把暖气设定在早上六点自动打开。
programme something to do something = 设定某物做某事
人「天生就会」也说 be programmed to do something。
⑨ He wrote a small program that sorts the photographs by date.
= 他写了个小程序，把照片按日期排序。
a program = 电脑程序、（美式）节目、计划
write a program = 写程序
a computer program = 电脑程序
⑩ She learned to program at school on a machine that filled a room.
= 她是上学时在一台占满一间屋子的机器上学会编程的。
program = 编程
program a computer = 给电脑编程
⑪ The firm employs forty programmers in an office above a bank.
= 那家公司在一家银行楼上的办公室里雇着四十名程序员。
a programmer = 程序员
a computer programmer = 电脑程序员
⑫ The course teaches programming to children from the age of eight.
= 那门课教八岁以上的孩子编程。
programming = 编程
a programming language = 编程语言
⑬ The station has changed its programming to attract younger listeners.
= 那家电台调整了节目编排，想吸引年轻些的听众。
programming = 节目编排
children's programming = 儿童节目
⑭ The oven has a programmable timer that can be set a week ahead.
= 那台烤箱有个可编程的定时器，可以提前一个星期设定。
programmable = 可编程的
a programmable timer = 可编程的定时器
⑮ The speech was a programmatic statement of what the party would do in office.
= 那篇讲话是那个党上台后要做什么的纲领性声明。
programmatic = 纲领性的
a programmatic statement = 纲领性声明
⑯ The records can be searched programmatically without opening each file.
= 那些记录可以用程序检索，不必一个个打开文件。
programmatically = 通过程序地
⑰ Programme music tells a story or paints a scene, like a storm at sea.
= 标题音乐讲一个故事或描绘一个场面，比如海上的风暴。
programme music = 标题音乐
跟不描绘什么的「纯音乐」相对。

progress
/ˈprəʊɡres/（名）/prəˈɡres/（动）
核心：名词，不可数，重音在前：进步、进展：make progress；前进、行进。习语 in progress 是（正式）正在进行中。
动词，重音在后：进步、进展；（正式）前进、行进；（时间）推移：as the evening progressed。
① She has made great progress in her reading this term.
= 这学期她的阅读进步很大。
make progress = 取得进步
make great progress = 进步很大
② We are making slow progress with the repairs to the roof.
= 我们修屋顶进展很慢。
slow progress = 进展缓慢
progress with something = 某事的进展
③ The ship made slow progress through the ice.
= 那艘船在冰里缓缓前行。
progress through something = 在某处行进
这是较正式的用法。
④ Please do not enter while the examination is in progress.
= 考试进行时请勿入内。
in progress = 正在进行中
work in progress = 还在做的活儿、半成品
⑤ The work is progressing well and should be finished by June.
= 工程进展顺利，六月前应该能完工。
progress well = 进展顺利
动词重音在第二个音节。
⑥ The pupils progress from simple sums to algebra in three years.
= 学生们三年里从简单的加减法学到代数。
progress from A to B = 从甲进到乙
progress to something = 进到某一步
⑦ The crowd grew noisier as the evening progressed.
= 夜越深，人群越吵。
as the evening progressed = 随着夜越来越深
as the day progresses = 随着这一天过下去
名词不可数：不说 a progress。
