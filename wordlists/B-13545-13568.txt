liking
核心：喜欢、爱好 —— 常说 a liking for。习语：to your liking（合你的意）、too … for my liking（对我来说太…）、
take a liking to（喜欢上）。
① She has a liking for strong coffee and old films.
= 她爱喝浓咖啡、爱看老电影。
a liking for something = 喜欢…
② I hope the room is to your liking; it has the best view of the lake.
= 希望这间房合您的意，它看湖的景致最好。
to your liking = 合你的意
③ The town is too quiet for my liking, with no cinema and only one cafe.
= 那座小城对我来说太安静了，没有电影院，只有一家咖啡馆。
for my liking = 依我的口味
④ The dog took a liking to the new postman and waits for him at the gate.
= 那条狗喜欢上了新来的邮递员，天天在大门口等他。
take a liking to = 喜欢上

= 那次攀登把队员们逼到了体力的极限。

off limits = 禁止入内的

= 你想花多少花多少，只要别太过分。
within limits = 在一定限度内
⑥ He was stopped by the police and found to be over the limit.
= 他被警察拦下，查出酒精超标。
over the limit = 酒后超标
⑦ With a voice like hers, the sky is the limit.
= 凭她那样的嗓子，前途不可限量。
the sky is the limit = 没有上限
⑧ The hall is small, so we must limit the audience to two hundred people.
= 礼堂小，我们得把观众限制在两百人以内。
limit to = 把…限制在
⑨ I try to limit myself to two cups of coffee a day.
= 我尽量每天只喝两杯咖啡。
limit yourself to something = 只…、把自己限制在
⑩ Lack of water is the main limiting factor for farming in the region.
= 缺水是限制那个地区农业的主要因素。
a limiting factor = 限制因素
