a regiment of hussars = 轻骑兵团
② Her great-grandfather was a hussar who fought in the Crimean War.
= 她的曾祖父是一名参加过克里米亚战争的轻骑兵。
a hussar = 轻骑兵

hustings

hustle
/ˈhʌsəl/
核心：一是推搡、催赶（hustle somebody out of somewhere）；二是（美式口语）拼命干、四处奔忙；
三是诈骗、骗钱。名词 hustle and bustle 是「熙熙攘攘、忙乱」。
干这种事的人 hustler（骗子；拼命往上爬的人）并入本条。
① Security guards hustled the protesters out of the building.
= 保安把抗议者推搡着赶出了大楼。
hustle somebody out of somewhere = 把某人推搡出某处
② She misses the hustle and bustle of city life.
= 她怀念城市生活的熙熙攘攘。
hustle and bustle = 熙熙攘攘、忙乱
③ If you want to make it in this business, you have to hustle.
= 想在这一行里出头，你就得拼命干。
hustle = 拼命干、四处奔忙（美式口语）
④ He hustled tourists at card games in the square.
= 他在广场上用纸牌游戏骗游客的钱。
hustle somebody = 骗某人的钱
⑤ A hustler at the bus station tried to sell them fake tickets.
= 汽车站的一个骗子想卖给他们假票。
a hustler = 骗子

hyacinth

= 一盆蓝色风信子让厨房里满是花香。

hybrid
/ˈhaɪbrɪd/
核心：杂交种、混合物 —— 两种不同的东西结合而成的产物。
生物上是杂交种（a hybrid of two species）；技术上最常见的是 a hybrid car（油电混合动力车）；
也泛指任何混合体（a hybrid of jazz and folk）。
动词 hybridize（杂交、使混合）与名词 hybridization（杂交）并入本条。
① The mule is a hybrid of a horse and a donkey.
= 骡子是马和驴的杂交种。
a hybrid of A and B = A 和 B 的杂交种
② They sold the old estate car and bought a hybrid.
= 他们卖掉了旧旅行车，买了一辆混合动力车。
a hybrid = 油电混合动力车
③ The album is an odd hybrid of jazz, folk and electronic music.
= 那张专辑是爵士、民谣和电子音乐的奇特混合体。
a hybrid = 混合体
④ Breeders hybridized two wild roses to get the new variety.
= 育种者把两种野玫瑰杂交，培育出了这个新品种。
hybridize something = 使杂交
⑤ Natural hybridization between the two species is rare.
= 这两个物种之间的自然杂交很少见。
hybridization = 杂交

hydra

比喻「难以根除、越治越多」的问题。生物上也指水螅。
① Corruption in the ministry was a hydra: every time an official was sacked, two more took his place.
= 那个部里的腐败就像九头蛇，每开除一个官员，就有两个补上。
a hydra = 九头蛇；难以根除的祸患
② Under the microscope the tiny hydra waved its tentacles at the water fleas.
= 显微镜下，那只小水螅朝水蚤挥动着触手。
a hydra = 水螅（生物）

hydrangea

hydrate

hydraulic
/haɪˈdrɒlɪk/
核心：液压的、水力的 —— 靠液体的压力传动：hydraulic brakes、a hydraulic lift。
副词 hydraulically 与复数名词 hydraulics（液压系统；水力学）并入本条。
复合词 hydraulic fracturing（水力压裂，常缩成 fracking）也收在这里。
① The truck's hydraulic arm lifted the container onto the ship.
= 卡车的液压臂把集装箱吊上了船。
hydraulic = 液压的
② The seats are raised hydraulically at the start of each show.
= 每场演出开始时，座椅都是用液压升起来的。
hydraulically = 用液压地
③ The plane was grounded after a fault in its hydraulics.
= 那架飞机因液压系统故障停飞了。
hydraulics = 液压系统
④ Hydraulic fracturing pumps water and sand into the rock to release the gas.
= 水力压裂是把水和沙泵进岩层，让天然气释放出来。
hydraulic fracturing = 水力压裂

hydro
/ˈhaɪdrəʊ/

① Most of the country's electricity comes from hydro.

hydro = 水电（口语）

= 他们在一家维多利亚时代的老水疗旅馆住了一个星期，泡温泉。
a hydro = 水疗旅馆（旧）

hydrocarbon

② Methane is the simplest hydrocarbon, with one carbon atom and four hydrogen atoms.

hydrogen
/ˈhaɪdrədʒən/

③ The first hydrogen bomb was far more powerful than the bombs dropped in the war.
= 第一颗氢弹的威力远远超过战争中投下的那些炸弹。
a hydrogen bomb = 氢弹
④ She cleaned the cut with hydrogen peroxide, and it fizzed.
= 她用双氧水清洗伤口，伤口冒起了泡。
hydrogen peroxide = 过氧化氢、双氧水
⑤ Many cheap biscuits are made with hydrogenated vegetable oil.
= 许多廉价饼干是用氢化植物油做的。
hydrogenated oil = 氢化油

hydrophobia
/ˌhaɪdrəˈfəʊbiə/

① The old doctor had seen two farm workers die of hydrophobia after being bitten by the same dog.
= 那位老医生曾见过两个农场工人被同一条狗咬后死于恐水症。
hydrophobia = 恐水症（狂犬病的旧称）
② The fabric has a hydrophobic coating, so rain simply runs off it.
= 那种面料有一层疏水涂层，雨水直接就流走了。
hydrophobic = 疏水的
