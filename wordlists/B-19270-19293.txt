right
/raɪt/
核心：形容词：正确的、对的：the right answer；合适的、恰当的：the right person for the job；（道德上）正当的、对的：it is right to do；右边的、右侧的；（英式口语）十足的、彻底的。
副词：正确地、对地；向右、在右边；（口语）就、正好、直接：right now、right here；一直、完全：right to the end；马上、立刻。
名词：右边、右方，单数；正当、正义，不可数；权利，可数也可不可数：the right to do、human rights；the right 是（政治上的）右派、保守派，单数。
动词：扶正、纠正、改正（错误）：right a wrong。副词 rightly 是正确地、理所当然地。
习语：right away 是马上、立刻；serve somebody right 是活该；in your own right 是凭自身的条件、本身就是；by rights 是按理说；put something right 是纠正、修好。
复合词：right-angled、right-angled triangle 是直角的、直角三角形，美式也说 right triangle；right-handed 是惯用右手的，right-hander 是惯用右手的人；
right-minded、right-thinking 是正派的、明事理的；right of way 是通行权、先行权；right-on 是（口语）政治上正确的；right-wing 是右翼的，right-winger 是右翼分子。
① Is this the right way to the station?
= 这是去车站的正确的路吗？
the right way = 正确的路
right = 正确的
② You were right, and I was wrong about the date.
= 日期的事你是对的，我错了。
be right = 是对的
③ She is the right person for the job, for she speaks four languages.
= 她是那份工作的合适人选，因为她会说四种语言。

④ It is not right to take things that belong to other people.

it is right to do something = 做某事是对的
⑤ He writes with his right hand and kicks with his left foot.
= 他用右手写字，用左脚踢球。
right hand = 右手
right = 右边的
⑥ Turn right at the traffic lights and the hotel is on your left.
= 在红绿灯处右转，旅馆就在你左边。
turn right = 向右转
⑦ Stay right here, and I will be back in a minute.
= 就待在这儿，我一会儿就回来。
right here = 就在这里
right now = 就现在
⑧ We read the book right to the end in one night.
= 我们一个晚上就把那本书一直读到了最后。
right to the end = 一直到最后
⑨ Everyone has the right to say what they think.
= 人人都有权说出自己的想法。
the right to do something = 做某事的权利
⑩ The group fights for human rights in many countries.
= 那个团体在许多国家为人权而斗争。
human rights = 人权
⑪ Children must learn the difference between right and wrong.
= 孩子们必须学会分辨是非。
right and wrong = 是非
⑫ The newspaper supports the right and wants lower taxes.
= 那份报纸支持右派，主张减税。
the right = 右派
⑬ The boat turned over, but the sailors managed to right it.
= 船翻了，但水手们设法把它扶正了。
right something = 扶正某物
⑭ Please call me right away if there is any news.
= 一有消息请马上给我打电话。

⑱ The mechanic will put the car right by tomorrow.

put something right = 把某事修好、纠正

⑳ A triangle with one angle of ninety degrees is a right-angled triangle.
