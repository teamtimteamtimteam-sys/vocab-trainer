electoral
核心：选举的：跟选举与选民有关，只作定语：the electoral system、electoral law。
① The electoral system rewards the party that comes second everywhere.
= 那套选举制度，反倒便宜了处处屈居第二的那个党。
the electoral system = 选举制度
electoral reform = 选举改革
② Electoral law says nothing at all about that kind of advertisement.
= 选举法对那类广告只字未提。
electoral law = 选举法
③ The electoral college can pick the candidate who came second in votes.
= 选举人团有可能选出票数居次的那位候选人。
the electoral college = 选举人团
④ Your name has to be on the electoral register four weeks before polling day.
= 名字得在投票日前四周就上到选民登记册上。
the electoral register = 选民登记册
an electoral vote = 选举人票
副词 electorally：在选举上、从选票上看：修饰整句或形容词， 常见搭配是 electorally risky、electorally impossible。
⑤ The policy is sound and electorally impossible in three regions.
= 那项政策本身站得住，可在三个大区选票上通不过。
electorally impossible = 选票上办不到的
⑥ Electorally, the smaller party gained more from losing than from winning.
= 从选票上看，那个小党输比赢得到的还多。
electorally = 在选举上
构词：electoral 加 -ly

electorate
核心：全体选民：一地有投票权的人的总和，作单数或复数看待都行。可数。
① The electorate is older than it was and votes more reliably.
= 选民比从前年长，投票也更稳。
the electorate = 全体选民
② A quarter of the electorate has never voted in a local election.
= 有四分之一的选民，从没在地方选举里投过票。
a quarter of the electorate = 四分之一的选民

Electra complex
核心：（心理分析）恋父情结：跟恋母的 Oedipus complex 相对的说法，
如今临床上用得少，多见于通俗读物。
① The Electra complex turns up twice in the essay and not once in the footnotes.
= 恋父情结在那篇论文正文里出现两次，脚注里一次也没有。
the Electra complex = 恋父情结
② Nobody in the seminar would defend the Electra complex as a clinical category.
= 讨论课上没有人愿意把恋父情结当成一个临床范畴来辩护。
the Oedipus complex = 恋母情结（与之相对的说法）

electric
核心：三支。一是用电的、带电的：an electric kettle；
二是（气氛）紧张而热烈的；三是名词复数 electrics，指车或房子的电路系统。
注意别混：electric 说具体的用电物件，electrical 偏「与电有关的」，electronic 是电子的。
① The electric kettle boils in ninety seconds and trips the fuse in three.
= 那把电水壶九十秒烧开，三秒就跳闸。
an electric kettle = 电水壶
electric light = 电灯
② The atmosphere in the hall was electric until the microphone failed.
= 大厅里的气氛热烈得要爆，直到话筒坏掉。
be electric = 气氛紧张而热烈
③ Electric cars are quiet enough that the law now requires a noise.
= 电动车安静到法律如今要求它们发出声响。
an electric car = 电动汽车
an electric bike = 电动自行车
④ The electric blanket goes on at nine and off before anyone gets into bed.
= 电热毯九点打开，还没人上床就关掉。
an electric blanket = 电热毯
⑤ She painted the shed electric blue and the whole lane talks about it.
= 她把那间棚子刷成亮蓝色，整条巷子都在议论。
electric blue = 亮蓝色
⑥ Two states retired the electric chair in the same year.
= 有两个州在同一年停用了电椅。
the electric chair = 电椅
⑦ An electric eel stuns its prey and its own jaw at the same time.
= 电鳗放电既击晕猎物，也震到自己的下颌。
an electric eel = 电鳗
⑧ The electric fence keeps the cattle in and teaches the dog once.
= 那道电围栏把牛拦住，也让狗一次就学乖。
an electric fence = 电围栏
⑨ He shaves with an electric razor on the train and nobody objects.
= 他在火车上用电动剃须刀刮胡子，也没人说什么。
an electric razor = 电动剃须刀
⑩ The electric shock threw him across the room and left one small mark.
= 那下电击把他弹到屋子那头，只留下一个小印子。
an electric shock = 电击

electrical
核心：与电有关的：说系统、设备、行业与专业。
electrical engineering 是电气工程，an electrical fault 是电路故障。副词 electrically。
① An electrical fault started the fire and the wiring was forty years old.
= 火是电路故障引起的，那些线已经用了四十年。
an electrical fault = 电路故障
electrical equipment = 电气设备
② She read electrical engineering and now works in insurance.
= 她读的是电气工程，如今做保险。
electrical engineering = 电气工程
③ An electrical storm took out the power and left the phones working.
= 一场雷暴把电断了，电话倒还通着。
an electrical storm = 雷暴
副词 electrically：用电地、靠电力地：说驱动或操作的方式。
④ The gates are electrically operated and open at exactly half past eight.
= 那些门是电动的，八点半准时开。
be electrically operated = 电动操作的
⑤ The seat adjusts electrically and jams in the one position nobody wants.
= 座椅靠电动调节，偏偏卡在谁也不想要的那个位置。
adjust electrically = 电动调节
构词：electrical 加 -ly

electrician
核心：电工：安装与检修电路、电气设备的人。可数。
① The electrician found the fault in nine minutes and charged for an hour.
= 电工九分钟就找到了故障，收了一个钟头的钱。
an electrician = 电工
call an electrician = 叫电工来
② A qualified electrician has to sign off the work before it is covered.
= 得有持证电工签字验收，保险才认。
a qualified electrician = 持证电工

electricity
核心：两支。一是电：作为能量与供应的电；二是（比喻）令人兴奋的气氛。不可数。
① Electricity went off at four and came back after the news.
= 四点停电，新闻播完才来。
electricity = 电
an electricity bill = 电费单
② There was an electricity in the room that the recording does not pick up.
= 屋里那股兴奋劲儿，录音里听不出来。
electricity = （比喻）令人兴奋的气氛

electrics
核心：（英式）电路系统：车子或房子里整套的电气部分。复数形式。
① The electrics failed in the rain and the engine was perfectly fine.
= 雨里电路出了毛病，发动机倒好好的。
the electrics = 电路系统
② Rewiring the electrics cost more than the roof did.
= 重新布线花的钱比修屋顶还多。
rewire the electrics = 重新布线

electrification
核心：两支。一是电气化：把铁路或地区改成电力驱动、通上电；
二是通电、带电的过程。不可数。
① Electrification of that line stopped at the county boundary for nine years.
= 那条线的电气化在郡界上停了九年。
the electrification of something = 某物的电气化
② Rural electrification changed the working day before it changed anything else.
= 农村通电，最先改变的是一天怎么干活。
rural electrification = 农村电气化

electrify
核心：三支。一是使电气化：给铁路、地区改电力；二是给…通电、使带电；
三是（比喻）使震动、使振奋。过去式 electrified。
① They electrified the line in stages and ran diesels on the rest.
= 他们分段把线路电气化，其余路段仍跑柴油机车。
electrify a line = 把线路电气化
② The fence is electrified and the sheep learned it in one afternoon.
= 那道围栏通了电，羊一个下午就学乖了。
be electrified = 通了电的
③ Her first three sentences electrified a room that had been half asleep.
= 她开头三句话，把半睡半醒的一屋子人震住了。
electrify somebody = 使某人震动、振奋

electrifying
核心：令人震撼的、扣人心弦的：说表演、演讲、比赛让人一下子提起精神。
① The final ten minutes were electrifying and the first eighty were not.
= 最后十分钟扣人心弦，前八十分钟不是。
electrifying = 令人震撼的
② She gave an electrifying performance to two hundred empty seats.
= 她对着两百个空座位献上了一场震撼的演出。
an electrifying performance = 震撼的演出

electrocardiogram
核心：心电图：把心脏的电活动记录成曲线的那张图，缩写 ECG，美式也说 EKG。可数。
① The electrocardiogram was normal and the pain turned out to be muscular.
= 心电图正常，那阵疼后来查出是肌肉的事。
an electrocardiogram = 心电图
② An electrocardiogram takes ten minutes and twelve sticky pads.
= 做一次心电图要十分钟，外加十二片黏电极。
have an electrocardiogram = 做心电图

electrocute
核心：使触电身亡：多用被动 be electrocuted；口语里也夸张地说「电着自己了」。
名词 electrocution。
① Two workers were electrocuted by a line that was supposed to be dead.
= 两名工人被一条本该已断电的线路电死。
be electrocuted = 触电身亡
② He nearly electrocuted himself changing a bulb with the switch still on.
= 他没关开关就换灯泡，差点把自己电死。
electrocute yourself = 把自己电着

electrocution
核心：触电致死：电流通过身体致死；也指用电刑处死。不可数。
① Electrocution is rare in modern kitchens and never impossible.
= 现在的厨房里触电致死很少见，可也从不是不可能。
electrocution = 触电致死
② The state replaced electrocution with injection in nineteen eighty-two.
= 那个州在一九八二年用注射取代了电刑。
death by electrocution = 电刑处死

electrode
核心：电极：把电流引进或引出某种介质（液体、气体、人体）的导体，
正极叫 anode，负极叫 cathode。可数。
① The electrode has to touch skin, not hair, or the trace is useless.
= 电极得贴到皮肤上，不能贴在头发上，否则波形没用。
an electrode = 电极
② Electrodes wear out fastest in the salt tank and get changed monthly.
= 盐槽里的电极磨损最快，每月换一次。
electrodes = 电极（复数）

electrodynamics
核心：电动力学：研究电荷、电流与电磁场相互作用的物理学分支。
作单数看待，不可数。
① Electrodynamics explains the motor on the bench and the light above it.
= 电动力学既解释台上那台电机，也解释它上头那盏灯。
electrodynamics = 电动力学
② Quantum electrodynamics predicts a number that matches measurement to ten decimal places.
= 量子电动力学算出的数，跟测量值到小数点后十位都对得上。
quantum electrodynamics = 量子电动力学

electroencephalogram
核心：脑电图：把大脑的电活动记录成波形的那张图，缩写 EEG。可数。
① The electroencephalogram showed nothing and the seizures continued.
= 脑电图什么也没查出来，抽搐还在继续。
an electroencephalogram = 脑电图
② An electroencephalogram needs twenty electrodes and a very still patient.
= 做脑电图要贴二十个电极，还得病人一动不动。
have an electroencephalogram = 做脑电图

electrolysis
核心：两支。一是电解：让电流通过液体使它分解，如电解水制氢；
二是（美容）电针脱毛：用电流破坏毛囊。不可数。
① Electrolysis splits water into hydrogen and oxygen and costs more energy than it returns.
= 电解把水分成氢和氧，耗掉的能量比拿回来的多。
electrolysis = 电解
② Electrolysis takes many sessions and works one follicle at a time.
= 电针脱毛要做很多次，一次只处理一个毛囊。
electrolysis = 电针脱毛

electrolyte
核心：两支。一是电解质：溶解后能导电的物质，也指电池里的导电液；
二是（医学）体液里的钠、钾等离子。可数。
① The battery leaked electrolyte and took the tray with it.
= 电池漏了电解液，连托盘都被腐蚀了。
electrolyte = 电解液
② Electrolyte levels are checked first when somebody collapses in the heat.
= 有人在高温里晕倒，最先查的就是电解质水平。
electrolyte balance = 电解质平衡

electrolytic
核心：电解的：跟电解过程有关的。只作定语。
① The electrolytic cell needs a membrane that outlasts the electrodes.
= 电解槽需要一张比电极更耐用的隔膜。
an electrolytic cell = 电解槽
② Electrolytic capacitors dry out with age and bulge before they fail.
= 电解电容年头一久就会干涸，失效之前会鼓包。
an electrolytic capacitor = 电解电容
构词：electrolysis 的形容词

electromagnet
核心：电磁铁：通电才有磁性、断电就没有的磁铁，线圈绕铁芯做成。可数。
① The electromagnet holds the door shut until the alarm cuts the current.
= 电磁铁把门吸住，直到警报切断电流。
an electromagnet = 电磁铁
② A scrapyard electromagnet lifts a car and drops it on command.
= 废车场的电磁铁一吸把车提起来，一松就撂下。
a scrapyard electromagnet = 废车场用的电磁铁

electromagnetic
核心：电磁的：跟电与磁互相作用有关的。
electromagnetic radiation 从无线电波一直排到伽马射线。
① The electromagnetic spectrum runs from radio waves all the way to gamma rays.
= 电磁波谱从无线电波一直排到伽马射线。
the electromagnetic spectrum = 电磁波谱
② Electromagnetic interference from the pump wiped out the radio.
= 那台泵的电磁干扰把收音机盖得没声了。
electromagnetic interference = 电磁干扰

electromagnetism
核心：电磁学、电磁作用：把电与磁当作同一种基本作用来看的理论与现象。不可数。
① Electromagnetism unified two subjects that had been taught separately for a century.
= 电磁学把分开教了一个世纪的两门学问统一了起来。
electromagnetism = 电磁学
② Everything on that bench works by electromagnetism, including the clock.
= 台上每样东西都靠电磁作用工作，连那个钟也是。
work by electromagnetism = 靠电磁作用工作

electrometer
核心：静电计：测量极小电荷或电位差的仪器。可数。
① The electrometer reads charges too small for any other meter in the room.
= 静电计读得出屋里别的仪表都测不到的微小电荷。
an electrometer = 静电计
② An electrometer drifts if anyone walks past in a nylon coat.
= 只要有人穿着尼龙外套走过，静电计的读数就会漂。
the electrometer reading = 静电计读数
构词：electro- 加 -meter

electron
核心：电子：原子里带负电的基本粒子，绕核分布；电流就是它在动。可数。
an electron microscope 是电子显微镜。
① An electron weighs about a two-thousandth of what a proton weighs.
= 一个电子的质量大约是质子的两千分之一。
an electron = 电子
② Free electrons are what make a metal conduct at all.
= 金属之所以导电，靠的就是自由电子。
free electrons = 自由电子
③ An electron microscope shows the surface as a landscape of ridges and pits.
= 电子显微镜把那个表面显示成一片起伏的脊与坑。
an electron microscope = 电子显微镜
④ The electron gun at the back of the old screen fired at thirty thousand volts.
= 老式显像管后面的电子枪，在三万伏下打出电子。
an electron gun = 电子枪
