inc.
核心：两个缩写同形。一是（美）股份有限公司，写在公司名称之后，常大写成 Inc.；
二是（英）「包括、含」，多见于价目，同 incl.。
① She works as an accountant for Smith & Jones Inc. in Chicago.
= 她在芝加哥的史密斯琼斯股份有限公司当会计。
Inc. = （美）股份有限公司
英式对应的写法是 Ltd 或 plc。
② The hotel charges ninety pounds a night inc. breakfast and parking.
= 那家酒店每晚九十英镑，含早餐和停车。
inc. breakfast = 含早餐
这一支是英式缩写，读作 including。

incalculable
核心：（正式）无法估量的、极大的 —— 多说损害、价值、损失。副词 incalculably 并入本条。
① The oil spill caused incalculable damage to the coastline.
= 那次漏油对海岸线造成了无法估量的损害。
incalculable damage = 无法估量的损害
② His support over the years was of incalculable value to the charity.
= 这些年来他的支持对那家慈善机构价值无法估量。
of incalculable value = 价值无法估量
③ The internet has made research incalculably easier.
= 互联网让研究工作不知轻松了多少。
incalculably = 无法估量地

incandescent
核心：白炽的、炽热发光的；（文）极其强烈的 —— 本义说物体受热发光（incandescent bulb）；
比喻说怒火、美丽、才华（incandescent with rage）。名词 incandescence 并入本条。
① Old incandescent light bulbs waste most of their energy as heat.
= 老式白炽灯泡的大部分能量都作为热量浪费掉了。
an incandescent bulb = 白炽灯泡
② He was incandescent with rage when he saw the damage to his car.
= 看到自己的车被损坏，他怒不可遏。
incandescent with rage = 怒不可遏
③ The incandescence of the lava lit up the whole valley at night.
= 熔岩的炽光在夜里照亮了整个山谷。
the incandescence of something = …的炽光

incantation
核心：咒语；念咒 —— 为施魔法而念诵的词句，也指念咒的行为。
① The witch muttered an incantation over the boiling pot.
= 女巫对着沸腾的锅念了一段咒语。
mutter an incantation = 念咒语
② The priests chanted incantations to bring rain to the dry fields.
= 祭司们念诵咒语，为干旱的田地求雨。
chant incantations = 念诵咒语

incapable
核心：没有能力的、不能的 —— 常接 of doing something。也说「不会干（某种坏事）」：
She is incapable of lying（她不会撒谎）。另有「无行为能力的」（醉得不省人事、神志不清）。
① He seems incapable of saying anything without offending someone.
= 他好像一张嘴就得罪人。
be incapable of doing something = 不能做…
② She is simply incapable of lying, even to protect a friend.
= 她就是不会撒谎，即使为了保护朋友也不会。
be incapable of lying = 不会撒谎
说「本性上干不出来」，带褒义。
③ The police found him drunk and incapable in the town square.
= 警察发现他在镇广场上醉得不省人事。
drunk and incapable = 醉得不省人事

incapacitate
核心：使丧失能力、使无法正常生活或工作 —— 多说伤病、药物。多用被动。
① The knee injury incapacitated him for several months and cost him his place in the team.
= 膝伤让他好几个月无法正常活动，还丢了队里的位置。
incapacitate somebody = 使某人丧失能力
② The guards were incapacitated by a gas that made them fall asleep.
= 守卫被一种让人昏睡的气体放倒了。
be incapacitated by something = 因…丧失行动能力

incapacity
核心：无能力；（英）因病丧失劳动能力 —— 不可数。incapacity benefit（英，旧称的伤残补助）并入本条。
① Their incapacity to deal with the crisis shocked the public.
= 他们应对危机的无能让公众震惊。
incapacity to do something = 无能力做…
② He has been off work because of incapacity since his back operation.
= 背部手术以后，他一直因丧失劳动能力而没上班。
incapacity = 丧失劳动能力
③ After the accident she lived on incapacity benefit for three years.
= 事故以后，她靠伤残补助生活了三年。
incapacity benefit = （英）伤残补助

incarcerate
核心：（正式）监禁、关押 —— 比 imprison 更正式，多见于法律、新闻。名词 incarceration 并入本条。
① He was incarcerated for twenty years for a crime he did not commit.
= 他因一桩自己没犯的罪被关了二十年。
be incarcerated = 被监禁
② The country has one of the highest rates of incarceration in the world.
= 那个国家的监禁率在世界上名列前茅。
the rate of incarceration = 监禁率

incarnate
核心：（正式）化身的、人形的 —— 放在名词后：the devil incarnate（魔鬼的化身），
beauty incarnate（美的化身）。也作动词（重音在后）：体现、使具体化。
① To the villagers, the new landlord was the devil incarnate.
= 在村民眼里，新来的地主就是魔鬼的化身。
the devil incarnate = 魔鬼的化身
放在名词后面，这是它最常见的位置。
② The statue incarnates the city's idea of itself as brave and free.
= 那座雕像体现了这座城市对自己勇敢自由的认识。
incarnate something = 体现…

incarnation
核心：化身、体现；（某一世的）肉身；（事物的）某一形态。三支：a quality 的化身；
宗教里神化作人形（the Incarnation 指道成肉身）；一个东西的某一版本（its previous incarnation）。
① In her grey suit and pearls, the old actress was the incarnation of elegance.
= 那位老演员一身灰色套装配珍珠，是优雅的化身。
the incarnation of something = …的化身
② The building, in its previous incarnation, was a cinema.
= 那栋楼以前是一家电影院。
a previous incarnation = 以前的形态
③ Many Hindus believe in a series of incarnations of the same soul.
= 许多印度教徒相信同一个灵魂会有一系列转世。
incarnations of a soul = 灵魂的一次次转世

incautious
核心：（正式）不谨慎的、轻率的 —— in- 加 cautious。副词 incautiously 并入本条。
① An incautious remark to a journalist cost him his job.
= 他对记者的一句不谨慎的话让他丢了工作。
an incautious remark = 不谨慎的话
② She incautiously left the door unlocked when she went out.
= 她出门时不小心没锁门。
incautiously = 不小心地

incendiary
核心：纵火的、能引起燃烧的；煽动性的。本义说炸弹、装置（incendiary bomb）；
比喻说言论（incendiary speech）。也作名词：燃烧弹；纵火者。
① The city was badly damaged by incendiary bombs during the war.
= 战争期间，那座城市被燃烧弹炸得面目全非。
an incendiary bomb = 燃烧弹
② The politician was criticized for his incendiary remarks about immigrants.
= 那位政客因发表煽动性的移民言论受到批评。
incendiary remarks = 煽动性言论
③ Police found an incendiary device hidden in the warehouse.
= 警方在仓库里发现了一个藏起来的燃烧装置。
an incendiary device = 燃烧装置

incense
核心：两个词同形。名词（重音在前）是香，焚香时点的；动词（重音在后）是激怒，多用被动。
形容词 incensed（愤怒的）并入本条。
① Every morning the old monk burns incense in front of the small wooden statue by the gate.
= 每天早上，老和尚都会在门边那尊小木像前焚香。
burn incense = 焚香
② The decision to close the library incensed the local residents.
= 关闭图书馆的决定激怒了当地居民。
incense somebody = 激怒某人
③ The young chess player was incensed at the suggestion that she had cheated in the final.
= 有人暗示那位年轻棋手在决赛中作弊，她怒不可遏。
be incensed at something = 对…大为愤怒

incentive
核心：激励、诱因 —— 促使人去做某事的东西，多是奖励、好处。常见 incentive to do something、tax incentives。
① The government offers tax incentives to companies that hire young people.
= 政府为雇用年轻人的公司提供税收激励。
tax incentives = 税收激励
② There is little incentive for students to work hard if exams are cancelled.
= 如果考试取消，学生就没什么动力去用功了。
an incentive to do something = 做…的动力
③ Staff receive incentive payments for meeting their sales targets.
= 员工完成销售指标可以拿到奖励金。
incentive payments = 奖励金

incentivize
核心：激励、以奖励促使 —— incentive 的动词，英式也拼 -ise。商务语体，有人嫌是行话。
① The bonus scheme is designed to incentivize staff to stay with the company.
= 奖金方案旨在激励员工留在公司。
incentivize somebody to do something = 激励某人做…
② Low interest rates incentivize people to borrow rather than save.
= 低利率促使人们借钱而不是存钱。
incentivize borrowing = 鼓励借贷

inception
核心：（正式）开端、创始 —— 机构、计划刚开始的时候。多用 since its inception、from its inception。
① The charity has helped over ten thousand families since its inception in 1995.
= 那家慈善机构自一九九五年创立以来，已帮助了一万多个家庭。
since its inception = 自创立以来
② She was involved in the project from its inception.
= 她从项目一开始就参与了。
from its inception = 从一开始

incessant
核心：不停的、没完没了的 —— 多说让人烦的事：噪音、雨、抱怨、要求。副词 incessantly 并入本条。
① The incessant noise from the building site kept the whole street awake.
= 工地上没完没了的噪音让整条街的人都睡不着。
incessant noise = 不停的噪音
② The dog barked incessantly from the moment its owners left the house.
= 主人一出门，那条狗就叫个不停。
incessantly = 不停地

incest
核心：乱伦 —— 近亲之间的性关系，多数国家的法律禁止。不可数。
① The novel deals with incest and its effect on three generations of a family.
= 那部小说写的是乱伦及其对一家三代人的影响。
incest = 乱伦
② He was charged with incest after the case was reported to the police.
= 这个案子报警以后，他被控乱伦罪。
be charged with incest = 被控乱伦

incestuous
核心：乱伦的；（贬）小圈子的、关系过于紧密的。前一支是本义；后一支比喻一个圈子里的人互相提携、
排斥外人（an incestuous world）。副词 incestuously 并入本条。
① The court heard evidence of an incestuous relationship between the two defendants.
= 法庭听取了两名被告之间存在乱伦关系的证据。
an incestuous relationship = 乱伦关系
② The art world in the city is small and incestuous, and everyone reviews their friends.
= 那座城市的艺术圈又小又封闭，人人都在给朋友写评论。
small and incestuous = 小圈子化的
③ The two companies are incestuously linked through their boards of directors.
= 那两家公司通过董事会盘根错节地互相勾连。
incestuously linked = 盘根错节地勾连

inch
核心：英寸（约 2.54 厘米，十二英寸为一英尺）。由这个小单位长出「一点点」的意思：
动词 inch 是一点一点地挪（inch forward、inch your way），名词构成一串习语：
every inch（完完全全）、inch by inch（一点一点地）、within an inch of（差一点就）、
not budge an inch（寸步不让）、give somebody an inch（and they'll take a mile，得寸进尺）。
① The screen is thirteen inches wide, so it fits easily in a small bag.
= 那块屏幕宽十三英寸，很容易装进小包里。
thirteen inches = 十三英寸
② The traffic inched forward for an hour before we reached the bridge.
= 车流一点一点往前挪了一个小时，我们才到桥边。
inch forward = 一点点地挪动
③ She inched her way along the narrow ledge, not daring to look down.
= 她沿着窄窄的崖壁一点一点挪过去，不敢往下看。
inch your way = 慢慢挪过去
④ The search teams went over every inch of the field but found nothing.
= 搜索队把那块田的每一寸都查遍了，却什么也没找到。
every inch of something = …的每一寸
⑤ In his uniform he looked every inch the soldier.
= 他穿上军装，完完全全是个军人的样子。
every inch = 完完全全、十足地
⑥ They rebuilt the old wall inch by inch, using the original stones.
= 他们用原来的石头，一点一点地把那堵老墙重新砌了起来。
inch by inch = 一点一点地
⑦ The bullet missed his heart by an inch, and he came within an inch of dying.
= 子弹差一英寸就打中他的心脏，他差点就死了。
within an inch of something = 差一点就…
⑧ Both sides refused to budge an inch, and the talks collapsed.
= 双方都寸步不让，谈判破裂了。
not budge an inch = 寸步不让
⑨ Let the children stay up late once and they'll expect it every night: give them an inch and they'll take a mile.
= 让孩子晚睡一次，他们就会指望每晚都这样：真是得寸进尺。
give somebody an inch = 给某人一点余地（他就得寸进尺）

inchoate
核心：（正式）刚开始的、未成形的 —— 想法、计划、情感还没理清、没成形。
① She had only an inchoate idea of what she wanted to do after university.
= 对大学毕业后想做什么，她只有个模模糊糊的想法。
an inchoate idea = 未成形的想法
② The protests were an expression of inchoate anger rather than a clear political movement.
= 那些抗议表达的是一种没理出头绪的愤怒，而不是一场目标明确的政治运动。
inchoate anger = 没理出头绪的愤怒

incidence
核心：（正式）发生率、发生的程度 —— 多说疾病、犯罪等坏事在人群中出现得多不多。
跟 incident（一件事）形近，意思完全不同。物理上另指入射（the angle of incidence，入射角）。
① There is a high incidence of heart disease in the region.
= 那个地区心脏病发病率很高。
a high incidence of something = …的高发生率
② The report shows a falling incidence of car crime in the city centre.
= 报告显示，市中心的汽车犯罪率在下降。
the incidence of crime = 犯罪发生率
③ The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection when light hits a mirror.
= 光照到镜子上时，入射角等于反射角。
the angle of incidence = 入射角

incident
核心：事件、事故 —— 一件发生了的事，常指不愉快的、暴力的或引起外交纠纷的事。
新闻里爱用它把事情说得轻描淡写。形容词用法（正式）：be incident to（伴随…而来的）。
incident room（警方的案件指挥室）并入本条；incidental 另立。
① Police are investigating an incident in which a man was injured outside a bar.
= 警方正在调查一名男子在酒吧外受伤的事件。
an incident = 事件
② The border incident nearly led to war between the two countries.
= 那次边境事件差点引发两国之间的战争。
a border incident = 边境事件
③ The parade passed off without incident, despite fears of trouble.
= 尽管有人担心出乱子，游行还是平安无事地结束了。
without incident = 平安无事地
④ Detectives set up an incident room in the village hall.
= 侦探们在村公所设立了案件指挥室。
an incident room = （英）案件指挥室

incidental
核心：附带的、次要的 —— 伴随主要事情而来，不是重点。常见搭配：incidental expenses（杂费）、
incidental music（配乐）、be incidental to（是…的附带部分）。副词 incidentally 另立。
① The hotel bill included a number of incidental expenses such as phone calls.
= 酒店账单里有一些杂费，比如电话费。
incidental expenses = 杂费
② The film's incidental music was written by a young composer.
= 那部电影的配乐是一位年轻作曲家写的。
incidental music = 配乐
③ For him, making money was incidental to the pleasure of building things.
= 对他来说，赚钱只是造东西这份乐趣之外的附带收获。
be incidental to something = 是…的附带部分
