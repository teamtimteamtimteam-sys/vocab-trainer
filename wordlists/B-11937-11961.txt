incognito
核心：隐姓埋名地、化名地 —— 从意大利语「不被认出的」借来。多说名人、王室微服出行。
作副词或形容词：travel incognito、remain incognito。
① The film star travelled incognito, wearing dark glasses and a cheap coat.
= 那位影星戴着墨镜、穿着便宜外套，隐姓埋名地出行。
travel incognito = 微服出行
② The king liked to visit the markets incognito to hear what people really thought.
= 国王喜欢微服去市场，听听百姓的真心话。
visit somewhere incognito = 微服造访某地
③ Browsing in incognito mode stops your history being saved on the computer.
= 用无痕模式浏览，电脑上就不会留下浏览记录。
incognito mode = （浏览器）无痕模式

incoherent
核心：语无伦次的；不连贯的、不合逻辑的。两支：人说话前言不搭后语（醉酒、激动、病中）；
论点、政策各部分彼此不一致。名词 incoherence 与副词 incoherently 并入本条。
① By midnight he was drunk and completely incoherent.
= 到半夜他已经醉得语无伦次了。
be incoherent = 语无伦次
② Critics described the government's energy policy as incoherent and short-sighted.
= 批评者说政府的能源政策前后矛盾、目光短浅。
an incoherent policy = 前后矛盾的政策
③ She was sobbing and talking incoherently about a car and a bridge.
= 她抽泣着，语无伦次地说着什么车和桥。
talk incoherently = 语无伦次地说
④ The incoherence of the plot made the film hard to follow.
= 情节的不连贯让那部电影很难看懂。
the incoherence of something = …的不连贯

income
核心：收入 —— 定期从工作、投资等得到的钱。可数不可数都常用：a high income、income from rent。
常见的是 on a low income（收入低）、live within your income（量入为出）。
income tax（所得税）与 income support（英，收入补助）并入本条。
① Families on a low income will receive extra help with heating bills.
= 低收入家庭将在取暖费上获得额外补助。
on a low income = 收入低
② Most of their income comes from renting out rooms to students.
= 他们的收入大部分来自把房间租给学生。
income from something = 来自…的收入
③ If you learn to live within your income, you will never be in debt.
= 学会量入为出，就永远不会欠债。
live within your income = 量入为出
④ Income tax is taken from your salary before you receive it.
= 所得税在你拿到工资前就被扣掉了。
income tax = 所得税
⑤ After losing her job she lived on income support for a year.
= 丢了工作以后，她靠收入补助生活了一年。
income support = （英）收入补助
英国旧时给低收入者的一种社会福利。

incomer
核心：（英）外来户、新来的居民 —— 从别处搬来定居的人，多说在小地方被当地人看作外人。
① The villagers were suspicious of incomers from the city who bought up the old cottages.
= 村民们对那些从城里搬来、买下老屋的外来户心存戒备。
an incomer = 外来户
② After twenty years in the town, she was still regarded as an incomer by some neighbours.
= 在镇上住了二十年，她在一些邻居眼里还是个外来户。
be regarded as an incomer = 被看作外来户

incoming
核心：进来的、到来的；新任的。两支：正在到来的（incoming calls、incoming flights、the incoming tide）；
即将接任的（the incoming president）。反义是 outgoing。
① The phone only accepts incoming calls because she hasn't paid the bill.
= 她没交话费，手机只能接不能打。
incoming calls = 呼入的电话
② The incoming tide covered the rocks where the children had been playing.
= 涨潮淹没了孩子们刚才玩耍的岩石。
the incoming tide = 涨潮
③ The incoming government has promised to cut taxes in its first year.
= 新一届政府承诺上任第一年就减税。
the incoming government = 新一届政府

incommensurable
核心：（正式）不可比的、没有共同标准的 —— 两样东西性质不同，没法放在同一把尺子上量。
① Some philosophers argue that happiness and money are incommensurable values.
= 有些哲学家认为，幸福和金钱是无法相提并论的价值。
incommensurable values = 不可比的价值
② The two theories use incommensurable concepts, so neither can prove the other wrong.
= 两种理论用的概念彼此不可通约，所以谁也证明不了对方错。
incommensurable concepts = 不可通约的概念

incommensurate
核心：（正式）不相称的、不成比例的 —— 常接 with：跟…不相称。多说报酬、惩罚与付出、过错不相称。
① The fine was incommensurate with the seriousness of the crime.
= 那笔罚款与罪行的严重程度不相称。
be incommensurate with something = 与…不相称
② Nurses complain that their pay is incommensurate with the responsibility they carry.
= 护士们抱怨，她们的工资与所承担的责任不相称。
incommensurate pay = 不相称的报酬

incommode
核心：（正式，旧）给…添麻烦、使不便 —— 今天多见于客套话或旧式文字。
① I hope our late arrival on Sunday evening will not incommode your household in any way.
= 希望我们周日晚上晚到，不会给贵府带来任何不便。
incommode somebody = 给某人添麻烦
② Thousands of commuters were greatly incommoded by the sudden closure of the main station.
= 总站突然关闭，给成千上万的通勤者带来了极大的不便。
be incommoded by something = 因…感到不便

incommunicado
核心：被隔离、不能与外界联系 —— 从西班牙语借来，多说囚犯被单独关押，也谑指人故意不接电话。
① The prisoners were held incommunicado for three weeks without seeing a lawyer.
= 犯人被单独关押了三个星期，连律师都没见到。
be held incommunicado = 被单独关押、与外界隔绝
② I'll be incommunicado for the weekend, so don't bother texting me.
= 这个周末我不跟外界联系，别给我发短信了。
be incommunicado = 联系不上（谑）

incomparable
核心：无与伦比的、无可比拟的 —— 好到没法比。副词 incomparably（无比地）与名词 incomparability 并入本条。
① The view from the top of the mountain was incomparable.
= 山顶的景色无与伦比。
incomparable beauty = 无与伦比的美
② The new trains are incomparably faster and more comfortable than the old ones.
= 新火车比旧火车快得多、舒服得多，简直没法比。
incomparably better = 好得无法比拟
③ Critics debated the incomparability of the two writers, who worked in completely different styles.
= 评论家争论这两位作家是否可以比较，因为他们的风格截然不同。
the incomparability of A and B = A与B的不可比

incompatible
核心：不相容的、合不来的 —— 两样东西或两个人放在一起行不通。说人（性格不合）、设备（不兼容）、
观念（互相矛盾）、血型与药物（不能相配）。名词 incompatibility 并入本条。
① They divorced after ten years, saying they were simply incompatible.
= 十年后他们离了婚，说两人就是合不来。
be incompatible = 合不来
② The new software is incompatible with older computers.
= 新软件与老式电脑不兼容。
be incompatible with something = 与…不兼容
③ His views on the environment are incompatible with his job at an oil company.
= 他的环保观点与他在石油公司的工作互相矛盾。
be incompatible with something = 与…相矛盾
④ Blood group incompatibility can cause problems during pregnancy.
= 血型不合可能在怀孕期间引起问题。
blood group incompatibility = 血型不合

incompetent
核心：无能的、不胜任的 —— 没有能力把工作做好。也作名词（an incompetent）。法律上另指无行为能力的。
名词 incompetence 与副词 incompetently 并入本条。
① The report blamed incompetent managers for the company's collapse.
= 报告把公司倒闭归咎于无能的管理层。
an incompetent manager = 无能的经理
② The inquiry found the police guilty of gross incompetence.
= 调查认定警方严重失职。
gross incompetence = 严重失职、极其无能
③ The renovation had been done so incompetently that the roof leaked within a month.
= 翻修做得一塌糊涂，不到一个月屋顶就漏水了。
incompetently = 无能地、拙劣地
④ The court ruled that the old man was mentally incompetent to sign the will.
= 法院裁定那位老人精神上无行为能力，不能签署遗嘱。
mentally incompetent = 无行为能力的（法律）

incomplete
核心：不完整的、未完成的 —— in- 加 complete。副词 incompletely 与名词 incompleteness 并入本条。
① The application was rejected because the form was incomplete.
= 申请被拒，因为表格没填完整。
an incomplete form = 没填完整的表格
② The novel was left incomplete when the author died.
= 作者去世时，那部小说还没写完。
be left incomplete = 未完成
③ The disease is still incompletely understood by scientists.
= 科学家对这种病的了解仍然不完整。
incompletely understood = 了解得不完整
④ The incompleteness of the records made it hard to trace her family.
= 记录不全，很难追溯她的家族。
the incompleteness of something = …的不完整

incomprehensible
核心：无法理解的、难懂的 —— 说语言、文字、行为让人看不懂。
名词 incomprehensibility 与副词 incomprehensibly 并入本条。
① The instructions were written in incomprehensible technical language.
= 说明书是用晦涩难懂的技术语言写的。
incomprehensible language = 难懂的语言
② It is incomprehensible to me why anyone would pay so much for a car.
= 我真不明白为什么有人会花这么多钱买一辆车。
be incomprehensible to somebody = 让某人无法理解
③ He mumbled something incomprehensibly and left the room.
= 他含糊不清地咕哝了一句，离开了房间。
incomprehensibly = 让人听不懂地
④ Readers complained about the incomprehensibility of the new tax forms.
= 读者抱怨新的税表难以看懂。
the incomprehensibility of something = …的难以理解

incomprehension
核心：不理解、茫然 —— 看不懂或想不通时的状态。多说脸上的表情：a look of incomprehension。
① She stared at the letter in total incomprehension.
= 她一脸茫然地盯着那封信。
in total incomprehension = 一脸茫然
② The tourists met the waiter's jokes with looks of polite incomprehension.
= 游客们对服务员的笑话报以礼貌而茫然的表情。
a look of incomprehension = 茫然的表情

inconceivable
核心：难以想象的、不可思议的 —— 在头脑里构想不出来，常说 it is inconceivable that。
副词 inconceivably 并入本条。
① It is inconceivable that the president did not know about the plan.
= 说总统不知道那个计划，实在难以想象。
it is inconceivable that = …是难以想象的
② Twenty years ago, working from home was almost inconceivable for most people.
= 二十年前，对大多数人来说在家办公几乎是不可想象的。
almost inconceivable = 几乎不可想象的
③ The universe is inconceivably large.
= 宇宙大得难以想象。
inconceivably = 难以想象地

inconclusive
核心：非决定性的、没有结论的 —— 说证据、结果、谈判、选举没能得出明确结论。副词 inconclusively 并入本条。
① The results of the test were inconclusive, so she had to take it again.
= 检查结果不能确定，她只好再做一次。
inconclusive results = 不确定的结果
② The evidence against him was inconclusive, and the jury found him not guilty.
= 对他不利的证据并不确凿，陪审团判他无罪。
inconclusive evidence = 不确凿的证据
③ The talks ended inconclusively after three days.
= 谈判进行三天后无果而终。
end inconclusively = 无果而终

incongruous
核心：不协调的、格格不入的 —— 跟周围环境、气氛不相称，显得奇怪。
名词 incongruity 与副词 incongruously 并入本条。
① The modern glass building looks incongruous among the old stone houses.
= 那栋现代玻璃建筑夹在老石头房子中间，显得格格不入。
look incongruous = 显得格格不入
② There was a strange incongruity between his gentle voice and his huge size.
= 他声音温柔，身材却魁梧，两者之间有种奇怪的不协调。
an incongruity between A and B = A与B之间的不协调
③ A pink plastic flamingo stood incongruously in the formal garden.
= 一只粉色塑料火烈鸟突兀地立在那座正统的花园里。
incongruously = 不协调地

inconsequential
核心：无关紧要的、微不足道的 —— 不会带来什么后果（consequence）。副词 inconsequentially 并入本条。
① Most of the changes to the contract were inconsequential, but one clause worried the lawyers.
= 合同的改动大多无关紧要，但有一条让律师们很担心。
inconsequential changes = 无关紧要的改动
② They chatted inconsequentially about the weather while waiting for the results.
= 等结果的时候，他们东拉西扯地聊着天气。
chat inconsequentially = 闲扯

inconsiderable
核心：（正式）微不足道的 —— 几乎只用在否定里：not inconsiderable（相当大的），是一种含蓄的强调。
① He spent a not inconsiderable sum of money on the restoration of the old farmhouse.
= 他花了一笔相当可观的钱修复那座老农舍。
a not inconsiderable sum = 相当可观的一笔钱
② The risk of flooding is not inconsiderable in a house built so close to the river.
= 房子建得离河这么近，被淹的风险可不小。
not inconsiderable = 相当大的
含蓄的说法，比直接说 considerable 更克制。

inconsiderate
核心：不体谅人的、不为别人着想的 —— 做事只顾自己。副词 inconsiderately 并入本条。
① It was inconsiderate of him to play loud music while the baby was sleeping.
= 孩子在睡觉，他却大声放音乐，太不体谅人了。
it is inconsiderate of somebody to do something = 某人做…太不体谅人
② Some drivers park inconsiderately across the pavement, forcing parents with prams into the road.
= 有些司机不顾别人，把车横停在人行道上，逼得推婴儿车的家长只能走到马路上。
park inconsiderately = 乱停车、不顾他人地停车

inconsistent
核心：不一致的、前后矛盾的；时好时坏的。三支：跟别的东西对不上（be inconsistent with）；
自相矛盾（inconsistent statements）；表现不稳定（an inconsistent player）。
名词 inconsistency 与副词 inconsistently 并入本条。
① His account of the evening is inconsistent with the evidence from the cameras.
= 他对那晚的描述与监控录像的证据对不上。
be inconsistent with something = 与…不一致
② The witness gave inconsistent answers about where she had been that night.
= 关于那晚去过哪里，证人的回答前后矛盾。
inconsistent answers = 前后矛盾的回答
③ The team has been inconsistent this season, winning one week and losing badly the next.
= 这个赛季那支球队表现时好时坏，这周赢了，下周就大败。
be inconsistent = 表现不稳定
④ The lawyer pointed out several inconsistencies in the police report.
= 律师指出了警方报告中的几处矛盾。
an inconsistency = 一处矛盾
⑤ The rules have been applied inconsistently from one school to another.
= 这些规定在各学校执行得不统一。
inconsistently = 不一致地

inconsolable
核心：极度悲伤的、无法安慰的 —— 伤心到怎么劝都没用。副词 inconsolably 并入本条。
① The little girl was inconsolable when her cat died.
= 小女孩的猫死了，她伤心得怎么哄都哄不好。
be inconsolable = 伤心得无法安慰
② He wept inconsolably at his brother's funeral, and nobody could get him to leave the grave.
= 在哥哥的葬礼上他哭得无法自抑，谁也没法把他从墓前劝走。
weep inconsolably = 哭得无法自抑

inconspicuous
核心：不显眼的、不引人注目的 —— 想不被注意到。副词 inconspicuously 并入本条。
① She sat at the back of the hall, trying to look as inconspicuous as possible.
= 她坐在大厅后排，尽量不让人注意到自己。
look inconspicuous = 显得不起眼
② The security cameras are small and inconspicuously placed above each door.
= 监控摄像头很小，不显眼地装在每扇门的上方。
inconspicuously = 不显眼地

inconstant
核心：（正式或文）易变的、不忠的 —— 多说爱人、感情、运气。名词 inconstancy 并入本条。
① In the old songs, the lover is always inconstant and the heart is always broken.
= 在那些老歌里，情人总是用情不专，心总是要碎的。
an inconstant lover = 用情不专的情人
② She could not forgive his inconstancy after he left her for her best friend.
= 他为了她最好的朋友离开了她，她无法原谅他的变心。
somebody's inconstancy = 某人的变心
