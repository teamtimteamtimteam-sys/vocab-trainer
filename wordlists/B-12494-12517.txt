intolerable
核心：无法忍受的、难以容忍的 —— 程度之重让人受不了。副词 intolerably 并入本条。
① The noise from the building site next door became intolerable.
= 隔壁工地的噪音变得让人无法忍受。
become intolerable = 变得无法忍受
② The heat in the office was intolerably high during the summer.
= 夏天办公室里热得让人受不了。
intolerably = 难以忍受地

intolerant
核心：不宽容的、偏执的；（身体）不耐受的。两支：不能容忍不同意见、宗教、生活方式（be intolerant of）；
医学上对某种食物或药物有不良反应（lactose intolerant）。名词 intolerance 并入本条，两支都有。
① As a manager he is intolerant of anyone who disagrees with him in meetings.
= 当经理的他容不下任何在会上跟他意见不一的人。
be intolerant of somebody = 容不下某人
② She is lactose intolerant and has to buy special milk for her morning coffee.
= 她乳糖不耐受，早上喝咖啡得买特制的牛奶。
lactose intolerant = 乳糖不耐受的
③ The report warns of growing religious intolerance in the region.
= 报告警告说，那个地区的宗教不宽容正在加剧。
religious intolerance = 宗教不宽容
④ Some people have an intolerance to wheat and feel ill after eating bread.
= 有些人对小麦不耐受，吃了面包就难受。
an intolerance to something = 对…不耐受

intonation
核心：语调、声调 —— 说话时声音的高低起伏；（音乐）音准。
① In English, a rising intonation often turns a statement into a question.
= 在英语里，上扬的语调常常能把陈述句变成疑问句。
a rising intonation = 上扬的语调
② The young violinist has excellent intonation for her age.
= 那位年轻小提琴手的音准以她的年龄来说非常出色。
intonation = 音准

intone
核心：吟诵、用单调的声音说 —— 像念经一样用平板的声音说或唱。
① The priest intoned a prayer as the coffin was carried into the church.
= 棺木被抬进教堂时，神父吟诵了一段祷词。
intone a prayer = 吟诵祷词
② "The meeting is now closed," the chairman intoned, without looking up.
= 「会议现在结束。」主席头也不抬，用平板的声音说道。
intone = 平板地说

intoxicate
核心：使喝醉；使陶醉、使兴奋。本义说酒、毒品让人醉；比喻说成功、爱情让人飘飘然。
形容词 intoxicated（醉的；陶醉的）、intoxicating（令人陶醉的）与名词 intoxication、intoxicant（致醉物）并入本条。
① The driver was found to be intoxicated and was arrested.
= 司机被发现醉酒，遭到逮捕。
be intoxicated = 醉酒
正式、法律用语，日常说 drunk。
② The young players were intoxicated by their sudden success.
= 突如其来的成功让年轻球员们飘飘然。
be intoxicated by something = 被…冲昏头脑
③ The intoxicating smell of jasmine filled the warm night air.
= 温暖的夜空中弥漫着令人陶醉的茉莉花香。
intoxicating = 令人陶醉的
④ He was charged with driving while in a state of intoxication.
= 他被控醉酒驾驶。
intoxication = 醉酒
⑤ Alcohol is the most widely used intoxicant in the world.
= 酒精是世界上使用最广泛的致醉物。
an intoxicant = 致醉物

intractable
核心：（正式）棘手的、难以解决的；（人）倔强的、难管的。名词 intractability 并入本条。
① Unemployment in the region has proved an intractable problem.
= 那个地区的失业问题证明极其棘手。
an intractable problem = 棘手的问题
② The intractability of the conflict has defeated every peace plan.
= 冲突之难解，让每个和平方案都落了空。
the intractability of something = …的难以解决
③ The teacher found the boy intractable and sent him to the head.
= 老师觉得那男孩难以管教，就把他送到校长那儿去了。
intractable = 难管的

intramural
核心：（美）校内的 —— 在同一所学校内部进行的（体育比赛等）。
① She played intramural volleyball with her friends from the dormitory.
= 她和宿舍的朋友们一起打校内排球赛。
intramural sports = 校内体育运动
② The university has a large intramural programme with more than thirty sports.
= 那所大学有一个庞大的校内体育项目，包括三十多种运动。
an intramural programme = 校内体育项目

intramuscular
核心：（医学）肌肉内的、肌注的 —— 说注射。
① The vaccine is given as an intramuscular injection in the upper arm.
= 那种疫苗是在上臂作肌肉注射。
an intramuscular injection = 肌肉注射
说注射的部位：打进肌肉里，而不是皮下或血管里。
② Some painkillers work faster when they are given by intramuscular injection rather than as tablets.
= 有些止痛药用肌肉注射比吃药片起效快。
by intramuscular injection = 通过肌肉注射

intranet
核心：（公司内部的）内联网 —— 只供一个组织内部使用的网络。
① All the forms you need can be found on the company intranet.
= 你需要的所有表格都能在公司内联网上找到。
the company intranet = 公司内联网
② The school uses its intranet to share homework and timetables with students.
= 那所学校用内部网络与学生分享作业和课程表。
an intranet = 内部网络

intransigent
核心：（正式，贬）不妥协的、固执的 —— 坚持立场、拒不让步。名词 intransigence 并入本条。
① The union accused management of being intransigent over pay.
= 工会指责资方在工资问题上毫不让步。
be intransigent over something = 在…上毫不让步
② The talks failed because of the intransigence of both sides.
= 由于双方都不肯让步，谈判失败了。
the intransigence of somebody = 某人的不妥协

intransitive
核心：（语法）不及物的 —— 动词后面不接宾语（sleep、arrive）。副词 intransitively 并入本条。
① Dictionaries mark intransitive verbs so that learners know they take no object.
= 词典会标出不及物动词，让学习者知道它们不带宾语。
an intransitive verb = 不及物动词
② Many verbs, such as open and break, can be used both transitively and intransitively.
= 许多动词，比如「open」和「break」，既可及物也可不及物地使用。
intransitively = 不及物地

intrastate
核心：（美）州内的 —— 在同一个州之内的（贸易、交通），跟 interstate 相对。
① Intrastate flights are cheaper because they are not taxed in the same way.
= 州内航班更便宜，因为它们的征税方式不同。
intrastate flights = 州内航班
② The law applies only to intrastate trade, not to goods sent to other states.
= 那项法律只适用于州内贸易，不适用于运往其他州的货物。
intrastate trade = 州内贸易

intrauterine
核心：（医学）子宫内的 —— intrauterine device（宫内节育器，缩写「IUD」）并入本条。
① Doctors monitor the baby's intrauterine growth with regular scans.
= 医生通过定期扫描监测胎儿在子宫内的生长情况。
intrauterine growth = 宫内生长
② She had an intrauterine device fitted after the birth of her second child.
= 生了第二个孩子后，她上了一个宫内节育器。
an intrauterine device = 宫内节育器

intravenous
核心：静脉内的、静脉注射的 —— 说输液、注射。副词 intravenously 并入本条。
① The patient was given intravenous fluids because he could not drink.
= 病人喝不了水，所以给他静脉输液。
intravenous fluids = 静脉输液
② The antibiotic is given intravenously every eight hours.
= 那种抗生素每八小时静脉注射一次。
intravenously = 经静脉地

intrepid
核心：（常谑）无畏的、勇敢的 —— 多说探险家、旅行者，常带点玩笑。
① The intrepid explorers crossed the desert on foot with only two camels.
= 那几位无畏的探险家只带着两头骆驼徒步穿越了沙漠。
intrepid explorers = 无畏的探险家
② A few intrepid swimmers went into the sea on New Year's Day.
= 元旦那天，有几个勇敢的泳者下海游泳了。
intrepid swimmers = 勇敢的泳者
带调侃：做别人觉得有点傻的冒险事。

intricate
核心：错综复杂的、精细的 —— 由许多小部分精巧地组合而成。名词 intricacy 与副词 intricately 并入本条。
① The old carpet has an intricate pattern of birds and flowers.
= 那张旧地毯上有鸟和花组成的精细图案。
an intricate pattern = 精细的图案
② Few people understand the intricacies of the tax system.
= 很少有人懂得税制的复杂细节。
an intricacy = 复杂之处
多用复数：the intricacies of something（…的复杂细节）。
③ The box is intricately carved with scenes from the life of the saint.
= 那个盒子上精雕细刻着那位圣徒一生的场景。
intricately carved = 精雕细刻的

intrigue
核心：（使）好奇、引起兴趣；阴谋、密谋。动词（重音在后）：让人很想知道（be intrigued by）；
名词（重音在前）：秘密的策划、勾心斗角（political intrigue）。
形容词 intrigued（好奇的）、intriguing（引人入胜的）与副词 intriguingly 并入本条。
① I was intrigued by her story about the letters she found in the attic.
= 她说在阁楼上找到那些信的事，让我非常好奇。
be intrigued by something = 对…感到好奇
② The novel is full of political intrigue at the royal court.
= 那部小说满是宫廷里的政治阴谋。
political intrigue = 政治阴谋
③ It is an intriguing question that scientists have not yet answered.
= 这是个引人好奇、科学家尚未解答的问题。
an intriguing question = 引人好奇的问题
④ Intriguingly, the letter was signed with a different name.
= 耐人寻味的是，那封信署的是另一个名字。
intriguingly = 耐人寻味的是
⑤ He intrigued with the king's enemies to seize the throne.
= 他勾结国王的敌人，密谋夺取王位。
intrigue with somebody = 与某人密谋
动词的这一支偏旧，多见于历史叙述。

intrinsic
核心：内在的、固有的 —— 本身就有、不依赖外部的。常见 intrinsic value、intrinsic to。副词 intrinsically 并入本条。
① The ring has little intrinsic value, but it means a lot to her.
= 那枚戒指本身不值钱，但对她意义重大。
intrinsic value = 内在价值
② Music is intrinsic to the culture of the island.
= 音乐是那座岛上文化的固有部分。
be intrinsic to something = 是…所固有的
③ There is nothing intrinsically wrong with borrowing money.
= 借钱本身并没有错。
intrinsically = 本质上

intro
核心：（口语）开场白、前奏；介绍 —— introduction 的截短，多说歌曲的前奏、节目的开头，也说把人介绍给别人。
① The song has a long guitar intro before the singing starts.
= 那首歌在开唱前有一段很长的吉他前奏。
a guitar intro = 吉他前奏
② Can you give me an intro to your friend who works in publishing?
= 你能把我介绍给你那个做出版的朋友吗？
an intro to somebody = 对某人的引荐

introduce
核心：介绍、引见；引进、推行；引入、开始讲；主持。几支：让两人认识（introduce A to B）；
第一次把新东西带进来使用（introduce a new law / a species）；把人引向某种经历（introduce somebody to jazz）；
节目、演讲开头的介绍。名词 introduction 另立。
① Let me introduce you to my sister, who has just moved here from Canada.
= 我来介绍一下我妹妹，她刚从加拿大搬来。
introduce A to B = 把A介绍给B
② On the first day each student stood up to introduce themselves to the class.
= 第一天，每个学生都站起来向全班作自我介绍。
introduce yourself = 自我介绍
introduce oneself = 自我介绍（正式写法）
③ The government plans to introduce a new tax on sugary drinks.
= 政府计划对含糖饮料征收一种新税。
introduce a tax = 开征新税
④ Rabbits were introduced into Australia in the nineteenth century.
= 兔子是十九世纪被引进澳大利亚的。
introduce something into somewhere = 把…引进某地
⑤ Her father introduced her to jazz when she was only six.
= 她父亲在她六岁时就带她领略了爵士乐。
introduce somebody to something = 让某人初次接触…
⑥ The host introduced the programme with a short song.
= 主持人用一首短歌开始了节目。
introduce a programme = 介绍节目、开场

introduction
核心：介绍、引见；引进、推行；导言、序言；入门。几支：把人介绍给别人（make the introductions）；
新事物的首次使用（the introduction of the euro）；书、演讲的开头部分；入门读物（an introduction to）。
① She made the introductions and then left us to talk.
= 她给我们互相作了介绍，然后让我们自己聊。
make the introductions = 作介绍
② The introduction of the new system caused months of confusion.
= 新系统的推行造成了几个月的混乱。
the introduction of something = …的推行
③ In the introduction, the author explains why she wrote the book.
= 作者在序言里解释了她为什么写这本书。
the introduction = 序言、导言
④ This book is a good introduction to the history of art.
= 这本书是艺术史的一本很好的入门读物。
an introduction to something = …入门

introductory
核心：介绍性的、入门的；开场的 —— 说课程、讲话、优惠。
① She is teaching an introductory course in psychology.
= 她在教一门心理学入门课。
an introductory course = 入门课程
② The magazine is available at a special introductory price of one pound.
= 那本杂志以一英镑的特价试售。
an introductory price = 试销价
③ After a few introductory remarks, the speaker turned to the main subject.
= 讲了几句开场白之后，演讲者转入了正题。
introductory remarks = 开场白

introspection
核心：内省、反省 —— 审视自己的想法和感受。不可数。
① Long walks alone gave him time for introspection.
= 独自长途散步让他有时间反省自己。
time for introspection = 反省的时间
② After the defeat, the party went through a period of painful introspection.
= 败选后，那个党经历了一段痛苦的自我反省。
a period of introspection = 一段反省期

introspective
核心：内省的、好反省的 —— 说人爱审视自己的内心，也说作品。
① She became quiet and introspective after her father died.
= 父亲去世后，她变得沉默而爱反省。
become introspective = 变得爱反省
② The album is more introspective than their earlier records.
= 那张专辑比他们早期的唱片更内省。
an introspective album = 内省的专辑
