sangria
/sæŋˈɡriːə/
核心：名词，不可数：桑格利亚汽酒：红葡萄酒加水果、柠檬水调成的西班牙冷饮。
① We drank a big jug of cold sangria with our dinner on the terrace in Seville.
= 在塞维利亚的露台上，我们吃晚饭时喝了一大壶冰镇桑格利亚。
a jug of sangria = 一壶桑格利亚
sangria = 桑格利亚汽酒
② The bar makes its sangria with red wine, oranges, apples and a little brandy.
= 那家酒吧用红酒、橙子、苹果和一点白兰地调桑格利亚。
make sangria = 调桑格利亚

sanguinary
/ˈsæŋɡwɪnəri/
核心：形容词（文学）：血腥的、伤亡惨重的；嗜杀的。多见于历史写作。
① It was one of the most sanguinary battles of the war, and thousands died in a single day.
= 那是那场战争中最血腥的战役之一，一天之内就死了几千人。
a sanguinary battle = 血腥的战役
sanguinary = 血腥的
② The history book describes the sanguinary rule of a king who executed hundreds of his enemies.
= 那本史书描写了一位处死了几百名政敌的国王的血腥统治。
sanguinary rule = 血腥统治

sanguine
/ˈsæŋɡwɪn/
核心：形容词（正式）：乐观的、充满信心的：be sanguine about；（文学）（脸色）红润的。副词 sanguinely 是乐观地。注意别和 sanguinary（血腥的）弄混。
① The manager is sanguine about the team's chances, even though they lost their last three games.
= 尽管球队连输了三场，经理对球队的前景仍很乐观。
be sanguine about something = 对某事乐观
sanguine = 乐观的
② The minister spoke sanguinely about the economy, but most experts expected prices to keep rising.
= 那位部长谈起经济时很乐观，可多数专家预计物价还会继续上涨。
sanguinely = 乐观地

sanitation

① Please sanitize your hands at the door before you visit the patients on the ward.

a sanitized version = 粉饰过的版本

sap somebody's confidence = 消磨某人的信心
④ Don't be such a sap; he is only being nice to you because he wants to borrow money.
= 别那么傻了，他对你好只是因为想借钱。
a sap = （口语）傻瓜
