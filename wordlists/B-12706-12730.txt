jalopy
/dʒəˈlɒpi/
核心：（口语，旧）破旧的汽车、老爷车。
① He drove an old jalopy that broke down every few miles.
= 他开着一辆每走几英里就抛锚的老爷车。
an old jalopy = 老爷车
② They bought a rusty jalopy for fifty dollars and drove it across the country.
= 他们花五十美元买了一辆锈迹斑斑的老爷车，开着它横穿全国。
a rusty jalopy = 锈迹斑斑的破车

jam
/dʒæm/
核心：两个来源的意思。一是果酱（strawberry jam）；二是本义「挤、塞」：
挤满、堵塞（a traffic jam；jam-packed 挤得满满的）；卡住（the door jammed）；用力塞（jam something into）；
干扰（无线电信号）；（口语）困境（be in a jam）；（音乐）即兴合奏（a jam session）。
jam jar（果酱瓶）并入本条。
① She spread strawberry jam on her toast.
= 她在吐司上抹了草莓酱。
strawberry jam = 草莓酱
② We were stuck in a traffic jam for two hours on the way to the airport.
= 去机场的路上，我们在车流里堵了两个小时。
a traffic jam = 交通堵塞
③ The window has jammed, and I can't open it.
= 窗户卡住了，我打不开。
jam = 卡住
④ He jammed his clothes into a bag and ran for the train.
= 他把衣服胡乱塞进包里，跑去赶火车。
jam something into something = 把…硬塞进…
⑤ The shops were jam-packed with people buying presents.
= 商店里挤满了买礼物的人。
jam-packed = 挤得满满的
⑥ The army jammed the enemy's radio signals.
= 军队干扰了敌人的无线电信号。
jam signals = 干扰信号
⑦ I'm in a bit of a jam; can you lend me some money until Friday?
= 我有点手头紧，能借我点钱到周五吗？
be in a jam = 陷入困境
⑧ The band met every Thursday for a jam session in the garage.
= 乐队每周四在车库里即兴合奏。
a jam session = 即兴合奏
⑨ She used an old jam jar to keep the pens on her desk.
= 她用一个旧果酱瓶装书桌上的笔。
a jam jar = 果酱瓶

② The constant noise of the building work jangled her nerves.
= 施工没完没了的噪音让她神经紧张。
jangle somebody's nerves = 使某人神经紧张
③ There was a jangle of bells as the shop door opened.
= 商店门一开，铃铛叮当乱响。
a jangle of something = …的叮当声

jar
/dʒɑː(r)/
核心：（玻璃）罐、广口瓶；（英，口语）一杯啤酒；刺耳、震动；不协调。
名词：装果酱、蜂蜜的罐子（a jar of honey）；英式口语 a jar = 一杯（酒）。
动词：震得难受（the fall jarred his back）；（声音、颜色）刺耳、不协调（jar with）。
① She keeps her coins in a big glass jar on the kitchen shelf.
= 她把硬币放在厨房架子上的一个大玻璃罐里。
a glass jar = 玻璃罐
② Do you fancy a quick jar at the pub after work on Friday?
= 星期五下班后想去酒馆喝一杯吗？
a jar = （英，口语）一杯酒
③ He jarred his knee when he jumped off the wall.
= 他从墙上跳下来时震伤了膝盖。
jar something = 震伤…
④ Her bright red dress jarred with the calm colours of the room.
= 她那条鲜红的裙子跟房间里柔和的色调很不协调。
jar with something = 与…不协调

jaundice
