life
/laɪf/
核心：生命；一生、寿命；生活；生物（总称）；活力、生气；（口语）无期徒刑。复数 lives。
习语：come to life（活跃起来、有了生气）、bring to life（使生动起来）、for life（终身）、way of life（生活方式）、
lose your life（丧生）、save somebody's life（救某人的命）、the time of your life（玩得痛快极了）、
that's life（生活就是这样）、full of life（充满活力）、a matter of life and death（生死攸关的事）。
搭配：life insurance（人寿保险，英式也说 life cover）、life sentence（无期徒刑）、life cycle（生命周期）、
life form（生命形态）、life force（生命力）、life history（生活史）、life story（生平）、life's work（毕生的事业）、
life coach（人生教练，这一行叫 life coaching）、life skill（生活技能）、life science（生命科学）、
life drawing（人体写生）、life peer（英：终身贵族）、life support（生命维持）、life support machine（生命维持机）、
life raft（救生筏）、life vest（美：救生衣）、life preserver（美：救生用具）；
形容词 life-size（与实物一样大的）、life-saving（救命的）、life-threatening（危及生命的）、life-or-death（生死攸关的）、
life-giving（赋予生命的）、life-affirming（让人感到活着真好的）、life-enhancing（提升生活品质的）。

lifeblood
/ˈlaɪfblʌd/

the lifeblood of the high street = 商业街的命脉

lift
/lɪft/
核心：举起、抬起、提起；（雾、情绪）消散、好转；解除（禁令、限制）；（口语）偷、抄袭。
名词：（英）电梯；搭便车；提升、鼓舞。
短语动词 lift off（火箭升空）。习语：give somebody a lift（让某人搭车；让某人精神一振）、not lift a finger（一点忙都不帮）。
① He lifted the box onto the table with both hands.
= 他用双手把箱子举到桌子上。
lift something onto the table = 把…举到桌上
② She lifted her eyes from the book and smiled at me.
= 她从书上抬起眼睛，对我笑了笑。
lift your eyes = 抬起眼睛
③ The fog lifted by ten, and the planes began to take off.
= 雾在十点钟散了，飞机开始起飞。
the fog lifts = 雾散了
这一支不及物：自己升起、消散。
④ The government has lifted the ban on travel to the island.
= 政府解除了前往该岛的旅行禁令。
lift a ban = 解除禁令
lift a siege / sanctions 同此。
⑤ The good news lifted everyone's spirits.
= 那个好消息让大家精神一振。
lift somebody's spirits = 让某人精神一振
⑥ Whole paragraphs of the essay had been lifted from a website.
= 那篇文章里整段整段是从一个网站上抄来的。
lift something from a website = 从网站上抄…
口语；shoplifting（商店行窃）里的 lift 也是这个意思。
⑦ The rocket lifted off at dawn from the base in the desert.
= 火箭拂晓时从沙漠里的基地升空。
lift off = （火箭）升空
⑧ Take the lift to the fifth floor and turn right.
= 坐电梯到六楼，然后右转。
take the lift = （英）坐电梯
美式说 elevator。英式的 fifth floor 是六楼。
⑨ Could you give me a lift to the station?
= 你能捎我去车站吗？
give somebody a lift = 让某人搭车
⑩ Winning the prize gave her a real lift.
= 得奖让她大受鼓舞。
give somebody a lift = 让某人精神一振

light
/laɪt/
核心：两个不同的词，拼法相同。
其一：光、光线；灯；（点烟的）火。动词：点燃；照亮。形容词：明亮的；（颜色）浅的。过去式 lit，也用 lighted（多作定语）。
短语动词：light up（点亮；点烟；（脸）亮起来）、light on / light upon（文：偶然发现）。
习语：come to light（暴露、为人所知）、bring to light（揭露）、shed light on（阐明）、in the light of（鉴于）、
see the light（恍然大悟）、set light to（点着、放火烧）、the light at the end of the tunnel（隧道尽头的光、希望）。
搭配：light bulb（灯泡）、light year（光年）、light show（灯光秀）、light meter（测光表）、light pen（光笔）、
light stick（荧光棒）、light pollution（光污染）、light-coloured（浅色的）。
其二：轻的；少量的、轻微的；轻松的；（食物）清淡的；（睡眠）浅的。名词 lightness（轻；明亮）并入本条。
习语 make light of（不当回事）。
搭配：light aircraft（轻型飞机）、light industry（轻工业）、light railway（轻轨）、light ale（淡色麦酒）、
light cream（美：稀奶油）；形容词 light-hearted（轻松愉快的，副词 light-heartedly）、light-headed（头晕的）、
light-fingered（爱小偷小摸的）、light-footed（脚步轻快的）。
① Light from the street lamp came in through the curtains.
= 路灯的光透过窗帘照进来。
light from the lamp = 灯光
这一支不可数。
② Turn off the lights when you leave the room.
= 离开房间时把灯关掉。
turn off the lights = 关灯
这一支可数：一盏灯。
③ Wait until the lights turn green before you cross.
= 等绿灯亮了再过马路。
the lights = 红绿灯
traffic lights 的简称。
④ Have you got a light? I have left my matches at home.
= 你有火吗？我把火柴落在家里了。
a light = （点烟的）火
⑤ He lit a candle and carried it up the stairs.
= 他点了一支蜡烛，端着上了楼。
lit = light 的过去式
light a candle = 点蜡烛
⑥ The stage was lit by a single lamp.
= 舞台只由一盏灯照着。
be lit by something = 被…照亮
这一支是「照亮」，不是「点燃」。
⑦ She carried a lighted candle into the dark room.
= 她端着一支点着的蜡烛走进黑屋子。
a lighted candle = 点着的蜡烛
作定语多用 lighted。
⑧ It gets light at about five in summer.
= 夏天五点左右天就亮了。
get light = 天亮
反义是 get dark。
⑨ She painted the walls light blue.
= 她把墙刷成了浅蓝色。
light blue = 浅蓝色
反义是 dark blue。
⑩ Her face lit up when she saw the present.
= 她看见礼物，脸一下子亮了起来。
light up = （脸）亮起来
⑪ Fireworks lit up the sky over the harbour.
= 烟花照亮了港口上空。
light up the sky = 照亮天空
及物用法。
⑫ He sat down and lit up a cigarette.
= 他坐下来，点了一支烟。
light up a cigarette = 点烟
⑬ New facts about the case came to light last week.
= 上周，这个案子的新情况浮出了水面。
come to light = 暴露、为人所知
⑭ The inquiry brought to light serious failures at the hospital.
= 那场调查揭露出医院的严重失职。
bring to light = 揭露
⑮ The letters shed light on the writer's early life.
= 那些信让人对作家的早年生活有了了解。
shed light on = 阐明、使人了解
也说 throw / cast light on。
⑯ In the light of the new evidence, the police reopened the case.
= 鉴于新证据，警方重新立案。
in the light of = 鉴于
美式说 in light of。
⑰ He finally saw the light and gave up smoking.
= 他终于想通了，把烟戒了。
see the light = 恍然大悟、想通了
⑱ Someone set light to the old barn during the night.
= 夜里有人放火烧了那座旧谷仓。
set light to = 点着、放火烧

⑲ After months of losses, we can see the light at the end of the tunnel.
= 亏了好几个月，我们总算看到了隧道尽头的光。
the light at the end of the tunnel = 困境将尽的希望
⑳ While tidying the attic, she lit upon a box of old letters.
= 整理阁楼时，她偶然发现了一盒旧信。
light upon = （文）偶然发现
light on = 偶然发现（同上）
㉑ The light bulb in the hall needs changing.
= 门厅的灯泡该换了。
a light bulb = 灯泡

㉘ Wear light-coloured clothes so that drivers can see you at night.
= 穿浅色衣服，夜里司机才看得见你。
light-coloured = 浅色的

lighten
/ˈlaɪtən/
核心：减轻（负担、工作量）；使变亮、变浅；（心情、气氛）轻松起来。短语动词 lighten up（口语：放轻松、别那么严肃）。
① The new lifting machine has lightened the work of the nurses on the night shift.
= 新的升降机减轻了夜班护士的工作。
lighten the work = 减轻工作
② The sky began to lighten in the east as the fishing boats left the harbour.
= 渔船出港时，东方的天空开始发亮。
the sky lightens = 天空发亮
③ She used lemon juice to lighten her hair.
= 她用柠檬汁把头发颜色弄浅。
lighten your hair = 把头发颜色弄浅
④ His joke about the broken projector lightened the mood in the meeting room.
= 他拿坏掉的投影仪开了个玩笑，会议室里的气氛轻松了些。
lighten the mood = 让气氛轻松
⑤ Lighten up; it was only a joke, and nobody meant to hurt your feelings.
= 放轻松点，不过是个玩笑，没人想伤你的心。
lighten up = （口语）放轻松

lighter
/ˈlaɪtə(r)/
核心：打火机；（旧）驳船 —— 在大船和岸之间转运货物的平底船。
① He took a lighter from his pocket and lit the fire.
= 他从口袋里掏出打火机，点着了火。
a lighter = 打火机
来自 light（点燃）。
② She bought a cheap plastic lighter at the station.
= 她在车站买了一个便宜的塑料打火机。
a plastic lighter = 塑料打火机
③ The cargo was carried from the ship to the quay in lighters.
= 货物是用驳船从大船运到码头的。
a lighter = 驳船
这一支来自「减轻船的负载」。

lighthouse
/ˈlaɪthaʊs/
核心：灯塔 —— 海边、礁石上给船只指路的高塔。
① The lighthouse on the rocks warns ships away from the coast.
= 礁石上的灯塔警告船只远离海岸。
a lighthouse = 灯塔
字面：放光的房子。
② The lighthouse keeper lived alone on the island for twenty years.
= 那位灯塔看守人独自在岛上住了二十年。
a lighthouse keeper = 灯塔看守人
今天的灯塔大多已自动化。

lighting
/ˈlaɪtɪŋ/
核心：照明、灯光（的布置和效果）；舞台灯光。lighting engineer（灯光师）并入本条。
① The lighting in the restaurant is soft and warm.
= 那家餐馆的灯光柔和温暖。
the lighting = 照明、灯光
不可数；一盏灯是 a light。
② Street lighting was put in along the road to the school.
= 通往学校的那条路上装了路灯。
street lighting = 路灯照明
③ The lighting engineer spent two days setting up the stage.
= 灯光师花了两天布置舞台。
a lighting engineer = 灯光师

lightly
/ˈlaɪtli/
核心：轻轻地；少量地、稍稍地；轻率地、不当回事地。
习语：get off lightly（从轻发落、没吃大亏）、take something lightly（不把…当回事）。
① She touched him lightly on the arm to show that she was not angry.
= 她轻轻碰了碰他的胳膊，表示自己没生气。
touch somebody lightly = 轻轻碰某人
② Fry the fish lightly in a little butter and serve it with slices of lemon.
= 用少许黄油把鱼稍微煎一下，配柠檬片上桌。
fry something lightly = 稍微煎一下
③ He got off lightly with a small fine after driving through a red light.
= 他闯了红灯，只被罚了一点款，算是从轻发落。
get off lightly = 从轻发落
④ This is not a decision to be taken lightly.
= 这可不是一个能草率作出的决定。
take something lightly = 不把…当回事
⑤ He walks lightly for such a big man and often surprises people in the corridor.
= 他那么大的个子，走路却很轻，常在走廊里吓人一跳。
walk lightly = 走路轻
