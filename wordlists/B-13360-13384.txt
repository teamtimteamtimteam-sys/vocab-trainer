leader
/ˈliːdə(r)/
核心：领导者、领袖；（比赛、行业里）领先的人或公司；（英）报纸的社论。
形容词 leaderless（群龙无首的）与 leader board（比赛的排行榜）并入本条。
① She was elected leader of the party at the age of forty.
= 她四十岁当选为该党领袖。
the leader of a party = 党的领袖
② He is a born leader whom people follow without being asked.
= 他是个天生的领导者，不用开口别人就跟着他。
a born leader = 天生的领导者
③ The company is a world leader in electric cars.
= 那家公司在电动汽车领域世界领先。
a world leader in something = …领域的世界领先者
④ The leader crossed the line ten seconds ahead of the next runner.
= 领跑者比第二名早十秒冲过终点线。
the leader = 领跑者
⑤ The paper's leader this morning attacks the new tax.
= 那家报纸今天早上的社论抨击了新税。
a leader = （英）社论
也说 leading article；美式说 editorial。
⑥ The army was leaderless after the general was killed.
= 将军阵亡后，那支军队群龙无首。
leaderless = 群龙无首的
⑦ Her name went to the top of the leader board after the third round.
= 第三轮过后，她的名字升到了排行榜的首位。
the leader board = 排行榜
高尔夫等比赛里显示领先者成绩的牌子。

leadership
/ˈliːdəʃɪp/
核心：领导地位、领导职务；领导才能；领导层（总称）。
① The party won three elections in a row under her leadership.
= 在她的领导下，那个党连赢了三次选举。
under somebody's leadership = 在某人领导下
② He took over the leadership of the miners' union after the long strike of 2010.
= 二〇一〇年那场漫长的罢工之后，他接任了矿工工会的领导职务。
the leadership of the union = 工会的领导职务
③ The job needs someone with strong leadership skills and ten years of experience in hospitals.
= 这份工作需要一个领导能力强、有十年医院工作经验的人。
leadership skills = 领导能力
④ The party leadership met last night to discuss the crisis.
= 党的领导层昨晚开会讨论那场危机。
the party leadership = 党的领导层
这一支指一群人。

⑦ The firm works at the leading edge of computer design.

⑧ The paper's leading article called for the minister to resign.

⑨ His grandfather served as a leading seaman on a warship.

⑩ She joined the air force and became a leading aircraftwoman within a year.
= 她加入空军，不到一年就升为一等兵。
a leading aircraftwoman = （英国空军）女一等兵
a leading aircraftman = （英国空军）一等兵

leaf
/liːf/
核心：叶子；（书的）一张、一页（正反两面）；（桌子的）活动面板。复数 leaves。
短语动词 leaf through（匆匆翻阅）。
习语：turn over a new leaf（改过自新、翻开新的一页）、take a leaf out of somebody's book（学某人的样）。
形容词 leafy（多叶的；绿树成荫的）、leafless（光秃秃没有叶子的）与 leaf mould（腐叶土）并入本条。
① A single yellow leaf fell from the tree onto the path.
= 一片黄叶从树上飘落到小路上。
a leaf = 一片叶子
② The children collected autumn leaves and stuck them in a book.
= 孩子们捡了秋天的落叶，贴在本子里。
leaves = leaf 的复数
③ The trees come into leaf in April.
= 那些树四月长叶。
come into leaf = 长出叶子
④ She leafed through a magazine while she waited for the dentist.
= 等牙医的时候，她随手翻着一本杂志。
leaf through = 匆匆翻阅
⑤ He promised to turn over a new leaf and stop coming to work late.
= 他保证改过自新，上班不再迟到。
turn over a new leaf = 改过自新
leaf 这里是书页：翻到新的一页。
⑥ You should take a leaf out of your sister's book and start saving.
= 你该学学你姐姐，开始存钱。
take a leaf out of somebody's book = 学某人的样
⑦ We pulled out the extra leaf of the table when the guests arrived.
= 客人到了，我们把桌子的活动面板拉了出来。
the leaf of a table = 桌子的活动面板
⑧ They live in a leafy suburb with large gardens.
= 他们住在一个绿树成荫、花园宽敞的郊区。
a leafy suburb = 绿树成荫的郊区
⑨ The leafless branches looked black against the winter sky.
= 光秃秃的树枝在冬日天空的映衬下黑黝黝的。
leafless branches = 光秃秃的树枝
⑩ She dug some leaf mould into the soil before planting the roses.
= 种玫瑰之前，她往土里掺了些腐叶土。
leaf mould = 腐叶土

league

lean

= 她把自行车靠在商店的墙上。

= 那座塔略微向一边倾斜。
lean to one side = 向一边倾斜
⑤ He has always leaned on his older sister for advice.
= 他一向依赖姐姐给他出主意。

⑥ The bank is leaning on him to pay back the loan this month.
= 银行正逼他这个月还贷款。

⑦ I am leaning towards the blue one, but I have not decided yet.

⑨ He is a tall, lean man with a quiet voice.

⑩ Use lean meat and cut off any fat before cooking.
= 用瘦肉，下锅前把肥的都切掉。
lean meat = 瘦肉
⑪ The company survived the lean years after the war by selling land.
= 那家公司靠卖地熬过了战后的萧条年月。
lean years = 萧条的年月
⑫ He has strong leanings towards the left of the party.
= 他明显倾向于党内的左派。

⑬ The leanness of the runners showed how hard they had trained.
= 那些跑步选手一个个精瘦，可见训练多刻苦。
leanness = 精瘦

lease
/liːs/
核心：租约、租契 —— 长期租用房屋、土地、设备的合同。动词：租用；出租。
习语 a new lease of life（英：重获生机；美式说 a new lease on life）。
名词 leasing（租赁业务）、leasehold（租赁保有；按租约持有的）、leaseholder（租约持有人）、
leaseback（售后回租）并入本条。
① We signed a two-year lease on a flat near the station.
= 我们签了车站附近一套公寓的两年租约。
a lease on a flat = 公寓的租约
② The lease runs out in March, and the rent will go up.
= 租约三月到期，房租要涨。
the lease runs out = 租约到期
③ The company leases its cars and never buys them.
= 那家公司的车都是租的，从来不买。
lease a car = 租车
④ The farmer leased the field to a neighbour for five years.
= 那位农夫把田租给了邻居，为期五年。
lease something to somebody = 把…租给某人
同一个词，租进、租出都说。
⑤ The operation gave him a new lease of life.
= 那次手术让他重获生机。
a new lease of life = 重获生机
⑥ Leasing has become a popular way to get a new car.
= 租赁已成为用上新车的流行方式。
leasing = 租赁（业务）
⑦ The flat is leasehold, with ninety years left to run.
= 那套公寓是租赁产权，还剩九十年。
leasehold = 租赁保有的
与 freehold（永久产权）相对。
⑧ The leaseholders in the building share the cost of repairing the roof.
= 楼里的租约持有人分摊修屋顶的费用。
a leaseholder = 租约持有人
⑨ The firm raised money through the leaseback of its head office.
= 那家公司通过总部大楼的售后回租筹了钱。
leaseback = 售后回租
把房产卖掉，再从买主手里长期租回来用。

leash
/liːʃ/
核心：（美）牵狗的绳子 —— 英式多说 lead。动词：用绳拴住。
习语 on a tight / short leash（被管得很紧）、strain at the leash（跃跃欲试）。
① She walks three big dogs on a leash along the beach every morning before work.
= 她每天早上上班前牵着三条大狗在海滩上遛。
on a leash = 用绳子牵着
② The coach keeps his young players on a tight leash.
= 那位教练把年轻球员管得很紧。
on a tight leash = 被管得很紧
③ The students were straining at the leash to start the experiment.
= 学生们跃跃欲试，急着开始做实验。
strain at the leash = 跃跃欲试
像狗把绳子绷得紧紧的想往前冲。
④ She leashed the dog before opening the front door.
= 她先把狗拴上绳，再打开大门。
leash a dog = 给狗拴上绳

least
/liːst/
核心：最少的、最小的（little 的最高级）；副词：最不。
习语：at least（至少；起码）、not in the least（一点也不）、least of all（尤其不）、
to say the least（说得客气点、至少可以这么说）、the least you can do（起码该做的）。
数学术语 least common multiple（最小公倍数）、least common denominator（最小公分母）并入本条。
① He did the least work of anyone in the group but took most of the praise.
= 组里就数他干的活最少，夸奖却大半归了他。
the least work = 最少的活
② We stayed in the least expensive hotel in the town, right beside the railway line.
= 我们住的是城里最便宜的旅馆，紧挨着铁路线。
the least expensive = 最不贵的
least + 形容词：最不…的。
③ It will cost at least five hundred pounds to repair the car.
= 修那辆车至少要花五百英镑。
at least = 至少
④ The food was cold, but at least it was cheap.
= 饭菜是凉的，不过起码便宜。
at least = 起码
这一支不是说数量，是说「好歹有一点好处」。
⑤ I am not in the least worried about the driving exam after so much practice.
= 练了这么多，我对驾照考试一点也不担心。
not in the least = 一点也不
⑥ Nobody wanted to go, least of all the children.
= 谁也不想去，孩子们尤其不想。
least of all = 尤其不
⑦ After the long journey, the dirty hotel room was disappointing, to say the least.
= 长途跋涉之后，那间脏兮兮的酒店房间，说得客气点，令人失望。
to say the least = 说得客气点
⑧ You broke her favourite cup, so the least you can do is say sorry.
= 你把她最喜欢的杯子打碎了，起码该道个歉。
the least you can do = 起码该做的
⑨ The least common multiple of four and six is twelve.
= 四和六的最小公倍数是十二。
the least common multiple = 最小公倍数
⑩ To add the fractions, first find the least common denominator.
= 要把这些分数相加，先求最小公分母。
the least common denominator = 最小公分母
英式多说 lowest common denominator。

leather
/ˈleðə(r)/
核心：皮革；（复数 leathers）摩托车手穿的皮衣裤。
形容词 leathery（像皮革的、粗糙坚韧的）、名词 leatherette（人造革）与 leatherback（棱皮龟）并入本条。
① She bought a black leather jacket with her first month's wages.
= 她用头一个月的工资买了一件黑色皮夹克。
a leather jacket = 皮夹克
② The seats of the car are covered in soft leather.
= 那辆车的座椅包着柔软的皮革。
covered in leather = 包着皮革
③ The rider took off his helmet and unzipped his leathers.
= 骑手摘下头盔，拉开了皮衣的拉链。
leathers = 摩托车皮衣裤
④ The old fisherman had a brown, leathery face.
= 那位老渔夫有一张晒成褐色、皮革般粗糙的脸。
a leathery face = 皮革般粗糙的脸
也说肉「老得嚼不动」。
⑤ The chairs in the cafe were covered in red leatherette.
= 咖啡馆的椅子包着红色人造革。
leatherette = 人造革
-ette 表示「仿的、小的」。
⑥ A leatherback came up the beach at night to lay her eggs.
= 一只棱皮龟夜里爬上海滩产卵。
a leatherback = 棱皮龟
最大的海龟，背上不是硬壳，是皮革似的厚皮。

leave
/liːv/
核心：离开；留下、丢下、忘带；让…保持某状态；把…留给、交给；剩下；遗赠。过去式、过去分词 left。
名词：假期、休假（on leave）；（正式）许可。
短语动词：leave behind（丢下；把…甩在后面）、leave out（漏掉、不包括）、leave off（停止；英：不再穿）、
leave aside（撇开不谈）。
习语：leave alone（别碰、别打扰）、leave it at that（到此为止）、leave a lot to be desired（很不理想）、
take your leave（正式：告辞）。
名词 leaver（离校生）、leavings（残羹剩饭）、leave-taking（告别）并入本条。
① The train leaves at six, so we should be at the station by half past five.
= 火车六点开，我们应该五点半前到车站。
the train leaves = 火车开出
② She left the office early to collect her son from school.
= 她提早离开办公室去学校接儿子。
leave the office = 离开办公室
③ He left home at eighteen and never lived with his parents again.
= 他十八岁离开家，再也没和父母一起住过。
leave home = 离家
④ I left my umbrella on the bus this morning.
= 今天早上我把伞落在公交车上了。
leave something on the bus = 把…落在公交车上
说了地点用 leave，不用 forget。
⑤ Leave the door open so that the cat can come in.
= 把门开着，好让猫进来。
leave the door open = 让门开着
⑥ Leave the washing-up to me; you have done enough today.
= 洗碗交给我吧，你今天干得够多了。
leave something to somebody = 把…交给某人
⑦ There is only a little milk left in the fridge.
= 冰箱里只剩一点牛奶了。
be left = 剩下
⑧ Her aunt left her the house and all the furniture.
= 她姑妈把那座房子和全部家具都留给了她。
leave somebody something = 把…遗赠给某人
⑨ He left a message with my secretary while I was out.
= 我不在的时候，他给我的秘书留了口信。
leave a message = 留口信
⑩ Leave the dog alone while it is eating.
= 狗吃东西的时候别去惹它。
leave alone = 别碰、别打扰
⑪ We had to leave the heavy bags behind at the hotel.
= 我们只好把那几个重包留在酒店。
leave behind = 丢下、留下
⑫ She soon left the other runners far behind.
= 她很快就把别的选手远远甩在了后面。
leave somebody behind = 把某人甩在后面
⑬ You have left out the most important name on the list.
= 你把名单上最重要的名字漏掉了。
leave out = 漏掉、不包括
⑭ Let's start again where we left off yesterday.
= 咱们从昨天停下的地方接着来。
leave off = 停止
⑮ Leaving aside the cost, do we even have time to do it?
= 撇开花费不谈，我们到底有没有时间做这件事？
leave aside = 撇开不谈
⑯ We disagree, so let's leave it at that and talk about something else.
= 我们意见不一，那就到此为止，聊点别的吧。
leave it at that = 到此为止
⑰ The service at the hotel left a lot to be desired.
= 那家酒店的服务很不理想。
leave a lot to be desired = 很不理想
委婉说法：还有很多该改进的。
⑱ Her brother is home on leave from the army for two weeks.
= 她哥哥从部队休假回家两个星期。
on leave = 在休假
⑲ She is taking six months' maternity leave after the baby is born.
= 孩子出生后，她要休六个月产假。
maternity leave = 产假
⑳ It was getting late, so he took his leave of the family and walked home.
= 天色已晚，他向那家人告辞，走回了家。
take your leave = （正式）告辞
㉑ The college holds a party for school leavers every July.
= 那所学院每年七月为离校生办一场聚会。
a school leaver = （英）离校生
㉒ The dogs fought over the leavings from the kitchen.
= 几条狗争抢厨房里的残羹剩饭。
leavings = 残羹剩饭
复数形式。
㉓ After a tearful leave-taking at the station, the soldiers boarded the train.
= 在车站一场含泪的告别之后，士兵们登上了火车。
a leave-taking = 告别

leaven
/ˈlevən/
核心：酵母、发酵剂；（比喻）让沉闷的东西活泼起来的一点成分。动词：使发酵；给…添点轻松。
① The baker keeps a piece of dough from each batch as leaven for the next.
= 面包师每一炉都留一块面团，当下一炉的酵头。
leaven = 酵头、发酵剂
② The long speech was leavened with a few good jokes.
= 那篇长讲话里穿插了几个不错的笑话，没那么沉闷了。
be leavened with jokes = 用笑话添点轻松
这一支是比喻，常用被动。
