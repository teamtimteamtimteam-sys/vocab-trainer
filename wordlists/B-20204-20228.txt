scrummy
/ˈskrʌmi/
核心：形容词（英式口语）：好吃的、美味的。比较级 scrummier。
① My grandmother made a scrummy chocolate cake with fresh cream for my tenth birthday.
= 我十岁生日时，祖母做了一个配鲜奶油的好吃的巧克力蛋糕。
a scrummy cake = 好吃的蛋糕
scrummy = 好吃的
② These little lemon biscuits are absolutely scrummy, so please have another one.
= 这些小柠檬饼干好吃极了，请再吃一块。
absolutely scrummy = 好吃极了

scrumptious
/ˈskrʌmpʃəs/
核心：形容词（口语）：（食物）美味的、可口的。比 delicious 更口语、带亲切感。
① The hotel served a scrumptious breakfast of fresh bread, eggs and homemade jam.
= 那家酒店的早餐很可口，有新鲜面包、鸡蛋和自制果酱。
a scrumptious breakfast = 可口的早餐
scrumptious = 美味的
② The strawberries from the farm were so scrumptious that we ate the whole basket.
= 农场的草莓太好吃了，我们把一整篮都吃光了。
so scrumptious = 那么美味

scrumpy
/ˈskrʌmpi/
核心：名词，不可数（英式）：（英格兰西南部的）农家烈性苹果酒：未经过滤、浑浊、酒劲大。
① We drank a pint of scrumpy at a little farm in Somerset and felt very sleepy afterwards.
= 我们在萨默塞特的一个小农场喝了一品脱农家苹果酒，之后觉得很困。
a pint of scrumpy = 一品脱农家苹果酒
scrumpy = 农家烈性苹果酒
② Be careful with the scrumpy; it is much stronger than the cider you buy in shops.
= 那农家苹果酒你小心点，比商店里买的苹果酒烈多了。
the scrumpy = 农家苹果酒

scrunch
/skrʌntʃ/
核心：动词：把……揉成一团：scrunch something up；（脚下）发出嘎吱声；皱起（脸）：scrunch up your face。名词，单数：嘎吱声。
① She scrunched up the letter and threw it into the fire without reading it.
= 她看都没看就把信揉成一团扔进了火里。
scrunch something up = 把某物揉成一团
scrunch = 揉成一团
② The snow scrunched under our boots as we walked through the silent forest.
= 我们走过寂静的森林，雪在靴子底下嘎吱作响。
snow scrunches = 雪嘎吱作响
③ Scrunch-dry your hair with your fingers if you want it to look curly and natural.
= 想让头发看起来卷曲自然，就用手指抓着吹干。
scrunch-dry = 用手抓着吹出卷发

scrunchy
/ˈskrʌntʃi/
核心：名词，可数，复数 scrunchies：（布包的）发圈、头绳。也拼作 scrunchie。形容词：（踩上去）嘎吱作响的。
① She tied her hair back with a bright pink scrunchy before she went running.
= 她出门跑步前，用一个亮粉色发圈把头发扎了起来。
a scrunchy = 发圈
② We walked along the scrunchy gravel path to the front door of the old house.
= 我们沿着踩上去嘎吱响的碎石路走到老房子的前门。
scrunchy gravel = 嘎吱作响的碎石

scruple
/ˈskruːpl/
核心：名词，可数或不可数：顾忌、顾虑（道德上的）：have no scruples about doing something、without scruple。动词：（正式，多用否定）有所顾忌：not scruple to do something。
① He has no scruples about lying to his customers if it means he can sell more cars.
= 只要能多卖几辆车，他对欺骗顾客毫无顾忌。
have no scruples about doing something = 做某事毫无顾忌
scruples = 顾忌
② She would not scruple to take the money from her own family if she needed it.
= 要是需要钱，她会毫无顾忌地拿自己家人的钱。
not scruple to do something = 毫无顾忌地做某事

scrupulous
/ˈskruːpjələs/
核心：形容词：一丝不苟的、极仔细的：scrupulous attention to detail；（道德上）严谨正直的、讲良心的。反义 unscrupulous。副词 scrupulously 是一丝不苟地；极其（干净、诚实）：scrupulously clean。名词 scrupulousness 另立条。
① The museum staff take scrupulous care of the old paintings and check them every day.
= 博物馆员工一丝不苟地照料那些古画，每天都检查。
scrupulous care = 一丝不苟的照料
scrupulous = 一丝不苟的
② He was scrupulous about paying back every penny that he had ever borrowed.
= 他借过的每一分钱都一丝不苟地还清。
be scrupulous about something = 在某事上一丝不苟
③ The kitchen of the restaurant was scrupulously clean, with not a crumb on the floor.
= 那家餐馆的厨房干净极了，地上连一点碎屑都没有。
scrupulously clean = 极其干净

scrupulousness
/ˈskruːpjələsnəs/
核心：名词，不可数：一丝不苟、严谨；（道德上的）正直。由 scrupulous 加 -ness 构成。
① Everyone admired the scrupulousness with which she checked every figure in the report.
= 大家都佩服她核对报告里每一个数字时的一丝不苟。
scrupulousness = 一丝不苟
② His scrupulousness in money matters made him the obvious choice to look after the club's accounts.
= 他在钱的事上一向严谨，自然成了管俱乐部账目的不二人选。
scrupulousness in money matters = 在钱的事上的严谨

scrutineer
/ˌskruːtəˈnɪə(r)/
核心：名词，可数（英式）：（选举的）监票员、检票员：检查投票是否公正的人。
① The scrutineers watched carefully as the votes were counted in the town hall.
= 市政厅里点票时，监票员们仔细地看着。
scrutineers = 监票员
② Each party is allowed to send a scrutineer to check that the election is fair.
= 每个政党都可以派一名监票员，检查选举是否公正。
send a scrutineer = 派监票员

scrutinize
/ˈskruːtənaɪz/
核心：动词，及物：仔细检查、细看、审查：scrutinize a document、scrutinize somebody's face。英式也拼作 scrutinise。名词 scrutiny 另立条。
① The lawyer scrutinized the contract carefully before he let his client sign it.
= 律师仔细审查了合同，才让他的委托人签字。
scrutinize something = 仔细审查某物
scrutinize = 仔细检查
② She scrutinized his face, trying to see whether he was telling the truth.
= 她仔细端详他的脸，想看出他是不是在说实话。
scrutinize somebody's face = 端详某人的脸

scrutiny
/ˈskruːtəni/
核心：名词，不可数：仔细检查、审查：come under scrutiny、close scrutiny、public scrutiny。
① The government's plans will come under close scrutiny when parliament discusses them next week.
= 下周议会讨论时，政府的计划将受到严格审查。
come under scrutiny = 受到审查
close scrutiny = 严格审查
② Famous actors and singers have to live their private lives under constant public scrutiny.
= 有名的演员和歌手不得不在公众的持续关注下过私生活。
public scrutiny = 公众监督
③ On closer scrutiny, the painting turned out to be a clever copy.
= 仔细一查，那幅画原来是一件精巧的仿品。
on closer scrutiny = 仔细一查

scud
/skʌd/
核心：动词，不及物（文学）：（云、船）飞驰、疾行：clouds scudding across the sky。
① Dark clouds scudded across the sky as the wind grew stronger before the storm.
= 暴风雨来临前风越刮越大，乌云在天上飞驰。
clouds scud = 云飞驰
scud = 飞驰、疾行
② The little sailing boat scudded across the bay in the strong afternoon wind.
= 下午风大，那条小帆船在海湾里疾驶而过。
scud across something = 疾驶过某处

scuff
/skʌf/
核心：动词：（鞋、地板等）磨损、刮擦：scuff your shoes；拖着脚走。名词，可数：磨痕、刮痕。形容词 scuffed 是磨损的（另立条）。
① On their first day, the children scuffed their new shoes playing football in the playground.
= 第一天上学，孩子们就在操场上踢足球，把新鞋磨坏了。
scuff your shoes = 把鞋磨坏
scuff = 磨损
② There were black scuffs on the white floor where people had dragged the chairs.
= 白色地板上有人拖过椅子的地方留下了黑色的刮痕。
scuffs = 磨痕

scuffed
/skʌft/
核心：形容词：（鞋、地板、家具）磨损的、有刮痕的。
① His shoes were scuffed and dirty after the long walk through the woods.
= 在树林里走了很长的路后，他的鞋又磨损又脏。
scuffed shoes = 磨损的鞋
scuffed = 磨损的
② The old piano was badly scuffed, but it still had a beautiful sound.
= 那架旧钢琴刮痕累累，可音色仍然很美。
badly scuffed = 刮痕累累

scuffle
/ˈskʌfl/
核心：名词，可数：扭打、混战：a scuffle broke out。动词，不及物：扭打、混战：scuffle with somebody。比 fight 规模小、时间短。
① A scuffle broke out between two fans outside the stadium, but the police stopped it quickly.
= 体育场外两名球迷扭打起来，不过警察很快就制止了。
a scuffle broke out = 发生扭打
a scuffle = 扭打
② Protesters scuffled with police as they tried to get into the building.
= 抗议者想冲进大楼时，与警察发生了推搡扭打。
scuffle with somebody = 与某人扭打

scuffling
/ˈskʌflɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：扭打；（小动物发出的）窸窣声、刮擦声。
① We heard a scuffling in the roof at night and realized that mice had got in.
= 夜里我们听到屋顶上有窸窣声，才知道老鼠钻进去了。
a scuffling = 窸窣声
② There was some scuffling at the back of the hall, and two men were asked to leave.
= 礼堂后面发生了一些推搡，有两个人被请了出去。
some scuffling = 一些推搡扭打

scull
/skʌl/
核心：名词，可数：（双桨划艇用的）短桨；单人（或双人）双桨赛艇。动词：用短桨划（船）。
① He rowed the little boat across the lake with a pair of light wooden sculls.
= 他用一对轻便的木制短桨把小船划过了湖。
a pair of sculls = 一对短桨
② Every Sunday she sculled slowly down the river in the early morning mist.
= 每个星期天清晨的薄雾里，她都划着双桨赛艇顺河慢慢而下。
scull down a river = 划桨顺河而下

sculler
/ˈskʌlə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：双桨赛艇运动员、划双桨船的人。
① The young sculler trained on the river every morning at six before going to school.
= 那位年轻的双桨赛艇运动员每天早上六点上学前都在河上训练。
a sculler = 双桨赛艇运动员
② She became the fastest sculler in the country and won a gold medal at the Olympics.
= 她成了全国最快的双桨赛艇手，还在奥运会上拿了金牌。
the fastest sculler = 最快的双桨赛艇手

scullery
/ˈskʌləri/
核心：名词，可数：（旧时大宅厨房旁的）洗涤间：洗碗、洗菜的小房间。
① In the old house, the maids washed the dishes in a cold scullery next to the kitchen.
= 在那座老宅里，女仆们在厨房旁边冰冷的洗涤间里洗碗。
a scullery = 洗涤间
② We turned the old scullery into a small laundry room with a washing machine.
= 我们把旧洗涤间改成了放洗衣机的小洗衣房。
turn a scullery into something = 把洗涤间改成某物

sculling
/ˈskʌlɪŋ/
核心：名词，不可数：双桨划船运动、单人双桨赛艇。
① Sculling is harder than it looks, because you have to use both arms at exactly the same time.
= 双桨划船比看上去难，因为两只胳膊得完全同时用力。

take up sculling = 开始玩双桨赛艇

sculpt
/skʌlpt/
核心：动词：雕刻、雕塑：sculpt a figure in stone；（比喻）塑造、使成形。
① The artist sculpted a beautiful horse out of a single block of white stone.
= 那位艺术家用一整块白石雕刻出了一匹漂亮的马。
sculpt something out of something = 用某物雕出某物
sculpt = 雕刻
② The wind and waves have sculpted the cliffs into strange shapes over thousands of years.
= 几千年来，风浪把悬崖塑造成了奇形怪状。
sculpt something into something = 把某物塑造成某形状

sculptor
/ˈskʌlptə(r)/
核心：名词，可数：雕塑家、雕刻家。
① The sculptor spent three years working on the huge statue in the city square.
= 那位雕塑家花了三年时间创作城市广场上的那座巨大雕像。
a sculptor = 雕塑家
② The young sculptor works mainly with metal and old pieces of machinery.
= 那位年轻雕塑家主要用金属和旧机器零件创作。
work as a sculptor = 当雕塑家

sculptress
/ˈskʌlptrəs/
核心：名词，可数（旧式）：女雕塑家。今天一般不分性别，都说 sculptor。
① The museum has a whole room of works by a famous sculptress from the nineteenth century.
= 那座博物馆有一整间展厅陈列一位十九世纪著名女雕塑家的作品。
a sculptress = 女雕塑家
② She was one of the first women to be called a sculptress in an age when few women made art.
= 在很少有女性从事艺术的年代，她是最早被称为女雕塑家的人之一。
be called a sculptress = 被称为女雕塑家

sculptural
/ˈskʌlptʃərəl/
核心：形容词：雕塑的、雕塑般的：sculptural forms。
① The new building has a sculptural shape, with curved white walls that look like waves.
= 那座新楼造型如雕塑一般，弯曲的白墙像波浪。
a sculptural shape = 雕塑般的造型
sculptural = 雕塑般的
② The modern garden behind the gallery is full of sculptural plants with large dramatic leaves.
= 美术馆后面那个现代花园里满是叶子硕大、造型夸张的雕塑感植物。
sculptural plants = 雕塑感植物

sculpture
/ˈskʌlptʃə(r)/
核心：名词：雕塑作品、雕像（可数或不可数）；雕塑艺术（不可数）。动词：雕刻。人是 sculptor（雕塑家）。
① The park is full of modern sculptures made of stone, metal and glass.
= 公园里满是用石头、金属和玻璃做的现代雕塑作品。
sculpture = 雕塑
sculptures = 雕塑作品
② She studied sculpture at art college before she became a famous painter.
= 她成为名画家之前，在美术学院学的是雕塑。
study sculpture = 学雕塑
③ The sculptor who made the statue in the square also made the one in front of the station.
= 广场上那座雕像的雕塑家，也做了车站前面那座。
sculptor = 雕塑家
