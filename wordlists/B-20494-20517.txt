self-possessed
/ˌself pəˈzest/
核心：形容词：沉着冷静的、镇定自若的。
① For a girl of fifteen, she was very self-possessed and answered the reporters' questions calmly.
= 作为一个十五岁的女孩，她非常镇定，从容地回答了记者的提问。
self-possessed = 沉着冷静的
② He remained self-possessed even when the angry crowd started shouting at him.
= 即使愤怒的人群开始冲他大喊，他依然镇定自若。
remain self-possessed = 保持镇定

self-possession
