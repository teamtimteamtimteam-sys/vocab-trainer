laureate
核心：获奖者 —— 尤指获重大奖项或荣誉的人。常见 a Nobel laureate（诺贝尔奖得主）、the Poet Laureate（桂冠诗人）。
① The lecture will be given by a Nobel laureate in physics.
= 那场讲座将由一位诺贝尔物理学奖得主主讲。
a Nobel laureate = 诺贝尔奖得主
② The Poet Laureate wrote a poem for the royal wedding.
= 桂冠诗人为王室婚礼写了一首诗。
the Poet Laureate = 桂冠诗人
古时用月桂枝叶编冠授给优胜者，故名。

laurel
核心：月桂树 —— 叶子常绿发亮；（复数）荣誉、殊荣。
习语：rest on your laurels（躺在功劳簿上）、look to your laurels（小心被人超过）。
a laurel wreath（月桂花环）并入本条。
① A laurel hedge hides the house from the road.
= 一道月桂树篱把房子挡住，从路上看不见。
a laurel hedge = 月桂树篱
② She won the prize last year, but she is not resting on her laurels.
= 她去年得了奖，但并没有躺在功劳簿上。
rest on your laurels = 躺在功劳簿上
③ The champion will have to look to his laurels now that the young players are improving.
= 年轻选手在进步，冠军得小心别被超过了。
look to your laurels = 小心被人超过
④ The winner of the race was crowned with a laurel wreath.
= 赛跑的优胜者被戴上了月桂花环。
a laurel wreath = 月桂花环
古希腊、罗马授给胜利者的。

lav
核心：（英，口语）厕所 —— lavatory 的简称。
① He's in the lav, so he'll be out in a minute.
= 他在上厕所，马上就出来。
in the lav = （英，口语）在厕所里

= 我奶奶家屋外的厕所冬天冷得要命。
an outside lav = 屋外的厕所
有点老派，今天更常说 loo。

lava

red-hot lava = 炽热的熔岩
② The island's beaches are made of black lava.
= 那座岛的海滩是黑色火山岩构成的。
black lava = 黑色火山岩
这一支指冷却变硬之后的。
③ He has an orange lava lamp on the desk in his bedroom.

a lava lamp = 熔岩灯
灯里的蜡团受热上下浮动，像熔岩。

lavatory

lavatorial humour = 屎尿屁式的幽默

lavender

pale lavender = 淡紫色

lavish
核心：奢华的、铺张的；（人）慷慨的、出手大方的。动词：lavish on / upon（把…大量地给予）。副词 lavishly 并入本条。
① They held a lavish wedding with five hundred guests.

a lavish wedding = 奢华的婚礼

lavish on = 把…大量地给予

lavish upon = 把…大量地给予（较正式）

= 酒店的房间用丝绸和黄金装饰得富丽堂皇。
lavishly decorated = 装饰得富丽堂皇

law
核心：法律 —— the law（一国的法律整体）；a law（一条法律、法规）；法学、律师这一行；（科学的）定律、规律。
口语里 the law 也指警察。
习语：against the law（违法）、break the law（犯法）、by law（依法、按法律规定）、above the law（凌驾于法律之上）、
law and order（治安、法治秩序）、take the law into your own hands（私自执法、自行报复）、
lay down the law（发号施令）、be a law unto yourself（我行我素）。
搭配 law court（法庭）、law school（法学院）并入本条。
① It is against the law to drive without insurance.
= 无保险驾车是违法的。
against the law = 违法
② He had never broken the law in his life until that night.
= 那天晚上之前，他这辈子从没犯过法。
break the law = 犯法
③ By law, children must stay at school until they are sixteen.
= 按法律规定，孩子必须上学到十六岁。
by law = 依法
④ Parliament passed a new law on smoking in public places.
= 议会通过了一项关于公共场所吸烟的新法律。
pass a law = 通过一项法律
这一支可数：一条具体的法。
⑤ She studied law at university and now works for a firm in London.
= 她大学学的是法律，现在在伦敦一家事务所工作。
study law = 学法律
这一支不带冠词：法学这门学科、这个行当。
⑥ Nobody, not even the president, is above the law.
= 没有人能凌驾于法律之上，连总统也不例外。
above the law = 凌驾于法律之上
⑦ The new mayor promised to restore law and order in the city.
= 新市长承诺恢复城里的治安。
law and order = 治安、法治秩序
⑧ When the police did nothing, the villagers took the law into their own hands.
= 警察不管，村民们就自己动手了。
take the law into your own hands = 私自执法、自行报复
⑨ My father used to lay down the law about what time we came home.
= 我们几点回家，我父亲过去总是说一不二。
lay down the law = 发号施令
⑩ Newton discovered the law of gravity in the seventeenth century.
= 牛顿在十七世纪发现了万有引力定律。
the law of gravity = 万有引力定律
⑪ The law of supply and demand explains why prices rise when goods are scarce.
= 供求规律解释了为什么货少时价格上涨。
the law of supply and demand = 供求规律
⑫ He ran off when he saw the law coming down the street.
= 他看见警察沿街走来，拔腿就跑。
the law = （口语）警察
⑬ She has always been a law unto herself and ignores what the manager says.
= 她一向我行我素，经理说什么都不听。
be a law unto yourself = 我行我素
⑭ Good citizens obey the law even when nobody is watching them.
= 好公民即使没人看着也守法。
obey the law = 守法
⑮ The case will be decided in a law court, not in the newspapers.
= 这个案子要在法庭上裁决，不是在报纸上。
a law court = 法庭
也说 a court of law。
⑯ He went to law school after four years as a police officer.
= 他当了四年警察之后去上了法学院。
law school = 法学院
美国的法学院是研究生阶段。

lawful

lawless

lay
核心：放、搁 —— 小心地平放下来；铺设；摆（餐桌）；下（蛋）。过去式、过去分词 laid。及物，后面要有宾语。
注意：lie（躺）的过去式也是 lay，两个词常被混用。形容词 lay 另指「非神职的；外行的」。
短语动词：lay down（放下；规定）、lay off（解雇；口语：别再…）、lay out（摆开；布局；口语：花一大笔钱）、
lay aside（搁置；存起来）、lay in（储备）、lay into（痛打、痛骂）、lay on（英：提供、安排）、
lay over（美：中途停留）、lay up（使卧床；储存）、lay about（挥拳乱打，常说 lay about you）。
习语：lay the table（摆餐桌）、lay the blame on（归咎于）、lay claim to（声称对…有权）、
lay a finger on（碰…一下）、lay eyes on（看见）。lay-by（英：路边停车带）与 laid-back（悠闲的）并入本条。
① She laid the baby gently in the cot and turned off the light.
= 她把婴儿轻轻放进小床，关了灯。
lay something in a place = 把…放在某处
laid 是过去式。
② He laid his hand on my shoulder and told me not to worry.
= 他把手搭在我肩上，叫我别担心。
lay your hand on something = 把手搭在…上
③ Workers are laying a new carpet in the hall this afternoon.
= 工人今天下午在门厅铺新地毯。
lay a carpet = 铺地毯
lay bricks / pipes / cables 同此。
④ Could you lay the table while I finish cooking?
= 我把饭做完，你来摆餐桌好吗？
lay the table = （英）摆餐桌
美式说 set the table。
⑤ The hens have laid six eggs since yesterday.
= 从昨天到现在，母鸡下了六个蛋。
lay eggs = 下蛋
⑥ The police laid the blame on the driver of the lorry.
= 警方把责任归到了卡车司机头上。
lay the blame on somebody = 归咎于某人
⑦ Two countries lay claim to the small island.
= 两个国家都声称对那座小岛拥有主权。
lay claim to = 声称对…有权
⑧ The soldiers laid down their weapons and walked out with their hands up.
= 士兵们放下武器，举着手走了出来。
lay down = 放下
⑨ The rules lay down what players may wear on the field.
= 规则规定了球员在场上可以穿什么。
lay down = 规定
⑩ The factory laid off two hundred workers when orders fell.
= 订单减少后，那家工厂解雇了两百名工人。
lay off = （因没活干而）解雇
⑪ Lay off the chocolate for a few weeks and see if you feel better.
= 几个星期别吃巧克力，看看是不是好些。
lay off something = （口语）别再碰…
⑫ She laid out the map on the table so that everyone could see it.
= 她把地图在桌上摊开，好让大家都看得见。
lay out = 摆开、摊开
⑬ The gardens were laid out in the eighteenth century.
= 那些花园是十八世纪规划布置的。
be laid out = 布局、规划
⑭ We had to lay out two thousand pounds on repairs to the roof.
= 我们修屋顶不得不花了两千英镑。
lay out money = （口语）花一大笔钱
⑮ She laid aside her book and went to answer the door.
= 她把书放到一边，去应门。
lay aside = 放到一边、搁置
也指「存起来」：lay aside money for old age。
⑯ We laid in plenty of food and candles before the storm.
= 暴风雨来之前，我们储备了许多食物和蜡烛。
lay in = 储备
⑰ His father laid into him for coming home at three in the morning.
= 他凌晨三点才回家，被父亲痛骂了一顿。
lay into = 痛打、痛骂
⑱ The company laid on a bus to take us from the station to the hotel.
= 公司安排了一辆大巴把我们从车站送到酒店。
lay on = （英）提供、安排
⑲ We laid over in Chicago for five hours on the way to Seattle.
= 去西雅图的路上，我们在芝加哥中途停留了五个小时。
lay over = （美）中途停留
⑳ She was laid up for a month with a broken leg.
= 她摔断了腿，卧床一个月。
be laid up = 卧床不起
lay up = 使卧床；储存、停用（车船）
㉑ He picked up a stick and laid about him as the dogs came closer.
= 那群狗逼近时，他抓起一根棍子四下乱打。
lay about you = 挥舞着乱打
lay about = 乱打、猛击
㉒ If you lay a finger on my sister, you will be sorry.
= 你要是敢碰我妹妹一下，会后悔的。
lay a finger on somebody = 碰某人一下
㉓ I knew she was the one from the moment I laid eyes on her.
= 从看见她的那一刻起，我就知道是她了。
lay eyes on = 看见
㉔ We stopped in a lay-by to look at the map.
= 我们在路边停车带停下来看地图。
a lay-by = （英）路边停车带
㉕ The new teacher is very laid-back and never raises her voice.
= 新来的老师很随和，从不提高嗓门。
laid-back = 悠闲的、随和的
㉖ The church is run by a priest and a group of lay people.
= 那座教堂由一位神父和一群平信徒管理。
lay people = 平信徒、非神职人员
这是另一个 lay：形容词。
㉗ The book explains the science in words that a lay reader can follow.
= 那本书用外行读者能懂的话解释这门科学。
a lay reader = 外行读者

layabout
核心：（英，口语，旧）游手好闲的人、懒汉。
① Her father called him a layabout who had never done a day's work.
= 她父亲说他是个游手好闲的家伙，一天活也没干过。
a layabout = （英，口语）懒汉
② A group of young layabouts hung around outside the shop all day.
= 一伙游手好闲的年轻人整天在那家店外面晃荡。
young layabouts = 游手好闲的年轻人
字面是「到处躺着的人」。

layaway
核心：（美）预付订金留货 —— 先付一部分钱，店家把货留着，付清再取。
① She put the winter coat on layaway and paid a little every week.
= 她付了订金把那件冬大衣留下，每周付一点。
on layaway = （美）预付订金留着
② The store offers layaway for toys in the months before Christmas.
= 那家店在圣诞节前的几个月提供玩具预付留货。
offer layaway = 提供预付留货
没有信用卡的年代很常见。

layer
核心：层 —— 一层东西盖在另一层上；（机构、体系里的）层级。动词：把…分层叠放。layer cake（夹层蛋糕）并入本条。
① A thin layer of dust covered the piano.
= 钢琴上蒙着薄薄一层灰。
a layer of dust = 一层灰
② Wear several layers of clothing so that you can take one off if you get hot.
= 多穿几层衣服，热了可以脱掉一件。
layers of clothing = 几层衣服
③ The plan would remove a whole layer of management.
= 那个方案将砍掉整整一层管理人员。
a layer of management = 一层管理人员
这一支是比喻：组织里的层级。
④ Layer the potatoes and onions in a dish and pour the cream over them.
= 把土豆和洋葱一层层码在盘里，浇上奶油。
layer something in a dish = 把…一层层码在盘里
⑤ She made a chocolate layer cake for his birthday.
= 她为他的生日做了一个巧克力夹层蛋糕。
a layer cake = 夹层蛋糕
⑥ The best layers in the flock give an egg almost every day.
= 鸡群里最能下蛋的几乎天天下一个。
a layer = 下蛋的鸡
来自 lay（下蛋）。

layette
核心：新生儿的全套用品 —— 衣服、小被子等。旧式说法。
① Her mother knitted a complete layette for the new baby.
= 她母亲给新生儿织了全套衣物。
a layette = 新生儿全套用品
② The shop sells layettes in white, yellow and pale green.
= 那家店卖白色、黄色和淡绿色的新生儿套装。
sell layettes = 卖新生儿套装
法语借词。

layman
核心：外行、门外汉；平信徒。复数 laymen。今天多用不分男女的 layperson。
① The doctor explained the operation in layman's terms.
= 医生用通俗的话解释了那个手术。
in layman's terms = 用通俗的话
最常见的用法。
② To the layman, the two paintings look exactly the same.
= 在外行看来，这两幅画一模一样。
to the layman = 在外行看来
③ The sermon was given by a layman because the priest was ill.
= 神父病了，那次布道是由一位平信徒讲的。
a layman = 平信徒
这一支是本义：不是神职人员的信徒。

layout
核心：布局、版面设计 —— 房屋、城市、页面各部分的安排方式。
① The layout of the flat is simple, with the bedrooms on one side.
= 那套公寓的布局很简单，卧室都在一侧。
the layout of a flat = 公寓的布局
② The magazine has changed its page layout to make room for more pictures.
= 那本杂志改了版面，好多放些图片。
page layout = 版面设计
来自 lay out（摆开、布置）。
