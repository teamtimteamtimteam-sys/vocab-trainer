landward

mind your language = 注意言辞
⑧ The two leaders do not speak the same language on trade.

speak the same language = 有共同语言

⑩ Deaf children at the school are taught in sign language.

lapwing
核心：凤头麦鸡 —— 黑白相间、头上有长冠羽的鸟，栖于田野，叫声尖细。也叫 peewit。
① A flock of lapwings rose from the ploughed field as the tractor came near.
= 拖拉机开近时，一群凤头麦鸡从犁过的田里飞了起来。
a flock of lapwings = 一群凤头麦鸡
飞起来翅膀一扑一扑，很慢。
② The lapwing lays its eggs on the bare ground in spring.
= 凤头麦鸡春天把蛋下在光秃秃的地上。
the lapwing = 凤头麦鸡
