shouty
/ˈʃaʊti/
核心：形容词（英式口语）：爱大声嚷嚷的、吵吵嚷嚷的；（文字）全用大写的、咄咄逼人的。
① Our new football coach is very shouty, but the players like him because he is fair.
= 我们的新足球教练很爱大声嚷嚷，不过球员们喜欢他，因为他公平。
shouty = 爱嚷嚷的
② Writing a whole email to your boss in capital letters looks shouty and rude.
= 给老板写邮件全用大写字母，看起来咄咄逼人、很不礼貌。
look shouty = 显得咄咄逼人

shove
/ʃʌv/
核心：动词：（粗鲁地）推、搡：shove somebody out of the way；（口语）随手放、乱塞：shove something in a bag。名词，可数：猛推：give somebody a shove。
① The man shoved me out of the way and jumped onto the bus before me.
= 那个男人把我推到一边，抢在我前面跳上了公交车。
shove somebody = 推搡某人
shove = 推
② Just shove your coat on the bed; we'll hang it up later.
= 把你的外套随手放床上吧，我们待会儿再挂。
shove something somewhere = 把某物随手放某处
③ The car wouldn't start, so we gave it a shove down the hill.
= 车发动不了，我们就推了它一把，让它顺坡滑下去。
give something a shove = 推某物一把
shove 比 push 更粗鲁、更用力。
④ He told the boys who were bothering him to shove off and leave him alone.
= 他叫那几个烦他的男孩滚开，别来打扰他。
shove off = （口语）走开、滚开
⑤ Shove up a bit so that your sister can sit down too.
= 往那边挪一挪，让你妹妹也能坐下。
shove up = （英式口语）挪一挪、腾地方
⑥ The old men in the pub play shove-halfpenny on a smooth wooden board every Friday.
= 酒吧里的老人们每个星期五都在一块光滑的木板上玩推硬币游戏。
shove-halfpenny = 推币游戏（英国酒吧游戏）

show
/ʃəʊ/
核心：动词（showed, shown 或 showed）：给…看、出示：show somebody something；表明、显示、证明：show that…；带领、引领：show somebody to their seat；演示、教：show somebody how to do something；露出、显露（感情、品质）：show your feelings；（电影、节目）上演、播放；看得出来、显现：the stain doesn't show。名词，可数：演出、表演、节目：a TV show；展览、展会：a flower show；（感情的）表现、显示：a show of strength；（单数）装样子、假象：for show。习语：steal the show 是抢尽风头；on show 是在展出；show your face 是露面；the show must go on 是演出必须继续、事情得照常进行。
① We went to see a show in London and had dinner afterwards.
= 我们去伦敦看了一场演出，之后吃了晚饭。
show = 演出
② When you have a minute, can you show me the photos from your holiday in Greece?
= 你有空的时候，能给我看看你在希腊度假的照片吗？
show somebody something = 给某人看某物
也可说 show something to somebody。
③ The results show that the new medicine works better than the old one.
= 结果表明，新药比旧药效果更好。
show that = 表明
主语常是 results、research、figures 这类表示证据的词。
④ When we arrived at the restaurant, the waiter showed us to a table by the window.
= 我们到了餐厅，服务员把我们领到靠窗的一张桌子。
show somebody to something = 把某人领到某处
⑤ My grandmother showed me how to make bread when I was ten.
= 我十岁时，奶奶教我怎么做面包。
show somebody how to do something = 教某人怎么做某事
⑥ He was very upset about losing his job, but he tried not to show it at dinner.
= 他丢了工作非常难过，但晚饭时尽量不表露出来。
show your feelings = 表露感情
⑦ The little girl's dog stole the show at the school concert by barking along to the music.
= 在学校音乐会上，那个小女孩的狗跟着音乐汪汪叫，抢尽了风头。
steal the show = 抢尽风头
⑧ The new paintings are on show at the town museum until the end of June.
= 那些新画作在镇博物馆展出到六月底。
on show = 在展出
⑨ The big car in his drive is just for show; he never takes it anywhere.
= 他车道上那辆大车只是摆样子的，他从来不开出去。
for show = 装门面
⑩ After what he did at the party, I'm surprised he dared to show his face here again.
= 他在派对上干了那种事，我很惊讶他还敢再来这里露面。
show your face = 露面
⑪ The lead actor was ill, but the show must go on, so his understudy played the part.
= 主演病了，但演出必须继续，于是由替补演员上场。
the show must go on = 演出必须继续
⑫ We waited outside the cinema for an hour, but he never showed up.
= 我们在电影院外面等了一个小时，可他始终没露面。
show up = 露面
口语，多指迟到或爽约的场合；正式说法用 arrive、appear。
⑬ The little boy kept jumping off the wall to show off in front of the girls.
= 那个小男孩为了在女孩们面前炫耀，一次次从墙上跳下来。
show off = 炫耀、卖弄
带贬义，多指在别人面前故意表现自己。
⑭ Let me show you around the new office before the meeting starts.

show around = 带（某人）参观（常拆开用：show somebody around）
⑮ The estate agent showed us round three flats near the station in one afternoon.

⑯ The new paint on the kitchen wall was so thin that the old colour showed through.

showy
/ˈʃəʊi/
核心：形容词：花哨的、艳丽的、炫耀的：showy flowers、a showy car。副词 showily 是花哨地。名词 showiness 另立条。
① The garden is full of showy red and orange flowers that bloom all summer.
= 花园里满是艳丽的红花和橙花，整个夏天都在开。
showy flowers = 艳丽的花
showy = 艳丽的
② He bought a big showy car to impress his neighbours, but he could not afford the petrol.
= 他买了辆很招摇的大车想让邻居们刮目相看，可汽油钱都付不起。
a showy car = 招摇的车
③ At her cousin's wedding, she was showily dressed in gold and silver from head to foot.
= 在她表姐的婚礼上，她从头到脚一身金银，打扮得很花哨。
showily = 花哨地
