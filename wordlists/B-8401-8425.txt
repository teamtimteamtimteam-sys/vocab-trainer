extinguisher
核心：灭火器：fire extinguisher 的常用简说，就是墙上挂的那种红罐子。可数。
① The extinguisher by the door was last checked in a year nobody can now read.
= 门边那只灭火器上次检查是哪一年，如今谁也认不出来。
a fire extinguisher = 灭火器
② He emptied one extinguisher into the engine bay and reached for another.
= 他把一只灭火器全喷进发动机舱，又去够第二只。
empty an extinguisher = 把灭火器喷光
构词：extinguish 加 -er

extirpate
核心：（正式）根除、连根铲掉：多说习俗、疾病、物种。比 eradicate 更书面。
名词 extirpation。
① The campaign set out to extirpate the disease and settled for controlling it.
= 那场运动本想根除那种病，最后只做到把它控制住。
extirpate a disease = 根除疾病
② A practice that took four centuries to grow cannot be extirpated by a circular.
= 长了四百年的做法，一纸通知铲不掉。
extirpate something = 根除某物

extirpation
核心：（正式）根除、铲除：连根拔掉这件事；生态学里专指某物种在一地绝迹。不可数。
① The extirpation of the wolf from the valley is dated to a single winter.
= 狼在那条山谷里绝迹，被断定发生在某一个冬天。
the extirpation of something = 对某物的根除
② Extirpation in one county does not mean extinction everywhere.
= 在一个郡绝迹，不等于处处都灭绝了。
extirpation = 局部绝迹
构词：extirpate 加 -ion

extol
核心：（正式）颂扬、盛赞：常说 extol the virtues of something，
多半带一点「夸得过了头」的味道。变形双写 l：extolled、extolling。
① The brochure extols the virtues of a beach that is mostly shingle.
= 那本小册子把一片多半是碎石的海滩夸得天花乱坠。
extol the virtues of something = 盛赞某物的好处
② He extolled the new system for a term and then quietly went back to paper.
= 他把新系统夸了一个学期，随后悄悄用回了纸。
extol something = 颂扬某物

extort
核心：（法律）敲诈、逼取：靠威胁把钱财或口供弄到手，
搭配 extort something from somebody。名词 extortion。
① Two men extorted money from shopkeepers who were too frightened to complain.
= 两个人向店主敲诈钱财，店主吓得不敢报案。
extort money from somebody = 向某人敲诈钱财
② A confession extorted under threat is worth nothing in court.
= 威胁之下逼出来的口供，在法庭上一文不值。
extort a confession = 逼取口供

extortion
核心：（法律）敲诈、勒索：用威胁手段索取钱财这项罪名，不可数；
日常也拿来夸张地说要价太狠。
① He was charged with extortion and released on a bail nobody expected.
= 他被控敲诈，后来以谁都没料到的条件获保释。
be charged with extortion = 被控敲诈
② Fifteen pounds for a taxi to the next street is plain extortion.
= 到隔壁街要十五镑车钱，简直是敲竹杠。
extortion = 敲诈、勒索

extortionate
核心：（价格、利率）高得像敲诈的：贵得没道理，比 expensive 重得多。
副词 extortionately。
① The interest is extortionate and the paperwork takes nine minutes to sign.
= 那利息高得像敲诈，签字手续九分钟就办完。
an extortionate rate of interest = 高得离谱的利率
② Extortionate prices at the station keep the little shop on the corner alive.
= 车站里那些吓人的价钱，养活了拐角上那家小店。
extortionate prices = 高得像敲诈的价格

extortionately
核心：贵得像敲诈地：几乎只修饰 priced 与 expensive。
① The station café is extortionately priced and full from six in the morning.
= 车站咖啡馆定价高得像敲诈，早上六点起就坐满了。
be extortionately priced = 定价高得像敲诈
② Parking by the pier is extortionately expensive and the machine takes only coins.
= 码头边停车贵得吓人，收费机还只收硬币。
extortionately expensive = 贵得吓人
构词：extortionate 加 -ly

extra
核心：四支。一是形容词，额外的：an extra day、at no extra charge；
二是副词，格外地：extra careful；三是名词，附加项目、额外收费，多用复数 extras；
四是名词，电影里的群众演员。
① They stayed an extra day and paid for a week because of the rate.
= 他们多住了一天，按那个费率却付了一星期的钱。
an extra day = 多出的一天
at no extra charge = 不另外收费
② The floor was extra slippery where the rain had come in under the door.
= 雨水从门底下渗进来，那一块地板格外滑。
extra careful = 格外小心
③ The price looks fair until you add up the extras.
= 那价钱看着公道，把附加项目加起来就不是了。
the extras = 附加项目
④ She worked as an extra for two summers and appears in one crowd scene.
= 她当了两个夏天的群众演员，只在一个人群镜头里露过面。
work as an extra = 当群众演员
⑤ The match went to extra time and the winning goal came in the last minute of it.
= 那场比赛打到加时，制胜球是加时最后一分钟进的。
extra time = 加时赛
⑥ Extra virgin olive oil goes on the salad and the cheap stuff goes in the pan.
= 特级初榨橄榄油浇沙拉，便宜的那种下锅。
extra virgin = 特级初榨的

extracorporeal
核心：（医学）体外的：让血液或循环在身体外面走一圈的那类技术，
最常见的说法是 extracorporeal circulation。
① Extracorporeal circulation keeps the patient alive while the heart is stopped.
= 体外循环在心脏停跳期间维持病人的生命。
extracorporeal circulation = 体外循环
② The unit has two extracorporeal machines and enough staff for one.
= 那个病区有两台体外循环机，人手只够开一台。
an extracorporeal machine = 体外循环机

extract
核心：四支，动词重音在后、名词在前。动词一是拔出、套出：extract a tooth、
extract information from somebody；二是提炼：extract oil from shale。
名词一是摘录、选段：an extract from a diary；二是提取物：vanilla extract。
名词还有 extraction 与 extractor。
① The dentist extracted two teeth and told him to come back in a fortnight.
= 牙医拔了两颗牙，叫他两星期后再来。
extract a tooth = 拔牙
② Nobody could extract a straight answer from that office in six weeks.
= 六个星期里，谁也没从那间办公室套出一句准话。
extract information from somebody = 从某人嘴里套出消息
③ The programme read an extract from the diary and left out the date.
= 节目里念了日记的一段，把日期略去了。
an extract from something = 某书的一段摘录
④ A teaspoon of vanilla extract costs more than the flour in the whole cake.
= 一小勺香草精，比整个蛋糕的面粉还贵。
vanilla extract = 香草精

extraction
核心：三支。一是拔牙、抽出：a tooth extraction；二是（矿物、油气的）开采、提炼；
三是（正式）血统、出身：of Irish extraction。
① The extraction took ten minutes and the swelling took nine days.
= 拔那颗牙用了十分钟，肿消下去用了九天。
a tooth extraction = 拔牙
② The extraction of oil from the shale needs more water than the town has.
= 从页岩里提炼石油，用的水比全镇的还多。
the extraction of oil = 石油的开采
③ She is a Londoner of Irish extraction and has never been to Cork.
= 她是伦敦人，爱尔兰血统，却没去过科克。
of Irish extraction = 爱尔兰血统的

extractive
核心：（正式、经济）采掘的：说采矿、油气这类把地下资源挖出来卖的行当 ——
the extractive industries。
① The extractive industries pay well for ten years and leave a hole behind.
= 采掘业能给十年好薪水，留下的是一个坑。
the extractive industries = 采掘业
② An extractive economy rises and falls with a price set somewhere else.
= 采掘型经济的起落，取决于别处定下的价格。
an extractive economy = 采掘型经济

extractor
核心：两支。一是抽风机、排气扇：an extractor fan，厨房里抽油烟的那个；
二是榨汁机一类把东西挤出来的器具。可数。
① The extractor fan runs all evening and the kitchen still smells of fish.
= 抽风机开了一整晚，厨房还是一股鱼味。
an extractor fan = 抽风机、排气扇
② A juice extractor takes four apples to fill one small glass.
= 榨汁机要用四个苹果，才榨满一小杯。
a juice extractor = 榨汁机
构词：extract 加 -or

extracurricular
核心：课外的：正课之外的活动，最常见的说法是 extracurricular activities；
口语里也拿来打趣，指本职或婚姻之外的事。
① Extracurricular activities fill four afternoons and the timetable calls them optional.
= 课外活动占掉四个下午，课表上却写着自选。
extracurricular activities = 课外活动
② His extracurricular reading is wider than anything on the syllabus.
= 他课外读的书，比大纲上的宽得多。
extracurricular = 课外的

extradite
核心：（法律）引渡：把嫌疑人从一国交给另一国受审，
搭配 extradite somebody to somewhere，多用被动。名词 extradition。
① He was extradited to face two charges and was acquitted of both.
= 他被引渡去应对两项指控，两项都判了无罪。
be extradited to somewhere = 被引渡到某国
② The treaty makes it possible to extradite suspects in either direction.
= 那份条约让两边都可以引渡嫌疑人。
extradite somebody = 引渡某人

extradition
核心：（法律）引渡：把人交给外国受审这件事，
常说 an extradition treaty、extradition proceedings。可数不可数都行。
① The extradition treaty was signed in 1974 and has been used eleven times since.
= 那份引渡条约 1974 年签订，此后用过十一次。
an extradition treaty = 引渡条约
② Extradition proceedings can run longer than the sentence at the end of them.
= 引渡程序拖起来，可能比最后判下的刑期还长。
extradition proceedings = 引渡程序

extrajudicial
核心：（正式、法律）法外的、不经法庭的：最重的说法是 extrajudicial killings，
指未经审判的处决。
① The report counts extrajudicial killings and names none of the units involved.
= 那份报告统计了法外处决的数目，却没点任何一支部队的名。
extrajudicial killings = 法外处决
② An extrajudicial settlement saves time and sets no precedent at all.
= 庭外了结省时间，却立不下任何先例。
extrajudicial = 不经法庭的

extramarital
核心：婚外的：几乎只出现在 an extramarital affair 这个说法里。
① An extramarital affair cost him the seat and not the marriage.
= 一桩婚外情让他丢了席位，婚倒没离。
an extramarital affair = 婚外情
② The novel treats extramarital love as a matter of timetables and taxis.
= 那部小说把婚外之情写成了时刻表和出租车的事。
extramarital = 婚外的

extramural
核心：两支。一是（英式，大学）校外的、面向社会开的：extramural classes 是进修课；
二是字面义，城墙之外的。
① The extramural classes fill up before the enrolled students have chosen theirs.
= 校外进修课报满的时候，在校生还没选上课。
extramural classes = 校外进修课
② An extramural cemetery lies where the old wall used to run.
= 老城墙原先的位置上，是一片城外墓地。
extramural = 城外的

extraneous
核心：（正式）无关的、外来的：多说材料、细节、声音跟正题不相干。
① The report is twenty pages of argument and forty of extraneous detail.
= 那份报告二十页讲道理，四十页是不相干的细节。
extraneous detail = 无关的细节
② Extraneous noise on the tape makes the whole second half unusable.
= 磁带上混进的杂音，让后半段整个没法用。
extraneous noise = 外来的杂音

extranet
核心：（计算机）外联网：把内部网络的一部分开放给客户或供应商用的网络。可数。
① Suppliers see the stock levels on the extranet and nothing else at all.
= 供应商在外联网上只看得到库存，别的一概看不到。
an extranet = 外联网
② The extranet went down on the very day the orders were due.
= 偏偏在交单那天，外联网瘫了。
on the extranet = 在外联网上

extraordinaire
核心：（来自法语，常带调侃）出类拔萃的：放在名词后面 ——
a chef extraordinaire、a fixer extraordinaire。
① The programme bills him as a gardener extraordinaire and shows two window boxes.
= 节目把他捧成园艺高手，镜头里只有两个窗台花箱。
a chef extraordinaire = 厨艺高手
② She is the department's problem-solver extraordinaire and still answers her own phone.
= 她是部里首屈一指的救火队员，电话还是自己接。
extraordinaire = 出类拔萃的

extraordinarily
核心：非常地、异乎寻常地：既当纯粹的强调词，也说事情做得罕见地好或怪。
① The hall was extraordinarily quiet for a room holding four hundred people.
= 一间坐着四百人的厅，安静得出奇。
extraordinarily quiet = 出奇地安静
② She writes extraordinarily well about weather and rather badly about people.
= 她写天气写得极好，写人就差些。
extraordinarily well = 极其出色地
构词：extraordinary 加 -ly

extraordinary
核心：四支。一是非凡的、了不起的：an extraordinary achievement；
二是离谱的、不寻常的，褒贬都能用；三是（正式）临时的、特别的：
an extraordinary general meeting；四是固定说法 extraordinary rendition。
副词 extraordinarily。
① It is an extraordinary achievement for a club with one full-time employee.
= 对一个只有一名全职员工的俱乐部来说，这是了不起的成就。
an extraordinary achievement = 非凡的成就
② The decision was extraordinary and the reasons for it fit on a postcard.
= 那个决定离谱得很，理由却一张明信片就写得下。
extraordinary = 离谱的、不寻常的
③ An extraordinary general meeting was called for the Tuesday after the accounts appeared.
= 账目公布之后的那个周二，召开了临时大会。
an extraordinary general meeting = 临时大会
④ Extraordinary rendition moved prisoners between countries without a court ever sitting.
= 非常规引渡把囚犯在国与国之间转移，法庭一次也没开过。
extraordinary rendition = 非常规引渡
