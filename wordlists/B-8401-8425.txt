extravagance
核心：两支。一是奢侈、铺张（不可数）：花钱没个节制；
二是可数，一件奢侈的东西、一笔铺张的开销。
① The extravagance of the opening night was still being discussed in March.
= 首演之夜有多铺张，到三月还有人在议论。
the extravagance of something = 某事的铺张
② A second oven is her one extravagance and it earns its keep at Christmas.
= 第二只烤箱是她唯一的奢侈，到圣诞节就把本钱挣回来了。
an extravagance = 一件奢侈的东西

extravagant
核心：三支。一是（人）挥霍无度的；二是（东西）奢华昂贵的；
三是（说法、要求）夸大过分的：extravagant claims。副词 extravagantly。
① He is extravagant with other people's money and careful with his own.
= 他花别人的钱大手大脚，花自己的钱很仔细。
be extravagant with money = 花钱大手大脚
② The label makes extravagant claims and the small print takes them back.
= 那标签上说得天花乱坠，小字又把话收了回去。
extravagant claims = 夸大的说法
③ An extravagant bunch of flowers arrived with no card attached.
= 一大束奢华的花送到了，连张卡片都没附。
extravagant = 奢华的

extravagantly
核心：两支。一是挥霍地、奢华地：spend extravagantly；
二是夸张地：praise something extravagantly。
① They spent extravagantly for one summer and carefully for the next ten years.
= 他们奢侈了一个夏天，随后十年过得都很仔细。
spend extravagantly = 挥霍无度
② The review praises the first act extravagantly and skips the second altogether.
= 那篇评论把第一幕夸上了天，第二幕干脆不提。
praise something extravagantly = 极尽夸张地称赞
构词：extravagant 加 -ly

extravaganza
核心：盛大铺张的演出或庆典：又长又华丽的那种场面，常说 a musical extravaganza。可数。
① The opening was a two-hour extravaganza with fireworks and one broken lift.
= 开幕式是两小时的盛大演出，有烟火，还有一部坏掉的电梯。
a musical extravaganza = 盛大的音乐演出
② The village turns its harvest supper into an extravaganza every tenth year.
= 那个村子每逢十年，把丰收晚餐办成一场盛典。
an extravaganza = 盛大铺张的演出

extraversion
核心：（心理学，较旧的拼法）外向性：性格朝外、爱与人打交道的那一面，
量表上跟内向相对。如今多写 extroversion。不可数。
① The questionnaire scores extraversion on a scale that runs from nought to sixty.
= 那份问卷用一张从零到六十的量表给外向性打分。
extraversion = 外向性（量表用语）
② High extraversion predicts who speaks first and not who gets listened to.
= 外向分高，能预测谁先开口，预测不了谁被听进去。
high extraversion = 高外向性

extravert
核心：（心理学，较旧的拼法）外向的人：跟人相处能长精神的那类人，
也作形容词。日常与新近的文献都写 extrovert。可数。
① The manual sorts the whole cohort into extraverts and introverts in one afternoon.
= 那本手册用一个下午，就把整批人分成外向型和内向型。
an extravert = 外向的人
② An extravert score on the test says nothing about how kind the person is.
= 测验上的外向得分，说明不了这人善不善良。
an extravert score = 外向型的得分

extreme
核心：四支。一是极度的：extreme heat、extreme caution；二是（观点、做法）过激的；
三是名词，极端、两头：go from one extreme to the other；
四是一组习语：in the extreme 是极其，go to extremes 与 take something to extremes
是走极端。副词 extremely。
① Extreme heat closed the two schools that have no shade in the yard.
= 酷热让那两所院子里没有树荫的学校停了课。
extreme heat = 酷热
extreme caution = 极度谨慎
② His views are extreme and his manners are impeccable.
= 他的观点很极端，礼数却挑不出毛病。
extreme views = 过激的观点
③ The weather here goes from one extreme to the other in a fortnight.
= 这里的天气两个星期就能从一个极端荡到另一个极端。
go from one extreme to the other = 从一个极端到另一个极端
④ The plan is cautious in the extreme and still frightened half the board.
= 那份方案谨慎到了极点，还是吓着了一半董事。
in the extreme = 极其、到了极点
⑤ Parents who go to extremes over homework raise children who hide it.
= 为作业走极端的家长，养出的是把作业藏起来的孩子。
go to extremes = 走极端
take something to extremes = 把某事做过了头
⑥ Extreme sports insurance costs more than the trip that makes you need it.
= 极限运动的保险，比那趟让你用得上它的旅行还贵。
extreme sports = 极限运动
⑦ Extreme fighting draws bigger crowds than the boxing it grew out of.
= 极限格斗的观众，比孕育它的拳击还多。
extreme fighting = 极限格斗
⑧ A priest gave extreme unction at four and the family arrived at five.
= 神父四点行了临终涂油礼，家属五点才赶到。
extreme unction = 临终涂油礼
⑨ Extreme vetting adds nine months to an application that used to take three weeks.
= 极端审查让原本三周办完的申请多拖九个月。
extreme vetting = 极端审查

extremely
核心：极其、非常：最常用的强调副词之一，修饰形容词与副词，
不能修饰动词（没有 extremely likes 这种说法）。
① The room is extremely cold and the meter takes coins that nobody carries.
= 那房间冷得厉害，电表还只收没人随身带的硬币。
extremely cold = 极冷
② She was extremely careful with the wording and careless with the date.
= 她在措辞上极其小心，日期倒写错了。
extremely careful = 极其小心
构词：extreme 加 -ly

extremism
核心：（贬）极端主义：政治或宗教上走极端、不容异见的那一套。不可数。
① The report traces extremism to three websites and one very ordinary hall.
= 那份报告把极端主义追到三个网站，外加一间再普通不过的礼堂。
political extremism = 政治极端主义
② Extremism grows fastest where nobody has argued back for years.
= 多年没人反驳的地方，极端主义长得最快。
extremism = 极端主义
构词：extreme 加 -ism

extremist
核心：（贬）极端分子；也作形容词：extremist groups、extremist views。可数。
① The group was small, extremist, and better funded than anyone had realised.
= 那个团体人不多，立场极端，资金比谁想的都足。
an extremist group = 极端组织
② Extremists on both sides quote the same paragraph for opposite ends.
= 两边的极端分子引用同一段话，各为相反的目的。
an extremist = 极端分子

extremity
核心：三支。一是（正式）末端、尽头：the northern extremity of the island；
二是（正式）极度、危难：in extremity；三是复数 extremities，指手脚四肢。
① A lighthouse stands at the northern extremity of the island and is not manned.
= 岛的最北端立着一座灯塔，没有人值守。
the extremity of something = 某地的尽头
② In extremity people ration the wrong things first.
= 到了危难关头，人先省的往往是不该省的。
in extremity = 在危难之中
③ Cold reaches the extremities first and the warning signs are easy to miss.
= 寒冷先侵到四肢末端，那些警讯很容易被忽略。
the extremities = 四肢末端

extricate
核心：（正式）使脱身、救出来：从困境或缠住的东西里弄出来，
搭配 extricate somebody from something，也常说 extricate yourself。
① It took an hour to extricate the driver from a van that had folded in half.
= 把司机从对折的货车里救出来，花了一个钟头。
extricate somebody from something = 把某人从某处救出来
② She extricated herself from the committee without ever saying why.
= 她从那个委员会脱了身，始终没说为什么。
extricate yourself from something = 从某处抽身

extrinsic
核心：（正式）外在的、非固有的：动机或价值来自外部，
跟内在的 intrinsic 相对，最常见的说法是 extrinsic motivation。
① Extrinsic motivation works while the prize lasts and stops the day it does.
= 外在动机在奖品还在时管用，奖品一没就停。
extrinsic motivation = 外在动机
② The value here is extrinsic and disappears the moment the frame comes off.
= 这东西的价值是外加的，框子一摘就没了。
extrinsic = 外在的

extroversion
核心：（心理学）外向：喜欢跟人来往、从热闹里补充精神的那种性格倾向。
不可数；较旧的拼法是 extraversion。
① Extroversion is measured with much the same questions in nearly every study.
= 差不多每项研究测外向，用的都是那几道题。
extroversion = 外向
② Extroversion is not the same thing as confidence, and the two get confused.
= 外向不等于自信，两者常被混为一谈。
extroversion and introversion = 外向与内向

extrovert
核心：外向的人：跟人待在一起就来精神的那类人；也作形容词，
如 an extrovert manner。可数，跟 introvert 相对。形容词还有 extroverted。
① The extrovert in the office organises everything and remembers no names.
= 办公室里那个外向的人张罗一切，却一个名字也记不住。
an extrovert = 外向的人
② An extrovert manner can carry an interview and cannot carry the job.
= 外向的做派能撑过面试，撑不了这份工作。
an extrovert manner = 外向的做派

extroverted
核心：外向的、爱交际的：说人的性格，作定语或表语都行，
比 extrovert 当形容词用更常见。
① He is extroverted with strangers and almost silent at home.
= 他跟生人很外向，在家几乎不出声。
be extroverted = 性格外向
② An extroverted class needs a different lesson plan and twice the space.
= 一个活跃外向的班，教案得换，地方得加倍。
extroverted = 外向的

extrude
核心：（技术）挤出、挤压成型：把材料从模孔里挤出来，做成管、条、型材。
名词 extrusion。
① The machine extrudes plastic tubing faster than anyone downstream can keep up.
= 那台机器挤出塑料管的速度，下游谁也跟不上。
extrude plastic = 挤压成型塑料
② Aluminium is extruded into window frames and cut to length on site.
= 铝被挤压成窗框型材，到现场再按长度裁。
be extruded into something = 被挤压成某形状

extrusion
核心：两支。一是（技术）挤压成型，也指挤出来的型材：aluminium extrusions；
二是（地质）岩浆的喷出与喷出物。
① Aluminium extrusions arrive in six-metre lengths and are cut to fit.
= 铝型材按六米一根送来，到场再裁着用。
an aluminium extrusion = 铝挤压型材
② The extrusion of lava reshaped the coastline over a single summer.
= 熔岩喷出，一个夏天就把海岸线改了模样。
the extrusion of lava = 熔岩的喷出
构词：extrude 加 -sion

extrusive
核心：（地质）喷出的：岩浆涌到地表冷却成的岩石叫 extrusive rock，
跟在地下慢慢冷却的侵入岩相对。
① Extrusive rock cools fast and the crystals never get a chance to grow.
= 喷出岩冷得快，晶体根本没机会长大。
extrusive rock = 喷出岩
② The cliff is extrusive at the top and something much older underneath.
= 那面崖顶上是喷出岩，底下的东西年代老得多。
extrusive = 喷出的

exuberance
核心：（褒）热情洋溢、劲头十足：人兴致高、话多、动作大；
也说草木长得旺。不可数。
① The exuberance of the first week gives way to a timetable by October.
= 头一周那股兴头，到十月就让位给时间表了。
the exuberance of something = 某段时期的热劲
② Youthful exuberance broke two chairs and mended nothing at all.
= 年轻人那股热劲弄坏了两把椅子，什么也没修好。
youthful exuberance = 年轻人的热劲

exuberant
核心：（褒）兴高采烈的、热情洋溢的：说人劲头足；也说草木茂盛、
风格繁复热闹。副词 exuberantly。
① The crowd was exuberant at half-time and very quiet on the way home.
= 中场休息时人群兴高采烈，回家路上安静得很。
an exuberant crowd = 兴高采烈的人群
② The border is exuberant by June and needs cutting back twice a year.
= 那条花境到六月长得极旺，一年得剪两回。
exuberant growth = 茂盛的长势

exuberantly
核心：兴高采烈地、热情洋溢地：多修饰 greeted、waved、danced，也说草木疯长。
① She greeted the returning crew exuberantly and only then asked about the boat.
= 她兴高采烈地迎接归来的船员，之后才问船怎么样。
greet somebody exuberantly = 热情洋溢地迎接某人
② The ivy grows exuberantly over a wall that was pointed only last spring.
= 常春藤在去年春天才勾过缝的墙上疯长。
grow exuberantly = 长得极旺
构词：exuberant 加 -ly

exude
核心：两支。一是（正式）散发、流露出某种气质：exude confidence；
二是渗出、分泌液体或气味。
① He exudes confidence in the corridor and checks the figures twice at his desk.
= 他在走廊里一身自信，回到桌前把数字核两遍。
exude confidence = 流露出自信
② The bark exudes a resin that sticks to everything within reach.
= 那树皮渗出一种树脂，够得着的东西全被粘上。
exude a resin = 渗出树脂

exult
核心：（正式）欢欣鼓舞、狂喜：搭配 exult in something 或 exult at something，
有时带一点得了便宜还张扬的意味。
① The winners exulted in a result that the referee is still explaining.
= 赢的一方为那个结果欢欣鼓舞，裁判至今还在解释。
exult in something = 为某事欢欣鼓舞
② She exulted at the news for a day and told almost nobody.
= 她为那消息高兴了一整天，却几乎没告诉任何人。
exult at something = 为某事狂喜

exultant
核心：（正式）欢欣鼓舞的、得意扬扬的：多修饰 crowd、shout、mood、tone。
副词 exultantly。
① An exultant crowd filled the square and left it spotless by morning.
= 欢腾的人群挤满广场，到早上地面干干净净。
an exultant crowd = 欢腾的人群
② His exultant tone did not survive the first question from the floor.
= 他那副得意的口气，没撑过台下的第一个问题。
exultant = 欢欣鼓舞的
