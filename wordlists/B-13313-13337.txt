latter
/ˈlætə(r)/
核心：the latter —— （刚提到的两者中的）后者；（形容词）后面的、后半的。与 the former（前者）相对。
副词 latterly（正式：近来；后期）与形容词 latter-day（当今的、现代版的）并入本条。
① He offered tea or coffee, and I chose the latter.
= 他问我要茶还是咖啡，我选了后者。
the latter = 后者
只能在两者之间用。
② She spent the latter part of her life in a cottage by the sea.
= 她的后半生是在海边的一座小屋里度过的。
the latter part of something = …的后半段
③ Sales fell in the latter half of the year.
= 下半年销售额下降了。
the latter half = 后一半
④ Latterly, his paintings have become darker and more serious.
= 近来，他的画变得更阴沉、更严肃了。
latterly = （正式）近来
⑤ The newspapers called him a latter-day Robin Hood who took from the rich.
= 报纸把他称作劫富济贫的当代罗宾汉。
a latter-day Robin Hood = 当代的罗宾汉
latter-day + 历史人物：今天的某某。

⑧ Your uncle is a real laugh once he starts telling stories.

⑩ They said her shop would fail, but she had the last laugh.

⑭ It is a laugh-out-loud comedy that kept the whole cinema shaking.
= 那是一部让人笑出声的喜剧，整个影院都笑得前仰后合。
laugh-out-loud = 让人笑出声的

⑮ The photograph shows three laughing children on a beach.
= 照片上是海滩上三个笑着的孩子。
laughing children = 笑着的孩子
