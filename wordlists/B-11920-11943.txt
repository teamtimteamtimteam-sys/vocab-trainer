incidentally
核心：两支。一是「顺便说一句」，放句首引出新话题，这是最常用的，incidental 没有这个用法，所以单立；
二是附带地、偶然地（incidentally mentioned）。
① Incidentally, have you seen my keys anywhere?
= 顺便问一句，你在哪儿看到过我的钥匙吗？
incidentally = 顺便说一句
口语里常用 by the way 代替。
② The problem was only mentioned incidentally at the end of the report.
= 那个问题只在报告结尾顺带提了一句。
be mentioned incidentally = 被顺带提及

incinerate
核心：焚化、烧成灰 —— 多说垃圾、医疗废物。名词 incineration 与焚化炉 incinerator 并入本条。
① Hospital waste must be incinerated at very high temperatures.
= 医院废物必须在极高温度下焚化。
incinerate waste = 焚化废物
② Campaigners oppose incineration because of the smoke it produces.
= 活动人士反对焚烧处理，因为会产生烟尘。
incineration = 焚烧处理
③ The town plans to build a new incinerator next to the river.
= 那个镇打算在河边新建一座焚化炉。
an incinerator = 焚化炉

incipient
核心：（正式）初期的、刚开始显露的 —— 多说坏事的苗头：incipient baldness、an incipient crisis。
① He was worried about his incipient baldness and bought three different shampoos.
= 他担心自己开始秃顶，买了三种洗发水。
incipient baldness = 初期秃顶
② The central bank acted quickly to stop the incipient crisis.
= 央行迅速出手，遏制了刚冒头的危机。
an incipient crisis = 初露端倪的危机

incise
核心：（正式）刻；切开 —— 在表面刻出图案，或（外科）切开。名词 incision（切口）并入本条。
① A pattern of leaves had been incised into the stone.
= 石头上刻着树叶的图案。
be incised into something = 刻在…上
② The surgeon made a small incision in the patient's side.
= 外科医生在病人侧腹切了一个小切口。
make an incision = 切开一个切口

incisive
核心：敏锐的、一针见血的 —— 说头脑、评论、分析切中要害。本义「能切进去的」。
副词 incisively 与名词 incisiveness 并入本条。
① Her incisive questions quickly exposed the weakness in his argument.
= 她几个一针见血的问题，很快就揭露了他论点的弱点。
incisive questions = 一针见血的问题
② He analysed the problem incisively and proposed three simple solutions.
= 他敏锐地分析了问题，提出了三个简单的解决办法。
incisively = 敏锐地
③ Readers admired the incisiveness of her columns.
= 读者们欣赏她专栏文章的犀利。
incisiveness = 犀利、敏锐

incisor
核心：门牙 —— 前面用来切断食物的牙齿，人类上下各四颗。跟 canine（犬齿）、molar（臼齿）并列。
① The boy knocked out one of his front incisors playing hockey.
= 那男孩打曲棍球时磕掉了一颗门牙。
an incisor = 门牙
② Rabbits' incisors never stop growing, so they need to chew hard food.
= 兔子的门牙会一直长，所以它们需要啃硬东西。
incisors = 门牙（复数）

incite
核心：煽动、激起 —— 鼓动人去做（暴力、违法的）事。常见 incite somebody to do something、
incite violence/hatred。名词 incitement 并入本条。
① After the match, a local politician was accused of inciting the angry crowd to attack the police.
= 比赛结束后，一名当地政客被指控煽动愤怒的人群袭击警察。
incite somebody to do something = 煽动某人做…
② The speech was designed to incite hatred against immigrants.
= 那番讲话的目的就是煽动对移民的仇恨。
incite hatred = 煽动仇恨
③ The preacher was jailed for five years for incitement to violence in his online videos.
= 那名传教者因在网上视频里煽动暴力被判入狱五年。
incitement to violence = 煽动暴力

incivility
核心：（正式）无礼、粗鲁 —— civility（礼貌）的反面。不可数时指态度，可数时指一句无礼的话或举动。
① Shop staff complained to the manager about the growing incivility of customers at the weekend.
= 店员们向经理抱怨，周末的顾客越来越无礼。
incivility = 无礼
② The televised debate was marked by personal insults and petty incivilities on both sides.
= 那场电视辩论里，双方都有人身攻击和一些小小的失礼之举。
petty incivilities = 小小的失礼

incl.
核心：包括 —— including 的书面缩写，多见于价目、广告、清单。
① The price is two hundred euros per person, incl. flights and hotel.
= 价格为每人两百欧元，包括机票和酒店。
incl. = 包括
② Rent: £800 per month incl. bills, available from the first of June.
= 租金：每月八百英镑，含水电等杂费，六月一日起可入住。
incl. bills = 含杂费

inclement
核心：（正式）（天气）恶劣的、寒冷多雨的 —— 几乎只说 weather。名词 inclemency 并入本条。
① The match was cancelled because of inclement weather.
= 由于天气恶劣，比赛取消了。
inclement weather = 恶劣天气
② The shepherds built stone huts to shelter from the inclemency of the winters.
= 牧羊人们搭起石屋，躲避冬天的严寒。
the inclemency of the weather = 天气的恶劣

inclination
核心：倾向、意愿；倾斜。本义是「斜过去」：身体或物体的倾斜（an inclination of the head，点头）；
由此比喻心里「偏向」某事 —— 意愿（have no inclination to do）、倾向（an inclination towards）。
① After a long week at work, I have no inclination to go out tonight; I'd rather stay in and read.
= 忙了一整周，我今晚一点也不想出去，宁愿待在家里看书。
have no inclination to do something = 不想做…
② Left to himself, he has a natural inclination towards laziness and late breakfasts.
= 没人管的时候，他天生就爱偷懒、爱晚起吃早饭。
an inclination towards something = 对…的倾向
③ She greeted him with a slight inclination of her head.
= 她微微点头跟他打招呼。
an inclination of the head = 点头
本义：身体向下倾斜。
④ When choosing a career, follow your own inclinations rather than your parents' wishes.
= 选择职业时，要顺从自己的意愿，而不是父母的愿望。
follow your inclinations = 顺从自己的意愿

incline
核心：倾斜；使倾向。动词（重音在后）：本义是斜（the road inclines），比喻让人偏向（incline somebody to/towards）；
（正式）I incline to the view that…（我倾向于认为）。名词（重音在前）是斜坡。形容词 inclined 另立。
① The path inclines steeply towards the river.
= 小路朝河边陡然下斜。
incline = 倾斜
② I incline to the view that we should wait another year.
= 我倾向于认为我们应该再等一年。
incline to the view that = 倾向于认为
③ His health problems inclined him towards an early retirement.
= 健康问题让他倾向于提前退休。
incline somebody towards something = 使某人倾向于…
④ The cyclists struggled up a steep incline at the end of the race.
= 比赛最后，骑手们吃力地骑上一段陡坡。
a steep incline = 陡坡

inclined
核心：倾向于…的、想要…的 —— 多作表语：be inclined to do something（有意做；往往会）。
另有「有…天赋的」：musically inclined。也作「倾斜的」。
① I'm inclined to agree with you, though I still have some doubts.
= 我倾向于同意你的看法，虽然还有些疑虑。
be inclined to do something = 倾向于做…
② He is inclined to lose his temper when he is tired.
= 他累了就容易发脾气。
be inclined to do something = 往往会…
说习性：容易怎样，多是不好的习惯。
③ Their son is musically inclined and plays three instruments.
= 他们的儿子有音乐天赋，会三种乐器。
musically inclined = 有音乐天赋的
④ The roof is inclined at an angle of thirty degrees.
= 屋顶倾斜三十度。
be inclined at an angle = 成一定角度倾斜

include
核心：包括、包含；把…算进去。两支：一个整体里含有某部分（The price includes breakfast）；
主动把人或事物算进去（include somebody in something）。形容词 included（包括在内的，放名词后）并入本条；
介词 including 另立。
① The price includes breakfast and a guided tour of the castle.
= 价格包括早餐和城堡导览。
include something = 包括…
② Don't forget to include your phone number on the form.
= 别忘了在表上写上你的电话号码。
include something on something = 把…写进…
③ They always include the new students in their weekend trips.
= 他们周末出游总会带上新来的学生。
include somebody in something = 把某人算进…
④ All the family came, the grandparents included.
= 全家人都来了，连祖父母也来了。
somebody included = 包括某人在内
放在名词后面，意思同 including。

including
核心：包括 —— 介词，引出整体里的一部分，常用来举例或强调某部分也算在内。
跟 included 的位置相反：including X 放前面，X included 放后面。
① Six people, including two children, were injured in the crash.
= 车祸中有六人受伤，其中包括两名儿童。
including somebody = 包括某人
② The ticket costs forty pounds, including a drink in the interval.
= 票价四十英镑，含幕间一杯饮料。
including something = 含…
③ The shop is open every day up to and including Christmas Eve.
= 那家店每天营业，一直到平安夜当天。
up to and including = 直到并包括

inclusion
核心：包括、列入；（社会上的）接纳、包容。两支：把某人或某物算进一个整体（her inclusion in the team）；
让弱势群体不被排除在外（social inclusion）。可数时指被列入的那一项。
① Her inclusion in the national team surprised everyone, including her.
= 她入选国家队让所有人都吃了一惊，包括她自己。
somebody's inclusion in something = 某人入选…
② The editor defended the inclusion of three unknown poets in the anthology.
= 编辑为诗选收入三位无名诗人的做法辩护。
the inclusion of something in something = 把…收入…
③ The charity works for the social inclusion of people with disabilities.
= 那家慈善机构致力于让残障人士融入社会。
social inclusion = 社会包容

inclusive
核心：包括一切的；包容的。三支：价格包含所有费用的（an all-inclusive holiday）；
（英）首尾都算在内的（from Monday to Friday inclusive）；接纳各类人、不排斥任何人的（an inclusive society）。
副词 inclusively 与名词 inclusiveness 并入本条。
① The price is fully inclusive of tax and service, so there is nothing more to pay.
= 价格已包含税费和服务费，不用再另付任何钱。
be inclusive of something = 包含…
② They booked an all-inclusive holiday where food and drinks were free all week.
= 他们订了全包式度假，一整周吃喝全免费。
an all-inclusive holiday = 全包式度假
③ The museum is closed from the 24th to the 26th December inclusive.
= 博物馆十二月二十四日至二十六日闭馆，首尾两天都包括在内。
from A to B inclusive = 从A到B（含首尾）
英式说法；美式说 through：Monday through Friday。
④ The school aims to be an inclusive community where every child feels welcome.
= 那所学校力求成为一个包容的集体，让每个孩子都感到被接纳。
an inclusive community = 包容的集体
⑤ The new guidelines ask writers to use language more inclusively.
= 新的写作指引要求作者用词更具包容性。
inclusively = 包容地
⑥ The inclusiveness of the festival attracted families from every part of the city.
= 那个节日的包容性吸引了来自城里各处的家庭。
inclusiveness = 包容性

incognito
核心：隐姓埋名地、化名地 —— 从意大利语「不被认出的」借来。多说名人、王室微服出行。
作副词或形容词：travel incognito、remain incognito。
① The film star travelled incognito, wearing dark glasses and a cheap coat.
= 那位影星戴着墨镜、穿着便宜外套，隐姓埋名地出行。
travel incognito = 微服出行
② The king liked to visit the markets incognito to hear what people really thought.
= 国王喜欢微服去市场，听听百姓的真心话。
visit somewhere incognito = 微服造访某地
③ Browsing in incognito mode stops your history being saved on the computer.
= 用无痕模式浏览，电脑上就不会留下浏览记录。
incognito mode = （浏览器）无痕模式

incoherent
核心：语无伦次的；不连贯的、不合逻辑的。两支：人说话前言不搭后语（醉酒、激动、病中）；
论点、政策各部分彼此不一致。名词 incoherence 与副词 incoherently 并入本条。
① By midnight he was drunk and completely incoherent.
= 到半夜他已经醉得语无伦次了。
be incoherent = 语无伦次
② Critics described the government's energy policy as incoherent and short-sighted.
= 批评者说政府的能源政策前后矛盾、目光短浅。
an incoherent policy = 前后矛盾的政策
③ She was sobbing and talking incoherently about a car and a bridge.
= 她抽泣着，语无伦次地说着什么车和桥。
talk incoherently = 语无伦次地说
④ The incoherence of the plot made the film hard to follow.
= 情节的不连贯让那部电影很难看懂。
the incoherence of something = …的不连贯

income
核心：收入 —— 定期从工作、投资等得到的钱。可数不可数都常用：a high income、income from rent。
常见的是 on a low income（收入低）、live within your income（量入为出）。
income tax（所得税）与 income support（英，收入补助）并入本条。
① Families on a low income will receive extra help with heating bills.
= 低收入家庭将在取暖费上获得额外补助。
on a low income = 收入低
② Most of their income comes from renting out rooms to students.
= 他们的收入大部分来自把房间租给学生。
income from something = 来自…的收入
③ If you learn to live within your income, you will never be in debt.
= 学会量入为出，就永远不会欠债。
live within your income = 量入为出
④ Income tax is taken from your salary before you receive it.
= 所得税在你拿到工资前就被扣掉了。
income tax = 所得税
⑤ After losing her job she lived on income support for a year.
= 丢了工作以后，她靠收入补助生活了一年。
income support = （英）收入补助
英国旧时给低收入者的一种社会福利。

incomer
核心：（英）外来户、新来的居民 —— 从别处搬来定居的人，多说在小地方被当地人看作外人。
① The villagers were suspicious of incomers from the city who bought up the old cottages.
= 村民们对那些从城里搬来、买下老屋的外来户心存戒备。
an incomer = 外来户
② After twenty years in the town, she was still regarded as an incomer by some neighbours.
= 在镇上住了二十年，她在一些邻居眼里还是个外来户。
be regarded as an incomer = 被看作外来户

incoming
核心：进来的、到来的；新任的。两支：正在到来的（incoming calls、incoming flights、the incoming tide）；
即将接任的（the incoming president）。反义是 outgoing。
① The phone only accepts incoming calls because she hasn't paid the bill.
= 她没交话费，手机只能接不能打。
incoming calls = 呼入的电话
② The incoming tide covered the rocks where the children had been playing.
= 涨潮淹没了孩子们刚才玩耍的岩石。
the incoming tide = 涨潮
③ The incoming government has promised to cut taxes in its first year.
= 新一届政府承诺上任第一年就减税。
the incoming government = 新一届政府

incommensurable
核心：（正式）不可比的、没有共同标准的 —— 两样东西性质不同，没法放在同一把尺子上量。
① Some philosophers argue that happiness and money are incommensurable values.
= 有些哲学家认为，幸福和金钱是无法相提并论的价值。
incommensurable values = 不可比的价值
② The two theories use incommensurable concepts, so neither can prove the other wrong.
= 两种理论用的概念彼此不可通约，所以谁也证明不了对方错。
incommensurable concepts = 不可通约的概念

incommensurate
核心：（正式）不相称的、不成比例的 —— 常接 with：跟…不相称。多说报酬、惩罚与付出、过错不相称。
① The fine was incommensurate with the seriousness of the crime.
= 那笔罚款与罪行的严重程度不相称。
be incommensurate with something = 与…不相称
② Nurses complain that their pay is incommensurate with the responsibility they carry.
= 护士们抱怨，她们的工资与所承担的责任不相称。
incommensurate pay = 不相称的报酬
