roost
/ruːst/
核心：名词，可数：（鸟的）栖息处、栖木。动词：（鸟）栖息、进窝。
习语：rule the roost 是当家作主、说了算；come home to roost 是（坏事）自食其果、报应到自己头上。
① At night the hens go back to their roost in the barn.
= 晚上母鸡们回到谷仓里的栖木上。
a roost = 栖息处
② Thousands of starlings roost under the old pier every winter.
= 每年冬天，成千上万只椋鸟栖息在那座旧码头下面。
roost = 栖息
③ In their house, it is the grandmother who rules the roost.
= 在他们家，是奶奶说了算。
rule the roost = 当家作主
④ He spent years telling lies to his friends, and now his lies have come home to roost.
= 他多年来对朋友撒谎，如今自食其果了。
come home to roost = 自食其果

rooster
/ˈruːstə(r)/
核心：（尤指美式）公鸡：英式多说 cock。可数。
① The rooster on the farm next door crows at four o'clock every morning.
= 隔壁农场的公鸡每天凌晨四点打鸣。
a rooster = 公鸡
② The weather vane on top of the church is shaped like a rooster.
= 教堂顶上的风向标是公鸡形状的。
shaped like a rooster = 公鸡形状的

root
/ruːt/
核心：名词，可数：（植物的）根；（牙齿、头发、指甲的）根部；（问题、麻烦的）根源：the root of the problem、root cause、be at the root of something；复数 roots 指一个人的出身、根、与故乡的联系；（语言）词根。
动词：（植物、插条）生根；（猪等）用鼻子拱地找食；（口语）翻找：root around / about in something。
习语：take root 是生根，也指想法、习惯扎下根来；put down roots 是（在某地）扎根；root and branch 是彻底地、从根本上。
① We dug up the weed very carefully so that no piece of the root was left behind in the soil.
= 我们非常小心地把那棵杂草挖出来，好让土里不留下一点根。
root = 根
the root of a plant = 植物的根
② The young willow quickly took root in the wet ground beside the river and grew tall within a few years.
= 那棵小柳树在河边的湿地里很快生了根，没几年就长得很高。
take root = 生根
③ The idea of working from home took root during the long lockdown and never really went away afterwards.
= 在家办公的想法在漫长的封控期间扎下了根，此后再也没有真正消失。
an idea takes root = 想法扎下根来
这一义从植物生根引申到想法、习惯、制度在人群中站稳。
④ Engineers spent several weeks looking for the root cause of the fire before they finally blamed the old wiring.
= 工程师们花了好几个星期寻找火灾的根本原因，最后才认定是老化的电线。
root cause = 根本原因
⑤ The doctor told him that stress at work, not his diet, was the root of the problem with his stomach.
= 医生告诉他，他胃病问题的根源是工作压力，而不是饮食。
the root of the problem = 问题的根源
⑥ Jealousy was at the root of the long quarrel between the two brothers over their father's farm.
= 兄弟俩为父亲的农场长年争执，根子在于嫉妒。
be at the root of something = 是某事的根源
⑦ After twenty years in New York, she went back to Ireland to discover her roots and find her family's old house.
= 在纽约住了二十年后，她回到爱尔兰寻根，想找到家族的老房子。
discover your roots = 寻根
roots = 出身、根
复数 roots 指人的来历、出身与故土的联系，多和 your、family 连用。
⑧ We moved house so often when I was a child that my family never had the chance to put down roots anywhere.
= 我小时候我们搬家太频繁，一家人在哪儿都没机会扎根。
put down roots = 扎根
⑨ The dentist explained that the root of the tooth was badly damaged, even though the top still looked healthy.
= 牙医解释说，虽然牙齿的上半部看着还好，牙根却已经严重受损。
the root of a tooth = 牙根
⑩ Teachers often show students the root of a word, such as port in export, to help them guess what it means.
= 老师常给学生指出单词的词根，比如「export」里的「port」，帮他们猜词义。
the root of a word = 词根
⑪ The new government promised root and branch reform of the tax system after years of scandals and waste.
= 经过多年的丑闻和浪费之后，新政府承诺对税制进行彻底改革。
root and branch = 彻底地、从根本上
⑫ She kept rooting around in her handbag for several minutes before she finally found her car keys.
= 她在手提包里翻找了好几分钟，才终于找到车钥匙。
root around in something = 在某处翻找
⑬ Cuttings from this plant will root easily if you leave them in a glass of water on a sunny windowsill.
= 这种植物的插条放在向阳窗台上的一杯水里，很容易生根。
cuttings root = 插条生根
⑭ The pigs were rooting in the mud under the oak trees for the acorns that had fallen in the night.
= 猪在橡树下的泥地里用鼻子拱，找夜里掉下来的橡子。
root in the mud = （猪等）拱地找食

rooted
/ˈruːtɪd/
核心：形容词，从 root 的过去分词来：be rooted in something 是植根于、根源在于；deeply rooted 是根深蒂固的；be rooted to the spot 是（吓得、惊得）呆立原地、动弹不得。
① Her love of music was rooted in the folk songs her grandmother sang to her every evening as a child.
= 她对音乐的热爱，植根于小时候祖母每晚给她唱的民歌。
be rooted in something = 植根于某事
② Prejudice of this kind is often deeply rooted, and one short training course will not change how people think.
= 这种偏见往往根深蒂固，一门短期培训课改变不了人们的想法。
deeply rooted = 根深蒂固的
多修饰偏见、信仰、传统、恐惧这类抽象名词，带「难以改变」的意味。
③ When the bear walked out of the trees, I was rooted to the spot and could not even scream for help.
= 那头熊从树林里走出来时，我吓得呆立原地，连呼救都喊不出来。
be rooted to the spot = 呆立原地

rooter

rooters = 助威的观众

rootin'-tootin'

a rootin'-tootin' cowboy = 粗豪张扬的牛仔

/ˈruːtləs/

rope
/rəʊp/
核心：名词：绳子、绳索（不可数，一根是 a rope / a piece of rope）；（珍珠、洋葱等穿成的）一串：a rope of pearls；the ropes 原指拳击台四周的围绳，由此有 on the ropes（岌岌可危）和「门道」两支。
动词：用绳子捆、拴：rope together。

= 农夫用一截旧绳子把大门拴上，免得羊跑出去。

a piece of rope = 一截绳子
② The climbers roped themselves together before they started to cross the dangerous ice above the valley.
= 登山者们在开始穿越山谷上方那片危险的冰面之前，用绳子把彼此拴在了一起。
rope together = 用绳子拴在一起
③ On my first day at the café, an older waiter showed me the ropes and explained how the till worked.
= 我在咖啡馆上班的第一天，一位老服务员教了我门道，还讲了收银机怎么用。
show somebody the ropes = 教人门道
④ It takes most new nurses a few months to learn the ropes on a busy hospital ward.
= 大多数新护士要花几个月，才能在忙碌的医院病房里摸清门道。
learn the ropes = 摸清门道
⑤ In the playground at lunchtime, the girls like to jump rope and sing a rhyme while they count every jump.
= 午饭时间在操场上，女孩们喜欢跳绳，一边唱儿歌一边数跳了多少下。

⑥ After three sleepless nights with the crying baby, the young parents were at the end of their rope.
= 被哭闹的宝宝折腾了三个不眠之夜后，那对年轻父母已经束手无策了。
at the end of one's rope = 束手无策
⑦ After losing its two biggest customers, the small company was on the ropes and close to closing down.
= 失去两个最大的客户后，那家小公司岌岌可危，眼看就要倒闭。
be on the ropes = 岌岌可危
从拳击手被打到围绳上、快撑不住引申而来。
⑧ She wore a long rope of pearls that had belonged to her grandmother to her sister's wedding.
= 她戴着祖母留下的一长串珍珠去参加姐姐的婚礼。
a rope of pearls = 一长串珍珠
⑨ Don't stop him yet; give him enough rope and he will soon make a mistake in front of the boss.
= 先别拦他；由着他去，他很快就会在老板面前出错。
give somebody enough rope = 由着某人去（迟早自食其果）

win a rosette = 赢得花结奖章
② The candidates and all their supporters wore large blue rosettes at the count on election night.
= 选举之夜计票时，候选人和他们所有的支持者都佩戴着大大的蓝色花结。
wear a rosette = 佩戴花结
③ The leaves of this little plant grow in a flat rosette close to the ground, like a green star.
= 这种小植物的叶子贴着地面长成扁平的莲座状，像一颗绿色的星星。
a rosette of leaves = 莲座状叶丛

rosewood
/ˈrəʊzwʊd/
核心：花梨木、红木：几种热带树的深红褐色硬木，做家具和乐器。不可数，也作定语：a rosewood box。
① The antique piano was made of dark rosewood and had been polished until it shone like glass.
= 那架古董钢琴是深色花梨木做的，被擦得像玻璃一样发亮。
rosewood = 花梨木
② My grandfather kept his war medals in a small rosewood box on the shelf above his bed.
= 我祖父把他的战争勋章放在床头架子上的一个小红木盒里。
a rosewood box = 红木盒子

rosin
/ˈrɒzɪn/
核心：松香：从松脂提炼出的固体，抹在弦乐器的弓毛上增加摩擦。不可数。动词：给（弓等）抹松香。
① Before every concert, the violinist rubbed rosin slowly along the hairs of her bow.
= 每次音乐会前，那位小提琴手都把松香沿着弓毛慢慢抹一遍。
rosin = 松香
② The cellist rosined his bow carefully while the rest of the orchestra were still tuning up.
= 乐团其他人还在调音时，那位大提琴手仔细地给琴弓上了松香。
rosin a bow = 给琴弓上松香

roster
/ˈrɒstə(r)/
核心：名词：值班表、轮值名单；（北美）球队名单、在册人员名单：on the roster。可数。动词（英式、澳式）：排班：be rostered on。
① The manager puts the staff roster on the noticeboard every Friday, so we know our shifts for the next week.
= 经理每周五把员工值班表贴在布告栏上，好让我们知道下周的班次。
a staff roster = 员工值班表
② The team has several new players on its roster this season after losing three of its stars in the summer.
= 夏天走了三名球星之后，这支球队本赛季的名单上有好几名新球员。
on the roster = 在名单上
③ I have been rostered on every weekend this month, so I won't be able to come to the party.
= 这个月每个周末都排了我的班，所以我去不了聚会。
be rostered on = 被排上班
这一动词义多见于英式、澳式英语，北美一般说 be scheduled。

rostrum
/ˈrɒstrəm/
核心：讲台、指挥台、领奖台：比地面高出一截、供人站上去讲话、指挥或领奖的台子。可数，复数 rostrums 或 rostra。
① The conductor stepped onto the rostrum and raised his baton, and the whole hall fell silent.
= 指挥走上指挥台，举起指挥棒，整个大厅安静下来。
the conductor's rostrum = 指挥台
② The minister walked slowly to the rostrum to deliver her speech to the party conference.
= 那位部长慢慢走上讲台，向党代会发表讲话。
walk to the rostrum = 走上讲台
③ The young swimmer stood on the top step of the rostrum while her national anthem played.
= 国歌奏响时，那位年轻的游泳选手站在领奖台的最高一级上。
on the rostrum = 在领奖台上
领奖台义英式常用 rostrum，北美多说 podium。

rosy
/ˈrəʊzi/
核心：粉红的、红润的（脸色、天色）；（比喻）乐观的、美好的：a rosy future、paint a rosy picture。形容词，比较级 rosier、最高级 rosiest。
① The children came in from the snow with rosy cheeks and wet gloves, asking for hot chocolate.
= 孩子们从雪地里进来，脸蛋红扑扑的，手套湿透了，嚷着要喝热巧克力。
rosy cheeks = 红润的脸颊
② The bank painted a rosy picture of the economy, but many families were still struggling to pay their bills.
= 银行把经济描绘得一片大好，可许多家庭还在为付账单发愁。
paint a rosy picture = 描绘美好前景
③ Their future looked rosy after both of them found good jobs in the same city.
= 两人都在同一座城市找到好工作之后，他们的前景看起来一片光明。
look rosy = 前景看好
比喻义常用于 future、picture、outlook，否定说 not look so rosy。
