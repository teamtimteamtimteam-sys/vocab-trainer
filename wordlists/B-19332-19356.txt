riser
/ˈraɪzə(r)/
核心：一是 early riser 是早起的人、late riser 是晚起的人；二是（楼梯的）竖板；三是（舞台上的）平台、台阶状看台，多用复数。
① My father is an early riser and has usually walked the dog before we wake up.
= 我父亲起得早，往往我们还没醒，他就已经遛完狗了。
an early riser = 早起的人
a late riser = 晚起的人
② The carpenter measured the height of each riser before building the new stairs.
= 木匠在做新楼梯之前量了每块竖板的高度。
a riser = 楼梯竖板
③ The choir stood on risers at the back of the stage so that everyone could see them.
= 合唱团站在舞台后面的阶梯台上，好让大家都看得见。
risers = 阶梯台

risible
/ˈrɪzəbl/
核心：（正式，贬义）可笑的、荒唐的、不值一提的。
① The company offered the workers a risible pay increase of less than one per cent.
= 公司给工人们开出不到百分之一的加薪，少得可笑。
a risible pay increase = 可笑的加薪
risible = 可笑的
② His claim that he had never seen the money before was frankly risible.
= 他说自己从来没见过那笔钱，老实说荒唐至极。
frankly risible = 荒唐至极

rising
/ˈraɪzɪŋ/
核心：形容词：上升的、增长的：rising prices；（太阳）正在升起的；新兴的、正在成名的：a rising star。名词，可数：起义、暴动。
① Rising prices mean that many families can no longer afford to eat meat every day.
= 物价上涨，很多家庭再也吃不起天天有肉的饭了。
rising prices = 上涨的物价
rising = 上升的
② We sat on the beach and watched the rising sun turn the sea pink.
= 我们坐在沙滩上，看初升的太阳把海面染成粉红色。
the rising sun = 初升的太阳
③ She is a rising star of British tennis and has already beaten two top players.
= 她是英国网坛的新星，已经打败过两位顶尖选手。
a rising star = 新星
④ The rising of 1798 was put down by the army within a few months.
= 1798 年的起义几个月内就被军队镇压了。
a rising = 起义
⑤ The walls of the old cottage were wet and smelly because of rising damp.
= 那座老农舍的墙因为地下返潮又湿又臭。
rising damp = （墙上的）返潮
⑥ The firefighters connected their hoses to the rising main on the ground floor of the tower.
= 消防员把水管接到大楼一楼的立管上。
a rising main = （楼房的）给水立管
⑦ She reads her horoscope every day and says her rising sign explains her shyness.
= 她每天看星座运势，说自己的上升星座能解释她为什么害羞。
a rising sign = 上升星座

risk
/rɪsk/
核心：名词：危险、风险，可数或不可数：a risk of、at risk；冒险，可数：take a risk；危险的人或事，可数：a fire risk、a security risk。
动词：冒……的危险、使……遭受危险：risk your life；冒险做：risk doing something。
习语：at your own risk 是风险自负；run the risk of 是冒……的风险；at the risk of 是冒着……的风险。
① He took a risk when he left his safe job to start his own business.
= 他辞掉安稳工作自己创业，是冒了险的。
take a risk = 冒险
② Smoking increases the risk of heart disease and many kinds of cancer.
= 吸烟增加患心脏病和许多种癌症的风险。
a risk of = …的风险
③ Climbing without a rope is risky, and climbing alone in winter is even riskier.
= 不系绳攀岩是冒险的，冬天独自攀岩更冒险。
risky = 冒险的
④ Children who do not get enough to eat are at risk of falling behind at school.
= 吃不饱的孩子在学校有跟不上的危险。
be at risk of something = 有某种危险
⑤ Those old curtains are a fire risk and should be thrown away.
= 那些旧窗帘有失火的危险，应该扔掉。
a fire risk = 火灾隐患
⑥ The firefighter risked his life to save the child from the burning house.
= 消防员冒着生命危险从着火的房子里救出那个孩子。
risk your life = 冒生命危险
⑦ I did not want to risk missing the last train, so I left the party early.
= 我不想冒误了末班车的险，就早早离开了聚会。
risk doing something = 冒险做某事
⑧ The sign said that people swim in the lake at their own risk.
= 告示上说在湖里游泳风险自负。
at your own risk = 风险自负
⑨ If you do not check the brakes, you run the risk of a serious accident.
= 不检查刹车，就冒着出严重事故的风险。
run the risk of something = 冒某种风险
⑩ At the risk of sounding rude, I think your plan will cost far too much.
= 恕我冒昧，我觉得你的计划花钱太多了。
at the risk of doing something = 冒着做某事的风险
⑪ Some managers encourage risk-taking because they believe new ideas need a chance to fail.
= 有些经理鼓励冒险，因为他们认为新想法需要有失败的机会。
risk-taking = 冒险（行为）

worry about the riskiness = 担心危险性

risky

= 借钱给朋友可能有风险，因为有时会断送友谊。

③ He drove riskily along the narrow mountain road, passing lorries on the bends.
= 他在狭窄的山路上冒险开车，在弯道上超卡车。
drive riskily = 冒险开车
riskily = 冒险地
④ The company invested riskily in new technology and lost most of its money.
= 那家公司冒险投资新技术，赔掉了大部分钱。
invest riskily = 冒险投资

risotto
