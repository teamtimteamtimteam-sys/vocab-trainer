filibuster
核心：（政治）冗长发言阻挠议事：靠一直讲话把议案拖死。名词与动词同形。
① The filibuster lasted eleven hours and the bill passed the following week.
= 那场冗长阻挠讲了十一个钟头，法案下一周照样通过。
a filibuster = 冗长发言阻挠
② He filibustered until the clock ran out and drank nine glasses of water.
= 他一直讲到散会时间，喝了九杯水。
filibuster a bill = 用冗长发言拖死一项议案

filigree
核心：金银丝细工：把细丝盘成花纹的装饰；引申指精细繁复的纹样。不可数。
① The filigree on the lid is silver and the box underneath it is tin.
= 盒盖上的细丝花是银的，底下的盒子是马口铁。
filigree = 金银丝细工
② A filigree of frost covered the window and lasted until nine.
= 一层花纹般的霜盖住窗子，一直留到九点。
a filigree of something = 某物结成的细密花纹

filing
核心：两支。一是归档这件活儿：do the filing；二是复数 filings，锉屑。
① The filing takes an afternoon a week and gets left for a month.
= 归档一星期要花一个下午，往往一拖就是一个月。
do the filing = 做归档
a filing cabinet = 文件柜
② Iron filings on a sheet of paper show the field better than any diagram.
= 纸上的铁屑，把磁场显示得比任何示意图都清楚。
iron filings = 铁屑
③ The filing clerk left in June and the filing itself did not.
= 那位归档员六月走了，归档的活儿留了下来。
a filing clerk = 归档员

Filipina
核心：菲律宾女子：只说女性；男性与统称用 Filipino。可数。
① A Filipina nurse trained here in 1998 and runs the ward now.
= 一位菲律宾护士 1998 年在这里受训，如今管着整个病房。
a Filipina = 菲律宾女子
Filipina nurses = 菲律宾籍护士
② Two Filipinas share a flat and send most of what they earn home.
= 两位菲律宾女子合租一套房，挣的大半寄回了家。

Filipino
核心：菲律宾的、菲律宾人；也指菲律宾语。女性单独称 Filipina。
① Filipino families in that street cook for everybody at Christmas.
= 那条街上的菲律宾家庭，圣诞节给所有人做饭。
a Filipino = 菲律宾人
② Filipino is taught in two schools in the city and spoken in forty.
= 菲律宾语在城里两所学校教，在四十所学校有人说。
Filipino = 菲律宾语

fill
核心：四支。一是装满、填满：fill a glass；二是填补空缺：fill a vacancy；
三是名词，足量：eat your fill；四是短语动词 fill in 与 fill up。
习语 fill the bill 是「正合适」。
① Fill the glass to the line and not to the top.
= 倒到那条线为止，别倒满。
fill a glass = 把杯子倒满
② The vacancy was filled by somebody who had left the year before.
= 那个空缺，由一位前一年离职的人补上了。
fill a vacancy = 填补空缺
③ They ate their fill and took the rest home in a box.
= 他们吃了个饱，剩下的装盒带回家。
eat your fill = 吃个饱
④ Fill in the form in black ink and fill up the car before you set off.
= 表格用黑墨水填，出发前把车加满油。
fill in something = 填写某物
fill up = 加满
⑤ A folding table fills the bill and costs eleven pounds.
= 一张折叠桌正合适，才十一镑钱。
fill the bill = 正合适
⑥ Fill out the form in block capitals and post it before Thursday.
= 表格用正楷填，周四之前寄出去。
fill out = 填写（美式）
⑦ They fill up on bread and leave the expensive part on the plate.
= 他们拿面包填饱肚子，贵的那部分留在盘子里。
fill up on something = 拿某物填饱肚子
⑧ A fill light softens the shadow that the main lamp makes.
= 补光灯柔化主灯打出的那道阴影。
a fill light = 补光灯

filler
核心：三支。一是填料、腻子：wood filler；二是（版面、时间的）补白：a filler item；
三是说话时的填充词，如 um 与 you know。可数。
① Wood filler dries lighter than the wood and everybody can see the repair.
= 木用腻子干了之后比木头浅，修过的地方谁都看得出。
wood filler = 木用腻子
② The bulletin ran a filler about a duck and dropped the council story.
= 那档新闻插了一条关于鸭子的补白，把议会那条撤了。
a filler item = 补白的小条目
③ The filler cap is on the wrong side at every petrol station in the county.
= 全郡的加油站，油箱盖都在不对的那一边。
a filler cap = 油箱盖

fillet
核心：（英式）去骨的肉片、鱼片；也作动词，给鱼或肉剔骨。美式拼 filet。
① The fishmonger fillets it in nine seconds and wraps it in two.
= 鱼贩九秒钟剔完骨，两秒钟包好。
fillet a fish = 给鱼剔骨
② A fillet of beef feeds four and costs what eight would cost elsewhere.
= 一块牛里脊够四个人吃，价钱抵别处八个人的。
a fillet of beef = 牛里脊

filling
核心：三支。一是（牙齿的）补料；二是（食物的）馅；三是形容词，扛饿的。
① The filling came out on a toffee and the dentist was closed until Monday.
= 补牙的填料被一块太妃糖粘掉了，牙医周一才开门。
a filling = 补牙的填料
② The filling is mostly onion and the pastry is mostly air.
= 那馅儿多半是洋葱，那酥皮多半是空气。
the filling = 馅料
③ Porridge is filling, cheap and taken very seriously in that house.
= 燕麦粥扛饿、便宜，在那家人眼里是很正经的东西。
a filling meal = 扛饿的一顿饭
④ The filling station on that road closed and the next one is nineteen miles away.
= 那条路上的加油站关了，下一个在十九英里外。
a filling station = 加油站

fillip
核心：（正式）提振、一剂强心针：给事情添把劲的东西。可数，
最常见的说法是 give something a fillip。
① The grant gave the whole scheme a fillip and paid for one van.
= 那笔拨款给整个项目提了口气，买下一辆货车。
give something a fillip = 给某事添把劲
② A dry week in June was exactly the fillip the harvest needed.
= 六月里干爽的一个星期，正是收成需要的那口气。
a fillip = 一剂提振

filly
核心：小母马：四岁以下的雌马，跟小公马 colt 相对。可数。
① The filly ran second and cost more than the horse that won.
= 那匹小母马跑了第二，身价却比冠军还高。
a filly = 小母马
a two-year-old filly = 两岁的小母马
② Two fillies were entered and only one of them had ever seen a crowd.
= 有两匹小母马报了名，只有一匹见过人山人海。

film
核心：四支。一是电影：a film star、see a film；二是胶卷：a roll of film；
三是薄膜、一层：a film of dust；四是动词，拍摄。
① A film star opened the shop and the queue went round two corners.
= 一位电影明星给那家店剪彩，队伍拐了两个弯。
a film star = 电影明星
see a film = 看电影
② A roll of film sat in a drawer for eleven years and came out fine.
= 一卷胶卷在抽屉里躺了十一年，冲出来居然没坏。
a roll of film = 一卷胶卷
③ A film of dust on the piano says how long the family has been away.
= 钢琴上那层薄灰，说明那家人走了多久。
a film of dust = 一层薄灰
④ They filmed the whole street in a day and used forty seconds of it.
= 他们一天拍完整条街，用上的只有四十秒。
film something = 拍摄某物
⑤ The film-maker spent a year on film-making and eleven minutes on the title.
= 那位电影人拍片花了一年，起片名花了十一分钟。
a film-maker = 电影人
film-making = 电影制作
⑥ The film certificate says fifteen and the queue is mostly twelve.
= 那部片子定级十五岁，排队的多半十二岁。
a film certificate = 电影分级
⑦ Film noir is mostly rain, hats and light coming through blinds.
= 黑色电影多半就是雨、帽子，还有百叶窗缝里透进来的光。
film noir = 黑色电影

filmgoer

a filmgoer = 常看电影的人
regular filmgoers = 常跑影院的观众
② The average filmgoer arrives after the trailers and leaves before the credits.
= 一般影迷预告片放完才进场，字幕没完就走。

filmic
核心：（正式）电影的、有电影感的：说手法与画面像电影，而不像戏剧或小说。
① The novel is filmic and three studios have optioned it and done nothing.
= 那部小说很有电影感，三家片厂买了改编权，都没动。
filmic = 有电影感的
② A filmic sense of pace is what makes the middle chapters move.
= 正是一种电影式的节奏感，让中间几章推得动。
filmic techniques = 电影化的手法

filming
核心：拍摄这件事：不可数 —— filming starts、filming schedule。
① Filming starts in April and the street will be closed for six weeks.
= 四月开拍，那条街要封六个星期。
filming = 拍摄
② Filming in that weather cost them nine days and one camera.

a filming schedule = 拍摄日程

filmography
核心：（影视）作品年表：某位导演或演员拍过的全部片子的清单。可数。

a filmography = 作品年表

filmstrip

① The filmstrip advanced with a click and half the class waited for the next one.

a filmstrip = 教学幻灯胶片

② A filmstrip and a tape recorder counted as technology in 1974.

filmy

filter

② They filter the water twice and still boil it before drinking.
= 他们把水过滤两遍，喝之前还是要煮开。

③ The system filters out anything with two exclamation marks in it.

④ News of the closure filtered through the building by lunchtime.
= 关门的消息到午饭时已经在整栋楼里传开。

filth

filthiness

filthy
核心：三支。一是极脏的：filthy hands；二是下流的：a filthy joke；
三是（英式口语）糟透的：filthy weather；另有 filthy rich，富得流油。副词 filthily。
① He came in with filthy hands and washed them in the wrong sink.
= 他手脏兮兮地进来，在不该用的那个水槽里洗了。
filthy hands = 脏手
② A filthy joke at that table cost him the contract.
= 在那张桌上讲了个下流笑话，他把合同弄丢了。
a filthy joke = 下流笑话
③ Filthy weather kept the boats in the harbour and the pub full.
= 恶劣的天气把船留在港里，把酒馆挤满。
filthy weather = 糟透的天气
filthy rich = 富得流油
副词 filthily：脏兮兮地、下流地，用得不多。
④ The yard was filthily neglected and the garden behind it was immaculate.
= 那个院子脏得没人打理，后头的花园却一尘不染。
filthily neglected = 脏得无人搭理
