inconspicuous
/ˌɪnkənˈspɪkjuəs/
核心：不显眼的、不引人注目的 —— 想不被注意到。副词 inconspicuously 并入本条。
① She sat at the back of the hall, trying to look as inconspicuous as possible.
= 她坐在大厅后排，尽量不让人注意到自己。
look inconspicuous = 显得不起眼
② The security cameras are small and inconspicuously placed above each door.
= 监控摄像头很小，不显眼地装在每扇门的上方。
inconspicuously = 不显眼地

inconstant
/ɪnˈkɒnstənt/
核心：（正式或文）易变的、不忠的 —— 多说爱人、感情、运气。名词 inconstancy 并入本条。
① In the old songs, the lover is always inconstant and the heart is always broken.
= 在那些老歌里，情人总是用情不专，心总是要碎的。
an inconstant lover = 用情不专的情人
② She could not forgive his inconstancy after he left her for her best friend.
= 他为了她最好的朋友离开了她，她无法原谅他的变心。
somebody's inconstancy = 某人的变心

incontestable
/ˌɪnkənˈtestəbəl/
核心：（正式）无可争辩的、毋庸置疑的 —— 多说事实、证据、权利。副词 incontestably 并入本条。
① The photographs provided incontestable proof that he had been at the scene.
= 那些照片提供了无可争辩的证据，证明他到过现场。
incontestable proof = 无可争辩的证据
② She is incontestably the finest player the club has ever produced.
= 她无疑是这家俱乐部培养出的最出色的球员。
incontestably = 毋庸置疑地

incontinent
/ɪnˈkɒntɪnənt/
核心：（医学）大小便失禁的 —— 控制不住排泄。名词 incontinence 并入本条。旧义「不能自制的」今已罕用。
① Many elderly patients become incontinent after a stroke and need extra care.
= 许多老年病人中风后会出现失禁，需要额外照护。
become incontinent = 出现失禁
② The pharmacy sells special pads for people who suffer from incontinence.
= 那家药店出售专供失禁患者使用的护垫。
suffer from incontinence = 患有失禁

incontrovertible
/ˌɪnkɒntrəˈvɜːtəbəl/
核心：（正式）无可辩驳的、确凿的 —— 比 incontestable 更强调「驳不倒」。副词 incontrovertibly 并入本条。
① The DNA test provided incontrovertible evidence that he was the father.
= DNA 检测提供了他就是父亲的确凿证据。
incontrovertible evidence = 确凿的证据

= 这些文件无可辩驳地表明，公司早就知道危险所在。
incontrovertibly = 无可辩驳地

inconvenience
/ˌɪnkənˈviːniəns/
核心：不便、麻烦；带来不便的事。也作动词：给…带来不便。公告里的套话：
We apologize for any inconvenience caused（给您带来不便，敬请谅解）。
① We apologize for any inconvenience caused by the delay to your train.
= 列车晚点给您带来的不便，我们深表歉意。
apologize for any inconvenience = 为带来的不便道歉
② Having no car in the village was a minor inconvenience compared with the peace and quiet.
= 跟那份清静比起来，在村里没车只是个小麻烦。
a minor inconvenience = 小麻烦
可数，指一件具体的麻烦事。
③ I don't want to inconvenience you, so I'll take a taxi from the station.
= 我不想麻烦你，我从车站打车过去。
inconvenience somebody = 给某人添麻烦

inconvenient
/ˌɪnkənˈviːniənt/
核心：不方便的、不合时宜的 —— 时间、地点、事实让人不便。副词 inconveniently 并入本条。
① Is this an inconvenient time to call? I can ring back later if you like.
= 现在打电话不方便吗？你愿意的话我可以晚点再打。
an inconvenient time = 不方便的时候
② The meeting is at an inconvenient time for staff who have children at school.
= 会议时间对孩子在上学的员工很不方便。
be inconvenient for somebody = 对某人不方便
③ The new station is inconveniently located two miles from the town centre.
= 新车站位置很不方便，离市中心两英里。
inconveniently located = 位置不便的

incorporate
/ɪnˈkɔːpəreɪt/
核心：包含、吸收、并入 —— 把某物作为一部分纳进整体（incorporate something into something）。
商业上指把公司注册成法人（be incorporated）。名词 incorporation 并入本条；形容词 incorporated 另立。
① The new design incorporates many of the suggestions made by the students.
= 新设计采纳了学生们提出的许多建议。
incorporate something = 包含、采纳…
② They plan to incorporate the old farm buildings into the new hotel.
= 他们打算把旧农舍并入新酒店。
incorporate something into something = 把…并入…
③ The business was incorporated in 2015 and now employs two hundred people.
= 那家企业二〇一五年注册成立，现在有两百名员工。
be incorporated = （公司）注册成立
④ The incorporation of local music into the festival made it very popular with residents.
= 节日融入了本地音乐，很受居民欢迎。
the incorporation of something into something = 把…融入…

incorporated

Incorporated = （美）股份有限公司
② Only incorporated businesses can apply for the government's new export grants.
= 只有注册为法人的企业才能申请政府新的出口补助。
an incorporated business = 注册为法人的企业

incorporeal

incorrect

the incorrectness of something = …的错误

incorrigible
/ɪnˈkɒrɪdʒəbəl/

incorruptible

increase
/ɪnˈkriːs/（动）/ˈɪŋkriːs/（名）
核心：增加、增长 —— 动词重音在后，名词重音在前。数量、程度、大小变大。
动词可及物（increase prices）也可不及物（prices increased）；名词常接 in（an increase in prices）。
习语 on the increase（在增加）。形容词 increased（增加了的）并入本条；副词 increasingly 另立。
① The population of the town has increased from five thousand to over twelve thousand.
= 那个镇的人口从五千增加到了一万二千多。
increase from A to B = 从A增加到B
② The company plans to increase prices by ten per cent next month.
= 那家公司计划下个月把价格提高百分之十。
increase something by = 把…增加（多少）
③ There has been a sharp increase in the number of people working from home.
= 在家工作的人数急剧增加。
an increase in something = …的增加
④ Crime in the city centre is on the increase again after two quiet years.
= 平静了两年之后，市中心的犯罪又在增加。
on the increase = 在增加
⑤ Smokers face an increased risk of heart disease and cancer.
= 吸烟者患心脏病和癌症的风险更高。
an increased risk = 增加的风险

increasingly
/ɪnˈkriːsɪŋli/

① It is becoming increasingly difficult to find cheap flats in the city.
= 在这座城市找便宜公寓越来越难了。
increasingly difficult = 越来越难
② Increasingly, young people are choosing to rent rather than buy a home.
= 越来越多的年轻人选择租房而不是买房。
increasingly = 越来越多地（句首）

incredible
/ɪnˈkredɪbəl/
核心：难以置信的；（口语）极好的、惊人的。两支：本义是「让人不信」；口语里多是赞叹「太棒了」。
副词 incredibly 并入本条，口语里常用作「非常」。
① Her story about being chased by a bear sounded incredible, but it was true.
= 她说自己被熊追过，听着难以置信，却是真的。

③ The exam was incredibly difficult, and half the class failed.
= 那场考试难得出奇，全班一半人没及格。
incredibly = 非常、极其

incredulous

名词 incredulity 与副词 incredulously 并入本条。
① She gave him an incredulous look when he said he had never heard of the band.
= 他说从没听说过那支乐队时，她一脸不信地看着他。
an incredulous look = 不相信的表情
② "You paid how much for that old bike?" her father asked incredulously.
= 「那辆旧自行车你花了多少钱？」她父亲不敢相信地问。
incredulously = 不敢相信地
③ The news that the factory would close was met with incredulity by the workers.
= 工厂要关门的消息让工人们难以置信。
be met with incredulity = 让人难以置信

increment

⑤ The university runs a business incubator that helps students turn ideas into companies.

a business incubator = 创业孵化器
